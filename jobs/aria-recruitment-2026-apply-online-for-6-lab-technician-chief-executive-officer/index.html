ARIA Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Centre of Excellence in Artificial Intelligence (CoE-AI), funded by the Government of Karnataka, is hiring on a full-time contractual basis through AI Research & Innovation Arena (ARIA), a registered Section 8 company hosted in IIIT Raichur. About ARIA

AI Research & Innovation Arena (ARIA) is a registered Section 8 company hosted at the Indian Institute of Information Technology Raichur (IIIT Raichur). The Centre of Excellence in Artificial Intelligence is being set up for innovation and entrepreneurship for regional development. Its stated aims include AI model development and prototyping, experimentation, training programs, incubation support, regional outreach, and partnerships with government, industry and academia.

Pay and Tenure

The remuneration in the vacancy table is consolidated, with no other allowances, and statutory deductions apply as per rules. The final amount may be fixed by the Selection Committee within the approved budget. Experienced and meritorious candidates may be offered a higher remuneration on the Committee's recommendation, within the approved project budget. All positions are purely temporary and contractual, co-terminus with the CoE-AI project, and are not regular posts of ARIA or IIIT Raichur. The initial engagement is for six months (a shorter term for Research Interns), extendable subject to an annual performance review and the availability of project funds. Selected candidates shall have no claim to regular appointment, absorption or any service benefits. The place of work is IIIT Raichur (Raichur, Karnataka), including field visits wherever the role requires.
