About RVNL

Rail Vikas Nigam Ltd. (RVNL) has its registered office at World Trade Center, Tower A, 6th to 9th Floor, Nauroji Nagar, New Delhi- 110029. This vacancy notice was signed by the Joint General Manager/HR. It invites officers for selection on deputation basis against the one post at Lucknow.

Who Can Apply

The notice invites officers of the IRSSE / S&T Department. The minimum eligibility is given separately for each level. Officers holding substantively: posts in analogous grade (CDA/IDA); OR posts in Level-12 (CDA) and having 8-12 years' service in Group 'A'; OR posts carrying grade IDA E-5 (Rs. 80000-220000/-) for four years. Officers holding substantively: posts in analogous grade (CDA/IDA); OR posts in Sr. Scale (Level-11); OR posts in Gr. 'B' (Level-10) and having 10 years of service in Group 'B' (Gazetted); OR posts carrying grade IDA E-4 (Rs. 70000-200000/-) for four years. Preference shall be given to candidates having relevant experience in telecom infrastructure, NLD networks, broadband connectivity, optical fibre cable (OFC) network planning, laying, construction, maintenance and operations, IT/ITES, communication systems and networking.
