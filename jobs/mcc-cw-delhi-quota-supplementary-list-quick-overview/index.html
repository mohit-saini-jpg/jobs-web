MCC CW Delhi Quota Supplementary List - Quick Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 12/08/2026

The Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) has published a Supplementary List of applicants seeking concession under the CW (Children/Widows of Armed Forces Personnel) Category for admission to UG medical courses (MBBS/BDS/BAMS/BUMS/BHMS) for the 2026-2027 academic session under the 85% Delhi Quota. The list has been issued by the Faculty of Medical Sciences (FMSC), University of Delhi, and shared on the official MCC website. Candidates who applied for CW category concession as part of NEET UG Counselling 2026 can check this list to know their eligibility status.

What Is the CW Category Supplementary List?

The CW (Children/Widows of Armed Forces, Paramilitary, and Police Personnel) category is a special reservation under the 85% Delhi Quota for children and widows of defence, paramilitary, and police personnel, applicable to admissions at Delhi University-affiliated medical colleges. A supplementary list is issued when additional candidates - who applied but were left out of, or added after, the original eligibility list - get their eligibility status confirmed following verification by the Kendriya Sainik Board (KSB), Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA), or Welfare and Rehabilitation Board (WARB).

This supplementary list adds new candidate entries to the CW eligibility record already published earlier by FMSC for NEET UG Counselling 2026.

Who Should Check This List?

This list is relevant for:

  • Candidates who applied for CW category concession under NEET UG Counselling 2026 for the 85% Delhi Quota.
  • Candidates who used the CW Portal re-opening window (9- 11 August 2026) to submit or correct their CW category application.
  • Wards or widows of Defence and Police personnel seeking admission to MBBS, BDS, or AYUSH courses (BAMS/BUMS/BHMS) at Delhi University-affiliated colleges such as Maulana Azad Medical College (MAMC), University College of Medical Sciences (UCMS), and Lady Hardinge Medical College (LHMC).

How to Check Your Name in the CW Supplementary List

  • Visit the official MCC website at mcc.nic.in/ug-medical-counselling.
  • Scroll to the "News & Events" section on the page.
  • Click on the link titled "Supplementary List of eligible candidates under CW Category for admission to UG Medical Courses (MBBS/BDS/BAMS/BUMS/BHMS) 2026-2027 under 85% Delhi Quota."
  • The PDF will open or download automatically.
  • Use your NEET Roll Number or Name to locate your entry and check your "Eligible/Not Eligible" status and remarks.

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Cross-check your NEET Roll Number and NEET All India Rank carefully against the entry shown in the PDF.
  • If marked "Not Eligible," refer to the remarks column, which explains the reason as verified by KSB/MHA/WARB.
  • Any discrepancy in priority category or personal details should be raised through the official grievance/helpline channels indicated by MCC or FMSC - specific contact details for this particular notice are not mentioned in the PDF itself.
  • This supplementary list should be read together with the main CW eligibility list and any subsequent notices; MCC has not specified whether further supplementary lists will follow.
  • Candidates should keep tracking the official MCC and FMSC websites regularly, since counselling-related notices are updated frequently during the NEET UG 2026 cycle.
