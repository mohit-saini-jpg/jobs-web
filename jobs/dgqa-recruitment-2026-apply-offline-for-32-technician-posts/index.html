DGQA Technician Eligibility Criteria

The DGQA Technician eligibility conditions cover nationality, qualification, age, service-related conditions, disqualifications and medical fitness. Candidates should read each condition carefully because the notification sets specific requirements for each post and category. Only Indian nationals are eligible to apply. No person is eligible for appointment who: has entered into or contracted a marriage with a person having a spouse living; or having a spouse living, has entered into or contracted a marriage with any person, provided that the Government may exempt any person from this rule if satisfied that such marriage is permissible under the personal law applicable to such person and the other party to the marriage and that there are other grounds for doing so; or is convicted or under trial for associating with criminal offences. Candidates must be in good mental and physical health

DGQA Technician Qualification

The DGQA Technician qualification differs for the two posts. Eligible candidates must also qualify a departmental Trade Skill Test in the trade of the post for which they are being considered for appointment. DGQA Technician Selection Process

The selection process has two stages: a Written Test and a Trade Test. A merit list is then prepared, and final appointment depends on document verification, medical examination and verification of character and antecedents. Eligible candidates have to appear in the Written Test, which will be conducted in one session, either in the forenoon or afternoon of the day. The test is of objective type with multiple choice questions only. There is no negative marking for wrong answers. The examination will be conducted in Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh. The exact date, time and venue will be mentioned in the Admit Card. The question papers will be as per the syllabus in Tenth Standard / Matriculation of Central / State Boards. The question paper is in three parts. Part (i) is in English and Hindi; Parts (ii) and (iii) are in English only. Candidates are shortlisted after the Written Test based on marks
