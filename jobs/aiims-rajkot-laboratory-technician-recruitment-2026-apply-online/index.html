About AIIMS Rajkot

The All India Institute of Medical Sciences, Rajkot, at Village Para Pipaliya, Khanderi, Rajkot, Gujarat 360110, describes itself in the notice as an Institute of National Importance under PMSSY, MoHFW, Government of India. The Department of Microbiology runs this recruitment for the NOHP-PCZ project. The Principal Investigator is Dr. (Col) Ashwini Agarwal.

Pay and Tenure

The Laboratory technician gets Rs 30000, consolidated and without any allowances. The person appointed will not get any other financial or non-financial benefits, facilities or special allowances beyond the fixed consolidated emoluments set by the funding agency. One exception: House Rent Allowance (HRA), if the funding agency's guidelines allow it, will be paid at the rate for Rajkot city (Class 'Y'). The post is purely on contract, for an externally funded research project. The person engaged will have no claim to any regular post in AIIMS Rajkot at any time. They also have no claim to continue in this project or any other project. The person appointed will not be treated as a regular employee of AIIMS Rajkot or the Government of India. The job is purely temporary, for 11 months. The Principal Investigator may extend or shorten it. The contract ends automatically when its term is over.

Selection and Interview

A screening committee will scrutinise the applications. The Laboratory technician will then be selected through a written exam/skill test followed by an interview. Selection is based purely on merit. Written test: the test is MCQ-based. Up to six (6) candidates per available post with the highest marks in the written test will be called for the interview. Final merit: the written exam counts for 80% and the interview for 20%. Date, time and venue: the written test/interview is on 10 December 2026. Reporting time is 9 AM at the Research Cell, Academic block, 3rd Floor, AIIMS Rajkot. What to bring: the original documents, when you report for the written exam/interview. NOC: if you work in a Government or Public Sector undertaking, or in any other research project at AIIMS Rajkot, bring a "No Objection Certificate" to the interview.
