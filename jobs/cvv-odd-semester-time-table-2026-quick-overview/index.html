Arrear and Supplementary Exams in December 2026

Arrear and supplementary papers are listed for many programmes, mostly between 10 December and 24 December 2026. The timetable uses asterisks to mark the exam type.

SymbolMeaning
*Arrear Examinations
**Supplementary Examinations
***Arrear & Supplementary Examinations

Papers without an asterisk are regular exams. Some courses appear on the same date in the same session for different programmes or semesters. Read your own programme table carefully.

What Do FN and AN Mean in the CVV Time Table?

FN stands for forenoon and AN for afternoon, as used in the timetable. FN exams run from 09:30 hrs to 12:30 hrs. AN exams run from 14:00 hrs to 17:00 hrs. Each session is three hours long.

Exam Venue: Where Will the CVV December 2026 Exams Be Held?

  • LP timetable: Lalita Pratishthanam, Onakkoor
  • PP timetable: Prajna Pratishthanam, Warriam Road

Use the venue printed at the top of the timetable that lists your programme. Seating details and room allocation are not mentioned in the timetables.

How to Check the CVV December 2026 Time Table

  • Open the Student Corner page on the official CVV website and go to the Examinations tab.
  • Choose the PDF that lists your programme and semester. The LP file covers Lalita Pratishthanam, and the PP file covers Prajna Pratishthanam.
  • Find your programme heading, for example "Master of Business Administration S1 (Regular)".
  • Match your course code and name to the date, day, session (FN or AN) and time.
  • Check the asterisks to see whether a paper is regular, arrear or supplementary.
  • Note any paper marked "To Be Announced Later" and watch for official updates.

Important Instructions from the Official Time Table

  • Candidates should refer to the schedule for their own degree programme and batch.
  • If the schedule changes for unavoidable reasons, the examination office will inform candidates.
  • The timetable is signed by the Controller of Examinations.
