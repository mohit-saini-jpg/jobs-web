IAF Agniveervayu Eligibility Criteria

Eligibility for this Open Recruitment Rally is based on date of birth, marital status, educational qualification, and domicile of the specified states/UTs. Candidates born between 01 January 2005 and 01 July 2009 (both dates inclusive) are eligible to appear in this Open Recruitment Rally. If a candidate clears all stages of the selection procedure, the upper age limit as on the date of enrolment should not exceed 22 years. Only unmarried male and female candidates are eligible for enrolment as Agniveervayu. Candidates must undertake not to marry during the 4-year engagement period; those found married during this period shall be dismissed from service. Female candidates must also undertake not to become pregnant during the engagement period; pregnancy leading to Low Medical Category will result in dismissal from service and debars selection into the Regular Cadre. IAF Agniveervayu Selection Process

Selection for Agniveervayu Intake 01/2027 involves document verification followed by multiple test stages, all typically conducted over the rally days. Verification of Eligibility: Original/DigiLocker-verified educational certificates and other applicable documents (domicile, COAFP, NCC, discharge certificate, etc.) are scrutinised prior to the Physical Fitness Test. Physical Fitness Test (PFT): PFT-I: 1.6 Km run — male candidates within 7 minutes, female candidates within 8 minutes. PFT-II (after 10 minutes recuperation): Male candidates — 10 push-ups (1 min), 10 sit-ups (1 min), 20 squats (1 min), with rest breaks between exercises. Female candidates — 10 sit-ups (1 min 30 sec), 15 squats (1 min), with rest breaks between exercises. IAF Agniveervayu Salary and Pay Package

Agniveervayu personnel are paid a customised Agniveer package that increases each year of the 4-year engagement, split between an in-hand component and a contribution to the Agniveer Corpus Fund (matched by the Government of India). In addition to the monthly package, Agniveervayu will receive Risk and Hardship allowances (as applicable in the Indian Air Force), Dress and Travel allowances, and perks such as ration, clothing, accommodation, and Leave Travel Concession (LTC). On completion of the 4-year engagement, Agniveervayu will receive a one-time Seva Nidhi package. The total contribution to the Agniveer Corpus Fund after 4 years is approximately ₹5.02 lakh from the candidate's monthly contributions, matched by approximately ₹5.02 lakh from the Government of India, resulting in an approximate Seva Nidhi payout of ₹10.04 lakh (absolute amount, excluding interest). IAF Agniveervayu Recruitment Rally 2026: Intake 01/2027 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is IAF Agniveervayu Recruitment Rally 2026?
A
It is an Open Recruitment Rally conducted by the Indian Air Force from 31 August to 07 September 2026 to select unmarried male and female candidates for Agniveervayu (Other Than Science Subjects) under Intake 01/2027, at Chumoukedima (Nagaland) and Sri Vijaya Puram (Andaman & Nicobar Islands).
Q2How many vacancies are available under Agniveervayu Intake 01/2027?
A
The number of vacancies is not specified in the official notification. Enrolment will be based on state-wise merit and state-wise vacancy availability.
Q3What is the age limit for IAF Agniveervayu Recruitment 2026?
A
Candidates must be born between 01 January 2005 and 01 July 2009 (both dates inclusive). The upper age limit as on the date of enrolment should not exceed 22 years.
Q4What is the educational qualification for IAF Agniveervayu (Other Than Science Subjects)?
A
Candidates must have passed 10+2/Intermediate/Equivalent in any stream with minimum 50% aggregate and 50% in English, or a two-year Vocational Course, or a three-year Engineering Diploma in specified trades, each with the same minimum percentage criteria.
Q5Who is eligible to appear at the Chumoukedima and Sri Vijaya Puram rallies?
A
Candidates with permanent domicile of the districts of Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Tripura, and Sikkim are eligible for the Chumoukedima rally, while candidates with permanent domicile of the districts of the UT of Andaman & Nicobar Islands are eligible for the Sri Vijaya Puram rally. Certain children of Air Force personnel (COAFP-I/COAFP-II) are also eligible irrespective of domicile, subject to conditions.
Q6What is the application fee for IAF Agniveervayu Recruitment 2026?
A
No application fee for the rally itself is specified in the official notification. Only the Appeal Medical Board process carries a fee of ₹40.
Q7What is the selection process for IAF Agniveervayu Recruitment 2026?
A
Selection includes document verification, a Physical Fitness Test (1.6 Km run, push-ups, sit-ups, squats), a written test (English and Reasoning & General Awareness), Adaptability Test-I, Adaptability Test-II, and a medical examination.
Q8What is the salary for IAF Agniveervayu?
A
Agniveervayu receive a monthly customised package starting at ₹30,000 in the 1st year and rising to ₹40,000 in the 4th year, plus Risk and Hardship, Dress, and Travel allowances, and other perks. A one-time Seva Nidhi package of approximately ₹10.04 lakh is paid on completion of the 4-year engagement.
Q9How can I apply for IAF Agniveervayu Recruitment Rally 2026?
A
This is a walk-in Open Recruitment Rally. Eligible candidates should report directly to the applicable rally venue on their scheduled reporting date with all required original documents; no separate online application process is mentioned in this notification.

The Provisional Select List is tentatively scheduled for 14 November 2026, and the Enrolment List is tentatively scheduled for 01 December 2026.
