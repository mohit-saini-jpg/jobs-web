5What to Do After the Round 3 Result: Freeze or Float
Sr.TypeTextHeadersRows
1paragraphOnce your Round 3 allotment is out, you must choose one of the available options on the portal within the deadline. This choice determines whether you continue in the counselling process and what happens to your current seat.
2table
  • Option
  • What It Means
    • Freeze
    • You accept the allotted seat permanently and exit further counselling rounds. No seat upgrades will be offered after this.
    • Float
    • You keep your current Round 3 seat as a guaranteed backup while remaining eligible for a possible upgrade in Round 4. Floating can never result in a worse seat than your current allotment.
    • Withdraw
    • You exit the counselling process entirely. A partial refund of the seat acceptance fee may be available if withdrawal is completed within the specified deadline; check the portal for the exact refund policy.
3paragraphSince Round 4 functions as an auto-freeze round - meaning any candidate still floating after Round 4 is automatically locked into the best seat they have held across all rounds - Round 3 is one of the last rounds where actively choosing to float carries a meaningful decision.

7Important Instructions for Round 3 Allotted Candidates
Sr.TypeStyleItems
1listunordered
  • Carefully verify your allotted institute, branch, and category on the allotment letter before proceeding.
  • Failure to pay the seat acceptance fee or complete physical reporting within the deadline results in automatic cancellation of the allotted seat, with no refund, as per the UPTAC counselling brochure.
  • Candidates should re-check their eligibility across categories in each round, since vacancy patterns shift as other candidates accept, upgrade, or withdraw seats.
  • Outstation (non-UP) candidates should keep checking their status each round, since All-India quota seats can open up as candidates withdraw or get allotted elsewhere.
  • Choices locked during Round 3 choice filling also apply to Round 4 allotment and the internal sliding round.
8Is Round 3 the Final Round of UPTAC 2026 Counselling?
Sr.TypeText
1paragraphNo. Round 3 is followed by Round 4 (an auto-freeze round), after which an Internal Sliding round and up to two Special Rounds are expected to be conducted, tentatively in August 2026, to fill any seats that remain vacant. Special Rounds are open to fresh registrations and CUET UG-qualified candidates as well, but availability of popular branches at top government colleges in these later rounds is typically very limited.
