BTSC Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection for both posts will be made on the basis of a Computer Based Test (CBT) and work experience, as per the following distribution: The written exam will consist of 100 objective-type multiple-choice questions carrying a total of 400 marks, in both Hindi and English. Exam duration: 2 hours. 4 marks awarded per correct answer; 1 mark deducted per wrong answer (negative marking applicable). The exam may be conducted in multiple shifts, with results prepared using a normalization process. Minimum qualifying marks: General category – 40%, BC – 36.5%, EBC – 34%, SC/ST/Women/PwD – 32%. Candidates residing outside Bihar (including women and PwD candidates) must secure the General category minimum of 40%. Candidates claiming work experience marks must have qualified the written exam; experience marks are added only after successful document verification.
