About ILBS

The notice describes ILBS as an autonomous super-specialty Institute of excellence under GNCT of Delhi and a Deemed-to-be-University by the UGC. Its stated vision is to be a global leader as a beacon of patient care, teaching and research in the field of liver, gall bladder and pancreatobiliary diseases. The Institute is at D – 1, Vasant Kunj, Delhi – 110070.

Pay and Tenure

The remuneration is printed as PB – III, GP - 6600 (pre-revised), 7th CPC applicable. The pay package is based on the 7th CPC at the pay level applicable for the Institute.
