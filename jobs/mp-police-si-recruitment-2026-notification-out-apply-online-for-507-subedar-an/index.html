MPESB Subedar Eligibility Criteria

Candidate must be a citizen of India. Non-MP-domicile candidates may apply only under the Unreserved (Open) category, with no reservation or age-relaxation benefit, and a flat maximum age of 33 years. A candidate with more than 2 living children, one of whom was born on or after 26 January 2001, is not eligible (with an exception where a single subsequent delivery results in twins/multiple births).

MPESB Subedar Age Limit

Age is reckoned as on the last date of online application (23.09.2026). MPESB Subedar Salary

All Subedar and Sub Inspector cadre posts (SAF, DEF, Arms, Photo, QD, Finger Print) carry Pay Level 9: ₹36,200 – ₹1,14,800. During the 3-year probation period, stipend is payable at 70% of minimum pay in year 1, 80% in year 2, and 90% in year 3, plus other allowances as approved by the government from time to time. Employees appointed on or after 01.01.2005 are covered under the Defined Contribution Pension Scheme.

MPESB Subedar Selection Process

Selection for MPESB Subedar & Sub Inspector Recruitment 2026 is conducted in two stages. Objective/MCQ type, 100 marks, 2-hour duration, no negative marking. Subjects: Hindi language comprehension, English language comprehension, Analytical ability, History, Geography, Science, Civics, Basic computer knowledge, Reasoning, Current Affairs. Separate merit lists are prepared for non-technical and technical posts; candidates equal to 10 times the advertised vacancies (per vertical category: UR/OBC/ST/SC/EWS) are called for the Main Exam. A 5% bonus mark is available at this stage only for specified categories (dependents of soldiers killed in recent India-Pakistan conflict, ex-servicemen, census/election-surplus staff, NCC "C" certificate holders, and returnees from Myanmar/Sri Lanka, among others).
