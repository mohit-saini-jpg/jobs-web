JNVST 2027 Quick Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 31/07/2026

JNVST 2027 Notification NVS Class 6 Admission: Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (NVS), under the Ministry of Education, Government of India, has released the Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test (JNVST) 2027 notification for admission to Class VI in Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas (JNVs) for the academic session 2027-28. The notification was released on 7 July 2026, and online applications are open from 7 July 2026 to 7 August 2026.

Students currently studying in Class V during the 2026-27 academic session can apply online. The entrance examination is scheduled for 28 November 2026. Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas are fully residential, co-educational schools that provide free education, boarding, lodging and textbooks to selected students, with admission granted purely through the JNV Selection Test.

JNVST 2027 Reservation Details

  • At least 75% of seats are reserved for candidates from rural areas.
  • Reservation for SC, ST and OBC candidates is provided as per Government of India norms.
  • A minimum of one-third of seats is reserved for girls.
  • Reservation is also available for eligible Divyang (differently-abled) candidates.

Note: Specific numeric seat vacancies for individual Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas are not available in the official notification.

JNVST 2027 Selection Process

Admission to Class VI under NVS for the 2027-28 session is finalized through the following stages:

  • Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test (JNVST)
  • Document verification of selected candidates
  • Confirmation of eligibility and admission by the concerned Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya

How to Apply Online for JNVST 2027

  • Visit the official NVS admission portal: https://cbseitms.rcil.gov.in/nvs/
  • Read the official notification carefully and confirm eligibility.
  • Complete the online registration form.
  • Fill in all required personal and academic details.
  • Upload the candidate's photograph, candidate's signature and parent's signature.
  • Upload Aadhaar details or residence certificate, if applicable.
  • Verify all entered information carefully before final submission.
  • Submit the application and save/print the confirmation page for future reference.

Important Instructions

  • Only one application per candidate is permitted; multiple applications may lead to rejection.
  • Candidates should ensure all uploaded documents meet the specified format and size requirements before submission.
  • Keep a copy of the submitted application and confirmation page safely for future correspondence, admit card download, and verification.
  • Any further instructions beyond those stated in the official notification are not available at this time; candidates should check navodaya.gov.in and the application portal periodically for updates.
