NHM Thiruvananthapuram Public Relations Officer : Job Overview

The District Health and Family Welfare Society Arogyakeralam, Thiruvananthapuram (National Health Mission) is filling 2 Public Relations Officer posts on contract in Thiruvananthapuram District at a consolidated Rs. 24,000/-. Applicants need MSW/MBA/MHA and two years' post-qualification experience. The online Google form on nhmtvm.com or arogyakeralam.gov.in must be submitted by 5.00 pm on 9 November 2026, and the printed documents must reach the DPM Office by 5.00 pm on 10 November 2026.

About the Society

The notice is issued from the Office of the District Programme Manager, District Health and Family Welfare Society Arogyakeralam, W&C Compound, Thycaud, Trivandrum-14. National Health Mission Thiruvananthapuram District is inviting the applications for the post in Thiruvananthapuram District on contract basis.

Who Can Apply

The age limit is not to exceed 40 years as on 01.11.2026. The notice prints no age relaxation. Applicants who already submitted applications under the earlier notification (NHM/TVM/368/2026-D9 dated 17.08.2026) will be considered under this notification and need not apply again. Claims about qualifications and experience must be supported by copies of the relevant documents, and originals may be asked for at the interview or certificate verification. Copies of appointment letters, salary certificates and pay slips are not accepted in place of a work experience certificate. Candidates should check the official notification for any further eligibility details.
PostQualification Required
Public Relations OfficerMSW/MBA/MHA, and two year post qualification experience

Pay and Tenure

The remuneration is Rs.24,000/- (consolidated). The selected candidate has to enter into a contract with the District Health and Family Welfare Society (Arogyakeralam) Thiruvananthapuram District and is governed by the NHM rules and regulations in force from time to time. The post is temporary during the period of operation of the Mission, with no claim to permanency of job or labour claims against the NHM, DHS or the Government.

Selection and Interview

Selection will be based on qualification, experience and performance in the Written Test/ Interviews. The Society may prioritize the interview for different categories of posts on the basis of requirements, as the vacancies are for immediate requirement. No TA/DA will be paid for the interview. The notice gives no interview date. All communications from the Society will be sent to candidates by email.
