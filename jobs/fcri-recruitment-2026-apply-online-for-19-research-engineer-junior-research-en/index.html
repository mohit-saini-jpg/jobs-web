FCRI Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

B.E./B.Tech. or equivalent in Mechanical Engineering with 5 years of relevant experience in the specific specialization applied for; OR M.E./M.Tech. or equivalent in Mechanical Engineering (with relevant specialization) and 3 years of relevant experience. Minimum 65% marks in the qualifying degree (60% for SC/ST category candidates applying against reserved posts). The Bachelor's/Master's degree must be a full-time regular course from a UGC-recognized Indian University, UGC-recognized Deemed University, or AICTE-approved course from an autonomous Indian institution. Desirable qualification for the Fluid Flow Research specialization: Ph.D. Detailed specialization-specific experience requirements (e.g., two-phase/multi-phase flow research, CFD in thermal and fluids engineering, design and testing of valves, fluid flow research) are given in the detailed notification at www.fcriindia.com. FCRI Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection is conducted in three stages: Screening of Applications: Applications are screened based on educational qualifications, experience criteria (where applicable), age, and payment of the application fee. Provisionally qualified candidates are notified via the Institute website. Written Test (Junior Research Engineer only): An online, bilingual (Hindi/English) Multiple Choice Question (MCQ) test is held at test centres in Coimbatore and/or Kochi (subject to availability based on the number of shortlisted candidates). Number of Questions: 100; Duration: 120 minutes Maximum Marks: 400 Marking Scheme: +4 for each correct answer, -1 for each incorrect answer, 0 for unattempted questions Research Engineer posts do not have a written test. Q3Can freshers apply for FCRI Junior Research Engineer post?
A
Yes, freshers may apply for the Junior Research Engineer post; no prior work experience is required. However, the Research Engineer post requires relevant post-qualification experience.
Q4Is there a written exam for FCRI Research Engineer post?
A
No, only Junior Research Engineer candidates need to appear for a written test. Research Engineer selection is based on a Personal Interview.
Q5What is the application fee for FCRI Recruitment 2026?
A
The application fee is ₹1,180/- per post for General, OBC, and EWS candidates. Women, SC/ST, and PwBD candidates are exempted from the application fee.
Q6What is the age limit for FCRI Recruitment 2026?
A
For Research Engineer, the maximum age ranges from 35 to 40 years depending on category. For Junior Research Engineer, it ranges from 30 to 35 years depending on category, as on the closing date of application.
Q7What is the salary for FCRI Research Engineer and Junior Research Engineer?
A
Research Engineer carries Level 10 pay (₹56,100–₹1,77,500), with an approximate minimum gross pay of ₹1,01,130. Junior Research Engineer carries Level 8 pay (₹47,600–₹1,51,100), with an approximate minimum gross pay of ₹83,800.
Q8What is the selection process for FCRI Junior Research Engineer?
A
Selection involves screening of applications, a written test, and a personal interview, with the final rank list based on 80% weightage to the written test and 20% weightage to the interview.
