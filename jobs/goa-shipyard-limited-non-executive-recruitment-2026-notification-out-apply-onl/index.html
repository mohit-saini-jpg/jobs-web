Goa Shipyard Limited Selection Process 2026

The selection process for Goa Shipyard Limited Non-Executive Recruitment 2026 consists of a Written Test, Document Verification and Skill/Trade Test. The written examination may be conducted through Computer Based Test (CBT) or Pen and Paper Based Test (PBT). The written examination will normally contain 25% General Aptitude questions and 75% questions related to the relevant subject or trade. The selection weightage differs according to the post category. The minimum qualifying standards apply to the Written Test and Skill Test as specified in the notification. Eligible internal fixed-term employees and advance trainees may receive skill-test weightage under GSL rules.
