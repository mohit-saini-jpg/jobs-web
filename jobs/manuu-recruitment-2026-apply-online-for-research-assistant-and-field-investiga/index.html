MANUU Recruitment 2026 - Eligibility Criteria

Research Assistant: Postgraduate degree in Social Sciences/ Education/ Adult Education or a related discipline with a minimum of 55% marks from a recognized university. Preference will be given to candidates who have qualified NET or possess an M.Phil. and Ph.D. The candidate should have excellent English writing skills, proficiency in MS Word, MS Excel, SPSS, and other relevant data analysis software, along with sound knowledge of quantitative and qualitative research methodologies. Experience in data collection, analysis, interpretation, and report writing will be an added advantage. Field Investigator: Postgraduate/Graduate in any Social Science discipline with at least 55% marks. Preference will be given to candidates with a four-year under-graduate programme and prior field survey experience.
