HAL Korwa Apprentice Selection Process 2026

HAL will select apprentices on a 100% merit basis using aggregate marks obtained in the relevant qualifying degree or diploma examination . Scrutiny of online applications and prescribed eligibility conditions. Preparation of merit lists based on 100% weightage of aggregate qualifying examination marks. Publication of the provisional selection list on the HAL Careers website. Verification of original documents of provisionally selected candidates. Submission of medical fitness and police verification certificates before joining. The notification does not prescribe a separate written examination or interview for merit preparation. The provisional selection list is scheduled to be published between 30 November and 2 December 2026 in the Careers section of the official HAL website. The selected candidates must attend original document verification at HAL Korwa between 3 and 9 December 2026

Documents Required for HAL Korwa Apprentice Recruitment 2026

Provisionally selected candidates must produce the original documents and self-attested copies during verification, along with one recent passport-size photograph. Provisionally selected candidates must submit the following certificates at the time of joining apprenticeship training: Medical Fitness Certificate: Issued by a Government Medical Officer not below the rank of Assistant Surgeon (Gazetted). Police Verification Certificate: Obtained from the Commissioner or Superintendent of Police of the candidate's place of residence. Original documents must be produced during verification and at joining as required by HAL. OBC candidates must produce a valid Non-Creamy Layer certificate in the prescribed Central Government format, issued within six months of the application closing date.

HAL Korwa Apprentice Training Duration 2026

The apprenticeship training period is one year under the Apprentices Act, 1961. Selected candidates will be required to execute an apprenticeship training contract. After completion of training, the apprenticeship contract will close. HAL has no obligation to offer temporary or permanent employment to candidates who complete the training.
