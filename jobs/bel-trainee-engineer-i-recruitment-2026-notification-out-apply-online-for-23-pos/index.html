Stipend and Training Period

Trainee Engineer-I will be engaged for an initial period of 2 years. This may be extended up to a maximum of one year (maximum tenure 3 years) based on project requirement and individual performance. A consolidated amount of Rs. 12,000 per year is paid for expenses such as insurance premium, attire allowance, stitching charges and footwear allowances. It is paid in two instalments: the first is processed in the first month's salary after joining, and the second after 6 months from the date of joining. Candidates posted to far flung and tough locations as and when project requirement arises will be paid 10% of the consolidated remuneration as Tough Location Allowance. Gratuity is admissible as per the applicable provisions of the Gratuity rules. BEL Trainee Engineer I Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection is through a written test, followed by document verification for those who qualify. Candidates are selected in order of merit, category-wise and discipline-wise. Candidates whose online applications are accepted and who meet the qualifying criteria will be provisionally shortlisted for the written test. The written test is held only at BEL Kotdwar on 13.12.2026 (Sunday). Each correct answer carries 1 mark and each wrong response carries a negative mark of 0.25. Minimum qualifying marks: 35% for General/EWS/OBC-NCL and 30% for SC/ST/PwBD. PwBD candidates are eligible for compensatory time of 20 minutes per hour. A scribe is permitted for Persons with Benchmark Disabilities as defined under Section 2(r) of the PwBD Act 2016, on production of the required medical certificate in the BEL format.   • 4Pay the fee through SBI Collect and enter the SBI Collect Reference Number generated after payment in the online application form.
  • 5Upload the documents listed under Documents to Upload below.
  • 6Submit only one application for a post. If a candidate applies more than once for one post, only the latest valid (completed) application is retained and the fee and other charges paid for the other applications are forfeited.
