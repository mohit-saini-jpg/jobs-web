ECHS Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Candidates meeting the qualitative requirement will be called for an interview at Station HQ on 02 November 2026. For the posts of Clerk and DEO, a written test and a computer practical test will also be conducted. Preference will be given to Ex-servicemen, Widows of Service Personnel and Veer Naris. No TA/DA is admissible for attending the interview.

Documents Required for ECHS Interview

Bring the following original documents to the interview, and submit 3 copies of the application along with self-attested copies of the documents: Certificates and mark sheets of 10th/Matric, 10+2 and Graduation/Post Graduation/Diploma/Course MD/MS certificate in the specialty concerned (for specialist posts) Work Experience Certificate Medical Fitness Certificate duly signed by CMO/SEMO Character Certificate duly signed by Tehsildar Discharge Book and PPO (for Ex-servicemen) AWPO Registration Slip (for Ex-servicemen applying for Clerk and DEO)

On-Job Training (OJT) for Key Appointments

Selected candidates for Medical Specialist, Radiologist, Gynaecologist and Medical Officer must complete 10 days of On-Job Training and obtain a certificate from the OIC Polyclinic before taking charge and signing the contract. The OJT period will remain unpaid.

ECHS Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

The engagement is purely contractual: 1 year for Ex-servicemen and 11 months for civil candidates. The contract can be renewed for an additional year, or until the candidate reaches the maximum age, subject to performance and other conditions. Applications received after 14 October 2026 will not be accepted. Candidates must reach Station HQ by 0900 hrs on 02 November 2026 with original documents. No TA/DA is admissible. Apply only for posts for which you meet the qualitative requirements.

ECHS Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Visit www.echs.gov.in and click on "Employment Opportunities".
  • 2Fill in the application form in the prescribed format.
  • 3Attach self-attested photocopies of testimonials supporting educational qualifications and work experience.
  • 4Prepare the application in triplicate.
  • 5Send it so that it reaches the OIC, Station HQ (ECHS Cell), by 14 October 2026.
  • 6Candidates applying for Clerk and DEO posts under the Ex-servicemen category must register with AWPO and enclose the AWPO Registration Slip with the application.

Q2How many total vacancies are available in this notification?
A
There are a total of 23 vacancies available across various posts including Radiologist, Gynaecologist, Medical Specialist, Medical Officer, Nursing Assistant, Pharmacist, and supporting staff.

A
The mode of application is strictly offline. Candidates must download the application form from the official website and send the hard copy in triplicate via post.

A
