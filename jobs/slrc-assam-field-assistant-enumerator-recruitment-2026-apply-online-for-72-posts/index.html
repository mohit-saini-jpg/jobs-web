SLRC Assam Selection Process 2026

The selection process comprises the following stages: Stage-I: OMR based Written examination of 175 marks with multiple choice objective type questions (Duration: 3 Hours). Candidates will be shortlisted at the ratio of 1:2 of the vacancy position. Stage-II: Computer skill test of 25 marks for the shortlisted candidates. Final Selection: Based on total marks secured in the written examination and computer skill test as per merit and reservations.

SLRC Assam Exam Pattern 2026

Exam Pattern Notes: The total number of questions will remain 150. There will be negative marking for wrong answers equivalent to 1/4th of the marks allotted for that question. Selecting more than one option will be treated as a wrong answer. Question papers will be available in Assamese, Bengali, English, Bodo, and Hindi.
