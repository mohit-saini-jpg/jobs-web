NSIC Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The National Small Industries Corporation Limited (NSIC), a Government of India Enterprise under the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises, is filling 42 reserved vacancies in nine posts, from Assistant Manager (E-0, Rs.30,000-1,30,000) to General Manager (E-5, Rs.80,000-2,20,000), on IDA 2017 pay scales. Only SC, ST and OBC (including PwBD) Indian citizens may apply. Apply online through the CAREER section of www.nsic.co.in from 21 November 2026 to 14 December 2026; the signed print-out with a detailed CV must reach NSIC by 21 December 2026, 6:00 p.m.

Pay and Tenure

Each post carries an IDA 2017 pay scale: Rs.80,000-2,20,000 (E-5), Rs.70,000-2,00,000 (E-4), Rs.60,000-1,80,000 (E-3), Rs.40,000-1,40,000 (E-1) and Rs.30,000-1,30,000 (E-0). The compensation package includes basic pay, Industrial DA, HRA, perquisites and allowances as per cafeteria approach, EPF and gratuity as per IDA 2017 pattern. Performance Related Pay applies as admissible and as per the Rules of the Corporation. The Corporation also has policies on House Building Advance, Vehicle Advance, Computer Advance, medical facilities for self and dependents, PF, gratuity, Superannuation Pension Scheme and leave encashment. The selected candidate must sign an agreement to serve NSIC for a minimum of 2 years after joining, failing which compensation of Rs.2,00,000 is payable. The waiting list/panel of selected candidates is valid for six months.

Selection and Interview

The selection stages differ by post. Scrutiny of applications against the eligibility criteria, followed by a Personal Interview of shortlisted candidates. A Computer Based Test (70% weightage) followed by a Personal Interview of shortlisted candidates (30% weightage). The test has 100 multiple choice questions for 100 marks. Each correct answer carries 1 mark and each wrong answer carries 0.25 negative marking. Minimum qualifying marks are 40 for General/OBC candidates and 35 for SC/ST/PwBD candidates. The notice does not print the date of the Computer Based Test. Candidates are shortlisted for the interview in the ratio of at least 1:5 or maximum 1:7 with respect to the number of posts advertised. NSIC Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in NSIC Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 42 reserved vacancies in nine posts: 11 for SC, 7 for ST and 24 for OBC, including PwBD posts. Assistant Manager (E-0) Technology has the most posts at 13.
Q2What is the last date to apply for NSIC Recruitment 2026?
A
The last date of the online application is 14 December 2026. The signed print-out with a detailed CV must reach NSIC by 21 December 2026 up to 6:00 p.m.
Q3What is the qualification for the NSIC Assistant Manager posts?
A
Assistant Manager (E-0) Technology needs a first class 4 years B.E./B.Tech with minimum 60% marks in the listed branches, plus a GATE score of 2025 or 2026. Assistant Manager (F&A) needs CA/CMA or a Commerce graduate with Two years MBA (First Class, 60%) in Finance.
Q4What is the age limit for NSIC Recruitment 2026?
A
The upper age limit is 45 years for General Manager, 41 for Deputy General Manager, 38 for Chief Manager, 31 for Deputy Manager and 28 for Assistant Manager, counted as on 14 December 2026.
Q5What is the application fee for NSIC Recruitment 2026?
A
The fee is Rs.1500 per application, paid through NEFT. SC/ST/PwBD/Women candidates and Departmental candidates pay no fee, and the fee is non-refundable.
Q6How do I apply for NSIC Recruitment 2026?
A
Apply online through the CAREER section of www.nsic.co.in, then send the signed print-out with a detailed CV and self-attested documents to Deputy General Manager - Human Resources, NSIC, NSIC Bhawan, Okhla Industrial Estate, New Delhi-110020, by 21 December 2026, 6:00 p.m.
Q7What is the selection process for NSIC posts?
A
Posts 1 to 7 use scrutiny of applications and a Personal Interview. Assistant Manager Technology uses 70% GATE score and 30% interview. Assistant Manager (F&A) uses a 70% Computer Based Test and 30% interview.
Q8What is the salary for NSIC Recruitment 2026?
A
The IDA 2017 pay scales are Rs.80,000-2,20,000 for General Manager, Rs.70,000-2,00,000 for Deputy General Manager, Rs.60,000-1,80,000 for Chief Manager, Rs.40,000-1,40,000 for Deputy Manager and Rs.30,000-1,30,000 for Assistant Manager.
Q9Who gets a relaxation in NSIC Recruitment 2026?
A
SC/ST/PwBD candidates get a 5% relaxation in the minimum marks. Age relaxation is "as per Govt. Rules", and 10 years in age and the First Class requirement are relaxable for Departmental employees.
Q10Is there a service bond, and can I apply for more than one post?
A
Yes, selected candidates must execute an agreement to serve NSIC for a minimum of 2 years after joining, or pay Rs.2,00,000 as compensation. A candidate can apply for one post only.
