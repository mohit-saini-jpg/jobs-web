HLL Associate Manager Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

HLL Lifecare Limited, a Government of India enterprise under the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, is filling 3 Associate Manager (Biomedical) posts in its HCD (Projects & Services – Division) on an Executive – Fixed Term Contract, with a basic pay range of Rs. 25,000 – 45,000 per month. The essential qualification is BE / B.Tech or M.Sc in the listed biomedical and electronics disciplines, or a Diploma. Applications go through a Google Form from 03.11.2026 to 18.11.2026 (Reference Code HLL/HR/107/2026).

About HLL

HLL Lifecare Limited is a Government of India enterprise under the administrative control of the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare. The notice says it commenced operations on 5 April 1969 and now works across healthcare manufacturing, retail pharmacy, diagnostics, imaging services, healthcare infrastructure development, procurement consultancy, hospital management and other allied services. It gives the HLL Group's annual business turnover as approximately ₹6,500 crore.

Who Can Apply

Only Indian Nationals are eligible to apply. The notice gives two alternative qualification routes for the post. BE/B.Tech/M.Sc holders: minimum 1 year of post-qualification experience. Diploma holders: minimum 7 years of post-qualification experience. For both, the experience must be in executing assigned activities at government hospitals, medical colleges and similar institutes, including surveys, technical documentation, tender support, inspections and project follow-up, in line with government procurement rules. The notice states that the cut-off date for age is the same as the cut-off date for calculating qualification and post-qualification experience. SC/ST/OBC/PwD candidates are eligible for relaxation as per Government of India directives; the notice does not print the relaxation amounts. Candidates should check the official notification for the discipline-specific eligibility

HLL Associate Manager (Biomedical) Job Description

The notice lists these tasks for the post: Conduct site visits and gap assessments across Government Hospitals, Medical Colleges and Institutes; prepare equipment inventories and room-wise requirements. Draft generic technical specifications and layouts; verify estimated costs using GeM, past orders and market rates. Support tender preparation, pre-bid replies, technical evaluation and comparative statements. Track tenders and purchase orders on GeM, CPPP and state e-procurement portals; maintain tender records. Follow up with suppliers on delivery, contract timelines, LD, performance security/BGs, insurance and import documents. Coordinate pre-dispatch and joint inspections, installation, testing and commissioning; check supplies against the PO. Prepare inspection checklists, punch lists, acceptance and handover documents. Verify bills against contract terms and maintain asset, warranty

Selection and Interview

The notice says all information submitted in the application will be verified with original documents at the time of the interview. If any information is found to be false, incorrect or not in conformity with the eligibility criteria, the candidature is liable to be rejected or cancelled at any stage of the recruitment process. The interview date, venue and mode are not specified in the notice. The decision of Management regarding selection will be final.
