About RITES and the Port of Colombo Assignment

RITES Ltd. is a Navratna Central Public Sector Enterprise under the Ministry of Railways, Govt. of India. According to the REOI, it is a premier multi-disciplinary consultancy organization in the fields of transport, infrastructure and related technologies. Sri Lanka Ports Authority (the client) has invited proposals for consultancy services for the Preparation of a Land Use Optimization Study for the Port of Colombo. RITES has been shortlisted by the client to submit its Request for Proposal. The assignment comprises a land use survey of port and near-port land, followed by a Land Use Optimization Plan for short-, medium- and long-term port and port-related logistics development. It aims to optimize land utilization within the Port of Colombo, improving operational efficiency, logistics integration and future development planning. RITES Experts Eligibility Criteria

RITES Experts Salary

The REOI does not fix a salary for any position. Instead, Annexure-IV asks each applicant to state the indicative expected remuneration for the position they are interested in. For a project, RITES will interact with shortlisted Experts to obtain their willingness and negotiate their salary expectations. Written consent on willingness, availability and agreed remuneration is obtained before the bid is submitted.

RITES Experts Selection Process

The REOI does not mention a written test or interview. The process described is as follows: Examination of applications: RITES checks that all requested documents are provided and complete. It may ask applicants for clarifications within the time it specifies. Evaluation: RITES uses the inputs submitted in the Annexure-II forms and any clarifications. It also reserves the right to assess an applicant's capability and capacity to perform where circumstances warrant. Empanelment: After evaluation, applicants are empanelled and informed by e-mail. The first list is prepared from applications received up to 08.10.2026, 17:00 hrs, and expanded later at intervals decided by RITES. Project-wise shortlisting: While bidding for a project, RITES scrutinizes the CVs of empanelled Experts to judge suitability against the project's RFP requirements.
