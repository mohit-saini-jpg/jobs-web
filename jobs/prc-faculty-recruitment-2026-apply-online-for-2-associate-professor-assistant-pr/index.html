About PRC

The Population Research Centre is located at University Campus, Hazratbal, Srinagar (J&K)-190006, and functions under the University of Kashmir, which the notice describes as accredited Grade "A++" by NAAC. The notice states that the posts are likely to continue with the continuation of the Centre by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare. The posts are likely to become vacant on 31-12-2026.

Department-wise Vacancies

The notice lists two posts, one each in the Population Research Centre, and does not name any further department.

Qualification and Experience

The research score of 75 is counted as per Appendix II, Table 2 of the UGC Gazette Notification dated 18.07.2018. Publications in predatory or dubious journals, or presentations in predatory or dubious conferences, are not considered for academic credit. From 14.6.2019, only research publications in UGC-CARE Listed, Scopus or Web of Science listed journals are considered. Each API score must be supported by documentary evidence in the Performance Based Appraisal System (PBAS) Proforma, with page numbers marked on the supporting documents. ISSN/ISBN, sole or co-author status, refereed or indexed status and local, national or international status must be shown in the publication part of the PBAS Proforma with documentary evidence. Candidates registered for the Ph.D programme before 11 July 2009 are governed by the rules of the institution awarding the degree and are exempt from NET/SLET/SE

Pay and Terms

In addition to the Pay Level, D.A., Medical Allowance, House Rent and other allowances admissible to the staff of the Population Research Centre are payable to appointees. The appointee is not entitled to GP fund or pension. Contributory Provident Fund/ NPS/ gratuity and leave encashment apply as permissible under the rules and guidelines of the Ministry. Terms of appointment are governed by the Kashmir & Jammu Universities Act, 1969, its Statutes and Regulations, and the Union Ministry of Health and Family Welfare and UGC rules, whichever applicable. No TA/DA is paid to candidates for appearing in the interview.

  • 4Key in personal details, educational qualifications and experience, then preview the form. Correct any errors before submitting, as no editing or updating is allowed after submission. Note the Form No.

  • 7For postal submission, super-scribe the envelope "Application for the post of ………………………, Post ID ………… Advertisement notice No. ………. Dated …………." It must reach by or before the last date. Forms received by post after the last date are rejected without notice.
  • 8Online forms close on 18-12-2026 at 4.00 p.m.; the hard copy last date is 24-12-2026. For further information, write to [email protected].

Q1How many vacancies are there in PRC Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 2 vacancies: one Associate Professor (PGD-PR-7) and one Assistant Professor (PGD-PR-8), both for UR.
Q2What is the last date to apply for PRC Recruitment 2026?
A
The last date for online forms is 18-12-2026 up to 4.00 p.m. The last date for submission of the hard copy is 24-12-2026.
Q3What is the qualification for Assistant Professor at PRC?
A
A Master's degree with 55% marks in Demography, Population Studies or the related listed subjects, plus NET (UGC/CSIR) or SLET/SET, or a Ph.D awarded under the UGC Regulations 2009 or 2016 that exempts from NET.
Q4What is the qualification for Associate Professor at PRC?
A
A Ph.D and a Master's degree with 55% marks in the listed subjects, plus eight years of teaching and/or research experience, seven publications and a research score of 75.
Q5What is the salary for PRC Associate Professor and Assistant Professor?
A
Associate Professor is paid at Level 13A, Rs. 131400-217100/-, and Assistant Professor at Level 10, Rs. 57700-182400/-. D.A., Medical Allowance, House Rent and other admissible allowances are payable in addition.
Q6What is the application fee for PRC Recruitment 2026?
A
The fee is Rs. 1200/-, payable through online mode only. Persons with benchmark disabilities (PwD) are exempted.
Q7How can I apply for PRC Recruitment 2026?
A
Register and submit the online form at kashmiruniversity.ac.in, then send a bound print-out with enclosures and the payment acknowledgement to the Recruitment Section, University of Kashmir, personally or by Speed Post/ Registered Post.
Q8Is the PRC Kashmir job permanent?
A
No, the posts are temporary and likely to continue with the continuation of the Population Research Centre by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare. The appointee is not entitled to GP fund or pension.
Q9Do earlier Associate Professor applicants need to apply again?
A
Yes. The post is re-advertised after earlier notices dated 27-12-2024 and 08-05-2026, and those who applied earlier must apply afresh if otherwise eligible.
Q10Will TA/DA be paid for the interview?

No. The notice states that no TA/DA is paid to candidates for appearing in the interview.
