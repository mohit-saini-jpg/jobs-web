AIIMS Gorakhpur Faculty Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

All India Institute of Medical Sciences, Gorakhpur (Kunraghat, Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh) has advertised 56 Group-A faculty posts across 27 departments: 16 Professor, 5 Additional Professor, 19 Associate Professor and 16 Assistant Professor. Posts are on regular, deputation or contractual basis, with pay from Level-12 to Level-14A of the 7th CPC. The Google form and the hard copy sent by Speed/Registered Post are due by 24 December 2026 (advertisement opened 25 November 2026; Advt. No. AIIMS/GKP/RECT/FACULTY/2026-27/287).

About AIIMS Gorakhpur

According to the notice, AIIMS Gorakhpur was established under PMSSY to improve regional imbalances in the availability of affordable and reliable tertiary healthcare and to add facilities for quality medical education. The institute started functioning from session 2018-19. Applications are invited by the Executive Director from Indian nationals and Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) cardholders under Section 7A of the Citizenship Act, 1955.

Qualification and Experience

Age and all other qualifications are counted as on the closing date, 24 December 2026. Experience is counted after obtaining the prescribed qualification. A relaxation of one year of teaching/research experience applies to candidates applying for SC/ST reserved posts, as per DoPT rules and guidelines. For super-speciality disciplines, the first essential qualification (MBBS or equivalent) stays the same and the second is D.M. or M.Ch (recognised under the IMC Act, 1956 or by the NMC, or NMC-recognised equivalent) in the subject of the post. Experience is counted after the qualifying degree and must be gained in an NMC-recognised department or equivalent. AIIMS Gorakhpur Faculty Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Applications must reach the institute in two ways: the Google form at https://forms.gle/L1fQkGNmDXDpboSd8, latest by 24 December 2026, and the signed hard copy sent by Speed/Registered Post to the Recruitment Cell by 24 December 2026, failing which the candidature will not be considered.
  • 2Fill in the application form in MS Word format as per the proforma given with the notice, take a printout, affix photograph and signature, and scan it in pdf format.
  • 3Upload on the Google form: the filled application, the filled Brief of the Candidate (Annexure-A, pdf), the filled Power Point Presentation (Annexure-B, MS PowerPoint), scanned educational certificates (SSLC to PG; Ph.D for non-medical posts), scanned experience certificates, other relevant documents and latest registration certificates (UG and PG).
  • 4Combine all relevant certificates into a single PDF file in the order listed in the notice and upload it on the Google form.
  • 5Pay the application fee, where applicable, online to the bank account given below.
  • 6Send the duly signed application form with Annexure-A and self-attested photocopies of supporting documents (age, qualification, experience, category) by Speed/Registered Post to: RECRUITMENT CELL, AIIMS GORAKHPUR, ADMINISTRATIVE BLOCK, KUNRAGHAT, GORAKHPUR – 273008.
  • 7Super-scribe the envelope: "APPLICATION FOR THE POST OF ... (name of the post applied for)".
  • 8Deputation applicants must also forward the application through proper channel with the printout, documents related to date of birth, education and experience, No Objection Certificate, Vigilance Clearance Certificate, APAR/ACR for the last 05 years and the Annexure-3 proforma. This must reach the same address within 15 days from the last date of online submission, with the envelope marked "Application for the Post of ... on Deputation basis in the Dept. Of ...". Applications without vigilance clearance and APAR/ACR dossiers will not be considered. Details cannot be changed after submission.
  • 9The fee is paid online to State Bank of India, Branch Giridharganj, Gorakhpur-273008; account holder Recruitment cell, AIIMS, Gorakhpur; Account No 42368584553; IFSC SBIN0018457. Any other instrument of payment is not acceptable and the fee is non-refundable. Annexure-2 states that the fee is exempted for all candidates applying as retired faculty on contract basis. A candidate applying for more than one post must apply separately and pay the fee for each post.
  • 10Based on bio-data, the Search cum Selection Committee may short-list candidates for interview. The interview will be held in Gorakhpur only. The list of eligible candidates, date, time and venue will be put up on the institute website. No TA/DA will be paid for appearing in the interview. Candidates called must produce original documents with photocopies for verification.

AIIMS Gorakhpur Faculty Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many faculty vacancies are there in AIIMS Gorakhpur Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 56 vacancies: 16 Professor, 5 Additional Professor, 19 Associate Professor and 16 Assistant Professor, across 27 departments. The vacancies are provisional and subject to variation.
Q2What is the last date to apply for AIIMS Gorakhpur faculty posts?
A
The last date is 24 December 2026. The Google form and the hard copy sent by Speed/Registered Post are both due by this date; the advertisement opened on 25 November 2026.
Q3What is the qualification for AIIMS Gorakhpur faculty posts?
A
MBBS or equivalent plus MD or MS in the subject of the post, recognised under the IMC Act, 1956 or by the NMC. Super-speciality disciplines require D.M. or M.Ch in the subject, with teaching experience that depends on the post.
Q4What is the age limit for AIIMS Gorakhpur faculty recruitment?
A
Professor and Additional Professor: not exceeding 58 years. Associate Professor and Assistant Professor: not exceeding 50 years, as on 24 December 2026. Relaxation is 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC and 5 years for PWD (OPH) and Government Servants/Ex-Servicemen.
Q5What is the salary for AIIMS Gorakhpur faculty posts?
A
Professor is Level-14A (168900-220400), Additional Professor Level-13A2+ (148200 – 211400), Associate Professor Level-13A1+ (138300 – 209200) and Assistant Professor Level-12 (101500 – 167400), plus usual allowances including NPA (if applicable).
Q6What is the application fee?
A
The fee is Rs. 2,000/- for General/OBC/EWS and Rs. 500/- for SC/ST. PwBD candidates and retired faculty applying on deputation or contractual basis are exempted. The fee is non-refundable.
Q7How do I apply for AIIMS Gorakhpur faculty posts?
A
Submit the Google form at https://forms.gle/L1fQkGNmDXDpboSd8 with the listed documents, and send the signed application with Annexure-A and self-attested copies by Speed/Registered Post to the Recruitment Cell, AIIMS Gorakhpur, by 24 December 2026.
Q8How are candidates selected?
A
The Search cum Selection Committee may short-list candidates based on bio-data, followed by an interview held in Gorakhpur. The interview date, time and venue will be put up on the institute website.
Q9Can retired faculty apply, and what will they be paid?
A
Yes. Retired faculty from INIs and Government Medical Colleges, up to age 68, may apply as Professor (Consultant) at Rs. 2,20,000 per month or Additional Professor (Consultant) at Rs. 2,00,000 per month, on contract for an initial period not exceeding one year.
Q10Can I apply for more than one post?
A
Yes. A candidate applying for more than one post in terms of different designation or department must apply separately for each post and pay the application fee for each post.
