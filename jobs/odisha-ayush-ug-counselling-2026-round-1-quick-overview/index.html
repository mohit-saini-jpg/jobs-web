Odisha AYUSH UG Counselling 2026 Round 1 - Quick Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 28/09/2026

The Odisha Joint Entrance Examination (OJEE) released the Round 1 provisional allotment list for BAMS and BHMS on 25 September 2026 (Notice No. OJEE/0529). Allotted candidates must report in person at the OJEE Office, Gandamunda, Bhubaneswar on 28 or 29 September 2026, depending on their college, and pay an admission fee of Rs 8,500 online.

How to Check the Odisha AYUSH Round 1 Allotment List 2026

  • Open the official OJEE website (ojee.nic.in) or the official allotment PDF linked below.
  • Find your NEET application number or NEET AIR in the list. The PDF has 14 pages.
  • Check your institute, course, and the columns "Allotted Seat Type", "Allotted Quota" and "Allotted Category". The notice asks candidates to verify these carefully.

The notice does not describe any other way to check the result, such as a login-based result page.

Important Instructions for Allotted Candidates

  • Report personally at the OJEE Office, Gandamunda, Bhubaneswar on the notified date and time.
  • Exercise the Freeze or Float option using your login ID.
  • Pay the admission fee of Rs 8,500 online using your login ID.
  • Come alone or with only one guardian.
  • Do not report at your allotted college at this stage. Wait until the entire counselling and admission process is completed.
  • All original documents will be verified. Deviation, tampering, or forgery can lead to cancellation of the allotment and may also cancel the candidature.
  • Any revision of the fee structure by Government order will automatically apply.

Who Should Apply Odisha AYUSH UG (Odisha Posts)

Odisha AYUSH UG (Odisha Posts) Fee & Scholarship Details

Course fee BAMS ke liye approx ₹1.5 lakh aur BHMS ke liye ₹1.2 lakh hai, state government ke scholarship schemes aur fee waiver options eligible meritorious students ke liye available hain.

Odisha AYUSH UG (Odisha Posts) Course & Career Insights

Odisha AYUSH UG (Odisha Posts) Admission Strategy
