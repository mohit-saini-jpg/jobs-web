Indian Bank Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection will be through (1) screening of applications by a Screening Committee and (2) an interview of the shortlisted candidates by a Selection Committee. Only candidates who meet the eligibility criteria will be called for interview, and they will be informed by email. If the number of applications is very large, the bank may adopt a written test, group discussion or preliminary interview, or a combination of these. At the interview, candidates must produce original certificates of qualification, experience, age and category as given in the application. Without them, the candidate will not be allowed to appear in the interview and the candidature will be cancelled.
