STPI Apprentice Trainee Stipend 2026

Selected Apprentice Trainees will receive a monthly stipend based on their qualification: B.E/B.Tech candidates: Rs. 15,000/- per month Bachelor in Arts/Commerce/Administration, etc.: Rs. 12,300/- per month The Screening-cum-Selection Committee may offer a higher stipend, up to Rs. 20,000/- per month, based on the candidate's performance in the interview. The stipend payable to apprentices will be facilitated through the Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) mechanism - the admissible Government share of stipend will be directly credited to the apprentices' bank accounts through the NATS portal after successful registration on the NATS Portal for DBT.

STPI Apprentice Trainee Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1STPI Apprentice Trainee Apply Online process requires mandatory prior registration on the NATS Portal, followed by application through STPI's own web portal. Only online applications will be accepted; offline applications are not considered.
  • 2Register on the National Apprenticeship Training Scheme (NATS) Portal at https://nats.education.gov.in before applying through STPI's portal - this is mandatory.
  • 3On the NATS Portal, click the "Student" section, then "Enroll" under the Student section, confirm you meet the eligibility criteria, and proceed to the Enrollment Form.
  • 4Fill in personal information: name, date of birth, gender, email, mobile number, and create a login ID and password. Enter your Aadhaar number and other required identification details.
  • 5Fill in educational details: highest qualification (Diploma/Graduate), institution name, year of passing, and percentage/CGPA.
  • 6Fill in communication and bank details: present and permanent address, and bank account details (account number, IFSC code, bank name) for stipend processing.
  • 7Review all details carefully and submit the NATS registration form; a unique Student Enrolment ID will be generated and sent to your registered email ID.
  • 8Visit STPI's Web Portal under the Career Section at www.stpi.in and fill the online application for Apprentice Trainee, mentioning your NATS Enrolment ID in STPI's Online application module.
  • 9Submit the completed application on or before 30.09.2026; applications received after the last date will not be considered.
