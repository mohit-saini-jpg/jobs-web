The Andhra Pradesh State Council of Higher Education (APSCHE) has released the AP EAMCET (AP EAPCET) 2026 Final Phase Seat Allotment Result on August 26, 2026. Candidates who have been allotted a seat must complete both online self-reporting and physical reporting at the allotted college from August 27 to August 31, 2026. Students can download their allotment order by logging in with the required credentials. This article provides the AP EAMCET Final Phase Seat Allotment 2026 result details, important dates, login steps, documents required and reporting process.

The AP EAMCET Final Phase Seat Allotment Result 2026 has been released on August 26, 2026. Candidates who registered and submitted their web options for the final phase can check the college and course allotted to them through the AP EAPCET admissions portal.

Candidates who have received an allotment should download the Provisional Seat Allotment Order and complete the required reporting formalities within the prescribed deadline.

How to Check AP EAMCET Final Phase Seat Allotment Result 2026

Candidates can follow the steps below to check and download the AP EAMCET Final Phase Seat Allotment Result 2026.

  • Visit the official AP EAPCET counselling portal at cap.apcfss.in.
  • Find and open the Final Phase Seat Allotment or Candidate Login link.
  • Enter the required login credentials such as your Hall Ticket Number and Date of Birth.
  • Submit the login details.
  • The AP EAMCET Final Phase Seat Allotment details will appear on the screen.
  • Check the allotted college, course/branch and other admission details carefully.
  • Download the Provisional Seat Allotment Order.
  • Take a printout and keep it safely for the self-reporting and college reporting process.

Details Mentioned on AP EAMCET Seat Allotment Order 2026

Candidates should carefully check the information printed on their allotment order after downloading it. It may include:

  • Candidate Name
  • Hall Ticket Number
  • AP EAPCET Rank
  • Allotted College
  • Allotted Course / Branch
  • Category
  • Allotment Details
  • Reporting Instructions
  • Admission and Tuition Fee Details, where applicable
  • Reporting Deadline

Candidates should follow the instructions mentioned in the allotment order before reporting to their allotted institution.

Documents Required for AP EAMCET College Reporting

Candidates who have been allotted a seat should keep their original certificates and required photocopies ready for admission.

The commonly required documents include:

  • AP EAMCET 2026 Rank Card
  • AP EAMCET 2026 Hall Ticket
  • Provisional Seat Allotment Order
  • Class 12 / Intermediate Marks Memo
  • Class 10 / SSC Marks Memo
  • Transfer Certificate (TC)
  • Study Certificates
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Residence / Local Status Certificate, where applicable
  • Caste / Community Certificate, where applicable
  • Income Certificate, where applicable
  • EWS Certificate, where applicable
  • Fee Payment Receipt
  • Other certificates required for the reservation or special category claimed by the candidate

Candidates should also follow any additional document requirements mentioned in their allotment order or by the allotted college.

AP EAMCET 2026 Post-Allotment Process

Getting a seat in the AP EAMCET Final Phase allotment does not by itself complete the admission process. Candidates have to follow the post-allotment procedure within the scheduled dates.

1. Online Self-Reporting

Candidates who are satisfied with the allotted seat must log in to the AP EAPCET counselling portal and complete the online self-reporting process.

Candidates should download or save the acknowledgement generated after completing self-reporting.

2. Report at the Allotted College

After completing online self-reporting, candidates must physically report to the college allotted to them.

Students should carry the required original certificates, photocopies, provisional allotment order and other documents mentioned in the admission instructions.

3. Complete Admission Formalities

The allotted college will verify the candidate's documents and complete the remaining admission formalities according to the applicable rules.

AP EAMCET Final Phase Reporting Dates 2026

Candidates allotted seats in the final phase must complete the required reporting process between:

August 27, 2026 and August 31, 2026

Both online self-reporting and physical reporting at the allotted institution should be completed within the prescribed period.

Candidates who fail to complete the required reporting formalities within the deadline may lose their allotted seat as per the counselling rules.

What to Do After AP EAMCET Final Phase Seat Allotment 2026?

Candidates who have received an allotment should complete the following steps:

  • Download the provisional allotment order.
  • Read all instructions printed on the allotment order.
  • Complete online self-reporting.
  • Keep all original certificates ready.
  • Report physically to the allotted college.
  • Submit the required documents for verification.
  • Complete the admission and fee-payment formalities, if applicable.
  • Obtain acknowledgement of admission from the institution.
