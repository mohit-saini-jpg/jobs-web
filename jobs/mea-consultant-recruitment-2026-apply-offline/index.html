Pay and Tenure

The remuneration package will be commensurate with the experience and profile of the selected candidate, subject to a maximum of Rs. 83,300/- (Rupees Eighty Three Thousand and Three Hundred only) per month and deduction of applicable taxes. The amount of consultancy fee so fixed shall remain unchanged during the term of engagement. No HRA shall be admissible. Period of consultancy: 01 (One) year. The contract can be extended further depending on assessment of performance, mutual willingness and the requirement. The contract may be terminated on one month's notice from either side or on payment of one month's pay in lieu thereof. Unauthorized absence may lead to termination of the engagement. The engagement is full time. Normal working hours are 0900 to 1730 hrs and are subject to change from time to time. Saturdays and Sundays remain closed as weekend off. MEA Consultant (Legal) in DE Division Job Description

Handling of various court cases in which Ministry/DE Division is party. Seeking legal opinion in various marital disputes, child abduction and matter related to Indian students abroad. Seeking legal opinion on various bills of DE Division including NRI Marriage bill. The job location is the Ministry of External Affairs (including Jawaharlal Nehru Bhawan, Sushma Swaraj Bhawan, Patiala House, ISIL Building and Kartavya Bhavan), New Delhi.

Selection and Interview

The Ministry of External Affairs will review the applications and shortlist candidates for interview / written test. Shortlisted candidates will be intimated by email. The date, time and venue of the interview will be conveyed through email at the address given by the applicant. The decision of the Government regarding selection of candidate will be final.
