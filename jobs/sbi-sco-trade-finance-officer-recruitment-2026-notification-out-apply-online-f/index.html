SBI SCO Trade Finance Officer Recruitment 2026 Salary

Selected officials will be eligible for Dearness Allowance (DA), House Rent Allowance (HRA), City Compensatory Allowance (CCA), Provident Fund, Contributory Pension Fund (NPS), Leave Fare Concession (LFC), medical facility, and other perquisites as per rules in force from time to time. JMGS-I (Maker role): Inputs trade transactions and uploads documents into banking systems, handles TI+ data entry, conducts document scrutiny before approval, and inputs screening data into relevant systems. MMGS-II (Checker role): Conducts TI+ checking to validate transactions, performs document scrutiny, facilitates screening against sanctions and AML policies, validates regulatory compliance, and acts as an OFAC releaser. Both roles require working in rotational shifts, which may be subject to change as per the Bank's requirements.

SBI SCO Trade Finance Officer Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection for the Trade Finance Officer - IBG post will be made on the basis of Shortlisting and Interview. Shortlisting: Merely fulfilling the minimum qualification and experience does not entitle a candidate to be called for interview. The shortlisting committee will decide the parameters, and an adequate number of candidates will be shortlisted. Interview: The interview carries 100 marks. Qualifying marks will be decided by the Bank. Merit List: The final merit list will be prepared in descending order based on interview scores. In case of a tie at the cut-off, candidates will be ranked by age in descending order. The call letter/intimation for interview will be sent by email or uploaded on the Bank's website; no hard copy will be sent.
