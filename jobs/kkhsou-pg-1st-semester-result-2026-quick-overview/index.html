KKHSOU PG 1st Semester Result 2026 - Quick Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 24/07/2026

The Krishna Kanta Handiqui State Open University (KKHSOU) has declared the result of the PG First Semester (1st Cycle) Backlog Examination 2026 for learners under both NEP and Non-NEP streams. The result covers the MA I Semester, MBA I Semester, and MCOM I Semester programmes for the examination held in March 2026. The official result notification was posted on the KKHSOU website on 24 July 2026, with individual result sheets carrying result dates of 23 and 24 July 2026.

Programmes and Subjects Covered

The result PDF covers the following programmes:

  • MA I Semester - results declared subject-wise for: Assamese, Economics, Education, English, Journalism and Mass Communication, Political Science, and Sociology
  • MBA I Semester
  • MCOM I Semester

Each result sheet lists the registration numbers of candidates who have cleared the backlog paper(s), college/study-centre-wise, along with a result-statistics summary (total, appeared, absent, clear, not clear, and result withheld) for that college and subject.

How to Check the KKHSOU PG 1st Semester Backlog Result 2026

  • Visit the official KKHSOU website Results section.
  • Open the notification titled "Declaration of Results - PG First Semester (1st Cycle) Examination (2026) Backlog Examination NEP/NON NEP."
  • Click on the "View File" link to open the official result PDF.
  • Locate your college/study centre code and name in the document, then check the subject-wise list under it.
  • Your result is shown as your Registration Number listed under the "Clear" heading for that subject. If your registration number does not appear, refer to the result-statistics table on that page to see the total not-clear or absent count for your college and subject.

How to Download the Result PDF and E-Mark Sheet

The consolidated result PDF is available directly from the official notification page via the "View File" link. As per the notification, individual e-mark sheets and NAD/ABC records will be made available on the KKHSOU website shortly - a specific release date for these is not mentioned in the notification.

What to Do If You Find a Discrepancy in Your Result

As per the official notification, the university has laid out a clear process for handling result discrepancies:

  • Learners who notice a discrepancy in their result must send a written application accompanied by all supporting documents.
  • The application must reach the Office of the Controller of Examinations, Resham Nagar, Khanapara, Guwahati-781022, or be sent by email only to [email protected].
  • The application must be submitted within 30 days from the date of declaration of the result.
  • No application will be entertained after this 30-day window.
  • In case of any discrepancy between the e-mark sheet and the hardcopy mark sheet, the hardcopy mark sheet issued by the university will be treated as valid.

Important Instructions

  • Learners are advised to carefully verify their results as soon as they are checked.
  • In case of any discrepancy, only the procedure and instructions specified in the result notification are to be followed.
  • The university has stated that utmost care has been taken in declaring the result; however, if any discrepancy is detected, it will be processed as per university norms.
  • The hardcopy mark sheet issued by the university takes precedence over the e-mark sheet in case of any mismatch.
