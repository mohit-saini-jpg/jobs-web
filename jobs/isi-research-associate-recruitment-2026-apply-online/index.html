Who Can Apply

Minimum qualification: Ph.D. degree in Statistics or Mathematics with at least one publication in a Science Citation Index (SCI) Journal. Candidates who have submitted their thesis may also apply. Researchers, particularly those who have recently obtained their PhD degree, are encouraged to apply. The selected candidate will be placed in Category-I, II or III depending on qualification and experience, as per DST OM No. SR/S9/Z-08/2018 dated 30 January 2019 and DST/PCPM/Z-06/2022 dated 26 June 2023. Candidates should check the official notification for the full eligibility conditions of the post. The age limit is 35 years as on 13 December 2026.

Pay and Tenure

HRA is paid on top of these emoluments as per the rules of the Institute. If the Institute is unable to provide hostel accommodation, House Rent Allowance is payable with the respective emoluments as per the rules of the Institute. The appointment is purely on a temporary, contractual basis. It is initially for one year from the date of joining and may be extended or terminated earlier, depending on availability of funds and the performance of the candidate. Based on performance, the term of research associateship can be up to a maximum period of 2 years.

ISI Research Associate (RA) Job Description

Research Associates are expected to get involved in teaching and research work in Statistics/Mathematics.

Selection and Interview

Applications will be short-listed. Short-listed candidates will be asked to give a seminar followed by an interaction with the faculty. A merit list will be prepared, and if the selected candidate fails to produce the original documents and medical fitness certificate at joining, the Institute will offer the position to the next candidate in that merit list. The notice does not give a date for the seminar.
