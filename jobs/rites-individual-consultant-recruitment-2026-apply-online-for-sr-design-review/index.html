RITES Consultant Experience Requirement 2026

Total Experience Required: 6 Years Minimum 4 years of specific experience in modelling, analysis and design of superstructure of RCC and PSC long span bridges. Experience with MIDAS and/or Sofistik is required. Experience area includes major river bridges, ROBs, flyovers and underpasses related to highway projects. Total Experience Required: 7 Years Minimum 5 years of specific experience in modelling, analysis and design of superstructure and substructure of RCC and PSC long span bridges. Knowledge of STAAD, MIDAS and/or Sofistik is required. Experience area includes river bridges, grade separators, flyovers and underpasses related to highway projects. Total Experience Required: 6 Years Minimum 4 years of specific experience in modelling, analysis and design of superstructure and substructure of RCC and PSC long span bridges.
