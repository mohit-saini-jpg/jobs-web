APPSC Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Not specified in the Brief Notification. Educational qualification for this post will be published along with the Detailed Notification on or before 6 October 2026. Applicants must possess a Four-year B.Sc. Degree / B.Sc. (Hons) Degree in Horticulture of a recognized University in the State, or of any other University accredited by the Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR), Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India, New Delhi. Applicants must possess the requisite qualification as on the date of the Detailed Notification. Additional conditions for Assistant Director (FSL) posts: candidates must have exceptionally good eyesight (colour blindness will be a disqualification); must have at least 5 years’ practical experience in a Forensic Science, Chemistry, Analytical, or Police Laboratory; further relevant experience is desirable.

APPSC Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

For all three posts, the examination consists of a single-level examination only ; no Screening Test is conducted irrespective of the number of applications received. The examination is held in online (CBRT) mode, and if conducted in multiple sessions, the Commission will apply normalisation of marks wherever necessary, per its own methodology (to be notified in due course). Syllabus highlights (Paper II – Common Subject): Ecosystems; Natural Resources; Biodiversity and Biotic Resources; Engineering Materials; Fluid Mechanics and Hydraulics; Process Calculations and Thermodynamics; Air, Water, Soil and Noise Pollution and Control Technologies; Solid and Industrial Waste Management; Global Environmental Problems, Policies and Legislations; Environmental Impact Assessment towards Sustainable Future. APPSC Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many total posts has APPSC advertised across these three notifications?
A
85 posts in total – 40 Assistant Environmental Engineer, 37 Horticulture Officer, and 8 Assistant Director (Forensic Science Laboratory).
Q2Do all three posts share the same application window?
A
Yes. Online applications for all three open on 6 October 2026 and close on 27 October 2026 at 11:59 PM.
Q3What is the qualification for Horticulture Officer?
A
A Four-year B.Sc. Degree / B.Sc. (Hons) Degree in Horticulture from a University recognized in Andhra Pradesh or accredited by ICAR.
Q4What is the qualification for Assistant Director (FSL)?
A
It varies by discipline – B.E. in Mechanical/Structural Engineering for Forensic Engineering, an M.Sc. in Physics (or allied subjects) for Physics, an M.Sc. in Chemistry (or B.Sc. with A.I.C. in Forensic Sciences) for Chemistry, and an M.Sc. in Botany/Zoology/Microbiology/Biochemistry (or allied subjects, or a Medicine degree with a Science Bachelor’s) for Biology.
Q5What is the selection process for these posts?
A
All three use a single-level online (CBRT) written examination with no interview or screening test. Assistant Environmental Engineer and Assistant Director (FSL) have a Paper I + Paper II structure totalling 450 marks; Horticulture Officer additionally has a qualifying English & Telugu test plus three papers (I, II, III) totalling 450 marks.
Q6Can I apply for more than one of these posts?
A
The notifications reviewed do not state a restriction against applying for more than one post, but each requires its own separate online application through psc.ap.gov.in.
Q7Where do I apply?
A
Applications for all three posts must be submitted online only through psc.ap.gov.in; applications through any other mode will not be entertained.
