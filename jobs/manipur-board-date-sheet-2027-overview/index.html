Manipur Board Date Sheet 2027 - Overview : Admit Card Overview

The Board of Secondary Education, Manipur (BSEM), and the Council of Higher Secondary Education, Manipur (COHSEM), have released the examination schedules for Classes 10, 11 and 12 for 2027. According to the official timetables, the HSLC Class 10 examinations will be held from 10 to 25 February 2027, the Class 11 examinations from 18 February to 31 March 2027, and the HSE Class 12 examinations from 11 February to 19 March 2027.

Manipur Board Date Sheet 2027 - Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 11/11/2026

BSEM issued the Class 10 HSLC timetable on 6 November 2026, while COHSEM published the Class 11 and Higher Secondary Class 12 schedules on 10 November 2026. Students can download the examination programmes through the official websites.

The examination timings differ by class. Class 10 papers will begin at 9:00 AM, Class 11 papers at 1:00 PM, and Class 12 papers at 10:00 AM. COHSEM has also scheduled the Class 11 practical examinations from 6 January to 6 February 2027 and the Class 12 practical examinations from 2 to 30 January 2027. Students should check the subject-wise dates, examination durations and practical examination instructions before preparing for the board examinations.

How to Download Manipur Board Date Sheet 2027?

Students can download the timetables from the relevant official board website.

For Class 10 HSLC Students

  • Visit bosem.in.
  • Open the official notifications service.
  • Find the HSLC Examination 2027 timetable notice dated 6 November 2026.
  • Open the attached HSLC examination programme PDF.
  • Check the subject-wise examination dates and durations.
  • Download and save the timetable.

For Class 11 and Class 12 Students

  • Visit cohsem.nic.in.
  • Open the Notice section.
  • Find the notification dated 10 November 2026 concerning the Class XI and Higher Secondary Examination 2027 programmes.
  • Select Annexure I for the Class 11 timetable.
  • Select Annexure II for the Class 12 timetable.
  • Open the relevant PDF and verify the examination dates, timings and practical examination notes.
  • Download the document for future reference.
