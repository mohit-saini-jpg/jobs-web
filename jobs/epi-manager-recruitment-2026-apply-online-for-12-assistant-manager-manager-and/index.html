EPI Age Limit 2026

The maximum age limit ranges from 32 to 42 years, depending on the post and reserved category. Age will be calculated as of 29 September 2026. SC and ST candidates: 5 years OBC Non-Creamy Layer candidates: 3 years Persons with Disabilities: 10 years Age relaxation will apply to the reserved posts according to Government rules. Reserved-category candidates applying against an unreserved post will not receive category-based age relaxation.

EPI Application Fee 2026

The official notification does not specify an application fee.

EPI Contract Period

The appointment will initially be made for one year. It may be extended up to three years based on satisfactory performance and organisational requirements.

EPI Selection Process 2026

Candidates will be shortlisted based on their educational qualification marks and relevant post-qualification experience. Initial scrutiny of online applications Shortlisting based on qualification and experience Written test, if required Personal interview Document verification If EPI receives a large number of applications, it may conduct a written test for screening candidates. Only shortlisted candidates will be called for the interview. Corporate Office, New Delhi Western Regional Office, Mumbai Project Coordination Office, Bhubaneswar Southern Regional Office, Chennai The interview date, venue and reporting time will be communicated to shortlisted candidates through email.

Documents Required for EPI Recruitment 2026

Candidates called for the interview must produce the following original documents along with one set of self-attested copies: System-generated online application form Updated resume or bio-data Class 10 certificate as proof of date of birth Class 10 and Class 12 mark sheets and certificates Graduation and postgraduate mark sheets and certificates Professional qualification certificates, if applicable Post-qualification experience certificates Appointment, joining and relieving orders, where applicable Pay slips, Form 16 or other remuneration proof SC, ST, OBC-NCL, EWS, PwD or Ex-Serviceman certificate, if applicable Aadhaar card, PAN card, voter ID or passport One recent passport-size colour photograph Any other relevant supporting document

EPI Manager Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

Candidates should verify their eligibility before submitting the application. Incomplete applications or applications without the required information may be rejected. The qualification percentage must be entered without rounding off. A score of 54.9% will be treated as below 55%. The age and experience cut-off date is 29 September 2026. The date of declaration of the qualification result will be treated as the date of acquiring the qualification. Selected candidates may be posted anywhere in India according to EPI requirements. No TA or DA will be provided for attending the interview. All recruitment correspondence will be sent to the registered email address. Candidates should regularly check the EPI website for corrigenda and recruitment updates.
