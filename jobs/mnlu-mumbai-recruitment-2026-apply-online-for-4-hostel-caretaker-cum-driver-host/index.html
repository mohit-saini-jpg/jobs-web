MNLU Mumbai Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Maharashtra National Law University Mumbai is hiring for 4 hostel posts on a one-year contract: 2 Hostel Caretaker (Female) posts and 2 Hostel Caretaker-cum-Driver (Male) posts. Candidates who have passed 10+2 and are 25 to 45 years old can apply. Both posts pay a consolidated salary of Rs. 25,000/-. The deadline for the online application is 15 November 2026 at 23.59 hrs, and you must also send a printed copy to the University in Powai, Mumbai.

About MNLU Mumbai

Maharashtra National Law University Mumbai was set up on 27th June 2014 by the Government of Maharashtra under Act VI of 2014. It is on the 2nd Floor of the MTNL-CETTM Building, Technology Street, Hiranandani Gardens, Powai, Mumbai - 400 076. These posts are for the University's hostel.

MNLU Mumbai Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection is by interview. Shortlisted candidates receive call letters. At the interview you must bring your original documents for verification, plus two sets of photocopies of all of them. The Selection Committee may relax or change the eligibility criteria or selection requirements for suitable candidates, subject to the rules and the Competent Authority's approval.

Service Conditions

The appointment is purely temporary, contractual and ad-hoc. It gives no right to a regular appointment, continuation, permanency or absorption in the University. The contract runs for one year. Any extension depends on satisfactory performance, the University's continuing needs and the prior written approval of the Competent Authority. Extensions are never automatic. Both posts are full-time residential service. The rules and regulations of the University and the Government, and the directives of the Government of Maharashtra, apply to these posts. The University may fill or not fill any post, or withdraw any post, without giving a reason.

MNLU Mumbai Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1Can men apply for the Hostel Caretaker post?
A
No. The Hostel Caretaker post is for women only. Men can apply for the Hostel Caretaker-cum-Driver (Male) post, which also has 2 vacancies.
Q2Do I need a driving licence?
A
Only for the Hostel Caretaker-cum-Driver (Male) post, which requires a four-wheeler Light Vehicle Driving Licence.
Q3Is experience compulsory?
A
For the Caretaker-cum-Driver post, yes: one year of experience in a similar field is essential, with a certificate as proof. For the female Caretaker post, one year of experience is listed under Desirable.
Q4Do I have to live on campus?
A
Yes. Both posts are described as "Full Time (Residential Service)".
Q5What is the age relaxation for reserved categories?
A
The upper age limit is relaxed by up to five years if you produce a valid caste or category certificate from the competent authority.
Q6Where do I send the printed application?
A
To the Registrar, Maharashtra National Law University Mumbai, 2nd Floor, CETTM MTNL Building, Technology Street, Hiranandani Gardens, Powai, Mumbai-400076. You can post it or hand it in.
Q7Can this contract become a permanent job?
A
No. The notice says the appointment gives no claim to regularisation, permanency or absorption. It may be extended only with written approval from the Competent Authority.
Q8What should I bring to the interview?
A
Your original documents for verification and two sets of photocopies of all of them.
Q9Is the Google form compulsory?
A
Yes. You must fill in your details on the Google link given in the notice and submit its printout with your application form.
