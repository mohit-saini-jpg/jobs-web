About DPMU Haveri

The notice is issued by the District Programme Management Unit of the National Health Mission, in the office of the District Health and Family Welfare Officer, District Administrative Building, Devagiri, Haveri, under the Government of Karnataka and Zilla Panchayat Haveri. It states that the posts are sanctioned and vacant under various national health programmes and PM-ABHIM in the district, and that they are filled with the approval of the Chief Executive Officer of the Zilla Panchayat and the President of the District Health and Family Welfare Society.

Department-wise Vacancies

The notice lists 59 posts across 21 designations. The table follows the notice's programme-wise break-up. The notice gives a reservation roster for each group of posts. The rows below show the main categories as printed; the notice also gives the break-up by women, rural, disabled, ex-servicemen, Kannada medium, project-displaced, third gender and sports persons, with reservation points. The Medical Officer roster points are 1–3 (NP-NCDs), 4–9 (ICU/HDU) and 10–13 (PM-ABHIM Namma Clinic). The nursing roster points are 250–253 (MH), 254–258 (CH), 259–262 (ICU/HDU), 263–265 (PM-ABHIM) and 266–267 (NP-NCDs). The Psychiatric Nurse, Psychologist, Audiologist/Speech Therapist, Optometrist and Physiotherapist posts are each shown under the general category.

Pay and Terms

The notice prints no pay amount for any post and says pay and age limit are to be fixed as per NHM/NUHM norms. All posts are filled on contract basis. The notice does not give the length of the contract. Appointments are made on merit and roster (reservation) basis. The Society reserves the right to increase or decrease the number of posts.

DHFWS Haveri Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

Candidates must appear in person; online, registered post and e-mail applications are not considered for any reason. KMC registration is compulsory for Medical Officers; provisional registration receipts are not considered. Nursing Officers and PHCOs need Karnataka Nursing Council registration; Lab Technicians need Karnataka State Paramedical Board registration. The notice says applications of those without the required registration are rejected. The District Health and Family Welfare Society reserves the right to increase or decrease the number of posts. Further information is available at the office during office hours and on the notice board.

DHFWS Haveri Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in DPMU Haveri Recruitment 2026?
A
The notice lists 59 posts in 21 designations, including 13 Medical Officers, 19 Nursing Officers (18 plus 1 for MHU), 8 Lab Technicians and 10 Primary Health Safety Officers.
Q2What is the last date to apply?
A
Applications are issued and received from 5 November 2026 to 16 November 2026, between 10:30 AM and 5:30 PM.
Q3Can I apply online or by post?
A
No. The notice says applications sent online, by registered post or by e-mail are not considered. Candidates must appear in person at the concerned office.
Q4What is the qualification for Medical Officer?
A
MBBS pass with compulsory internship and KMC registration, plus the Convocation/Degree Certificate. For NP-NCDs, a minimum of 3 years' hospital work experience is also required.
Q5What is the qualification for Nursing Officer?
A
Female Nursing Officers (MH, CH, ICU/HDU) need a General Nursing Diploma from an institution recognised by the Karnataka Government and Karnataka Nursing Council registration. PM-ABHIM Nursing Officers need B.Sc or Diploma in Nursing. Other nursing posts have their own conditions listed above.
Q6What is the age limit?
A
The notice prints a limit only for Nursing Officer (MHU): maximum 45 years.
Q7Is this a permanent job?
A
No. The notice says the posts are filled on contract basis under National Health Mission programmes and PM-ABHIM.
Q8How are candidates selected?
A
The notice says appointment is made on merit and roster (reservation) basis.
Q9Where do I apply for the Nursing Officer (MHU) and X-Ray Technician posts?
A
At the office of the District Tuberculosis Eradication Officer, District Hospital premises, Haveri, between 10:30 AM and 5:30 PM.
Q10Which documents must be submitted with the application?
A
Self-attested photocopies of the original documents and 2 copies of a recent photograph, along with marks cards and caste or other reservation certificates as listed in the notice.
