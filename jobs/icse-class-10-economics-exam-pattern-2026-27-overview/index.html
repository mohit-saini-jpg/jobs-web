ICSE Class 10 Economics Exam Pattern 2026-27 - Overview : Syllabus Overview

The Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations (CISCE) has prescribed the examination structure for Economics (Subject Code 64) for the ICSE Class 10 Board Examination 2027. The subject carries 100 marks, including an 80-mark written theory examination and 20 marks for internal assessment. The written examination has a duration of two hours, with an additional 15 minutes provided for reading the question paper.

According to the Economics question-paper blueprint, the theory examination consists of two sections: Section A (40 marks), which is compulsory, and Section B (40 marks), which offers internal choice. Candidates are required to answer four out of six structured questions in Section B. The paper assesses economic concepts, definitions, comparisons, interpretation of demand and supply, and the application of economic principles to practical situations.

Students preparing for the 2026-27 academic session should understand the marking scheme, question format, internal project requirements and passing criteria before beginning their final revision. The official CISCE syllabus for Examination Year 2027 is available at Official Website.

ICSE Economics Section A Exam Pattern 2026-27

Section A is the compulsory part of the Economics theory examination and carries 40 marks.

The blueprint includes objective questions and short conceptual responses designed to assess knowledge across the syllabus.

Questions may test:

  • Definitions of Important Economic Terms
  • Basic Features of Factors of Production
  • Law of Demand and Law of Supply
  • Identification of Market Structures
  • Functions of Money and Banks
  • Types of Inflation
  • Direct and Indirect Taxes
  • Consumer Rights and Duties

How to Prepare for ICSE Class 10 Economics Exam 2027?

Students can improve their preparation by combining conceptual study with regular answer-writing practice.

  • Read the complete CISCE Class 10 Economics syllabus.
  • Prepare definitions and characteristics of important economic terms.
  • Revise factors of production and their roles.
  • Practise drawing and labelling demand and supply curves.
  • Learn differences between the main market structures.
  • Revise the functions of commercial and central banks.
  • Understand monetary policy, taxes and public finance.
  • Prepare short notes on inflation and consumer protection.
  • Practise the official specimen question paper.
  • Attempt previous board examination questions where applicable.
  • Allocate enough time to the compulsory and structured-answer sections.
  • Complete the internal project carefully and submit it on time.
