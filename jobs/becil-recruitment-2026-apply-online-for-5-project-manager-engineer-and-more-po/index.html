BECIL Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Shortlisting: applications are screened on credentials and suitability for the post, using pre-approved criteria. Interview: shortlisted candidates are called for an interview in virtual or physical mode. Only shortlisted candidates are informed. Screening-cum-Selection Committee: a committee set up by BECIL selects candidates. Candidates present their original credentials (experience, achievements, suitability) during the interview. Final selection: based on qualifications, experience, suitability for the post and interview performance. Document verification: candidature stays provisional until original documents for age, qualification, experience and category are verified. Selected candidates must submit a Police Verification Report.
