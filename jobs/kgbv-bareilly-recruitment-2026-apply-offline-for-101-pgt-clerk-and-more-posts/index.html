KGBV Bareilly Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Vacancies will be calculated post-wise, subject-wise, and category-wise at the district level, and applications will be invited accordingly. A separate merit list will be prepared post-wise, subject-wise and category-wise for selecting candidates. For Peon, Watchman and Cook posts, selection will be based on an interview. For other posts, candidates will be ranked using the following quality-marks weightage: If two or more candidates secure equal quality marks, the older candidate (by age) will be ranked higher in the merit list. If quality marks and age are also equal, candidates will be ranked in alphabetical order of their name (in English). Certificates submitted by shortlisted candidates for teaching and non-teaching posts will be verified from the concerned board/institution, and original educational records will be kept secured with the District Project Office. KGBV Bareilly Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

All posts under this notification are reserved exclusively for women candidates. The maximum age for serving in a Kasturba Gandhi Awasiya Balika Vidyalaya is 60 years. Selection of teaching and non-teaching staff in the upgraded KGBVs will be on a contract basis for a period of 11 months 29 days. If a working teacher's performance is found satisfactory, a new contract of 11 months 29 days will be issued for the following academic session after approval of the District Magistrate - meaning a fresh service contract is executed every year. The annual evaluation of a teacher's work and conduct will be carried out by the concerned Block Education Officer. The contract will provide that if services are found unsuitable, they may be terminated by giving one month's notice. In cases of serious circumstances, financial irregularity, or serious indiscipline, the District Basic Education Officer may place substantiated evidence before the District Magistrate, and upon approval, the contract may be terminated along with lodging of a First Information Report (FIR) against the concerned person. Incomplete, erroneous, unclear, unsigned, or improperly formatted applications, or those lacking required enclosures, will be rejected and will not be considered. Applications where only photocopies of certificates are sent by registered/speed post, without the original application form being filled and attached, will be treated as rejected. The district-level selection committee reserves the right to cancel the notification at any time, and no claim in this regard will be entertained. Full authority for any changes or corrections rests with the district-level selection committee. The number of posts advertised may increase or decrease. Clerical errors, if any, will be condoned.
