HP NEET MDS Round 2 Counselling 2026 - Important Dates : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 22/09/2026

Atal Medical and Research University (AMRU), Himachal Pradesh has released the HP NEET MDS 2026 Round 2 counselling merit list on the official website. The list is titled "Merit List of MDS 2nd Round Counselling 2026-27" and is meant for candidates who registered for the second round of state quota MDS counselling in Himachal Pradesh. The merit list decides which candidates are eligible to take part in the choice filling and seat allotment process for MDS courses in the state's dental colleges for the 2026-27 academic session.

How to Download the HP NEET MDS 2026 Round 2 Merit List PDF

Candidates can follow these steps to check their name and rank in the merit list and download the PDF:

  • Visit the official AMRU website: amruhp.ac.in.
  • Go to the "Notifications" or "Counselling" section on the homepage.
  • Click on the link titled "Merit List of MDS 2nd Round Counselling 2026-27."
  • The merit list will open as a PDF file in a new tab.
  • Use Ctrl+F (or Cmd+F on Mac) to search your roll number or name within the PDF.
  • Download and save the PDF, or take a printout for reference during choice filling and document verification.

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Regularly check amruhp.ac.in, as counselling dates for state authorities are sometimes revised.
  • Cross-check your name, roll number, category and NEET MDS score in the merit list carefully.
  • Report any discrepancy in your merit list entry through the mechanism specified by AMRU, within the given timeline.
  • Complete choice filling and locking well before the deadline of September 23, 2026, to avoid last-minute technical issues.
  • Keep all original documents ready before the reporting/joining dates of September 29-30, 2026.
  • Inclusion in the merit list does not guarantee a seat; final allotment depends on rank, choices filled and seat availability.
