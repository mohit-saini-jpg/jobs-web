GSV Faculty Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Gati Shakti Vishwavidyalaya (GSV), a Central University under the Ministry of Railways, Government of India, has invited applications for 19 faculty positions in seven engineering domains: 6 Professor posts (Academic Pay Level-14, Rs 1,44,200-2,18,200) and 13 Associate Professor posts (Academic Pay Level-13A, Rs. 1,31,400-2,17,100). Applications are online only at https://gsv.ac.in/careers/, opening on 18 November 2026 and closing on 31 December 2026 (11:59 PM).

About the University

Gati Shakti Vishwavidyalaya is a Central University established through an Act of Parliament in 2022, at Lalbaug, Vadodara, Gujarat. The notice describes it as India's first University focussed on transport and logistics related education, multidisciplinary research and training. It invites candidates with an excellent academic background for a wide range of faculty positions depending on qualifications, experience and specialisation.

GSV Faculty Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

The notice lists eight steps, ending in an offer of the position. Selection is based on interview, and only shortlisted candidates are told the date, time and mode of the interview. The shortlisting committee gives its recommendation on the suitability of candidates for the respective faculty position. Candidates recommended by the committee are called for a seminar and interaction. The seminar and interaction is held. Recommendation letters are sought from referees identified after the seminar and interaction. A final shortlist is made based on the application, faculty input, seminar, interaction and referee comments. Shortlisted candidates are called for a personal interview. The Selection Committee conducts the interview. Selected candidates are offered the position, as advertised, on approval of the competent authority. GSV Faculty Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Apply through the link on the GSV website, https://gsv.ac.in/careers/. The notice allows the online mode only.
  • 2Apply between 18 November 2026 (application opens) and 31 December 2026, 11:59 PM (application closes).
  • 3Keep ready the photo, signature and all documents for educational or technical qualifications, experience, publications and other details, in the format and size specified on the portal.
  • 4If applying for more than one position or for positions in multiple disciplines, fill in a separate online application for each.
  • 5Complete the form in all respects, including uploading all documents and paying the fee, by the closing time. The notice says this is the sole responsibility of the applicant.
  • 6The fee is payable through the payment gateway by Internet banking, debit card, credit card or UPI, through the online application mode only.
  • 7Government, semi-government or autonomous body employees should send the application through proper channel (a letter from the employer uploaded during the online application) or produce a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the employer at the time of the interview. For the NOC route, a declaration as per Proforma-A is submitted with the online application.
  • 8Candidates in a private organisation, or on contract or temporary basis in a government, semi-government or autonomous organisation with no rule for issuing an NOC or forwarding applications, must upload a declaration as per Proforma-B with the online application.
  • 9Proforma-A states that the original hard copy of the NOC will be submitted at the interview, and that an application may be rejected summarily and the candidate not allowed to appear in the interview if the NOC is not submitted.
  • 10Outstation candidates called for interview may be reimbursed travel expenses from the place of residence in India to the place of interview (Vadodara) and back by the shortest route. This applies as per the instruction in the call letter, on production of tickets or invoice.
