Educational Qualification

Candidates must possess the following qualifications as of the closing date of the online application: A good academic record with at least 55% marks , or its equivalent under the applicable grading system, in a Master’s degree in Anthropology from a recognized Indian university. Candidates must have cleared the eligibility test for Assistant Professor, such as NET conducted by UGC or CSIR , or a similar test accredited by the State Public Service Commission. HPPSC Assistant Professor Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

The HPPSC Assistant Professor Anthropology selection process includes the following stages: Paper I – Objective Type Screening Test Paper II – Descriptive Subject Aptitude Test Document Verification Personality Test Paper I will be conducted as a Computer Based Test or offline screening test. It will carry 100 marks and have a duration of one hour. The detailed syllabus for Paper II will be uploaded separately on the official HPPSC website. One-fourth of the marks assigned to a question will be deducted for every incorrect answer in Paper I. If more than one answer is marked, it will be treated as incorrect and the same penalty will apply. Candidates who do not want to answer a question must select option “E”. If no option is selected, one-fourth of the marks assigned to that question will be deducted. The Personality Test will carry 30 marks and will be held at the HPPSC office in Shiml
