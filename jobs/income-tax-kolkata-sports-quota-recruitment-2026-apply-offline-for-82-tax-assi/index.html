Income Tax Department Sports Quota Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Applications are scrutinized for nationality, age, educational qualification, and best sporting performances between 01.01.2022 and 30.06.2026. Eligible shortlisted candidates are called before the Selection Committee for Document Verification of attested photocopies and Forms submitted with the application; only documents submitted with the original application are considered. Candidates already employed must submit a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from their present employer at Document Verification. Shortlisted candidates then undergo a Ground/Field Proficiency Test to evaluate their proficiency in the respective Sport/Game. Candidates shortlisted for Tax Assistant (TA) must additionally qualify a Data Entry Skill Test at 8,000 key depressions per hour. Q1What is Income Tax Department Sports Quota Recruitment 2026?
A
It is the recruitment of meritorious sportspersons to Tax Assistant (TA) and Multi-Tasking Staff (MTS) posts in the Income Tax Department, West Bengal & Sikkim Region, under DoPT OM No. DOPT-1720781414772 dated 04.03.2024.
Q2How many vacancies are available?
A
82 total vacancies - 25 for Tax Assistant and 57 for Multi-Tasking Staff - spread across 17 recognized Games/Sports.
Q3What is the last date to apply?
A
The completed application must reach the Office of the Joint Commissioner of Income Tax, Kolkata, on or before 30.09.2026.
Q4What is the qualification required?
A
A Degree from a recognized University (or equivalent) for Tax Assistant; Matriculation/Class 10 pass (or equivalent) for Multi-Tasking Staff - both assessed as on 01.07.2026.
Q5What is the age limit?
A
18 to 27 years for Tax Assistant and 18 to 25 years for Multi-Tasking Staff, as on 01.07.2026, with age relaxation of 5 years for UR/OBC and 10 years for SC/ST candidates.
