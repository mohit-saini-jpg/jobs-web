KNRUHS Result 2026 - Quick Overview : Result Overview

The Kaloji Narayana Rao University of Health Sciences (KNRUHS), Telangana, has published the results of the MDS Part-II examinations held in September 2026 and the B.Sc Nursing First Semester examinations held in July 2026. According to the university's results page, all four notifications are dated 26 November 2026 and cover both regular and backlog candidates.

How to Check KNRUHS MDS Part-II Result September 2026

  • Open the official KNRUHS results page.
  • Find the notification titled "Results of MDS Part-II Examinations - September 2026" and choose Regular or Backlog candidates, whichever applies to you.
  • Click "View Document" to open the PDF.
  • Press Ctrl + F (or use the search option on your phone) and enter your hall ticket number.
  • Check your result and save or print the PDF for your records.

How to Check KNRUHS B.Sc Nursing 1st Semester Result July 2026

  • Go to the KNRUHS results page.
  • Select "Results of B.Sc Nursing First Semester Examinations - July 2026" for either Regular or Backlog candidates.
  • Click "View Document" to open the result PDF.
  • Search the document for your hall ticket number.
  • Download the PDF and keep a copy for future reference.

Regular vs Backlog Candidates: Which Result Should You Open?

  • Regular candidates are students who appeared for the examination as part of their current batch.
  • Backlog candidates are students who appeared to clear papers from earlier attempts.

KNRUHS has published a separate PDF for each group, so make sure you open the one that matches your status. If you cannot find your number in one, check the other before contacting your college.

What You Need to Check Your Result

  • Your hall ticket number
  • A device with internet access and a PDF viewer

The exact layout of the PDF, such as whether it lists marks, grades or only pass or fail status, is not stated in the notification listing. Open the document to see the format used.

What to Do After the Result

  • Download the result PDF and note your outcome.
  • Contact your college for the marks memo or provisional certificate. The process and dates for these are not available in the notification listing.
  • If you want to apply for recounting or revaluation, check the KNRUHS website for a separate notification. Eligibility, fee and last date are not mentioned in the results listing.
  • If you see a discrepancy in your result, raise it with your college and the KNRUHS examination branch.

Important Instructions

  • Use only the official KNRUHS website to check results. Avoid unverified third-party links.
  • Verify your hall ticket number carefully before reading the result.
  • Results in the PDF are the official notification. Keep a saved copy.
  • Check the KNRUHS website regularly for updates on revaluation, recounting or memos.
