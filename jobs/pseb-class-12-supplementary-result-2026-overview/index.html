PSEB Class 12 Supplementary Result 2026 - Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 26/08/2026

The Punjab School Education Board (PSEB), headquartered in Mohali, conducts a supplementary (compartment) examination each year for Class 12 students who could not clear one or two subjects in their main board exam, or who wish to reappear to improve their marks. The PSEB Class 12 Supplementary Examination 2026 was held from July 4 to July 6, 2026. Students who appeared in this exam can check their result online at the official website, pseb.ac.in, using their roll number and name.

How to Check PSEB Class 12 Supplementary Result 2026 Online

  • Visit the official PSEB website: pseb.ac.in.
  • Look for the link titled "Senior Secondary (10+2) Supplementary Result 2026" on the homepage or results section.
  • Enter your roll number (in the format usually prefixed like PB12) and/ or your name as required.
  • Click "Find Result" or "Submit."
  • Your subject-wise marks and qualifying status will be displayed on screen.
  • Download the result page and take a printout for reference until the original marksheet is issued.

How to Check Result via SMS

  • Open the messaging app on your mobile phone.
  • Type your message in the format: PB12 <Roll Number>
  • Send it to 5676750.
  • You will receive your subject-wise marks and qualifying status by text message.

Details Mentioned on the PSEB Class 12 Supplementary Scorecard

  • Student's name and roll number
  • Father's/ mother's name
  • Subject-wise marks (theory and practical, where applicable)
  • Total marks and overall percentage
  • Qualifying status (pass/ fail per subject and overall)
  • School name and code

What to Do After Checking Your Result

  • If you pass: Download and save the provisional online marksheet. Collect the original marksheet from your school once issued.
  • If you still don't clear a subject: Contact your school for guidance on re-appearance options and revaluation, if applicable.
  • If you want to apply for revaluation: Check the official PSEB notification for the revaluation window and fee, as this schedule is announced separately after the supplementary result.

Important Instructions for Students

  • The online result is provisional. It must be verified against and eventually replaced by the original marksheet from your school.
  • Double-check all personal and academic details on the scorecard; report discrepancies to your school or the PSEB regional office immediately.
  • Keep your admit card/ roll number safe, as it is required to access the result.
  • Avoid relying on unofficial or third-party websites for your final marksheet - always confirm details against the official PSEB record.
