RBSE 12th Half Yearly Time Table 2026-27 - Overview : Admit Card Overview

The RBSE 12th Half Yearly Time Table 2026-27 has been released and subsequently revised by the Directorate of Secondary Education, Rajasthan, Bikaner. According to the latest departmental schedule, Class 12 half-yearly examinations will be held from 25 November to 12 December 2026 under the State-Level Common Examination Scheme.

RBSE 12th Half Yearly Time Table 2026-27 - Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 26/11/2026

Students studying in Science, Commerce and Arts streams can check their subject-wise examination dates, timings and revised instructions. The revised timetable includes important changes to the examinations originally scheduled for 28 November 2026. The Chemistry, History, Business Studies and Agricultural Chemistry examinations will now be conducted from 2:00 PM to 5:15 PM on 28 November, while the Economics examination has been rescheduled to 12 December 2026. Students should follow the revised schedule rather than the earlier timetable that ended on 7 December.

How to Download RBSE 12th Half Yearly Time Table 2026-27?

Students can obtain the timetable and revised examination notice through the Rajasthan education portals or their respective schools.

  • Visit the official Rajasthan Shala Darpan website at rajshaladarpan.rajasthan.gov.in.
  • Check the latest examination notices and circulars.
  • Look for the State-Level Half-Yearly Examination Time Table 2026-27.
  • Open the timetable for Classes 9 to 12.
  • Find the Class 12 examination schedule.
  • Check the subsequent partial revision regarding 28 November and 12 December.
  • Download the relevant timetable and amendment documents, where available.
  • Compare your subject-wise examination dates with the updated schedule.
  • Contact your school if there is any uncertainty about a paper's timing.

Students should retain the revised timetable and follow the latest directions issued by their school.

RBSE 12th Half Yearly Time Table 2026-27: Economics Exam Date

The Class 12 Economics half-yearly examination has been rescheduled to 12 December 2026.

The revised examination will take place in the first shift from 9:30 AM to 12:45 PM.

Economics was previously listed on 28 November in the original schedule. The latest departmental revision supersedes that earlier date.

RBSE 12th Half Yearly Chemistry Exam Date 2026

The Rajasthan Class 12 Chemistry half-yearly examination remains scheduled for 28 November 2026, but its timing has changed.

The paper will be held from 2:00 PM to 5:15 PM, instead of the earlier second-shift timing of 1:15 PM to 4:30 PM.

The same revised shift applies to History, Business Studies and Agricultural Chemistry.

RBSE Class 12 Half Yearly Exam Gap 2026-27

The examination timetable includes an interval from 1 to 3 December 2026, during which no regular Class 12 half-yearly examinations are listed.

Students can use this gap to prepare for the papers scheduled on 4 and 5 December.

However, the examination period does not end on 7 December because the Economics examination has been moved to 12 December.

RBSE 12th Half Yearly Examination 2026-27 Important Instructions

The State-Level Common Examination timetable includes several instructions for schools and examination administration.

  • Schools must maintain the confidentiality of examination question papers.
  • School heads must verify the examination timetable before distributing question papers.
  • Examinations must follow the revised subject-wise dates and shift timings.
  • If a government holiday is declared during the examination period, the affected examination will be rescheduled according to departmental instructions.
  • Schools must arrange examination papers for subjects not included in the common timetable.
  • Students with special needs must receive applicable examination accommodations under Rajasthan Board rules.
  • Students should follow their schools' reporting-time and examination-day instructions.
