RWF Sports Quota Recruitment 2026-27 Eligibility Criteria

Sportspersons must satisfy the minimum sports norms and educational qualification prescribed for the respective post/level, as on the date of the notification. Candidates appearing for or awaiting results of their final examination are not eligible to apply. No relaxation in minimum educational qualification is permissible. No post is exclusively reserved for any community such as SC/ST/OBC. Recognized sports achievements must fall within the current and/or immediate previous two financial years, i.e., on or after 1 April 2024, and the candidate must be an active player. Sportspersons possessing norms for a higher pay level who apply against vacancies of a lower level will also be considered, provided they submit an undertaking that they will not claim the higher level after joining Railways.

RWF Sports Quota Recruitment 2026-27 Minimum Sports Norms

Sportspersons must fulfil the minimum sports achievement norms prescribed for the level they are applying for, based on representation, ranking, or position achieved in recognized championships. Additional sport-specific norms apply for Cricket, Hockey, Badminton, Table Tennis, Football, and Kabaddi, including current Annual All India/World Rankings for Badminton and Table Tennis, and specific representation criteria for Cricket and Hockey, as detailed in the full notification. International Championships for recruitment purposes are categorized as Category-A (Olympic Games), Category-B (World Cup, World Championships, Asian Games, Commonwealth Games, Youth Olympics, Champions Trophy-Hockey), and Category-C (Commonwealth Championships, Asian Championships/Asia Cup, SAF Games, USIC/World Railways Championships, World University Games).

RWF Sports Quota Recruitment 2026-27 Selection Process

All eligible candidates will be called for document verification and trials. Only candidates declared FIT (securing 25 or more out of 40 marks for game skill, physical fitness, and coach's observations during trials) will be assessed further; those declared UNFIT will not proceed. Distribution of marks: Game skill, physical fitness & coach's observations during trials - 40 marks; Assessment of recognized sports achievements as per norms - 50 marks; Educational qualification - 10 marks; Total - 100 marks. Minimum qualifying marks: Level-2 (GP 1900) - 65 marks; Level-1 (GP 1800) - 60 marks. The offer of appointment will be given purely on merit; in case of a tie, preference is given to the younger candidate.
