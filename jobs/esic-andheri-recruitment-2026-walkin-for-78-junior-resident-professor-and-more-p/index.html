ESIC Andheri Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection will be made on the basis of acquired academic credentials and performance of candidates in the interview before the selection board. In case the turnout of candidates for Senior Resident (Dental) and Junior Resident posts is more than 30, the competent authority at their discretion shall conduct a screening test consisting of multiple-choice questions in the concerned discipline and ESIC-related topics to shortlist up to three times candidates per vacancy in order of merit.

ESIC Andheri Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

The engagement for Teaching Faculty and Senior Residents will initially be for a period of one year, extendable up to three years subject to satisfactory performance, or until regular incumbents join, whichever is earlier. For Junior Residents, the engagement is for one year only. No private practice is allowed in any capacity after joining. Candidates working in Govt./Autonomous/Statutory Institutions should preferably bring an NOC from their employer. The upper age limit, tenure, and reservations are governed by Government of India and NMC guidelines. In the event suitable reserved category candidates are not available, posts may be filled by candidates from other categories on a purely temporary contract basis for 44 days.
