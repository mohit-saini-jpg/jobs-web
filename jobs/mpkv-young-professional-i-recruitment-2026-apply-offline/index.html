About MPKV

Mahatma Phule Krishi Vidyapeeth (MPKV) is the Government of Maharashtra agricultural university at Rahuri. This recruitment is for its All India Coordinated Research Project on Integrated Farming Systems, which is run from the office of the Chief Agronomist in the PGI Building, MPKV, Rahuri, Dist. Ahilyanagar 413722. The posts serve the project's Pilot Project on Crop Diversification (PPCD).

MPKV Young Professional-I Vacancy Details 2026

The notice fixes the pay as per ICAR rules. The application form asks you to name the district you are applying for (Nashik, Nandurbar or Kolhapur). Collect data from farmers' fields. MPKV Young Professional I Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

A Selection Committee will interview the eligible candidates. You must attend the interview on the date, time and place set for it. You also pay your own costs, because no TA/DA is paid for attending. Bring photo identity, which you have to show during the "Verification of Document" process. The notice says it does not mean the post will necessarily be filled, and the Selection Committee has discretionary powers.

Service Conditions

Nature of appointment: purely temporary and contractual. It is a tenure appointment and can be ended at any time without a reason. You cannot claim to be made permanent in this post or any similar post. Contract period: six months to begin with. It can be continued every six months, depending on the project's needs and your progress and performance. Termination: the appointment can be ended at 24 hours' notice for negligence in work, gross negligence in duties or grave misconduct. Control: YP-I staff work under the administrative control of the Chief Agronomist, AICRP-IFS, MPKV, Rahuri, who may keep an attendance register. Whole-time work: you must work full time on the assignment and cannot hold any other job, paid or unpaid, during this period. MPKV Young Professional I Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1Which districts are the MPKV Young Professional-I posts for?
A
There is one post each for Nashik, Nandurbar and Kolhapur. You choose the district in item 2 of the application form.
Q2Can I apply online?
A
No. You must send a hard-copy application with self-attested copies of your certificates to the Chief Agronomist, AICRP on Integrated Farming Systems, MPKV, Rahuri 413722.
Q3What will the Young Professional-I do?
A
The work includes collecting data from farmers' fields, doing a baseline survey of each selected farmer, entering data online and running training programmes in farmers' fields.
Q4Is the job permanent?
A
No. It is a contract tenure appointment, first for six months. It can be extended every six months depending on the project's needs and your performance, and it gives no right to be made permanent.
Q5Do existing project staff get preference?
A
Yes. Preference will be given to existing candidates working in this project. Knowledge of computer, technical and administrative work is also desirable.
Q6Is travel allowance paid for the interview?
A
No. You attend the interview at your own cost, and no TA/DA is paid.
Q7What should I bring for document verification?
A
Bring photo identity. It has to be produced during the "Verification of Document" process.
Q8What happens if I leave before the tenure ends?
A
You must give one month's notice in writing or pay one month's salary, and hand over charge to the Principal Investigator (Pilot Project on Crop Diversification) and the Chief Agronomist.
