Goa Shipyard Limited Eligibility Criteria 2026

Applicants must possess the educational qualifications and professional experience prescribed for the relevant Expert or Specialist position. Eligibility is determined based on the candidate's qualifications, overall service and relevant work experience. For positions 1 to 8, candidates must have completed at least 15 years of overall service, including a minimum of 5 years of relevant experience in the concerned field. For the HR/IR Expert position, candidates must have at least 25 years of overall service, including 10 years of relevant experience. Relevant experience includes naval equipment maintenance, shipbuilding, ship repair, HVAC systems, gas turbines, dockyard operations, ship lift systems and industrial relations, depending on the position. The HR/IR role requires experience in unions, industrial relations and wage or pay revision matters, including senior-level experience.

Goa Shipyard Limited Salary 2026

Goa Shipyard Limited has not announced a fixed salary amount for the Expert and Specialist positions. Selected candidates will receive consolidated monthly remuneration negotiated during the selection process. The Selection Committee will determine the remuneration after considering the candidate's equivalent government pay, job profile, educational qualification, nature and duration of experience, and previous employment cost-to-company (CTC).

Goa Shipyard Limited Selection Process 2026

The selection process for Goa Shipyard Limited Expert and Specialist Recruitment 2026 involves screening of applications based on the prescribed eligibility and experience requirements, followed by an interview for shortlisted candidates. GSL management may apply additional shortlisting criteria depending on the number and quality of applications received. Interviews may be conducted through video conferencing or in person. Interview call letters will be sent to shortlisted candidates through their registered email addresses. No travelling allowance or daily allowance will be paid to candidates attending a personal interview. The notification does not prescribe a written examination for these contractual Expert and Specialist positions.
