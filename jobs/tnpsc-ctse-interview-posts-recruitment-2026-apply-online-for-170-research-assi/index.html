TNPSC CTSE Interview Posts Selection Process 2026

Selection for TNPSC CTSE Interview Posts Recruitment 2026 is made through a Written Examination followed by an Interview. Written Examination: Conducted as a Computer Based Test (CBT) with Paper I (Part A – Tamil Eligibility Test, Part B – General Studies, Part C – Aptitude and Mental Ability) and Paper II (Subject Paper related to the chosen post). For Tamil Reporter and English Reporter posts, Paper II is a Stenography subject paper of Diploma standard. Minimum Qualifying Marks: Part B and Part C of Paper I and Paper II are evaluated only if a candidate scores at least 40% (60 marks) in Part A of Paper I. Onscreen Certificate Verification: Conducted before physical certificate verification and interview; candidates are admitted in the ratio of 1:3/1:4 (posts without experience requirement) or 1:10 (posts requiring experience), based on minimum qualifying marks and reservation rules.
