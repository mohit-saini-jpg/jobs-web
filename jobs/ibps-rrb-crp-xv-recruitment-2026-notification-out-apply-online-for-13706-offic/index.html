IBPS RRB Eligibility Criteria & Qualification

Nationality: a candidate must be a citizen of India, or a subject of Nepal/Bhutan, or a Tibetan refugee who arrived before 1 January 1962 intending to settle permanently in India, or a person of Indian origin migrated from certain specified countries with an eligibility certificate from the Government of India. Educational qualification (as on the last date of online registration, i.e. 21.09.2026) varies by post: Candidates awarded CGPA/OGPA must convert it to a percentage as per their university's own conversion criteria, and the result for the qualifying degree must have been declared on or before 21.09.2026.

IBPS RRB Age Limit

Age is reckoned as on 01.09.2026. Age relaxation is available as follows: 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC (non-creamy layer), 10 years for Persons with Benchmark Disability (PwBD), and additional relaxation for Ex-Servicemen and (for Office Assistant only) widows, divorced women, and women legally separated from their husbands who have not remarried. Relaxation for SC/ST/OBC is cumulative with only one other applicable category. Candidates are advised to check whether they satisfy the prescribed age limit before applying. IBPS RRB Selection Process

Office Assistant (Multipurpose): Preliminary Exam → Main Exam → Provisional Allotment (based on Main Exam marks and actual vacancies reported by the RRB). Officer Scale-I: Preliminary Exam → Main Exam → Common Interview → Provisional Allotment. Officer Scale-II & III: Single-level online exam → Common Interview → Provisional Allotment. Interview is out of 100 marks; minimum qualifying marks are 40% (35% for SC/ST/OBC/PwBD candidates). The relative weightage for Officer posts is 80% online exam and 20% interview. Interviews for Officer posts are coordinated by the Nodal Regional Rural Bank with the assistance of NABARD and IBPS.

IBPS RRB CRP-XV Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Visit the official IBPS website, www.ibps.in, and open the "CRP for RRBs" link on the home page.
  • 2Click "CLICK HERE TO APPLY ONLINE FOR CRP-RRBs-OFFICERS (Scale-I, II and III)" or "CLICK HERE TO APPLY ONLINE FOR CRP-RRBs-OFFICE ASSISTANTS (Multipurpose)", as applicable.
  • 3Click "CLICK HERE FOR NEW REGISTRATION" and enter basic details to generate a provisional registration number and password (also sent by SMS/email).
  • 4Log back in using the provisional registration number and password to complete the rest of the form.
  • 5Upload photograph, signature, left thumb impression, a handwritten declaration, and your Class 10 (SSC/SSLC) certificate, plus any category/disability certificates applicable to you, as per the scanning specifications in Annexure-III.
  • 6Pay the application fee/intimation charges online.
  • 7Candidates applying for both Office Assistant and an Officer post, or reconsidering their choice of Officer scale, must register and pay separately for each post.
  • 8Take a printout of the final submitted application form for future reference.

IBPS RRB CRP-XV Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is IBPS RRB Recruitment 2026?
A
IBPS RRB Recruitment 2026 is the Common Recruitment Process (CRP RRBs-XV) conducted by the Institute of Banking Personnel Selection for recruitment of Office Assistant (Multipurpose) and Officer Scale-I, II and III posts in Regional Rural Banks.
Q2When will IBPS RRB Recruitment 2026 online application start?
A
The online application process for IBPS RRB Recruitment 2026 will start on 01 September 2026.
Q3What is the last date to apply for IBPS RRB Recruitment 2026?
A
The last date to submit the online application and pay the application fee/intimation charges is 21 September 2026.
Q4Which posts are available under IBPS RRB CRP XV 2026?
A
The recruitment includes Office Assistant (Multipurpose), Officer Scale-I (Assistant Manager), Officer Scale-II (Manager) and Officer Scale-III (Senior Manager) posts.
Q5What is the qualification required for IBPS RRB Recruitment 2026?
A
Candidates generally need a Bachelor's Degree or relevant professional qualification depending on the post. Officer Scale-II and III posts also require prescribed qualifications and work experience.
Q6What is the age limit for IBPS RRB Recruitment 2026?
A
The age limit varies by post. It is 18 to 28 years for Office Assistant, above 18 and below 30 years for Officer Scale-I, above 21 and below 32 years for Officer Scale-II, and above 21 and below 40 years for Officer Scale-III. Applicable age relaxation will be provided as per IBPS rules.
Q7What is the selection process for IBPS RRB Recruitment 2026?
A
Office Assistant candidates will undergo Preliminary and Main examinations. Officer Scale-I candidates will have Preliminary, Main and Interview stages, while Officer Scale-II and III candidates will appear for a Single Online Examination followed by an Interview.
Q8When will the IBPS RRB 2026 exams be conducted?
A
Officer Scale-I Preliminary Exam is scheduled for 21 and 22 November 2026. Office Assistant Preliminary Exam will be held on 06, 12 and 13 December 2026. The Officer Scale-I Main and Officer Scale-II/III Single Exam are scheduled for 20 December 2026, while the Office Assistant Main Exam is scheduled for 30 January 2027.
Q9How many vacancies are available in IBPS RRB Recruitment 2026?
A
An indicative 13,706 posts across all RRBs and post categories, computed from the state-wise Annexure-I tables. This is a computed figure since one bank (Tripura Gramin Bank) has not yet reported its numbers, and vacancies may change based on the RRBs' actual reported requirement.
Q10Can I apply for more than one IBPS RRB post?
A
Yes - a candidate can apply for the Office Assistant post and also for one Officer post (Scale-I, II, or III), but only one post within the Officer cadre. Fees must be paid separately for each post applied for.
