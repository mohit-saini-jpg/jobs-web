LBS Nursing Admission 2026 Provisional Rank List : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 02/09/2026

The LBS Centre for Science and Technology, a Government of Kerala undertaking, published the Provisional Rank List for admission to Professional Degree Courses in Nursing and Allied Health Science for the academic year 2026-27 on 1 September 2026 at 5:00 PM. This rank list decides eligibility for the Centralized Allotment Process (CAP) to B.Sc. Nursing and 18 other allied health science degree courses in Kerala's government, government-aided, and self-financing colleges. Candidates can check the rank list on the official portal by selecting the relevant rank list category and quota.

How the Rank List Is Prepared (Selection Process)

  • Rank List I: based on marks scored in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology together in the final year of the qualifying examination (maximum 300 marks).
  • Rank List II (BASLP): based on Physics, Chemistry, and Biology/Mathematics/Computer Science/Statistics/Electronics/Psychology together (maximum 300 marks).
  • Rank List III (BPO): based on Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Mathematics, and English together (maximum 400 marks).
  • Marks from boards other than Kerala's Directorate of Higher Secondary Education are normalized using a standard formula so that scores are comparable across boards.
  • Tie-breaking: Higher marks in Biology, then Chemistry, then Physics, then overall Plus One + Plus Two percentage, then English marks, then higher age - applied in that order for Rank List I. A similar sequence (Chemistry, Physics, third subject, aggregate, English, age) applies for Rank Lists II and III.

Exact marks, cutoffs, or individual candidate scores are not available in the official notification beyond what candidates can view after logging in with their Application Number, Registration ID, and Password.

How to Check the LBS Nursing Provisional Rank List 2026

  • Go to the official rank list page: lbscentre.in/nursingparam2026/Ranklist.aspx
  • Select the applicable Ranklist (I to VII) based on your course.
  • Select your Quota (State Merit, EWS, SC, ST, or the applicable community quota).
  • View the published rank list for your selected course and quota.
  • For your individual index mark and rank details, log in at lbscentre.in/nursingparam2026/Login.aspx using your Application Number, Registration ID, and Password.

Important Instructions

  • The rank list is provisional. Candidates should verify their details and raise complaints, if any, through the candidate portal within the window LBS Centre specifies (exact objection/rectification dates for this rank list are not available in the official notification as of this writing).
  • Only candidates who satisfy nativity, academic, and age eligibility conditions as per the Prospectus will be finally considered eligible for allotment.
  • Keep your Application Number, Registration ID, and Password confidential - these are required for all future logins, option registration, and allotment stages.
  • The next major steps after the Provisional Rank List are typically option registration, trial allotment, and the Centralized Allotment Process (CAP); specific 2026 dates for these will be notified separately on the official website.
  • Records related to this admission cycle will be preserved only till 31 March 2027, as per the Prospectus.
