SCL Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

The selection process depends on the post. In every case, the final selection is subject to meeting the advertised eligibility criteria and to merit in the Computer Based Test (CBT) and interview, wherever applicable. A Common Merit List is prepared and offers of appointment are issued in order of merit. The CBT is objective type (MCQ) with a total duration of 120 minutes, made up of two parts of 60 minutes each. Each right answer carries 1 mark and each wrong answer carries a negative mark of 0.25. Compensatory time is available for eligible PwBD candidates. Qualifying marks: 50% in Part A and Part B together for the UR category, and 40% in Part A and Part B together for PwBD categories against notified reserved vacancies.
