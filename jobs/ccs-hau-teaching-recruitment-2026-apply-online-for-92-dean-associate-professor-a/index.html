CCS HAU Teaching Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Apply through the University online portals hau.ac.in and recruitmentccshau.in from 07 November 2026 midnight onwards. The online window closes on 07 December 2026 midnight.
  • 2Pay the application processing fee through the online payment gateway after the application form has been successfully submitted.
  • 3Send a copy of the online submitted application form with the uploaded documents, duly self-attested, to the Registrar (Recruitment Branch), CCS HAU, Hisar - 125 004.
  • 4Deliver the copy by hand or through speed post/registered post so that it reaches by 14 December 2026, 4.30 PM. The notice says the online application will not be considered for further processing otherwise.

CCS HAU Teaching Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in CCS HAU Recruitment 2026?
A
The notice carries 92 teaching posts: 3 Dean/Director posts, 20 Associate Professor/equiv., 26 Assistant Professor, 33 Assistant Scientist and 10 posts at Sr. No. 7 to 13.
Q2What is the last date to apply online for CCS HAU Advt. No. 3/2026?
A
The online portal closes on 07 December 2026 midnight. Applications opened on 07 November 2026 midnight onwards.
Q3What is the last date to send the hard copy of the application?
A
A copy of the online application with self-attested uploaded documents must reach the Registrar (Recruitment Branch), CCS HAU, Hisar - 125 004 by 14 December 2026, 4.30 PM, by hand or speed post/registered post.
Q4What is the qualification for the Dean and Director posts?
A
These posts need a doctoral degree in the branches listed for each post, with at least 8 years of experience as a Professor or in an equivalent position.
Q5What is the qualification for Assistant Professor posts 5A to 5H?
A
A Master's degree with 55% marks in the relevant subject, plus NET in the relevant discipline or an exempting Ph.D., or a Ph.D. from a foreign university ranked among the top 500.
Q6What is the application fee for CCS HAU Recruitment 2026?
A
The fee is Rs.1200 (inclusive GST), Rs.300 for OSC/DSC/BCA/BCB/EWS of Haryana domicile, and Rs.600 for females of Haryana domicile (Rs.150 for those in OSC/DSC/BCA/BCB/EWS). Persons with disabilities and ESM of Haryana are exempted.
Q7What is the pay for CCS HAU teaching posts?
A
Dean and Director posts carry Rs.144200-218200 in AL-14 with unfurnished rent free accommodation, Associate Professor/equiv. posts Rs.131400-217100 in AL-13A, and Assistant Professor, Assistant Scientist and posts 7 to 13 Rs.57700-182400 in AL-10.
Q8Are the Dean and Director posts permanent?
A
No. The posts at Sr. No. 1 to 3 are filled on tenurial basis for 4 years only. A person who has already served a term as Dean/Director in this or any other University is not considered for a second term.
Q9Who gets the reservation benefit?
A
Only OSC/DSC/BCA/BCB/Persons with Disabilities/ESM/EWS candidates who are domicile of Haryana State, with the certificate issued by the competent authority attached to the application form.
Q10How can a fee refund be claimed for the earlier advertisements?
A
For the 11 listed posts of Advt. No. 3/2022, 4/2022 and 2/2023, send bank account details, proof of payment, the post details and the advertisement number to [email protected] within two months from the date of publication of the advertisement.
