RSSB Junior Engineer Eligibility Criteria & Qualification

Educational qualification requirements differ by post. Candidates must also have working knowledge of Hindi written in the Devanagari script and knowledge of Rajasthani culture for all three JE posts. Candidates in the final year of the relevant course may also apply, but they must acquire the prescribed qualification by the date of the examination; otherwise they will not be considered eligible for this recruitment. RSSB Junior Engineer Age Limit 2026

As per the notification, age is calculated with reference to 1 January 2027 . A candidate must have attained 18 years of age and must not have crossed 40 years of age as on that date. RSSB Junior Engineer Application Fee 2026

The application fee is a One Time Registration (OTR) fee paid via SSO ID and is payable through e-Mitra kiosk, Citizen Service Centre (CSC), net banking, ATM-cum-debit card, or credit card. Candidates who have already deposited the OTR fee earlier are not required to pay it again. Candidates from other states who fall under SC/ST/OBC/MBC/EWS categories are treated as General category candidates for this recruitment and must pay the General category fee.

RSSB Junior Engineer Salary 2026

The Junior Engineer post carries a pay scale of Pay Matrix Level-10 as per the 7th Pay Commission adopted by the Rajasthan Government. Selected candidates will be appointed as probationary trainees for a 2-year probation period . During probation, a fixed monthly remuneration (as prescribed by the state government from time to time) will be paid, instead of the regular pay scale. No additional allowances such as House Rent Allowance, Dearness Allowance, City Compensatory Allowance, or Special Pay are payable during the probation period. On successful completion of probation (training, passing the required examination, and satisfactory conduct), the candidate becomes eligible for the regular pay scale and confirmation/permanency.

RSSB JE Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Applications for RSSB JE Recruitment 2026 must be submitted online through the SSO Rajasthan portal. Offline applications will not be accepted under any circumstances.
  • 2Visit the SSO Rajasthan portal at sso.rajasthan.gov.in and log in using your SSO ID.
  • 3Go to Citizen Apps (G2C) and select the Recruitment Portal.
  • 4Click on "Apply Now" for the RSSB Junior Engineer Joint Direct Recruitment 2026.
  • 5If you have not already paid the One Time Registration (OTR) fee, complete OTR registration first by entering your category (Unreserved/Reserved), disability status, and home state, then pay the prescribed OTR fee.
  • 6Complete the online application form carefully, upload the required live photograph (not older than 1 month) and visible body mark details.
  • 7Fill in educational qualification and other required details in the Document Details section as per the updated process notified on the RSSB website.
  • 8Review the application preview (note: preview is not the same as final submission) and click Final Submit.
  • 9Note down and save the generated Application ID/print of the submitted application for future reference.
  • 10Candidates are strongly advised to complete their OTR registration and payment well before the last date to avoid last-minute technical issues.
