MECON FTFT Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

Candidates employed in Central/State Government, PSUs, or Autonomous Bodies must either forward their application through proper channel, or produce a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from their present employer at the time of interview; without this, they will neither be interviewed nor paid TA. Furnishing wrong/false information, or producing overwritten/illegible documents, will lead to disqualification; if discovered at any stage (including after appointment), the candidature or offer of appointment will be cancelled. Candidates not shortlisted for interview, or not selected, will not be individually communicated about the status of their candidature. Canvassing in any form will disqualify the candidature. Candidates must have an active email ID and mobile number valid for at least the next one year, as no change is allowed after submission and all communication is via email only.
