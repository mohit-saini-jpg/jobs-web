DRDO TBRL Paid Internship Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Terminal Ballistics Research Laboratory (TBRL), Defence Research and Development Organization, Ministry of Defence, has invited applications for 43 paid internships of six months (January 2027 – June 2027) in 12 branch codes: 10 for under-graduate engineering students (38 places) and 2 for second-year M.Sc. students (5 places). Applications are sent by post to The Director, TBRL, Sector-30, Chandigarh - 160030, and the last date of receipt is 13 November 2026. Applications received after the due date will be rejected.

About TBRL

Terminal Ballistics Research Laboratory (TBRL) describes itself as a premiere research laboratory under DRDO, Ministry of Defence, Government of India. It is located at Sector-30, Chandigarh. The internships are offered under the internship scheme introduced by the Government of India, and the interns work at TBRL, Range Ramgarh, District: Panchkula, Haryana.

Where and How to Send

Students must apply in the prescribed form only. The application form is printed in the notice and can also be downloaded from the DRDO website, https://drdo.gov.in . The notice does not name the postal mode (speed post, registered post or by hand). Stipend and Terms of the Internship

Duration: 6 months, starting from 1 January 2027 tentatively. Stipend: Rs. 5,000/- per month, a total of Rs. 30,000/- (Rupees Thirty Thousand Only), paid in two equal instalments of Rs. 15,000/- after 3 months and Rs. 15,000/- after 6 months, subject to attendance of a minimum of 15 days in a month. It is paid directly to the student's bank account. The student must submit a progress report after 3 months and a project report after 6 months / completion of the training. TBRL will issue the internship completion certificate after successful completion, which includes clearing evaluation of project work, submitting the project report and completing other formalities. A student who leaves midway or does not complete the 6 months receives no completion certificate and no payment. No accommodation or transport is provided. DRDO TBRL Paid Internship Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in TBRL Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 43 paid internship vacancies: 38 for under-graduate engineering students and 5 for post-graduate M.Sc. students. The notice calls the number tentative, and it may increase or decrease.
Q2What is the last date to apply for the TBRL paid internship?
A
The last date of receipt of applications is 13 November 2026. Applications received after the due date will be rejected.
Q3What is the qualification for the TBRL internship?
A
Students must be pursuing a full-time B.E. / B.Tech. / B.Sc. (Four Year Course) or second-year M.Sc. in the respective discipline, with 75% or above marks from an AICTE / UGC approved college / university. Students with backlogs are not eligible.
Q4What is the age limit?
A
The student must be less than 28 years of age as on 31 December 2026.
Q5What is the stipend?
A
The stipend is Rs. 5,000/- per month, a total of Rs. 30,000/-, paid in two instalments of Rs. 15,000/- after 3 and 6 months. It requires a minimum attendance of 15 days in a month.
Q6How do I apply for the TBRL internship?
A
Send the filled prescribed application form, the college request letter and the enclosures by post to The Director, TBRL, Sector-30, Chandigarh - 160030, Kind Attention: To HRD Division. Write "Application for Paid Internship: Starting January 2027" and the Branch Code on the envelope.
Q7How are interns selected?
A
Selection is on merit, based on cumulative marks percentage / CGPA up to the last semester / year or the candidate's profile, with an online / telephonic interview / interaction as required, and document verification. Selected students are told by official e-mail.
Q8Where will the internship be held?
A
All interns report at TBRL, Range Ramgarh, District: Panchkula, Haryana. The notice says no accommodation or transport is provided.
Q9Does the internship lead to a job at DRDO?
A
No. TBRL / DRDO has no obligation to offer employment, and an intern cannot claim any right to employment on completing the internship.
Q10When will the internship start?
A
The internship is tentatively planned to start on 1 January 2027 for 6 months. Selected students are to be informed in the first or second week of December 2026.
