JKPSC Assistant Professor Eligibility Criteria

Eligibility varies by post. The core requirement for most Assistant Professor disciplines is a Master's degree with 55% marks (50% for SC/ST/differently-abled candidates and PhD holders who obtained their Master's before 19 September 1991), along with NET/SLET/SET or an equivalent PhD under the applicable UGC regulations. Alternate routes exist for candidates with a PhD from a foreign university ranked in the world's top 500 (QS, THE, or ARWU). JKPSC Assistant Professor Selection Process

Selection is made as per Rule 45 of the J&K Public Service Commission (Business & Procedure) Rules, 2021, as amended. There will be a written examination consisting of a single paper of 3 hours' duration with 120 multiple-choice questions. The syllabus is available on the JKPSC official website. The syllabus for the post of College Director of Physical Education & Sports will be notified separately. The examination will be held at Srinagar and Jammu centres; candidates must indicate their preferred centre, and centre allotment is at the sole discretion of the Commission.

Documents Required

Mandatory: Date of Birth Certificate (Secondary School/Matric Certificate) Domicile Certificate of the UT of J&K Graduation Degree PG Degree NET/SET/SLET/PhD (any one) Mandatory if claiming category benefit: Category Certificate Disability Certificate In-service Certificate signed by HOD Additional documents for weightage (as applicable): M.Phil certificate with result notification, PhD notification, JRF/NET/SET/SLET certificate, experience certificates, list of published books, gold medal certificate, national/state/institution award certificate, list of research papers, and post-doctorate certificate (minimum 1 year).
