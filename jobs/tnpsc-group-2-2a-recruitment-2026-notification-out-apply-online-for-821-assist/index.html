TNPSC Group 2 Age Limit 2026

Age is reckoned as on 01.07.2026 . The minimum and maximum age limits vary by post and by community category (Others vs. SC/SC(A)/ST/MBC/DC/BC(OBCM)/BCM), with additional age concessions for Persons with Benchmark Disability, Ex-servicemen, and Destitute Widows. Additional age relaxation is available for Persons with Benchmark Disability (up to 50 years / no maximum age limit depending on post) and Ex-servicemen (up to 42–55 years depending on post/qualification). No maximum age limit means the candidate should not have completed 60 years of age as on 01.07.2026. Exact post-wise figures are listed in Sections 4.1.1 and 4.1.2 of the official notification. TNPSC Group 2 Selection Process 2026

TNPSC Group 2 selection process 2026 for both Group II and Group IIA Services consists of the following successive stages: Common Preliminary Examination: A single objective-type paper (screening test only); marks are not counted for final merit. Main Examination: Separate Main Examinations for Group II Services and Group IIA Services. Each Main Examination has two papers - Paper I (Tamil Eligibility Test, qualifying in nature) and Paper II (counted for ranking). Onscreen Certificate Verification: Based on Main Examination Paper II marks, candidates are admitted in the ratio of 1:3 (General category) and 1:2 (Reserved categories). Physical Certificate Verification and Counselling: Candidates are admitted in the ratio of 1:3 (General category) and 1:1.5 (Reserved categories), based on rank. Applications are accepted only through online mode; no offline or postal applications are entertained.   • 1Visit the official TNPSC application portal, www.tnpscexams.in.
  • 2Complete One Time Registration (OTR) by paying Rs. 150/- registration fee, if not already registered (OTR is valid for five years).
  • 3Log in using your OTR User ID and Password, and click "APPLY" against this recruitment.
  • 4Verify the auto-filled OTR details and fill in the additional details required for this recruitment.
  • 5Upload your photograph (taken on or after the date of notification) and signature in the specified format and size.
