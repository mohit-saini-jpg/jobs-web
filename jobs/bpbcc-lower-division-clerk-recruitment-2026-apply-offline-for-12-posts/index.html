BPBCC Lower Division Clerk Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Bihar Police Building Construction Corporation (Bihar Police Bhavan Nirman Nigam), a Government of Bihar undertaking, invites applications for 12 Lower Division Clerk (Nimn Vargiya Lipik) posts on contract basis at Rs. 28,000/- per month. The application in the prescribed form must reach the office of the Additional Director General of Police-cum-Chairman-cum-Managing Director, Bihar Police Building Construction Corporation, Kautilya Nagar, Patna-14, Bihar, by post or in person, by 5.30 PM on 14 December 2026. Applications sent by email will not be considered.

BPBCC Lower Division Clerk Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection for the engagement will be made by a selection committee constituted by the Corporation. Shortlisted candidates will be informed about the interview on the email address given by them or on the Corporation website. At the time of the interview, candidates must bring the original certificates of date of birth, educational qualification, caste, residence, experience and computer knowledge, the certificate of Accounting Software (Tally etc.), basic bookkeeping/taxation knowledge if they have it, and other related certificates. No travelling allowance of any kind will be paid for the interview.

BPBCC Lower Division Clerk Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

Candidates with a criminal case registered, serious charges pending, punishment imposed, a vigilance case or departmental proceedings pending will not be engaged. The engagement will be for one year; the period may be extended as required on the basis of efficiency and performance. The engagement will be temporary, and there will be no claim to regularisation after engagement. If the contract is not extended before it ends, the engagement will be treated as ended automatically, without any order being issued. If anything stated in the application is found incorrect, the candidature will stand forfeited (declaration in the application form).
