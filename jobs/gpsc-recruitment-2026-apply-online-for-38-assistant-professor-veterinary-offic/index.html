Konkani Language Relaxation Clause

As per the notification, in case suitable candidates with knowledge of Konkani are not available, the GPSC may recommend a candidate found otherwise fit. This requirement can be relaxed by the Government on the Commission's recommendation, if considered necessary, in the case of Group 'A' posts in Professional Colleges and Consultants in the Directorate of Health Services.

GPSC Selection Process 2026

The notification states that prescribed Educational Qualifications (EQs) are the minimum requirement, and mere possession of the EQs does not entitle a candidate to be called for interview. Where the number of applications received is large, the Commission will shortlist candidates to be called for interview, as per the "Instructions" available on the Commission's website. Further details of the selection process (written exam, if any, and interview weightage) are not available in the official notification .

Required Documents for GPSC Recruitment 2026

A specific list of documents to be uploaded/submitted is not available in the official notification . Candidates are advised to refer to the "Instructions" section on the GPSC website before filling out the application, and to keep education certificates, experience certificates, and identity proof ready in general practice.

GPSC Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Visit the official GPSC website at https://gpsc.goa.gov.in or the Government of Goa portal at https://cbes.goa.gov.in/.
  • 2Carefully read the "Instructions" available on the Commission's website before filling the application.
  • 3Fill in the online application form for the relevant post appropriately.
  • 4Pay the application fee online, or generate an e-challan for offline payment at an SBI branch in Goa.
  • 5For online payment, download the e-receipt confirming successful payment status.
  • 6For offline payment, get the bank stamp on the e-challan and submit it to the Commission's office on or before the last date.
  • 7After successful payment, download the completed application form.
  • 8Ensure a valid email address and mobile number are entered, as all communication from the Commission will be sent through these.
