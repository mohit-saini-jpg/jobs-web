VMMC & Safdarjung Hospital Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Multidisciplinary Research Unit (MRU) of Vardhman Mahavir Medical College & Safdarjung Hospital, New Delhi, funded by the Department of Health Research, has invited applications for Data Entry Operator (1 post), Scientist 'B' (Non-Medical) and Scientist 'C' (Non-Medical) on a contractual basis of six months, extendable. Pay is ₹24,356/- per month for the Data Entry Operator, 56,000/- plus HRA for Scientist 'B' and 67000/- plus HRA for Scientist 'C'. Applications are sent by email, with a Google form to be filled as well, latest by 10/12/2026.

About the Multidisciplinary Research Unit

The notice comes from the Office of the Principal, Vardhman Mahavir Medical College & Safdarjung Hospital, New Delhi - 110029, which is affiliated to Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University, Delhi. The posts are for the Multidisciplinary Research Unit (MRU), funded by the Department of Health Research, and are to be filled on a purely temporary basis at the hospital. The posts are contractual, not regular.

The place of posting for all three posts is MRU, VMMC & Safdarjung Hospital, New Delhi. The notice prints no category-wise break-up.

Pay and Tenure

The Data Entry Operator gets a consolidated salary of ₹24,356/- per month. The notice says this is as per the DHR guidelines (applicable minimum wages notified by the Govt. of NCT of Delhi that is revised periodically). Scientist 'B' gets a consolidated salary of 56,000/- Plus, HRA as applicable, and Scientist 'C' gets 67000/- + HRA, as admissible. Appointment will be on a contractual basis for six months, extendable for further period subject to satisfactory performance and other considerations up to the Finance Commission Cycle of 5 years. The posts are purely contractual, and the candidate has no right to claim any type of permanent employment under DHR/VMMC & Safdarjung Hospital or continuation of services in any other project. The Institute does not have residential facilities for contractual staff. No TA/DA is paid for attending the interview or joining the post.

Selection and Interview

Shortlisted candidates will be informed of the date and time of the interview by email. For the post of Data Entry Operator, candidates will be intimated about the written test, speed test or interview by email. The written test for Data Entry Operator covers basic knowledge of Computer Applications. The notice prints no interview date, venue or marking scheme. At the interview, candidates bring the printed hard copy of the duly filled application form with self-attested supporting documents as per checklist, 2 sets of photocopies of relevant original documents and a recent passport size photo. Original documents are verified at the interview. In the absence of valid original documents, the candidate is not considered eligible.
