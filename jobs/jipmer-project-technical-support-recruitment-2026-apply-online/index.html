About JIPMER

The Jawaharlal Institute of Postgraduate Medical Education and Research (JIPMER) is a medical education and research institute with its letterhead address at Dhanwantri Nagar, Puducherry-6, and a campus at Karaikal. These posts are in an externally funded research project run by the Department of Community Medicine, JIPMER Karaikal. The Project Investigator is Dr L. Karthik Balajee, Additional Professor, and the PREVENT-D Study looks at drowning events and emergency response in Puducherry through multi-source surveillance.

JIPMER Recruitment 2026 Job Description

Staff will be assigned duties in Puducherry and/or Karaikal according to project requirements. The work includes hospital and administrative record review, community visits, interviews, verification of drowning events, environmental site assessments and digital data collection. Travel between study sites may be required. Coordinate surveillance activities across the study locations. Maintain the site calendar, permission tracker and progress records.
