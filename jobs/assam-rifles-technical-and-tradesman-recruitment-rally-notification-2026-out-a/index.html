Assam Rifles Technical & Tradesman Rally 2026 Selection Process

Selection proceeds through several sequential stages; only candidates qualifying each stage move to the next. RFID-Based Physical Standard Test (PST) & Physical Efficiency Test (PET): Height/chest/weight measurement and the qualifying run, as detailed above. Documentation Verification (DV): Conducted alongside PST/PET reporting; candidates must produce the original of the payment receipt/challan and the printed application form/notification copy. Computer Based Test (CBT): 100-mark objective-type question paper. Minimum pass marks: 35% for General/EWS candidates; 33% for SC/ST/OBC candidates. Candidates successful in the RFID PET/PST, Documentation Verification and CBT are shortlisted to the Trade Skill Test. Assam Rifles Technical And Tradesman Recruitment Rally : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many posts has Assam Rifles advertised in this Rally?
A
354 Group C posts across 18 technical trades, all-India, with no state-wise quota.
Q2What is the last date to apply?
A
28 October 2026, till 23:59 hrs; the online portal opens on 28 September 2026.
Q3Can women apply for this Rally?
A
No. All 18 advertised trades are open to male candidates only.
Q4What is the age limit?
A
Most trades require 18–23 years; Cipher, Draughtsman, Operation Theatre Technician and Physiotherapist require 18–25 years, as on the cut-off date of 1 July 2026.
Q5What is the selection process?
A
RFID-based Physical Standard Test (PST) and Physical Efficiency Test (PET), Documentation Verification, a Computer Based Test (CBT), a Trade Skill Test, and a Detailed/Review Medical Examination, followed by a trade- and category-wise Merit List.
Q6What is the application fee?
A
Rs. 100/- for Group C posts; no fee for SC/ST and Ex-Servicemen candidates. The fee is non-refundable.
Q7Is there a quota for Ex-Servicemen?
A
Yes, 10% of vacancies are earmarked for Ex-Servicemen in most of the advertised trades; unfilled ESM vacancies go to non-ESM candidates of the same category.
Q8Where do I apply?
A
Online only, through assamrifles.gov.in; only one application per candidate is accepted.
