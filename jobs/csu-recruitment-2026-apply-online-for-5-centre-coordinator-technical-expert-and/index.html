CSU Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Central Sanskrit University (CSU), New Delhi, has invited online applications for Centre Coordinator and four Vertical Expert posts (one position each) in the Gyan Bhartam Cell of the Cluster Centre at CSU, under a MoU with Gyan Bhartam, Ministry of Culture. The engagement is contractual for 11 months (purely temporary and project based), with fixed monthly remuneration of ₹60,000 for the Centre Coordinator and ₹40,000 for each Expert. Applications are accepted online at csu.co.in/gyanbhartam until 30 November 2026, 11:30 PM (IST).

About CSU

Central Sanskrit University is established by an Act of Parliament, 2020, and is located at 56-57, Institutional Area, Janakpuri, New Delhi-110058. This recruitment follows a MoU between Gyan Bhartam, Ministry of Culture, Delhi and the University for hiring a Centre Coordinator and Vertical Experts in the Gyan Bhartam Cell of the Cluster/Independent Centre. The positions serve the activities under the Gyan Bhartam Project.

Vacancy Details

CSU has announced 5 positions in total, one for each post. The University reserves the right to increase or decrease the vacancies according to circumstances. Reservation (SC/ST/OBC/PWD/EWS) applies as per CSU/Govt. of India rules on the subject. The notice prints no category-wise break-up.

Pay and Tenure

The Centre Coordinator receives ₹60,000 per month and each of the four Expert posts ₹40,000 per month as fixed remuneration. The positions are purely contractual for 11 months or until completion of the Project, whichever is earlier, and are co-terminus with the Project. No other compensation apart from the consolidated remuneration is admissible, even if the candidate attends the office on holidays or works beyond normal office hours. Contract staff are not entitled to Confirmation, LTC, Medical reimbursement or other benefits admissible to regular University employees.

CSU Recruitment 2026 Job Description

Ensure academic verification of the authenticity of data pertaining to all 5 verticals of Gyan Bharatam. Coordinate effectively with the concerned department and Nodal Officer at State/UT and district level for implementation of Gyan Bharatam. Ensure coordination with staff of all verticals at the centre and good team management. Prepare weekly and monthly progress reports of work done by the Centre and forward them to Gyan Bharatam. Monitor proper fund utilization by the Centre, forwarding financial progress and timely UC to Gyan Bharatam. Coordinate closely with Gyan Bharatam to achieve the targets set for the centre, and act as focal point for all coordination with Gyan Bharatam. Selection and Interview

Only candidates found eligible and shortlisted under the prescribed eligibility criteria and other applicable provisions will be called for the Skill Test and/or Interview, as applicable. The detailed schedule, venue and instructions will be sent to shortlisted and eligible candidates at least one week before the scheduled date(s), through their registered email addresses only, so the Spam/Junk folder should also be checked.
