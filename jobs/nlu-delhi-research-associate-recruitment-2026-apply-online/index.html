NLU Delhi Research Associate Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

National Law University Delhi (NLU Delhi) is hiring one Research Associate on a six-month contract for the MoHUA-funded project "Housing for All in Viksit Bharat: A Review of The Legal Framework and Procedural Bottlenecks to Optimise Affordable Housing in Delhi & Haryana", at ₹ 50,000 per month. The essential qualification is an LL.M. with specialisation and minimum 55% marks. Applications are accepted only through the online form at careers.nludelhi.ac.in by 20 November 2026, 23:59 Hrs.

About NLU Delhi

National Law University Delhi is a law university at Sector-14, Dwarka, New Delhi. According to the notice, it was established in 2008 on the initiative of the High Court of Delhi and the Govt. of NCT of Delhi. Since May 2026 the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs has recognised it as one of its Regional Centres to research on Urban Housing affairs. The project is a comprehensive legal mapping of the framework in which the housing ecosystem of Delhi and Haryana operates. It identifies legal and procedural bottlenecks through stakeholder consultations. Its stated objective is to help the Ministry and other policymakers improve access to affordable housing, especially for the socioeconomically weaker sections of society.

Vacancy Details

The notice invites applications for one Research Associate on a contractual basis. The post table prints "Position: One (2)"; the text of the call and the figure "One" both point to a single post.

Who Can Apply

Essential qualifications: LL.M. with specialisation (preferably in Constitutional Law/Real Estate Law and Urban Development Law) from a recognised Indian or foreign university with minimum 55% marks (or an equivalent grade in a point scale wherever a grading system is followed), and a good academic record. Demonstrable research work/publication and interest in urban studies, urban development and the law, constitutional law, local land and municipal laws, real estate, and other allied areas/themes. Desirable qualifications: Familiarity with quantitative and/or qualitative research methods, and the ability to assist in literature review, instrument development, data collection, data analysis and report writing. Proficiency in MS Office applications and data analysis tools. Prior experience in research projects. Proficiency in academic writing and ability to manage multiple tasks and meet

Pay and Tenure

The monthly remuneration is ₹ 50,000/- per month. The appointment is contractual for six months and is further extendable subject to project requirements and performance. The notice does not mention HRA or other allowances. No TA/DA will be paid for attending the interview or interaction. The notice states that the position shall lie with the Principal Investigator of the Project, or as may be decided by the University subsequently. The rules and regulations of National Law University Delhi apply to the selected candidate.

NLU Delhi Research Associate Job Description

The Research Associate is expected to: Assist in research, review and analysis of statutes, policies, guidelines, regulatory frameworks and other available literature on the core and allied areas of the Project. Conduct doctrinal, empirical and comparative research (including literature review, case/document analysis and data-driven inquiries). Support the drafting of research outputs, including reports, briefs, research papers and internal working papers. Coordinate with project investigators and external consultants where required.

Selection and Interview

Selection has two stages. First, applications are screened for completeness and relevance. Candidates are shortlisted on an assessment of the application materials submitted through the online form. Second, shortlisted candidates are invited for an interview. Shortlisting does not guarantee an interview or selection, and only shortlisted candidates will be contacted. If a candidate hears nothing from the University within 2 weeks after the deadline, the application should be assumed unsuccessful. The interview date and mode are not specified in the notice.
