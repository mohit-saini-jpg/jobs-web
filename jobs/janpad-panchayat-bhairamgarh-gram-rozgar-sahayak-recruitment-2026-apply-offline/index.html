Janpad Panchayat Bhairamgarh Gram Rozgar Sahayak : Job Overview

Gram Rozgar Sahayak posts on a lump-sum honorarium of Rs. 9960/- are open in 33 Gram Panchayats listed by the Office of the Chief Executive Officer, Janpad Panchayat Bhairamgarh (कार्यालय मुख्य कार्यपालन अधिकारी, जनपद पंचायत भैरमगढ़), District Bijapur (Chhattisgarh). Only native residents of the Gram Panchayats may apply, and the completed form must be submitted in person or by post at the Janpad Panchayat Bhairamgarh office during office hours, so that it is received by 30 November 2026, 5:30; applications received after this date and time will not be considered.

About Janpad Panchayat Bhairamgarh

Janpad Panchayat Bhairamgarh is the block-level panchayat body of District Bijapur, Chhattisgarh, headed by its Chief Executive Officer. This recruitment is issued under the Viksit Bharat Rozgar aur Ajeevika ke liye Guarantee Mission (Gramin) Act 2025 (VB-G RAM G), following letters from the office of the Commissioner, VB-G RAM G, Nava Raipur Atal Nagar, and Zila Panchayat Bijapur. Janpad Panchayat Bhairamgarh Gram Rozgar Sahayak : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection is on merit out of 100 marks, worked out as follows: Among candidates with equal marks, the older candidate is selected first. Where no eligible application is received from a Gram Panchayat, a candidate of the same category from the same Janpad Panchayat is selected on merit. If PwD (Divyang) candidates are not available, candidates from other categories may be taken; if SC/ST women candidates are not available, men of the same category will be taken. After scrutiny as per the prescribed norms, the list of candidates will be pasted on the Janpad Panchayat notice board for claims and objections.

Janpad Panchayat Bhairamgarh Gram Rozgar Sahayak : Important Instructions

The appointment is for one year, fully temporary, from 01 January to 31 December. After that, if needed, continuing for one more year may be considered with the consent of both sides, based on suitability, efficiency, annual work appraisal and conduct, as per government instructions. Either side may end the appointment during the contract by giving one month's notice or one month's pay in lieu of it. Contract appointees are governed by the Chhattisgarh Civil Services (Conduct) Rules, 1965. The posts are for field work, so 100 percent disabled candidates will not be accepted. Applications without self-attested copies will not be accepted; incomplete, unclear or faulty applications will not be considered and no information will be sent about them. Not eligible: those earlier removed from any post by Chhattisgarh Ajeevika Samvardhan Samiti or any government department for indiscipline, misconduct, arbitrariness or irregularity; giving false information cancels the candidature. Not eligible: an applicant who married before the minimum age prescribed for marriage; a candidate who married a person already having a living spouse (the Government may exempt if satisfied there is sufficient reason); a candidate with more than two living children, one of whom was born on or after 26 January 2001. Candidature may be cancelled if an applicant directly or indirectly disrupts or tries to influence the recruitment process. The District Collector has the right to take the final decision on any dispute or problem arising during the recruitment.

Janpad Panchayat Bhairamgarh Gram Rozgar Sahayak : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many Gram Panchayats are covered in the Bhairamgarh Gram Rozgar Sahayak recruitment 2026?
A
The notice lists 33 Gram Panchayats of Janpad Panchayat Bhairamgarh, from Ambeli to Tushwal, each with its category; the number of posts is stated to be variable.
Q2What is the last date to apply?
A
Applications must be received at the Janpad Panchayat Bhairamgarh office by 30 November 2026, 5:30; those received later will not be considered.
Q3What is the qualification for Gram Rozgar Sahayak?
A
12th pass (10th pass for ST and SC) and residence in the concerned Gram Panchayat. For the Bastar and Surguja divisions, the priority order is 12th passed, 10th passed, 8th passed.
Q4What is the age limit?
A
The minimum age is 18 years as on 01.01.2026; for the maximum age, the relaxation orders of the Chhattisgarh General Administration Department apply.
Q5What is the salary of a Gram Rozgar Sahayak in Bhairamgarh?
A
The post carries a lump-sum honorarium of Rs. 9960/-.
Q6How do I apply?
A
Fill the prescribed form (available on the Janpad Panchayat Bhairamgarh notice board or www.bijapur.gov.in), attach self-attested documents and submit it in person or by post at the Janpad Panchayat Bhairamgarh office during office hours.
Q7How will candidates be selected?
A
On merit out of 100 marks: 70 for marks in the minimum qualification, up to 15 for rural development scheme experience, and 15 for a PGDCA/DCA/ITI diploma or certificate or six months as Panchayat-cum-Data Entry Operator; ties go to the older candidate.
Q8Is the Gram Rozgar Sahayak job permanent?
A
No. The appointment is a fully temporary contract for one year, from 01 January to 31 December, and an extension by one year may be considered with the consent of both sides.
Q9Who can apply for a Gram Panchayat's post?
A
Native residents of that Gram Panchayat; the selected candidate must be a resident of the same Gram Panchayat. If no eligible application comes from a Gram Panchayat, a same-category candidate from the same Janpad Panchayat may be selected on merit.
