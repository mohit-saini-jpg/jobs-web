About Chennai Port

Chennai Port Authority is a Major Port Authority. Its administrative office is at No. 1, Rajaji Salai, Chennai - 600 001 (phone +91-044-2531 2000 and +91-044-2536 2201). The notice was sent to the Chairpersons of all Major Port Authorities except KPL, so that they can circulate it among their officers.

Chennai Port Chief Engineer Vacancy Details 2026

The selected officer joins the HoD post in the Rs. 1,00,000 to Rs. 2,60,000 scale. After 3 years of regular service in the post, he or she will be moved up to the scale of Rs. 1,20,000 to Rs. 2,80,000 (pre-revised Rs. 51300-73000). This depends on meeting the conditions in the Ministry's Letter No.A-12023/15/2018-PE-I dated 19 November 2019. Any changes the Ministry directs from time to time will also apply.

Chennai Port Chief Engineer Eligibility 2026

Who can apply: suitable and willing officers of Major Port Authorities who meet the eligibility criteria in the Recruitment Rules (RR), including the Note in the RR attached as Annexure-I. Crucial date: eligibility is judged as on 1 January 2027, the date the vacancy arises, under MoPSW's letter No.I-26/25/2013-PE.I dated 26 September 2019. Service record: the overall grading in the ACRs / APARs must not be below "Very Good". Educational qualification: the official notification does not specify the minimum educational qualification in its text. The Recruitment Rules in Annexure-I cannot be read in the notice copy. See the Recruitment Rules (Annexure-I) in the official notification for the qualification, experience and other eligibility rules for this post.

Chennai Port Authority Chief Engineer Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection is by merit. The officer's overall ACR / APAR grading must not be below "Very Good". Candidates are chosen by the Service Selection Committee. The Ministry considers the candidature once the application reaches it through proper channel. The Selection Committee considers a candidate only after the Port has sent the remaining documents, such as the APARs and vigilance clearance.

Conditions in the Notice

If the Port receives an advance copy of an application, the Ministry will not consider the candidature unless the application arrives through proper channel within 15 days of the last date of receipt of applications. If the applicant received any major or minor penalty in the last 10 years, the forwarding authority may send the disciplinary case and its documents with the application. The vigilance report follows the format in Ministry letter No.A-12023/2/2021-PE-I dated 20 January 2021. An officer who withdraws after being selected by the Service Selection Committee may be barred from selection to HoD level posts in all Major Port Authorities for two years. Incomplete applications, applications that do not follow this procedure and late applications will not be considered.

Chennai Port Authority Chief Engineer Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Apply on the Online Application Portal (OAP) of the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways. The notice accepts no application made any other way.
  • 2Print the application uploaded on the OAP and give it to your administrative (parent) Port to forward.
  • 3The Port forwards the application with the documents below, before the November 2026 due date. The envelope is superscribed "Application for the post of Chief Engineer, Chennai Port Authority".
  • 4Vigilance clearance or APARs may take time. In that case the Port forwards the application with the competent authority's approval before the due date, and sends the remaining documents without unnecessary delay.
  • 5If time is short, the forwarding Port may send the application, with its forwarding and the OAP printout, by email. It then sends the complete application and documents by registered post.
  • 6Copies of ACRs / APARs for the last 5 years (2021-2022 to 2025-26). An officer not below the rank of Dy.HoD must attest every page.
  • 7If an ACR / APAR for any of these years is pending or not available, a "No Report Certificate" along with the ACRs / APARs of the preceding years.
  • 8Attested photocopies of all certificates: proof of educational qualification, and of present and past work experience in the respective post and pay scale.
  • 9No objection certificate from the respective Port.
  • 10Certificate by the Head of the Office (Annexure-II). It confirms that the particulars and certificates are correct, that the officer has no pending vigilance case, that his or her integrity is certified, and that no penalty was imposed in the last 10 years.
  • 11The applicant's undertaking that the candidature will not be withdrawn if selected.
  • 12Vigilance and Administrative Clearance of the concerned Port in the proforma at Annexure-III, signed by the Chief Vigilance Officer.
  • 13Two recent passport-size colour photographs in a sealed envelope.

Chennai Port Authority Chief Engineer Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1Can a fresh engineer apply for the Chennai Port Chief Engineer post?
A
No. The post is filled by Absorption, and only officers of Major Port Authorities who meet the Recruitment Rules in Annexure-I can apply.
Q2From when will the Chief Engineer post be vacant?
A
The post is likely to fall vacant from 1 January 2027. The same date is the crucial date for judging eligibility.
Q3What APAR grading is needed?
A
The overall grading in the ACRs / APARs must not be below "Very Good". APARs for 2021-2022 to 2025-26 must be sent, attested on each page.
Q4Will the pay scale go up after joining?
A
Yes. After 3 years of regular service, the officer moves up to Rs. 1,20,000 to Rs. 2,80,000, subject to the Ministry's conditions in its letter dated 19 November 2019.
Q5Can I send the application directly to Chennai Port?
A
No. You apply on the OAP and hand the printout to your own Port. That Port forwards it through proper channel with an NOC and vigilance clearance.
Q6What should be written on the envelope?
A
The envelope is superscribed "Application for the post of Chief Engineer, Chennai Port Authority".
Q7What if my vigilance clearance is not ready in time?
A
Your Port can forward the application before the due date with the competent authority's approval, without the vigilance clearance or APARs, and send those documents afterwards.
Q8What happens if I withdraw after being selected?
A
You may be barred from selection to HoD level posts in all Major Port Authorities for two years.
