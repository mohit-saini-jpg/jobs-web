DFO Nicobar Mazdoor Selection Process 2026

Physical Test Level-I: Running Test (qualifying) Physical Test Level-II: Long Jump (qualifying) Physical Test Level-III: Weight Carrying Test (for final selection and merit) Verification of certificates/testimonials Character and antecedents verification Medical examination from the appropriate authority Final selection is based on qualifying and the merit secured in the Physical Test, followed by the verifications and medical examination. Candidates failing any test will not be given a second chance under any circumstances.

DFO Nicobar Mazdoor Exam Pattern 2026

The notice describes only a Physical Test in three levels. Before Levels I, II and III, every candidate must furnish an undertaking (Annexure-1, in the format prescribed by the District Nodal Officer) that he/she is physically fit and mentally sound. The Running Test is qualifying in nature. The Long Jump is qualifying in nature and must be cleared within three attempts. Level-III is meant for final selection of candidates. A particular time is fixed after the weight carrying test so that the stipulated number of candidates (exact number of vacancies notified plus 10% of vacancies notified) covers the distance within that time; the merit list is prepared on that basis. The merit list is prepared for the exact number of notified vacancies, with a wait list limited to a maximum of 10% of the notified vacancies. Ties are resolved first by date of birth (the older candidate gets preference),
