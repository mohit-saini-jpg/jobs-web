NHM Dhamtari Recruitment 2026 Notification PDF

The Office of Chief Medical and Health Officer, District Health Society, Dhamtari, Chhattisgarh has officially released Notification No. NHM/भर्ती/2026-27/3820 dated 11 August 2026 for the recruitment of 38 candidates on a contractual basis under the National Health Mission. The recruitment includes various posts such as 2nd ANM, Medical Officer (AYUSH), MPW, Community Health Officer, Nursing Officer, Staff Nurse, Laboratory Technician, Physiotherapist, Psychologist, Dental Assistant, Radiographer/X-Ray Technician and Secretarial Assistant. The official notification is available on the Dhamtari district website. Download NHM Dhamtari Recruitment 2026 Official Notification PDF Official Website

NHM Dhamtari Recruitment 2026 Important Dates

The walk-in interview process for all 38 posts will be conducted in seven batches between 31 August 2026 and 18 September 2026. Registration on each interview day begins at 9:00 AM. The exact application start date and last date to apply are not available in the notification, since candidates must appear directly at the venue on their allotted walk-in date rather than applying online in advance. Notification release date: 11 August 2026 Walk-in interview dates: 31 August, 02, 07, 09, 11, 15, and 17 September 2026 (registration), with eligible/final lists and interviews held the following day in each batch

NHM Dhamtari Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Candidates will be shortlisted and selected through a combination of educational qualification marks, experience marks, and written test/interview/skill test marks, based on the following weightage: Educational Qualification: Up to 65% of total marks converted from qualifying marks (varies by post; technical posts also carry separate weightage for technical qualification) Experience: Maximum 15 to 20 marks depending on the post, calculated at the rate of 2-4 marks per year of relevant government/semi-government experience, subject to a maximum of 5 years Written Test/Interview/Skill Test: Up to 20 marks, including bonus marks of up to 15 marks for candidates who have worked under the National Health Mission, and 10 bonus marks for staff who served during the COVID-19 pandemic for at least 6 months (as per government order dated 03.02.2023) Reserved category candidates must secure at leas

NHM Dhamtari Walk-in Interview Schedule 2026

On each registration day, candidate registration is held from 9:00 AM to 11:00 AM, the eligible/ineligible list is published by 5:00 PM, and objections can be raised from 5:00 PM to 5:30 PM. The final list is published the next day at 10:00 AM, followed by the interview/written test from 11:00 AM onwards. If the process for any batch cannot be completed on the scheduled day, it will continue on the next working day, which will be notified on the office notice board.

NHM Dhamtari Recruitment 2026 - Apply Walkin : Important Instructions

All appointments are purely temporary and contractual, valid up to 31 March 2027, and may be extended based on satisfactory performance review. No travelling allowance (TA) will be paid to candidates for appearing at any stage of the recruitment process. Selected candidates must give a bond on a Rs. 50 stamp paper and submit a No Objection Certificate if applying elsewhere. Selected candidates will be required to reside at their place of posting; the posting location will not be transferable or changeable at a later stage. All information submitted in the application must be true; providing false information will lead to immediate cancellation of candidature or termination of service, even after appointment. NHM Dhamtari Recruitment 2026 - Apply Walkin : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1This recruitment does not involve online submission. Candidates must apply in person on their designated walk-in interview date, following these steps:
  • 2Download and fill the prescribed application form available on the official Dhamtari district website (www.dhamtari.gov.in) or the CMHO office notice board.
  • 3Attach a recent self-attested passport-size photograph in the space provided on the form.
  • 4Arrange all required certificates in the sequence specified in the notification (receipt, application form, educational qualification, residence certificate, caste certificate, employment registration certificate, experience certificate, and other certificates) and staple them securely.
  • 5Attach the original Demand Draft/Banker's Cheque for the application fee, drawn in favour of "District Health Society, Dhamtari".
  • 6Report in person at the Office of the CMHO, District Dhamtari, between 9:00 AM and 11:00 AM on the walk-in date allotted to your post (see schedule below).
  • 7Complete registration; the list of eligible/ineligible candidates will be displayed the same evening by 5:00 PM.
  • 8Submit any objection to the eligibility list between 5:00 PM and 5:30 PM on the same day, if applicable.
  • 9Report the next day at 10:00 AM for the final eligible candidates list, followed by the interview/written/skill test from 11:00 AM onwards.
  • 10Applications sent by speed post, registered post, ordinary post, or any other medium will not be accepted. Applications received after the specified date and time will also not be accepted.
