Rewari District Court Stenographer Grade III Eligibility Criteria

A Bachelor's degree in Arts, Science, or Commerce, or an equivalent qualification from a recognized university. Proficiency in computers, specifically Word Processing and Spreadsheets. Must qualify an English Shorthand Test at 80 W.P.M. and Transcription at 20 W.P.M. on computer.

Rewari District Court Stenographer Grade III Age Limit

Minimum age: 18 years Maximum age: 42 years, as on 01.07.2025 Age relaxation is applicable to candidates belonging to Scheduled Castes, Backward Class-A and B of Haryana, and to those already in Government service, as per applicable Haryana Government norms. Candidates should ensure that they meet the prescribed age limit as on the specified cut-off date. Rewari District Court Stenographer Grade III Selection Process

Candidates will be required to qualify the following stages: English Shorthand Test at 80 W.P.M. and Transcription at 20 W.P.M. on computer. A Computer Proficiency Test (CPT), which is qualifying in nature. The Stenography/Shorthand test is scheduled to be held on 28.09.2026 , and the Computer Proficiency Test on 05.10.2026 . Candidates must bring their original documents and valid ID proof at the time of the test. No TA/DA will be admissible for appearing in the test.

Rewari District Court Stenographer Grade III 2026 Salary Structure

Selected candidates will receive a consolidated remuneration of ₹25,500/- per month for the ad-hoc period, which lasts for six months or until regular appointments are made by the Hon'ble Punjab & Haryana High Court, Chandigarh, or until the ad-hoc Stenographer Grade III staff become eligible for promotion to Stenographer Grade-II, whichever is earlier.
