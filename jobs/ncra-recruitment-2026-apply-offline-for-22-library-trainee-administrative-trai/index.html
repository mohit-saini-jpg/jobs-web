NCRA Recruitment 2026 Tenure and Project Terms

Sr. No. 1–5 posts are under the SKA (Square Kilometre Array) India project and will remain under the SKA Project for a period of 3 years. Sr. No. 6–9 trainee posts are not covered under the SKA Project; their appointment/training tenure is for an initial 1 year, extendable by 1 more year (maximum 2 years), subject to satisfactory performance. There is no absorption into regular NCRA service after training. The HQ mentioned for each post is for the present; all posts are transferable within NCRA's campuses at Pune, Khodad and Ooty. Candidates may be required to work in shifts, beyond office hours, and on Saturdays, Sundays and holidays, as per the exigencies of the Centre.
