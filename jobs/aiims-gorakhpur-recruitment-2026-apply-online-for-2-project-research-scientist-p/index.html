About AIIMS Gorakhpur

The All India Institute of Medical Sciences, Gorakhpur, in Uttar Pradesh, describes itself as an autonomous organisation under the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Government of India. These posts belong to ongoing extramural projects funded by the Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR). The Principal Investigator is Dr. U Venkatesh, Assistant Professor, Department of Community Medicine & Family Medicine.

AIIMS Gorakhpur Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Document verification: Only candidates found eligible here may take the interview. Written examination: This may be held before the interview if many eligible candidates turn up. Walk-in interview: Held on 16 November 2026 at 9:00 AM at the Department of Community & Family Medicine, Academic Block (MNC building), AIIMS, Gorakhpur. If there are many eligible candidates, the appointing authority may use higher criteria to shortlist candidates for the interview, written test or personal discussion. Meeting the essential qualification does not guarantee shortlisting. The decision of the Screening Committee, Interview Committee or competent authority is final.

Service Conditions

Both positions are purely temporary and contractual and end with the project. They give no right to a regular post, absorption or transfer at AIIMS Gorakhpur, ICMR or any Government body. The first engagement runs up to 31 March 2027. It may be extended until the project ends, depending on funds, performance and approval. The engagement may be ended at any time, without notice, if performance is not satisfactory. Staff will normally work at the study or project site but may be deputed to other sites. Provident Fund, LTC, medical claims and other regular service benefits are not provided. Leave follows ICMR or project rules. Selected candidates must join within 15 days of the result or offer letter. They must also sign the declaration, code of conduct and confidentiality undertaking. The engagement follows the ICMR Extramural Research Grant guidelines.
