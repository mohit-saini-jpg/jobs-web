Who Can Download the ICAI CA Admit Card 2026?

The admit card is available only to candidates who:

  • Are registered with ICAI for the CA Foundation or CA Intermediate course.
  • Successfully submitted the examination form for the September 2026 session within the prescribed window (including the late-fee window, where applicable).
  • Have a valid SSP (Self Service Portal) login ID and password.

Candidates whose exam form was not accepted or was incomplete will not be able to generate an admit card. Specific information on exam form deadlines and late-fee cut-offs for this cycle is not available in the source used for this article; check the official ICAI exam form notification for those details.

What Details Are Mentioned on the CA Admit Card?

Based on ICAI's standard practice and the current release, the admit card typically includes:

  • Candidate's name and photograph
  • Registration number and roll number
  • Exam level (Foundation or Intermediate) and group/papers opted for
  • Exam date, day, and reporting/exam timing
  • Exam centre name and address
  • Exam-day guidelines and instructions

How to Download the ICAI CA Foundation & Inter Admit Card 2026

Follow these steps to download your hall ticket:

  • Visit the official website - icai.org or eservices.icai.org.
  • Click on the link for "Admit Card for CA Foundation/Intermediate September 2026 exam" on the homepage.
  • You will be taken to the login page of the SSP portal.
  • Enter your registration number/SSP ID and password, then submit.
  • Your admit card will appear on the screen.
  • Check every detail carefully, then download and save the PDF.
  • Take at least two printouts - one to carry to the exam centre and one to keep as a backup.

Forgot Your ICAI SSP Login Password?

If you cannot remember your SSP portal password, use the "Forgot Password" option on the login page. You can typically reset it using your registered email address, registration number, or a security question, and then follow the on-screen instructions to set a new password.

What If There Is an Error on Your Admit Card?

If you notice any mistake in your name, roll number, exam centre, or any other detail on the admit card, report it to ICAI's examination department or the official helpdesk immediately, and well before the exam date, so it can be corrected in time.

Important Instructions for Exam Day

  • Reach the exam centre well before the reporting time mentioned on the admit card.
  • Carry only the items permitted by ICAI; unauthorised material may not be allowed inside the exam hall.
  • Keep your admit card and ID proof accessible for verification at the entrance.
  • Without a valid admit card, entry to the exam centre will not be permitted.
