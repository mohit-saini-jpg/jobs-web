About OHPC

Odisha Hydro Power Corporation Ltd. was incorporated in 1995 as a hydro power generation company of Odisha and functions under the state's Energy Department. Its installed capacity has grown from 1273.53 MW to 2099.8 MW, with 6 projects within the state and one interstate project in operation. This Gold rated State PSU also promotes renewable energy investment through its wholly owned subsidiary GEDCOL and is taking up new hydro projects, including Pumped Storage Projects and Small Hydro Projects.

OHPC Managing Director Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1Can private sector executives apply for the OHPC Managing Director post?
A
Yes, if they are employed in a regular capacity in a Private Sector Enterprise with turnover of more than Rs.500 Crores in any of the last three years, and meet the other conditions. They forward the application through their Controlling Officer.
Q2Is an MBA compulsory?
A
No. The requirement is an engineering degree, and an MBA or PGDM is only an added advantage.
Q3How long is the appointment?
A
It runs for 5 years or up to the age of 65 years, whichever is earlier, and includes 1 year of probation.
Q4Can a government officer join on deputation?
A
Candidates from Government, CPSUs and SPSUs may be considered on deputation, but the deputation period shall not be less than 3 years.
Q5Is it enough to post the application by the last date?
A
No. The application must reach the Energy Department in Bhubaneswar by 15 December 2026, and late applications shall not be considered.
Q6Does OHPC pay for travel to the interview?
A
Outstation candidates appearing for the interview will be provided economy class air fare for the to and fro journey on submission of original documents.
Q7What happens if a selected candidate declines the post?
A
A candidate who gives unwillingness after the interview or after the offer of appointment shall be debarred for two years from being considered for a Board Level post in any State PSU. No request for relaxation would be entertained.
Q8Which pay level counts for the senior-level experience?
A
The notice uses the scale of Rs.1,23,100-2,15,900/-, level-15 of the pay matrix under the ORSP Rules, 2017, as the benchmark. Internal candidates need 3 years at this level or higher, while other candidates may qualify through 5 years in a senior level position or by drawing this scale, its equivalent or higher.
