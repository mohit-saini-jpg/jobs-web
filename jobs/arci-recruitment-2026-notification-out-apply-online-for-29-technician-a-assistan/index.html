ARCI Selection Process

Selection begins with preliminary screening/short-listing of applications by a Committee constituted by the Competent Authority, ARCI. The Committee considers academic performance, relevance of experience for the post applied, number of years of relevant experience (based on data in the online application) and other parameters as deemed necessary. Screened-in candidates undergo a Computer Based Competitive Written Examination (CWE), followed by Personal Interview/Trade/Skill test (as applicable). ARCI may also administer any other selection method/tool/test at the sole discretion of the Competent Authority. For Officer "B", shortlisted candidates make a crisp presentation highlighting achievements across the broad spectrum of the work experience mentioned for the post, followed by a personal interview at ARCI, Hyderabad. The examination centre for CWE is Hyderabad only for all posts.
