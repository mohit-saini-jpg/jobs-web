Pay and Tenure

Every pay figure is printed as monthly emoluments plus HRA @20%. For post 8710, Rs. 35,000 + HRA @20% applies to candidates who have qualified CSIR-UGC/ICAR/ICMR/NET including lectureship/assistant professorship, or GATE, or national level examinations conducted by Central Government Departments like DBT/DST or equivalent and/or their agencies/institutions. Rs. 28,000 + HRA @20% applies to candidates who do not fall under that category.

Selection and Interview

Applications are screened for short-listing as per the criteria fixed by the Competent Authority. Meeting the essential qualification alone gives no right to be called for interview. Shortlisted candidates appear for the interview at CSIR - IMMT, Bhubaneswar. No interview call letter is issued separately. The selection committee recommends final selection based on the candidates' performance and may also make panels for future project needs. The interview dates are tentative. Any change in date or time will be informed to shortlisted candidates through the CSIR-IMMT website. The reporting time on both days is 09.00 am to 10.30 am.

CSIR-IMMT Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in CSIR-IMMT Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 13 posts in 12 post codes (8701 to 8712): nine Project Associate - I, one Senior Project Associate, two Project Associate - II and one Project Scientist - II.
Q2What is the last date to apply for CSIR-IMMT project posts?
A
The last date for receipt of online applications is 20.11.2026 (up to 05.00 pm). Queries by e-mail must reach [email protected] on or before 19.11.2026 (up to 04.00 pm).
Q3What is the qualification for CSIR-IMMT Project Associate - I?
A
It depends on the post code. It is an M.Sc. in the subject listed for 8701 to 8704 and 8708, B.E./B.Tech. in Electronics/Electrical Engineering for 8705 and in Computer Science for 8706. For 8707 it is B.E./B.Tech. in Metallurgy and Materials Science, or M.Sc. in Material Science, or Integrated M.Sc. in Material Science & Engineering.
Q4What is the age limit for CSIR-IMMT Recruitment 2026?
A
The maximum age is 35 years for Project Associate - I and Project Associate - II, and 40 years for Senior Project Associate and Project Scientist - II, counted on the last date of submission of application. Relaxation is 05 years for SC/ST/PwBD/Women and 03 years for OBC, respectively.
Q5What is the salary of CSIR-IMMT project posts?
A
Project Associate - I gets Rs. 25,000, Senior Project Associate Rs. 42,000 and Project Scientist - II Rs. 67,000. Project Associate - II gets Rs. 28,000, or Rs. 35,000 for post 8710 if the NET/GATE or equivalent condition is met. HRA @20% is added to each.
Q6Is HRA paid on top of the pay at CSIR-IMMT?
A
Yes. Every monthly emolument in the notice is printed as an amount plus HRA @20%, for example Rs.25000/- + (HRA @20%) for Project Associate - I.
Q7How do I apply for CSIR-IMMT Recruitment 2026?
A
Register and apply online through the portal available in the CSIR-IMMT website (rects.immt.res.in), then take a printout after successful submission. Candidates applying for more than one post must apply separately for each post code.
Q8What is the selection process for CSIR-IMMT project posts?
A
Applications are screened for short-listing, and shortlisted candidates attend an interview at CSIR - IMMT, Bhubaneswar. The selection committee recommends final selection based on performance. Interview dates are tentatively 26.11.2026 and 27.11.2026.
Q9Is the CSIR-IMMT project job permanent?
A
No. The positions are purely temporary and co-terminus with the project, or 06 years in total, whichever is earlier. The engagement gives no right or claim for regularization to a permanent post of CSIR.
Q10Can I apply for more than one post at CSIR-IMMT?
A
Yes. Candidates who wish to apply for more than one post need to apply separately for each post code.
