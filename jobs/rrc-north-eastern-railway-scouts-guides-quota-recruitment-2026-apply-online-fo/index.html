NER Scouts & Guides Quota Selection Process

Candidates found eligible will be assessed on the basis of a written examination (60 marks) and marks on certificates (40 marks), for a total of 100 marks. Candidates found eligible for appointment must also pass the prescribed medical examination conducted by the Railway administration. Separate Computer Based Tests (CBT) will be conducted for Level-2 (Group 'C') and Level-1 (erstwhile Group 'D') posts. The paper consists of 40 objective type questions (40 marks) and 01 essay type question (20 marks) relating to the Scouts and Guides organization and its activities and General Knowledge, as per the syllabus. 01 mark is awarded for each correct answer and 1/3 mark is deducted for every incorrect answer. RRC North Eastern Railway Scouts & Guides Quota : Important Instructions

Candidates should ensure they fulfil all eligibility norms before applying. Qualifications/certificates acquired after the last date of application will not be considered. A candidate will be called for the written examination only if considered to fulfil all eligibility criteria as per the application. Candidature remains provisional until certificates are examined at document verification. Verification of all documents/certificates is mandatory for appointment. Mere selection and recommendation for appointment does not confer any right to recruitment/appointment in Railway. A Second class free journey pass from the nearest Railway station mentioned in the application form will be issued to SC and ST candidates whenever they are called at any stage of recruitment. Persons appointed against the Scouts & Guides Quota to the category of clerks must acquire the prescribed typing proficiency within two years from the date of appointment. Their appointment will be provisional subject to this, and no additional time will be given; services of those who fail will be summarily terminated.
