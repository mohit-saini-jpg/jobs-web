Kerala High Court Office Attendant Vacancy Details

There are a total of 17 (Seventeen) anticipated vacancies for the post of Office Attendant. Vacancies that may arise during the period of validity of the ranked list shall also be filled up from the list. As per Rule 11(2) of the Kerala High Court Service Rules, 2007 and in accordance with G.O. (P) No. 5/2023/SJD dated 01.10.2023, 4% of vacancies in the category of Office Attendant are reserved for eligible differently abled candidates.

Kerala High Court Office Attendant Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must be qualified Indian Citizens. Suitable categories of benchmark disabilities: (a) Blindness & Low vision (b) Deaf and Hard of Hearing (c) Locomotor Disability including Cerebral Palsy, Leprosy Cured, Dwarfism, Acid Attack Victims, with minimum one functional arm and leg each (d) Autism Spectrum Disorder (Mild, Moderate), Intellectual Disability (Mild), Specific Learning Disability, Mental Illness (40% to 70%) (e) Multiple disability involving above combinations. Minimum functional requirement: Sitting, Standing, Walking, Manipulation by Fingers, Seeing.
PostQualification Required
Office AttendantShould have passed S.S.L.C or equivalent and should not have acquired graduation. All qualifications must be acquired on or before the date of closure of filing of online application.

Kerala High Court Office Attendant Age Limit

Candidates born between 02/01/1986 and 01/01/2008 (both days inclusive) are eligible to apply, with specific relaxations as follows: Scheduled Castes / Scheduled Tribes: Candidates born between 02/01/1981 and 01/01/2008 (both days inclusive) are eligible to apply. Other Backward Classes: Candidates born between 02/01/1983 and 01/01/2008 (both days inclusive) are eligible to apply. Ex-servicemen / Ex-GREF / Disembodied Territorial Army: Can exclude the period of service in defense forces, GREF, or Territorial Army, and the period of unemployment on discharge up to a maximum of five years, provided the candidate has not completed 50 years of age.

Kerala High Court Office Attendant Selection Process

Selection will be on the basis of Written Test and Interview. The written test will be of Objective Type to be answered in an OMR Answer Sheet with a total duration of 75 minutes and 100 marks. Each question carries 1 mark. For every incorrect answer, 1/4 mark will be deducted. The medium of the test will be bilingual in English & Malayalam. The interview carries 10 marks. The minimum marks required for being included in the ranked list is 35% in the interview.
