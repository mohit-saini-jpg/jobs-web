What Is a Provisional Seat Matrix

A provisional seat matrix is a preliminary list of seats available for a counselling round, published before the option entry window closes. It reflects seats that remain vacant after the previous round, including seats that were not accepted, were surrendered, or were newly added. Because it is provisional, KEA may revise the numbers before the final seat allotment is generated. Candidates are advised to rely on the final, updated matrix at the time of locking their options and not assume that provisional figures are final.
