IHM Lucknow Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Institute of Hotel Management Catering Technology and Applied Nutrition, Lucknow (an autonomous body under the Ministry of Tourism, Government of India) has invited applications under Advertisement No. IHMLKO- 03/2026 for 2 posts: one Senior Lecturer (Pay Level 10, Rs. 56,100 – 1,77,500, direct recruitment, post marked ST) and one Office Superintendent (consolidated pay of ₹42,720 per month, short term contract). Applications must reach the Principal by Speed Post on or before 2 December 2026.

About IHM Lucknow

The Institute of Hotel Management Catering Technology and Applied Nutrition, Lucknow is an autonomous body under the Ministry of Tourism, Government of India. Its address is Sector -G, Aliganj, Lucknow-226024, Uttar Pradesh. The notice says educational qualifications, experience, age limit, pay scale and other terms of service are as prescribed by the NCHMCT, Ministry of Tourism, GoI, and the Recruitment Rules of the Institute.
