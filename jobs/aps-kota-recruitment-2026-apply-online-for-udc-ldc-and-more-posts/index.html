APS Kota Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Army Public School, Kota, a private unaided English-medium co-education school on Mala Road, Kota (CBSE Affiliation No. 1780012), requires teaching and administrative staff: PGTs, TGTs, PRTs, pre-primary teachers, Librarian, UDC, LDC, Receptionist, Science Lab Attendant, Adm Supervisor and Electrician. The application form is filled in online on the school website www.apskota.in, and hard copies of all supporting documents with a demand draft of Rs 250/- must be sent to the school by post by 06 December 2026. This is a private school job, not a government post.

APS Kota Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is the last date for APS Kota Recruitment 2026?
A
Hard copies of all supporting documents with the demand draft of Rs 250/- must be sent to the school by post by 06 December 2026.
Q2What is the qualification for APS Kota PGT posts?
A
A PGT needs a two-year integrated MA/M.Sc. of RCE (NCERT), or post-graduation with 50% and three-year integrated B.Ed/M.Ed, or a Master Degree with 50% in the subject, plus B.Ed with 50% from an NCTE recognized institution.
Q3What is the age limit for APS Kota teacher posts?
A
For PGT, TGT and most PRT posts, fresh candidates must be below 40 years and experienced candidates below 57 years as on 01 April 2027; pre-primary posts require 25 to 40 years.
Q4What is the application fee for APS Kota Recruitment 2026?
A
The fee is Rs 250/-, paid by demand draft sent by post with the hard copies of the supporting documents.
Q5How can I apply for APS Kota Recruitment 2026?
A
Fill in and submit the form online at www.apskota.in, then post the hard copies of your documents with a Rs 250/- demand draft in a sealed envelope, with the post name written on it, by 06 December 2026.
Q6What is the selection process for APS Kota posts?
A
Selection is based on QR/Experience/Merit, and only shortlisted candidates will be called for an interview, whose date will be intimated later.
Q7Is APS Kota a government job?
A
No. The notice describes Army Public School, Kota as a private unaided school.
Q8Is CTET or TET required for APS Kota PRT posts?
A
Yes. PRT (All Subjects) and PRT (Art & Craft) Teacher require a pass in CTET/TET conducted by CBSE/State Govt.
Q9What is the age limit for UDC, LDC and Receptionist at APS Kota?
A
The maximum age is 50 years as on 01 April 2027, and up to 55 years for ESM clerks.
Q10Can a candidate with a diploma in music apply for PRT (Music Teacher)?
A
No. The notice says candidates who have obtained a diploma in music are not eligible for PRT (Music Teacher); the same applies to a diploma in dance for PRT (Dance Teacher).
