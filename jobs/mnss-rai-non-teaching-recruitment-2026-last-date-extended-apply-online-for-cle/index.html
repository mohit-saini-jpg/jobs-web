MNSS Rai Selection Process 2026

Selection for the post of Clerk will be based on a Written Test, conducted as per the norms prescribed by the Government of Haryana. Selection for the post of Steno-Typist will be based on a Written Test, followed by a Shorthand Test and Typing Test in both Hindi and English. In case of a tie in marks, preference will be given to the candidate who is older in age, as determined by the Date of Birth (DOB).
