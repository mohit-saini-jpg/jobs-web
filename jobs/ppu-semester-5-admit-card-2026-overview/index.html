How to Download PPU Semester 5 Admit Card 2026?

Students can follow these steps to download the Patliputra University UG Semester 5 admit card:

  • Visit the official student services portal at ppuponline.in.
  • Find the Examination section on the homepage.
  • Click on the UG Regular Sem-5 Admit Card download option.
  • Open the admit card search page.
  • Enter the required Exam Form Number.
  • Enter the date of birth in DD-MM-YYYY format.
  • Fill in the CAPTCHA displayed on the screen.
  • Submit the details to retrieve the admit card.
  • Check the examination details and download the available hall ticket.
  • Take a clear printout for examination use.
