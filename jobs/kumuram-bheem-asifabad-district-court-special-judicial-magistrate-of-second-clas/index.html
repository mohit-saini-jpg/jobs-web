Kumuram Bheem Asifabad District Court Special Judicial : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1Is the Special Judicial Magistrate position permanent or on contract, and for how long?
A
The term of the post is one year from the date of assumption of charge, and the terms may be changed as per the directions of the Hon’ble High Court for the State of Telangana. Powers conferred automatically stand withdrawn upon attaining 65 years of age.
Q2Are allowances paid on top of the base remuneration?
A
Yes, selected candidates are paid a remuneration of Rs. 45,000/- plus Rs. 5,000/- as conveyance allowance per month.
Q3Who is disqualified from applying for this position?
A
Practicing advocates, members of any State or Local Authority, members of the State Legislature or Parliament, persons dismissed or compulsorily retired for misconduct from government service, persons convicted or facing pending charges for moral turpitude, persons of unsound mind, insolvents, and contractors or businessmen having a subsisting contract with the government are disqualified.
Q4What is the working schedule for these courts?
A
As per High Court Circular ROC.No.802/SO/97 dated 29.01.1998, Special Judicial Magistrate of Second Class Courts function for 05 days in a week from Monday to Friday on working days between 7.30 AM and 10.30 AM.
