About GSSSB Recruitment 2026

The Gujarat Subordinate Service Selection Board (GSSSB) is a Gujarat government recruitment board. Its office is at Block No. 2, First Floor, Karmayogi Bhavan, Sector-10-A, Gandhinagar. In this recruitment it selects Food Safety Officers for the office of the Commissioner, Food and Drugs Control Administration, which works under the control of the Health and Family Welfare Department. Official updates are posted on gsssb.gujarat.gov.in, and applications are taken through OJAS.

GSSSB Vacancy 2026

GSSSB Recruitment 2026 has 84 Food Safety Officer, Class-3 posts in all. The notice says the number of posts may go up or down. Reserved posts are only for candidates from the reserved classes of Gujarat origin. Posts for women are reserved under the General Administration Department notification dated 11/11/2014. If no suitable woman candidate is available, the post goes to a male candidate of the same category. The posts left after the women's reservation are not reserved for men. Men and women can both apply for them, and selection is by merit on marks or normalised marks. Ex-servicemen get a 10% reservation. A selected ex-serviceman is adjusted against his own category (UR, EWS, SEBC, SC or ST). If no suitable ex-serviceman is available, the post goes to an eligible candidate of that category. GSSSB Eligibility Criteria 2026

The educational qualification for Food Safety Officer, Class-3 follows the recruitment rules notified by the Health and Family Welfare Department on 02/07/2016 (No. GP/4/FSO/10/2016/1791[1791]/JH). The degree must come from a university established or incorporated by or under a Central or State Act in India, or from an institution recognised as such or declared a deemed university under section 3 of the University Grants Commission Act, 1956. An equivalent qualification recognised by the Government is also accepted. The recruitment rules also say that no person who has any financial interest in the manufacture, import or sale of any article of food shall be appointed as a Food Safety Officer. GSSSB Salary 2026

For the first five years, appointment is on the fixed pay of Rs. 40,800 per month and the other benefits under the Finance Department resolutions. The latest of these is the resolution dated 18/10/2023 (No. Kharch-2002-57-(Part-4)-Z.1). If service is found satisfactory at the end of five years, the candidate becomes eligible for regular appointment in the Level-5 pay scale. This is subject to the General Administration Department resolution dated 23/10/2015 and the Finance Department resolutions dated 28/03/2016 and 18/01/2017. It is also subject to the Supreme Court's decision in SLP No.14124/2012 and SLP No.14125/2012.

GSSSB Selection Process 2026

One-stage MCQ competitive examination (CBRT or OMR), with Part-A and Part-B in a single question paper. Provisional result for all candidates who appear, published on the Board's website. Document verification for about two times the number of posts in each category, chosen by total marks from among candidates who meet the qualifying standard in both parts. If enough candidates are not available after this, more candidates from the provisional merit list may be called by category. Selection list, and a waiting list where applicable, prepared by category on merit (General Administration Department resolution dated 27/07/2018). The Board recommends the selected candidates to the department concerned for appointment, in order of Merit Number. Widows: Widow candidates get 5% of their CBRT/OMR marks added for preference in selection. They must not have remarried at the time of appointment and

GSSSB Exam Pattern 2026

The examination is MCQ-based and is held in one sitting on CBRT/OMR. The paper is set at the level of the educational qualification, and the syllabus content follows that qualification. There are 90 questions in Part-A and 120 in Part-B, 210 questions in all. Candidates get 3 hours (180 minutes) for Part-A and Part-B together. Negative marking: 1/4 of the marks for the question is deducted for each wrong answer. Qualifying standard: at least 40% in Part-A and at least 40% in Part-B, each counted separately. The minimum is not lower than 40% for any category. After the exam, a provisional answer key will be published. The final answer key will be published after the representations on it are considered. GSSSB FSO Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Applications for Advertisement No. 465/202627 are accepted online only, on ojas.gujarat.gov.in, from 27 October 2026 (18:00 hours) to 10 November 2026 (23:59 hours). Only candidates with an OTR (One Time Registration) on OJAS can apply. Candidates who have not registered must complete the OTR first.
  • 2Go to https://ojas.gujarat.gov.in. Click Apply under "On line Application" and select GSSSB.
  • 3Click the name of Advertisement No. 465/202627, Food Safety Officer, Class-3, and then Apply. Read the advertisement under More Details, then click Apply now.
  • 4Enter your Registration No. and date of birth, then click "Apply with OTR". The Personal Details are already filled in from your registration. Fill in every field marked with a red asterisk (*).
  • 5Enter your SSC details: roll number, month and year of passing, and the board name as printed on the marksheet.
  • 6For the fee refund, enter your bank account details (name as in the passbook, bank name, IFSC code and account number). Upload the first page of the passbook in JPEG, JPG or PNG format. These details cannot be changed offline once submitted.
  • 7Fill in the Educational Details. Under "Declaration", select "Yes" and click "save" to generate the Application Number.
  • 8Click Upload Photograph and enter the application number and date of birth. Upload the photo (5 cm high, 3.6 cm wide) and the signature (2.5 cm high, 7.5 cm wide), each in jpg format and no larger than 15 kb. The photo must be passport size, with a white background and the date it was taken printed on it. That date must not be more than one year before the last date to apply.
  • 9Click "Confirm Application" in the Online Application tab, check all details, edit if needed, and confirm. Note the "confirmation number". Then use Print Application, select the advertisement, enter the confirmation number and date of birth, and print the application. Pay the fee online by 13 November 2026 (23:59 hours).
  • 10No change of any kind is possible after the application is confirmed. To change any detail, candidates must apply again before the last date, get a new confirmation number and pay the fee for it. The category entered cannot be changed later.
  • 11Only one application should be made. If there are several, the last confirmed one is accepted. For unreserved candidates, it is the last confirmed application with the fee paid. Fees paid on the earlier applications are not refunded.
  • 12The Application Form cannot be downloaded after the last date.
  • 13Candidates should keep five copies of the photograph. The same photo must be pasted on the exam attendance sheet and shown at every later stage.
  • 14For help with qualification, age, the online form or fee payment, call the Board's office at 079-23258916.
  • 15A scribe or compensatory time is given, on request, to candidates with a benchmark disability who have limits on writing, and to candidates with a temporary disability who cannot write. This follows Office Memorandum F.No.34-02/2015-DD-III dated 29/08/2018 and the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016.
  • 16After the admit card notice is published, get the form from the "Umedvar Upyogitao" tab, under "For Divyang candidates", on the Board's website, or from https://gsssb.gujarat.gov.in/Home/ph. Email the filled form with the required certificates to [email protected] at least two days before the exam. Requests received later are not considered.
  • 17Candidates with locomotor disability (both arms affected, BA, at least 40%) need only the disability certificate. Other candidates also need the Appendix-I certificate from the Chief Medical Officer, Civil Surgeon or Medical Superintendent.
  • 18Compensatory time is 20 minutes per hour. The Board's decision is sent to the candidate by email. On exam day, the candidate must show the original certificates and the approval letter to the centre in-charge.

GSSSB FSO Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in GSSSB Recruitment 2026?
A
Advertisement No. 465/202627 has 84 Food Safety Officer, Class-3 posts: 40 UR, 08 EWS, 19 SEBC, 07 SC and 10 ST. The notice says the number may go up or down.
Q2When does the GSSSB Food Safety Officer online application start?
A
The online application opens on 27 October 2026 at 18:00 hours on ojas.gujarat.gov.in.
Q3What is the last date to apply for GSSSB Food Safety Officer 2026?
A
The last date to apply online is 10 November 2026 (23:59 hours). The fee can be paid online until 13 November 2026 (23:59 hours).
Q4What is the GSSSB Food Safety Officer application fee?
A
The fee is Rs. 500 for unreserved candidates. It is Rs. 400 for women of all categories, SEBC, SC, ST, EWS, candidates with disabilities and ex-servicemen. The fee is refunded to candidates who appear in the exam.
Q5What is the age limit for the GSSSB Food Safety Officer post?
A
Candidates must be 18 to 37 years old on 10 November 2026. The maximum after relaxation is 42 or 45 years depending on the category, and ex-servicemen get their service period plus three years.
Q6What qualification is needed for GSSSB Food Safety Officer?
A
Candidates need a Master's Degree in Chemistry, or a degree in Food Technology, Diary Technology, Biotechnology, Oil Technology, Agriculture Science, Veterinary Science, Bio-Chemistry, Microbiology or Medicine. A bachelor's, master's or Doctorate degree in the listed Food Technology and Agricultural Science disciplines is also accepted. Basic computer knowledge and knowledge of Gujarati or Hindi are needed as well.
Q7What is the salary of a GSSSB Food Safety Officer?
A
The pay is a fixed Rs. 40,800 per month for the first five years. After that, with satisfactory service, the officer becomes eligible for regular appointment in the Rs. 29,200 – 92,300 (Level-5) pay scale.
Q8What is the selection process for GSSSB Food Safety Officer 2026?
A
Selection starts with a one-stage MCQ exam (CBRT/OMR). About two times the number of posts in each category are then called for document verification, and the selection list is prepared category-wise on merit.
Q9Is there negative marking in the GSSSB Food Safety Officer exam?
A
Yes, 1/4 of the marks for a question is deducted for each wrong answer. The paper has 210 questions (Part-A 90, Part-B 120) in 3 hours, and candidates need at least 40% in each part.
Q10Why are no posts for candidates with disabilities shown in GSSSB Advertisement No. 465/202627?
A
The posts reserved for candidates with disabilities in this cadre are advertised separately under Advertisement No. 466/202627, a special recruitment drive. Candidates with eligible disabilities can still apply under this advertisement and get the age relaxation.
