IOCL Bongaigaon Apprentice Recruitment 2026 – Quick Summary

Who is recruiting? Indian Oil Corporation Limited (IOCL), Bongaigaon Refinery. Which posts? Trade Apprentice – Attendant Operator (Chemical Plant), Trade Apprentice (Boiler), and Technician Apprentice – Instrumentation. How many vacancies? 58 (tentative; the number may increase or decrease at the management's discretion). Who can apply? 3-year B.Sc (Maths, Physics, Chemistry or Industrial Chemistry) for Codes 101 and 103; 3-year Diploma in Instrumentation and related disciplines for Code 107, with minimum 50% marks (45% for SC/ST and PwBD candidates for their reserved seats). Application start date: 28.09.2026 (10:00 Hrs). Last date: 18.10.2026 (17:00 Hrs). Application mode: Online – registration and application on NAPS/NATS, plus a Microsoft Form on www.iocl.com. Both steps are compulsory. Job location: Bongaigaon Refinery, Indian Oil Corporation Limited.

IOCL Bongaigaon Apprentice Reservation Policy

Reservation for SC, ST, OBC (NCL), PwBD and EWS is in terms of the numbers shown in the seat table. Unfilled SC/ST seats may be filled by ST/SC candidates on merit; if still unfilled, first by OBC and then by unreserved candidates. Unfilled OBC seats may be filled by SC/ST candidates and then by unreserved candidates. Unfilled EWS seats may be filled by unreserved candidates. Unfilled PwBD seats may be filled by candidates meeting the minimum physical fitness standards in the Rules. All are on merit based on marks in the prescribed qualification. Minimum qualification marks are relaxed by 5% for SC/ST/PwBD candidates against reserved positions. IOCL Bongaigaon Apprentice Selection Process

There is no written test . Candidates registered on NAPS/NATS and applying against the notification are considered as follows. Selection is on the percentage of marks, in descending order, in the prescribed qualification for the trade applied for (aggregate of all subjects of all years/semesters), among candidates meeting the eligibility criteria. First-time apprenticeship applicants get preference. Tie-break: senior by date of birth first; if the date of birth is the same, higher Matriculation percentage. PwBD candidates who availed relaxation in qualification marks are considered against reserved seats irrespective of position in the select list (in order of merit within the category). Shortlisted candidates, subject to seats in each trade, are called for document verification and then pre-engagement medical examination. Medical Fitness and Declarations

Selected candidates must meet the physical fitness standards under the Apprentices Act and amendments. Candidates should read the 'Guidelines and Criteria for pre-engagement medical examinations' on www.iocl.com. PwBD medical examination follows the RPwD Act, 2016. Only candidates declared fit are engaged. Candidates with reported ailments, deficiencies or abnormalities must declare them in the application. Candidates declared unfit in a medical examination by any refinery unit or PSU, while seeking apprenticeship or regular employment in the past, must declare it with reasons. Candidates must declare if they have been arrested, prosecuted, detained, fined, convicted by a court, or debarred/disqualified by any Public Service Commission.
