SSC Grade C Stenographer LDCE Eligibility Criteria 2026

The SSC Grade C Stenographer LDCE 2026 is restricted to eligible departmental candidates serving in the participating services and cadres. The required length of approved service and other conditions vary by cadre. The crucial date for calculating approved service is 1 July 2026 for the Central Secretariat, Railway Board, Indian Foreign Service Branch (B) and Election Commission of India cadres. For Armed Forces Headquarters and Central Vigilance Commission, it is 1 January 2026. Important: Candidates should read the detailed cadre-wise service conditions in the official notification. Meeting a general service-duration requirement alone may not establish eligibility, as additional rules and exceptions apply.

SSC Grade C Stenographer LDCE Exam Pattern 2026

The examination includes a Computer Based Examination, Stenography Skill Test and Evaluation of Service Records. The Computer Based Examination will have a duration of two hours. Eligible candidates entitled to compensatory time will receive two hours and forty minutes. The questions will be objective-type multiple-choice questions, with a negative marking of 0.25 marks for each incorrect answer. Candidates shortlisted in the Computer Based Examination will be required to appear for the Stenography Skill Test, subject to the conditions prescribed in the notice. Eligible candidates entitled to scribe assistance will receive the applicable extended transcription time. Candidates must take the Skill Test in the language selected in their application. SSC Grade C Stenographer LDCE Selection Process 2026

The selection process consists of the following stages: Computer Based Examination (Part A) Stenography Skill Test (Part B) Evaluation of Service Records / APARs (Part C) Preparation of the final merit list and recommendation for inclusion in the applicable cadre select list Candidates must secure the required qualifying standards at the relevant stages. Final selection will be based on aggregate performance and the applicable cadre-wise vacancy and reservation provisions.
