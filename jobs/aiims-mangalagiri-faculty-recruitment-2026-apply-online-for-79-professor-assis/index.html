AIIMS Mangalagiri Faculty Recruitment 2026 Vacancy Details

Vacancies are distributed across 33 departments and four faculty grades: Professor, Additional Professor, Associate Professor, and Assistant Professor. Category-wise breakup (UR: Unreserved, OBC: Other Backward Classes, SC: Scheduled Caste, ST: Scheduled Tribe) is provided for each department. AIIMS Mangalagiri Faculty Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

A duly constituted Standing Selection Committee will conduct the selection process for all posts. Interviews will be held at AIIMS Mangalagiri in a mode decided by the Executive Director. Candidates called for interview must produce all relevant original documents for verification and may be asked to submit an affidavit or declaration as decided by the institute. After document verification, the Standing Selection Committee will conduct the interview/selection process only for eligible candidates against the post applied for. Mere appearance in the interview does not confer any right to selection. Candidates must score the minimum qualifying marks (suitability standard) shown below to be considered for empanelment in order of merit. AIIMS Mangalagiri Faculty Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is AIIMS Mangalagiri Faculty Recruitment 2026?
A
It is a recruitment drive by All India Institute of Medical Sciences, Mangalagiri, Andhra Pradesh, to fill 79 Faculty (Group-A) posts of Professor, Additional Professor, Associate Professor, and Assistant Professor on Direct Recruitment/Deputation basis across 33 departments, notified vide AIIMS/MG/Admin/Recruitment/03/Faculty/2026/01 dated 11.08.2026.
Q2How many vacancies are available in AIIMS Mangalagiri Faculty Recruitment 2026?
A
A total of 79 posts are available: 17 Professor, 11 Additional Professor, 14 Associate Professor, and 37 Assistant Professor posts across 33 departments. The vacancies are provisional and subject to change by the institute.
Q3What is the last date to apply for AIIMS Mangalagiri Faculty Recruitment 2026?
A
The closing date of online applications is 45 days from the date of publishing in the Employment Newspaper; the exact calendar date is not specified in the notification. The hard copy of the application must reach the institute within 10 days of the online closing date.
Q4What is the qualification for AIIMS Mangalagiri Faculty Recruitment 2026?
A
Medical candidates generally need a recognized medical qualification plus a postgraduate degree (MD/MS), and/or D.M./M.Ch. for super-specialities, along with the prescribed years of teaching/research experience that vary by post. Non-medical candidates need a relevant Master's degree and a Doctorate degree, with prescribed experience after the doctorate. Full details are given in the Eligibility section above.
Q5What is the age limit for AIIMS Mangalagiri Faculty Recruitment 2026?
A
The maximum age is 58 years for Professor and Additional Professor, and 50 years for Associate Professor and Assistant Professor, as on the closing date of online application, with age relaxation applicable as per rules for PwBD, SC/ST, OBC, and Government servant candidates.
Q6What is the application fee for AIIMS Mangalagiri Faculty Recruitment 2026?
A
The fee is Rs. 3,100 (Rs. 3,000 + Rs. 100 processing fee) for UR/EWS/OBC candidates, Rs. 2,100 (Rs. 2,000 + Rs. 100 processing fee) for SC/ST/Women candidates, and Rs. 100 (processing fee only) for PwBD candidates, who are exempted from the application fee.
Q7How can I apply online for AIIMS Mangalagiri Faculty Recruitment 2026?
A
Applications must be submitted online through the AIIMS Mangalagiri website, which will open the application portal shortly, followed by sending a signed hard copy of the application and supporting documents to the Recruitment Cell within 10 days of the online closing date, and emailing self-attested documents as a single PDF to [email protected].
