Ambedkar Nagar Anganwadi Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Only female candidates are eligible to apply for both Anganwadi Karyakatri and Anganwadi Sahayika posts. Minimum age: 18 years Maximum age: 35 years Cut-off date for age calculation: 1 July 2026 The date of birth mentioned in the High School (Class 10) certificate/mark sheet will be considered valid for age calculation. Age relaxation details for any reserved category are not mentioned in the official notification. The minimum educational qualification for both Anganwadi Karyakatri and Anganwadi Sahayika posts is Intermediate (Class 12) or an equivalent qualification. Preference in selection is given as per the local Gram Sabha/Ward where the Anganwadi Kendra is located, as explained in the selection priority section below.

Ambedkar Nagar Anganwadi Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

The selection process is conducted entirely by the District Magistrate through a District-level Selection Committee, as per the government order dated 17 September 2025. Chairperson: Chief Development Officer or a senior administrative officer of Additional District Magistrate rank, nominated by the District Magistrate Member Secretary: District Programme Officer of the district Member: District-level SC/ST officer nominated by the District Magistrate Member: District-level OBC officer nominated by the District Magistrate Member: A Group 'A' or 'B' woman officer posted in the district, nominated by the District Magistrate Member/Presenter: Concerned Child Development Project Officer (CDPO) of the project A quorum of at least 4 members is mandatory for the Selection Committee meeting, and the presence of the Chairperson and District Programme Officer is compulsory. Ambedkar Nagar UP Anganwadi Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

Only online applications submitted correctly and completely through upanganwadibharti.in will be accepted; no offline application or physical document will be entertained.
