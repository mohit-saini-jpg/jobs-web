AIIMS Deoghar Junior Resident Recruitment 2026 - Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must hold a graduate (MBBS) degree from a recognized University/Institute. Candidates who have already completed 18 months of Junior Residency will not be considered eligible for this post. The date for determining eligibility with regard to age, educational qualification, and experience will be the date on which the candidate appears for the interview/written test examination.
