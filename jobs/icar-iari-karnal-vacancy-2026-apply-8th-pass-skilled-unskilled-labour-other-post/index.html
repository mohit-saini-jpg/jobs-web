ICAR IARI Karnal Vacancy 2026, Apply 8th pass Skilled : Job Overview

ICAR IARI Karnal Vacancy 2026: ICAR-Indian Agricultural Research Institute (IARI), Regional Station, Karnal has released the Walk-in Interview notice for recruitment to a total of 5 posts of Young Professional-II (YP-II), Young Professional-I (YP-I), Skilled Labour and Un-Skilled Labour. This recruitment will be purely on a temporary and contractual basis under different projects. Eligible candidates can directly attend the Walk-in Interview at ICAR-IARI Regional Station, Karnal, on 11th September 2026 at the scheduled time along with their filled application form, original documents, and their attested photocopies. YP-II will get ₹42,000 per month, YP-I will get ₹30,000, Skilled Labour will get ₹22,500, and Unskilled Labour will get ₹15,000 per month as fixed consolidated emoluments.
