About INFLIBNET Centre

The notice describes the INFLIBNET Centre as an Autonomous Inter University Centre of the University Grants Commission, with its headquarters at Gandhinagar, Gujarat. The Centre runs programmes to modernise libraries and information centres in universities, colleges, institutes of higher learning and R & D institutes in India using computer and communication technology. Its major programmes include ONOS, Infistats, INFED, Shodhgangotri, Shodh-Chakra, Vidwan, Vidya-Mitra, IRINS, NIRF and SOUL, along with several other national-level projects.

Pay and Tenure

The posts carry the usual allowances as per Govt. of India rules, as applicable from time to time. Selected candidates will be covered under the New Pension Scheme (NPS) only. Scientist-E (Library Science): This post is filled on deputation for a period of One year. Deputation will be as per the existent GoI Rules. Scientist-B (Computer Science): This post is notified against a lien vacancy. If the person holding the lien does not come back, the appointed candidate is likely to be continued and made permanent. If that person comes back, the appointed candidate will have to vacate the post. Senior Assistant/Office Assistant-II: Direct Recruitment. At present the place of posting is Gandhinagar, Gujarat. However, selected candidates are liable to be posted for work anywhere in India as and when required.

INFLIBNET Recruitment 2026 Job Description

The Scientist-E will be a senior member of a group of young library professionals. The group's work includes: interacting with and assisting the University Libraries in their computerization work; creation of machine-readable databases; organizing information services; conducting training programs for library professionals in computerized library operation; arranging seminars etc. The Scientist-B will be a senior member of a group of young computer professionals. The group's work includes: development of various library related software packages; setting up of networking facilities; advising and assisting university libraries in computerization process; training the library professionals in use of computer etc. drafting and noting; administrative and establishment matters; finance and accounts; purchase and stores matters.

INFLIBNET Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q2What is the last date to apply for INFLIBNET Recruitment 2026?

The online application closes on 20 November 2026 at 5:30 p.m. The hard copy with all self-attested enclosures must reach the Centre by 30 November 2026 at 5:30 p.m.
Q3What qualification is required for INFLIBNET Scientist-E and Scientist-B?
A
Scientist-E (Library Science) requires "Ph. D + 9 Yrs. exp. OR M. Lib / M.L.Isc/ eqvt. + 15 Yrs relevant experience". Scientist-B (Computer Science) requires "B.E./MCA/ eqvt. + 1 Yrs. relevant exp".
Q4What qualification is required for INFLIBNET Senior Assistant/Office Assistant-II?
A

B.Com/M.Com and knowledge of Tally, PFMS, TDS/GST, audit procedure/rules and financial statements are desirable.

A
The upper age limit is 45 Years for Scientist-E (Library Science) and 35 years for Scientist-B (Computer Science) direct recruits. For Senior Assistant/Office Assistant-II it is 30 years, or 35 years for cent. & State govt. Autonomous bodies and university employees.
Q6What is the salary for INFLIBNET posts?
A
Under the 7th CPC, Scientist-E is at Pay Level 13 (123100-215900), Scientist-B at Pay Level 10 (56100-177500) and Senior Assistant/Office Assistant-II at Pay Level 6 (35400-112400). The usual allowances as per Govt. of India rules are paid on top, and appointees come under NPS.
Q7What is the INFLIBNET application fee?

The fee is Rs. 1,000/-, paid through SBI Collect. It is non-refundable and non-transferable. SC/ST, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen and Women candidates are exempt but must upload a supporting certificate.
Q8How are candidates selected in INFLIBNET Recruitment 2026?
A
A screening test, written test, skill test or interview may be conducted. Candidates are shortlisted on the basis of qualifications and quality of relevant experience, and call letters are sent only by email.
Q9Is the INFLIBNET Scientist-B post permanent?

Scientist-B (Computer Science) is filled against a lien vacancy. The appointee is likely to be continued and made permanent if the lien holder does not return, but must vacate the post if the lien holder comes back.
Q10Can I apply for more than one INFLIBNET post?

Yes. You must submit a separate online application and fee for each post, and send a separate hard copy with its own enclosures for each one.
