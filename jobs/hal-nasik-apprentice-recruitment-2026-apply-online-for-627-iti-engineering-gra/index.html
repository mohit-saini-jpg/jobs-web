HAL Nasik Apprentice Eligibility 2026

Candidates should have passed ITI in the respective trade recognized by an NCVT/SCVT-affiliated institute. Trades under Sr. No. 1–13 require a 2-year regular ITI course; trades under Sr. No. 14–17 require a 1-year regular ITI course. Candidates already registered as an apprentice elsewhere, or who have completed an apprenticeship, are not eligible. Registration on the NAPS portal (www.apprenticeshipindia.gov.in) is mandatory, and the Registration Number (starting with A0XXXXX) must be indicated in the application form. Engineering Graduates: 4-year B.E./B.Tech/B.Pharm degree in the respective branch from a recognized university. Diploma: 3-year Diploma in the respective branch approved by the State Board of Technical Education & AICTE (Medical Lab Technician: Government-approved Diploma, minimum 1-year duration). Non-Technical Graduates: 3/4-year degree (BA/B.Com/B.Sc/BBA) in the respect

HAL Nasik Apprentice Selection Process

Selection is merit-based on qualifying marks — there is no written examination or interview. Shortlisted candidates undergo physical document verification before joining. ITI candidates: Merit score is computed with 70% weightage to SSC (10th) marks and 30% weightage to ITI marks (marks of all semesters considered). Engineering Graduate/Diploma/Non-Technical Graduate candidates: Merit score is based on the passing mark list/certificate of the qualifying degree or diploma. Online registration on NAPS/NATS 2.0 portal and submission of application form. Preparation of merit list based on qualifying marks. Physical document verification of shortlisted candidates (2nd & 3rd week of September 2026). Publication of final shortlisted candidates’ list (4th week of September 2026). Joining for apprenticeship training (1st & 2nd week of October 2026), subject to medical fitness and police verificat

HAL Nasik Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1ITI candidates: Register on the NAPS portal (www.apprenticeshipindia.gov.in) with SSC (10th) and ITI education details and obtain a Registration Number (starts with A0XXXXX). Ensure the profile is 100% complete, including mandatory e-KYC and Beneficiary ID.
  • 2Engineering Graduate/Diploma/Non-Technical Graduate candidates: Register on the NATS 2.0 portal (https://nats.education.gov.in) with degree/diploma education details and obtain a Student ID/Enrollment ID. Candidates who completed engineering after a diploma must upgrade their profile to Engineering Graduate. Mandatory e-KYC and APAAR ID are required.
  • 3After obtaining the Registration Number/Student ID, open the application form via the link or QR code and fill in all details accurately as per documentary proof. The form can be filled only once and cannot be corrected after submission.
  • 4Upload original scanned documents (Aadhaar Card, SSC/Degree/Diploma certificate, marksheets, Bank Passbook, etc.) on the portal profile. Mobile screenshots, internet result copies, or photocopies are not accepted.
  • 5On successful submission, the message “Thank You! Your Form is Submitted Successfully!” will be displayed.

HAL Nasik Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many total apprentice posts are available at HAL Nasik?
A
A total of 627 posts are available - 355 ITI trade posts and 272 Engineering Graduate/Diploma/Non-Technical Graduate posts.
Q2What is the last date to apply for HAL Nasik Apprentice Recruitment 2026?
A
The last date to submit the online application for both notifications is 4 September 2026.
Q3Which portal should ITI candidates register on?
A
ITI candidates must register on the NAPS portal (www.apprenticeshipindia.gov.in) and obtain a Registration Number before filling the application form.
Q4Which portal should Engineering Graduate/Diploma/Non-Technical Graduate candidates register on?
A
They must register on the NATS 2.0 portal (https://nats.education.gov.in) and obtain a Student ID/Enrollment ID.
Q5Is there an age limit for HAL Nasik apprentice posts?
A
No age limit is specified in either official notification.
Q6What is the stipend for HAL Nasik apprentices?
A
Stipend ranges from ₹10,560 to ₹12,300 per month depending on the category — ₹11,040/₹10,560 for ITI (fully paid by HAL), and ₹12,300/₹10,900 for Engineering Graduate/Diploma (50% by HAL, 50% by BOAT via DBT).
Q7Is there a written exam or interview for selection?
A
No. Selection is purely merit-based on qualifying marks, followed by document verification. There is no written test or interview.
Q8Is there any application fee?
A
No, the engagement process is completely free of cost; candidates need not pay any registration or application fee.
Q9Will I get a permanent job at HAL after completing the apprenticeship?
A
No. This is a 1-year apprenticeship training under the Apprentices Act, 1961, and HAL has no obligation to offer permanent employment after completion.
Q10When will the apprenticeship training begin?
A
Joining is tentatively scheduled for the 1st and 2nd week of October 2026, after document verification and publication of the shortlisted candidates’ list.
