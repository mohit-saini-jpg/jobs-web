DH&FWS Purba Bardhaman ANM (Community Health Assistant-Urban) Eligibility Criteria 2026

PostQualification Required
ANM (Community Health Assistant-Urban) (XV-FC & NUHM)Passed ANM course from an Institute recognized by Indian Nursing Council and registered with West Bengal Nursing Council OR passed GNM course. Proficient in Bengali and permanent resident of Purba Bardhaman. Female only.
Tuberculosis Health Visitor (TBHV)Graduate in science with minimum 1-year experience working as MPW/LHV/ANM/Health Worker and certificate course in computer operations (minimum 2 months). Preferential: Training course for MPW.
Senior Treatment Supervisor (STS)Graduate in science subject, well conversant with computer programming including MS Word and Excel, and permanent two-wheeler driving license with ability to drive. Preferential: MSW and at least 1 year experience in National Health Programme.
Senior Tuberculosis Laboratory Supervisor (STLS)Graduate with DMLT or BMLT from a recognized institution, permanent two-wheeler driving license with ability to drive, and well conversant with computer programming including MS Word and Excel. Preferential: Minimum 1 year experience in National Health Programme.
Pharmacist-NUHMTwo years Diploma in Pharmacy (D-Pharm) (Allopathic) recognized by Government of West Bengal and registered as "A" category pharmacist under West Bengal Pharmacy Council. Proficiency in Bengali and computers.
Staff Nurse -NUHMCompleted GNM training course from an institute recognized by Indian Nursing/WB Nursing Council OR B.Sc. Nursing. Must be registered under WB Nursing Council and proficient in local language. Female only.
Multi Rehabilitation WorkerBachelor degree in Physiotherapy with at least 2 years of working experience in a hospital. Desirable: Master degree in Physiotherapy.
