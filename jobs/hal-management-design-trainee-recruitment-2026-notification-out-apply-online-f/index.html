HAL Design Trainee/Management Trainee Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection comprises an Online Computer Based Selection Test (All-India basis) followed by a Personal Interview at Bengaluru. The test will be of 2.5 hours duration, comprising Multiple-Choice Questions (MCQs) in three parts: Part I - 20 MCQs on General Awareness; Part II - 40 MCQs on English & Reasoning; Part III - 100 MCQs on the concerned discipline. The test can be taken in Hindi or English, as per the candidate's choice indicated at the time of applying (cannot be changed later). Candidates will be shortlisted for interview based on Online Test marks, in the ratio of 1:5 to the number of vacancies in each discipline and category. The Final Merit List will be prepared with a weightage of 85% for the Online Test and 15% for the Interview . HAL Design Trainee/Management Trainee Recruitment 2026 Placement & Emoluments

Selected candidates will be posted at any of HAL's Production, Overhaul, or Service Divisions/Research & Design Centres/Offices, based on Company requirements. Training programme duration: 52 weeks , comprising theoretical and On-the-Job training at various locations. During training, Trainees will draw a stipend comprising Basic Pay of ₹50,000 , plus Variable Dearness Allowance and Canteen Allowance. Bachelor Accommodation is also admissible during training, as per Company Rules. On successful completion of training, Trainees will be absorbed as Engineers in the Grade-II Scale of Pay of ₹50,000 - ₹1,60,000 , with eligibility for Basic Pay, Variable Dearness Allowance, other Perks & Allowances under the Cafeteria system, Company Accommodation/HRA, Performance Related Pay, Medical Facilities, Provident Fund, Gratuity, and other superannuation benefits as per Company Rules.

HAL Design Trainee/Management Trainee Recruitment 2026 Service Agreement Bond

Selected candidates must execute a Service Agreement Bond to serve HAL for a period of 5 years (excluding the training period). In case of breach of the Service Agreement (during training or after absorption as Engineer), the candidate is liable to reimburse actual training expenses (including recruitment expenses and all remuneration/expenses incurred during training), subject to a maximum of ₹15,00,000 (Rupees Fifteen Lakhs only).

HAL Design Trainee/Management Trainee Recruitment 2026 Pre-Employment Medical Standards

Candidates provisionally selected, including PwBDs, must undergo a Pre-employment Medical Examination before joining HAL. Candidates must have sound health and meet the medical standards prescribed by the Company; appointment is subject to a satisfactory medical report. The decision of the Company's Doctor in this regard is final and binding, and no relaxation in health standards will be allowed. For PwBDs, suitability for appointment will be decided based on Reports of the Medical Board attached to the Special Employment Exchanges for Physically Handicapped; their appointment will remain provisional until Disability Certificate verification is completed.
