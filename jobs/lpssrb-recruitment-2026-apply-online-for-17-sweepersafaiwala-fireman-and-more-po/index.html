LPSSRB Selection Process 2026

The selection process consists of the following sequential stages: Physical Standard Test (PST): Qualifying in nature. Physical Endurance Test (PET): Qualifying in nature. Scrutiny of original documents/testimonials: Qualifying in nature. Computer Test / Driving Test / Trade Test: 50 marks for Fireman Ministerial (Computer Test, 35 wpm typing speed with 90% accuracy; failing disqualifies from further process); 50 marks for Fireman Driver (Driving Test); maximum of 100 marks for Sweeper/Safaiwala (Trade Test, no written exam will be conducted). Written Examination: 100 marks for Fireman and Fireman Driver. For Fireman, the general select list is prepared on the basis of marks obtained in the Written Examination. For Fireman Driver, marks obtained in the driving test (50 marks) are added to the marks obtained in the written examination.

LPSSRB Exam Pattern 2026

The written examination for Fireman and Fireman Driver will be conducted either as an OMR Based Test or Computer Based Test or Tablet Based Test. The question standard is approximately Matriculation level. The paper comprises objective/MCQ type questions, carrying 1 mark each. The question paper will be in English only. There is no negative marking.
