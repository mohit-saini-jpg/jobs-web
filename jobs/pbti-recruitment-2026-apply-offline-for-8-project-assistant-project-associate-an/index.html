PBTI Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Punjab Biotechnology Incubator (PBTI), under the Department of Science, Technology & Environment, Government of Punjab, has invited applications for eight project positions under Advt. No. PBTI/01/PRO/2026-27. The posts are purely contractual, for one year or the project duration, with fixed monthly emoluments of Rs. 20,000 to Rs. 49,000. A hard copy of the application must be received at PBTI, Mohali, by 4 December 2026, and Proforma-II must also be e-mailed to [email protected] on or before the due date.
