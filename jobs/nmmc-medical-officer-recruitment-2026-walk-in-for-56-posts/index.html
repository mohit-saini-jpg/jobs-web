NMMC Medical Officer Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Navi Mumbai Municipal Corporation (NMMC), through the Ekatmik Arogya Kutumb Kalyan Society, has invited applications for 56 Medical Officer (MBBS) posts under the National Urban Health Mission (NUHM), funded by the 15th Finance Commission for Urban Health Centres. These are purely contractual, fixed-honorarium positions filled through a direct walk-in interview scheduled for August 5, 2026, at the NMMC Health Department, CBD Belapur, Navi Mumbai.

NMMC Medical Officer (MBBS) Notification 2026 PDF Out

The notification has been issued by the Medical Health Officer and Member Secretary, Ekatmik Arogya Kutumb Kalyan Society, Navi Mumbai Municipal Corporation, for filling vacant Medical Officer posts under the 15th Finance Commission-funded Urban Health Centres. The recruitment is being conducted purely on a temporary, contractual, fixed-honorarium basis, and eligible candidates are required to appear directly for a walk-in interview along with the prescribed application form and original documents. NMMC Medical Officer (MBBS) Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

MBBS degree from a recognized university. Registration with the Maharashtra Medical Council (or equivalent recognized medical council, as applicable) is mandatory. The qualification must have been obtained from a university/institution recognized by the Government. Maximum age: 70 years, as per the circular of the Mission Director, National Urban Health Mission, dated 25.05.2019, calculated as on the last date of application. Candidates aged 60 years or above are required to submit a physical and mental fitness certificate issued by the District Surgeon along with their application.

NMMC Medical Officer (MBBS) 2026 Honorarium

This is a fixed monthly honorarium paid under a contractual engagement; it is not a regular pay scale. The engagement is for a period of 6 months, as specified in the notification.

NMMC Medical Officer (MBBS) Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection for the Medical Officer (MBBS) post is made directly through a walk-in interview; there is no written examination. Candidates must report for registration, document scrutiny, and verification between 10:00 AM and 1:00 PM on the interview date, followed by the interview on the same day. Merit lists for Urban Health Centres functioning under the 15th Finance Commission are prepared based on the priority order in which the centres become operational. Eligible candidates from the merit list are called for document verification; appointment orders are issued only after successful verification of original documents. How to Attend the NMMC Medical Officer (MBBS) Walk-in Interview 2026

Download or obtain the prescribed application format as specified in the notification. Fill in the application accurately with personal, educational, and category-related details. Attach self-attested copies of all required documents listed above with the application. Report to the Aarogya Vibhag, 3rd Floor, NMMC Head Office, CBD Belapur, Navi Mumbai, on 05.08.2026 between 10:00 AM and 1:00 PM for registration. Carry all original documents along with self-attested copies for scrutiny and verification; candidates who do not produce original documents will be declared ineligible. Appear for the direct interview, conducted on the same day following document verification.
