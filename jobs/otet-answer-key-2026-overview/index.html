How to Download OTET Answer Key 2026 PDF

  • Visit the official BSE Odisha website at bseodisha.ac.in.
  • Click on the "OTET 2026" link on the homepage.
  • Select "OTET-2026 Answer Keys" from the notifications list.
  • Choose the relevant paper - Paper-I, Paper-II (Social Science), or Paper-II (Math & Science).
  • The answer key PDF will open on screen.
  • Download and save the PDF for future reference.
  • Compare your marked responses with the official key to calculate your tentative score.

OTET Paper-Wise Answer Key Details

Report any discrepancy you find in your response sheet through the official objection portal only.

OTET Answer Key Marking Scheme

Response TypeMarks Awarded
Correct Answer Marked+1 mark
Incorrect Answer Marked0 marks (no negative marking)
Question Left Unattempted0 marks

There is no negative marking in OTET 2026. You can estimate your tentative score simply by adding 1 mark for every correct answer matched against the official key.

How to Raise Objection Against OTET Answer Key 2026

The objection link has been activated on bseodisha.ac.in from 24th July 2026. Only online objections supported by valid documentary evidence will be considered by the Board.

  • Visit the official BSE Odisha website at bseodisha.ac.in.
  • Log in using your existing candidate account credentials.
  • Open the "OTET-2026 Answer Key Challenge Online Application" link.
  • Select the subject and question number you wish to challenge.
  • Upload valid supporting documents or reference material.
  • Pay the objection fee (if applicable) through the online payment system.
  • Review your submitted objection carefully.
  • Submit the form and save the acknowledgement receipt.
