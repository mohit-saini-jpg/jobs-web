NIH Assistant Professor Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

NIH Kolkata will hold walk-in interviews on 15, 16, 17 and 18 December 2026, with reporting at 9:30 A.M. at the National Institute of Homoeopathy, Block-GE, Sector-III, Salt Lake, Kolkata-700106. The National Institute of Homoeopathy (NIH), an autonomous institute under the Ministry of Ayush, Govt. of India, invites eligible Indian candidates for 11 Assistant Professor posts on contract basis in six departments, at consolidated remuneration of ₹55,000/- per month.

These posts are for NIH, Kolkata.

NIH Recruitment 2026 Job Description

To act as a guide for PGT's as per MSR of NCH (erstwhile CCH). Providing patient care to the OPD & IPD patients. Taking theoretical, clinical and practical classes for UG & PG students. Conducting seminars, journal club, etc. Any other assignments assigned by the Competent Authority from time to time.

What to Bring

All original documents and self-attested photocopies of the credentials in support of eligibility. The duly filled-in prescribed application format, with one passport size photograph. The format is on pages 5 and 6 of the notice and is headed "Application for the post of ... at NIH, Kolkata". For existing teachers, a No Objection Certificate from the present employer. The application format asks, among other items, for the department applied, category, date of birth, Aadhaar number, subject of specialization in MD (Homoeopathy), MQRL screenshot, NTET score/certificate and qualifying year, registration details with the Central/State Board of Homoeopathy, educational qualification from Matriculation onwards, experience and a signed declaration.

What Happens on the Day

If more candidates report for each category of posts, Director, NIH reserves the right to conduct one screening written test. The walk-in interview is then held at the ratio of 05 (five) candidates for one (01) post, according to the order of merit in the screening test. The notice does not describe the written test further.

Terms of Engagement

The engagement is on contract basis for one (01) year or till filling up of the posts on regular basis or till further order, whichever is earlier. The contract does not confer any right for regular appointment in the Ministry/organization. It may be terminated at any time with one-month prior notice by either party. The Institute also reserves the right to terminate it at any time without notice and without assigning any reason. No other allowances are paid apart from the consolidated remuneration. Paid leave of absence may be allowed @ 1.5 days for each completed qualified month of service. Accumulation of leave beyond a calendar year is not allowed. Normal office timing applies from Monday to Saturday. Contractual Teachers may also be called on Sunday or any holiday in case of exigencies. NIH Assistant Professor Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1When is the NIH Kolkata walk-in interview for Assistant Professor?
A
The walk-in interviews are on 15, 16, 17 and 18 December 2026. Materia Medica and Practice of Medicine are on 15 December, Pediatrics and Case Taking & Repertory on 16 December, Gynaecology & Obstetrics on 17 December, and Physiology & Bio-Chemistry on 18 December.
Q2What is the reporting time and venue?
A
Reporting time is 9:30 A.M. on all scheduled dates. The venue is National Institute of Homoeopathy (NIH), Block-GE, Sector-III, Salt Lake, Kolkata-700106.
Q3How many Assistant Professor vacancies are there?
A
There are 11 vacancies: Materia Medica 3, Case Taking & Repertory 3, Practice of Medicine 2, and one each in Pediatrics, Gynaecology & Obstetrics and Physiology & Bio-Chemistry. The number may vary, as the notice states.
Q4What is the qualification required?
A
A Post Graduate Degree in Homoeopathy included in the 2nd Schedule of the HCC Act, 1973 or NCH act 2020, plus registration with the Central/State Board of Homoeopathy. For four departments the degree is in the Concerned Subject.
Q5What is the age limit?
A
The upper age limit is 45 years (63 years for the retired person) as on the date of the walk-in interview. It is relaxable for SC / ST / OBC / PwBD candidates as per Extant Rules of Government of India.
Q6What is the salary for NIH Assistant Professor posts?
A
The consolidated remuneration is ₹55,000/- per month. No other allowances are paid apart from it, and tax is deducted at source.
Q7What should I carry to the interview?
A
Carry all original documents and self-attested photocopies supporting eligibility, the duly filled-in prescribed application format and one passport size photograph. Existing teachers may also bring a No Objection Certificate from their present employer.
Q8How are candidates selected?
A
Selection is by walk-in interview. If more candidates report, Director, NIH reserves the right to hold a screening written test first, and interviews then follow at five candidates per post in order of merit.
Q9Is this a permanent job?
A
No, it is a contract engagement for one year or till the posts are filled on a regular basis or till further order, whichever is earlier. It gives no right to regular appointment.
Q10Can I apply for more than one department?
A
Yes, but a separate application must be made for each subject. An application for multiple subjects in a single form will be summarily rejected.
