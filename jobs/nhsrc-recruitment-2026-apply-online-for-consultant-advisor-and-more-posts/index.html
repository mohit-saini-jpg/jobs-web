NHSRC Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria and Qualification

Only full-time regular bachelor's degrees (minimum 3 years) and full-time regular postgraduate qualifications (minimum 2 years) are considered. Diploma, certificate, executive programmes, and qualifications obtained through distance, online, part-time, or correspondence mode are not considered. Only post-qualification experience is counted. Desirable qualification for the Consultant, Senior Consultant, and Legal Consultant posts includes contributions to published articles, documents, reports, chapters, or monographs. The Advisor post also lists published work in health systems or public health as desirable.

NHSRC Recruitment 2026 Salary

The fee offered within each band is commensurate with the candidate's qualification and experience. In addition to remuneration, NHSRC Consultants are entitled to free accidental insurance, subsidized medical insurance, mobile bill reimbursement, laptop reimbursement (as per NHSRC policy), 30 days consolidated leave, fully paid maternity leave for female consultants (as per Government of India policy), performance-linked increments, and TA/ DA plus per diem for on-duty visits. The Advisor (PHA) post additionally includes a dedicated sedan car.

The notification states that if the recruitment process includes a written test, the minimum qualifying mark will be 70%. NHSRC Recruitment 2026 Important Notes

Candidates selected through this recruitment process may also be considered for other vacant positions at NHSRC requiring similar skill sets and at an appropriate level.
