About the Institute

All India Institute of Medical Sciences, Vijaypur, Jammu is described on its letterhead as a Central Autonomous Body under PMSSY, MoH&FW, Government of India. This post is purely temporary and on contract basis, and it serves the ICMR-funded NHRP project on UTY in oral cancer cell stemness. Queries go to the Principal Investigator, Dr Manish Kumar, Associate Professor, Department of Biochemistry, AIIMS Vijaypur, Jammu.

Who Can Apply

First Class Post Graduate Degree, including the integrated PG degrees in life sciences/Biochemistry/Biotechnology. Second Class Post Graduate Degree, including the integrated PG degrees with PhD. Experience in conducting, analysing and report writing of molecular cancer research studies. Publication in peer reviewed journal, preferably in PubMed indexed journal. Basic course/workshop in qualitative research methods; hands on experience on small animal handling and conducting research. Knowledge of using Graphpad (Prism) or strata software for experiment result analysis. Good reading, writing and communication skills in English and Hindi. Hands on experience on molecular biology, especially RT-PCR and western blotting, MTT Assay, RNA Sequencing and Next generation Sequencing techniques and data analysis. AIIMS Jammu Project Research Scientist I (Non-Medical) Job Description

Accountability for designing, conducting and analysing experiments to meet the project's objectives. The study needs a person trained in molecular biology, i.e. cloning, transfection, RNA and DNA isolation, western blotting and RT-PCR. The work involves extensive eukaryotic cell and bacterial culture work, spheroid preparation, animal experiments etc. for cell freezing and culture respectively. Accountability for designing, conducting and analysing animal experiments at AIIMS New Delhi during 2027 to 2028, i.e. the third year of the project. Coordinate with ICMR and update on project progress; participate in review meetings/trainings. Perform any other work assigned by the PI/Co-PI. Selected candidates are expected to work from the AIIMS Vijaypur campus and to travel to AIIMS Delhi, as mentioned in the job responsibilities.

Selection and Interview

The stages are: list of eligible candidates, shortlisting if needed, and interview. A list of eligible candidates will be uploaded on the official website of AIIMS Jammu and on the job portal. Merely possessing the prescribed qualifications does not ensure a call for interview. Candidates may be shortlisted based on the merit needed for the project. If the number of applications for the post exceeds 10, candidates will be shortlisted on past academic performance and research experience. Candidates falling in the merit may be called in a ratio of 10:1 for the interview, or as decided by the competent authority. Selection of the shortlisted candidates is based on scores obtained in the interview. In case of a tie in the final marks, seniority on the basis of date of birth is considered.
