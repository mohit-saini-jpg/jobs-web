About CWC

The Central Water Commission is part of the Department of Water Resources, River Development & Ganga Rejuvenation in the Ministry of Jal Shakti, Government of India. CWC Chief Draughtsman Eligibility 2026

Age limit: The maximum age for appointment by deputation is 56 years, counted on the closing date for receipt of applications. Who can apply: Officers of the drawing cadre under the Central Government, State Governments or Union Territories, statutory or autonomous organisations, public sector undertakings, universities or recognised research institutes who meet either condition below: (a) they hold an analogous post on a regular basis in their parent cadre or department; or (b) they have five years of regular service in a post in Level-6 of the pay matrix (Rs. 35400-Rs.112400), or an equivalent post, in their parent cadre or department. Essential qualification and experience: Diploma in Civil or Mechanical Engineering from a recognised university or institute. Three years' experience in the concerned field, gained in Central Government, State Government or Union Territory administration

Duties of a Chief Draughtsman

Administrative supervision of draftsmen of other categories, plus overall supervision, guidance and checking for accuracy. Drafting structural, fabrication, reinforcement and survey drawings with CADD software such as AutoCAD. Checking project estimates. Checking tender documents and construction schedules. Checking topographical contours, maps, GIS and hydrological data. Checking specifications for tenders, NITs and agreements. Checking rate analysis, including rates for non-scheduled items. Keeping drawings in digital form, including uploading and downloading them in the 'Digital Archival System for Engineering Drawings' maintained by CWC. Making sure new drawing staff are fully oriented. Keeping an account of scientific and mathematical instruments.

CWC Chief Draughtsman Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

A Selection Committee will assess the information in each candidate's CV, together with the supporting documents for essential qualification and work experience. The candidate's declaration in the proforma confirms this.

Service Conditions

The post is General Central Service Group 'B' Gazetted, Non-Ministerial. The deputation period ordinarily will not exceed three years. This includes any deputation in another ex-cadre post held immediately before this appointment, in the same or another Central Government organisation or department. Posting can be at CWC headquarters, any CWC field organisation or the encadred organisations listed above. Applicants can give three choices of organisation in the proforma. These choices are subject to administrative requirement. Other deputation terms follow the Government of India's orders and rules as issued from time to time. Once you apply, you cannot withdraw your candidature.

CWC Chief Draughtsman Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Download the vacancy circular and the Bio-Data/CV proforma from the CWC website under Citizen Corner → Recruitment.
  • 2Fill in the proforma in block letters and paste a recent passport-size photograph. The form asks for your service details, your employment history in date order, your present pay and emoluments, and your choice of posting.
  • 3Sign the declaration. Your employer must countersign it with seal, at a rank not below Under Secretary to the Government of India. Your Cadre Controlling Authority must also complete its certification.
  • 4Prepare the application in duplicate and attach the documents listed below.
  • 5Your employer forwards the application through proper channel. Only officers who can be relieved immediately if selected should be forwarded.
  • 6Send it to: Under Secretary, Establishment-X Section, Central Water Commission, 3rd Floor(S), Sewa Bhawan, R.K. Puram, New Delhi-110066. It must arrive within 60 days of the circular's publication in the Employment News.
  • 7Documents to attach:
  • 8Copies of ACRs/APARs for the last five years up to 2024-25, attested by the Controlling Authority.
  • 9Vigilance Clearance Certificate.
  • 10Certificate of any major or minor penalty imposed in the last 10 years.
  • 11Integrity Certificate.
  • 12Officers already on deputation: Cadre Clearance, Vigilance Clearance and Integrity Certificate, with the application forwarded by the parent cadre or department.
  • 13Officers from organisations that do not follow Central Government pay scales: the latest salary slip, showing basic pay with the scale and rate of increment, dearness pay or other allowances, and total emoluments.
