How to Download NEET UG 2026 Stray Vacancy Round Result?

The provisional allotment result is available as a PDF on the official MCC website.

  • Visit mcc.nic.in.
  • Open the UG Medical Counselling section.
  • Select Current Events or the latest UG counselling notices.
  • Find the link titled "Provisional Result for Stray Vacancy Round".
  • Open the allotment PDF.
  • Use the PDF search facility to locate the relevant rank or allotment details.
  • Check the allotted institution, course, quota, category and remarks.
  • Download the provisional result for reference.

How to Raise Grievances Against NEET UG 2026 Provisional Allotment?

Candidates can use MCC's dedicated online grievance facility to report discrepancies.

  • Visit the official MCC UG counselling website.
  • Open the candidate login facility.
  • Log in using the required registered credentials.
  • Access the Candidate Grievances and Query Redressal System.
  • Select the applicable grievance or query option.
  • Enter the allotment-related discrepancy and supporting information requested by the system.
  • Review the submission carefully.
  • Submit the grievance before 11:00 AM on 20 October 2026.
  • Save the available acknowledgement for reference.

Important Instructions for NEET UG Stray Vacancy Candidates

  • Download and review the provisional allotment PDF.
  • Check the rank, allotted institute, course, quota and category.
  • Report any discrepancy through MCC's Candidate Grievances and Query Redressal System before 11:00 AM on 20 October 2026.
  • Do not assume that a provisional allotment guarantees admission.
  • Wait for the final allotment result before approaching the allotted institution.
  • Download the final allotment letter when MCC activates the facility.
  • Follow the latest college-reporting schedule and admission rules.
