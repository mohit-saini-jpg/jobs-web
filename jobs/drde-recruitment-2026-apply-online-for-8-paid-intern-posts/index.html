Who Can Apply

Applicants must be Indian citizens pursuing Graduate or Post Graduate courses and must have a good academic record with 75% & above marks (or ≥ 7.5 CGPA) from AICTE/UGC approved Colleges/Universities. Candidates must also possess a recent referral/sponsorship letter from their respective college or university. The age limit is Below 28 Years as on 14th Nov, 2026. Pay and Tenure

The internship will run for a minimum duration of 06 months, starting from 01st January, 2027 onwards. A fixed stipend of Rs. 5,000/- per month will be paid for the entire duration in two equal installments: Rs. 15,000/- after completion of 03 months, and Rs. 15,000/- after completion of 06 months of internship. No boarding or lodging expenses are admissible. Accommodation is not provided by DRDO/DRDE. No TA/DA will be paid for joining.

Selection and Interview

Candidates will be shortlisted based on educational qualifications and on fulfilling the prescribed eligibility criteria (minimum 75% marks or ≥ 7.5 CGPA). Mere applying with required documents or fulfilling the required criteria will not ascertain candidature/paid internship. If required, an interaction will be conducted through telephonic mode or Video Conferencing (VC). Only shortlisted candidates will be informed for the interaction or offer of internship, and they must report to the Lab/Establishment as per the terms and conditions mentioned in the internship offer letter. Final selection does not confer any right for absorption in DRDO.
