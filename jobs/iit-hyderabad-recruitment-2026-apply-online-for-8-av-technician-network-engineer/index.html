The engagement is purely on a contractual, temporary basis for 11 months. Further extension, if any, may be granted subject to satisfactory performance and the requirement of the Institute. IIT Hyderabad Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Applications are accepted online only, using the link against each position. The notice says offline applications, if submitted, will be summarily rejected, and a printout of the online form need not be submitted to the Institute. The online form is available through the link under Temporary Positions at https://iith.ac.in/careers/, and Annexure-I gives the form-filling instructions.
  • 2Register on the online application portal with a valid and active email ID. It must remain functional throughout the recruitment process, as call letters, notifications and updates are sent only to this ID.
  • 3Fill in the personal information: name as in the 10th Standard/Matriculation certificate, date of birth with valid proof, mobile number, address, category, and Yes/No details for ex-servicemen, PwBD and any pending criminal or disciplinary case.
  • 4Upload a recent passport-size colour photograph (JPG/JPEG, 1 MB, taken within the last one month) and the signature in the same way.
  • 5Click "Save & Next", then use "Add Education" to enter qualifications and upload certificates in PDF format (maximum 1 MB per document).
  • 6Use "Add Employment" to enter employment details and upload supporting documents in PDF format (maximum 1 MB per document). Additional relevant certificates can be added with "Add Certificate".
  • 7Open "Review & Submit", verify the details, accept the declaration by selecting the checkbox, and submit. A PDF copy of the application is generated and sent to the registered email ID.
  • 8Photograph and signature in JPG/JPEG, 1 MB each.
  • 9Proof of date of birth (10th Standard Certificate/Mark Sheet/Birth Certificate).
  • 10Educational certificates, uploaded in PDF.
  • 11Experience certificates on the organisation's letterhead, showing designation, period of service, nature of work assignment(s) and pay, with the date of issue, and the name, designation, signature and stamp of the issuing authority.
  • 12For SC/ST/OBC-NCL/EWS candidates, the certificate in the prescribed Government of India format: OBC certificates must show Non-Creamy Layer, a Central List entry and issue within the last two years; the EWS certificate must be for the current financial year.
  • 13Ex-servicemen certificate, and a PwBD certificate indicating a disability of 40% or more, where applicable.
