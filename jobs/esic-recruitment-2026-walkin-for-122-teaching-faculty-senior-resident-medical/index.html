ESIC Bhubaneswar Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Teaching Faculty (Professor/Associate Professor/Assistant Professor): Not exceeding 67 years Senior Resident (Pre/Para-clinical): Not exceeding 50 years Senior Resident (Clinical): Not exceeding 45 years Medical Officer: Not exceeding 35 years Age relaxation is applicable as per prevailing Government of India norms. Retired or currently working Professors may also apply for eligible posts.

ESIC Bhubaneswar Recruitment 2026 Educational Qualification

For Teaching Faculty: Qualifications must be in accordance with the latest guidelines and minimum standard requirements of the National Medical Commission (NMC/MCI), as per Gazette Notification No. CG-DL-E-05072025-264399 dated 30.06.2025. For Medical Officers: A recognised MBBS degree qualification included in the First Schedule, Second Schedule, or Part-II of the Third Schedule (other than licentiate qualifications) of the Indian Medical Council Act, 1956. Candidates with qualifications under Part-II of the Third Schedule must also satisfy the conditions of sub-section (3) of section 13 of the same Act, and must have completed compulsory rotating internship. All candidates must possess updated Medical Registration with the NMC or the concerned State Medical Council; candidates without updated registration must submit proof of having applied for it.

ESIC Bhubaneswar Selection Process 2026

Selection is based on the candidate's acquired academic credentials and performance in the interview, conducted by a duly constituted selection board. The result will be published only in the Recruitments section of the official website, www.esic.gov.in . The offer of appointment will be sent through e-mail only. Selected candidates must join as per the instructions mentioned in the offer of appointment.

Required Documents for ESIC Bhubaneswar Walk-in Interview

Candidates must appear before the Document Verification Committee on the interview date and bring original documents along with self-attested photocopies of the following: Certificate of passing Class 10th (for proof of Date of Birth) All mark sheets, attempt details, and Degree Certificate of MBBS All mark sheets, attempt details, and Degree Certificate of MD/MS/DNB/Other Examination Valid EWS/OBC-NCL/SC/ST/PwBD Certificate in the format applicable for Central Government jobs, wherever applicable State/NMC Medical Registration Certificate (updated) Proof of Publication(s) Experience Certificate Recent passport-size photograph Relieving/NOC Certificate from previous/current employer, if applicable Aadhaar Card Completion Certificate of ICMR/NMC approved Basic Course in Biomedical Research, if any NMC-approved Revised Basic Course Workshop/Basic Course in Medical Education Workshop Certif

Important Instructions - ESIC Bhubaneswar Recruitment 2026

No private practice is permitted in any capacity after joining as teaching faculty/Medical Officer at ESIC MCH Bhubaneswar. Contractual staff are not entitled to service benefits such as Provident Fund, Pension, Gratuity, Medical Allowance, Medical Benefits, Seniority, or Promotion. Hostel accommodation/quarters will be provided to the extent possible but cannot be claimed as a matter of right; if availed, no HRA will be payable. Any absence exceeding 7 days without prior approval will be treated as voluntary abandonment, leading to automatic termination and forfeiture of one month's salary. Faculty must record attendance both manually and through the Aadhaar Enabled Biometric Attendance System (AEBAS). The valid OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) Certificate must not be issued more than one year prior to the cut-off date, and must be issued by an officer not below the rank of Sub-Divisional Officer

ESIC Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Offline Application Only: There is no online application process. Candidates must bring the completed offline application form (attached with the advertisement) along with self-attested copies of the required documents and checklist at the time of document verification.
  • 2Document Verification First: Only candidates whose documents are successfully verified by the Document Verification Committee will be permitted to appear before the Interview Board.
  • 3Multiple Post Applications: Candidates may apply for more than one post if they meet the eligibility criteria for each; however, a separate application form, documents, and applicable fee must be submitted for each post.
  • 4Reach the Venue on Time: Report between 09:00 AM and 10:00 AM at the Office of the Dean, ESIC Medical College & Hospital, Chandrasekharpur, Bhubaneswar, Odisha (Near Hotel Mayfair), on the applicable interview date.

Q1Is there an online application process for ESIC Bhubaneswar Recruitment 2026?
A
No. This is a walk-in interview recruitment. Candidates must submit the offline application form along with documents directly at the venue on the interview date.
Q2What is the last date to apply for ESIC Bhubaneswar Recruitment 2026?
A
There is no online "last date" since applications are submitted in person on the interview date. Faculty posts (Professor, Associate Professor, Assistant Professor) will be interviewed on 03.08.2026, and Senior Resident/Medical Officer posts on 05.08.2026.
Q3What is the total number of vacancies in ESIC Bhubaneswar Recruitment 2026?

A total of 122 vacancies are notified across Professor (11), Associate Professor (19), Assistant Professor (30), Senior Resident (56), and Medical Officer (6) posts.
Q4What is the age limit for ESIC Bhubaneswar teaching faculty posts?
A
The age limit for teaching faculty is not exceeding 67 years as on 31.07.2026, with age relaxation as per Government of India norms.

A
