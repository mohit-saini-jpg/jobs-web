DU NCWEB 5th Cut-Off 2026 - Quick Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 01/09/2026

DU NCWEB 5th Cut-Off 2026: The Non-Collegiate Women's Education Board (NCWEB), Delhi University has released the 5th cut-off list for admission to the BA (Programme) and B.Com courses for the 2026-27 academic session. According to the official notification, online admission against the 5th cut-off will be done from 10:00 A.M. on 1st September 2026 (Tuesday) to 11:59 P.M. on 2nd September 2026 (Wednesday) at the respective teaching centres. Female candidates who have not yet secured admission can check the centre-wise, category-wise cut-off marks below and apply within the admission window.

Key Highlights - DU NCWEB B.Com 5th Cut-Off 2026

  • Hansraj College recorded the highest B.Com cut-off among all NCWEB centres, at 79.25% for the OBC and EWS categories.
  • Miranda House followed closely with a 79% cut-off for OBC and 79.25% for EWS.
  • General category B.Com admission is shown as CLOSED at several top centres, including Aryabhatta College, College of Vocational Studies, Deen Dayal Upadhyay College, J.D.M College, Maharaja Agrasen College, Maitreyi College, Moti Lal Nehru College, Rajdhani College, Ramanujan College, SGGSC of Commerce, Hansraj College, and Miranda House.
  • The lowest B.Com cut-off for the General category among open centres is 35%, at Aditi Mahavidyalaya and Bhagini Nivedita College.

How to Apply Against DU NCWEB 5th Cut-Off 2026

  • Visit the official NCWEB admission portal.
  • Log in using your registered credentials.
  • Check whether your marks meet the 5th cut-off for your category and desired subject combination at your chosen teaching centre, using the tables above.
  • Select the eligible teaching centre and subject combination.
  • Submit your choice within the admission window: 1 September 2026 (10:00 AM) to 2 September 2026 (11:59 PM).
  • Complete any further verification or fee payment steps as directed on the portal.

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Admission against the DU NCWEB 5th cut-off will be done strictly within the window of 1 September 2026 (10:00 AM) to 2 September 2026 (11:59 PM).
  • Admission will be processed only at the respective teaching centres shown against each course and category.
  • Colleges marked "CLOSED" for a category are not accepting further applications in that category through the 5th cut-off round.
  • Colleges marked with an asterisk (*) hold classes on Saturdays; those without an asterisk hold classes on Sundays.
  • Candidates should recheck the official PDF on the NCWEB website before finalising their centre and subject choice, as figures for less common subject combinations are spread across multiple pages of the notification.
