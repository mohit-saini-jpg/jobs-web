ISRO SAC Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Space Applications Centre (SAC), Ahmedabad, a lead Centre of the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO), Department of Space, has invited online applications for 26 apprentice positions under Advertisement No. SAC: 03:2026: 9 Graduate Apprentice, 5 Technician Apprentice and 12 Trade Apprentice positions. The stipend is ₹12,300, ₹10,900 and ₹10,560 or ₹11,040 a month, for a one-year training. Applications are accepted only on www.sac.gov.in or careers.sac.gov.in from 0930 hours on 11 November 2026 to 1700 hours on 2 December 2026.

About SAC

Space Applications Centre (SAC), Ahmedabad is one of the lead Centres of ISRO, Department of Space, Government of India. The notice says SAC focuses on the design of space borne instruments for terrestrial and planetary missions of ISRO and on the development and operationalization of space technology for national development.

Who Can Apply

Applications are invited under the Apprentices Act, 1961 (as amended from time to time). Only Indian nationals are eligible. Graduate and Technician Apprentice applicants must hold the degree or diploma from a Government recognized University/Board/Institute coming under Maharashtra, Gujarat, Goa, Chhattisgarh, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan and Dadra Nagar Haveli & Daman & Diu (UTs). Trade Apprentice applicants must hold ITI (NTC/STC) from a recognized institute of Gujarat State. The degree, diploma or ITI must have been passed in the relevant field in or after December 2023. The tentative date of joining is January/February 2027. A candidate who passed after December 2023 but completes three years on the expected date of joining is not eligible. ISRO SAC Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Candidates are shortlisted or empaneled on the basis of the information furnished in the online application, with due weightage to reservation categories. The result (percentage) of the qualifying exam (Diploma/Degree/I.T.I.) declared by a Government recognized University/Board/Institute is considered to shortlist candidates. Candidates must produce documentary evidence of the marks they enter. Only shortlisted or empaneled candidates are called for document verification and further process, subject to availability of vacant positions. Intimation is sent only by e-mail. The notice prints no written test and gives no date for document verification.

Documents Required

At document verification, the hard copy of the online application summary must be produced with self-attested copies of the following: Proof of date of birth SSC (10th) and/or HSC (12th) mark sheets and certificate Mark sheets and certificate of the qualifying exam (Degree/Diploma/I.T.I.) declared by a Government recognized University/Board/Institution Latest OBC (NCL) / Caste Certificate / Income and Asset Certificate (EWS) / Tribe certificate / Disability certificate (if applicable) Aadhar Card Candidates of SC / ST / OBC(NCL) / PwBD / EWS category must hold a valid certificate issued by a competent authority in the prescribed format for recruitment to Central Government posts, for the current financial year. Original documents (including certificates of all educational qualifications) must be produced when asked; failing that, the candidate is considered disqualified.

ISRO SAC Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

Any discrepancy in name, age, Diploma/Degree/ITI result and year of passing, or caste/tribe in the online application, at any stage, means disqualification and no claim for apprenticeship training. Candidates who do not meet the prescribed medical fitness criteria at the time of joining have their candidature automatically cancelled. Apprentices have no claim whatsoever for employment in SAC, Ahmedabad or any other Centre/Unit of ISRO/Department of Space after the training period. Candidates shortlisted for document verification are not eligible for Travelling Allowance. Canvassing in any form is a disqualification, and no interim correspondence is entertained. Women candidates are encouraged to apply. General enquiry: 079-26913021/22. Technical queries only: 079-26915510/5513. Timings 1000 to 1700 hours, Monday to Friday.

ISRO SAC Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Register the application online only, through the SAC website https://www.sac.gov.in or https://careers.sac.gov.in, between 0930 hours on 11-11-2026 and 1700 hours on 02-12-2026.
  • 2Register with a valid e-mail ID. Only one registration is possible with one e-mail ID, and the e-mail ID must be correct because shortlisted candidates are informed only by e-mail.
  • 3Fill the fields marked * with correct data and enter the characters shown in the security image. If the image is unclear, click the "Regenerate Image" button.
  • 4Enter the marks or result as percentage only. Upload the essential qualification certificates (B.E./B.Tech/Graduate/Diploma/ITI) as .pdf files of maximum 1MB each; the marks, class, month and year of passing must be verifiable from them.
  • 5Check all information, then submit. The application can be submitted only once and no correction is possible afterwards.
  • 6Note the 14-digit Online Registration Number, used for all future correspondence.
  • 7Take a print-out of the application summary before 1700 hours on 02-12-2026; the site will not be open for printing after that time.
  • 8Applications submitted without uploading the requisite documents are treated as invalid. Data entered in the online application is considered final.

ISRO SAC Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is the last date to apply for SAC Recruitment 2026?
A
The last date is 02-12-2026. The site remains open for online registration until 1700 hours on that day, and applications opened on 11-11-2026 at 0930 hours.
Q2Which positions are covered by SAC Advertisement No. SAC: 03:2026?
A
The notice covers 26 position codes: 9 Graduate Apprentice (01 to 09), 5 Technician Apprentice (10 to 14) and 12 Trade Apprentice (15 to 26) positions.
Q3What is the qualification for SAC Graduate Apprentice?
A
Posts 01 to 06 need B.E. / B.Tech. or equivalent in the respective field, and posts 07 to 09 need graduation in the respective field, both with minimum 60% aggregate marks from a Government recognized University/Board/Institute.
Q4What is the qualification for SAC Technician and Trade Apprentice?
A
Technician Apprentice needs a Diploma in Engineering / Technology in the respective field with first class from a recognized State Board of Technical Education. Trade Apprentice needs ITI (NTC/STC) from a recognized institute of Gujarat State; posts 15 to 18 also list SSC/Matriculation/10th.
Q5What is the age limit for SAC apprentice positions?
A
Posts 01 to 09 have minimum 18 and maximum 28 years; posts 10 to 26 have minimum 18 and maximum 35 years, as on 02-12-2026. OBC (NCL) get 3 years and SC/ST 5 years relaxation in the upper limit.
Q6What is the stipend for SAC apprentices?
A
Graduate Apprentices get ₹12,300 and Technician Apprentices ₹10,900 per month. Trade Apprentices get ₹10,560 (posts 15 to 16) or ₹11,040 (posts 17 to 26) per month.
Q7How do I apply for SAC Recruitment 2026?
A
Apply online only through https://www.sac.gov.in or https://careers.sac.gov.in. Register with a valid e-mail ID, upload the qualification certificates as .pdf files of maximum 1MB, and submit once.
Q8How are candidates selected for SAC apprenticeship?
A
Candidates are shortlisted or empaneled on the percentage of the qualifying exam entered in the online application, with due weightage to reservation categories. Shortlisted candidates are then called for document verification, subject to vacancies.
Q9Is the SAC apprenticeship a permanent job?
A
No. The training lasts one year under the Apprentices Act, 1961, and apprentices have no claim for employment in SAC or any other ISRO/Department of Space Centre or Unit after the training.
Q10Who is not eligible for SAC apprenticeship?
A
Those already undergoing or who have undergone apprenticeship training, those with one year or more of job experience, and those who completed three years after qualifying on the date of joining are not eligible.
