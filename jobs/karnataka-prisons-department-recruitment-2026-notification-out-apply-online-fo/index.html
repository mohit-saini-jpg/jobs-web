Karnataka Prisons Department Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

SSLC or currently prescribed equivalent courses are as notified in Government Order No. Sichasu 81 Sevane 2017, dated 27.02.2018. Candidates must have obtained the prescribed qualification result as on the closing date of application submission - higher qualifications will not be considered as a substitute for the specifically prescribed qualification. As per Government Notification No. Sichasu 102 Sevane 2022 dated 18.03.2025 (read with Notification No. Sichasu 43 Sevane 2020 dated 05.03.2021), candidates who studied Kannada as a first or second language subject in SSLC/equivalent, or studied through Kannada medium, are exempt from a separate Kannada Language Test. Other candidates must qualify a Kannada Language Test conducted by KEA (or a body designated by Government) within a specified time, failing which their appointment (even if otherwise selected) will not be finalised. Minimum age: 20 years, as on the closing date of application. As per Government Order No. Sichasu 262 Sevani 2025 dated 29.01.2026, the previous upper age limit has been increased by 5 years. Karnataka Prisons Department Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Negative Marking: Each question has 4 options; for each wrong answer, 0.25 marks (1/4th) is deducted. In the OMR answer sheet, if a candidate marks 5 or more options for the same question, or leaves an option unshaded, 0.25 marks will be deducted per such question after a grace period. Candidates are shortlisted for PST and Endurance Test in a 1:5 ratio to notified vacancies, based on combined percentage of Competitive Exam marks and educational qualification marks, category-wise. Candidates appear for PST/Endurance Test entirely at their own risk regarding health; the recruitment committee is not responsible for any mishap during these tests. Candidates are advised to undergo their own medical fitness check and physical practice before appearing. Karnataka Prisons Department Recruitment 2026 Training, Probation & Pension

Training: Selected candidates must undergo basic training at institutions specified by the Director General & Inspector General of Prisons, Karnataka, and must pass all prescribed tests during training. Probation Period: 2 years from the date of appointment. Pension: The National Pension Scheme (NPS) applies, as per Government Order No. FD (SPL) 04 PEN 2005, dated 31.03.2006.

Karnataka Prisons Department Recruitment 2026 Documents Required

At the time of Document Verification, candidates must carry a call letter along with any one of the following original photo-ID documents: Passport, Driving Licence, PAN Card, State/Central Government or PSU Service ID Card, Bank Passbook with photograph, Registered deed/Pattas with photograph, Caste Certificate with photograph, Ex-Servicemen's pension book/pension payment order, NREGA Job Card with photograph, Voter ID (EPIC), and Aadhaar Card.

Karnataka Prisons Department Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is Karnataka Prisons Department Recruitment 2026?

Q2How many vacancies are there in Karnataka Prisons Department Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 869 total vacancies: 576 under the general cadre (560 Warder + 16 Instructor Grade-II) and 293 under the Kalyana Karnataka cadre (5 Jailor + 275 Warder + 5 Warder cum Nursing + 8 Instructor Grade-II).
