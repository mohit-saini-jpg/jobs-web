About the Jangaon District Recruitment

The notification comes from the Office of the District Collector and District Magistrate, Jangaon District, in Telangana. The posts serve the Sakhi One Stop Centre under Mission Shakti, and the District Child Protection Unit (DCPU) and Child Help Line 1098 under Mission Vastalya. The District Employment Officer (FAC), who is Member/Convenor, forwarded this revised notification for release by Lr.No. A1/OS/47/2026-27, dated 29 October 2026, after the District Collector, as Chairman of the District Outsourcing Committee, gave permission.

Jangaon District Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1Which posts can I apply for without a high school pass?
A
Multi Purpose Staff/Cook, Security Guard/Night Guard and Driver (1098) ask only that you be literate. A high school pass is preferred for all three, and the Driver also needs a Heavy Driving Licence.
Q2Which nursing qualifications are named for the paramedical posts?
A
The notice names B.Sc. Nursing, GNM and MPHW (Female), or a recognised paramedical diploma, along with a background in the health sector.
Q3Can I send my application by post?
A
Yes, registered post is allowed. The District Selection Committee is not responsible for postal delays, so a late-arriving envelope will not be excused.
Q4Where do I hand in the application in person?
A
Take it to the Inward Section on the Ground Floor of the Collectorate, Jangaon.
Q5What can I take into the exam hall?
A
Only a valid identity proof, a black or blue ball point pen and an exam pad.
Q6Is the medical test compulsory?
A
Yes. The notice makes it mandatory for every candidate appearing for the exam.
Q7Is this a permanent government job?
A
The engagement is on outsourcing basis, and continued service depends on need and satisfactory performance.
Q8Which post has the highest age limit?
A
Centre Administrator, at 50 years. Every other post stops at 44 years.
Q9Where will the merit list be published?
A
It will be displayed on the Jangaon District website, with a separate list prepared for each category.
Q10I studied privately. Which document replaces the study certificates?
A
Enclose a residency certificate from the Tahsildar / MRO concerned.
