UKSSSC Technician Grade II Eligibility Criteria

Eligibility varies slightly according to the Technician Grade II post and trade. Candidates must possess the required educational qualification, relevant vocational certificate and prescribed work experience. The required two years of experience must be relevant to the concerned trade and should be obtained after acquiring the prescribed qualification. The notification permits experience certificates from Government, Semi-Government, Limited, Private Limited, registered private companies or registered firms, as applicable. For direct recruitment to the advertised Group C posts, candidates must satisfy the eligibility conditions prescribed by the Uttarakhand Government. The notification states that applicable candidates should fulfil the required Uttarakhand employment registration, domicile/permanent residence or educational conditions prescribed for direct recruitment. UKSSSC Technician Grade II Selection Process

Selection for UKSSSC Technician Grade II Recruitment 2026 will be based primarily on an offline written competitive examination. Candidates who fail to obtain the prescribed minimum marks will not be treated as qualified in the written examination. The notification states that the examination will use an OMR answer sheet. Candidates should carefully follow all OMR instructions. Use of whitener or scratching/cutting of options is prohibited and may attract negative marking or other action as prescribed. The merit list will be prepared on the basis of marks obtained in the written competitive examination. Where two or more candidates obtain equal marks, preference will first be determined according to age, with the older candidate placed higher. Further tie-breaking will be carried out according to the rules stated in the notification.
