How to Download AP TET Hall Ticket 2026

Candidates can download the APTET Hall Ticket 2026 online by following the steps given below.

  • Visit the official APTET website: tet2dsc.apcfss.in
  • Click on the AP TET Hall Ticket 2026 link displayed on the homepage.
  • Log in using one of the following combinations: Registration Number/Application Number and Date of Birth, OR Registered Mobile Number and Date of Birth, OR Aadhaar Number and Date of Birth.
  • Enter the security code (captcha) shown on the screen.
  • Click submit to view and download the AP TET Admit Card 2026.
  • Check all details carefully and take a printout for exam day.
