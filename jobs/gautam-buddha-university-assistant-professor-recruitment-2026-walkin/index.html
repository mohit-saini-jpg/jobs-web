About GBU

Gautam Buddha University is a university in Greater Noida, Gautam Budh Nagar, Uttar Pradesh. This walk-in is for its Department of Education of Training under the School of Humanities and Social Sciences. The posts are for Academic Session 2026-27.

Posts and Eligibility

The notice offers 02 posts of Assistant Professor (On Contract for Fixed Duration) in the Department of Education of Training, School of Humanities and Social Sciences. The required specialization and qualification printed in the vacancy table is Core Botany. Candidates must hold their qualifications at the time of submitting the application. These qualifications must meet the norms prescribed by the concerned regulatory bodies, such as CoA/AICTE/UGC. The notice also says that applicants should be from a reputed Institute/University. They may have evidence of published research work and teaching experience at the University/IIT/NIT level. The University follows the reservation norms of the U.P. Government for SC, ST, OBC and PwDs. Only the approved list of SC, ST and OBC categories is applicable at Gautam Buddha University. The conditions for reserved categories are: SC/ST and OBC-NCL ca

Pay and Terms of Engagement

The monthly emolument (honorarium) for Assistant Professor (OCFD) is Rs. 57,700/- (Rs. Fifty Seven Thousand Seven Hundred only) per month for those having NET or Ph.D., as per UGC/CoA guidelines and other regulatory bodies like AICTE, at the entry level of Assistant Professor. The notice adds in brackets: "Candidates having M.Tech or equivalent in I.C.T./Engineering/PG Qualification in the relevant discipline/subject in accordance with the UGC regulations/rules of Concerned Regulatory Bodies". The honorarium finalized by the Selection Committee at the interview is final and stands for the entire duration of the engagement. A change in qualification after the interview will not change the honorarium. Selection does not give a right to the allowances and benefits admissible to regular faculty. The terms of engagement printed in the notice are: The engagement is for Academic Session 2026-27

GBU Assistant Professor (OCFD) Job Description

Perform all duties assigned by the University. Complete the entire examination process for all types of examination (Regular/Back paper/Special paper/Repeat, etc.) for the courses allocated, even if the term of contract is complete.
