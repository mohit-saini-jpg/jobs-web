About the Institute

Indian Institute of Technology Jammu is located at Jagti, NH-44, Nagrota, Jammu (Jammu and Kashmir) – 181221. This post is for a research project sanctioned by the National Technical Textile Mission. The notice is dated 09.11.2026, and Dr. Vinay Sharma of the Department of Biosciences & Bioengineering is named for correspondence.

Who Can Apply

Only Indian nationals can apply. The essential qualification is a Ph.D. in Nanotechnology/Nanomaterials/Biotechnology/Material Science or in any other relevant discipline to the project. The candidate should have a consistently good academic record. The desirable qualifications are: Prior experience with Nanomaterial synthesis, Characterization, Bacterial and Mammalian cell culture, Textile applications. An excellent publication record. The upper age limit is 35 years. A relaxation of 5 years is given to SC/ST/OBC/women/physically challenged candidates. The notice prints no date for counting age. Candidates should check the official notification for discipline-specific eligibility.

Pay and Tenure

The consolidated salary is INR 58000 + 16% HRA per month, as per NTTM and approved project norms. The post is purely contractual. The initial period is 12 months, extendable to 1 more year based on project need and satisfactory performance. The post is co-terminus with the project.

Selection and Interview

Candidates may be shortlisted based on merit and need for the project. Having the prescribed qualification alone does not ensure a call for interview. Only shortlisted candidates will be called, and they will be informed of the date and time by e-mail. The interview will be held using the online platform. Shortlisted candidates must appear on the interview date with an updated CV and original and attested photocopies of mark sheets/certificates supporting their academic qualifications.
