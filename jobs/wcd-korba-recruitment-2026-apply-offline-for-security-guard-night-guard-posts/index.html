WCD Korba Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

The selection process is based on merit prepared through an academic percentage weightage system, experience weightage, and a skill test. Academic Weightage: Maximum 60 marks awarded based on the percentage obtained in the minimum required educational qualification. Experience Weightage: Maximum 10 marks awarded, calculated at 02 marks for each additional year of experience beyond the minimum required experience. Bonus Marks: Up to 10 bonus marks awarded at 02 marks per year for work experience in the women's helpline or Sakhi One Stop Center. Skill Test: 20 marks allocated for a skill test, which includes testing knowledge of the local language and dialect. The total merit is calculated out of 80 marks, and candidates are called for skill tests or interviews in a ratio of 1:5 or 1:10 per post.
