BEML Executive Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

BEML will shortlist eligible candidates for assessment based on the applications received and the relevant eligibility criteria. The company may restrict the number of candidates called for assessment and increase the qualifying percentage depending on the number of applicants. The recruitment process includes the following applicable stages: Screening and shortlisting of eligible applications. Assessment of shortlisted candidates through the process notified by BEML. Verification of original documents and eligibility claims. Pre-employment medical examination. Final selection and appointment, subject to satisfactory verification and medical fitness.

BEML Executive Recruitment 2026 NOC Requirements

Candidates employed in Central or State Government departments, autonomous bodies, quasi-government organisations and public sector undertakings must follow the proper-channel or No Objection Certificate requirements prescribed by BEML. Alternatively, they may produce a position-specific NOC from their present employer at the assessment stage. The notification also explains circumstances in which applicants without an NOC may attend the interview, subject to furnishing a proper relieving letter at joining and forgoing certain past-service benefits or pay protection.
