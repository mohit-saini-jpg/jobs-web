GRSE Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must possess the qualifications prescribed for their selected post as on 1 October 2026 . Journeyman posts require 10th Pass and relevant National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC) or National Trade Certificate (NTC) qualifications. Driver (Vehicle) posts require a valid Commercial LMV Driving Licence and six years of relevant driving experience. Driver applicants must have a clean driving record without major accidents, police complaints related to accidents, serious traffic violations, reckless driving or driving under the influence of alcohol during the preceding six years. The notification excludes candidates holding specified higher professional qualifications, including Engineering/Technology degrees, MBA, CA, CMA, CS, LL.B., MCA, Ph.D. or equivalent higher professional qualifications. GRSE Journeyman Stipend 2026

Selected Journeymen will undergo two years of training on a fixed monthly stipend. PF contributions and applicable statutory deductions will be made according to the company's rules. Medical and hospitalization benefits are subject to the notified conditions. After successful completion of the two-year training, Journeymen may be considered for absorption into the permanent Semi-Skilled (SSK) grade, subject to performance, vacancies, medical fitness, disciplinary clearance and organizational requirements. Important: Completion of Journeyman training does not automatically guarantee permanent absorption. The notified conditions must be fulfilled.

GRSE Recruitment 2026 Exam Pattern

The written examination for both recruitment categories is an OMR-based test consisting of 100 objective questions for 100 marks , with a duration of two hours. The qualifying marks differ for Journeyman and Driver (Vehicle). The Practical Trade Test carries 50 marks and is qualifying in nature. Candidates must obtain at least 50%, or the applicable relaxed qualifying percentage, to be considered for final merit. The final merit list will be prepared using written examination marks among candidates who qualify in the required Practical Trade Test and other applicable selection stages.

GRSE Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is the organization conducting this recruitment?
A
The recruitment is conducted by Garden Reach Shipbuilders &amp; Engineers Limited (GRSE), a premier defence shipbuilding company located in Kolkata, West Bengal, for filling up various technical and driving positions.
Q2How many total vacancies are announced under GRSE Advt No 2026/05 (J&amp;OP)?
A
A total of 58 vacancies have been announced under this notification, distributed across multiple trades of Journeyman and Driver (Vehicle) positions for eligible candidates meeting the trade criteria.
Q3What is the starting and last date to apply online?
A
The online registration process starts on 30 October 2026 and candidates can submit their online applications until 28 November 2026 through the official GRSE application portal.
Q4What is the essential qualification required for Journeyman posts?
A
Candidates applying for Journeyman positions must have passed Matriculation (10th standard) along with a National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC) or National Trade Certificate (NTC) in the respective relevant trades.
Q5What is the age limit for Journeyman and Driver posts?
A
The maximum age limit is 26 years for Journeyman posts and 32 years for Driver (Vehicle) posts as on 01.10.2026, with applicable age relaxations provided for reserved categories like SC, ST, and OBC.
Q6How much is the application fee for General and OBC candidates?
A
General and OBC category candidates are required to pay an application fee of Rs. 472/- online, whereas SC, ST, PwBD, and internal candidates are fully exempted from paying any fee.
Q7What is the salary or stipend structure for Journeymen during training?
A
Journeymen will undergo a 2-year training period on a fixed stipendiary basis, receiving Rs. 24,000/- per month during the first year and Rs. 26,000/- per month during the second year.
Q8What is the selection process for GRSE Recruitment 2026?
A
The selection process comprises a Written Test, a Physical Efficiency Test for specific operator and handling roles, Document Verification, and a Practical or Trade Test to evaluate operational skills.
Q9Are candidates with higher professional qualifications eligible to apply?
A
No, candidates possessing higher professional qualifications such as Graduate or Post Graduate Degrees in Engineering, Technology, Pharmacy, MBA, CA, CMA, CS, LL.B, MCA, or Ph.D are explicitly not eligible.
Q10How can eligible candidates apply for these posts?

Eligible candidates can apply exclusively through online mode by visiting the career section of the official GRSE website at www.grse.in or via the designated online application portal before the closing date.
