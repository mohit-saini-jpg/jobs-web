GSSSB Bagayat Madadnish Vacancy 2026: Category-wise Details

The vacancy break-up for the 100 Bagayat Madadnish posts is as follows: Within the total 100 vacancies, posts are reserved for women as follows: 13 under UR, 3 under EWS, 8 under SEBC, 2 under SC, and 4 under ST (30 women-reserved posts in total). Additionally, of the total 100 vacancies, 4 posts are reserved for PwD (Divyang) candidates - 1 each for Low Vision (40–70%), Deaf/Hard of Hearing (40–70%), Locomotor Disability, and Multiple/Other Disabilities - and 10 posts (10%) are reserved for Ex-Servicemen candidates. Category abbreviations: UR = Unreserved (General), EWS = Economically Weaker Section, SEBC = Socially and Educationally Backward Class, SC = Scheduled Caste, ST = Scheduled Tribe. GSSSB Bagayat Madadnish Salary

Selected candidates will be appointed on a fixed pay of ₹26,000 per month for the first five years, as per the applicable Finance Department resolutions. On satisfactory completion of five years of service, candidates will be eligible for regular appointment in the Level-4 pay scale of the 7th Pay Commission, subject to the outcome of pending Supreme Court SLP No. 14124/2012 and SLP No. 14125/2012.

GSSSB Bagayat Madadnish Selection Process

Selection for the GSSSB Bagayat Madadnish Recruitment 2026 will be made through a single-stage competitive examination, followed by document verification: The exam is a Computer Based Response Test (CBRT) / OMR-based objective (MCQ) test, consisting of two parts - Part-A and Part-B - in a single question paper. Part-A (90 marks, 90 questions): Reasoning & Data Interpretation (30 marks), Quantitative Aptitude (30 marks), and Constitution of India, Current Affairs, and Gujarati & English Comprehension (30 marks). Part-B (120 marks, 120 questions): Subject-specific questions related to Horticulture and its practical applications, covering Fruit Science, Vegetable Science, Floriculture & Landscape Architecture, Post-Harvest Technology, Natural Resource Management, Plant Protection, Basic Science, Social Science, and current trends in these fields.
