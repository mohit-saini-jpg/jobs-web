About MHU

Maharana Pratap Horticultural University (MHU) is a horticultural university at Karnal, Haryana. Its campuses and units named in this notice are the Department of Fruit Science, the office of the Director of Research at the EEI campus (Nilokheri) and the Horticultural Research Centres (HRCs). This post serves a Department of Fruit Science scheme on climate-resilient underutilized fruit based systems for nutrition security and livelihood enhancement in Haryana. The Principal Investigator is Dr. Surender Singh, Prof. & Head, Department of Fruit Science.

Pay and Tenure

The pay is INR 42,000/- per month (Fixed), without HRA, as per University rules. The appointment is on a contract basis and is co-terminus with the project. The maximum period of engagement as a Young Professional is three years (1+1+1) or the duration of the scheme/project, whichever is earlier. The Young Professional will not be a regular employee of the university. They will not be entitled to any CPF/GPF or any other benefit given to regular employees. Leave is as per university norms. The services can be terminated, after the Young Professional is given an opportunity to respond, if their work and conduct are not found satisfactory. The services are also liable to be terminated at any time, without any reason being given, because the funding agency does not release the grant or for administrative reasons. In that case the university gives one month's notice or one month's emoluments

MHU Young Professional-II Job Description

The Young Professional-II works under the supervision of the Principal Investigator. The duties are: Assisting in field demonstrations Baseline surveys and data collection Farmer mobilization Training and capacity-building programmes Monitoring and documentation Preparation of reports Other scheme-related activities

Selection and Interview

Candidates are selected through an interview. The notice does not mention a written test.
