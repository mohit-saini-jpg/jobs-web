NTPC Executive Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

NTPC Limited has opened online applications for 6 Executive posts in the Civil, Electrical and Mechanical disciplines, 2 in each. The posts are fixed-term, and the people selected will work on the Gas Engine based power project at Great Nicobar Island (GNI). Engineers with a B.E/B.Tech and at least one year of post-qualification executive experience can apply until 6 PM on 13 November 2026. The pay is a consolidated Rs. 71,000 a month, plus accommodation or HRA and medical cover.

About NTPC

The notice describes NTPC Limited as India's largest power conglomerate, with an installed capacity of over 91 GW. It also states that NTPC plans to reach a total installed capacity of 149 GW by 2032. NTPC is hiring experienced professionals on a fixed-term basis for its Gas Engine based power project at Great Nicobar Island to support this growth.

NTPC Executive Eligibility 2026

The upper age limit is 32 years for all three posts. Age, experience and qualification are counted as on the last date for receipt of online applications, which is 13 November 2026. For all three posts, the experience may be gained in industrial, infrastructure, EPC, construction, maintenance or project environments, or in a combination of these. All qualifications must be full-time courses from universities or institutions recognised and approved in India. Only Indian nationals are eligible. Where a vacancy is identified for the category, the upper age limit is relaxed as follows: SC/ST: 5 years OBC-NCL: 3 years PwBD: 10 years Ex-Servicemen: as per Government guidelines Category certificates must be valid for the applicable year and issued by the competent authority in the latest Government of India format. Pay and Service Conditions

Pay: a consolidated amount of Rs. 71,000 per month. Benefits: company accommodation or HRA and a retention benefit. Medical facility: covers the employee, spouse, dependent parents and two children. Tenure: 1 year. It can be extended for a maximum of two (02) more years, based on organisational requirement and the candidate's performance. Posting: Great Nicobar Island (GNI). The posts are transferable to any NTPC office, station, project, JV or subsidiary at the sole discretion of the management. Health: before joining, candidates must pass a medical examination by the NTPC Medical Board or Officer. Its decision is final, and there is no relaxation in the prescribed health standards. The detailed medical norms are published on careers.ntpc.co.in.

NTPC Executive Recruitment 2026 : Application Fee Details

The fee can be paid in two ways: Online: by net banking, debit card or credit card through the online application form. Offline: at any SBI branch, using the pay-in-slip printed from the application portal. The money goes into A/C No. 30987919993 at the CAG branch, New Delhi (Code: 09996). The bank will issue a Journal Number and a Branch Code, and both must be entered in the online application. NTPC is not responsible if a fee is deposited in the wrong account. Fees are not refunded under any circumstances.

NTPC Executive Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

If required to limit the number of candidates, NTPC may hold a multi-stage selection process. This may include any of the following, or a combination of them: Application shortlisting or screening, based on qualification, percentage of marks and years of required post-qualification experience Written or computer-based test Personal interview The management may also raise the minimum eligibility standards. Meeting the minimum criteria does not give a candidate the right to be shortlisted or called for a test or interview. Canvassing in any form will disqualify the candidate.

NTPC Executive Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in NTPC Advt. No. 15/26?
A
There are 6 posts: 2 Executive (Civil), 2 Executive (Electrical) and 2 Executive (Mechanical). All 6 are in the UR category.
Q2What is the salary of an NTPC Executive at Great Nicobar Island?
A
A consolidated Rs. 71,000 per month. The post also comes with company accommodation or HRA, a retention benefit, and medical cover for the employee, spouse, dependent parents and two children.
Q3How much experience do I need?
A
At least 01 year of post-qualification executive experience in your discipline. This can be in industrial, infrastructure, EPC, construction, maintenance or project environments.
Q4Is this a permanent job?
A
No. The engagement is on a fixed-term basis for 1 year. It can be extended by up to two (02) more years, depending on organisational requirement and performance.
Q5Can I be transferred from Great Nicobar Island?
A
Yes. The initial posting is at GNI, but the posts are transferable to any NTPC office, station, project, JV or subsidiary at the management's discretion.
Q6Do women candidates have to pay the fee?
A
No. Female candidates are exempt, as are SC, ST, PwBD and XSM candidates. General, EWS and OBC candidates pay Rs. 500.
Q7What age relaxation do OBC and PwBD candidates get?
A
OBC-NCL candidates get 3 years and PwBD candidates get 10 years above the 32-year limit, where a vacancy is identified for their category.
Q8Do I need to send a hard copy of the application?
A
No. NTPC states that no document is to be sent by post. Download and keep the application slip with your unique application number.
Q9Which experience certificate format should I upload?
A
The experience or service certificate must be in NTPC's prescribed sample format. Upload it together with your offer letter, joining order, 2 pay slips for every year and Form-16 for each organisation.
Q10Is a medical test required before joining?
A
Yes. Candidates are examined by the NTPC Medical Board or Officer before joining, and no relaxation in health standards is allowed.
