How to Apply / Complete JNU Counselling 2026

  • Visit the official JNU admission/counselling portal, jnuee.jnu.ac.in.
  • Check your name and rank in the applicable merit list (UG 1st list, UG 2nd list, or PG merit lists as per your programme).
  • Complete pre-enrolment registration within the specified window.
  • Pay the applicable academic/semester fee to block your allotted seat.
  • Report for physical verification of documents and admission/registration on the scheduled dates.
  • If your seat is not confirmed in the earlier rounds, watch for the last call for expression of willingness against vacant seats.

Important Instructions for JNU Counselling 2026

  • Carry all original documents along with photocopies for verification; incomplete documentation can lead to disqualification.
  • Complete fee payment strictly within the deadline specified for each merit list round, as late payment can result in loss of the allotted seat.
  • Candidates without a valid category certificate will automatically be considered under the general category.
  • Keep checking the official JNU counselling portal regularly for updates on vacant seats and further rounds.
