NPSC Stenographer Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must fulfil the following eligibility conditions to apply for all Item Nos. 1 to 12 under NPSC Stenographer Recruitment 2026. NPSC Stenographer Age Limit

Age is reckoned as on 01.01.2026. Minimum Age: 21 years Maximum Age: 32 years Age Relaxation: SC/ST candidates: Upper age limit relaxable by 5 years Serving Government employees: Age concession equal to the number of years in service, up to a maximum of 5 years

NPSC Stenographer Reservation Policy

100% of the vacancies are reserved for candidates belonging to (a) Any Naga Tribe, (b) Kuki, (c) Kachari, (d) Garo, and (e) Mikir, provided such persons are Indigenous Inhabitants of the State of Nagaland. Implementation of reservation for Backward Tribes in the state is in accordance with Notification No. RCBT-5/87(pt-II) dated 14 April 2011, 15 June 2012 & 23 March 2022. Reservation for Persons with Benchmark Disability, wherever applicable as sanctioned by the user department, is in accordance with Government Notification No. AR-3/Gen-9/97 dated 13 August 2020, No. AR-8/NSSB-9/2022/57 dated 5 May 2022, and No. AR-3/Gen-9/97(Vol-II) dated 8 December 2025. For availing reservation under Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (fresh vacancies), Form C, Form D and Form E are mandated as per Notification No. AR-3/GEN-9/97 dated 13 August 2020.
