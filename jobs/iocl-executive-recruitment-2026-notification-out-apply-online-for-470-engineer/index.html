IOCL Executive Recruitment Selection Process

Selection comprises three stages: Computer Based Test (CBT), Group Discussion (GD) & Group Task (GT), and Personal Interview (PI). Each question carries 1 mark; 0.25 marks deducted for every incorrect answer. Section order is fixed - Section A first, then Section B. CBT will be held at 83 test centers across India (city list available in the official notification and syllabus link below). Minimum composite score required: General/EWS/OBC(NCL) – 45%, SC/ST – 40%, PwBD – 30%. GD/GT and PI each require a minimum 40% pass mark.

Important Conditions

Candidates having their graduation/diploma in combined, integrated, or interdisciplinary subjects (e.g., Mechatronics, Robotics) are not eligible for the Graduate Engineer or Diploma Engineer posts. No change of category (Gen/EWS/SC/ST/OBC(NCL)/PwBD) is permitted once submitted in the online application. Selected candidates will be placed anywhere in India, including overseas offices, at Corporation discretion. Final selection is provisional and subject to subsequent verification of certificates, testimonials, and medical fitness. Candidates must clear Pre-Employment Medical Examination (PEME) guidelines before joining. Any use of unfair means, impersonation, or misconduct during the process will lead to disqualification and may result in debarment from future IOCL examinations. IOCL Executive Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is the name of the organization conducting Indian Oil Recruitment 2026?
A
Indian Oil Corporation Limited (IOCL) is conducting the Indian Oil Recruitment 2026 for various Engineers/Officers, Law Officers, Assistant Quality Control Officers and Junior Engineers/Junior Officers posts.
Q2How many posts are available in IOCL Executive Recruitment 2026?
A
A total of 470 Executive vacancies are notified across five posts - Graduate Engineer, Officer (Marketing), Law Officer, Assistant Quality Control Officer, and Diploma Engineer — comprising 411 posts in Grade A, 9 in Grade A0, and 50 in Grade E0.
Q3What is the last date to apply for IOCL Executive Recruitment 2026?
A
Online applications close on 3 September 2026 at 5:00 PM.
Q4What is the age limit for IOCL Executive posts?
A
The maximum age varies by post — 26 years for Graduate Engineer and Diploma Engineer, 28 years for Officer (Marketing), and 30 years for Law Officer and Assistant Quality Control Officer, as on 1 July 2026, with applicable relaxation.
Q5Is work experience required for IOCL Executive posts?
A
Yes, for Law Officer (minimum 2 years post-qualification legal experience) and Assistant Quality Control Officer (minimum 2 years relevant lab/testing experience). No experience is required for Graduate Engineer, Officer (Marketing), or Diploma Engineer.
Q6What is the application fee?
A
₹1,000 + GST for General/EWS/OBC(NCL) candidates. SC/ST/PwBD candidates are exempt.
Q7What is the selection process?
A
Computer Based Test (CBT), followed by Group Discussion (GD), Group Task (GT), and Personal Interview (PI), with CBT carrying 85% weightage in the composite score.
Q8What is the pay scale for IOCL Executive posts?
A
Basic pay ranges from ₹30,000 (Grade E0) to ₹50,000 (Grade A), with approximate annual CTC between ₹11.1 lakhs and ₹18.4 lakhs depending on the post.
Q9When is the IOCL CBT exam scheduled?
A
The Computer Based Test is tentatively scheduled for 24 September 2026, with admit cards issued from 15 September 2026.
Q10Is there a service bond for selected candidates?

Yes, selected candidates must serve a minimum bond period of 3 years, with bond amounts ranging from ₹35,000 to ₹3,00,000 depending on grade and category.
