Pay and Tenure

Annual CTC is negotiable and will depend upon experience, current emoluments, and place of posting. The variable pay structure, depending on the performance score of the officer, is as follows: 99% to 100%: 100% of variable pay 97% to 98.99%: 90% of variable pay 94% to 96.99%: 80% of variable pay 90% to 93.99%: 70% of variable pay Below 90%: Nil Tenure and Contract Terms: Engagement is on a Fixed Term Employment basis for 3 years, which may be renewed for a further period of 2 years at the discretion of the Bank at mutually agreed terms and conditions (total engagement period not to exceed 5 years). The contract can be terminated by giving 3 months' notice from either side or payment/surrender of 3 months' compensation amount in lieu thereof.

Selection and Interview

The selection process comprises three stages: Shortlisting, Interview, and CTC Negotiation. Shortlisting: Fulfilling minimum eligibility criteria does not guarantee a call for interview. The shortlisting committee constituted by the Bank will decide shortlisting parameters to call an adequate number of candidates. Interview (100 Marks): The interview carries 100 marks. For the posts of GM & Chief AI officer, DGM (Risk and Operational Analytics), DGM (AI Governance and Compliance), and DGM (Data Engineering, Model Application Dev and AI Ops), shortlisted candidates must deliver a 10-minute presentation on their relevant job profile, projects undertaken, or milestones achieved. Qualifying marks in the interview are decided by the Bank. Intimation or call letters for the interview will be uploaded on the Bank's website or sent by email; no hard copy will be sent. SBI Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Candidates must submit their applications online through the official careers portal of State Bank of India. No hard copy of the application or documents needs to be sent by post.
  • 2Visit the official SBI careers portal and navigate to the current openings page.
  • 3Locate the link for Specialist Cadre Officers recruitment under advertisement CRPD/SCO/2026-27/30.
  • 4Register online by entering basic details to generate a provisional registration number and password. The facility for editing saved application details is available up to three times only before final submission.
  • 5Fill in the online application form carefully with educational, professional, and personal details.
  • 6Upload scanned copies of photograph, signature, biodata, resume, ID proof, age proof, educational certificates, experience certificates, salary slips/Form-16, and other required documents in the prescribed format and size.
  • 7Pay the non-refundable application fee of Rs. 750/- (exempted for SC/ST/PwBD) online using Internet Banking, Debit Card, Credit Card, or UPI.
  • 8Submit the completed application form and print a copy of the system-generated application form and e-receipt for future reference.
  • 9Document Upload Specifications:
  • 10Photograph: Recent passport style colour picture (20 kb to 50 kb, JPG/JPEG format, preferred dimensions 200 x 230 pixels, light-coloured or white background).
  • 11Signature: Signed on white paper with Black Ink pen (10 kb to 20 kb, JPG/JPEG format, preferred dimensions 140 x 60 pixels; signatures in capital letters are not accepted).
  • 12Other Documents: Biodata, Resume, ID proof, Proof of Date of Birth, Educational Certificates, Experience Certificates, Caste/EWS/PwBD certificate (if applicable), Form-16/Offer Letter/Latest Salary Slip, and CTC negotiation sheet must be uploaded in PDF format on A4 size, with file size not exceeding 500 kb each.

SBI Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are released under SBI Recruitment 2026?
A
A total of 6 vacancies have been announced for Specialist Cadre Officers on a Fixed Term Employment basis, with 1 post for each of the 6 advertised roles.
Q2What is the last date to apply for SBI SCO Recruitment 2026?
A
The last date for online registration of application and payment of fees is 9 November 2026.
Q3What is the educational qualification required?
A
Candidates for GM and DGM posts require B.E./B.Tech/M.Tech in CS/IT/Electronics/ECE/EEE or equivalent, or M.Sc/MA/M.Stat/MCA. Architect posts require a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, IT, Data Science, AI, Business Administration, or related disciplines.
Q4What is the age limit for these posts?
A
As on 30.09.2026, the age limit is 40 to 55 years for GM & Chief AI Officer, 35 to 50 years for DGM posts, and 30 to 45 years for AI Solution Architect and Enterprise AI Architect posts.
Q5What is the salary offered for SBI Specialist Cadre Officers?
A
The annual CTC upper range is Rs. 160.00 Lacs for GM & Chief AI Officer, Rs. 120.00 Lacs for DGM posts, and Rs. 90.00 Lacs for AI Architect posts, bifurcated into 90% fixed pay and 10% variable pay.
Q6What is the application fee?
A
The application fee and intimation charge is Rs. 750/- for General, EWS, and OBC candidates, while SC, ST, and PwBD candidates are exempted from fee payment.
Q7How to apply for SBI SCO Recruitment 2026?
A
Candidates must apply online through the SBI official careers portal, complete the registration form, upload the required scanned documents and photographs, and pay the fee by 9 November 2026.
Q8What is the selection process?
A
Selection consists of shortlisting based on qualification and experience, followed by a 100-mark interview (including a 10-minute presentation for GM and DGM posts), and CTC negotiation.
Q9Is this a permanent job with State Bank of India?
A
No, the engagement is on a Fixed Term Employment contract for an initial period of 3 years, renewable for up to 2 additional years at the discretion of the Bank, not exceeding 5 years in total.
Q10Can a candidate apply for more than one post?
A
Candidates are not allowed to apply for more than one post; if a candidate applies for multiple posts, only the last valid completed application will be retained and previous fees stand forfeited.
