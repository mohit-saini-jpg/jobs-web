ESIC Hospital Kollam Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Employees' State Insurance Corporation hospital at Ezhukone, Kollam will hold a walk-in interview on 20 November 2026. Registration is from 9.00 AM to 10.00 AM and the interview starts from 10.00 AM at the Administrative Block, ESIC Hospital, Ezhukone. It is for contractual engagement of Specialists (Full time/Part time) and Senior Residents (1 Year), 16 vacancies in all, with approximate pay of Rs.145059 per month for the Full time Specialist and for the Senior Resident (PG Degree) posts (Advt. About ESIC Ezhukone

ESIC Hospital, Ezhukone is a hospital of the Employees' State Insurance Corporation in Kollam. The notice is issued by its Medical Superintendent. The engagement is on contract and does not create a regular post: Full time Specialists may continue only until a regular incumbent joins.

Posts and Eligibility

For ICU, a Specialist needs a PG degree or PG Diploma in Anaesthesia/General Medicine/Pulmonology/Critical care, registered with the Medical Council. For ICU, a Senior Resident needs a PG Degree or PG Diploma in Anesthesiology/General Medicine/Pulmonary Medicine/Emergency Medicine/Critical care medicine, registered with the Medical Council. All additional post graduate qualifications must also be registered with the Medical Council. Eligibility for the walk-in, covering educational qualifications, medical council registration, experience certificate and community certificate, is decided as on the date of interview strictly on the documents the candidate submits. * Backlog vacancy for SC. For both posts, if no candidate is available in the advertised category, the post may be filled for 44 days with a UR candidate. ESIC Ezhukone Recruitment 2026 Job Description

Full Time Specialist: working hours 09.00 AM to 04.00 PM on week days and 09.00 AM to 01.00 PM on Saturdays. Should attend emergency calls and visit as and when required. Part Time Specialist: works a 4-hour session per day, four days a week (16 hours). The Specialist is responsible for all cases under treatment under his/her supervision in his/her discipline. To work as per ESIC norms for Senior Residency. Professionally responsible for all the cases attended, and to attend floor duties as required.

What to Bring

Bring the following in original along with one self-attested copy of each: Matriculation Certificate in support of Date of Birth. MBBS degree certificate in original. Post Graduate Certificate (Degree/Diploma) in original, the qualification prescribed for the post. TCMC/Kerala Dental Council/MCI Registration Certificate, for basic and additional qualification. Experience Certificate in original. Caste Certificate and non-creamy layer certificate, if applicable. Undertaking for Senior Residents, attached to the application form. The notice also attaches an application form (name, date of birth, post applied for, department, qualification details, TCMC registration number, experience, category, mobile number, email ID, Aadhar number and PAN number, with a recent passport size photograph signed across). Its enclosure list matches the documents above. SC/ST/OBC/EWS certificates must be valid

What Happens on the Day

Candidates register between 9.00 AM and 10.00 AM, and the walk-in interview starts from 10.00 AM. PWD candidates will be considered as per rules and suitability to job. If selected, the candidate enters into an agreement with the Medical Superintendent, ESIC Hospital, Ezhukone, on Non-judicial stamp paper of Rs.200. After joining, a security deposit of Rs. 30,000 is submitted as a Demand draft in favour of 'ESIC Fund Account No.1' payable at SBI, Keralapuram. It is refundable after successful completion of the contract and production of a 'No dues certificate'.

ESIC Hospital Kollam Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1When is the ESIC Ezhukone walk-in interview 2026?
A
The walk-in interview is on 20 November 2026, from 10.00 AM. Registration is from 9.00 AM to 10.00 AM.
Q2Where is the ESIC Ezhukone walk-in interview held?
A
It is held at the Administrative Block, ESIC Hospital, Ezhukone.
Q3How many vacancies are there in ESIC Ezhukone Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 16 vacancies: 6 for Specialist (Full time) and 9+1* (10) for Senior Resident (1 Year), where the extra one is a backlog vacancy for SC.
Q4What is the qualification for the Specialist post?
A
MBBS degree with TCMC registration and Post Graduate Degree/DNB, or Diploma in the concerned speciality. Experience needed is 3 years for degree holders or 5 years for diploma holders.
Q5What is the qualification for the Senior Resident post?
A
Post Graduate Degree/DNB, or Diploma in the concerned specialty after MBBS with TCMC registration. For ICU, a PG Degree or PG Diploma in the listed subjects is required.
Q6What is the age limit for Specialist and Senior Resident?
A
Specialists must not exceed 69 years as on the date of interview, and tenure beyond 70 years is not allowed. Senior Residents must not exceed 45 years as on the date of interview, with relaxation for reserved candidates as per rules.
Q7What is the salary for ESIC Ezhukone Specialist and Senior Resident?
A
Full Time Specialist gets about Rs.145059 per month and Part Time Specialist Rs. 60,000 per month. Senior Resident gets approximately Rs.145059 (Level 11, 7th CPC) for PG Degree holders, with Rs.1350 deducted for Diploma-only holders.
Q8Which documents should I carry to the interview?
A
Carry originals with one self-attested copy of each: matriculation certificate, MBBS certificate, PG certificate, registration certificate, experience certificate and caste/non-creamy layer certificate if applicable. Senior Residents also need the Undertaking.
Q9Is the ESIC Ezhukone job permanent?
A
No, it is a contractual engagement. Full Time Specialists get one year, extendable up to three years on satisfactory performance or till a regular incumbent joins; Part Time Specialists and Senior Residents get one year.
Q10Is a security deposit required?
A
Yes, for selected candidates. A Rs. 30,000 Demand draft in favour of 'ESIC Fund Account No.1' payable at SBI, Keralapuram is due after joining, and a Rs.200 non-judicial stamp paper agreement is needed.
