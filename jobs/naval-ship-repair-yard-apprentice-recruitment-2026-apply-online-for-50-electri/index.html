NSRY Apprentice Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Nationality and Character: Only Indian nationals can apply. Candidates should not have been convicted of an offence, be under trial, or be facing chargesheet proceedings under Indian law or the rules of educational institutions. Since NSRY is a defence establishment, applications with questionable or suspicious antecedents will be summarily rejected without intimation, and if subsequent police verification does not provide a clean certificate, a selected candidature will be cancelled. Educational Qualification: Minimum educational qualification is Matriculation followed by ITI passed in the respective trade under NCVT or SCVT. NSRY Apprentice Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Shortlisting for Interview: Preliminary selection is made on the basis of a merit list drawn up trade- and category-wise, based on the aggregate percentage of marks obtained in the ITI trade and in Std X (10th). Shortlisted candidates are called for an interview at the Apprentice Training School, NSRY, Sri Vijaya Puram, carrying 50 marks, which tests technical skills in the respective trade. Call letters are issued to eligible candidates via their registered email ID after scrutiny of documents. Final Merit List: Prepared by considering interview marks. In case of a tie with the same marks, the candidate's date of birth is compared (the elder candidate is placed higher); further ties are resolved by the alphabetical order of the first name, then middle name, then surname, as per the Std X certificate.
