Deoghar Home Guard Vacancy 2026 Details

The vacancies below are described in the notification as provisional in nature and may be revised (increased, decreased, or modified) without prior notice. 50% of posts in both Rural and Urban Home Guard categories are earmarked for women. Deoghar Home Guard Eligibility Criteria

Rural Home Guard: The candidate must be a permanent resident of the same block in Deoghar district from which they are applying, supported by a self-attested residence certificate issued by the competent authority. Urban Home Guard: The candidate must be an ordinary resident of the urban area of Deoghar district, supported by a self-attested residence certificate issued by the competent authority.

Deoghar Home Guard Age Limit

Candidates must be between 19 and 40 years of age as on 01.01.2026 for both Rural and Urban Home Guard posts. The notification does not specify any separate upper age relaxation for reserved categories. To avoid any confusion regarding age eligibility, candidates can use our Age Calculator to calculate their exact age based on the prescribed cut-off date before applying.

Deoghar Home Guard Qualification

Candidates must attach self-attested certificates supporting their claimed educational qualification. Candidates seeking relaxation in physical standards must attach a caste certificate issued by the competent authority. Candidates applying for the technically skilled Urban Home Guard vacancies must be skilled in a technical field and either employed in a related office or self-employed with relevant technical knowledge. The notification lists the following as generally recognised technical categories: doctor, engineer, advocate, lecturer/professor, nurse, computer operator, typist, licensed driver (HMV/LMV), cook, carpenter, cobbler, washerman, barber, electrician, gardener, plumber, and similar trades. A technical proficiency certificate issued by the competent authority is required.

Deoghar Home Guard Application Fee

The application fee is Rs. 100 (one hundred rupees only) , payable through the online payment gateway on the official website at the time of submitting the application. The application is considered final only after the fee is successfully paid.

Deoghar Home Guard Salary / Allowance

As stated explicitly in the notification, this is a voluntary enrolment and not a salaried government post. There is no fixed monthly salary. Enrolled Home Guards receive a duty allowance at rates fixed by the government, paid only for the number of days they are actually deployed on duty as required.

Deoghar Home Guard Selection Process

Selection is conducted as per the Home Department, Government of Jharkhand notification No. 5442 dated 20.10.2014, through the following stages: Physical Efficiency Test (PET): Comprising a 1-mile race, high jump, long jump, and shot put, with marks awarded on a sliding scale for each event. Separate scoring standards apply for (i) Rural and Non-Technical Urban candidates, and (ii) Technically skilled Urban candidates, with separate benchmarks for male and female candidates in each. Hindi Writing Ability Test: Qualifying in nature, for candidates who clear the PET. Rural candidates are tested at a 7th-class level and Urban candidates at a 10th-class level. Full marks: 100; qualifying marks: 30. Technical Skill Test: Applicable only to technically skilled Urban Home Guard candidates who clear the PET and Hindi Writing Test. Qualifying in nature. Full marks: 100; qualifying marks: 30. Meri
