Who Can Check This Result

This result is applicable only to students of the following category:

  • UG students (B.A./B.Sc./BCA) of Cotton University
  • Students of the 2021 and 2022 batches with pending arrear or repeat papers in the subjects listed above
  • Students who appeared for the Second, Fourth, or Sixth Semester arrear/repeat examinations conducted in 2026

How to Check Cotton University UG Result 2026 Online

Follow these steps to check and download your Second, Fourth, or Sixth Semester arrear/repeat result:

  • Visit the official Cotton University results portal at results.cottonuniversity.ac.in.
  • Under the "Under Graduate (B.A./B.Sc./BCA/Diploma) Results" section, locate the result matching your semester and batch, dated 12/08/2026.
  • Click on the relevant result link. This will redirect to the linked result-hosting page (apply.cottonuniversity.ac.in).
  • Enter your required login/roll details as prompted on that page to view your result.
  • Check your marks or status carefully and download or save the result for reference.
  • Note that this online result is provisional; keep an eye out for the original mark sheet, which is issued separately by the university.

Important Instructions

  • The Computer Centre has stated it is not responsible for any inadvertent error in the results published online.
  • Results published on the internet are meant for immediate information only.
  • These online results cannot be treated as original mark sheets.
  • Original mark sheets will be issued by the university separately.
  • Students should cross-verify their result with the university's academic/examination branch in case of any discrepancy.
