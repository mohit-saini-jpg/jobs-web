GBPUAT Associate Professor Qualification

Ph.D. in the concerned, allied or relevant discipline. Master's degree with at least 55% marks or an equivalent grade. At least eight years of teaching or research experience in a position equivalent to Assistant Professor. At least seven publications in peer-reviewed, NAAS-rated or UGC-listed journals. A minimum research score of 75 under the prescribed assessment criteria. Recognized basic veterinary qualification included in Schedule I or II of the Indian Veterinary Council Act, 1984. Registration with a State Veterinary Council or Veterinary Council of India. Ph.D. and Master's degree in the relevant discipline with at least 55% marks. At least eight years of eligible teaching or research experience. At least seven prescribed journal publications and a research score of 75. Ph.D. in the concerned, allied or relevant discipline. GBPUAT Assistant Professor Qualification

Master's degree with at least 55% marks in the concerned, relevant or allied subject. NET conducted by UGC, ICAR or CSIR, or an equivalent accredited examination such as SLET or SET. Candidates holding an eligible Ph.D. may receive exemption from NET, SLET or SET under the applicable UGC regulations. B.V.Sc. and A.H. degree from a college recognized by the Veterinary Council of India. Registration with the State or Central Veterinary Council. Relevant Master's degree with at least 55% marks. NET, SLET or SET qualification, or an eligible Ph.D. exemption under the applicable rules. Candidates must possess BE, BTech or BS and ME, MTech or MS, or an Integrated MTech in the relevant branch, with first class or equivalent in at least one of the degrees. The detailed list of acceptable concerned, allied and relevant disciplines is provided in Annexure V of the official notification.

Qualification Relaxation

A relaxation of 5% at Bachelor's and Master's levels is available to eligible SC, ST, OBC Non-Creamy Layer and differently-abled candidates. The relaxation is based only on qualifying marks and does not include grace marks. Eligible Ph.D. holders who obtained their Master's degree before 19 September 1991 may receive relaxation from 55% to 50%.

The fee must be paid through a Demand Draft drawn in favour of Comptroller, G.B. Pant University of Agriculture and Technology, Pantnagar . The Demand Draft must be payable at any one of the following branches: State Bank of India, Branch Code 1133 Punjab National Bank, Branch Code 4446 UCO Bank, Branch Code 0678 The application fee is non-refundable. Reservation Conditions

The advertised posts are backlog vacancies reserved for SC, ST and OBC candidates. Uttarakhand reservation benefits are available only to permanent residents of Uttarakhand. Horizontal reservation applies to eligible women, persons with disabilities, dependants of freedom fighters, ex-army personnel and orphans under Uttarakhand Government rules. Reserved-category candidates from other states will be considered under the unreserved category. Applicants claiming reservation must attach the applicable caste and Uttarakhand domicile certificates.

GBPUAT Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

The university may use one or more of the following stages: Screening of applications Academic or research score assessment Written or qualifying examination Presentation Interview The scrutiny committee will assess the candidate's academic and research score. A merit list will be prepared using the verified academic and research score. Candidates may be shortlisted at ten times the number of candidates for the first vacancy and five times for every additional vacancy. Shortlisted candidates will attend an interview carrying a maximum of 20 marks. Final selection will be based on the marks obtained in the interview. The academic score will be used for shortlisting candidates for interview. Final selection will be based on interview performance. The university may also conduct a written examination, qualifying examination or presentation when required.

GBPUAT Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

The application must reach the university by 21 October 2026 at 5:00 PM. The university will not be responsible for postal delays. Qualifications and experience will be calculated as of the application closing date. Incomplete applications or applications received after the deadline will not be considered. Original certificates must be produced during the interview. In-service candidates should apply through the proper channel. No TA or DA will be paid for attending a test or interview. Merely possessing the minimum qualification does not guarantee an interview call. Selected candidates may be posted at any university campus, research centre or Krishi Vigyan Kendra. Applicants must submit separate forms and fees when applying for multiple posts.

  • 10Sign every page of the application, annexures and supporting documents.
  • 11Place the completed application and enclosures in an envelope.
  • 12Write the post name, serial number, discipline and college name on the envelope.
  • 13Send the application through Speed Post or Registered Post so that it reaches the university by 21 October 2026 at 5:00 PM.
  • 14Applications submitted through email, fax or any other electronic mode will not be accepted.
