IIT Delhi Recruitment 2026 Job Description

The notice gives one set of working requirements for all project fellows: Visit the NCR-based Thermal Power Plants regularly to collect samples, analyse data and carry out chemical study of the foam from the FGD units. Work in close coordination with IITD and NTPC's TPPs in the NCR for data collection and analysis. Modelling and optimisation can be done through CFD simulation. Project fellows will be encouraged to register for the PhD programme under the Institute rules. A fellow admitted to the PhD programme will work on a PhD topic related to the project only, and will give time to it after completing the course work. The Government of India has mandated 7% mixing of biomass in thermal power plants for co-firing.
