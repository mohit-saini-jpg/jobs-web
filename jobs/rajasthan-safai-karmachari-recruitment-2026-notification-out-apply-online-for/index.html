Rajasthan Safai Karmchari Age Limit 2026

As per the Rajasthan Contractual Hiring to Civil Posts Rules, 2022, an applicant must have attained a minimum age of 21 years and must not have exceeded 40 years, as calculated on 1 January 2027 (the first 1 January following the last date of application). These age relaxations are non-cumulative - a candidate can avail the benefit of only the single relaxation most favourable to them, not a combination of more than one provision.

Rajasthan Safai Karmchari Educational Qualification / Eligibility

The applicant must be a bona fide resident (मूल निवासी) of Rajasthan. No specific educational qualification is prescribed for the Safai Karmchari post. The applicant must have at least 1 year of experience of sanitation (safai) work in any urban local body of the state. This experience can be continuous for 1 year, or accumulated across separate periods totalling 1 year. The 1-year experience certificate must be issued by the concerned urban local body, in the prescribed proforma, and must be issued after 1 August 2026 and before the last date of application (28 September 2026) to be considered valid. Candidates must be of good mental and physical health, free of any defect that would hinder efficient discharge of duties; a health certificate from the Chief Medical & Health Officer or a medical jurist of their district of residence is required if selected, and police verification will be

Rajasthan Safai Karmchari Selection Process 2026

Selection is made through a lottery system (developed by the Rajasthan Department of Information Technology & Communication) among eligible candidates, category-wise, according to the number of advertised posts in each urban local body, in the following priority order: Priority 1: Candidates with 1 year of sanitation experience in the same urban local body they are applying to. Priority 2: If posts remain unfilled after Priority 1, candidates with 1 year of sanitation experience in another urban local body within the same district are included in the lottery. Priority 3: If posts still remain unfilled, candidates with 1 year of sanitation experience in any urban local body anywhere in the state are included in the lottery.

Rajasthan Safai Karmachari Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Visit the Local Self Government Department portal, https://lsg.urban.rajasthan.gov.in/, or the SSO Rajasthan portal, https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in.
  • 2Log in to the SSO portal and select the Recruitment Portal under Citizen Apps (G2C), then click Apply Now.
  • 3If not already registered, complete One Time Registration (OTR) by selecting your category (Unreserved/Reserved: EWS/OBC-NC/MBC-NC/SC/ST/SAH), disability status if applicable, and home state, and pay the applicable OTR fee.
  • 4Once OTR is complete, apply through the SSO portal for the recruitment; information entered during OTR (name, father's name, date of birth, gender) cannot be changed at this stage.
  • 5Fill in all required details in the online application form, upload the required documents, capture a live photograph, and upload your scanned signature as per the specified format.
  • 6Select your preferred urban local body (only one ULB can be selected) and confirm all entered details before final submission.
  • 7Submit the application and note the Application ID generated after submission - if no Application ID is received, the application has not been successfully submitted (a Preview is not considered a submission).
  • 8Retain a printout of the submitted application form for future reference and document verification.
