Rajasthan Safai Karmachari Recruitment 2026 - Eligibility Criteria

The applicant must be a domicile of Rajasthan. Nationality: Indian citizen, or a subject of Nepal or Bhutan, or a Tibetan refugee who came to India before 1 January 1962 with the intention of permanently settling, or a person of Indian origin who has migrated from Pakistan, Burma, Sri Lanka, East African countries (Kenya, Uganda, and the United Republic of Tanzania comprising former Tanganyika and Zanzibar), Zambia, Malawi, Zaire, or Ethiopia with the intention of permanently settling in India. For applicants under categories (b) to (e) above, a certificate of eligibility issued by the Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India, must be submitted.

Rajasthan Safai Karmachari Bharti 2026 - Educational Qualification

No specific educational qualification has been prescribed for the post of Sanvida Safai Karmachari. Candidates only need to fulfil the experience criteria mentioned below.

Rajasthan Safai Karmachari Recruitment 2026 - Experience Requirement

Candidates must have at least 1 year of experience in sanitation work in any urban local body of Rajasthan to be eligible. This 1-year experience can be either continuous or accumulated in separate spells that together total 1 year. The experience certificate must be issued in the prescribed format by: The Chief Municipal Officer (or an officer authorised by them), if the candidate was directly employed by the urban local body; or The contractor/authorised agency (in case of contract-based deployment), duly counter-signed by the concerned senior manager/chief manager of solid waste at the ULB, and further countersigned by the Chief Municipal Officer (for Municipal Councils/Boards) or the Deputy Commissioner (Personnel)/Commissioner-authorised officer not below the rank of Deputy Commissioner (for Municipal Corporations). As on 1 January 2027, the candidate must have completed 21 years of age and must not have exceeded 40 years of age, as per the provisions of the Rajasthan Contractual Hiring to Civil Posts Rules, 2022. Rajasthan Safai Karmachari Recruitment 2026 - Reservation Details

Reservation for Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, Other Backward Classes, More Backward Classes, and Economically Weaker Sections is applicable as per prevailing Rajasthan Government norms (vertical reservation). Women: 30% horizontal reservation, category-wise. Persons with Disabilities (Divyangjan): 4% horizontal reservation. Ex-servicemen: 15% horizontal reservation. Outstanding sportspersons: 2% horizontal reservation. Reservation for candidates from the Sahariya primitive tribe of Baran district is applicable as per government orders, subject to clear declaration in the online application form. Candidates from states other than Rajasthan belonging to SC/ST/OBC/MBC/EWS categories will be treated as General category candidates.

Rajasthan Safai Karmachari Recruitment 2026 - Selection Process

There is no written examination for this recruitment. Selection to the post of Sanvida Safai Karmachari will be made purely through a lottery system, conducted using online software developed by the Department of Information Technology and Communication, Government of Rajasthan, category-wise as per the number of vacancies advertised for each ULB. The lottery process will follow this order of priority: First, candidates possessing 1 year of sanitation work experience in the same ULB (for which they applied) will be included in the lottery. If posts remain vacant after step 1, candidates with 1 year of sanitation work experience in any other ULB of the same district will be included. If posts still remain vacant after steps 1 and 2, candidates with 1 year of sanitation work experience in any ULB across the state will be included in the lottery.
