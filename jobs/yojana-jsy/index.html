जननी सुरक्षा Yojana (JSY) Overview

JSY ek central government scheme hai jo garib pregnant women ko cash assistance deti hai. Ye scheme especially low‑income families ko safe delivery aur health services tak pahunch banane ke liye design ki gayi hai.

जननी सुरक्षा Yojana (JSY) Expert Analysis

Scheme ka objective maternal mortality kam karna aur institutional deliveries badhana hai. Cash incentive se families ko hospital birth choose karne ka motivation milta hai, jo health outcomes improve karta hai.

Who Is Eligible: जननी सुरक्षा Yojana (JSY)

Eligibility hai 19‑45 saal ki pregnant women jo BPL, APL ya SC/ST category ki ho, aur jinhone apna first live birth ya subsequent birth plan kiya ho.

जननी सुरक्षा Yojana (JSY) Application Process

1. Aadhaar, ration card aur pregnancy certificate ready rakho. 2. Local ASHA ya ANM se registration karvao. 3. Application form ko accurately fill karo aur required documents attach karo.

जननी सुरक्षा Yojana (JSY) Benefits Details

Maa ko cash incentive milta hai – Rs 5,000 for first live birth and Rs 3,000 for subsequent births. Ye amount delivery expenses, nutrition aur post‑natal care me help karta hai.

How जननी सुरक्षा Yojana (JSY) Works

ASHA workers pregnant women ko identify karte hain, registration verify karte hain, aur cash disbursement bank transfer ya Jan Dhan account ke through hoti hai within 30 days of delivery.

जननी सुरक्षा Yojana (JSY) Application Tips

Application time pe submit karo, sabhi documents original aur photocopy dono attach karo, aur ASHA se follow‑up rakho taaki verification delay na ho. Common rejection ka reason incomplete paperwork hota hai.

जननी सुरक्षा Yojana (JSY) FAQs

Q1JSY ka cash benefit kab milta hai?
A
Delivery ke baad, usually 30 din ke andar bank transfer ya Jan Dhan account me paisa credit ho jata hai.
Q2Kya private hospital delivery par bhi benefit milta hai?
A
Haan, agar hospital government approved hai aur delivery certificate valid hai to cash incentive milta hai.
Q3Agar pregnancy loss ho jaye to kya benefit milta hai?
A
Loss case me cash incentive nahi milta, lekin scheme ke under medical care aur counseling free milti hai.
