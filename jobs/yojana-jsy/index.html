जननी सुरक्षा Yojana (JSY) Overview

JSY ek central government scheme hai jo pregnant women ko financial support deti hai. Ye scheme especially low‑income families ke liye design ki gayi hai, taaki safe delivery aur post‑natal care ensure ho sake.

जननी सुरक्षा Yojana (JSY) Expert Analysis

Scheme ka maksad maternal mortality ko kam karna aur institutional deliveries ko badhava dena hai. Cash incentive timely milne se mothers ko health services use karne mein asani hoti hai.

Who Is Eligible: जननी सुरक्षा Yojana (JSY)

Eligibility hai pregnant women aged 19‑45, belonging to BPL, SC/ST, OBC ya any other priority category, jo government health facility mein delivery plan kar rahi ho.

जननी सुरक्षा Yojana (JSY) Application Process

1. Aapke local health centre se JSY registration form lein. 2. Aadhaar, ration card, pregnancy certificate aur bank account details ready rakhein. 3. Form ko fully fill kar ke health worker ko submit kar dein.

जननी सुरक्षा Yojana (JSY) Benefits Details

Cash incentive 5,000 ₹ for first pregnancy aur 7,000 ₹ for subsequent pregnancies hoti hai, jo delivery ke baad ya post‑natal check‑up ke baad directly bank account mein transfer hoti hai.

How जननी सुरक्षा Yojana (JSY) Works

Health worker ya ASHA worker aapke ghar jaake registration verify karta hai, pregnancy confirm karta hai, aur delivery ke baad payment process initiate karta hai. Disbursement usually 30‑45 din ke andar hoti hai.

जननी सुरक्षा Yojana (JSY) Application Tips

Form ko accurately fill karein, sab documents original aur updated ho. Aadhaar aur bank details mismatch na ho, aur delivery ke liye government hospital choose karein taaki payment release ho sake.

जननी सुरक्षा Yojana (JSY) FAQs

Q1JSY ka cash benefit kab milta hai?
A
Cash benefit delivery ke baad ya post‑natal check‑up ke baad, jab health worker verification complete kar leta hai, tab bank transfer ke through milta hai.
Q2Kya private hospital mein delivery karne par bhi JSY milta hai?
A
Haan, lekin aapko private hospital ka bill submit karna hota hai aur scheme ke guidelines follow karne hote hain, tabhi payment release hoti hai.
Q3Agar pregnancy certificate lost ho jaye to kya karein?
A
Aap apne nearest PHC ya sub‑centre se duplicate pregnancy certificate issue karwa sakte hain, uske baad registration process continue karein.
