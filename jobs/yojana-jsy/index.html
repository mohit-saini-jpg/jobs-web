जननी सुरक्षा Yojana (JSY) Overview

Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY) is a government scheme launched to promote institutional delivery among poor pregnant women. Under JSY, pregnant women receive financial assistance for safe delivery in a hospital or a government health facility.

जननी सुरक्षा Yojana (JSY) Expert Analysis

JSY is a crucial initiative to reduce maternal and infant mortality rates in India. It has been successful in increasing institutional deliveries, especially among rural and disadvantaged populations.

Who Is Eligible — जननी सुरक्षा Yojana (JSY)

All pregnant women, especially those from Below Poverty Line (BPL) families, are eligible for JSY benefits. They can apply for the scheme through their local ASHA worker or Anganwadi centre.

जननी सुरक्षा Yojana (JSY) Application Process

To apply for JSY, pregnant women need to register themselves with the ASHA worker or Anganwadi centre. They should also ensure they have all necessary documents, including their Aadhaar card and ration card.

जननी सुरक्षा Yojana (JSY) Benefits Details

Under JSY, pregnant women receive a cash benefit of ₹1,400 for institutional delivery. This amount is credited directly into their bank account, helping them cover delivery expenses and other medical costs.

How जननी सुरक्षा Yojana (JSY) Works

JSY works through a network of ASHA workers, Anganwadi centres, and government health facilities. Pregnant women are identified and registered for the scheme, and they receive financial assistance for safe delivery.

जननी सुरक्षा Yojana (JSY) Application Tips

To ensure successful application under JSY, pregnant women should register themselves early and ensure they have all necessary documents. They should also choose a government health facility for delivery to receive the cash benefit.
