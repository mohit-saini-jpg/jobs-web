Kodagu District Court Selection Process

Selection is based on the percentage of marks scored in the qualifying examination, marks scored in a computer typewriting skill test (typing a given 15-minute passage), and interview marks. The qualifying test carries a maximum of 100 marks with 50 as the minimum to pass, and the interview carries a maximum of 5 marks. Selection is through a driving test and an interview, conducted per the Karnataka Subordinate Courts Rules; shortlisted candidates attend at their own expense. Candidates are shortlisted for interview in a 1:25 ratio per notified post/category, based on percentage of marks in the qualifying examination. The interview carries a maximum of 10 marks, and final selection is based on interview marks.   • 7Submit the application, then download and retain a copy of the submitted application and fee receipt until the recruitment process is complete; produce it at the time of the skill test/interview.
  • 8Candidates should apply well before the last date, without waiting until the deadline.

Kodagu District Court Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is Kodagu District Court Recruitment 2026?
A
It is a direct recruitment drive by the Principal District and Sessions Court, Kodagu-Madikeri, for 39 posts of Typist, Driver, Process Server, and Peon/Attender, released across four separate notifications dated 28-08-2026.
Q2How many vacancies are there in Kodagu District Court Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 39 vacancies in total: 8 Typist (including 1 backlog post), 2 Driver, 6 Process Server, and 23 Peon/Attender posts.
Q3What is the last date to apply online for Kodagu District Court Recruitment 2026?
A
The last date to submit the online application and pay the fee is 03-10-2026, up to 11:59 PM.
Q4What is the qualification required for Kodagu District Court Typist posts?
A
Candidates need a pass in 2nd PUC or an equivalent qualification, along with a pass in the Senior Grade Typewriting examination in Kannada and English, or an equivalent secretarial qualification.
Q5What is the qualification for Driver, Process Server, and Peon posts?
A
All three require a pass in SSLC or an equivalent qualification. The Driver post additionally requires a current Heavy Vehicle Driving Licence, and a driving licence is preferred for Process Server applicants.
Q6What is the age limit for Kodagu District Court Recruitment 2026?
A
The minimum age is 18 years. The maximum age is 40 years for General candidates, 43 years for Categories 2A/2B/3A/3B, and 45 years for SC/ST/Category-1 candidates, as on the last date for applying, with further relaxations available in specific cases.
Q7What is the application fee for Kodagu District Court Recruitment 2026?
A
The fee is ₹300 for General candidates and ₹150 for Categories 2A/2B/3A/3B, payable only online via the SBI e-Pay portal. SC, ST, Category-1, and PWD candidates are exempted from the fee.
Q8How can candidates apply for Kodagu District Court Recruitment 2026?
A
Candidates must apply online only, through https://kodagu.dcourts.gov.in/online-recruitment/, by the last date. No offline or other mode of application is accepted.
Q9What is the selection process for Kodagu District Court Recruitment 2026?
A
Selection is based on qualifying-exam marks combined with a computer typing skill test for Typist posts, a driving test for Driver posts, and a shortlisting ratio followed by an interview for Process Server and Peon posts.
Q10What is the salary for these posts?
A
Typist and Driver posts carry the pay scale of ₹34,100–67,600; Process Server carries ₹31,775–61,300; and Peon/Attender carries ₹27,000–46,675, plus applicable allowances.
