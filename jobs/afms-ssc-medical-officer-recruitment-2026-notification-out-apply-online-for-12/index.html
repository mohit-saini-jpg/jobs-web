AFMS Medical Officer Medical Examination 2026

Interview-qualified candidates must undergo a medical examination by the Special Medical Examination Board (SMB) constituted by the Directorate General Armed Forces Medical Services. Candidates declared medically unfit may seek an Appeal Medical Board according to the prescribed procedure. A Review Medical Board may be permitted at the discretion of DGAFMS. Final medical fitness is established only after approval of the medical examination documents.

AFMS SSC Medical Officer Tenure 2026

The initial Short Service Commission tenure for fresh civilian entrants is five years . Extensions depend on willingness to continue, availability of vacancies, eligibility and applicable service policies. Extension is not automatic. Eligible SSC officers may receive opportunities to appear for the Departmental Permanent Commission examination under the applicable rules. The notification provides up to three chances after completing two years of service and before completing nine and a half years, subject to the prescribed eligibility conditions and attempt restrictions. Important: Permanent Commission is not guaranteed by selection into the SSC Medical Officer recruitment.
