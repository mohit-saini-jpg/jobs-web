PAN CARD CORRECTION FAQs

Q1PAN card correction ka process kitna time leta hai?
A
Online submission ke baad, generally 7‑10 din me correction ho jati hai, lekin peak period me thoda delay ho sakta hai.
Q2Kya offline correction bhi possible hai?
A
Haan, aap nearest PAN service centre ya TIN‑FACILITATOR se offline form submit kar sakte hain, lekin online method faster hota hai.
Q3Correction fee kitni hoti hai?
A
Current fee INR 110 (online) ya INR 120 (offline) hai, payment net banking, credit/debit card ya UPI se ki ja sakti hai.
