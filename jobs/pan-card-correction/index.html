PAN CARD CORRECTION FAQs

Q1PAN correction ke liye kitna fee lagta hai?
A
Online correction ke liye Rs. 110 ka fee hota hai, jo credit/debit card ya net banking se pay kiya ja sakta hai.
Q2Correction ke baad PAN card kitne din me milta hai?
A
Online application ke baad 15-20 din me updated PAN card post ya email ke through mil jata hai.
Q3Kya offline correction bhi possible hai?
A
Haan, aap nearest PAN service centre ya bank branch me form submit karke offline correction kar sakte hain, lekin online process faster hota hai.
