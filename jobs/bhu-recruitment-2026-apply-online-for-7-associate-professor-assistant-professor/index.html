About BHU

Banaras Hindu University is in Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh. Its letterhead describes it as an institution of national importance established by an Act of Parliament. These posts are at the university's Centre for Vedic Science, also called the Vedic Vigyan Kendra. The Government of Uttar Pradesh sanctioned the posts.

Application Fee

The fee is Rs. 1000/-. It is non-refundable and is paid online by Internet Banking, Debit Card or Credit Card. The notice's header charges the fee to UR and OBC candidates for Teaching and Group 'A' posts. SC, ST and PwBD candidates pay nothing. The "Points to Note" section also exempts women and transgender candidates. If you applied under Advt. No. 09/2025–2026 dated 08.04.2026, you may apply afresh or update your form without paying the fee again.

BHU Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection follows UGC norms for both teaching and non-teaching posts at the Vedic Vigyan Kendra. For Assistant Professor, the academic score in Appendix II (Table 3A for Universities, Table 3B for Colleges) of the UGC Regulations 2018 is used only to shortlist candidates for interview. The final selection depends only on interview performance. Meeting the eligibility rules does not guarantee an interview call. BHU may use stricter criteria to shortlist, including a written test. For Group A posts, preference goes to candidates with higher qualifications and merit, or with higher marks in the written test. For posts with a specialisation, shortlisting may be done only from candidates who have that specialisation.

Other Conditions

Canvassing in any form disqualifies a candidate. BHU will not reply to interim correspondence. BHU will check documents and antecedents at appointment or during service. Fake documents or hidden antecedents lead to termination. The university may withdraw any advertised post at any time without giving a reason. It may also correct mistakes in the selection process even after an appointment letter is issued. The teaching posts at the Centre for Vedic Science that were advertised on deputation under Advt. No. 06/2024–2025 dated 16.12.2024 are cancelled. Those applicants can claim a refund by emailing the Deputy Registrar (RAC) at [email protected] . BHU Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Register on the Recruitment & Assessment Cell portal on the BHU website. The application runs on the Samarth/CU-Chayan portal, and the Vedic Vigyan Kendra has its own separate online form.
  • 2Keep these ready to upload: a passport-size photograph in JPEG format of no more than 50 kb, and a scan of your signature of no more than 50 KB.
  • 3Fill every mandatory field. Click "Save and Proceed" after each tab. Incomplete applications are rejected. If you are logged out, log in again with the details BHU emailed to you.
  • 4State your specialisation for posts that have one.
  • 5Submit a separate application for each position. A reserved-category candidate who also wants to be considered for unreserved posts must fill separate forms for the reserved and unreserved posts.
  • 6Pay the fee online by 12 November 2026. You cannot change anything after final submission.
  • 7Download the PDF of the application. Print and sign it, and have it forwarded by your employer if you are employed.
  • 8Send the printout with all enclosures to the Office of the Registrar, Recruitment & Assessment Cell, Holkar House, B.H.U., Varanasi-221005 (U.P.). It must reach by 17 November 2026.
  • 9Documents to send or keep ready:

BHU Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many Assistant Professor posts are there in BHU Advt. No. 09/2025-26?
A
There are 3, one each in Vedic Studies (VVK02), Vyakaran (VVK03) and Vedic Mathematics & Khagol Vigyan (VVK04). The Vyakaran post is reserved for OBC.
Q2Is the Associate Professor post open to reserved categories?
A
Both Associate Professor (Vedic Studies) posts are listed under UR (unreserved).
Q3What is the pay for the Associate Professor post?
A
It is in PB-4 (Rs. 37400-67000) with an Academic Grade Pay of Rs. 9000/-.
Q4Do I need NET for the Assistant Professor posts?
A
You need NET or SLET/SET, unless you hold a Ph.D. under the UGC Regulations 2009 or 2016, or a Ph.D. from a foreign university ranked in the top 500 by QS, THE or ARWU.
Q5What is the age limit for the Computer Programmer post?
A
The age limit is 45 years. Eligibility is judged as on 12.11.2026.
Q6How much experience does a Computer Programmer need?
A
You need five years of programming experience with a B.E./B.Tech., or three years with an M.E./M.Tech., M.Sc. Computer Science or MCA. Both routes need at least 55% marks.
Q7Where do I send the printed application form?
A
Send it to the Office of the Registrar, Recruitment & Assessment Cell, Holkar House, B.H.U., Varanasi-221005 (U.P.). It must arrive by 17 November 2026, 5:00 p.m.
Q8I applied under the earlier Advt. 09/2025–2026 dated 08.04.2026. Do I pay again?
A
No. You can apply afresh or update your earlier form without paying the application fee again.
Q9How is the Assistant Professor selection made?
A
Candidates are shortlisted using the academic score in Appendix II (Table 3A/3B) of the UGC Regulations 2018. Selection is then based only on the interview.
Q10What size should the photo and signature be?
A
The photograph must be in JPEG format and no more than 50 kb. The signature scan must not exceed 50 KB.
