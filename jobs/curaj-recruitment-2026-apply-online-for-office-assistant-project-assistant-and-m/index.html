CURAJ Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

CURAJ Incubation Foundation, Central University of Rajasthan, is hiring a Project Coordinator (₹75,000 per month, fixed), a Project Assistant (₹25,000 per month, fixed) and an Office Assistant (₹15,000 per month, fixed) for its BIRAC-supported E-YUVA Center. The posts are contractual for one year at first and are co-terminus with the project. Eligible Indian nationals can apply online through a separate Google Form for each post from 26 October 2026 until 15 November 2026 (11:59 PM).

About CURAJ

Central University of Rajasthan (CURAJ) is a central university in Rajasthan. Its CURAJ Incubation Foundation runs the E-YUVA Center, which is supported by BIRAC. According to the notice, the center's work covers innovation and entrepreneurship in the biotechnology/bio-pharma domain: it mentors fellows and startups, holds technical workshops and supports product-oriented research.

Vacancy Details

The notice lists three posts without printing the number of vacancies for each one, so the total is counted as 3, one for each post. Pay and Tenure

All three salaries are fixed monthly amounts. The Project Coordinator (₹75,000) and Project Assistant (₹25,000) salaries come "with scope of 5% annual increment". The notice prints no such line for the Office Assistant (₹15,000). Selected candidates are not entitled to any other benefits that are admissible to the regular employees of the Central University of Rajasthan. Appointments are purely temporary and contractual for one year, extendable up to three years (co-terminus with the project) based on performance. No claim for regularization will be entertained. The engagement gives no right to claim regular appointment in the University or continued services in the University. When the engagement period in the offer ends, the candidate is not required to report for duty unless the University grants an extension. CURAJ Recruitment 2026 Job Description

Acts as the overall coordinator of the E-YUVA Center and reports directly to the Chief Coordinator / Principal Project Investigator (PI). Responsibilities include: Administrative, scientific and financial support Applicant mobilization, branding and outreach, revenue generation Mentoring fellows (UG to Post-Doctoral level) Organizing technical workshops and supporting product-oriented research Mentoring startups; assisting entrepreneurs in proposals, pitches, business plans and fund-raising Executing innovation and entrepreneurship activities in the biotechnology/bio-pharma domain Any other responsibility assigned by the Chief Coordinator/PI from time to time Provides scientific and administrative support to the E-YUVA Center and reports to the Project Coordinator/Chief Coordinator/PI. Duties include: Laboratory maintenance and monitoring instruments Coordination with fellows Procurement

Selection and Interview

The University reserves the right to conduct a Written Test, Skill Test, Presentation and/or Interview, as deemed necessary. The interview date will be announced later, and the schedule of interview will be published separately on the university website. No TA/DA will be paid for attending the interview. If any dispute or ambiguity arises in the selection process, the decision of the University will be final.
