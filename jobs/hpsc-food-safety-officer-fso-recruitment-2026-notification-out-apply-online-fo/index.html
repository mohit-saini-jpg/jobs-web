HPSC Food Safety Officer Selection Process

Generally, the Commission follows a three-stage recruitment process: Screening Test, followed by a Subject Knowledge Test, and Interview . However, the Commission may also decide to hold the Subject Knowledge Test and Interview directly, without a Screening Test. If the number of applications is large, the Commission may adopt any one or more of the following methods for shortlisting candidates for the next stage: Percentage of marks in the minimum prescribed educational qualification Percentage of marks in different educational qualifications, with weightage decided by the Commission Desirable qualifications, where more than one is prescribed Higher educational qualifications than the minimum/essential qualification prescribed Higher experience in the relevant field than the minimum prescribed Counting of experience before or after acquiring the minimum/essential qualification A Screeni

HPSC Food Safety Officer FSO Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Visit the official HPSC website, hpsc.gov.in, and register online using a mobile number.
  • 2After successful registration, a URN (Unique Registration Number) will be created.
  • 3Log in using the login ID and apply against the advertisement as per your qualification.
  • 4Fill in all particulars carefully, including name, parentage, date of birth, category, qualification, marks obtained, and passing year.
  • 5Provide your Aadhaar Number or Virtual ID (VID), which is required for Aadhaar authentication during biometric attendance.
  • 6Upload the required scanned documents and pay the application fee online.
  • 7After payment, take a printout of the application form, check it carefully for errors, sign it, and upload the signed copy.
  • 8Keep a printout of the final signed and submitted application form, as it must be produced along with supporting documents when called upon by the Commission.
  • 9No other means or mode of application submission will be accepted. Applications submitted without an uploaded signed copy of the application form will be considered incomplete, and such candidature will be automatically rejected.
