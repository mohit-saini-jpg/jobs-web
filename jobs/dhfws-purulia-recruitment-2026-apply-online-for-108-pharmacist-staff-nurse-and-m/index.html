About CMOH Purulia

The Office of the Chief Medical Officer of Health (CMOH), Purulia works under the Department of Health & Family Welfare, Government of West Bengal, from the Zilla Swasthya Bhawan Campus, Ranchi Road, Purulia. The CMOH is also the Secretary of the District Health and Family Welfare Samiti, Purulia, and this recruitment is for contractual posts under the National Health Mission for that Samiti. The notice is signed by Dr. Asok Biswas, CMOH & Secretary, District Health and Family Welfare Samiti, Purulia.

Qualification and Experience

Every post requires the candidate to be a permanent resident of West Bengal and able to read, write and speak Bengali. The notice states that the prescribed conditions are non-relaxable. Essential: Permanent resident of West Bengal. Able to read, write and speak Bengali. BHMS/BAMS/BUMS degree from an institution recognised by the respective council. Completed compulsory internship. Registration Certificate of the Respective Council should be obtained. BHMS qualification must be included in the Second Schedule of the Homoeopathy Central Council Act, 1973. Candidates must be registered either in Part-A of the State Register of Homoeopathic Medical Practitioners maintained by the Council of Homoeopathic Medicine, West Bengal or in Part I of the Central Register maintained by Central Council of Homoeopathy. Pay and Terms

The engagement to these posts is purely contractual and temporary in nature. The notice does not mention the length of the contract or any allowances on top of the consolidated remuneration. Selected candidates may be posted at any health facility/institution/unit under the administrative control of the District AYUSH Samity/District Health & Family Welfare Samiti, as decided by the competent authority. No claim for posting at a particular place will be entertained. No TA/DA is admissible for appearing in the Computer Test, Written Test, Interview, Document Verification or any other stage of the recruitment process. The District Health & Family Welfare Samiti reserves the right to cancel the recruitment process at any stage without assigning any reason.
