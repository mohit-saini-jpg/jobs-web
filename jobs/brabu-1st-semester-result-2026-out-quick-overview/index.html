Who Can Check This Result?

This result applies to students who:

  • Are enrolled in the BA, BSc, or BCom undergraduate CBCS programme under BRABU
  • Belong to the 2025-29 academic session
  • Appeared in the Semester-I examination held in January 2026

How to Check BRABU 1st Semester Result 2026 Online

Follow these steps to check your BRABU UG Semester-I result:

  • Visit the official BRABU examination portal at result.brabu.ac.in/ug_result.php
  • Click on the link titled "UG(CBCS) Semester-I Session 2025-29 Provisional Result."
  • You will be redirected to the dedicated result page.
  • Select your course, examination session, semester, and college from the dropdown menus.
  • Enter your valid University Roll Number or Registration Number.
  • Complete the captcha, if shown, and click the Search/Submit button.
  • Your provisional result will appear on the screen.
  • Download and save the result, then take a printout for future reference.

Details Required to Check the Result

  • Course (BA / BSc / BCom)
  • Examination session (2025-29)
  • Semester (Semester-I)
  • College name
  • University Roll Number or Registration Number

What Details Appear on the BRABU Provisional Marksheet

Once your result is generated, the provisional marksheet typically displays the following information. Individual formatting may vary and should be verified against your actual downloaded copy:

  • Student's name and father's/mother's name
  • University Roll Number and Registration Number
  • College name and course details
  • Subject-wise marks (internal and external, where applicable)
  • Total marks obtained and total marks out of
  • SGPA/CGPA, where applicable under the CBCS pattern
  • Overall result status (Pass/Fail/Reappear)

Important Instructions for Students

  • The result available online is provisional. It is meant for immediate reference and is not a substitute for the original marksheet issued by the university or college.
  • Keep multiple copies (digital and printed) of your provisional result safe, as it may be required for admission to the next semester, scholarship applications, or verification purposes.
  • If your result does not appear, recheck the course, session, semester, and college selected, confirm your roll number is correct, and try again after some time, as the server can be slow due to heavy traffic.
  • If your result shows as pending, it may be due to internal marks, practical marks, or data updates not yet reflected. In such cases, wait for a university or college notice rather than assuming an error.
  • According to publicly available information, BRABU generally does not offer a general revaluation option; students who are dissatisfied with their marks can apply for scrutiny of their answer sheets once the university issues an official notice with the process, fee, and last date. This is not officially confirmed for the current result and should be verified on the official website.

Steps After Checking Your Result

  • Download and verify your provisional marksheet for any discrepancies in name, roll number, or subjects.
  • Report any errors to your college examination department as soon as possible.
  • Keep the marksheet ready for Semester-II admission/form fill-up formalities.
  • Watch for an official university notice regarding scrutiny or correction, if applicable.
