BHEL Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Bharat Heavy Electricals Limited (A Govt. of India Undertaking) Solar Business Division, Bengaluru, has invited applications for 3 Project Engineer (FTA Gr II) and 3 Project Supervisor (FTA Gr III) posts in Solar PV erection and commissioning. The posts are on fixed tenure for an initial 16 months or till completion of the assignment, whichever is earlier. The pay is an all-inclusive consolidated Rs. 84,000 per month in the first year for Project Engineers and Rs. 45,000 per month in the first year for Project Supervisors. Candidates need a full-time degree (Engineers) or diploma (Supervisors)

About the Company

BHEL describes itself as a Public Sector Undertaking incorporated in 1964, serving sectors including power, transportation, defence, aerospace, oil and gas, and process industries. These posts are for BHEL's Solar Business Division, Bengaluru, and the selected candidates report to the Project Director/Site-In-charge at the project site.

Vacancy Details

BHEL has notified 6 vacancies in total: 3 Project Engineer and 3 Project Supervisor posts. The notice calls the number tentative and says the actual requirement may vary based on further assessment. Reservation for Persons with Disability and Ex-servicemen will be as per Government directives. Vacancies reserved for OBC are meant only for Non-Creamy Layer candidates. Where reservation points are not available, candidates may apply subject to meeting the general eligibility standards and will be considered under UR.

Pay and Tenure

Selected candidates are paid an all-inclusive monthly consolidated remuneration. Medical benefit: reimbursement of the premium paid for a Mediclaim Policy up to Rs. 5 lakhs for self, spouse and dependent children. Selected candidates are covered under BHEL's Group Personal Accident Policy. The posts are purely temporary, offered on fixed tenure for an initial 16 months or completion of the assignment, whichever is earlier. They are not against any permanent vacancy and give no entitlement to regular or permanent employment in BHEL.

Selection and Interview

All eligible applications are screened against the eligibility conditions, and candidates who fulfil them are called for a Personal Interview. If more eligible applications are received, candidates are shortlisted in the ratio of 1:10 to the number of vacancies in each category, in order of merit. Merit is based on marks in the qualifying examination (Degree/Diploma as applicable). Shortlisted candidates are informed individually by e-mail, at the address given in the application form. The interview date and venue are not mentioned in the notice. BHEL verifies eligibility against original documents after the candidate is called for interview, and the candidature stays provisional at all stages. BHEL Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in BHEL Recruitment 2026?

Q2What is the last date to apply for BHEL FTA 01/2026?
A
Online submission closes on 16 December 2026. The printed application with documents must reach BHEL by 23 December 2026 (16.30 hrs), or by 28 December 2026 from far flung areas.
Q3What is the qualification for BHEL Project Engineer and Project Supervisor?
A
Project Engineers need a full-time degree in Engineering/Technology in Electrical, Civil or Mechanical. Project Supervisors need a full-time diploma in Electrical or Civil. Both need minimum 60% (50% for SC/ST) and 2 years' experience.
Q4What is the age limit for BHEL Recruitment 2026?
A
The upper age limit is 32 years as on 01.11.2026. It is relaxable by 5 years for SC/ST and 3 years for OBC (Non-Creamy Layer), with further relaxations for PwD and certain J&K domiciles.
Q5What is the salary for BHEL Project Engineer and Project Supervisor?
A
Project Engineers get Rs. 84,000 per month in the first year and Rs. 88,000 in the second year. Project Supervisors get Rs. 45,000 and Rs. 48,000 respectively, all-inclusive and consolidated.
Q6What is the application fee for BHEL FTA 01/2026?
A
The non-refundable fee is Rs. 236/- for UR and OBC (NCL) candidates. SC/ST/PwD/Ex-Servicemen are exempted. It is paid online through NEFT/UPI.
Q7How do I apply for BHEL Recruitment 2026?
A
Apply online at careers.bhel.in, print the application with its acknowledgement No., and post it with documents to Addl GM/ HR, BHEL-SBD, Prof CNR Rao Circle, IISc Post, Bengaluru – 560012, Karnataka.
Q8How are candidates selected?
A
Eligible applications are screened and candidates are called for a Personal Interview. If applications are many, they are shortlisted 1:10 in order of merit, based on Degree/Diploma marks.
Q9Is this a permanent job?

No. The posts are temporary, on fixed tenure for an initial 16 months or completion of the assignment, whichever is earlier, and give no right to regular employment in BHEL.
Q10Where will the BHEL project staff be posted?
