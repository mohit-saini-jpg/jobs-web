Kerala University Time Table 2026 - Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 27/08/2026

The University of Kerala has published exam time tables for two separate examinations to be held in September 2026. Both notifications were issued by the Controller of Examinations, University Buildings, Thiruvananthapuram. The two schedules are for different programmes and different student batches - they are covered together here because both were released around the same time under the "Kerala University Time Table 2026" update.

Who This Time Table Applies To (Eligibility / Applicable Batches)

  • BA Honours English: Students of the 2022 and 2023 admission batches appearing for the Fifth Semester supplementary examination.
  • B.Tech: Students of the 2020, 2021, and 2022 admission batches (2020 Scheme) appearing for the Eighth Semester supplementary examination at University College of Engineering, Kariavattom.

Specific eligibility conditions (such as minimum attendance, arrear clearance rules, or fee-payment cut-off dates) are not available in the notifications provided and should be confirmed on the official Kerala University examination portal.

How to Check/Download the Kerala University Time Table 2026

  • Visit the official Kerala University examination login page: exams.keralauniversity.ac.in/Login/check3.
  • Alternatively, go to the main university website at keralauniversity.ac.in and navigate to the examinations/notifications section.
  • Locate the relevant notification - Fifth Semester BA Honours English (ES X/2026) or Eighth Semester B.Tech Supplementary (2020 Scheme).
  • Cross-check your course code and exam date against the tables above.

The exact step-by-step download/login procedure on the portal is not described in the source notifications; the steps above are based only on the links provided in the notification.

Important Instructions

  • Reporting time, hall ticket requirements, and conduct-of-exam instructions are not specified in either notification as provided - candidates should refer to the official portal for these details.
  • Note the different reporting timings for the BA Honours English exam: 1:30 PM-4:30 PM on all days except Friday, and 2:00 PM-5:00 PM on Friday (11.09.2026).
  • B.Tech candidates should note all four exam sessions are scheduled for 09:30 AM-12:30 PM.
  • Both notifications are signed by the Controller of Examinations, University Buildings, Thiruvananthapuram.
