SSSC Punjab Clerk Eligibility Criteria

The applicant should possess a Bachelor of Arts or Bachelor of Science degree, or an equivalent qualification, from a recognised University. The applicant should also have passed the Matriculation examination with Punjabi as one of the subjects, and must have proficiency in the operation of computers. The applicant must fulfil the qualification condition as on the closing date of the Online Application Form. No person who has entered into or contracted a marriage with a person having a spouse living, or who, having a spouse living, has entered into or contracted a marriage with any other person, shall be eligible for appointment - unless the Hon'ble High Court is satisfied that such a marriage is permissible under the personal law applicable to such a person and exempts them from this rule. SSSC Punjab Clerk Selection Process

Selection involves an online written examination, followed by a qualifying Computer Proficiency Test, and finally checking of original testimonials/interaction, with the final merit list based solely on written exam marks. Duration of the exam is 2 hours. Qualifying marks are 33% in each subject, and a candidate must additionally secure at least 40% marks in aggregate in the Written Examination to be considered to have qualified. There is negative marking of 1/4 mark for every wrong answer in the objective (MCQ) portion; unattempted questions receive no credit or discredit. The subjective English Composition part is checked only for candidates who secure 33% or more marks in General Knowledge.
