About AIIMS Nagpur

AIIMS Nagpur was established under the Pradhan Mantri Swasthya Suraksha Yojana (PMSSY) with the objective of improving regional imbalances in the availability of affordable and reliable tertiary healthcare services, and to augment facilities for quality medical education in the country. The Institute has been functioning since the 2018-19 session and is envisioned to develop into an institute with the highest standards of teaching, training and research. Through this AIIMS Nagpur Senior Resident Recruitment 2026 notification, the Executive Director invites applications from Indian Nationals for the post of Senior Resident as per the Residency Scheme of the Government of India.

AIIMS Nagpur Senior Resident Recruitment 2026 Salary

Selected Senior Residents will be placed at Level 11, Cell No. 01, as per the 7th Central Pay Commission (7th CPC) , which corresponds to a pay of Rs. 67,700/- per month, plus usual allowances including Non-Practising Allowance (NPA), if applicable. As per the terms and conditions, appointees are not entitled to Provident Fund, Pension, Gratuity, Medical Attendance Treatment, Seniority, Promotion, or other benefits admissible to regularly appointed Government servants.

AIIMS Nagpur Senior Resident Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection is made purely on the basis of an interview conducted at AIIMS Nagpur. Key points on the selection process, as given in the notification: The date of interview will be notified separately on the AIIMS Nagpur website. If a large number of applications are received, shortlisting for the interview will be done based on qualification, additional qualification, experience, etc., or through a written test. The interview may spill over to the next day; candidates should be prepared to be available on the following day if necessary. The decision of the Executive Director, AIIMS Nagpur, on selection is final and binding, and no representation will be entertained. The offer of appointment, when made, is provisional and subject to verification of educational and personal credentials by the competent authority.
