RIMCEE 2026 - Quick Overview : Job Overview

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has issued a public notice dated 25 November 2026 for candidates appearing in the Rashtriya Indian Military College Entrance Examination (RIMCEE) 2026. The notice sets out the exam schedule, the entry window, break timings and the rules for entering and leaving the examination centre.

RIMCEE 2026 - Quick Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 26/11/2026

The exam is being conducted by NTA on behalf of the Ministry of Defence and is scheduled for Sunday, 6 December 2026. This year, the exam will be held in three consecutive shifts on a single day, with a lunch break and a short break in between. Candidates must also arrive within a fixed entry window, as no one will be allowed in after 9:30 AM.

The RIMC entrance exam will be held on 6 December 2026. The complete day schedule given in the NTA notice is as follows.

Duration of Each Paper

Based on the timings in the notice, the time slots work out as follows. The notice does not separately state the paper duration, so these are the scheduled slots.

  • English: 10:00 AM to 12:00 Noon (2 hours)
  • Mathematics: 01:00 PM to 02:30 PM (1 hour 30 minutes)
  • General Knowledge: 03:00 PM to 04:00 PM (1 hour)

RIMCEE 2026 Reporting Time and Entry Rules

Candidates can enter the examination centre between 8:00 AM and 9:30 AM. Entry closes strictly at 9:30 AM, and the notice states that no candidate will be allowed to enter after that time under any circumstances.

  • Follow the reporting time and all other instructions printed on your Admit Card.
  • Entry to the examination centre closes at 9:30 AM sharp.
  • Once you enter the premises and complete the prescribed security checks, you cannot leave the centre until the entire examination is over.
  • Candidates and their parents or guardians should make travel and exam-day arrangements in advance.

RIMCEE 2026 Lunch Break Rules

The gap between Shift 1 (English) and Shift 2 (Mathematics) is a 45-minute lunch break, from 12:00 Noon to 12:45 PM. The notice lays out the following rules for this break.

  • Candidates will have their meals at a designated location within the examination centre, as directed by the Centre Superintendent.
  • Candidates will not be permitted to leave the examination centre during the examination schedule.
  • Candidates must return to their allotted examination seats by 12:45 PM positively.

Can Candidates Bring Food to the RIMC Entrance Exam?

Yes. The notice advises candidates to bring their own dry lunch or food items, such as sandwiches, parathas and fruits, packed in a tiffin box. The tiffin box must clearly show the candidate's Name and Application Number. Tiffin boxes will be kept at a designated place in the examination room, as directed by the Invigilator.

Short Break Between Mathematics and General Knowledge

There is a 15-minute interval from 2:30 PM to 2:45 PM between the Mathematics and General Knowledge papers. Candidates may use the washroom facilities during this time, but they must be back in their allotted seats before the next paper begins.

RIMCEE 2026 Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Read the NTA notice and your Admit Card carefully before exam day.
  • Reach the centre within the 8:00 AM to 9:30 AM entry window.
  • Carry only dry food items in a tiffin box labelled with your Name and Application Number.
  • Do not plan to step out of the centre between papers. Leaving is not permitted once you have entered and cleared security.
  • Be in your seat by 12:45 PM after lunch and before the next paper starts after the short break.
  • Keep checking the official NTA websites for updates.
