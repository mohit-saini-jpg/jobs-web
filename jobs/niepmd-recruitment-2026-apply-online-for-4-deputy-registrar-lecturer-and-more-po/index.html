About NIEPMD

NIEPMD (D) is a national institute under the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (Divyangjan), Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment, Government of India. It is located at Muttukadu, East Coast Road, Kovalam (P.O), Chennai - 603 112, Tamil Nadu.

Application Fee

The fee can only be paid online. The Institute does not accept any other mode of payment. If an application arrives late, it is not considered and the fee is forfeited.

NIEPMD Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

A duly constituted screening committee shortlists the applications. It then decides the final list of candidates called for the interview or written test. The management may call only the candidates it judges to rank high on the eligibility criteria. Holding the essential and desirable qualifications and the experience does not by itself give a right to be considered. The management may limit the number of candidates called for interview, depending on the response for each post. The management may decide not to fill a post for administrative reasons. It may also reject any application or cancel this notification at any stage.

Service Conditions

Probation: Candidates selected by direct recruitment are first appointed on probation for 02 years. After a performance review, the appointment may be confirmed once probation is completed. The Competent Authority may extend probation if needed. Pension: Direct recruits are covered by the National Pension Scheme, in force from 01/01/2004. Deputation tenure: Deputation, including short term contract, is for 03 years at first. It can be extended up to 05 years, subject to DoPT norms. Serving government employees taken on deputation follow DoP&T instructions. Place of posting: NIEPMD(D), Chennai.

NIEPMD Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1Can a fresh candidate apply for the Deputy Registrar (Admin) post?
A
No. This post is filled only on deputation, including short term contract. It is open to officers in Level-11, or in Level-10, Level-9 or Level-8 with 5, 7 or 8 years of regular service respectively.
Q2Which post is reserved for PwD candidates?
A
The Lecturer in Special Education (Locomotor and Cerebral Palsy) post is Unreserved - PwD when it is filled by direct recruitment.
Q3Is RCI registration compulsory?
A
Yes, for both Lecturer posts and the Rehabilitation Officer (Independent Living) post. RCI registration as a professional is an essential qualification for all three.
Q4What is the age limit for the Rehabilitation Officer post?
A
35 years for direct recruitment and 56 years for deputation, counted on the closing date of the online application.
Q5How much experience does the Rehabilitation Officer post need?
A
Five years of experience in a reputed organisation that focuses on Adults with Disabilities/Vocational Training & Employment.
Q6Who does not have to pay the application fee?
A
Candidates from the SC/ST/PwBD categories, transgender candidates and female candidates. Everyone else pays Rs. 500 plus Rs. 90 GST, which is Rs. 590 in total.
Q7Which document can lead to rejection if it is missing?
A
The Experience-cum-Service Certificate from your present employer. It must be issued after this advertisement was published. If it is not uploaded, the application is rejected.
Q8How long is the probation for direct recruits?
A
02 years. The Competent Authority can extend it if necessary.
Q9I applied under Notification No.5(R)/2025. Is that application still valid?
A
No. That advertisement was published in Employment News dated 20.12.2025 to 26.12.2025, and it has been cancelled due to service exigencies. You need to apply under Employment Notice No.02(R)/2026.
Q10Will NIEPMD pay travel costs for the interview?
A
No. No TA or DA is paid, and candidates must arrange their own travel, boarding and lodging.
