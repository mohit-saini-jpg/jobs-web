BEL Management Industrial Trainee Selection Process

Selection is purely on the basis of performance in both academics and the interview. Statutory reservation is applied as per Government norms. Selected candidates will be informed through e-mail, followed by publication of the selected candidates' list on the BEL website. No TA/DA (travel or dearness allowance) will be paid to candidates appearing for the selection process.

BEL MIT Walk-In Interview Details

There is no online or postal application for this recruitment. Eligible candidates should report in person on the interview date with the required documents: Important: Carry one set of photocopies of all required documents along with originals for verification. No TA/DA is payable for attending the interview.

Important Conditions

Only Indian Nationals who have passed CMA (Intermediate) or CA (Intermediate) are eligible; candidates who have acquired or are pursuing higher qualifications are not eligible. Suppression of facts or furnishing false information will lead to disqualification or rejection of candidature. Canvassing in any form will result in disqualification. No TA/DA will be paid to candidates appearing for the selection process. The initial training tenure is one year; an extension of one more year may be considered after reviewing the candidate's performance, and the total tenure including extension shall be a maximum of three years.

BEL Industrial Trainee Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1Do I need to apply online for BEL MIT 2026?
A
No. This is a walk-in recruitment – eligible candidates report directly for the interview with the required documents; there is no online or postal application.
Q2When and where is the walk-in interview?
A
10 September 2026 at 9:00 AM, at the Centre for Learning and Development, BEL, Jalahalli, Bengaluru – 560013.
Q3What is the educational qualification required?
A
A pass in CMA (Intermediate) or CA (Intermediate); candidates who already hold or are pursuing a higher qualification are not eligible.
Q4What is the age limit?
A
Maximum 25 years as on 1 September 2026, with relaxation of 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC, and 10 years for persons with disabilities.
Q5What is the stipend for MIT?
A
₹25,000 per month in the 1st year, ₹27,000 in the 2nd year, and ₹30,000 in the 3rd year.
Q6How long is the MIT training tenure?
A
Initially one year, extendable by one more year based on performance, for a maximum total tenure of three years.
