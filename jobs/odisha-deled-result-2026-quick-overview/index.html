Odisha DElEd Result 2026 - Quick Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 27/08/2026

The Odisha DElEd (Diploma in Elementary Education) Result 2026, also known as the Odisha CT Entrance Result 2026, is expected to be declared on 30 August 2026. This is the result for the entrance examination that decides admission into the two-year D.El.Ed teacher training programme offered through colleges affiliated with the State Council of Educational Research and Training (SCERT), Odisha, in association with the Directorate of Teacher Education, Odisha. Once released, candidates will be able to check their result and download their scorecard on the official website, scert.samsodisha.gov.in.

How to Download Odisha DElEd Result 2026 (Step-by-Step)

  • Visit the official website - Go to scert.samsodisha.gov.in on your browser.
  • Find the result link - Look for the "Odisha DElEd Result 2026" or "Scorecard" link on the homepage once it is activated.
  • Click on the link - This will take you to a login page.
  • Enter your login details - Provide your Registration/Roll Number and Date of Birth in the fields provided.
  • Submit the form - Click submit to view your result.
  • Check your scorecard - Your Odisha DElEd Result 2026 scorecard will be displayed on the screen.
  • Download and save - Download the PDF and take a printout for future reference during counselling and admission.

Details Mentioned on the Odisha DElEd Scorecard 2026

Based on the pattern followed in previous years' scorecards, candidates can generally expect the following details on their Odisha DElEd Result 2026 scorecard. Exact fields may vary and should be confirmed against your own downloaded scorecard.

  • Candidate's name and roll number
  • Date of birth and category
  • Section-wise marks obtained
  • Total marks secured
  • Qualifying status

What Happens After the Odisha DElEd Result 2026

Once the result and merit list are out, candidates who qualify will move to the next stages of the admission process:

  • Choice filling of preferred D.El.Ed colleges within the allotted window
  • Seat allotment based on merit rank and choices filled
  • Document verification at the allotted institution
  • Payment of admission fee to confirm the seat
  • Commencement of classes for the two-year D.El.Ed programme

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Keep your Registration/Roll Number and Date of Birth handy before the result is declared to avoid last-minute issues.
  • Download and save multiple copies of your scorecard - a soft copy and a printout - as it will be required during counselling and document verification.
  • Cross-check all details on the scorecard, including name, roll number, and marks. Report any discrepancy to SCERT Odisha promptly.
  • Rely only on the official website, scert.samsodisha.gov.in, or official SAMS Odisha communication for result and counselling updates. Avoid unverified sources or third-party links for your login credentials.
  • Keep checking the official website regularly as counselling and reporting deadlines are usually time-bound, and missing them can affect admission.
