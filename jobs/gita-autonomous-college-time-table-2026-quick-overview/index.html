GITA Autonomous College Time Table 2026 - Quick Overview : Admit Card Overview

GITA Autonomous College, Bhubaneswar has released the exam time table for 2026-27 through notice No. Exam.Deptt/1538/2026. It covers the revised 7th and 5th semester B.Tech schedule, the 3rd semester B.Tech schedule, and the revised 3rd semester MCA schedule, for both Regular and Back candidates. Theory exams start on 16 November 2026 and the last theory paper in this notice is on 5 December 2026.

GITA Autonomous College Time Table 2026 - Quick Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 12/11/2026

Theory exams begin on 16 November 2026, and the last theory paper in the notice is on 5 December 2026 for 7th and 3rd semester B.Tech (the 5th semester B.Tech theory runs until 8 December 2026). Practical exams are scheduled from 2 December to 15 December 2026. Theory papers are held in the 1st Sitting (10:00 AM to 01:00 PM) for the 7th and 3rd semesters and MCA, and in the 2nd Sitting (02:00 PM to 05:00 PM) for the 5th semester. Students should check their branch, subject and Regular/Back category in the tables below.

Question Paper Format

The notice gives the same question format for the theory papers:

  • Duration 3 hours. Total marks 100. The question paper has 3 parts.
  • Part 1 has 12 short answer questions (answer any ten) of 2 marks each, covering the entire syllabus.
  • Part 2 has 10 focused short answer type questions (answer any eight) of 5 marks each, covering the entire syllabus.
  • Part 3 has 4 long answer type questions (answer any two) of 20 marks each, covering the entire syllabus.

How to Read the GITA Time Table

  • Find your programme and semester section below (7th Sem B.Tech, 5th Sem B.Tech, 3rd Sem B.Tech or 3rd Sem MCA).
  • Look for your branch code in the Branch column.
  • Check the Reg / Back column. "Reg" means Regular, "Back" means Back paper candidates, and "Reg / Back" means both.
  • Note the date and the sitting for your semester.
