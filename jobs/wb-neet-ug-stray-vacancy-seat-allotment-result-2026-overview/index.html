WBMCC has published the online stray vacancy seat matrix and the provisional list of eligible candidates on its official website.

WB NEET UG Stray Vacancy Round 2026 Eligibility

The stray vacancy round is intended for eligible NEET UG 2026 qualified candidates who satisfy WBMCC's counselling participation requirements.

Candidates must meet the applicable qualification, registration, verification, domicile or category requirements under the relevant State Quota, Management Quota or NRI Quota provisions.

Participation is subject to the eligibility restrictions specified in the counselling information bulletin and notices, including rules relating to previously allotted or joined seats.

The final eligibility of an allotted candidate is subject to verification by the counselling authority and designated admission centre.

WB NEET UG 2026 Stray Round Reporting Centre for Private Colleges

WBMCC has specified designated admission arrangements for candidates allotted seats in participating private institutions.

For private medical colleges, admission formalities will be carried out at the designated government medical colleges in Kolkata according to the applicable WBMCC notice.

Candidates should check the official institution-wise reporting notice to identify the correct venue for their particular allotment.

Students should not assume that every private-college allotment requires direct reporting to the allotted private institution.

What Happens After WB NEET UG Stray Vacancy Seat Allotment?

Once the allotment result is released, candidates receiving seats should complete the following admission steps.

  • Check the official stray vacancy allotment result.
  • Confirm the allotted MBBS or BDS course, institution and quota.
  • Download the allotment letter or relevant confirmation document.
  • Read the official reporting instructions.
  • Identify the correct admission and verification venue.
  • Arrange the original documents and applicable certificates.
  • Pay the prescribed admission or college fee according to the official instructions.
  • Complete physical document verification.
  • Submit the required bond, where applicable.
  • Obtain the official admission acknowledgement after successful completion of the process.

A provisional allotment does not by itself confirm admission. Candidates must complete all required admission formalities within the reporting window.

WB NEET UG Stray Vacancy Seat Allotment 2026: What if No Seat Is Allotted?

Candidates who do not receive a seat in the online stray vacancy round should check the official counselling website for any subsequent announcements.

Admission through a later vacancy-filling procedure is possible only if the competent authority formally announces such a process and the candidate meets its eligibility requirements.

Students should not assume that another counselling round will automatically take place after the scheduled online stray vacancy round.
