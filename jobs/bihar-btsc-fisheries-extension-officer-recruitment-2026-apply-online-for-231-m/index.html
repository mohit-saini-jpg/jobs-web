Reservation Policy

Reservation benefit is available only to candidates who are permanent (domicile) residents of Bihar . Candidates residing outside Bihar are not eligible for any reservation benefit. SC/ST candidates must submit a caste certificate and a domicile certificate. BC/EBC candidates must submit a creamy-layer exclusion certificate and a domicile certificate. EWS candidates must submit an income & asset certificate and a domicile certificate. All such certificates must be issued by a competent authority notified by the respective State Government of the applicant's permanent area of residence. 35% horizontal reservation for women is applicable only to female candidates who are Bihar domiciles, as per vacancy availability. PwBD reservation is admissible only on the basis of a Disability Certificate issued by a duly constituted Medical Board (Central/State Government), with at least 40% benchmark

Bihar BTSC Fisheries Extension Officer Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection is based on a competitive Computer Based Test (CBT) combined with marks for relevant work experience, as per the following breakup: CBT marks are converted to the merit score by multiplying the percentage scored by 0.75 (e.g., 60% in the CBT = 45 marks). CBT: 100 objective (MCQ) questions worth 100 marks, in Hindi and English, of 2 hours duration. Syllabus is based on the subject matter relevant to the post and is published separately on the BTSC website. Negative marking: +1 mark for each correct answer; −0.25 mark for each wrong answer. The exam may be held in multiple shifts/sessions; a normalization process will be applied to prepare the score card. Minimum qualifying marks: 30% in the CBT, for all categories. Work-experience marks are counted only for candidates who qualify the CBT, based on a Salary Payment Certificate (Annexure-I format) issued by the Director, Fisheries
