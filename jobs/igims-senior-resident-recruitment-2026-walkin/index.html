IGIMS Senior Resident Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Indira Gandhi Institute of Medical Sciences (IGIMS), Patna will hold a walk-in interview on 23 November 2026 (Monday) at 02:30 PM for Senior Resident posts in the Department of ENT. Candidates report from 01:00 PM, and the last reporting time is 02:00 PM, at Room no 32, ENT H.O.D. chamber, Ground floor, OPD Building, I.G.I.M.S., Patna-14. There are 3 posts on an ad-hoc basis for 89 days under the Senior Residency Programme, with Pay + NPA and other allowances as per rule. The walk-in is open to eligible Indian citizens.

About IGIMS Patna

Indira Gandhi Institute of Medical Sciences, Patna-14 (Sheikhpura, Patna) describes itself as an autonomous institute of the Government of Bihar and a statutory university created by an Act of the Bihar State Legislature. This walk-in is for its Department of ENT and is conducted by the H.O.D., ENT.

Posts and Eligibility

IGIMS has advertised 3 Senior Resident posts in the Department of ENT under the 89-day Senior Residency Programme. The institute says that when Senior Residents are needed urgently, a department may appoint them on an ad-hoc basis for a period of 89 days through a walk-in interview, until the full-time recruitment of senior residents (3 years tenure) is completed. Only candidates with a Bihar State domicile get the reservation benefit. Applicants from outside Bihar are not entitled to it. The number of vacancies in the department may increase or decrease. Application Form and Fee

The application form (Proforma for the post of Senior Resident) can be downloaded from www.igims.org. Candidates bring the filled form to the walk-in interview, together with the fee and their documents. The cost of the application form is non-refundable and must be paid by Demand Draft payable to "The Director, IGIMS, Patna". Fees paid by any other mode, such as cash, cheque, money order, postal order, pay order, banker's cheque or postal stamps, will not be accepted. Such applications will be summarily rejected. The proforma asks for the following details: Your NMC/State Medical Council registration number. Your date of birth (with proof) and your age on the cut-off date. Report at the venue from 01:00 PM on 23 November 2026. Reporting after 02:00 PM will not be accepted. Document verification takes place from 01:00 PM to 02:00 PM. IGIMS Senior Resident Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1When is the IGIMS Senior Resident walk-in interview 2026?
A
The IGIMS Senior Resident (ENT) walk-in interview is on 23 November 2026 (Monday) at 02:30 PM.
Q2What is the reporting time and venue for the IGIMS walk-in?
A
Candidates report from 01:00 PM, and the last reporting time is 02:00 PM, at Room no 32, ENT H.O.D. chamber, Ground floor, OPD Building, I.G.I.M.S., Patna-14. Reporting after 02:00 PM will not be accepted.
Q3How many Senior Resident vacancies are there in IGIMS ENT?
A
There are 3 vacancies: EBC 01 (Backlog), EWS 01 and SC 01 (Backlog). The number may increase or decrease.
Q4What qualification is needed for the IGIMS Senior Resident post?
A
Candidates need a medical qualification included in schedule I & II of the NMC Act and an NMC recognized P.G. qualification of MS (ENT). They must also be registered with the Central/State Medical Council.
Q5What is the age limit for the IGIMS Senior Resident walk-in?
A
The upper age limit is 45 years as on 31 October 2026. SC/ST candidates get 05 years of relaxation, EBC/BC candidates get 03 years and female candidates of all categories get 03 years.
Q6What is the application fee for the IGIMS Senior Resident post?
A
The fee is Rs. 1000/- for UR/EWS/BC candidates and Rs. 250/- for SC/ST/EBC candidates. It is non-refundable and must be paid by Demand Draft payable to The Director, IGIMS, Patna.
Q7How is the selection made for IGIMS Senior Resident?
A
The Selection Committee selects candidates on the basis of their bio-data and the interview, using a 100-mark scheme. The minimum qualifying levels are 50 percentile for UR, 45 percentile for BC/EBC/EWS and 40 percentile for SC/ST.
Q8Is the IGIMS Senior Resident job permanent?
A
No. The appointment is on an ad-hoc basis for 89 days, until the full-time recruitment of senior residents (3 years tenure) is completed.
Q9Can candidates from outside Bihar get the reservation benefit?
A
No. Only Bihar State domicile candidates get the reservation benefit. Applicants from outside Bihar are not entitled to it.
Q10Is TA/DA paid for attending the IGIMS walk-in interview?
A
No. TA/DA is not admissible for attending the interview or for joining the post if selected.
