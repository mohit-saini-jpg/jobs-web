RGNAU Teaching Salary 2026 - Post-wise Pay Scale

RGNAU Teaching Recruitment 2026 offers pay under the applicable 7th CPC pay matrix. Professor posts carry Level 14 pay of ₹1,44,200-₹2,18,200, Associate Professor posts carry Level 13A pay of ₹1,31,400-₹2,17,100, and Assistant Professor posts carry Academic Level 10 pay of ₹57,700-₹1,82,400 per month. Additional allowances are admissible as per rules. The starting basic pay is ₹1,44,200 for Professor, ₹1,31,400 for Associate Professor and ₹57,700 for Assistant Professor. Actual gross and in-hand salary will depend on applicable allowances, deductions and other service conditions. The notification does not specify a fixed in-hand salary amount. Suitable residential accommodation may be provided in the University campus as per allotment rules. Appointees will be governed by the National Pension System (NPS).
