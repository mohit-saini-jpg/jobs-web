DMHO Bhadradri Kothagudem Selection Process 2026

Selection will be made strictly on merit using a 100-mark evaluation system . The score comprises 90 marks for the basic qualifying examination and 10 marks for age weightage. The recruitment notification awards 0.5 marks for each completed year of age from 18 years , up to a maximum of 10 marks, calculated as on 1 July 2026. For example, a candidate aged 28 completed years would receive 5 age-weightage marks, while a candidate aged 38 or older would reach the maximum age weightage of 10 marks. The age-weightage score is added to the qualifying-examination component to calculate the merit score out of 100. The recruitment notice prescribes selection based on qualifying-examination marks and age weightage. It does not announce a separate written examination in the stated selection criteria.
