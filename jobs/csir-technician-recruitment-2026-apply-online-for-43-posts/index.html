CSIR Technician Eligibility Criteria

For every trade code (260101 to 260110), the essential qualification follows the same pattern - any one of the following: SSC/10th standard or equivalent with Science subjects, minimum 55% marks, plus an ITI certificate or National/State Trade Certificate in the relevant trade; OR SSC/10th standard or equivalent with Science subjects, minimum 55% marks, plus 2 years of full-time experience as an apprentice trainee from a recognised institution in the relevant trade; OR SSC/10th standard or equivalent with Science subjects, minimum 55% marks, plus 3 years of work experience in the relevant trade in a Ministry/Department/Organization/Public Sector Undertaking/Autonomous Body under the Government of India/State/UT. The relevant trade for each post code is: COPA (260101), Desktop Publishing Operator (260102), Computer Hardware & Network Maintenance (260103), Medical Lab Technician (260104),

CSIR Technician Salary

The Technician (1) post carries Group-C, Pay Matrix Level-2 pay of Rs. 19,900-63,200/-. In addition to basic pay, selected candidates receive Pay and Allowances such as House Rent Allowance (HRA) and Travelling Allowance at Central Government rates applicable to CSIR employees at the place of posting. Other benefits include Leave Travel Concession, reimbursement of medical expenses, and Children's Education Allowance, as per CSIR rules. Accommodation may be provided as per CSIR Residence Allotment Rules, subject to availability (HRA is not admissible where accommodation is provided). New entrants are covered under the National Pension System (NPS). CSIR also offers career advancement opportunities under its Assessment Promotion Scheme for Technical & Support staff.

CSIR Technician Application Fee

The fee can be paid online through SBI Collect. Applications submitted without the prescribed fee, where exemption does not apply, will be summarily rejected. The fee once paid is non-refundable and cannot be adjusted against any other examination or recruitment process. Candidates applying for multiple post codes must pay the fee separately for each post code applied to.

CSIR Technician Selection Process

Selection for CSIR Technician (1) posts follows a multi-stage process: Applications received within the stipulated period are screened by a Screening Committee. Candidates recommended/shortlisted by the Screening Committee are invited for a Trade Test, which is qualifying in nature. Candidates who qualify the Trade Test appear for a competitive written examination comprising Paper-I, Paper-II, and Paper-III. Minimum threshold marks for the Trade Test and each paper are fixed by the Selection Committee. Paper-II and Paper-III are evaluated only for candidates who clear the Paper-I threshold. The final merit list is prepared solely on the basis of marks obtained in Paper-II and Paper-III. The syllabus for the Trade Test and Paper-III (Concerned Subject) will be notified separately on the official CSIR website. CSIR Technician Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Eligible candidates must apply online - no other mode of application is accepted.
  • 2Visit the website: Go to www.csir.res.in or recruitment.csir.res.in and access the link "Technician (1)" / "Technician - 2026", preferably using Google Chrome.
  • 3Register: Click "Apply Online" and register with your name, email address, and mobile number to receive a User ID and password.
  • 4Complete your profile: Fill in Gender, Date of Birth, Marital Status, Category, PwBD status (if applicable), Ex-Serviceman status (if applicable), and Govt./CSIR employee status. Once verified and locked, these profile details cannot be changed.
  • 5Fill the application form: The form has 6 tabs - Apply, Personal Details, Education, Experience, Additional Details, Uploads, and Preview & Submit.
  • 6Upload documents: Scanned photograph (JPG, under 50 KB, 200×150 px), Live Photo Capture, signature (JPG, under 50 KB, 50×150 px), and PDF certificates (each under 1 MB) - proof of date of birth, educational qualifications (10th onwards), experience certificate, caste/disability certificate (if applicable), Discharge Book (if ESM), and NOC from present employer (if applicable).
  • 7Pay the application fee: Applicable candidates pay Rs. 500/- online via SBI Collect and upload the e-Receipt/transaction receipt before the closing date.
  • 8Preview and submit: Verify all details on the Preview page before final submission, as details cannot be edited afterward. Note the generated Application No. and take a printout for your records.
  • 9Candidates applying for multiple post codes must submit a separate application (with separate fee, where applicable) for each post code. e-Admit Cards/Roll Numbers will be issued for the Trade Test/Written Examination; no separate call letters will be posted, and all communications will be published on the CSIR website.
