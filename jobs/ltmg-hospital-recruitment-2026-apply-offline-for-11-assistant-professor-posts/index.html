LTMG Hospital Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Lokmanya Tilak Municipal General Hospital & Medical College (LTMG Hospital), Sion, Mumbai has invited applications for 11 Assistant Professor posts on contract basis till 31 March 2027: Biochemistry 4, Obst & Gync. 3, Biostatistician 1 and Radiology 3. Pay is Rs. 1,10,000 per month for Assistant Professor and Rs. 80,000 per month for Assistant Professor Biostatistician. Handwritten application forms must reach the central dispatch section by 4.00 pm on 4 December 2026, and interviews are on 8 December 2026.

Qualification and Experience

The notice prints one set of educational qualifications and experience requirements for all the posts listed. If the MS-CIT certificate and the SSC or HSC (Marathi subject) certificate are not available at the time of interview, they must be submitted within three months (MS-CIT) and six months (SSC or HSC with Marathi subject) from the appointment date. For the discipline-specific eligibility of each post, refer to the official notification and the National Medical Commission Regulations it cites.

Pay and Terms

The appointment is purely on contract basis and not regular, for the period till 31.03.2027. The appointed candidate can be discontinued at any time if not required. A candidate who wishes to resign must give one month's notice in advance. If Assistant Professors become available on a regular basis through direct service, the contractual appointment will be terminated. Selected candidates are eligible only for Casual Leave and Special Casual Leave as per circular No. Director/M.E. & M.H/3644 dated 14.10.2022, and for no other leave. The selected candidate will be appointed at LTMG Hospital & Medical College and can be transferred to Municipal Medical College as per requirement.
