Territorial Army Eligibility Criteria 2026

The notification specifies the following categories: Ex-servicemen who are pension holders only. Ex-women employees, including premature retirees, of the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEF&CC) and State Forest Departments. Eligible candidates must have completed a minimum of 20 years of service in MoEF&CC or a State Forest Department, as applicable to the category specified in the notice. Eligible candidates should be enrolled within five years from the date of retirement. Junior Commissioned Officers (JCOs) can serve up to 55 years of age. Other Ranks (OR) can serve up to 50 years of age. The medical category must be SHAPE-1. The required character standard is Exemplary/Very Good. The notice provides a relaxation of 5 cm in height and corresponding weight for women from the North-East and hill regions. It also mentions relaxation for Gorkhas, Assamese and Garhwalis

Territorial Army Recruitment 2026 Physical Tests

The physical parameters listed for male candidates include: 1-mile run Pull-ups 8-foot ditch Zigzag balance beam Candidates who pass the physical tests will proceed to the next stages specified in the recruitment notice.

Proficiency Test

The proficiency test includes a trade test for tradesmen. For eligible ex-women candidates, the notice specifies a related test concerning afforestation and nursery management.

Territorial Army Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

According to the notification, the recruitment process includes the following stages: Screening and physical tests Proficiency or trade test, as applicable Interview by a Board of Officers Medical examination All candidates who pass the physical tests will undergo a medical examination. Candidates who qualify in the physical tests will be interviewed by a Board of Officers.

Documents Required for Territorial Army Recruitment Rally 2026

Candidates should bring the following documents as listed in the notification: Original discharge book Original Pension Payment Order (PPO) Eight recent passport-size coloured photographs Character certificate from the Village Sarpanch or Police Authority Education certificate Domicile certificate Carry the original documents specified in the notice for verification. The notification warns that overwriting or tampering with certificates and documents is not acceptable.

Territorial Army Recruitment 2026 Salary and Benefits

The notification does not specify a fixed salary amount for these vacancies. It states that enrolled personnel will receive the basic pay applicable to their rank and service group, along with Dearness Allowance as admissible from time to time. No annual increment is admissible under the terms stated in the notice. Other allowances, including Children Education Allowance, Hill Compensatory Allowance, House Rent Allowance and Road Mileage Allowance, may be admissible as specified in the notification. The notice also states that enrolled personnel will be entitled to canteen and medical facilities as applicable to other units. No additional pension, increment or gratuity is admissible for the service rendered in the CETF Battalion under the conditions stated in the notice; pension entitlement earned during previous service remains untouched.

Territorial Army Recruitment 2026 Job Nature and Enrolment Period

The Territorial Army is described in the notice as a voluntary organisation and essentially a part-time job. Employment is temporary and subject to government sanction and organisational requirements. Permanent employment is not promised. The enrolment period is two years from the date of enrolment. It may be extended one year at a time, subject to satisfactory performance, suitability for continued service and continuation of the project by the Ministry of Jal Shakti, Government of India.

How to Attend the Territorial Army Recruitment Rally 2026

Check the eligibility conditions in the recruitment notice carefully. Confirm that you belong to an eligible ex-servicemen or ex-women employee category and meet the applicable service requirements. Prepare the original documents listed in the notification. Report at 7:00 AM on 16 November 2026 at Major Dhyan Chand Stadium, New Cantt, Prayagraj, Uttar Pradesh. Attend the screening, physical tests, proficiency or trade test, interview and medical examination as applicable. The uploaded notice describes a recruitment rally and does not provide a separate online application link or application fee. Candidates should use the official recruitment notice for confirmation of any additional instructions.

Important Instructions for Candidates

The recruitment process is free. Do not pay a bribe to anyone. Selection may be cancelled if a candidate is found to have paid a bribe. Overwriting or tampering with certificates and documents is not acceptable. Producing fake documents or providing false information may lead to legal punishment and termination of service. Mobile phones are not permitted at the rally site. The authorities conducting the rally will not be responsible for accidents or injuries sustained by candidates during the recruitment rally. Messing and accommodation will not be provided during the recruitment rally.
