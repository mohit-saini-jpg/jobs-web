Haryana Labour Silai Machine Yojana Online Form Overview

Haryana Sarkar ne labour workers ke liye Silai Machine Yojana launch ki hai. Online form bhar ke aap free sewing machine pa sakte hain, bas kuch basic documents chahiye.

Haryana Labour Silai Machine Yojana Online Form Expert Analysis

Yeh scheme skill development ko boost karti hai aur ghar baithe extra income ka source deti hai. State government ka direct support isko reliable banata hai.

Who Is Eligible: Haryana Labour Silai Machine Yojana Online Form

Jo bhi Haryana ka registered labour worker hai, 18-45 saal ke beech, aur apna Aadhar card proof kar sakta hai, woh eligible hai.

Haryana Labour Silai Machine Yojana Online Form Application Process

1. Apna Aadhar, bank passbook aur labour card ready rakhein. 2. Official portal par login karke form ko dhyan se fill karein. 3. Sabhi documents ki scanned copy clear honi chahiye.

Haryana Labour Silai Machine Yojana Online Form Benefits Details

Har eligible applicant ko Rs. 12,000 ki silai machine milti hai, jo ki ek mahine ki extra earning ka potential create karti hai. Machine ke saath training bhi di ja sakti hai.

How Haryana Labour Silai Machine Yojana Online Form Works

Apply karne ke baad, verification team documents check karti hai, phir machine ko applicant ke bank account me direct transfer ya nearest centre se handover kiya jata hai.

Haryana Labour Silai Machine Yojana Online Form Application Tips

Form bharte waqt sabhi fields ko accurately fill karein, duplicate entries avoid karein, aur deadline se pehle submit karein. Common rejection ka reason incomplete documents hota hai.

Q1Yojana ke liye kaun kaun se documents chahiye?
