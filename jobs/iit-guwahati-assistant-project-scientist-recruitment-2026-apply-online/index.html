IIT Guwahati Assistant Project Scientist Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The School of Agro and Rural Technology at the Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati has invited online applications for one Assistant Project Scientist post in the project "Soil Fertility Assessment Using Soil Organic Carbon Detection Kit: Capacity Building and Implementation in Assam, North-East India". The total pay is Rs. 23000 for 3 months, with no HRA or medical. The essential qualification is a Postgraduate degree in Science/Humanities, or a Bachelor's degree in Science/Humanities with a minimum of 55% marks plus 4 years' experience. Who Can Apply

The notice lists one post, Assistant Project Scientist, with 01 vacancy. Previous experience of working with farmers and rural communities. Familiarity with field data collection, documentation and stakeholder interaction. Knowledge of local languages of Northeast India will be preferred. Prior experience in report writing, data analysis and data presentation. Willingness to travel extensively across Northeast India for fieldwork. The 55% marks condition and the 4 years' experience belong to the Bachelor's degree route, as the notice prints them. For the full eligibility, see the official notification.
