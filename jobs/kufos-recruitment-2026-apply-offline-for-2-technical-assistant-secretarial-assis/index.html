KUFOS Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Kerala University of Fisheries and Ocean Studies (KUFOS), Kochi, has invited applications for one Technical Assistant and one Secretarial Assistant post on contract basis under the MoES-DOM-NIOT-sponsored MSc Marine Biology Programme-2026. Applications in the prescribed format must reach The Registrar, Kerala University of Fisheries and Ocean Studies, Panangad P.O, Madavana, Kochi-682 506 on or before 4.30 pm on 4 December 2026.

KUFOS Recruitment 2026 Job Description

The Technical Assistant is expected to assist in the smooth functioning of the laboratory activities of the MSc Marine Biology Programme. The assignments would include, but would not be restricted to: Preparation of laboratories for practical classes Preparation of solutions and reagents Handling and maintenance of laboratory equipment Maintenance of chemicals and laboratory records Assisting students and faculty during practical sessions Maintaining stock registers and other general laboratory activities of the programme The Secretarial Assistant is expected to assist in the smooth functioning of the administrative activities of the MSc Marine Biology Programme. The assignments would include but would not be restricted to: Preparation and maintenance of official documents and correspondence Student records, attendance records and programme related files Data entry Preparation of reports
