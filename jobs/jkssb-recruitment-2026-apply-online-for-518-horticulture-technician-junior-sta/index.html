JKSSB Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Admission to the examination is provisional; the Board does not undertake detailed scrutiny of applications for eligibility at the time of the written examination. Ineligible candidates, once identified, will not be allowed to participate in subsequent stages, and issuance of an Admit Card does not confer any right to participation in later stages of selection.

Facility for Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD)

Candidates with Benchmark Disabilities who are otherwise eligible and hold a valid disability certificate may avail the facility of a Scribe and/or compensatory time of 20 minutes per hour of examination, on producing a Certificate from a competent Medical Board/Authority (Annexure-B) and an Undertaking (Annexure-C). The scribe must be arranged by the candidate, must not be a candidate for the same examination, must not be from the same institution or a relative of the candidate, and must hold a lower qualification than the candidate. Candidates must have applied as a PwBD candidate at the time of application and must submit the required documents to the Observer on the day of the examination.

Document Verification (DV)

Candidates shortlisted for Document Verification must appear with original documents along with self-attested photocopies, including Birth Certificate, Domicile Certificate, Category Certificate, and qualification certificates, all of which must be legally valid on or before the cut-off date. Failure to appear for DV or to produce the required original documents on the scheduled date will result in the candidate not being considered for selection. No TA/DA is paid for participation in the written test or document verification.
