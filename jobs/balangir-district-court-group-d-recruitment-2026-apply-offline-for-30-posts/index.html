Balangir District Court Group D Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Office of the District Judge, Balangir (Balangir District Court) will engage 30 persons on a daily-wage, purely temporary basis for Group-D services in the various Courts of the Judgeship of Balangir under Advertisement No. 04 of 2026. Applications in the Annexure-A format must be sent by Speed Post only to The Registrar, Civil Courts, Balangir (Patnagarh Road), P.O. / Dist. - Balangir, PIN - 767001, Odisha, and must reach the office on or before 18 November 2026 (by 5:00 P.M.). Candidates must have passed at least Class-VIII and be 18 to 42 years old.

Balangir District Court Group D Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection shall be made through a Walk-in Interview of the eligible candidates whose applications have been received within the prescribed period and found complete in all respects. The date, time and venue of the interview will be notified later on the District Court website and on the notice boards of the District Court. Preference for cooking, driving, house chores, electric and plumbing skills applies subject to overall merit. The decision of the Authority regarding shortlisting and selection shall be final and binding, and no correspondence in this regard shall be entertained. No TA/DA will be paid to applicants for appearing in the interview.
