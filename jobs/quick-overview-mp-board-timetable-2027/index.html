Notification DatePost Date: 14/08/2026

The Madhya Pradesh Board of Secondary Education (MPBSE), Bhopal, has released the examination timetable for the Class 10 and Class 12 board exams 2027. The board exams will be held for regular, private, and specially-abled students appearing for the High School (Class 10) and Higher Secondary (Class 12) examinations. Students can check the confirmed exam dates, subject-wise schedule released so far, and download steps below.

How to Download the MP Board Timetable 2027

  • Visit the official MPBSE website: mpbse.nic.in.
  • Click on the "Time Table" or "Latest Notifications" section on the homepage.
  • Select the link for the MP Board Class 10 or Class 12 timetable 2027.
  • The date sheet will open as a PDF on the screen.
  • Download and save the file, and keep a printout for reference during the exam period.

Important Instructions for Students

  • Exams will be held from 9:00 am to 12:00 noon; students should reach the exam center well before the reporting time.
  • The timetable applies to regular, private, and specially-abled students, though specific subject groupings may differ for each category.
  • Schools have been instructed by the board to ensure proper arrangements and facilities for students during the examination period.
  • Students should cross-check their individual subject codes and exam dates against the official PDF, since combined subject groups (e.g., regional languages) are listed together on shared dates.
  • Always download the timetable from the official mpbse.nic.in website to avoid relying on unverified copies.
