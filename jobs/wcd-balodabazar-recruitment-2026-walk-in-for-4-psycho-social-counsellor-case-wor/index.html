WCD Balodabazar Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Women and Child Development Balodabazar-Bhatapara (WCD Balodabazar) is conducting a walk-in interview to fill 4 vacancies for Psycho-social Counsellor (1 vacancy) and Case Worker (2 vacancies) posts on a contract basis. Female candidates who are native residents of Chhattisgarh can attend the walk-in interview on 14 October 2026 reporting at 10:00 AM at the Office of the District Programme Officer, Women and Child Development Department.
