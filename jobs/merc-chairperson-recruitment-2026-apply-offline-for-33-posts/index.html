MERC Chairperson Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Maharashtra Electricity Regulatory Commission (MERC) has 33 Chairperson posts to fill in Consumer Grievance Redressal Forums (CGRF) across the State of Maharashtra, one post for each location. The posts are for retired senior judicial officers, retired civil servants, retired engineering principals or professors, and retired senior electrical engineers of the Government. Applications go by post to The Secretary, MERC, Mumbai, and must reach by 25 November 2026, 5:00 pm.

About MERC

The Maharashtra Electricity Regulatory Commission is based at the World Trade Centre, Cuffe Parade, Mumbai. It is filling these posts under Section 42(5) of the Electricity Act, 2003. The appointments follow the Maharashtra Electricity Regulatory Commission (Consumer Grievance Redressal Forum & Ombudsman) Regulations, 2020 and the MERC (Consumer Grievance Redressal Forum & Electricity Ombudsman) (First Amendment) Regulations, 2024. These Regulations govern the appointment, the terms of service and the functioning of the posts.

Where and How to Send

Send your personal profile or application form "as per format", with attested copies of the certificates that support your qualification, experience, age and so on, to this address: The Secretary, Maharashtra Electricity Regulatory Commission, 13th Floor, Centre-1, World Trade Centre, Cuffe Parade, Mumbai 400 005. Prepare your personal profile or application form as per the format. List up to five locations where you would prefer to work as Chairperson. Attach attested copies of the certificates that support your qualification, experience and age. Write on the envelope: Application for the post of Chairperson, CGRF . Send the application so that it reaches the address above on or before 25 November 2026 by 5:00 pm. If you are applying for more than one location, send only one application. MERC Chairperson Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in MERC Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 33 Chairperson posts in Consumer Grievance Redressal Forums, one for each location. Twenty-seven of them are MSEDCL circles.
Q2What is the last date for the MERC Chairperson application?
A
Your application must reach MERC, Mumbai on or before 25 November 2026 by 5:00 pm. MERC will not consider applications that arrive after the due date.
Q3Who is eligible for the MERC CGRF Chairperson post?
A
You must be one of these: a retired senior judicial officer, a retired civil servant not below the rank of an Additional Collector or equivalent, a retired Principal of a reputed Engineering college, a retired Professor of the Electrical Engineering Department of a reputed institute, or a retired senior electrical engineer of the Government. A working knowledge of the vernacular language of Maharashtra is preferred.
Q4What is the salary of a MERC CGRF Chairperson?
A
The pay is Rs. 50,000/- per month for Kalyan-1 Circle, Thane Urban Circle, Gigaplex Estate Private Limited and Adani Electricity Mumbai Ltd. It is Rs. 40,000/- per month for the other locations. Statutory taxes are deducted at source.
Q5Are allowances paid on top of the MERC Chairperson remuneration?
A
No. The remuneration already includes sitting fees, honorarium and all other allowances, such as conveyance allowance and telephone charges. A Chairperson holding the additional charge of another CGRF gets 25% additional remuneration, plus TA/DA at the Chief Engineer rate.
Q6How do I apply for the MERC Chairperson post?
A
Send your personal profile or application form, with attested copies of your certificates, to The Secretary, Maharashtra Electricity Regulatory Commission, 13th Floor, Centre-1, World Trade Centre, Cuffe Parade, Mumbai 400 005. Write "Application for the post of Chairperson, CGRF" on the envelope.
Q7What is the selection process for MERC Chairperson?
A
If necessary, the selection may be based on an interview conducted by MERC. The Commission will also check the integrity and background of applicants.
Q8Can I apply for more than one CGRF location?
A
Yes. You can name up to five preferred locations in a single application, and you should not send separate applications. The Commission may still appoint you to any other vacant position.
Q9Is the MERC Chairperson post permanent?
A
No. The Chairperson holds office for a fixed term of three years. The Commission may extend the term by up to two years, subject to an overall age limit of 65 years.
Q10Can retired employees apply, and how is their pay fixed?
A
Yes. The post is only for retired persons in the categories listed in the notice. Their pay is the fixed monthly remuneration set for each location, either Rs. 40,000/- or Rs. 50,000/-, and it includes all allowances.
