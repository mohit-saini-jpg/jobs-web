Vidyasagar University Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Eligible Indian Nationals.
PostQualification Required
Medical OfficerMBBS degree from recognized University affiliated to Medical Council of India, Registration Certificate from West Bengal Medical Council or Medical Council of India, and at least 10 years' experience of medical practice in a Government / Military / Quasi Government hospital (10 years' experience in hospital may be relaxed in case of private practice of reputation for 15 years). Desirable: Post graduate Degree in any of the branches of Medical Science or Diploma in Public and Tropical Medicine.
Assistant RegistrarUniformly good academic record with a Master's Degree with minimum 55% marks or its equivalent grade, at least 10 years' experience in a supervisory capacity in a University or a Research Institute or a Government/ Quasi Government organisation, and at least 5 years' administrative experience. Desirable: A degree in Business Management or Law or Statistics or Planning.
Training and Placement Officer (Purely on contract basis)Uniformly good academic record with a Master's Degree with minimum 55% marks or its equivalent grade, and minimum of 7 years' experience in the supervisory capacity in an organization engaged in production or in teaching in a recognized Institute of Post Graduate Learning. Desirable: Experience in Major Industrial capacity in India particularly in West Bengal OR Experience in marketing or sales activities of a public sector organization.
Assistant Professor (LLM) (Purely on contract basis)Good academic record with at least 55% marks at Masters Degree level in LLM (Specialization: Business Law) along with NET/SLET/SET or Ph.D. exemption as per UGC norms.
Assistant Professor (Population, Health and Development) (Purely on contract basis)Good academic record with at least 55% marks at Masters Degree level in a relevant subject (Specialization: Population and Demography / Health and Population Geography / Population and Development Studies / Urban and Regional Planning / Population and Environmental Studies / Gender and Development Studies / Regional Development and Planning / Human Geography / Population and Social Development) along with NET/SLET/SET or Ph.D. exemption as per UGC norms.
Assistant Professor (Santali) (Purely on contract basis)Good academic record with at least 55% marks at Masters Degree level in Santali (Specialization: Linguistics) along with NET/SLET/SET or Ph.D. exemption as per UGC norms.
