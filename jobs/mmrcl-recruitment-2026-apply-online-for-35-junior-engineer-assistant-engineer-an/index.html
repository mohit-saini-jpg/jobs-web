MMRCL Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection will be based on shortlisting of eligible and experienced candidates followed by a Personal Interview . Application Screening: MMRCL will examine applications for educational qualifications, relevant experience, eligibility and other requirements. Shortlisting: Candidates may be shortlisted for interview based on their professional qualifications and experience in the relevant field. Personal Interview: Shortlisted applicants will be called for an interview. Document Verification: Candidates must produce the required educational, employment, experience and category documents when requested. Final Selection: Appointment will be subject to the applicable recruitment requirements and other prescribed conditions.

MMRCL Recruitment 2026 Surety Bond

Selected candidates appointed on regular or contractual basis must execute the prescribed surety bond under the MMRCL Surety and Training Cost Recovery Bond Policy, revised on 13 January 2026. Bond Service Period: The service period for regular appointments is three years. For contractual appointments, it is one year less than the contract duration, subject to a maximum of three years. Important: The Surety and Training Cost Recovery Bond Policy does not apply to candidates appointed on deputation basis.

MMRCL Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1Which is the lowest-graded post in this recruitment?
A
The Jr. Engineer-II posts in grade W6 carry the lowest pay scale, Rs. 35,280 – 67,920. They have 6 vacancies across E&M, D / Civil/Architectural and S&T.
Q2Which post has the most vacancies?
A
Deputy Engineer (Civil/Planning) has 04 vacancies, split as one each for UR, EWS, OBC and SC.
Q3Can a private-sector employee apply?
A
Yes, each post detailed in the notice has a private-sector route with its own experience and CTC floor. Deputy Engineer applicants, for example, need 07 years in Executive grade and a minimum CTC of Rs. 10 Lacs.
Q4Are the Jr. Engineer posts open on deputation?
A
No. Systems Analyst / Jr. Engineer-I and all three Jr. Engineer-II posts are on Contract (05 yrs) only.
Q5Which posts are offered on a regular basis?
A
Only General Manager (Buildings and Maintenance), Deputy General Manager (E&M) and Deputy General Manager (HQ/Systems) are offered on Regular or Deputation basis.
Q6Can AMIE holders apply?
A
For the General Manager and the two Deputy General Manager posts, AMIE (B.E/B.Tech) candidates who enrolled on or before 31 May 2013 may be considered.
Q7Can a Cost Accountant apply for the Finance posts?
A
Yes, provided the candidate is also a Commerce graduate from a recognised university. Assistant General Manager (Finance) asks for 09 years of post-qualification experience, and Junior Manager (Finance) for a minimum of 03 years.
Q8Which posts are reserved for ST candidates?
A
One vacancy each in Assistant General Manager (Civil) and Assistant General Manager (E&M) is reserved for ST candidates. Each has a maximum age of 40 years.
