How to Download AIIMS NEET 2026 Round 2 Cutoff/Allotment Letter

The category-wise closing ranks in this article come from the published Round 2 result and are for reference only; they are not a downloadable "cutoff PDF" candidates need to save. What a candidate actually needs to download is their own Round 2 provisional seat allotment letter, confirming whether a seat has been allotted to them and at which rank. This is done through the general MCC counselling process, as follows:

  • Go to the official MCC NEET UG counselling website, mcc.nic.in.
  • Click on the NEET UG counselling/Round 2 result or seat allotment link on the homepage.
  • Log in using your NEET roll number, application number, or the credentials created during counselling registration, along with your password/date of birth as required.
  • Once logged in, your Round 2 seat allotment status will be displayed, showing the allotted institute, course, and category (if a seat has been allotted).
  • Click the download/print option to save the provisional allotment letter as a PDF.
  • Take a printout of the allotment letter, as it is typically required during document verification and reporting at the allotted institute.

How Is the AIIMS NEET Round 2 Cutoff Determined?

The AIIMS NEET Round 2 cutoff is not a fixed number decided in advance. It emerges after MCC completes the Round 2 seat allotment process based on:

  • Candidates' NEET UG 2026 All India Rank (AIR)
  • Category (Open/General, EWS, OBC, SC, ST)
  • Number of seats available at each AIIMS institute in that category for Round 2
  • Choices filled by candidates during Round 2 counselling

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Treat the closing ranks in this article as provisional, since they are based on the provisional seat allotment result.
  • Candidates allotted a seat in Round 2 need to proceed with the admission process, which includes document verification, as per MCC's schedule.
  • The source does not specify exact document verification deadlines or a complete list of required documents, so candidates should refer to the official MCC notice for this information rather than relying on assumed dates.
