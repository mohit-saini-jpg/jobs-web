GSSSB Sanitary Inspector Selection Process

Selection is through a single-stage competitive written examination (CBRT/OMR based), conducted in two parts: Total 210 questions (multiple-choice), 210 marks, combined duration of 3 hours (180 minutes) for Part A and Part B. Negative marking: ¼ mark deducted for each wrong answer. Qualifying standard: Minimum 40% separately in both Part A and Part B, for all categories. Document verification is called for candidates up to approximately twice the number of vacancies per category, based on provisional merit. Final selection list and waiting list are prepared based on marks obtained, category-wise vacancies, and document verification outcome.
