How to Check CCSU MBBS Result 2026 Online

Follow these steps to check your MBBS 3rd Year II Prof. Supplementary result on the CCSU result portal:

  • Visit the official CCS University result portal at result.ccsuniversity.ac.in.
  • On the homepage, choose the relevant result category - for professional/regular courses this is usually listed under "All Yearly Regular/Professional Courses Result" or the "Declared Result Notice" section.
  • Locate and click the entry for M.B.B.S.-3 YEAR-II PROF. (Supplementary), June 2026, dated 07/08/2026.
  • Enter your roll number/enrollment number (and any other login detail requested on the portal) in the search field.
  • Submit the form to view your result.
  • Download and save the result page, or take a printout for future reference.

Note: The exact field labels and login requirements on the CCSU result page (roll number, enrollment number, or registration number) are not detailed on the official release notes page. Candidates should follow the on-screen instructions shown on the portal at the time of checking.

What to Do If the Result Is Not Showing

  • Recheck that you are using the correct roll number/enrollment number as issued by CCSU.
  • Try accessing the portal after some time, as result servers can be slow immediately after release due to heavy traffic.
  • If your result still does not appear, contact your college's examination cell or the CCSU examination department directly.

The official notice does not provide a specific helpline number or email ID for this particular result, so candidates are advised to reach out through their college or the university's main website contact page for support.

Revaluation/ Rechecking Information

This information is not available on the official release notes page. Candidates who wish to know about rechecking, revaluation, or photocopy of answer sheets should check the CCS University's main website or the examination department for a separate notification, if issued.

Important Instructions

  • Cross-check all details on the result (name, roll number, subject-wise marks) carefully once it is available.
  • Keep a downloaded copy or printout of the result safe, as it may be required for admission, internship, or verification purposes.
  • The result released online is generally provisional; the original/final marksheet is issued separately by the university or affiliated college as per its own process - this has not been confirmed in the source notice and should be verified with your college.
  • In case of any discrepancy in marks or personal details, contact the university/college examination section rather than relying solely on the online result.
