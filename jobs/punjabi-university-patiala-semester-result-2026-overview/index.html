Punjabi University Patiala Semester Result 2026 - Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 27/07/2026

Punjabi University, Patiala has declared fresh examination results on its official result portal, results.pupexamination.ac.in. The newly released results cover four programmes: two under the NEP 2020 Five Year UG-PG structure (Sem-II, May-2026 session) and two postgraduate programmes (Sem-I Dec-2025 and Sem-IV). Students enrolled in these courses can check and download their provisional result directly from the official portal using the links below.

How to Check Punjabi University, Patiala Result 2026 Online

  • Visit the official Punjabi University, Patiala result portal at results.pupexamination.ac.in.
  • Click the direct result link matching your exact programme, semester, and exam session from the list above.
  • Enter your Roll Number/Registration Number and any other detail asked for on that specific page.
  • Submit the form to view your provisional result.
  • Check your name, roll number, course, semester, and subject-wise marks carefully.
  • Download or take a printout of the result for admission, re-appear, or revaluation purposes.

Important Instructions

  • The result displayed online is provisional. Treat the original marksheet issued by the university as the final document.
  • Re-appear and Private/Distance Education results, where marked as such, are included in the same update.
  • In case of any discrepancy in name, roll number, or marks, contact the Punjabi University, Patiala examination branch through official channels rather than relying only on the online copy.
  • Keep checking the official result portal, as Punjabi University, Patiala typically releases results for different programmes in phases.
