SMC Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Surat Municipal Corporation (SMC) (સુરત મહાનગરપાલિકા) is inviting online applications to prepare a waiting list for 18 posts: 1 Chief Fire Officer (ચીફ ફાયર ઓફીસર) (future vacancy, Pay Matrix Rs. 67700-208700, Level-11) and 17 Instrumentation Technician posts (first three years fixed monthly salary Rs. 16,500/-). Applications are accepted on suratmunicipal.gov.in from 11:00 AM on 18 November 2026 to 11:00 PM on 2 December 2026. The fee is Rs. 500 for unreserved candidates and Rs. 400 for reserved categories.

Vacancy Details

The notice lists 18 vacancies in total. The Chief Fire Officer post is a vacancy expected to arise in the future; the Instrumentation Technician posts are current vacancies. Applications are invited to make a waiting list (pratikshayadi). SC, ST, SEBC and EWS reservations apply only to candidates of Gujarat origin in those categories. Women's reservation is 33% of the posts in each category.

Who Can Apply

Degree from a university set up under a Central or State Act, or an institution recognised or deemed a university under Section 3 of the University Grants Commission Act, 1956. Experience: about seven years' whole-time service in the Fire Brigade, of which three years on a post not below Station Officer or Assistant Divisional Officer or equivalent, in Government, local bodies, Government Undertaking Board, Corporation or a Limited Company established under the Companies Act, 2013. Pay and Tenure

Chief Fire Officer: Pay Band 15600-39100, Grade Pay 6600; Pay Matrix Rs. 67700-208700 (Level-11). Instrumentation Technician: first three years fixed monthly salary Rs. 16,500/-; then Pay Band 5200-20200, Grade Pay 1900; Pay Matrix Rs. 19900-63200 (Level-2). Chief Fire Officer must complete the Divisional Officer's Course at the National Fire Service College or a recognised institute during probation. Probation may be extended by one year if the Appointing Authority is satisfied the delay was beyond the candidate's control. Those who have already completed the course need not repeat it. A selected candidate who has not passed the computer examination (CCC/CCC+ or equivalent) must pass it within one year of appointment. The notice states that candidates with an equivalent degree or SMC computer training submit proof.

Selection and Interview

SMC will prepare a waiting list. The Standing Committee (for Chief Fire Officer) and the Appointing Authority (for Instrumentation Technician) will fix the selection criteria based on basic qualification and/or experience, and may hold a retention test, practical test or oral interview depending on the number of applications. Candidates are told about call letters by message on the registered mobile and e-mail after the call letter is published on the SMC website. Document verification is done in original; candidates bear their own expenses for verification and tests. The final waiting list follows the recruitment rules and Staff Selection Committee decisions; where merit is equal, the older candidate is preferred. The notice does not give test dates or a syllabus.

Government, semi-government or company employees must produce a No Objection Certificate at document verification. Candidates previously retired, resigned or removed from Government or public service must state this; concealment may cancel the appointment. Reserved category candidates may apply for unreserved posts, but then only the unreserved selection criteria apply. Candidates must keep the registered mobile number and e-mail active; SMC is not responsible if messages are not received. SMC Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in SMC Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 18 vacancies: 1 Chief Fire Officer (future vacancy) and 17 Instrumentation Technician posts.
Q2What is the last date to apply?
A
The last date is 2 December 2026, up to 11:00 PM. Applications opened at 11:00 AM on 18 November 2026.
Q3What is the qualification for Instrumentation Technician?
A
I.T.I. (Instrumentation) with a minimum of 2 years' experience in industrial automation control systems, field instruments and PLC based control. SCADA experience is preferable.
Q4What is the qualification for Chief Fire Officer?
A
A Bachelor's degree plus a Divisional Officers Course or Fire Prevention Course recognised by the National Fire Service College, Nagpur, or a B.E./B.Tech/B.Sc in Fire or Fire and Safety, with about seven years' Fire Brigade experience.
Q5What is the age limit?
A
Not more than 45 years for Chief Fire Officer and not more than 35 years for Instrumentation Technician, counted on 2 December 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories of Gujarat origin as per rules (age not above 45 years).
Q6What is the salary?
A
Chief Fire Officer: Pay Matrix Rs. 67700-208700 (Level-11). Instrumentation Technician: Rs. 16,500 fixed monthly for the first three years, then Pay Matrix Rs. 19900-63200 (Level-2).
Q7What is the application fee?
A
Rs. 500 for unreserved candidates and Rs. 400 for all women, SEBC, EWS, SC, ST and Divyangjan. It is paid online and is not refundable.
Q8How do I apply for SMC Recruitment 2026?
A
Register and apply online at https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in, upload photo and signature, and pay the fee through Confirm & Pay.
Q9Can I apply for both posts?
A
Yes, but only one application per post is valid; if more than one is made for a post, the last confirmed application counts.
Q10Is the job permanent or a waiting list?
A
The advertisement invites applications to prepare a waiting list (pratikshayadi) for the posts; the Instrumentation Technician posts carry a fixed monthly salary for the first three years.
