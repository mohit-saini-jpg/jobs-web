SBI SCO CTC and Contract Period

Annual CTC is negotiable and depends on the candidate's experience, current emoluments, and place of posting. All posts are eligible for Performance Linked Pay (0% to 45% of Fixed Pay) and an Annual Increment Band, based on performance rating as per the Bank's policy. The contract period is 5 years, renewable for another 4 years at the Bank's discretion, and can be terminated by either side with 2 months' notice or payment/surrender of 2 months' compensation in lieu. SBI SCO Application Fee

The application fee and intimation charges (non-refundable) are ₹750 for UR/EWS/OBC candidates; there is no fee for SC/ST/PwBD candidates. Fees must be paid online through the payment gateway integrated with the application, using Debit Card, Credit Card, or Internet Banking. The fee, once paid, will not be refunded or adjusted for any other examination or selection.

SBI SCO Selection Process

Shortlisting: mere fulfilment of minimum qualification and experience does not entitle a candidate to be called for interview; the shortlisting committee decides the parameters and number of candidates called. Interview: one or more rounds of personal, telephonic, or video interview, carrying 100 marks; the qualifying marks are decided by the Bank. CTC Negotiation: conducted individually with shortlisted candidates, at the time of interview or after completion of the interview process. Merit List: prepared in descending order based on interview scores only; candidates tied at the cut-off mark are ranked by age, in descending order.
