About NIOT

NIOT is an institution under the Earth System Sciences Council (ESSC), an autonomous body under the Ministry of Earth Sciences, Govt. of India. Located in Chennai, it promotes technology development in mission mode activities in Ocean Engineering and Ocean Resource utilization.

NIOT Manager Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Short-listing: a duly constituted Screening Committee adopts its own criteria. If applications are large in number, NIOT may restrict the candidates called for interview to a reasonable limit, using one or more suitable criteria. Screening list: the list of screened in/screened out applicants will be notified on the NIOT website. Interview: selection is by interview of the shortlisted candidates. Meeting the essential qualifications does not by itself entitle a candidate to an interview. Mention all qualifications and experience in the relevant area beyond the prescribed minimum in the application, with supporting documents.

Service Conditions

Pay and allowances: the post carries basic pay and usual allowances, including Dearness Allowance (DA), House Rent Allowance (HRA) and Transport Allowance (TA), as admissible to employees of Central Autonomous Bodies. Increment: an employee is eligible for annual increment as per rules. Other benefits: Leave, New Pension Scheme, reimbursement of Medical Expenses, Leave Travel Concession, Children Education Allowance and Newspaper allowance are available as per Rules. Posting: the appointment will be at the NIOT Headquarters at Chennai. Appointees are liable to be posted to any field unit of NIOT in India on administrative exigencies. Probation: two years from the date of taking over charge of the post. It may be extended at the discretion of the Competent Authority, and confirmation is considered after successful completion, in accordance with the extant rules.

NIOT Manager Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1Is an MBA compulsory for the NIOT Manager post?
A
No. MBA, LLB, Diploma in Management, CA, ICWA and SAS are desirable. The essential degree is a Master Degree from a recognized University.
Q2Can SC, ST or OBC candidates get age relaxation?
A
No. The single vacancy is Unreserved, so these candidates must meet the 45-year limit and compete in the standards of the Unreserved category.
Q3Does contract or part-time experience count?
A
Part-time, casual/daily wage and free lancer experience is not counted for short listing. The notice does not list contract work among these exclusions.
Q4Can a Central Government employee get extra age relaxation?
A
Yes. Regular employees of NIOT or Central Government Departments can get up to five years if their post is in the same line or allied cadres and their service is useful for this post.
Q5Where will the selected Manager work?
A
The appointment will be at the NIOT Headquarters at Chennai, though the appointee can be posted to any NIOT field unit in India.
Q6How long is the probation period?
A
Probation is two years from the date of taking over charge, and the Competent Authority may extend it.
Q7How will shortlisted candidates receive the interview call?
A
The call letter will be sent to the candidate's registered e-mail. The screened in/screened out list will appear on the NIOT website.
Q8Will NIOT pay travel expenses for the interview?
A
Only outstation SC/ST candidates shortlisted for interview are paid to-and-fro second-class rail fare. It is paid from the place of journey or normal residence, whichever is nearer to Chennai Central Railway station, on production of train tickets or other proof of journey, including bus tickets.
