ESIC Alwar Recruitment 2026 Notification PDF

Employees' State Insurance Corporation (ESIC), Ministry of Labour & Employment, Government of India has released the ESIC Alwar Recruitment 2026 notification for the recruitment of Professor, Associate Professor, Assistant Professor, Senior Resident, Adjunct Faculty and Super Specialist posts at ESIC Medical College & Hospital, Desula, Alwar. The recruitment is being conducted on a contractual basis through walk-in interview/document verification, with interviews scheduled from 30 September 2026 and subsequent dates as specified in the notification. ESIC Alwar Recruitment 2026 Salary and Pay & Allowances

As per the notification: no other allowances will be paid in addition to the consolidated remuneration; no private practice is allowed during the tenure of service in ESIC; and a non-medical candidate is not entitled to NPA (Non-Practice Allowance). ESIC Alwar Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection is made on the basis of the candidate's performance in an interview before the Selection Board. The result is displayed on the official website, www.esic.gov.in. Selected candidates must join immediately or as per the schedule after receipt of the offer of appointment. The interview date is intimated/announced separately on the ESIC website as per administrative feasibility. For the post of Senior Resident in Dental, if the number of candidates exceeds 5 times the number of posts applied for, a qualifying screening test will be conducted to limit the number of candidates called for the verbal interview.

ESIC Alwar Recruitment 2026 - Apply Walkin : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1This recruitment does not use an online application portal; it follows a walk-in-interview process with document verification. As per the notification:
  • 2Download the detailed advertisement and the prescribed application form from www.esic.gov.in.
  • 3Fill the application separately for each post, strictly in the prescribed format. Applications submitted in any other format, or incomplete applications, will be summarily rejected.
  • 4This is a weekly rolling advertisement: candidates can submit their application by Tuesday of every week; if a vacancy exists in the concerned department, the candidate will be called after scrutiny of documents, and the date of interview will be informed via email or phone call.
  • 5Report for document verification at ESIC MCH, Desula, MIA, Alwar (Raj)-301030, between 09.00 AM and 10.30 AM, on the scheduled interview date applicable to the specialty (see Important Dates table above).
  • 6Carry original certificates as evidence of age (date of birth), educational qualification, medical council registration (State/MCI/NMC), and experience certificate, along with one set of self-attested photocopies and two recent passport-size photographs.
  • 7Filled-in application forms can also be sent to [email protected].
  • 8Candidates working in Central Government / State Government / Central or State Autonomous Bodies / Central or State PSUs must produce a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from their employer at the time of document verification.
  • 9No TA/DA is paid to candidates for appearing in the walk-in interview. Mere submission of an application or appearance in an interview does not confer any right to be considered eligible unless the candidate reports on the scheduled date and time.

The number of vacancies is provisional and may increase or decrease depending on actual requirement. ESIC Alwar Recruitment 2026 - Apply Walkin : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is ESIC Alwar Recruitment 2026?
A
It is a rolling recruitment by ESIC Medical College & Hospital, Alwar (Advertisement No. 05/2026, dated 22-09-2026) for Teaching Faculty (Professor, Associate Professor, Assistant Professor), Senior Resident (3 Years), Senior Resident against GDMO (1 Year), Adjunct Faculty, and case-to-case Super Specialist empanelment, on a contractual basis, through walk-in interview.
Q2How many vacancies are available in ESIC Alwar Recruitment 2026?
A
Vacancy numbers vary by specialty and reservation category. The notification does not state one combined overall total; the full department-wise and category-wise break-up is given in the vacancy table above, and the numbers are provisional.
Q3What is the last date to apply for ESIC Alwar Recruitment 2026?
A
There is no single "last date" as this is a weekly rolling advertisement. The scheduled interview dates are 30.09.2026 (Adjunct Faculty and Super Specialist document submission), 06.10.2026 and 07.10.2026 (Teaching Faculty/Senior Resident/SR against GDMO), and 07.10.2026 (Senior Resident in Dental application submission).
Q4What is the qualification required for ESIC Alwar Recruitment 2026?
A
It depends on the post: Teaching Faculty and Super Specialist Faculty require qualifications as per the NMC Medical Institutions (Qualifications of Faculty) Regulations, 2025; Senior Residents require a PG degree (MD/MDS/MS/DNB); Adjunct Faculty require a postgraduate degree plus 6 to 8 years of relevant work experience depending on seniority.
Q5What is the age limit for ESIC Alwar Recruitment 2026?
A
69 years for Teaching Faculty and Super Specialist Faculty (case-to-case empanelment); 45 years for Senior Residents generally; 50 years for Senior Residents in Anatomy, Biochemistry, Physiology, Pharmacology, Microbiology, Pathology, Forensic Medicine and Community Medicine. Age is relaxable by 5 years for SC/ST and 3 years for OBC candidates. For Super Specialist empanelment, the upper age limit is 65 years as on the date of empanelment.
Q6What is the application fee for ESIC Alwar Recruitment 2026?
A
Rs. 500/- for all categories other than SC/ST/ESIC regular employees/Female candidates/Ex-servicemen/PH candidates, who are exempted (Nil fee). The fee is collected at the time of interview.
Q7How can I apply for ESIC Alwar Recruitment 2026?
A
There is no online application portal mentioned in the notification. Candidates must download the prescribed application form from www.esic.gov.in, fill it separately for each post, and report for document verification/interview on the scheduled date at ESIC MCH, Desula, MIA, Alwar. Forms can also be emailed to [email protected].
Q8What is the selection process for ESIC Alwar Recruitment 2026?
A
Selection for Teaching Faculty, Senior Resident and Adjunct Faculty is based on performance in an interview before the Selection Board. For Senior Resident in Dental, a qualifying screening test may be conducted first if applications exceed 5 times the vacancies. For Super Specialist empanelment, selection is based on performance before the Selection Committee after document scrutiny.
