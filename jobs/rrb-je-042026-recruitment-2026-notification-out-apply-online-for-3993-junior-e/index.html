RRB JE & DMS Selection Process 2026

Selection comprises four stages: 1st Stage CBT, 2nd Stage CBT, Document Verification (DV) and Medical Examination (ME). Duration: 90 minutes (120 minutes for scribe-eligible candidates). This stage is of screening nature; the normalized score is used only for shortlisting to the 2nd Stage CBT, at 15 times the community-wise vacancy for each RRB. Duration: 120 minutes (160 minutes for scribe-eligible candidates); a virtual calculator is provided on screen. Negative marking: 1/3rd mark deducted for each wrong answer, in both stages of CBT. Minimum qualifying marks: UR & EWS 40%, OBC-NCL & SC 30%, ST 25% (relaxable by up to 2 marks for PwBD candidates in case of shortage against reserved vacancies). Candidates must possess the prescribed educational qualification as on the closing date of application; those awaiting final results are not eligible to apply. Negative marking of 1/3rd mark per wrong answer applies at both stages of CBT. Request for change of examination centre, date, or shift will not be entertained under any circumstances. Candidates found medically unfit for their opted post will not be offered any alternative appointment. Once empanelled against a post/Railway/category, candidates forfeit the right to be considered for any other post/category within the same Pay Level. RRB JE 04/2026 Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Visit the official RRB website and use the link provided for CEN No. 04/2026.
  • 2Create an Account using a valid personal mobile number and email ID (these cannot be changed later); use existing login credentials if already registered for an earlier RRB CEN.
  • 3Optionally authenticate identity using DigiLocker/Aadhaar at the account-creation stage for smoother verification later.
  • 4Choose only one RRB and indicate order of preference for Railway Zones/Production Units and posts/categories you are eligible for.
  • 5Fill in personal and educational details, and select the medium of examination and Exam Group.
  • 6Capture a live photograph through the application module (pre-existing photos are not accepted) and upload your signature in JPG/JPEG format (30–49 KB).
  • 7SC/ST candidates seeking a free train travel pass must upload their SC/ST certificate in PDF (under 400 KB).
  • 8Pay the examination fee online via net banking, debit/credit card, or UPI.
  • 9Submit the application and note down the Registration Number for future reference.
  • 10Use the modification window (16–25 September 2026) if corrections are needed, on payment of a ₹250 modification fee per occasion.
