About IUST

The Islamic University of Science & Technology (IUST) is a university in Kashmir. This notice lists its departments and centres for languages, sciences, engineering, management, planning, geomatics, AYUSH Sciences and molecular medicine, along with the Kalam Academy of Skill Development & Training (KAST). Legal disputes about this recruitment can be heard only in the courts at Srinagar.

IUST Assistant Professor Vacancy Details 2026

The two Mathematical Sciences posts marked * need a Master's degree in Data Sciences, Actuarial Sciences, Statistics or Applied Statistics. Reservation follows the rules of the Government of the Union Territory of Jammu & Kashmir. Persons with disabilities get a 4% horizontal reservation across all vertical categories, and this depends on suitable candidates being available. Vacancies reserved for Persons with Benchmark Disabilities are included within these numbers; the official notice gives the break-up. EWS posts are only for EWS candidates. EWS vacancies are not carried forward, though, so a post with no suitable EWS candidate goes to the Unreserved (UR) category. UR candidates may apply for EWS posts at their own risk and will be considered only if no suitable EWS candidate is found. The University may increase or decrease the number of posts. IUST Assistant Professor Eligibility 2026

English Language & Literature, Urdu Language & Literature, Journalism & Mass Communication, Education, Zoology, Physics, Chemistry: A Master's degree with at least 55% marks in the subject (50% for SC/ST/OBC non-creamy layer) or an equivalent grade on a point scale from an Indian University, or an equivalent degree from an accredited foreign university. Mathematical Sciences: A Master's degree with at least 55% marks in Mathematics/Applied Mathematics. For the posts marked *, a Master's degree with at least 55% marks in Data Sciences/Actuarial Sciences/Statistics/Applied Statistics. Food Technology, Civil Engineering, Electronics & Communication Engineering, Computer Science & Engineering, Mechanical Engineering: B.E./B.Tech./B.S. and M.E./M.Tech./M.S., or Integrated M.Tech., in the concerned/relevant/allied branch, with first class or equivalent in either degree. IUST Assistant Professor Recruitment 2026 : Application Fee Details

The application processing fee is Rs. 1000/- (Rupees One Thousand only). It is non-refundable and is paid online by debit card, credit card or net banking. Candidates in the PwD category do not pay the fee.

Service Conditions

Appointees are bound by the Statutes, Ordinances, Regulations and Rules of the University and its code of professional ethics. Where the University has no service rules of its own, the rules of the Government of the UT apply. Selected candidates are covered by the New Pension Scheme (NPS). Each appointment depends on verification of reserved-category certificates and other claims, and on the outcome of any court case pending before a competent court.

IUST Assistant Professor Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Go to www.iust.ac.in, open the recruitment/application portal and read the detailed instructions.
  • 2Register with a valid Email ID and Mobile Number. Keep your Username and Password safe.
  • 3Enter your personal details, educational qualifications, experience and other particulars. Information you leave out of the relevant columns will not be considered.
  • 4Preview the form and submit it. You cannot edit it after submission.
  • 5Pay the fee online, unless you are a PwD candidate.
  • 6Print the submitted form and attach self-attested copies of all relevant documents, certificates and testimonials. Arrange them in the same order as the serial numbers generated for each entry.
  • 7Deliver the hard copy to the office of the Registrar, IUST, by 4:00 PM on 7 December 2026. If you send it by post, make sure it arrives by that time. If the hard copy is late, your online application can be rejected.
  • 8If you are already employed, forward your application through your employer.
  • 9A detailed CV covering your qualifications, experience and published work. For each publication, give the journal/book title, paper title, volume number, page numbers, year, ISSN/ISBN, authorship (sole or joint), whether it is refereed and indexed, and whether it is local, national or international.
  • 10A valid category certificate from the Competent Authority. EWS candidates need an EWS certificate in the prescribed format under UT of J&K rules.
  • 11Experience certificates issued by the competent authority of each institution.
  • 12Ph.D. holders: the Annexure certificate (page 9 of the notice), signed by the Registrar, Dean Academic Affairs or Dean Research, confirming that the degree complies with the UGC 2009 Regulation. Without it, your Ph.D. will not be considered.
  • 13Foreign degree holders: an equivalence certificate from the concerned regulatory authority.
  • 14Original documents, to be shown at the interview.
  • 15Applications that arrive late or are incomplete will not be considered. Fake documents or hiding material information can lead to debarment, termination and criminal action. IUST Assistant Professor Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many Assistant Professor posts does IUST Kashmir have in this notice?
A
There are 48 posts. KAST has the most, with 7, and English, Education, Mathematical Sciences, Physics, Chemistry, Mechanical Engineering and Management Studies have 3 each.
Q2Do I have to send a hard copy after applying online?
A
Yes. Send a print-out of the submitted form with self-attested documents to the Registrar's office by 4:00 PM on 7 December 2026, or your online application can be rejected.
Q3Is NET compulsory?
A
NET/SET/SLET is required unless you have a Ph.D. that complies with the UGC 2009 Regulations, or a Ph.D. from a foreign university ranked in the top 500 of QS, THE or ARWU.
Q4What qualification do the KAST posts need?
A
All seven KAST posts need a Ph.D. with first class or equivalent in every preceding degree. The branches are Software Engineering, Solar PV, Food Technology, Mechanical Engineering and E&CE.
Q5Who does not have to pay the fee?
A
Candidates in the PwD category. Everyone else pays the non-refundable fee online by debit card, credit card or net banking.
Q6Can UR candidates apply for EWS posts?
A
Yes, at their own risk. They are considered only if no suitable EWS candidate is available, because EWS vacancies are not carried forward.
Q7How many candidates will be called for interview?
A
Ordinarily, 20 candidates for one post and 5 more for each additional post. They are ranked on a 100-mark score: 80 for academic record, 10 for research publications and 10 for teaching experience.
Q8Which Ph.D. certificate must I attach?
A
The Annexure on page 9 of the notice, on your university's letterhead and signed by the Registrar, Dean Academic Affairs or Dean Research. It has one version for Ph.D. registrations before 11 July 2009 and one for registrations on or after that date.
Q9What pension scheme covers selected candidates?
A
Selected candidates are covered by the New Pension Scheme (NPS). They are also bound by IUST's Statutes, Ordinances, Regulations and Rules.
