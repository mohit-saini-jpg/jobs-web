ISRO ICRB Recruitment 2026 - Eligibility

Only Indian nationals are eligible to apply.
PostEducational Qualification
Assistant / Upper Division Clerk (Post No. 1, 3 & 5)Graduation with a minimum of 60% marks or CGPA of 6.32 on a 10-point scale from a recognized university, completed within the stipulated duration of the course, AND proficiency in the use of computers.
Junior Personal Assistant / Stenographer (Post No. 2, 4 & 6)Graduation with minimum 60% marks or CGPA of 6.32/10 (completed within stipulated duration) OR Diploma in Commercial/Secretarial Practice with the same marks criteria plus one year of experience as Steno-Typist/Stenographer; AND a minimum speed of 60 words per minute in English Stenography; AND proficiency in the use of computers. (Apprenticeship Training Certificate under BOAT/NATS for DCP/DCCP holders is considered equivalent to one year of Steno-Typist/Stenographer experience.)

ISRO ICRB Recruitment 2026 Application Fee

Application fee for all posts is ₹100. However, all candidates must initially pay a processing fee of ₹500 per application, uniformly. Fee is payable only online through the Bharatkosh e-Payment gateway via Internet Banking, UPI, or Debit Cards (domestic). No other mode of payment (cash, cheque, draft, money order, IPO, etc.) is accepted. The processing fee is refunded in full to candidates exempted from the fee (women, SC/ST/PwBD, Ex-Servicemen) who appear in the written test/CBT; for all other candidates, ₹100 is deducted and the balance refunded, provided the candidate appears in the written test/CBT. Refund is not considered if a candidate fails to appear for the written test/CBT.

ISRO ICRB Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

A single objective-type paper of 150 minutes duration (with compensation time for PwBD candidates) will be conducted at 38 venues across India, including Agartala, Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Bhopal, Bhubaneshwar, Chandigarh/Mohali, Chennai, Dehradun, Delhi, Ernakulam, Gorakhpur, Gurugram, Guwahati, Hyderabad, Indore, Jaipur, Jammu, Jodhpur, Kanpur, Kavaratti, Kolkata, Lucknow, Mangaluru, Mumbai, Mysore, Nagpur, Patna, Port Blair, Pune, Raipur, Ranchi, Shillong, Tiruchirapalli, Thiruvananthapuram, Varanasi, Vijayawada, and Visakhapatnam. Marking pattern: +1 for correct answer, -0.25 for wrong answer. No TA is paid for attending the written test. Based on written test performance, candidates are shortlisted for the skill test in the ratio of 1:5 (maximum) to the category-wise number of vacancies. ISRO ICRB Recruitment 2026 Remuneration

Selected candidates will be appointed as Assistant / Junior Personal Assistant / Upper Division Clerk / Stenographer in Level 4 of the Pay Matrix. Minimum basic pay: ₹25,500 per month. Additional allowances: Dearness Allowance (DA), House Rent Allowance (HRA), and Transport Allowance as per extant Central Government rules. Employees will be governed by the New Pension Scheme. Other benefits: medical facilities for self and dependents, subsidised canteen, limited quarters facility (in lieu of HRA), Leave Travel Concession, Group Insurance, and House Building Advance, as admissible per Central Government orders.

Appointees are liable to be posted or transferred to any Centre/Unit of the Department of Space/ISRO at any time, based on requirement.
