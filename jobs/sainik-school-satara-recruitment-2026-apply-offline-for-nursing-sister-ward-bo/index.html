Sainik School Satara Recruitment 2026 Notification PDF

Sainik School Satara, functioning under the Sainik Schools Society, Ministry of Defence, New Delhi, has released the Sainik School Satara Recruitment 2026 notification for recruitment to 7 different posts, including Medical Officer, Counsellor, Riding Instructor, Quarter Master, Ward Boy, PEM/PTI cum Matron and Nursing Sister. A total of 8 vacancies have been announced, including 2 vacancies for Ward Boy and 1 vacancy each for the remaining posts. The applications are invited through offline mode by Post or by Hand only, and the completed application must reach Sainik School Satara on or before 09 October 2026. Sainik School Satara Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Download the prescribed application format from the official school website, www.sainiksatara.org.
  • 2Fill in the application form completely and attach self-attested copies of educational qualification and experience certificates.
  • 3Affix a passport-size photograph and superscribe the name of the post applied for on the envelope.
  • 4Enclose one self-addressed envelope (9"x4") with Rs. 56/- postage stamps affixed.
  • 5For General and OBC candidates, enclose an A/c payee Demand Draft of Rs. 250/- (non-refundable) drawn in favour of "Principal, Sainik School Satara", payable at Satara. SC/ST candidates are exempt from the fee.
  • 6OBC/SC/ST candidates should also attach a copy of their Caste Certificate; OBC candidates must submit an NCL Certificate.
  • 7Send the completed application along with all required documents by Post or by Hand to: Principal, Sainik School Satara, Satara-415001, Maharashtra, so that it reaches on or before 09 October 2026.
  • 8A separate application is required for each post applied for. Applications sent by e-mail will not be accepted.
