RRC SER Vacancy 2026

RRC SER Age Limit 2026

The age limit for all three levels is a minimum of 18 years and a maximum of 25 years as on 01.01.2027. The notice prints the date-of-birth range as not earlier than 02.01.2002 and not later than 01.01.2009. No age relaxation (upper or lower) is allowed. Proof of date of birth is a copy of the Matriculation or equivalent certificate from a recognised Board, OR the Birth Certificate, which must be uploaded.

RRC SER Selection Process 2026

Sports Trials, where performance is assessed by the Trial Committee. Certificate (Sports & Educational) Verification by a duly constituted Recruitment Committee of South Eastern Railway. The venue and date of the trials and certificate verification will be sent to eligible candidates in due course. Selected candidates must also meet the required medical fitness standards before appointment. Selection is purely on merit, against the vacancies notified in each discipline. When two or more sportspersons score the same marks in a discipline, the younger candidate gets preference. The names of successful candidates will be published separately. Their candidature stays provisional until their qualifications and certificates are verified and found genuine, and other formalities are complete.

South Eastern Railway Sports Quota Group C and Group D : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Applications are accepted only online, between 31 October 2026 and 30 November 2026 (23.59 Hrs). Applications sent directly to RRC will not be entertained.
  • 2Visit the RRC-SER website, www.rrcser.co.in, and click the "Online/E-Application for Sports Quota(OA) / 2026-27" link, then "New Registration".
  • 3Fill in your personal details: Name, Father's name, Date of Birth, Community, E-mail address and Mobile number. These must match your Matriculation or equivalent certificate. Note the registration number and password sent to you by E-mail/SMS.
  • 4Log in with the registration number and password, and complete the registration step by step.
  • 5Enter your bank account details for the fee refund, or confirm that the refund should go to the account used for payment.
  • 6Enter the details of a valid photo identity card and upload it.
  • 7Upload the scanned photograph, signature and LTI, and the self-attested certificates for date of birth, educational/technical qualification, caste (SC/ST/OBC/EBC/EWS), income, OBC and minority self-declarations, and sports achievements.
  • 8Pay the fee by credit card, debit card or internet banking.
  • 9Submit the application. Note the Unique Registration Number generated at submission, which is needed at later stages.
  • 10Take two (02) printouts of the completed application form. No change is allowed after final submission, and the hard copy must be the same as the one submitted online.
  • 11Use your own mobile number and a valid, active e-mail ID, and keep them active until recruitment ends. RRC sends all communication only by SMS and E-mail and will not accept requests to change them. For help with the online application, use the Help Line Number and E-mail ID shown on the RRC/SER website.
  • 12Photograph: colour, passport size, white/light background, 3.5 cm X 3.5 cm, not older than three months from the date of application. JPG/JPEG, scanned at 100 DPI, up to 70 KB.
  • 13The photo must show a clear front view, without cap or sunglasses. If you wear glasses, there must be no glare and the eyes must be clearly visible. It must match your appearance at the trial, document verification and medical test.
  • 14The same photo must be used at every stage. The notice advises keeping at least 06 (Six) copies.
  • 15Signature and LTI: in black ink on white paper, within a box of 50 mm x 20 mm or 140X60 pixels. JPG/JPEG at 100 dpi, file size 10-40 KB. Both must be the applicant's own.
  • 16The signature must be in Hindi or English, not in capital or disjointed letters, and the same at every stage. A different signing style at the trial, document verification or medical examination may lead to cancellation.
  • 17A self-attested copy of a valid photo identity card issued by a government body (Aadhar card/PAN card/Driving License/Voter ID Card). Carry this card with the e-call letter at every stage.
  • 18A self-attested certificate in proof of educational qualification issued by the Board/Council/University (para 3.5).
  • 19Sports achievement certificates only for Championships concluded on or after 01.04.2024, from recognised Sports Association of Indian University.
  • 20A self-attested certificate in proof of age (Matriculation/10th certificate).
  • 21Self-attested certificates in proof of all recognised sports achievements as per paras 3.2, 3.3 and 3.4.
  • 22SC/ST: a self-attested community certificate from the Competent Authority (Annexure-I).
  • 23OBC-NCL: a self-attested community certificate (Annexure-II) and a self-declaration that the candidate is not in the Creamy Layer (Annexure-IIA).

South Eastern Railway Sports Quota Group C and Group D : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in RRC SER Sports Quota Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 54 posts: 5 in Group 'C' Level-4/Level-5, 16 in Group 'C' Level-2/Level-3 and 33 in Group 'D' Level-1.
Q2When does the RRC SER Sports Quota online application start?
A
Online applications open on 31 October 2026 on www.rrcser.co.in. The notification was published on the website on 26 October 2026.
Q3What is the last date to apply for RRC SER Sports Quota 2026?
A
The last date is 30 November 2026, up to 23.59 Hrs. Applications received after the closing date will be rejected.
Q4What is the application fee for RRC SER Sports Quota 2026?
A
The fee is Rs. 500 for UR/OBC and Rs. 250 for SC/ST/Ex-Servicemen/Persons, Women, Minorities and Economic Backward Classes, paid online. Rs. 400 (UR/OBC) or Rs. 250 (the others) is refunded to candidates who actually appear in the trial, after deducting bank charges.
Q5What is the age limit for RRC SER Sports Quota Recruitment 2026?
A
Candidates must be at least 18 and at most 25 years old as on 01.01.2027. No upper or lower age relaxation is allowed.
Q6What qualification is needed for the Level-1 sports quota posts?
A
Level-1 needs 10th pass or ITI or equivalent or National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC) granted by NCVT, plus the Level-1 sports norms. Level-4/5 needs a University Degree or its equivalent, and Level-2/3 needs class 12th (10+2) or one of the listed matriculation-plus options.
Q7What is the pay for RRC SER Sports Quota posts?
A
All posts are in PB-1 Rs. 5,200-20,200. The Grade Pay is Rs. 2400/2800 for Level-4/5, Rs. 1900/2000 for Level-2/3 and Rs. 1800 for Level-1.
Q8How are candidates selected in RRC SER Sports Quota Recruitment 2026?
A
Selection is by sports trials followed by certificate (sports and educational) verification, marked out of 100. The minimum qualifying marks are 70 for Grade Pay 2400/2800, 65 for 1900/2000 and 60 for 1800, with at least 25 out of 40 in the trial.
Q9Are any RRC SER Sports Quota posts reserved for SC/ST/OBC/EWS?
A
No. No post is reserved for SC/ST/OBC/EWS communities, and candidates are selected on overall merit in their sports discipline.
Q10Is there a service bond for RRC SER Sports Quota selection?
A
Yes. Selected candidates must submit a Service Bond to serve 5 years with the railway.
