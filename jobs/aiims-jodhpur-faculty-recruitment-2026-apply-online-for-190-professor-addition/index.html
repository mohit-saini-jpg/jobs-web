AIIMS Jodhpur Faculty Selection Process

There is no written examination. Selection is through bio-data-based shortlisting followed by a Personal Interview at AIIMS Jodhpur. Merely possessing the prescribed minimum qualification does not entitle a candidate to selection; the Selection Committee shortlists candidates for interview based on bio-data. Shortlisted candidates must produce all original documents at the time of interview. Interviews will be held at AIIMS Jodhpur; no TA/DA is paid for appearing. Minimum interview cut-off marks: 60% for EWS/UR, 55% for OBC, 50% for SC/ST/PwBD categories.

Documents Required

Educational Qualification Certificates as specified in the Recruitment Rules for the applied post (medical qualifications must have valid National/State Medical Council registration) Certificate of Equivalent Qualification (if applicable), stating the authority, order number, date, and specific equivalence declared Experience Certificate(s) issued by the Head(s) of the organization, stating exact "from" and "to" dates, nature of experience (teaching/research/both), and recognition of the organization (MCI/NMC for medical, DCI for dental, UGC for non-medical) DNB/DrNB equivalence documentary evidence, if applicable Category certificate (EWS/SC/ST/OBC) in the DoPT-prescribed format, with non-creamy layer status for OBC candidates PwBD Disability Certificate from a competent Medical Board (Central/State Government constituted), if claiming benchmark disability benefits No Objection Certific

Important Conditions

If a candidate indicates a category (EWS/SC/ST/OBC/General) in the application form but later requests a change, such requests will not be entertained by the Institute. OBC candidates must submit a valid caste certificate in the DoPT-prescribed format with non-creamy layer status; a certificate issued in FY 2026-27 is required, though older certificates/acknowledgement slips are provisionally accepted pending renewal. EWS candidates must submit a valid Income & Asset Certificate for FY 2026-27 (based on FY 2025-26 income), issued by a competent authority; older certificates are provisionally accepted pending renewal. PwBD reservation (4% horizontal) applies to posts identified suitable for specific benchmark disabilities across 11 departments: Biochemistry, Microbiology, Otorhinolaryngology (ENT), Psychiatry, Diagnostic and Interventional Radiology, Urology, Pulmonary Medicine, Radiation

AIIMS Jodhpur Faculty Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Visit the official website www.aiimsjodhpur.edu.in and locate the online faculty recruitment application link.
  • 2Register and complete the online application form, ensuring photo and signature are clearly visible in preview (small or unclear images will lead to rejection).
  • 3Upload Educational Qualification Certificates as specified in the Recruitment Rules, along with any Certificate of Equivalence if applicable, and a valid Experience Certificate stating exact dates and nature of experience.
  • 4Upload category certificate (EWS/SC/ST/OBC) in the prescribed format, if claiming reservation.
  • 5Upload No Objection Certificate (NOC) if currently employed in Government/Semi-Government/Government-funded institutions.
  • 6Pay the prescribed application fee online through the AIIMS Jodhpur payment gateway.
  • 7Submit the application before the closing date and retain a copy of the application form and payment proof for your records (no physical documents need to be sent).

AIIMS Jodhpur Faculty Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many faculty posts are available at AIIMS Jodhpur?
A
A total of 190 Group ‘A’ faculty posts are available across 40 departments and 4 grades — Professor (34), Additional Professor (17), Associate Professor (26), and Assistant Professor (113).
Q2What is the last date to apply?
A
Online applications close at 5:00 PM on 12 September 2026.
Q3What is the age limit for AIIMS Jodhpur faculty posts?
A
Professor/Additional Professor: not exceeding 58 years; Associate Professor/Assistant Professor: not exceeding 50 years, as on the closing date, with applicable category relaxation.
Q4Is there a written exam for AIIMS Jodhpur faculty recruitment?
A
No. Selection is through bio-data-based shortlisting followed by a Personal Interview at AIIMS Jodhpur; there is no written test.
Q5What is the application fee?
A
₹3,000 for General/OBC/EWS candidates; ₹200 for SC/ST/Women/PwBD candidates (refundable to those who appear for interview).
Q6Can Non-Medical candidates apply?
A
Yes, but only for Anatomy, Biochemistry, and Physiology departments (Professor/Additional Professor/Associate Professor grades), requiring a relevant Master’s degree plus a Doctorate from a recognized university.
Q7Are OCI cardholders eligible to apply?
A
Yes, Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) cardholders under Section 7(A) of the Citizenship Act, 1955 are eligible to apply for teaching posts as per MHA guidelines.
Q8What is the pay scale for AIIMS Jodhpur faculty posts?
A
Basic pay ranges from ₹1,01,500 (Assistant Professor, Level 12) to ₹1,68,900 (Professor, Level 14A), plus usual allowances and NPA for medically qualified candidates.
Q9Can I apply for more than one post?
A
Yes, but a separate online application must be submitted for each post applied to.
Q10What is the minimum interview cut-off for selection?
A
60% for EWS/UR, 55% for OBC, and 50% for SC/ST/PwBD categories.
