UPESSC Assistant Professor Selection Process

Selection for UPESSC Assistant Professor posts is based on a merit list prepared from a 360-mark written examination and a 40-mark interview. The written examination consists of 120 objective-type (multiple-choice) questions - 30 questions on General Knowledge and 90 questions on the relevant subject. Duration of the written examination is 2 hours. Each correct answer carries 3 marks; 1 mark is deducted for a wrong answer or multiple answers marked for a single question (negative marking). Candidates are shortlisted for interview based on written examination marks; being called for interview or merely appearing in/passing the written exam does not by itself entitle a candidate to final selection. UPESSC Assistant Professor Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

The registration and application form must be filled in English only. If a candidate submits more than one application for the same post, only the last submitted application will be considered valid; the fee paid will not be refunded or adjusted. Candidates with a declared false statement in their application will have their candidature cancelled and may be debarred from future UPESSC examinations/selections, along with other punitive action. The High School (or equivalent) certificate's date of birth is the only valid proof of age; no other document is accepted. Reserved-category candidates must submit their caste/EWS certificate in the prescribed Uttar Pradesh Government format; the benefit of reservation is available only to candidates who are original residents of Uttar Pradesh. A married woman candidate's caste certificate showing her as the wife of a person belonging to an SC/ST/OBC category is not valid - only a caste certificate issued from the paternal side is accepted for women candidates. Candidates must obtain a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from their current employer, to be produced at the time of interview if demanded by the Commission. Candidates must accurately declare any pending court case or conviction; concealment or false declaration discovered later will lead to cancellation of candidature/selection. No travel allowance (TA/DA) is payable for appearing in the written examination or interview. PwD candidates are entitled to scribe facility (as per Government orders dated 4 December 2019 and 8 September 2022) and 20 minutes extra time per hour; the scribe must be arranged by the candidate and registered with the Commission at least one week before the written exam.
