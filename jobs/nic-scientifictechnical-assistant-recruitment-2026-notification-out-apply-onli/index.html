NIC Scientific/Technical Assistant-A Vacancy 2026

NIC has advertised a total of 376 vacancies (computed from the discipline-wise figures given in the notification) for the Scientific/Technical Assistant-A post, spread across three disciplines: Computer Sciences & Information Technology, Electronics and Communication Engineering, and Data Science and Artificial Intelligence. The notification clarifies that the number of advertised posts is tentative and may increase or decrease at NIC's sole discretion. In addition to the vertical reservation shown above, horizontal reservation for Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD) is provided: 15 posts in Computer Sciences & IT (Locomotor disability-4, Hard of Hearing-4, Multiple Disabilities-7, including a 3-post PH-MD backlog vacancy within this figure), 2 posts each in Electronics & Communication Engineering and Data Science & AI (Locomotor disability-1, Hard of Hearing-1 in each discipline)

NIC Scientific/Technical Assistant-A Salary

The Scientific/Technical Assistant-A post carries a pay of Level 6 under the 7th Central Pay Commission, i.e., ₹35,400 to ₹1,12,400 per month, in addition to applicable allowances as per Government of India norms.

NIC Scientific/Technical Assistant-A Application Fee

Candidates applying for more than one discipline must pay ₹800 separately for each discipline applied for. The fee can be paid only through online mode (Credit Card/Debit Card/Net Banking); Cash, Demand Draft, Pay Order, or Cheque will not be accepted. Bank charges towards remittance, if any, must be borne by the candidate. The application fee, once paid, is non-refundable.

NIC Scientific/Technical Assistant-A Selection Process

Only candidates fulfilling the eligibility criteria and who have completed registration on the NIC portal will be considered for further selection. A category-wise merit list will be prepared for each discipline based on GATE Score, with equal weightage given to GATE 2024, GATE 2025, and GATE 2026 scores. Candidates must submit their best GATE score from any one of the eligible years for the discipline applied. In case of a tie: (1) the older-year GATE score is ranked higher; (2) if the score and year are the same, the older candidate is ranked higher; (3) if still tied, the candidate with a higher percentage in the essential qualification is ranked higher. Shortlisted candidates will be called for Document Verification before being offered a provisional appointment.
