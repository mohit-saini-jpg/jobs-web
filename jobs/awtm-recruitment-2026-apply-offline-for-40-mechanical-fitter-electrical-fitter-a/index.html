AWTM Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Candidates will be selected based on marks obtained in the NCTVT (NCVT) Trade Test / Practical Test alone. The cut-off percentage to be called for the trade test will be determined by Academy of Weapon Technology & Management, Ichapur based on total marks obtained in NCTVT. The trade test will be conducted by AWTM Ichapur. The merit list will be prepared based on combined marks obtained in the NCTVT examination and the trade/practical test, carrying a weightage of 80% and 20% respectively. Shortlisted candidates in order of merit will be called for document verification up to the limit of notified vacancies per discipline and category. A ratio of 1:10 will be maintained for vacancies and interviews if applicable.

Q1What is AWTM Recruitment 2026?

AWTM Recruitment 2026 is an offline recruitment drive announced by the Academy of Weapon Technology and Management, Ichapur, to fill 40 contract vacancies across various trades like Fitter, Machinist, Turner, Electroplater, and Heat Treatment Operator.
Q2How many total vacancies are available in this recruitment?
A
A total of 40 vacancies are available across 6 designated trades under advertisement number 01/Hiring-GSF/AWTM/2026-27.
Q3What is the last date to apply for AWTM Recruitment 2026?
A
The last date for receipt of offline applications at AWTM Ichapur is 2 November 2026.
Q4What is the educational qualification required for these posts?
A
Candidates must have passed Matriculation (Class 10) along with an NTC or NAC certificate issued by NCTVT in the relevant trade specific to the post applied for.
Q5What is the age limit for applicants?
A
The age of general candidates must be between 18 and 35 years as on the closing date for receipt of applications, with permissible relaxations for reserved categories.

Yes, the application fee is Rs. 100/- payable via Demand Draft. SC/ST, Ex-Servicemen, and physically disabled (PwD) candidates are exempted from the fee.
Q7How do I apply for AWTM Recruitment 2026?
A
Candidates must download the application form, fill it out entirely in Block Letters, attach the required self-attested documents, fee draft, and photographs, and mail it via post to the Chief General Manager at AWTM Ichapur.
Q8What is the selection process for these posts?
A
Selection is based entirely on marks secured in the NCTVT exam (80% weightage) and the Trade/Practical Test (20% weightage) conducted by AWTM Ichapur, followed by document verification.
Q9Are these permanent positions or on contract?
