Indian Navy Sports Quota Recruitment 2026 Pay & Allowances

During the initial training period, a stipend of ₹14,600 per month is admissible. On successful completion of initial training, candidates are placed in Pay Level 5 (Petty Officers) or Pay Level 6 (Chief Petty Officers). Additionally, Military Service Pay (MSP) @ ₹5,200 per month, plus Dearness Allowance (DA) as applicable, is paid. Insurance cover (on contribution) of ₹75 lakhs is applicable for all sailors. Promotion prospects exist up to the rank of Master Chief Petty Officer (equivalent to Subedar Major). Opportunities for promotion to Commissioned Officer also exist for those who perform well, qualify prescribed examinations/selection processes, and meet eligibility conditions stipulated from time to time. Indian Navy Sports Quota Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Deserving candidates would be called to appear for trials at designated Naval Centres. Candidates qualifying trials will undergo medical examination at INS Hamla, Mumbai/INS India, Delhi. The Offer of Enrolment will be forwarded to the selected candidates only, which will be strictly determined by requirement in the particular sports discipline and availability of vacancies. Requests for change of venue of selection trials will not be entertained. Incomplete applications will not be accepted. Short-listing criteria will be based on higher sports achievements. Call-up letters will be posted to eligible shortlisted candidates, indicating date, time, and place for the selection trial. All original certificates and mark sheets must be produced at the time of the selection trial. Candidates declared Medically Temporary Unfit in the recruitment medical examination can avail a specialist review

Indian Navy Sports Quota Recruitment 2026 Notification : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Download the application form from www.joinindiannavy.gov.in, or prepare it as per the given format on A4-size paper.
  • 2Fill in the application form completely - only one application form is to be forwarded; candidates who send more than one application for the same entry will be disqualified.
  • 3Write the type of entry, sports discipline, and achievements clearly on top of the envelope containing the application. The envelope should be of brown colour (Example: "Direct Entry Petty Officer/Chief Petty Officer - 02/2026 KABADDI - NATIONAL LEVEL").
  • 4Punch and tie firmly with a strong thread, in sequence, the following self-attested documents to the application: (a) Matriculation Certificate (for DOB verification), (b) 10th Mark Sheet, (c) NCC/Sports Certificate (if applicable), (d) Domicile Certificate, (e) 10+2 Certificate along with mark sheet.
  • 5Attach two self-addressed envelopes of size 22 x 10 cm, with a ₹10 stamp affixed on one envelope.
  • 6Submit one additional recent coloured passport-size photograph along with the application, with name and signature on the reverse of the photograph - the background of the photograph should be blue. Computer-generated/digital photographs will not be accepted.
  • 7Send the application only by Ordinary Post to: The Secretary, Indian Navy Sports Control Board, Naval Headquarters, Ministry of Defence, Nausena Bhawan, Room No. W-016, Delhi Cantt, New Delhi - 110010.
  • 8Applications received through Speed Post, Registered Post, or Couriers will be rejected.
  • 9Ensure the application reaches by the closing date - 15.10.2026 (general) or 22.10.2026 (for candidates from North East states, J&K, Ladakh, Andaman & Nicobar, and Lakshadweep & Minicoy Island).

Indian Navy Sports Quota Recruitment 2026 Notification : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is Indian Navy Sports Quota Recruitment 2026?
A
It is a recruitment drive by the Indian Navy inviting unmarried male and female sportspersons to enrol as Sailors - Direct Entry Petty Officer (Sports Entry) or Chief Petty Officer (Sports Entry) - under Sports Quota Entry 02/2026 Batch.
Q2What is the last date to apply for Indian Navy Sports Quota Recruitment 2026?
A
The last date of receipt of application is 15.10.2026 for most candidates, and 22.10.2026 for candidates from North East states, J&K, Ladakh, Andaman & Nicobar, and Lakshadweep & Minicoy Island.
Q3What is the age limit for Indian Navy Sports Quota Recruitment 2026?
A
Candidates must be 17½ to 25 years of age as on the date of commencement of the course, born between 01 September 2001 and 28 February 2009 (both dates inclusive).
Q4What is the educational qualification required?
A
Candidates must be 10+2 qualified in any stream, or have passed an equivalent examination.
Q5What sports disciplines are covered under this recruitment?
A
For men: Athletics, Aquatics, Artistic Gymnastics, Basketball, Boxing, Cricket, Equestrian, Football, Fencing, Golf, Handball, Hockey, Kabaddi, Kayaking & Canoeing, Rowing, Sailing, Shooting, Squash, Tennis, Volleyball, Weightlifting, and Wrestling. For women: Athletics, Aquatics, Kayaking & Canoeing, and Rowing & Sailing.
Q6How can I apply for Indian Navy Sports Quota Recruitment 2026?
A
Applications must be downloaded/prepared as per the prescribed format and sent only by Ordinary Post (not speed post, registered post, or courier) to the Indian Navy Sports Control Board, New Delhi, along with the required documents.
Q7What is the selection process for Indian Navy Sports Quota Recruitment 2026?
A
Selection involves sports trials at designated Naval Centres based on higher sports achievements, followed by a Medical Examination at INS Hamla, Mumbai, or INS India, Delhi, and preparation of a select list based on available vacancies per discipline.
Q8What is the pay during training and after enrolment?
A
A stipend of ₹14,600 per month is paid during initial training. After successful completion, candidates are placed in Pay Level 5 (Petty Officers) or Pay Level 6 (Chief Petty Officers), plus Military Service Pay of ₹5,200 per month and applicable DA.
Q9What are the height and vision standards for this recruitment?
A
Minimum height is 157 cm for male and 152 cm for female candidates. Vision must be at least 6/12 in both eyes without glasses, or 6/6 with glasses.
