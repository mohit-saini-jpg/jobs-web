KRCL Recruitment 2026 is open to the following categories of candidates: Land Loser Candidates: Candidates whose land was acquired for construction of the Konkan Railway Line between Roha (Maharashtra) and Thokur (Karnataka), irrespective of the percentage of land lost. KRCL Recruitment 2026 Exam Pattern (CBT)

The recruitment process varies by post group: Section Controller, Station Master, Assistant Loco Pilot: CBT → Computer-Based/OMR-based Aptitude Test → Document Verification (DV) → Medical Examination. Merit is prepared with 70% weightage to CBT marks and 30% to Aptitude Test marks. Senior Section Engineer (Mechanical/S&T), Junior Engineer (Civil/Mechanical), Goods Train Manager, Commercial Supervisor, Technician-III (Mechanical), ESTM-III: CBT → Document Verification → Medical Examination. Track Maintainer: CBT → Document Verification → Physical Efficiency Test (PET) → Medical Examination. The CBT is of screening nature with negative marking - one mark is deducted for each wrong answer. Questions are set in five languages: English, Hindi, Marathi, Konkani, and Kannada (the Aptitude Test for SCOR/SM/ALP is in English and Hindi only).
