National Library LIS Intern Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Holders of a Master Degree in Library and Information Science below 35 years of age can apply for one of 25 one-year LIS Intern positions for 2026-27 at the National Library, Ministry of Culture, Government of India, on a stipend of Rs 25,000/- per month. The positions are allotted language-wise across 23 Indian languages, and applications must be submitted online through the link on the National Library website. The notice (No. ADM/S-I(40)/Intern.26-27/5143, dated 30 October 2026) does not print a last date.

About National Library

The National Library is an institution of the Government of India under the Ministry of Culture. Under its programme "Young Professional Graduates from Library & Information Science as LIS Interns", it engages professional LIS graduates for one year to work in its language sections and divisions, including the Reading Room, Stack area, Acquisition division and Laboratory division.

Vacancy Details (Language-wise)

The National Library needs 25 (twenty five) LIS Interns, allotted across 23 languages. Hindi and Tamil have 2 interns each; every other language has 1. The total number of LIS interns and their language-wise allocation may be changed as and when required.

Who Can Apply

The same eligibility applies to all 23 language positions. Age limit: the age of the candidate must be below 35 years on the last date of application. The notice does not mention any age relaxation. Earlier interns: the Library does not engage the same Intern more than once. Candidates who have already completed their Internship programme at National Library shall not be considered. Language competence after selection: if, after final selection, an Intern is found not well competent in reading/writing the concerned language, a language board will be constituted, and on its recommendation the Library will terminate that internship with immediate effect. Candidates should check the official notification for the full eligibility conditions of each language position.

Pay and Tenure

The amount paid to the LIS Intern is to be treated as "Stipend": Rs 25,000/- (Rupees Twenty five thousand only) per month to each Intern. The stipend amount includes the medical insurance policy of the candidate. The candidate must obtain a compulsory medical insurance policy from a recognized insurance company for the period of the LIS Internship. The stipend will be paid within the 5th of every month. The internship lasts 01 (One) Year from the date of engagement of each Intern, with 40 hours per week of on-task activity. Five Day Week: all days except Saturday, Sundays and other public holidays followed at the National Library. Interns normally work at the Divisions posted from 9.30 am to 6:00 pm. Selection and Interview

Selection runs in this order: short-listing on merit, then an examination on LIS (MCQ), a practical exam and an interview. The Top-Scorer in each language would be selected as LIS Intern, and a panel would be drawn accordingly. A Screening/Selection Committee scrutinises all applications and short-lists on merit, based on the highest percentage at Master of Library & Information Science, BLISC, Graduation, 12 Standard and 10 Standard. Short-listed candidates would be called for interview on a 1:10 basis (top 10 candidates by percentage) or any other criteria decided by the Competent Authority. Total marks are 100: 45% weightage on Academic & Professional Qualification (Academic 15 marks, Professional 30 marks) and 55% weightage on the subjective, practical and interview stages. These stages carry 100 marks, reduced to 55%. The notice does not mention the number of questions, the duration
