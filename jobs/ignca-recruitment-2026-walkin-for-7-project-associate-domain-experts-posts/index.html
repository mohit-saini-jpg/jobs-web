About IGNCA Recruitment 2026

The Indira Gandhi National Centre for the Arts (IGNCA) is an autonomous institution under the Ministry of Culture, Government of India, visualised as a centre encompassing the study of all arts within the context of the natural and human environment. IGNCA acts as the implementing body for the National Mission on Cultural Mapping (NMCM), an initiative aiming to preserve and promote India's cultural heritage and revitalise rural economies. These positions are purely on a contractual basis under the NMCM Unit for an initial period of one year.

Posts and Eligibility

The notification invites applications for a total of 7 contractual vacancies across two distinct designations. Both posts require one year of experience in Government, Semi-Government, or recognised institutions in the domain of preparing research-based write-ups, documentation, and cultural profiles on tangible and intangible heritage, rituals, belief systems, oral histories, and cultural traditions across different parts of India. For both posts, the following desirable experience is outlined: Experience in community engagement and collection of primary data, including oral histories, traditional knowledge, customs, rituals, festivals, and folk traditions. Working knowledge of digital documentation, cultural mapping, and database management. Proficiency with computer applications. Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English and Hindi.
