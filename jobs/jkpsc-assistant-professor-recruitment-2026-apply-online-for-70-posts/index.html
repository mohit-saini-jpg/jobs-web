JKPSC Assistant Professor Selection Process

Selection for the JKPSC Assistant Professor Recruitment 2026 will be made in terms of Rule 45 of the J&K Public Service Commission (Business & Procedure) Rules, 2021, as amended up to date. The process includes: A written examination consisting of a single paper of 3 hours' duration, carrying 120 multiple choice questions. The syllabus is available on the official JKPSC website. The written examination will be held at Srinagar and Jammu centres, as opted by the candidate; centre allotment is at the sole discretion of the Commission. Admit cards will be available for download about two weeks before the examination date on jkpsc.nic.in. Document verification will follow, where candidates must produce original certificates supporting all claims made in the application, including educational qualification, age, category, and domicile.
