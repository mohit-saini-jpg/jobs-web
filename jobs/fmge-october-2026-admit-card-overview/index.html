FMGE October 2026 Admit Card - Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 22/10/2026

FMGE October 2026 Admit Card is scheduled to be released by the National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS) on 27 October 2026. Registered candidates will be able to download their admit cards through the official website, natboard.edu.in, using their candidate login credentials. The Foreign Medical Graduate Examination will be conducted on 31 October 2026.

NBEMS released the examination city intimation information on 20 October 2026. The admit card will provide the exact examination centre address, reporting time and important instructions. The admit-card release is scheduled for 27 October, but activation of the download link has not yet been independently confirmed.

FMGE October 2026 Admit Card Release Date

NBEMS has scheduled the release of the FMGE October 2026 admit card for 27 October 2026.

Candidates who successfully registered for the October 2026 examination will be able to access their hall tickets through the official FMGE examination portal after NBEMS activates the download facility.

The admit card is an essential document for entry into the examination centre. It will contain candidate details, the allotted test centre, examination schedule and reporting instructions.

Candidates should download the document, verify the information and retain a clear printout for examination day.

How to Download FMGE October 2026 Admit Card?

After the admit-card facility becomes active, candidates can follow these steps.

  • Visit natboard.edu.in.
  • Open the Examinations section.
  • Select the Foreign Medical Graduate Examination (FMGE).
  • Choose the October 2026 examination session.
  • Open the relevant application or candidate login facility.
  • Enter the registered User ID and Password.
  • Complete any verification displayed by the portal.
  • Find the FMGE October 2026 Admit Card option.
  • Open and download the hall ticket.
  • Verify the personal details, examination centre and reporting instructions.
  • Take a clear printout and preserve a copy for examination day.

What Documents Are Required for FMGE October 2026 Exam?

Candidates must carry the documents prescribed in the official FMGE examination instructions.

  • Printed FMGE October 2026 admit card.
  • Original valid photo identity document accepted by NBEMS.
  • Any additional photograph or identification document specifically required by the information bulletin or admit card.

Candidates should check the exact accepted ID types and document specifications in the current NBEMS information bulletin.

The examination city intimation slip alone is not valid for entry into the examination centre.

FMGE October 2026 Exam Day Instructions

  • Download and print the admit card before the examination.
  • Check the allotted examination centre address carefully.
  • Reach the venue according to the reporting time printed on the admit card.
  • Carry the prescribed original identity document.
  • Follow all security and identity-verification instructions.
  • Do not bring prohibited electronic devices or other restricted items into the examination centre.
  • Follow the directions issued by NBEMS and the examination centre staff.

Candidates should follow the latest admit-card instructions if they differ from general guidance.

FMGE October 2026 Result Date

The FMGE October 2026 result is scheduled to be declared by 30 November 2026.

NBEMS will publish the result through its official examination website.

Candidates should check the official result notice for the publication date, individual result access method and further instructions.

FMGE October 2026 Admit Card Not Downloading?

If the admit-card facility is active but a candidate cannot access the document, the following checks may help.

  • Confirm that the official FMGE October 2026 login has been activated.
  • Enter the correct registered User ID and Password.
  • Check whether the examination session selected is October 2026.
  • Use the authorised password recovery option if credentials are forgotten.
  • Review any NBEMS notices about document deficiencies or application issues.
  • Contact the official NBEMS support facility if the issue continues.

Candidates should avoid using third-party login services and should not share their passwords or OTPs.
