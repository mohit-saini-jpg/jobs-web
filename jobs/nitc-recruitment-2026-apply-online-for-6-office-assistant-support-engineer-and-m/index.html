NITC Eligibility 2026

Candidates for all posts must be below 56 years of age as on 1 January 2026. For the Support Engineer (Civil) post, the three years of required experience must cover: handling maintenance and civil works supervising repair and maintenance activities preparing and estimating works coordinating with contractors checking measurements and bills monitoring ongoing works maintaining buildings and infrastructure making sure works are done to quality standards and on time keeping the relevant records in line with the applicable rules, standards and procedures The three years of experience is required for the two Office Assistant posts and the Support Engineer post. For the GYM Trainer post, experience is only desirable. See the official notification for the full eligibility of each post.

Q2What is the time limit on the last day?
A
The online form must be submitted by 5.00 PM on 4 November 2026.
Q3Is a written examination certain?
A
No. A written examination may be held depending on the number of applications received, and applicants will be told if it is. The written examination or interview is tentatively set for 5 November 2026.
Q4What mark is needed for the General Administration post?
A
You need any degree from a recognized board with a minimum of 60% aggregate, plus three years of experience in any administrative office.
Q5Can a diploma holder apply for Office Assistant (Accounts)?
A
Yes. The post accepts Graduation or Equivalent, or a Diploma in Commercial Practice. You also need three years of accounts experience, including GST filing, TDS compliance and tally.
Q6Do GYM Trainers need experience?
A
No, it is not mandatory. One year of experience as a GYM Trainer in a reputed Gymnasium is only desirable. A Plus Two (10+2) and a valid certification in Fitness GYM Training are mandatory.
Q7Which degree is required for Support Engineer (Civil)?
A
B.Tech/BE in civil engineering, along with three years of experience as a Civil Engineer or Support Engineer handling maintenance and civil works.
Q8Will selected candidates be employed directly by NITC?
A
No. The notice says the posts will be filled through a manpower agency at NITC hostels.
