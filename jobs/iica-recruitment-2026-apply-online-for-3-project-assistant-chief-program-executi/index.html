About IICA

The Indian Institute of Corporate Affairs is an autonomous institution registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860 by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, Government of India. Its 14-acre campus is at Plot No.6, 7 & 8, Sector-5, IMT Manesar, District-Gurugram, Haryana. It provides knowledge and professional training through five Schools and eleven Centres, and also serves as a Government think tank on corporate laws, policies and governance.

IICA Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

All applications received by the due date are screened against the minimum educational qualifications and experience prescribed. The duly constituted Selection Committee shortlists candidates. The Institute reserves the right to devise its own shortlisting criteria. Shortlisted candidates are called for a presentation and/or interview at IICA Campus, Manesar or through video conference, on a date and time communicated separately. Holding the minimum qualifications does not by itself entitle a candidate to be called. IICA communicates only with shortlisted candidates and does not entertain interim correspondence or personal enquiries. No TA/DA is permissible for attending the interview. Final results will be published on the IICA website once the process concludes. Selected candidates may have to undergo a medical examination, if required.

Service Conditions

The engagement is for one year at first, and may be extended or curtailed depending on performance and requirement. WNS Project positions can be extended up to the duration of the Project, as they are co-terminus with it. The assignment is full time. The person must attend office on all working days, and on holidays if required. Selected candidates must sign a contract with IICA and join the duties immediately. Either side can end the contract with two months' notice, or two months' consolidated emoluments in lieu. A consultant cannot apply for any position inside or outside IICA while the contract is valid, or before 02 years of joining the position (where the contract provides for extension after one year), whichever is later. To apply internally for another position, the consultant must terminate the present contract. Leave and increments are as per the policy of the Institute.

IICA Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1Does completed articleship count as experience for the Project Assistant post?
A
Yes. Successful completion of articleship of CA/CS/ICWA is considered equivalent to one year of experience.
Q2Will I get HRA or DA along with the monthly pay?
A
No. The Rs. 75,000/-, Rs. 1,00,000/- and Rs. 40,000/- amounts are consolidated, and no other allowances or benefits are admissible.
Q3Can a candidate older than 63 apply?
A
The limit of 63 years may be relaxed, but only in the case of an exceptional candidate.
Q4Where will the interview take place?
A
The presentation and/or interview will be held at IICA Campus, Manesar or through video conference.
Q5Which document is accepted as proof of date of birth?
A
Only the date of birth recorded in the Matriculation, Higher Secondary Examination Certificate or an equivalent certificate is accepted, and no later request to change it will be considered.
Q6Will my application be rejected if I do not attach certificates?
A
Yes. An application received without copies of the required certificates will be rejected, and no appeal will be entertained. Mark sheets alone in place of a Degree, Diploma or Certificate are also not accepted.
Q7Does the Project Assistant job involve travel?
A
Yes. Travel to field areas across India for data collection, meetings and business development is a must.
Q8How long can the WNS Project posts continue?
A
They start with one year and can be extended up to the duration of the Project, based on performance and project requirements.
Q9Can a selected candidate leave before the contract ends?
A
Yes. The contract can be ended by giving two months' notice or two months' consolidated emoluments in lieu.
Q10Can canvassing affect my application?
A
Yes. Canvassing in any form will disqualify the candidate.
