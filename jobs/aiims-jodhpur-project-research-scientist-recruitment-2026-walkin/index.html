AIIMS Jodhpur Project Research Scientist Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

AIIMS Jodhpur will hold a walk-in interview on 03 December 2026; reporting starts at 09:00 AM and closes at 09:30 AM at Room No. 220, School of Public Health, 2nd Floor, Academic Block, AIIMS Jodhpur. Two Project Research Scientist-I posts (one Medical, one Non-Medical) are to be filled on a purely temporary basis under an ICMR-funded extramural research project, with pay of ₹67,000 + 20% HRA (Medical) and ₹56,000 + 20% HRA (Non-Medical) per month. The maximum age is 45 years as on the date of the walk-in interview.

About AIIMS Jodhpur Project

The posts are in an Extramural Research Project funded by ICMR, titled "Generating evidence for lowering the screening age threshold in the NP-NCD program through Economic Evaluation and Decision Modelling of Mass and High-Risk Population-Based Screening (Mass-PBS and HR-PBS) of Youth (18–29 Years) in Rajasthan for Diabetes and Hypertension". The Principal Investigator is Dr. Manoj Kumar Gupta, Department of Community Medicine & Family Medicine, AIIMS Jodhpur.

Posts and Eligibility

Master's degree in Public Health or allied sciences with more than 2 years of experience in Non-Communicable Disease (NCD)-related projects. Proficiency in Hindi, English, and the local language. Master's degree in Public Health or allied sciences with 2 years of experience in Non-Communicable Disease (NCD)-related projects. Age concession to the extent of service rendered in other research projects may be admissible for experienced and skilled persons on a case-to-case basis. All educational, professional and technical qualifications should be from a recognized Board/University and full-time. The experience requirement should be experience acquired after obtaining the minimum educational qualification required for the post. AIIMS Jodhpur Recruitment 2026 Job Description

Overall coordination, supervision, and management of research activities, ensuring adherence to the approved study protocol. Coordinate training programmes for doctors, project staff, and other stakeholders. Supervise record maintenance, data management, statistical analysis, report writing, and documentation. Manage project budgets, monitor expenditure, and perform other duties assigned by the Principal Investigator. Overall conduct, coordination & supervision of all research activities. Maintain project timeline, coordinate with PI/Co-I's, patient recruitment, implementation & training, data analysis, report writing.

Essential qualification and experience certificates enclosed with the application form; otherwise the application is considered incomplete. A passport size photo (the application format has a space for it). A "No Objection Certificate" at the time of interview, for persons working in Govt. or Public Sector undertaking.

What Happens on the Day

Reporting starts at 09:00 AM on 3 December 2026 and closes at 09:30 AM the same day; no additional candidates will be entertained for interview after that. A written examination will be conducted if a large number of eligible applications are received. Mere fulfilment of the essential qualification and experience does not confer any right to be called for the interview. Appointment will be subject to verification of original documents and medical fitness.
