Kushinagar Anganwadi Helper Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Only female candidates are eligible to apply for the advertised Anganwadi Helper vacancies. The notification specifies residence, educational qualification and age-related conditions that candidates must satisfy. The minimum educational qualification for the post of Anganwadi Helper is Intermediate (Class 12th) or equivalent. The notification states that the merit will be determined from the Intermediate qualification up to postgraduate qualification. Candidates should use the educational qualification and marks information specified in their official academic documents while completing the online application. The prescribed minimum age for the Anganwadi Helper post is 18 years, while the maximum age is 35 years. The age will be calculated as on 1 July 2026. The date of birth mentioned in the candidate's High School marksheet and certificate will be considered valid for determining age.

Kushinagar Anganwadi Helper Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

The selection process will be conducted through the departmental portal on the basis of applications received online and according to the applicable government instructions. The notification specifically states that no written examination or interview will be conducted for the selection process. The merit for Anganwadi Helper will be determined based on educational qualifications from Intermediate up to postgraduate level, as specified in the notification. If no eligible candidate of a particular category is available in the Gram Sabha, selection will be carried out according to the category-wise procedure through the concerned Nyaya Panchayat, as stated in the notification.
