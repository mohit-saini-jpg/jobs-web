ONGC CA CMA Industrial Trainee Eligibility Criteria 2026

Applicants must have passed the relevant CA or CMA Intermediate examination, secured at least 60% overall marks in Class 12 and been born on or after 1 January 2000. Candidates must satisfy all three conditions. Passing CA/CMA Intermediate alone is insufficient if the Class 12 percentage or date-of-birth requirement is not met.

ONGC CA CMA Industrial Trainee Stipend 2026

Selected candidates will receive a monthly stipend of Rs. 22,000 to Rs. 27,000 , depending on the training location and applicable training year. Important: The stipend table specifies Year 1 and Year 2 rates. However, the CA training duration is 9-12 months and CMA training duration is 15-18 months. Candidates should not assume that every trainee will complete two full years or receive the Year 2 stipend. Leave will be governed by the respective ICAI or ICMAI rules and regulations. Selected trainees must make their own arrangements for boarding, lodging and travelling.

ONGC CA CMA Industrial Trainee Selection Process 2026

Assessment of prescribed educational eligibility. Consideration of the stated ONGC ward and first-attempt preference criteria. Interview, where conducted, through online, offline or telephonic mode. Final selection at the discretion of ONGC. The notification does not specify a separate written examination, minimum interview marks or detailed selection weightage. Candidates must attend any interview at their own expense.

ONGC CA CMA Industrial Trainee Recruitment 2026 Important Instructions

Applicants must have passed CA or CMA Intermediate and secured at least 60% overall in Class 12. Candidates must have been born on or after 1 January 2000. The application must be submitted through the prescribed Google Form by 15 November 2026. Both CA/CMA Intermediate group marksheets, including failed attempts, must be uploaded separately. Preference will be given to ONGC wards and candidates who passed the examination on their first attempt. Interview may be conducted online, offline or telephonically. Candidates must bear their own interview expenses. Selected trainees must arrange their own accommodation, boarding and travel. ONGC reserves the right to make the final selection.
