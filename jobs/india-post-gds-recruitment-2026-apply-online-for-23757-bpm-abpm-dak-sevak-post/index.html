Post NameBrief Role
Branch Postmaster (BPM)Overall in-charge of a single-handed Branch Post Office (B.O.) - day-to-day postal operations, IPPB transactions, marketing/promotion of Department services, mail conveyance and delivery. Must arrange own accommodation for the Branch Post Office and reside in the post village.
Assistant Branch Postmaster (ABPM)Assists the BPM - sale of stamps/stationery, mail conveyance and doorstep delivery, IPPB transactions; may perform combined BPM duties when required. Must reside within the delivery jurisdiction of the concerned Post Office.
Dak SevakEngaged in Post Offices, Sorting Offices, Mail Offices, Transit Mail Offices, or Delivery Centres - handling of mail/parcels, sale of stamps and stationery, IPPB transactions, and assisting the Postmaster/Sub-Postmaster in office functioning. Must reside within the delivery jurisdiction of the concerned Post Office.

India Post GDS Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Eligibility for all GDS posts (BPM/ABPM/Dak Sevak) is assessed as on the date of the notification (for qualification) and the last date of application submission (for age), as detailed below. Note: Past experience or service of any kind is not considered for selection, as per the notification.

India Post GDS Recruitment 2026 Salary (TRCA)

GDS are not regular Central Government employees; they are paid emoluments in the form of Time Related Continuity Allowance (TRCA), which carries an annual increase of 3% (subject to fulfilment of conditions under the GDS Rules), along with Dearness Allowance on TRCA as declared by the Government of India from time to time. GDS are also entitled to GDS Gratuity and the Service Discharge Benefit Scheme (akin to the National Pension System for regular employees).

India Post GDS Recruitment 2026 Application Fee

A fee of ₹100/- (Rupees One Hundred only) is payable by applicants for all posts notified in their choice of Division. Payment can be made online through recognized Credit/Debit cards, Net Banking, or UPI (usage charges as applicable will be levied). The fee, once paid, is non-refundable, and payment must be completed strictly within the application closing date and time (up to 1700 hrs). Fee-exempted categories: All female applicants, SC/ST applicants, PwBD applicants, and Transwomen applicants Applicants who are exempted may submit their application without making any payment

India Post GDS Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

There is no written examination for GDS engagement. Selection is entirely merit-based, computed from the candidate's Class 10 (SSE) academic record. India Post GDS Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Applications must be submitted online only, in two stages - One-Time Registration (OTR) followed by the Online Application Form.
  • 2Visit the GDS Online Engagement Portal: https://indiapost.gov.in/gdsonlineengagement
  • 3Complete One-Time Registration (OTR) using a valid mobile number and email ID (verified via OTP), entering personal details and Class 10 board/year of passing information, to generate a unique Registration Number
  • 4Log in using the Registration Number and password received via SMS, then click "Apply Online" under the "Active Notification" tab
  • 5Fill in Personal Details, Board/Educational Details (Circle, Board Name, passing year, marks obtained), and select Division and post preferences based on eligible vacancies
  • 6Upload a recent photograph (JPG/JPEG, 30-100 KB, 320×400 px, 4:5 aspect ratio) and signature (JPG/JPEG, 20-100 KB, 300×120 px, 5:2 aspect ratio) as per prescribed specifications
  • 7Preview the application and make any corrections before final submission
  • 8Pay the applicable fee (if not exempted) through the available online payment modes within 10 minutes of initiating payment
  • 9Submit the application on or before the last date, and use the two-day edit/correction window (23-24 September 2026) if any corrections are needed
