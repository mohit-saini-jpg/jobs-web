RMLIMS Vacancy 2026 - Post-Wise Details

RMLIMS has advertised 74 vacancies across six posts under Group B, C and D. The Programmer, Medical Physicist and Radiation Safety Officer posts fall under Group B; Stenographer and Receptionist fall under Group C; and Hospital Attendant Grade-II falls under Group D. The category-wise breakup for each post is given in parentheses in the table below. In addition, horizontal reservation for Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD) is provided: 1 post each in Receptionist, Stenographer and Hospital Attendant Gr-II categories. The Institute has clarified that the number of advertised vacancies may increase or decrease.

RMLIMS Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection will be made through a two-stage Computer Based Test (CBT), conducted bilingually in Hindi and English. Screening Examination: Qualifying in nature, held only to shortlist candidates for the Main Examination. Marks from this stage are not carried forward, and only 10 times the number of advertised posts (category-wise) are shortlisted for the Main Exam Main Examination: Final selection is based solely on marks obtained in this exam, prepared category-wise based on merit and reservation norms Exam Pattern: Both Screening and Mains exams carry 100 marks each, of 2 hours' duration, consisting only of Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) — 60 marks on the subject related to the post, 10 marks each on General English, General Knowledge, Reasoning, and Mathematical Aptitude Negative Marking: 1 mark is awarded for each correct answer; 0.25 mark is deducted for each wrong answer Qualifying
