About IIIT Pune

IIIT Pune describes itself as an "Institute of National Importance". It was set up by an act of Parliament under the Public Private Partnership (PPP) model, under the Ministry of Education, Government of India. The Institute runs B.Tech, M.Tech and Ph.D. programmes from its permanent campus in the foothills of the Western Ghats at Talegaon (Maval), Dist. Pune, Maharashtra - 410507.

Service Conditions

Grade-II (Level-10): This post is purely contractual. The contract runs for one year from joining and is renewed every year on performance, up to a maximum of 5 years. Grade-I (Level-12): One year of probation, then regularisation if probation and the other Institute requirements are met. Probation may be extended. Appointees are covered under the National Pension Scheme (NPS 2004) as per Government of India rules. IIIT Pune Teaching Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1Can I apply for Grade-II without any experience?
A
Yes. Annexure-II lists Nil experience and Nil credit points for Assistant Professor Grade-II. You still need a Ph.D. in the relevant or equivalent discipline and a first class in your preceding degrees.
Q2How much experience is needed for Assistant Professor Grade-I?
A
You need 03 years after the Ph.D., or 06 years of total teaching and research experience in a reputed academic institute, R&D lab or relevant industry. You also need 20 cumulative credit points.
Q3What counts as first class if my university does not define it?
A
The notice treats minimum 60% marks or 6.5 CGPA as first class.
Q4Is the Grade-II post permanent?
A
No. It is purely contractual. The contract runs one year at a time and is renewed on performance, up to a maximum of 5 years.
Q5What is the written test cut-off?
A
The cut-off is 35% for SC/ST/PWD candidates and 40% for all other categories. The test is qualifying only, and the final merit depends entirely on the presentation-cum-interview.
Q6Do women candidates pay a lower fee?
A
Yes. Female candidates pay the same as SC/ST/PwBD candidates: 1000 + GST 18%, which is Rs. 1180/-.
Q7I want to apply in two departments. Is one form enough?
A
No. You need a separate complete application and fee for each post or department. A single form covering more than one department is summarily rejected.
Q8Where do I send the hard copy?
A
Send it to The Registrar, Indian Institute of Information Technology Pune, PO – Talegaon, Pune, Maharashtra – 410507. It must arrive by 9 December 2026, 05:30 PM.
Q9I did my Ph.D. at IIIT Pune. Can I apply?
A
Only if you have 3 years of experience outside IIIT Pune after your Ph.D. This is the notice's in-breeding restriction.
Q10Will travel expenses be paid for the test or interview?
A
No. No TA/DA is paid for the written test, the presentation-cum-interview or any other stage at the Talegaon campus.
