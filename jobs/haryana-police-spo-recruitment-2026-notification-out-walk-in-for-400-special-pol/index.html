Haryana Police SPO Eligibility 2026

Eligible category: Ex-servicemen of the Armed Forces/Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs), or ex-Constables of the disbanded Haryana State Industrial Security Force (HSISF)/Haryana Armed Police (HAP) Battalions. Educational qualification: Minimum 10+2 from a recognized board. Minimum service: At least 5 years of service in the Armed Forces/CAPFs. Candidates must not have been removed/dismissed from service in the Armed Forces/CAPFs on grounds of indiscipline, misconduct, or medical unfitness. Medical category at discharge must be ‘A’. Character certificate at discharge must be ‘Exemplary/Outstanding’. Preference is given to candidates with experience handling telecommunication equipment, particularly those who served in the Signal Corps of the Armed Forces/CAPFs. Haryana Police SPO Selection Process

Important: No written or physical test will be conducted for engagement of SPOs. Candidates appear in person on the date fixed for their district with the completed application form, original documents, one set of attested photocopies, affidavit, and photographs. Documents pertaining to qualification, age, caste/category, and other certificates claimed in the application are verified from the concerned issuing authorities. Selected candidates undergo a medical examination before an appointment letter is issued. Character and antecedents of selected candidates are verified from the concerned district police authority by the Police Telecommunication Wing, Haryana, before issuing appointment letters. Selected SPOs undergo a 15-day capsule course to reorient themselves to Police Department requirements.

Documents Required

Duly filled application form (original) All relevant original documents (service record, discharge certificate, medical category and character certificate at discharge, educational certificates, age proof, category certificate) One set of attested photocopies of all relevant documents Duly sworn affidavit as per the prescribed format Aadhaar Card photocopy PAN Card photocopy Bank Passbook photocopy 4 passport-size colour photographs

Terms & Conditions

Engagement is for 1 year, or until regular appointment of personnel to these posts, whichever is earlier. SPOs will not be posted at their home police station; preference for the home district is given at deployment, subject to availability of posts. SPOs may be deployed anywhere in Haryana during emergent situations for short durations. Services can be terminated at any time before the 1-year period, without notice, on grounds of indiscipline, misconduct, unsatisfactory performance, or non-requirement, by order of the Superintendent of Police, Telecommunication. The order/decision of SP Telecommunication, Haryana is final; no appeal lies before any superior officer. The category options listed on the application form are General (GEN), BC-A, BC-B, and SC - as per Haryana state category classification.

  • 1Download and fill the application form (in CAPITAL LETTERS) covering personal details, service details (enlistment/enrollment date, discharge date, medical category and character certificate at discharge), Aadhaar, PAN, bank passbook, category, education, and address details.
