EIL Associate Modellers Vacancy 2026 Details

The 22 vacancies are distributed across six position codes based on discipline. Position codes ending in "1" are for Diploma holders (6 years relevant post-qualification experience) and those ending in "2" are for B.E./B.Tech/B.Sc (Engg.) holders (4 years relevant post-qualification experience), except Position Code C, which is open to Diploma in Chemical holders only. EIL Associate Modellers Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must meet the educational qualification and relevant experience prescribed for the position code they are applying to, as on the cut-off date of 31 July 2026. EIL Associate Modellers Selection Process

On the day of selection, applications and certificates will be screened to confirm eligibility for the post applied for. Only candidates meeting the eligibility requirements in full will proceed further. The selection process will be conducted at EIL's Delhi, Gurugram, Chennai, Kolkata and Vadodara offices for position codes A1 to F2. Candidates must report at 08:00 AM; the exact date and venue will be intimated separately by email. Depending on the number of candidates, the process may extend up to 08:00 PM and, if required, continue the next day, in which case candidates must arrange their own stay. Only candidates who have registered online and report with a printout bearing a valid registration number will be considered.
