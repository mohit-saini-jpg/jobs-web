Tamil Nadu NEET UG 2026 Stray Round Seat Allotment : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 22/10/2026

Tamil Nadu NEET UG 2026 Stray Round Seat Allotment Result has been released on 22 October 2026 by the Directorate of Medical Education and Research (DMER), Tamil Nadu. The Selection Committee has published separate provisional MBBS/BDS allotment lists for Government Quota, Management Quota and the 7.5% reservation for government school students at tnmedicalselection.net.

Allotted candidates must complete the applicable tuition fee payment and download their provisional allotment orders by 26 October 2026 at 12 noon. Candidates must follow the official reporting instructions because downloading the allotment order is treated as joining under the stray-round counselling rules.

Tamil Nadu NEET UG 2026 Stray Vacancy Allotment Result Released

The Tamil Nadu Selection Committee has published provisional allotment lists for the stray vacancy round of MBBS and BDS counselling for the academic session 2026–27.

The official website lists the following results dated 22 October 2026:

  • Government Quota, including the applicable 92.5% quota seats.
  • Management Quota, including seats covered by the relevant counselling provisions.
  • 7.5% special reservation for eligible Tamil Nadu government school students.

Candidates who participated in the applicable counselling category can download the corresponding PDF and search for their application number or general rank.

The lists are provisional. Candidates must complete the prescribed allotment-order download, fee payment and physical reporting procedures to secure admission.

Tamil Nadu NEET UG 2026 Provisional Allotment Order Download

Candidates who receive a stray vacancy seat must access the official UG counselling portal to download their individual provisional allotment order.

The scheduled download period is from 22 October until 26 October 2026 at 12 noon.

The Selection Committee states that applicable tuition fee payment must be completed before the provisional allotment order can be downloaded.

Students should check the fee payable for their particular college and course through the official portal.

How to Download Tamil Nadu NEET UG 2026 Allotment Order?

  • Visit the official Tamil Nadu medical selection website.
  • Open the UG MBBS/BDS counselling section.
  • Choose the applicable stray vacancy round candidate service.
  • Sign in using the credentials requested by the portal.
  • Check the individual allotment information.
  • Complete the applicable tuition fee payment using the authorised online payment options.
  • Download the provisional allotment order after satisfying the payment conditions.
  • Check the college and reporting details printed on the order.
  • Save the payment receipt and allotment order.
  • Report to the allotted institution within the notified deadline.

Do not share the counselling login ID, password or OTP with other individuals.

Tamil Nadu NEET UG 2026 Reporting Last Date

The last date to report to the allotted medical or dental institution is 26 October 2026 at 5 PM.

Candidates must appear in person with the required original certificates and scanned copies for verification at the selected institution.

The college will check academic eligibility, community and reservation documents, and other applicable admission details.

Allotted candidates should verify the reporting location, applicable fees and document requirements before travelling.

Important Rule: Downloading the Allotment Order Counts as Joining

The Tamil Nadu Selection Committee has issued a specific instruction for candidates allotted seats during the stray vacancy round.

Downloading the provisional allotment order is considered joining the allotted seat under the official stray-round counselling provisions.

However, candidates must still physically report to their allotted colleges within the prescribed deadline.

If a candidate downloads the allotment order but does not report, the Selection Committee may treat the case as discontinuation and apply the prescribed fee and counselling penalties.

Candidates should therefore read the complete admission conditions before downloading the order or deciding not to join the allotted institution.

Tamil Nadu NEET UG 2026 Stray Round Additional Security Deposit

The Selection Committee requires an additional security deposit for applicable candidates participating in the general stray vacancy counselling category.

CourseAdditional Security Deposit
MBBSRs. 5,00,000
BDSRs. 2,00,000

The additional security deposit is liable to forfeiture if an allotted candidate fails to join the course, subject to the official counselling rules.

Eligible SC, SCA, ST and SCC candidates meeting the prescribed annual parental or guardian income limit of less than Rs. 2.5 lakh and the income-certificate submission requirements are exempt from the additional security deposit.

The 7.5% special reservation for Tamil Nadu government school students has separate fee exemptions.

Tamil Nadu NEET UG 2026 Stray Round Registration Fee

QuotaRegistration Fee

These registration charges are different from the additional security deposit and applicable tuition fee.

Tamil Nadu NEET UG 2026 Government School Students 7.5% Quota

The Selection Committee has published a separate stray vacancy provisional allotment list for candidates under the 7.5% special reservation for eligible Tamil Nadu government school students.

The official allotment PDF dated 22 October 2026 contains seven candidate entries, all allotted to BDS institutions.

The separate counselling schedule for this special-reservation category specifies that no processing fee, security deposit or tuition fee is applicable to eligible candidates under the 7.5% reservation.

Students must still download their provisional allotment orders and physically report to their allotted colleges within the prescribed dates.

Tamil Nadu NEET UG 2026 Stray Round Eligibility

The Selection Committee's stray vacancy counselling schedule specifies that only eligible candidates who are not holding an MBBS or BDS seat under the All India Quota or State Quota can participate.

The following candidates are not eligible:

  • Candidates who have already joined MBBS or BDS seats through All India Quota or State Quota counselling.
  • Candidates holding seats under Government, Management or NRI quotas covered by the stated restrictions.
  • Candidates who received a seat in Tamil Nadu State Counselling Round 3 but failed to join it.

Eligibility also depends on the applicable merit list, candidate category, registration status and counselling instructions.

What Happens If a Candidate Does Not Join the Stray Round Seat?

The Selection Committee has notified penalties for candidates who receive stray vacancy allotments but fail to join or discontinue their allotted courses.

Under the applicable general counselling rules, candidates may lose their security deposit, additional security deposit and tuition fee, and may be required to pay the prescribed discontinuation fee.

The Selection Committee also specifies that affected candidates will not be allowed to participate in Tamil Nadu UG medical counselling for the subsequent one academic year.

Students should refer to the applicable Government Quota or Management Quota prospectus for the precise discontinuation conditions.

Documents Required for Tamil Nadu NEET UG Stray Round Reporting

The official counselling notice requires candidates to report in person with original certificates and scanned copies of the original records.

Candidates should arrange the following documents where applicable:

  • Provisional allotment order issued by the Selection Committee.
  • NEET UG 2026 scorecard and admit card.
  • Class 10 and Class 12 academic certificates.
  • Valid photo identity proof.
  • Community and reservation certificates where applicable.
  • Nativity or domicile-related certificates where required.
  • Documents supporting 7.5% government school reservation eligibility, where applicable.
  • Income certificate where required for fee exemption.
  • Fee payment acknowledgement, where applicable.
  • Other original certificates specified in the relevant prospectus and college instructions.

The list is a preparatory checklist. Candidates must follow the complete document requirements published by the Selection Committee and allotted institution.
