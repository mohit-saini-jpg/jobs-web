GSSSB Municipal Accountant Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

The recruitment process consists of two stages. Stage 1 – Preliminary Examination (Objective/MCQ): A screening test of 150 marks, conducted in CBRT/OMR mode, to shortlist candidates for the Main Examination. Marks scored in the Preliminary Examination are not counted towards the final merit list. Stage 2 – Main Examination (Descriptive): Consists of two papers of 100 marks each. The final merit list is prepared based on marks obtained in the Main Examination only. Approximately seven times the number of advertised vacancies will be shortlisted for the Main Examination, based on availability. Candidates must appear in both papers of the Main Examination; absence in either paper renders the candidate ineligible for selection. Each question carries 1 mark, and 0.25 marks are deducted for every incorrect answer (negative marking). The Gujarati and English language papers are set at the Stand
