About NMPA

New Mangalore Port Authority is a port under the Govt. of India (Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways), located at Panambur, Mangalore - 575010. Its letterhead calls it "Fully Solar Powered" and "An ISO 9001:2015, 14001:2015, 45001:2018 & ISPS Compliant Port", and it carries the Sagarmala logo. The office phone numbers printed on the notice are 0824 - 2407341 and 2887399.

Who Can Apply

The vacancy is open to eligible and willing officers of Major Port Authorities who have the qualifications and experience set out in the Recruitment Rule (RR) in Annexure I. The RR was published in the Gazette of India: Extraordinary. It classes the post as Class-I and as a selection post, with a scale of pay of 10750-300-16750 (pre-revised). The crucial date for deciding eligibility is 20 November 2026 . Under the RR, absorption/deputation will be of officers holding analogous posts, or the post of Asst. Secretary Gr-I in the scale of pay of Rs.9100-15100 with 5 years regular service in the grade, in a Major Port Trust. The RR lists the following qualifications for direct recruitment: Essential (i): A degree from a recognised university. Essential (ii): Five years experience in executive cadre in the field of General Administration, Personnel, Industrial Relations etc. in an Industrial/

Pay and Tenure

The post is in the scale of pay of Rs.50,000-1,60,000 (Pre to pre revised Rs.10750-16750). Annexure VI sets out the following terms and conditions of deputation at New Mangalore Port Authority: Period of deputation: 3 years, extendable to 4 years. In exceptional circumstances it can be extended to 5 years. The Central Government decides this for a post whose holder is regarded as the Head of Department, and the Chairman decides it for any post below the level of Head of Department. Pay: The employee may choose to have pay fixed in the deputation post under the normal rules. Or the employee may draw the pay of the post held in the parent department plus a deputation allowance, under the conditions as modified from time to time. NMPA Senior Assistant Secretary Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

Only applications received through proper channel within the due dates, with all the required documents, will be considered for the post. Under the Annexure-III undertaking, the applicant agrees to accept the appointment if selected and not to withdraw. If any information given is found to be incorrect or false, the candidature will stand cancelled. If shortcomings are found after appointment, the service is liable to be terminated. The declaration in the application form states that false information makes the applicant liable to disqualification, apart from any legal action deemed fit. The NMPA Management reserves the right to make any changes if the need arises, without giving any reason.

NMPA Senior Assistant Secretary Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step
