About ESIC Recruitment 2026

Employees' State Insurance Corporation is a statutory body established under the Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948, operating under the administrative control of the Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India, with its headquarters at Panchdeep Bhawan, C.I.G. Marg, New Delhi. In view of statutory mandates under Section 37 of the ESI Act, 1948, Section 119 of the Social Security Code, 2020, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, ESIC is strengthening its Actuarial Branch and establishing a dedicated Data & Analytics Branch.

ESIC Selection Process 2026

Selection is conducted through a two-stage process: preliminary scrutiny and shortlisting, followed by a personal interview. There is no written or online test. Applications received online within the prescribed period are scrutinized for eligibility based on submitted documents. Eligible applications are assessed on essential and desirable qualifications along with years of relevant post-qualification experience. Candidates are shortlisted in order of merit strictly up to a ratio of 1:10 candidates per vacancy, or such other ratio determined by the Competent Authority. All candidates tied at the cut-off score are shortlisted. Lead and sign off periodic actuarial valuations of the ESI Scheme covering liabilities, assets, and funding position. Develop long-term financial projections (25 to 75 years) incorporating demographic, economic, morbidity, and policy assumptions. Design and oversee stress testing, scenario analysis, and sensitivity testing to assess scheme resilience. Advise ESIC on contribution rate adequacy, benefit structures, wage ceiling revisions, and scheme expansion proposals.
