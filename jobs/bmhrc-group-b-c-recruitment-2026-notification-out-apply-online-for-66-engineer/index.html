BMHRC Group B &amp; C Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

The BMHRC selection process consists of the following stages: Computer Based Test Skill test for the Personal Assistant post Document verification Medical fitness and final appointment formalities There will be no interview for these Group B and Group C non-gazetted posts. Selection will be based on performance in the CBT, the applicable skill test and successful document verification.

Personal Assistant Skill Test

Dictation: 10 minutes at 80 words per minute. English transcription: 50 minutes on a computer. Hindi transcription: 65 minutes on a computer. A relevant skill certificate must be furnished.

Documents Required

Class 10 certificate as proof of age Relevant degree and mark sheets Diploma or postgraduate qualification certificates, where applicable Registration certificate issued by the relevant State Council SC, ST, OBC, EWS or PwBD certificate in the prescribed Government of India format Experience certificate, where required No Objection Certificate for candidates employed in a government department, PSU or autonomous body Valid supporting documents for age relaxation Application form printout when called for document verification

Important Instructions for Applicants

The crucial date for determining age, qualification and experience is 16 October 2026. Submit a separate online application for every post. Examinations for different posts may be conducted during the same shift. Applicants must enter their name and date of birth exactly as recorded in the Class 10 certificate. No correction will be allowed after final submission. Admit cards will not be sent by post. No TA or DA will be paid for attending the examination. The vacancies are provisional and may increase or decrease. Selected candidates carry an All India service liability and may be posted anywhere in India. Candidates should rely on the official notification for complete eligibility conditions.
