KEA Residential School Teacher Eligibility Criteria

The same educational qualification applies to both the KK and RPC cadres: PUC with minimum 50% marks and a 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education; OR PUC with minimum 50% marks and a 4-year Bachelor's Degree in Elementary Education; OR PUC with minimum 50% marks and a 2-year Diploma in Education (Special Education) For candidates belonging to SC/ST/Category-1 of OBC, or a candidate with disability, the minimum marks required in PUC is relaxed to 45% AND the candidate must have passed the Teacher Eligibility Test (TET) conducted by the Government of Karnataka or the Government of India Candidates must possess the required qualification and supporting certificates by the last date of application submission - provisional results pending a final mark sheet are not accepted, and a qualification obtained after the due date cannot be claimed. KEA Residential School Teacher Selection Process

Selection is through a competitive written examination, conducted either as a Computer-Based Test (CBT) online, or an Offline OMR-based test, as decided for the post. The exam consists of two papers, each of 100 questions/100 marks, with a duration of 2 hours each: Paper-1: General knowledge of current affairs; everyday-life topics; school-level Indian Constitution topics; Indian History (with special reference to Karnataka); Indian Geography (with special reference to Karnataka); state and regional economics; Panchayat Raj institutions and rural cooperative societies, and Karnataka's economic development schemes; Karnataka's environment-related issues and development topics Paper-2: General Kannada, General English, and Computer Knowledge Candidates must score a minimum of 35% aggregate marks in the competitive exam to be considered eligible; those scoring below this are disqualified. A
PUC (min. 50%, or 45% for SC/ST/Cat-1/PwD) with a Diploma or Bachelor's Degree in Elementary Education (or Special Education), plus a passed TET (Teacher Eligibility Test).

Q8What is the selection process for KEA Residential School Teacher Recruitment 2026?

A competitive written exam (two papers of 100 marks each, with negative marking), plus a qualifying Kannada Language Test for candidates who need it. A minimum of 35% aggregate is required in the main exam.
