Who Was Considered for This Mock Allotment

The mock allotment covered two categories of candidates under KEA's combined option entry system, which allows UGCET and UGNEET preferences to be submitted together:

  • UGCET 2026 candidates who are in the process of Round 2 seat allotment for engineering and other CET-based courses.
  • UGNEET 2026 candidates who qualified for medical and dental admissions and had entered options for the first round.

Candidates who had not entered any options for medical or dental courses can still add these preferences during the option modification window, provided they are eligible.

Courses Covered Under This Mock Allotment

The UGCET Round 2 and UGNEET Round 1 mock allotment applies to admissions in the following courses:

  • Engineering
  • Architecture
  • Yoga and Naturopathy
  • Farm Sciences
  • Veterinary courses
  • B.Sc Nursing
  • B.Pharma
  • Pharm-D
  • BPT (Bachelor of Physiotherapy)
  • BPO
  • Allied Health Sciences
  • Medical and Dental courses

How to Check the Karnataka UGCET/UGNEET 2026 Mock Allotment Result

  • Visit the official KEA website at kea.kar.nic.in or cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea.
  • Locate the mock seat allotment link for UGCET-UGNEET 2026 on the homepage or notifications section.
  • Click on the relevant result link for your category (UGCET Round 2 or UGNEET Round 1).
  • Enter your login details when prompted, typically your CET application number and date of birth.
  • Click submit to proceed.
  • View your mock allotment result on the screen, showing your provisionally allotted college and course.
  • Verify the details - allotted institution, course, category, and rank used.
  • Review whether changes are needed in your list of options based on this outcome.
  • Make any changes to your options before the modification deadline of August 20, 2026, 9 AM.

How to Modify Your Options After the Mock Allotment

If a candidate is not satisfied with the provisional college or course shown in the mock allotment, KEA allows them to add, delete, or rearrange their option entries before the deadline. Key points to remember:

  • The modification window closes on August 20, 2026, at 9 AM.
  • Candidates who skipped medical or dental options earlier can still add them now, if eligible.
  • The mock allotment does not block or reserve a seat - it is only a preview based on current preferences.
  • The actual Round 2 seat allotment will be based on the final list of options as they stand after this modification window closes.

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • The mock seat allotment result does not represent a confirmed admission offer.
  • Seat allotment in the real round is carried out strictly based on candidate merit (rank) and the priority order of options entered.
  • UGNEET-qualified candidates who have not yet registered or linked their roll number under Clause-A or Clause-Y should complete this step by August 18, 2026, 6 PM, before proceeding to option entry.
  • Candidates should keep checking the official KEA website regularly, as KEA has indicated further updates will follow.
  • Details such as application fee, document verification schedule for Round 2, and the exact final allotment date were not specified in KEA's mock allotment notice - these should be confirmed directly from the official website.
