APSC Veterinary Officer Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for APSC VO/BVO Recruitment 2026, candidates must meet the nationality, domicile, educational, and age criteria detailed below. The candidate must be an Indian Citizen as defined in Articles 5 to 8 of the Constitution of India and must be a permanent resident of Assam. As proof of domicile, candidates must upload either a valid Permanent Resident Certificate issued in Assam for educational purposes or a valid Employment Exchange Registration Certificate.

APSC Veterinary Officer Qualification

Candidates must possess at least a Degree in Animal Husbandry & Veterinary Science from any recognized University/Institution. Applicants are required to upload relevant mark sheets/grade sheets of each semester/year reflecting the subjects, along with the final pass certificate.

APSC Veterinary Officer Age Limit

Candidates should not be less than 21 years and not more than 38 years of age as on 01-01-2026. Age is calculated based on the Class-X/Class-XII Examination Admit Card/Pass Certificate/Marksheet issued by a recognized Central/State Board/Council where the Date of Birth is clearly reflected; no other document is accepted for age proof. Candidates with less than 40% disability will not be considered for any relaxation applicable to Persons with Benchmark Disability.

APSC Veterinary Officer Selection Process

The selection procedure will be notified later by the Commission through a corrigendum/addendum. The Commission will decide the mode of selection considering the status, cadre, and grade of the post, and the number of applications received. The Commission may also conduct the examination/test zone-wise depending on the number of candidates from each district, based on the following six zones. Candidates must, if required, attend a personal interview/Viva-Voce at a place fixed by the Commission. The Commission does not defray travelling or other expenses of candidates summoned for interview.

APSC Veterinary Officer Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Applicants must apply online through the APSC Recruitment website; no other mode of application is accepted.
  • 2Visit the official APSC recruitment website: apscrecruitment.in
  • 3New applicants must click on 'Register Here' and complete the One-Time Registration (OTR) process with basic details, using a valid and active email address and mobile number
  • 4After registration, log in using the registered email/mobile number and password
  • 5Fill in all required registration details as asked in the form; mandatory fields are marked with an asterisk (*)
  • 6Read the eligibility criteria carefully before applying
  • 7Upload required documents/certificates (Date of Birth, Educational Qualifications, caste/category, etc.) in PDF format, not exceeding 4 MB, legible in print, scanned at 200 dpi in greyscale (recommended)
  • 8Upload a recent Photograph (50 KB – 200 KB) and Signature (50 KB – 200 KB)
  • 9Tick the declaration checkbox to enable the Preview option, and check all uploaded documents for clarity
  • 10Click 'Proceed to Pay' to continue, or 'Cancel' to make corrections
  • 11Complete the payment of the prescribed application fee
  • 12Note that no editing of the application is allowed after final submission
  • 13Take a printout of the submitted Online Application Form for future reference
  • 14Before filling the application form, candidates must note that certificates relating to age, caste, and minimum educational qualification will be fetched from the OTR profile. Candidates should update their OTR profile if any additional documents are to be uploaded in support of the advertised post.
