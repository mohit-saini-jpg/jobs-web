NHRC Eligibility Criteria 2026

Joint Director (Research): Master of Laws or Social Science or Statistics + 5 years research experience. Assistant Registrar: Bachelor's degree with Law. Senior Research Officer: Master's in Social Science or Statistics + 3 years research experience. Principal Private Secretary: Bachelor's degree. Assistant Information Officer (Assistant Director (Publication)): Bachelor's or PG Diploma in Journalism + 3 years supervisory experience in PR/publicity. Research Officer: Master's in Social Science (Desirable: Law + 3 years research). Deputy Superintendent of Police: Bachelor's degree + experience in investigation. Assistant Accounts Officer: Bachelor's degree + Subordinate Audit/Accounts Service exam + 3 years cash/accounts experience. Personal Assistant: 12th pass + shorthand 100 wpm + typing on computer. Inspector: Bachelor's degree + 5 years experience in vigilance/investigation/intellige
