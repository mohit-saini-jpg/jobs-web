SCR GDCE Notification 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Only serving regular/permanent employees of erstwhile South Central Railway (drawing pay on a substantive basis, excluding MACP/Ad-hoc promotion, etc.), working in all Departments/Branches of Hd.Qrs./Divisions/Workshops/Units. Candidates working in a grade pay/level lower than the grade pay/level of the notified posts are eligible. Candidates cannot apply for the same post they are presently working on. Employees working in a 7th CPC Level lower than the Grade Pay/Scale for which GDCE is being conducted are eligible to appear. SCR GDCE Notification 2026 Selection Process

Selection is through a Single Stage Computer Based Test (CBT), followed by Document Verification (DV) and Medical Examination. There is no interview. Only a single application for all three categories of notified posts has to be submitted by the candidate through the official RRC website link. Question papers are Objective Multiple Choice Type, available in English, Hindi, Telugu, Kannada, Tamil, Marathi, and Urdu. Negative Marking: 1/3rd of the marks allotted to a question is deducted for each wrong answer. Minimum qualifying marks: UR – 40%, OBC – 30%, SC – 30%, ST – 25%. These percentages may be relaxed by 2% for PwBD candidates in case of a shortfall against reserved vacancies. In case of a tie in marks, the older candidate is ranked higher; if age is also equal, alphabetical order of name (a to z) is used to break the tie. SCR GDCE Notification 2026 Document Verification

After CBT results are declared, candidates equal to the number of notified vacancies will be called for Document Verification/Medical Examination; the e-Call letter will be available for download from the RRC website. At Document Verification, shortlisted candidates must produce original documents of Date of Birth, Educational Qualification, Caste Certificate, relieving letter, etc., along with one set of self-attested photocopies of all certificates, 2 recent passport-size photographs (self-attested on the back), and a valid photo ID - the Railway Identity Card issued by the candidate's Department in ORIGINAL is mandatory on the day of Document Verification. Certificates in languages other than English or Hindi must be accompanied by an attested translation in English/Hindi.

RRC South Central Railway GDCE Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

Only eligible serving regular/permanent South Central Railway employees (excluding RPF/RPSF) may apply - this is an internal departmental recruitment, not open to the general public. Candidates should report for CBT/Document Verification with the relieving letter (e-Call letter) along with a valid Railway Photo ID card - the name, date of birth, and photo on the ID must match the details furnished in the online application, failing which the candidate may not be permitted to appear. Only applications sent through ONLINE mode are valid - offline/manual applications will be treated as invalid. Applications without a scanned photo/signature, incomplete applications, multiple applications by the same candidate, or applications without valid Community Certificate/proof of age/requisite educational qualification proof will be treated as invalid. There is no supplementary CBT for absentee candidates under any circumstances; requests for postponement of any activity or for change of venue, date, or shift will not be entertained. Selected candidates under GDCE will undergo the same duration of initial training as directly recruited employees through RRBs. There is no separate reservation of posts for Economically Weaker Sections (EWS) in this GDCE, as per Railway Board's letter No. E(NG)I-2018/PM1/23 dated 02.05.2019 (RBE-72/2019). Selected employees are liable to be posted anywhere on erstwhile South Central Railway; the decision of Railway Administration in this regard is final. Employees recommended for a particular category of post with a higher Medical classification will not be considered for any alternative post with a lower medical classification in the event of medical unfitness. In the event of any mis-statement/discrepancy in particulars, or if the employee has suppressed relevant information or does not satisfy eligibility criteria at any stage, the application/candidature will be cancelled, or services terminated, without notice. Candidates who have been debarred for life from all RRB/RRC examinations, or debarred for a period not yet completed, need not apply - such candidature will be rejected during any stage of recruitment as and when detected.
