BEL Medical Officer (E-II) Job Description

The Medical Officer is required to take care of various activities like: Medical services for employees in the factory (consultation and treatment). Processing of medical bills: checking prescriptions and reports, and line item wise checking of bills with the Schedule of charges (SOCs) of empanelled hospital rates and standard hospital rates, which vary from case to case. Administration of the CMA Scheme and monitoring of the BERECHS Scheme. Scrutiny and processing of bills in SAP. Liaisoning with panel hospitals, OSH Code authorities for health related matters, and the Medicine Dispensing Agency (Empanelled Pharmacy). Periodic medical checkup under the Maharashtra OSH Code/Rules, and coordination and conduct of preventive health checkup for employees. Medical consultation for dependents in BEL Township.
