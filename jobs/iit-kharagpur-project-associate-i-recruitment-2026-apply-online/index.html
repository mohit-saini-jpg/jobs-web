IIT Kharagpur Project Associate I Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur, has invited online applications for 2 Project Associate-I - Research posts in the DBT-sponsored project "IndiaCAN-Twin: Towards AI-Powered Digital Twin Platform for Early Detection and Personalized Care in Indian Oral Cancer". The consolidated compensation is up to Rs. 31,000 per month, depending on qualification and experience, for 24 months or until the project ends, whichever is earlier. The application fee is printed as 0 for all categories, and the last date is 20 November 2026.

Who Can Apply

The candidate should be excellent in Programming and Data Structures, Probability and Statistics, and Discrete Maths. The candidate should preferably have a GATE score and a CGPA > 8.0. The notice states that the qualifications and experience prescribed are the minimum, and mere possession of them does not entitle a candidate to be called for the interview. Candidates should check the official notification for the full eligibility terms.

Pay and Tenure

The Consolidated Compensation is printed as up to Rs. 31,000 per month, depending on qualification and experience. Under the qualification details, the notice also prints these amounts: 37,000/- + HRA to scholars who are selected through (a) CSIR-UGC NET or GATE, or (b) a selection process through national-level examinations conducted by Central Government departments and their agencies and institutions. 28,000/- + HRA for others who do not fall under the above. The assignment is temporary, for 24 months or until the project is terminated, whichever is earlier.

Selection and Interview

The notice refers to an interview but states that meeting the minimum qualifications and experience does not entitle a candidate to be called for it. It does not describe the selection stages or the interview date.
