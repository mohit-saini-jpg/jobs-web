What the JCECEB PECE Scorecard 2026 Contains

The Jharkhand Polytechnic Result 2026 scorecard is not just a mark sheet it also carries information candidates will need during counselling. According to the official result notice, the scorecard includes:

  • Candidate's name
  • Roll number
  • Application/registration number
  • Date of birth
  • Photograph (if applicable)
  • Category
  • Gender
  • Parent's name
  • Examination name
  • Marks obtained
  • Rank secured
  • Qualifying status/result status
  • Instructions for counselling and admission

How to Check Jharkhand Polytechnic Result 2026 Online

Follow these steps to download your JCECEB PECE scorecard:

  • Visit the official JCECEB website at jceceb.jharkhand.gov.in.
  • On the homepage, click on the PECE result link.
  • Enter your roll number and submit the form.
  • Your Jharkhand Polytechnic Result 2026 PDF will appear on the screen.
  • Download the scorecard and take a printout for future reference, including counselling.

Jharkhand Polytechnic Counselling 2026: Process Overview

Only candidates who have qualified the PECE 2026 exam are eligible to take part in counselling. The full process, as outlined by JCECEB, includes the following stages:

  • Online counselling registration
  • Payment of counselling fee
  • Choice filling and locking of preferred colleges and branches
  • Seat allocation
  • Document verification
  • Payment of admission fee
  • Reporting to the allotted institution

Candidates who successfully complete every stage will be offered admission to diploma courses at government and private polytechnic institutes across Jharkhand.

Documents Required for Jharkhand PECE Counselling 2026

The exact document checklist for the 2026 counselling round has not been detailed in the official result notice reviewed for this article, so candidates should always verify the final list in JCECEB's counselling information bulletin. Based on the standard documents JCECEB has asked for in previous PECE and JCECE counselling rounds, candidates are generally advised to keep the following ready:

  • PECE 2026 admit card
  • PECE 2026 scorecard/rank card
  • Class 10 mark sheet and passing certificate (for age and qualification proof)
  • Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS), if applicable
  • Domicile/residence certificate
  • Income certificate, if applicable for fee concession or category benefit
  • Counselling call letter/provisional seat allotment letter
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Any other document specified in the official counselling bulletin

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Cross-check every detail on your scorecard name, roll number, category, and marks immediately after downloading it.
  • Only candidates declared qualified in the PECE 2026 result are eligible to register for counselling.
  • Complete online registration and choice filling before the counselling deadline; late entries are typically not entertained.
  • Lock your choices carefully, since colleges and branches selected during choice filling directly affect your seat allotment.
  • Keep both digital and printed copies of your scorecard and allotment letter for document verification.
  • Report to the allotted institute within the admission window mentioned in your provisional allotment letter.
