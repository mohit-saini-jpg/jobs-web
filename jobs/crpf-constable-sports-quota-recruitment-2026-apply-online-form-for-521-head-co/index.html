CRPF Sportsperson Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection proceeds through Identity Check, Documentation, Physical Standard Test (PST), Sports Trial Test, and Detailed/Review Medical Examination, in sequence. All candidates undergo document verification, producing original documents/certificates relating to age, education, sports achievement, domicile and category. Candidates unable to produce the prescribed originals are eliminated at this stage; those qualified proceed to the PST. Marks are awarded for the single highest medal/position achieved during the eligible window (30 September 2024 to 30 September 2026); if a candidate has more than one medal, only the highest-scoring one counts. Important Conditions

Application Fee: Male candidates belonging to UR/OBC/EWS pay Rs. 100/- through the recruitment website; the fee is exempted for all female candidates and for SC/ST/Ex-Servicemen candidates. The fee is neither refunded nor adjusted against any other recruitment process, under any circumstances. Selected candidates must submit a bond to serve the Force for a minimum of 3 years (or a specific period as prescribed), backed by an undertaking bond of Rs. 1,00,000/-, to promote sports in the Force, before they can resign for better employment elsewhere or on any other ground. Posts carry All India service liability; selected candidates are liable to serve anywhere in India or abroad, and will be governed by the CRPF Act 1949 and Rules 1955. CRPF Constable (Sports Quota) Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many posts has CRPF advertised under the Sports Quota?
A
521 posts – 182 Head Constable (GD) and 339 Constable (GD) – across 22 sports disciplines.
Q2What is the last date to apply?
A
11 November 2026, till 23:59 hrs; the application window opened on 12 October 2026 at 9:00 AM.
Q3Who is eligible to apply?
A
Meritorious male/female sportspersons who have represented the country, a State/UT, their University, or their School in recognized competitions at specified levels, within the last two years, and who meet the age and educational qualification for the post.
Q4What is the age limit?
A
18 to 23 years as on the cut-off date of 1 August 2026, with age relaxation of 5 to 13 years depending on category and Ex-Servicemen status.
Q5Is there a written exam?
A
No. Selection is based on a merit list built from sports achievement marks, followed by a Physical Standard Test, a qualifying Sports Trial Test, and a medical examination – there is no written test or interview.
Q6What is the application fee?
A
Rs. 100/- for male UR/OBC/EWS candidates; exempted for all female candidates and for SC/ST/Ex-Servicemen candidates.
Q7What is the pay scale?
A
Head Constable (GD): Level 4, Rs. 25,500–81,100. Constable (GD): Level 3, Rs. 21,700–69,100.
Q8Is there a service bond?
A
Yes. Selected candidates must execute a Rs. 1,00,000/- bond committing to serve the Force for at least 3 years.
