MCC NEET UG 2026 Round 2 Registration - Quick Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 03/09/2026

MCC NEET UG 2026 Round 2 Registration for PwD Candidates: The Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) has opened registration and choice filling for Round 2 of NEET UG Counselling 2026 on 3rd September 2026 at the official portal, mcc.nic.in. Along with the general Round 2 process, MCC has activated a dedicated PwD (Persons with Disabilities) portal and released a fresh medical assessment schedule for candidates applying under the PwD category. PwD candidates participating in Round 2 must complete their medical assessment within the prescribed timeline and ensure their assessment details are generated online on the MCC portal, in addition to completing the standard registration, fee payment, and choice filling steps.

How to Apply for MCC NEET UG 2026 Round 2 Registration Process

  • Visit the official MCC counselling website, mcc.nic.in.
  • Click on the "UG Medical Counselling" tab, then select "New Registration 2026" (for first-time Round 2 applicants) or log in with existing Round 1 credentials.
  • Read the instructions carefully and accept the terms and conditions.
  • Enter the required details: NEET UG roll number, registration number, name, parent's/mother's name, date of birth, and security code.
  • Set a password; login credentials will also be generated and sent to your registered email address.
  • Complete the registration form with personal, educational, and NEET UG 2026 result details, and upload a scanned photograph and signature as specified.
  • Pay the applicable registration fee and refundable security deposit online.
  • Take a printout of the completed registration form for your records.
  • PwD candidates should additionally log in to the MCC PwD portal to generate their medical assessment details and report to the assigned Medical Assessment Board within the notified schedule.

How to Fill Choices in MCC NEET UG 2026 Round 2

  • Log in to your account on mcc.nic.in using your NEET roll number and password.
  • Open "Available Choices" and click on "Choice Filling."
  • Select your preferred courses and colleges; each selected choice moves from the left basket to the right (selected) basket.
  • Arrange your choices in order of preference using the up/down arrow buttons, or swap positions as needed.
  • Review the final preference order carefully.
  • Lock your choices and submit the form before the choice-filling deadline. Choices cannot be edited after locking.

Important Instructions

  • Candidates who did not register in Round 1 must submit a fresh registration and complete choice filling to participate in Round 2.
  • Candidates allotted a seat in Round 2 who wish to upgrade further can opt for upgrade willingness while continuing to hold their current seat, as per MCC's Freeze/ Float rules.
  • Candidates need not physically join the Round 1 college if they wish to exit freely before the next round; however, to retain and upgrade a Round 1 seat, physical joining and an upgrade willingness declaration are required.
  • PwD candidates must ensure their medical assessment is completed within the prescribed window, or they risk their PwD-category claim not being considered for seat allotment.
  • Carefully verify all entered details before final submission; errors can affect seat allotment.
  • Keep track of every counselling round and deadline, since missing a step can result in disqualification from that round.
