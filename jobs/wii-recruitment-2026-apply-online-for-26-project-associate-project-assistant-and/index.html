About the Institute

The notice describes WII as an autonomous institution under the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC), Government of India, which offers training programmes, academic courses and advisory in wildlife research and management. Its Recruitment and Placement Cell is at Chandrabani, Dehradun, Uttarakhand-248002. The positions are for research projects and the notice states that they are temporary for the project period.

Pay and Tenure

Pay is printed as monthly emoluments, with HRA shown as "+ HRA" for every post except the Project Manager, whose emoluments are printed as 60,000 to 1,25,000 and fixed depending on experience. Most Project Associate positions carry two pay figures, one for NET/GATE qualified candidates and one for non-NET/GATE candidates, exactly as in the vacancy table. For Project Associate – I in Projects 01 and 09 the notice prints the lower figure (30,000 + HRA) against NET/GATE candidates and the higher figure (37,000 + HRA) against non-NET/GATE candidates. The advertised positions are purely temporary for the project period and will stand abolished after the project is completed. The Institute's rules and regulations govern the project's tenure and the code of conduct of selected candidates. Candidates not found suitable for a post may be considered for a position at a lower grade on the Interview

Selection and Interview

Applicants are first shortlisted, and the top ten (10) shortlisted candidates against each advertised project/position are called for an online interview before a duly constituted Selection Committee. The notice prints no interview date. Shortlisting is based on essential qualification, age limit, percentage of marks in Bachelor's/Master's Degree, specialization relevance in Masters, research experience and publications (papers published in journals, books, and papers presented in workshops/seminars/symposiums/conferences), as per WII rules. Meeting the minimum qualification and experience does not automatically entitle an applicant to an interview. The list of shortlisted candidates and the interview result will be uploaded in the "Latest News & Announcements" section of the Institute's website at https://wii.gov.in/news .
