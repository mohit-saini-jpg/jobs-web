NDUAT Associate Professor Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Seven Associate Professor posts in six veterinary and livestock departments of Acharya Narendra Deva University of Agriculture & Technology (ANDUAT), Kumarganj, Ayodhya (U.P.), carry Academic Level 13A (7th CPC) with a rationalized entry pay of Rs. 1,31,400/- and are to be filled on a regular basis under Advertisement No. 16/2026. Indian nationals send the prescribed application form, available on www.nduat.org, with a demand draft by Registered/Speed Post. The application must reach the university on or before 5 December 2026 up to 5.00 PM.

NDUAT Associate Professor Recruitment 2026 : Age Limit

There is no upper age limit for the ANDUAT Associate Professor posts. A person whose age is less than 18 years shall not be appointed to any post in the university. Age is determined with regard to the closing date of the application, 5 December 2026, and the university reserves the right to relax the age/qualifications/experience at its discretion.

Pay and Terms

The posts will be filled on a regular basis. The posts are temporary but likely to continue. Service conditions, including pay matrix level and age of superannuation, will be as per Government of Uttar Pradesh rules. The selected candidate will be governed by the New Pension Scheme of the Government of India as applicable w.e.f. 1st April 2005. Selected candidates must perform duties as per the Rules of the University and may be assigned any duty, even during non-working hours/holidays, and any duty within or outside the university depending on the exigency of work. The university may offer the post at a level lower than that advertised, depending on qualifications, experience and performance. The appointment is provisional until the caste certificate and character & antecedents are verified.

NDUAT Associate Professor Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

A high level committee will review the API score submitted by the candidate and consider issuing interview call letters. Scoring follows Table 2, Appendix II of the UGC Regulation 2018, and is based on evidence such as copies of publications, project sanction letters, utilization and completion certificates, patent acknowledgements and students' Ph.D. award letters. A research score of at least 70 as per Appendix II, Table 2 is the qualifying mark of screening for Associate Professor / equivalent post. The score must come from at least three of the six categories, and the combined score from Policy Documents and Invited lectures/paper presentations is capped at thirty percent of the total. Research paper scores are augmented by impact factor: 5 points without impact factor, rising to 30 points for an impact factor above 10.
