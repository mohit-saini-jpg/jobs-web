GIMS Staff Nurse Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must possess, by the last date of application, either: B.Sc Nursing (4-year course) from an Indian Nursing Council/State Medical Faculty recognised Institute or University, or B.Sc Nursing (Post Basic) (2-year course) from an Indian Nursing Council recognised institute/university, and be registered as Nurse & Midwife in State/Indian Nursing Council; OR Diploma in General Nursing Midwifery (GNM) from an Indian Nursing Council recognised Institute/Board/Council, registered as Nurse & Midwife in State/Indian Nursing Council, with two years' experience in a minimum 50-bedded hospital after acquiring the above qualification. Only permanent residents (Domicile) of Uttar Pradesh are eligible to apply for reserved posts (both horizontal and vertical reservation); SC/ST/OBC/EWS/DFF/Women/Ex-servicemen candidates who are not original residents of UP will be considered Unreserved (UR). GIMS Staff Nurse Selection Process

Selection is through a Computer Based Test (CBT) of objective-type questions related to nursing, with 80% questions on theoretical knowledge and 20% on practical knowledge. No candidate is allowed to enter the examination without an admit card issued by the designated examination body. Negative marking applies: 0.25 mark is deducted for each wrong answer; unanswered questions carry no penalty. Candidates with equal marks are ranked by age, with the older candidate placed higher in the merit list. Minimum qualifying marks: 40% for General, EWS, and OBC candidates; 35% for SC and ST candidates. The "Mark for Review" option is available during the CBT within the active section's time only; a question answered and marked for review is evaluated as the final answer, while one left unanswered and marked for review is treated as unattempted.
