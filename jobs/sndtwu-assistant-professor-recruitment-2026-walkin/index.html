About SNDTWU

SNDT Women's University is based in Mumbai. This notice is for the University Department of Psychology at its Churchgate Campus, and the vacancy is for an unaided teaching post purely on a temporary basis, only for the academic year 2026-2027.

Pay and Terms

Pay: fixed monthly remuneration of up to Rs. 52000/- per month for the academic year 2026-2027. The appointment is purely temporary and strictly only for the academic year 2026-2027. A selected person has no enforceable right against the University to continuation in the succeeding academic session under any circumstances. A selected person may avail only applicable Casual leave during the academic session. No other leave and allowances are permitted.

Documents Required

Mark list, degree certificate and experience certificate. Photocopies of all relevant certificates; self-attested copies are to be attached in support of the information given, and all information must be substantiated by supporting documents. All original documents of credentials, to be produced at the time of the interview. A "No Objection Certificate" from the present employer at the time of the interview, if working in a University department / institution or affiliated college.
