Bihar Vidhan Sabha Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Graduates can apply online for 54 posts in the Bihar Legislative Assembly Secretariat (Bihar Vidhan Sabha Sachivalaya), Patna – 50 Assistant Section Officer (Sahayak Prashakha Padadhikari, Pay Level 7, Rs. 44,900-1,42,400) and 4 Assistant Caretaker (Sahayak Avdhayak, Pay Level 4, Rs. 25,500-81,100) posts under Advertisement No. 01/2024. The application window is open from 7 November 2026 (11:00 AM) to 27 November 2026, and candidates who applied for these posts earlier need not apply again.

Who Can Apply

The candidate must be a citizen of India. For both posts – Assistant Section Officer and Assistant Caretaker – the candidate must hold a graduate or equivalent degree from a university recognised by the State Government or the Central Government by the last date for filling the online application. A candidate with the required qualification may apply for more than one post. A separate application for each post is not needed: ticking the box beside the posts shown in the registration form makes the candidate an applicant for each ticked post, and only one fee is payable. If two candidates are in contention for one post, the desirable qualification fixed for that post decides. The post-wise desirable qualification is given in the Schedule attached to the Bihar Vidhan Sabha Sachivalaya (Recruitment and Service Conditions) Rules, 2018. Bihar Vidhan Sabha Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection is in two stages: a Preliminary Examination (objective, multiple choice, OMR answer sheet) and a descriptive Main Examination. A post-wise merit list is prepared on the basis of the Main Examination. Each question carries 4 marks; 1 mark is deducted for each wrong answer. The medium is Hindi/English; if the Hindi and English papers differ, the English version is valid. Minimum qualifying marks: General 40%, Backward Class 36.5%, Extremely Backward Class 34%, SC/ST, women and candidates with disabilities 32%. If many applications are received, the preliminary may be held in more than one phase, and results will be prepared through normalisation. Bihar Vidhan Sabha Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Open the Apply Online link given in the advertisement; detailed instructions are on the Bihar Vidhan Sabha Sachivalaya website, https://vidhansabha.bihar.gov.in.
  • 2Register and fill the online form between 7 November 2026 (11:00 AM) and 27 November 2026, using your own working mobile number and email ID.
  • 3Tick the box beside each post you are applying for.
  • 4Upload scanned copies of your educational and reservation certificates, a recent photograph and your signature, and check that the images are clear.
  • 5Pay the fee by Net Banking, Credit Card or Debit Card; bank charges are added to the fee. Print and keep the bank receipt.
  • 6After final submission, log in again and download the filled form from the "Download Filled Application Section" on the dashboard; print two copies.
  • 7Check that every page of the printout shows the Bar Code and the Application Number; without either, the application is treated as incomplete and is not accepted.
  • 8Printed, typed or handwritten applications outside the online form are not accepted. Candidates who already applied in the 2024 windows need not apply again. The notice does not give a separate last date for fee payment in the new window.
  • 9The fee is not refundable. SC/ST candidates who pay the SC/ST fee but later fail to produce the certificate, or whose certificate is doubted, may be disqualified; they may voluntarily pay the fee for general candidates to keep their candidature safe.

Bihar Vidhan Sabha Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in Bihar Vidhan Sabha Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 54 posts under Advertisement No. 01/2024: 50 Assistant Section Officer and 4 Assistant Caretaker. The number of posts may decrease or increase.
Q2What is the last date to apply?
A
The last date to apply online is 27 November 2026; applications open on 7 November 2026 at 11:00 AM.
Q3Do I need to apply again if I applied in 2024?
A
No. Candidates who have already applied for these posts need not apply again.
Q4What is the qualification for Assistant Section Officer and Assistant Caretaker?
A
A graduate or equivalent degree from a university recognised by the State or Central Government, held by the last date of the online application.
Q5What is the age limit?
A
The minimum age is 21 years on 01-08-2023; the maximum is 37 years for Unreserved men, 40 years for Unreserved women and BC/EBC candidates, and 42 years for SC/ST candidates.
Q6What is the salary?
A
Assistant Section Officer gets Pay Level 7 (Rs. 44,900-1,42,400) and Assistant Caretaker Pay Level 4 (Rs. 25,500-81,100), plus other admissible allowances as per rules.
Q7What is the application fee?
A
Rs. 600 for Bihar-domicile EBC, BC, EWS and Unreserved men and for all candidates from outside Bihar; Rs. 150 for Bihar-domicile SC, ST, PwD (40% or more) and all Bihar-domicile women.
Q8What is the selection process?
A
An objective Preliminary Examination of 100 questions and 400 marks with 1 mark negative marking, followed by a descriptive Main Examination; the merit list is based on the Main Examination.
Q9Can I apply for both posts with one fee?
A
Yes. Eligible candidates tick both posts in the registration form; only one application and one fee are needed.
