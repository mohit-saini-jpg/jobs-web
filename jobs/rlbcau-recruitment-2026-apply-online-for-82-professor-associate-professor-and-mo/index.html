About RLBCAU

Rani Lakshmi Bai Central Agricultural University is a central agricultural university at Jhansi-Gwalior Road, Jhansi-284003, Uttar Pradesh, established under the Rani Lakshmi Bai Central Agricultural University Act, 2014. As per its rules, the Board of Management of the University is the appointing authority for all regular teaching, statutory and non-teaching posts at pay level 10 or above.

RLBCAU Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

RLBCAU selects candidates through screening of applications on a score card, followed by an interview of shortlisted candidates. The notice adds that the process of selection may be by a presentation/seminar/interview or a combination thereof. The screening committee shortlists candidates on the basis of a score card prescribed for each post or other criteria it fixes. A minimum of 60% marks in the screening is needed for an interview call for all posts except Comptroller. The ratio of vacancies to candidates called shall not normally exceed 1:20 and shall not be less than 1:3 for each post. Shortlisting may use desirable qualifications, higher qualifications or experience than the minimum, experience counted before or after the essential qualification, or any other criteria the Screening Committee decides.
