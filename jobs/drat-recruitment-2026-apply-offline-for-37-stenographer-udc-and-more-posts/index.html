DRAT Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Debts Recovery Appellate Tribunal, Allahabad (Government of India, Ministry of Finance, Department of Financial Services) is filling 37 posts on deputation basis in DRAT, Allahabad and the Debts Recovery Tribunals (DRTs) under its jurisdiction. The posts are Section Officer, Private Secretary, Assistant, Account Assistant, Stenographer Grade I and Upper Division Clerk. Officers of the Central Government, State Governments, Courts and Tribunals may apply. Applications go through proper channel to the Secretary/Registrar at Prayagraj and must reach by 28 December 2026, 05:30 PM.

DRAT Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

The selected candidate will be appointed on deputation basis initially for a period of up to 03 years. This may be extended as per norms, subject to satisfactory performance and other terms and conditions. The terms of deputation are governed by Department of Personnel and Training O.M. No. 6/8/2009-Estt.(Pay-II) dated 17.06.2010 and O.M. No. 2/6/2016-Estt(Pay-II) dated 17.02.2016, as amended from time to time. The period of deputation, including any deputation in another ex-cadre post held immediately before this appointment (in the same or another Organization or Department of the Central Government), shall ordinarily not exceed three years. Departmental officials in the feeder grade who are in direct line of promotion are not eligible for appointment on deputation. A deputationist in the DRAT/DRTs under the jurisdiction of DRAT, Allahabad is not eligible for absorption or promotion. Officials who volunteer for a post will not be allowed to withdraw their names later. No action will be taken on an advance copy of the application.

DRAT Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in DRAT Allahabad Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 37 vacancies: Section Officer 1, Private Secretary 2, Assistant 7, Account Assistant 6, Stenographer Grade I 13 and Upper Division Clerk 8. The number of vacancies may undergo change.
Q2What is the last date for DRAT Allahabad Recruitment 2026?
A
Applications must reach the Secretary/Registrar, Debts Recovery Appellate Tribunal, Prayagraj through proper channel on or before 28 December 2026 by 05:30 PM.
Q3Who is eligible for the DRAT Allahabad deputation posts?
A
Officers under the Central Government, State Government, Courts or Tribunals who hold an analogous post on a regular basis, or who have the regular service each post requires in the feeder grade. For example, Assistant needs ten years in Level-4. The Account Assistant post is open only to Central Government officers who have also had cash and account training at the Institute of Secretariat Training and Management or an equivalent Central Government institute.
Q4What is the age limit for DRAT Allahabad Recruitment 2026?
A
The applicant's maximum age should not exceed 56 years on the closing date, 28 December 2026.
Q5What is the salary for DRAT Allahabad posts?
A
Section Officer and Private Secretary are in Level-7 (Rs. 44900-142400/-). Assistant, Account Assistant and Stenographer Grade I are in Level-6 (Rs. 35400-112400/-). Upper Division Clerk is in Level-4 (Rs. 25500-81100/-).
Q6How do I apply for DRAT Allahabad Recruitment 2026?
A
Fill in the Annexure-I pro-forma and attach attested photocopies of your APARs for the last five years. Your parent office then sends it through proper channel, with the Annexure-II certificate, to the Secretary/Registrar, Debts Recovery Appellate Tribunal, First Floor, E-10 B Building, BSNL Premises, Patrika Marg, Civil Lines, Prayagraj -211001 (U.P.).
Q7How long is the deputation in DRAT Allahabad?
A
The appointment is on deputation basis initially for a period of up to 03 years, which may be extended as per norms subject to satisfactory performance. The period, including any immediately preceding ex-cadre deputation, shall ordinarily not exceed three years.
Q8Where will the selected officers be posted?
A
Section Officer is posted at Lucknow and Private Secretary at Dehradun & Jabalpur. Assistant postings are at Lucknow, Allahabad, Jabalpur and Dehradun. Account Assistant, Stenographer Grade I and Upper Division Clerk postings are at Allahabad, Lucknow, Dehradun, Jabalpur, Patna and Ranchi. The place of posting may undergo change.
Q9Can feeder-grade officials in the DRAT or DRTs apply?
A
No. Departmental officials in the feeder grade who are in direct line of promotion are not eligible for appointment on deputation. A deputationist in the DRAT/DRTs under DRAT, Allahabad is not eligible for absorption or promotion.
Q10Can I withdraw my name after applying?
A
No. The circular asks forwarding departments to make sure that officials who volunteer for the post are not allowed to withdraw their names later.
