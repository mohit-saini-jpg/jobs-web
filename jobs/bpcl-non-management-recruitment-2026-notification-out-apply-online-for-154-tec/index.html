BPCL Recruitment 2026 Application Fee

General, EWS, and OBC (NCL) candidates are required to pay a non-refundable application fee of ₹590/- (₹500/- plus 18% GST, i.e., ₹90/-), plus payment gateway charges if any, through online payment mode only. SC, ST, PwBD, and Ex-Servicemen (ESM) candidates are exempted from payment of application fee.

BPCL Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Stage 1 – Computer Based Test (CBT): 100 objective-type questions of 1 mark each, 120 minutes duration, conducted in English only, with no negative marking. The paper has two sections — Domain Knowledge (60 marks) and General Aptitude comprising English Language, Quantitative Aptitude, Logical Reasoning, and General Awareness & Current Affairs (40 marks). Stage 2 – Skill/Proficiency Test (SPT): Qualifying in nature, conducted at the respective refinery locations for candidates who clear the CBT cut-off. Stage 3 – Pre-Employment Medical Examination: Final appointment is subject to candidates meeting BPCL's Medical Fitness norms and standards. Merely qualifying the CBT does not guarantee a call for SPT; shortlisting depends on the number of positions, reservation ratios, and relative performance.
