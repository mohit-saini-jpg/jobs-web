About Orissa High Court Civil Judge (Senior Division)

The High Court of Orissa at Cuttack conducts this recruitment through its Registrar, Examination. The Limited Departmental Competitive Examination, 2026 fills vacancies in the cadre of Civil Judge (Senior Division) on the basis of merit from among officers already serving as Civil Judge (Junior Division). The recruitment is conducted under the O.S.J.S. and O.J.S. Rules, 2007 and the amendments made to them.

Who Can Apply

This is an in-service examination. The notice invites applications only from eligible Judicial Officers in the cadre of Civil Judge (Junior Division). Open-market candidates are not invited. Qualifying service: an officer must have completed three years of qualifying service as Civil Judge (Junior Division) as on 01.04.2026. How service is counted: the date of entry into judicial service as Civil Judge (Junior Division) will be taken into consideration for calculating the three years. Intimation: officers must apply under intimation to their concerned Controlling Authorities. The official notification does not specify the minimum educational qualification, as eligibility rests on service in the Civil Judge (Junior Division) cadre. The notice prints no age limit. Candidates should check the official notification and the O.S.J.S. and O.J.S. Rules, 2007 for the full eligibility conditions.

Pay and Tenure

The scale of pay for Civil Judge (Senior Division) is ₹1,11,000 – ₹1,63,030 with usual D.A. and other allowances as admissible from time to time. Every candidate promoted to the posts in the cadre of Civil Judge (Senior Division) through this examination shall be on probation as per Rule 33 of the O.S.J.S. & O.J.S. Rules, 2007.

Selection and Interview

Selection has three parts: a written examination of two papers, a viva-voce, and an evaluation of CCRs / PARs / ACRs. A merit list is then drawn up. The written examination has two papers. Each paper carries 100 marks and lasts 2 (Two) hours.
