AIIMS Bhubaneswar Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

AIIMS Bhubaneswar Recruitment 2026 has 3 posts in the Department of Urology for an ICMR-funded multi-centre bladder cancer trial: Project Research Scientist-I (Non-Medical), Project Technical Support-III and Project Technical Support-II. All three are purely temporary contract posts for 11 months, which may be extended. The pay is a consolidated Rs. 56000/-, Rs. 28000/- or Rs. 20,000/- per month plus HRA @20%. Send the application in the prescribed format to [email protected] so that it reaches by 05.00 PM on 30 October 2026. The tentative date of the walk-in interview is 3 November 2026.

About AIIMS Bhubaneswar

All India Institute of Medical Sciences, Bhubaneswar is a Statutory Body under the aegis of the Ministry of H & F.W., Govt. of India. It is located at Sijua, Post: Dumuduma, Bhubaneswar-751019. These posts are in an ICMR-funded project led by PI Dr. Kalandi Barik, Assistant Professor, and Co-PI Dr Swarnendu Mandal, Associate Professor, both of the Department of Urology, AIIMS Bhubaneswar.

Pay and Tenure

The emoluments are consolidated per month, with HRA @20% on top of the pay for each post: Rs. 56000/- (Project Research Scientist-I (Non-Medical)), Rs. 28000/- (Project Technical Support-III) and Rs. 20,000/- (Project Technical Support-II). The rates of emoluments/stipend are as per the guidelines and as per the sanction. The posts will be filled purely on a temporary contract basis. The tenure of each post is 11 months, which may be extended with satisfactory performance and project extension. Further continuation or extension depends on the needs of the project, performance evaluation and the approval of the competent authority, case by case. Benefits of Provident Fund, Pension Scheme, Leave Travel Concession, Medical claim, etc. are not admissible. A contract appointee has no claim to a regular post in the institute, the Funding Agency or any Department of the Government of India. The

Selection and Interview

Candidates are selected through a walk-in interview. The tentative date is 3 November 2026 (Tuesday). Candidates must report between 09.30 AM and 10.30 AM at the Seminar Room, Urology OPD, Second Floor of Hospital Building, AIIMS Bhubaneswar. The selection committee or appointing authority will decide who is engaged for each position. A candidate who does not bring the original certificates at the time of joining, or whose documents show a discrepancy, will not be allowed to join. The position will then be offered to candidates on the waiting list.
