PSEB Date Sheet 2027 - Overview : Admit Card Overview

The Punjab School Education Board (PSEB), Mohali, has announced the PSEB Date Sheet 2027 examination schedule for Classes 8, 10 and 12 for the academic session 2026-27. According to the schedule on 20 November 2026, Class 8 written examinations will take place from 5 to 18 March 2027, Class 10 examinations from 19 March to 20 April 2027, and Class 12 examinations from 5 March to 23 April 2027.

How to Download PSEB Date Sheet 2027?

  • Visit the official Punjab School Education Board website at pseb.ac.in.
  • Open the Date Sheet section.
  • Find the 2027 annual examination date sheet for the required class when published.
  • Select Class 8, Class 10 or Class 12, as applicable.
  • Open the corresponding date sheet PDF.
  • Check the subject names, examination dates and shift timings.
  • Download and save the official timetable for reference.

PSEB Admit Card 2027

Students appearing for the Punjab Board examinations will need to follow the admit card distribution and examination centre instructions issued by PSEB and their respective schools.

The overall examination schedule does not confirm an exact admit card release date for Classes 8, 10 or 12.

Students should contact their schools for applicable hall ticket arrangements and monitor the official PSEB website for further updates.

Why Has PSEB Announced the 2027 Exam Schedule Early?

According to contemporary news reports, the board has announced the broad examination periods in advance while considering the Punjab Assembly elections scheduled for 2027.

The early announcement is intended to help organise examination administration and reduce potential disruption from election-related arrangements.

The detailed date sheets will provide students with the paper-wise examination timetable needed for final preparation.
