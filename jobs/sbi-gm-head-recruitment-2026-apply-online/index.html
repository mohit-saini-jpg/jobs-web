Pay and Tenure

Annual CTC is negotiable and depends on experience, current emoluments in the present employment and place of posting. CTC will be offered based on industry standards, the candidate's experience and last drawn salary. CTC is split into Fixed Pay and Variable Pay in the ratio 80:20. Besides CTC, no other perks shall be paid. Annual increment from the second year onwards can be up to 10% of the previous year's Annual CTC, subject to a performance appraisal score of at least 90%. Variable pay is payable only on securing a minimum of 90% marks in the performance appraisal; appraisals are held half-yearly as on 31 March and 30 September. If the contract ends before 6 months (from 2nd year onwards), variable pay is released on a pro-rata basis, subject to income tax deduction. Selection and Interview

Selection is based on Shortlisting and Interview, followed by CTC Negotiation. Shortlisting: a shortlisting committee of the Bank decides the parameters and calls an adequate number of candidates for interview. Meeting the minimum qualification and experience does not guarantee a call. Interview: carries 100 marks; qualifying marks will be decided by the Bank. Documents are verified with originals at the interview. Merit list: prepared on interview scores only; candidates with equal marks at the cut-off are ranked by age in descending order. CTC Negotiation: held with candidates one by one in order of merit; a candidate who does not agree is treated as a Voluntary Exit and the next candidate is called.
