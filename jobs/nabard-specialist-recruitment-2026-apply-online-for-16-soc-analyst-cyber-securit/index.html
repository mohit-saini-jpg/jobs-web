NABARD Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

The selection will be by way of interview. Candidates will be shortlisted for interview in the ratio of 1:3 on the basis of qualification, experience, etc. Selected candidates will have to undergo a pre-recruitment medical examination and final appointment will be subject to being declared medically fit.
