About the Law Department, Bihar

The Law Department is a department of the Government of Bihar and is based in Patna. Secretary-cum-Legal Remembrancer Balram Dubey issued this notice. Willingness forms go to the office of the Advocate General, Bihar, at the High Court, Patna.

Law Department Bihar Additional Advocate General and Additional Standing Counsel Vacancy Details 2026

The notice does not give a fixed amount. Law officers who are engaged receive the fee and any other facilities that the Law Department, Government of Bihar, decides and notifies from time to time. Law Department Bihar Additional Advocate General and Additional Standing Counsel Eligibility 2026

The notice is open to advocates who are eligible under the criteria fixed in the Bihar Law Officers (Engagement) Rules, 2023. The notice does not repeat those criteria. The official notification does not specify the minimum educational qualification. The willingness format asks for the following details, so candidates should have them ready: Enrolment number as an advocate AOR (Advocate-on-Record) number Where and in which courts you practise, and the name of your association Length of practice Whether you are a designated Senior Advocate Any earlier empanelment or engagement Whether any criminal or disciplinary proceeding has been brought against you Income tax paid in the last 3 years (assessment year, income and income tax) For the full eligibility conditions for each post, see the official notification and the Bihar Law Officers (Engagement) Rules, 2023.

Bihar Law Department Recruitment 2026 : Important Dates & Timelines

Counted from the notice date of 27 October 2026, with only Saturdays and Sundays left out, the 15 working days end on 17 November 2026. Public holidays in between would make the deadline later. The notice says willingness sent after the 15-day period "shall not be entertained".

Bihar Law Department Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

A Selection Committee set up under Rule 4 of the Bihar Law Officers (Engagement) Rules, 2023 selects and recommends advocates, and engagement follows that recommendation. Sending a willingness does not give anyone a right to be engaged. All rights over selection and engagement stay with the Law Department, and its decision is final.

Service Conditions

Other terms and conditions of the engagement are those set out in the Engagement Rules, 2023, or notified by the Law Department, Government of Bihar, Patna.

Bihar Law Department Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many posts are vacant?
A
There are 13 posts in total. The notice lists 03 posts of Addl. Advocate General and 10 posts of Additional Standing Counsel.
Q2In which court will the selected law officers work?
A
They will act as law officers of the Government of Bihar in the Supreme Court of India.
Q3Who can send a willingness?
A
Advocates who meet the eligibility criteria of the Bihar Law Officers (Engagement) Rules, 2023 can apply.
Q4Where should the willingness be sent?
A
Send it to the office of the Advocate General, Bihar, High Court, Patna. You can email it to [email protected], send it by speed post or courier, or deliver it by hand.
Q5What is the deadline?
A
The deadline is 15 working days from the date the notice is published on the Law Department website. Counted from the notice date of 27 October 2026, and leaving out only weekends, that is 17 November 2026.
Q6Which documents must be attached?
A
You need your enrolment certificate, your Bar Association or Bar Council identity card, and copies of your income tax returns for the last three years.
Q7What fee will law officers be paid?
A
They will receive the fee and any other facilities that the Law Department, Government of Bihar, decides and notifies from time to time.
Q8Who makes the selection?
A
A Selection Committee set up under Rule 4 of the Bihar Law Officers (Engagement) Rules, 2023 makes the selection. The Law Department's decision is final.
Q9Is an AOR number needed in the form?
A
Yes. Item 7 of the willingness format asks for your AOR number. The form also asks whether you are a designated Senior Advocate.
