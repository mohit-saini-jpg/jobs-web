India Post Skilled Artisan Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must possess a recognized certificate in the relevant trade or have passed 8th standard with at least one year of experience in that trade. MV Mechanic applicants must additionally hold a valid Heavy Motor Vehicle driving licence. Important: The notification provides two alternative educational qualification routes. Candidates who possess a recognized trade certificate need not assume that the one-year experience condition automatically applies to their route. However, supporting documents must be furnished for the eligibility route claimed. Candidates applying for the MV Mechanic trade must possess a valid HMV driving licence to drive vehicles for testing purposes. The application must include a self-attested copy of the licence or licence extract. India Post Skilled Artisan Age Limit 2026

The corrigendum retains an age limit of 18 to 30 years as on 1 July 2018 for unreserved candidates. This historical cut-off date is explicitly stated in the notification and must not be replaced with a 2026 date. For Ex-Servicemen, the notification provides relaxation after deduction of qualifying military service in the Motor Vehicle Mechanic trade from actual age. The additional relaxation is up to three years for UR, six years for OBC and eight years for SC/ST candidates, under the stated conditions. SC/ST/OBC-NCL age relaxation applies in respect of vacancies reserved for the respective category.

The Skilled Artisan posts carry a pay scale of Rs. 19,900 to Rs. 63,200 under Level 2 of the 7th Central Pay Commission Pay Matrix, plus admissible allowances. The notified pay scale is not an in-hand salary figure. Actual monthly pay will depend on applicable allowances and deductions.

India Post Skilled Artisan Selection Process 2026

Selection will be made through a Competitive Trade Test based on the syllabus of the respective trade. Applications will first be examined against the prescribed qualifications and supporting documents. Scrutiny of applications and eligibility documents. Shortlisting of eligible candidates for the trade test. Competitive Trade Test in the respective trade. Selection based on the prescribed qualification, experience, category-wise vacancies and trade test performance. The syllabus, test date, venue and duration will be communicated to eligible candidates through their Hall Permits. The notification does not specify a common written examination pattern or total marks for the trade test. Important: Meeting the minimum qualification does not guarantee a call for the trade test. The department will not entertain correspondence regarding rejected applications.
