ONGC Apprentice Salary 2026 - Monthly Stipend

The ONGC Apprentice salary per month ranges from Rs. 8,200 to Rs. 12,300 , depending on the apprentice category and educational qualification. These amounts are monthly training stipends rather than permanent employee salaries. Note: The apprenticeship training duration is 12 months. ONGC will not reimburse transportation, boarding, or lodging expenses during training. Completion of the apprenticeship does not guarantee permanent employment in ONGC.

ONGC Apprentice Selection Process 2026

The selection process for ONGC Apprentice Recruitment 2026 is based on the marks obtained in the prescribed qualifying examination. The notification does not prescribe a separate written examination for these apprenticeship opportunities. Applications will be received through the NAPS and NATS portals. ONGC will verify the eligibility and qualifications of applicants. A provisional merit list will be prepared based on qualifying examination marks. Shortlisted candidates will be called for document verification. The final merit list will be prepared after verification and application of the relevant reservation rules. Selected candidates must complete the required apprenticeship engagement formalities. In case of a tie in qualifying examination marks, preference will be given to the older candidate. The scheduled result / selection date is 15 December 2026 , subject to official updates.
