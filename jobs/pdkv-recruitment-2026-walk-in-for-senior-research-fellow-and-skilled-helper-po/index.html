PDKV Akola Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

The selection process for these posts is through a walk-in interview only. No separate written examination is mentioned in the notification. Candidates will be shortlisted based on scrutiny of their applications. Shortlisted candidates will be called for the walk-in interview. The interview for both Senior Research Fellow and Skilled Helper posts will be conducted on 4th August 2026 at 11:00 AM in the chamber of the Head, Department of Plant Pathology, Dr. PDKV, Akola. If the number of candidates is large, the interview process may continue on the next day.

Documents Required for Dr. PDKV Akola Walk-in Interview

Candidates must carry their original documents along with one set of self-attested photocopies for verification at the time of the interview. The required documents include: Proof of Date of Birth Educational Qualification certificates (SSC and above) Reprints of published research papers, if any Experience certificate, if any No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the current employer, if already employed

PDKV Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

These posts are purely temporary and contractual, and the term of engagement is linked to the project period, subject to satisfactory performance. The appointment does not entitle the appointee to any implicit or explicit claim on any regular University post. No accommodation will be provided to selected candidates. Candidates without essential qualification certificates on the date of interview will not be eligible to attend. Selected candidates must join the assignment physically within seven days from the date of issue of the appointment order, failing which the order will be treated as cancelled. The Senior Research Fellow and Skilled Helper will not be entitled to any regular leave and are considered full-time workers, with attendance maintained through a register. The engagement can be discontinued at any time without notice on grounds of unsatisfactory work or any other valid reason. Candidates who wish to resign must give one month's prior notice, failing which one month's pay may be withheld. The Principal Investigator (PI) and Head of the Department of Plant Pathology reserve the right to cancel or postpone the interview without assigning any reason. Selected candidates must submit a statement accepting the terms and conditions on a Rs. 100/- bond paper after appointment. Any form of canvassing will lead to disqualification of the candidature. The decision of the Principal Investigator of the project will be final and binding in all respects. No TA/DA will be paid for attending the interview. Separate interview call letters will not be sent to candidates.
