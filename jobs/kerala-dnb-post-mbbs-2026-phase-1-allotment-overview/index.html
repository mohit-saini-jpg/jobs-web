The Commissioner for Entrance Examinations (CEE), Kerala, has released the Kerala DNB (Post MBBS) 2026 First Phase Allotment List on 18 November 2026 for the 2026-27 academic session. Kerala DNB Post MBBS 2026 Phase 1 Allotment - Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 19/11/2026

The first phase allotment list contains 8 allotted candidates. Seat allocation is based on the candidates' Kerala State merit ranks and online options. Candidates who have received allotments must download their Allotment Memo and report to their allotted institutions between 19 and 23 November 2026, by 4:00 PM. Candidates who fail to join within the deadline will lose their current allotment and existing options and will not be considered for the second phase.

How to Download Kerala DNB First Phase Allotment List 2026?

Candidates can follow these steps to download the official first phase allotment list:

  • Visit the CEE Kerala website at cee.kerala.gov.in.
  • Open the DNB (Post MBBS) 2026 Candidate Portal.
  • Select the Allotment List option.
  • Click on Phase 1 Allotment List.
  • Open the official allotment PDF.
  • Search for your application number or NEET rank.
  • Check the allotted institution, course and seat type.
  • Download the PDF for future reference.

How to Download Kerala DNB 2026 Allotment Memo?

Candidates allotted a seat must download the individual Allotment Memo from their candidate portal. This document is required when reporting for admission.

  • Visit CEE Kerala DNB Post MBBS Candidate Portal.
  • Enter your application number.
  • Enter your password and the displayed access code.
  • Log in to your candidate account.
  • Click the Allotment Result link to check the allotted seat.
  • Download and print the Allotment Memo from the candidate home page.
  • Keep the printed memo ready for admission at the allotted institution.

Kerala DNB First Phase Allotment 2026 Reporting Dates

The official CEE Kerala notification directs candidates allotted seats in the first phase to report to their respective institutions between 19 November and 23 November 2026. The final deadline is 4:00 PM on 23 November 2026.

Allotted candidates must carry the required documents prescribed in the official Information Bulletin and pay the applicable fee during admission. The published allotment notification does not specify a single common admission fee for all institutions.

What Happens if Candidates Do Not Join the Allotted Seat?

CEE Kerala has issued specific instructions regarding non-joining after the first phase allotment.

A candidate who does not take admission at the allotted institution before 23 November 2026 at 4:00 PM will lose the current allotment and all registered options. Such candidates will not be considered for the second phase allotment.

The official notification also states that candidates who join a first phase seat and subsequently vacate it will not be considered for Phase 2.

Candidates should therefore carefully review their allotment, eligibility, documents and admission obligations before making a decision.

Kerala DNB Post MBBS 2026 Second Phase Allotment

Further allotment activity will depend on seat availability and subsequent instructions from CEE Kerala. The first phase allotment notification dated 18 November 2026 does not announce a specific second phase schedule.

Candidates should monitor the official DNB (Post MBBS) admission portal for further notices. The rules regarding non-joining and vacating seats in Phase 1 must be considered before expecting eligibility for subsequent allotment rounds.
