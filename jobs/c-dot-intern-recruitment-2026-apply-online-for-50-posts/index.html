C-DOT Intern Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Paid Internship Scheme at C-DOT invites eligible engineering students for R&D work at C-DOT campuses at Delhi/Bengaluru. This page covers the winter batch 2027. The official notification does not specify the age limit or the application fee.

About C-DOT

C-DOT describes itself as a premier Telecom Technology R&D Centre of Govt. of India, with its campus at Mehrauli, New Delhi-110030. The objective of the Paid Internship Program is to attract young talent from the top 30 engineering institutes as per NIRF ranking for interning at C-DOT for R&D work. It gives young scholars an insight into the related technical work and possible future employment.

The closing time is not printed in the notice. Interns are generally inducted twice each year, during Summer and Winter, based on requirement.

C-DOT Intern Vacancy 2026

The total number of interns to be engaged is a maximum of 50 per year, and a maximum of 5 interns will be selected from one institute for internship in a calendar year. The final vacancy position may vary, and offers will be issued based on the final vacancy position at the time of final selection.

InternMaximum 50 per year

C-DOT Intern Eligibility Criteria 2026

Second-year exams must be completed and the results declared; the current semester should be between 5 and 8. Minimum CGPA 7.0 out of 10 / 70% or above in B.Tech/BE, as on the last semester for which result has been declared. Possessing the minimum qualifications does not guarantee an internship. Candidates with exposure in the area of intended internship and a good academic background are given preference. Only students of the listed 30 colleges may apply, and must select their own college from the drop-down list during registration. A candidate can apply for internship only once during his/her course. Candidates should check the official notification for the list of the 30 institutes and the branch-wise eligibility details.

C-DOT Intern Salary 2026

In addition to the stipend, interns may use basic facilities like the C-DOT canteen at subsidized rates.

C-DOT Intern Selection Process 2026

Shortlisted candidates are contacted via email; the list/panel of shortlisted candidates for interview is informed through individual e-mails. Offers are based on eligibility, academic background and demand. C-DOT publishes the final results on its website at the conclusion of the recruitment process. Final offer letters are sent by e-mail to selected candidates and their respective institutes. C-DOT will not entertain any query regarding the recruitment/selection process and will not correspond with candidates about reasons for non-selection.

Duration and Terms of the Internship

The internship lasts a minimum of three months and a maximum of one year. Interns not completing the minimum requisite period will not be issued any certificate. For final-year students, the internship duration in the No-Objection Certificate must not go beyond the last working day of the last semester of the course. The scheme also allows a semester to be completed at C-DOT based on R&D work, with supervision from both C-DOT and the institute. The internship is at C-DOT campuses at Delhi/Bengaluru.

C-DOT Intern Important Instructions
