KEA Selection Process 2026

Under the Karnataka Prosecutions and Government Litigations (Recruitment) Rules, 2012, selection has three stages: Preliminary examination – multiple-choice questions on an Offline-OMR sheet. In each paper (Paper-1 / Paper-2), SC and ST candidates need 35% and General and other categories 40%; qualifying candidates are called to the main examination in the ratio 1:10. Main examination – descriptive questions answered in writing. In each paper (Paper-1 / Paper-2 / Paper-3), SC and ST candidates need 35% and General and other categories 40%; qualifying candidates are called to the interview in the ratio 1:3. Interview – maximum 25 marks, conducted by the Selection Committee constituted under Government Order No. ಒಇ 95 ಪಿಪಿಇ 2026 dated 22.09.2026. It tests general knowledge, the ability to grasp points of law, understanding and suitability for the post. Questions may be answered in Kannada

KEA Exam Pattern 2026

Part-1 and Part-2 of Paper-1 are held at the same time. The OMR answer sheet has a fifth option. For a question left unanswered, the fifth option must be shaded; up to five extra minutes are given after the last bell for this. If no option, including the fifth, is shaded for a question, one-fourth (0.25) of a mark is deducted for that question. All question papers are in Kannada and English. Candidates may answer in Kannada or English, but each answer sheet must be written entirely in one language.
