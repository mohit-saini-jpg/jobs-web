CSIR NML Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection is through a Walk-in Interview/Test — there is no separate written examination stage unless invoked below. If the number of candidates turning up for a project is very high, a screening committee may shortlist candidates via a written test or other criteria, and only shortlisted candidates will then be interviewed. Candidates must bring the filled application form, original certificates, and self-attested photocopies on the day of the walk-in itself — no other mode of application is accepted. Final selection is at the discretion of the competent authority, CSIR-NML; the decision is final and binding, and no correspondence will be entertained. Engagement is subject to availability of project funds and is not a regular CSIR appointment.

How to Attend the Walk-in Interview

Download and fill the Project Staff Application Form (Advt. No. T/PS/04/2026) in the prescribed proforma — a separate form is required for each Group/Project applied for. Affix a recent self-attested colour passport-size photograph on the form. Attach self-attested photocopies of all educational, experience, age-proof and category certificates as applicable. Report to CSIR-NML, Burmamines, Jamshedpur-831007 (Jharkhand) between 9:00 AM and 11:00 AM on the date corresponding to your project's Group (see schedule above). Carry all original certificates/documents for verification along with the filled application and self-attested copies. Appear for the interview/test as conducted on the day. Important: No TA/DA is paid for attending the walk-in interview/test. Final-semester/year students awaiting results (without final result in hand) are not eligible to attend.

Documents Required

Filled-in Project Staff Application Form with affixed colour photograph SSC/SSLC/10th certificate (for name & date of birth proof) 10+2, Diploma/ITI, Graduation, Post-Graduation, Ph.D. certificates & mark sheets (as applicable to the post) CSIR-UGC NET/GATE certificate, where applicable (for PAT-I/PAT-II/JRF posts) Experience certificates/relieving letters, where required Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PwBD), where applicable Aadhaar Card and PAN Card Proof of joining OM, if presently working as Project Staff in CSIR-NML/other CSIR Lab

Important Terms & Conditions

Engagement confers no right to continuation, absorption, or regularization in CSIR/CSIR-NML; it can be terminated with one month's notice (or one month's stipend in lieu) from either side. Candidates already engaged as Project Staff in CSIR-NML or any other CSIR Lab/Institute for 6 years or more are not eligible . Those engaged for less than 6 years will have their tenure automatically end on completion of 6 years. Hindi may be used as the medium of conversation during the interview. Providing false information may result in a ban from future CSIR-NML recruitment/engagement, which may also be shared with other CSIR labs and government agencies. The number and level of positions may increase, decrease, or be cancelled at any stage, even after the selection process concludes.
