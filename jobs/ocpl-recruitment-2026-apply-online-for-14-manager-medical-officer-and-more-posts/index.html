OCPL Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

Applications submitted only in soft copy without receiving the hard copy by 14.12.2026 will be summarily rejected. No application will be received by hand or courier. Candidates working in PSUs or Government sectors must apply through proper channel or produce a "No Objection Certificate" at the interview for salary protection. To and fro travel fare (2nd/3rd AC train or bus fare, or sleeper class for S-2 grade) will be reimbursed to candidates attending the personal interview based on communication address. Basic qualifications must be acquired through regular full-time courses from recognized institutions (distance learning, part-time, or correspondence courses are not accepted, except AMIE where applicable). The e-mail ID provided in the application must remain valid for at least one year for all future recruitment communications.

OCPL Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Visit the career section of the official OCPL website at www.ocpl.org.in between 10:00 AM on 14.10.2026 and 5:00 PM on 14.11.2026.
  • 2Click on the online application link and read the instructions carefully before filling out the form.
  • 3Upload a scanned recent colour passport size photograph (3.5 cm X 4.5 cm, size 25 KB to 50 KB) and signature (3.5 cm X 1.5 cm, size 25 KB to 35 KB) in .jpg/.jpeg format.
  • 4Submit the online application successfully and note down details. If incomplete, register again.
  • 5Download the submitted application form and attach self-attested photocopies of all supporting documents for age, qualification, experience, and pay scale/CTC.
  • 6Super-scribe the name of the post applied for on the envelope.
  • 7Send the hard copy of the application along with documents by Speed Post or Registered Post to: Dy. General Manager (HR), Odisha Coal and Power , Zone - A, 4th floor, Fortune Towers, Chandrasekharpur, Bhubaneswar - 751023, Odisha, to reach on or before 5:00 PM on 14.12.2026.
