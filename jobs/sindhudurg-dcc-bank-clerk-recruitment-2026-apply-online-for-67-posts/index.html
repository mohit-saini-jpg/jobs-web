Candidates must hold a Graduate or Post-Graduate degree in any discipline with at least 40% marks, and must have passed the Matriculation (10th) examination with Marathi as one of the subjects. Sindhudurg DCC Bank Clerk Salary 2026

Selected candidates will be paid a stipend of Rs. 18,000/- per month during the probation period. The probation period for the post of Clerk is 18 months. After successful completion of probation, subject to satisfactory performance, the probationary Clerk will be placed in the Bank's regular Clerk grade and paid total emoluments as per the bank's Clerk pay scale. The exact pay scale and detailed salary structure after confirmation are not specified in the provided notification.

Sindhudurg DCC Bank Clerk Selection Process 2026

Selection for the post of Clerk will be based on an Online Examination followed by a Personal Interview. The online examination pattern is as follows. The online examination will be conducted in English and Marathi, with a duration of 75 minutes. Candidates must score a minimum of 50% (45 marks) as cut-off marks to be shortlisted for the personal interview; the bank reserves the right to increase or decrease this cut-off. Shortlisting for interview is also subject to validation of educational qualifications and experience based on submitted documents. The final merit list will be prepared in descending order based on combined scores from the online examination and the interview. The personal interview will be held at Sindhudurg.
