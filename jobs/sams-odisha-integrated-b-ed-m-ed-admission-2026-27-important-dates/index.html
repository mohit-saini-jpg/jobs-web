SAMS Odisha Integrated B.Ed.-M.Ed. Admission 2026-27 : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 20/08/2026

The 3-Year Integrated B.Ed.-M.Ed. programme is offered through the Student Academic Management System (SAMS) Odisha and is open to candidates who have completed a Post Graduate (P.G.) examination in eligible Science, Social Science, or Humanities subjects. Admission is based on centralized selection using career marks and applicable reservation rules - there is no separate entrance test for this course.

How to Apply for SAMS Odisha 3-Year Integrated B.Ed.-M.Ed. Admission 2026

The Common Application Form must be submitted online through the SAMS Odisha portal. Since the information entered is used throughout the admission process, candidates should complete each field carefully.

  • Visit the official SAMS Odisha admission portal.
  • Locate the section for 3-Year Integrated B.Ed.-M.Ed. Admission 2026-27.
  • Read the Information Brochure and eligibility conditions thoroughly.
  • Complete the Common Application Form (CAF).
  • Enter personal, educational, and other required details correctly.
  • Select the eligible HEIs/choices as permitted by the admission system.
  • Review all information carefully before final submission.
  • Submit the CAF within the deadline of 30 August 2026, 11:45 PM.
  • Save or print the submitted application details for future reference.
  • Regularly check the SAMS student account for seat allotment and further instructions.

Important Instructions for Applicants

  • Apply only through the official SAMS admission portal.
  • Read the Information Brochure carefully before submitting the CAF.
  • Verify that your P.G. subject qualifies under the eligibility conditions.
  • Enter academic marks and personal information accurately.
  • Do not wait until the last day to submit the application.
  • Check your SAMS student login after every selection round.
  • Complete fee payment within the prescribed time once a seat is allotted.
  • Follow the reporting instructions issued by the allotted HEI.
  • Retain copies of the submitted CAF and payment confirmation.
  • Keep monitoring the official portal for any revised timeline.
