Notification DatePost Date: 22/08/2026

The Himachal Pradesh Board of School Education (HPBOSE) conducted the Class 12 (Plus Two) compartment/supplementary examinations 2026 from July 3 to July 16, 2026, for students who could not clear one or more subjects in the annual Class 12 exam. As of the latest available reports, HPBOSE has not yet officially declared the Class 12 Supplementary Result 2026. The HPBOSE Class 12 supplementary result for 2026 was released on August 21, 2026.

Who Should Check the HPBOSE Class 12 Supplementary Result 2026?

Students who appeared for the HPBOSE Class 12 compartment/supplementary exam 2026 should check this result. This generally includes students who:

  • Failed to clear one or two subjects in the HPBOSE Class 12 Annual Result 2026
  • Applied for and appeared in the supplementary exam held between July 3 and July 16, 2026

Students who cleared all subjects in the annual exam do not need to check the supplementary result.

How to Check HPBOSE Class 12 Supplementary Result 2026 Online

Once declared, students will be able to download their provisional marksheet by following these steps:

  • Visit the official HPBOSE website: hpbose.org
  • Click on the "Results" tab on the homepage
  • Select the link for "HPBOSE Class 12 Supplementary Result 2026" (or "Compartment Result 2026")
  • Enter your roll number as printed on the admit card
  • Click submit/search
  • Your result will be displayed on the screen - download and save it, or take a printout

How to Check HPBOSE 12th Supplementary Result 2026 via SMS

Students can also check their result through SMS once it is declared:

  • Type: HP12 Roll_Number (for example, HP12 206151051)
  • Send it to: 5676750

How to Download HPBOSE 12th Supplementary Result 2026 via DigiLocker

  • Visit digilocker.gov.in or open the DigiLocker app
  • Log in using your Aadhaar-linked credentials
  • Navigate to the HPBOSE / education documents section
  • Download your Class 12 supplementary marksheet once it is uploaded by the board

Passing Marks for HPBOSE Class 12 Supplementary Exam

As per HPBOSE norms for the Class 12 exam, students must secure a minimum of 33% marks in each subject (theory and practical combined) and an overall aggregate of at least 33% to be declared pass.

What Details Will Appear on the HPBOSE Class 12 Supplementary Marksheet?

  • Student's name
  • Roll number
  • School name
  • Father's and mother's name
  • Subject-wise marks for the supplementary subject(s)
  • Total marks and qualifying status

The online marksheet is provisional. Students must collect the original marksheet-cum-certificate from their respective school after the official declaration.

After the HPBOSE Class 12 Supplementary Result 2026: What Next?

  • Download and save the provisional result from hpbose.org or DigiLocker
  • Collect the original marksheet from your school
  • If not satisfied with marks, apply for rechecking (verification of totalling) or revaluation within the window announced by HPBOSE
  • Proceed with college admissions, entrance exam applications (CUET, JEE, NEET, etc.), or further studies as applicable

Important Instructions for Students

  • Keep your admit card or roll number safely until the result and marksheet collection are complete
  • Cross-check all personal and subject details on the marksheet immediately after download
  • Report any discrepancy to your school or HPBOSE without delay
  • Rely only on the official website (hpbose.org) or DigiLocker for your final result - avoid unverified third-party links
