About DME Tripura Recruitment 2026

The Directorate of Medical Education, Government of Tripura, manages medical, dental, and allied health educational institutions across the state. This recruitment process aims to engage qualified dental faculty members on a contractual basis for Agartala Government Dental College & I.G.M Hospital, Agartala under the Health & Family Welfare Department, Govt. of Tripura.

Department-wise Vacancies

The notification announces 5 vacancies across dental specialties: In case of non availability of ST /SC candidate, suitable candidate will be selected from UR candidate. Qualification and Experience

The upper age limits on the date of application are: Professor: Age should not be more than 55 (fifty-five) years on the date of application. Pay and Terms

The engagement is on contractual basis for a period of 11 (eleven) months which will be renewed further, subject to satisfactory performance. The authority reserves the right for placement of all the above-mentioned faculties employed on contractual basis to any discipline at any time as per the need of the institutions. Leave rules will be governed as per TDES rules. TA & DA is not admissible for appearing in the interview.

DME Tripura Faculty Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection will be done as per 50% from Academic performance index (API) as calculated by the department + 50% from Interview / personality test (Total-100 marks). The date of interview will be notified later on.
