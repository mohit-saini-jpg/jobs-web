Pay and Tenure

The positions are purely on a temporary basis for the DHR project titled "Health Technology Assessment-Centre of Excellence" in the Department of Preventive and Social Medicine, JIPMER. The likely duration is one year with a possibility of extension up to two years based on performance. The consolidated remuneration for Senior Health Economist is Rs. 1,23,000/- per month plus HRA, and for Project Scientist-III is Rs. 78,000/- per month plus HRA. Hiring of manpower may vary according to the study undertaken, but within the aforesaid budget, and in exceptional cases, a 10% variation will be permitted after approval of the competent authority.

Selection and Interview

Applicants will be shortlisted based on the details furnished in the application, CV, and documentary proof provided. The selection process includes an interview covering Health Technology Assessment, Research Methodology, and Basic Epidemiology. The final selection will be based on a CV, application, and interview. Interviews may be conducted in person or via videoconferencing at the discretion of the Principal Investigator. The interview is tentatively scheduled for the first week of November 2026, with exact dates notified to shortlisted applicants through email.
