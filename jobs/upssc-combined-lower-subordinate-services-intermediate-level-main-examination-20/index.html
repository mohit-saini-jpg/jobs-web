UPSSSC Lower Subordinate Eligibility Criteria 2026

Applicants must satisfy the relevant post-wise recruitment rules, educational qualifications, age requirements and UPSSSC PET 2025 conditions. Candidates must have appeared in UPSSSC PET 2025 and possess a score issued by the Commission. Applicants will be shortlisted for the Main Examination on the basis of their PET 2025 normalized scores and merit, subject to the rules in the notification. Candidates with an absolute or normalized score of zero or less will not be shortlisted. Candidates provisionally appearing in PET 2025 results under investigation will be considered only in accordance with the Commission's final decisions. Educational qualifications differ by post. The notification refers to the prescribed recruitment service rules for each department and provides the required qualifications in its eligibility section. UPSSSC Lower Subordinate Selection Process 2026

Selection is conducted through the PET 2025-based shortlisting process followed by the Main Written Examination and subsequent procedures prescribed by UPSSSC. Shortlisting using UPSSSC PET 2025 normalized scores. Main Written Examination for shortlisted candidates. Post allocation and merit-based selection under the notified rules. Verification of educational qualifications, category certificates and other eligibility documents. The Commission will publish the examination date and further selection instructions through its official website.

UPSSSC Lower Subordinate Exam Pattern 2026

The Main Written Examination will consist of 100 objective-type questions carrying 100 marks . The examination duration is 2 hours , and one-fourth of the assigned marks will be deducted for each incorrect answer.
