UP Board Exam Centre List 2027 - Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 27/10/2026

UP Board Exam Centre List 2027 has been released by the Uttar Pradesh Madhyamik Shiksha Parishad (UPMSP) on 26 October 2026. The board has proposed 7,526 examination centres for the Class 10 and Class 12 board examinations 2027. Students, parents and school authorities can check the provisional examination centre list at Official Website and submit objections online until 28 October 2026.

How to Check UP Board Exam Centre List 2027?

  • Visit the official UP Board website at upmsp.edu.in.
  • Find the section related to examination centre determination for 2027.
  • Open the proposed examination centre list.
  • Select the relevant district or available centre-list document.
  • Find the name or code of the student's school.
  • Check the proposed examination centre assigned to the school.
  • Verify the institution and allocation details.
  • Download or save the relevant list for reference.
  • If an error is found, follow the official objection procedure before 28 October 2026.

How to Submit UP Board Exam Centre Objection 2027?

  • Visit the official UPMSP website.
  • Open the proposed examination centre notice dated 26 October 2026.
  • Download the prescribed objection form from the second page of the notice.
  • Enter the district name and school details.
  • Provide the applicant's name and mobile number.
  • Explain the examination centre issue clearly.
  • Provide a valid reason and supporting evidence.
  • Add the applicant's name and signature.
  • Submit the representation online through the process provided by UPMSP for the district centre determination committee.
  • Complete the submission no later than 28 October 2026.

Important Instructions for UP Board Exam Centre List 2027

  • The proposed centre list was released on 26 October 2026.
  • A total of 7,526 centres have been shortlisted provisionally.
  • The centre allocation applies to Class 10 and Class 12 board examinations.
  • Students should verify their school's proposed examination centre.
  • Objections must be submitted online by 28 October 2026.
  • The prescribed form requires a valid reason and supporting evidence.
  • District authorities will review complaints before finalising centre arrangements.
  • District-level lists must be forwarded to the board by 6 November 2026.
  • Students should check for the final examination centre list after the review process.
