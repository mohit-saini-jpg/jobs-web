SSB Odisha Lab Assistant Eligibility 2026

A candidate should have passed Bachelor Degree with Honours in the respective practical subject (as applicable to the post/subject applied for). Must be a citizen of India and a resident of the State of Odisha. Must be able to speak, read, and write Odia, and must have had Odia as a language subject in the H.S.C. Examination (or equivalent), or have passed a test in Odia language equivalent to the Middle School Standard. Candidates with academic qualifications from outside Odisha must produce proof of equivalence from a recognized University/Institution/Council/Board of Odisha, and proof of affiliation of their institution to a recognized University/Council/Board. Candidates with grades/grade-points must furnish the numerical equivalence of grades issued by the examining body. Must not have more than one spouse living (subject to exemptions under personal law, as permitted by Government)

SSB Odisha Lab Assistant Age Limit

A candidate falling under more than one category gets only the single most beneficial relaxation. Persons with Disabilities are entitled to cumulative age relaxation of 10 years, over and above normal relaxation, as per the Odisha Civil Services (Fixation of Upper Age Limit) Rules, 1989. PwD candidates belonging to SEBC/SC/ST are eligible for a cumulative age relaxation of up to 15 years. PwD candidates with temporary disability will not get age relaxation and will not be considered in subsequent selection stages. Only the Date of Birth mentioned in the High School Certificate (or equivalent) issued by the concerned Board/Council is accepted as age proof.

Method of Selection

Selection is based solely on a Written Test (OMR/CBT Mode). Questions are prepared as per the syllabus published on the SSB Odisha website ( ssbodisha.ac.in/syllabus ). In case of a tie, preference goes to the candidate with higher aggregate marks in the Bachelor's Degree (or equivalent); if the tie persists, the older candidate is selected. If conducted in OMR mode, the exam is held in different districts of Odisha (based on candidates' preferred district); if conducted in CBT mode, it is held in multiple shifts at designated centres across the state.
