HUDCO Recruitment 2026 Salary

HUDCO offers pay on the IDA pay-scale (w.e.f. 01.01.2017), along with an approximate annual Cost to Company (CTC) for each level. The CTC includes minimum Basic Pay, Industrial Dearness Allowance (IDA) at 55.7% of basic pay, HRA (30% of basic pay), Perks & Allowances (35% of basic pay), superannuation benefits (30% of basic pay), and Performance Related Pay (PRP) at maximum potential as per DPE slabs. In addition, employees get conveyance, company-leased accommodation (in lieu of HRA), medical reimbursement, loans and advances, group insurance, and other social security/functional benefits. Actual PRP payout depends on individual, team, and company performance, and IDA rates may change as notified by the Ministry of Finance.

HUDCO Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection for HUDCO Executive Recruitment 2026 is based solely on performance in a personal Interview; there is no written examination specified in the notification. Applications are first scrutinised for eligibility with respect to age, post-qualification experience, current pay level, and other notified criteria. Only prima-facie eligible applications are shortlisted for interview, generally in a ratio of 1:15 (subject to availability). If applications for a post are high, HUDCO may apply a higher shortlisting criterion than the specified minimum eligibility. Document verification is done before the interview; shortlisted candidates must bring all original certificates. The interview carries a total of 100 marks, split as: Job Knowledge (50 marks), Quality and nature of work experience (20 marks), and Overall personality (30 marks).
