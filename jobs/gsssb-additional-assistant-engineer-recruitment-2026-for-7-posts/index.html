GSSSB Additional Assistant Engineer Eligibility Criteria 2026

Candidates applying for GSSSB Additional Assistant Engineer (Mechanical) posts must satisfy the educational qualification, computer knowledge, language and age requirements prescribed in the respective notifications. Applicants must possess a Diploma in Mechanical Engineering from the Technical Examination Board, Gujarat, a recognized university or institution, or an equivalent qualification recognized by the government. The Ports and Transport Department has also accepted a Diploma in Automobile Engineering as an equivalent qualification through its communication dated 8 September 2026. In addition, candidates must possess basic knowledge of computer applications as prescribed under the applicable Gujarat Civil Services rules and adequate knowledge of Gujarati, Hindi or both. The minimum age for GSSSB AAE Recruitment 2026 is 18 years, and the maximum age is 33 years as on 9 November 202

GSSSB Additional Assistant Engineer Selection Process 2026

The selection process includes a competitive examination conducted through Computer Based Recruitment Test (CBRT) or OMR mode. The examination consists of objective-type multiple-choice questions divided into Part A and Part B. Further selection procedures will be conducted according to the Board's recruitment rules. The examination contains 210 questions carrying 210 marks, with a total duration of 180 minutes. One-fourth of the marks assigned to a question will be deducted for an incorrect answer. The minimum qualifying standard is 40% separately in Part A and Part B, as prescribed in the notifications.
