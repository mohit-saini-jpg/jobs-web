Details Mentioned on Uttarakhand B.Ed Admit Card 2026

Candidates should verify all the details printed on their admit card as soon as it is downloaded. The Uttarakhand B.Ed hall ticket 2026 contains the following information:

  • Name of the candidate
  • Application number
  • Date of birth
  • Candidate's photograph
  • Signature of the candidate
  • Exam date and timings
  • Exam centre address

If any detail on the admit card is incorrect, candidates are advised to contact the university helpdesk immediately, as this information was not specified in the source notification.

How to Download Uttarakhand B.Ed Admit Card 2026?

Candidates can download the Uttarakhand B.Ed 2026 hall ticket by following the steps given below:

  • Visit the official website: ukentrance.samarth.edu.in.
  • Click on the "Uttarakhand B.Ed Admit Card 2026" download link.
  • Enter your login credentials, such as username and password.
  • Click on the "Submit" button.
  • The Uttarakhand B.Ed admit card 2026 will appear on the screen.
  • Download and take a printout of the admit card for future reference.

Uttarakhand B.Ed 2026 Exam Day Instructions

Candidates must follow these instructions to be permitted entry into the Uttarakhand B.Ed exam centre:

  • Reporting time: Candidates should reach the exam centre well within the reporting time. Those arriving after the reporting time will not be allowed inside.
  • Documents required: Carry the admit card and a valid government ID proof to the test centre.
  • Identification proof: Any one of the following is accepted along with the admit card - Voter ID, Aadhaar Card, Driving Licence, PAN Card, or Passport.
  • Prohibited items: Electronic devices such as mobile phones, smart watches, and calculators are not allowed inside the exam hall.
