MPSC JSO Recruitment 2026 - Job Profile

Selected Junior Scientific Officers will conduct scientific examination and analysis of forensic exhibits in their respective division (DNA, Fingerprint, Chemistry, Ballistics & Toolmarks, Questioned Documents, Photography, Cyber Forensics). Duties also include attending crime scenes for identification, documentation, collection and preservation of evidence (especially for DNA and Fingerprint divisions), maintaining chain of custody, operating relevant forensic instruments and techniques, preparing scientific reports, assisting investigating agencies, and deposing as an expert witness in courts.

MPSC JSO Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

For all seven posts, working experience in a recognized forensic science laboratory is listed as desirable (not mandatory).

MPSC JSO Age Limit 2026

A candidate must not be less than 18 years and not more than 30 years of age as on 1st August 2026. The upper age limit is relaxable by up to 5 years for ST/SC candidates, or as may be notified by the Government from time to time. Accepted proof of age documents are HSLC/HSSLC Certificates, Voters ID, or Digitised Birth Certificate. A candidate must achieve the pass mark in the qualifying test of Mizo Language Proficiency as prescribed by the Government from time to time. However, a candidate who studied Mizo as a subject in Class-X (HSLC) or above within Mizoram, or who opted for Mizo as MIL outside Mizoram, is exempted from taking this qualifying test paper, as per Mizoram Gazette Notification No. A-11019/1/2021-P&AR(GSW) dated 09.04.2024 (Issue No. 247).

MPSC JSO Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

The notification states that a Direct Recruitment Examination for the post will be conducted by the Mizoram Public Service Commission, and that the syllabus for the examination is available for download on the Commission's official website. MPSC JSO Recruitment 2026 - Required Documents

Candidates must upload the following documents on the online portal before the closing date: Marksheets of HSLC and above Educational Certificates of HSLC and above Proof of Computer Proficiency Proof of Mizo Language Proficiency SC/ST Certificate (for SC/ST candidates only) Proof of date of birth Proof of educational qualification relevant to the post applied for Any other supporting document, to be uploaded in the "Others" box/field of the OTR page (multiple documents can be uploaded here) Where a document type on the OTR profile page requires both a certificate and its accompanying marksheet, both must be scanned together into a single PDF, or photographed together clearly, before uploading. Uploaded documents must not be protected/locked in any way that prevents viewing or printing - a protected file will be treated as not uploaded.
