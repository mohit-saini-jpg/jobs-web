About KSMHA

The Keralam State Mental Health Authority is a statutory authority constituted under section 45 of the Mental Healthcare Act 2017. Its office is at Red Cross Road, Thiruvananthapuram. The vacancies are in the Mental Health Review Boards at Kannur and Kozhikode, which were constituted as per Notification no. A/217/2017/KSMHA dated 25.01.2021 (available at www.ksmha.org). KSMHA has sent this notification to the Director of Health Services, the Director of Medical Education, the Director of Indian Systems of Medicine, the State Mission Director of the National Health Mission and the Project Director of the e-Health Mission, Thiruvananthapuram, and asked them to publish it on their official websites and notice boards.

Where and How to Send

Each post has its own application form in the notification: "Application Form for the Appointment of Member in Mental Health Review Board, Kannur" for the Member - Psychiatrist post, and "Application Form for the Appointment of Member in Mental Health Review Board, Kozhikode" for the Member - Medical Practitioner post. The application, with self-attested copies of the certificates and documents proving age, educational qualifications, experience, etc., may be submitted by post or by hand. KSMHA Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in KSMHA Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 2 vacancies: 1 Member - Psychiatrist in the Mental Health Review Board, Kannur, and 1 Member - Medical Practitioner in the Mental Health Review Board, Kozhikode.
Q2What is the last date to apply for KSMHA Recruitment 2026?
A
The last date of submission is 10 November 2026 (Tuesday), before 5.15 PM.
Q3What qualification is required for the KSMHA Member posts?
A
The Member - Psychiatrist needs the qualification set out in Section 2(1)(y) of the Mental Healthcare Act 2017. The Member - Medical Practitioner needs the qualification set out in Section 2(1)(n) of the Mental Healthcare Act, 2017.
Q4What honorarium does a Mental Health Review Board Member get?
A
A serving Government Psychiatrist/ Medical Practitioner gets Rs.2000/- per month. A retired Government Psychiatrist/ Medical Practitioner gets an honorarium limited to Rs.50,000/- [Last pay – pension (before commutation)]. Any other person nominated gets Rs. 1,000/- per sitting + TA.
Q5How do I apply for KSMHA Recruitment 2026?
A
Send the application form with self-attested copies of your certificates by post or by hand to the Chief Executive Officer, Keralam State Mental Health Authority, Red Cross Road, Trivandrum – 695 035.
Q6What should be written on the application envelope?
A
Write "Application for the appointment of Member of the Mental Health Review Board, Kannur/ Kozhikode" on the envelope.
Q7Which documents must be attached to the application?
A
Attach an attested copy of a certificate proving your date of birth, and self-attested copies of the certificates of degree, post graduation and medical council registration. Also attach the documents proving age, educational qualifications and experience, a description of your mental health experience and your Curriculum Vitae.
Q8What is the term of a Mental Health Review Board Member?
A
A Member holds office for five years or up to the age of seventy years, whichever is earlier. A Member is eligible for reappointment for another term of five years or up to the age of seventy years, whichever is earlier.
Q9Are retired Government doctors eligible for DA and HRA as Board Members?
A
No. A retired Government Psychiatrist/ Medical Practitioner gets an honorarium limited to Rs.50,000/- and is not eligible for DA and HRA.
Q10Can a political party office-bearer become a Mental Health Review Board Member?
A
No. Under Rule 19 of the Mental Healthcare (Central Mental Health Authority and Mental Health Review Boards) Rules, 2018, a member who holds any office in any political party during his tenure in the Board stands disqualified.
