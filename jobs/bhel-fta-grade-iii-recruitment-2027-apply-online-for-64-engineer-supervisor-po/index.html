BHEL FTA Salary 2027

The BHEL FTA Salary 2027 varies according to the position and year of engagement. Project Engineers will receive Rs.84,000 per month in the first year and Rs.88,000 per month in the second year. Project Supervisors and Hindi Translators will receive Rs.45,000 per month in the first year and Rs.48,000 per month in the second year. Selected candidates will also be eligible for reimbursement of the premium for a Mediclaim Policy with coverage of Rs.5 lakh for self and eligible family members, including spouse and dependent children, according to the notification. Important: These positions are on a fixed-tenure basis for two years. The advertised remuneration is not a permanent BHEL employee pay scale, and the appointment does not create a right to regular employment.

BHEL FTA Selection Process 2027

The BHEL FTA Selection Process 2027 is based on personal interviews. The number of candidates invited for interview will depend on the number of eligible applicants for each position. Where the eligible applicants exceed the prescribed ratio, BHEL will use aggregate marks in the qualifying examination to restrict the number of candidates shortlisted for interview. BHEL will examine the submitted applications to determine whether candidates meet the prescribed educational qualification, experience, age and other eligibility conditions. If the number of eligible applicants for a post is up to ten times the number of vacancies, all eligible candidates will be considered for personal interview. If the number exceeds this ratio, candidates will be shortlisted based on aggregate marks in their qualifying Degree or Diploma examination, as applicable.
