Instant E-PAN APPLY Link FAQs

Q1Instant E-PAN apply link kaise access karun?
A
CSC portal (https://csc.gov.in) par login karein, Services > PAN Services > Instant E-PAN Apply par click karein.
Q2Kya documents digital upload karne se koi problem hoti hai?
A
Sirf clear scanned copies ya PDF upload karein, size limit 2 MB per document, aur Aadhaar number match hona chahiye.
Q3Fee payment kaise hoti hai?
A
Online payment gateway se credit/debit card ya net banking se fee pay kar sakte hain, receipt download kar lein.
