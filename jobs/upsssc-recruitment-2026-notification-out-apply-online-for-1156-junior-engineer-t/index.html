UPSSSC Junior Engineer Mechanical Eligibility Criteria 2026

Candidates must satisfy both the UPSSSC PET 2025 requirement and the educational qualifications prescribed for their selected post. The notification includes separate technical qualifications and equivalent diploma disciplines for different departments. Candidates must have participated in the Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET) 2025. A PET 2025 scorecard must have been issued by the Commission. Shortlisting for the main examination will be based on normalized PET 2025 scores. Candidates with zero or negative normalized scores will not be shortlisted. Candidates whose PET 2025 results are provisionally marked or under investigation may be considered subject to the Commission's stated conditions. UPSSSC Junior Engineer Mechanical Exam Pattern 2026

The main examination will consist of 100 objective-type questions carrying 100 marks. The duration will be two hours, and one-fourth of the marks assigned to a question will be deducted for an incorrect answer.
