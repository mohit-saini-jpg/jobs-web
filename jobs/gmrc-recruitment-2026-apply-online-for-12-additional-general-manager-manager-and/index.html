About GMRC

Gujarat Metro Rail Corporation (GMRC) Limited is a 50:50 Special Purpose Vehicle jointly owned by the Government of India and the Government of Gujarat. The notice says it is entrusted with implementing the Ahmedabad Metro Rail Project and the Surat Metro Rail Project and with their Operations & Maintenance. The posts here are in Rolling Stock for these two projects. This is a recruitment by a company (SPV), not a regular government department post.

Vacancy Details

The notice lists 12 vacancies in total across six posts. It says the number of posts may vary based on further assessment of requirements. The notice prints one combined figure of 5 for the Engineer – Sr. Grade and Engineer – Jr. Grade posts and does not split it between the two grades. It does not print a category-wise break-up.

Who Can Apply

Graduate engineering in Electrical / Mechanical / Electrical & Electronics / Electronics & Communication / Power Electronics / Instrumentation or equivalent discipline from a Govt. recognized University/Institute. Candidates should have relevant experience such as planning, preparation of technical specification, administration of contracts, installation, testing, commissioning and statutory approvals of Rolling Stock system in railways/metros/other PSUs/private sector. They should preferably also be experienced with the latest electrical / rolling stock technologies of urban and suburban rail systems. Work experience: applicants from Government organizations, Metro Rail, Railway PSUs or other PSUs must have 15 years of post-qualification executive experience, of which relevant work experience should be 8 years. Pay and Tenure

The posts are offered on contract, deputation or post-superannuation basis for a minimum period of 3 years, extendable up to 5 years based on performance, subject to the standard terms and conditions of the organization. Apart from the pay, other benefits are paid as per the Company Policy. Selected candidates will be given Group medical coverage and Group personal accident insurance as per the governing policies of GMRCL. For IDA/CDA scale employees, the percentage of HRA may vary according to the place of posting as per Government guidelines and company policy.

Selection and Interview

The selection process comprises Personal Interviews followed by Document Verification and Medical Examination, as per the category prescribed for the post. It would judge different facets of knowledge, skills, experience, expertise, aptitude and physical fitness. Call letters for interview are issued to shortlisted applicants through registered email, with the time, date and venue. The venue, date and time of the interview will be informed in advance. Any request for a change in date or venue will not be entertained. Document verification is done before the interview. Candidates must carry their original certificates, failing which they will not be allowed to attend the interview. Preference is given to candidates with experience in the same grade or the immediate lower grade with 2 years' experience in the grade. GMRC Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in GMRC Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 12 vacancies in Rolling Stock: Additional General Manager 1, Joint General Manager 1, Sr. Deputy General Manager 1, Manager 3, Asst. Manager 1, and Engineer – Sr. Grade / Jr. Grade 5. The notice says the number of posts may vary.
Q2What is the last date to apply for GMRC Recruitment 2026?
A
The last date for submission of the online application is 5 December 2026.
Q3What is the qualification required for GMRC Rolling Stock posts?
A
All six posts require graduate engineering in Electrical / Mechanical / Electrical & Electronics / Electronics & Communication / Power Electronics / Instrumentation or equivalent discipline from a Govt. recognized University/Institute, plus post-specific experience.
Q4What is the age limit for GMRC Recruitment 2026?
A
The maximum age on contract basis is 53 years (Additional General Manager), 50 (Joint General Manager), 48 (Sr. Deputy General Manager), 40 (Manager), 35 (Asst. Manager) and 32 (Engineer posts). Age is reckoned as on 21 November 2026. The limits are higher for deputation and post-superannuation basis on the three senior posts.
Q5What is the salary for GMRC Rolling Stock posts?
A
Pay is in the Revised IDA scale: ₹100000-260000 for Additional General Manager, ₹90000-240000 for Joint General Manager, ₹80000-220000 for Sr. Deputy General Manager, ₹60000-180000 for Manager, ₹50000-160000 for Asst. Manager, ₹35000-110000 for Engineer – Sr. Grade and ₹30000-120000 for Engineer – Jr. Grade.
Q6How do I apply for GMRC Recruitment 2026?
A
Apply online through the "Careers" section of http://www.gujaratmetrorail.com. Only online applications are accepted. Register, log in, select the position, fill in the form, preview it and submit before 5 December 2026.
Q7What is the selection process for GMRC Recruitment 2026?
A
Selection comprises Personal Interviews followed by Document Verification and Medical Examination, as per the category prescribed for the post. Shortlisted applicants receive call letters with the date, time and venue by registered email.
Q8Is the GMRC Rolling Stock job permanent or on contract?
A
The posts are offered on contract, deputation or post-superannuation basis for a minimum period of 3 years, extendable up to 5 years based on performance, subject to the organization's standard terms and conditions.
Q9Can retired employees apply for GMRC Recruitment 2026?
A
Retired or serving officers may apply on post-superannuation basis for Additional General Manager, Joint General Manager and Sr. Deputy General Manager, with a maximum age of 62 years. Consolidated pay is ₹1,45,000 per month for the first two posts and ₹1,00,000 per month for Sr. Deputy General Manager.
Q10Is there any preference for Gujarat candidates?
A
Preference will be given to candidates who have worked in Gujarat State for at least 03 years and are conversant with the Gujarati language.
