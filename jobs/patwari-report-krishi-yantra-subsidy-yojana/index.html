Who Is Eligible: PATWARI REPORT krishi yantra subsidy yojana

How PATWARI REPORT krishi yantra subsidy yojana Works

Scheme ke tahat farmer ko pehle Patwari Report submit karni hoti hai, phir approved vendor se equipment purchase hota hai. Subsidy amount directly farmer ke bank account me transfer hoti hai, usually 30-45 din me.
