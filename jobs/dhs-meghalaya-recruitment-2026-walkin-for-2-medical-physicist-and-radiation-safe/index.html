DHS Meghalaya Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The walk-in interview is on 25 November 2026 at 10:00 AM in Conference Room No: 401, Directorate of Health Services (MI), Laitumkhrah, Shillong, with registration between 9:00 AM and 10:00 AM in Room No: 402. The Office of the Director of Health Services (MI), Government of Meghalaya, will fill one post of Medical Physicist-cum-Radiation Safety Officer (RSO) at ₹1,00,000 a month and one post of Medical Physicist at ₹70,000 a month, both on contractual basis in the Radiation Oncology Department, Civil Hospital, Shillong. Citizens of India may attend.

About DHS Meghalaya

The Office of the Director of Health Services (MI) is an office of the Government of Meghalaya, located in Shillong. This walk-in is for posts under the Phase-II Radiotherapy Services of the DAE Cancer Wing, Radiation Oncology Department, Civil Hospital, Shillong. Both posts are placed in the Department of Radiation Oncology, Civil Hospital, Shillong.

Posts and Eligibility

The pay is a consolidated remuneration. The notice names no allowances in addition to it. M.Sc. in Medical Physics/Radiological Physics or equivalent from a recognized University OR M.Sc. in Physics from a recognized University with a Post Graduate Diploma/Degree in Radiological/Medical Physics from a recognized University WITH an approval/certificate from the Competent Authority to function as Radiation Safety Officer (RSO) in Radiotherapy M.Sc. in Medical Physics/Radiological Physics or equivalent from a recognized University OR M.Sc. in Physics from a recognized University with a Post Graduate Diploma/Degree in Radiological/Medical Physics from a recognized University The candidate must have completed a minimum 12 months internship in a recognised well equipped Radiation Therapy department and hold AERB e-LORA registration For both posts, the age limit is up to 32 years. What to Bring

Candidates must submit a duly filled Standard Form of Application, along with an updated Curriculum Vitae (CV), self-attested photocopies of relevant certificates/documents and one recent passport-size photograph. The Standard Form of Application is not attached to this notice. The following documents must be brought in ORIGINAL on the day of the interview for verification, along with self-attested photocopies: Updated Curriculum Vitae (CV) All relevant educational qualification certificates, including Degree/Diploma certificates applicable to the post Mark sheets of all relevant examinations/qualifications, including those pertaining to the qualifying Degree/Diploma Relevant Work Experience Certificate(s), if available Scheduled Tribe (ST)/Scheduled Caste (SC) Certificate issued by the Competent Authority, wherever applicable Birth Certificate or SSLC/Matriculation Admit Card/Certificat

What Happens on the Day

Report for registration between 9:00 AM and 10:00 AM in Room No: 402, Directorate of Health Services (MI), Shillong on 25 November 2026. Hand in the filled Standard Form of Application with the CV, self-attested photocopies and photograph. Produce the original documents for verification. Attend the walk-in interview, which begins at 10:00 AM in Conference Room No: 401, Directorate of Health Services (MI), Laitumkhrah, Shillong. Candidates who fail to produce the required original certificates/documents on the day of the interview may not be permitted to appear for the interview.

DHS Meghalaya Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

The engagement shall be purely on a contractual basis. The engagement shall not confer upon the selected candidate any right or claim for regular appointment. Registration (Room No: 402) and the interview (Conference Room No: 401) are in different rooms of the Directorate of Health Services (MI).

Q1When is the DHS Meghalaya Medical Physicist walk-in interview?
A
The walk-in interview is on 25 November 2026 at 10:00 AM, with registration between 9:00 AM and 10:00 AM in Room No: 402.
Q2Where is the walk-in interview held?
A
The interview is held in Conference Room No: 401, Directorate of Health Services (MI), Laitumkhrah, Shillong.
Q3How many vacancies are there?
A
There are 2 vacancies: 1 Medical Physicist-cum-Radiation Safety Officer (RSO) and 1 Medical Physicist.
Q4What qualification is needed for the Medical Physicist post?
A
M.Sc. in Medical Physics/Radiological Physics or equivalent, or M.Sc. in Physics with a Post Graduate Diploma/Degree in Radiological/Medical Physics, plus a minimum 12 months internship in a recognised well equipped Radiation Therapy department and AERB e-LORA registration.
Q5What extra requirement does the Medical Physicist-cum-RSO post have?
A
Along with the M.Sc. qualification, the candidate needs an approval/certificate from the Competent Authority to function as Radiation Safety Officer (RSO) in Radiotherapy.
Q6What is the age limit, and who gets a relaxation?
A
The age limit for both posts is up to 32 years. The upper age limit is relaxable by 5 years for ST/SC candidates.
Q7What is the salary?
A
The consolidated remuneration is ₹1,00,000/- per month for Medical Physicist-cum-RSO and ₹70,000/- per month for Medical Physicist.
Q8What documents should I bring to the walk-in?
A
Bring a filled Standard Form of Application, an updated CV, one passport-size photograph, and originals with self-attested photocopies of certificates, mark sheets, experience certificates, ST/SC certificate, date-of-birth proof, identity proof and RSO approval or AERB e-LORA registration where applicable.
Q9Is the DHS Meghalaya Medical Physicist job permanent?
A
No. The engagement is purely on a contractual basis and gives no right or claim for regular appointment.
Q10Do local candidates get preference?
A
Yes. The notice states that preference will be given to Local Candidates.
