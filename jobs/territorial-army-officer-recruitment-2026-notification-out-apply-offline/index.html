Terms and Conditions of Service

Territorial Army is a part-time commitment with mandatory two months of training every year and does not offer a full-time career Service does not guarantee pension; it is subject to embodied service as per organisational requirement Commission is granted in the rank of Lieutenant, subject to fulfilling TA Rule 20A Promotion up to Lieutenant Colonel is by time scale; promotion to Colonel and Brigadier is by selection Candidates undergo Pre-Commission Training and are commissioned only on its successful completion

Territorial Army Recruitment 2026 Application Fee

No application fee has been specified in the notification. The application form is available free of cost for download from the official website.

Territorial Army Officer 2026 Selection Process

Screening and interview by the Army Headquarters Selection Board (ASB) at Directorate General Territorial Army, New Delhi Number of candidates called for interview depends on total applications received and vacancies to be filled Recommended candidates undergo Medical Examination at Armed Forces Clinic, New Delhi Final selection is subject to Police Verification There is no written examination for this recruitment; selection is through interview only.
