MANIT Bhopal Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must possess the educational qualification and other eligibility requirements prescribed under the Recruitment Rules, 2019 for Non-Teaching Staff in National Institutes of Technology . The academic and technical qualifications vary by post. Candidates must satisfy all essential conditions as on 10 December 2026 . Candidates applying for the 6 Junior Assistant vacancies must have passed Class 12 from a recognized Board. They must also have a minimum typing speed of 35 words per minute and proficiency in computer word processing and spreadsheet applications.
