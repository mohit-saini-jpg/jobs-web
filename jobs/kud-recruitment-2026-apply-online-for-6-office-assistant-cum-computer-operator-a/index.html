KUD Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Six temporary posts are open at the UGC-funded Research Centre for Women's Studies (RCWS), Karnatak University, Dharwad: 2 Assistant Professor, 1 Project Associate, 1 Project Assistant, 1 Office Assistant-cum-Computer Operator and 1 Library Assistant. Monthly consolidated pay runs from Rs.20,400 to Rs.57,700, and the posts are initially for one year. Applications must reach [email protected] by email or the Director, PMEB by post on or before 30 November 2026.

About the Centre

Karnatak University is at Pavate Nagar, Dharwad. The recruitment is conducted by its Planning, Monitoring and Evaluation Board (PMEB) for the Research Centre for Women's Studies (RCWS), whose project is funded by the University Grants Commission (UGC), New Delhi. Selected candidates work exclusively on this UGC-funded project at RCWS, Karnatak University, Dharwad.

Qualification and Experience

The notice prints one essential qualification for both teaching posts, in two alternative routes: Route 1: Master degree in women studies with NET or KSET in Women's Studies or Equivalent / MA in Inter-disciplinary subjects related to MA in Women's studies (Faculty of Social Science) and Diploma in Women's Studies with NET or KSET in interdisciplinary subject. The Master Degree needs minimum 55% for GM and 50% for SC/ST, Cat-1 candidates. OR Route 2: Ph.D. in Women's Studies or Ph.D. in Inter-disciplinary subjects (Women's Based Studies) and Diploma in Women's Studies. Preference is given to candidates with a Ph.D. or NET/KSET in the relevant subject(s) with Diploma in Women's Studies, wherever applicable. The computer skill is shown by a valid certificate of a minimum one year Computer Training Course, or Advanced Diploma in Computer Applications, or Diploma in Computer Application, or

Pay and Terms

The posts are purely temporary and initially for a period of one year. The tenure may be extended, subject to satisfactory performance, satisfactory progress of the work, and the availability of funds with RCWS from UGC, New Delhi. Appointment is based on merit and roster and does not confer any right to claim regular appointment or entitlement to housing, medical facilities, or other benefits available to regular University employees. Selected candidates must abide by the applicable rules, regulations, ordinances, statutes and Acts of the University, UGC and RCWS, as applicable from time to time.

A
For teaching posts it is Rs.1000 for General and other eligible candidates and Rs.500 for SC/ST/Category-I. For non-teaching posts it is Rs.600 and Rs.300 respectively, paid online only at SBI Collect.
