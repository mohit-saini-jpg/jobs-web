GMC Narayanpet Recruitment 2026 Application Fee

Candidates must pay an application fee of ₹500 (Rupees Five Hundred only), via Demand Draft drawn on the State Bank of India, in favour of the "Superintendent, Govt. General Hospital, Hospital Development Society, Narayanpet."

GMC Narayanpet Recruitment 2026 Important Instructions

Posts are on outsourcing basis only, for a period up to 31.03.2027, or till the actual need ceases, or till the regular posts are filled up, whichever is earlier - this does not confer any right to regular appointment. Candidates must possess the minimum qualification specified for the respective post; original certificates must be produced for scrutiny along with the application. For further queries, candidates may contact the College at Telephone Number 08506282506. Any updates or corrigenda regarding this recruitment will be published on the official websites listed above.
