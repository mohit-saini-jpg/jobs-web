BECIL Social Media Analyst Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1Can a graduate from any subject apply for the Social Media Analyst post?
A
Yes, if the degree is from a recognized University/Institute. Along with named subjects such as Journalism and Marketing, the notice accepts any other recognized graduate degree, but the 2-3 years of relevant experience is still required.
Q2Is this a permanent central government job?
A
No. The post is purely on contract basis, and the selected candidates will be deployed in the Ministry of Mines.
Q3Is there a written exam?
A
The notice does not fix a written exam. After BECIL shortlists applications, the Client may use an interview, assessment, skill test or another evaluation method.
Q4How will shortlisted candidates be informed?
A
They will receive the date, time and venue or mode through their registered email and/or telephone. BECIL also asks candidates to monitor the Spam/Junk folder of their registered email account.
Q5Do SC/ST and PWD candidates pay a fee?
A
No, their application processing fee is NIL. Only candidates in all other categories pay the Rs.295/- fee.
Q6Will TA/DA be paid for attending the interview?
A
No. TA/DA is not admissible for any test, document verification, interview, personal interaction or skill test, or for joining duties upon selection.
Q7Is police verification required?
A
Yes. The selected candidate must submit a valid Police Verification Report at Document Verification after selection, and failure to do so makes the selection liable to cancellation.
Q8Can a candidate contact the Ministry of Mines directly about this recruitment?
A
No. Candidates must not approach the Client/Principal Employer directly, and canvassing in any form leads to disqualification. Queries can go to the BECIL helpdesk on 0120-4177850 / 0120-4177860.
Q9Does BECIL charge anything besides the application fee?
A
No. BECIL says it does not demand or accept any other payment at any stage and does not use any agent, intermediary or consultant for recruitment. Fraudulent offers may be reported to BECIL.
