Assam Rifles Technical and Tradesman Vacancy 2026 - 354 Posts

Assam Rifles has announced 354 vacancies across 17 technical and tradesman categories . The recruitment includes Warrant Officer, Havildar and Rifleman ranks in technical, mechanical, medical-support and other trades. Appendix-B of the official notification provides the reservation-wise distribution of all 354 vacancies. Ex-Servicemen Reservation: The notification earmarks 10% of vacancies in the notified trades for eligible Ex-Servicemen. If suitable Ex-Servicemen candidates are unavailable, the reserved positions may be filled by eligible non-Ex-Servicemen candidates belonging to the respective categories. The 10% Ex-Servicemen reservation is included in the published vacancies and must not be added separately to the total of 354.

Assam Rifles Trade Skill Test 2026

The Trade Skill Test assesses the practical skills required for the selected technical or tradesman position. The notification specifies that the Trade Skill Test consists of a Computer-Based Practical Skill Test and a Trade-Specific Practical Skill Test , as applicable. The test is qualifying in nature, and no marks are awarded for the Trade Skill Test in the final merit calculation. Shortlisting is based on CBT merit in the relevant trade and category. Candidates who qualify the Trade Skill Test proceed to the applicable document verification and medical examination. The notification specifies shortlisting of approximately four candidates per vacancy for the subsequent document verification and medical assessment process, subject to the applicable trade and category provisions.

Assam Rifles Medical Examination 2026

Candidates qualifying in the recruitment stages must undergo a Detailed Medical Examination (DME). Medical examinations will be conducted according to the Uniform Guidelines for Recruitment Medical Examination prescribed for Central Armed Police Forces and Assam Rifles. Where permitted, candidates declared unfit may undergo a Review Medical Examination (RME) under the relevant medical procedures. Important: Candidates must satisfy the prescribed medical requirements. Passing the written examination and Trade Skill Test does not remove the medical-fitness requirement.

Assam Rifles Salary 2026

Selected candidates will receive pay and allowances according to the applicable salary structure for Assam Rifles personnel. The uploaded recruitment notice states that the pay scale and allowances will be admissible according to the pay scales applicable to Assam Rifles personnel. Exact basic pay, allowances and service benefits depend on the applicable rank and appointment rules.
