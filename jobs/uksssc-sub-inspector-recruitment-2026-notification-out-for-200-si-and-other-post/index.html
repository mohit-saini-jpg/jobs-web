UKSSSC Sub Inspector Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Uttarakhand Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UKSSSC) has released the UKSSSC Sub Inspector Recruitment 2026 Notification on 25 November 2026 for 200 Group C vacancies. The recruitment covers Sub Inspector (Civil Police), Sub Inspector (Intelligence), Platoon Commander, Fire Station Second Officer and Excise Sub Inspector posts in Uttarakhand.

UKSSSC Sub Inspector Vacancy 2026

UKSSSC has announced 200 vacancies across five categories of posts. Sub Inspector (Civil Police) accounts for the highest number of vacancies with 93 posts, followed by Platoon Commander with 81 posts. The combined number of Sub Inspector (Civil Police) and Sub Inspector (Intelligence) vacancies is 112. The remaining 88 vacancies are distributed among Platoon Commander, Fire Station Second Officer and Excise Sub Inspector posts. Horizontal reservation provisions for eligible women, ex-servicemen, dependents of freedom fighters and other applicable categories are specified separately in the official notification. Candidates should refer to the original vacancy tables for complete reservation details.

UKSSSC Sub Inspector Salary 2026

Selected candidates for Sub Inspector (Civil Police), Sub Inspector (Intelligence), Platoon Commander and Fire Station Second Officer posts will receive a Level 7 pay scale of Rs. 44,900 to Rs. 1,42,400. Excise Sub Inspectors will receive a Level 5 pay scale of Rs. 29,200 to Rs. 92,300. The advertised pay scales represent basic pay under the respective pay levels. Actual monthly remuneration will depend on applicable allowances, deductions and Government service rules.

UKSSSC Sub Inspector Selection Process 2026

UKSSSC will conduct the recruitment in two principal competitive stages. The first stage consists of physical standards and physical efficiency examinations, while the second stage consists of a 200-mark objective written examination for candidates who qualify in the physical tests. Physical Standards Test (PST): Candidates must satisfy the prescribed physical measurement requirements applicable to their selected posts. Physical Efficiency Test (PET): Candidates must qualify in the prescribed physical activities. Written Examination: Candidates who qualify in the physical examinations will be eligible to appear for the objective competitive written examination. Document Verification: Educational qualifications, age, reservation claims and other eligibility documents will be verified. UKSSSC Sub Inspector Exam Pattern 2026

The UKSSSC Sub Inspector written examination will be conducted in offline mode using OMR answer sheets. The examination carries a total of 200 marks and consists of objective multiple-choice questions based on the educational qualification level prescribed for the posts. Candidates marking more than one answer for a question will have that response treated as incorrect. No negative marks will be awarded for questions left unanswered. Candidates obtaining less than the prescribed minimum qualifying marks will not be considered eligible for the merit list. The actual selection cutoff may be higher depending on vacancies, competition and applicable reservation provisions.

UKSSSC SI Medical Examination and Fitness Requirements

Candidates selected for appointment must possess the prescribed physical and mental fitness required to perform the duties of the advertised posts. Appointment is subject to satisfactory medical examination and fulfilment of the applicable service conditions. The notification specifies medical assessment requirements covering vision, physical fitness and other conditions that may interfere with the efficient discharge of official duties.

UKSSSC Sub Inspector Service Bond

The notification specifies a service bond requirement for selected candidates appointed as Sub Inspector (Civil Police), Sub Inspector (Intelligence), Platoon Commander and Fire Station Second Officer. Selected candidates will be required to execute the prescribed bond agreeing to serve for at least five years from the commencement of training. Candidates leaving service before completing the prescribed period may be required to repay applicable training expenditure and payments in accordance with the bond conditions.
