MP ESB Sub Engineer Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must hold the educational/technical/professional qualification prescribed by the concerned demanding department's own departmental recruitment rules, as on the date of submitting the application. This qualification is specified by each department in its demand letter sent to the Board and varies by post code and technical stream (Electrical, Mechanical, Civil, Computer Science, Electronics & Telecommunication, Fashion Technology, Architecture & Interior Designing, Modern Office Management, Hotel Management, Agriculture Engineering, Textile Design, Automobile Engineering, or Refrigeration & Air Conditioning Engineering). Candidates must indicate their post preference (priority) while applying. If a candidate qualifies the exam and is recommended by the Board but does not fulfil the appointment criteria prescribed for that post, their candidature for that post will be rejected a

MP ESB Sub Engineer Age Limit

Age is calculated as on 1st January 2026 for all posts under this combined Junior Service (Combined Eligibility) exam. No age relaxation is admissible for Economically Weaker Section (EWS) candidates as per the applicable General Administration Department circular. Age relaxation for physically disabled/ex-servicemen candidates is as per Government of Madhya Pradesh orders issued from time to time. In no case will the maximum age limit, after including all applicable relaxations, exceed 45 years (except contract employees and categories specified under Rule 14). Additional incentive-based age relaxation of 5 years is available for candidates rewarded under the Inter-Caste Marriage Incentive Scheme, and for sportspersons honoured with the Vikram Award, as per applicable General Administration Department memoranda. MP ESB Sub Engineer Selection Process

Selection is based on performance in the single computer-based online exam described above. Only candidates securing the minimum qualifying marks are included in the merit list of successful candidates - 50% for unreserved category candidates, and 40% for SC/ST/OBC and Divyangjan candidates (with up to a 10% relaxation from the unreserved cut-off). EWS candidates are also given a 10% relaxation in minimum qualifying marks, though final selection from the EWS category list is made strictly on merit. Being included in the merit list does not by itself confer a right to appointment. MPESB Group 3 Sub Engineer Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

Aadhaar registration is mandatory for all candidates; multi-level biometric verification is mandatory at the time of exam entry and during the exam, and Aadhaar must be unlocked at the time of biometric verification. Candidates must carry an original photo identity proof (Voter ID, PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Driving Licence, or Passport) to the exam centre; e-Aadhaar is valid only if verified through UIDAI. Candidates without a valid original photo ID will not be allowed to sit for the exam. Candidates will be allowed entry into the exam room only until the specified reporting time; late candidates will not be granted entry. Use of electronic devices such as mobile phones, calculators, log tables, digital watches, sunglasses, and any unfair-means material is strictly prohibited in the exam room. Candidates must safely retain their online application number, as the admit card can be obtained only through it. Candidates must bring a black ball-point pen and their downloaded admit card to the exam hall. No candidate will be permitted to leave the exam room between the start and end of the exam. Any technical error causing delay in the start of the exam will not disadvantage candidates - full time will still be provided to attempt the paper. The Board's role is limited to conducting the exam and providing results to the concerned departments; the entire responsibility for the appointment process rests with the respective department. Candidates from Madhya Pradesh's special primitive tribal communities (Baiga, Sahariya/Saharia, and Bharia) applying for non-executive posts under the Scheduled Tribe category must send their applications with all required documents directly to the concerned department in hard copy (Format-2 of the rule book) rather than to the ESB, by the application closing date; applications sent to ESB in such cases will not be considered valid. Where the department's post is executive in nature, no exam exemption applies to these communities and they must apply online as Scheduled Tribe candidates.
