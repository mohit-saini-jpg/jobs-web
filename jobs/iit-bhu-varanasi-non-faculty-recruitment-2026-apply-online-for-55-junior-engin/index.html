IIT (BHU) Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Applications are screened based on the data submitted in the online form; more stringent criteria than the advertised minimum may be applied for shortlisting, and candidates with higher qualification/merit may be preferred. The selection process may include a Screening Test/Written Test/Presentation/Group Discussion/Personal Interview, as applicable to each post; the exact stages and schedule are not detailed in the notification and will be emailed to shortlisted candidates. Mere eligibility does not entitle a candidate to be called for the selection process. The Institute may call only the requisite number of candidates for the selection process after shortlisting on qualification, suitability, and experience. The Institute reserves the right to offer a post on contract basis instead of a regular post, depending on qualification, experience, and/or performance in the selection process.
