Kaliabor College of Education 1st Merit List 2026 - Quick : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 12/08/2026

Kaliabor College of Education (KCE), Kuwaritol, Nagaon, Assam, released its 1st Merit List for B.Ed admission 2026 on 11 August 2026, based on the GUBEDCET-2026 (Gauhati University B.Ed Common Entrance Test 2026) score. The notice covers the Unreserved (open) category merit list along with separate reserved-category lists for SC, OBC/MOBC, ST(P), ST(H), EWS, and PWD, plus first waiting lists for most categories. Selected candidates must report for offline admission on 12 August 2026, between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM. This article summarises the official notice dates, category-wise selection counts, cut-off mark ranges, and the admission process without republishing the full roll number list; candidates should verify their own name and roll number against the official notice issued by the college.

How to Check Your Name in the KCE Merit List

  • Visit the official Kaliabor College of Education notice board or the college website.
  • Open the 1st Merit List notice dated 11 August 2026.
  • Go to the list matching your applied category (Unreserved, SC, OBC/MOBC, ST(P), ST(H), EWS, or PWD).
  • Match your GUBEDCET-2026 roll number against the list to check whether you are in the selected list or the waiting list.
  • If selected, note the admission date, time, and documents required before reporting to the college.

Important Instructions

  • Selected candidates must report for admission on 12 August 2026 between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM.
  • Candidates who fail to get admitted within this window will have their candidature automatically cancelled, without any prior information.
  • Admission will be conducted offline only.
