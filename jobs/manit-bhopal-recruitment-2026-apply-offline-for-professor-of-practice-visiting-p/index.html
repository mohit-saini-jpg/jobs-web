PostQualification Required
Professor of Practice (PoP)Candidates should have more than 15 years of professional experience at the level of senior management position. Master's degree in engineering/sciences/humanities or a related field. Ph.D. is desirable but not essential (lack of Ph.D. must be offset by demonstrated domain knowledge obtained from deep experience).
Visiting ProfessorPhD in the respective field and possessing one of the degrees from the Centrally Funded Institution (CFIs) under the Ministry of Education, Government of India. Adjunct FacultyPhD in the respective field and possessing one of the degrees from the Centrally Funded Institution (CFIs) under the Ministry of Education, Government of India with 15 years’ experience in the relevant field in R&D labs/IITs/NITs and Expert in domain area.

MANIT Bhopal Salary

Further, applicable expenses for attending conferences and seminars incurred as per Institute rules up to Rs. 20,000/- per month shall be considered for reimbursement, subject to an annual ceiling of Rs. 1,00,000/- per year for Professor of Practice and Visiting Professor.
PostPay Level Pay
Professor of PracticeConsolidated emolument upto Rs. 1,75,000/- per month (shall not exceed Last Pay minus Pension in case of reemployment after retirement). Fixed/unchanged during engagement. No DA, increments or other allowances.
Visiting ProfessorMaximum monthly ceiling of Rs. 1,50,000/- (shall not exceed Last Pay minus Pension for retired persons). Alternatively, Rs. 3,000/- per period / Rs. 10,000/- per day + TA (as per Institute norms) with a maximum monthly ceiling of Rs. 1,50,000/- (including TA). Fixed/unchanged; no DA or increments.
Adjunct FacultyRs. 3,000/- per period / Rs. 10,000/- per day + TA (as per Institute norms), subject to a monthly ceiling of Rs. 1,50,000/- (including TA) per month.
