HPRCA Clerk Eligibility Criteria

Candidates applying for HPRCA Clerk Recruitment 2026 must meet the educational qualification, typing speed, and domicile-linked schooling conditions given below. Eligibility is assessed as on 11.09.2026, the closing date for submission of the ORA. In addition, the notification states that a candidate shall be eligible for appointment only if he/she has passed Matriculation and 10+2 from a school/institution situated within Himachal Pradesh; this condition does not apply to Bonafide Himachalis.

HPRCA Clerk Salary

Selected candidates will be engaged as a Job Trainee under the Job Trainee Scheme-2025 (notified by the Department of Personnel vide notification No. PER(AP)-C-B(15)-3/2024 dated 19.07.2025, and a subsequent letter dated 26.09.2025) and will receive a consolidated fixed amount of ₹12,500/- per month. A detailed regular pay scale/pay band structure beyond this consolidated Job Trainee amount is not specified in the notification.

HPRCA Clerk Selection Process

Computer Based Test (CBT)/written screening test of 120 marks, of 1½ hours (one and a half hours) duration, or as decided by the Aayog The test consists of 120 multiple-choice questions, each carrying 1 mark, covering: General Knowledge (including HP General Knowledge and Current Affairs); Everyday Science, Logical Reasoning, Social Science, General English and General Hindi of Matric Standard; and Word Processing on computer Minimum qualifying marks: 45% for General category, 40% for SC/ST/OBC/Wards of Freedom Fighters — relaxable by up to 5% (to 40% for General and 35% for SC/ST/OBC/WFF) if sufficient candidates are not available Shortlisted candidates (based on CBT/screening test merit) must then appear for a prescribed typing skill test, which is qualifying in nature In multi-shift CBT exams, scores are normalized using the Z-Score (Mean and Standard Deviation) Method Final selection

HPRCA Clerk Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Sign Up: Register on the HPRCA portal (hprca.hp.gov.in) by creating a username and password
  • 2One-Time Registration (OTR): Complete OTR by providing personal information, educational qualifications, work experience (if any), a recent photograph and signature, and relevant supporting documents
  • 3Complete/Update Profile: Ensure the profile is complete before applying; existing users can edit details such as qualification or contact information
  • 4Apply for the Post: View the list of available posts on the HPRCA portal and click "Apply" against the Clerk post
  • 5Select Preferred Exam District: Choose preferred district(s) for the examination from the dropdown menu (subject to availability of centre/nodes)
  • 6Confirm Application Details: Review the auto-filled application form before proceeding
  • 7Pay Application Fee: Pay ₹800/- through the available online payment gateway (Credit Card/Debit Card/Net Banking)
  • 8Submission Confirmation: After successful payment, the application is considered complete
  • 9The downloaded/printed copy of the online application, along with necessary original certificates and self-attested photocopies, must be produced at the time of document verification. No offline application form is accepted.
