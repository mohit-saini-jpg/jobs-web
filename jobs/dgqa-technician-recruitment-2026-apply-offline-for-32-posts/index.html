DGQA Eligibility Criteria 2026

Only Indian nationals are eligible to apply. Applicants must possess the required educational and technical qualifications from recognized institutions. Additional requirements and conditions for each post are as follows: Specified Trades for Technician (Skilled): Computer Hardware and Networking, Computer Operator and Programming Assistant, Cutting & Sewing, Electrician, Fitter, Information Technology and Electronic System Maintenance, Instrument Mechanic, Machinist, Painter, Pattern Maker, Plumber, Refrigeration and Air conditioner Mechanic, Tool and Die Maker or Tools and Die (Jig and Fixture). DGQA Selection Process 2026

The recruitment process consists of the following stages in order: Written Test Departmental Trade Skill Test Document Verification and Medical Examination In case where more than one candidate secures equal marks (trade-cum-station wise), the tie will be resolved by applying the following methods in order of sequence: Date of birth, with older candidates placed higher. Candidate with higher marks in Numerical Aptitude. Candidate with higher marks in English Language & Comprehension.

DGQA Exam Pattern 2026

The Written Test will be objective type multiple choice questions only, conducted in one session (forenoon or afternoon) at Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh. Question papers will follow the syllabus of Tenth Standard / Matriculation of Central/State Boards. Part (i) will be bilingual in English and Hindi. Part (ii) and Part (iii) will be in English only. There is no negative marking for wrong answers. Candidates shortlisted based on Written Test marks and reservation categories will be called for the Trade Test.

DGQA Technician Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Candidates can apply only through the offline postal route. Each applicant can submit only one application indicating a specific Trade and Station.
  • 2Type the application form neatly on A4 size paper using Arial font, size 12, as per Appendix 'A', and print it double-sided.
  • 3Fill in the required personal, educational, technical, and trade details in block letters, signing every page of the application.
  • 4Affix one recent coloured self-attested passport size photograph in the application form and self-signed photographs on each of the two Admit Cards (Appendix 'B').
  • 5Attach self-attested copies of all required certificates (Aadhaar, birth certificate, qualification, ITI certificates, experience, caste/EWS/ESM if applicable) along with signed examination instructions (Appendix 'C').
  • 6Enclose one self-addressed envelope of size 30 cms x 12 cms affixed with a postal stamp of Rs 60/- for dispatch of the admit card.
  • 7Superscribe the main envelope clearly as "APPLICATION FOR THE POST OF TECHNICIAN (SKILLED)" or "APPLICATION FOR THE POST OF TECHNICIAN (SEMI SKILLED)".
  • 8Send the application packet by ordinary post or Speed Post to: The Controller, Controllerate of Quality Assurance (General Stores), DGQA Stores Complex, Ashok Path, Meerpur Cantt, Kanpur – 208004, Uttar Pradesh.
