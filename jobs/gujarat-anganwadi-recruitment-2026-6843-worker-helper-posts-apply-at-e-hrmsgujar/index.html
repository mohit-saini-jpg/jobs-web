Gujarat Anganwadi Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Eligible applicants must be Indian women who meet the prescribed educational qualification, age and local residence requirements for the chosen Anganwadi centre. Age and eligibility requirements are assessed according to the applicable closing-date conditions. Applicants must meet the local residence condition for their selected Anganwadi centre. Residence anywhere in Gujarat does not automatically establish eligibility for every centre. The applicant must meet the prescribed minimum one-year local residence requirement. For rural Anganwadi centres, the relevant revenue village condition applies. For urban centres, the relevant municipal or municipal-corporation election ward condition applies. A prescribed residence certificate from the concerned Mamlatdar is required. The certificate must comply with the relevant issue-date requirements.

Gujarat Anganwadi Recruitment 2026: 6843 Worker &amp : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection is based on the prescribed merit criteria, including educational qualification and marks. Merit lists are prepared according to the relevant Anganwadi-centre and recruitment rules. Serving Anganwadi Helpers applying for Worker positions may receive preference under the applicable provisions. The available recruitment information describes merit-based selection rather than a separate written examination. Candidates must enter correct educational marks and provide supporting certificates. Where grades or scores are used instead of percentage marks, the prescribed conversion documentation is required. Candidates who passed an examination through multiple attempts must provide the relevant original marksheets as instructed.

Documents Required

Applicants should prepare the applicable supporting documents for their online application. Date-of-birth proof. Class 10 marksheet and certificate. Class 12 marksheet and certificate, where applicable. Recognised diploma certificates and marksheets, where applicable. Prescribed local residence certificate. Caste or reservation certificate, where applicable. Widow certificate, where applicable. Official grade-to-marks conversion certificate, if required. Marksheets for all applicable examination attempts. Prescribed self-declaration. Identity proof.

Q2How many vacancies are available in Gujarat Anganwadi Recruitment 2026?

Q3Who can apply for Gujarat Anganwadi Recruitment 2026?
A
Eligible female candidates who satisfy the local residence requirements can apply for Anganwadi Worker and Anganwadi Helper posts.
Q4Is Class 10 enough for the Anganwadi Worker post?
A
Class 10 alone does not meet the stated Worker qualification. Candidates need Class 12, or Class 10 along with an AICTE-recognised diploma of at least two years. Class 10 is the minimum qualification for Helper.
Q5What is the age limit for Gujarat Anganwadi Recruitment 2026?
A
The age limit for Anganwadi Worker and Helper applicants is 18 to 33 years. Serving Anganwadi Helpers applying for Worker posts can have a maximum age limit of 43 years as per applicable rules.
Q6Can a woman apply for an Anganwadi centre outside her village or urban ward?
A
Applicants must satisfy the prescribed local residence requirement for the selected Anganwadi centre. Rural and urban centres have different local-area conditions.
Q7What is the last date to apply for Gujarat Anganwadi Recruitment 2026?
A
The last date to submit the online application is 14 October 2026 up to 12:00 midnight.
Q8What is the selection process for Gujarat Anganwadi Recruitment 2026?
A
Selection will be based on a merit list prepared according to the prescribed recruitment rules, considering educational qualifications and marks.
Q9What is the honorarium for Gujarat Anganwadi Worker and Helper posts?
A
Selected Anganwadi Workers will receive Rs. 10,000 per month, while Anganwadi Helpers will receive Rs. 5,500 per month as honorarium.

Candidates can apply online through the Gujarat e-HRMS official website by selecting the Anganwadi Worker and Helper Recruitment 2026 advertisement.
