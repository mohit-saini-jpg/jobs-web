VVCMC Recruitment 2026 Application Fee

The VVCMC examination fee is Rs. 1,000 for Open category candidates and Rs. 900 for eligible reserved-category, orphan, persons with disabilities and EWS candidates. Ex-servicemen are exempted from paying the examination fee. Candidates can pay the examination fee through the online payment gateway using the following facilities: Debit Card (RuPay, Visa, MasterCard or Maestro) Credit Card Internet Banking IMPS Cash Cards or Mobile Wallets The examination fee is non-refundable. Any applicable bank transaction charges must be borne by the candidate. Applicants should retain the generated e-receipt and a printout of the completed application form.

VVCMC Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

The selection process for VVCMC Group C posts consists of an online competitive examination followed by document verification. The official notification explicitly states that no oral examination or interview will be conducted for the advertised Group C positions. Application Scrutiny: Applications will be reviewed based on the information submitted by candidates. Additional shortlisting criteria may be applied if the number of applications is excessively high. Online Examination: Eligible candidates will appear for an objective online competitive examination. Publication of Marks: The examination marks of eligible candidates will be published through the official municipal corporation website. Document Verification: Candidates falling within the probable selection range will be called for verification of original certificates and supporting documents. Final Selection: Category-wise sele

VVCMC Recruitment 2026 Exam Pattern and Syllabus

The VVCMC recruitment examination will be conducted online in an objective multiple-choice format. The notification refers to an examination duration of 120 minutes. Questions may assess the candidate's knowledge of the relevant post, professional subject, duties, responsibilities and related developments. The detailed post-wise examination syllabus, subject-wise marks and applicable examination instructions should be checked through subsequent announcements on vvcmc.in . Where examinations are conducted in different shifts or question paper sets, the municipal corporation may apply the prescribed score-equating procedure to account for differences in question difficulty.
