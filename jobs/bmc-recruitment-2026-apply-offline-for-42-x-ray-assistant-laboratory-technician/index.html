Where and How to Send

The notice asks candidates to submit the fully filled application, with all documents of educational qualification and the original fee receipt attached, at the inward/outward department of LTMG Hospital by 5:30 PM on 02-12-2026. The notice states that applications received by post will not be considered, and that applications submitted or received at the office or anywhere else after 02.12.2026 will not be considered. The application is addressed to the Dean, Lokmanya Tilak Municipal Medical College and General Hospital, Sion, Mumbai - 400022. Posts, Eligibility and Pay

The pay for each post is a lump-sum honorarium per month. The contract period is 15.12.2026 to 31.03.2027. The notice prints no category-wise break-up of these vacancies. No DA, HRA or any other allowance is payable besides the lump-sum honorarium. All posts: the age of the candidate must not be more than 38 years. BMC Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

The notice describes the selection in the following steps: A merit list is prepared from the prescribed educational qualification and the marks obtained in the interview. The selection list is prepared from the candidates in the merit list. The place, date and time of the interview are informed two days in advance by email, to the email ID given in the application. A valid email ID in the application is therefore required. Candidates must attend the interview at their own cost. The Trade Test applies to X-ray Assistant, Laboratory Assistant and E.C.G. Technician, as printed in their rows. The notice does not give its date or syllabus.

BMC Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

Before applying, candidates must satisfy themselves that they meet the qualifications and conditions of the post they apply for. The mobile number, email ID and correspondence address (with PIN code) in the application must be clear and complete. A candidate who has been convicted by a court in a case involving moral turpitude or a criminal case, or who has a police inquiry or court case pending, must give that information. False information, false documents or concealed information at any time after the selection process begins or after appointment cancels the candidature or ends the appointment without notice. BMC has not authorised anyone to sell or accept application forms. The Dean, LTMG Hospital, may stop the selection process at any stage for administrative or other reasons.

Terms of the Contract Appointment

The engagement is strictly on contract. The appointee cannot claim a right to permanent service in the Corporation or any regular service benefits. A one-day break in service is given before every 90 days of the contract service. An agreement on Rs. 500 bond paper is taken at the time of appointment. Weekly off follows the sessions of the department to which the appointee is posted. Three casual leaves are allowed for January 2027 to March 2027, and no other leave. Biometric attendance must be marked daily according to duty hours. Absence without permission is treated as leave without pay (LWP). Work must be done in three shifts in any department of LTMG Hospital and Medical College, Sion. The Dean, LTMG Hospital, may end the contract without prior notice if the work is not satisfactory. Q1How many vacancies are there in the BMC LTMG Hospital contract recruitment 2026?

Q2What is the last date to apply?
A
The completed application must be submitted at the hospital's inward/outward department by 5:30 PM on 2 December 2026. Applications submitted or received after 02.12.2026 are not considered.
Q3What is the qualification for Dialysis Technician?
A
The qualification is a B.Sc. in Dialysis Technology from a recognised university, or 12th pass with a 1 year / 6 months certificate course in Dialysis Technology. The Marathi and computer certificate conditions for all posts also apply.
Q4What is the age limit?
A
The age of the candidate must not be more than 38 years. The notice prints no age relaxation.
Q5What is the monthly pay for each post?
A
It is a lump-sum honorarium per month. X-ray Assistant and Laboratory Assistant get Rs. 16000, Laboratory Technician, Neurology Technician, Dialysis Technician and E.C.G. Technician get Rs. 20000, and Physiotherapist and Occupational Therapist get Rs. 40000.
Q6What is the application fee?
A
The fee is Rs. 870 + 18% GST Rs. 157 = Rs. 1027. It is non-refundable under any circumstances.
Q7Where is the fee paid and can the application be sent by post?
A
The fee is paid at the Cashier Department (Room No. 15), Ground Floor, College Building, LTMG Hospital, from 25.11.2026 to 02.12.2026, 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM on working days. Applications by post are not considered.
Q8How are candidates selected?
A
A merit list is prepared from the prescribed qualification and the marks obtained in the interview, and the selection list is made from it. The interview details are sent by email two days before.

No. The posts are purely on contract, from 15.12.2026 to 31.03.2027. Appointees cannot claim a right to permanent service in the Corporation or any regular service benefits.
Q10Are DA and HRA paid in addition to the honorarium?
