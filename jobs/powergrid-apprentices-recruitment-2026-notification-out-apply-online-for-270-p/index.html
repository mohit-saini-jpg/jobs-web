POWERGRID Apprentice Selection Process

Applications are screened in merit order based on eligibility criteria and the qualification prescribed for each trade, region-wise Incomplete or ineligible applications are summarily rejected without intimation Reservation for SC/ST/OBC(NCL)/PwBD is applied as per the Apprentices Act, 1961 Candidates are called for document verification in a ratio of 1:5 against the notified training slots, in merit order Shortlisted candidates are informed through their registered email; no TA/DA is paid for attending document verification A final merit list is prepared from candidates found suitable after document verification Selected candidates must accept a tripartite apprenticeship contract on the NATS/NAPS portal and submit a medical certificate and police verification before engagement For PwBD candidates in Western Region-II, reservation/relaxation is subject to submission of a medical certific

  • 1Register as a candidate/student on NAPS (for ITI-Electrician) at apprenticeshipindia.gov.in, or on NATS (for Degree/Diploma in Engineering/Civil) at nats.education.gov.in, and complete/update the profile with all required documents
