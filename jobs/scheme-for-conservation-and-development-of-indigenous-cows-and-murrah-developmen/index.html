Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Overview

Sarkari Yojana for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows and Murrah Development is a government scheme that aims to conserve and develop indigenous cows and improve the livelihood of farmers. This scheme is a great opportunity for farmers to get financial assistance and improve their cattle rearing skills.

Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Expert Analysis

This scheme is a step towards promoting indigenous cow breeds and improving the dairy sector in India. It will not only benefit farmers but also contribute to the country's economic growth.

Who Is Eligible — Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a

All farmers who own indigenous cows and Murrah buffaloes are eligible to apply for this scheme. They must have a valid Aadhaar card and bank account.

Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Application Process

To apply for this scheme, farmers must submit their application form along with required documents such as Aadhaar card, bank account details, and cattle registration certificate. They must also ensure that their cattle are vaccinated and have a valid health certificate.

Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Benefits Details

The scheme provides financial assistance of ₹10,000 per annum to each beneficiary farmer. This amount will be credited directly to their bank account. The scheme also provides training and capacity building programs for farmers to improve their cattle rearing skills.

How Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Works

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare. The application process is online, and farmers can submit their applications through the official website. The scheme is funded by the central government, and the funds are disbursed to the beneficiaries through direct benefit transfer.

Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Application Tips

To ensure successful application, farmers must ensure that their application form is complete and accurate. They must also submit all required documents and ensure that their cattle are in good health. Common rejection reasons include incomplete application form, incorrect Aadhaar card details, and lack of cattle registration certificate.
