Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Overview

Government of India has launched a scheme for conservation and development of indigenous cows and Murrah buffalo. This scheme aims to promote the welfare of these animals and improve the livelihoods of farmers. The scheme provides financial assistance to farmers for the purchase of indigenous cows and Murrah buffalo.

Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Expert Analysis

This scheme is a step towards promoting the conservation of indigenous breeds and improving the dairy sector in India. It will also help in improving the livelihoods of farmers and providing employment opportunities in rural areas.

Who Is Eligible — Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a

Farmers who own less than 5 acres of land and are engaged in dairy farming are eligible to apply for this scheme.

Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Application Process

To apply for this scheme, farmers need to submit an application form along with required documents such as identity proof, land ownership proof, and bank account details. They should also ensure that they have a valid bank account in their name.

Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Benefits Details

The scheme provides a subsidy of up to ₹50,000 per animal for the purchase of indigenous cows and Murrah buffalo. This subsidy will help farmers in improving their dairy business and increasing their income.

How Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Works

The scheme is implemented by the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare. The department will provide financial assistance to farmers through banks and other financial institutions. The subsidy will be disbursed directly into the bank account of the farmer.

Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Application Tips

To ensure successful application, farmers should ensure that they meet the eligibility criteria and submit all required documents. They should also ensure that they have a valid bank account in their name and that the account is linked to their Aadhaar card.
