Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Overview

Ye yojana desh ki gaay aur Murrah buffalo ki suraksha aur development ke liye launch hui hai. Aspirants ko iske benefits aur apply karne ka tarika simple steps mein mil jayega.

Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Expert Analysis

Scheme ka maksad gaay ki population badhana aur dairy sector ko boost karna hai, isse rural economy ko direct fayda hoga. Government financial assistance aur training provide karti hai, jo sustainable livestock management ko aage badhata hai.

Who Is Eligible: Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a

Gaon ke kisaan, dairy farmers, livestock breeders aur jo bhi indigenous gaay ya Murrah buffalo palte hain, wo is scheme ke liye eligible hain.

Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Application Process

1. Apna land record, cattle ownership documents aur bank passbook ready rakhein. 2. Online portal par registration ke liye Aadhaar aur mobile number verify karen. 3. Required forms ko dhyan se fill karen aur supporting documents attach karen.

Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Benefits Details

Scheme ke tahat eligible beneficiaries ko har 6 mahine mein Rs. 10,000 tak ki financial assistance mil sakti hai, plus subsidised feed aur veterinary care, jo milke total cost ka 30% tak cover karti hai.

How Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Works

State animal husbandry department scheme ko implement karta hai, local officers applications verify karte hain, phir funds ko bank transfer ya direct cash disbursement ke through beneficiaries tak pahunchaya jata hai.

Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Application Tips

Application form ko sahi se fill karen, sabhi required documents attach karen aur deadline se pehle submit karen. Common rejection reasons – incomplete documents, mismatched cattle details, ya outdated Aadhaar – se bachne ke liye double check karen.

Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a FAQs

Q1Is there any age limit for applicants?
A
Nahi, scheme sabhi age groups ke cattle owners ke liye khuli hai, bas cattle ownership proof hona chahiye.
Q2How often can I receive the financial assistance?
A
Har 6 mahine mein ek baar, jab aapki cattle health aur development report approve ho jati hai.
Q3Can I apply if I own both indigenous cows and Murrah buffaloes?
A
Haan, dono categories ke liye aap ek hi application mein details de sakte hain aur dono ke liye benefits milenge.
