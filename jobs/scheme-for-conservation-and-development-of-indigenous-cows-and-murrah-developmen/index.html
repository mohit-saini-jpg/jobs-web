Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Overview

Ye scheme desh ki gaay aur Murrah buffalo ki suraksha aur development ke liye launch hui hai. Farmers, breeders aur gaay palan karne wale log isse financial support aur technical madad le sakte hain.

Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Expert Analysis

Scheme ka maksad indigenous breeds ko protect karna aur dairy productivity badhana hai, jisse rural economy ko boost milega. Government subsidies aur training se breed improvement asaan ho jayega.

Who Is Eligible: Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a

Gaay ya Murrah buffalo ke malik, dairy farmers, animal husbandry students aur NGOs jo breed conservation me kaam karte hain, eligible hain.

Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Application Process

1. Apna land record aur animal ownership documents ready rakhein. 2. Aadhaar, PAN aur bank account details verify kar lein. 3. Online portal par registration ke liye clear photographs aur breed certificate upload karein.

Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Benefits Details

Scheme ke tahat eligible beneficiaries ko Rs. 10,000 se Rs. 25,000 tak ki subsidy mil sakti hai, jo gaay ki health care, artificial insemination aur fodder supply me use hoti hai.

How Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Works

State Animal Husbandry Departments scheme ko implement karte hain, local extension officers application verify karte hain, aur funds ko direct bank transfer ya cash grant ke roop me beneficiaries tak pahunchate hain.

Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Application Tips

Application form ko dhyan se fill karein, sabhi required documents attach karein, aur deadline se pehle submit karein. Common rejection reasons me incomplete documents ya wrong breed certification shamil hain.

Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a FAQs

Q1Is scheme ka koi age limit hai?
A
Nahi, scheme sabhi age groups ke gaay aur Murrah buffalo owners ke liye khuli hai, bas ownership proof zaroori hai.
Q2Subsidy ka payment method kya hai?
A
Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) ke through bank account me credit hota hai, ya kuch states me cash grant bhi mil sakta hai.
Q3Application ka deadline kab tak hai?
A
Har financial year ke March tak online portal par apply kar sakte hain; state wise thoda variation ho sakta hai.
