Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Overview

Ye scheme desh ki gaay ki suraksha aur Murrah breed ki development ke liye launch hui hai. Farmers, gaushalas aur NGOs ko financial assistance milti hai, jisse gaay ki health aur milk production improve hoti hai.

Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Expert Analysis

Government ka yeh initiative rural economy ko boost karta hai aur traditional livestock ko modern techniques se jodta hai. Proper documentation se beneficiaries ko timely fund milna ensure hota hai.

Who Is Eligible: Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a

Gaon ke dairy farmers, gaushala owners, aur NGOs jo indigenous cows ki breeding ya health programs chalate hain, unke liye yeh scheme hai.

Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Application Process

1. Apna land record, milk production data aur gaay ka pedigree certificate ready rakhein. 2. Online portal par registration ke liye Aadhaar aur bank account details verify kar lein. 3. Application form ko dhyan se fill karein aur required documents scan karke upload karein.

Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Benefits Details

Scheme ke tahat har eligible beneficiary ko Rs. 10,000 se Rs. 25,000 tak ka grant mil sakta hai, jo gaay ki health check-up, artificial insemination aur fodder purchase me use hota hai.

How Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Works

State Animal Husbandry Department scheme ko implement karta hai. Application online submit hoti hai, phir verification ke baad funds ko direct bank transfer (DBT) ke through beneficiaries ke account me bheja jata hai.

Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Application Tips

Form fill karte waqt sabhi fields ko accurately fill karein, duplicate documents avoid karein, aur deadline se pehle submit karein. Common rejection reasons me incomplete documents aur mismatched Aadhaar details shamil hain.

Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a FAQs

Q1Is scheme ka koi age limit hai?
A
Nahi, scheme me age limit nahi hai; bas applicant ko gaay ki ownership ya management ka proof dena hota hai.
Q2Fund ka disbursement kitna samay me hota hai?
A
Verification ke baad, usually 30-45 din ke andar DBT ke through fund transfer ho jata hai.
Q3Kya ek hi applicant multiple grants le sakta hai?
A
Haan, agar applicant ke paas alag-alag projects (jaise breeding, fodder, health) hain to har project ke liye alag grant mil sakti hai, lekin total amount scheme ke ceiling limit ke andar hi rehna chahiye.
