Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Overview

Ye scheme gaon ke kisano aur gaay palan karne walon ko indigenous cows aur Murrah breed ki suraksha aur development ke liye financial assistance deti hai. Aspirants ko iske benefits aur apply process ka short guide milta hai.

Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Expert Analysis

Government ka focus indigenous cattle ko protect karna aur Murrah breed ko improve karna hai, isliye scheme me subsidies, health care aur breeding support shamil hai. Yeh rural economy ko boost karta hai aur dairy productivity badhata hai.

Who Is Eligible: Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a

Gaon ke kisaan, gaay palan karne wale, dairy farmers aur NGOs jo indigenous cattle ki conservation me kaam karte hain, ve eligible hain.

Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Application Process

1. Apna land record aur cattle ownership documents ready rakhein. 2. Aadhaar, bank account details aur recent passport size photo ready rakhein. 3. Online portal ya nearest panchayat office se application form download karke sahi tarah fill karein.

Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Benefits Details

Scheme ke tahat eligible beneficiaries ko 10,000 se 30,000 rupaye tak ki subsidy mil sakti hai, jo gaay ki health care, artificial insemination aur fodder purchase me use hoti hai. Yeh financial aid milne se dairy income me noticeable growth hoti hai.

How Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Works

Scheme ka implementation state agriculture department ke through hota hai. Application approve hone ke baad, funds ko bank transfer ya direct cash disbursement ke through beneficiary ke account me bheja jata hai. Monitoring agencies regular health check-ups aur breed improvement programs conduct karti hain.

Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a Application Tips

Application me sabhi required documents attach karein, especially cattle ownership proof. Form me galat ya incomplete info se rejection ho sakta hai. Time limit ka dhyan rakhein aur portal par status regularly check karte rahein.

Scheme for Conservation and Development of Indigenous Cows a FAQs

Q1Kaun kaun se documents zaroori hain scheme ke liye?
A
Aadhaar card, land record, gaay ka ownership proof, recent passport size photo, bank account details aur pichle 2 saal ka milk production record.
Q2Scheme ka benefit kitna milta hai?
A
Beneficiary ki gaay ki health aur breed ke hisaab se 10,000 se 30,000 rupaye tak ki subsidy milti hai, jo specific activities jaise AI, fodder purchase aur health care me use hoti hai.
Q3Application kaise aur kahaan submit karen?
A
Online portal (www.indianagri.gov.in) par form download karke fill karein, ya nearest panchayat office/ agriculture department office me manual submission kar sakte hain.
