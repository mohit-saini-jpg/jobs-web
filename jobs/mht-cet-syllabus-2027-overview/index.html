Is There Negative Marking in MHT CET 2027?

No. The Maharashtra CET Cell has confirmed that there will be no negative marking in MHT CET 2027. Mathematics questions carry two marks each, while Physics, Chemistry and Biology questions carry one mark each.

Incorrect answers will not attract a negative marking penalty. Candidates should nevertheless use the available examination time carefully and focus on answering questions accurately.

MHT CET 2027 Important Topics

Candidates should cover all the prescribed syllabus topics rather than preparing only selected chapters. The official examination pattern assigns approximately 80% of questions to Class 12, making thorough preparation of the current Class 12 curriculum important.

The Class 11 chapters specifically listed in the official PDF should also be studied. Physics includes topics such as motion, gravitation and electrostatics; Chemistry includes bonding, atomic structure and organic chemistry; Mathematics includes trigonometry, functions and probability; and Biology includes biomolecules, respiration, nutrition and excretion.

How to Prepare for MHT CET 2027?

Candidates should prepare according to the officially prescribed curriculum and give appropriate attention to both Class 11 and Class 12 topics.

  • Download the current official MHT CET 2027 syllabus.
  • Prepare all the prescribed Class 12 topics and the specified Class 11 chapters.
  • Allocate more study time to Class 12 because it carries approximately 80% weightage.
  • Practise application-based questions in Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics and Biology.
  • Review subject-wise formulas, concepts and problem-solving methods.
  • Practise previous examination papers and mock tests.
  • Use timed practice sessions to improve speed and accuracy.
  • Revise difficult topics regularly before the examination.
