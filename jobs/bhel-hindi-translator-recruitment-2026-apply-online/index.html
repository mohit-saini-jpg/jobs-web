BHEL Hindi Translator Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Bharat Heavy Electricals Limited has one unreserved vacancy for an Official Language Translator (Hindi Translator) at its Power Sector Southern Region headquarters in Chennai. The post is on a fixed-term basis and pays ₹45,000/- a month in the first year. Candidates need a Hindi and English academic background and 2 years of translation experience. Online applications are open until 17 November 2026, and a signed hard copy must also reach Chennai.

About BHEL

BHEL describes itself as a leading Indian engineering organisation that supplies products and services for power generation and transmission, industry, transportation, oil and gas, renewable energy and defence. Its network includes 16 manufacturing divisions, 4 Power Sector regional centres, 8 service centres, 15 regional offices, 4 overseas offices and many project sites. This recruitment is for the headquarters of its Power Sector Southern Region in Chennai.

BHEL Hindi Translator Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1Is the online application enough for this BHEL post?
A
No. After applying online, you must also send the downloaded, signed application with a photograph and documents to BHEL in Chennai by post or courier.
Q2Do fee-exempted candidates need to send the hard copy?
A
Yes. SC, ST, ESM and PwBD candidates pay no processing fee, but sending the application form to the Pallikaranai address is still mandatory for them.
Q3Does apprenticeship count towards the 2 years of experience?
A
No. Any training or apprenticeship period is left out of the 2 years of post-qualification experience.
Q4Can a graduate apply without a Master's degree?
A
Yes, if the Bachelor's degree from a recognised university has Hindi and English as main subjects and the translation experience requirement is met.
Q5Will BHEL pay for travel to the interview?
A
Yes. On proof of travel, BHEL reimburses the actual to-and-fro fare up to second-class rail fare by the shortest route. The fare is counted from the starting station or the postal address, whichever is nearer to the interview venue.
Q6Can I change my category after submitting the online form?
A
No. PwBD, Jammu and Kashmir and ex-servicemen candidates will not be allowed to change the category later.
Q7Can this job become permanent?
A
The appointment gives no right to any regular or permanent job in BHEL, and the post is not against a permanent vacancy.
Q8What happens if false information is found after appointment?
A
BHEL may end the candidature or service without notice if a candidate gave false information, hid a relevant fact or did not meet the eligibility criteria. Any attempt at recommendation or undue influence is also a disqualification.
Q9Where will changes to this recruitment appear?
A
Any corrigendum or extension will be published only on the BHEL careers website. BHEL may cancel, limit, extend or reopen the process without notice or reasons. The notification is issued in Hindi and English, and the English version prevails if the two differ.
