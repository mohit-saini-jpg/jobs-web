UCSL Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Objective Type Test (50 marks) + Personal Interview (20 marks) + PowerPoint Presentation on work experience (30 marks) = 100 marks total. The Objective Type Test is 60 minutes, 50 MCQs, covering General Knowledge (10 marks) and Subject-based questions (40 marks), with no negative marking. Minimum pass marks: 50% (UR/EWS), 40% (SC/ST for reserved vacancies). Phase I - Written Test: Objective Type (40 marks) + Descriptive Type (40 marks). Phase II - PowerPoint Presentation on work experience (20 marks). Total 100 marks. The Objective Test's General section (Part A) covers General Knowledge, General English, Reasoning, and Quantitative Aptitude (5 marks each), plus Discipline-related questions (20 marks).   • 2Complete the one-time Registration on the SAP online application portal.

  • 4Upload all required certificates (proof of age, educational qualification, experience, caste/category, disability, etc.) along with a recent passport-size colour photograph.
