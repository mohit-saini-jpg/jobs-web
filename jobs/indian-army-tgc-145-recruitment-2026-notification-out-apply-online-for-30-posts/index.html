Indian Army TGC-145 Recruitment 2026 – Quick Summary

Who is recruiting? Indian Army, through the Directorate General of Recruiting, for training at the Indian Military Academy (IMA), Dehradun. Which posts? 145th Technical Graduate Course (TGC-145) leading to Permanent Commission as a Lieutenant in the Indian Army. How many vacancies? 30 (tentative), across Civil, Computer Science, Electrical, Electronics, Mechanical and Misc Engineering streams. Who can apply? Unmarried male candidates with a BE/B.Tech or M.Sc Computer Science in a notified engineering stream (or final-year candidates meeting the conditions below), aged 20 to 27 years as on 01 July 2027. Application start date: 30 September 2026, 1500 Hrs. Last date: 29 October 2026, 1500 Hrs. Application mode: Online only, at www.joinindianarmy.nic.in. Training location: Indian Military Academy (IMA), Dehradun. SSB interviews are held at Prayagraj (UP), Bhopal (MP), Bengaluru (Karnataka)

Indian Army TGC-145 Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

A candidate must be either: (i) a citizen of India, or (ii) a subject of Nepal, or (iii) a person of Indian origin who has migrated from Pakistan, Burma, Sri Lanka, or the East African countries of Kenya, Uganda, the United Republic of Tanzania, Zambia, Malawi, Zaire, Ethiopia or Vietnam, with the intention of permanently settling in India. Candidates in categories (ii) and (iii) must have a certificate of eligibility issued by the Government of India, except Gorkha subjects of Nepal, for whom this certificate is not necessary. Where required, this certificate must be attached with the application. Only unmarried male candidates are eligible to apply.

Indian Army TGC-145 Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Shortlisting of Applications: The Integrated HQ of MoD (Army) reserves the right to shortlist applications based on the vacancy-to-call-up ratio for each engineering stream, without assigning any reason. A cut-off is applied in each stream on the cumulative percentage of marks obtained up to the final semester/year. Percentages given by the college/university after applying their own CGPA-to-percentage formula are considered final; a candidate whose converted percentage falls below the cut-off should not report for SSB even if shortlisted as per the marks entered in the application. Allotment of Selection Centre and Dates: After shortlisting, candidates must log in to the website and select their SSB dates and centres, available on a first-come, first-served basis. Type of Commission, Seniority and Training

Grant of Commission: Selected candidates are granted a Short Service Commission on probation in the rank of Lieutenant, from the date of course commencement or the date of reporting at IMA Dehradun, whichever is later. Permanent Commission: On successful completion of training, cadets are granted Permanent Commission in the Army in the rank of Lieutenant. Ante Date Seniority: Engineering Graduates of TGC entry are granted one year of ante-date seniority from the date of commission, in the rank of Lieutenant, for seniority, promotion and pay increment purposes. Induction in IMA: Selected candidates are detailed for training at IMA Dehradun according to their position in the final order of merit (stream-wise), up to the number of available vacancies. Indian Army TGC-145 Pay, Stipend and Allowances

Fixed Stipend for Cadet Training: Rs. 56,100 per month (starting pay in Level 10 of the Pay Matrix) throughout training at IMA Dehradun. On successful commissioning, the officer's pay is fixed in the first cell of Level 10; the training period is not treated as commissioned service, but arrears of admissible allowances for the training period are paid to cadets. Military Service Pay (MSP): Rs. 15,500 per month, fixed, for officers from Lieutenant to Brigadier rank. Dearness Allowance: Admissible at the same rates and conditions as applicable to civilian personnel, from time to time. Para Reserve Allowance: Rs. 2,625 per month. Technical Allowance: Tier-I – Rs. 3,000 per month; Tier-II – Rs. 4,500 per month. Indian Army TGC-145 Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Applications are accepted only online, on www.joinindianarmy.nic.in.
  • 2Click 'Officer Entry Apply/Login' and then 'Registration' (not required if already registered on the website).
  • 3Fill the online registration form after reading the instructions carefully. After registering, click 'Apply Online' under the Dashboard; a page 'Officers Selection Eligibility' will open. Click 'Apply' shown against Technical Graduate Course.
  • 4A page 'Application Form' will open. Read the instructions carefully and click 'Continue' to fill details under Personal information, Communication details, Education details, and details of previous SSB (if any). Click 'Save & Continue' before moving to the next segment.
  • 5After filling the last segment, you reach a 'Summary of your information' page, where you can check and edit entries already made. Only after confirming the details are correct, click 'Submit'.
  • 6Candidates must click 'Submit' each time they reopen the application to edit any detail.
  • 7After the online application finally closes on the last day, candidates must, 30 minutes later, take out two printed copies of their application bearing the Roll Number.
  • 8Aadhaar number and Matriculation certificate are mandatory for registration on the website. Candidates are advised to read the notification carefully before filling the online application, to avoid mistakes and rejection. Only one online application should be submitted; multiple applications from the same candidate will lead to cancellation of candidature, with no representation entertained.
  • 9No changes can be made to details submitted in the online application after its closure. CGPA/Grades must be converted into marks as per the formula adopted by the concerned university for filling 'Marks obtained in Graduation'; any discrepancy found at any stage of selection will lead to cancellation of candidature. The candidate's name, parents' names, and date of birth in the profile and online application must exactly match the Matriculation/Secondary School Examination Certificate (or equivalent) issued by the concerned Board of Education; any variation will lead to cancellation.

Indian Army TGC-145 Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is Indian Army TGC-145 Recruitment 2026?
A
It is the recruitment of unmarried male Engineering Graduates for the 145th Technical Graduate Course, commencing July 2027 at the Indian Military Academy (IMA), Dehradun, leading to Permanent Commission in the Indian Army.
