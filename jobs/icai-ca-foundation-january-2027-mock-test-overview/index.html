ICAI CA Foundation January 2027 Mock Test - Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 22/10/2026

ICAI CA Foundation January 2027 Mock Test Schedule has been released by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI) on 21 October 2026. Mock Test Paper Series I will begin on 1 December 2026, while Series II will start on 14 December 2026. Students preparing for the January 2027 Foundation examination can participate in physical or virtual mode and download the official timetable from icai.org.

Both mock test series cover Accounting, Business Laws, Quantitative Aptitude and Business Economics. The first two papers will run from 2 PM to 5 PM, while the remaining two papers are scheduled from 2 PM to 4 PM. Question papers will be uploaded by 1:30 PM on their respective test dates, and answer keys will be made available within 48 hours.

ICAI CA Foundation January 2027 Mock Test Schedule Released

The Board of Studies (Academic) of ICAI has announced two Mock Test Paper series for students preparing for the CA Foundation January 2027 examination.

The official announcement, dated 21 October 2026, states that Series I will commence on 1 December and Series II will commence on 14 December.

Students can attempt these mock tests virtually or through physical sessions organised by ICAI branches and regional offices.

The tests are designed to help candidates practise answering examination papers within the prescribed duration and assess their preparation before the January 2027 examination.

How to Download ICAI CA Foundation January 2027 Mock Test Papers?

ICAI will upload the mock question papers through its Board of Studies platforms.

  • Visit the official BoS Knowledge Portal at boslive.icai.org.
  • Log in using the applicable student credentials.
  • Access the available mock test paper resources.
  • Select CA Foundation and the January 2027 examination session where available.
  • Open Mock Test Paper Series I or Series II.
  • Select the required subject and paper date.
  • Download the question paper after ICAI uploads it.
  • Attempt the paper within its scheduled time limit.
  • Download the answer key when it becomes available.

How to Register for ICAI CA Foundation Physical Mock Tests?

Students interested in attending physical mock tests can contact their respective ICAI branches or regional offices.

ICAI has also specified the BoS Mock Test Paper Registration Portal for registration and access.

  • Visit bosmtp.icai.org.
  • Click Student Login.
  • If you are a first-time user, click Register Now.
  • Enter your ICAI Registration Number and Date of Birth.
  • Complete the initial registration process.
  • Enter the registered email address recorded in your ICAI Self Service Portal account.
  • Enter the OTP received by email to log in.
  • Follow the available mock test registration instructions.
  • Contact your local ICAI branch for physical test arrangements.

Physical test availability and seating arrangements should be confirmed with the relevant branch or regional office.

CA Foundation January 2027 Mock Test Answer Key Release

ICAI has announced that answer keys will be uploaded within 48 hours of the scheduled commencement of the respective mock test paper.

Students can download the official answers to compare their responses and identify areas requiring improvement.

The 48-hour rule applies to both Series I and Series II. The announcement does not give separate exact answer-key publication times for individual papers.

Are ICAI CA Foundation January 2027 Mock Tests Compulsory?

The official Board of Studies announcement invites students preparing for the January 2027 Foundation examination to participate in the mock test series.

It does not state that participation in the mock tests is mandatory or that candidates must complete them to appear in the January 2027 examination.

Students can use the mock papers as a preparation resource and check with their local branch about the physical test facility.

CA Foundation January 2027 Exam Dates

The CA Foundation January 2027 examination is scheduled to take place on 3, 5, 7 and 9 January 2027, according to the examination timetable for the session.

The December mock test series is separate from the actual CA Foundation examination. Students should consult ICAI's examination notification and admit card for confirmed paper-wise examination details.

How to Prepare Using ICAI CA Foundation Mock Tests?

Students can use the two mock test series to practise under examination-like conditions.

  • Attempt Accounting and Business Laws within three hours each.
  • Complete Quantitative Aptitude and Business Economics within two hours each.
  • Practise at the official 2 PM starting time where practical.
  • Compare answers with ICAI's answer keys once published.
  • Identify topics where mistakes or time-management difficulties occurred.
  • Revise weak areas before attempting Series II.
  • Use the second series to check whether accuracy and speed have improved.
