How to Check NEET UG Cutoff 2026

Candidates should use the MCC counselling portal to verify the official NEET UG 2026 seat allotment information and college-wise ranks before making admission decisions.

  • Visit the MCC official counselling website at mcc.nic.in.
  • Open the NEET UG counselling information available on the portal.
  • Locate the relevant round's seat allotment or cutoff information.
  • Check the AIIMS college, category, quota and allotted rank details.
  • Compare the available official information with the reported figures in this article.

Important Instructions

  • Qualifying is not admission. The cutoff only makes you eligible for counselling. Your seat depends on your rank, category and quota.
  • Register for counselling on time. Do this on the official MCC site (mcc.nic.in) for All India Quota seats. Also register with your state counselling authority for state-quota seats.
  • Keep documents ready. You need your NEET scorecard and admit card, Class 10 and 12 marksheets, ID proof, passport photos, and category, EWS or PwBD certificates.
  • Fill choices carefully. Check previous-year closing ranks, fees and bond conditions before locking your choices. Don't rely on only one college.
  • Use official sources only. Check nta.nic.in and mcc.nic.in for dates, and ignore agents or fake links that promise guaranteed seats.
