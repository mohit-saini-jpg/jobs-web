IMSc Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

About IMSc

The Institute of Mathematical Sciences (IMSc), Chennai describes itself as a National Institute of higher learning which fosters high quality fundamental research in frontier disciplines of Mathematical Sciences, with an intensive teaching programme for the award of M.Sc (by Research) and Ph.D. Degree. It is located at 4th Cross Road, CIT Campus, Taramani, Chennai 600 113. These positions are under the Institute's Apex Projects, which include sub-projects on library information resource management, IT and media infrastructure, health and disease, quantum materials, science communication and computational biology.
