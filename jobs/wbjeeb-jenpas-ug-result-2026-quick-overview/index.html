How to Download JENPAS UG Result 2026 (Rank Card)

Follow these steps to download the JENPAS UG 2026 rank card from the official website:

  • Visit the official website: wbjeeb.nic.in
  • Click on the ‘Examinations’ tab and select ‘JENPAS (UG)’.
  • Click on the link that reads ‘Rank Card for JENPAS UG 2026’.
  • Enter your application number, password, and security PIN, then click ‘Sign In’.
  • Your JENPAS UG 2026 rank card will appear on the screen.
  • Check all the details carefully and download/print the rank card for future reference.

Details Mentioned on the JENPAS UG Rank Card

  • Candidate's name and roll number
  • Application number
  • Category (General/OBC/SC/ST, etc.)
  • Gender
  • Subject-wise and total marks obtained
  • Overall rank/merit position

WBJEEB does not release a separate merit list for JENPAS UG in order to maintain confidentiality of individual scores. Every candidate who appeared for the exam receives a rank card, and there is no separate marksheet - the rank card itself is the official score-cum-rank document used for counselling.

JENPAS UG 2026 Counselling: What Happens After the Result

Once the JENPAS UG 2026 result is out, WBJEEB conducts centralised online counselling for seat allotment. Based on the process followed in previous years:

  • Counselling is conducted entirely online at wbjeeb.nic.in - there is no offline counselling.
  • Only candidates with a valid qualifying rank can register for counselling.
  • Counselling typically involves three rounds, including a mop-up round for seats that remain vacant.
  • Candidates must pay a counselling registration fee (approximately ₹500, based on previous years - the exact 2026 fee should be confirmed from the official notice).
  • Seat allotment is based on rank, category, and the choices filled and locked by the candidate.
  • Document verification is carried out by the allotted institute at the time of physical reporting.

The exact JENPAS UG 2026 counselling schedule has not been confirmed as of the writing of this article. Candidates should regularly check wbjeeb.nic.in for official counselling dates.

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Keep the application number and password safe, as these are required to download the rank card and to register for counselling.
  • Cross-check all details on the rank card, including name, roll number, category, and marks.
  • Download and print multiple copies of the rank card, since it is required at every stage of counselling.
  • There is no provision to edit details after the result is declared; report discrepancies, if any, to WBJEEB through the official contact channels.
  • Do not rely on unofficial links or third-party websites for downloading the rank card - always use wbjeeb.nic.in or wbjeeb.in.
