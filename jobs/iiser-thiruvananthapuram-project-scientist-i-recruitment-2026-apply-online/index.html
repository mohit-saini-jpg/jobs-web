Who Can Apply

Candidates must possess the essential educational qualification and meet the age limit prescribed by the funding agency. Essential Qualification: Doctoral degree in Physics. Candidates with thesis submitted can also apply. Specialisation should be on condensed matter physics. Desirable Qualifications: Prior experience in depositing thin films using high vacuum deposition systems and gas sensing. Age Limit: Maximum 33 years.

Pay and Tenure

The selected candidate will receive a fellowship amount of Rs. 58,000 per month plus HRA. The tenure of the post is 1 year, which is extendable to an additional 0.5 year based on satisfactory performance.

Selection and Interview

Shortlisting of candidates is conducted by a Selection Committee constituted for this purpose if the number of applications received is high, based on qualifications and experience over and above the minimum prescribed. Information regarding the interviews will be sent by email to all shortlisted candidates.
