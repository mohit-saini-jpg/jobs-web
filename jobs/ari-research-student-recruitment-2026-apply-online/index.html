ARI Research Student Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Biodiversity & Palaeobiology (Plant & Diatoms) Group of MACS - Agharkar Research Institute (ARI), Pune, an autonomous institute under the Department of Science & Technology, Govt. of India, has invited online applications for 1 temporary Research Student post under the MGB (Maharashtra Gene Bank, Nagpur) sponsored project SP-371. The essential qualification is First Class in M.Sc. Botany, the fixed monthly stipend is Rs.28000/-, and applications are accepted at http://aripune.res.in from 09.11.2026 to 20.11.2026.

About ARI

MACS - Agharkar Research Institute is an autonomous institute under the Department of Science & Technology, Govt. of India, located at G. G. Agarkar Road, Pune 411 004. This post sits in its Biodiversity & Palaeobiology (Plant & Diatoms) Group. The project co-ordinator / Principal Investigator is Dr. M.N. Datar, Scientist E.

Who Can Apply

Desirable qualifications: Specialization in plant identification. Experience of field work in Western Ghats, herbarium preparation, QGIS and Species Distribution Modelling. Age limit: 30 Years as on closing date of advt. Relaxation will be applicable for the OBC, SC, ST etc. categories according to Govt of India regulations. The prescribed essential qualifications are minimum, and possession of them alone does not entitle a candidate to be called for interview. Whether a qualification is equivalent to the essential and desirable qualifications is decided at the discretion of the relevant committee constituted for scrutinizing the applications. Pay and Tenure

The monthly stipend is Rs.28000/- (Fixed). The project period runs up to 26.03.2028. The position is purely on contractual basis, and the selected candidate may be assigned to another project based on Institutional requirement. Selected candidates will have no right for appointment against any ARI or funding agency's post.

Selection and Interview

The shortlist of candidates for the online interview, and the day, date and time of the interview, will be displayed on the Institute website. Candidates have the option to appear for the interview in Hindi language as well.
