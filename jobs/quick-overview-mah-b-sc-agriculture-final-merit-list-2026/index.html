How to Check MAH B.Sc Agriculture Final Merit List 2026

Candidates can download and check their rank in the final merit list by following these steps:

  • Visit the official portal:agri2026.mahacet.org
  • Click on the link titled "Display of Final Merit List (UG Agriculture 2026- 27)" on the homepage or notifications panel.
  • Open or download the PDF file.
  • Use the search shortcut (Ctrl + F on computers) to locate your name, Application ID, or entrance exam Roll Number.
  • Verify your assigned State Level Rank, Category Rank, and weightage points carefully.

What Happens After the Final Merit List?

Once the final merit list is out, the CAP process for MAH B.Sc Agriculture 2026 moves through the following stages:

  • Display of Seat Matrix: The CET Cell publishes the Category-wise Seat Matrix for CAP Round 1 on the official website, around the same time as the merit list release.
  • Submission of Option Forms (Preferences): Qualified candidates must log in to their accounts between August 13 and 14, 2026, to enter and submit their preferred choices of agricultural universities, constituent colleges, and courses.
  • CAP Round 1 Allotment: Based on the final merit ranks and locked-in options, the Round 1 Seat Allotment list will be announced on August 16, 2026 (after 5:30 PM).
  • Reporting to Allotted College: Candidates allotted seats must report to their designated colleges between August 17 and 19, 2026 (9 AM to 6 PM).

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Check your final merit rank carefully before submitting college and course preferences.
  • Candidates who had raised grievances against the provisional merit list should verify whether their corrections are reflected in the final list.
  • Submit option forms only within the window of August 13 to 14, 2026.
  • Keep checking agri2026.mahacet.org regularly, as CAP schedules can be revised by the authorities.
  • Use only the official portal link to download the merit list or access the seat matrix, to avoid misinformation.
