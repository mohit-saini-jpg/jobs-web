WB Police Driver Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Download the application format from the West Bengal Police Website (https://wbpolice.gov.in/) or Howrah GRP Website (https://howrahgrp.wb.gov.in/).
  • 2Print the application on white A4 size paper.
  • 3Fill up the application form in CAPITAL LETTERS.
  • 4Paste one recent passport-size photograph (4.5 cm x 3.5 cm) self-attested on the top right of the application.
  • 5Attach self-attested copies of Aadhaar Card, Voter ID Card, PAN Card, Driving License, Educational Qualification certificates, and Experience certificate.
  • 6Superscribe the envelope as "APPLICATION FOR THE POST OF DRIVER (CONTRACTUAL), 2026".
  • 7Submit the application personally in the DROP BOX or by Speed Post at the Office of the Superintendent of Railway Police, Howrah, 2nd Floor Yatri Niwas, New Complex, Howrah Rly Station, Howrah- 711101 on or before 21 October 2026.
