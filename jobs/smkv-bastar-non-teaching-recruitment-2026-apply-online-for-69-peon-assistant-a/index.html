SMKV Bastar Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Qualification Cut-off: The required educational and other eligibility qualifications must be completed by 4 November 2026. Candidates applying for the 23 Peon vacancies must have passed Class 10 from a recognized board or institution. The notification does not prescribe a computer diploma or typing certificate for Peon. Applicants for Data Entry Operator, Assistant Grade-3 and Store Keeper must possess graduation or the prescribed Class 12 plus Modern Office Management (MOM) Diploma qualification. A one-year computer diploma and the applicable typing certificate are additionally required. Applicants for Technical Assistant and Research Assistant must have at least two years of experience in an equivalent position at a recognized institution, along with the relevant postgraduate qualification. Experience must be supported by the prescribed certificate.

SMKV Bastar Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection for the combined SMKV Bastar non-teaching vacancies includes an OMR-based written examination, skill or practical examination and preparation of a final merit list. The number of written examination questions differs for Peon compared with the other posts. Written Examination: Objective-type test of 100 marks for Peon or 120 marks for Group C posts. Skill / Practical Examination: Candidates shortlisted on written-test merit must appear for the relevant practical assessment carrying 30 marks. Experience Evaluation: Eligible SMKV Bastar daily-wage experience may contribute up to 10 marks. Final Merit List: Prepared out of 100 marks after converting the written examination score and adding applicable practical and experience marks. Document Verification: Verification of educational, domicile, category and other prescribed certificates.

SMKV Bastar Skill Test and Final Merit Calculation 2026

Candidates must secure at least 30% marks in the written examination. Based on written-test merit, up to 10 candidates per vacancy will be shortlisted for the skill or practical examination. The practical examination carries 30 marks, and candidates must obtain at least 50% of those marks to qualify. For Technical and Clerical Posts: Written merit marks = (Marks obtained out of 120 / 120) × 60. For Peon: Written merit marks = (Marks obtained out of 100 / 100) × 60. The 10 experience marks are specifically for eligible daily-wage work experience at SMKV Bastar under the prescribed recruitment rules. They are not automatically awarded for general experience at other institutions.

SMKV Bastar Non-Teaching Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Eligible permanent residents of Chhattisgarh can submit online applications for the 69 non-teaching vacancies through the SMKV Bastar recruitment portal from 16 October to 4 November 2026.
  • 2Visit the official university website at www.smkvbastar.ac.in.
  • 3Open Career → Recruitment Portal → Applicant Login → New Registration.
  • 4Complete the online registration with the required details.
  • 5Receive the User ID and Password through the registered mobile number.
  • 6Return to Applicant Login and sign in using the registered credentials.
  • 7Open .
  • 8Select Advertisement No. 3660/2026, 3662/2026 or 3666/2026 according to the position.
  • 9Enter the requested personal information and save the details.
  • 10Complete the educational qualification section.
  • 11Enter typing certificate details where applicable to DEO, Assistant Grade-3 or Store Keeper.
  • 12Upload the relevant educational, domicile, caste, computer qualification, experience and other prescribed certificates.
  • 13Upload a recent colour passport-size photograph and signature according to the notified size requirement of 30 KB to 100 KB.
  • 14Select two preferred examination-centre districts.
  • 15Review all information through Preview and Confirm.
  • 16Select Proceed to Payment and pay the applicable application fee.
  • 17Complete the application submission and retain a printout for future use.
  • 18Important: The three advertisements must be selected separately in the application portal. Candidates should verify the eligibility, fee and submission requirements for their chosen post before proceeding.
