RFCL Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Scrutiny of applications will be done based on the information and documents submitted; eligible candidates will be provisionally shortlisted and called for a Personal Interview . The shortlist will be placed on the RFCL website under "Careers." If a large number of applications are received, RFCL may conduct a Computer-Based Test in Delhi-NCR and/or other cities for shortlisting candidates for interview. No TA, or Boarding & Lodging, will be paid/arranged for appearing in this test. Test modalities, if conducted, will be published on the RFCL website. Candidates must qualify the Personal Interview with a minimum average of 50% marks . Tie-breaker for equal total marks: percentage of marks in the qualifying examination; if still equal, the candidate with the earlier date of birth is considered senior in the merit list. RFCL Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Eligible and interested candidates should apply online from 26 August 2026 (08:00 hrs) to 24 September 2026 (17:00 hrs) on the RFCL website: www.rfcl.co.in → Careers.
  • 2Only one application per candidate per post is allowed. Details can be edited before final submission, but cannot be changed once the online form is finally submitted - fill in carefully.
  • 3After successful online submission, take a printout of the application form.
  • 4Send the printed application, along with self-attested copies of all requisite supporting documents (educational qualifications, experience certificates, caste certificate, PwBD certificate, etc.), in a sealed envelope superscribed: "APPLICATION FOR THE POST OF …………………………… IN RFCL - 01/2026"
  • 5Post the sealed envelope to: Dy. General Manager (HR), Ramagundam Fertilizers and Chemicals Limited, Corporate Office, 4th Floor, Wing – A, Kribhco Bhawan, Sector-1, Noida, Uttar Pradesh – 201301
  • 6Ensure the hard copy reaches RFCL by 1 October 2026 (or 8 October 2026 for candidates from notified far-flung areas, with "FAR-FLUNG AREA" superscribed on the envelope).
  • 7Incomplete application forms, forms not supported by self-attested documents, forms not fulfilling eligibility criteria, forms received at any other RFCL address, or forms received after the due date will be summarily rejected.
