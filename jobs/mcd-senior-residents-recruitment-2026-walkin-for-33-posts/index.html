MCD Senior Residents Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Municipal Corporation of Delhi, Hindu Rao Hospital will hold a walk-in interview for Senior Residents on 29 October 2026 (Thursday), with reporting time from 09:00 AM to 12.00 AM. The venue is Hindu Rao Hospital, 5th Floor, Library, G – Block, Malka Ganj, Delhi – 110007. The interview is for 33 Senior Resident posts on contract basis across 13 departments of Hindu Rao Hospital & North Delhi Municipal Corporation Medical College, and it is open to PG and Diploma candidates. The pay is Pay Matrix Level – 11 plus usual allowances as admissible.

About Hindu Rao Hospital

Hindu Rao Hospital is a hospital of the Municipal Corporation of Delhi at Malka Ganj, Delhi – 110007. The Senior Residents recruited in this walk-in will serve at Hindu Rao Hospital & North Delhi Municipal Corporation Medical College. The notice was issued by the Office of the CMO (Administration)/HRH and signed by the CMO (Administration) and the Medical Superintendent, Hindu Rao Hospital.

Posts and Eligibility

Every vacancy is for the post of Senior Resident, filled for a period of 01 year on contract basis. The notice gives the department-wise vacancies according to the 200-point reservation chart. The notice prints a total of 33 vacancies. Dermatology and Psychiatry are listed with 0 vacancies. The category-wise break-up for Obst. & Gynaecology is given in the official notice. The number of posts for all categories may increase or decrease, and the posts may also be withdrawn completely. Reservation of posts will be as per Govt. of India Rules. A total of 1 post each will be reserved for Person with Disabilities (PWD) candidates with One Arm affected (OA) / One Leg affected (OL) in a Non-surgical branch, from among the advertised posts. What to Bring

Candidates bring the application on the prescribed Performa to the interview. The Performa can be downloaded from www.mcdonline.nic.in and www.hindurao.com and is printed in the notice (pages 5 and 6). Bring originals along with photocopies of all the required documents. The application form's checklist names these documents. They must be self-attested, numbered from top to bottom and arranged in this serial order: Demand Draft (bank name and DD number) Matriculation Certificate (for age proof, age as on date of interview) D M C Regn. No. (Permanent) MBBS Degree P G Degree (MD/MS/DNB) PG Diploma Experience Certificate (if any) Caste Certificate (if any) Copy of AADHAAR CARD Bring your Aadhaar Card to the interview. Candidates who are in service must bring a 'No Objection Certificate' from their Head of Institution/employer. Without it they will not be allowed to appear for the interview.

MCD Senior Residents Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

No TA/DA will be paid for appearing in the interview. If an SC/ST/OBC/EWS certificate turns out to be false or fake, services will be terminated without any further reason being given. Further action may be taken under the Indian Penal Code (IPC) for producing a fake or false certificate. No private practice whatsoever will be allowed during residency. Doctors found guilty of gross misconduct or negligence of duties at any time shall be terminated without notice or payment in lieu of the notice period. Inter-hospital/inter-institutional transfer shall not be permitted. The Appointing Authority reserves the right to assign any duty as and when required. No extra allowance will be admissible for such an assignment.

MCD Senior Residents Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1When is the Hindu Rao Hospital Senior Resident walk-in interview?
A
The walk-in interview for Senior Residents is on 29 October 2026 (Thursday), and the reporting time is 09:00 AM to 12.00 AM.
Q2Where is the Hindu Rao Hospital walk-in interview held?
A
It is held at Hindu Rao Hospital, 5th Floor, Library, G – Block, Malka Ganj, Delhi – 110007.
Q3How many Senior Resident vacancies are there in Hindu Rao Hospital Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 33 vacancies. The largest numbers are in Anaesthesia (8), Gen. Medicine (7) and Gen. Surgery (5), and Dermatology and Psychiatry are listed with 0.
Q4What qualification is required for a Senior Resident post at Hindu Rao Hospital?
A
Candidates need an MBBS Degree from an MCI/NMC Recognized University/Institution and a PG degree/DNB/Diploma in the concerned specialty from a recognized University/Institute registered with DMC/NMC. They must also hold valid Delhi Medical Council registration at the time of interview.
Q5What is the age limit for Hindu Rao Hospital Senior Residents?
A
As on the date of interview, the limit is not exceeding 45 years for UR & EWS, 48 years for OBC and 50 years for SC, ST. PWD candidates get 10 years' relaxation.
Q6What is the salary of a Senior Resident at Hindu Rao Hospital?
A
The pay is Pay Matrix Level – 11 plus usual allowances as admissible.
Q7Are allowances paid on top of the pay?
A
Yes. Usual allowances as admissible are paid on top of Pay Matrix Level – 11, but no extra allowance is admissible when the Appointing Authority assigns an additional duty.
Q8What is the application fee for the Hindu Rao Hospital walk-in?
A
The fee is Rs. 1000/- for Unreserved, EWS and OBC candidates and Rs. 500/- for SC, ST candidates, paid by Bank Draft/Pay Order in favour of "COMMISSIONER, MUNICIPAL CORPORATION OF DELHI", payable at Delhi. Persons with Disability are exempted, and the fee is non-refundable.
Q9What documents should I bring to the interview?
A
Bring the application on the prescribed Performa, the Demand Draft, and the originals and photocopies of the Matriculation Certificate, DMC registration, MBBS Degree, PG Degree/PG Diploma, the experience and caste certificates (if any) and your Aadhaar Card. In-service candidates must also bring a No Objection Certificate.
Q10How will Senior Residents be selected, and is the job permanent?
A
Selection is through an interview by a Selection Board, marked out of 25 (PG Degree 20 or PG Diploma 17, plus 05 for interview performance). The job is on contract for one year, or until the posts are filled regularly through UPSC or on contract by MCD, whichever is earlier. It gives no claim to regular appointment.
