KPSC Assistant Controller & Audit Officer Eligibility Criteria and Qualification

The educational qualification prescribed for both Assistant Controller and Audit Officer posts is identical: Candidates must hold a Master's degree in Commerce (M.Com), or an MBA (Finance), or an MBA (Financial Management), or an MBA/M.Com (Financial Analysis), or any other Master's degree with 'Finance' as one of the subjects, notified by the University Grants Commission, from a university established by law in India. Alternatively, candidates who are members of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India, or members/associate members of the Institute of Cost Accountants of India, are also eligible. Candidates who have appeared for an examination whose passing would render them educationally qualified, but whose results have not yet been declared, are also eligible for admission to the Preliminary Examination. KPSC Assistant Controller & Audit Officer Selection Process

Selection follows a two-stage process under the Karnataka Civil Services (Recruitment) Rules, 2011 - a Preliminary Examination (objective, multiple-choice) followed by a Main Examination (descriptive, written) for candidates shortlisted at the Preliminary stage. The Preliminary Examination question papers are set in both Kannada and English. Marks obtained in the Preliminary Examination are used only for shortlisting candidates for the Main Examination and do not count towards the final merit ranking. Based on vacancies notified, candidates are admitted to the Main Examination in a 1:20 ratio, based on marks scored in the Preliminary Examination. Marks obtained in Kannada and English papers (Paper 1 and Paper 2) are used only for determining eligibility - a minimum of 30% in each and an average of 35% across both is required - and do not count towards the merit ranking. KPSC Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Visit the KPSC website, kpsconline.karnataka.gov.in, and click "New Registration" for One Time Registration (OTR) if not already registered.
  • 2Complete OTR registration using a valid e-mail ID and mobile number, and secure your login credentials.
  • 3Log in to the Applicant Login page using your Enrolment Number, Mobile Number, or e-mail ID with OTP, and complete or update your profile under "Registration of Applicant Details".
  • 4Click the "Apply to Post" tab against the relevant notification and select the specific post(s) you wish to apply for.
  • 5Fill in all details accurately, including personal details, educational qualification, and category details, and upload your photograph (200 KB, dimensions as specified) and signature (70 KB) in JPEG/JPG format only.
  • 6Review all details carefully before final submission - once an application is submitted, no request for correction will be entertained.
  • 7Pay the applicable fee online through net banking, debit card, credit card, or UPI.
  • 8After successful submission and payment, verify that your application shows "Application submitted", "Payment done", and "Application is accepted" against the QR code before considering the process complete.
  • 9Applications can be submitted only through the KPSC website; applications received through any other mode will not be accepted.
