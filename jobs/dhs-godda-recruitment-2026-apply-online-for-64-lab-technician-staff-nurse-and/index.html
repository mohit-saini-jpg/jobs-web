DHS Godda Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection for DHS Godda Recruitment 2026 will be made through a merit list prepared on the basis of the following marking scheme, totalling 100 marks: As per the notification: if, after scrutiny of applications, the number of applications received for any post is fewer than 10, the merit list will be prepared based on educational and training marks only, without a written test/skill test. If more than 10 applications are received after scrutiny, a Written Test/Skill Test will be conducted, and marks from education, training, and the written/skill test will be combined to prepare the merit list. The final merit list will be approved by the District Selection Committee, Godda, and published on the official Godda district website, https://godda.nic.in.
