About BMC Senior Resident

Hinduhridaysamrat Balasaheb Thackeray Medical College & Dr. R. N. Cooper Municipal General Hospital is a municipal medical college and general hospital of the Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation, located in Juhu, Mumbai 400 056. The Dean of the college and hospital has issued this notice for Senior Residents in the Radiology Department.

Department-wise Vacancies

There are 7 Senior Resident posts in total, all in the Radiology Department. Qualification and Experience

The age limit is 38 years for Open category candidates and 43 years for Reserved category candidates. The notice does not give an "as on" date for age. Candidates holding medical qualifications must be registered with the Maharashtra Medical Council and hold a valid certificate. Blacklisted candidates and candidates who have completed 8 posts will not be considered. Candidates who registered on or before 31 October 2026 and have not completed a total of 08 Resident Posts must submit an undertaking. The undertaking states that their total tenure of Resident Posts is less than 4 years. Without it, they will not be considered for a post. A candidate who does not join the post when offered, or who abandons the post before the tenure ends, is not eligible for appointment to any of the Municipal Medical Institutions for 3 years from the date of leaving the post. Pay and Terms

Tenure: The posts are for the tenure from 6 November 2026 to 31 January 2027. The tenure is likely to be terminated before 31 January 2027 if Bonded/DNB candidates become available. Security deposit: A selected candidate must pay Rs.10,000/- as a security deposit at the time of joining. It is paid by Demand Draft/Pay Order drawn in favour of "BrihanMumbai Municipal Corporation". No appointment order is given without it. Refund and forfeiture: The deposit is refunded when the post is completed, after recovery of any dues. It is forfeited if the candidate leaves the post before completing the tenure. It is also forfeited if the refund is not claimed within six months after completion. Continuing in the same institution: The deposit can be carried over if the candidate continues a tenure post in the same institution. BMC Senior Resident Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

A candidate found to have given false information or a false certificate, or to have withheld information, in the application form will not be considered for appointment. If already appointed, the appointment may be terminated. Selected candidates should report for duty immediately. A candidate who does not may be blacklisted for 3 years. The notice states that the selection/appointment of candidates for the post of Registrars & House officers can be changed if a BONDED CANDIDATE / DNB STUDENT becomes available.

BMC Senior Resident Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many Senior Resident vacancies are there in BMC Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 7 Senior Resident posts in the Radiology Department: OPEN 4, SC 1, ST 1 and VJNT 1. The notice says the number of posts is not fixed but variable.
Q2What is the last date to apply for the BMC Senior Resident post?
A
Completed forms must be handed in by 3 November 2026, between 10.30 am and 01.30 pm or between 02.00 pm and 05.00 pm. Applications received after the due date and time will not be accepted.
Q3What qualification is needed for Senior Resident (Radiology)?
A
Candidates must have an M.D degree or D.N.B or D.M.R.E in Radiology from a recognized University. Candidates holding medical qualifications must also be registered with the Maharashtra Medical Council and hold a valid certificate.
Q4What is the age limit for the BMC Senior Resident post?
A
The age limit is 38 years for Open category candidates and 43 years for Reserved category candidates.
Q5What is the salary of a BMC Senior Resident?
A
The pay is 6700 + DP 3350/- + D.A. Rs.47637/- + Add. Stipend Rs.27000/-, which totals Rs.84687/-.
Q6What is the application form fee?
A
The form costs Rs. 1027/- (Form Fees Rs.870/- + GST 18%). It is paid when buying the form at the Administrative office, D Wing, 1st Floor, between 26 October 2026 and 3 November 2026 (except Saturday and Sunday).
Q7Can I send the BMC Senior Resident application by post?
A
No. Application forms sent by post will not be considered. The form must be handed in at the Administrative office, Ground Floor, Hinduhridaysamrat Balasaheb Thackeray Medical College & Dr. R. N. Cooper Municipal General Hospital, Juhu, Mumbai – 400 056.
Q8When and where is the counselling for Senior Resident (Radiology)?
A
Counselling is on 4 November 2026 at 11.00 am onwards, at New College building, 3rd Floor, 5th Lecture Hall. The list of eligible candidates will be displayed on 4 November 2026 after 3.00 pm, and the final list on 5 November 2026 after 3.00 pm.
Q9Is the BMC Senior Resident post permanent?
A
No. The post is for a tenure from 6 November 2026 to 31 January 2027. The tenure is likely to be terminated before 31 January 2027 if Bonded/DNB candidates become available.
Q10Is there a security deposit for selected candidates?
A
Yes. Selected candidates must pay Rs.10,000/- by Demand Draft/Pay Order in favour of "BrihanMumbai Municipal Corporation" at joining. The deposit is refunded when the post is completed, subject to recovery of dues. It is forfeited if the candidate leaves before completing the tenure.
