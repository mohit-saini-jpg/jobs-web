Job Profile & Responsibilities of Oil India Contractual Chemist (Fields)

Deployment at drilling/workover wells as well-site chemist. Preparation and maintenance of drilling mud and workover brine solutions at well sites. Addressing critical well situations such as pipe stuck, mud loss, and well kick/blowout. Assisting in well cementing operations. Carrying out gas testing, calculating mud hydraulics, and monitoring lithology of drill cuttings. Maintaining adequate stock of drilling and workover fluid chemicals as per operational requirement. Maintaining vigilance on safety and pollution control measures related to chemical activities at well sites.

Skills & Experience Required for Oil India Contractual Chemist

Experience in handling drilling/workover fluids. Experience in laboratory analysis of drilling/workover fluids. Experience in handling various chemicals used in drilling/workover fluids. Experience in addressing downhole complications in drilling/workover wells. Ability to generate daily well reports, downhole problem reports, and cementing reports. Knowledge of MS Office and computer applications is desirable. Good communication skills.

Period of Contractual Engagement

The initial period of contractual engagement is 01 (one) year, extendable for another 01 (one) year based on departmental requirement, job performance, conduct, and physical fitness. The total period of engagement will be a maximum of 02 (two) years.
