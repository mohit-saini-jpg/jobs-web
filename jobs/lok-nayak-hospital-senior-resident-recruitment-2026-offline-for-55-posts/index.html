Lok Nayak Hospital Senior Resident Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Lok Nayak Hospital (LNH), New Delhi, under the Office of the Medical Director, Government of NCT of Delhi, has released a walk-in interview notification for the recruitment of Senior Residents (SR) on ad-hoc basis (44 days). The notice (F.No.17773 (Part-I)/SR interview/E-I/LNH/2026, dated 22.07.2026) invites eligible Indian Nationals to appear for a walk-in interview against 55 vacancies spread across 15 departments. This article covers vacancy details, eligibility, age limit, salary, application process, required documents and important dates as mentioned in the official notification.

Lok Nayak Hospital SR Recruitment: Eligibility Criteria

Essential Qualification: MBBS with Post Graduate Degree/DNB/Diploma or Post Doctoral Degree (or equivalent) in the concerned specialty from an NMC/MCI recognized university/institute, and preferably valid DMC registration for the concerned specialty/super-specialty. The tenure of the PG Degree/DNB/Diploma or Post Doctoral Degree (or equivalent) must be completed, and the result must also be declared, before the date of interview. Non-PG candidates: If an applicant does not hold the above qualification, they will be treated as a Non-PG Senior Resident and may be appointed for 44 days or till these posts are filled with a suitable PG-qualified candidate, whichever is earlier. The age of the Senior Resident should not be more than 45 years as on 01.07.2026. Lok Nayak Hospital SR Salary 2026

Selected Senior Residents will be paid as per 7th CPC, Pay Matrix Level-11 (Rs. 67,700 - Rs. 208,700), along with other usual allowances admissible per month as per rules.

Tenure of Senior Resident Post

The tenure will be for a period of 44 days (maximum 3 years, including any service already rendered as Senior Resident on an ad-hoc/regular basis in any NMC-recognized teaching/non-teaching institution), or till a regular Senior Resident joins - whichever is earlier. This is in accordance with the Residency Scheme of GOI dated 5th June 1992 and the subsequent extended Residency Scheme notified vide Circular No. F.No.121/26/2010/H&FW/DSHFW/1996-2045 dated 10th June 2011. No NOC of any kind will be issued to the Senior Resident before the candidate completes at least 44 days of continuous service. No permission/leave will be granted to attend a conference/seminar/workshop/fellowship programme lasting more than three days at any point during the tenure.

Lok Nayak Hospital SR Recruitment: Selection Process

The mode of selection is through interview only. No waiting list will be prepared; only a rank list will be prepared for this interview, which will remain valid till the next interview notice is published. If the number of applications is very high, the interview may continue on the next working day. This will be informed verbally to candidates by the Board Members on the date of interview at closing time. Candidates who are not interviewed on the said date must not leave the venue without the knowledge of the Board Members - no separate communication will be made in this regard.

Required Documents for LNH Senior Resident Application

Interested and eligible candidates must apply in the prescribed application format along with original and self-attested photocopies of the following documents: MBBS Degree, Post Graduate Degree, DMC Certificate/registration acknowledgment for PG Degree, Caste Certificate, and proof of awards/publications (if applicable) Identity proof: Aadhar card, Voter ID card, Driving license, Passport, etc. 2 (two) passport-size photographs on a white background, attested by the HoD of the concerned department (as required in the application form) Candidates must also produce an NOC (No Objection Certificate) from their present employer.
