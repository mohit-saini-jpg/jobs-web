Terms of Contract

Tenure of all posts is 1 (one) year, extendable up to 3 years, or attainment of 70 years of age, whichever is earlier. The contractual engagement may be terminated/discontinued from either side by giving one month's prior notice, without assigning any reason. In case of spot resignation by a selected candidate, one month's salary must be deposited to the ESIC account through challan. No claim for service benefits such as PF, Pension, Gratuity, Medical Allowance, Medical Benefits, Seniority, or Promotion will be admissible. Being a contractual appointment, absence from work for a period beyond 7 (seven) days without proper permission of the competent authority will amount to voluntary abandonment of engagement and automatic termination.
