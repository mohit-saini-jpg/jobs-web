About SMP Kolkata

Syama Prasad Mookerjee Port, Kolkata, formerly Kolkata Port Trust, is a Major Port Authority with its office at 15, Strand Road, Kolkata - 700 001. The post is under its Kolkata Dock System and will fall vacant with effect from 1 February 2027. The circular is addressed to the Secretaries of all Major Port Authorities.

Who Can Apply

Applications are invited from suitable and willing officers who satisfy the notified Recruitment Rules for the post of Dy. Chief Engineer (Annexure-I of the circular). Applications are routed through the applicant's Administrative Port. The crucial date for determining eligibility is 1 February 2027. Degree or equivalent in Civil Engg from a recognized University/Institution (4 year Degree course). Nine years experience in executive cadre in Planning/Construction/Design/Maintenance preferably of Port and Marine Structures in an Industrial/Commercial/Govt. Undertaking. The Recruitment Rules list the post as filled by absorption through composite method, failing which by deputation from other Govt. organizations, and failing both by direct recruitment. Pay and Tenure

The Dy. Chief Engineer post carries the pay scale of Rs. 80,000-2,20,000/- (pre-revised Rs. 32,900-58,000/-) and is a Class-I post. Under the Ministry's letter No. A-29018/4/2018-PE-I dated 30.05.2022 and 01.06.2022, the Appointing Authority as well as the Disciplinary Authority for all Dy. HOD level posts is the Central Government, i.e. Secretary (PSW). The notice does not mention allowances.

Selection and Interview

According to the Recruitment Rules, selection is by merit, for which the benchmark in overall grading in the APARs will not be below "very good". The recommendations of the Service Selection Committee will be conveyed to the Ministry for approval before the appointment order is issued.

SMP Kolkata Dy. Chief Engineer Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

If the Port receives an advance copy of an application, the candidature will not be considered unless the application is received through proper channel by 17.12.2026 (within 15 days of the online last date). SMP Kolkata Dy. Chief Engineer Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Register on the Online Application Portal (OAP) of the MoPS&W website, onlinevacancy.shipmin.nic.in.
  • 2Apply for the post of Dy. Chief Engineer (Dy. CE) through OAP between 3 November 2026 and 2 December 2026.
  • 3Take a printout of the filled-in application and put it in an envelope with the documents listed below.
  • 4Superscribe the envelope "Application for the post of Dy. Chief Engineer (Dy. CE)".
  • 5Send it through your Administrative Port, which verifies the certificates while forwarding the application.
  • 6The envelope must reach the Office of the Secretary, Syama Prasad Mookerjee Port, Kolkata, 15 Strand Road, Kolkata-700001 on or before 17 December 2026.
  • 7Certified copies of ACRs/APARs for the last 5 years, attested on each page by an officer not below the rank of Dy. HOD.
  • 8Attested copies of all certificates of educational qualification and present and past work experience in the respective post and pay scale, verified by the administrative port. For appointment or promotion on "officiating", "ad-hoc" or "pro-forma" basis, the details of "regularization" should be indicated clearly.
  • 9Administrative Certificate / No objection certificate from the competent authority of the respective Port (Annexure-II). It certifies, among other things, that no disciplinary or vigilance case is pending, the officer's integrity, the APARs from 2021-2022 to 2025-26, and that the Port has no objection to releasing the officer if selected.
  • 10Undertaking of the applicant that the candidature will not be withdrawn, if selected.
  • 11Vigilance comments / clearance certificate in the pro-forma at Annexure-III, prescribed by CVC vide circular No. 01.03.2026 dated 12.03.2026, including details of penalties, if any, in the last ten years (Sl. No. 10 of Annexure-III). It is to be furnished and signed by the CVO or HOD.
  • 12Self attested passport size photographs (two numbers).
