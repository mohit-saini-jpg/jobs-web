Eligibility Criteria

The selection is open exclusively to Scheduled Caste (SC) and Scheduled Tribe (ST) candidates. Educational Qualification: Bachelor's Degree in Agriculture / Horticulture / Forestry / Botany / Microbiology / Biotechnology from a recognised University/Institute, with working knowledge of computers. Candidates who have not yet received their final degree certificate need not appear for the test. Candidates must obtain the original Degree certificate within two years from the date of issue of the Provisional Degree certificate. Those who have not done so within this period are not eligible to apply. Candidates who have undergone training in any office of Spices Board earlier need not apply again, whether or not they completed that training.

Salary/Stipend

Stipend: Rs. 20,000 per month. In case of travel to spice fields, TA will be paid. Period of training: Two years from the date of joining. Leave eligibility: 01 day per month. The selection as Trainee (Development) is only a training and is not linked to regular appointment under any circumstances; it cannot be regularised at any stage.

Age Limit (as on the date of Written Test)

Maximum Age: Not exceeding 35 years as on the date of the Written Test. Before submitting the application, candidates are advised to verify that they meet the required age criteria. Use our Age Calculator to calculate your exact age as per the official cut-off date.

Spices Board Trainee Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection is through a written test (walk-in test / computer proficiency) conducted at the designated centres. Candidates must report at the venue before the scheduled time for certificate verification and must bring all original documents and certificates, along with one set of self-attested copies each, covering identity, age and educational qualifications. Candidates who report after the specified time, or who fail to submit the application online, will not be permitted to take the test. No TA/DA will be given to candidates who attend the written test. A rank list is maintained and is valid for one year from the date of publication of results, unless a fresh notification for the same training is issued. It is operated only when a vacancy arises from non-joining of a ranked candidate, or from a trainee leaving by death or resignation within one year of joining.
