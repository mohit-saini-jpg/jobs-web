SBI SO Selection Process 2026

The selection process consists of an online written examination followed by a personal interview . The final merit calculation gives 70% weightage to Professional Knowledge and 30% to the interview. Online Written Test: Candidates appear in the objective examination. Shortlisting: SBI shortlists candidates based on the examination results and applicable requirements. Personal Interview: Shortlisted candidates attend an interview carrying 100 marks. Document Verification: Educational qualifications, service experience, medical fitness and category certificates are verified. Final Merit List: Prepared using normalized Professional Knowledge and interview marks. SBI reserves the right to modify the selection process, including considering shortlisting followed by interview, if required.

SBI SO Exam Pattern 2026

The online written examination contains 200 objective questions carrying 200 marks , with a total duration of two hours. Reasoning Ability, English Language and General Awareness are qualifying in nature. Marks obtained in these three qualifying sections are not counted toward final merit. Candidates must obtain the minimum qualifying marks fixed by SBI in the applicable sections. Professional Knowledge carries 100 marks and contributes to final merit. There is no negative marking for incorrect answers. If candidates have the same score at the cut-off point, SBI will rank them according to age in descending order.

SBI Security Officer Job Responsibilities

Selected Manager and Deputy Manager (Security) officers will be responsible for improving security arrangements at SBI branches and offices. Conduct periodic security audits of branches and offices. Review existing security arrangements and recommend improvements. Organize security drills, lectures and employee awareness programmes. Monitor security systems and reduce incidents caused by security lapses. Support implementation of disaster management plans. Visit branches and other administrative units to assess security requirements. Coordinate with civil, military, police and other Government authorities. Perform additional security administration duties assigned by SBI. Selected officers may be posted or transferred to SBI offices anywhere in India according to the Bank's requirements.
