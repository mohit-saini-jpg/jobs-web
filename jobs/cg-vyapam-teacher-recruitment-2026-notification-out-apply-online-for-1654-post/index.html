CG Teacher Recruitment Selection Process

Selection for CG Teacher Recruitment 2026 is based on a written examination (LST26) conducted by CGSSB. The exam consists of 100 objective-type questions, carrying 1 mark each. Total exam duration is 2 hours (10:00 AM to 12:15 PM). Each question has four options; candidates must fully darken one circle using a blue or black ballpoint pen on the OMR sheet. Negative marking applies: 1/4 mark is deducted for a wrong answer or for marking more than one option. Unattempted questions are awarded zero marks. Document verification, final selection, and appointment are handled separately by the recruiting department and are outside CGSSB's scope.
