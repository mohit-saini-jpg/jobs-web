Kerala PSC Recruitment 2026 Important Instructions

The Gazette dated 30 October 2026 covers Category 162/2026 to 173/2026. There are 16 currently notified vacancies across these categories. Applications close on 2 December 2026. One Time Registration is mandatory for online application. No application fee is required. Applicants must satisfy the exact post-specific qualifications. Kerala PSC Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Visit the official Kerala PSC website at www.keralapsc.gov.in.
  • 2Open the Notifications section and locate the Extra Ordinary Gazette dated 30 October 2026.
  • 3Choose the relevant notification from Category 162/2026 to 173/2026.
  • 4Download and read the complete post-specific notification carefully.
  • 5Open the Kerala PSC Thulasi One Time Registration portal.
  • 6Complete One Time Registration if not already registered.
  • 7Log in using the registered User ID and password.
  • 8Verify personal information, educational qualifications, community details and other eligibility information in the profile.
  • 9Select the relevant recruitment notification and click the Apply Now button.
  • 10Review the application information and ensure that the prescribed qualification and community conditions are satisfied.
  • 11Submit the online application by 2 December 2026.
  • 12Save or print the application from the My Applications section for future reference.
  • 13Follow the examination confirmation instructions if Kerala PSC subsequently announces a test for the selected post.
  • 14Important: Applications cannot ordinarily be deleted or altered after submission. Candidates should ensure that all profile information and eligibility claims are correct before applying.
