MTWU Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Mother Teresa Women's University, Kodaikanal (Dindigul District, Tamil Nadu) has invited applications from suitable women candidates for the post of Registrar and Controller of Examinations at Pay Matrix Level 14 plus allowances admissible under University rules, for a term of 3 years at a time. Fifteen years of experience is required, and the filled-in application in eight copies must reach the University by 5 December 2026 under Notification No. 401.

About Mother Teresa Women's University

Mother Teresa Women's University is a university established under the Act of Tamil Nadu 1984. Its address in the notice is Attuvampatty, Kodaikanal-624 101, Dindigul District, Tamilnadu. The notice is signed by the Registrar i/c and dated 06.11.2026 at Kodaikanal.

Who Can Apply

The holder of the post shall be an academician not lower than that of a University Professor. A Master Degree with at least 55% of the marks or its equivalent grade of B in the UGC seven-point scale. At least 15 years of experience as Assistant Professor in the AGP of Rs.7000/- and above, or 8 years of service in the AGP of Rs.8000 and above including as Associate Professor, along with experience in educational administration. OR comparable experience in a research establishment and (or) other institutions of higher education. OR 15 years of administrative experience, of which 8 years as Deputy Registrar or an equivalent post. Knowledge of Tamil is essential (written and spoken). Qualification is relaxable in exceptional cases. Age: the notice states that the age of superannuation for the post is 58 years. It prints no separate minimum or maximum age for applying. Pay and Terms

The pay is Pay Matrix Level 14 plus allowances admissible under the rules of the University. The notice prints no rupee amount. The term of appointment is 3 years at a time. The University reserves the right to fill up or not to fill up the post.

Application Fee

The fee is paid as an SBI demand draft drawn in favour of "The Registrar, Mother Teresa Women's University", payable at Kodaikanal. It is not refunded under any circumstances.

MTWU Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

The University reserves its right to shortlist the candidates. Candidates called for the interview will not be paid any TA/DA. The notice does not describe the interview marks or other stages.

MTWU Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

Canvassing in any form will be a disqualification. The notice gives the University's own right to shortlist; it does not guarantee an interview call. The application form asks whether any vigilance, disciplinary or legal case is pending, and asks for the period required to join duty if offered the post. False or incorrect information will lead to cancellation of candidature or appointment, as per the declaration in the form.
