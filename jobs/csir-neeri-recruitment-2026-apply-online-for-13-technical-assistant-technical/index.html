CSIR NEERI Technical Assistant and Technical Officer Selection Process

Selection for both posts follows a two-stage process: Trade Test (Qualifying): Screened and eligible candidates are called for a Trade Test in the relevant area/ discipline. Competitive Written Examination: Candidates who qualify the Trade Test appear for a written exam of 200 questions across three papers, in a 3-hour OMR/ Computer-Based Objective Multiple-Choice format, set in English and Hindi (except the English language paper). Paper II and Paper III are evaluated only for candidates who cross the minimum threshold marks in Paper I, and the final merit list is prepared based on performance in Paper II and Paper III combined.
