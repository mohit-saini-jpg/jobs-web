HPNLU Accountant Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Himachal Pradesh National Law University, Shimla (HPNLU) is engaging one Accountant on a purely contractual, full-time basis for its Court and Civil Liberty Project, run by the Centre for Philosophy and Comparative Law Studies, at ₹ 25000/- per month. The essential qualification is a B. Com. with 1-3 years minimum working experience of accounting. Applications are accepted only through the University's ERP portal from 23 November 2026 (0000 hrs) to 7 December 2026 (2359 hrs), with an application fee of ₹ 500/-.

About the University and the Project

Himachal Pradesh National Law University, Shimla was established by the State Government in 2016 by an Act of the Legislature (Act 16 of 2016). The Accountant will work on the Court and Civil Liberty Project, which aims to give free and effective legal representation to under-trial prisoners in Himachal Pradesh, including appeal, review and revision where required, through a team of lawyers and social workers. In its first year the project covers the Model Central Jail, Kanda (Shimla) and Sub-Jail Kaithu (Shimla). From the second year, other jails in the State, including Solan and Kullu, are to be covered. The project's geographical scope is the State of Himachal Pradesh, and its tenure is 3 years (01 July 2026 till 30 June 2029).

Who Can Apply

The single post is unreserved (UR). The notice lists these mandatory qualifications for the Accountant: Graduate from Commerce (B. Com.). 1-3 years minimum working experience of accounting. Experience of file management and financial management. Proficiency in Hindi and English. Proficiency in computer. The notice lists these as desired qualifications : 3 years' experience of work. Proficiency in MS Office, Excel, Tally etc and Management Information System. Experience of working with such project implemented by international or national organisations. The age limit is not specified in the notice. For the full eligibility conditions of the post, candidates should check the official notification.

Pay and Tenure

Emolument and remuneration: ₹ 25000/- per month. Nature of employment: purely contractual, full-time engagement for the purposes of the Project. Tenure of employment: one year, with the scope of renewal of contract based on the Performance Report, for another year(s). The person engaged shall not accept any other employment (part-time or full-time), nor engage in any other practice, during the engagement with the project. The position does not entail any relation with HPNLU, Shimla, or any claim in future with respect to employment. The University acts as the facilitator for project implementation, including payment of remuneration and providing infrastructure.

HPNLU Accountant Job Description

Assisting the Project Coordinator in all matter related to the finances of the Project. File Management of all project related documents. Drafting, documenting, recording, etc. of the project progress with respect to the financial matters and utilization of funds of the Project. Ensuring timely filing of documents wherever and if need arises with respect to financial management of the Project. All and any work related to fulfilment of project objectives. Project staff are also expected to follow the project principles printed in the notice: access to justice as enshrined in the Constitution of India, confidentiality about the project and the data of prisoners, under-trials and witnesses, respect and responsibility, integrity and professionalism.

Selection and Interview

According to the notice's schedule, screening takes place on 8 December 2026, information about the call for interview is given on 10 December 2026, and the interview is on 12 December 2026. The notice does not mention the interview venue or mode. No TA/DA will be paid to any candidate for attending the recruitment process.
