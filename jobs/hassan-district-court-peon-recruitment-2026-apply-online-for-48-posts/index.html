Hassan District Court Peon Selection Procedure

Recruitment follows the Karnataka Civil Services (General Recruitment) Rules and the Karnataka Subordinate Courts (Clerk and other posts) (Recruitment) Rules 1982, as amended in 2007 and 2021. There is no written examination. Candidates are called for an interview based on the marks obtained in their SSLC examination, in the ratio of 1:10 against the number of vacancies notified for each post/category (i.e., 10 candidates called per vacancy, ranked by SSLC marks). The interview assesses the candidate's efficiency and general knowledge, and carries 10 marks. Final selection is based on a combination of SSLC marks and interview performance, per the applicable recruitment rules. Selected candidates undergo a probation period of 2 years as per the Karnataka Civil Services (Probation Rules) 1977. Important Conditions

Applicants must be Indian citizens. Where reservation categories have no eligible/available candidates, those posts will be filled from other reservation-category candidates; if SC/ST posts remain unfilled even then, they will be filled from General category candidates as per applicable rules. Candidates called for the eligibility test/interview must appear at their own expense; no travel allowance is payable. Selected candidates will be governed by the New Pension Scheme (NPS) or the pension scheme notified by the Government, as applicable. The notified vacancies are subject to change. Already-employed government servants selected for this post must submit a "No-Objection Certificate" from their current employer within 30 days of publication of the select list.
