Is yojana se renewable energy adoption badhegi aur kheti‑badi me paani ki supply sustainable hogi. Subsidy aur loan facility dono milne se small farmers ko bahut fayda hoga.

Who Is Eligible: Haryana सोलर वाटर पंपिंग Yojana List जारी 01/01/2026

Ye scheme un kisanon, landless farmers aur rural households ke liye hai jinko irrigation ya domestic water supply ke liye solar pump chahiye.

1. Apna land record, electricity bill aur bank passbook ready rakhein. 2. Scheme ke official portal par online registration karein aur required documents upload karein. 3. Local Panchayat ya Krishi Vibhag se verification ka proof lein.

How Haryana सोलर वाटर पंपिंग Yojana List जारी 01/01/2026 Works

Application online submit hoti hai, phir Gram Panchayat verification, state level technical review aur finally subsidy disbursement bank account me transfer hoti hai. Process 4‑6 weeks me complete hota hai.
