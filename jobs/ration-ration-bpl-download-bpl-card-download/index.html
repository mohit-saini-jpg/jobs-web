Q1Mera BPL status kaise check karun?
A
Official govt portal par jaakar "BPL Card Download" ya "Ration Card Status" option select karein, apna ration card number aur Aadhaar enter karein, aur result turant mil jayega.
Q2Kya BPL list har mahine update hoti hai?
A
Haan, har mahine state government apni updated BPL list publish karti hai. Aap portal ya local Ration Office se latest list download kar sakte hain.
Q3Agar mera BPL status reject ho jaye to kya karein?
A
Reject hone ki reason check karein, missing ya incorrect documents ko correct karke phir se apply karein. Agar still issue ho, to nearest Ration Office me jaakar grievance register kar sakte hain.
