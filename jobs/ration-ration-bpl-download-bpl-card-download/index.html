Ration कार्ड कट ह या नही और कितना Ration मिलता ह चक करे BPL FAQs

Q1Mera ration card cut ho gaya hai, kya main phir bhi BPL list me dekh sakta hoon?
A
Haan, aap online portal par apna Aadhaar aur ration card number dal kar cut status check kar sakte hain aur BPL list me apna naam dekh sakte hain.
Q2BPL list download karne ke liye koi fee hai?
A
Nahi, BPL list aur ration card status ka download bilkul free hai. Sirf internet connection ki zarurat hoti hai.
Q3Agar mera ration cut ho jata hai to kya karna chahiye?
A
Cut ka reason portal par check karein, required documents (income proof, Aadhaar) update karein, aur nearest ration office me grievance file karein.
