Q1Mujhe apna BPL status kaise pata chalega?
A
Official state portal ya mAadhaar app par Ration Card number dal kar 'BPL Status Check' option select karein, wahan aapko instant result mil jayega.
Q2BPL card download karne ke liye kaunse documents chahiye?
