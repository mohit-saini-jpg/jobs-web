Q1Mera ration card cut ho gaya hai, kya main BPL list mein aa sakta hoon?
A

Q2BPL card download karne ke liye kaunse documents chahiye?
A
Aadhaar card, latest address proof (electricity bill ya passport), aur existing ration card number. Kuch states mein income certificate bhi maangi ja sakti hai.
Q3Download ki hui BPL card kaise verify karun?
A
PDF download karne ke baad usme diye gaye BPL ID aur aapka ration card number cross-check karein. Agar details match hoti hain to aapka card valid hai; koi mismatch ho to nearest food department office mein clarification lein.
