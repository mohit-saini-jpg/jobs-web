Q1Mujhe apna BPL status kaise check karna hai?
A
Official food supply department ki website par jaake 'BPL List Download' section me apna ration card number ya Aadhaar number dal kar status check kar sakte hain.
Q2Agar mera BPL card cut ho gaya hai to kya karein?
A
Nearest fair price shop me jaake grievance register karein, ya online portal par 'Re‑verification' request submit karein with supporting documents.
Q3Kya BPL list har mahine update hoti hai?
A
Haan, state government har quarter ya mahine ke end me updated BPL list publish karti hai, isliye regular check karte rahen.
