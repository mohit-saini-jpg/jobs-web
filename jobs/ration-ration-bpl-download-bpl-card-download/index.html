Q1Mujhe apna BPL status kaise check karna hai?
A
Official state ration portal ya nearest fair price shop pe jaake apna ration card number aur Aadhaar enter karein, ya SMS service se "BPL " bhejein.
Q2BPL list download karne ke liye kaunse documents chahiye?
A
Aadhaar, existing ration card, aur recent electricity/gas bill. Online portal pe PDF format mein list free download hoti hai.
Q3Agar mera ration card cut ho gaya hai to kya karein?
A
Nearest ration office ya FPS pe jaake grievance register karein, aur updated BPL status ke liye appeal process follow karein.
