Ration कार्ड कट ह या नही और कितना Ration मिलता ह चक करे BPL FAQs

Q1Mera Ration Card BPL list mein nahi dikh raha, kya karun?
A
Sabse pehle apna Aadhaar se link check karein, phir nearest Food Supply office mein grievance register karein. Updated list har mahine publish hoti hai, to regular check karte rahen.
Q2BPL status milne ke baad ration ki quantity kaise pata chalegi?
A
State portal par aapke family members ki count aur allotted wheat‑rice quantity listed hoti hai. Usually 5‑10 kg per member per month, lekin state policy ke hisaab se thoda vary kar sakta hai.
Q3Online download ke baad kya physical card milta hai?
A
Haan, online list se aap BPL card ka PDF download kar sakte hain. Physical card ke liye aapko nearest Fair Price Shop ya Food Supply office se collect karna hota hai, ya agar AAY scheme hai to direct bank account mein subsidy transfer hoti hai.
