Q1Mera Ration Card cut ho gaya hai, kya main BPL list mein aunga?
A
Agar aapka card cut hai to bhi aap BPL criteria meet karte hain to list mein aapka naam aayega; aapko fair price shop se priority ration mil sakta hai.
Q2BPL card download ka process kya hai?
A
Official govt portal ya nearest fair price shop par jaake latest BPL list PDF download karein, ya Aadhar se login karke online download karein.
Q3Ration cut hone par kitna extra ration milta hai?
A
Cut Ration Card holders ko BPL families ke barabar ration milta hai – 5‑10 kg wheat aur 2‑5 kg rice per month, plus occasional kerosene subsidy.
