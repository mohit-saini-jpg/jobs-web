Q1Mera ration card cut ho gaya hai, kya main BPL list mein automatically appear ho jaunga?
A
Nahi, cut hone ke baad aapko apna Aadhaar‑Ration link update karna hoga aur local Ration Office se BPL verification karwani hogi.
Q2BPL list ka PDF kaise download karun?
A
State govt ki official website ya mPin app par login karein, ‘BPL Card Download’ section select karein, apna district/tehsil choose kar ke PDF download karein.
Q3Agar meri details list mein nahi dikh rahi, to kya karein?
A
Nearest Ration Office ya Common Service Centre (CSC) par jaake grievance register karein, required documents (Aadhaar, ration card, income proof) submit karein aur correction process shuru karein.
