Q1Mera Ration Card cut ho gaya hai, kya main BPL list me aunga?
A
Agar aapka Ration Card cut hua hai to aapko turant nearest Fair Price Shop ya online portal par BPL status verify karna chahiye. Cut hone ka reason samajhkar required correction karne se aap list me aa sakte hain.
Q2BPL card download karne ke liye kaunse documents chahiye?
A
Aapko Aadhaar card, latest Ration Card, aur address proof (bijli bill ya passport) ki scanned copy ya photo upload karni hoti hai.
Q3BPL card milne ke baad ration ki quantity kitni milti hai?
