Q1Mera Ration Card cut ho gaya hai, kya main BPL list mein aata hoon?
A
Agar aapka household income BPL criteria meet karta hai, to cut Ration Card bhi BPL list mein include ho sakta hai. Latest list download karke verify karein.
Q2BPL card download ka process kya hai?
A
Official state food supply website par jaake 'BPL Card Download' option select karein, Aadhar aur Ration Card details fill karein, aur PDF format mein list save ya print karein.
Q3Ration ki quantity aur subsidy kitni hoti hai?
A
Standard BPL ration mein 5‑10 kg wheat, 2‑5 kg rice, aur kabhi‑kabhi pulses ya oil ki extra subsidy hoti hai, jo total value ₹2,000‑₹3,500 ke aas‑pās hoti hai.
