Q1Mera ration card cut ho gaya hai, kya main abhi bhi BPL list me aunga?
A
Agar aapka card cut hua hai to aapko turant nearest Ration Office me jaake re-verification karani hogi, tabhi aap BPL list me aayenge.
Q2BPL list download karne ke liye kaunse documents chahiye?
A
Aadhaar card, existing ration card number, aur recent passport size photo sufficient hain. Kuch states me income certificate bhi maang sakti hain.
Q3Download karne ke baad benefits kaise claim karun?
A
Download ki hui PDF ko print karke apne nearest Fair Price Shop me le jaayein, wahan staff aapke details verify karke regular ration supply shuru kar dega.
