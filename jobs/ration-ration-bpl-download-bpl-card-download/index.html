Ration कार्ड कट ह या नही और कितना Ration मिलता ह चक करे BPL FAQs

Q1Mera ration card cut ho gaya hai, kya main turant BPL card le sakta/ sakti hoon?
A
Haan, aap nearest Ration Office ya state ke online portal pe turant BPL card ke liye apply kar sakte hain. Required documents upload karne ke baad verification hoti hai aur card 7‑10 din me mil jata hai.
Q2BPL card ke liye kaunse documents zaroori hain?
A
Aadhaar card, latest ration card, income certificate (NSS/SSS), bank passbook ya passbook details, aur recent passport size photo. Sabhi documents scanned PDF ya JPEG format me upload karein.
Q3BPL card milne ke baad mujhe kitni extra ration milti hai?
A
State ke hisaab se alag hota hai, lekin aam taur pe 5‑10 kg wheat ya rice per month milti hai, jo market price ke hisaab se ₹500‑₹800 ki bachat hoti hai.
