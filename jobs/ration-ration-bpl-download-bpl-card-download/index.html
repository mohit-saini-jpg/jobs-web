Ration कार्ड कट ह या नही और कितना Ration मिलता ह चक करे BPL FAQs

Q1Mera Ration Card cut ho gaya hai, kya main BPL list mein aunga?
A
Agar aapka Ration Card cut hua hai, to aapko turant nearest Fair Price Shop ya online portal par apni BPL status verify karni chahiye. Cut status automatically BPL eligibility ko affect nahi karta, lekin verification zaroori hai.
Q2BPL card download ka process kya hai?
A
Official govt website ya state food corporation portal par login karein, apna Aadhaar aur Ration Card number enter karein, aur 'Download BPL List' option select karein. PDF ya image format mein aapka BPL card mil jayega.
Q3Ration ki quantity aur price kaise decide hoti hai?
A
Ration ki quantity (5‑10 kg wheat/ rice) central govt ke guidelines ke hisaab se fixed hai. Subsidised price state government decide karti hai, jo market rate se bahut kam hota hai.
