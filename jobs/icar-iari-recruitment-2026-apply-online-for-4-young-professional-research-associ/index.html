ICAR IARI Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The contractual appointment is for an initial period of six months or the project duration (whichever is earlier), offering a fellowship of up to Rs 67,000/- per month plus 30% HRA for Ph.D. holders, with eligibility open

Pay and Tenure

The engagement is purely temporary, contractual, and co-terminus with the project end date. The initial appointment will be for six months or until the project duration, whichever is earlier. Extension of tenure is based on the satisfactory performance of the incumbent. Research Associate: Rs 61,000/- per month + 30% HRA for Master's degree holders; Rs 67,000/- per month + 30% HRA for Ph.D. holders. Senior Research Fellow: Rs 37,000/- per month + 30% HRA. If performance is non-satisfactory, he/she will be terminated from the post with one-month notice. Young Professional-II: Rs 42,000/- consolidated per month. There is no provision for re-engagement after the termination of the scheme, and selected fellows cannot claim regular appointment at ICAR-IARI.

Documents Required

Neatly typed and completed application form/biodata with self-attested passport-size photograph. Self-attested copies of 10th standard mark sheet and certificate. Self-attested copies of 12th standard mark sheet and certificate. Self-attested copies of Under-graduation (UG) degree and mark sheets. Self-attested copies of Post-graduation (PG) degree and mark sheets. Ph.D. degree certificate (if applicable). NET / GATE / ICAR-SRF qualification certificate (where applicable). Office orders for research experience, in addition to experience certificates issued by the Principal Investigators (PIs). Project work report/thesis, testimonials, and published research papers supporting research claims. No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the current employer, if currently working.

Selection and Interview

Applications will be screened after the last date of receipt. Only candidates possessing the essential and desirable qualifications will be shortlisted and called for an interview. The interview date and details will be communicated directly to the shortlisted candidates by email. No TA/DA will be paid for attending the interview.

ICAR IARI Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are announced under ICAR-IARI Recruitment 2026?
A
A total of 4 unreserved temporary vacancies are available, consisting of 1 Research Associate (RA), 2 Senior Research Fellows (SRF), and 1 Young Professional-II (YP-II).
Q2What is the last date to submit the application for ICAR-IARI Recruitment 2026?
A
The last date for receipt of the online application and biodata is 11 November 2026.
Q3What is the salary for the Research Associate post?
A
The Research Associate post carries a monthly fellowship of Rs 61,000/- plus 30% HRA for Master’s degree holders and Rs 67,000/- plus 30% HRA for Ph.D. degree holders.
Q4What is the educational qualification required for Senior Research Fellow (SRF)?
A
Candidates must have a Master’s degree in Molecular Biology and Biotechnology, Biotechnology, Plant Physiology, Plant Biochemistry, or Life Sciences with a 4/5-year Bachelor's degree (1st Division), or a 3-year Bachelor's and 2-year Master's degree with NET/GATE/ICAR-SRF and 2 years research experience, or a 5-year integrated B.Tech + M.Tech, or a Ph.D.
Q5What is the age limit for RA, SRF, and YP-II posts?
A
For RA and YP-II, the age limit is 40 years for men and 45 years for women. For SRF, the age limit is 35 years for men and 40 years for women, with relaxation for SC/ST, OBC, and PwD candidates as per GoI rules.
Q6Are allowances like HRA provided for these positions?
A
Yes, 30% HRA is admissible on top of the monthly fellowship for the Research Associate and Senior Research Fellow posts, whereas Young Professional-II receives a consolidated salary of Rs 42,000/-.
Q7How are candidates shortlisted and selected?
A
Applications are screened based on essential and desirable qualifications, and only shortlisted candidates are called for an interview, the date of which will be communicated via email.
Q8Is this a permanent government job?
A
No, all posts are purely temporary, contractual for an initial duration of six months or the project period, and co-terminus with the project scheme without any claim for regular appointment.
