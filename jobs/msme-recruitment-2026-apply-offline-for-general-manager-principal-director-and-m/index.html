MSME Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

DC MSME Recruitment 2026 is for 7 Chief Executive Officer (CEO) posts in the autonomous bodies of the Office of Development Commissioner (MSME), New Delhi, Ministry of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises, Government of India. The posts are General Manager, Principal Director and Director at Kolkata, Kannauj, Firozabad, Bhubaneswar, Jalandhar, Ludhiana and Agra. Pay is at Level-12 to Level-13A (as per 7th CPC), with an Entry Pay (Basic) of Rs. 78,800/- to Rs. 1,31,100/- plus DA, TA and HRA (in case accommodation is not provided). Most posts ask for 15 years' experience. The posts are on contract

About DC MSME

The Office of Development Commissioner (MSME), New Delhi, works under the Ministry of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises, Government of India. This recruitment fills the CEO posts of its autonomous bodies: the Central Tool Room & Training Centres (CTTC) at Kolkata and Bhubaneswar, the Fragrance & Flavour Development Centre (FFDC) at Kannauj, the Centre for Development of Glass Industry (CDGI) at Firozabad, the Central Institute of Hand Tools (CIHT) at Jalandhar, the Central Tool Room (CTR) at Ludhiana and the Central Footwear Training Institute (CFTI) at Agra.

DC MSME Recruitment 2026 Job Description

The notice gives one set of duties for all 7 CEO posts: Be responsible for the day-to-day management and affairs of the autonomous body, enhancing the competitiveness of MSMEs in the sector, with a focus on improving their access to technology, providing skill up-gradation and offering advocacy support. Provide leadership in business growth, building client relationships and manpower development. Oversee and provide guidance for Marketing, Production, Training, Design, Consultancy, R&D etc. as required. Work towards building the competencies of the Centre in new and upcoming areas of technology. Co-ordinate and exercise general supervision over all activities of the Centre. Be responsible for revenue generation of the centre for self sustenance.

MSME Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in DC MSME Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 7 vacancies, 1 for each post, since the notice does not print a number. They are General Manager at CTTC Kolkata, CTTC Bhubaneswar and CTR Ludhiana, Principal Director at FFDC Kannauj, CDGI Firozabad and CIHT Jalandhar, and Director at CFTI Agra.
Q2What is the last date for the DC MSME CEO posts?
A
The last date of receipt of application is 23 November 2026. The application must reach the Office of the Development Commissioner (MSME), New Delhi, by that date.
Q3What qualification is needed for General Manager, CTTC, Kolkata?
A
The essential qualification is a Degree in Mechanical /Production Engg or equivalent from a recognized University / Institution. The post also needs 15 years' experience in Production/Training Department of a Tool Room/reputed Engineering Industry, of which 10 years must be in a responsible technology/ Management Position.
Q4What is the age limit for DC MSME Recruitment 2026?
A
The age limit is 50 years for the General Manager and Principal Director posts and not exceeding 45 years for Director, CFTI, Agra. Age is worked out on the closing date for receipt of applications, 23 November 2026.
Q5Who gets age relaxation, and how much?
A
For most posts the relaxation is up to 5 years for SC/ST/Internal candidates and 3 years for OBC. For FFDC Kannauj, the 5 years also cover employees of Govt., Semi-Govt./Quasi Govt. Organizations / Autonomous Bodies and departmental candidates, and the 3 years also cover Ex-servicemen/PH. For CDGI Firozabad, the 5 years also cover departmental candidates and employees of Govt/semi-Govt organizations.
Q6What is the salary for the DC MSME CEO posts?
A
The General Manager posts and Principal Director, CIHT, Jalandhar are at Level-13A, with an Entry Pay (Basic) of Rs. 1,31,100/-. The Principal Director posts at FFDC and CDGI are at Level-13, with Rs. 1,23,100/-, and Director, CFTI, Agra is at Level-12, with Rs. 78,800/-.
Q7Is HRA paid on top of the basic pay?
A
Yes, all 7 posts carry allowances as per Government Rules. These include DA, TA and HRA (in case accommodation is not provided), as per norms, on top of the basic pay.
Q8How do I apply for DC MSME Recruitment 2026?
A
Fill in the Proforma from the detailed advertisement and attach your supporting documents. Send it by speed post or registered post to the Additional Development Commissioner (TR), Office of the Development Commissioner (MSME), Room No. 32082, 2nd Floor, Kartavya Bhawan-3, Janpath Road, New Delhi-110001, so that it reaches by 23 November 2026.
Q9Are the DC MSME CEO posts permanent?
A
No, the posts are to be filled on contract basis for 5 years initially. They can be extended further, based on the review of performance, till the age of superannuation.
Q10Can I apply for more than one post?
A
Yes, but you must send a separate application for each post or location. Each envelope must say "application for the vacancy of" followed by the position and location you are applying for.

A
