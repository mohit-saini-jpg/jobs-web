WBCAP Merit List 2026 - Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 27/07/2026

The West Bengal Centralised Admission Portal (WBCAP) released the WBCAP Merit List 2026 at wbcap.in. The merit list has been prepared for admission to undergraduate (UG) courses based on Class 12 board examination marks, and is available institution-wise on the official portal as well as on respective college websites. Candidates whose names appear on the merit list must complete the admission process between 9 June and 15 June 2026.

Details Mentioned on WBCAP Merit List 2026

After downloading the merit list, candidates should verify the following details mentioned in it:

  • Name of candidate
  • Category
  • Date of Birth
  • Subjects studied in Class 12
  • Marks obtained in Class 12 Board Examination
  • Merit marks
  • College name
  • University name
  • Academic session

How to Download WBCAP Merit List 2026?

Candidates can check and download the WBCAP Merit List 2026 by following these steps:

  • Visit the official WBCAP portal at wbcap.in
  • Log in using registered credentials
  • Click on the merit list tab available on the homepage
  • Select the university and college applied for
  • The college-wise merit list will appear on the screen
  • Download the merit list PDF for future reference
  • Search for your name or application number in the list

What's Next After WBCAP Merit List 2026?

Selected candidates must complete the admission process within the prescribed dates by:

  • Paying the admission fee through the respective college portal using online payment methods
  • Uploading requisite documents for verification
  • Attending physical document verification as scheduled by the allotted college
  • Confirming admission only after successful document verification

WBCAP Merit List 2026 Important Instructions

  • Complete the admission process only within the dates specified for your round (regular, upgraded, or mop-up).
  • Keep a downloaded copy of both the merit list and the seat allotment letter.
  • Watch for your college's separate notice on the physical verification schedule.
  • If not admitted in the first round, check the mop-up round dates (applications from 7 to 16 July 2026) for vacant seats.
