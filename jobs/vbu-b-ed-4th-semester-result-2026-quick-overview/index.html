How to Check VBU B.Ed 4th Semester Result 2024-26

  • Visit the official VBU website: www.vbu.ac.in
  • Go to the Results section under the Examination/Notice menu.
  • Look for the notice titled "Result of B.Ed. (4th Semester) Examination of 2024-26".
  • Click on the relevant result link or open the VBU result portal directly at result.vbuuniv.in.
  • Enter your required login details (roll number/registration number) as prompted on the portal.
  • Your B.Ed. 4th Semester Result will be displayed on the screen.
  • Download and save the result, and take a printout for future reference.

Important Instructions

  • This result is provisional in nature unless stated otherwise in the official PDF; always verify with the official marksheet issued by the university.
  • In case of any discrepancy in marks or personal details, candidates should contact the VBU examination department or their respective college.
