About IIITDM Kancheepuram

IIITDM Kancheepuram, Chennai is an Institute of National Importance established in 2007 by the Ministry of Education, Government of India. It is located on the outskirts of Chennai, close to the IT corridor, industrial areas and educational institutions, and is a centre of higher learning for design- and manufacturing-oriented engineering education. The Institute offers B.Tech, Dual Degree, M.Tech, M.Des and PhD programmes in Computer Science and Engineering, Electronics and Communication Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, and Smart Manufacturing and Product Design, along with interdisciplinary PhD programmes.

Pay and Terms

The initial engagement shall ordinarily be for one year (two consecutive semesters). It is extendable for a further period of up to one year based on performance, academic requirements, mutually agreed terms and approval of the Competent Authority, subject to applicable guidelines. The engagement shall be contractual and shall not confer any claim to a regular faculty appointment. The engagement shall be governed by the guidelines issued by the Ministry of Education vide ref. no. 21-81/2014-TS-II dated 16th February 2015.
