Hyderabad PwD Backlog Selection Process 2026

The two groups of posts are selected in different ways. For Junior Assistant, the written examination and oral interview are waived. Selection is made on the highest marks secured in the relevant degree (qualifying academic examination) along with usage of computers (G.O.Ms.No.74, General Administration (Services-A) Department, dated 14.02.2007). The District Selection Committee will conduct a test in "proficiency in Office Automation with usage of computer and associated software" to select meritorious candidates (G.O.Ms.No.133 & 135 GAD (SER-B), dated 12.05.2014). Selection is based on parameters fixed by the Task Force Committee / District Selection Committee, out of an aggregate of 50 marks. DEPwD Hyderabad Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in Hyderabad PwD Backlog Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 83 backlog posts: 3 Junior Assistant (Group-IV) posts and 80 Class-IV posts. The notice calls these approximate numbers.
Q2What is the last date to apply?
A
Applications must be submitted up to 2 December 2026 at 5.00 PM at the Assistant Director's office in Nampally, Hyderabad.
Q3Who can apply for these posts?
A
Only Hyderabad local persons with benchmark disabilities (VI, HI, LD or ID) with at least 40% disability can apply. Hearing impaired candidates need 75% or more.
Q4What is the age limit?
A
Candidates should have completed 18 years and should not have completed 54 years of age as on 01.07.2026.
Q5What is the qualification for Junior Assistant and Office Subordinate?
A
Junior Assistant needs a degree with proficiency in Office Automation with usage of computer. Office Subordinate needs a pass in VII class.
Q6What is the salary for these posts?
A
PRC, 2020 Scales are applicable to the respective posts.
Q7How are Class-IV candidates selected?
A
Class-IV candidates are selected on 50 marks: 15 for age, 30 for disability and 5 for multiple disability. Ties go to the higher degree of disability, then to the elder candidate.
Q8Is there a written exam for Junior Assistant?
A
No, the written examination and oral interview are waived. Selection is on degree marks, and the District Selection Committee will conduct a test in proficiency in Office Automation.
Q9Can I apply for more than one post?
A
Yes, but you must submit a separate application for each post with all the required documents.
Q10Can non-local candidates apply?
A
No. All posts are Hyderabad local posts and non-local candidates are not eligible to apply.
