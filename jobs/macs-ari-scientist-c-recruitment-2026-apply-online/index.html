About MACS-ARI

MACS-Agharkar Research Institute (ARI), Pune is an autonomous institute of the Department of Science and Technology (DST), Government of India. It describes itself as committed to promoting science and technology, with emphasis on high standards of research and development. Its research covers the biological sciences in six thematic areas: Biodiversity & Palaeobiology, Bioenergy, Bioprospecting, Developmental Biology, Genetics & Plant Breeding, and Nanobioscience.

Vacancy Details

The notice advertises 2 Scientist-C posts on a direct recruitment basis. The place of work for the first post is the Genetics & Plant Breeding Group of ARI, Pune. For the second post, it is the Developmental Biology Group of ARI, Pune.

ARI Pune Recruitment 2026 Job Description

Undertake a breeding programme in wheat, soybean and grape for high yield, improved quality and tolerance to biotic and abiotic stresses. Implement genomic-assisted breeding approaches, including GWAS, QTL mapping, SNP-based analyses and genomic selection (GS). Develop, manage and evaluate bi-parental, multi-parent (MAGIC/NAAM) and RIL breeding populations. Conduct biofortification research and trait discovery for nutritional and quality-related traits. Plan, conduct and analyse multi-location and multi-season field trials. Carry out germplasm characterisation, evaluation and core collection development. Apply marker-assisted selection (MAS) and integrate molecular tools into breeding pipelines. Perform plant phenotyping using morphological, physiological and biochemical methods. Conduct quality analyses, including oil, protein, minerals, fatty acid compositions and volatile profiling. C

Selection and Interview

Having the essential qualifications does not by itself entitle a candidate to an interview. A committee decides whether a qualification is equivalent to the essential and desirable qualifications. If many applications are received, the Institute may limit the number of candidates called for interview through shortlisting. The shortlisting criteria may include desirable qualifications and/or experience beyond the minimum. Only shortlisted candidates will be informed by email of the date, time and venue of the next stage of selection. Outstation candidates called for interview will be paid two-tier AC rail fare both ways by the shortest route. A reserve panel, in order of merit, will be prepared from the candidates called for interview. It remains valid for one year from the date of interview and may be used to fill the advertised vacancy and vacancies arising later.
