Punjab and Haryana High Court Driver and Frash Eligibility Criteria

Candidates in all categories, for both posts, must not be less than 18 years and not more than 35 years of age as on 18/09/2026. For Ex-servicemen, the upper age limit is relaxed by the number of years equal to their defence service plus 3 years. Punjab and Haryana High Court Driver and Frash Application Fee

The application fee for both posts is payable online and is non-refundable, with online transaction charges levied extra. For the Frash post only, there is no application fee for candidates who are dependents of Ex-servicemen disabled or deceased during active operation, provided they upload the requisite proof at the time of application and produce the original at testimonials checking. Punjab and Haryana High Court Driver and Frash Selection Process

Selection to the post of Driver is made on the basis of performance in a Practical Test-cum-Interview. The High Court may apply any suitable shortlisting criteria for provisionally allowing candidates to appear, informed via a public notice at the appropriate time. Selection does not confer any right to claim appointment; candidates proposed for appointment first undergo a trial period, with confirmed appointment only after its successful completion. Candidates for the post of Frash are selected on the basis of performance in an Interview of 50 marks (25 marks for Suitability to the Job, 25 marks for Etiquette/Manners). Candidates equivalent to 4 times (category-wise) the advertised vacancies, in order of merit in the interview, are then called for a qualifying Physical Endurance Test (PET): an 800-metre race, long jump, and high jump. Q1What is P&H High Court Recruitment 2026?
A
It refers to two recruitment drives by the High Court of Punjab and Haryana at Chandigarh: Employment Notice No. 04/DRV/HC/2026 for 25 Driver posts, and Employment Notice No. 05/Frash/HC/2026 for 31 Frash posts.
Q2How many total vacancies are available?
A
A combined 56 vacancies are available: 25 for Driver (21 General, 3 SC/ST/BC, 1 Ex-Servicemen) and 31 for Frash (26 General, 3 SC/ST/BC, 2 Ex-Servicemen).
Q3What is the last date to apply for P&H High Court Recruitment 2026?
A
The last date for online registration for both the Driver and Frash posts is 18 September 2026, up to 11:59 P.M.

A
For Driver, candidates need 10+2 pass with Punjabi/Hindi in matriculation and a valid LMV driving licence. For Frash, the notification requires knowledge of Hindi and the regional language.
Q5What is the age limit for both posts?
A
Candidates for both Driver and Frash must be between 18 and 35 years of age as on 18/09/2026, with age relaxation for Ex-servicemen equal to their defence service plus 3 years.
