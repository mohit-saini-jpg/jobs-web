136 Infantry Battalion Territorial Army Recruitment Rally : Job Overview

136 Infantry Battalion (Territorial Army) Ecological, Mahar has announced a Recruitment Rally 2026 for Ex-Servicemen from the Armed Forces and Ex-Women Employees of the Ministry of Environment, Forest & Climate Change (MoEF & CC) and State Forest Departments. A total of 62 vacancies are available for Soldier General Duty and Tradesmen posts on an All India basis.

What is 136 Infantry Battalion Territorial Army Recruitment Rally 2026?

The 136 Infantry Battalion (Territorial Army) Ecological, Mahar Recruitment Rally 2026 is being conducted for enrolment of eligible Ex-Servicemen and eligible former women employees of MoEF & CC and State Forest Departments. Candidates who fulfil the prescribed service, medical, character and physical conditions can attend the recruitment rally directly at Kamptee, Nagpur.

136 Infantry Battalion TA Rally Eligibility Criteria 2026

Candidate should be an Ex-Serviceman and Pension Holder from the Armed Forces. Ex-Servicemen from all over India are eligible to attend the recruitment rally. Candidate should be in Medical Category SHAPE-I. Character should be Exemplary / Very Good. Candidate should not be involved in any civil or criminal case. Applicant should be a former woman employee of the Ministry of Environment, Forest & Climate Change (MoEF & CC) or State Forest Department. She should have completed a minimum of 20 years of service in MoEF & CC / State Forest Department. Eligible candidates may attend the recruitment rally as per the conditions mentioned in the recruitment notice.

136 Infantry Battalion TA Rally Age and Service Conditions

No minimum age limit has been specified for eligible Ex-Servicemen and Ex-Women Employees. Candidates should get enrolled within five years from the date of discharge/retirement as on the date of recruitment. Ex-Servicemen belonging to Other Ranks will be enrolled as Sepoy and can serve up to 50 years of age.

136 Infantry Battalion TA Terms of Engagement 2026

Enrolment will be purely on merit and availability of vacancies. Service rendered in Eco TA is non-pensionable. Initial enrolment will be for two years. Based on performance, engagement may be extended by one year. Ex-Servicemen belonging to Other Ranks will be enrolled as Sepoy. They can serve up to 50 years of age. Once enrolled, candidates will be liable to serve anywhere in India. An enrolled individual may be discharged if found undesirable or unsuitable. The previous pension being drawn by an Ex-Serviceman will remain untouched.
