PRATYUSH X-ray Technician Eligibility 2026

Upper age limit: 35 years. You must meet one of the following: B.sc in Radiography/Medical Imaging Technology or equivalent (3 years course) from a recognized University/Institution, with 2 years of experience in operating radio-diagnosis equipment in an established Institute/ Hospital/ Imaging Centre; OR DMIT with 3 years of experience in operating radio-diagnosis equipment in an established Institute/ Hospital/ Imaging Center. Experience will not be counted without a valid experience certificate. For exceptionally meritorious candidates, the Institute may at its discretion relax the age limit or experience, or offer higher pay, subject to approval of the competent authority. See the official notification (PDF) for the full eligibility conditions for this post.

Job Responsibilities

Prepare patients for radiological procedures and take X-rays following established procedures for patient care and safety. Protect patients and staff from radiation exposure by following sound procedures that meet or exceed recommended industry standards. Process radiological film. Obtain quality diagnostic radiographs as requested by the Physician, PA or NP. Keep all equipment in good working condition and in line with applicable laws and regulations. Identify and file reports as required. Request, retrieve and track files to and from other departments or centres. Maintain the production and quality of radiographs by following set standards, developing radiographs, checking results and making adjustments. Working hours will be as set by the Health Centre.

Service Conditions

Contract period: The appointment is initially for 01 (one) year. It may be extended depending on performance and requirements. Nature: The position is purely temporary. Being called for interview or being selected gives no claim to a permanent post at IIT Bhubaneswar. Place of posting: IIT Bhubaneswar. Jurisdiction: Any dispute about the selection will be heard only by the Courts/Tribunals in Bhubaneswar.
