About IGIMS

Indira Gandhi Institute of Medical Sciences is at Sheikhpura, Patna - 800014. Its letterhead describes it as an autonomous institute of the Government of Bihar and a statutory university created by an Act of the State Legislature, recognised by UGC and NMC. This notice was issued by the institute's Department of Anaesthesiology & Critical Care Medicine.

IGIMS Senior Resident Vacancy Details 2026

The notice says the number of vacancies may increase or decrease. Reservation applies only to candidates with Bihar domicile. Applicants from outside Bihar do not get the benefit of reservation. Handicapped candidates get reservation as per Government of Bihar rules. Where a post is meant for a female candidate and no eligible woman is available, a male candidate may be considered. IGIMS Senior Resident Eligibility 2026

The upper age limit is 45 years. Age relaxation follows State Government rules: SC/ST: 05 years EBC (MBC) and BC: 03 years Female candidates of all categories: 03 years Persons working in a Government institution: relaxation as per rule A medical qualification included in Schedule I & II of the MCI Act. NMC-recognised P.G. qualification of MD/DNB in Anaesthesiology. Registration with the Central or a State Medical Council. Only Indian citizens can apply. These are minimum requirements and do not automatically make a candidate eligible for interview. Candidates should check the official notification for the full eligibility conditions of the post.

IGIMS Senior Resident (Anaesthesiology) Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

The Selection Committee of the institute selects candidates based on their bio-data at the walk-in interview. Candidates must present their thesis and publications in PowerPoint and should bring them on a pen-drive. The committee's panel is valid for 06 (six) months only. Being selected on merit does not give a right to appointment, because the institute decides on appointing the candidates the committee recommends.

Service Conditions

The appointment is under the 89 days Senior Residency programme. The posts are non-practising. Private practice of any kind, direct or indirect, including laboratory work, is prohibited, and an appointment will be terminated if this rule is broken. Selected candidates must sign a legal bond. If they quit or resign mid-way, they must pay one month's emoluments for every year of the Sr. Residency programme not completed. Living on campus is compulsory if accommodation is provided. If it is not provided, HRA is paid as per rule. Pay + NPA is paid, along with other allowances as per rule.
