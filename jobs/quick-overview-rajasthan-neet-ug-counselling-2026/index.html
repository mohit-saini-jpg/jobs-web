The NEET UG Medical and Dental Admission/Counselling Board, Rajasthan, has released the Round 1 schedule for NEET UG (Medical/Dental) Counselling 2026. How to Apply for Rajasthan NEET UG Counselling 2026

  • Visit the official portal, ug.neetrajasthan.com, or medicaleducation.rajasthan.gov.in.
  • Click on the registration/application link and read the instructions in the information bulletin carefully.
  • Complete Part 1 of the application form using your NEET UG 2026 details.
  • Complete Part 2 of the application form with personal, academic and category details. The board has stated that completing both parts is mandatory for further stages.
  • Pay the non-refundable application fee online (₹2,500 or ₹1,500, as applicable) before the deadline of August 17, 2026 (4:00 PM).
  • Pay the applicable security deposit during the choice-filling window (August 14–20, 2026).
  • Fill and lock your college/course choices before the auto-lock deadline of 11:55 PM on August 20, 2026.
  • Check the Round 1 seat allotment result on August 22, 2026.
  • If allotted a seat, deposit the tuition fee and report to SMS Medical College, Jaipur, with original documents between August 23 and August 27, 2026.

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Both Part 1 and Part 2 of the application form must be completed; incomplete applications cannot proceed to choice filling or seat allotment.
  • Defence, Para Military and NRI candidates must appear physically for verification on August 19, 2026, at the Academic Block, SMS Medical College, Jaipur. A separate notice on this will be published on the website.
  • Choice filling and security deposit both close at fixed deadlines; late submissions will not be accepted.
  • The schedule for Round 2 and subsequent rounds has not yet been announced and will be notified separately by the board.
  • Candidates are advised to regularly check ug.neetrajasthan.com for updates, corrigenda, and further notifications.
