OAVS Selection Process 2026

OAVS selection is based on Computer Based Test, Interview and Performance Test wherever applicable. The online question paper will be bilingual in English and Odia. The Principal CBT contains 180 questions for 180 marks and has a duration of 180 minutes. There is negative marking of 0.25 mark for every incorrect answer. Part I is qualifying. Candidates must secure at least 5 marks each in English and Odia and 13 marks in aggregate in Part I for Parts II, III and IV to be evaluated. The Principal Interview carries 60 marks. Final merit uses 70% weightage for CBT Parts II, III and IV and 30% for Interview. The TGT CBT is for 200 minutes and carries 200 marks. There is 0.25 negative marking for every wrong answer. OAVS Teacher Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

Applications are accepted online only. Candidates must ensure eligibility before applying. All eligibility details entered in the application are subject to verification. Wrong or false information may lead to cancellation at any stage, including after appointment. Round-off of qualification percentage is not permitted. Candidates should be competent to teach in English and Odia medium where prescribed. Knowledge of computer application is desirable for teaching posts where specified. TGT candidates must possess the prescribed OSSTET/OTET/CTET Paper-II qualification. Candidates with Special B.Ed. or D.Ed. are not eligible for the specified Principal, PGT and TGT posts. Government/Semi-Government/PSU employees must produce NOC at document verification/interview. Original documents along with self-attested copies must be produced at verification. The selected candidate may be posted anywhere in Odisha and cannot claim a posting of choice. Selected candidates must sign a bond to remain in service for at least 2 years from the date of joining. Canvassing in any form will disqualify the candidate. Candidates must carry printed E-Admit Card and valid original Photo ID to the CBT. Mobile phones, pagers, Bluetooth devices and other communication devices are prohibited at the examination centre. PwD candidates eligible for a scribe must arrange their own scribe as per the prescribed conditions. Eligible PwD candidates are allowed compensatory time of 20 minutes per hour of examination. No TA/DA is admissible for attending the Interview. Candidates may respond in either Odia or English during Interview. The number of vacancies may increase or decrease.
TGT Social Studies recruitment is subject to the final outcome of WP(C) No. 7538/2016. PET recruitment is subject to the final outcome of WP(C) No. 14488/2018. Art Teacher recruitment is subject to the final outcome of WP(C) No. 32290/2024.
