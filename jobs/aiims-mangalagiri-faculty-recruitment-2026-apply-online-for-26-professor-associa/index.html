AIIMS Mangalagiri Faculty Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

All India Institute of Medical Sciences, Mangalagiri, Andhra Pradesh (an autonomous institute under MoHFW, Govt. of India) has re-advertised 26 Group A faculty posts in 17 departments on direct recruitment / deputation basis: 11 Professor, 5 Additional Professor, 6 Associate Professor and 4 Assistant Professor. The posts carry pay from Level 12 to Level 14-A of the Pay Matrix. Online applications open on 20 November 2026 and close on 31 December 2026, and the signed hard copy must reach the Recruitment Cell by 10 January 2027.

About AIIMS Mangalagiri

All India Institute of Medical Sciences, Mangalagiri, Guntur Dist., Andhra Pradesh, is an Autonomous Institute of National Importance. The notice describes it as one of the new AIIMS and apex healthcare institutes being established by the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Government of India, under the Pradhan Mantri Swasthya Suraksha Yojana (PMSSY). Its stated aim is to correct regional imbalance in quality tertiary level healthcare and to attain self-sufficiency in graduate, postgraduate and higher medical education and training.

Qualification and Experience

Each post has separate rules for medical candidates (general disciplines and super specialty disciplines) and for non-medical candidates. Teaching and/or research experience must be in recognized institutions in the subject of speciality, and experience in Dental / Physiotherapy / Nursing College is not considered. Experience counts only after obtaining the prescribed educational qualification, and is counted as on 31 December 2026. Selection and appointment are subject to production and verification of documentary evidence of all eligibility conditions. Essential for medical candidates: (1) a medical qualification included in the I or II schedule or part II of the third schedule to the Indian Medical Council Act of 1956 (holders of part II qualifications must also fulfil the conditions in section 13(3) of the Act); (2) a postgraduate qualification, e.g. MD/MS or a recognized equivalent

Pay and Terms

The pay levels are as given in MoH&FW (INI-I-Section), GoI Letter No. V-16020/28/2017-INI-I (Pt.), dated 23rd August 2018. The pay is the same for posts on direct recruitment and deputation basis. All the posts carry usual allowances as admissible to Central Government Employees of similar status at AIIMS Mangalagiri. The appointment will be subject to review and probation of two years. A faculty member, after rendering fixed years of service on a grade, may be considered for assessment promotion, subject to the Rules and Regulations applicable from time to time. The appointee shall be on the whole-time appointment of AIIMS Mangalagiri and shall not accept any other assignment, paid or otherwise, or engage in private practice of any kind. AIIMS Mangalagiri Faculty Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many faculty vacancies are there in AIIMS Mangalagiri Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 26 posts: 11 Professor, 5 Additional Professor, 6 Associate Professor and 4 Assistant Professor across 17 departments. The notice says the vacancies are provisional and subject to variation.
Q2What is the last date to apply?
A
The online application closes on 31 December 2026, having opened on 20 November 2026. The signed hard copy must reach the Recruitment Cell by 10 January 2027.
Q3What is the qualification for Assistant Professor?
A
Medical candidates need the medical qualification under the Indian Medical Council Act of 1956 and a postgraduate qualification (e.g. MD/MS or equivalent) with three years of teaching/research experience, or D.M./M.Ch. for super specialty with one year (none for a 3 years recognized D.M./M.Ch.). Non-medical candidates need a postgraduate qualification and a Doctorate with three years of experience.
Q4What is the age limit?
A
The limit is not exceeding 58 years for Professor and Additional Professor, and not exceeding 50 years for Associate Professor and Assistant Professor, as on the closing date of the online application. Relaxation is 10 years for PwBD, 5 years for SC/ST on reserved posts, 3 years for OBC on reserved posts and 5 years for Government servants.
Q5What is the salary for AIIMS Mangalagiri faculty posts?
A
Minimum pay is Rs.1,68,900 (Level 14-A) for Professor, Rs.1,48,200 (Level 13-A2+) for Additional Professor, Rs.1,38,300 (Level 13-A1+) for Associate Professor and Rs.1,01,500 (Level 12) for Assistant Professor. NPA is added for medically qualified candidates only.
Q6What is the application fee?
A
UR/EWS/OBC candidates pay Rs. 3100 in total (Rs. 3,000 plus Rs. 100 processing fee), and SC/ST/Women pay Rs. 2100 (Rs. 2000 plus Rs. 100). PwBD candidates pay only the Rs. 100 processing fee, and the fee is not refundable.
Q7How do I apply for AIIMS Mangalagiri faculty posts?
A
Fill the online form on www.aiimsmangalagiri.edu.in and take a printout. Send the signed hard copy with documents by Speed Post / Courier to the Recruitment Cell, AIIMS Mangalagiri, and e-mail the self-attested documents as one PDF to [email protected].
Q8What is the selection process?
A
A Standing Selection Committee conducts the selection through an interview at AIIMS Mangalagiri, after verification of original documents. Candidates must score at least 50% (UR/EWS), 45% (OBC) or 40% (SC/ST) in the interview, with PwBD as per applicable category relaxation.
Q9Can I apply for more than one post?
A
Yes. Candidates who wish to apply for more than one post must apply separately online for each post and pay the application fee for each post.
Q10Are the posts open on deputation, and for how long?
A
Yes, the posts are advertised on direct recruitment / deputation basis, and reservation does not apply to deputation posts. The initial period of deputation is 03 years, further extendable as per DoPT regulations.
