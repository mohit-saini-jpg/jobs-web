The notification specifies that only the consolidated monthly honorarium is paid during the contract period, with no other allowance or facility.

NHM Surguja Age Limit

Age is reckoned as on 01 January 2026 Minimum age: 18 years Maximum age: 64 years for managerial/administrative posts; 70 years for medical (doctor) posts Age relaxation for reserved categories: Not specified in the notification

NHM Surguja Selection Process

Candidates will be shortlisted and selected as follows: Educational Qualification – 65% weightage: based on the average percentage of marks obtained across all years of the Graduation/Post-Graduation qualification (grade-to-percentage conversion applies where relevant, per the grading scheme followed by the candidate's institution) Skill Test / Written Test – 20 marks: conducted post-wise (written or computer-based, as applicable); candidates must score a minimum of 30% to remain eligible Experience – 10 or 15 marks: only experience relevant to the applied post is counted. Candidates currently/previously working under NHM Chhattisgarh get 3 marks/year (maximum 15); other candidates get 2 marks/year (maximum 10). Experience is counted only for government, semi-government, or government-aided institutions, and only for work performed after completing the prescribed qualification COVID-19 B

CMHO Surguja Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

Only candidates who are original residents of Chhattisgarh are eligible; a residence certificate is mandatory at the time of document verification. Incomplete, unclear, or erroneous applications will be rejected; attaching unnecessary documents can also invalidate the application. No certificate or document will be accepted after the application deadline under any circumstance. All posts are purely contractual for one financial year at a time, governed by the NHM Chhattisgarh Contractual Human Resources Policy 2018 (as amended); the contract is automatically terminated on regular recruitment to the post. Selected candidates must make their place of posting their headquarters and reside there; the posting location is non-transferable, and requests for transfer will be treated as indiscipline. Selected candidates must submit an affidavit on ₹50 stamp paper in the prescribed format after selection. Candidates currently employed in government/semi-government institutions must submit a No Objection Certificate from their employer. Candidates previously dismissed from government service, or terminated from NHM or any government department for indiscipline, financial irregularity, or misconduct, are not eligible for any post. Selection does not automatically confer a right to appointment; final appointment depends on actual vacancy availability and other criteria. The recruitment process is subject to the final order of the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India in SLP(C) No. 19668/2022; appointments are made subject to the outcome of that petition. Any legal dispute arising from this recruitment falls under the jurisdiction of the Surguja (Ambikapur) courts only, and the final decision on any dispute rests with the appointing authority/Chairperson of the District Selection Committee, Surguja. All updates related to this recruitment will be published only on www.surguja.gov.in and the office notice board - no other medium will be used for communication.
