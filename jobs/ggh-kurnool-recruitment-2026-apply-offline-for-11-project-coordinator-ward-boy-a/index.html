GGH Kurnool Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Government General Hospital, Kurnool (Health, Medical and Family Welfare Department, Government of Andhra Pradesh) is filling 11 scheme posts on contract basis in the Drug De-Addiction Centres at GGH Kurnool (5 posts) and GGH Nandyal (6 posts), initially for one year. Pay ranges from Rs.5,000 to Rs.25,000 per month by post, and the posts need qualifications from Class 8th pass to Graduate, with experience. The application form is downloaded from the Kurnool or Nandyal District website and the filled-in form is submitted at the office of the Addl.DME/Superintendent, GGH, Kurnool, from 17.11.202

About GGH Kurnool

Government General Hospital, Kurnool is a government hospital under the Health, Medical and Family Welfare Department of Andhra Pradesh. This notice covers its Drug De-Addiction Centre and the Drug De-Addiction Centre at GGH Nandyal. Selection is made by a District Selection Committee headed by the Collector and District Magistrate, Kurnool.

Pay and Tenure

The salary per month for each post is: Project Coordinator cum Vocational Counsellor 25,000/-; Ward Boy 13,000/-; Counsellor/Social Worker/Psychologist 17,500/-; Peer Educator 10,000/-; Yoga Therapist/Dance/Music/Art Teacher 5,000/-. The contract is initially one year from the date of joining and may be extended for a further period as per Government instructions issued from time to time. The District Selection Committee reserves the right to terminate the contract of any candidate at any time with one month notice or as per directions of the Government. The notice states that the appointments are purely temporary on contract basis and liable for termination at any time.

GGH Kurnool Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

The notice scores candidates out of a total of 100 marks. No written test or interview is described in the notice. 75% of the marks are allocated to the aggregate of marks obtained in all years of the qualifying examination. Marks awarded for languages are not considered for the aggregate. Up to 10 marks are given at 1.0 mark per completed year after acquiring the requisite qualification as shown in the pass certificate, reckoned up to the date of notification as per Govt. Memo no.4274/D1/2013, HM&FW (D1) Dept., dt.10.07.2014. Weightage up to 15 marks is given to candidates working on Contract/Outsourcing/Honorarium basis, including COVID-19 service, subject to satisfactory service certified by the competent authority. GGH Kurnool Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in GGH Kurnool Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 11 vacancies in total: 5 in the Drug De-Addiction Centre at GGH Kurnool and 6 in the Drug De-Addiction Centre at GGH Nandyal, across five posts.
Q2What is the last date to apply?
A
Physical applications are accepted from 17.11.2026 to 26.11.2026, between 10.30 AM and 5.00 PM. Applications received after the stipulated time are rejected summarily.
Q3What is the qualification for the posts?
A
Ward Boy needs Class 8th pass with experience in hospitals, health care centres or de-addiction centres. Project Coordinator cum Vocational Counsellor and Counsellor/Social Worker/Psychologist need a graduate with experience; Peer Educator needs literacy and 1-2 years of sobriety as an ex-drug user. Yoga Therapist/Dance/Music/Art Teacher needs at least three years of experience in the discipline.
Q4What is the age limit?
A
The upper age limit is 42 years as on 01.07.2026. Relaxation is 5 years for SC, ST, BC and EWS, 3 years plus service for ex-servicemen, and 10 years for differently abled persons, with a maximum of 52 years.
Q5What is the salary for each post?
A
Salary per month is Rs.25,000 for Project Coordinator cum Vocational Counsellor, Rs.13,000 for Ward Boy, Rs.17,500 for Counsellor/Social Worker/Psychologist, Rs.10,000 for Peer Educator and Rs.5,000 for Yoga Therapist/Dance/Music/Art Teacher.
Q6What is the application fee?
A
OC candidates pay Rs.250/- and SC/ST/BC/Physically challenged candidates pay Rs.200/-, by Demand Draft/Banker Cheque in favour of "Hospital Development Society Fund, Superintendent, Government General Hospital, Kurnool".
Q7How do I apply for GGH Kurnool Recruitment 2026?
A
Download the form from the Kurnool or Nandyal District website, fill it in, attach attested copies of the documents and the fee instrument, and submit it at the O/o the Addl.DME/Superintendent, GGH, Kurnool.
Q8Can I apply online?
A
No. The notice states that an application sent through any mode other than the prescribed offline mode (physical application) will not be entertained.
Q9How are candidates selected?
A
Selection is on 100 marks: 75% for aggregate marks in the qualifying examination, up to 10 marks for completed years after the requisite qualification, and weightage up to 15 marks for contract, outsourcing or honorarium service including COVID-19 service.
Q10Is this a permanent job?
A
No. The posts are on contract basis, initially for one year from the date of joining, and may be extended as per Government instructions. The appointments are liable for termination at any time.
