About Jayashankar Bhupalpally Recruitment 2026

Government of Telangana permission for 333 service categories across the state was given in G.O.Rt.No.1508, Fin (HRM.VII) Dept., Dt:21/08/2026. These comprise 38 services on contract and 295 services on outsourcing basis. Following that order, the Director, Department of AYUSH, T.G., Secunderabad, asked the Collector and District Magistrate, Jayashankar Bhupalpally District, to fill only the 10 posts sanctioned for this district through the District Selection Committee (DSC). The posts are meant exclusively for the AYUSH Department under the Public Health Programme in the National AYUSH Mission. Selected candidates will work under the administrative control of the Director, Department of AYUSH, T.G., Secunderabad.

Department-wise Vacancies

All 10 posts are in Jayashankar Bhupalpally District. The notice gives the following post-wise count with roster points: In the roster, seven points are OC, two are SC (Group.II) and one is BC-A. For the Psychosocial Counselor post, the notice adds that a panel of eligible candidates may be empaneled and engaged on a call basis as and when their services are required. District Collector and Magistrate, Jayashankar : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

The District Selection Committee of the District Health Society makes the selection on a total of 100 marks. Ninety marks come from the qualifying examination, or from a written test for MPW, and ten marks are given for age. The steps are described below. AYUSH Medical Officer, Pharmacist, Psychosocial Counselor and Yoga Professional: 90 marks based on the marks obtained in the qualifying examination. Multi Purpose Worker: 90 marks for a written test conducted by the concerned authorities. The notice does not give the test date, syllabus or pattern. Age weightage applies to all posts and is counted from 18 years of age. A candidate gets 0.5 mark for each completed year, up to a maximum of 10 marks, with age reckoned as on 1.7.2026. District Collector and Magistrate, Jayashankar : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in Jayashankar Bhupalpally Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 10 posts: AYUSH Medical Officer (4), Pharmacist (1), Psychosocial Counselor (1), Yoga Professional (1) and Multi Purpose Worker (MPW) (3).
Q2What is the last date to apply?
A
Applications must reach the IDOC by 31 October 2026, between 10:30 AM and 05:00 PM. Applications received after the due date will be summarily rejected.
Q3What qualification is needed for the Multi Purpose Worker post?
A
The candidate must have passed the VII Class examination and must be able to ride a Bicycle / Motorcycle.
Q4What is the age limit?
A
The age limit is 18 to 44 years as on 01/07/2026. SC, ST and BC candidates get 5 years of relaxation, Ex-Service men get 3 years plus their armed forces service, and disabled persons get 10 years.
Q5What is the salary of an AYUSH Medical Officer?
A
An AYUSH Medical Officer gets a consolidated Rs.40,000/- per month. The Yoga Professional gets Rs.28,000/-, and the Pharmacist and MPW get Rs.15,000/- each.
Q6How is the Psychosocial Counselor paid?
A
The Psychosocial Counselor is paid @ Rs.500/- per session with atleast 200 visits in a year, on call. A panel of eligible candidates may be empaneled.
Q7How do I apply?
A
Download the form from the official website of Jayashankar Bhupalpally District. Submit it with self-attested certificates, in person or by registered post, to IDOC, Room No:G5, Ground Floor, O/o. the Collector & District Magistrate, Jayashankar Bhupalpally District.
Q8How will candidates be selected?
A
Selection is on 100 marks: 90 for qualifying examination marks (or a written test for MPW) and up to 10 for age, at 0.5 mark per completed year from 18. A merit list will be displayed on the website.
Q9Is this a permanent job?
A
No. The posts are on an outsourcing basis up to 31.03.2027. After that, services continue as per need and subject to satisfactory performance.
Q10Can an AYUSH Medical Officer apply in other districts?
A
Yes. The AYUSH Medical Officer is a Multi Zonal cadre post with 95% local reservation. A candidate can apply in any district in the concerned Multi Zone, but selection is restricted to one district.
