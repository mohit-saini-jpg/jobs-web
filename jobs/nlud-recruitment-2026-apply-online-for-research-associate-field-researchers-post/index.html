NLUD Research Associate and Field Researchers Eligibility Criteria

Indian or foreign university graduates meeting the academic criteria may apply.
PostQualification Required
Research Associate- Postgraduate Degree in Law (LL.M.) from a recognized Indian or foreign university with a good academic record.- Demonstrated understanding of doctrinal and empirical research methodologies.- Knowledge of substantive and procedural international human rights law.- Proficiency in English.- Skilled in MS Office applications and other research tools.- Prior experience of working in a research project in public international law, international human rights law, criminal law, or fundamental rights is desirable.
Field Researchers- Graduate degree in Law or Social Sciences from a recognised Indian or foreign university, with a good academic record.- Demonstrated understanding of qualitative and empirical research methodologies.- Proficiency in English, including strong written and oral communication skills.- Proficiency in MS Office applications, and other research and documentation tools.- Knowledge of statistics and/or experience with data collection, analysis and documentation is desirable.- Prior experience in field research, stakeholder consultations, or research projects in law, human rights, social sciences or related areas is desirable.

The application must be submitted only through the online form; physical or email applications will not be considered.
