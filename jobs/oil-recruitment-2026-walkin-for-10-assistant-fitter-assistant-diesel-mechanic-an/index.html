About OIL

Oil India Limited (OIL) describes itself as a Maharatna Public Sector Undertaking and the second largest national upstream Oil and Gas company, with pan India presence and a global footprint. This requirement is for immediate engagement at Field Headquarters, Duliajan. The work is purely temporary and also involves working in shifts, in arduous and hazardous jobs at remote or far-flung OIL installations, and on "On-Call" duty basis.

Posts and Eligibility

Eligibility is counted as on the date of registration for the Walk-in for Personal Assessment. Candidates must possess all the qualifications and requirements below. Minimum 02 (Two) years post qualification work experience in operation and maintenance of Reciprocating Compressor / Pump / IC Engine, or operation and maintenance of gas pipeline jobs, or maintenance of mechanical workshop setup, or installation / maintenance of crude oil storage tank, in Government / Semi-Government / PSU / Public Limited / Private Organizations providing service to E&P company. Candidates first register at the venue between 07:00 A.M. and 09:00 A.M.; no candidate will be allowed to register beyond that date and time under any circumstances. OIL Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

A candidate who does not meet the eligibility conditions, or lacks original documents, certificates, testimonials or Licence, will not be allowed to appear in the Walk-in. No Travelling Allowance or Daily Allowance is paid for appearing. Candidates arrange their own travel and accommodation, and no reimbursement is provided for joining. A provisionally selected candidate must join immediately. If the candidate does not join on the stipulated date, an extension of a maximum of 15 days is allowed; failure to join within that timeline cancels the provisional selection. Engagement is subject to pre-engagement formalities: document checking, a medical fitness certificate from a Govt. Registered Medical Practitioner, and a Police verification certificate. A candidate working elsewhere must produce an original Release Letter from the present employer. Contract employees may not take up any other assignment during the engagement, are liable to be placed in any location as deemed fit by the Competent Authority, and must arrange accommodation at their own cost. All applicable taxes are borne by the candidate. The engagement does not confer any right or claim to employment or regularization in Oil India Limited. Unfair means, impersonation, misbehaviour, false or forged documents, suppression of information, or canvassing in any form can lead to disqualification or rejection. Disputes fall under the jurisdiction of the Dibrugarh district court only. OIL reserves the right to cancel or postpone the Contractual Engagement Process at any stage without assigning any reason. The notice also warns against fraudulent offers: OIL does not authorize anyone outside the company to offer a job on its behalf. Candidates should keep checking OIL's website (https://www.oil-india.com > OIL for All > Carrer at OIL > Current Openings/ Results) for updates.

OIL Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1When is the OIL walk-in for the Duliajan contractual posts?
A
The Walk-in for Personal Assessment is on 14 December 2026. Registration for all three posts is on the same day, from 07:00 A.M. to 09:00 A.M.
Q2Where is the OIL walk-in venue?
A
The venue is Duliajan Club, Oil India Limited, Duliajan. Candidates must register at the venue within the stated time.
Q3How many vacancies are there in the OIL contractual recruitment?
A
There are 10 vacancies: 4 Contractual Assistant Fitter, 4 Contractual Assistant Diesel Mechanic and 2 Contractual Mechanical Supervisor posts.
Q4What is the qualification for OIL Assistant Fitter and Assistant Diesel Mechanic?
A
Class 10th from a Government Recognized Education Board and a trade certificate in Fitter (or Diesel Mechanic) trade from a Government Recognized Institute. Minimum 2 years' post qualification experience is also required.
Q5What is the qualification for OIL Mechanical Supervisor?
A
Class 10th from a Government Recognized Education Board and a passed 3-year Diploma in Mechanical Engineering from a Government Recognized Institute, with minimum 2 years' post qualification work experience.
Q6What is the age limit for the OIL posts?
A
The minimum is 18 years. The maximum for Fitter is 35 (General), 38 (OBC NCL) and 40 (SC) years; for Diesel Mechanic 35 (General), 38 (OBC NCL) and 40 (ST) years; for Mechanical Supervisor 45 (General) and 48 (OBC NCL) years.
Q7What is the salary for OIL contractual Fitter and Supervisor?
A
Assistant Fitter and Assistant Diesel Mechanic get a fixed emolument of ₹ 21,450.00 per month plus ₹ 825.00 per working day. Mechanical Supervisor gets ₹ 24,960.00 per month plus ₹ 960.00 per working day.
Q8How are candidates selected in the OIL walk-in?
A
Selection is by Walk-in for Personal Assessment of 100 marks, with 50 as qualifying marks for all categories. Final selection is on merit by marks obtained.
Q9Is the OIL job permanent?
A
No, it is purely contractual. The initial period is 6 months, extendable for three more tenures of 6 months each, up to a maximum of 24 months. It gives no right to regularization.
Q10Who can attend the OIL walk-in?
A
The notice invites personnel with domicile of Assam and Arunachal Pradesh who meet all the qualification, experience and age conditions. Candidates must bring original documents and self-attested copies.
