NIOS Vocational Exam Date Sheet 2026 - Overview : Admit Card Overview

The National Institute of Open Schooling (NIOS) has released the NIOS Vocational Exam Date Sheet 2026 for the October-November 2026 public examinations of Vocational Education courses. According to the official timetable, the theory examinations will be held from 14 to 22 December 2026. Students can download the subject-wise date sheet PDF from the official NIOS vocational examination website, voc.nios.ac.in.

How to Download NIOS Vocational Date Sheet 2026?

Students can follow these steps to download the NIOS vocational examination timetable:

  • Visit the official NIOS website at nios.ac.in.
  • Open the Vocational Education portal at voc.nios.ac.in.
  • Go to the Notifications section.
  • Find the Date Sheet for Public Examinations (Theory) of Vocational Education Courses, October-November 2026.
  • Open the timetable PDF.
  • Find the examination date corresponding to your registered subject code.
  • Check the examination start time and end time.
  • Download and save the official PDF for future reference.

NIOS Vocational Result 2026

The official date sheet states that the results are likely to be declared approximately 8 to 10 weeks after the last examination. The final theory paper is scheduled for 22 December 2026.

NIOS has not announced an exact result declaration date. Once declared, candidates will be able to check the result through the official vocational examination website.

The Marks Statement-cum-Certificate will be made available through the candidate's concerned Accredited Vocational Institution.

NIOS Vocational Exam Date Sheet 2026 Important Instructions

  • Check the correct examination date for each registered vocational subject.
  • Match the subject code in the date sheet with the registered course.
  • Confirm the paper-specific duration and examination end time.
  • Download the hall ticket when it becomes available.
  • Follow the examination centre and reporting instructions printed on the hall ticket.
  • Follow the separate instructions for online examination papers, where applicable.
  • Confirm practical examination arrangements with the concerned AVI.
  • Check the official NIOS website for examination notices and updates.
