OPSC AAE Eligibility 2026

A candidate must hold a Bachelor's Degree in Agriculture Engineering from any University or Institution recognised by any State Government or the Government of India. Candidates must be able to read, write and speak Odia, and must satisfy at least one of the following: Passed Middle School Examination with Odia as a language subject; or Passed Matriculation or an equivalent examination with Odia as the medium of examination in a non-language subject; or Passed Odia as a language subject in the final examination of Class VII from a School or Educational Institution recognised by the Government of Odisha or the Central Government; or Passed a test in Odia at Middle English School Standard, conducted by the School & Mass Education Department, Government of Odisha, or the Board of Secondary Educati

OPSC AAE Selection Process

Selection is through a competitive examination comprising a Written Examination followed by a Personality Test (Interview). Both papers are Objective Type (MCQ) with OMR-based evaluation; each question carries 1 mark. Negative marking: 25% of the marks allotted to a question is deducted for each wrong answer; unattempted questions receive zero marks. The Commission will fix qualifying marks for each paper and/or in aggregate, category-wise. Candidates meeting the qualifying marks are shortlisted for the Personality Test in a ratio of about 1:2 of the advertised vacancies (category-wise), subject to a minimum of 5 candidates per category. Carries 25 marks . The Select List is drawn in order of merit based on aggregate marks (Written Examination + Interview, out of 225) for each category, equal to the number of advertised vacancies. OPSC AAE Syllabus Highlights

The syllabus is at Bachelor's Degree standard. Key topics include: Paper-I, Section A (General Engineering): Computer programming & data structures, workshop practice, applied electronics & instrumentation, common statistical data on agriculture, units & dimensions, surveying & levelling, building construction & cost estimation, greenhouse design. Paper-I, Section B (Farm Machinery & Power): Farm machinery, farm power, tractor systems & controls, farm power/machinery management, human engineering & safety, precision agriculture, renewable energy engineering, systems engineering. Paper-II, Section A (Agricultural Processing & Food Engineering): Engineering properties of food materials, grain processing, drying & storage of crops, horticultural produce processing, thermal processing, food laws (FSSAI, BIS, Codex). Paper-II, Section B (Soil & Water Conservation Engineering): Fluid mechanics
