AP High Court Civil Judge (Junior Division) Recruitment 2026 Vacancy Details

A total of 119 posts are notified, comprising 96 posts under Direct Recruitment and 23 posts under Recruitment by Transfer. Selection against the BC-E vacancies will be subject to the outcome of Civil Appeal Nos. 2628-2637/2010 pending before the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India. Vertical reservations (Backward Classes A to E, EWS, SC and ST) and horizontal reservations (women and Persons with Benchmark Disabilities) are as per the AP State Judicial (Service & Cadre) Rules, 2026, read with Rules 22 and 22-A of the AP State and Subordinate Service Rules, 1996 (G.O.Ms.No.77 dated 02-08-2023).

Eligibility Criteria

Eligibility conditions differ for Direct Recruitment and Recruitment by Transfer. All Law graduates who obtained their Law Degree within the preceding one year as on the date of notification are eligible to appear for the Civil Judge (Junior Division) notification for 2026. This is as per the judgment in Bhumika Trust Vs. Union of India and others (Writ Petition (Civil) No. 1110/2025, dated 21-08-2026) of the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India, and pending amendments to the AP State Judicial (Service and Cadre) Rules, 2026. The candidate must have good character and be free from any infirmity that renders him/her unfit for such appointment. Direct Recruitment means appointment of a person who is not in the service of the Government of India/State. Salary/Stipend

The pay and stipend details given in the notification are as follows: Pay Scale: Rs. 77,840 - 1,36,520 for Civil Judge (Junior Division). Training Period (1 year at the Judicial Academy): Selected candidates are designated as "Trainee Judicial Officers" and are paid a fixed emolument equivalent to one-half of the remuneration payable to a Civil Judge (Junior Division), along with the facilities and benefits ordinarily available to trainees in the Academy. Law Clerkship (1 year): After the training, Trainee Judicial Officers undergo one year of structured Law Clerkship, with the same emoluments as during their training and no further allowance. The first six months are under the supervision of the Principal District/District and Sessions Judges or Members of the Higher Judicial Services, and the remaining six months under an Hon'ble Judge of the High Court of Andhra Pradesh. Age Limit (as on 01-09-2026)

Age is calculated as on 01-09-2026, the first day of the month in which the notification was published. Maximum Age: Applicants should not have completed 35 years of age. AP High Court Civil Judge Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

The selection process for Direct Recruitment consists of the following stages: Screening Test, Written Examination and Viva Voce. 100 multiple choice objective type questions for 100 marks, with a duration of 2 hours. Applicants securing 40% and above marks are short-listed in the ratio of 1:10 of the available vacancies for the Written Examination. Candidates with identical marks are all called for the Written Examination. The Screening Test marks are not added to the Written Examination marks and are not counted for the final order of merit. Securing the minimum qualifying marks does not vest any right to be considered for the Written Examination. Questions must be answered only in English (except the translation test). AP High Court Civil Judge Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Eligible candidates can apply for AP High Court Civil Judge (Junior Division) Recruitment 2026 through online mode only. Follow the steps below:
  • 2Visit the official High Court of Andhra Pradesh website, https://aphc.gov.in, and open the online application portal (available from 01-10-2026 to 26-10-2026).
  • 3Read the notification carefully and select the category (Direct Recruitment or Recruitment by Transfer) and the applicable reservation category.
  • 4Fill in the application with correct details and opt for three Screening Test centres in the order of preference.
  • 5Upload the required documents. For Direct Recruitment: certificate of date of birth/SSC, Law Degree certificate, and community certificate for SC/ST/BC/Persons with Benchmark Disabilities/EWS claims. BC applicants must upload the latest Non-Creamy Layer certificate issued in 2025 or 2026 (otherwise the candidature is considered under Open Competition). EWS applicants must upload the latest EWS certificate issued in 2025 or 2026 by the Tahsildar, showing gross annual family income below Rs. 8,00,000/-. Persons with Benchmark Disabilities must upload the Medical Board certificate, and ex-servicemen the Discharge Certificate.
  • 6For Recruitment by Transfer, also upload proof of declaration of probation, the employer's proceedings permitting the Law Degree course after entering service, and the latest Service and Conduct Certificate. The latest No-objection Certificate from the employer must be submitted at the time of verification of original certificates.
  • 7Pay the application/examination fee online (Rs. 1,500/- or Rs. 750/-, as applicable) and submit the application on or before 26-10-2026 (11:59 PM).
  • 8Download the Hall Ticket from the High Court website one week before the Screening Test.
  • 9Once the application is submitted, candidates cannot edit options or replace documents. Original certificates must be produced on the day mentioned by the High Court for verification, failing which the candidature will be rejected. The High Court will not entertain correspondence, and no information will be sent to unsuccessful candidates. Candidates must regularly visit the High Court website for updates, as no separate information is provided in any other mode. No TA/DA will be paid for the Screening Test, Written Examination or Viva Voce.
