HCL Apprentice Aadhaar and Profile Verification Requirements

The notification requires applicants to maintain accurate and fully Aadhaar-verified profiles on the apprenticeship portal. Details such as name, father's name, mother's name and date of birth must match the Class 10 and ITI records. Applicants must ensure their bank account details and eKYC are updated. Incorrect or mismatched information may result in rejection or problems during apprenticeship contract processing.

Hindustan Copper Apprentice Medical Fitness 2026

Engagement of shortlisted candidates is subject to their being declared medically fit according to Hindustan Copper Limited's requirements.

HCL Apprentice Contract and Training Conditions

After selection, the apprenticeship contract must be generated through the Government portal and accepted by the candidate according to the notified procedure. The notice states that the contract is subject to approval by RDSDE Bhopal . Candidates must regularly check their registered email and apprenticeship portal for contract-related instructions. Candidates who have already completed apprenticeship training under NAPS are not eligible for another apprenticeship contract in a different trade. Candidates with an active contract elsewhere must satisfy the prescribed resignation and acceptance requirements before another contract can be processed.
