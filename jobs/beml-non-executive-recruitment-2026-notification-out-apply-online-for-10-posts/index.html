BEML Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Only Indian Nationals are eligible to apply. Candidates must satisfy the educational qualification and age limit criteria mentioned below as on 8th September 2026. 3-year full-time Polytechnic Engineering Diploma in Electronics & Communication, Electrical & Electronics, Instrumentation, Industrial Electronics, or Mechatronics. BEML Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection for the Non Executive (Tenure Basis) post will be based on the following process: The Computer Based Test will be of 2 hours duration, with 1 mark for each correct answer and no negative marking. The qualifying marks for the written test are fixed at 60%, relaxable by 5% for SC/ST/PwD candidates. Final selection is based purely on merit (marks scored) in the written test. Shortlisted candidates will be called for Document Verification, limited to the number of notified vacancies (discipline/category-wise). Candidates qualifying in Document Verification must undergo a Pre-Employment Medical Examination. A Provisional Offer is issued after Document Verification, followed by a Final Offer of Engagement after a satisfactory Medical Report.
