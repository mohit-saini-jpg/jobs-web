BHU Nursing Officer Recruitment 2026 - 381 Posts

The largest recruitment category is Nursing Officer , with 381 positions across Sir Sunderlal Hospital, Trauma Centre, other medical units and the National Centre of Ageing. Candidates must possess one of the following nursing qualifications: Diploma in General Nursing and Midwifery (GNM) with five years of Nursing Officer experience in the prescribed hospital setting. B.Sc. Nursing or B.Sc. (Hons.) Nursing with at least 55% marks and three years of Nursing Officer experience. Post Basic B.Sc. Nursing with at least 55% marks and three years of Nursing Officer experience. The experience must be in a minimum 100-bedded reputed hospital or any Government/Corporate hospital, as specified in the notification. Candidates must also be registered as a Nurse or Midwife with the State Nursing Council. Maximum Age: 35 years, before applicable relaxation. Pay Level: Level 7, Rs. 44,900 to Rs. 1,42,4

BHU Medical Officer Recruitment 2026 - 29 Posts

The University has advertised 29 Medical Officer positions under three post codes. Candidates must possess MBBS with a minimum of two years of clinical experience in a Government, Corporate or Government-recognized hospital after completing the mandatory internship. Preference may be given to candidates holding MD/MS/DNB in the relevant advertised specialties, including Geriatric Medicine, Respiratory Medicine, General Medicine, General Surgery, Obstetrics and Gynaecology, Anaesthesiology, Critical Care or Emergency Medicine. Maximum Age: 40 years. Pay Level: Level 10, Rs. 56,100 to Rs. 1,77,500. The original notification distinguishes the essential MBBS and experience requirement from the desirable postgraduate qualifications.

BHU Non-Teaching Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

The selection procedure differs between Group A and Group B/C positions. The University may conduct the following stages: Screening of online applications. Written test for shortlisting, if required. Interaction or presentation for further shortlisting, if required. Interview for finally shortlisted candidates. Original document verification. Important: The written examination and interaction/presentation are not mandatory stages for every Group A post. They may be used when considered necessary by the University. Online application submission. Written examination. Skill test for candidates shortlisted from the written examination. Verification of original documents. Final selection according to University recruitment rules.
