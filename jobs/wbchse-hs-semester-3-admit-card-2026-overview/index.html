WBCHSE HS Semester 3 Result 2026 - Overview : Result Overview

The West Bengal Council of Higher Secondary Education (WBCHSE) will announce the WBCHSE HS Semester 3 Result 2026 on 27 November 2026. According to the Council's official notification dated 19 November 2026, the Higher Secondary Semester III Examination 2027 results will be declared through a press conference beginning at 2:30 PM at Vidyasagar Bhavan, Salt Lake, Kolkata.

WBCHSE HS Semester 3 Result 2026 - Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 27/11/2026

Students will be able to check their results and download their statements of marks online from 3:00 PM through the official result website. Students who appeared for the West Bengal Class 12 Semester 3 examinations held between 21 September and 14 October 2026 should keep their examination roll number and registration number ready. As of 26 November 2026, the result is awaited and the official website states that marks will be available after 3:00 PM on 27 November.

How to Check WBCHSE HS Semester 3 Result 2026?

Students can follow these steps to check their West Bengal Class 12 Semester III results once the online facility is activated.

  • Visit the official result portal at result.wb.gov.in.
  • Look for the Higher Secondary Semester III Examination 2027 result link.
  • Open the result checking facility.
  • Enter your examination roll number.
  • Enter your registration number.
  • Complete any additional verification displayed by the website.
  • Submit the details to view your Semester III result.
  • Check the displayed subject-wise marks and candidate details.
  • Download the statement of marks and save a copy.
  • Contact your school if any important information appears incorrect.

The Council requires the examination roll number and registration number to retrieve the result. Students should keep their admit cards ready before accessing the portal.

WBCHSE HS Semester 3 Result 2026 Not Showing?

If students cannot access their result, they should first confirm that the official online release time has passed.

  • Check the result only after 3:00 PM on 27 November 2026.
  • Use the official government result portal.
  • Enter the correct examination roll number.
  • Check the registration number carefully.
  • Complete any verification shown on the result page.
  • Try accessing the official result through another authorised website if the main portal is temporarily unavailable.
  • Contact the school if the details do not match the student's records.

If the website is displaying a result availability message before 3:00 PM, it does not mean that the student's result has been withheld.

WBCHSE Semester 3 Result 2026: What Happens Next?

After the declaration of Semester III results, students will continue with the remaining requirements of the Higher Secondary Examination 2027 cycle.

These include Semester IV and, where applicable, the Semester III supplementary examination. The Council will also administer the prescribed project and practical assessments according to its examination calendar.

The Semester III result is not the same as the final combined Higher Secondary result. Students should follow the Council's subsequent examination and result notices.
