खोया हुआ Voter Card खोजे FAQs

Q1Voter Card duplicate banane ke liye kitna fee lagta hai?
A
Online application ke liye usually Rs 50‑100 ka nominal fee hota hai, jo payment gateway ya BLO office par cash/cheque se pay kiya ja sakta hai.
Q2Agar Aadhaar linked nahi hai, toh kya karna padega?
A
Aapko manual verification ke liye address proof (e.g., electricity bill) aur old Voter Card copy present karni hogi, tabhi duplicate issue hoga.
Q3Duplicate Voter Card milne mein kitna samay lagta hai?
A
Online request ke baad 7‑10 din ke andar card post ho jata hai; BLO office se turant issue karne par same din ya next day mil sakta hai.
