Q1Voter Card replacement ke liye kaunse documents chahiye?
A
Aadhaar card, passport, driving licence ya koi bhi government issued photo ID ke saath address proof (bijli bill, bank statement) ka copy.
Q2Online application ka process kitna time leta hai?
A
Online form submit karne ke baad, verification aur printing ka process usually 15-30 din ke andar complete ho jata hai, lekin peak periods mein thoda delay ho sakta hai.
Q3Agar offline centre jaana ho toh appointment kaise book karein?

National Voter's Service Portal (NVSP) par login karke nearest Electoral Office select karein, preferred date aur time slot choose karein, aur confirmation receipt save karein.
