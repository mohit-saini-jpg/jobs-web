About ERNET India

ERNET India is described in its circular as an autonomous scientific society under the Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology, Govt. of India. Its office is at Block-1, A-Wing, 5th Floor, DMRC IT Park, Shastri Park, Delhi-110053. The circular is signed by the Registrar & Director (P&A).

Who Can Apply

Officers who meet one of the two service conditions below, and whose services can be spared immediately upon selection, may apply. A person working on analogous posts in Govt/ PSUs/ Autonomous bodies on regular basis; OR A person working in Pay Matrix Level-12 (as per 7th CPC) and having 5 years regular service in the grade and equivalent field. Essential qualification: Graduate. PG Diploma in Personnel/Finance and experience in the field of Personnel/Administration. The maximum age limit for appointment on deputation is 56 years as on the closing date for receipt of applications. The notice does not print a minimum age. For the discipline-specific eligibility of the post, candidates should refer to the official notification.

Pay and Terms

The post is in Pay Matrix Level-13 (as per the 7th CPC) on deputation basis for a period of three years. The period of deputation, including any deputation held against another ex-cadre post immediately preceding this appointment in the same or any other organisation, shall ordinarily not exceed five years, subject to the applicable Recruitment Rules and Government instructions. The pay and other terms and conditions of deputation are governed by the applicable instructions issued by the Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT), including O.M. No. 6/8/2009-Estt. (Pay-II) dated 17.06.2010, as amended from time to time, wherever applicable.
