KNRUHS Telangana NEET UG 2026 Merit List - Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 25/08/2026

The Kaloji Narayana Rao University of Health Sciences (KNRUHS), Telangana, has released the provisional merit list for MBBS and BDS admissions under the Competent Authority Quota for the 2026-27 academic year on August 24, 2026. The list names candidates who completed online registration and document verification and are provisionally eligible for the next stage of counselling. A separate list of ineligible candidates has also been uploaded on the official KNRUHS website. Candidates who spot an error in their NEET AIR, state rank, marks, domicile status, or category - or who have been marked ineligible - can raise a grievance before the deadline.

Important Dates

  • Provisional merit list released: On or before August 24- 25, 2026 (as per the official notification uploaded on the KNRUHS website)
  • Last date to raise a grievance: August 26, 2026, before 12:00 PM (Noon)
  • Final merit list and seat matrix: To be released after grievances are reviewed. An exact date has not been specified in the official notification.
  • Phase-I web option filling: Will follow the release of the final merit list and seat matrix. A specific schedule has not been announced yet in the official notification.

Who Should Check the KNRUHS Merit List

This list is relevant to NEET UG 2026 candidates from Telangana who applied for MBBS/ BDS admission under the Competent Authority Quota and had completed their online registration and document verification with KNRUHS. Candidates should check both the eligible candidates' list and the ineligible candidates' list to confirm their status.

What to Verify in the Merit List

The official notification advises candidates to carefully check the following details against their own records:

  • NEET UG All India Rank (AIR)
  • State rank (as allotted by KNRUHS)
  • NEET UG marks/ score
  • Local area/ domicile status
  • Category (reservation category as applicable)

How to Download the KNRUHS NEET UG 2026 Provisional Merit List

  • Open the official KNRUHS provisional merit list PDF link shared by the university on its website (Direct PDF Link).
  • Once the PDF opens, use the Ctrl+F (Find) function on your keyboard.
  • Type your name, roll number, or application number to quickly locate your entry.
  • Cross-check your NEET AIR, state rank, marks, category, and domicile status shown against the merit list.
  • If your name appears in the separate ineligible candidates list instead, note the reason mentioned (where provided) before deciding on a grievance.

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Check both the eligible and ineligible candidates' lists carefully - a name can appear on only one of the two.
  • Keep scanned, attested copies of NEET mark sheet, SSC/ Intermediate bonafide certificates, study certificates, and caste/ domicile certificate ready in advance.
  • Send grievance emails well before the deadline to avoid last-minute technical issues; late submissions will not be considered.
  • Continue to track the official KNRUHS website for the final merit list, seat matrix, and Phase-I web option entry schedule.
