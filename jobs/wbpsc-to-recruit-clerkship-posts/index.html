WBPSC Clerkship Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must have passed the Madhyamik Examination conducted by the West Bengal Board of Secondary Education or an equivalent examination. Applicants must possess elementary knowledge of computer operation. Candidates must have the ability to type on a computer at a speed of 20 words per minute in English or 10 words per minute in Bengali . Candidates must select their English or Bengali typing option while submitting the application. The computer operation and typing ability will be assessed only after candidates qualify in both Part-I and Part-II of the examination. An applicant must be an Indian citizen, either by birth or registration, or another national declared eligible by the Government of India, subject to the required eligibility certificate. Candidates must be able to read, write and speak Bengali. This condition does not apply to candidates whose mother tongue is Nepali. F

WBPSC Clerkship Salary 2026

Candidates appointed through the WBPSC Clerkship Examination will receive pay under Level 6, ranging from Rs. 22,700 to Rs. 58,500 , along with admissible Dearness Allowance (DA), Medical Allowance (MA) and House Rent Allowance (HRA) in accordance with ROPA, 2019. WBPSC Clerkship Selection Process 2026

The WBPSC Clerkship selection process includes two successive examinations followed by a test of elementary computer operation and typing ability. Part-I Examination (Objective Type): The first stage of the recruitment examination. Candidates obtaining the qualifying marks, subject to shortlisting by the Commission, will become eligible for Part-II. Part-II Examination (Conventional Written Type): Shortlisted candidates will appear in the written examination on a later date announced by WBPSC. Computer Operation and Typing Test: Candidates qualifying in Part-I and Part-II will be assessed for computer knowledge and the required typing speed. Final Merit List: The merit list will be prepared on the basis of marks secured in Part-I and Part-II, provided candidates have qualified in the computer operation and typing assessment. WBPSC Clerkship Application Edit Window 2026

WBPSC has scheduled the application edit window from 28 October to 1 November 2026 . Applicants can log in to their individual accounts and edit certain fields, where permitted by the Commission. WBPSC Clerkship Job Location and Posting Preferences

Recruitment will be made to eligible posts in the Government of West Bengal's Secretariat Departments, Directorates, District offices and regional offices, including Kolkata.
