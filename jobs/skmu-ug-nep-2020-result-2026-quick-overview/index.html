Streams and Sessions Covered in the Result

The PDF is divided by session, stream and result status. Two session groups appear in the document:

  • 22-26, 23-27, 24-28: a combined group for the earlier admission batches.
  • 2025-2029: the batch admitted in 2025.

The four streams printed in the headings are:

  • Humanities and Social Sciences
  • Natural and Physical Sciences
  • Mathematics, Statistics and Computer Applications
  • Commerce and Management

Result Categories: PASS, PROMOTED and U.F.M.

Each list in the PDF carries one of three headings. The document does not define them in detail, so use the notes printed on the result pages (explained in the next section) as your guide.

StatusWhat the PDF says
PASSListed as a result category. A separate definition is not given in the PDF.
PROMOTEDListed as a result category. For the 2025-2029 session, the PDF notes that a student needs a minimum of 4 CGPA to get promoted from Sem II to Sem III.
U.F.M.Listed as a result category with four numbers. The full form is not given in the PDF. The abbreviation is commonly used for unfair means cases, but the document does not confirm this.

Passing and Promotion Rules Printed in the PDF

The notes at the top of each page differ by session group.

For sessions 22-26, 23-27 and 24-28

  • The result is prepared on the basis of performance in both Semester I and Semester II, in accordance with NEP (2020) regulation.
  • Examinees who have not passed at least 9 out of 12 papers in Semester I and Semester II are declared as fail.

For session 2025-2029

  • The result is prepared on the basis of NEP (2020) regulation.
  • To get promoted from Sem II to Sem III, a student has to procure a minimum of 4 CGPA.

The PDF does not give a separate list of failed candidates, a minimum-marks rule per paper or a pass percentage.

How to Check SKMU UG Sem 2 Result 2026

  • Open the official SKMU website at skmu.ac.in and go to the results section, or use the university's examination portal.
  • Look for the notice titled Provisional Result of All Colleges UG SEM-II (NEP-2020) EXAM-2026 and open the PDF.
  • Open the section for your stream. Each page shows the session, stream and result status at the top.
  • Press Ctrl + F (or use the search option in your PDF app) and type your 12-digit number.
  • Read the status printed above your number: PASS, PROMOTED or U.F.M.
  • Download the PDF or take a screenshot of the relevant page for your records.

What to Do After the Result

  • If your number is under PASS or PROMOTED: follow your college's notice for the next semester. Admission or registration steps for Sem III are not mentioned in the PDF.
  • If your number is not found: check that you searched the right stream and session, and that you typed all 12 digits correctly. If it is still missing, contact your college or the university's examination department. The PDF does not explain why a number may be absent.
  • If your number is under U.F.M.: contact the university's examination department for the correct procedure. The PDF does not describe any process.
  • If you want marks or CGPA: the PDF does not show them. Check with your college or the university for the marksheet.
