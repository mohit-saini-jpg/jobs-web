JMI Admission 2026-27 Selection Process

  • Merit-based selection: Applicable to most UG and PG programmes (BAG, BBA, BCOM, MA courses, M.Com, PG Diploma, Diploma, Certificate, and Advanced Diploma programmes), based on the qualifying examination.
  • Entrance test: Applicable to B.Ed. (ODL) and MBA (ODL). The entrance test is a 2-hour, 100-question multiple-choice exam held only in Delhi, with one mark per correct answer and no negative marking.
  • Qualifying marks + interview: Applicable to MBA (OL) and B.Sc. (Biosciences) - ODL. Selection is based on 70 marks weightage from qualifying exam marks and 30 marks from a personal interview.

B.Ed. Entrance Test Syllabus

  • General English Comprehension - 10 marks
  • Logical Analytical Reasoning - 20 marks
  • Educational & General Awareness - 25 marks
  • Teaching-Learning at School - 25 marks
  • Any one teaching subject (Maths/Science/Social Science/English/Hindi/Urdu) - 20 marks

Total intake for B.Ed. is 500 seats (50 at each of the 10 Learner Support Centres).

MBA Entrance Test Syllabus

  • English Comprehension (Verbal Ability, Critical Reading, Reading Comprehension) - 20 marks
  • Quantitative Aptitude & Numerical Ability - 20 marks
  • Data Analysis & Sufficiency - 20 marks
  • Reasoning & General Intelligence - 20 marks
  • Current Affairs & Economic and Business Environment - 20 marks

Total intake for MBA (ODL) is 300 seats (50 each at IIMT Rohtak, Haryana, and Jhankar College of Education, Gurugram, Haryana).

How to Apply for JMI Admission 2026-27

  • Create an Academic Bank of Credit ID (ABC ID) and a UGC DEB ID before starting the application, as both are required during registration.
  • Visit the official CDOE admission portal at jmicoe.in.
  • Register using a valid, active email ID (JMI may use this email for future communication).
  • Complete the registration form and verify the email link sent to the registered email address to receive the login credentials.
  • Log in with the email address and password, and select the desired programme(s).
  • Fill in the application form and upload a color passport-size photograph (white background, maximum 100KB, JPG/JPEG) and a scanned signature (maximum 100KB, JPG/JPEG).
  • Use the preview option to verify all entries before final submission. Corrections are allowed for all fields except "Name" and "Date of Birth" before confirming submission.
  • Pay the applicable application fee online through Credit Card, Debit Card, or Net Banking.
  • Submit the form and take a printout of the confirmation for future reference.
  • Check application status anytime by logging in at jmicoe.in.

Important Instructions for JMI Admission 2026-27

  • No application form will be accepted after the last date of submission, i.e., 10 August 2026.
  • There is no provision for a printed/offline application form; applications must be submitted online only.
  • Candidates submitting more than one application form for the same programme risk rejection.
  • Candidates whose qualifying examination results have not yet been declared may still apply but must submit the required certificates within the given time period, or their admission will be cancelled.
  • Filling out the online form and receiving an admit card does not guarantee admission; document verification and fee payment are mandatory to complete the process.
  • Submission of forged or fake documents will lead to legal/criminal proceedings.
  • Candidates are advised to regularly check the official CDOE website for admission-related notices, as the university reserves the right to modify prospectus provisions through addendum/corrigendum.
  • Foreign nationals cannot enroll in ODL programmes; only learners residing in India may enroll in ODL mode, as per UGC regulations. Foreign/NRI students and diplomats should refer to the separate fee structure and application process detailed in the official prospectus.
