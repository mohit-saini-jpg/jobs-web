NHM MP Staff Nurse Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The National Health Mission (NHM), Madhya Pradesh, has released the NHM MP Staff Nurse Recruitment 2026 notification for 301 contractual Staff Nurse vacancies under the Public Health and Family Welfare Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh. Eligible candidates can apply online through the MPOnline portal at iforms.mponline.gov.in. The application process started on 20 November 2026, and the last date to submit the online application is 5 December 2026. Selected candidates will receive a monthly contractual salary of ₹28,700.

NHM MP Staff Nurse Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must meet the educational qualification, nursing registration, domicile and age requirements prescribed in the official selection rulebook. Candidates must satisfy all the following requirements: Passed Class 12 under the 10+2 education system with Physics, Chemistry and Biology as compulsory subjects. Completed a four-year B.Sc. Nursing programme from a recognized nursing institution, or completed General Nursing and Midwifery (GNM) followed by a two-year Post Basic B.Sc. Nursing programme. Possess valid and active registration with the Madhya Pradesh Nurses Registration Council. Important: GNM qualification alone is not listed as sufficient for this recruitment. Candidates applying through the GNM route must also have completed the required two-year Post Basic B.Sc. Nursing programme. NHM MP Staff Nurse Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

The selection of candidates for NHM MP Staff Nurse Recruitment 2026 will be based on a computer-based online written test conducted through MPOnline, followed by the prescribed verification process. The recruitment examination will consist of multiple-choice questions based on the syllabus approved by the department. The rulebook specifies different minimum qualifying percentages for various categories. The minimum qualifying marks are not necessarily the final selection cut-off. Selection will depend on merit ranking, applicable reservation provisions, vacancies and other official conditions. NHM MP also reserves the right to reduce minimum qualifying marks if the required number of eligible candidates is not available.
