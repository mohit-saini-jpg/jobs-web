India Optel Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Project Technician: Screening and shortlisting for a Trade Test (Practical) based on percentage of marks in the final NTC/NAC examination (candidates called at twice the number of positions); the Trade Test is qualifying in nature, and final merit is based solely on NTC/NAC marks. Welfare Officer: Screening of applications, shortlisting for interview (twice the number of positions) based on percentage of marks in the Bachelor's Degree, followed by a personal interview; final merit is based on interview performance. Junior Project Engineer: Screening, shortlisting for interview (twice the number of vacancies) based on percentage of marks in Diploma/B.Sc., followed by a personal interview; final merit is a weighted score of 75% Diploma/B.Sc. marks and 25% interview performance.   • 2Download the application format given at Annexure-A to the advertisement from the IOL website (https://indiaoptel.in), under the 'Career' section.
  • 3Fill the application form in BLOCK LETTERS only, entering all required details including qualification, experience, and category.

  • 7Send the completed application by Speed Post only to: The Chairman & Managing Director, India Optel Limited, OFILDD Campus, Raipur, Dehradun (Uttarakhand) – 248008.

  • 9Candidates applying for more than one post must submit a separate application for each post.
