EIL Associate Engineer Selection Process 2026

There is no written test. Selection is through screening followed by a Personal Interview. On the day of the interview, applications and certificates are first screened to confirm eligibility for the post applied. Only candidates meeting the eligibility requirements in all respects proceed to the Personal Interview. Depending on the number of candidates, interviews may run until 8:00 PM on the same day, and may continue the next day if required – candidates must make their own stay arrangements in such cases. Furnishing wrong or false information is a ground for disqualification; EIL will not be responsible for any resulting disqualification. The selected candidate will be allowed to join only if found medically fit.
