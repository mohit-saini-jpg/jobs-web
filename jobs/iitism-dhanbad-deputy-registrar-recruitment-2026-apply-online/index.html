About IIT (ISM) Dhanbad

The Indian Institute of Technology (Indian School of Mines), Dhanbad, was formerly called the Indian School of Mines and was established in 1926. It is located in Dhanbad – 826 004, Jharkhand. It has 17 full-fledged departments that offer undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral courses in Engineering, Pure and Applied Sciences, Management, and Humanities and Social Sciences.

IIT(ISM) Dhanbad Deputy Registrar Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Preliminary shortlisting based on the essential qualifications and experience. If a large number of applications is received, the Institute may decide its own method of shortlisting. Presentation and Interview before the Selection Committee. The final selection depends on performance at this stage. The Institute may hold it online. A Written Test and a Computer or other Test may be held, if required, under the Recruitment Rules of IIT (ISM) Dhanbad. Shortlisted candidates who attend the Presentation and Interview are paid to-and-fro rail fare. This covers the shortest route from their normal place of residence in India to Dhanbad, limited to AC-3 tier, and is paid on submitting a claim with the tickets. Meeting the qualifications does not by itself guarantee a call for the selection process.

Service Conditions

Deputation period: up to 22.11.2028 at most, or until the regular incumbent returns from deputation, or until further orders, whichever is earlier. If the lien holder rejoins IIT (ISM) Dhanbad, the selected candidate goes back to the parent office or previous post. Other deputation terms follow Institute rules. Posting: the Institute may assign or transfer the selected candidate to any section or department within the Institute. Disclosure: candidates must give full details of past employment, including any disciplinary proceedings, charge sheets or vigilance matters. Hiding such information, whether found before or after appointment, can lead to action under the rules.
