Goa Samagra Shiksha Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Address the application to the State Project Director, Goa Samagra Shiksha, Secondary Education, Directorate of Education, Porvorim.
  • 2Submit the application with the necessary documents on the day of the interview.
  • 3Submit a separate application for each sector if applying for more than one sector.
  • 4Report at 9.45 am at the Directorate of Education, Porvorim Goa with original documents.
  • 5Submit one set of copies of the certificates to the interview panel.
  • 6Attend on 12 November 2026.
