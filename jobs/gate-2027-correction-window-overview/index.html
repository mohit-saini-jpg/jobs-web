GATE 2027 Correction Window - Overview : Job Overview

The Indian Institute of Technology Madras (IIT Madras) has opened the application correction facility for the Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering (GATE) 2027. Candidates who have already submitted their application forms can make permitted changes through the GATE Online Application Processing System (GOAPS). The correction window opened on 10 November 2026 and will close on 14 November 2026.

GATE 2027 Correction Window - Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 11/11/2026

The GATE 2027 application data correction facility allows registered candidates to modify specified details, including their name, date of birth, gender, category, examination city choices and existing test paper, by paying the applicable fee. According to the official IIT Madras fee schedule, most permitted changes cost ₹500 per paper, while certain changes affecting fee-concession eligibility cost ₹1,500 per paper.

IIT Madras has also provided a separate free application defect rectification facility until 30 November 2026. This facility is intended for candidates whose applications have been flagged during official scrutiny. The two correction processes have different purposes and deadlines. Candidates should complete the process applicable to their application status through the official GOAPS portal.

How to Edit GATE 2027 Application Form?

Candidates can make permitted application changes during the active correction window using their registered GOAPS account.

  • Visit the official GATE 2027 website at gate2027.iitm.ac.in.
  • Click the GOAPS Login option.
  • Enter the registered login credentials requested by the portal.
  • Open the submitted GATE 2027 application.
  • Choose the available application data correction or modification facility.
  • Select the particular field that requires correction.
  • Enter the correct information according to the official instructions.
  • Upload supporting documents if required for the selected modification.
  • Review the details and the applicable correction charges.
  • Complete the prescribed online payment.
  • Submit the correction request through GOAPS.
  • Save the available payment and submission confirmations.
  • Monitor the application status for verification and approval by the concerned zonal institute.

How to Rectify GATE 2027 Application Scrutiny Defects?

Candidates who receive defect notifications should correct the specified problems through their official GOAPS accounts.

  • Open the GATE 2027 GOAPS Portal.
  • Log in using the registered credentials.
  • Check the application status shown on the dashboard.
  • Read the defect notification received through email or GOAPS.
  • Identify the incorrect or missing information.
  • Follow the correction instructions displayed by the portal.
  • Upload the required corrected document if requested.
  • Submit the defect rectification.
  • Monitor the application status for further scrutiny updates.

GATE 2027 DigiLocker Instructions

IIT Madras has issued important instructions concerning DigiLocker accounts used during GATE 2027 registration.

Candidates should not delete the verified or unverified DigiLocker account used to submit their application and pay the examination fee.

The institute states that future communications will be associated with the DigiLocker account used during application submission.

Applicants with unverified DigiLocker accounts may need to complete the requested consent or photo-identification procedures if notified during scrutiny.

GATE 2027 Application Correction Payment

Applicants requesting a paid data modification must complete the payment indicated by GOAPS.

The applicable amount depends on the modification category and the number of examination papers.

After payment, candidates should preserve the transaction confirmation and monitor their application status.

Important: A successful fee payment does not by itself confirm that the requested change has been approved. IIT Madras requires verification and approval by the respective zonal authorities.

What If GATE 2027 Correction Is Not Showing?

Candidates who cannot access a correction option should first verify the applicable deadline and their application status.

  • Confirm that the candidate-requested correction window is still open.
  • Use the official GOAPS login.
  • Check whether the submitted application is accessible.
  • Identify whether the issue concerns a paid modification or scrutiny defect.
  • Review the fields available for editing.
  • Check for email notifications from the zonal institute.
  • Ensure the relevant DigiLocker account remains accessible.
  • Contact the official GATE helpdesk if the issue cannot be resolved.
