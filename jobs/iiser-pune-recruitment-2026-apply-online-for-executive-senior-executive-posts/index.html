About IISER Pune

The notice describes IISER Pune as a premier institute dedicated to research and teaching in the basic sciences. It says the Institute was established in 2006 by the Ministry of Human Resource Development (renamed Ministry of Education in August 2020) and was declared an Institute of National Importance by an Act of Parliament in 2012.

Vacancy Details

The notice announces one post, 01 (One), named Executive / Senior Executive – Industry Relations. It also says the number of posts may vary depending on requirements during the selection process.

Who Can Apply

Only Indian nationals may apply. The educational qualification must have been obtained from recognized Universities / Institutions. Candidates should have specific experience in Industry–Academia Collaboration, with demonstrated expertise in designing, planning, organizing and coordinating workshops, networking events and stakeholder engagement activities involving researchers, industry professionals, entrepreneurs and academic institutions, to promote and foster effective industry–academia partnerships and collaborations. The age limit is not more than 45 years as on the last date of application. Age relaxation commensurate with educational qualification/s and/or relevant experience/s may be considered for candidates having qualification and experience higher than advertised, with the prior approval of the competent authority. Pay and Tenure

Consolidated emoluments are Rs. 55,000/- to Rs. 75,000/- per month for Executive and Rs. 75,000/- to Rs. 1,00,000/- per month for Senior Executive, commensurate with qualifications and relevant work experience. The appointment is initially for six months and is extendable for a further period, subject to continuation of the project and satisfactory performance of the incumbent. The appointment will be governed by the terms and conditions of the Funding Agency.

IISER Pune Recruitment 2026 Job Description

The incumbent will proactively support the Institute's efforts towards identifying, building and nurturing long-term relationships with industry / corporates, foundations and industry associations. The person will work under the supervision of Sr. PTO (Endowment & Outreach) and Associate Dean (Industry), O/o Dean (International Relations and Outreach). The broad responsibilities include: Partner Identification & Research: research to identify and map potential industry partners, CSR foundations and funding agencies aligned with IISER Pune's research, outreach and institutional priorities. Due Diligence & Profiling: analyse company CSR priorities, funding patterns and strategic interests, and prepare summary reports to support the reporting officer. Proposal Support: assist in preparing concept notes, proposals, presentations and background materials for donor meetings and industry engage

Selection and Interview

The notice mentions a Screening Committee and an interview. The prescribed qualifications and experience are the bare minimum, and meeting them does not entitle a candidate to be called for interview. If many applications are received, the Screening Committee may restrict the number of candidates called for interview to a reasonable limit. It will consider qualifications and experience over and above the minimum prescribed. The interview date, time and other details are not specified in the notice and will be put up on the Institute website.
