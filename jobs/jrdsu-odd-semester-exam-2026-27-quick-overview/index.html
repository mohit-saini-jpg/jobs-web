JRDSU Odd Semester Exam 2026-27 - Quick Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 13/10/2026

Jagadguru Rambhadracharya Divyang State University (JRDSU), Chitrakoot, has issued an exam notice dated 08 October 2026 for the odd semester of session 2026-27. The online application for the main exam, back paper and grade improvement exam runs from 16 October 2026 to 31 October 2026, and the exam is proposed to start from 24 November 2026.

How to Apply for the JRDSU Exam Form 2026-27

The notice gives the following process. Steps 1 to 4 and step 5 are stated explicitly for supplementary / grade improvement applicants; the notice does not separately describe the printout, fee and submission steps for regular main exam or back paper applicants.

  • Go to the official application portal: https://jrdsu.org/login.
  • Fill the exam application form online between 16 October 2026 and 31 October 2026.
  • Take a printout of the application form.
  • Deposit the fee at the Accounts Department (Lekha Vibhag).
  • Get the application forwarded by your concerned department, then submit it to the Examination Department.
  • Along with the supplementary / grade improvement application, submit the original marksheet of the concerned semester to the Examination Department.
  • The Dean / Head of Department / Department In-charge examines the application, makes any required corrections or verification, checks the question paper (or question paper code) and the semester exam fee receipt, and forwards the application on the ERP Portal by 02 November 2026.
  • Admit cards for the odd semester exam are issued from 03 November 2026.

Important Instructions

  • Fill the application only within the window of 16 to 31 October 2026.
  • Applications are checked by the Dean / Head of Department / Department In-charge, so make sure the form details and the fee receipt are correct before submission.
  • Supplementary / grade improvement applicants must submit the original marksheet of the relevant semester.
  • Departments must forward verified applications on the ERP Portal by 02 November 2026.
  • The exam date is proposed and may be updated by the university.
