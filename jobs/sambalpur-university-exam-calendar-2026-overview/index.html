Sambalpur University Exam Calendar 2026 - Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 29/08/2026

Sambalpur University, Jyoti Vihar, Burla, has issued Notification No. 04480/EG-III dated 28.08.2026, announcing the dates of commencement of different examinations for the Academic Session 2026-27. The notification covers form fill-up windows and examination commencement dates for a wide range of programmes, including +3 (BA/BSc/BCom), Bachelor of Law (LLB), Bachelor of Education (B.Ed), Postgraduate/M.Tech courses, MBA, Integrated B.Ed-M.Ed, Executive MBA, BCA, BBA, BBALLB, MBBS, MD/MS, BAMS (New Course), BSc Nursing (Traditional and Semester), BHMS (old and new course), Post Basic Nursing, MSc Nursing, BPT, MBA (Financial Management), BEMT, BMLT, MD (Ayurveda), BPEd, MEd, BALLB, B.Lib & Information Science, PGDIR&PM, and BJMC. The order was issued by the Controller of Examinations "By Order of the Vice-Chancellor."

Important Instructions

  • Details of commencement of examinations not covered in this list will be notified later on.
