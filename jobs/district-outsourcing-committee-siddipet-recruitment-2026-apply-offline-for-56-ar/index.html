About Siddipet Model Schools

The posts are in the Telangana Model Schools of Siddipet District, Telangana. The recruitment is run by the District Outsourcing Committee, Siddipet, and the notification is signed by the District Employment Officer, IDOC - Siddipet, the District Education Officer, IDOC - Siddipet, and for the District Collector, Siddipet. The pay is a gross remuneration paid through an outsourcing agency.

5 years' experience in State Government or Central Government institutions or programmes on contract or outsourcing basis, or in private institutions, will be given priority. Siddipet Model Schools Age Limit 2026

For all four posts, the minimum age is 21 years and the maximum age is 44 years, reckoned as on 01.09.2026, as per A.P. State and Subordinate Services Rule 12 (1) (v) adopted by Telangana State and G.O. Ms. No. 30 GAD (Ser) Dept., Dt. 08/02/2024. The notification also states that candidates born between 01.09.1980 and 31.08.2008 are only eligible to apply. The notification adds a note that the Government of Telangana issued an adhoc rule raising the upper age limit by 10 years and an additional 2 years, i.e. from 34 to 46, for a period of 2 years from the date of issue of the Government Order, in addition to G.O. Ms.No. 42, GAD (Ser.A) Dept., Dt.19/03/2022.

Siddipet Model Schools Selection Process 2026

Selection is made on merit marks, with total marks counted out of 50, followed by original certificate verification and the merit lists. Merit marks calculated from academic records and experience (see the table below). Publication of registered candidates list and scrutiny of applications. Publication of provisional list. Original certificate verification on 04/12/2026 and 05/12/2026 at DEO office, IDOC, Siddipet. Provisional merit list after certificate verification, then the final merit list. For B.Tech / B.E / MCA / BCA students, there is no specialization merit for the Computer Teacher post. Only experience in the same position counts; for example, for the Librarian post only Librarian experience will be considered. For the Art Teacher post, MFA/BFA candidates will be given priority. Roster point will be followed for each category of post.

Siddipet Model Schools Recruitment 2026 Important Instructions

The appointment is purely temporary, up to 23.04.2027, or till the vacancies are filled by promotion or regular appointment, or till the actual need ceases, whichever is earlier. The outsourcing placement does not confer any right for future Government appointment. The engaged employee will be under the administrative control of the District Education Officer on whole time basis. The selected candidate will be eligible for 15 days of leave in a calendar year on cumulative basis as per existing norms. If a selected candidate does not join within the stipulated time, the next eligible merit candidate will be selected. Filled applications with Xerox copies of documents are also to be submitted on the day of certificate verification, and candidates should carry all original certificates.
