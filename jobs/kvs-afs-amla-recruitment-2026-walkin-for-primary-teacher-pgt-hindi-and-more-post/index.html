KVS Recruitment 2026 Job Description

The duties and responsibilities assigned to contractual teachers and instructors in the Vidyalaya include: Regular classroom teaching activity and student notebook or assignment checking. Invigilation duty and examination evaluation work as scheduled by the Vidyalaya. Assisting students and colleagues in the preparation and organization of various curricular and co-curricular activities. Taking care of the functioning, daily management, monitoring, maintenance of laboratory setups (including Atal Tinkering Labs/ATL), robotics activities, and assisting in hackathons for vocational instructors. All other administrative or academic work assigned by the Principal.

What to Bring

Candidates attending the walk-in interview must report in person with the following documents: A printed copy of the fully completed online application form. Original educational qualification certificates and mark sheets (Class 10, Class 12, Graduation, Professional degrees, and CTET mark sheets/certificates). Original experience certificates from recognized institutions, if applicable (experience entered must not be less than 6 months or more than 3 years). One complete set of self-attested photocopies of all original mark sheets, certificates, and testimonials. Recent passport-size photographs. Original valid photo identity proof containing residential address (such as Voter ID Card, Aadhaar Card, or other valid government photo ID).

What Happens on the Day

The selection process on 27 October 2026 follows these stages: Candidates must report at PM Shri Kendriya Vidyalaya AFS Amla at 09:00 AM for physical attendance and initial verification of the application printout and original identity documents. Document verification will be conducted to confirm the basic eligibility criteria for the respective post. If more than 10 eligible candidates report for any single post, the Vidyalaya may organize a written screening test to shortlist candidates. Eligible or shortlisted candidates will then appear before the interview panel. The interview process may continue until late in the evening. No travel allowance or daily allowance (TA/DA) will be paid for attending the test or interview.
