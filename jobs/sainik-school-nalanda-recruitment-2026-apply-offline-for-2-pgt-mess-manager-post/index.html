Sainik School Nalanda Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Sainik School Nalanda, a residential public school run under the Sainik Schools Society, Ministry of Defence, and affiliated to CBSE, New Delhi, has invited applications for two contractual posts: one PGT (Physics) and one Mess Manager, both for the UR category. Applications on the prescribed format must reach the Principal at Vill- Nanand, PO- Pawapuri, Dist- Nalanda, Bihar, Pin Code- 803115, on or before 7 November 2026. The notice does not print a time for the deadline.

About Sainik School Nalanda

Sainik School Nalanda is described in the notice as a residential public school run under the aegis of the Sainik Schools Society, Ministry of Defence, Government of India, and affiliated to CBSE, New Delhi. The school is at Vill- Nanand, PO- Pawapuri, Dist- Nalanda, Bihar. The notice is signed by the Principal and dated 4 October 2026.

Where and How to Send

The application must be sent to: The Principal, Sainik School Nalanda, Vill- Nanand, PO- Pawapuri, Dist- Nalanda, State- Bihar, Pin Code- 803115. The notice allows Ordinary, Registered or Speed Post, courier or delivery by hand. The application must reach the school on or before 7 November 2026. The school says it is not responsible for postal delay or loss of documents en route, and applications reaching after the due date will not be accepted. A scanned advance copy of the application and supporting documents may also be forwarded to [email protected] . The notice does not give a subject line for this email. The prescribed application format is available on the school website, www.sainikschoolnalanda.edu.in . A passport size photograph goes with it. For further details, call 06112-291507 or 8340218798 (0830h-1400h and 1700h-1900h) on all working days.

Posts, Eligibility and Pay

Essential: Master's degree in Physics from a recognized university with at least 50% marks; B Ed Degree; CTET qualified. Desirable: preference will be given to candidates with higher qualifications, teaching/working experience in English medium Public Schools, especially Sainik Schools, proficiency in spoken English, ICT applications, and games as well as co-curricular activities. Age: 21 to 50 Years, counted on the last date of receipt of application. Essential: Matriculation or equivalent; at least five years' experience of running a catering organization independently in the civil, in the Defence Services or in any other similar organization; ability to maintain mess accounts. Desirable: Diploma/Degree in catering from a recognized institution; JCO or equivalent from catering trade or with more than 5 years catering experience.
