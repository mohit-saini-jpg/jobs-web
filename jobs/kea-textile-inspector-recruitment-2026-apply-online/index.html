About KEA Textile Inspector

The Karnataka Examinations Authority, a body of the Government of Karnataka, conducts this recruitment for the Handloom and Textiles Department. The posts belong to the Group-C cadre of the Kalyana-Karnataka region, and the appointing authority fills them on the basis of the competitive examination held by KEA. The notice states that post-wise and category-wise vacancy details given by the Handloom and Textiles Department will be published separately, that they are tentative, and that they may be revised according to Government orders issued from time to time.

Who Can Apply

The qualification and certificate must be held on the last date fixed for applications. Candidates who have taken the examination and are awaiting results are not eligible. No qualification other than the one prescribed will be considered. Original marks cards and certificates must be produced at document verification; candidature is cancelled if they are not produced. If a candidate found ineligible takes part in the process, the candidature will be cancelled at any stage. Candidates should check the official notification for the full eligibility conditions of the post. Under the Karnataka Civil Services (General Recruitment) Rules, 1977, candidates must have completed 18 years on the last date fixed for submission of applications. Pay and Tenure

The Textile Inspector post carries the pay scale of Rs. 54175-1375-58300-1500-64300-1650-74200-1900-85600-2300-99400. Candidates selected by direct recruitment will be on probation for two years. The National Pension System (NPS) applies to the post in the Handloom and Textiles Department.

Selection and Interview

Selection is through a competitive examination in Offline-OMR mode, with a compulsory Kannada Language Test for candidates who are not exempted. Paper-1 covers current events, everyday science, broad features of the Indian Constitution, history and geography of India with special reference to Karnataka, State and regional administration, development of Karnataka's economy including rural development, Panchayat Raj and rural cooperative institutions, and environmental issues and development of Karnataka. A minimum of 35% marks in the competitive examination is compulsory; candidates below it are not eligible for selection. Each question has 4 options; 0.25 mark is deducted for every wrong answer.
