ESIC Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

ESIC Hospital, Sonagiri, Bhopal-462022 (M.P) will hold a walk-in interview on 4 December 2026 for doctors, with reporting at the Administration Office on the hospital's ground floor from 9:15 AM to 11:00 AM. Under Advertisement No 04/2026 of the Employees' State Insurance Corporation (ESIC), the hospital will fill 19 contractual Senior Resident posts (3-year tenure) and 18 Full Time/Part Time Contractual Specialist posts. Senior Residents and Full Time Specialists get pay level 11 with a basic pay of Rs 67,700 plus allowances, and Part Time Specialists get Rs 60,000 per month.

About ESIC Bhopal

ESIC Hospital, Sonagiri is a hospital of the Employees' State Insurance Corporation in Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh. The recruitment is conducted by the Medical Superintendent, ESIC Hospital, Sonagiri, Bhopal, who is also the controlling authority for the Full Time and Part Time Contractual Specialists. Senior Residents are engaged under the Central Residency Scheme.

Posts and Eligibility

The 37 posts are spread over 15 departments, as printed in the notice: The reservation for Persons with Benchmark Disability (PWD) will follow the prevailing Government of India guidelines, and PwBD reservation may be interchanged with other PwBD categories as per Government of India/DoPT guidelines. The notice says the number of vacancies and category-wise vacancies may vary or are likely to increase or decrease. What Happens on the Day

Report at the Administration Office, Ground Floor, ESIC Hospital, Sonagiri, Bhopal between 9:15 AM and 11:00 AM with the documents. Applications are scrutinised and documents verified the same day; no candidate is allowed for document verification after 11:15 AM. Only candidates whose documents are verified and who are found eligible are allowed to attend the walk-in interview. If vacancies remain unfilled after 4 December 2026, interviews will be conducted on the second Wednesday of the month, and candidates should watch www.esic.gov.in for the status of unfilled vacancies. No TA/DA will be paid for attending.

ESIC Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

Selected candidates must sign a contract agreement on a Non-Judicial Stamp Paper of Rs. 500 before joining, at their own cost. Contractual Specialists and Senior Residents must deposit a security of Rs. 10,000 by Demand Draft in favour of ESI Fund A/c. No.1, Bhopal at joining, refundable after the contract on production of a "No dues certificate". Selected candidates must have a professional indemnity policy with a minimum sum assured per annum of 30 lakhs for Anesthesiologists, Rs. 20 lakhs for General Surgeons, ENT, Eye, Orthopedics, OBGY etc., and 10 lakhs for General Medicine, Dentists, Radiologists, Pathologists etc. A copy must be submitted within 7 days of engagement. The Contractual Specialist will be responsible for all cases under treatment under his supervision in his discipline. Appointment is subject to medical fitness; selected Specialists must submit a written acceptance/consent letter before the medical examination. The engagement may be terminated by either side with one month's notice without assigning a reason; selected candidates have no claim to regularisation. If reserved category candidates are not available, the vacancy may be filled for less than 45 days as a de-reserved seat. The Medical Superintendent reserves the right to alter the date, cancel the interview, or vary or cancel any of the posts. Any change will be intimated only on www.esic.gov.in.

ESIC Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1When is the ESIC Bhopal walk-in interview?
A
The walk-in interview for Senior Resident and Specialist posts is on 4 December 2026 at ESIC Hospital, Sonagiri, Bhopal.
Q2What is the reporting time and venue?
A
Candidates must report at the Administration Office, Ground Floor, ESIC Hospital, Sonagiri, Bhopal from 9:15 AM to 11:00 AM; those reporting after 11:15 AM will not be entertained.
Q3How many vacancies are there?
A
There are 37 vacancies: 19 Senior Resident posts and 18 Full Time/Part Time Contractual Specialist posts across 15 departments.
Q4What qualification is needed for Senior Resident?
A
MBBS with PG Degree/PG Diploma in the required discipline from a recognized university, or MBBS with 2 years working experience in the same discipline (not from a private clinic).
Q5What qualification is needed for Specialist?
A
MBBS and PG Degree in the concerned/broad specialty with three years work experience, or Diploma with five years work experience, along with registration with the Medical Council of India or a State Medical Council.
Q6What is the age limit?
A
Senior Resident: not exceeding 45 years; Specialist: not exceeding 67 years, both as on the date of interview, with relaxation for SC/ST/OBC/PWD/Ex-serviceman as per DOPT rules.
Q7What is the salary?
A
Senior Residents and Full Time Specialists get entry pay level 11 with basic pay of Rs 67,700; Part Time Specialists get Rs 60,000 per month for four-hour sessions four days a week.
Q8Are allowances paid on top of the pay?
A
Senior Residents and Full Time Specialists get other allowances as admissible in ESIC; Part Time Specialists get Rs 15,000 per month for an emergency call duty undertaking and Rs 800 per hour above 16 hours a week.
Q9How will candidates be selected?
A
Applications and documents are verified on the interview day, and only candidates found eligible are allowed to attend the walk-in interview.
Q10Can I apply for both posts?
A
The notice says an application should be submitted for each post separately, using the Annexure-A form.
Q11How can I apply for ESIC Recruitment 2026 – Walkin for 37 Senior Resident, Specialist Posts?
A
