About DMIL

Delhi Metro International Limited is a wholly owned subsidiary of the Delhi Metro Rail Corporation. According to the notice, it was incorporated to undertake international metro rail projects and national projects within India, covering operations and maintenance, consultancy, design, planning and project development in the metro and allied urban transport sectors. It was set up to use the expertise of its parent company DMRC and offer professional services on commercial principles. This is a recruitment by a public sector company subsidiary.

Who Can Apply

The notice invites experienced officers working in Railways, Metro organizations, Rail PSUs or private organizations engaged in Metro Rail operation, and those who are working or have worked in a Metro Railway Company, Railways or the MRTS sector with relevant experience. Age and experience are reckoned as on 31.10.2026. Experience required, for both bases of engagement: (a) A minimum of 20 years of professional experience in rail based transportation system, of which 13 years must be in an executive grade in a rail based MRTS holding responsibilities of operations of trains, OCC, DCC and stations management. (b) Out of the total experience in (a), 5 years of experience as Head/Operations or 8 years of experience as Deputy Head Operations in underground rail based MRTS. Only candidates possessing the experience shown in the notice's experience column are considered for direct recruitment

Pay and Tenure

For direct recruitment/immediate absorption, the pay is printed as Rs 120000-280000 CDA pay scale Level 14 Rs 144200-218200. A selected employee is absorbed in a grade corresponding to the substantive grade in the parent cadre, and service rendered in the parent cadre, including on deputation, is counted for the next promotion. If a candidate is absorbed in a lower pay scale, he/she is given all perks, facilities and lease rental applicable to HOD while posted at MML-3, Mumbai, as per rules of the company. The Head Operations is eligible for all perks and facilities applicable to regular employees till superannuation age of 60 years. After superannuation, he may be engaged on post retirement contractual engagement basis as per rules of the company, with salary and perks as per rules in vogue. DMIL Recruitment 2026 Job Description

The incumbent is responsible for the direct daily operations of all revenue and non-revenue trains, trains and station crews, terminals, yards and stations. The role covers transportation services planning, and customer and employee safety and security. It includes daily interaction and cooperation with external agencies and other third parties, including other contractors and consultants. The initial posting is in Mumbai for the O&M work of Mumbai Metro Line-3. Selected candidates are liable to be posted or transferred to any office or project site under the control of the corporation in India or abroad.

Selection and Interview

For contractual engagement, the selection comprises a two-stage process of Personal Interview and Medical Fitness Examination. The notice says the selection process judges different facets of knowledge, skills, comprehension, aptitude and physical fitness. Candidates have to qualify the Screening Process and the Medical Examination (as applicable) before being adjudged suitable. The Medical Examination is in the Executive/Technical category, and its details are available on the DMRC website. Candidates who fail the prescribed medical test are not given any alternative employment, and the decision of the Corporation is final. Selection does not confer any right to engagement unless the Corporation is satisfied, after such inquiry as it considers necessary, about the candidate's character and antecedents.
