KPESRB Junior Manager Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

About KPESRB and Kerala State Textile Corporation

The Board is based at Vellayambalam, VikasBhavan P.O, Thiruvananthapuram, and issued this notification. Kerala State Textile Corporation Ltd is a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 1956. It is a Public Sector Undertaking under the Industries Department, Government of Kerala, with seven textile Mills across Kerala and one textile testing lab.

Minimum 2 years' post qualification experience in the HR department of a Government/Quasi Government or a Public/registered Private Sector Industrial Undertaking. KPESRB Junior Manager (Personnel) Job Description

Maintain employee records, personnel files, and HR databases. Process appointments, promotions, transfers, and leave, attendance, and retirement matters. Monitor attendance, absenteeism, and shift schedules. Assist in maintaining industrial relations and liaise with employees and trade unions. Ensure compliance with labour laws, standing orders, and company policies. Coordinate employee welfare schemes, employee amenities and statutory benefits. Organize employee training and development programmes. Prepare personnel reports, correspondence, and manpower statements.
