TNFWCCB Recruitment 2026 Qualification
PostEssential Qualification
Cyber Forensics Analyst (CFA-01, CFA-02)M.Tech./M.E. in Cyber Forensics / Digital Forensics / Cyber Security, or M.Sc. in Cyber Forensics / Digital Forensics / Cyber Security from a recognized University. GIS ExpertBachelor's or Master's Degree in Geography, Geo-informatics, Remote Sensing & Forestry, or a related discipline from a recognized University (a Post Graduate Diploma/Diploma in GIS & Remote Sensing may also be considered where the candidate has a relevant Bachelor's Degree), along with a minimum of 2 years of relevant experience in GIS, spatial analysis, mapping or related work.
Training CoordinatorMaster's Degree in Wildlife Science, Forestry, Zoology, Botany, Environmental Science, Life Science, Social Science, Management or related disciplines from a recognized University, along with experience in training coordination, capacity building, programme management, or related work.

Toll Free OperatorBachelor's Degree in any discipline from a recognized University, plus a Diploma/Certificate in Computer Applications or equivalent computer qualification, along with experience in toll-free helpline/call centre operations, data entry, computer operation or public grievance handling.
Data Entry OperatorBachelor's Degree in any discipline from a recognized University, plus a Diploma/Certificate in Computer Applications or equivalent computer qualification, along with experience in helpline operation, call centre operations, data entry, computer operation or public grievance handling.
Green FellowPostgraduate (PG) degree in Environmental Science, Sustainability, Public Policy, Climate Change, Agriculture, or related disciplines. OR an Undergraduate (UG) degree with a minimum of 2 years of active field experience.
