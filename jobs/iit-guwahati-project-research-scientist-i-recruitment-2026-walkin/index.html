IIT Guwahati Project Research Scientist I Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

About IIT Guwahati Project Research Scientist I

This recruitment is conducted by the Research and Development Section at the Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati, located in Guwahati-781039, Assam. The selected candidate will be appointed for a duration of 05 months in the Department of Biosciences and Bioengineering for a research project entitled "Understanding the host metabolome to formulate effective diagnostics against dengue virus infection" under the contact of Sachin Kumar (Principal Investigator).
