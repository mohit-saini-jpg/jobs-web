Bombay High Court Cook Recruitment 2026 - Important Dates

17 August 2026 Last Date to Submit Application (Speed Post): 10 September 2026, up to 5:00 PM The notification does not mention separate dates for the practical exam, physical fitness test, or interview. These will be published later on the official website, bombayhighcourt.gov.in.

Bombay High Court Cook Recruitment 2026 - Vacancy Details

A total of 18 posts of Swayampaki (Cook) are being filled, with 1 post reserved for persons with disabilities (PwD, 4% reservation). Out of the total vacancies, 17 candidates will be placed on the selection list and 4 candidates on the waiting list. The notification states that posts reserved for persons with disabilities will be filled in the future, once the Bombay High Court finalises its reservation roster/policy for such posts.

Bombay High Court Cook Recruitment 2026 - Eligibility Criteria

The candidate's character and conduct must be good. The candidate must have passed at least Class 4 (4th standard). The candidate must be able to read, write, and speak both Marathi and Hindi. Candidates holding a "Cook Course" certificate from a recognised institution will be given preference. The candidate must have practical knowledge of and experience related to cooking. The candidate must be able to prepare all types of vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes.

Bombay High Court Cook Recruitment 2026 - Educational Qualification

The minimum educational qualification required is a pass in Class 4 (4th standard). Candidates who have cleared a higher qualification (10th, 12th, or diploma) but do not possess a 4th standard mark sheet must enter a notional/assumed score of 50% (50 out of 100) for the 4th standard qualification while filling the application. Candidates who have completed a recognised cooking diploma or certificate course are preferred.

Bombay High Court Cook Recruitment 2026 - Salary/ Pay Scale

Selected candidates will be paid as per the 7th Pay Commission, Pay Matrix Level S-3, in the pay range of Rs. 16,600 to Rs. 52,400 , along with other allowances admissible as per applicable rules.

Bombay High Court Cook Recruitment 2026 - Application Fee

No fee is required at the time of submitting the application. However, only candidates who are shortlisted after screening of applications must submit a fee of Rs. 200 (Rupees Two Hundred only) in the form of a Demand Draft or Postal Order drawn in favour of "Registrar, High Court Bench at Aurangabad" , payable from a nationalised bank. This fee is non-refundable and must be carried at the time of the practical examination.

Bombay High Court Cook Recruitment 2026 - Selection Process

Shortlisted candidates will be called for the following stages, carrying a total of 50 marks: Candidates who fail the practical cooking test are not eligible to appear for the remaining stages (physical fitness test and interview). Final selection and the waiting list will be prepared strictly on the basis of merit (total marks obtained across all three stages).

Bombay High Court Cook Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

If a candidate submits more than one application, only the last (most recently submitted) application will be considered. The 4th standard mark sheet is compulsory; candidates lacking this certificate must follow the notional-marks instruction mentioned under Educational Qualification. Candidates with grade-based (non-percentage) school mark sheets must obtain a converted percentage/marks statement from their school and submit it with the application. Preference will be given to candidates holding a cooking diploma/certificate, or with one year or more of experience as a cook/chef in a 4-star or 5-star hotel. Candidates with a pending criminal case, prior criminal conviction, or pending departmental inquiry (for court/government employees) must disclose full details in the application. Incomplete or incorrect applications will be rejected. If false information is detected at any stage, even after appointment, the candidature or appointment will be cancelled. The shortlist will be prepared based on the number of applications received, educational merit, and cooking experience. Failing the practical cooking test disqualifies a candidate from the remaining selection stages. Carrying a valid original photo ID (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Driving Licence, Voter ID, etc.) is mandatory at the time of every test/interview; entry will be denied without it. The selection list and waiting list will remain valid for 2 years from the date of publication on the official website. Appointment of selected candidates is subject to medical examination and police character verification. The initial appointment will be on probation for 2 years. Attempting to contact or influence members of the selection committee, or any court official, regarding the selection process will lead to disqualification. The number of vacancies is provisional and may increase or decrease for administrative reasons. The High Court reserves the right to change, postpone, or cancel the recruitment process, or any stage of it, at any time, without assigning any reason. Candidates are advised to visit the official website, bombayhighcourt.gov.in, at least once a week for updates on schedules and further notices.
