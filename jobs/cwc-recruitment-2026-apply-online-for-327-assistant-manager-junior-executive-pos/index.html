CWC Recruitment 2026 Salary

The CWC salary for the Assistant Manager posts is Rs. 40000-140000 in the E-1 scale, and for the Junior Executive posts it is Rs. 29000-93000 in the S-V scale (IDA pattern). Besides basic pay and IDA, candidates are entitled to other allowances, perks and benefits as per the policies of the Corporation. The notification also lists CPF, contributory pension, gratuity, LTC, performance related pay or variable pay, and medical reimbursement as admissible for regular employees. Selected candidates are appointed on probation. Assistant Manager candidates have to furnish a bond of Rs. 1,00,000 and Junior Executive candidates a bond of Rs. 50,000, to serve the Corporation for a minimum of two years, at the time of joining.

CWC Recruitment 2026 Application Fee

The CWC application fee is Rs. 850 plus intimation charges of Rs. 500 (Rs. 1,350 in total) for UR, EWS and OBC candidates. SC, ST, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen and women candidates are exempt from the application fee but must pay intimation charges of Rs. 500. The amount is inclusive of GST, and the payment gateway charges are borne by the candidate. The fee is non-refundable and is paid through the online payment gateway only. Payment by DD, cheque or postal order is not accepted. Payment can be made by debit card (RuPay/Visa/MasterCard/Maestro), credit card, internet banking, UPI, IMPS, cash cards or mobile wallets. A candidate applying for more than one post must apply and pay the fee separately for each post. On successful payment, an e-receipt is generated. CWC Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

The CWC selection process depends on the post. Candidates must first register for the online recruitment process for each post. Candidates are shortlisted from the online test in the ratio of 1:5 for the Assistant Manager posts (Interview) and 1:3 for the Junior Executive posts (Document Verification). The Interview and Document Verification are conducted at Delhi. For the Assistant Manager posts, the Interview carries 30 marks. The minimum qualifying marks in the Interview are 50% for Unreserved and EWS candidates, 45% for OBC and 40% for SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen candidates. The weightage of the online exam and the Interview is 85:15. For the Junior Executive posts, appointment is based on the marks obtained in the online test, after successful Document Verification. CWC Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Candidates can apply online only from 17.10.2026 to 15.11.2026. No other mode of application is accepted. A candidate can apply for one or more posts according to the qualification and eligibility, but must apply and pay the fee separately for each post. Not more than one application should be submitted for a particular post.
  • 2Keep ready a scanned photograph, signature, left thumb impression and a hand-written declaration, a valid personal e-mail ID and mobile number, and the details for online payment.
  • 3Go to www.cewacor.nic.in, click the link "Career @CWC (Direct Recruitment)-2026" and then "Click here to apply online for Advertisement No. 2026/01".
  • 4Choose "Click here for New Registration" and enter the name, contact details and e-mail ID. A Provisional Registration Number and Password are generated and also sent by e-mail and SMS.
  • 5Fill in the application form. The "Save and Next" option can be used to save the data and verify it. The name of the candidate and the father/husband must be spelt as in the Class 10 certificate/mark sheet.
  • 6Upload the photograph, signature, left thumb impression and hand-written declaration, and capture the live photograph by webcam or mobile phone.
  • 7Preview the form, click "Complete Registration" after verifying, then use the "Payment" tab to pay the fee and click "Submit".
  • 8Take a printout of the application form and the e-receipt. Do not send them to CWC.
  • 9The text of the declaration is: "I, (Name of the candidate), hereby declare that all the information submitted by me in the application form is correct, true and valid. I will present the supporting documents as and when required." Visually impaired candidates or others who cannot write may get the text typed and put their left thumb impression below it. The online application is not registered unless the photograph, signature, left thumb impression and hand-written declaration are uploaded as specified.
