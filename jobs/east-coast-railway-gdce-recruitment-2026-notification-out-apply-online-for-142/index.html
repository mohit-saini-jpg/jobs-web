ECoR GDCE Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for ECoR GDCE Recruitment 2026, a candidate must be a regular employee of East Coast Railway (including erstwhile Waltair division/SCoR), excluding RPF/RPSF staff, currently holding lien in ECoR. Employees who have resigned or transferred out of East Coast Railway are not eligible. Employees already in the same grade/pay level as the notified post can apply for GDCE from Non-Safety to Safety posts and Safety to Safety posts; employees in a lower grade/pay level can apply for all notified categories, provided they hold the prescribed educational qualification. ECoR GDCE Selection Process

Selection under ECoR GDCE 2026 follows a single-stage Computer Based Test (CBT), followed by post-specific additional stages, Document Verification, and Medical Examination: There is negative marking in CBT: 1/3rd mark deducted for each wrong answer. There is no negative marking in CBAT. Minimum qualifying marks in CBT: UR – 40%, OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) – 30%, SC – 30%, ST – 25% (relaxable by 2 marks for PwBD candidates in case of shortage against reserved vacancies). Shortlisting for CBAT/Typing Skill Test is done at 8 times the number of vacancies, based on CBT merit. For ALP, final merit uses 50% weightage for CBT and 50% for CBAT. For Station Master, it is 70% CBT and 30% CBAT. Candidates equal to the number of vacancies are called for Document Verification, based on CBT/CBAT merit. Final appointment is subject to passing the prescribed Medical Fitness Test.
