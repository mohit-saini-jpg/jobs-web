AIIMS Raebareli Faculty Eligibility Criteria 2026

For all four posts, candidates must hold a medical qualification recognised under the Indian Medical Council Act, 1956 (First or Second Schedule, or Part II of the Third Schedule), along with a recognised postgraduate qualification such as MD/MS, or an equivalent recognised qualification in the concerned discipline. For super-specialty subjects, candidates additionally require an M.Ch. (for surgical super-specialties) or D.M. (for medical super-specialties) or an equivalent recognised qualification. Candidates holding a DNB qualification in broad or super specialties must submit documentary evidence of DNB equivalence with MD/MS/DM/MCh as per the NMC notification dated 14.02.2022, as specified in the official notification. AIIMS Raebareli Faculty Selection Process 2026

Selection will be carried out by a duly constituted Standing Selection Committee. AIIMS Raebareli reserves the right to conduct a written test in addition to the interview in departments where the number of applicants is 30 or more. No TA/DA is paid for appearing in the interview.
