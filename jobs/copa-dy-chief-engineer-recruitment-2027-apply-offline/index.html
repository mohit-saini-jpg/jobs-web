CoPA Dy. Chief Engineer Recruitment 2027 : Job Overview

Cochin Port Authority will fill one post of Dy. Chief Engineer (Civil) by absorption through composite method. The post is open to eligible Superintending Engineer-level officers of the Civil Engineering discipline serving in Major Ports. Applications go through proper channel to the Administrative Office of Cochin Port Authority, Kochi-682009, and must reach on or before 8 January 2027. Pay is in the scale of Rs.80000-220000.

Cochin Port Authority is a Major Port Authority with its Administrative Office on Willingdon Island, Kochi, Kerala. The notice is issued by the Secretary of Cochin Port Authority to the Secretaries of all Major Ports, with a copy to the Ministry of Ports, Shipping & Waterways, Government of India. The Dy. Chief Engineer (Civil) post will fall vacant with effect from 1 March 2027.
