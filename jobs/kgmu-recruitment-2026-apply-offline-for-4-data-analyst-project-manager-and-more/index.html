KGMU Manager cum Data Analyst Job Description

The Data Analyst will be responsible for managing, processing, analysing and interpreting survey data. The role includes ensuring data quality and accuracy, and contributing to analytical reports, presentations and other research outputs. Contribute to developing and refining survey tools, manuals and other research-related documents. Train and support field and research staff on data collection, data management and quality assurance procedures. Manage and maintain survey datasets and the data collection system. Monitor data collection across study sites, and conduct data cleaning, validation, recoding and labelling. Prepare analysis-ready datasets, codebooks and data dictionaries, and develop statistical analysis plans.
