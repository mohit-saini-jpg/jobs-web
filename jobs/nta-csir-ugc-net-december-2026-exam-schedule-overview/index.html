CSIR UGC NET December 2026 Exam Purpose

The Joint CSIR UGC NET examination is conducted to determine eligibility for the following categories, subject to the applicable examination and qualification rules:

  • Award of Junior Research Fellowship (JRF) and appointment as Assistant Professor
  • Appointment as Assistant Professor and admission to PhD programmes
  • Admission to PhD programmes only

The examination covers five scientific disciplines and is conducted by the National Testing Agency on behalf of the Council of Scientific and Industrial Research.
