Is NATS Registration Mandatory for DRDO ITR Apprentice 2026?

Yes. NATS registration is an eligibility requirement. Candidates with the listed degree or diploma qualifications must have registered their names on the National Apprenticeship Training Scheme (NATS) portal. The notification warns that non-registered candidates are likely to be rejected. NATS portal: https://nats.education.gov.in/ Applicants should enter their NATS enrollment number in the corresponding field of the DRDO ITR application form.

DRDO ITR Apprentice Selection Process 2026

DRDO ITR will select candidates based on a Written Test, Personal Interview, or both , conducted for shortlisted candidates. ITR reserves the right to determine the shortlisting method. Meeting the minimum qualification does not automatically guarantee that an applicant will be shortlisted. Written test and interview updates, including call letters, will be provided through the official website or email. Candidates are responsible for downloading and printing their admit cards or call letters. Selected candidates must undergo document verification before the apprenticeship contract is created. They must also provide a Medical Fitness Certificate at the time of joining.

Is DRDO ITR Apprenticeship a Permanent Government Job?

The official notification clearly states that ITR is not obligated to offer employment to apprentices after they complete their training. Candidates should consider this opportunity as a fixed-duration apprenticeship rather than a guaranteed permanent position.

Other Important Conditions Candidates Should Know

All correspondence, including offer letters for selected candidates, will take place through email. ITR will not provide TA/DA to candidates called at any stage of the selection process. Government quarters, hostel accommodation and transport will not be provided during the written test/interview or training period. PwBD candidates are eligible only for designated trades under the applicable Apprenticeship Rules. Reservation will apply according to the relevant Apprentices Act and Rules. Incorrect information or attempts to influence the selection process may result in disqualification.

Who Should Consider Applying for DRDO ITR Apprentice 2026?

This apprenticeship may be relevant to recent engineering and diploma graduates who want a 12-month training opportunity at a DRDO laboratory. It also provides openings for eligible BBA, B.Com and Library Science graduates. Before applying, candidates should check three points carefully: Academic eligibility: Is your exact course or branch listed, and did you pass between 2022 and 2026 as a regular candidate? Experience eligibility: Have you completed less than one year of job experience or training after obtaining the required qualification? Application readiness: Do you have NATS registration and sufficient time for your physical application to reach ITR before the deadline?
