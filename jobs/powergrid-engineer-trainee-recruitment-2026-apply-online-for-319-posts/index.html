POWERGRID Engineer Trainee Selection Process

There is no separate POWERGRID written examination for this recruitment. Selection is based on the candidate's valid GATE 2026 score followed by Group Discussion and Personal Interview. Candidates will be shortlisted category-wise for Group Discussion and Personal Interview on the basis of their normalized marks out of 100 in the corresponding GATE 2026 paper. Group Discussion carries 3% weightage and does not have separate qualifying marks. Candidates may participate in Group Discussion and Interview in either Hindi or English. Final appointment is subject to the candidate being found medically fit according to POWERGRID's prescribed pre-employment medical standards.
