How to Download Kerala PG Dental Vacancy List 2026?

Candidates can download the official Kerala MDS 2026 vacant seat list through the CEE Kerala PG Dental admission website.

  • Visit the official website at cee.kerala.gov.in.
  • Open the PG Dental 2026 Candidate Portal.
  • Select the Notifications and Press Release section.
  • Find the link titled "Vacancy list as on 15.10.2026".
  • Open the vacancy list document.
  • Check the college code, MDS course, category and seat count.
  • Download the PDF for reference during subsequent allotment activities.

Important Instructions

  • Latest status: CEE Kerala has delayed the Round 3 MDS seat allotment while awaiting the revised counselling schedule from the Medical Counselling Committee (MCC). No new date was announced in the sources I found.
  • Option registration: Round 3 option registration ran from 7 October to 12 October 2026, 10:00 AM. Registration is closed, so check cee.kerala.gov.in for any reopening.
  • Seat and vacancy details: CEE Kerala’s MDS 2026 admission is to fill 70 seats. Half the government college seats go through the All India Quota, and CEE fills the remaining state quota seats and all private self-financing seats. A vacancy list, if released, will be on the CEE portal.
  • Earlier phases: The first-phase allotment list was released on 5 September 2026. Another source shows a provisional first-phase list posted on 10 October. The dates conflict across sources, so check the official portal.
  • Where to check: Candidates view allotment details on the PG Dental 2026 Candidate Portal at cee.kerala.gov.in using their application number and password. Keep your documents ready for the next phase and check the portal regularly for the vacancy list and the revised schedule.

Who Should Apply Kerala PG Dental (Kerala Posts)

Kerala PG Dental (Kerala Posts) Application Tips

1. Apna updated MDS mark sheet, registration certificate aur passport size photo ready rakhein. 2. Official website par login karke online application form fill karein, sabhi fields accurately bharain. 3. Application fee ka receipt save kar lein aur PDF copy download kar lein.
