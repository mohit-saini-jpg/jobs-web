RSSB JE Revised Recruitment 2026 - Instructions for Previous Applicants

Candidates who submitted valid applications for the original 874-post recruitment do not need to apply again. However, they should review the revised department vacancies and department preference rules. Candidates who previously applied for Junior Engineer Civil Degree or Diploma positions can change their department preference order free of charge from 22 December to 31 December 2026 . The Board will allocate departments according to category-wise merit and preferences submitted by candidates. However, the candidate's name, father's name, date of birth, photograph, signature, thumb impression and handwriting specimen are excluded from the specified correction facility. Corrections will not be accepted after the prescribed window, and offline requests will not be entertained.
