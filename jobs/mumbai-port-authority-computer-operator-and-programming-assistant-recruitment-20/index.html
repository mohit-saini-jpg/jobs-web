MbPA Selection Process 2026

The stages are a merit list, verification of original documents and selection procedure at the ATC office, and a physical fitness test. The merit list is prepared on the percentage of total marks obtained in both semesters of the COPA ITI Trade Exam. Preference goes to candidates with a higher qualification in addition to the minimum educational qualification, in descending order: Post Graduate, Graduate, 3 Years Diploma and 12th Passed. If two candidates have the same marks, the older candidate is placed above. Preference is given to wards of MbPA employees; a separate merit list is prepared for wards of MbPA employees and for other candidates. The merit lists will be displayed on the notice board at the ATC office and uploaded on www.mumbaiport.gov.in . Candidates on the merit list are called to the ATC office with all original documents. MbPA Apprenticeship Terms and Important Instructions

After selection, the candidate enters into a Contract of Apprenticeship in the form prescribed under the Apprentices Act, 1961, to be registered with the Regional Directorate of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (RDSDE). For a minor, the guardian or parent enters into the contract. Final selection is subject to this registration. An apprentice who abandons the contract before completing the course is liable to pay the cost of training (maximum three months' stipend) or an amount decided by RDSDE; legal action is started if this is not paid. MbPA has no obligation to offer employment during or after the apprenticeship, and the apprentice cannot claim employment on that ground. Two kinds of leave are admissible: Casual Leave 12 days and Medical Leave 15 days. The apprentice must observe the conduct rules applicable to workmen in the Port Authority. Disputes are referred to RDSDE. All

Mumbai Port Authority Computer Operator and Programming : Important Instructions

After selection, the candidate enters into a Contract of Apprenticeship in the form prescribed under the Apprentices Act, 1961, to be registered with the Regional Directorate of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (RDSDE). For a minor, the guardian or parent enters into the contract. Final selection is subject to this registration. An apprentice who abandons the contract before completing the course is liable to pay the cost of training (maximum three months' stipend) or an amount decided by RDSDE; legal action is started if this is not paid. MbPA has no obligation to offer employment during or after the apprenticeship, and the apprentice cannot claim employment on that ground. Two kinds of leave are admissible: Casual Leave 12 days and Medical Leave 15 days. The apprentice must observe the conduct rules applicable to workmen in the Port Authority. Disputes are referred to RDSDE. All apprentices get practical training at various sections / Departments of MbPA. After satisfactory completion, each appears for a test under NCVT guidelines and receives the NCVT certificate on passing. An applicant who applies as physically handicapped must produce the original certificate from a competent medical board (Persons with Benchmark Disabilities, not less than 40% disability). Without a copy of the certificate, the application is considered in the General Category. Candidature is cancelled if required original documents are not produced or a discrepancy is found. A candidate found ineligible at any stage is dismissed without notice. No daily, conveyance or travelling allowance is paid for document verification and medical examination. MbPA is not responsible for sending a reply or call to candidates not selected, and its decision on eligibility, acceptance or rejection and mode of selection is final.
