MAH LLB CAP 2026 Overview Table : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 13/08/2026

The State Common Entrance Test (CET) Cell, Maharashtra, under the Directorate of Technical Education (DTE), has released a revised Centralised Admission Process (CAP) schedule for admission to the 3-Year LLB and 5-Year Integrated LLB courses for the academic year 2026-27. The revision follows delays linked to pending Bar Council of India (BCI) approval for several law colleges. Registration and counselling activity for both courses are being conducted through separate portals - llb3cap26.mahacet.org for the 3-year LLB course and llb5cap26.mahacet.org for the 5-year integrated LLB course.

How to Register and Fill the College Option Form

Action
Visit the relevant CAP portal - llb3cap26.mahacet.org for 3-year LLB or llb5cap26.mahacet.org for 5-year LLB.
Register or log in using your MH CET Law 2026 credentials.
Complete registration and upload the required documents within the notified window.
Check the alphabetical list and raise a grievance/edit application if required, within the resolution window.
After the final merit list is published, log in during the College Option Form Filling period and submit/arrange your college preferences.
Check the seat allotment result on the scheduled date and time (5:00 PM).
If allotted a seat, choose Freeze or Betterment (where applicable) and report to the allotted college within the reporting window.

How to Check MAH LLB CAP Seat Allotment Result

  • Go to the official CAP portal for your course (llb3cap26.mahacet.org or llb5cap26.mahacet.org).
  • Log in with your registered candidate ID and password/date of birth as prompted.
  • Open the seat allotment/result section for the relevant round.
  • View and download the allotment letter.
  • Proceed to report to the allotted college within the notified reporting dates.

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Round 1, 2, and 3 allotment results are published at 5:00 PM on the scheduled date for both courses.
  • Candidates not yet registered but holding a valid MH CET Law 2026 qualification can still register for Round III and the institutional-level round between August 5 and September 5, 2026.
  • The Betterment option can be used a maximum of two times and is not available in Round III.
  • Round III is the final CAP round; there is no auto-freeze, so candidates must actively select Freeze or Betterment after each allotment (where the option is available).
  • Always cross-check dates on the official portal, as CAP schedules are subject to further revision by the CET Cell.
