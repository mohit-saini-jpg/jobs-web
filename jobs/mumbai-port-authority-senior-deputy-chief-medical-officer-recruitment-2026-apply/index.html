About MbPA

Mumbai Port Authority is a Major Port Authority based in Mumbai. This recruitment notice is issued by its General Administration Department, Port House, 2nd floor, S.V. Marg, Ballard Estate, Mumbai - 400 001, and is addressed to the Chairpersons of the Major Port Authorities. Applications are received through the Online Application Portal of the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways.

Department-wise Vacancies

The notice fills one post, which will be vacant with effect from 9 May 2027. Annexure I (the eligibility table for the post) shows 8 posts of Sr. Dy. Chief Medical Officer in all. As per its remarks, one post each is operated in the faculties of (i) Medicine (General Medicine or Pediatrics or Cardiology), (ii) Surgery (General Surgery or ENT or Ophthalmology or Orthopedic), (iii) Obst & Gynec, (iv) Pathology and Bacteriology, (v) Anaesthesia and (vi) Radiology, and two posts would be for administration of the hospital department. The Chairman may at any time change the faculty/function in which the post is to be operated, considering the hospital requirements.

Qualification and Experience

Annexure I states that the educational qualifications prescribed for direct recruits will apply in the case of promotion/absorption/deputation, while the age and the experience prescribed for direct recruits will not. Under Annexure I, the following officers are eligible for absorption: Officers holding analogous posts, or holding the post of Dy. Chief Medical Officer (Specialist) and equivalent Specialist posts in the Medical Deptt. in the scale of pay of Rs.13000-18250 with 3 years regular service in the grade in a Major Port Trust; or Dy. Chief Medical Officer (Specialist) and equivalent Specialist posts in the Medical Deptt. with 2 years regular service in the grade and a combined regular service of 7 years in the scales of pay of Rs.10750-16750 and Rs.13000-18250 in the Specialist cadre of the Medical Deptt. in a Major Port Trust. Pay and Terms

The scale column of Annexure I prints the scale of pay of the post as Rs.16000-400-20800. The post is a Class-I Selection post. The period of probation is shown as N.A. in Annexure I. The post is filled by absorption through composite method. The notice does not mention allowances.

Mumbai Port Authority Senior Deputy Chief Medical Officer : Important Instructions

As per the Ministry's circular No.A-29018/4/2081/PE-I dated 11.8.2021, where an advance copy is received, the candidature will not be considered if the application is not received through proper channel from the Port within 15 days of the last date of receipt of applications. Incomplete applications or applications received after the due date will not be considered. A Port official who withdraws candidature after selection by the Services Selection Committee will be liable for debarment from future selection to Dy. HoD level posts in all Major Port Authorities for two years. Annexure III asks for service details, Annual Property Return status, positions held in the ten preceding years and any punishment, proceedings, complaints or allegations in the last 10 years. Mumbai Port Authority Senior Deputy Chief Medical Officer : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Submit the application through the Online Application Portal (OAP) of the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways, onlinevacancy.shipmin.nic.in, between 30 November 2026 and 29 December 2026. No application submitted other than through OAP will be accepted.
  • 2Submit the hard copy of the application form uploaded in OAP, along with the requisite documents, to your administrative Port for onward forwarding to MbPA.
  • 3The administrative Port forwards the application superscribed "Application for the post of Sr. Deputy Chief Medical Officer (Administration) in Mumbai Port Authority" on or before 13 January 2027.
  • 4The Port encloses all relevant documents at one time; piecemeal or part forwarding will not be considered.
  • 5Applications received after 13 January 2027 will not be considered.
