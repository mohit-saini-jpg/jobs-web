KEA Junior Engineer Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection will be based on the competitive examination conducted by KEA , the compulsory Kannada language examination wherever applicable, and verification of eligibility and documents. Online Application: Candidates submit their applications through the KEA recruitment portal. Competitive Examination: KEA will conduct the prescribed objective examination for the notified technical posts. Compulsory Kannada Language Examination: Candidates who are not exempt under the notified rules must qualify in the Kannada language examination. Document Verification: KEA or the concerned department will verify educational qualifications, age, reservation and other eligibility certificates. Final Selection: Selection will be subject to examination performance, applicable reservation rules and verification of eligibility.

KEA Junior Engineer Exam Pattern 2026

The competitive examination will consist of two papers, each carrying 100 marks . The examination will be conducted in offline OMR format. Paper 1 covers subjects including current affairs, everyday science, geography, Indian Constitution, Indian history, Karnataka history, Karnataka administration, rural development, Panchayat Raj institutions and other general knowledge topics. Paper 2 will assess technical knowledge relevant to the prescribed educational qualifications and the selected post. Candidates should follow the detailed syllabus and further examination instructions issued by KEA. The notification prescribes a deduction of 0.25 marks for each incorrect answer in the competitive examination.

KEA Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1Which post has the most vacancies?
A
Assistant Town Planner has the most, with 105 of the 187 posts. Among Junior Engineer (Civil) posts, the Greater Bengaluru Authority has the largest share with 46.
Q2Can I apply if I am waiting for my final diploma result?
A
No. You must hold the prescribed qualification and certificate on 26 November 2026, and candidates awaiting results are not eligible.
Q3Can I pay the fee after the application closes?
A
Yes, by one day. Applications close on 26 November 2026, while the fee can be paid online until 27 November 2026.
Q4Do I need AUTOCAD training for the Junior Engineer post?
A
It is not mandatory, but the Greater Bengaluru Authority gives preference to candidates trained in AUTOCAD. The notice prints no such preference for the other organisations.
Q5What is the qualifying mark in the competitive examination?
A
Candidates need at least 35% marks to qualify, and merit is prepared from those who reach it. In the Kannada Language Test, the qualifying score is 50 out of 150 unless the candidate is exempt.
Q6Will I lose marks for leaving a question blank?
A
Yes, if you do not shade the 5th option on the OMR sheet. An unshaded question costs 0.25 mark, the same as a wrong answer.
Q7Can I correct a mistake after submitting the application?
A
No, nothing can be edited once the application is submitted. The one exception is a wrongly claimed reservation, which can be deleted from the Candidate Verification Slip before mock allotment begins.
Q8Can I be selected under both KEA notifications?
A
No. A candidate can get only one post across this notice and notification No. ED/KEA/40/Ne.Vi/2026 (KK), decided by the options entered, eligibility, reservation and merit.
Q9Which ID should I carry to the examination?
A
Carry one original ID from the accepted list, such as a PAN card, Aadhaar card, passport or voter ID, along with your admit card.
Q10Does the National Pension System apply to every post?
A
No. It does not apply to the 12 Junior Engineer (Civil) posts at Karnataka Rural Infrastructure Development Limited, but it applies to the other organisations listed.

A
