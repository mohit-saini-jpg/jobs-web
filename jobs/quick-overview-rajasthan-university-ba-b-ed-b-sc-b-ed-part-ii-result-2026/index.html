How to Check and Download the Rajasthan University Part-II Result 2026

  • Go to the official result portal: result.uniraj.ac.in.
  • On the homepage, locate the result link for "B.A. B.Ed. PART-II (INTEGRATED COURSE) Exam- 2026" or "B.Sc. B.Ed. PART-II (INTEGRATED COURSE) EXAM.-2026," as applicable to your course.
  • Click the relevant link - this opens the result login page in a new tab.
  • Enter your roll number or full name exactly as printed on your admit card.
  • Click "Submit" to view your result.
  • Check your marksheet on screen, then download and print it for your records.
