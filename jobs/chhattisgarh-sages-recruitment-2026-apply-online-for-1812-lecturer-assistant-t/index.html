Chhattisgarh SAGES Recruitment 2026 Vacancy Details

A total of 1,812 contractual posts are notified across 33 districts of Chhattisgarh. The district-wise and category-wise breakup (with the 100-point reservation roster) is given in Annexure 'A' of the notification. For example, Raipur district has 141 vacancies. Reservation follows the Chhattisgarh Lok Seva (Reservation for SC, ST and Backward Classes) Act, 1994, and the 100-point roster as per General Administration Department directions, district-wise. 30% of posts are reserved for women (horizontal reservation) under the Chhattisgarh Civil Services (Special Provisions for Appointment of Women) Rules, 1997. Widows and divorced women are given preference. 7% of posts are reserved for persons with disabilities, and reservation for ex-servicemen is as per Government rules.

Eligibility Criteria

All candidates must complete the minimum qualification on or before the last date of online application. Candidates must also have studied in the English medium from Class 6 up to the highest degree for the English medium posts. This English-medium condition does not apply to Lecturer and Teacher posts for Hindi and Sanskrit. The candidate must be a native (domicile) resident of Chhattisgarh and must attach a certificate issued by the competent authority. Candidates must pass the CG TET/CTET (Upper Primary level) for Teacher and Headmaster (Upper Primary) posts, and CG TET/CTET (Primary level) for Headmaster (Primary) and Assistant Teacher posts. Educational qualifications will be recognized as per the NCTE notifications issued from time to time and court judgments. Salary/Stipend

The contract staff are paid a fixed monthly lump-sum honorarium (ekmusht masik mandey) based on the post: Lecturer and Headmaster (Upper Primary School): Rs. 38,100 per month Headmaster (Primary School), Teacher (all subjects), PT Teacher and Teacher (Computer): Rs. 35,400 per month Assistant Teacher and Laboratory Assistant: Rs. 25,300 per month Librarian: Rs. 22,400 per month No special pay, dearness allowance, compensatory allowance or house rent allowance is given, and there is no annual increment under contract appointment. Contract employees are not eligible for pension, death benefit or any other benefit available to regular Chhattisgarh Government employees.

Age Limit (as on 01-01-2026)

The age is counted as on 01-01-2026. Candidates must have completed 21 years and must not have completed 35 years of age. Age relaxation is as follows: Minimum Age: 21 years Maximum Age: Under 35 years SC/ST/OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) of Chhattisgarh: Up to 5 years Women Candidates: Up to 10 years (as per the Chhattisgarh Civil Services (Special Provisions for Appointment of Women) Rules, 1997) Persons with Disabilities: As per General Administration Department directions. Government Employees (permanent or temporary) and employees of Chhattisgarh corporations/boards: Up to 38 years. Retrenched government servants: Relaxation by the earlier temporary service period (maximum 7 years), but the resulting age must not exceed the upper limit by more than 3 years. Chhattisgarh SAGES Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection is through a Computer Based Test (CBT) only, followed by document verification. The notification does not mention a separate interview or the exam pattern. The CBT will be conducted in English medium. The selection list will be prepared on the basis of the CBT result. Technical help from expert institutions will be taken with the State Government's permission. The selection list is valid for 6 months from the date of issue. A waiting list of up to 25% of the vacant posts (minimum one name) will be prepared for each category and is valid for 9 months. Candidates will be informed about document verification online through the department's portal, and absent candidates will be treated as ineligible. Selected candidates are subject to character verification (police verification). Selected candidates in the disability category will be examined by the District Medical Board for bench

Chhattisgarh SAGES Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Eligible candidates can apply for Chhattisgarh SAGES Recruitment 2026 through online mode only, between 01-10-2026 and 21-10-2026. Offline applications, whether sent by registered post or otherwise, are not valid. Follow the steps below:
  • 2Visit the official portal of the Directorate of Public Instruction, Chhattisgarh: https://eduportal.cg.nic.in.
  • 3Read the notification carefully and check the minimum educational qualification, TET requirement, age limit and domicile condition for the post you want.
  • 4Fill in the online application form with correct details and apply for the post (and subject) you are eligible for.
  • 5Submit the application on or before 21-10-2026 (up to midnight). Applications received after the stipulated date and time will not be considered.
  • 6Keep the required documents ready, including educational and TET certificates, the Chhattisgarh domicile (native resident) certificate, the permanent caste certificate (for SC/ST/OBC), the No Objection Certificate (if employed), and other certificates for reservation/relaxation claims. These are required for document verification.
  • 7A candidate can apply for only one subject in the Lecturer posts (more than one subject makes the application invalid). Similarly, one subject each can be chosen for Headmaster (Upper Primary)/Teacher, and for Headmaster (Primary)/Assistant Teacher. The PT Teacher, Librarian, Computer Teacher and Laboratory Assistant posts have one application each.
  • 8A candidate who meets the qualifications can submit a maximum of 7 separate applications, and separate exam dates/batches will be assigned accordingly.
  • 9Incomplete and erroneous applications will not be accepted, and no separate notice will be given for rejected applications.
  • 10SC/ST/OBC candidates must attach a permanent caste certificate from the competent authority. Married SC/ST women must provide a caste certificate showing their father's name.
  • 11Candidates must check the portal regularly for exam dates, document verification and other updates.
