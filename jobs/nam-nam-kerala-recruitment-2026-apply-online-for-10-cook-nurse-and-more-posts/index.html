NAM NAM Kerala Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

National AYUSH Mission, Kannur (നാഷണൽ ആയുഷ് മിഷൻ, കണ്ണൂർ) is making temporary appointments on a contract basis, through district-level selection, to six posts in Kannur district: Nurse, Ayurveda Therapist (Male), Yoga Instructor, Cook, Unani Therapist and Multi Purpose Health Worker. The pay ranges from 10,500/- for Cook to 17,850/- for Nurse. Eligible candidates must apply online on careers.nam.kerala.gov.in by 19/11/2026; the interview date will then be informed by email/SMS.

About NAM Kannur

The National AYUSH Mission office in Kannur is run by the District Programme Manager and works from the B-Block, second floor, Civil Station, Kannur. The notices for these posts were issued on 12.11.2026 and signed by Dr. Hareesh Krishnan P P, District Programme Manager, National AYUSH Mission, Kannur. The posts are in AYUSH institutions of the district, including Ayushman Arogya Mandir (AYUSH) centres and the Government Homoeopathy Hospital, Aralam.

Vacancy Details

The notices print a number for three posts, which together come to 10 vacancies. For Nurse, Ayurveda Therapist (Male) and Unani Therapist, the notices say only "expected vacancy" without a number. The notices state that the number of vacancies is temporary and subject to change.

Who Can Apply

The notice for Yoga Instructor lists these qualifications, separated by "/": Degree in BNYS/BAMS from a Government-recognised university MSc (Yoga) MPhil (Yoga) PG Diploma in Yoga of not less than one year from a Government-recognised university Certificate course of one year duration in Yoga from a recognised university or a Government department YCB (Yoga Certification Board) Diploma in Yogic Science and Sports Yoga conducted by SCOLE Kerala Certificate Course in Yoga (of one year duration) The same notice then adds: must have passed a Yoga Teacher Training Course (DYT course) of not less than one year from the State Resource Centre. The upper age limit is counted as on 01-11-2026 . The notices do not mention any age relaxation. The Ayurveda Therapist post is advertised for men ("Ayurveda Therapist – Male"). Selection and Interview

Selection for all six posts of NAM Kannur Recruitment 2026 is through an interview, described in the notices as a walk-in interview held after the online application. The interview date is not printed in the notices. It will be sent to candidates by email/SMS. The interview is held at the AYUSH Mission District Programme Manager office, B-Block, second floor, Civil Station, Kannur. The notices do not give a reporting time.
