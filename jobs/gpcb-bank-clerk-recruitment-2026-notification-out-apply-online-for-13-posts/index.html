Gandevi Bank Clerk Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Eligibility is assessed as on 10 August 2026. Candidates must be domiciled in Gujarat State and must produce relevant original documents (with a photocopy) in support of their identity and eligibility, as and when called for. No change in the category, age, educational qualification, or other details provided during online registration will be permitted at any stage after submission of the application. The minimum qualification for recruitment to the Clerical cadre is a Bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognised university with at least 55% marks. However: Candidates holding a Bachelor of Arts (B.A.) degree are not eligible . Candidates holding degrees in Engineering are also not eligible , except those holding a degree in Information Technology or Computer Science (BE/B.Tech or equivalent). Gandevi Bank Clerk Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection consists of an Online Written Examination followed by a Personal Interview. A candidate must qualify in both the Online Examination and the Interview to be considered for the subsequent recruitment process. All test papers will be available only in English. The Bank reserves the right to modify the structure of the examination, which will be intimated through its website. A penalty of 1/4th of the marks assigned to a question is applied for each wrong answer (negative marking). Test-wise and total scores are reported with decimal points up to two digits, and scores may be equated across sessions to adjust for minor differences in difficulty level. The examination will tentatively be conducted in online mode at Surat, Vapi, and Vadodara. No request for change of examination centre will be entertained.
