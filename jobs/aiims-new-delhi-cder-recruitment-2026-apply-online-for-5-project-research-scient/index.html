AIIMS New Delhi (CDER) Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Five project posts, one each of Project Research Scientist III (Medical), Project Research Scientist I (Medical) and Project Technical Support III, II and I, are open in the CDER, AIIMS-ICMR project "Project ORO-PAL" (Project Code: I-2048) at AIIMS New Delhi, with a tenure of 3 years and monthly pay from Rs. 18,000 to Rs. 93,000 plus HRA. Essential qualifications run from 10th plus a diploma to BDS with MDS. Applications go through Google Forms by 25 November 2026, and the interview is on 30 November 2026.
