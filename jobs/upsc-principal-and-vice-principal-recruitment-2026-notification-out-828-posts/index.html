UPSC Principal and Vice-Principal Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection will be made through a Combined Recruitment Test (CRT) to shortlist candidates for interview, followed by a Personality Test/Interview . Key details: CRT will be held on 01 November 2026 at 15 designated centres. The test is of 2 hours' duration, objective type (MCQs), available in English and Hindi, carrying a maximum of 300 marks. There is negative marking: one-third of the marks assigned to a question is deducted for each wrong answer. Weightage of CRT and Interview marks is 75:25 for determining final merit. Minimum suitability standard in interview: General/EWS – 50 marks, OBC – 45 marks, SC/ST/PwBD – 40 marks (out of 100).
