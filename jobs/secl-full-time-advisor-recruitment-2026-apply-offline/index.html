About SECL

South Eastern Coalfields Limited is a Government of India Undertaking and a Mini Ratna company, a subsidiary of Coal India Limited, with its registered office at Seepat Road, Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh. This engagement is issued by its Executive Establishment Department under CIL's Policy for availing the services of retired CMDs/Directors/Senior Level Executives as full-time/part-time Advisors, as amended from time to time.

SECL FULL TIME ADVISOR at DCC Job Description

The Advisor's role is to expedite mutation of DCC land acquired through 09 Nos. of notifications under the West Bengal Land Requisition and Acquisition Act, 1948. The work will broadly include: Supervising the job of mutation/updating of RoR/updating the nature of the acquired land of DCC in favour of the project. Pursuing with the State Govt of West Bengal regarding expediting mutation of land of DCC acquired in 09 Nos. of notifications. Liaising with the Govt of West Bengal for arranging necessary documents for the job. Any other jobs assigned as per the requirement of DCC and SECL. The selected Advisor would be stationed at DCC or as per the requirement of the Company, must maintain the integrity and secrecy of the Company's business, must not engage in any other business during the tenure, and must devote whole time to the company.

Selection and Interview

SECL reserves the right to shortlist candidates for the interview/selection process. Selection will be done on the basis of performance in the interview. No TA/DA will be paid to any candidate for appearing in the interview/selection process. The interview date is not specified in the notice.
