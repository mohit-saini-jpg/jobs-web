UPESSC Principal Headmaster Selection Process

Selection for both posts is based on a total of 400 marks: a written examination of 360 marks and an interview of 40 marks. The final merit list is prepared from the marks obtained out of these 400 marks. Every question has four options, of which only one is the most suitable answer. The answer bubble must be darkened with a black ball point pen on the OMR sheet. The question paper will be in Hindi and English, except for language subjects. Each correct answer carries 3 marks. For every wrong or multiple answer, 1 mark is deducted (negative marking). The cut-off and marks obtained by candidates will be displayed on the Commission's website after the selection process is complete. The syllabus for the written examination is available on www.upessc.up.gov.in. If more than one candidate has equal merit, marks are compared in this order: Marks in the written examination. UPESSC Principal Headmaster Appointment and School Allotment

Allotment of schools to selected candidates is done by the authorised officer, the Director, Secondary Education, U.P., Prayagraj. The Commission will conduct the written exam and interview for the Principal and Headmaster posts in aided non-government schools (boys/girls). Appearing in the written exam or even succeeding in it does not make a candidate finally eligible for selection as Principal or Headmaster. Candidates must confirm for themselves that they meet the minimum qualifications. UPESSC Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1The notification gives the following application process. Only online applications through the Commission's OTR-based system are accepted. Applications sent by any other means will not be accepted.
  • 2Visit the official website www.upessc.up.gov.in.
  • 3Complete One Time Registration (OTR). Registration is compulsory before applying.
  • 4Read the User Manual (Hindi and English) and watch the tutorial videos available on the website before applying.
  • 5Fill the registration and application form in English only.
  • 6Upload your scanned signature on a white background and a passport-size photograph, both in JPG or JPEG format with a file size between 10 KB and 100 KB.
  • 7Upload certificates and documents in PDF format, with a file size between 20 KB and 200 KB.
  • 8Pay the application fee online as per your post (₹2,000 for Principal, ₹1,500 for Headmaster).
  • 9Submit the application. Take three printouts for future use and keep the fee receipt safe.
  • 10The notification does not give a direct application link or screen-by-screen steps. Candidates should use the official website and the User Manual.
  • 11After the application is finally submitted, no change is allowed in category, sub-category, date of birth, gender and similar details. Fill in all details correctly while registering and submitting.
  • 12A chance to correct errors will be given until the date fixed by the Commission (25 November 2026). Fees paid for an erroneous application will not be refunded.
  • 13If a candidate submits more than one application for the same post, only the last application is accepted and all others are treated as cancelled.
  • 14An application will be rejected if the photograph or signature is not uploaded in the prescribed size, or if the declaration is not accepted.
  • 15If the declaration made in the application is found to be false, the candidate's candidature will be cancelled. The candidate may be debarred from this and all other selections/examinations and face other penal action.
  • 16If at any stage it comes to light that a candidate has hidden information or filled it wrongly, the candidature will be cancelled and the candidate may be debarred from future examinations/selections.
  • 17Candidates must keep three copies of the submitted application. Two copies must be submitted to the Commission office at the time of interview, with two separate sets of the documents.
  • 18A candidate who tries to cheat, shows indiscipline or misbehaves in the exam hall can have candidature cancelled and may be barred from future examinations.
  • 19Bring a black ball point pen to the exam hall. Use only black ball point pen to fill the boxes and bubbles on the OMR sheet. Blue or other colour ball pens, fountain pens, gel pens, sketch pens, H.B. pencils and whitener are prohibited.
  • 20Once a bubble is darkened, no change is accepted. If more than one bubble is darkened for a question, it will not be evaluated and no marks are given.
  • 21Use of eraser, nail, blade or whitener on the OMR sheet is prohibited. An OMR sheet damaged in any way will be cancelled and not evaluated.
  • 22Fill the required information on the OMR sheet correctly and darken only the related bubbles. Do not make any other mark such as dots or ink marks. If the OMR sheet is not evaluated because of a mistake, the candidate is solely responsible.
  • 23Candidates can download the OMR answer sheet format from the Commission's website and practise before the exam.
  • 24Candidates with disabilities will be given a scribe as per the Government Orders dated 4 December 2019 and 8 September 2022. The scribe must be brought by the candidate. Such candidates must get the written exam scribe details registered at the Commission office at least one week before the exam. An additional 20 minutes per hour is allowed. No travel allowance is paid to the scribe.

UPESSC Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is UPESSC Principal Headmaster Recruitment 2026?
A
Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) has invited online applications for Principal (Advt. No. 08/2026) and Headmaster (Advt. No. 09/2026) posts in aided non-government schools of the Secondary Education Department, Uttar Pradesh.
Q2How many vacancies are available in UPESSC Principal Headmaster Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 2,647 vacancies: 1,475 for Principal (1,318 in boys' schools and 157 in girls' schools) and 1,172 for Headmaster (1,037 in boys' schools and 135 in girls' schools). The number of vacancies may increase or decrease.
Q3What is the last date to apply for UPESSC Principal Headmaster?
A
The last date to deposit the online application fee is 22 November 2026. The last date for corrections in the online application is 25 November 2026.
Q4When does UPESSC Principal Headmaster online application start?
A
Online registration (OTR) and application started on 23 October 2026.
Q5What is the qualification for UPESSC Principal and Headmaster?
A
A postgraduate degree with at least 50% marks, a B.Ed. degree (B.Ed. or B.P.Ed. for Headmaster) recognised by NCTE, and at least 4 years of teaching experience as Lecturer or Assistant Teacher in Class 9 to 12, or 4 years as Headmaster (High School for Principal; Junior High School for Headmaster), as detailed above.
Q6What is the age limit for UPESSC Principal Headmaster?
A
The minimum age is 30 years as on 1 July 2026, and the maximum age is 62 years.
Q7What is the UPESSC Principal Headmaster application fee?
A
The fee is ₹2,000 for Principal and ₹1,500 for Headmaster. The same fee applies to all categories listed in the notification.
Q8How can I apply online for UPESSC Principal Headmaster?
A
Register through One Time Registration on www.upessc.up.gov.in, fill the application in English, upload the photograph, signature and documents, pay the fee online and submit the application.
Q9What is the selection process for UPESSC Principal Headmaster?
A
Selection is based on a 360-mark written examination (120 multiple choice questions, 2 hours) and a 40-mark interview, making a total of 400 marks.
Q10What is the salary of UPESSC Principal Headmaster?
A
Principal is paid in Level-12, ₹78,800 to ₹2,09,200. Headmaster is paid in Level-10, ₹56,100 to ₹1,77,500.
