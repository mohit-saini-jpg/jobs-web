About FCI Dhar

The letterhead describes Food Craft Institute, Dhar as an institute of the Department of Tourism, Government of Madhya Pradesh, affiliated to NCHMCT, Noida. It is located at Gram Jetpura, Tehsil Dhar, Dis. Dhar (M.P.) 462101. The staff are needed to run its diploma and craft courses. The detailed notice and the application forms are issued by the HR Manager of Agile Security Force Private Limited. The agency's address is 3rd Floor, Uma Enclave, Road No 09, Banjara Hills, Hyderabad-500034.

Vacancy Details

The notice gives post-wise numbers that add up to 10 posts. No category-wise break-up is printed. FCI Dhar also needs a cook, cleaning staff, a gardener and security staff. The notice does not give the number or the pay for these jobs. For more information, it directs applicants to www.sihmind.mp.gov.in or to the office of the State Institute of Hotel Management, Indore, Tarik Khan ki Tekri, DPS / near Silicon Valley, Indore Bypass Road, Rau, Indore (M.P.) 453331.

Who Can Apply

Each post has its own qualification and experience, as printed below. The notice does not specify an age limit for any post. Candidates must meet any one of these three routes: Route 1: Both degrees below, with at least 60% aggregate marks in either the bachelor's or the master's degree: a full-time bachelor's degree in Hospitality and Hotel Administration / Hotel Management after 10+2, from a recognised university; and a full-time master's degree in Hospitality and Hotel Administration / Hotel Management / Tourism Management. OR Route 2: A full-time bachelor's degree in Hospitality and Hotel Administration / Hotel Management after 10+2, from a recognised university, with at least 60% aggregate marks, plus at least 2 years of industry experience. OR Route 3: NHTET qualified (conducted by NCHMCT) with the percentage prescribed for qualifying as Teaching Associate. Candidates with a Ph.D.

Pay and Tenure

Teaching Associate: Rs. 25,000 per month, consolidated. Accountant: salary of Rs. 18,000 to Rs. 20,000, which the notice marks as negotiable. Store Keeper/Clerk and Hostel Warden: Collector rate (skilled) for District Dhar, approximately Rs. 16,000. Lab Attendant and Maintenance/Foreman/Electrician: Collector rate for District Dhar, approximately Rs. 14,000 to Rs. 16,000. All engagements are through the outsourcing agency. The notice calls the Teaching Associate post full time. The length of the engagement is not specified in the notice.

Selection and Interview

The notice mentions an interview, a teaching ability test and a subject skill test. It says that candidates coming for these will not be paid any TA/DA (travel or daily allowance). The notice does not give the date or venue of these stages. It also does not say which test applies to which post.
