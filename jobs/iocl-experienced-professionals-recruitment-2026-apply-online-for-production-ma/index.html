Detailed Work Experience & Job Profile
PostWork Experience Required
Production Manager (Grade C)Minimum 10 years of post-qualification work experience in company(ies) having Poly Butadiene Rubber (PBR) units, including at least 5 years of producing Polybutadiene rubber in units with PBR production technology in India or abroad. Must include finalisation of Product Matrix, operation of PBR units, and organising repair/routine maintenance of PBR units.
Senior Production Manager (Grade D)Minimum 14 years of post-qualification work experience in company(ies) having PBR units, including at least 7 years of producing Polybutadiene rubber in units with PBR production technology in India or abroad. Same experience scope as Production Manager, at a senior/leadership level.
Senior Manager – PBR-Marketing (Grade D)Minimum 14 years of total post-qualification work experience with at least 7 years of relevant experience in rubber marketing or allied products, preferably in organisations catering to the tyre manufacturing industry, including exposure to synthetic rubber products such as PBR/SBR or similar elastomers.

IOCL Selection Process 2026

Eligibility of candidates is verified prior to the Personal Interview; only candidates meeting the eligibility criteria and having uploaded relevant, valid documents proceed further. Document Verification: Physical document verification of shortlisted candidates is conducted on the day of the Personal Interview. Personal Interview: Post document verification, eligible shortlisted candidates are interviewed by Committee(s) for final selection. Final selection and joining is subject to meeting the physical fitness criteria described in the Pre-Employment Medical Examination (PEME) Guidelines, available on the "Latest Job Opening" section of www.iocl.com. Candidates must meet PEME parameters for Technical Functions.
