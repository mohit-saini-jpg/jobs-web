How Is the Seat Allotment Prepared?

The MHT CET CAP Round 1 seat allotment is prepared based on the following factors:

  • Candidate's final merit rank (MHT CET/JEE Main, as applicable)
  • Category and reservation policy
  • Preferences filled during the option/choice-filling process
  • Availability of seats across participating institutes

Freeze, Float, and Slide: What Do They Mean?

After checking the allotment result, candidates must choose one of the following options during the seat acceptance process:

OptionMeaning
FreezeCandidate accepts the allotted seat as final and does not want to participate in further CAP rounds.
FloatCandidate accepts the current seat but wants to be considered for an upgrade to any college/course in the next round. If a better seat is allotted, the earlier seat is cancelled.
SlideCandidate accepts the current seat but wants to be considered for an upgrade to a different course within the same allotted institute in the next round.

How to Check and Download the MHT CET CAP Round 1 Seat Allotment Result 2026

  • Visit the official MHT CET CAP portal: fe2026.mahacet.org or cetcell.mahacet.org.
  • Log in using your Application ID and Password (or Date of Birth, as applicable).
  • Click on the "CAP Round 1 Seat Allotment Result" link.
  • View your allotted institute, branch, and category details on screen.
  • Download and save the Provisional Seat Allotment Letter in PDF format.
  • Print a hard copy of the allotment letter - a mobile screenshot may not be accepted at reporting centres.

What to Do After Checking Your Allotment Result

  • Review the allotted college, branch, and category carefully.
  • Decide whether to choose Freeze, Float, or Slide, based on your priorities.
  • Complete the online seat acceptance process and pay the applicable seat acceptance fee between August 3 and August 5, 2026.
  • Report to the allotted institute (physically, where required) with original documents and photocopies within the same window.
  • Complete institute-level document verification and collect your admission confirmation receipt.

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Complete seat acceptance and reporting strictly within the August 3–5, 2026 window; missing the deadline may lead to forfeiture of the allotted seat.
  • Carry a printed copy of the allotment letter - screenshots may not be accepted at facilitation centres.
  • Keep both original documents and self-attested photocopies ready before reporting.
  • Ensure your Non-Creamy Layer certificate (if applicable) is dated on or after April 1, 2024.
  • Candidates who neither accept the seat nor report within the deadline may forfeit the Round 1 allotment and could be excluded from further CAP rounds, as reported.
  • Candidates who Freeze their seat will not be considered for CAP Round 2.
  • Use an updated browser (Chrome or Firefox) and clear your cache if you face login issues due to heavy traffic on the portal.
