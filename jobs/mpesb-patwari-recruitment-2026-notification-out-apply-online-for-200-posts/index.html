MPESB Patwari Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must meet the qualification, age, and category-specific conditions below to be eligible for MPESB Patwari Recruitment 2026. To apply for the MPESB Patwari Recruitment 2026, candidates need a graduate degree from a recognised university in any discipline. In addition, candidates must hold a CPCT (Computer Proficiency Certification Test) score card along with Hindi typing qualification. Candidates who have not cleared CPCT at the time of selection will be allowed to appear for it, but they must clear it within 3 years of their probation period after appointment, failing which the appointment will be cancelled. The age limit for MPESB Patwari Recruitment 2026 (PwD category, for permanent residents of Madhya Pradesh) is 18 to 45 years. Age will be calculated as on 1 January 2026. MPESB Patwari Salary

Selected candidates for the Patwari post will draw a pay scale of Rs. 5,200–20,200 with Grade Pay of Rs. 2,100. During the probation period, candidates on direct recruitment will receive a fixed stipend: 70% of the minimum pay scale in the first year, 80% in the second year, and 90% in the third year. Full pay scale benefits begin once the probation period is successfully completed, as per the applicable Madhya Pradesh government circular.

MPESB Patwari Selection Process

Selection for MPESB Patwari Recruitment 2026 is based on a computer-based online examination followed by document verification and district-wise counselling. The exam is conducted online in two shifts per day. Minimum qualifying marks are 50% for unreserved candidates and 40% for SC/ST/OBC/PwD candidates (a 10% relaxation from the unreserved cut-off). EWS candidates also get a 10% relaxation on the minimum qualifying marks as per the applicable government circular. After the exam, MPESB will prepare a percentile-based merit list of qualified candidates. District allotment is done through a single round of counselling, based on the district preferences candidates submitted in their application form, matched against merit rank and category-wise vacancy.
