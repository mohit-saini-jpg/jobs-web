About Lucknow Anganwadi Recruitment 2026

The notice is issued by the District Programme Officer, Lucknow, Shreyash Kumar, for the Child Development Projects (Bal Vikas Pariyojana) running in the urban and rural areas of Lucknow district. The recruitment is a direct recruitment process held under Government Order No. 2025/3313/58-1-2025 (1917687) dated 17 September 2025 of the Principal Secretary, Child Development Service and Nutrition, Uttar Pradesh, Lucknow. The notice states that the entire recruitment process will be completed on the basis of this Government Order.

Vacancies by Project / Block

The 428 posts are spread over 10 Child Development Projects of Lucknow district, as printed in the notice. Who Can Apply

Only women candidates are eligible for the advertised posts. Qualification: Minimum educational qualification is Intermediate or its equivalent. Age limit: Minimum 18 years and maximum 35 years. On 01.07.2026 the applicant's age must not be less than 18 years or more than 35 years. Proof of age: Only the date of birth recorded in the High School marksheet and certificate is accepted. Residence: The applicant must be a resident of the same gram sabha, or of the concerned ward in urban areas, in which the post is vacant. The women-only condition applies to the Anganwadi Helper post as well. The notice does not state the qualification, age limit or residence condition for the Anganwadi Helper post; check the full qualification details on the district office notice boards, Government Order No. 2025/3313/58-1-2025 (1917687) dated 17 September 2025, and the detailed information uploaded on htt

UP Anganwadi Lucknow Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection will be made on the basis of preference and merit. Merit will be decided on the marks obtained from Intermediate to Post-graduation. In selection, preference will be given as per rules to BPL, widow and divorced / abandoned women, according to the Government Order. The notice sets out this order among residents of the same gram sabha, or ward in urban areas: Respectively, a widow from a family living below the poverty line, a legally divorced / abandoned woman, and a woman from a BPL family. If no widow, divorced or abandoned candidate from a BPL family is found, a woman from a family living below the poverty line of the same gram sabha / ward gets preference. If no BPL widow, divorced or abandoned woman, or BPL family candidate is found, preference goes respectively to a widow, an abandoned woman and a divorced woman living above the poverty line in the same gram sabha / ward.
