Bihar DElEd Admission Eligibility Criteria 2026

Candidates must meet the following educational qualification and eligibility conditions to apply for Bihar DElEd Counselling 2026:

  • Candidates must have secured at least 50% marks in Higher Secondary (+2) or an equivalent examination.
  • A relaxation of 5% in qualifying marks is available for all reserved category and divyang (disabled) candidates.
  • Urdu candidates who have passed the Maulvi examination with 50% marks are also eligible.
  • Candidates who qualified 10+2 under a vocational course, or Intermediate/10+2 after Madhyama, or Intermediate/10+2 after Foukaniya, are eligible.
  • Candidates holding only a Shastri, Polytechnic, ITI, or any other equivalent qualification are not eligible for D.El.Ed admission.

How to Apply for Bihar DElEd Counselling 2026

  • Visit the official Bihar DElEd website: bsebdeled.com.
  • Find and click the link for the Common Application Form (CAF) for DElEd Counselling 2026.
  • Register using your required details and generate a login (User ID and Password).
  • Fill in the CAF with personal, educational, and category details as asked.
  • Pay the applicable application fee online as per your category.
  • Submit the form and keep a printout/PDF copy of the confirmation for future reference.
