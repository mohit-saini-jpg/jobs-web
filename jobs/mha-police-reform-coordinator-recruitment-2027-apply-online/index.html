MHA Police Reform Coordinator Recruitment 2027 : Job Overview

The Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA), Government of India, has invited nominations of serving police officers in the rank of DIG/IG or equivalent for secondment to the United Nations post of Police Reform Coordinator, P-5 in the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization Mission in the Central African Republic (MINUSCA), Bangui. The nominations go through proper channel and must be sent by email to [email protected] and [email protected]. Applications received after 15 January 2027 will not be considered.

About the Notice

The circular is issued by the Pers. Policy Section of the Police-II Division, Ministry of Home Affairs, Kartavya Bhawan-3, New Delhi. It forwards a vacancy announcement received from the Ministry of External Affairs through the Permanent Mission of India to the United Nations (PMI to UN). The post is advertised by the UN Department of Peace Operations (DPO) for MINUSCA, a peacekeeping mission whose posts are financed by the support account of peacekeeping operations.

Where and How to Send

Individual officers do not apply directly. Nominations of eligible and willing officers are forwarded through proper channel to MHA: State officers: through the Home Department of the State concerned. Officers of organisations: with the approval of the Head of Organisation. AGMUT cadre officers: through the UT Division, MHA. The Chief Secretaries of UTs and the Commissioner of Police, Delhi have been told that nominations are to be submitted through the UT Division, MHA only. All documents of each nominated candidate must be compiled in a separate single file (PDF format only) and emailed to [email protected] and [email protected] before 15 January 2027. Under the Digital India initiative, documents are sent in digital mode only and no hard or physical copies are required. The notice does not prescribe an email subject line.

Posts, Eligibility and Pay

The UN Job Opening 2026-MINUSCA-89650-DPO dated 27 October 2026 sets these requirements for the Police Reform Coordinator, P-5: Education: Advanced university degree (Masters or equivalent) in law, police management, law enforcement, security studies, criminal justice, business or public administration, program management, change management, or related area. A first level university degree with a combination of relevant academic qualifications and extensive experience in law enforcement, in the areas of management, planning and administration may be accepted in lieu of the advanced university degree. Graduation from a certified Police Academy or other national or international Law Enforcement Training Institution is required. MHA Recruitment 2026 Job Description

Under the guidance and supervision of the Police Commissioner, the Reform Coordinator is responsible for the development of the Police and Gendarmerie in the Central African Republic. Duties as printed include: Review the existing local Police and Gendarmerie structure and systems, including personnel and skills requirements and the criminal justice system, as the basis for reform recommendations. Develop plans, programs and project proposals for the reform, restructuring and institutional development of the National Police and Gendarmerie; propose fund-raising initiatives on international funding. Advise and assist local counterparts on reform initiatives and on recruitment, vetting and selection policies and procedures. MHA Police Reform Coordinator Recruitment 2027 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

The selection decision is taken by the United Nations. The final decision on selecting an individual is also subject to human rights screening. Candidates approved for the roster may be selected for positions with similar functions (same functional title and level) without a new Job Opening, and remain on the roster for two years after the first day of the month following the selection decision.

MHA Police Reform Coordinator Recruitment 2027 : Important Instructions

Nominations without Vigilance clearance and Cadre clearance (for all officers) will be summarily rejected. No format other than the enclosed specimen (duly typed) will be accepted; hand-written PHP will not be accepted. Secondment is for an initial one year, with possible extension for a second and third year; in exceptional circumstances a fourth year, but not longer. During secondment, police personnel are not eligible for reassignment or transfer to any other seconded position. Currently serving active-duty seconded police personnel on a UN staff contract will not be considered; earlier seconded officers may reapply after a break of at least one year. Officers should not make direct queries with MHA or UNHQ regarding selection; MHA will not entertain personal queries from individual applicants. For enquiries the notice prints the telephone 011-24010236 (Deepak Sharma, Under Secretary)

MHA Police Reform Coordinator Recruitment 2027 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is the last date for MHA Police Reform Coordinator nominations?
A
Nominations must reach MHA by 15 January 2027; applications received after this date will not be considered.
Q2Who can be nominated for the Police Reform Coordinator, P-5 post?
A
Officers in active police service in the rank of DIG/IG or equivalent who meet the UN Job Opening criteria can be nominated through proper channel.
Q3What qualification is required?
A
An advanced university degree (Masters or equivalent) in a related area, or a first level degree with relevant qualifications and extensive law enforcement experience, plus graduation from a certified Police Academy or Law Enforcement Training Institution.

A
A minimum of 10 years (12 years in absence of advanced degree) of active policing experience, including seven years at senior policy making level.

A
Yes, fluency in oral and written French is required, and knowledge of English is highly desirable.
Q6How are the nominations sent?
A
All documents are compiled in a single PDF and emailed through proper channel to [email protected] and [email protected]; no hard copies are required.
Q7What documents are needed?
A
The P-11 form, EAC, Annexure-II bio-data, APAR/ACR gradings of the last five years, the Human Rights Certificate and a signed self-attestation.
Q8How long is the secondment?
A
The initial period is 12 months (extendible), with possible extension for a second and third year and, in exceptional circumstances, a fourth year but not longer.
Q9Will a nomination without vigilance clearance be accepted?
A
No, nominations without Vigilance clearance and Cadre clearance will be summarily rejected.
