Gauhati University Web Developer Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Gauhati University, Guwahati is filling 1 Web Developer post on a purely contractual basis at a consolidated remuneration of ₹50,000/- a month, initially for eleven (11) months. Indian citizens with a Bachelor's Degree in a listed computing or allied discipline (or any Bachelor's Degree with a recognised web/software development certification or diploma) and at least 2 years of hands-on website and CMS experience can apply online through the Gauhati University Non-Teaching Recruitment Candidate Portal from 23 November 2026 until 8 December 2026 (midnight).
