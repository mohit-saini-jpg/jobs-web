Milkfed Verka Deputy Manager Salary Structure 2026

During the probation period of 2 years, selected candidates will be paid a minimum basic pay of Rs. 56,100/- per month after statutory deductions. This probation pay does not include other allowances admissible to regular employees, except travelling allowance and mobile allowance. On successful completion of probation and regularization, the candidate will be allowed the regular pay scale of the post along with all admissible allowances as per Milkfed's service rules.

Milkfed Verka Deputy Manager Selection Process 2026

Selection is based on a written test and relevant work experience The written test will be of 2 hours' duration with 100 MCQs across 5 sections/components, maximum marks 100 Negative marking of 0.25 marks applies for every wrong answer Final merit = 80% weightage of written test marks + marks for relevant work experience (maximum 20 marks) Experience marks are awarded on a graded scale, from 1 mark for every completed 3 months up to a maximum of 20 marks for 5 years or more of relevant experience Minimum qualifying marks: 40% for Unreserved category, 35% for all reserved categories The written test will be conducted only in Chandigarh Test medium: English, except questions on Punjabi language In case of a tie in final score, the older candidate is ranked higher
