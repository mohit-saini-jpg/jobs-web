About IBPS

The Institute of Banking Personnel Selection is an autonomous body set up by the Reserve Bank of India, Central Financial Institutions and Public Sector Banks. These posts are based at its office at IBPS House, Kandivali (East), Mumbai, which is also where the selection process takes place.

Application Fee

The fee (intimation charges) is Rs. 250/- per candidate. It is paid online only, between 29 October 2026 and 12 November 2026, by Debit Card (RuPay/Visa/MasterCard/Maestro), Credit Card, Internet Banking, IMPS, Cash Card, Mobile Wallet or UPI. The candidate pays any bank transaction charges. To apply for more than one post, submit a separate form and fee for each post. If you apply more than once for the same post, only your latest complete application counts and the other fees are forfeited.

IBPS Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

IBPS first screens the applications against the minimum eligibility criteria and gives preference to candidates staying in Mumbai. Shortlisted candidates are called for document verification, an online examination and a personal interview. All of these take place at IBPS House, 90 Ft DP Road, Behind Thakur Polytechnic, Off. W E Highway, Kandivali (East), Mumbai 400101. The exam is in English, with a composite time of 90 minutes for each post. IBPS may change the exam structure. Negative marking: 0.25 of the marks for a question is deducted for each wrong answer. Unanswered questions carry no penalty. Cut-off: you need a minimum score in each test, and a total high enough to be shortlisted. IBPS fixes the cut-offs based on the number of vacancies. Interview: candidates who pass the online exam are interviewed the same day, or the next day depending on the response. Merit list: a committee

Service Conditions

Probation: 1 year from the date of joining. Confirmation depends on completing it satisfactorily and on the IBPS staff rules. Service bond: Rs. 1 lakh. You must serve at least 2 years from joining or pay Rs. 1 lakh to IBPS. The bond period may be extended by the number of loss of pay (LOP) days. Posting: Mumbai only, as a permanent posting. You must arrange your own accommodation. Candidates from outside Mumbai are welcome if they are committed to relocating permanently. Commitment: all positions require full 24x7 commitment to the Institute. At joining: a medical fitness certificate from a civil surgeon or a doctor empanelled with LIC of India/PSBs, and police verification, are mandatory.

IBPS Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Go to www.ibps.in and click "CLICK HERE TO APPLY ONLINE" on the Home Page.
  • 2Click "CLICK HERE FOR NEW REGISTRATION" and enter your basic details. Note down the provisional registration number and password, which are also sent to you by email and SMS.
  • 3Upload your photograph (4.5cm × 3.5cm, 20kb-50kb), signature (10kb-20kb, not in capital letters), left thumb impression (20 KB-50 KB), handwritten declaration (50 KB-100 KB) and brief resume (pdf). Also upload your SSC/SSLC/10th certificate and your documents for age, qualification and experience (PDF, A4, up to 500 KB each). Capture a live photo by webcam or mobile phone.
  • 4Write the declaration in English on white paper with black ink, not in capital letters: "I, _______ (Name of the candidate), hereby declare that all the information submitted by me in the application form is correct, true and valid. I will present the supporting documents as and when required."
  • 5Check every detail using "SAVE AND NEXT". No changes are allowed after you click "COMPLETE REGISTRATION".
  • 6Pay the fee online and keep a printout of the e-receipt and the application form. Do not send the printout to IBPS.
  • 7Use a personal email ID and keep it active, because IBPS may send call letters to it.
  • 8Bring the originals and one set of self-attested photocopies of:
  • 9Proof of date of birth (birth certificate, SSLC/Std. X certificate or leaving certificate)
  • 10Photo ID such as a PAN Card, Passport, Permanent Driving Licence, Voter's Card, Aadhar/E-aadhar or Employee ID. A Ration Card or Learner's Licence is not accepted.
  • 11Address proof (electricity bill, passport, property agreement, telephone bill or bank photo passbook)
  • 12Mark sheets and certificates of your qualifications
  • 13Experience certificates showing the nature of your duties
  • 14Your latest payslip and your appointment, promotion or relieving letter
  • 15A No Objection Certificate in original, if you work in a government or quasi-government office, a PSU, a nationalised bank or a financial institution

IBPS Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many posts has IBPS advertised?
A
There are three posts: Server Administrator Gr. 'D', Assistant Gr. 'B' and Programming Assistant Gr. 'B'. The notice prints no vacancy numbers, so each post counts as 1 vacancy. A wait list valid for six months will also be kept.
Q2What is the starting salary of an IBPS Assistant?
A
Basic pay is ₹ 25,500/-, with a total emolument of about ₹ 54,210 a month and an annual CTC of about ₹ 10.57 Lakhs.
Q3Can freshers apply?
A
No. The Assistant and Programming Assistant posts need at least 2 years of post-qualification experience, and Server Administrator needs at least 3 years.
Q4Is a distance-education degree accepted?
A
No. Degrees obtained through correspondence, distance education or the open university system are not accepted.
Q5Is there negative marking in the online exam?
A
Yes. A quarter (0.25) of the marks for a question is deducted for each wrong answer. Questions left blank carry no penalty.
Q6Where will the exam and interview be held?
A
Both take place only at IBPS House, 90 Ft DP Road, Behind Thakur Polytechnic, Off. W E Highway, Kandivali (East), Mumbai 400101, and candidates pay their own travel costs.
Q7Is there a service bond?
A
Yes. Selected candidates sign a Rs. 1 lakh bond and must serve at least 2 years, or pay Rs. 1 lakh to IBPS.
Q8Can I apply for more than one post?
A
Yes, but you need a separate application form and a separate Rs. 250 fee for each post. An application for one post cannot be transferred to another.
Q9Does IBPS provide accommodation in Mumbai?
A
No. You must arrange your own housing, and candidates already living in Mumbai get preference.
Q10What does the handwritten declaration need to look like?
A
Write it in English, in your own handwriting, with black ink on white paper, and not in capital letters. Scan it at 800 x 400 pixels with a file size of 50 KB-100 KB.
