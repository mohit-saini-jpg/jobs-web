SSUHS GNM Entrance Exam Result 2026 - Details

SSUHS declared the GNMEE 2026 result 38 days after the exam, a slightly longer gap than the previous year, when the exam was held on 13 July 2025 and the result was declared in the first half of August 2025, about four weeks later.

Candidates who appeared for the exam can log in on the official result portal using their Application Number and Password to view their scorecard, subject-wise marks, and merit position. This result is for admission to GNM courses in government and private institutes across Assam, under the Directorate of Health Services.

As per the official Educational Notice (No.SSUHS/30/2026/4195, dated 5 August 2026) issued by the Controller of Examinations, SSUHS has fixed category-wise minimum qualifying percentiles along with the corresponding marks for the GNM Entrance Examination 2026. SSUHS GNMEE 2026 Marks Distribution

The GNM Entrance Exam consists of 100 questions of 1 mark each, set at the Class 10th standard. The scorecard shows subject-wise marks along with the total score.

There is no negative marking in the SSUHS GNM Entrance Exam.

How the SSUHS GNM Merit List 2026 Is Prepared

Merit is decided on the total marks scored out of 100. In case two or more candidates score the same total, the tie is resolved in this order:

  • Marks scored in English (checked first)
  • Marks scored in General Science (if the tie continues)
  • Marks scored in Mathematics (final tie-breaker)

SSUHS has not published a separate minimum qualifying mark list along with the GNMEE 2026 result; the merit list is prepared directly from the total marks scored. Candidates who appear in the merit list proceed to counselling and document verification conducted by the Directorate of Health Services.

How to Check SSUHS GNM Entrance Exam Result 2026

Follow these steps to check your scorecard and merit position:

  • Open the SSUHS GNM Entrance Result link given in the Important Links section below.
  • Log in to the candidate portal using your Application Number and Password.
  • Your scorecard with subject-wise and total marks will appear on screen.
  • Check the merit list to find your rank and qualifying status.
  • Download the scorecard and keep a printout for counselling and admission.

Note: You will not be able to log in and view the result without your Application Number and Password. Keep these credentials safe until the admission process is complete.

What Happens After the SSUHS GNM Result 2026

Candidates who qualify and appear in the merit list are called for counselling, where the Directorate of Health Services verifies eligibility, category, and other required documents before seats are allotted.

Important Instructions

  • Use only your registered Application Number and Password to log in no other login method is available.
  • Download and save the scorecard immediately after checking the result; keep multiple copies.
  • SSUHS has not released a separate cutoff or minimum qualifying marks list check your own score and rank directly on the scorecard.
  • Keep original documents ready in advance, as counselling requires document verification.
  • For queries related to hall ticket or login credential issues, refer to the SSUHS GNM Admit Card page.
