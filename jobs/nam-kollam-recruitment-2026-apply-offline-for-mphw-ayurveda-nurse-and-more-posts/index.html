NAM Kollam Important Instructions

Applications must be submitted in a sealed envelope either directly or through post; E-mail applications will not be accepted. Incomplete or incorrect application forms will be summarily rejected. Applications without copies of certificates will not be accepted and will be rejected. Failure to submit an equivalency certificate for equivalent qualifications will result in rejection. Candidates must possess a personal E-mail ID and Mobile Number for all communications regarding the recruitment process. Furnishing false, tampered, or fabricated information will lead to rejection of the candidature at any stage.
