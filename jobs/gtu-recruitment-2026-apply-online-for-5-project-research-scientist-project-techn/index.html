About GTU

Gujarat Technological University was established by the Government of Gujarat under Gujarat Act No. 20 of 2007. The posts are in its School of Applied Sciences and Technology (GTU-SAST) at the GTU Chandkheda Campus, Ahmedabad, where the Major Research Project "Scalable Engineering and Environmental Technologies for Exposure Prevention" has been sanctioned by the Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR), Govt. of India. The Principal Investigator of the project is Prof. (Dr.) Vaibhav Bhatt.

Selection and Interview

Candidates will be shortlisted for the interview based on their qualifications and relevant experience. Candidates are required to attend the interview at their own expense, and no TA/DA shall be provided for attending the interview.
