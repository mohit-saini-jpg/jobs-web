VMC Apprentice Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must hold the qualification specified for their chosen trade in the table above. The Office Assistant trade (S. VMC Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Applying involves two separate steps, both of which must be completed for the application to be accepted.
  • 2Register as an apprentice on the national Apprenticeship India portal: apprenticeshipindia.gov.in.
  • 3After registering your apprenticeship trade preference on that portal, complete your eKYC update on the same portal.
  • 4After registering the trade preference and updating eKYC on apprenticeshipindia.gov.in, submit the online application through the VMC website: vmc.gov.in/Recruitment/Recruitment.aspx.
  • 5Ensure the mobile number and e-mail address entered in the application form are correct and currently in your own use, as these will be used for all further document-verification communication.
  • 6Candidates who complete only one of the two steps - portal registration or the VMC website application - will not have a valid application; both are mandatory.
