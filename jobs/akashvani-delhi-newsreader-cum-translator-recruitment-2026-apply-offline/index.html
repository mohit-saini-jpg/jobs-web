About Akashvani News Services Division

Prasar Bharati calls itself India's public service broadcaster. Its News Services Division: Akashvani is based in New Delhi and prepares and broadcasts radio news. The Urdu unit of the division is filling this post.

Akashvani Newsreader-cum-Translator (Urdu) Vacancy Details 2026

The job is to translate news stories and news-based text from English to Urdu and from Urdu to English, and to read the news bulletins. Candidates who pass the selection are recommended for empanelment. The Head of Office, Director General (News), approves the empanelment. FREEJOBALERT DAILY QUIZ Preparing for Govt Exams? Akashvani Newsreader-cum-Translator (Urdu) Eligibility 2026

Interested candidates of Indian origin who live in Delhi and the NCR area. Applicants must be at least 21 years old and no older than 50 years. Graduate from a recognized university. Diploma/Degree in Journalism/Mass Communication from a recognised institution/University. Proficiency in English and Urdu language. A voice suited to broadcasting. Diploma/Degree in Translation from a recognised institution. Two years of experience of journalistic work in Radio/TV/Print. Knowledge of basic computer applications. Candidates who work in Government or autonomous bodies must get a No Objection Certificate from their employer. For the complete eligibility conditions of the post, check the official notification.

Akashvani Delhi Newsreader cum Translator Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

There are two stages of selection. Both test your professional competence and your knowledge of Urdu. All tests and interviews are held in Delhi. Candidates who score 50 or more in the written test are called for the voice test. The Selection Committee recommends for empanelment those who score 50 or more in the voice test and interview. Only shortlisted candidates are informed, and they are told by email. The final results are posted on www.newsonair.gov.in .
StagePattern
Written test100 marks, three hours. Covers translation from English to Urdu and from Urdu to English, short notes on current affairs and general knowledge. All answers must be written in Urdu.
Voice test and interview100 marks. The voice test checks voice quality, modulation, diction, pronunciation and accent. It may be followed by an interview with the Selection Committee to judge your suitability for radio news.

Service Conditions

Working on a casual assignment basis for NSD: Akashvani gives no right to regularisation of service. Bookings are offered from time to time, depending on the organisation's needs. They will not normally exceed six days in a month. The casual assignee must not take part in any activity that could harm the image of Akashvani/Prasar Bharati. They must also not be linked to any political or other activity that could affect the objectivity and accuracy of Akashvani News.

Q1Who can apply for the Akashvani Urdu Newsreader-cum-Translator post?

Yes. Along with graduation, you need a Diploma/Degree in Journalism/Mass Communication from a recognised institution or university.

No, experience is not required. Two years of journalistic work in Radio/TV/Print is listed only as a desirable qualification, along with a Diploma/Degree in Translation and knowledge of basic computer applications.
