CGSSB Assistant Teacher Recruitment 2026 Exam Pattern

The written exam will consist of 100 objective-type (multiple choice) questions. Each question carries 1 mark. Total duration of the exam is 2 hours (10:00 AM to 12:15 PM as per admit card timing). Each question has 4 answer options; candidates must select one correct option. Negative marking is applicable: 1/4th mark will be deducted for a wrong answer or for marking more than one option for the same question. No marks (zero) will be given for unattempted questions. Answers must be marked on the OMR sheet using a black or blue ball-point pen only.

Documents Required on Exam Day

Printed copy of the admit card (single-sided printout of every page) One original photo ID proof: Voter ID Card / Driving Licence / PAN Card / Aadhaar Card Black or blue ball-point pen Note: The admit card should be preserved even after the exam, as it may be required again at the time of document verification, selection, or joining.

CGSSB LSAT26 Exam Day Guidelines and Dress Code

Candidates must reach the exam centre at least 2 hours before the exam start time for identity verification and frisking. The main gate of the exam centre will close 30 minutes before the exam starts. Since the exam begins at 10:00 AM, the gate will close at 9:30 AM. CGSSB Assistant Teacher Answer Key Objection Process

After the exam, CGSSB will release a model answer key on its website. Candidates who disagree with the model answer key can raise objections online by logging in with their registration/profile ID and date of birth. A fee of Rs. 50/- per question must be paid online to raise an objection; objections without fee payment are not accepted. Objections sent by post, email, or submitted in person at the CGSSB office will not be accepted. Each objection must be submitted separately for each question; combined objections for multiple questions in a single submission will be rejected. All objections will be examined by subject experts, and their decision on the final answer key will be treated as final.
