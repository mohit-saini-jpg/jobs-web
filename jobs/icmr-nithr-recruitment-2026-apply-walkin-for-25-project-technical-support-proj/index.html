ICMR NITHR Selection Process

Selection is made through a walk-in interview conducted by the selection committee at ICMR NITHR, Jabalpur. Only eligible candidates as per essential qualification and age criteria will be allowed to appear. Candidates must carry the duly filled application form with original and photocopies of certificates. Late, delayed, or incomplete applications will be summarily rejected without intimation. Meeting the essential qualification does not guarantee selection. Selected candidates must produce original documents before joining; if found ineligible, selection will be cancelled immediately.

Important Terms & Conditions

All posts are purely temporary and co-terminable with the respective project. Age is reckoned from the date of the walk-in interview. Age relaxation for SC/ST/OBC is as per DoPT/Government of India norms; no relaxation for unreserved posts. Persons already in regular time-scale government service are not eligible. Preference is given to local candidates and those with higher qualifications/experience. The number of vacancies may vary at a later stage. Any addendum/corrigendum will be published only on www.nirth.res.in.

ICMR NITHR Recruitment 2026 - Apply Walkin : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Download the application format from www.nirth.res.in or www.main.icmr.nic.in.
  • 2Fill the application form completely with full address, PIN code, mobile number, and email ID.
  • 3Attach self-attested copies of certificates (qualification, age proof, experience, category certificate).
  • 4Provide details of two responsible references from your locality.
  • 5Report to ICMR NITHR, Nagpur Road, Post Garha, Jabalpur, on the applicable interview date, between 9:00 AM and 11:00 AM.
  • 6Submit the filled application with documents at the venue; no entry after 11:00 AM.
  • 7No TA/DA is paid for attending the interview or joining.
