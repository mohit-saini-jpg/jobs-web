OSSSC Ophthalmic Officer Exam Pattern 2026

The written examination carries 100 marks and has a duration of two hours . It will be held in objective multiple-choice format through OMR or CBRE mode, as notified later. Meeting the minimum qualifying marks does not guarantee selection. Final shortlisting depends on examination performance, category-wise merit and the number of vacancies. OSSSC Ophthalmic Officer District Preference and Posting 2026

Ophthalmic Officer is a District Cadre post . Candidates are recruited for district establishments under the Health & Family Welfare Department. The commission will consider the district preference, merit position, applicable reservation and vacancies during the final allocation process. The commission may notify an option to revise or re-exercise district preferences before the allotment stage. If a candidate does not use the available revision facility, the district preferences already submitted may be treated as final. Candidates should monitor official recruitment notices and meet any specified preference-revision deadlines.

OSSSC Ophthalmic Officer Document Verification 2026

After the written examination, OSSSC will prepare a provisional merit list and shortlist eligible candidates for document verification. The commission may shortlist candidates for verification based on the number of vacancies and the applicable category-wise requirements, including a minimum shortlisting ratio of 1.25 times the notified category-wise vacancies where applicable. Original qualification, council registration, age, language and reservation certificates must be produced before the competent verification authority. Important: Provisional inclusion in the merit list does not guarantee appointment. Any discrepancy between the online application and original documents may lead to rejection.

OSSSC Ophthalmic Officer Admit Card 2026

OSSSC will announce the written examination date and admit card availability through its official website. Eligible candidates must download their admission letter using the designated applicant login or admission-letter facility. The commission will not send admission letters by post. Applicants should regularly check official notices, their registered mobile number and their email account.
