UP Anganwadi Pilibhit Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

This is a direct recruitment for Anganwadi Worker and Anganwadi Helper posts in the child development projects running in the urban and rural areas of Pilibhit district, made under Government Order No. 1/1092245/2025/3313/58-1-2025 (1917687) dated 17 September 2025.

About Pilibhit Anganwadi Recruitment 2026

The recruitment is conducted by the Office of the District Programme Officer, Janpad Pilibhit, which oversees the child development (Bal Vikas) projects of the district. The posts are at Anganwadi centres run under these projects in urban and rural areas, and the notice was signed by District Programme Officer Yugal Kishore Sanguri. A copy of the notice was sent to the Director, Bal Vikas Sewa evam Pushtahar, Uttar Pradesh, Lucknow, and it was to be published in Dainik Jagran and Dainik Hindustan.

Pilibhit Anganwadi Eligibility Criteria 2026

Other conditions printed in the notice: Only women candidates are eligible for the advertised posts. The applicant must be a permanent resident of the ward / gram sabha of the vacant post. If no application is received from the gram sabha, residents of the concerned Nyay Panchayat are considered. For proof of residence, the residence certificate must carry the name of the ward / gram panchayat; this is mandatory. Two women of the same family will not be appointed as Anganwadi Worker / Helper at one Anganwadi centre. For the centre-wise eligibility of each post, check the detailed notice on http://upanganwadibharti.in and the notice boards listed above.

Pilibhit Anganwadi Age Limit 2026

Applicants for both posts must be a minimum of 18 years and a maximum of 35 years old on the cut-off date of 01.07.2026. Only the date of birth recorded in the High School marksheet / certificate is accepted for age. The notice does not mention any age relaxation.

Pilibhit Anganwadi Salary 2026

The notice describes both posts as honorarium-based but does not mention the honorarium amount or any allowances.

Pilibhit Anganwadi Selection Process 2026

Merit is determined on the basis of the candidate's qualifications from Intermediate up to postgraduate. As per the Government Order, preference in selection is given to women from families living below the poverty line, widows, abandoned (parityakta) women and divorced women. Selection follows the SC / ST / OBC reservation laid down in Government Order No. 1/1092245/2025/3313/58-1-2025 (1917687) dated 17 September 2025. For the poverty-line preference, the income limit fixed by Government Order No. 22/2015/2123/26-2-2015 dated 14.09.2015 (as amended) of Social Welfare Section-2, Uttar Pradesh is applied. At present it is Rs 46080.00 a year in rural areas and Rs 56460.00 a year in urban areas.

Pilibhit Anganwadi Recruitment 2026 Important Instructions

If any error is found in the application, or any fact, figure, or marks obtained / maximum marks in the educational records are found wrong or forged, the candidature will be cancelled. The number of vacancies may be decreased or increased. For more information, contact the Child Development Project Officer's office of your development block or Room No. 37, District Programme Officer Office, Vikas Bhawan, Pilibhit during office hours on working days. All Child Development Project Officers of Pilibhit have been directed to paste the full details of the posts on the notice boards of project offices, Tehsils, development block offices and the concerned gram panchayats / wards.
