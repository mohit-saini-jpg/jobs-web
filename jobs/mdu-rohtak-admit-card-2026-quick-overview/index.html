Notification DatePost Date: 02/09/2026

Maharshi Dayanand University (MDU), Rohtak, has announced the Mercy Chance/ Special Chance scheme 2026 for eligible students seeking an additional opportunity to clear their pending examinations. The application process reportedly began on 15 May 2026, with the last date extended to 30 July 2026. The admit card is reported to have been released on 1 September 2026, while the examinations are expected to be conducted in September 2026. Candidates can download their admit cards using their Student ID/No. or Registration Number through the official MDU admit card portal. Since some dates and fee details are based on third-party reporting, candidates should verify the latest information on the official MDU website before proceeding.

What You Need to Download the Admit Card

The official admit card portal only asks for one of the following identifiers - no other document upload is required at this stage:

  • Student ID/No, OR
  • Registration Number

The portal does not list any additional documents (such as photo ID or fee receipt) as mandatory for downloading the admit card itself. However, candidates should carry a valid original photo ID proof to the exam center, as is standard practice, even though this is not explicitly stated on the admit card download page.

How to Download MDU Mercy Chance/ Special Chance Admit Card

  • Go to the official admit card portal at student.mdu.ac.in/getadmitcard.
  • Enter your Student ID/No or Registration Number in the field provided.
  • Submit the details to view your Reappear Admit Card.
  • Check all details on the admit card carefully - name, roll number, exam center, subjects, and exam schedule.
  • Download and print the admit card for use at the exam center.

Important Instructions

These instructions are taken directly from the official MDU admit card portal:

  • If your fee shows as "Un-Paid" despite paying online, email [email protected] with your Registration No./Student ID and the date of payment.
  • If your status shows "Not Eligible (NE)", contact the concerned Result Branch at Pariksha Sadan (in front of the DDE Building), MDU Rohtak, for eligibility clearance along with all relevant documents - at least 3 days before the exam.
  • Candidates appearing for a practical subject should report to the practical exam center, not the theory center shown on the admit card. The theory exam center is shown by default, but the exam roll number remains the same. For the theory and practical date sheet of any course, contact the Conduct Branch (Ground Floor, Pariksha Sadan, MDU Rohtak).
  • If there is any issue with the admit card, Paper ID, or if the admit card is not available on the website, email [email protected].
