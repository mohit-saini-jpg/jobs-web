About NPC

The National Productivity Council is an autonomous body under DPIIT, Ministry of Commerce & Industry, Government of India, with its office at 5-6 Institutional Area, Lodhi Road, New Delhi - 110003. According to the notice, it promotes productivity consciousness in the country for sustainable socio-economic development through consultancy, research and training activities in different sectors of the economy. NPC Head Quarter (HQ), New Delhi invites these applications purely on a contractual basis for its Chennai office.

Where and How to Send

Applications are sent by email to [email protected] . The notice states the deadline as "on or before 23/11/2026 by 3:00 pm"; it does not say whether this is for receipt or dispatch. Fill in the application in the format of Annex-AF, "Application for engagement as contractual person in NPC", printed in the notice. Fill in the Appendix, "Details of Experience", printed with the form. Attach self-attested copies of all requisite documents and a latest photo. Email the application to [email protected] on or before 23/11/2026 by 3:00 pm. Write the post code in the email subject; the notice gives PE/01 as the example. Incomplete applications, and applications without self-attested copies of documents, will be rejected.

Posts, Eligibility and Pay

NPC has advertised one post, Project Executive (AIP), post code PE/01, with 3 vacancies. The type of engagement is Full Time and the place of deployment is Chennai. Qualification: Graduate in any discipline. Skill: Good command over MS Office. Experience: 0-5 years. Preference: Will be given to Graduates in Engineering with experience. Remuneration: Rs 28,000 – Rs 50,000 per month based on experience. Contract period: 2 years. The notice mentions no other allowance on top of this remuneration, and no age limit. Candidates should check the official notification for the full conditions.

NPC Project Executive (AIP) Job Description

Assist consultants in execution of consultancy assignments, including data collection, analysis and report preparation. Coordinate training programmes, including participant registration, faculty coordination and training material preparation. Prepare presentations, project reports, training materials and other required documentation. Liaise with client organisations, participants and stakeholders for information sharing and coordination. Provide administrative and logistical support for consultancy assignments and training programmes. Maintain project records, monitor progress and assist consultants in meeting deliverables and timelines.

Enclosures

Application in the Annex-AF format, with the Appendix "Details of Experience". Self-attested copies of all requisite documents. Latest photo. Self-attested copy of proof of date of birth. Self-attested copy of Aadhaar. Self-attested copies of educational/technical qualification documents, and experience certificates. Copy of PPO, with the date of retirement and the office last worked in, in case of a retired person.
