AP Police Prakasam District Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The 7th (IR) Battalion, APSP, Maddipadu (Prakasam District) is recruiting a total of 37 posts on an outsourcing basis across nine categories: Record Assistant, Office Subordinate, Sweepers, Sweeper (Medical), Scavengers, Scavenger (Medical), Cooks, Dhobis and Barbers. Candidates who meet the age criteria and the post-wise educational/skill qualification can apply. Applications must be submitted in person or by post to the Commandant, 7th (IR)BN, APSP, Maddipadu, Ongole, Prakasam District, on or before 11.09.2026. Selection is through a written/read-write test, a computer or skill test (as applicable), and an interview. Register for Job Alerts

AP Police Prakasam District Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

The candidate must satisfy the educational/skill qualification prescribed for the post applied for. The candidate must be of sound health, active habits, and free from bodily defects or infirmities that would render them unfit for the service. The candidate's character and antecedents must qualify them for the service. The candidate must be a citizen of India. A person in a bigamous marriage is not eligible for appointment. A person dismissed from a State or Central Government service, or from an undertaking of the Central/State Government or local or other authorities, is not eligible. A person convicted by a court of law for an offence involving moral turpitude is not eligible. Candidates resorting to or bringing influence of any kind will be summarily disqualified and are liable for prosecution as per law.

AP Police Prakasam District Recruitment Salary Structure

All notified posts - Record Assistant, Office Subordinate, Sweepers, Sweeper (Medical), Scavengers, Scavenger (Medical), Cooks, Dhobis and Barbers - carry a monthly remuneration of Rs. 15,000/-. Leave benefits: Outsourcing staff are entitled to one casual leave per month. As per G.O.Ms.No. 17, Finance (HR-I-Plg-Policy) Department, dated 31.01.2019, married women employees working on an outsourcing basis are eligible for 180 days of paid maternity leave, equivalent to their existing remuneration, along with applicable statutory benefits such as EPF and ESI.

AP Police Prakasam District Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

The selected candidates must serve at least one year from the date of appointment. Resigning within one year (unless taking up a permanent government job) requires forfeiting Rs. 10,000/- to the Government and submitting an affidavit/bond to this effect. Selection is on an outsourcing basis; selected candidates will not be regarded as members of the regular service and have no preferential right to any other government appointment. Appointment may be terminated at any time during service for administrative reasons. Outsourcing staff must reside at the Bonafide Headquarters and remain available 24 hours on call during emergencies. No private practice is allowed during the contract period. Disciplinary control is as per the APCS (CCA) Rules, 1991, and other applicable AP State Government outsourcing employee rules. Merely securing minimum qualifying marks does not confer any right to selection. Candidates who don't provide working contact numbers, or whose phones are switched off/out of network/unanswered when the department calls for the skill test, will be marked absent and removed from the eligible candidates list; the skill test cannot be rescheduled. Furnishing false information or suppressing information in the application or enclosures will lead to summary rejection and may result in prosecution as well as debarment from this and future recruitments. In case two or more candidates secure equal total marks, the elder candidate (by age) will be given preference for provisional selection. The decision of the Department/District Selection Committee at all stages of the recruitment is final and binding on all concerned.
