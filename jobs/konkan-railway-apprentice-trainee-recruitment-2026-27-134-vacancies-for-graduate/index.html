KRCL Apprentice Trainee Stipend

Stipend is paid through the Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) mechanism, combining the Government of India's DBT share and KRCL's establishment share. Candidates must have a bank account in their own name and a PAN card to receive the stipend, and must retain the same Aadhaar-linked bank account until training completion. Candidates must also make their own lodging and boarding arrangements during training.

KRCL Apprentice Trainee Recruitment 2026-27 Selection Process

For all categories, marks obtained across all years/semesters are summed to arrive at an aggregate percentage, and a merit list is prepared accordingly. No rounding off is done, and no weightage is given to any particular semester or year. In case of a tie in marks, the older candidate is preferred; if dates of birth are also the same, the candidate who passed matriculation earlier is selected. Land loser candidates (as defined in the notification, whose land was acquired for the Konkan Railway Project) are given first preference in selection. Candidates from the districts under KRCL's jurisdiction - Raigad, Ratnagiri and Sindhudurg (Maharashtra), North Goa and South Goa (Goa), and Uttara Kannada, Udupi and Dakshina Kannada (Karnataka) - are given second preference.
