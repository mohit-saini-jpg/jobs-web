About BHEL Haridwar

The notice is issued by the office of SDGM (HR), BHEL, HEEP, Ranipur, Haridwar, on its letterhead dated 27 November 2026 (Ref No: HR/RTX/PTMC/2026). BHEL, Haridwar is conducting the walk-in interview for medical professionals meeting the required specifications, for engagement as Part Time Medical Consultant in its Hospital at Haridwar. Working hours follow the duty schedule or roster decided by the Chief Medical Services.

Qualification and Experience

Applicants should have their MD / MS / DNB / PG Diploma and MBBS Degree recognized by Medical Council of India / National Medical Council. Applicants must be registered with National Medical Council / State Medical Council. Internship will not be considered as work experience. The upper age limit as on 01.11.2026 is 65 years, relaxable up to 70 years. The notice does not say which candidates may use the relaxation. For all posts the same limit applies. Candidates should refer to the official notification for the discipline-specific eligibility of each post.

Pay and Terms

Based on qualification and hours of engagement, the emoluments per hour are as follows. Call duty charges for specialty in Gynaecology, Paediatrics and Orthopedics: up to Rs. 18,000/- per month. Conveyance (local travel): may be given as per actuals on case to case basis, with an upper ceiling of Rs. 4,500/- per month for distance up to 30 KMs one-way and Rs. 6,000/- per month for distance beyond 30 KMs one-way. Payment towards remuneration and conveyance will be against submission of invoice as independent professional for rendering the services. Tenure: purely temporary basis for one year; subsequent renewals limited to a maximum period of 3 years, subject to satisfactory performance and at the discretion of Management. Notice period: one month, on either side, for discontinuing the part time engagement. Working hours: as per the duty schedule/roster decided by Chief Medical Services.

BHEL Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in BHEL Haridwar PTMC recruitment 2026?
A
There are 11 positions for Part Time Medical Consultant across eight specialities. The notice says the number of positions is tentative and subject to revision on assessment.
Q2What is the last date to apply for BHEL PTMC recruitment?
A
Details must be emailed to [email protected] by 12 December 2026. The walk-in interviews are on 14 and 15 December 2026.
Q3What is the qualification for BHEL Part Time Medical Consultant posts?
A
Specialist posts need MD/ MS/ DNB/ PG Diploma in the respective field. The GDMO post needs MBBS with 1-year experience. Degrees must be recognised by MCI/NMC, and registration with National Medical Council / State Medical Council is required.
Q4What is the age limit for BHEL PTMC posts?
A
The upper age limit as on 01.11.2026 is 65 years, relaxable up to 70 years.
Q5What is the pay for BHEL Part Time Medical Consultants?
A
Emoluments are hourly. Specialists with MD/MS/DNB get Rs. 640/- per hour (Rs. 960/- per hour for once/twice/thrice a week, maximum 2 hours per day), PG Diploma holders Rs. 520/- per hour (Rs. 780/- per hour), and GDMO Rs. 400/- per hour.
Q6How can I apply for BHEL Haridwar PTMC recruitment?
A
Email your name, specialisation, date and time for the walk-in interview, contact number and email id to [email protected] by 12.12.2026. Then attend the walk-in interview with the Bio-Data Form and documents.
Q7What is the selection process for BHEL PTMC?
A
Selection is through walk-in interviews. Candidates called for interview will not be paid any TA / DA.
Q8Is the BHEL PTMC job permanent?
A
No, the engagement is purely on a temporary basis for one year. Renewals are limited to a maximum period of 3 years, subject to satisfactory performance and at the discretion of Management, with a one-month notice period on either side.
Q9Are conveyance and call duty charges paid on top of the hourly emoluments?
A
The notice provides for conveyance charges as per actuals, up to Rs. 4,500/- per month (up to 30 KMs one-way) or Rs. 6,000/- per month (beyond 30 KMs one-way). Call duty charges for Gynaecology, Paediatrics and Orthopedics are up to Rs. 18,000/- per month.
Q10Do government employees need an NOC for the BHEL walk-in interview?
A
Candidates presently employed in Govt./ Nationalized Banks/ Autonomous Bodies/ PSUs may produce a No Objection Certificate at the interview. If it is difficult to obtain, the NOC or relieving order can be produced at joining in the event of selection.
