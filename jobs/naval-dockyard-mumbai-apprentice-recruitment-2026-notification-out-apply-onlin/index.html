Naval Dockyard Mumbai Apprentice Eligibility Criteria

Eligibility depends on the trade applied for. Age, educational qualification and other norms are determined as on the crucial date, which is the closing date for online registration. Candidature is accepted only provisionally, and all claims are verified at the document verification stage. If any claim is found unsubstantiated at any stage, the candidature will be cancelled and the decision of Naval Dockyard Mumbai will be final.

Naval Dockyard Mumbai Apprentice Qualification

The minimum educational qualification follows Schedule I of the Apprenticeship Rules, 1992. ITI trades eligible for each designated trade are as per Schedule I, as amended from time to time. The notification says applications are invited from Non-ITI candidates (2 trades), ITI-qualified candidates (30 trades) and freshers. Candidates who have passed ITI can apply in their respective trades or corresponding NCVT-approved trades as per Schedule I. For example, an NCVT Turner or Tool and Die Maker certificate holder can apply for the Machinist trade. Candidates should check the matching NCVT trade before applying, and will be cancelled if they apply for a trade not relevant to their ITI trade.

Naval Dockyard Mumbai Apprentice Age Limit

As per the Apprentices Act, 1961, there is no upper age limit for apprenticeship training. Only a minimum age applies, counted on the crucial date (the closing date for receipt of online applications). Date of birth is taken from the Class X certificate issued by the concerned Education Board. If the date of birth is not mentioned there, the birth certificate issued by the Competent Authority is used. For the Rigger trade, if the candidate does not have a Class X certificate, the birth certificate issued by the Competent Authority is used. Note in the notification: an upper age limit is set in employment rules for the post of Tradesman Skilled in Naval Dockyards. It will apply to all ex-Naval Apprentices of Dockyard Apprentice School, Mumbai applying for that post after completing apprenticeship training. Naval Dockyard Mumbai Apprentice Reservation Policy

Reservation for SC and ST is as per Schedule II A of the Apprentice Rules, 1992, and for OBC (NCL) as per Maharashtra Act No. VIII of 2004. Reserved vacancies are filled separately from eligible SC, ST and OBC candidates, who are either lower in merit than the last candidate on the merit list of the UR category or SC/ST/OBC (NCL) candidates who have availed age relaxation, irrespective of their position in the merit list. Reserved vacancies for PwBD and ESM are filled from eligible candidates of the respective category who are lower in merit than the last candidate on the merit list of that category but otherwise found suitable for appointment even by relaxed standard. PwBD and ESM reservations are horizontal and cut across the vertical SC/ST/OBC(NCL) reservation (inter-locking reservation). Candidates selected against PwBD and ESM quota are placed in the appropriate category of UR/SC/ST

Naval Dockyard Mumbai Apprentice Selection Process

Selection is carried out as per extant guidelines of the Competent Authority. The stages are as follows. Screening of applications: Naval Dockyard Mumbai will not carry out detailed scrutiny of online applications before the examination. Detailed scrutiny of documents is done at a subsequent stage. Shortlisting: Fulfilling the basic criteria does not automatically entitle a candidate to the written examination. If applications are too many in proportion to vacancies, the authority may shortlist applications, in the ratio of 1:25 for every available vacancy in each trade and category. Merit calculation: Equal weightage (50:50) is given to marks in Class 10th from a recognised Board and marks in the ITI consolidated statement of marks for AITT issued by NCVET. For Crane Operator (SI), 100% weightage is given to Class 10th. For Rigger, 100% weightage is given to Class 8th. Written examinati
