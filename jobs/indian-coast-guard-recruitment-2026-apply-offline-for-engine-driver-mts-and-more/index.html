Indian Coast Guard Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Indian Coast Guard Regional Headquarters (East), Chennai, under the Government of India, Ministry of Defence, invites applications by Ordinary Post for five Group 'C' posts: Store Keeper Grade-II, Engine Driver, Lascar, Civilian Motor Transport Driver (Ordinary Grade) and MTS (MT Cleaner). Applications go to The Commander, Coast Guard Region (East), Near Napier Bridge, Fort St George (PO), Chennai - 600 009, and the last date of receipt of applications is 21 December 2026.

Indian Coast Guard Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection runs through scrutiny of applications, document verification, a written examination, a trade test where applicable, and a merit list. All applications are scrutinised against the eligibility criteria and required documents. Admit cards or call letters are then issued to shortlisted candidates for document verification and the written examination. Shortlisted candidates bring their original documents, including the original admit card or call letter, and two sets of self-attested Xerox copies, as directed in the admit card. Thumb impression and photograph are captured and verified at document verification or before entering the examination hall. Candidates whose biometric does not match at any stage are not allowed for the written examination, and refusal to take part may lead to cancellation of candidature. Indian Coast Guard Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is the last date to apply for ICG Recruitment 2026?
A
The last date of receipt of applications is 21 December 2026. Applications must be sent by Ordinary Post only to The Commander, Coast Guard Region (East), Near Napier Bridge, Fort St George (PO), Chennai - 600 009.
Q2Which posts are covered by this ICG recruitment?
A
The posts are Store Keeper Grade-II, Engine Driver, Lascar, Civilian Motor Transport Driver (Ordinary Grade) and MTS (MT Cleaner), all General Central Service, Group 'C', Non-Gazetted, Non-Ministerial.
Q3What is the qualification for Store Keeper Grade-II?
A
12th Pass from a recognised Board or University, plus one year's experience in handling stores from a recognised firm or a Central or State Govt. organisation or Public Sector undertaking.
Q4What is the qualification for Engine Driver?
A
Matriculation pass from a recognised Board or equivalent, and a certificate of competency as Engine Driver from a recognised Government Institute or equivalent. Two years service as Sarang on a vessel of over four hundred Boat Horse Power is desirable.
Q5What is the age limit for ICG Lascar and Engine Driver?
A
Both have an age limit of 18 to 30 years, counted as on 21 December 2026. The Engine Driver limit is relaxable by 05 years for SC candidates, and the Lascar limit is printed for the EWS Category.
Q6What is the pay level for these posts?
A
Store Keeper Grade-II and Civilian Motor Transport Driver are in Pay Level-02, Engine Driver is in Pay Level-04, and Lascar and MTS (MT Cleaner) are in Pay Level-01 in the pay matrix.
Q7How are candidates selected?
A
Applications are scrutinised, and shortlisted candidates undergo document verification and a one-hour written examination of 80 objective questions. A trade test follows for qualifiers where applicable, and the merit list is based on written exam marks.
Q8Is there negative marking in the ICG written exam?
A
No. Each correct answer carries one mark and there is no negative marking. The qualifying mark is 50%, and 45% for SC and ST candidates.
Q9What must be written on the envelope?
A
Write "APPLICATION FOR THE POST OF" with the post name, and the category applied for (UR/EWS/OBC (Non Creamy Layer)/SC/ST), in BOLD letters. Applications without these notations are summarily rejected.
Q10Should original certificates be sent with the application?
A
No. Send only self-attested Xerox copies with name and date; no original certificates are to be forwarded. Originals are brought at document verification by shortlisted candidates.
