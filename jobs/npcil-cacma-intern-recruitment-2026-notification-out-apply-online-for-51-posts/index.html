NPCIL CA/CMA Intern Eligibility Criteria

NPCIL CA/CMA Intern eligibility covers nationality, qualification and age. Candidates should read the criteria carefully and confirm they meet every condition before applying, because candidature can be cancelled at any stage if a candidate is found ineligible. Only Indian nationals are eligible to apply. The candidate must have passed the Inter CA or Inter CMA examination. The candidate must be within the prescribed age limit as on 26/10/2026. Applicants without final marks will not be considered and need not apply.

NPCIL CA/CMA Intern Qualification

The internship is offered to candidates who have passed Inter CA or Inter CMA and are to be trained in the field of Finance and Accounts. The notification states that eligibility for practical training at NPCIL Units and HQ will be as per the terms and conditions provided by the respective institutions. The notification also states that these prescribed qualifications are the minimum and that possessing them does not entitle a candidate to be called for the interview. Candidates must enter the exact percentage of marks in the qualification section of the form. Marks must not be rounded off, so 49.99% should not be entered as 50%.

NPCIL CA/CMA Intern Stipend (Salary)

Selected interns receive a monthly stipend rather than a salary, because the notification treats them as trainees. The stipend is Rs 20,000 per month. An additional Rs 5,000 per month is paid where accommodation is not available at the NPCIL Unit or HQ. Accommodation: A suitable accommodation (single room or bare minimum accommodation) is provided at Units on payment of the normal license fee. If accommodation is not available at Units and HQ, Rs 5,000 per month is given in lieu of accommodation. Transport and medical: Free site transportation subject to availability, and free OPD and first aid facility only. Leave: 1 day of paid leave per month, which is 12 days for one year. For extensions, leave is pro-rata for the extended period. Stipend is deducted proportionately for absence other than admissible leave. NPCIL CA/CMA Intern Selection Process

The selection is based on Inter CA/Inter CMA marks followed by a virtual personal interview. Screening is done on the aggregate of marks, so only screened-in candidates are informed by e-mail about the further selection process. Screening: Candidates are shortlisted for the personal interview in the ratio of 1:5, based on the aggregate marks obtained in the Inter CA/Inter CMA examination. Personal Interview: Selection of interns is by personal interview, which candidates can attend in virtual mode at the respective Unit/Region. Certificate Verification and Medical Examination: Candidates are engaged after qualifying the standard medical examination and after satisfactory verification of certificates. Police and Character Verification: Interns are engaged based on the police verification certificate issued by District Police authorities, and a character/conduct certificate must be submitt
