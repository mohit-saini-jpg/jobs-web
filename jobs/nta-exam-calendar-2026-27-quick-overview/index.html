NTA Exam Calendar 2026-27 - Quick Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 17/09/2026

The National Testing Agency (NTA) released the NTA Exam Calendar 2026-27 on 16 September 2026 through an official Public Notice on its website. The calendar gives the tentative examination schedule for major national-level entrance and eligibility tests that NTA plans to conduct between December 2026 and March 2027, including UGC NET (December Cycle), Joint CSIR UGC NET, JEE Main Session 1, the NIFT Entrance Examination, CMAT, and CUET PG.

How to Download the NTA Exam Calendar 2026-27

  • Visit the official NTA website: nta.ac.in.
  • Look for the notice titled "NTA Examination Calendar 2026-27" on the homepage or under the "Public Notices" / "Latest News" section.
  • Click on the link to open the calendar PDF.
  • Review the exam-wise tentative dates relevant to you.
  • Download and save the PDF for future reference; check back regularly, as the calendar is subject to revision.

How to Apply for Exams Listed in the Calendar

Since this calendar is not an application notification, there is no single "apply" link for it. Once an individual exam's registration opens, the general process is expected to follow the usual NTA pattern:

  • Visit the dedicated exam portal once the registration link is activated.
  • Complete new registration using basic details, a valid mobile number, and email ID.
  • Fill in the application form with personal, academic, and communication details.
  • Upload photograph, signature, and any other required documents in the specified format and size.
  • Pay the application fee through the available online payment modes.
  • Submit the form and download/print the confirmation page for your records.

Important Instructions

  • The NTA Exam Calendar 2026-27 gives tentative dates only; it is not confirmation of final exam dates.
  • Dates may change due to administrative, academic, statutory, logistical, or other unforeseen reasons, as clarified by NTA in the notice.
  • Detailed notifications, application schedules, eligibility criteria, and exam patterns for each exam will be released separately and closer to the respective exam.
  • Candidates should regularly check the official NTA website and the specific exam portal for updates on registration, admit card, exam city intimation, and results.
  • Where two exams fall on overlapping or adjacent dates (for example, NIFT and SHRESHTA on the same tentative date, or AISSEE landing on the JEE Main buffer date), candidates appearing for more than one exam should track each notification separately to avoid a scheduling clash.
