University of Kashmir Eligibility Criteria 2026

A minimum of eight years of experience of teaching and/or research in an academic/research position equivalent to that of Assistant Professor in a University, College or Accredited Research Institution/Industry. A minimum of seven publications in peer-reviewed or UGC listed journals and a total research score of 75 as per Appendix II, Table 2 of UGC Guidelines, July 2018. Published research work of good quality on any aspect of Iqbal studies. Published research work of good quality on any aspect of Kashmiri Culture, History, Religion or Philosophy, preferably relevant to Reshi Movement/ Spiritual tradition/identity of Kashmir. Only publications in journals indexed in UGC-CARE Listed Journals, Scopus or Web of Science are considered for all academic purposes, with effect from 14.6.2019. Publications in predatory/dubious journals or conferences are not considered for academic credit. Each

University of Kashmir Selection Process 2026

Candidates are informed about the interview through the E-mail/SMS registered at the time of registration. The University is not responsible for non-delivery of E-mail/SMS due to technical or any other issues. Candidates must produce original documents relating to age, qualifications, experience, fitness and other claims at the time of interview. Selected candidates must produce them again before joining. Academic/Research score claims are made under UGC Guidelines, July 2018, Appendix II, Table 2, and must be backed by the PBAS Proforma. Selected candidates are placed on probation for one or two years as per the recommendation of the Selection Committee. The marking scheme for the interview is not specified in the notice.

Documents Required

Printout of the online application form with enclosures, numbered as per the auto-generated serial numbers, self-attested and in the same order. Online payment acknowledgement. One (1) set of Performance Based Appraisal System (PBAS) Proforma. Four (04) copies of the latest Bio-data. Four (04) copies of seven (07) best publications as books and/or research/policy papers, in spiral binding only, not attached to the PBAS Proforma. AIU "Equivalence Certificate" for post graduate/Ph.D. degrees from a foreign university.

  • 2Register through the "Register as an Applicant" link, using a valid Email id and mobile number. Remember the Username and Password.

  • 8Superscribe the envelope: "Application for the post of ………………………, Post ID ………… Advertisement notice No. ………. Dated …………." The hard copy must reach by or before 23 December 2026, and a postal application received after the last date is rejected summarily without any notice.
