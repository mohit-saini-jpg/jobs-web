IIT (BHU) Junior Research Fellow (Civil Engineering) : Job Overview

The Department of Civil Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology (Banaras Hindu University), has invited applications for 1 Junior Research Fellow post in the project "Characterization of Groundwater Depletion Induced Land Subsidence as a Function of Aquifer Properties by Spaceborne Geodetic Techniques including DGPS Observations and Dual Frequency NISAR", at Rs 37,000 a month with HRA @18%. The essential qualification is an M.E./M.Tech. or M.Sc. in the listed disciplines with a qualifying GATE/NET or equivalent national level examination. Applications are sent by email to [email protected]

About IIT (BHU) Civil Engineering NISAR Project

The post sits in the Department of Civil Engineering of IIT (BHU), Varanasi, under Prof. Anurag Ohri, the Principal Investigator (PI) named on the notice. The project studies groundwater depletion induced land subsidence as a function of aquifer properties, using spaceborne geodetic techniques, DGPS observations and dual frequency NISAR.

Who Can Apply

Post Graduate Degree (M.E./M.Tech.) in Water Resources/Geoinformatics/Remote Sensing & GIS/Engineering Geosciences, OR M.Sc. degree in Geoinformatics/Remote Sensing & GIS, Geography/Geology/Applied Geology, Earth Sciences or equivalent. AND must have qualified GATE/ NET or any national level examination conducted by central government departments or institutes like DST, IIT, ISRO etc. Experience in SAR image processing, DGPS observations, GIS/Remote Sensing tools, and programming (Python); knowledge of groundwater modelling preferred. The upper age limit is 30 years. The notice prints no cut-off date for age. Relaxation of marks and age may be granted to OBC/SC/ST/Women/physically challenged candidates as per govt norms. Candidates should check the official notification for the discipline-specific eligibility of the post.

Pay and Tenure

The monthly salary is Rs 37,000/- with HRA @18%. IIT (BHU) has all rights to decide the duration of appointment in each position. The performance of project staff positions will be reviewed accordingly, and the positions may be extended for the total project duration. A selected candidate is expected to join immediately.

Selection and Interview

The notice says the PI is free to set the benchmark and call only the eligible candidates for Test and/or Interview for any position. Only shortlisted candidates will be communicated to appear in the interview, and no other communication will be entertained. Candidates are requested to check their email regularly and visit https://www.iitbhu.ac.in/positions_available for updates. No TA/DA will be paid for attending the interview.
