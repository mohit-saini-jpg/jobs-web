SAIL CFP Dresser Cum Compounder Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Chandrapur Ferro Alloy Plant (CFP), a unit of Steel Authority of India Limited (SAIL), holds a walk-in interview on 26 November 2026 at the HR- L & D hall, Chandrapur Ferro Alloy Plant, Chandrapur, Maharashtra. Document verification reporting is at 10.00 AM and the interview is at 02:30 PM. There are 3 Proficiency Trainee places for Dresser Cum Compounder (Male) in the M&HS department. The training lasts 18 months with a stipend of Rs.10000 per month plus a Knowledge Enhancement Allowance. The recruiter is a Government of India enterprise.

About SAIL CFP

Chandrapur Ferro Alloy Plant (CFP), Mul Road, Chandrapur, is a unit of Steel Authority of India Limited, which the notice describes as a Maharatna company. The notice invites eligible Dresser Cum Compounder (Male) candidates for Proficiency Training in the Occupational Health Centre (OHS) and CFP Dispensary and OHS under the M&HS department.

Posts and Eligibility

The notice lists these alternative qualifications for the post, all valid on the date of the walk-in interview: Intermediate (10+2) with Science from any university / institution recognized by the Central or State Govt (with working experience in any Hospital / Nursing Home / Registered Doctor); OR Diploma or Certificate Course in Dresser / Compounder from any university / institution recognized by the Central or State Govt; OR B.Sc (Nursing); OR Diploma or Certificate course in General / Auxiliary Nursing & Midwife (GNM / ANM) from a recognized Institute / College affiliated to Indian Nursing Council (INC), with Certificate of Registration. The upper age limit is 30 years, valid as on the date of the walk-in interview. The minimum age of engagement as Proficiency Trainee Dresser Cum Compounder (Male) is 18 years. What to Bring

Filled-in and signed application in the prescribed format (Annexure-A), with a recent passport size colour photograph pasted on it Letter of undertaking (Annexure-B) on non-judicial stamp paper (Rs.10/-) Qualification documents; the application form asks for self-attested pass certificates and mark sheets for educational and professional qualification (all years / semesters) Aadhar Card and PAN Card Birth Certificate; the form names the Matriculation / Secondary / Class X pass certificate as proof of date of birth Caste Certificate (SC / ST / OBC-NCL / EWS candidates) Address Proof and passport size photo Experience Certificate, and Internship Certificate if applicable State Nursing Council Registration certificate, which the form lists for Trainee Nurse Originals are checked at the interview and at joining. The notice gives no postal address or email for sending applications.

What Happens on the Day

Candidates report at 10.00 AM for document verification; the interview is at 02:30 PM. Screened candidates, chosen on a first-cum-first-serve basis, are interviewed. If more candidates appear, the interview may continue on another day, subject to their appearance at the scheduled time. Selection for Proficiency Training is made by judging performance in the interview. In case of a tie in the final cut-off score, the candidate with the higher percentage of marks in the aggregate of the qualifying qualification is selected. Selected candidates undergo a medical check-up by SAIL-CFP Hospital / Dispensary and report for training at the plant with immediate effect. Training starts shortly after selection is completed and an Offer of Admittance as Proficiency Trainee Dresser Cum Compounder (Male) is issued.

SAIL CFP Dresser Cum Compounder Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

A candidate who cannot produce the original certificates at interview or joining will not be allowed to appear or join, and the candidature will be cancelled. Candidates with a GNM / ANM / B.Sc (Nursing) qualification who lack the Certificate of Registration at joining must deposit it within three months of admittance. The admittance stays provisional until then. A Certificate of Proficiency is issued by the CFP-OHS centre after 18 months on satisfactory performance. It is not issued for provisional engagement (where registration is not submitted) or if the full training period is not completed. The engagement is purely for Proficiency Training and gives no claim to appointment in Chandrapur Ferro Alloy Plant. Candidates attend the interview at their own cost.
