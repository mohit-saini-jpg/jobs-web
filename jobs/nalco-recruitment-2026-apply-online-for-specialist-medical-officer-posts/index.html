NALCO Recruitment 2026 Age Limit

Specialist (E03 grade): Maximum 38 years as on 17/08/2026 Medical Officer & Specialist (E02 grade): Maximum 35 years as on 17/08/2026 Internal candidates on the immediate lower scale of pay: No upper age limit, subject to Company Rules Age relaxation: 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC(NCL), 10 years for PwBD-UR, 13 years for PwBD-OBC(NCL), 15 years for PwBD-SC/ST candidates Relaxation for SC/ST/OBC(NCL) is subject to availability of a post in that particular category Ex-servicemen and family members of those who died in the 1984 riots will get relaxation as per Government of India directives Maximum upper age of any applicant shall not exceed 56 years, including all possible age relaxations

NALCO Medical Officer & Specialist Salary Structure 2026

The Medical Officer (E02) and Specialist (E02) posts carry a pay scale of ₹70,000-3%-2,00,000, while the Specialist (E03) post carries a pay scale of ₹80,000-3%-2,20,000. In addition to Basic Pay and DA as per the Industrial DA pattern, selected candidates will receive Perks (35% of basic pay), Non-Practicing Allowance (20% of basic pay), Performance Related Pay based on Company and individual performance, Company accommodation or HRA, medical facilities for self and dependent family members, Post-Retirement Medical Benefit, Leave Encashment, Pension as per NPS, Contributory Provident Fund, Group Insurance, and Gratuity, as per Company Rules.
