Pay and Terms

Pay: fixed salary of Rs. 30,000/- per month. Work: teaching students of the P.G. and UG level academic programmes of multiple durations run by the Centre of Professional Courses, A.M.U., Aligarh. Tenure: purely temporary till the end of the academic session 2026-2027. The appointment is liable to be terminated at any time without any notice or assignment of reason(s). The number of posts and the salary may be varied at the time of interview. The University reserves the right to get selected applicants examined by an Internal Medical Board.

AMU Assistant Professor Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection is through scrutiny and short-listing of applications, followed by an interview on 12 December 2026. The University may constitute a committee to scrutinize the applications and short-list applicants. The University reserves the right to restrict the number of applicants called for interview to a reasonable number on the basis of Rules / Criteria or any other condition it may deem fit. The list of eligible candidates for interview will be displayed on the webpage of the Centre of Professional Courses, AMU. No correspondence will be made with applicants who are not short-listed. Candidates called for interview will not be paid TA/DA. If an inadvertent mistake in scrutiny or selection is detected at any stage, even after an interview or appointment letter is issued, the University reserves the right to modify, withdraw or cancel the selection process.
