JMI Application Fee 2026

The fee depends on the group of the post. Group A covers posts at Pay Level 10 and above: Assistant Registrar, Assistant Finance Officer, Executive Engineer (Civil), Internal Audit Officer and Superintendent Engineer (Civil). Group B and C covers posts at Pay Level 7 and below, which are all the other posts. The fee is submitted online along with the application, only through JMI's portal jobs.jmi.ac.in. Divyangjan are exempted from the fee on producing a valid copy of the relevant PwBD certificate. SC and ST candidates get a 50% concession. All other categories pay the full fee. Candidates who applied earlier under Advt. No. 02/2025-26 (NT) dated 02.12.2025 for posts marked * must apply afresh online. They are exempted from the fee for that position on enclosing the original acknowledgement receipt of that application, or a valid Indian Post slip/Courier company slip, with their applica

JMI Selection Process 2026

All non-teaching positions will be filled as per the Recruitment Rules of Jamia Millia Islamia. Selection will be based on the candidates' performance in the written / skill / trade test / interview, etc. Screening and shortlisting: The University may constitute a Screening Committee to scrutinise applications and shortlist candidates. It reserves the right to limit the number of candidates called for interview, using qualifications and experience higher than the minimum or any other condition it deems fit. Assistant Registrar: Appointment under direct recruitment will be made through an All India open competition by conducting a Written Test and Interview. Call letters: Call letters for the written/skill/trade test/interview will be sent only to shortlisted candidates, at the e-mail address given in the application form. JMI Non Teaching Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in JMI Recruitment 2026?
A
Advt. No. 01/2026-27 (NT) lists 85 non-teaching posts across 35 positions, including 10 Upper Division Clerk (UDC) posts and 6 Beldar posts. The notice says the number is tentative and may be increased or decreased.
Q2When does the JMI Recruitment 2026 online application start?
A
Online applications on jobs.jmi.ac.in start on 12 November 2026.
Q3What is the last date for JMI Recruitment 2026?
A
The last date for the online application is 11 December 2026. The hard copy with documents must reach the Recruitment & Promotion Section, Registrar's Office, JMI by 21 December 2026.
Q4What is the application fee for JMI Recruitment 2026?
A
For Group A posts (Pay Level 10 and above), the fee is 1000 for UR/OBC and 500 for SC/ST. For Group B and C posts (Pay Level 7 and below), it is 700 for UR/OBC and 350 for SC/ST. PwBD (Divyangjan) candidates pay NIL in both groups.
Q5What is the age limit for JMI non-teaching posts 2026?
A
The age limit is 40 years for 32 posts. It is preferably below 56 years for Internal Audit Officer, Superintendent Engineer (Civil) and Executive Engineer (Civil). Age is reckoned as on 11 December 2026, and relaxation applies as per Central Govt. orders.
Q6What qualification is required for JMI UDC 2026?
A
The UDC post requires a Bachelor's Degree and two years' experience as Lower Division Clerk or equivalent in a Central/State Govt. office, university or autonomous institution. Candidates also need English typing @ 35 wpm or Hindi/Urdu typing @ 30 wpm, and proficiency in computer operations.
Q7What is the salary in JMI Recruitment 2026?
A
Pay ranges from Pay Level 1 (₹18,000 – 56,900) for posts such as Beldar and Wireman to Pay Level 13 (₹1,23,100 – 2,15,900) for Superintendent Engineer (Civil). The UDC post is at Pay Level 4 (₹25,500 – 81,100).
Q8What is the selection process for JMI Recruitment 2026?
A
Selection is based on performance in the written / skill / trade test / interview, etc., after shortlisting. For Assistant Registrar, it is an All India open competition with a Written Test and Interview.
Q9Do candidates who applied under Advt. No. 02/2025-26 (NT) need to apply again?
A
Yes. Earlier applicants for the posts marked * must apply afresh online. They are exempted from the fee on enclosing the original acknowledgement receipt or a valid post/courier slip, and they get age relaxation if they met the age limit on the last date of that advertisement.

Ans: You can apply online through the official recruitment link provided for JMI Non Teaching Recruitment 2026 Notification Out – Apply Online for 85 UDC, Assistant and More Posts on www.topsarkarijobs.com.
