NLU Delhi Assistant Librarian Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

National Law University Delhi (NLU Delhi) has called for applications for one Assistant Librarian (UR) on a contractual basis at an honorarium of Rs.90,000/- pm, with age preferably below 45 years. The essential qualification is a Master's Degree in Library Science, Information Science or Documentation Science or an equivalent professional degree with at least 55% marks, plus NET or a Ph.D. as per UGC Regulations. Apply only online at careers.nludelhi.ac.in by 23:59 Hrs on 6 December 2026; the fee is Rs. 1,000/-, and SC/ST and PwD candidates are exempted.

About the University

National Law University Delhi, at Sector-14, Dwarka, New Delhi, was established in 2008 with the initiative of the High Court of Delhi and the Govt. of NCT of Delhi. The notice says the Justice T. P. S. Chawla Library supports the teaching and research needs of faculty, students and legal researchers through electronic as well as traditional forms of information.

Who Can Apply

The notice states the essential qualification and experience as per the UGC Regulations on Minimum Qualifications for Appointment of Teachers and Other Academic Staff in Universities and Colleges and Measures for the Maintenance of Standards in Higher Education, 2018, as amended from time to time. A Master's Degree in Library Science, Information Science or Documentation Science or an equivalent professional degree, with at least 55% marks (or an equivalent grade in a point-scale, wherever the grading system is followed). A consistently good academic record, with knowledge of computerization of a library. Besides the above, the candidate must have cleared the National Eligibility Test (NET) conducted by the UGC, CSIR or a similar test accredited by the UGC like SLET/SET, or be or have been awarded a Ph.D. Degree in accordance with the University Grants Commission (Minimum Standards and P

Pay and Tenure

The post carries an honorarium of Rs.90,000/- pm, shown against Academic Pay Level - 10 (Rs. 57,700 - 1,82,400). The appointment is contractual. The notice says it will be purely temporary, will not confer any right to regularization and will not carry benefits applicable to regular faculty. Depending on the candidate's performance, the University, after adopting appropriate procedure, may consider absorbing the candidate as a regular employee. The notice adds that this is not binding. The notice does not print the length of the contract.

Selection and Interview

Candidates will be shortlisted based on scrutiny of applications and other criteria decided by the University. Mere possession of the prescribed qualifications does not entitle a candidate to be called for an interview. The University's decision regarding eligibility, shortlisting and selection is final and binding, and no correspondence will be entertained. The notice mentions an interview or interaction but prints no date or mode. No TA/DA will be paid for attending it.
