Scheme ka maksad kisan ko financial support dena hai, taaki wo beej, khaad ya irrigation ke liye cash flow maintain kar saken. Timely disbursement se crop cycle smooth hoti hai aur farm income stable rehti hai.

Who Is Eligible: PM Kisan Yojana Installment Update Farmer अपनी Crop की पेमें

Jo bhi kisan 2020-21 ke baad land record me registered hain aur PM Kisan ke liye pehle se hi eligible hain, unhe yeh installment milti hai.

PM Kisan Yojana Installment Update Farmer अपनी Crop की पेमें Application Process

1. Apna 12-digit Kisan ID aur bank account details ready rakhein. 2. Aadhaar linked bank account hona zaroori hai, isliye pehle se verify kar lein. 3. Official portal ya mParivahan app se payment status check karen, screenshot le kar record me rakhein.

PM Kisan Yojana Installment Update Farmer अपनी Crop की पेमें Benefits Details

Har eligible farmer ko Rs 6,000 per year milta hai, 3 installments me batwara. Yeh cash infusion se input cost cover hoti hai, jaise beej, fertilizer aur irrigation, jo overall farm profit ko boost karta hai.

How PM Kisan Yojana Installment Update Farmer अपनी Crop की पेमें Works

Scheme ka implementation state agriculture department ke through hota hai, data central PM Kisan portal me upload hota hai. Payment direct bank transfer (DBT) se hoti hai, isliye delay kam hota hai. Farmers ko bas apna Aadhaar aur bank account link karna hota hai.

PM Kisan Yojana Installment Update Farmer अपनी Crop की पेमें Application Tips

Aadhaar aur bank details ko sahi tarike se link karen, land records me koi mismatch na ho. Portal par payment status check karte waqt correct Kisan ID use karen, aur har installment ke baad receipt save karen taaki future verification me problem na aaye.

PM Kisan Yojana Installment Update Farmer अपनी Crop की पेमें FAQs

Q1Mujhe apni PM Kisan payment ka status kaise check karna hai?
A
Official PM Kisan portal ya mParivahan app par login karen, apna 12-digit Kisan ID dalen aur ‘Payment Status’ section me latest installment dekh sakte hain.
Q2Agar payment account me nahi aayi to kya karun?
A
Sabse pehle Aadhaar-bank linking verify karen, phir land record me apna naam check karen. Agar sab sahi hai to nearest agriculture office ya PM Kisan helpline (1800-180-1200) par complaint register karen.
Q3Kya har saal 3 installments milti hain?
A
Haan, Rs 6,000 ka total amount 3 equal installments me milta hai – har 4 mahine baad. Agar koi installment miss ho jati hai to next cycle me rectify kiya jata hai.
