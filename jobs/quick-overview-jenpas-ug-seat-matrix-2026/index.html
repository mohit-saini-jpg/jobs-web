Courses Covered Under JENPAS UG 2026

JENPAS UG scores are used for admission to undergraduate nursing, paramedical, and allied health science courses in West Bengal. Based on previous WBJEEB counselling cycles, courses typically include:

  • BSc Nursing
  • BPT (Bachelor of Physiotherapy)
  • BMLT (Bachelor of Medical Laboratory Technology)

The exact list of courses and specialisations for the 2026 counselling cycle is confirmed in the official JENPAS (UG)-2026 Information Bulletin. Candidates should verify the current list on wbjeeb.nic.in, as it may vary slightly from previous years.

JENPAS UG 2026 Counselling Process: Step by Step

The JENPAS UG 2026 counselling process is conducted entirely online and comprises the following steps:

  • Registration: Log in to the WBJEEB e-Counselling portal using your JENPAS (UG) 2026 roll number, date of birth, and other required credentials.
  • Fee Payment: Pay the non-refundable counselling registration fee of INR 500 online.
  • Choice Filling: Enter your preferred colleges and courses in order of preference.
  • Choice Locking: Lock your selections before the deadline; once locked, choices cannot be modified.
  • Seat Allotment: WBJEEB releases the seat allotment result for each round based on your rank and the choices you locked.
  • Seat Acceptance & Reporting: If allotted a seat, pay the seat acceptance fee and report to the allotted institute within the specified window for document verification and admission.

Seat allotment is conducted in three rounds - Round 1, Round 2, and a Mop-Up Round - with unfilled, revoked, or upgraded seats carried forward to subsequent rounds.

How to Check JENPAS UG Seat Matrix 2026 Online

Candidates can check the JENPAS UG 2026 seat matrix by following these steps:

  • Visit the official website, wbjeeb.nic.in/jenpas-ug/.
  • Click on the link for the JENPAS (UG)-2026 Seat Matrix.
  • On the next page, select the round, stream, institute type, institute, programme, gender, and quota/category as prompted.
  • View or download the seat matrix showing seat availability for your selected filters.
  • Use this information while filling and ordering your choices during counselling.

Important Instructions for JENPAS UG 2026 Candidates

  • Choices, once locked during choice filling, cannot be modified - verify all selections carefully before locking.
  • Candidates should complete registration, fee payment, and choice filling within the round-specific deadlines to avoid missing a round.
  • Keep checking the official WBJEEB website regularly, as counselling schedules and seat matrices may be updated via official addenda.
  • Carry all original documents at the time of reporting; admission may be denied for incomplete or missing documentation.
  • Course-wise and category-wise seat details should always be verified from the official seat matrix PDF, not from third-party estimates.
