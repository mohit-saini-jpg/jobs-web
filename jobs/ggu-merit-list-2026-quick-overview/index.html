GGU Merit List 2026 - Quick Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 03/08/2026

Guru Ghasidas Vishwavidyalaya (GGU), Bilaspur has released the GGU Merit List 2026 for Undergraduate (UG ) Admission 2026-27. The first merit list, based on CUET Ug 2026 scores, is available on the official website, ggu.ac.in, and on the SAMARTH admission portal. Candidates who find their names on the GGU UG Merit List 2026 must complete fee payment and document verification within the notified deadlines to confirm their seat.

This article explains the important dates, how to check and download the GGU Merit List 2026, required documents, and the next steps for admission at Guru Ghasidas Vishwavidyalaya.

Eligibility for GGU Merit List 2026

  • Candidates must have appeared for CUET UG 2026 for admission to Ug programmes at GGU.
  • Programme-wise minimum qualification and eligibility criteria are set by GGU for each UG course.
  • Specific subject-wise or percentage-based eligibility criteria are not available in the source material and should be verified from the official GGU admission notification.

Documents Required for GGU UG Admission Verification

Candidates whose names appear in the GGU Merit List 2026 must carry the following for document verification:

  • Original mark sheets and certificates
  • Self-attested photocopies of all original documents
  • CUET UG 2026 scorecard
  • Category certificate (if applicable)
  • Age proof and identity proof

How to Check and Download the GGU Merit List 2026

  • Visit the official GGU admission website at ggu.ac.in or the SAMARTH portal.
  • Look for the link related to "UG Admission 2026-27 Merit List 1."
  • Log in to the SAMARTH portal using your registered credentials.
  • Check your name, rank, and allotted programme on the merit list.
  • Download your offer letter from the portal.
  • Pay the admission fee online before the deadline.
  • Keep a printed copy of the merit list and offer letter for reporting day.

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Check your name and rank on the GGU Merit List 2026 as soon as it is published.
  • Pay the admission fee only through the official SAMARTH portal before July 6, 2026.
  • Report to the GGU Bilaspur campus on July 20, 2026, with all original and self-attested documents.
  • Missing the fee payment deadline will lead to cancellation of the provisional seat.
  • Keep checking the official website for updates on GGU Merit List 2 and any spot round notice.
  • For programme-specific instructions not covered here, refer directly to the official GGU notification, as some details are not available in the source used for this article.
