About NIFT

The National Institute of Fashion Technology (NIFT) was set up in 1986 under the Ministry of Textiles, Govt. of India. It is a statutory institute governed by the NIFT Act 2006 and has 20 campuses across the country. Its head office is at NIFT Campus, Hauz Khas, New Delhi. NIFT describes itself as an institute of fashion education that also works as a knowledge service provider to the Union and State governments in design development and in the positioning of handlooms and handicrafts.

Send the application, with self-attested copies of documents in support of qualification, experience, age and so on, to this address: The Registrar, NIFT Campus, Hauz Khas, Near Gulmohar Park, New Delhi- 110016. It must reach that address on or before 20 November 2026 by 05:00 PM. Every letter from NIFT, including any interview/interaction letter, will be sent only to the email address you give in the application form. NIFT Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection shall be based on performance in the personal interview / interaction or on any other selection criteria fixed by NIFT or the Selection Committee, and NIFT's decision shall be final.
