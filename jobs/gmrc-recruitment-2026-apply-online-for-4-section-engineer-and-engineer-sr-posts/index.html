About GMRC

Gujarat Metro Rail Corporation (GMRC) Limited is a Special Purpose Vehicle jointly owned by the Government of India and the Government of Gujarat. It is entrusted with implementing the Ahmedabad Metro Rail Project and the Surat Metro Rail Project and with their Operations & Maintenance. This notice is signed by the I/C General Manager (HR).

Pay and Tenure

The posts are on Contract/Deputation/Post-superannuation basis for a minimum period of 3 years, extendable up to 5 years based on performance, subject to the standard terms and conditions of the organization. For deputation, pay is as per standard terms and conditions. Apart from the pay, other benefits are paid as per Company Policy, and selected candidates get Group medical coverage and Group personal accident insurance as per GMRCL policies. For IDA/CDA scale employees, the percentage of HRA may vary with the place of posting as per Government guidelines and company policy.

Selection and Interview

The selection process comprises Personal Interviews followed by Document Verification and Medical Examination as per the category prescribed for the post. It judges different facets of knowledge, skills, experience, expertise, aptitude and physical fitness. Preference is given to candidates with experience in the same grade or immediate lower grade with 2 years' experience in the grade. If sufficient suitable candidates are not available, candidates from a lower pay scale who meet the prescribed qualifications, total experience and age criteria may be considered. Document verification is done before the interview. Candidates must carry original certificates, failing which they will not be allowed to attend the interview.
