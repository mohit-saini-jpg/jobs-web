Where and How to Send

The application must be made in the prescribed Application Format (Annexure-I), which is printed with the notice and can also be downloaded from www.cec.nic.in . It must be sent through proper channel with the forwarding certificate from the Head of Office (Annexure-II). An applicant may send an advance copy directly to CEC, provided a formal copy is also submitted through the proper channel (Parent Office) before the last date. Separate applications may be forwarded for each post.

CEC Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection is by short-listing followed by an interview. Only short-listed candidates will be called for the interview, and submission of an application does not confer any right to be called. If the response is exceptionally high, CEC reserves the right to restrict the number of candidates called for interview to those having a higher percentage of marks in the requisite qualification. Merely possessing the prescribed qualification and requisite experience would not entitle a candidate to be called for an interview. The decision of the Selection Committee shall be final. No TA/DA shall be paid for attending the Written Test / Skill Test / Interview, as the case may be.

CEC Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in CEC Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 4 vacancies, one post each of Joint Director (Hardware), Chief Administrative Officer, Producer and Computer Programme Assistant, all on deputation basis.
Q2What is the last date to apply for CEC Recruitment 2026?
A
Applications must reach CEC within 30 days from the date of advertisement (21 November 2026), which is about 21 December 2026. If the closing date is a holiday, the next working day is the last date.
Q3What is the qualification for the Computer Programme Assistant post?
A
A Graduate in Science / Commerce with 55% marks and a certificate or diploma in Computer applications from an institution of repute, with at least two years experience of using PC and familiarity with programmes like dBase, Fox Pro, Quattro and Lotus.
Q4What is the age limit for CEC deputation posts?
A
The maximum age limit is not exceeding 56 years as on the last date of receipt of applications; in-service candidates are eligible for age relaxation as per Rules.
Q5What is the pay for each post?
A
Joint Director (Hardware) gets Pay Level-13 (Rs.1,23,100 - 2,15,900/-), Chief Administrative Officer Pay Level-12 (Rs.78,800 - 2,09,200/-), Producer Pay Level-10 (Rs.56,100 - 1,77,500/-) and Computer Programme Assistant Pay Level-06 (Rs.35,400 - 1,12,400/-), as per the 7th CPC.
Q6How do I apply for CEC Recruitment 2026?
A
Send the filled Annexure-I with the Annexure-II forwarding certificate and enclosures by e-mail to [email protected], and send the hard copy through proper channel to the Administrative Officer (Admin.), CEC, IUAC Campus, Aruna Asaf Ali Marg, New Delhi – 110067.
Q7What is the selection process?
A
Applications are short-listed and only short-listed candidates are called for an interview; CEC may restrict the number called to those with a higher percentage of marks in the requisite qualification.
Q8Who can apply for these CEC posts?
A
Only officers of Central or State Governments, UT Administration, PSUs, Universities, recognised institutes and research institutions, and autonomous or statutory organisations who meet the eligibility criteria for the post.
Q9How long is the deputation period?
A
The deputation is for an initial period of one year, which may be extended further as per requirements, and may be terminated earlier at the discretion of CEC.
Q10Can I apply for more than one post?
A
Yes, the notice says separate applications may be forwarded for each post.
