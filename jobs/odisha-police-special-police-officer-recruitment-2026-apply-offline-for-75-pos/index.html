Odisha Police SPO Selection Process

The selection test will be held at Reserve Police Ground, Raurkela on 23.10.2026 at 06:30 AM. As per the notification, the test includes verification of original documents, a medical test, a physical test and an interview. Candidates will be put through a medical test. A Medical Board will test their physical fitness, and the decision of the Board is final and binding. Persons with physical deformities will not be eligible for appointment. Only those found medically fit will be allowed to participate in further tests. Candidates should be physically fit and mentally alert. They should be physically capable of discharging field duty by putting in hard labour for at least 8 hours a day, during day or night.
