CSL Ship Draftsman Trainee Salary / Stipend

Selected candidates will undergo a two-year training period and will be paid a monthly stipend, along with eligibility for extra hours of work beyond normal work hours. There will be a performance evaluation at the end of the first year, and the second year of training will be given only to successful candidates. Depending on organisational requirements, vacancy availability and individual performance, successful trainees may be considered for contractual posts for a period of 5 years, at CSL's discretion. The notification does not specify a regular/permanent appointment at the end of training, and CSL clarifies that it is not liable for regular appointment of trainees during or after the two-year training period.

CSL Ship Draftsman Trainee Selection Process

Selection for CSL Ship Draftsman Trainee Recruitment 2026 is entirely through a Practical Test carrying 100 marks. Candidates are required to prepare a drawing using CAD software, and the test will be held at CSL, Kochi. Only candidates who clear certificate verification will be permitted to appear for the Practical Test, which will be scheduled on a later date. The rank list for each discipline will be prepared based on marks scored in the practical test among candidates meeting the eligibility requirements; in case of a tie, relative merit will be decided based on seniority in age. CSL may relax the minimum pass mark if the number of qualified candidates is insufficient. The rank list will remain valid for up to 18 months from the date of reporting of the last candidate advised to join, or until a fresh notification is issued for the same post.
