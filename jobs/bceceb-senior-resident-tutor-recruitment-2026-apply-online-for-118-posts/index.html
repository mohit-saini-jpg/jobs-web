BCECEB Senior Resident/Tutor Eligibility Criteria

As per the NMC (Teachers Eligibility Qualifications in Medical Institutions Regulations, 2025), DNB / DNB Super Speciality qualifications are also recognized as valid for selection to the Senior Resident/Tutor post. The minimum educational qualification for Senior Resident/Tutor posts is a Postgraduate degree in the concerned subject/specialty. Only in case of a shortage of eligible postgraduate-degree candidates, candidates with a minimum qualification of Postgraduate Diploma in the relevant subject will be considered; however, such diploma holders will have 10 marks deducted in the merit list compared to postgraduate degree holders, and postgraduate degree holders will rank higher in merit even if diploma holders secure the same marks. Candidates holding a postgraduate qualification recognized as equivalent under Medical Council teacher eligibility qualification norms are also eligible

BCECEB Senior Resident/Tutor Age Limit

The age limit is calculated as on 1 August 2026, as per General Administration Department Resolution No. 294 dated 7 January 2016. Members of the Bihar State Health Service cadre are entitled to a 5-year age relaxation as per the applicable service rules. Candidates who complete the eligibility conditions between 1 August 2025 and 1 August 2026 are also covered under this age limit, provided they meet other eligibility conditions. Persons with Disabilities (Divyang) are entitled to an additional 10-year relaxation in the maximum age limit; however, only one relaxation provision (either on the basis of disability or any other applicable basis) can be availed - the two cannot be combined. Before submitting the application, candidates are advised to verify that they meet the required age criteria. Use our Age Calculator to calculate your exact age as per the official cut-off date.

BCECEB Senior Resident/Tutor Reservation Policy

The panel for tenure posts will be prepared as per the reservation policy notified by the Government of Bihar. Reserved category applicants must submit their caste certificate through the competent authority. Reservation benefit will be granted only on submission of a Non-Creamy Layer caste certificate applicable to the State of Bihar; candidates who do not submit such a certificate will be treated as belonging to the Unreserved category. Reservation for women, differently-abled candidates, and freedom fighters' grandchildren/granddaughters will be provided as per the norms prescribed by the General Administration Department.

BCECEB Senior Resident/Tutor Terms for Appointment to Tenure Post

Only the subjects mentioned in the Prospectus will be filled through this panel. Doctors holding a postgraduate qualification in more than one subject and wishing to apply for multiple subjects must apply separately, department-wise, and mention this clearly in their online application. Candidates whose degree is not recognized by MCI/DCI/NMC will not be considered for candidature. Upon appointment to the tenure post, doctors will be paid as per Pay Level-09; those already serving in the Health Service cadre will continue to draw their existing pay, along with Dearness Allowance and other admissible allowances. Candidates who have already completed a three-year tenure as Senior Resident/Tutor in a particular subject are not eligible for reappointment to the tenure (three-year) post in the same subject. BCECEB Senior Resident/Tutor Selection Process

A merit panel will be prepared for the tenure posts of Senior Resident/Tutor based on educational qualification and the applicable reservation policy. Postgraduate degree holders will rank higher in merit than postgraduate diploma holders, even in case of equal marks. Selected/shortlisted candidates will be intimated about the Counselling/Interview schedule after submission of the application. Candidates must appear for Counselling/Document Verification on the scheduled date with all required original certificates and one extra photocopy of each. No travel allowance will be paid for attending the Counselling. The Department's decision regarding post-selection posting will be final, and no claims regarding posting will be entertained.
