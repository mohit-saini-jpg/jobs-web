DEI Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria and Qualification

Eligibility is post-specific. Teaching post qualifications are governed by the UGC Regulations, 2018 (and subsequent amendments) or NCTE/AICTE norms, as applicable. Key common conditions: Applicants must be Indian nationals (including Persons of Indian Origin) or Overseas Citizens of India (OCIs). A minimum of 55% marks (or an equivalent grade) at the master's level is the essential qualification for direct recruitment of teachers and equivalent cadres at any level. A 5% relaxation at Bachelor's and Master's level is allowed for SC/ST/OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) and specified categories of Persons with Disabilities, based on qualifying marks only (no grace marks). DEI Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection is based on a Seminar-cum-Presentation, Aptitude Test, Trade Test, or Interview, depending on the post, conducted on the recommendation of the Institute's Screening Committee. The notification clarifies that not all applicants are necessarily called for this stage, and no TA/DA is admissible for attending it. Candidates must carry original documents, self-attested annexures, and photographs for verification at this stage; failure to produce these may result in the candidate not being allowed to appear.

  • 1Visit the official Institute website, www.dei.ac.in.
  • 2Locate the online application link for Advertisement No. 09 DEI - JULY 2026 and register/submit the application form with the required details.

  • 4After payment, download the PDF of the submitted application.
