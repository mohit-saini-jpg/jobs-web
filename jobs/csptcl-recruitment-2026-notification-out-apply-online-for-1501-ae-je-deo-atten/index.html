CSPTCL Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

The recruitment process includes an online Computer-Based Test (CBT). Depending on the post, shortlisted candidates may also have to undergo an interview, physical examination, shorthand test, typing test or document verification. The online CBT will consist of 100 multiple-choice questions for 100 marks , with a duration of two hours. The examination carries negative marking of 0.25 marks for every incorrect answer. Minimum qualifying marks are 40% for UR/OBC candidates and 30% for SC/ST candidates. The question paper will be available in English and Hindi.

CSPTCL Recruitment 2026 Important Conditions

Candidates should carefully review the following requirements before submitting an application. Only Chhattisgarh domicile candidates are eligible for the notified Class III and IV posts. Selected candidates must fulfil the prescribed medical fitness and document verification requirements. Selected candidates are required to execute the applicable service bond for a minimum four-year service period, including probation and training.
