What Is the CHSE Odisha Instant/ Improvement Exam?

The Instant/Improvement Examination is a scheme introduced by CHSE Odisha in 2026. It allows students who have already cleared their Plus II (Class 12) annual exam to appear for a single-day test to improve their marks in one particular subject, without waiting for the next full annual exam cycle. The scheme is aimed at helping students improve their overall scores in time for college admissions. Separately, "Instant" category candidates are those completing pending requirements from an earlier exam cycle. Both categories were evaluated together and had their results declared on the same day.

How to Check and Download CHSE Odisha Class 12 Improvement Result 2026

Follow these steps to check your result online:

  • Visit the official CHSE Odisha result website: results.odisha.gov.in.
  • Look for the link related to the "Annual Higher Secondary Instant/Improvement Examination 2026" result.
  • Enter your correct examination roll number and registration number in the fields provided.
  • Submit the details to view your result on screen.
  • Check your subject-wise marks, qualifying status, and other details carefully.
  • Download and save the marksheet, or take a printout for reference.

Candidates can also access their digital marksheet and certificate through DigiLocker, as confirmed by CHSE Odisha.

Details Required to Check the Result

  • Examination roll number (Instant/Improvement Exam 2026)
  • Registration number

These details are available on the admit card issued for the Instant/Improvement Examination 2026.

What Details Appear on the CHSE Odisha Improvement Marksheet 2026?

Based on the official announcement, the online marksheet is provisional in nature and displays subject-wise performance and qualifying status. The following details are officially confirmed as part of the result declaration process:

  • Subject-wise result of the Instant/Improvement Examination 2026
  • Overall pass/fail status
  • Division (1st, 2nd, or 3rd), where applicable

How Will Certificates and Marksheets Be Issued?

CHSE Odisha has confirmed the following distribution process:

  • Digital copies of marksheets and certificates will be issued through DigiLocker.
  • Hard copies of certificates and marksheets will be issued by CHSE and distributed to students through their respective higher secondary schools by August 15, 2026.

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Use the correct roll number and registration number exactly as printed on your admit card.
  • The result available online is provisional; the original certificate will be issued later through your school.
  • Download and save a copy of the online marksheet immediately after it is declared, for use in admissions or other applications.
  • In case of any discrepancy in your result, contact your school authorities or CHSE Odisha directly.
  • Rely only on the official website, results.odisha.gov.in, and avoid unofficial or third-party links claiming to show results.
