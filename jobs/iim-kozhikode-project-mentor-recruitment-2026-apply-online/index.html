IIM Kozhikode Project Mentor Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Indian Institute of Management, Kozhikode (भारतीय प्रबंध संस्थान, कोषिक्कोड) has one position of Project Mentor on contract at IIMK-Kochi Campus, initially for one year, with a consolidated monthly remuneration of Rs.39,800/- (including accommodation allowance of Rs.3,500/- and telephone allowance of Rs.300/-). Post-Graduates or Graduates in any subject from a recognized university with relevant experience, up to a maximum age of 40 years, may apply online at the careers page of iimk.ac.in latest by 5:00 p.m. on 9 December 2026.

About IIM Kozhikode Project Mentor

Indian Institute of Management, Kozhikode is a management institute located at IIM Kozhikode Campus P.O, Kozhikode - 673570. The Project Mentor post is at its Kochi campus, where the Project Mentor will support faculty coordinators in running executive training programmes such as the Senior Management Programme and other academic programmes. The notification was issued by the AO (HR-Staff Matters) on 19 November 2026.

Who Can Apply

Post-Graduation or Graduation in any subject from a recognized university. Minimum 2-3 years for Post Graduates and 4-5 years for Graduates in analytical roles and/or client engagement/ account management roles. In addition to the qualification and experience, the candidate should possess: Good analytical skills Proficiency in handling MS Office Fluency and strong command of oral and written English Experience in developing and managing institutional clients and relationships, especially in the service sectors and/or managing executive training programmes in recognized educational institutions. The maximum age limit for the IIM Kozhikode Project Mentor post is 40 years. The notice does not mention an age relaxation or a cut-off date for counting age. Candidates must ensure before applying that they meet these criteria; a candidate found ineligible at any stage of the recruitment process

Pay and Tenure

The accommodation allowance of Rs.3,500/- and the telephone allowance of Rs.300/- are part of the Rs.39,800/- figure, not paid on top of it. The engagement will purely be on contract basis, initially for a period of one year. Extension, if any, will be subject to job requirements and performance.

IIM Kozhikode Project Mentor Job Description

As printed under Roles and Responsibilities, the Project Mentor is required to support faculty coordinators in managing executive training programmes such as the Senior Management Programme and other academic programmes, especially in terms of: Regularly engaging participants, who primarily include senior executives Tracking and acting on participant feedback Coordinating with teaching faculty as needed Assisting in Kochi programme promotions, admissions, open-houses, assignment assessments and other related activities to promote and maintain a top-notch course experience for participants She/he may also be required to attend/monitor and assist in the conduct of the online programme sessions on a regular or need basis, whenever they happen (weekends/weekdays)

Selection and Interview

Applications will be screened, and shortlisted candidates alone will be interviewed. The date and time of the interview will be notified later; interview time slots will be communicated by e-mail. Candidates who have applied should visit the Institute website and check their emails regularly for any change of interview schedule. The decision of the Director, IIMK regarding the selection will be final.
