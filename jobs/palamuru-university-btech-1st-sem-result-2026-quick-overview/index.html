4How to Check Palamuru University B.Tech Revaluation Result 2026
Sr.TypeTextStyleItems
1paragraphFollow these steps to check your result on the official portal:
2listordered
  • Open the official result link: results.palamuruuniversity.com.
  • Enter your Hall Ticket Number in the format shown on the portal (example format: 17044126457001).
  • From the Year/Sem dropdown, select the revaluation option for Semester I Regular (shown on the portal as "RV_ISem-Regular").
  • Submit the form to view your revised grade/marks.
  • Download or take a screenshot of the result page for your records.
5Important Instructions
Sr.TypeStyleItems
1listunordered
  • Use only the official Palamuru University result portal to check your result; avoid third-party or unofficial websites.
  • Keep your hall ticket number handy before visiting the result page.
  • The revised marks from revaluation, once confirmed, typically replace the earlier marks for the subject(s) revalued — students should verify this with the Examination Branch, as this specific detail is not stated on the notifications page.
  • For any discrepancy in the result, contact the Controller of Examinations office directly rather than relying on unofficial sources.
