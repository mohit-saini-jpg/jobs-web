NHAI Recruitment 2026 - Eligibility Criteria

General Manager (Law): Bachelor of Law + 14 years experience including 6 years in contractual matters/arbitration/land acquisition. General Manager (Transport Planning): B.Tech in Civil Engineering + Post Graduate in Transport Planning + 14 years experience including 6 years in road project evaluation. General Manager (Transport Economist): Post Graduate in Economics + 14 years experience including 6 years in economic analysis of transport/road projects. General Manager (Environment): Graduate/Post Graduate in Environment Science/Engineering or related fields or IFS officer + 14 years experience including 9 years in environment clearance/EIA/EMP. General Manager (Electronics): B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering + 14 years experience including 6 years in Electronics & Communication/IT in Govt/PSU. General Manager (Information Technology): B.Tech/BE/MCA in Computer Science/I
