About SCI

The Shipping Corporation of India Limited is a Government Company incorporated on 2 October 1961, with its registered office at Mumbai, Maharashtra. The notice describes it as a Schedule 'A' Navratna CPSE listed on NSE and BSE, with three segments: Bulk Carrier & Tankers, Liner Services, and Technical and Managed vessels services. It employed 445 regular shore employees (Executive 424, Non-Executive 21) as on 30.06.2026, along with about 1855 seafarers.

SCI Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Adequate experience/exposure at senior level of management in a large organization of repute, of which at least five years during the last ten years should have been in shipping and shipping operations. Experience in tramp trading connected with shipping organization/ sales, purchase and/or commercial operations is desirable. The applicant must, on the date of application and on the date of interview, be employed in a regular capacity, not in a contractual/ad-hoc capacity, in one of the following: A Central Public Sector Enterprise (CPSE), including a full-time functional Director in the Board of a CPSE. Central Government Group 'A' Officers, including the Armed Forces of the Union and All India Services, and officers from Public Sector Banks / Financial Institutions / Autonomous Bodies, etc. A State Public Sector Enterprise (SPSE) with annual turnover of Rs. 5,000 crore or more. SCI Recruitment 2026 Job Description

The Director (Bulk Carriers & Tankers) is a member of the Board of Directors and reports to the Chairman & Managing Director. The Director is responsible for operations, performance and profitability of the B&T fleet/operating division of SCI, as also performance of the Company.
