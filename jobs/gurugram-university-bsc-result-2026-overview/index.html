5How to Check and Download the Gurugram University Re-appear Result (May-2026)
Sr.TypeStyleItems
1listordered
  • Visit the official Gurugram University result notices page: https://gurugramuniversity.ac.in/allNotifications/resultNotice/index.php
  • Look for the notification titled "Re-appear Result Notification-1 (Result Sheet) May-2026" dated 23-07-2026.
  • Click on the notification link to open the result sheet PDF.
  • Use the search function (Ctrl+F on desktop) inside the PDF and enter your roll number to locate your result.
  • Check your subject-wise status and overall result carefully.
  • Download and save the PDF, or take a screenshot, for future reference.
