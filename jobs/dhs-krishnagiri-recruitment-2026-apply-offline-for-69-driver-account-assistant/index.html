Krishnagiri DHS Important Instructions

These posts are purely temporary and will not be made permanent at any time. Applications are accepted only in person or by Speed Post - no online or email applications are mentioned in the notification. The following certificate copies must be compulsorily attached with the application: (1) 10th class mark certificate, (2) 12th class mark certificate, (3) Degree/Diploma certificate and registration certificate, (4) Degree/Diploma mark certificates, (5) Community/Caste certificate, (6) Previous experience certificate approved by a competent authority, and (7) Priority/special certificates, if any. Applications without the above certificate copies will be rejected. The Dentist post will be filled on a rotation basis. The number of vacant posts is subject to change. Full details of the posts are available on the Krishnagiri district website, krishnagiri.nic.in.
