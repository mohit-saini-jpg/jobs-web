About NCSK

The National Commission for Safai Karamcharis is a commission under the Department of Social Justice & Empowerment, Government of India, with its office at 'B' Wing, 4th Floor, Lok Nayak Bhawan, Khan Market, New Delhi-110003. The notice says these engagements are being made to strengthen the working infrastructure of the Commission.

Vacancy Details

The notice lists 12 vacancies across nine posts. For each post, the Commission accepts applications from three groups: serving officials, retired officials and qualified professionals.

Who Can Apply

The age limit is the same for every post. Serving officials should not be more than 45 years of age. Retired officials should not be more than 62 years of age. Qualified professionals should not be more than 45 years of age. The notice leaves the "As on" date for age blank, so no cut-off date is printed. Serving officials: Must be serving in analogous or preferably higher posts of Central Government/State Govt/UTs and PSUs. Preference goes to officers with experience in making presentations and reports, analysing data, and devising policy and schemes. Knowledge of English and Hindi writing, computers and E-office is also preferred. Retired officials: Must have superannuated from analogous or higher posts of Central Government/State Govt/UTs and PSUs. The same preference applies, with knowledge of English and Hindi writing/computers. Qualified professionals: Graduates/Professional Graduat

Pay and Tenure

Pay depends on the type of applicant: Serving officials (deputation): Pay is as per Govt. Rules for deputation, for every post. Retired officials: Remuneration is as per the Department of Expenditure's O.M. No. 3-25/2020-E.IIIA dated 09.12.2020 for Retired Government Servants, for every post. Qualified professionals get a fixed remuneration on short-term contract, set by experience: The deputation, contractual or short-term contract period is initially 01 year. It can be extended based on requirement and the individual's satisfactory performance. Deputation allowance will be paid as per existing rules. Terms of deputation are governed by DOP&T's O.M. No. 6/8/2009-Estt. (Pay-II) dated 17.06.2010 and Government instructions issued from time to time.

NCSK Recruitment 2027 : Important Instructions

Applications received after the last date or otherwise found incomplete shall not be entertained. The Cadre Controlling Authority must certify that the officer, if selected, will be relieved immediately. It must also certify that integrity is beyond doubt and that no vigilance case is pending. If a penalty has been imposed during the last 10 years, a penalty statement may be enclosed instead of the no-penalty certificate. Retired applicants declare that they were clear from the vigilance angle at retirement. They must also declare that no disciplinary or judicial action is pending against them. For queries, contact Telephone No. 24618119. The notice is signed by Raj Kapil, Consultant (A & C), Tel No. 24649354.
