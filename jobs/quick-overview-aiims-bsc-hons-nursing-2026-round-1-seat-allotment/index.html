How to Check and Download the AIIMS BSc Nursing Round 1 Seat Allotment Result

  • Visit the official AIIMS examination website,aiimsexams.ac.in
  • Go to the B.Sc. (Hons.) Nursing 2026 counselling section, usually listed under the Academics or Counselling tab.
  • Click on the link for "AIIMS BSc (Hons.) Nursing Round 1 Seat Allotment Result 2026."
  • Log in using your registration ID and password, or open the published PDF list.
  • Use Ctrl+F (Cmd+F on Mac) to search for your roll number or registration number if checking the PDF list.
  • Check your allotted AIIMS institute and overall rank.
  • Download and save the result/allotment letter for future reference.

Options Available After Seat Allotment

Once a seat is allotted in Round 1, candidates must log in to the counselling portal and select one of the available options before the deadline. Failing to choose an option, or failing to report to the allotted institute, results in forfeiture of the seat.

OptionWhat It Means
Option 1Accept the allotted seat and do not participate in further counselling rounds (freeze the seat).
Option 2Accept the allotted seat but continue to participate in Round 2 for a possible upgrade.
Option 3Decline/refuse the allotted seat and exit the counselling process entirely.
Option 4Decline/refuse the allotted seat but continue to participate in Round 2.

If a candidate who selected Option 2 is upgraded to a different AIIMS institute in Round 2, the Round 1 allotment is automatically cancelled.

What Happens If You Don't Get a Seat in Round 1

Candidates who are not allotted a seat in Round 1, or who select Option 2/Option 4 to remain in contention, will automatically be considered in Round 2 based on the same choices submitted between July 31 and August 6, 2026. AIIMS has confirmed that fresh choice filling will not be reopened for Round 2 - the original preference order will be reused. Candidates should therefore ensure their original choices remain valid, since the same order will apply to later rounds.

Important Instructions

  • Rely only on the official website, aiimsexams.ac.in, for the seat allotment result, acceptance links, and any schedule revisions. Avoid third-party apps or unverified social media channels for allotment PDFs.
  • Complete the online acceptance (Option selection) and physical reporting within the notified window; both run in parallel, not sequentially.
  • Keep checking the portal regularly, since AIIMS counselling dates have been revised in past rounds this year.
  • Save a copy/screenshot of the result and allotment letter as soon as it is available.
