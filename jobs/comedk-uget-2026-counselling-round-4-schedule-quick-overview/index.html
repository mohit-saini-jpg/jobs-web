COMEDK UGET 2026 Counselling Round 4 Schedule - Quick : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 25/08/2026

The Consortium of Medical, Engineering and Dental Colleges of Karnataka (COMEDK) is conducting the fourth and final round of UGET 2026 counselling for admission to B.Tech/ B.E. programmes at its member engineering colleges. Round 4 is the supplementary round meant for candidates who did not get a seat in Round 1, 2, or 3, as well as Reject & Upgrade and Accept & Upgrade candidates carried forward from Round 3. This article explains the Round 4 schedule status, eligibility, process, and everything candidates need to check before choice filling opens, based on the official COMEDK counselling document and portal.

COMEDK Round 4 Allotment Rules - What Happens to Different Candidate Categories

Candidate Status Entering Round 4What Happens
Not allotted a seat in Round 1, 2 or 3 (first allotment in Round 4)Gets the option of "Accept & Freeze" or "Reject & Withdraw." If the allotted seat is not liked, the candidate can select "Reject & Withdraw" and exit counselling.
Reject & Upgrade status, allotted a seat in Round 4Must mandatorily join the allotted college. Failure to join can lead to forfeiture of fees and a penalty of five times the fee paid.
Reject & Upgrade status, not allotted a seat in Round 4Exits COMEDK counselling and receives a refund of the total fee paid.
Accept & Upgrade status from Round 3Gets only the "Accept & Freeze" option in Round 4. If a higher preference is allotted, the earlier Round 3 seat is cancelled automatically; otherwise the Round 3 seat remains valid. Must join the allotted college.

How to Participate in COMEDK Round 4 Counselling: Step-by-Step Process

  • Visit the official COMEDK counselling website, comedk.org.
  • Log in using your Application Number/User ID and password.
  • Check your candidate status (Reject & Upgrade, Accept & Upgrade, or Not Allotted) shown in the login.
  • Go to the "Option Entry" or choice filling section once it is activated for Round 4.
  • Add, reorder, edit, or delete your preferred college and branch combinations within the choice filling window.
  • Save and lock your choices before the deadline.
  • Check the Round 4 seat allotment result once published on the portal.
  • If allotted a seat, choose "Accept & Freeze" or, if eligible, "Reject & Withdraw," and complete the fee payment within the given window.
  • Report to the allotted college in person with a printout of the allotment letter and fee receipt, within the reporting window.

Important Instructions for COMEDK Round 4 Candidates

  • There is no seat cancellation facility after the Round 3 cancellation period ends - Round 4 allotments (except first-time Reject & Withdraw cases) are largely binding.
  • Reject & Upgrade candidates who wish to skip Round 4 entirely must use the exit option during the specified exit window after Round 3; once Round 4 begins, this choice cannot be reversed.
  • Always check the respective college's reporting hours in advance, especially around weekends and public holidays, before travelling to report.
  • Keep your registration number, password, and all verified documents accessible throughout the Round 4 process.
  • Since Round 4 is the last round of the main counselling process, any seats vacant after Round 4 may go to a further vacancy round if COMEDK announces one - check the official portal for updates.
