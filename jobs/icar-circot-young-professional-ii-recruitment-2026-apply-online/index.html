Pay and Tenure

A consolidated amount of Rs. 42,000/- (Rupees Forty Two Thousand Only) per month will be paid to the selected Young Professional-II. The appointment is purely temporary, contractual and co-terminus with the project and may be terminated at any time. The engagement is for one year, extendable for two more years (one year at a time), subject to requirement of the services and satisfactory performance after evaluation by the Director, ICAR-CIRCOT, Mumbai. The maximum duration is three years (1+1+1). Casual leave is 08 days in a calendar year on pro-rata basis, with 02 restricted holidays as per the rules of Govt. of India/ICAR. Un-availed leave is not carried forward to the next calendar year. Compensatory leave in lieu of duty on holidays may be allowed, but not more than 02 leaves can accrue in a month. It can be accumulated only up to 05 days at a time and may be availed within 03 months

Selection and Interview

The notice provides for a hybrid mode interview for short listed candidates at ICAR-CIRCOT, Adenwala Road, Five Gardens, Matunga, Mumbai 400019. The application form asks candidates to state the mode of interview (Online/Offline). Reporting time is 10.00 am and interview time is 11:00 a.m. on 25 November 2026. No separate interview call letter will be issued to candidates. Candidates should ensure they fulfil all eligibility conditions. The decision of the Director, ICAR-CIRCOT, Mumbai is final and binding regarding selection to the post.

ICAR-CIRCOT Young Professional II Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

The engagement does not constitute a regular job or appointment of any nature in the ICAR, and there is no provision for absorption or re-employment in ICAR-CIRCOT or ICAR after completion of the time bound project. The place of work is ICAR-CIRCOT, Mumbai, but the Young Professional may need to travel to any place in India for project related or office work. Selected Young Professionals will sign a declaration of secrecy and Non-Disclosure Agreement before reporting. To resign before the contract expires, one month's notice is required; otherwise the competent authority reserves the right to forfeit the consolidated emoluments.
