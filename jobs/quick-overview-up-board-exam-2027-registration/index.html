Quick Overview - UP Board Exam 2027 Registration : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 01/09/2026

UP Board Exam 2027 registration update: The Uttar Pradesh Madhyamik Shiksha Parishad (UPMSP) has extended the registration timeline for students appearing in the Class 10 (High School) and Class 12 (Intermediate) board examinations 2027. Schools now have additional time to collect examination fees, deposit them in the treasury, upload student data, and complete verification and correction on the official portal.

Who Is Eligible to Register

UP Board registration 2027 is open to students studying in UPMSP-recognised and affiliated schools. Broadly, the following categories can register:

  • Regular students currently studying in Class 10 or Class 12 in an affiliated institution
  • Private candidates who meet UPMSP's eligibility guidelines
  • Compartment or improvement candidates, where permitted by the board
  • Students who satisfy the eligibility criteria prescribed by UPMSP for the 2027 board examinations

How UP Board Class 10, 12 Registration 2027 Is Completed (School-Level Process)

Because UPMSP registration is not a direct student-login process, the school follows a defined sequence of steps on the official portal:

  • Admission and fee collection: The school completes student admission formalities and collects the examination fee (with late fee, if applicable) within the notified date.
  • Fee deposit in treasury: The head of the institution deposits the collected fee in the government treasury through a challan within the prescribed date.
  • Uploading student data: The school uploads student academic details and challan/fee information on the official portal (examreg.upmsp.edu.in / upmsp.edu.in).
  • Verification: The principal verifies the uploaded data on the portal to check for errors in personal or academic details.
  • Correction, if needed: Any discrepancies identified during verification are corrected and re-uploaded within the correction window. Corrections are generally allowed only until midnight on the last day of this window, and no new student entries are accepted once the correction phase closes.
  • Final submission to DIOS: The school submits the final list of registered candidates, along with photographs and the treasury sheet/challan copy, to the District Inspector of Schools (DIOS) office by the notified date.

Important Instructions for Schools and Students

  • Registration is mandatory - only students registered through this process will be eligible to receive an admit card and appear in the UP Board 2027 exams.
  • All information uploaded must be accurate; UPMSP has indicated it will not accept corrections or approvals after the prescribed timelines close.
  • Schools are responsible for verifying student data (name, parentage, date of birth, gender, subject, photograph) before the correction window ends.
  • No new student registrations are typically permitted once the correction phase is underway.
  • Students should regularly confirm with their school that their details have been correctly uploaded, since the registration is done by the institution and not directly by the student.
  • For the most current and legally accurate dates, always cross-check with the official UPMSP notification, as revised schedules can be updated further by the board.
