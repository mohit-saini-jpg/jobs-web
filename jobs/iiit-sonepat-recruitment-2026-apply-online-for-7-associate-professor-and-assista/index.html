About IIIT Sonepat Recruitment 2026

Indian Institute of Information Technology Sonepat is an Institute of National Importance established in 2014 under the provisions of the Indian Institute of Information Technology PPP- Act, 2017. The institute has its permanent campus at Kilohrad, Sonepat, and operates from its office at IIT Delhi Sonepat Campus, I-Tech Technopark Plot No 4 Rajiv Gandhi Education City, Rai, Sonipat, Haryana. The institute is dedicated to imparting high-quality technical education and cultivating a rigorous culture of innovation and research in Information Technology and its allied disciplines.

Qualification and Experience

Qualifications and experience follow the Four-Tier Flexible Faculty Structure as per Ministry of Education guidelines (F.No.27-11/2011 TS.1 dated 23 April 2014). All degrees acquired by the applicant must be from an Institute / University recognized by the UGC/AICTE/AIU/GOI. All applicants shall have a Ph.D. in the relevant or equivalent discipline and First Class in the preceding degrees (Bachelor's Degree onwards). In case First Class is not mentioned in the preceding degrees by the University/Institution, candidates should have passed and secured at least 6.5 CGPA (on a 10-point scale) or 60% marks in aggregate.   • 1Visit the official Samarth recruitment portal at https://iiitsonepatrec.samarth.edu.in/.
  • 2Complete registration and fill in the online application form with personal, educational, and experience details.
  • 3Upload self-attested copies of certificates, testimonials, category certificate (if applicable), and recent photograph.
  • 4Pay the non-refundable application processing fee online through the payment gateway.
  • 5Submit the online application form on or before 21 November 2026 (up to 11:59 PM) and note down the unique application/form reference number.
  • 6Download and print the filled-in application form PDF from the portal.
  • 7Prepare a properly spiral-bound hard copy, sequentially paginating every page, and attach self-attested copies of all supporting documents, experience letters, pay slips, and the Annexure-I NOC/VCC if serving in Government/PSU.
  • 8Place the spiral-bound application into a preferably A4/Legal/A3-sized envelope and superscribe the cover with: APPLICATION FOR THE POST OF ____________________.
