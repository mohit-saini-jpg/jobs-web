ESIC Asansol Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

ESIC Medical College & Hospital, Asansol (West Bengal), under the Employees' State Insurance Corporation (Ministry of Labour & Employment, Government of India), will hold walk-in document verification and interviews on 1, 2, 3 and 4 December 2026 for 110 contractual posts: 12 Professor, 19 Associate Professor, 25 Assistant Professor, 52 Senior Resident and 2 Junior Resident. Total consolidated pay (approx.) ranges from ₹1,24,692 to ₹2,66,732 a month depending on the post. Reporting time is 9:30 AM to 10:30 AM under Advertisement No. 02 of 2026.

About ESIC Asansol

ESIC Medical College & Hospital, Asansol is at Sen Raliegh Road, Vivekanand Sarani, Asansol - 713305 (W.B.). The notice is issued by the Dean (I/C) under the Employees' State Insurance Corporation. It invites eligible candidates for vacant teaching faculty posts (Professor, Associate Professor and Assistant Professor) and for Senior and Junior Resident positions on a contractual basis.

Department-wise Vacancies

The notice lists 24 departments, with each post's count and category break-up. Vacancies add up to 12 Professor, 19 Associate Professor, 25 Assistant Professor and 52 Senior Resident posts. A further 2 Junior Resident posts (1 OBC, 1 UR) are printed separately, making 110 in all. The notice calls the vacancies provisional: they may increase or decrease as per actual requirement for teaching and patient care. Junior Resident: total 2 posts (1 OBC, 1 UR). Qualification and Experience

The cut-off date for age, qualifications and other eligibility criteria is 01 December 2026. Retired faculties may also apply for eligible posts. All medical candidates must hold updated Medical Registration (NMC/State Medical Council) with all post-MBBS/MD/MS qualifications. Candidates without updated registration must produce proof of having applied for it at the time of interview, and selected candidates must submit the updated registration certificate before joining. For the discipline-specific eligibility of each post, refer to the official notification. Age relaxation is as per the prevailing norms of the Government of India. The maximum tenure of engagement for teaching faculty is till 70 years of age, whichever is earlier, provided other rules and regulations are satisfied.

ESIC Asansol Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection is by document verification followed by an interview. It is on the basis of academic credentials and performance in the interview conducted by the duly constituted Selection Board. The result will be published only on the "Recruitments" section of www.esic.gov.in . The offer of appointment will be sent by e-mail only, and selected candidates must join as per the instructions in the offer letter. Interview opportunity is provisional and subject to detailed scrutiny of the eligibility criteria.

Documents Required

Candidates must bring all original documents. A candidate whose document is found not in order at verification will not be allowed in the interview. A candidate claiming reservation without the valid certificate will not be allowed to appear against the reserved category, but may be considered as a UR candidate subject to fulfilment of the eligibility criteria.

ESIC Asansol Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1This is a walk-in recruitment: candidates attend the venue on the interview date of their department and post, carrying the offline application form attached with the advertisement. The notice prints no last date for applying.
  • 2Venue: ESIC Medical College & Hospital, Asansol, Vivekanand Sarani, Asansol – 713305, West Bardhaman (WB). Candidates reporting after 10:30 AM on the scheduled interview date will be treated as absent.
  • 3Complete the offline application form attached with the advertisement. Candidates may apply for multiple posts if eligible, with a separate application, documents and fee (if applicable) for each post.
  • 4Attach self-attested copies of the documents listed in Clause (J) and the checklist.
  • 5Prepare the Demand Draft for the ₹500 fee in favour of "ESI Fund A/c No.1" payable at Asansol, unless you belong to an exempt category.
  • 6Report between 9:30 AM and 10:30 AM on the interview date for your department or post, carrying all original documents and testimonials.
  • 7Appear before the Document Verification Committee. Only candidates whose documents are successfully verified will be allowed to appear before the Interview Board.
