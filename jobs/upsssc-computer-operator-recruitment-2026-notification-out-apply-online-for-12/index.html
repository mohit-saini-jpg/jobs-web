UPSSSC Computer Operator Eligibility Criteria

Only candidates who appeared in the Preliminary Eligibility Test-2025 (PET-2025) and have been issued a PET-2025 score card by the Commission are eligible to apply for this Main Examination. Shortlisting for the Main Examination is done on merit based on the normalized PET-2025 score; candidates with a zero or negative absolute or normalized PET-2025 score will not be shortlisted. Candidates whose PET-2025 result is marked "Under Investigation" or "Provisional" are allowed to apply provisionally, subject to the Commission's final decision on their case.

UPSSSC Computer Operator Qualification

A Bachelor's degree from a university established by law in India, or an equivalent qualification recognized by the government, along with a Diploma in Computer Science or a Computer Science "O" Level certificate from NIELIT (or an equivalent institution recognized by the government). Three years of experience in the field of computer applications is required. The same mandatory qualification applies - a Bachelor's degree or its recognized equivalent, along with a Diploma in Computer Science or NIELIT "O" Level certificate - but with two years of experience in the field of computer applications. Other things being equal, preference in direct recruitment will be given to a candidate who has served in the Territorial Army for at least 2 years, or who holds a National Cadet Corps (NCC) "B" certificate.

UPSSSC Computer Operator Selection Process

Selection is based on a written (Main) examination , conducted under the Uttar Pradesh Advocate General and Law Officer Establishment Service Rules, 2009 (as amended), following the two-tier examination system adopted by the Commission (in which only candidates who appeared in the Commission's Preliminary Eligibility Test can apply for the Main Examination). Shortlisting for the written Main Examination is done on the basis of each candidate's normalized PET-2025 score. Category-wise, 15 times the number of advertised vacancies will be shortlisted for the written examination - including all candidates who hold a normalized score (to two decimal places) equal to the final category-wise cut-off score. Provisions of the Uttar Pradesh Public Examination (Prevention of Unfair Means) Act, 2024, dated 6 August 2024, apply to this examination. The Commission does not correspond individually with candidates or advise them on their eligibility; all updates are published on upsssc.gov.in, and the Commission's decision on a candidate's eligibility is final.
