NHPC Recruitment Through GATE 2026 Notification

Unlike recruitment drives that conduct a separate written examination, NHPC will shortlist candidates using normalized marks obtained out of 100 in the relevant GATE 2026 paper. Shortlisted applicants will participate in a Group Discussion and Personal Interview for final selection. The appointment includes one year of training in E2 Grade, followed by absorption as an Engineer or Officer and a one-year probation period upon successful completion of training. NHPC Trainee Engineer and Officer Salary 2026

NHPC Trainee Engineers and Officers will be placed in the E2 Grade pay scale of Rs. 50,000 to Rs. 1,60,000 with a 3% increment structure under the Industrial Dearness Allowance (IDA) pattern. The approximate annual emoluments for newly appointed employees will be Rs. 16 to 17 lakh. The compensation package includes basic pay and applicable benefits under NHPC company rules: Dearness Allowance (DA) House Rent Allowance (HRA) Cafeteria Allowance Performance Related Pay (PRP) Medical facilities Provident Fund (PF) Pension and gratuity After successfully completing one year of training, candidates will be absorbed as Engineers or Officers in the same pay scale and will undergo a one-year probation period.

NHPC Trainee Engineer Selection Process Through GATE 2026

NHPC will prepare the final selection merit list using GATE 2026 marks, Group Discussion and Personal Interview performance. GATE marks carry the highest weightage of 75%, while Group Discussion and Personal Interview together account for the remaining 25%. GATE-based shortlisting: Candidates will be shortlisted according to normalized marks obtained in the corresponding GATE 2026 paper. Shortlisting ratio: Normally, candidates numbering up to five times the vacancies will be shortlisted. The ratio may be exceeded when candidates in the same discipline and category obtain equal marks at the shortlisting threshold. Consent form: Shortlisted applicants must submit their consent to participate in Group Discussion and Personal Interview within the notified schedule. NHPC Trainee Engineer and Officer Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are available in NHPC Recruitment Through GATE 2026?
A
NHPC has announced 47 Trainee Engineer and Officer vacancies across Electronics and Communication, Environment, Information Technology, Geology and Geophysics disciplines. The total includes seven backlog vacancies.
Q2What is the last date to apply for NHPC Trainee Engineer Recruitment 2026?
A
The last date to apply online is 26 December 2026 at 5:00 PM. Online registration begins on 27 November 2026 at 10:00 AM through nhpcindia.com.
Q3Is GATE 2026 mandatory for NHPC Trainee Engineer and Officer posts?
A
Yes. Candidates must possess a valid GATE 2026 score in the examination paper corresponding to their selected discipline. GATE scores from other years are not accepted.
Q4Can MCA graduates apply for NHPC Recruitment 2026?
A
Yes. Candidates with a recognized full-time regular MCA qualification and a valid GATE 2026 score in Computer Science and Information Technology (CS) can apply for Trainee Engineer/Officer (IT), subject to the prescribed marks and other eligibility requirements.
Q5Can M.Sc. Environmental Science graduates apply for NHPC recruitment?
A
Yes. Candidates possessing a full-time regular M.Sc. in Environmental Science can apply for Trainee Engineer/Officer (Environment), provided they have the required educational marks and a valid GATE 2026 score in the ES paper.
Q6What is the salary for this recruitment?
A
It depends on the post: Rs. 50,000-3%-1,60,000 (IDA) for Trainee Engineer (E&amp;C), Rs. 50,000-3%-1,60,000 (IDA) for Trainee Engineer/ Officer (Environment), Rs. 50,000-3%-1,60,000 (IDA) for Trainee Engineer/ Officer (IT), Rs. 50,000-3%-1,60,000 (IDA) for Trainee Officer (Geology), Rs. 50,000-3%-1,60,000 (IDA) for Trainee Officer (Geophysicist).
Q7What is the maximum age limit for NHPC Recruitment Through GATE 2026?
A
The maximum age limit is 30 years as of 26 December 2026. Eligible reserved category candidates can claim age relaxation as specified in the official recruitment advertisement.
Q8Are there other conditions for this recruitment besides qualification and age?
A
Yes. Only Indian Nationals can apply. Valid GATE-2026 scorecard in the corresponding paper is required. Selected candidates should be medically fit as per extant NHPC guidelines. SC/ST and PwBD candidates with 50% marks in qualifying examination are eligible to apply.
Q9Who cannot apply for this recruitment?
A
Final year appearing students. Candidates without a valid GATE-2026 score (GATE score of any other year is not valid). Candidates from disciplines other than those listed for each post.
Q10How will candidates be selected for this recruitment?
A
Shortlisting on normalized GATE-2026 marks (out of 100) in the corresponding paper. Group Discussion and Personal Interview. Possible online/offline Psychometric Assessment. Empanelment on weighted score: GATE-2026 75%, Group Discussion 05%, Personal Interview 20%.
