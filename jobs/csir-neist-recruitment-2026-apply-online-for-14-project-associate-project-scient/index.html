CSIR-NEIST Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

CSIR – North East Institute of Science & Technology (CSIR-NEIST), Jorhat, Assam, is engaging 14 project staff on a purely temporary basis under Advertisement No. 10/2026-27-REC (PS) dated 13 November 2026: one Project Scientist-II, one Senior Project Associate, eleven Project Associate-I and one Project Associate-II, across seven research projects. Pay ranges from Rs.25,000/- to Rs.67,000/- per month plus HRA, depending on the post code and, for most posts, on NET or GATE qualification. Pay and Tenure

HRA is paid on top of the consolidated pay for every post code. For most Project Associate posts the pay depends on whether the candidate has NET or GATE qualification, as printed below. For Post Code 1010, the higher rate covers CSIR-UGC/ICAR/ICMR NET or GATE qualifiers, or those who have qualified national level examinations conducted by central government departments and/or their agencies/institutions. The engagement is on a purely temporary basis for a specific period, co-terminus with the duration of the project, and will not confer any right for regularization in the institute. The project completion date may be extended or curtailed depending upon the grant of the Funding Agency. The maximum tenure of engagement of a project staff is six (6) years, counting engagement in different projects in the same institute or in different CSIR institutes. Selection and Interview

Applicants will be shortlisted by the Project Team on the basis of academic performance and experience. Only shortlisted candidates will be called for an offline interview at CSIR-NEIST, Jorhat. During selection, due weightage will be given to the academic performance and experience of candidates. The list of shortlisted candidates, with the final date and time of the interview, will be uploaded on the CSIR-NEIST website. No TA/DA will be paid for attending the interview.
