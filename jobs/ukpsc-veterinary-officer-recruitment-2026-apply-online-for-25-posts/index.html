UKPSC Veterinary Officer Selection Process

Selection to the post of Veterinary Officer will be made through an Objective Type (Multiple Choice) written examination, based on which a merit/proficiency list will be prepared. The exam consists of three papers. The total marks for the written examination are 800. A negative marking of one-fourth (1/4) of the marks allotted to a question will be deducted for each wrong answer, and for multiple answers marked to a single question. No marks are deducted for unattempted questions. Use of calculators or any calculation device is not permitted in the exam hall. The written examination will be conducted at exam centres in Haridwar and Haldwani; if the number of candidates is low, the exam may be held only at Haridwar centres.
