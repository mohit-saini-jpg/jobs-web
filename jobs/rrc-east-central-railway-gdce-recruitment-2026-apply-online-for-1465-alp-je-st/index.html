Who Can Apply?

The applicant must be a regular employee of East Central Railway or Rail Wheel Plant, Bela. There is no minimum service requirement. Employees who have resigned or transferred from East Central Railway are not eligible. RPF and RPSF personnel cannot apply under this GDCE notification. Employees cannot apply for the same post on which they are presently working. The applicant must possess the required qualification by 15 October 2026. The qualification must be recorded in the employee’s service register.

Educational Qualification

The 50% marks requirement for Commercial Cum Ticket Clerk will not apply to SC, ST, Persons with Benchmark Disabilities, ex-servicemen and candidates possessing a qualification higher than Class 12. Candidates should check the post-wise trade, engineering discipline and medical-standard requirements in Annexure A of the official notification.

Minimum Qualifying Marks

For the aptitude test, candidates must obtain a minimum T-score of 42 in each test battery. No category-based relaxation is allowed in this requirement.

Senior Clerk Typing Test

Candidates appearing for the Senior Clerk post must achieve either of the following typing speeds on a computer: 30 words per minute in English; or 25 words per minute in Hindi. The typing test is qualifying in nature. Its marks will not be added while preparing the merit list.

RRC East Central Railway GDCE Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

Candidates must confirm their post-wise educational and medical eligibility before applying. The candidate’s name, father’s name and date of birth must match the service records and educational certificates. Post preferences cannot be changed after final submission. Incomplete or incorrectly completed applications may be rejected. Applications containing invalid photographs or signatures may be rejected. No request to change the examination centre, date or shift will be accepted. The notified vacancies are provisional and may increase, decrease or become nil. Candidates should regularly check the RRC/ECR website for examination and call-letter updates

RRC East Central Railway GDCE Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Visit the East Central Railway official website.
  • 2Open the Hajipur Headquarters section.
  • 3Select RRC Patna and open the GDCE section.
  • 4Click the application link for notification number RRC/ECR/HRD/Rectt/GDCE/2026.
  • 5Select the New Registration option.
  • 6Enter the 11-digit employee ID and date of birth.
  • 7Provide the required personal, service, educational and contact details.
  • 8Log in using the registration number and password received through email or SMS.
  • 9Upload the required photograph, signature and supporting certificates.
  • 10Select eligible posts and enter the post preferences carefully.
  • 11Review and submit the online application.
  • 12Save the submitted application as a PDF or take a printout for future reference.
  • 13Only one online application should be submitted. Candidates do not need to send an application printout or copies of certificates to RRC/ECR by post.
