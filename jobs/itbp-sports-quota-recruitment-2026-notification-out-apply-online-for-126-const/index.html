ITBP Constable (GD) Sports Quota Selection Process & Marking Scheme

Selection follows: Documentation → Physical Standard Test (PST) → Sports Trial Test (qualifying) → Merit List (based on sports achievement marks) → Detailed Medical Examination (DME) → Review Medical Examination (RME), if required. There is no written examination. The official notification lists 18 competition tiers in total (including Youth Olympics, World Championship Quadrennial/Biennial, Junior World Championship, Asian Championship Quadrennial/Biennial/Annual, Junior Asian Championship, World University Games, and other MYA&S-cleared international events). Candidates get marks for only the single highest medal/participation secured in the 01.01.2024–01.11.2026 window. Documentation: Verification of original certificates (age, education, caste, sports achievement); identity/biometric verification is mandatory Physical Standard Test (PST): Height, chest (male only) and weight checked
