IOB Local Bank Officer Selection Process 2026-27

Selection is made through an Online Examination, followed by a Language Proficiency Test (LPT) for candidates who have not studied the applied state's local language in Std. X or Std. XII, and a Personal Interview. There is a penalty of 0.25 marks for each wrong answer, with no penalty for unattempted questions. There is no sectional cut-off, but the minimum qualifying aggregate is 30% for reserved category candidates and 35% for unreserved candidates. Candidates shortlisted for the Personal Interview who have not studied the applied state's local language in Std. X or Std. XII must qualify an LPT before the interview. Candidates who fail the LPT will not proceed further in the selection process. The Personal Interview carries 100 marks. Indian Overseas Bank LBO Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

Candidates can apply for only one state; a candidate applying for one state's vacancy is not eligible to apply for any other state. Selected candidates will be posted within the applied state for the first 12 years of service or until promotion to SMGS-IV, whichever is earlier. Application Fee/Intimation charges, once paid, are non-refundable and cannot be adjusted against any other recruitment process. If multiple applications are submitted for the same state, only the last submitted application will be considered. Ration Card and Learner's Driving Licence are not accepted as valid photo ID proof. Request for change of examination centre, Personal Interview date, or centre will not be entertained. Candidates working in Government/Quasi-Government/PSU roles must produce a No Objection Certificate at the time of Personal Interview.

IOB LBO Examination Centres

The online examination will be conducted at various centres across India, including Chennai, Madurai, Coimbatore, Tiruchirappalli in Tamil Nadu; Bengaluru, Hubli-Dharwad, Mysuru in Karnataka; Mumbai/Thane/Navi Mumbai, Pune, Nagpur in Maharashtra; and Ahmedabad/Gandhinagar, Surat, Rajkot in Gujarat, among other centres nationwide. The Bank reserves the right to add, delete, or reassign examination centres based on response and administrative feasibility.
