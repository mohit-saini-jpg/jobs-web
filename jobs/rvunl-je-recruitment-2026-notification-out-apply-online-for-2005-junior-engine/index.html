RVUN Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection is through a single Computer-Based common written competitive exam (Online mode), of 2 hours' duration, consisting only of Objective Type (MCQ) questions, with negative marking for wrong answers and no interview. Part A (60% weightage): For Junior Engineer-I, this is at Degree-in-Engineering level in the candidate's discipline. For Junior Accountant, this covers Corporate Accounting, Income Tax, Cost Accounting, Theory & Practice of Auditing, and Management Accounting/Advanced Cost Accounting/Cost & Management Audit/Computer Application in Accounting/Taxation Law & Practice/Advanced Financial Accounting, at Graduation Degree level. Part B (40% weightage): Reasoning & Mental Ability; Mathematics (Class-XII level for JE-I, Class-X level for Junior Accountant); General Knowledge & Everyday Science (with special reference to Rajasthan); Hindi General (Class-X level); English Genera

RVUNL JE Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Applications must be submitted online only, through one of the official websites, between 5 August 2026 (10:00 AM) and 25 August 2026 (Midnight). No other mode of application is accepted.
  • 2Visit any of the official websites: energy.rajasthan.gov.in, or the Nigam-specific portals (rrvun, rrvpn, jvvnl, jdvvnl, avvnl).
  • 3Click "APPLY ONLINE," then "CLICK HERE FOR NEW REGISTRATION" and enter your Name, Contact details and Email ID to generate a Provisional Registration Number and Password (also sent by email/SMS).
  • 4Fill the application form, using "SAVE AND NEXT" to save progress; carefully verify all details, as no change is possible after clicking "FINAL SUBMIT."
  • 5Upload scanned Photograph (4.5cm x 3.5cm), Signature (black ink, not in capital letters), Left Thumb Impression, and a Hand Written Declaration (in English, in the candidate's own handwriting) as per the specifications in the official advertisement. A Live Photograph must also be captured via webcam or mobile phone.
  • 6Indicate your preference of Companies (RVUN/RVPN/JVVN/AVVN/JdVVN) - this preference is final and irrevocable, with no second opportunity for change.
  • 7Preview the application, click "FINAL SUBMIT," then proceed to the "Payment" tab and pay the applicable fee online.
  • 8Take a printout of the E-Receipt and the submitted Online Application Form for future reference - these are required at the time of Documents Verification.
  • 9A candidate may apply for more than one different post, but not submit multiple applications for the same post (only the latest valid application is retained in case of duplication, and fees paid for earlier attempts are forfeited).
