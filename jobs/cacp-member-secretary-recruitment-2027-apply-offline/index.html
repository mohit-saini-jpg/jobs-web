About CACP

The Commission for Agricultural Costs and Prices (CACP) is an attached office under the administrative control of the Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India. The recruitment is handled by the Economics and Statistics (Establishment) Section of the Department at Krishi Bhawan, New Delhi. The circular is addressed to the Secretaries of Central Government Ministries and Departments, the Secretaries (Agriculture) and Secretaries (Planning) of all State Governments and Union Territories, and the Registrars of all agricultural universities.

CACP Member Secretary Job Description

The circular lists these duties of the Member Secretary, CACP: Performing the administrative functions of the Commission and assisting the Chairman as Head of Department in financial and administrative matters of the Commission. Assisting the Commission in work related to price policy of agricultural commodities. Participating in the deliberations and meetings of the Commission. Advising or informing the Chairman on any issue in the interest of the Commission. Accompanying the Commission during field visits. Undertaking research and analytical work in areas agreed by the Commission and approved by the Chairman. Providing an interface between the Commission and the rest of the world. Supervising and guiding the work of the technical divisions of the Commission.

Selection will be made by a Search-cum-Selection Committee duly constituted as per the guidelines of the Department of Personnel and Training. Q3What qualification is needed for CACP Member Secretary?
A
IES or ISS officers holding analogous posts or with three years' regular service in Level-13 may apply; other government, university or research institute officers also need a Ph.D. in Agricultural Economics or Agricultural Statistics, twelve years' teaching or research experience, quantitative skills and government administration experience.

Q8Is the CACP Member Secretary post permanent?
