About NSIC

The National Small Industries Corporation Ltd. describes itself as a Schedule 'A' premier Mini-Ratna Government of India Enterprise under the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises. Its office is at NSIC Bhawan, Okhla Industrial Estate, New Delhi. The posts are regular appointments in various functional areas of the Corporation, and for all posts preference is given to candidates from CPSEs/Govt./Deemed Govt. Institutions.

Pay and Tenure

Pay scales are in the IDA pattern (2017). The compensation package includes basic pay, Industrial DA, HRA, perquisites and allowances as per the cafeteria approach, EPF and gratuity, as per IDA 2017 pattern, with Performance Related Pay as admissible and as per Rules of the Corporation. The Corporation also has policies on House Building Advance, Vehicle Advance, Computer Advance, medical facilities for self and dependents, PF, gratuity, Superannuation Pension Scheme and leave encashment. These are regular appointments. A selected candidate must execute an agreement to serve NSIC for a minimum of 2 years after joining, failing which the incumbent is liable to compensate NSIC Rs.2,00,000/-. Selection and Interview

The selection stages depend on the post. The notice does not give an interview date. Selection consists of a Written Test (Computer Based Test) with 70% weightage and a Personal Interview of shortlisted candidates with 30% weightage. The test has 100 multiple choice questions for 100 marks. Each correct answer carries 1 mark, with 0.25 negative marking for each wrong answer. Minimum qualifying marks are 40 for General/OBC candidates and 35 for SC/ST/PwBD candidates. Candidates are shortlisted for the interview in the ratio of at least 1:5 or at most 1:7 with respect to the number of posts advertised. Management reserves the right to devise its criteria, including raising the minimum eligibility standards, to restrict the number of candidates called. NSIC Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in NSIC Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 41 regular vacancies in nine posts: Chief General Manager 1, Deputy General Manager 3, Chief Manager 5, Manager (E-2) 2, Manager (F&A) 6, Deputy Manager 7, Deputy Manager (F&A) 4, Assistant Manager (Technology) 11 and Assistant Manager (F&A) 2.
Q2What is the last date to apply for NSIC Recruitment 2026?
A
The last date of the online application is 14 December 2026. The signed print-out with a detailed CV must be received by 6:00 p.m. on 21 December 2026.
Q3What is the qualification for NSIC Assistant Manager (Technology)?
A
A first class 4 years B.E./B.Tech degree with minimum 60% marks (5% relaxation to SC/ST/PwBD) in the listed engineering branches is required. Only GATE qualified candidates shall apply, and GATE scores of 2025 and 2026 are considered.
Q4What is the age limit for NSIC posts?
A
The upper age limit is 55 years for Chief General Manager, 41 for Deputy General Manager, 38 for Chief Manager, 34 for both Manager posts, 31 for both Deputy Manager posts and 28 for both Assistant Manager posts, counted as on 14 December 2026. Relaxation is as per Govt. Rules.
Q5What is the application fee for NSIC Recruitment 2026?
A
The fee is Rs.1500 per application, paid through NEFT only. SC/ST/PwBD/Women candidates and Departmental candidates pay no application fee, and the fee is non-refundable.
Q6How do I apply for NSIC Recruitment 2026?
A
Apply online through the CAREER section of www.nsic.co.in, pay the fee if applicable, and take the print-out of the form with the Application Number. Send it with a detailed CV and self-attested documents to the Deputy General Manager – Human Resources at NSIC Bhawan, Okhla Industrial Estate, New Delhi-110020.
Q7What is the selection process for NSIC Recruitment 2026?
A
For posts 1 to 7 it is scrutiny of applications and Personal Interview. For Assistant Manager (Technology) it is 70% GATE score and 30% interview. For Assistant Manager (F&A) it is a CBT (70%) and interview (30%).
Q8What is the salary for NSIC Recruitment 2026?
A
Pay scales are in the IDA 2017 pattern: Rs.1,00,000-2,60,000 for Chief General Manager (E-7), Rs.70,000-2,00,000 for Deputy General Manager (E-4), Rs.60,000-1,80,000 for Chief Manager (E-3), Rs.50,000-1,60,000 for Manager (E-2), Rs.40,000-1,40,000 for Deputy Manager (E-1) and Rs.30,000-1,20,000 for Assistant Manager (E-0).
Q9Is there a written exam in NSIC Recruitment 2026?
A
A Computer Based Test of 100 questions, 100 marks and 90 minutes is held only for Assistant Manager (F&A) (E-0), with 0.25 negative marking per wrong answer. The other posts are filled by scrutiny and interview, or GATE score and interview.
Q10Can I apply for more than one post, and is there a service bond?
A
One candidate can apply for one post only. A selected candidate must sign an agreement to serve NSIC for a minimum of 2 years after joining, failing which the candidate is liable to pay Rs.2,00,000/- compensation.
