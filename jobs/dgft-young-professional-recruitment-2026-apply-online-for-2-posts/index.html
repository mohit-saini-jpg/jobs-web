About DGFT Panipat

The notice describes the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT) as an attached office of the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, headed by the Director General of Foreign Trade. DGFT makes and carries out the Foreign Trade Policy, whose main aim is to promote India's exports. It also issues scrips/authorisations to exporters and checks the obligations attached to them through a network of 24 regional offices. The Panipat office (Office of the Joint DGFT) is at BSNL CSC Building, Near Police Station, G.T. Road, Panipat - 132103, and its phone numbers are 0180-2650984 and 2651427.

Pay and Tenure

Remuneration: A consolidated fee of Rs. 52,000/- per month, minus Professional Tax/TDS as applicable. It is paid once the controlling Officer certifies that the work for each period has been completed. Young Professionals do not get any other allowance or facility on top of this fee. Travel: Young Professionals may have to travel to any place in India. On tour, they get the same TA/DA as an Assistant Section Officer of the Central Government. Tenure: The job starts at one year and may be extended up to three years, depending on work performance and efficiency. No extension is allowed beyond three years under any circumstances. The period starts from the date of joining DGFT as a Young Professional. Nature of the job: The job is purely temporary. DGFT Panipat Young Professionals Job Description

Work on policy formulation and on running the schemes under the Foreign Trade Policy. Working hours are normally 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM on working days, with a half-hour lunch break. When the work demands it, Young Professionals may have to stay late and may be called on Saturdays, Sundays and other holidays.

Selection and Interview

Successful candidates will be selected through a written test or an interview of shortlisted candidates. Shortlisted candidates will be told by email to appear for the written test or interview. The office will not accept any request to change the date or time of the written test or interview. The job also depends on checking your documents of education and experience. If any information or document you submit is found false or wrong at any stage, the job will be ended at once and action will be taken as per the rules.
