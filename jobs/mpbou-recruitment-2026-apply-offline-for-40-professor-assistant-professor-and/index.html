MPBOU Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

The selection process involves screening followed by an interview: For Assistant Professor , candidates are shortlisted using an Academic Record score (based on Graduation, Post-Graduation, M.Phil., Ph.D., NET/ JRF/ SLET/ SET, research publications, teaching/ post-doctoral experience, and awards), as detailed in Annexure-III, out of a total of 100 marks. For Associate Professor and Professor , candidates are shortlisted using an Academic and Research Score (ARS) covering research papers, publications, ICT/ e-content contributions, research guidance and projects, patents/ policy documents/ awards, and invited lectures/ conference papers, as detailed in Annexure-IV. In both cases, meeting the eligibility conditions does not guarantee an interview call - the university applies stringent shortlisting criteria and may place a reasonable limit on the number of candidates called for interview.

  • 1MPBOU does not have a fully online application submission portal for this recruitment. The process is as follows:
  • 2Download the prescribed application format (Annexure-I) and other required annexures from the official website, https:/ / mpbou.edu.in, and print it on A-4 size paper.
  • 3Pay the application fee online through net banking/ RTGS to the bank account specified above, and keep the payment receipt.

  • 5Number and cross-reference all attachments against the relevant annexures in the application form.
  • 6Send the completed application only by Registered Post/ Speed Post/ Courier to the Registrar, Madhya Pradesh Bhoj (Open) University, Raja Bhoj Marg, Kolar Road, Bhopal – 462016, superscribing the post applied for on the envelope. Applications will not be accepted by hand.
  • 7Ensure the application reaches the university on or before 12 October 2026, by 6:00 PM.
  • 8Candidates already in government/ semi-government service should apply through proper channel; an advance copy may be sent directly, but a No Objection Certificate or duly forwarded application must be produced before/ at the time of interview.
