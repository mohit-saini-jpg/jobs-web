6How to Participate in the Final Mop-Up Round
Sr.TypeStyleItems
1listunordered
  • Visit the official NBEMS counselling portal linked at natboard.edu.in.
  • Log in using registered credentials.
  • Fill choices/options for available seats in order of preference between August 21 and August 24, 2026, up to 5:00 PM.
  • Track the allotment result on August 27, 2026.
  • If allotted a seat, report for physical joining at the allotted hospital between August 27 and September 1, 2026.
7Important Instructions
Sr.TypeStyleItems
1listunordered
  • The seat matrix released is indicative; candidates should verify final seat availability on the official portal before submitting choices.
  • Check the management type, category, PwBD status, and service-bond applicability of each hospital before finalising preferences.
  • Choice filling closes at 5:00 PM on August 24, 2026 - no extensions have been mentioned in the notification.
  • Physical joining must be completed within the window specified (August 27 to September 1, 2026) to retain the allotted seat.
