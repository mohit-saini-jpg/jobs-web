Who Can Apply

The essential qualification for JRF is a Post Graduate degree in basic science OR a Graduate / Post Graduate Degree in a Professional Course, selected through one of the following: National Eligibility Tests: CSIR-UGC NET including Lectureship (Assistant Professorship), and GATE. A national eligibility examination conducted by Central Government departments and their agencies and institutions such as DBT, DST, DAE, DOS, DRDO, MoE, ICAR, ICMR, IIT, IISc, IISER, NISER, etc. Desirable qualifications, project-wise: Project E2.4: BE/B.Tech/M.Sc. Pay and Tenure

Engagement is purely on a temporary / contractual basis and co-terminus with the completion of the project. After joining, shared accommodation is provided in the hostel as per institute policy. Selected candidates must register for the Ph.D. programme. The date of joining is on or before 31 December 2026.

Selection and Interview

Candidates are shortlisted based on merit and need of the project. Possession of the minimum qualification alone does not guarantee an invitation to the interview, and NIT Rourkela reserves the right to fix higher criteria for shortlisting. Only shortlisted candidates are informed. The activity schedule lists a test / interview in person at NIT Rourkela on 2 December 2026, with the result of scrutiny intimated by email on 30 November 2026 and selection intimated by email on 5 December 2026. Selection and joining will be cancelled in case of any suppression of information or document submitted.
