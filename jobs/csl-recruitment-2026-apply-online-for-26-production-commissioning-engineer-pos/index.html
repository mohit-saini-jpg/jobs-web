Cochin Shipyard Limited (CSL) has released its Production & Commissioning Recruitment 2026 notification, inviting online applications from Ex-Indian Navy and Ex-Indian Coast Guard Personnel for 26 posts on contract basis. CSL Production & Commissioning Selection Process

The CSL Recruitment 2026 selection process is conducted out of 100 marks at CSL, Kochi. Based on the number of applications received, CSL may shortlist candidates by scrutinising eligibility through the certificates uploaded during application. Only shortlisted candidates are permitted to attend the selection process, and original certificates with self-attested copies must be produced at the time of Certificate Verification. A rank list is prepared based on marks secured in the selection process. If two or more candidates secure the same marks, relative merit is decided based on seniority in age. The rank list remains valid for up to 18 months from the date of reporting of the last candidate, or until a fresh notification for the same post is issued.
