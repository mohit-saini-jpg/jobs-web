IREL Apprentice Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Eligibility is trade-specific, as shown in the Vacancy tables above. In addition, the following general conditions apply to all applicants: Qualifications acquired through Distance Learning Mode, Part Time Mode, or Correspondence Mode will not be considered. Candidates who have already undergone, or are currently undergoing, apprenticeship training under the Apprentices Act, 1961 in any Government/Public Sector/Private industrial organisation are not eligible . Candidates should not apply for more than one trade at a time. Candidates holding a higher/professional qualification - such as ME/M.Tech, MCA, MBA or its equivalent, CA/ICWA, Ph.D, or LLB - need not apply, as these are considered higher than the prescribed induction-level qualification for the advertised seats. IREL Apprentice Recruitment 2026 Stipend

The rate of stipend payable to apprentices will be as prescribed under the Apprenticeship Rules, 1992, and as notified by the Government of India from time to time. IREL Apprentice Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

There is no written examination for this apprenticeship engagement. Candidates who fulfil the criteria relating to educational qualification, marks, and age are considered eligible for engagement as Apprentices on the basis of a merit list prepared from the marks scored in the specified educational qualification, subject to the candidate being declared "Medically Fit" by IREL (India) Limited's Medical Officer. In case of a tie in marks, higher marks in the specified qualification will be considered first; if the tie persists, seniority in date of birth will be taken into account. Within the merit list, the following order of priority is applied, as per the notification: Land Affected Category: Candidates belonging to the Land Affected Category (with documentary proof of having given land to IREL) are considered regardless of the marks obtained. IREL Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Applying for this recruitment involves two mandatory steps: online portal registration, followed by submission of a hardcopy application by post. Applications will not be accepted without prior registration on the relevant Government web portal.
  • 2Eligible candidates must first register themselves on the appropriate Government of India apprenticeship portal, depending on the category applied for: For Trade Apprentices: register on the Portal of the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship, National Apprenticeship Training Scheme (http://www.apprenticeshipindia.org). For Graduate/Technician Apprentices: register on the National Apprenticeship Training Scheme portal of MHRD (http://www.nats.education.gov.in). Registration on the relevant portal must be completed before applying against this advertisement.
  • 3For Trade Apprentices: register on the Portal of the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship, National Apprenticeship Training Scheme (http://www.apprenticeshipindia.org).
  • 4For Graduate/Technician Apprentices: register on the National Apprenticeship Training Scheme portal of MHRD (http://www.nats.education.gov.in).
  • 5While applying through the portal, candidates must use IREL’s Establishment Registration Number: E05203301006 for Trade Apprenticeship, and STNKKC000004 for Graduate/Technician Apprenticeship.
  • 6Submitting the hardcopy of the application is mandatory for screening and selection, even after online portal registration.
  • 7Fill the application in the proforma given in the notification (Annexure A), preferably typewritten on A4 size paper.
  • 8Superscribe the outer cover of the envelope as: "APPLICATION FOR ENGAGEMENT OF APPRENTICE AGAINST NOTIFICATION NO. IREL (India) Limited/MK/Apprentices Engagement/2026/01".
  • 9Affix a recent passport-size photograph on the space provided in the application form, and enclose all supporting self-attested documents listed in the Documents Required section above.
  • 10Send the completed application to: The Chief Manager - HRM (Legal & ER), IREL (India) Limited, Manavalakurichi, Kanyakumari District, Tamilnadu-629252.
  • 11The filled-in application must reach IREL on or before 31 August 2026. Applications received after this date will not be considered.
  • 12The application should be sent by post, preferably by speed-post. Applications sent through any other mode will also be considered; however, IREL will not be responsible under any circumstances for delay or loss during transit, and no correspondence will be entertained regarding this.

IREL Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is IREL Apprentice Recruitment 2026?
A
It is a notification issued by IREL (India) Limited, Manavalakurichi, inviting applications for 39 Graduate, Technician and Trade Apprentice seats under the Apprentices Act, 1961, at its Kanyakumari District unit in Tamil Nadu.
Q2How many vacancies are available in IREL Apprentice Recruitment 2026?
A
A total of 39 seats are available - 4 Graduate Apprentice seats, 4 Technician Apprentice seats, and 31 Trade Apprentice seats across ten designated trades.
Q3What is the last date to apply for IREL Apprentice Recruitment 2026?
A
The completed application must reach IREL (India) Limited on or before 31 August 2026. Applications received after this date will not be considered.
Q4What is the educational qualification required for IREL Apprentice posts?
A
Qualification depends on the trade: BE (Electrical & Electronics/Civil/Mechanical) for Graduate Apprentices, a relevant Diploma for Technician Apprentices, and a passed ITI certificate in the specific trade (or Computer Operator and Programming Assistant qualification for PASAA) for Trade Apprentices.
Q5What is the age limit for IREL Apprentice Recruitment 2026?
A
Candidates must be a minimum of 18 years and a maximum of 25 years of age as on the last date of application, with age relaxation of up to 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC (Non-Creamy Layer), and 10 years for PWD candidates.
Q6How can I apply for IREL Apprentice Recruitment 2026?
A
Candidates must first register on the relevant Government apprenticeship portal (apprenticeshipindia.org for Trade Apprentices, or nats.education.gov.in for Graduate/Technician Apprentices) and then submit a hardcopy application in the prescribed proforma by post to the Chief Manager – HRM (Legal & ER), IREL (India) Limited, Manavalakurichi, on or before 31 August 2026.
Q7What is the selection process for IREL Apprentice Recruitment 2026?
A
There is no written examination. Selection is based on a merit list of qualifying marks, subject to defined priority categories (such as Land Affected candidates, wards of employees, and residents of specified villages/districts/state), followed by document verification and a medical fitness examination.
Q8What is the stipend for IREL apprentices?
A
The stipend will be paid as prescribed under the Apprenticeship Rules, 1992, and as notified by the Government of India from time to time. The exact amount is not specified in the official notification.
