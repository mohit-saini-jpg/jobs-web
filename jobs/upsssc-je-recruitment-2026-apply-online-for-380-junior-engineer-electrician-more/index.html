UPSSSC JE Electrical Eligibility Criteria 2026

Candidates must have participated in UPSSSC Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET) 2025. A PET 2025 scorecard must have been issued by the Commission. Main examination shortlisting will be based on normalized PET 2025 scores. Candidates with zero or negative absolute or normalized PET scores will not be shortlisted for the main examination. Candidates whose PET 2025 results are marked Provisional or Under Investigation may apply provisionally, subject to the Commission's final decision. Qualification Cut-off Date: Candidates must possess the required essential qualifications and relevant supporting certificates by 27 November 2026. Qualifications must satisfy the service rules applicable to the selected department. Equivalent technical qualifications are governed by the recognition orders included with the notification. UPSSSC JE Electrical Syllabus 2026

The official syllabus covers Electrical Engineering, Computer and Information Technology, and Uttar Pradesh General Knowledge. Electrical Engineering carries 65 marks, Computer and IT carries 15 marks, and Uttar Pradesh General Knowledge carries 20 marks. Basic Electrical Engineering: Current, Voltage, Power, Energy, Resistance, Inductance, Capacitance and Circuit Laws. Magnetic Circuits and Electromagnetism: Magnetic Materials, Flux, MMF and Electromagnetic Induction. AC Fundamentals and Machines: Polyphase Systems, Star and Delta Connections, Induction Motors and AC Waveforms. Electrical Measurements: Ammeters, Voltmeters, Wattmeters, Multimeters, CRO and Fault Detection. DC Machines and Transformers: Construction, Working Principles, Motor Braking, Voltage Regulation and Autotransformers.   • 4Select Advertisement No. 25-Exam/2026 for the Combined Junior Engineer (Electrical) Main Examination.

  • 6Click Apply Online and authenticate using the PET 2025 Registration Number.

  • 14Ensure successful payment adjustment and final submission.
  • 15Download and print the completed application form for future reference.
