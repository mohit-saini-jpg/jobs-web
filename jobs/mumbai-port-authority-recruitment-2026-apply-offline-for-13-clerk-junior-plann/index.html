Mumbai Port Authority Recruitment 2026 Terms of Contractual Engagement

Contract period: 3 years, extendable by 1 more year at the discretion of Mumbai Port Authority. Nature of engagement: Full-time; contractual personnel cannot take up any other assignment during the engagement period. Experience certificate: Issued only on successful completion of the engagement; not issued to those who do not complete at least 1 year. Medical facility: Available at the Mumbai Port Authority hospital/ dispensary along with medicines; no outside referral and no facility for family/ dependents. Leave: 20 days Casual Leave per year plus public holidays, and an additional 7 days of paid leave per annum specifically for seminars, conferences, and workshops. No leave carry-forward or encashment is permitted. Duty hours: 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM, or as decided by the Administration; a weekly off (normally Sunday) applies, with compensatory rest for work on a weekly off/ national holi

Mumbai Port Authority Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Download the application format (Annexure-II) from www.mumbaiport.gov.in, under the People & Career/ Jobs/ Advertisements/ Contractual/ Other Engagements menu.
  • 2Fill in the application format with all required details and the declaration.
  • 3Attach self-attested copies of documents/ certificates relating to age, qualification, and experience, and enclose two references from your field with their contact details.
  • 4Send the completed application to the address below before the last date, 12.10.2026: The Chief Engineer, Civil Engineering Department, Port House, 3rd Floor, Shoorji Vallabhdas Marg, Mumbai – 400001
  • 5Superscribe the envelope as: "Application for the post of ___ on contract basis."
  • 6Note: Mumbai Port Authority Recruitment 2026 uses an offline (postal) application process; the notification does not mention an online application/ apply-online facility. Merely submitting a resume/ CV, or an incomplete application, will lead to rejection, and delayed applications will not be accepted.
