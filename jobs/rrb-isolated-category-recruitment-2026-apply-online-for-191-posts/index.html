RRB Isolated Category Selection Process 2026

The recruitment involves a single-stage Computer Based Test (CBT) , followed by a Translation Test for Junior Translator candidates, where applicable, and subsequent Document Verification and Medical Examination. Computer Based Test: Eligible candidates appear for the prescribed objective examination. Translation Test: Junior Translator (Hindi) candidates shortlisted from CBT must qualify in the translation test. Document Verification: Candidates are shortlisted according to merit, vacancy availability and applicable reservation rules. Medical Examination: Candidates must meet the medical standards applicable to their selected post. Final Empanelment: Subject to merit, qualifications, medical fitness and successful verification. RRBs reserve the right to conduct additional recruitment stages where necessary under the notification.

RRB Isolated Category CBT Exam Pattern 2026

The standard CBT consists of 100 objective questions carrying 100 marks , with a duration of 90 minutes. Eligible candidates using a scribe receive 120 minutes. The notified subject-wise distribution is indicative and may vary slightly in the actual question paper. These are minimum eligibility percentages, not guaranteed selection cut-offs. A relaxation of two marks may be allowed for PwBD candidates where a shortage exists against reserved PwBD vacancies, as prescribed.

RRB Junior Translator Hindi Exam Pattern 2026

Junior Translator (Hindi) candidates will appear for a specialized CBT followed by a qualifying Translation Test. The CBT is designed to assess knowledge of both Hindi and English. At least 50% of the questions will assess language proficiency, including approximately 20% English and 30% Hindi. Remaining questions cover General Knowledge, General Intelligence, Simple Arithmetic and basic computer knowledge. Important: Translation Test marks are not added to the final merit score. Candidates who fail to obtain 60% in the Translation Test will not be considered for further selection even if they performed well in CBT.

RRB Isolated Category Photograph and Signature Requirements

The application system captures a live photograph. Candidates are not required to upload an existing passport-size photograph during registration. Use a computer webcam or mobile front camera.
