The Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB) has released the online Dummy Registration Card (Dummy Panjiyan Card) for the Annual Examination 2027 for Class 10th (Matric/Secondary) and Class 12th (Intermediate) students. What Is the Dummy Registration Card 2027?

The dummy registration card is a preliminary record containing a student's registration details, uploaded by BSEB for verification before the final registration card is issued. It is meant for students of the 2025-27 session appearing in the Secondary (Class 10th) and Intermediate (Class 12th) Annual Examination 2027, whose institutions submitted the online registration application, including the required declaration, within the stipulated time.

Who Can Download the Dummy Registration Card 2027

  • Students enrolled in the 2025-27 session appearing for the Secondary (Class 10th) Annual Examination 2027.
  • Students enrolled in the 2025-27 session appearing for the Intermediate (Class 12th) Annual Examination 2027.
  • Only those students whose registration application and declaration were submitted by their school/college within the time set by the Board.

How to Download BSEB Dummy Registration Card 2027

Students and institution heads can download the dummy registration card by following these steps:

  • Open the official website: biharboardonline.org for Class 10th (Secondary) or intermediate.biharboardonline.com for Class 12th (Intermediate).
  • Click on the link labelled "Click Here to Download Dummy Registration Card."
  • On the next page, enter the required details: School Code, Student's Name, Father's Name, Date of Birth (and Faculty - applicable only for Class 12th/Intermediate students).
  • Click the Submit button.
  • The dummy registration card will be displayed on screen. Download and save it, or take a printout for further use.

Process for Institutions (Principals/ Heads)

Principals of the concerned schools and colleges can log in to the respective portal (biharboardonline.org for Secondary, intermediate.biharboardonline.com for Intermediate) using their User ID and Password starting 25th July 2026 to download the dummy registration cards. They are required to provide two signed and stamped copies to each student for verification. Students also have the option to download their own copy directly from the official websites.

Important Instructions

  • Keep the School Code, Student's Name, Father's Name, and Date of Birth (as per school records) ready before starting the download.
  • Class 12th students must also select the correct Faculty (Arts/Science/Commerce) while filling the details.
  • Download and verify the card within the given window: 25 July 2026 to 14 August 2026.
  • Check all details carefully (name, father's name, date of birth, subjects) and report discrepancies to your school or the BSEB helpline promptly.
  • Keep both the digital copy and a signed, stamped printed copy safely for future reference, as advised for institution-issued copies.
