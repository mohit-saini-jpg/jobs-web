Indian Army TES 57 Selection Process 2026

Selection for Indian Army TES 57 begins with shortlisting based on JEE Main 2026 CRL, followed by the Services Selection Board (SSB) interview, medical examination and final merit list. The final merit list is based on SSB marks, subject to medical fitness, eligibility and vacancy availability. Indian Army TES 57 Training Period 2027

Selected candidates will undergo four years of integrated military and engineering training. The programme consists of three years at a Cadets Training Wing and one year at the Indian Military Academy, Dehradun. Candidates who successfully complete the four-year training programme will be awarded an Engineering degree. Candidates recommended by the SSB must submit preferences for all three Cadets Training Wings. Final allocation is decided by the Indian Army.

Indian Army TES 57 Salary and Training Stipend 2026

Indian Army TES-57 cadets receive a stipend of Rs. 56,100 per month during their training at the Indian Military Academy after completing the initial three years of training. On successful commissioning as a Lieutenant, they become eligible for the Level 10 pay scale of Rs. 56,100–1,77,500 and applicable allowances. The advertised annual CTC is an approximate figure and is not the same as guaranteed monthly take-home salary. Promotion to higher ranks is subject to the relevant service conditions and selection requirements.
