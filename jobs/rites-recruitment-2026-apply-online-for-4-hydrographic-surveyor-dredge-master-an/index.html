RITES Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Four Individual Consultant positions at Gandhidham / Kandla, Gujarat are open with RITES Limited: Hydrographic Surveyor (1), Dredge Master (MOT Grade I/II) (2) and Financial Expert (1). Each engagement has an initial tenure of 12 months and payment of Rs. 1,60,000/- per month (Hydrographic Surveyor and Dredge Master) or Rs. 1,25,000/- per month (Financial Expert). The posts need experience in dredging work. Applications are submitted online in the Career Section of the RITES website (www.rites.com) from 25 November 2026 to 16 December 2026 (till 23:00). There is no application fee.
