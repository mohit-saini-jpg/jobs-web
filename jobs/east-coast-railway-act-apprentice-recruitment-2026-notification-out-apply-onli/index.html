ECoR Act Apprentice Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification: Matriculation (10th Class) under the 10+2 system with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks from a recognized Board, held as on 21.08.2026 (the notification date). Technical Qualification: An ITI certificate in the relevant trade, affiliated to NCVT/SCVT, as per the Apprentices Act, 1961. Applicants whose SSC or ITI results are awaited as on the notification date are not eligible Applicants who have failed in ITI must not apply; if inadvertently engaged, the apprenticeship will be terminated immediately Engineering graduates and diploma holders are not eligible under this notification (they fall under a separate apprenticeship scheme) For Laboratory Technician (Pathology): candidate must be 12th pass (10+2 system) with Physics, Chemistry and Biology, with a minimum of 50% marks in both 10th and 12th For Secretarial Assistant (Stenography): candidate must hold a Nati

ECoR Act Apprentice Age Limit

Age is reckoned as on the closing date, 15.09.2026. General age limit: 15 to 24 years SC/ST candidates: upper age limit relaxed by 5 years OBC candidates: upper age limit relaxed by 3 years PwBD candidates: upper age limit relaxed by 10 years Ex-Servicemen: 10 additional years to the extent of Defence service rendered, plus 3 years, provided a minimum of 6 months of service at a stretch (applicable to the Ex-Serviceman applicant personally, not to their children) Children of deceased/disabled Ex-Servicemen, children of Ex-Servicemen, children of serving Jawans, and children of serving Officers are also considered for engagement under specified categories, with the same age relaxation as per their community To avoid any confusion regarding age eligibility, candidates can use our Age Calculator to calculate their exact age based on the prescribed cut-off date before applying.

ECoR Act Apprentice Stipend

Selected candidates will be paid a monthly stipend at the prescribed rate during the training period, as per extant rules and directives issued by the Railway Board from time to time. The notification does not specify an exact stipend amount. No hostel accommodation is provided during training - apprentices must make their own arrangements.

ECoR Act Apprentice Selection Process

Selection is based solely on a merit list prepared from the percentage of marks obtained in Matriculation (minimum 50% aggregate) - there is no written test or interview The percentage is calculated using marks from all subjects (not the "best of five" approach); candidates with a CGPA must convert it to the equivalent percentage In case of tied marks, preference goes to the older candidate; if dates of birth are also identical, whoever passed Matriculation earlier is prioritized A trade-wise and community-wise merit list is prepared; candidates are called for Document Verification (DV) at ECoR Headquarters, Bhubaneswar, based on merit in their preferred trade Candidates are shortlisted strictly by academic merit and the order of preference of Unit–Department–Trade exercised in their application; a candidate is considered for only one combination, and once selected against a higher prefe
