ESIC Teaching Faculty & Senior Resident Eligibility Criteria

Age is reckoned as on 31.08.2026. Teaching Faculty (Professor/Associate Professor/Assistant Professor): 69 years (not exceeding) Senior Resident - Clinical Subjects: 44 years (not exceeding) Senior Resident - Non-Clinical/Laboratory Science Subjects: 49 years (not exceeding) The upper age limit is relaxable by 5 years for SC/ST and 3 years for OBC candidates, on submission of the prescribed category certificate. The maximum tenure of engagement is till 70 years of age for Teaching Faculty, 45 years for Senior Resident in clinical subjects, and 50 years for Senior Resident in non-clinical/laboratory science subjects. Candidates should ensure that they meet the prescribed age limit as on the specified cut-off date. You can use our Age Calculator to check your exact age and confirm your eligibility before applying. ESIC Teaching Faculty & Senior Resident Salary

Monthly pay is released based on the Biometric Attendance System data for the individual's working hours, with all absences regularized through leave due within the stipulated time frame. The engagement is purely contractual for a period of 01 year from the date of joining, renewable subject to willingness, satisfactory performance, and institute requirement. Senior Resident selected in General Medicine will be posted to Medical ICU, Respiratory ICU, and Casualty on a rotational basis; those in General Surgery will be posted in the Trauma Care Ward and Surgical ICU on rotation. Senior Residents in Orthopedics, General Surgery, and General Medicine must also perform compulsory on-site duty in Casualty as scheduled by the Hospital Administration. The engagement may be terminated by either side with one month's prior notice; spot resignation requires depositing one month's salary to the ESI

ESIC Teaching Faculty & Senior Resident Selection Process

Selection will be made on the basis of the candidate's acquired academic credentials and performance in an interview before the Selection Board. The result will be displayed on the official website, www.esic.gov.in, and the offer of appointment will be sent through e-mail. Selected candidates must join as per the instructions mentioned in the offer of appointment.
