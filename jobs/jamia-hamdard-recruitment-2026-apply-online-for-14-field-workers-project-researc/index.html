Jamia Hamdard Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Jamia Hamdard, New Delhi has invited applications for 14 temporary posts under the ICMR-funded research project "Scale up of competency Driven Program Management for Strengthening NTEP – An Implementation Research to catalyze performance of selected districts of five states", under Principal Investigator Dr. N Ravichandran. Pay is Rs. 26,000 to Rs. 72,800 per month, consolidated, with no HRA. Send your Resume/CV to [email protected] up to 25 November 2026, 05:00 p.m.; interviews are on 26 and 27 November 2026.

About the Project

The posts are under a research project run from Jamia Hamdard, Hamdard Nagar, New Delhi, and funded by ICMR. The notice says the project involves fieldwork for data collection, training and capacity building for TB healthcare functionaries, so travel outside Delhi and within India and the selected intervention districts is required.

Who Can Apply

The notice prints no age limit. It says candidates "fulfilling the required qualifications, experience, and age criteria" may send their CV. Qualification and experience must be in a relevant discipline/field and from a reputed institution/organization recognized by the relevant authority. Experience counts from the date of completion of the minimum educational qualification. Essential: Post Graduate in any discipline with 5-6 years' experience project management, or MBBS/MPH/MHA/PGDHM/MBA (Health Management)/MPS/MS (Epidemiology)/MD (Preventive and Social Medicine) with 3 to 4 years of experience in public health programme management level or research experiences, or PhD (in public/community Health areas). Desirable: Experience in working in TB elimination program at field level or at program management level for 3 years; strongly demonstrated report writing skills. Essential: Post Grad

Pay and Tenure

The first four posts are offered for 36 months; the notice prints no period for Field Workers (PTS-II). All posts are contractual, purely temporary and project-based, and the engagement is co-terminus with the project, subject to sanction of the grant and date. Engagement is subject to periodical performance, and unsatisfactory performance can result in termination after 15 days of notice. No provident fund, HRA, Leave Travel Concession, Medical Claim or similar benefits are admissible.

Selection and Interview

Shortlisted candidates are called for personal discussion/interview at Jamia Hamdard, Hamdard Nagar, New Delhi-110062. Document verification and the interview take place on the same day, from 9.30 am onwards. Candidates reporting after the scheduled date/time are not allowed to appear. Meeting the essential qualification and experience does not guarantee selection, and the decision of the selection committee is final.
