UPSIC Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Administrative Reforms Department of the Government of Uttar Pradesh (Uttar Pradesh Shasan) has invited applications by registered post or by hand for 1 State Chief Information Commissioner (Rajya Mukhya Soochna Ayukt) and 10 State Information Commissioner (Rajya Soochna Ayukt) posts in the Uttar Pradesh Information Commission, Lucknow. Applications must reach the Joint Director, Administrative Reforms Department, Lucknow by 5:00 PM on 22 December 2026, and can be sent from 23 November 2026. Pay is Rs 2,25,000 per month (fixed).

About UPSIC

The Uttar Pradesh Information Commission is a body constituted under the Right to Information Act, 2005 to exercise the powers given by the Act and to perform the functions assigned to it. It is located in Lucknow. The Commission is to have a State Chief Information Commissioner and not more than ten State Information Commissioners, and its present composition can be seen at www.upsic.gov.in .

UPSIC Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

A search committee chaired by the Principal Secretary, Administrative Reforms Department, Government of Uttar Pradesh will screen all the applications received. The appointment is then made by the Hon'ble Governor on the recommendation of the committee chaired by the Hon'ble Chief Minister, under Section 15(3) of Chapter 4 of the Right to Information Act, 2005. The notice does not mention a written test or interview.

UPSIC Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in UPSIC Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 11 posts: 1 State Chief Information Commissioner and 10 State Information Commissioner posts in the Uttar Pradesh Information Commission.
Q2What is the last date to apply for UPSIC Recruitment 2026?
A
Applications must reach the Administrative Reforms Department by 5:00 PM on 22 December 2026. Applications received after that time will not be accepted.
Q3What is the qualification for State Information Commissioner in Uttar Pradesh?
A
The applicant must be an eminent person in public life with wide knowledge and experience in law, science and technology, social service, management, journalism, mass media or administration and governance.
Q4What is the salary of the State Chief Information Commissioner and State Information Commissioner?
A
Each of them receives Rs 2,25,000 per month (fixed). Any pension being received at the time of appointment is reduced from the pay as the notice describes.
Q5What is the application fee?
A
The fee is Rs 2000/- per post, paid by an SBI demand draft in favour of "Principal Secretary, Administrative Reforms Department, Government of Uttar Pradesh, Lucknow".
Q6How do I apply for the UP State Information Commissioner post?
A
Fill in Proforma-1 and Proforma-2, attach the demand draft, and send them to the Joint Director, Administrative Reforms Department, Darbari Lal Sharma Bhawan, U.P. Secretariat, Lucknow-226001 by registered post from 23 November 2026, or deliver by hand on a working day between 10:00 AM and 5:00 PM.
Q7How are the Commissioners selected?
A
A search committee headed by the Principal Secretary, Administrative Reforms Department screens the applications. The Governor then appoints on the recommendation of the committee headed by the Chief Minister.
Q8Can I apply for both posts?
A
Yes. An applicant applying for both State Chief Information Commissioner and State Information Commissioner must submit separate application forms, each with the prescribed fee.
Q9How long is the term, and can a Commissioner be reappointed?
A
The term is 3 years from taking office or until age 65, whichever is earlier. There is no reappointment to the same post, but a State Information Commissioner may be appointed State Chief Information Commissioner.
Q10I applied under the July 2025 notice. Do I need to apply again?
A
Yes, if you are still interested, you must apply again for the State Information Commissioner post. You do not pay the fee again, as it was already paid.
