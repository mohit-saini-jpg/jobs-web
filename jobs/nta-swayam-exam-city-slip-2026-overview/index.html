How to Download NTA SWAYAM Exam City Slip 2026?

Candidates can follow these steps to download the SWAYAM July 2026 advance city intimation slip:

  • Visit the official NTA SWAYAM website at exams.nta.nic.in/swayam/.
  • Find the Advance City Intimation Slip for SWAYAM July 2026 Semester link.
  • Open the candidate login facility.
  • Enter your Application Number.
  • Enter your registered Password.
  • Complete any additional verification requested by the portal.
  • Submit the login details to view your examination city slip.
  • Check the allotted examination city and available examination details.
  • Download and save the city intimation slip for reference.

Candidates should use only the official NTA examination website to enter their application number and password.

NTA SWAYAM Admit Card 2026

NTA SWAYAM 2026 Course Code imb26_mg250 Exam Date

NTA has announced an exception for SWAYAM course code imb26_mg250.

They should regularly check the official examination website for the separate schedule notification.
