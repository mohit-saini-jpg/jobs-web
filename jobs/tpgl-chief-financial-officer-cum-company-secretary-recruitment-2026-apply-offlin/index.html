About TPGL

Tripura Power Generation Limited is a Government of Tripura Enterprise. Its Corporate Office is at Bidyut Bhavan (Old Building), North Banamalipur, Tripura. The CFO-cum-CS will report to the Managing Director of the Corporation and will also act as a Staff manager in the organisation.

TPGL Chief Financial Officer cum Company Secretary : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection will be based on interview only. Only shortlisted candidates will be called for the interview. If necessary, applicants may be shortlisted by written test, academic records, or higher educational qualifications and experience. The decision of the Screening Committee and Appointing authority on shortlisting is binding. No T.A. / D.A. will be paid for attending the interview. The selection committee may relax any of the terms for selection and appointment.

Service Conditions

The appointment is contractual for 3 (three) years. The appointing authority may extend it by mutual consent. There is no pension from the Government of Tripura or TPGL. EPF benefits are available if desired and applicable. Either party can end the contract by giving 30 days' notice or paying 1 (one) month's salary. Other service conditions will be the same as for Tripura State Civil Service Officers of equivalent grade. A Government of Tripura officer appointed on deputation will be governed by the service conditions of his original employment. In-service candidates may apply directly to TPGL. If selected, they must submit a 'Release Order' or 'Deputation Order' from their present employer when joining, or the joining will not be accepted. The number of posts may increase or decrease. The Corporation may also choose not to fill the vacancy.

TPGL Chief Financial Officer cum Company Secretary : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What qualification do I need for the TPGL CFO-cum-CS post?
A
You need CA/ ICWA along with F.C.S. You also need at least 20 years of experience in areas such as budgeting, tariff formulation, internal audit and Company Secretary functions.
Q2Can I apply if I am above 60 years of age?
A
Yes. The upper age limit is 65 years. A candidate who is retired or above 60 is paid Rs.1, 00, 000/- per month on a consolidated basis, without linkage to any pension.
Q3What will the starting basic pay be for a candidate below 60?
A
The minimum basic pay at level 20 is ₹118500. DA is paid on top at the present rate of 44%, along with HRA of ₹10000 and CA of ₹400 per month.
Q4Is the post permanent?
A
No. It is a contractual appointment for 3 years that may be extended by mutual consent. Either side can end it with 30 days' notice or 1 month's salary.
Q5Will I get a pension?
A
No pension is given by the Government of Tripura or TPGL. You can get EPF benefits, with the employer contributing 12% on (Basic + DA).
Q6Can a government officer apply on deputation?
A
Yes. In-service candidates may apply directly to TPGL. If selected, they must produce a Release Order or Deputation Order from their present employer when joining.
Q7Will there be a written test?
A
Final selection is through an interview only. A written test may be used only to shortlist applicants if necessary, along with academic records and experience.
Q8Are travel expenses paid for the interview?
A
No. TPGL does not pay T.A. / D.A. for attending the interview.
Q9Should I send original certificates with the application?
A
No. Send only self-attested photocopies. Bring the originals to the interview for verification.
Q10Who does the CFO-cum-CS report to?
A
The officer reports to the Managing Director of the Corporation and also acts as a Staff manager in the organisation.
