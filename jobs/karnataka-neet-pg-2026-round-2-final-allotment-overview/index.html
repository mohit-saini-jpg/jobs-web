Karnataka NEET PG 2026 Round 2 Final Allotment - Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 26/11/2026

The final allotment follows the provisional seat allotment list published on 25 November 2026. Candidates allotted seats in Round 2 must complete the prescribed admission formalities within the revised counselling schedule. The last date to download the fee challan and complete fee payment is 30 November 2026 at 4:00 PM. Students should follow the final allotment result rather than relying on their earlier provisional allocation.

How to Check Karnataka NEET PG 2026 Round 2 Final Allotment?

Candidates can follow the steps below to check the KEA PGNEET 2026 second-round final seat allotment result.

  • Visit the official KEA website at cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea/.
  • Open the PGET Medical 2026 counselling section.
  • Find the Round 2 Final Seat Allotment Result link.
  • Open the allotment PDF or candidate result facility displayed by KEA.
  • If a PDF is provided, search for your NEET PG rank or other candidate identifier.
  • If individual login is required, follow the official authentication instructions.
  • Check the allotted college, course, category and other available details.
  • Verify whether the final allotment differs from the provisional allotment.
  • Download or save the result for reference.
  • Follow the fee payment and admission order instructions if a seat has been allotted.

Candidates should rely on the final allotment published by KEA for their subsequent admission decisions.

Karnataka NEET PG Round 2 Choice 2 Fee Adjustment

KEA's revised counselling instructions provide for adjustment of fees already paid by candidates who selected Choice 2 in Round 1 and subsequently received a seat in Round 2.

Students should check their individual fee challan and payment status rather than assuming they must pay the entire course fee again.

Karnataka NEET PG Round 2 Admission Process 2026

After checking the final seat allotment result, candidates should complete the required admission steps.

  • Open the Karnataka NEET PG Round 2 final allotment result.
  • Check the allotted college, postgraduate course and category.
  • Verify the applicable fee payable for the allotted seat.
  • Download the fee challan from the KEA portal.
  • Complete payment by 30 November 2026 at 4:00 PM.
  • Book the required slot for submission of original documents.
  • Submit the applicable original documents at KEA, Bengaluru, within the prescribed period.
  • Download the admission order after completing the required formalities.
  • Report to the allotted medical college by 1 December 2026.
  • Complete document verification and admission formalities as instructed by the institution.

Candidates should check the official admission and counselling rules for any conditions related to previous allotments, upgradation, surrender or non-reporting.
