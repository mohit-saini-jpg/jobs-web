EIL Recruitment 2026 Salary

EIL Salary for this recruitment is based on the IDA pay scale of the position, with Total CTC calculated on the basic pay at the lowest point of the scale. Post-Retirement Medical Coverage Scheme applies to candidates who are 45 years of age or below on the date they join the regular rolls of the company.
