NTPC Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

The notification does not give a fixed selection schedule. It states that, to restrict the number of candidates for further selection, management reserves the right to conduct a multi-stage selection process. This may include application shortlisting/screening (based on qualification, percentage of marks, years of post-qualification or site experience, and similar factors), a written/computer-based test, a personal interview, or a combination of these. Management may also raise the minimum eligibility standards as required. Meeting the minimum eligibility criteria does not give a candidate the right to be shortlisted, called for a written test or interview, or considered further in the selection process.
