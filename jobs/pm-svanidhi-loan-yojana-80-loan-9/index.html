PM SVANidhi Loan Yojana: इस स्कीम से छोटे दुकानदारों और रेहड Overview

PM SVANidhi Yojana chhote vyapariyon ko bina collateral ke 80,000 tak ka loan deti hai, sirf 9% annual interest rate ke saath. Yeh scheme especially dukandaar, street vendors aur rehadi-patari walon ke liye design ki gayi hai.

PM SVANidhi Loan Yojana: इस स्कीम से छोटे दुकानदारों और रेहड Expert Analysis

Yeh loan scheme micro‑entrepreneurs ko cash flow boost deti hai, jisse inventory ya expansion ke liye turant paisa mil jata hai. Low interest aur simple eligibility se adoption rate kaafi high hai.

Who Is Eligible: PM SVANidhi Loan Yojana: इस स्कीम से छोटे दुकानदारों और रेहड

Jo bhi chhote dukandaar, street vendor, ya rehadi‑patari ka kaam karte hain aur apni annual turnover 5 lakh se 2 crore ke beech hai, wo eligible hain.

PM SVANidhi Loan Yojana: इस स्कीम से छोटे दुकानदारों और रेहड Application Process

1. Aadhaar, PAN aur shop ka address proof ready rakhein. 2. Last 6 mahine ka bank statement aur turnover proof (sales register ya GST return) jama karein. 3. Online portal ya nearest bank branch se application submit karein.

PM SVANidhi Loan Yojana: इस स्कीम से छोटे दुकानदारों और रेहड Benefits Details

80,000 tak ka loan milne se inventory purchase, rent ya working capital ke liye immediate fund milta hai, jo business ki sales aur profit margins ko 10‑15% tak badha sakta hai.

How PM SVANidhi Loan Yojana: इस स्कीम से छोटे दुकानदारों और रेहड Works

Applicant portal ya bank branch par form fill karne ke baad, verification team documents check karti hai. Approval hone par loan amount directly applicant ke bank account me transfer hota hai, usually 7‑10 din me.

PM SVANidhi Loan Yojana: इस स्कीम से छोटे दुकानदारों और रेहड Application Tips

Application me sabhi documents clear scan karein, correct PAN/Aadhaar link karein, aur turnover proof ko latest rakhein. Common rejection ka reason incomplete address proof ya mismatched financial data hota hai.
