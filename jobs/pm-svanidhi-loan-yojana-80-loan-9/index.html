Q1PM SVANidhi loan ke liye maximum kitna amount mil sakta hai?
A
Scheme ke tahat aapko 80,000 Rs tak ka loan mil sakta hai, jo aapki repayment capacity aur business size par depend karta hai.
Q2Is loan par interest rate kitna hai aur repayment period kya hai?

Interest rate fixed 9% per annum hai. Repayment period usually 12 se 24 mahine ka hota hai, jo bank ya NBFC ke terms par depend karta hai.
Q3Kya collateral ya guarantee ki zaroorat hoti hai?

Nahi, is scheme ke liye koi collateral ya guarantee required nahi hoti. Bas aapka KYC aur business proof sufficient hai.
