Haryana Labour Tour Fund Yojana Online Form Overview

Haryana Labour Tour Fund Yojana is a Sarkari Yojana launched by Haryana government to provide financial assistance to labourers for their tour and travel expenses. This Yojana aims to promote tourism and economic growth in the state.

Haryana Labour Tour Fund Yojana Online Form Expert Analysis

This Yojana is a great initiative by the Haryana government to support the labour community. It will not only boost tourism but also provide financial relief to the labourers.

Who Is Eligible — Haryana Labour Tour Fund Yojana Online Form

Labourers registered with the Labour Department of Haryana are eligible to apply for this Yojana.

Haryana Labour Tour Fund Yojana Online Form Application Process

To apply for Haryana Labour Tour Fund Yojana, labourers need to submit their online application form along with required documents such as ID proof, address proof, and bank account details.

Haryana Labour Tour Fund Yojana Online Form Benefits Details

Under this Yojana, labourers will receive a financial assistance of ₹5,000 to ₹10,000 for their tour and travel expenses.

How Haryana Labour Tour Fund Yojana Online Form Works

The Haryana Labour Tour Fund Yojana is implemented by the Labour Department of Haryana. Labourers can apply online through the official website of the department. The application process involves submitting the online form along with required documents and payment of application fee.

Haryana Labour Tour Fund Yojana Online Form Application Tips

To ensure successful application, labourers should carefully fill the online form and attach all required documents. They should also ensure that their bank account is linked to their Aadhaar card.
