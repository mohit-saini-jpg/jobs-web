NBEMS DNB Post Diploma Final Mop-Up Round 2026 - Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 22/08/2026

The National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS) has released the Indicative Seat Matrix for the Final (Mop-Up) Round of counselling for admission to DNB Post Diploma courses for the 2026 admission session. As per the official seat matrix, a total of 1,131 seats are vacant across government, private, railway, ESI, public sector, and other participating hospitals. The seat matrix lists seats hospital-wise and speciality-wise, along with category-wise distribution and whether a service bond applies at each institution.

What the Seat Matrix Covers

According to NBEMS, the indicative seat matrix provides:

  • Hospital-wise and speciality-wise seat details
  • Category-wise (reservation) distribution of seats
  • PwBD seat allocation, where applicable
  • Whether a service bond condition applies at the respective hospital
  • Management type of the hospital (government, private, railway, ESI, public sector, or other participating institutions)

Candidates are advised to check the management type, reservation status, PwBD allocation, and service-bond condition of each hospital carefully before finalising their choices.

How to Participate in the Final Mop-Up Round

  • Visit the official NBEMS counselling portal linked at natboard.edu.in.
  • Log in using registered credentials.
  • Fill choices/options for available seats in order of preference between August 21 and August 24, 2026, up to 5:00 PM.
  • Track the allotment result on August 27, 2026.
  • If allotted a seat, report for physical joining at the allotted hospital between August 27 and September 1, 2026.

Important Instructions

  • The seat matrix released is indicative; candidates should verify final seat availability on the official portal before submitting choices.
  • Check the management type, category, PwBD status, and service-bond applicability of each hospital before finalising preferences.
  • Choice filling closes at 5:00 PM on August 24, 2026 - no extensions have been mentioned in the notification.
  • Physical joining must be completed within the window specified (August 27 to September 1, 2026) to retain the allotted seat.
