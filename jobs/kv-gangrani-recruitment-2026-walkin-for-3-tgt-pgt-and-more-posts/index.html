  • 4Report at the school premises on 19.11.2026 at 9:00 AM with the form, 01 latest passport size photograph, all original certificates and self-attested photocopies.
  • 5Registration is from 09:00 AM to 10:00 AM; no candidate will be allowed after 10 AM.
