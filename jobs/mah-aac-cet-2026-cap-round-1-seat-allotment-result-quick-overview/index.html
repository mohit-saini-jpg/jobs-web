MAH AAC CET 2026 CAP Round 1 Result: What Happened

The CET Cell declared the CAP Round 1 allotment result for MAH AAC CET 2026 based on candidates' merit rank in the entrance exam, their reservation category, the institute and course preferences filled during the option/choice-filling stage, and the number of seats available at each participating BFA college. Candidates who have been allotted a seat need to log in to the official portal and confirm their admission by accepting the allotted seat online before August 3, 2026.

How to Check MAH AAC CET 2026 CAP Round 1 Seat Allotment Result

  • Visit the official MAH CET counselling portal at cetcell.mahacet.org.
  • Click on the "CAP Round 1 Seat Allotment" link for MAH AAC CET 2026.
  • Log in using your registered email ID or application number and password.
  • View your seat allotment result displayed on the screen.
  • Verify the allotted college and course details carefully.
  • Download and save the allotment letter for future admission formalities.

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Candidates satisfied with their allotted seat must confirm admission by accepting the seat online before August 3, 2026.
  • Carefully verify the allotted college, course, and category details on the allotment letter before accepting the seat.
  • Keep login credentials (application number/email ID and password) ready to avoid last-minute access issues.
  • Save and print the allotment letter, as it will be required during document verification and reporting at the institute.
