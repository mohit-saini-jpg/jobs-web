Collector Office Bijapur Case Worker Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

Appointment is for a first term of up to 2 years on the consolidated service fee. The District Collector may extend it for 1 year each time based on work ability, conduct and the controlling officer's recommendation; if not extended, the appointment ends automatically. The appointment is fully temporary and may be ended without giving a reason. Either side may end it with one month's notice, or one month's pay instead. The posts last only as long as the scheme runs under the Government of India. After that, the department has no responsibility for the service providers. The service fee is as per limits set by the Government of India. If the Government of India raises it in future, the fee will be raised accordingly. Only the consolidated service fee is payable; no other pay, allowances or ex-gratia grants are given. Service providers are governed by the Chhattisgarh Civil Services (Conduct) Rules, 1965, and must follow them. Maternity leave benefit applies. Travel allowance is paid as per rules if sent on tour or training. A selected candidate must pass a medical examination and submit a fitness certificate within 1 month of selection; if found unfit, service is ended. Police verification of background is mandatory before appointment, and the certificate must be given to the office when taking charge. After joining, the selected candidate must keep all case and victim data confidential and submit an affidavit to that effect; a breach leads to action to end service. Incomplete, unclear and faulty applications are rejected without separate information. An applicant found ineligible at any stage can be rejected. The District Collector's decision on selection is final.
