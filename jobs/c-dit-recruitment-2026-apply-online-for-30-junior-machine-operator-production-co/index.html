C-DIT Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Centre for Development of Imaging Technology (C-DIT), an autonomous institution set up by the Government of Kerala, is filling 30 temporary posts on a 1 year contract or till the completion of the project, whichever is earlier, on consolidated pay of Rs. 21,070 to Rs. 40,000 per month depending on the post. The posts are Production Coordinator (1), Junior Production Coordinator (2), Senior Machine Operator (18), Junior Machine Operator (8) and Graphic Designer (Security Documents Production) (1), in the Hologram printing/production unit at Thiruvallam Campus, Thiruvananthapuram. About C-DIT

C-DIT, at Chithranjali Hills, Thiruvallam P.O., Thiruvananthapuram – 695 027, describes itself as an autonomous institution set up by the Government of Kerala, functioning under the Dept. of Electronics & IT. It is an approved Total Solution Provider (TSP) and Accredited Agency for Government IT initiatives, and undertakes projects in IT/ITES, e-Governance, web development, digitisation and digital transformation. These posts are for shift work in the Hologram printing/production unit of its Optical Image Processing and Security Products Division.

Four posts have an age limit of not above 50 years on the date of notification; the Graphic Designer post has not above 55 years. Pay and Tenure

Pay for every post is stated as consolidated remuneration per month. Each post is a 1 year contract or till the completion of the project, whichever is earlier. Selection and Interview

C-DIT will decide the selection process based on the number of applications; it may include a skill test or interview. A committee constituted by C-DIT shortlists applications based on the Biodata and details of previous experience. Initial verification uses the application details and uploaded documents. Possessing the prescribed educational qualification does not by itself qualify a candidate; selection depends on the required professional experience and proven skills. Only shortlisted candidates are eligible for the written test, skill test or interview, held at centres allotted by C-DIT. Candidates may choose a test centre wherever permitted, but allocation is at C-DIT's sole discretion, subject to a minimum number of candidates at each centre. Candidates are shortlisted for group discussion or interview based on preliminary evaluation of the written or skill test, as decided by C-DI
