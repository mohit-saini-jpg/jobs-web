GMC KB Asifabad Selection Process

Candidates will be selected based on merit, duration of teaching experience, and interview. For Professor and Associate Professor posts, the duration of teaching experience in that cadre and total teaching experience will be considered. Selection is nationwide, with first preference given to local candidates for Assistant Professor-level posts; candidates from elsewhere will be considered if local candidates are not available. Professor and Associate Professor posts belong to the state cadre. DNB candidates will be selected as per the latest NMC guidelines. Rule of Reservation will be followed.

How to Attend the GMC KB Asifabad Walk-In Interview

Download the application form from the official website, www.gmckumurambheemasifabad.org. Fill the application form completely and attach one set of self-attested photocopies of all required documents. Report to the O/o Principal, Government Medical College, Kumuram Bheem Asifabad, on 11-09-2026, between 11:00 AM and 3:00 PM. Carry all original certificates along with the filled application and photocopies; applications that are incomplete or missing necessary enclosures will be summarily rejected. Attend the interview; original certificates will be retained under the custody of the Director, GMC, Siddipet, at the time of joining. There is no online or postal application process for this recruitment - candidates must appear in person on the interview date.

GMC KB Asifabad Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

This is a temporary contract/honorarium appointment up to 31-03-2027, or till vacancies are filled by promotion/regular appointment, or till the need ceases, whichever is earlier; it does not confer any right to future government appointment. Non-local candidates must get their qualification registered with the Telangana State Medical Council within one week of selection, for their appointment to be confirmed. Selected faculty are eligible for 15 days of leave per calendar year, on a cumulative basis. The selected candidate must submit a relieving order from their previous institute, failing which the selection stands cancelled. Candidates already working on contract in the same cadre at any other Government Medical College in Telangana are not eligible to apply. The contract is liable to be terminated at any time without assigning any reason; the selected faculty may similarly terminate the contract with one month's prior notice. The engaged faculty will be under the administrative control of the institute on a whole-time basis, and services may be utilized in any manner required by the Competent Authority without additional remuneration claims. If a selected candidate does not join, the next eligible merit candidate will be selected.
