How to Check and Download Bihar DCECE-PE 2026 1st Round Allotment Result

  • Visit the official website of the Bihar Combined Entrance Competitive Examination Board (BCECEB) at bceceboard.bihar.gov.in.
  • Go to the "Download Result" or "Online Portal" section.
  • Click on the "DCECE Bihar Polytechnic 1st Round Allotment Result 2026" link relevant to your course (PE, PM, or PMM).
  • Enter your Registration Number / Enrollment Number.
  • Enter your Password / Date of Birth.
  • Enter the Captcha Code.
  • Click on "Login / Submit."
  • Your allotment result will appear on the screen - check all details carefully.
  • Click "Download" and save the PDF to your device.
  • Take a clear printout for reporting and further admission formalities.

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Carrying the admit card is compulsory for entry into the examination hall, wherever applicable during subsequent processes.
  • Cross-check your name, roll number, allotted college, and course carefully in the allotment result.
  • In case of any error or discrepancy in your allotment or admit card, contact the BCECEB authority immediately for correction.
  • Keep multiple printouts of the allotment letter, as it is generally required at the time of document verification and reporting to the allotted institute.
  • Check the official counselling schedule for reporting deadlines after each allotment round.
