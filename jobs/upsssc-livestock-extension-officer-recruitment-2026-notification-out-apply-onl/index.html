UPSSSC Pashudhan Prasar Adhikari Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must fulfil all conditions below to be eligible for UPSSSC Pashudhan Prasar Adhikari Recruitment 2026. Must have appeared in the Preliminary Eligibility Test 2025 (PET-2025) and hold a valid PET-2025 score card issued by the Commission. Must not have scored zero or a negative absolute/normalized score in PET-2025. Must hold the mandatory educational qualification (see below) by the last date of application, 29-09-2026.
Qualification TypeRequirement

LanguageKnowledge of Hindi in the Devanagari script

UPSSSC Pashudhan Prasar Adhikari Age Limit

Candidates must have attained 21 years of age and must not have exceeded 40 years of age as on 1 July of the calendar year in which the vacancies are advertised for direct recruitment. Upper age limit relaxation is admissible to candidates of Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, and such other categories as notified by the Government from time to time, "to the extent specified" - the notification does not state the exact number of relaxation years for these categories. Serving employees of State Government services get a 5-year relaxation in the maximum age limit, as per Government Order No. 2-E.M./2001-Ka-4-2013 dated 27 August 2013. UPSSSC Pashudhan Prasar Adhikari Salary

The Pashudhan Prasar Adhikari post carries a pay of Level-5 in the pay matrix, with a minimum of ₹29,200 and a maximum of ₹92,300. UPSSSC Pashudhan Prasar Adhikari Selection Process

Selection to the Pashudhan Prasar Adhikari post is made on the basis of a written examination, as per the UP Pashupalan Vibhag, Pashudhan Prasar evam Kukkut Vikas Seva Niyamawali, 2002 (as amended). The written exam pattern is detailed below. The exam is objective/multiple-choice type, with a duration of 2 hours (120 minutes). Each question carries 1 mark. Negative marking applies: 25 percent (¼) of the marks prescribed for a question is deducted for each wrong answer. Shortlisting of candidates to sit the main exam itself is based on the normalized PET-2025 score, category-wise, up to 15 times the number of notified vacancies (including all candidates tied at the cut-off normalized score).
