WBPSC IDO Exam Pattern and Syllabus 2026

The written examination will consist of 100 multiple-choice questions carrying one mark each , with a duration of 1 hour 30 minutes . Yes. The written examination carries a penalty of 0.25 marks for every incorrect multiple-choice answer. These percentages apply to the aggregate marks from the written examination and interview. They are minimum qualifying standards, not guaranteed selection cut-offs. The commission may further relax qualifying marks if a substantial number of reserved posts remain unfilled. If two or more candidates receive the same aggregate marks, the older candidate will receive preference based on date of birth while preparing the recommendation.
