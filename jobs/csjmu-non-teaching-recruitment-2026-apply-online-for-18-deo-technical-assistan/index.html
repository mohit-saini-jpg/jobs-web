CSJMU Non Teaching Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria and Qualification

Eligibility for the CSJMU Non Teaching Recruitment 2026 differs post-wise. Candidates should carefully match their qualification and experience against the specific post before applying, as the notification does not describe candidates as generally eligible - each post has distinct essential and desirable qualifications.
Post NameEssential Qualification
Senior Server AdministratorGraduation or post-graduation in Information Technology or a related field, with a minimum of 4 years of hands-on experience in Data Center Operations. Proficiency in cloud technologies, Active Directory, virtualization platforms (Hyper-V/VMware), Linux and Windows server administration, Sophos Firewall, SAN storage management, IT support, and infrastructure monitoring tools is required.

Desirable: strong interest in AI, Machine Learning, and automation; basic-to-intermediate Python programming; familiarity with frameworks such as TensorFlow, PyTorch, or Scikit-learn; knowledge of APIs, databases, and web technologies.
Physiotherapy Rehabilitation Coordinator15 years of experience in Physiotherapy with a degree, OR 20 years of experience in clinical and supervisory work in a Physiotherapy department of a university or government hospital. Registration with the State Medical Faculty is required.

CSJMU Non Teaching Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is CSJMU Non-Teaching Recruitment 2026?
A
It is a recruitment drive by Chhatrapati Shahu Ji Maharaj University, Kanpur, to fill 18 contractual Non-Teaching positions across seven posts under the Self-Finance Scheme (SFS), as per Advertisement No. CSJMU/SFS/973/2026 dated 27 August 2026.
Q2How many vacancies are available in CSJMU Non-Teaching Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 18 vacancies in total, spread across Senior Server Administrator, Junior Server Administrator, Technical Assistant, Data Entry Operator, Junior Establishment Officer, AI Developer/AI Implementation Coder, and Physiotherapy Rehabilitation Coordinator posts.
Q3What is the last date to apply for CSJMU Non-Teaching Recruitment 2026?
A
The last date to submit the online application is 30 September 2026. The application window opened on 31 August 2026.
Q4What is the qualification required for CSJMU Non-Teaching Recruitment 2026?
A
Qualification requirements vary by post, ranging from a diploma in mechanical engineering (Technical Assistant) to postgraduate/graduate degrees in IT with specific years of experience (Server Administrator posts) to specialized clinical experience (Physiotherapy Rehabilitation Coordinator). See the eligibility table above for post-wise details.
Q5What is the age limit for CSJMU Non-Teaching Recruitment 2026?
A
A specific age limit is not mentioned in the notification. The contractual appointment period is 3 years and is capped at not beyond 60 years of age.
Q6What is the application fee for CSJMU Non-Teaching Recruitment 2026?
A
The application fee is ₹1,000/- for general/UR/OBC/EWS candidates and ₹750/- for SC/ST candidates, payable online with the application.
Q7How can I apply online for CSJMU Non-Teaching Recruitment 2026?
A
Applications must be submitted online through the Samarth portal accessible via www.kanpuruniversity.org or www.csjmu.ac.in, along with the application fee and scanned supporting documents.
Q8What is the selection process for CSJMU Non-Teaching Recruitment 2026?
A
The selection process differs by post: skill test plus written examination for Server Administrator, Technical Assistant, and Data Entry Operator posts; written examination, presentation, and interview (60:20:20) for Junior Establishment Officer; interview only for AI Developer; and skill assessment plus interview for Physiotherapy Rehabilitation Coordinator.
