OPTCL Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Odisha Power Transmission Corporation Limited (OPTCL), a state-owned power utility engaged in the transmission of electricity in Odisha, invites applications from young and meritorious aspirants for engagement as Degree (Engineering and Non-Engineering), Diploma, and Trade (ITI) Apprentices for a period of one year, under the Apprentices Act, 1961 (as amended in 1961, 1973, and 2014). OPTCL Apprentice Eligibility Criteria

The prescribed qualification must be from a recognised Board/Council/University/Institute, completed as a regular full-time course. As noted in the notification, for Non-Engineering Apprentices, graduation in non-engineering disciplines such as BA, B.Sc, B.Com, or BBA, or equivalent, shall be preferred. Only Indian nationals belonging to the state of Odisha are eligible to apply. Candidates must have the minimum physical standard prescribed in Clause 4 of the Apprenticeship Rules, 1992, and amendments thereof. Candidates who have already completed apprenticeship training under the Apprentices Act, 1961, or who have job experience of one year or more, are not eligible to apply. Candidates who are currently appearing for (i.e., have not yet passed) the requisite essential qualifying examination are not eligible to apply. Candidates must hold the passing certificate or final year marksheet

OPTCL Apprentice Age Limit

OPTCL Apprentice Stipend

As per the notification, OPTCL will pay 50% of the stipend to Degree Engineering Apprentices, Diploma Technician Apprentices, and Degree Non-Engineering Apprentices, with the remaining 50% paid by the Board of Apprenticeship Training (referred to as BOPT in the notification) through DBT. The funding split for the ITI Trade Apprentice stipend is not separately specified in the document. Apprentices are entitled to the stipend from the start of the online contract date, subject to satisfactory performance during the training period.

OPTCL Apprentice Selection Process

Selection will be made on the basis of academic merit, written test, or interview, as required for the respective discipline. The notification does not specify a fixed common formula for all disciplines; the exact mode of selection applied may vary by discipline. Key points on the process: OPTCL follows a transparent and merit-based apprenticeship selection process. No TA/DA or other expenses will be paid to candidates for attending document verification or interview. At any stage of the selection process, if a candidate is found to have furnished false or incorrect information, the candidature or engagement is liable to be cancelled. The decision of the Management of OPTCL is final and binding on all candidates on all matters relating to eligibility, acceptance or rejection of the application, mode of selection, and cancellation of the selection process (in part or full). OPTCL Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Applications must be submitted online in two stages - first registering on the relevant government apprenticeship portal, and then applying through the OPTCL website:
  • 2Register on NATS/NAPS portal: Degree Engineering (Electrical, IT, Telecom), Diploma (Electrical, Telecom, Civil), and Degree Non-Engineering (HR, Finance, Law) aspirants must register on the NATS 2.0 portal at nats.education.gov.in.   • 4Follow the detailed procedure: The full step-by-step application procedure is uploaded on the "Apply Online" page of the OPTCL website.
