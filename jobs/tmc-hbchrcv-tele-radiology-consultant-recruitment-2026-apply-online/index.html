Tata Memorial Centre is a grant-in-aid institution under the Department of Atomic Energy, Government of India. Homi Bhabha Cancer Hospital & Research Centre located in Visakhapatnam operates under this umbrella. The centre offers specialized cancer care, research, and educational programs, engaging medical professionals through contractual and bonded fixed term appointments to support specialized patient services such as tele radiology.
