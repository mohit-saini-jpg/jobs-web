VITEEE 2027 Revised Exam Pattern Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 22/10/2026

VITEEE 2027 Exam Pattern Revised: Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT) has reduced the B.Tech entrance examination duration from 2 hours 30 minutes to 2 hours (120 minutes). The revised examination will contain 100 multiple-choice questions carrying 400 marks. VIT has introduced fixed sectional timers, with 50 minutes for Mathematics/Biology, 30 minutes each for Physics and Chemistry, and 10 minutes for English and Aptitude combined.

Candidates will receive four marks for each correct answer, while one mark will be deducted for every incorrect response. The computer-based examination is tentatively scheduled from 27 April to 2 May 2027. Registration is open at viteee.vit.ac.in until 31 March 2027.

VITEEE 2027 Sectional Timers Explained

One of the most important changes in VITEEE 2027 is the introduction of fixed sectional time limits.

Each subject block has a defined duration. Candidates must attempt the questions within the available time instead of freely distributing the full 120 minutes across the entire paper.

Timed SectionAvailable TimeQuestions
Mathematics or Biology50 Minutes40
Physics30 Minutes25
Chemistry30 Minutes25
English and Aptitude10 Minutes10
Total120 Minutes100

The published examination update states that responses are saved and locked when a sectional timer expires, preventing candidates from returning to earlier questions after the section closes.

The official VIT website independently confirms the fixed time allocation for each block. Candidates should follow the instructions displayed during the examination for the precise navigation and section-locking procedure.

Can Candidates Return to Previous Sections in VITEEE 2027?

According to the published revised-pattern explanation, candidates cannot revisit a completed section after its allotted time expires.

This means students should carefully review their attempted answers while the relevant section remains active.

VIT's official portal confirms the use of sectional timers. However, the full computer-based test navigation rules should be checked through the official examination instructions and mock test once available.

VITEEE 2027 Negative Marking and Marking Scheme

VITEEE 2027 has negative marking for incorrect answers under its revised examination pattern.

Response TypeMarks Awarded
Correct Answer+4
Incorrect Answer−1
Unanswered Question0
Marked for Review0

For example, a candidate answering 60 questions correctly and 20 incorrectly, while leaving the remaining 20 unanswered, would receive 220 marks.

The score is calculated as 60 multiplied by four, minus 20 marks for incorrect responses.

Since wrong answers reduce the score, candidates should avoid unsupported guessing and manage their time carefully.

VITEEE 2027 Preparation Strategy for Revised Pattern

Students should adjust their preparation to the shorter duration and fixed sectional limits.

  • Practise full-length mock tests containing 100 questions in 120 minutes.
  • Allocate 50 minutes to Mathematics or Biology during practice.
  • Attempt Physics and Chemistry using separate 30-minute timers.
  • Complete English and Aptitude together within ten minutes.
  • Practise accurate answering because wrong responses carry a one-mark penalty.
  • Review errors after each practice test and improve time management.
  • Use the official syllabus and VIT's mock test when available.

VITEEE 2027 Important Instructions

  • The revised exam duration is two hours instead of two hours and 30 minutes.
  • The examination has 100 questions carrying 400 marks.
  • Fixed sectional timers apply to Mathematics/Biology, Physics, Chemistry and combined English–Aptitude.
  • Each correct answer earns four marks, while each incorrect answer carries a one-mark penalty.
  • Register by 31 March 2027 and check the official portal for examination updates.

Naye pattern mein MCQs ka weightage thoda badha hai, especially Maths aur Physics. Time limit same hai, lekin question difficulty moderate se high tak vary karegi.

Who Should Follow VITEEE 2027 Revised Exam Pattern Overview

VITEEE 2027 Revised Exam Pattern Overview Study Plan Tips

1. Har subject ke core concepts pe focus karein, weekly mock tests schedule karein. 2. Previous year papers ko analyse karke question patterns samjhein. 3. Time-bound practice se speed aur accuracy dono improve karein.

VITEEE 2027 Revised Exam Pattern Overview Weightage & Marks Distribution

Physics 30 marks, Chemistry 30 marks, Mathematics 30 marks, English 10 marks – total 100 marks. Har section ka equal weightage hai, isliye balanced preparation zaroori hai.

VITEEE 2027 Revised Exam Pattern Overview Subject-Wise Strategy

Physics: conceptual clarity aur problem solving, especially Mechanics aur Electromagnetism. Chemistry: Physical aur Organic basics, reaction mechanisms. Mathematics: calculus, algebra, coordinate geometry. English: reading comprehension aur grammar basics.

VITEEE 2027 Revised Exam Pattern Overview Revision Strategy

Revision ke liye 2-week crash plan banayein: pehle weak topics ko cover karein, phir full-length mock tests lein, last 3 days me quick formula revision aur error analysis par dhyan dein.
