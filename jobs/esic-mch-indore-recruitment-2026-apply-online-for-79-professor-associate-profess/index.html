ESIC MCH Indore Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

ESIC Medical College & Hospital, Indore (M.P.) has 79 provisional contract vacancies under Rolling Advertisement No. 09 of 2026: Professor (19), Associate Professor (27), Assistant Professor (8) and Senior Resident (25). Fixed basic pay runs from ₹67700.00 (Pay Level 11) to ₹123100.00 (Pay Level 13), with allowances. The contract is for 01 year, extendable up to 3 years. Candidates email a scanned application by 4:00 PM on the 15th of the month, and the November 2026 walk-in interview is on 18 November 2026 (reporting 9:00 AM, no entry after 9:00 AM). The notice is dated 6 November 2026.

Qualification and Experience

The date for eligibility of the qualifying degree and experience is the 15th of every month. All candidates must hold updated Medical Registration (NMC or State Medical Council). A candidate without updated registration must show and submit a copy of the Applied for document at the interview. Otherwise the candidate may not be interviewed, or the result may be withheld. A selected candidate must submit the updated registration certificate when reporting to join. Age relaxation is as per the prevailing norms of the Government of India. The maximum tenure of engagement is till 70 years of age for teaching faculty, or the contract period, whichever is earlier. Retired or working Professors may also apply for the eligible posts. Pay and Terms

The remuneration is consolidated and covers NPA@20% (wherever applicable and admissible), DA@63%, HRA@20% and TA@7200+DA thereon. It may change as per Headquarters instructions. It is revised automatically when the Dearness Allowance rate is revised, effective from the date of notification of Government orders. Arrears are not admissible for any rate revision with retrospective effect. The tenure is 01 year, or joining of a regular incumbent, or attainment of 70 years of age, whichever is earlier. The institute intends to engage faculty for 01 year, extendable up to 3 years on satisfactory performance and functional requirement. Either side may end the engagement with one month's prior notice without assigning a reason. On spot resignation, one month's salary is to be deposited to the ESI Account through Challan. ESIC MCH Indore Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1The notice runs a monthly cycle. Vacancy details are published on the institute website from the 1st to the 7th. Emailed applications are accepted up to 4:00 PM on the 15th. The walk-in interview is held on the 18th. If the 15th or 18th is a holiday, the next working day applies. The interview may also be held as notified from time to time.
  • 2Fill the Format of Application printed with the notice in black/blue ink in BLOCK letters. Submit one form for each post applied for, with a recent self-attested passport size photograph showing the face and both ears, signed across the photograph.
  • 3Scan the form, the Demand Draft (if applicable) and the self-attested documents in the order of the checklist, and merge them into one single PDF file.
  • 4Email that single PDF, in one email only, to [email protected] by 4:00 PM on the 15th of the month. Write Name, Post and Department in the subject. No email is accepted after the deadline.
  • 5Send the original application form, self-attested documents and original Demand Draft (as Interview Fee, if applicable) to The Dean, ESIC Medical College & Hospital, Nanda Nagar, Indore (M.P) - 452011. They should reach on or before the date of interview, which the notice gives as the 17th of every month.
  • 6Write Recruitment of Teaching Faculty/Senior Residents on contract basis, with the Name of Post and Department, on the envelope.
  • 7Report for the offline interview on the 18th, from 9:00 AM onwards. For November 2026 the date is 18 November 2026, and no candidate is allowed after 9:00 AM. The venue is not specified in the notice.
  • 8Mere submission of an application gives no right to be interviewed unless the candidate reports on the scheduled date and time with the required documents. The interview opportunity is provisional, subject to detailed scrutiny of eligibility. For assistance, call the recruitment officer-in-charge on 94250 94383 (Dr. Himanshu Soni) from 9:00 am to 4:00 pm only. Queries can also be sent to [email protected].
  • 9The fee is paid through Demand Draft in favour of ESI Fund A/c No.-1, payable at Indore. Fee once paid will not be refunded under any circumstances.
  • 10Selection is made on the basis of acquired academic credentials and performance in the interview before the Selection Board. The result will be displayed in the Recruitments section of www.esic.gov.in. The offer of appointment is sent through email only, and selected candidates join as per the instructions in the offer. Any amendment to the notification will appear at www.esic.gov.in/recruitments.
