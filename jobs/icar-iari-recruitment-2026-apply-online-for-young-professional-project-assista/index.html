IARI Young Professional Selection Process

The notification describes the selection steps as follows. Candidates send their application in the prescribed Performa, with self-attested scanned documents, to the AAO (Environmental Sciences) by email. Applications are screened. Eligible candidates shortlisted based on project work requirement are called by email for the online interview. The online interview is tentatively fixed for the first week of November 2026. Details and reporting time will be communicated by email, and all candidates shall adhere to the instructions. Original documents will be verified at the time of appointment. Selected candidates must also produce a medical certificate at the time of joining. The notification does not mention a written test, marks distribution or a merit list.

Documents Required for IARI Recruitment 2026

As per the terms and conditions, the following must be sent by email by 31 October 2026: Application form, filled strictly as per the annexed format (Annexure I) Self-attested scanned certificates from X class onwards Date of birth certificate NET/equivalent certificate Bachelor's and master's degree certificates or provisional certificates A recent photograph Proof of experience and publications Written declaration regarding relatives employed at ICAR-IARI (as per the declaration format), to reach by 31 October 2026 Failure to submit the required documents along with the application will lead to rejection of the application, and no further communication will be entertained on this matter.
