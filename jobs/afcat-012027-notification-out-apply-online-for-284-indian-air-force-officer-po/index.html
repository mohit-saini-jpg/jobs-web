AFCAT 1 2027 Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must be Indian citizens and meet the prescribed educational, age, marital-status and medical requirements for their selected entry and branch. Candidates must have passed Mathematics and Physics at the 10+2 level and possess one of the following qualifications: Graduation of at least three years in any discipline from a recognized university. Four-year BE/B.Tech degree from a recognized university. Cleared Section A and B examinations of the Institution of Engineers (India) or Aeronautical Society of India, as prescribed. Important: Mathematics and Physics at the 10+2 level are mandatory for the Flying Branch. Candidates must also satisfy the applicable age and medical standards. AFCAT 1 2027 Age Limit

Age eligibility is calculated as on 1 January 2028 , the scheduled course commencement period. Both dates in the specified date-of-birth ranges are inclusive. The Flying Branch relaxation up to 26 years applies only to candidates holding a valid and current Commercial Pilot Licence issued by DGCA (India). Candidates must be unmarried when the training course begins. Marriage is not permitted during training. Candidates who marry during training are liable to be discharged and required to refund Government expenditure incurred on their training.

AFCAT 1 2027 AFSB Interview and Testing

Candidates qualifying under the relevant entry route will be called for Air Force Selection Board (AFSB) testing. AFSB assessment involves Officer Intelligence Rating and psychological, group and interview assessments according to the prescribed testing stages. Flying Branch candidates may additionally be required to qualify in the Computerised Pilot Selection System (CPSS). Candidates who previously failed CPSS or were suspended from Flying Training at Air Force Academy are not eligible for the Flying Branch under the notification. Candidates recommended by AFSB must satisfy the prescribed Indian Air Force physical and medical standards. The requirements differ by Flying, Ground Duty Technical and Ground Duty Non-Technical branches. For Flying Branch training, the notification additionally requires a minimum nude body weight of 48 kg before commencing Stage-I fixed-wing flying training.

AFCAT NCC Special Entry 2027

NCC Special Entry provides eligible NCC Air Wing certificate holders an alternative route to Flying Branch selection. Applicants must possess an NCC Air Wing Senior Division 'C' Certificate acquired within the prescribed two-year period preceding the AFCAT registration date. Eligible NCC Special Entry candidates are exempt from the AFCAT written examination and are called directly for AFSB testing. They must still satisfy the educational, age, marital-status and medical requirements for Flying Branch.

AFCAT GATE Score Entry 2027

GATE Score Entry is available for eligible Aeronautical Engineer (Electronics) and Aeronautical Engineer (Mechanical) candidates. Candidates must possess a completed BE/B.Tech qualification in one of the accepted engineering disciplines and qualify in the corresponding prescribed GATE subject. The GATE result declaration date must fall between 1 January 2024 and 31 December 2027 , as specified in the notification. Applicants must provide their GATE score details and supporting qualification records. The entry is subject to the applicable shortlisting and AFSB selection procedures.

AFCAT Permanent Commission and Short Service Commission

The extension of Ground Duty SSC service is subject to vacancies, service requirements, willingness, merit and suitability. The notification also states that SSC officers are not entitled to pension under the applicable commission terms. Grant of Permanent Commission at a later date is not automatic.
