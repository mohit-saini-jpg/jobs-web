How to Check Calicut University Result 2026 Online

Follow these steps to check and download your result:

  • Visit the official Calicut University results portal at results.uoc.ac.in.
  • Locate and click the result link matching your exact examination - for example, "First Semester PG Diploma in Data Science and Analytics Examination 11/2025."
  • Enter your register number (and any other requested detail, such as date of birth or security code) in the space provided.
  • Click "Get Result" or "Submit" to view your result on the screen.
  • Download or take a printout of the result/ mark sheet for your records.

What to Do If the Result Is Not Showing

  • Recheck that you have entered the correct register number and selected the correct course, semester, and examination session.
  • Clear your browser cache or try a different browser/device, as the portal can be slow during high-traffic periods right after a result is announced.
  • Wait and try again after some time, since server load is often heavy on the day of release.
  • If the issue continues, contact your college examination cell or the Calicut University Pareeksha Bhavan helpdesk directly, as this article's source does not include a specific helpline number.

Important Instructions

  • Always verify your result only through the official portal, results.uoc.ac.in, or the Calicut University's official website.
  • Avoid third-party or unofficial websites for checking or downloading your result.
  • Save a digital copy and take a printout of your result for admission, higher-study, or employment purposes.
  • If you find any discrepancy in your marks or personal details, report it to your college examination cell or Pareeksha Bhavan promptly.
  • This provisional online result is generally not a substitute for the official grade card/certificate issued by the university; check the official notification for the process to obtain the original document.
