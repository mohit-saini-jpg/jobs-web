ASC Centre (South) Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Eligibility requirements vary by post. All posts require Matriculation (Class 10) or equivalent as the base qualification, with additional trade-specific requirements as listed below. Certain posts require candidates to clear physical fitness and endurance standards before appearing for the written test.
PostQualification Experience Required
Civilian Catering Instructor (CCI)Matriculation or equivalent; Diploma/Certificate in catering from a recognised institution; one year of experience in catering as an instructor is desirable.

MTS (Chowkidar)Matriculation or equivalent; should be proficient in trade work.

Fire Engine Driver (FED)Matriculation or equivalent; should be proficient in trade work; at least three years’ experience driving heavy vehicles and a valid driving licence; preferably certified after attending a fireman course.
FiremanMatriculation or equivalent; must be conversant with the use and maintenance of extinguishers, hose fittings, fire engines, trailers, pumps and foam branches; familiar with first-aid fire fighting appliances and trailer fire pump; must know elementary fire fighting principles and be able to perform assigned fire crew duties.
Leading FiremanMatriculation or equivalent; must be conversant with the use and maintenance of all types of extinguishers, hose fittings and fire equipment; must be capable of training, guiding and controlling the working of the B crane, and capable of taking independent charge of a shift.

ASC Centre (South) Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection to all Group C posts under this recruitment is made solely on the basis of marks obtained by candidates in the written test, subject to qualifying in the Skill/Physical/Practical test, as applicable to the post. Skill/Physical/Practical Test: All candidates are first examined for their technical trade skill and physical fitness (as applicable to the post). This test is qualifying in nature. Written Test: Only candidates who clear the technical trade and physical test are eligible to appear for the written test. The written test consists of four objective-type papers, conducted over a duration of 2 hours, with negative marking of 0.25 marks for each wrong answer. Total duration for all four parts combined is 2 hours. Question papers will be bilingual (English & Hindi), except for the General English paper, which will be in English only.
