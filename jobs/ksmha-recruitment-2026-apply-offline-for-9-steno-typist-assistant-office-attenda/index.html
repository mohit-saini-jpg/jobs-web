KSMHA Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Keralam State Mental Health Authority (KSMHA), Thiruvananthapuram, has invited applications for 9 posts on a one-year contract basis at the Mental Health Review Boards constituted under the Mental Healthcare Act, 2017: 2 Assistant, 3 Steno-Typist and 4 Office Attendant. Consolidated monthly pay is Rs. 20,280 to Rs. 34,180 depending on the post. The qualification ranges from 7th standard pass to any degree with computer knowledge. Applications on the prescribed form, with a demand draft, go by hand or post to the chosen Mental Health Review Board by 26 November 2026.

About KSMHA

The Keralam State Mental Health Authority is constituted as per the Mental Healthcare Act, 2017, and its office is at Red Cross Road, Thiruvananthapuram. The posts are for the Mental Health Review Boards constituted under the same Act. The notice is dated 10.11.2026 and is signed by the Chief Executive Officer.

Pay and Tenure

The pay is a consolidated monthly amount, quoted in the notice with G.O. (P) No.24/2026/Fin dated 18.02.2026. The appointment is purely on contract basis, and the period of contract for all posts is one year. The notice names no allowances.

Selection and Interview

The notice says a detailed scrutiny of applications and credentials will be conducted before interview or appointment. Selection norms will be as applicable for contract posts in the Government of Keralam. With the approval of the Government of Keralam, KSMHA reserves the absolute right to shortlist candidates for interview based on merit, to conduct an independent screening test and skill test, or to delegate the entire recruitment process to a government-approved testing agency, depending on the total volume of applications. The decision of the Government and KSMHA is final and binding. Candidates will be intimated by email or post about further procedures. The notice prints no interview or test date.

A
The last date for submitting the application is 26 November 2026.
Q3What is the qualification for KSMHA Assistant, Steno-Typist and Office Attendant?
A
Assistant needs any degree with computer knowledge. Steno-Typist needs SSLC pass with typewriting (English and Malayalam lower, KGTE/MGTE, with computer word processing) and shorthand English and Malayalam lower. Office Attendant needs 7th standard pass.
Q4What is the age limit for KSMHA posts?
A
All three posts have an age limit of not more than 45 years as on 01.01.2026. For Steno-Typist, retired State/Central Government employees may be not more than 62 years.
Q5What is the application fee?
A
The fee is Rs. 200 per post, and Rs. 100 for SC/ST candidates, paid by demand draft in favour of the Chief Executive Officer, Keralam State Mental Health Authority, payable at Thiruvananthapuram.
Q6What is the salary for KSMHA posts?
A
The consolidated monthly pay is Rs. 34,180 for Assistant, Rs. 24,590 for Steno-Typist and Rs. 20,280 for Office Attendant.

Q8Is the KSMHA job permanent?

No. The appointment is purely on contract basis, and the period of contract for all posts is one year.
