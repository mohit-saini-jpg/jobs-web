About KUFOS

Kerala University of Fisheries & Ocean Studies is a university at Panangad P.O., Kochi 682 506, Kerala. The guest faculty panel is for its HQ and CoF, Payyannur. It covers four faculties: Fisheries Science, Ocean Science and Technology, Fisheries Engineering and Fisheries Management. The notification is signed by the Registrar.

Where and How to Send

Applications must be in the prescribed format attached to the notification. They are sent by post to: The Registrar, Kerala University of Fisheries and Ocean Studies, Panangad P.O., Pin - 682 506 The application form asks for the following details: Name of post applied for, and the notification number and date. Personal details: name in English block letters, date of birth, age, sex, marital status, nationality, category (General/OBC/SC/ST), father's name, permanent and correspondence addresses, phone, mobile and e-mail. Qualifications (UG, PG, NET/SLET, PhD) with name of institution, % of marks/OGPA and class/rank. Additional qualification, list of publications and teaching experience (institution, post held, period). Details of present job, and the name and address of the present employer. A signed declaration with date and place.

Posts, Eligibility and Pay

All 20 subjects are for the post of Empanelled Guest Faculty. The same remuneration applies to every subject: Rs. 1500/- per hour for theory and Rs. 750/- per hour for practical , subject to a maximum of Rs.3000/- per day and Rs. 40,000/- per month. No additional honorarium shall be claimed for additional duties entrusted by the Dean of Faculty or by the university. The age limit for all subjects is "As per UGC Guidelines". Essential: Master of Fisheries Science (MFSc) in Aquaculture/Fish Genetics & Breeding/Fish Nutrition and Feed Technology/Fish Physiology & Biochemistry with specialization in Fish Nutrition/Fish Biotechnology with specialization in Aquaculture. Essential: ICAR/UGC/CSIR NET or PhD. Essential: Bachelor degree in Fisheries Science approved by ICAR. Desirable: PhD in the relevant subject. Teaching experience in the relevant subject. KUFOS Guest Faculty Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Candidates are called for an interview. The University reserves the right to restrict the number of candidates called for interview, on the basis of qualifications/suitability to the post with the experience prescribed in the notification. The notification attaches "Table 3A: Criteria for short-listing of Candidates for Interview for the Post of Assistant Professors in Universities", reproduced below. If teaching/post doctoral experience is less than one year, the marks are reduced proportionately. Maximum marks: M.Phil + Ph.D 30; JRF/NET/SET 07; awards 03. The number of candidates called for interview shall be decided by the concerned Universities. Total of 100: Academic Score 80, Research Publications 10, Teaching Experience 10. KUFOS Guest Faculty Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is the last date to apply for KUFOS Guest Faculty 2026?
A
Applications must be sent by post on or before 4.30 pm on 14 December 2026.
Q2What qualification is needed for KUFOS Guest Faculty?
A
The requirement varies by subject. Fisheries Science subjects need an MFSc in the listed specialisation, ICAR/UGC/CSIR NET or PhD, and an ICAR-approved Bachelor degree in Fisheries Science. Most other subjects need a Master's degree with at least 55% marks and NET/SLET or a similar test, or PhD.
Q3What is the age limit for KUFOS Guest Faculty 2026?
A
The notification gives the age limit as "As per UGC Guidelines".
Q4What is the remuneration for KUFOS Guest Faculty?
A
Remuneration is Rs. 1500/- per hour for theory and Rs. 750/- per hour for practical. It is subject to a maximum of Rs.3000/- per day and Rs. 40,000/- per month.
Q5What is the application fee?
A
The fee is Rs. 1500/- for candidates other than SC/ST and Rs. 375/- for SC/ST candidates. It is paid online to Account No. 67149674791 (Finance Officer, KUFOS) and is not refundable.
Q6How do I apply for KUFOS Guest Faculty?
A
Send the prescribed form by post, with certificate copies and fee proof, to The Registrar, Kerala University of Fisheries and Ocean Studies, Panangad P.O., Pin - 682 506. Superscribe the advertisement number and the post applied for.
Q7How are guest faculty selected?
A
Candidates are called for an interview. The University may restrict the number called on the basis of qualifications/suitability and experience. The attached Table 3A scores academic record (80), research publications (10) and teaching experience (10).
Q8Can I apply for more than one subject?
A
Yes. A separate application must be submitted for each post, with the application fee paid accordingly.
Q9Is NET compulsory for PhD holders?
A
No. NET/SET/SLET is not required for candidates possessing a PhD Degree as per UGC Regulations/ICAR Guidelines. It is also not required in disciplines for which NET/SLET/SET has not been conducted.
Q10How long is the KUFOS guest faculty panel valid?
A
The empanelled list is valid for three year. Deployment of empanelled guest faculty is limited to requirement.
