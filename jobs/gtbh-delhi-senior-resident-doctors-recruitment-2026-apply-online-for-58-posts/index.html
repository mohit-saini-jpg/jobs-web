GTBH Delhi Senior Resident Doctors Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Guru Teg Bahadur Hospital (GTBH), Office of the Medical Director, Govt. of NCT of Delhi, is engaging Senior Resident Doctors on an ad-hoc basis against 58 vacant posts in 13 departments, at Rs.67,700/- + NPA in pay level 11. Applications are made through a Google form that is de-activated on 19 November 2026 at 11:30 a.m., and interviews are held on 20 and 21 November 2026 at Skill Lab, 3rd Floor, Administration Block, GTB Hospital, Delhi.

Pay and Terms

Admissible emoluments are Rs.67,700/- + NPA in pay level 11, and other allowances as admissible under the rules. The initial appointment is for 44 days. It can be extended for a further 44 days, with a one day break, after a written request from the doctor that is recommended and forwarded by the concerned HOD, or till the appointment is done through the Central Appointment Committee, whichever is earlier. The maximum tenure of the Senior Resident post is 3 years, including the previous tenure served in any Govt. Hospital/Institute/Organization. The ad-hoc appointment does not give a claim over the post. When a regular appointee joins from the Central Appointment Committee, services will be terminated without any notice. SRs who have already completed 3 years may apply under relaxed criteria, admissible for one year on an ad-hoc basis. A separate merit list is prepared for fresh candidat
