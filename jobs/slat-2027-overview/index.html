SLAT 2027 - Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 18/08/2026

The Symbiosis Law Admission Test (SLAT) 2027 is conducted by Symbiosis International (Deemed University) for admission to BA LLB (Hons.) and BBA LLB programmes at Symbiosis Law Schools (SLS) in Pune, Noida, Hyderabad, and Nagpur. As of now, SIU has officially confirmed the SLAT 2027 exam date, registration dates, or application fee on its official website, slat-test.org. Based on the exam's usual annual cycle, the SLAT 2027 test is widely expected to be held in December 2026 across two sessions, with registrations expected to open around August 2026.

  • Question type: Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs), available in English only.

  • Exam duration: 60 minutes (previous cycles gave 20 extra minutes to PwD candidates).
  • Number of attempts: Two (Test 1 and Test 2); the higher of the two scores is considered for merit.

SLAT 2027 Application Fee

  • SLAT test fee: approximately ₹2,250 (per test attempt) - subject to confirmation.
  • Programme fee: approximately ₹1,000 per Symbiosis Law School programme applied to, payable separately for each SLS campus/programme.

How to Apply for SLAT 2027

Based on the registration process followed in previous SLAT cycles, the general steps to apply are :

  • Visit the official SLAT website, slat-test.org.
  • Click on the SLAT 2027 registration link on the homepage.
  • Register using a valid, functional email ID and mobile number.
  • Verify your registration using the OTP sent to your email/mobile.
  • Fill in personal details, Class 12 details, and communication address exactly as per official records.
  • Select the Symbiosis Law School(s) and programme(s) you wish to apply for.
  • Upload the required documents and photograph as specified.
  • Pay the SLAT test fee and the applicable per-programme fee.
  • Submit the form and download/print the confirmation page for future reference.

Important Instructions for SLAT 2027

  • SLAT applications typically do not have a correction window, so candidates should fill the form carefully before final submission.
  • All fees (test fee and programme fee) have historically been non-refundable and non-transferable.
  • Without paying the separate programme fee for a Symbiosis Law School, the application for that programme is not considered, regardless of SLAT score.
  • Candidates should keep their login credentials safe, as these are needed to download the admit card and check results.
  • Always cross-check any date or fee mentioned in third-party articles (including this one) with the official website, slat-test.org.
