IIM Bodh Gaya Faculty Eligibility Criteria

For academic positions, the qualification, experience and related requirements are governed by the Ministry of Education guidelines. Candidates should have a demonstrated ability to make relevant intellectual contribution to experienced and potential managers through teaching, training research and published work. Candidates should also have a strong academic background and understanding of current developments in the related field. Faculty members are expected to carry out research, design and teach courses, engage in institution development, and conduct training programs and consultancy. Meeting the minimum qualifications and experience does not by itself entitle a candidate to be called for the interview.

IIM Bodh Gaya Faculty Salary

The notification gives the following pay scale under the 7th CPC and the entry pay for each faculty position.
