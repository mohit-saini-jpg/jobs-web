JIPMER Project Research Scientist-I (Medical) Job Description

The engaged Project Research Scientist-I (Medical) will be responsible for the following duties: Overall coordination and day-to-day management of the multicentre SAFE-FEED trial at the coordinating centre. Coordinating with all participating centres to ensure uniform implementation of the study protocol. Organizing site initiation activities, investigator training, and periodic investigator meetings. Monitoring participant screening, recruitment, enrolment, and follow-up progress across centres. Ensuring protocol adherence, quality assurance, and consistency of study procedures across participating centres. Overseeing the implementation of the study randomisation process and ensuring adherence to the approved randomisation procedures and allocation concealment requirements. Overseeing data integrity, data quality checks, and timely completion of study documentation. Coordinating central

Selection and Interview

Applicants will be shortlisted based on the details furnished in the application, CV, and the documentary proof provided. The list of eligible candidates will also be displayed on the JIPMER website. The final selection will be based on CV, application, and written test and in person interview. Topics for evaluation/syllabus: Clinical research methodology, clinical trial conduct, randomised controlled trials and randomisation procedures, Good Clinical Practice (GCP), research ethics, neonatal and paediatric research, basic biostatistics, clinical trial data management (including REDCap), multicentre study coordination, and interpretation of clinical research findings.
