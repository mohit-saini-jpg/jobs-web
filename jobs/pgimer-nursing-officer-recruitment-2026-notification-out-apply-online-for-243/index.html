PGIMER Nursing Officer Eligibility Criteria 2026

PGIMER Nursing Officer eligibility 2026 is based on educational qualification, professional registration, and (for some candidates) experience, along with the prescribed age limit. Candidates must possess one of the following qualification combinations: Option A: B.Sc. (Hons) in Nursing from a recognized University or Institute, OR Regular course in B.Sc. Nursing from a recognized University or Institute, OR Post Basic B.Sc. Nursing from a recognized University or Institute; AND Registered as a Nurse or Nurse and mid-wife (RN or RN & RM) with a State Nursing Council. Option B: Diploma in General Nursing and Mid-wifery (GNM) from a recognized Board or Council; Registered as a Nurse or Nurse and mid-wife from a State Nursing Council; AND one year of experience in a minimum fifty-bedded hospital after acquiring the above qualification. PGIMER Nursing Officer Selection Process 2026

Selection to the post of Nursing Officer will be made purely on the basis of a Computer Based Test (CBT); there is no interview for this Group 'B' (Non-Gazetted) post, as per Government of India instructions. General/EWS category: minimum 40% marks SC/ST/OBC categories: minimum 35% marks PwBD candidates: minimum qualifying marks as prescribed for their respective caste category (General/SC/ST/OBC) Reserved category candidates applying against an unreserved (UR) post: minimum 40% for all categories Computer Based Test (CBT): Objective-type MCQs as per the pattern above. Shortlisting: Candidates who qualify the CBT as per the minimum qualifying criteria will be shortlisted category-wise.   • 2Visit the official PGIMER website: www.pgimer.edu.in and go to the Recruitment link on the Home Page.
  • 3Complete Online Registration by filling mandatory details: Advertisement Number, Advertisement Date, Name of Applicant (as per matriculation certificate), Date of Birth (as per matriculation certificate), Mobile Number, and Email ID. These details cannot be changed once registered.

  • 7Upload legible scanned copies of certificates/documents supporting educational qualification (from Matric onwards), mark sheets, date of birth proof, category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/PwBD, where applicable), experience certificate(s), and NOC from present employer (if employed).
  • 8Indicate your preferred examination city carefully, as it cannot be changed later.
  • 9Click Submit after completing all sections, and proceed to pay the applicable application fee through Net Banking, Credit Card, or Debit Card.
  • 10Take a printout of the completed application form for future reference and document verification.
  • 11Candidates already in government/semi-government/autonomous body employment must forward their application through proper channel to the Administrative Officer, Recruitment Cell of the Institute, within 10 days after the last date fixed for online submission; the online application is treated as an advance copy.
