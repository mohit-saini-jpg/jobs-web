Oil India Superintending Research Scientist Selection Process 2026

For Grade A & Grade B Posts (Table I & II): Computer Based Test (CBT) carrying 100 marks (85% weightage) and Personal Interview (PI) carrying 15 marks (15% weightage). For Grade A Posts under Table III (Hindi Officer & Confidential Secretary): Computer Based Test (CBT) carrying 100 marks (100% weightage for merit) followed by a qualifying Proficiency Test / Skill Test (100 marks, qualifying marks 50 out of 100).

Oil India Superintending Research Scientist Exam Pattern 2026

The total duration of the Computer Based Test (CBT) is 1 hour and 30 minutes. Compensatory time of 20 minutes per hour is provided to PwBD candidates. Qualifying Marks for CBT (Grades A & B - Table I & II): 50 out of 100 for UR, OBC(NCL) & EWS posts; 40 out of 100 for SC, ST & PwBD posts. Qualifying Marks for CBT (Table III): 50 out of 100 for UR and OBC (NCL); 40 out of 100 for PwBD. Final Score Formula (Grades A & B): (CBT Marks obtained out of 100 × 0.85) + PI Marks obtained out of 15.

Oil India Superintending Research Scientist Job Description 2026

Superintending Research Scientist: Conducting research, analytical, experimental, and technology development activities including laboratory, pilot-scale, field validation, and scale-up studies; designing, executing, and evaluating research projects; operating laboratory and pilot plant equipment; ensuring HSE compliance. Hindi Officer: Implementation and promotion of Official Language (Rajbhasha) policies; translation of official documents between Hindi and English; monitoring and reporting on Official Language implementation. Confidential Secretary: Managing confidential correspondence, scheduling and coordination of meetings, handling office documentation, liaison with internal and external stakeholders, and proficiency in Shorthand and transcription.
