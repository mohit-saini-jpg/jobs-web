About SBI Recruitment 2026

The recruitment is conducted by the Central Recruitment & Promotion Department of State Bank of India at its Corporate Centre, Mumbai. Both posts are in the bank's Specialist Cadre and will have no provision for conversion into the General cadre. The work centres on research and development of derivative and other market products for the bank's corporate and retail clients.

Vacancy Details

The notice lists 7 vacancies: 1 for Chief Manager and 6 for Manager. The PwBD vacancy is horizontal and is covered under the parent category. The bank states that the number of vacancies, including reserved vacancies, is provisional and may vary according to its actual requirement. The PwBD vacancies are for candidates with Visual Impairment - Low Vision (VI-LV). The notice defines Low Vision as either of the following: visual acuity not exceeding 6/18 or less than 20/60 up to 3/60 or up to 10/200 (Snellen) in the better eye with best possible corrections; OR limitation of the field of vision subtending an angle of less than 40 degrees up to 10 degrees. Reserved-category and PwBD candidates for whom no reservation is mentioned may apply against Unreserved vacancies if they meet all the Unreserved-category eligibility criteria.

SBI Recruitment 2026 Job Description

The selected candidate will support the development and delivery of customized solutions to clients. The candidate must also ensure that product offerings stay aligned with evolving market dynamics and regulatory expectations. The job profile includes: Conducting research followed by development of new derivative products in line with the prevailing market. Evaluating the performance of the product on an ongoing basis. Assisting marketing of derivative products to the clients. Providing periodic updates to the Top Management. Key Responsibility Areas (KRAs): Lead product conceptualization and development of new derivative instruments. Selection and Interview

Selection for both posts is on the basis of shortlisting and interview. Shortlisting: Meeting the minimum qualification and experience does not give a right to an interview. A shortlisting committee of the bank will decide the shortlisting parameters and call an adequate number of candidates. Interview: The interview carries 100 marks. The bank will decide the qualifying marks. The bank will also decide the interview venue or centre, and no request for a change will be entertained. Merit list: The merit list will be prepared in descending order of interview scores only. Candidates with the same marks at the cut-off point will be ranked according to their age in descending order. Call letter: The interview intimation will be sent by email or uploaded on the bank's website. No hard copy will be sent. Shortlisting is provisional and done without verifying documents.
