Qualification and Experience

The notice does not print a separate qualification or experience requirement. It refers both to the UGC Regulations, 2018. These conditions apply without prejudice to the HPNLU Shimla Executive and Service Regulations, 2020 (as amended from time to time) or any other law in force: An applicant must not be engaged or appointed elsewhere on a salary or honorarium payment basis. An applicant should not have been involved in any criminal case, whether pending or convicted. An applicant must not be facing any disciplinary proceeding before the Bar Council of India, the Bar Council of the State, Institutes or any other authority. For the full eligibility of the Professor of Law post, see the official notification and the UGC Regulations, 2018 it refers to.

Pay and Terms

The Professor of Law (Regular) post is at Academic Level 14 (UGC Pay scale) . The notice says that the service conditions, including salary and allowances, shall follow the Rules and Regulations of Himachal Pradesh National Law University, Shimla. It does not list the allowances one by one. The relevant Regulations/Rules of the University govern the terms and conditions of eligibility, appointments and services. All appointments are provisional, subject to verification of documents. Selected candidates must join within the period given in their joining orders. Ordinarily, no request to extend the joining time will be entertained.

HPNLU Professor of Law Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Candidates apply only online. The University will not accept any offline application. The online application form is available on the University's website, hpnlu.ac.in, and candidates submit it by registering on the ERP Portal of HPNLU, Shimla (erphpnlu.in).
  • 2Register on the ERP Portal of HPNLU, Shimla (erphpnlu.in). Note down the User ID and Password, because you will need them for all future references.
  • 3Fill in complete and correct details in the Online Application Form. Give a working mobile number and email ID, and keep them working until the selection process ends.
  • 4Read the API Score Criteria, then fill out the prescribed API Score Form and upload it.
  • 5Pay the application fee of Rs. 3000/- through the payment gateway on the ERP Portal.
  • 6Submit the online application by 21.11.2026 (05:00 P.M.).
  • 7Download the application form from the ERP Portal. Attach all supporting documents and certificates for the API Points you claim in the API Score Form.
  • 8Send the hard copy to the Office of Registrar, Himachal Pradesh National Law University, Shimla, 16 Mile, Shimla-Mandi National Highway, Village Ghandal, Post Shakarah, Sub-Tehsil Dhami, Dist. Shimla - 171014 (Himachal Pradesh). It must reach by 28.11.2026 (05:00 P.M.).
  • 9The fee can only be paid online, through the payment gateway on the University's ERP Portal. The University accepts no other mode of payment. The fee is non-refundable, and applications without the required fee will be rejected.
  • 10Only shortlisted candidates will be invited for the test/interview. If the University receives a large number of applications, it may, at its discretion, limit the number of candidates to a reasonable figure. It may do this on the basis of qualifications or experience higher than the minimum prescribed for the post, or by any other criteria it decides. Interview dates will be notified on the University website and emailed to shortlisted applicants at the email ID given in the application form. No TA/DA will be paid.
  • 11Applicants who already applied for the post of Professor (Regular Basis) under Advertisement No. HPNLU-A(7)-11(Advt.)/2022-40 dated 30 January 2026 do not need to apply again. They may update their application on the ERP Portal using their User ID and Password.
