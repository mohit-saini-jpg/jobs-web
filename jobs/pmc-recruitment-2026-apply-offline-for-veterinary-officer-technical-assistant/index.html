PMC Recruitment 2026 - Selection Process

The notification does not describe a written examination. Based on the terms and conditions, the process works as follows: Applications submitted with self-attested documents are scrutinized by the Health Department. Eligible and ineligible candidates are notified from time to time on the PMC website (https://www.pmc.gov.in). A merit list is prepared according to the number of applications received, and selection is made on the basis of qualification and experience; preference is given to candidates with more experience and higher educational qualification, though the final decision rests with the administration. The decision of the selection committee is final, and the final merit list remains valid for 1 year. Selected candidates are required to execute an agreement on a Rs. 500 bond paper.

Key Terms and Conditions PMC Recruitment 2026

These are contract posts and carry no relationship with the regular establishment of Pune Mahanagarpalika; as per the Maharashtra Government's General Administration Department resolution dated 25 August 2005, no contractual employee can claim permanency regardless of years served. The total number of posts advertised is subject to change. Each post is for a period of 11 months and 29 days; positions automatically lapse when the relevant project/scheme ends. Experience gained in government, semi-government, local self-government bodies, or private organizations will be considered. Any candidate found using political or other pressure during selection will be disqualified. The Additional Municipal Commissioner, Pune Mahanagarpalika, reserves the right to change or cancel the selection process at any stage without assigning reasons. Selected candidates are bound to work only within the Met
