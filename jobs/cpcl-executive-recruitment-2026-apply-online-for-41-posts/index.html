CPCL Executive Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must possess the prescribed educational qualification and relevant post-qualification work experience as on 1 October 2026. The exact degree, minimum marks and experience requirements depend on the selected position. Important: The Manager and Senior Manager designations share one vacancy under CPCL08. The grade of appointment will be decided by CPCL based on the applicant's eligibility, experience, suitability and selection performance. The relaxed marks requirement is not available indiscriminately for every discipline. For example, the Civil Engineer vacancy permits 55% for eligible SC candidates against reserved positions, while the sole Instrumentation Engineer post has a 60% requirement without a separately notified reserved-category marks concession. Marks Calculation: CPCL calculates aggregate percentage using marks obtained in all subjects across all semesters or year

CPCL Executive Salary 2026

CPCL offers IDA pay scales with initial basic pay ranging from Rs. 30,000 to Rs. 90,000 per month , according to the selected position and grade. In addition to basic pay, eligible employees receive Industrial Dearness Allowance, HRA, cafeteria allowance, conveyance maintenance, performance-related pay, provident fund, gratuity, medical benefits, leave travel benefits and other applicable allowances under CPCL rules. Salary Clarification: CTC includes various employer benefits and performance-related components. It should not be treated as take-home salary. No pay protection is admissible under the notified conditions.

CPCL Executive Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

The selection process differs between Post Codes CPCL01 to CPCL07 and CPCL08. Applicants should prepare according to the assessment pattern applicable to their chosen position. Computer Based Test (CBT). Shortlisting based on CBT performance. Group Discussion / Group Task. Personal Interview. Document Verification. Pre-Employment Medical Examination. Computer Based Test (CBT). Skill Test. Personal Interview. Document Verification. Medical Examination. Manager/Senior Manager (Secretarial) candidates will be shortlisted based on qualification, experience and overall suitability, followed by Group Discussion/Group Task and Personal Interview. The official notification does not prescribe a CBT for CPCL08.

CPCL Executive Reservation and Certificate Requirements

Reservation is available according to Government of India rules and the post-wise allocation in the notification. Candidates claiming OBC Non-Creamy Layer reservation must possess a valid Central Government format certificate issued by the competent authority on or after 1 April 2026 . OBC candidates belonging to the creamy layer are not eligible for OBC-NCL reservation benefits. Applicants claiming EWS reservation must provide the prescribed Income and Asset Certificate issued by the competent authority on or after 1 April 2026 . PwBD reservation applies only to identified functional disability categories suitable for each post. Eligible candidates must possess the prescribed disability certificate. CPCL Executive Admit Card and CBT Examination 2026

CPCL will make CBT admit cards available through its recruitment website. The tentative release period is approximately 10 days before the examination . Candidates will be required to download the call letter using the prescribed login credentials and follow the instructions regarding photograph, original photo ID and other documents. Admit cards will not be sent by post. The CBT is tentatively scheduled for December 2026 or January 2027, with Chennai as the proposed examination location.
