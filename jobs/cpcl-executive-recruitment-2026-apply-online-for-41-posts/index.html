CPCL Executive Vacancy 2026 - 41 Posts

CPCL has announced 41 Executive vacancies across engineering, information systems, official language and secretarial disciplines. Engineer (Chemical) accounts for the highest number of vacancies, with 22 posts. Vacancy Clarification: The Manager (Secretarial) and Senior Manager (Secretarial) designations are grouped under Post Code CPCL 08 with a combined allocation of one vacancy. They should not be counted as two separate openings. The advertised vacancies are tentative and may increase or decrease according to management decisions, reservation rules and applicable Government guidelines. Of the 41 advertised Executive posts, 37 vacancies are for engineering positions across five disciplines.
