UPSC Section Officers and Stenographers LDCE Selection Process 2026

For the Section Officers' Grade-B LDCE, admission to the Written Examination and evaluation of Service Record is purely provisional, subject to candidates satisfying the prescribed eligibility conditions. If it is found, on verification before or after the Written Examination or evaluation of Service Records, that a candidate does not fulfil any eligibility condition, their candidature will be cancelled by the Commission. Where candidates' Final Marks (aggregate) are equal, ties will be resolved in the following order: The candidate securing more marks in the written total will be ranked higher. If still equal, the candidate securing more marks in Paper-II and Paper-III put together will be ranked higher. UPSC Section Officers and Stenographers LDCE 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1UPSC LDCE Apply Online process uses the Commission's common Online Application Portal, which applies to both the Section Officers' and Stenographers' examinations.
  • 2Visit the Commission's online application portal: https://upsconline.nic.in.
  • 3Complete Account Creation, Universal Registration, and the Common Application Form (CAF) — these three modules are common to all UPSC examination applications and can be filled anytime. A Universal Registration Number (URN) is generated on registration, common for all Commission examinations for the candidate's lifetime.
  • 4Fill the fourth module — the Examination Specific Form — for the Combined Section Officers' (Grade-'B') LDCE, 2026 and/or the Combined Stenographers' (Grade-'B'/Grade-'I') LDCE, during the application window (16.09.2026 to 06.10.2026). This generates an Application Number specific to that examination.
  • 5Upload your photograph and also capture a live photograph while filling the CAF, ensuring both are clear as per the instructions on "Instructions and FAQs > Instruction for filling the form > Photos and Signature".
  • 6Sign three times (one below the other) on a plain white paper using black ink, and upload the scanned signature; it must be clear and legible.
  • 7Submit the requisite information and supporting documents for claims such as date of birth and educational qualification, along with the URN, CAF, and Examination Specific Module (including fee and centre selection); failure to provide required information/documents will result in cancellation of candidature.
  • 8(For Section Officers' LDCE) Take a printout of the completed online application by 06.10.2026, and submit the complete printed copy to your respective Ministry/Department for verification/certification. Your Ministry/Department will forward it to the concerned Cadre Controlling Authority (CCA), with a copy endorsed to the Commission, so that it reaches UPSC by the prescribed date of 23.10.2026, addressed to the Under Secretary (E-VI), Union Public Service Commission, Dholpur House, Shahjahan Road, New Delhi–110069.
  • 9Download the e-Admit Card from the Commission's website once issued, on the last working day of the week preceding the examination date; no admit card will be sent by post.
  • 10Applicants are strongly advised to use their Aadhaar Card as an ID document for easy, effortless, and seamless verification and authentication of ID and other details.

UPSC Section Officers and Stenographers LDCE 2026 : Important Instructions

Candidates should carefully refer to the Notice and Rules before filling the application to confirm eligibility; prescribed conditions cannot be relaxed. Candidates must finally select their preferred examination centre before submitting the application (Section Officers' LDCE); no request for change of centre will be entertained under any circumstances. Admission to the examination at all stages is purely provisional, subject to satisfying prescribed eligibility conditions; candidature will be cancelled if found ineligible at any stage. Candidates must produce their official Identity Card at the time of the examination, along with the e-Admit Card. Signatures on the application form, Attendance List, and correspondence with the Commission must be identical; any variation may lead to cancellation of candidature. No correspondence will be entertained to change any entries made in the application form after submission, as forms are processed by a computerized system. Candidates are not entitled to any Travelling Allowance from the Commission for attending the examination. Only candidates whose printed copy of the online application is forwarded by the concerned CCA (for the Section Officers' LDCE) will be considered for admission to the examination; the Commission is not responsible for non-receipt or delay in receipt of applications, and late applications will be summarily rejected. Applicants must provide a valid and active email ID while creating their account, as the Commission may use electronic mode to contact candidates. A one-time facility is available to update/modify the URN Profile; changes will apply only to applications submitted after the URN Profile is re-locked, not to applications already submitted. All communications regarding an application should be addressed to the Under Secretary (E.VI), UPSC, Dholpur House, Shahjahan Road, New Delhi-110069, and must include: name and year of examination, URN, Application Number, Roll Number (if received), full name in block letters, complete postal address, and valid registered email ID and mobile number. Communications lacking these particulars may not be attended to.
