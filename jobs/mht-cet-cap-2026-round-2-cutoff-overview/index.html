MHT CET CAP 2026 Round 2 Cutoff - Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 17/08/2026

The State Common Entrance Test Cell, Maharashtra, has released the MHT CET CAP 2026 Round 2 cutoff. The cutoff shows the closing percentile at which seats were allotted to candidates in the second round of the Centralised Admission Process (CAP) for engineering admissions. Competition remained sharp for computer-related branches: COEP CSE closed at 99.97 percentile for the GOPENS category, VJTI IT touched 99.99 percentile, and PICT Computer Engineering closed at 99.77 percentile. VJTI Mumbai recorded the highest overall competition, with Computer Engineering closing at 99.9923 percentile and Information Technology at 99.9572 percentile.

MHT CET All India (AI) Seat Cutoff

All India seats accept both MHT CET and JEE Main scores, and the cutoff for these seats is reported separately from Maharashtra State seats.

  • COEP, Computer Science and Engineering: All India merit number 361, corresponding to a JEE Main percentile of 99.2748
  • COEP, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning: All India merit number 413, corresponding to a JEE Main percentile of 99.1999
  • PICT, Computer Engineering: All India rank 893, corresponding to 98.6885 percentile
  • PICT, Artificial Intelligence and Data Science: All India rank 999, corresponding to 98.5860 percentile

Why Round 2 Cutoffs Differ From Round 1

The Round 2 seat matrix showed more than 54,700 vacant engineering seats across participating institutes after Round 1, giving more candidates a chance to secure admission in Round 2. Because of this higher seat availability, cutoffs for less-preferred or newly available branches may ease compared to Round 1, while high-demand branches such as Computer Science and Engineering, Information Technology, and Artificial Intelligence and Data Science are likely to remain competitive with high closing percentiles.

How to Check the MHT CET CAP Round 2 Cutoff

  • Visit the official MHT CET CAP 2026 counselling website.
  • Open the CAP Round 2 Cutoff link on the homepage.
  • Select the relevant cutoff list - Maharashtra State (MH) seats or All India (AI) seats.
  • Download the cutoff PDF for the round.
  • Use Ctrl+F (or the search function) to find your desired college, course, or choice code in the PDF.
  • Check the closing rank or merit score listed for your specific category.

Who Should Check This Cutoff

  • Candidates who have already received a seat allotment in Round 2 and want to confirm or compare their allotted college and branch.
  • Candidates planning their choices for the next counselling round based on how Round 2 closed.

Important Instructions

  • There is no single MHT CET cutoff that applies to all colleges - the closing percentile varies by institute, branch, category, seat type (MH/AI), and seat availability.
  • Candidates allotted a seat in Round 2 must report to the allotted institute and complete the admission process within the reporting window.
  • Cutoff PDFs are released separately for Maharashtra State seats and All India seats - check the correct list based on your candidature.
