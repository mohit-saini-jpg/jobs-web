IAF LDC Recruitment 2026 - Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification 12th Class pass from a recognized Board. Typing speed of 35 words per minute (wpm) in English or 30 wpm in Hindi on computer. (35 wpm and 30 wpm correspond to 10500 KDPH / 9000 KDPH on an average of 5 key depressions for each word. Time allowed: 10 minutes). Age Limit 18 to 25 years as on the last date of receipt of application. Age Relaxation (as applicable) Ex-Servicemen : Period of service in the Armed Forces shall be deducted from actual age. If the resultant age does not exceed the prescribed maximum age by more than 3 years, the candidate shall be deemed to satisfy the age condition. SC/ST/OBC candidates applying against Unreserved (UR) posts are not entitled to any relaxation in age limit, experience, etc. Other relaxations as per Government of India rules for eligible categories. Nationality Only Indian citizens are eligible.

IAF LDC Recruitment 2026 - Selection Process

Scrutiny of Applications – All applications will be scrutinized for age limit, minimum qualification, documents and certificates. Written Test – Shortlisted candidates will be called for the written examination. Skill Test (Typing Test) – Qualifying in nature only. Document Verification – Original certificates and copies of Annexure to be produced. Written Examination Details Syllabus: (i) General Intelligence (ii) English Language (iii) Numerical Aptitude (iv) General Awareness Question-cum-answer paper will be set in both English and Hindi . Marking System : Written Test – 100% weightage Skill Test – Qualifying only The written test centre may be different from the place to which the application was sent (purely for administrative convenience).
