Kerala PG Dental MDS 2026 - Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 07/09/2026

The Commissioner for Entrance Examinations (CEE), Kerala, has released the first phase centralised allotment list for admission to Post Graduate Dental (MDS) courses for the academic year 2026 on the official website on September 5, 2026 at cee.kerala.gov.in. Candidates who have been allotted a seat in this round are required to complete their admission formalities at the allotted college by September 11. The allotment has been made through the Centralised Allotment Process (CAP), based on candidates' rank in the Kerala PG Dental merit list and the options they submitted online.

How to Check Kerala PG Dental MDS 2026 Allotment Result

  • Visit the official website: cee.kerala.gov.in.
  • Locate and click on the "PG Dental 2026" or "MDS 2026 - Candidate Portal" link.
  • Log in using your application number and password.
  • Click on the "Allotment Result" link to view your first phase allotment status.
  • Download and print the allotment memo for reporting to the allotted college.

How to Complete Admission After Allotment

  • Download the allotment memo from the candidate portal.
  • Pay the applicable fee as instructed in the allotment memo.
  • Report to the allotted college in person, along with all original documents listed above, before the deadline of September 11.
  • Complete document verification and admission formalities at the college as per the college's instructions.

Important Instructions

  • Candidates who fail to complete admission at the allotted college by the deadline may lose their current allotment as well as any higher-order options, depending on CEE's rules for the round.
  • Keep the allotment memo and all original documents ready well in advance to avoid last-minute issues.
  • Any grievance or complaint regarding the allotment should be raised through the official channel specified by CEE Kerala, if such a facility is provided for this round.
  • Regularly check cee.kerala.gov.in for updates on second phase allotment, mop-up rounds, or any revised schedule.
