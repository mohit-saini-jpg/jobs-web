About NIPER Ahmedabad

The National Institute of Pharmaceutical Education & Research (NIPER) - Ahmedabad is described in the notice as an Institute of National Importance. It was established by an Act of Parliament under the aegis of the Department of Pharmaceuticals, Ministry of Chemicals & Fertilizers, Government of India. The Institute is located at Palaj, Gandhinagar-382355, Gujarat. This notification covers direct recruitment to non-faculty posts on a regular basis through open competition on an all-India basis. The notice states that the Institute strives to have a workforce that reflects gender balance, and it encourages women candidates to apply.

Vacancy Details

NIPER Ahmedabad has advertised 6 posts in total: 4 Unreserved and 2 for Scheduled Castes. The table below shows the posts as printed in the notification. Three of the posts are against lien vacancies. The notice prints these notes: System Engineer: the lien vacancy is likely to be regular by 26-06-2027, if the lien is vacated by the lien holder or terminated by the Institute, whichever is earlier. Accountant: the lien vacancy is likely to be regular by 29.08.2027, on the same condition. Junior Hindi Translator: the lien vacancy is likely to be regular by 04.05.2028, on the same condition. The UR category vacancy is against a post on which an individual holds a lien. If that individual joins back NIPER Ahmedabad, the selected candidate will be reverted to the parent organisation or post previously held, or their services will be terminated with immediate effect, as the case may be. NIPER Ahmedabad Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in NIPER Ahmedabad Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 6 non-faculty posts in total, 4 Unreserved and 2 reserved for SC.
Q2What is the last date to apply for NIPER Ahmedabad Recruitment 2026?
A
The online application closes on 21 December 2026 at 11:59:59 PM. The hard copy must reach the Institute by 28 December 2026.
Q3What qualification is needed for the NIPER Ahmedabad Accountant post?
A
The Accountant posts require a B.Com from a recognised University/institute and 3 years of experience in a Central / State Govt., University, PSU, autonomous body or other recognised institute of repute. M.Com / MBA in Finance is desirable.
Q4Can a 12th pass candidate apply to NIPER Ahmedabad?
A
Yes, for the Junior Technical Assistant post, which requires Senior Secondary (10+2) with Science Subject. This post is reserved for SC and has an age limit of not exceeding 27 years.
Q5What is the age limit for NIPER Ahmedabad Recruitment 2026?
A
The limit is not exceeding 40 years for System Engineer, 35 years for Public Relation Officer, Accountant and Junior Hindi Translator, and 27 years for Junior Technical Assistant, counted on the last date for online submission.
Q6What is the application fee?
A
The fee is Rs. 2,000/- for the Pay Level 10 post (System Engineer) and Rs. 1,000/- for all other posts. SC, ST and PwBD applicants are exempted, and the fee is non-refundable.
Q7What pay level do the NIPER Ahmedabad posts carry?
A
The posts carry 7th CPC Pay Level 10 (System Engineer), Level 8 (Public Relation Officer), Level 7 (Accountant), Level 6 (Junior Hindi Translator) and Level 4 (Junior Technical Assistant), plus DA, HRA and Transport Allowance as per rules.
Q8Is a hard copy of the application required?
A
Yes. The printout of the online application, with self-attested copies of certificates, must be sent by Speed Post / Registered Post to The Registrar, NIPER Ahmedabad, Palaj, Gandhinagar-382355, Gujarat, and must reach by 28 December 2026.
Q9What does "Against Lien Vacancy" mean in this notice?
A
These posts are held on lien by an existing employee. If that employee joins back, the selected candidate will be reverted or their services terminated. If the employee does not return, the selected candidate will be considered for permanent absorption, subject to probation.
Q10Can I apply for more than one post at NIPER Ahmedabad?
A
Yes, if you meet the eligibility for each post. You must apply for each post separately and pay the application fee separately.
