RailTel Apprentice Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must satisfy the qualification, experience and age conditions listed below to be considered for RailTel Apprentice Recruitment 2026. Candidates who obtained their qualification through distance learning or part-time mode are not eligible to apply. Any engineering graduate or diploma holder who has undergone, or possesses, training or job/work experience of one year or more after attaining the qualification mentioned above is not eligible. Candidates who completed their graduation or diploma more than three years ago will also not be considered for this apprenticeship. Candidates who have already completed an apprenticeship, terminated one midway, or are currently pursuing an apprenticeship with another organization are not eligible either.

RailTel Apprentice Selection Process

Selection for RailTel Apprentice Recruitment 2026 will be based on the following process as described in the notification: A merit list will be prepared region/state/location-wise on receipt of online applications, with preference given to Government Institution pass-outs. Shortlisting will be done based on the merit list prepared using marks obtained in the required qualification (minimum eligibility), and/or through an interview as required. Only candidates found medically fit as per RailTel's norms will be considered for engagement. Merely meeting the eligibility criteria does not assure engagement. Candidature is provisional and subject to subsequent verification of certificates/testimonials at any stage; false or incorrect information can lead to cancellation of candidature or engagement.
