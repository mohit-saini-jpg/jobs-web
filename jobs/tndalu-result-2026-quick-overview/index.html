Who Can Check the TNDALU Result 2026?

  • Students who appeared for UG law programme examinations (BA LLB, BBA LLB, B.Com LLB, B.Sc LLB, 3-year LLB) under TNDALU or its affiliated colleges.
  • Students who appeared for PG law programme examinations under TNDALU.
  • Candidates who applied for LLB admission through TNDALU/SOEL and are awaiting the seat allotment result, where applicable.

How to Check TNDALU Result 2026 Online

  • Visit the official website: tndalu.ac.in.
  • Go to the "Results" or "Examinations" section on the homepage.
  • Select the relevant link - for example, UG Result, PG Result, or Seat Allotment Result, depending on what has been released.
  • Enter the requested login details, such as registration number and date of birth.
  • Submit the details to view your result or marksheet on screen.
  • Download and save the result, and take a printout for future reference.

Details Usually Mentioned on the TNDALU Marksheet

  • Student's name and registration number
  • Course and semester name
  • Subject-wise marks obtained
  • Total marks and result status (pass/fail/reappear)
  • College/affiliated institution name

Exact format details can vary; refer to your downloaded marksheet as the final reference.

What After the TNDALU Result 2026?

  • Students who have passed can proceed to the next semester or academic year as per university guidelines.
  • Final-year/final-semester students can use the marksheet for further studies, bar enrolment processes, or job applications, subject to university-issued provisional or degree certificates.
  • For admission-related results (LLB seat allotment), selected candidates are typically required to complete reporting and document verification at the allotted college within the timeline specified by the university.
