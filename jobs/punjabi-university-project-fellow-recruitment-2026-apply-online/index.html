Punjabi University Project Fellow Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Department of Pharmaceutical Sciences and Drug Research, Punjabi University, Patiala, has invited applications for two (2) Project Fellows in a RUSA-2 sponsored research project on compounds for combating Alzheimer's disease. The stipend is Rs. 25,000 per month (consolidated), the post lasts till 31/01/2027, and the essential qualification is M.Pharm in Pharmaceutical Chemistry with First Class Division. Applications go by email to [email protected] within 10 days of the advertisement dated 20/11/26, which works out to about 30 November 2026 (10 days from 20 November 2026).

The application format asks candidates to state whether they are GPAT/GATE/NET qualified (Yes/No) and to list experience with the current occupation first; experience of a complete year will be counted. Pay and Tenure

Each Project Fellow gets a stipend of Rs. 25,000/- per month, which the notice describes as consolidated. The appointment is for the duration of the project till 31/01/2027; if applicable, an extension may be given as per RUSA-2 norms.

Selection and Interview

The notice refers to an interview for candidates who are shortlisted. No TA/DA will be provided for attending the interview. The interview date and venue are not specified in the notice.
