Zilla Panchayat Kolar Recruitment 2026 Salary Structure

Selected candidates for both the Homoeopathy Specialist Doctor and Ayurveda Specialist Doctor posts will be paid a consolidated monthly remuneration of Rs. 52,550/- plus Rs. 5,000/- PG Allowance (PG allowance is applicable only to postgraduate degree holders). The monthly amount is released subject to satisfactory performance of duties.

Zilla Panchayat Kolar Application Fee 2026

The official notification does not mention any application fee details. Candidates are advised to check the official notification PDF or contact the District Ayush Office, Kolar, for confirmation before applying.

Zilla Panchayat Kolar Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection to the above posts will be made based on: Merit (marks obtained in the qualifying degree) - 70% weightage Experience - 20% weightage Interview conducted by the District Ayush Committee - 10% weightage The merit list for the Specialist Doctor posts will be prepared based on the marks secured in the qualifying degree.

Required Documents for Kolar Ayush Doctor Recruitment 2026

Filled application form (downloaded from the official website) Proof of educational qualification with mark sheets/certificates Category/reservation certificate, if applicable (including sub-reservation) Age proof (SSLC marks card) Residence proof/certification from the concerned Municipality or Tahsildar Proof of relevant experience (only experience in government-registered Homoeopathy/Ayurveda hospitals or clinics is considered) Two passport-size photographs Aadhaar copy Original documents related to qualification, reservation, age, residence, and annual income must be produced at the time of the District Ayush Committee interview.

Zilla Panchayat Kolar Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

The contract appointment is purely temporary and will end on 31 March 2027, subject to further extension decisions by the Ayush Directorate and District Ayush Committee. Selected candidates must stay at the headquarters station and be available for emergency duty round the clock. The contract appointment can be terminated at any time without assigning a reason, or by either party giving one month's notice. Candidates will not have any right to permanent appointment, pension, retirement gratuity, pay scale, or other allowances. The contract appointment is specific to the hospital mentioned; there is no provision for transfer or deputation to another hospital, clinic, or district. Incomplete applications and applications received after the due date will be rejected without notice. Applications received after the last date will not be considered.
