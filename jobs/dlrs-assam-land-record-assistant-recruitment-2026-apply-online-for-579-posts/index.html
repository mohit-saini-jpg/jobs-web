Assam DLRS Land Record Assistant Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must have passed the Recorders Certificate Class Course (RCCC) Training from the Assam Survey & Settlement Training Centre, Dakhingaon, Guwahati-40, in the mandated examination, with an official result declared by the Director of Land Records & Surveys, Assam, covering both Survey and Settlement subjects, prior to the commencement of Government Notification No. LA&A/429557/158 dated 13.08.2026. Candidates must have proficiency in the use of computers, with a minimum typing speed of 30 WPM in English and 15 WPM in Assamese/Bengali. This recruitment is restricted to candidates who have already completed the RCCC Training and cleared the mandated examination prior to the specified cut-off - it is not open to candidates without this prior training/certification.

Assam DLRS Land Record Assistant Recruitment 2026 Age Limit

Candidates should not be less than 21 years and not more than 40 years of age as on 01.01.2026. Age relaxation for reserved category candidates is applicable as per Government OM No. ABP.6/2016/51, dated 02.09.2020. The age limit will be calculated based on the Class-X/Class-XII Examination Admit Card/Pass Certificate/Mark Sheet issued by a recognised Central/State Board/Council, where age/date of birth is clearly mentioned.

Assam DLRS Land Record Assistant Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection is through a competitive examination comprising two components, for a total of 100 marks. The Written Examination carries negative marking of 0.25 marks for each incorrect answer. A practical test assessing both English and Assamese or Bengali typing. Candidates whose applications are accepted will be shortlisted and notified in due course to appear for the Written Examination (MCQ pattern) on a date/time to be notified. Candidates who clear the category-wise minimum qualifying marks in the Written Examination, in a ratio decided by the Selection Board, will be called for the Skill Test. Only candidates declared qualified by the Selection Board on the basis of the Written Examination will appear for the Skill Test.
