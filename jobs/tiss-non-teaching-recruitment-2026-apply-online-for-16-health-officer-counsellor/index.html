TISS Eligibility Criteria

The applicant must be a citizen of India.
PostQualification Required
Deputy RegistrarMaster’s Degree with at least 55% marks or equivalent grade 'B' in UGC seven-point scale, plus nine years of experience as Assistant Professor in Academic Level 10 and above with experience in educational administration; OR comparable experience in research establishment/higher education institutions; OR 5 years of administrative experience as Assistant Registrar in Pay Level 10 or equivalent. (For Deputation: Officers holding analogous posts on regular basis or with five years regular service in pay level 11). One Deputy Registrar position requires specialization in Finance/Accounts, Audit and related fields.
Deputy LibrarianMaster's Degree in Library Science/Information Science/Documentation Science with at least 55% marks or equivalent grade, eight years experience as Assistant University Librarian/College Librarian, evidence of innovative library services including ICT integration, and a Ph.D. Degree in Library Science/Information Science/Documentation Science/Archives and Manuscript Keeping/Computerization of Library.
Assistant LibrarianMaster’s Degree in Library Science/Information Science/Documentation or equivalent professional degree with at least 55% marks, consistently good academic record with knowledge of library computerization, and must have cleared NET/SLET/SET or awarded Ph.D. in accordance with relevant regulations.
Assistant RegistrarMaster’s Degree with at least 55% of marks or its equivalent grade of ‘B’ in the UGC seven-point scale.
Assistant Manager (Publications)Master’s Degree in Social Sciences with at least 55% marks or equivalent grade ‘B’ in UGC seven-point scale with ten years' experience, minimum seven years' experience in publishing or public relations industry, proficiency in computerized administrative systems, good writing and social media skills, and well-versed with DTP programs like Coral Ventura, Coral Draw, Adobe Indesign, QuarkXpress, and online platforms.
System Analyst-cum-ProgrammerFirst class in M.E./M.Tech. (Computer Science & Technology/Information Technology) with two years of relevant experience; OR First class in Master in Computer Applications (MCA) with two years of relevant experience; OR First class M.Sc. (Computer Science/Information Technology) from recognized University/Institute with three years of relevant experience.
Health OfficerMBBS recognized by Medical Council of India, registered with MCI/State Medical Council, and three years of working experience in a hospital attached with a medical college/corporate hospital.
Field Work CoordinatorMaster’s Degree in Social Work with at least 55% of marks or its equivalent grade of ‘B’ in UGC seven-point scale, and 15 years' experience in field work.
CounsellorMasters in Social Work / Counselling / Applied Psychology with ten years of experience in counselling adolescent students, strong leadership, administrative, written and oral communication skills, and ability to plan and conduct training programs.
Section OfficerA Bachelor’s Degree in any stream from any recognised Institute/University, and three years of experience in supervisory level in Pay Level 6 in areas related to Administration/Establishment/Finance/Academic matters in a University or other Institutions of Higher Education/Research Establishment/PSU/Government organizations.
Section Officer (Security)A Bachelor’s Degree in any stream from any recognised Institute/University with five years experience as Security Supervisor/Supervisory position in Security in a Govt. office, Educational Institution, Private organisation of repute with annual turnover of Rs. 200/- crores and above; OR Person who has served in the Army or such Uniformed service at JCO level or equivalent or above with 10th standard pass or Army class I Examination or an equivalent examination AND holding a valid driving license (LMV/Motor Cycle).
