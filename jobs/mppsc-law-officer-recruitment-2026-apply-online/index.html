MPPSC Law Officer Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Madhya Pradesh Public Service Commission (Madhya Pradesh Lok Seva Aayog), Indore, has invited online applications for one permanent Law Officer (Vidhi Adhikari) post in the Commission, a Gazetted Class I post in the pay scale of Rs. 15600-39100 (Grade Pay 6600). Candidates need a law degree with at least 60% marks and five years of advocacy in Service Matters before the High Court. Applications are accepted online on www.mponline.gov.in and www.mppsc.mp.gov.in from 23 November 2026 to 22 December 2026. Who Can Apply

Applications are invited from citizens of India and from candidates of other categories recognised under the Constitution of India. The single vacancy is unreserved (UR). None of the posts is reserved for women or candidates with disabilities. Graduate degree in Law with a minimum of 60 percent marks from a recognised university. If the marksheet uses a grading system instead of marks, the candidate must submit an affidavit on the percentage value of the grade, supported by the relevant records of the university concerned. The affidavit must show the formula or principle used to calculate the percentage. The qualification, experience and all other eligibility conditions must be held by the online last date, 22 December 2026. A candidate who acquires them after that date will be treated as ineligible. Pay and Tenure

The Law Officer post is permanent (sthayi) and classed as Gazetted Class I, in the pay scale of Rs. 15600-39100 (Grade Pay 6600). Selected candidates will be appointed on a probation period of two years. The age of superannuation will follow the Madhya Pradesh Government Servants (Superannuation Age) Act, 1967, as amended from time to time.

MPPSC Law Officer Job Description

Legal responsibility related to court cases.

Selection and Interview

Selection for the MPPSC Law Officer post will be made on the basis of an interview. After scrutiny of documents, 12 candidates, in order of the marks obtained in the qualifying examination, and candidates with marks equal to the cut-off, will be called for interview, subject to eligible candidates being available. The interview carries 100 marks, and the minimum qualifying mark is 41. Final selection will be in order of merit of marks obtained in the interview. If more than 500 candidates apply, a written examination will be held. Candidates equal to three times the advertised posts in each category or sub-category, and those with equal marks, will then be called for interview. Final selection will be on the combined written and interview marks, and in that case there are no minimum qualifying marks for the interview. MPPSC Law Officer Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Fill in the online application from 23 November 2026 to 22 December 2026 on www.mponline.gov.in or www.mppsc.mp.gov.in, either at an authorised MPOnline kiosk or from home or an internet cafe.
  • 2Keep scanned files of a recent passport-size colour photograph (light or white background, no cap, hat or dark glasses, no selfie) and your signature ready for upload; kiosks scan them free of charge.
  • 3Submit the form, check the details in the popup window (Cancel to correct, OK to submit), then click "proceed for payment"; the form stays "Unpaid" until the fee is paid.
  • 4Pay the fee by credit card, debit card or internet banking, or in cash at the kiosk (take the receipt). Check that the printout shows the amount and "Payment Done", and keep the application number for all correspondence.
  • 5Download the Attestation Form, Personal Details Sheet and Attendance Sheet from the "Download Formate" link on www.mppsc.mp.gov.in, fill them in and make three, six and one photocopies respectively.
  • 6Sign the online application at the marked place and attach self-attested copies of all documents and one copy each of the three forms. Keep the remaining copies for the interview, where they must be shown with the original documents.
  • 7Put the set in a large envelope marked "Advertisement No. 14/2026 dated 04.11.2026, Law Officer, Application for the post of Madhya Pradesh Public Service Commission", with your name and address as sender, and send it by registered post or speed post to: Secretary, Madhya Pradesh Public Service Commission, Residency Area, Indore (Madhya Pradesh) PIN - 452001. An envelope without the advertisement number and post or department name will not be considered.
  • 8The application must reach the Commission office by 28 December 2026 at 6:00 PM. It may also be handed in at the office on working days between 10:00 AM and 6:00 PM, against a receipt. Keep one set of everything you send.
  • 9No bank draft or cheque is accepted. The fee is refunded online to the candidate's account only if the advertisement is cancelled, and refund claims for excess or wrong payment are not accepted. Apart from the application fee and portal fee, no other amount is payable; if a kiosk operator asks for more, complaints can be made to the MPOnline helpline 0755-6720200.

  • 12Candidates submitting with a late fee must first give an undertaking that they hold all qualifications and experience as per the advertisement. The late fee is not refunded if the candidature is later cancelled.
  • 13Applications received late without the late fee are rejected, and the online fee is not refunded.
