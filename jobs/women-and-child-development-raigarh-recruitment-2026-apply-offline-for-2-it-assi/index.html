About WCD Raigarh Recruitment 2026

The Government of India has started "Mission Shakti", an umbrella scheme for the safety, protection and empowerment of women under the integrated women empowerment programme. Under it, a State Hub for Empowerment of Women (SHEW) has been set up in the state and a District Hub for Empowerment of Women (DHEW) in every district. The two contract posts in Raigarh are for running this Hub for Empowerment of Women, and the eligibility table is given for the district-level hub (DHEW).

Terms of Appointment

Selection is initially on contract / consolidated pay for a maximum of 02 years. After that, the District Collector may extend the contract by 1 year each time, based on work ability, performance, conduct, the department's need and the controlling officer's recommendation; otherwise the appointment ends automatically. The appointment is fully temporary and may be ended without giving a reason; during the contract either side can end it with one month's notice or one month's pay instead. The posts last only as long as the Government of India runs the scheme; the department has no responsibility for the staff after the posts end.
