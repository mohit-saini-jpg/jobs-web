About CSL

Cochin Shipyard Limited describes itself in the notice as a listed premier Miniratna Schedule 'A' Company of the Government of India; its letterhead calls it a Government of India enterprise and a Miniratna company under the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways. Both posts in this notification are with its ship repair unit in Kolkata, CSL Kolkata Ship Repair Unit (CKSRU). That unit works on dry docks, berths, ship docking and afloat repairs.

Vacancy Details

The notice has 2 vacancies, both in grade PS1 and both unreserved (UR). CSL reserves the right to increase or decrease the number of vacancies, not to fill the vacancy, or to cancel the recruitment process, as per its requirement. Government of India directives on reservation apply. The posts have not been identified as suitable for PwBDs. The notice gives the reason: the work calls for frequent visits to work sites, shops, docks and ships under construction and repair. It also involves climbing vertical ladders, moving through narrow passages and manholes, and working in confined spaces and at heights.

Selection and Interview

The selection for both posts has these stages: Phase I offline test (Objective Type – 40 Marks and Descriptive Type – 40 Marks), then Certificate Verification, then Phase II PowerPoint presentation on work experience (20 Marks). The process will be conducted at CKSRU, Kolkata, or through interview via electronic media. The objective test lasts 45 minutes. It has Part A (General) and Part B (Discipline related). Each question carries one mark, and there is no negative marking. The descriptive offline test is worth 40 marks and lasts 90 minutes, with discipline related questions. CSL will evaluate the descriptive answer sheets only of candidates who reach the minimum pass mark in the objective test and meet the eligibility requirements.
