How to Check MPESB ADDET Result 2026

Follow these steps to check and download your MPESB ADDET Result 2026:

  • Visit the official MPESB website: esb.mp.gov.in
  • Click on the "MPESB ADDET Result 2026" link on the homepage.
  • Enter your Enrollment Number, Registration Number, or Date of Birth as required.
  • Enter the security captcha/TAC code if prompted.
  • Click the Login/Submit button.
  • Your MPESB ADDET Result 2026 will be displayed on the screen.
  • Download the result and take a printout for future reference (required for counseling and document verification).

MPESB ADDET Important Instructions

  • Keep your Enrollment Number, Registration Number, and Date of Birth handy before checking the result.
  • Download and save a copy of your result for future reference.
  • Candidates must carry a printed admit card along with valid original photo ID at the examination centre - entry is not allowed without these.
  • For any discrepancy in the result, contact the MPESB official helpline or check the official website for correction/grievance procedures.
