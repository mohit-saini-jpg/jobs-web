UKPSC Selection Process 2026

The selection process comprises three consecutive stages: Preliminary Examination: Objective-type screening test consisting of General Studies and General Aptitude Test. Main Examination: Written descriptive-type examination comprising four papers. Personality Test (Interview): Viva-voce test for candidates provisionally successful in the main written examination.

UKPSC Exam Pattern 2026

Negative Marking: One-fourth (1/4th) of the marks assigned to that question will be deducted for each incorrect answer or multiple answers. Prelims marks are not added for final merit determination. Qualifying Marks Note: Candidates must obtain a minimum of 35% marks in General Hindi (Paper-I).

UKPSC Lower PCS Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Visit the official UKPSC website at psc.uk.gov.in or pscuk.net.in.
  • 2Navigate to the Menubar and click on "How to Apply", view Advertisement Details, Important Dates, and Instructions, then click "Apply Now".
  • 3Fill in correct basic information, generate a password, and click Submit to view Basic Information.
  • 4Verify all details, tick the declaration box ("I have verified all the details entered by me in the registration form and wish to submit the same"), and submit.
  • 5Note primary registration confirmation and receive login details via SMS and email.
  • 6Log in, fill in High School, Intermediate, Graduate, and other educational details, add them to the grid, fill other details, and continue.
  • 7Upload scanned passport-size photograph and signature as per instructions.
  • 8Tick the declaration ("I hereby declare that the photograph & signature are correct and accurate representation of myself") and continue to review data.
  • 9Check all details carefully, tick the final declaration, and complete the online application process by clicking the Final Button, then print the application.
  • 10Pay the application fee through Net Banking, Debit Card, Credit Card, or UPI by 26 November 2026 (11:59:59 PM).
