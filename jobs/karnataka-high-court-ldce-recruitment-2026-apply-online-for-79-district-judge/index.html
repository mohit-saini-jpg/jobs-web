Karnataka HC District Judge (LDCE) Vacancy 2026

79 District Judge Posts (25% LDCE Category) The notification does not provide a category-wise (SC/ST/OBC) reservation breakup for these 79 posts – selection is stated to be strictly on the basis of merit. The number of posts is expressly subject to alteration depending on the outcome of Writ Petition (Civil) No. 1022/1989 (All India Judges' Association & others v. Union of India & others), pending before the Hon'ble Supreme Court.

Karnataka HC LDCE 2026 Eligibility

Applications are invited only from Senior Civil Judges who, as on the date of this notification (28.08.2026), satisfy all of the following conditions: Currently serving as a Senior Civil Judge , having put in not less than 3 years of service as a Senior Civil Judge . Completed a minimum of 7 years of total service in the combined cadre of Civil Judge & Senior Civil Judge. Attained the age of 35 years . Important: This LDCE is open only to eligible serving Senior Civil Judges of the Karnataka Judicial Service – it is not a recruitment for the general public or for candidates outside the judicial service.

Karnataka HC LDCE 2026 Selection Process

Selection is through the Limited Departmental Competitive Examination (LDCE) , consisting of a Written Examination followed by a Viva-voce , strictly on the basis of merit. The procedure is as laid down under Rule 4-B of the Karnataka Judicial Service (Recruitment) (Amendment) Rules, 2025; applicants are advised to read these rules carefully. The list of applicants found eligible for the Written Examination and Viva-voce will be announced on the High Court of Karnataka's official website. Eligible applicants will be intimated through SMS and e-mail, and must download their Admission Card/Call Letter from the High Court website. Applicants must appear for the Written Examination, and for the Viva-voce if called, at their own expense, at the place and time decided and notified by the High Court of Karnataka.
