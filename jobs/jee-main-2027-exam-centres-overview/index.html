How to Select JEE Main 2027 Exam City Preferences?

Candidates can choose examination-city preferences during the JEE Main online application process.

  • Visit the official website at jeemain.nta.nic.in.
  • Open the JEE Main 2027 application facility once activated.
  • Complete the required registration and personal details.
  • Access the examination-city preference section.
  • Review the cities available for selection under the application rules.
  • Choose the permitted cities in order of preference.
  • Review the selected preferences before final submission.
  • Complete the remaining application and fee-payment steps.

How to Check JEE Main 2027 Allotted Exam Centre?

Candidates will be able to check their allotted examination venue after NTA releases the admit cards for the relevant session.

  • Visit jeemain.nta.nic.in.
  • Open the JEE Main 2027 admit card link when available.
  • Log in using the credentials requested by the official portal.
  • Download the admit card for the applicable session.
  • Check the examination city, centre name and complete address.
  • Verify the examination date, shift and reporting instructions.
  • Save and print the admit card for examination-day use.

JEE Main 2027 Examination Centre Guidelines

Candidates must follow the instructions provided by NTA on the official admit card and examination notices.

  • Check the exact venue address and reporting time before the examination.
  • Reach the examination centre before the reporting deadline printed on the admit card.
  • Carry a printed admit card and the prescribed photo identification.
  • Carry additional photographs or declarations only as required in the official instructions.
  • Do not bring prohibited electronic devices, calculators or unauthorised study materials.
  • Follow the security screening and identity-verification procedures.
  • Check the permitted stationery and dress-related instructions before examination day.
  • Preserve the admit card for subsequent admission processes if required.

Important Instructions for JEE Main 2027 Candidates

  • Use the state-wise city list only as an advance reference until NTA issues the official 2027 list.
  • Select examination-city preferences carefully during online registration.
  • Do not assume that the first preferred city will be allotted.
  • Check the advance city intimation slip when released.
  • Download the admit card separately to obtain the exact venue.
  • Make travel arrangements based on the final allotment details.
  • Follow the examination reporting time and security rules printed on the admit card.
