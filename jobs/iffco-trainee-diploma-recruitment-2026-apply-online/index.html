IFFCO Trainee- Diploma Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

IFFCO Trainee- Diploma (Chemical) is a one-year training post at the Phulpur Unit of Indian Farmers Fertiliser Cooperative Limited (IFFCO), Prayagraj, on a monthly consolidated stipend of Rs 31,000 plus applicable allowances as per rules. Eligible Diploma (Chemical) holders who have completed an apprenticeship can apply online at careers.iffco-recruitments.in until 02.12.2026, under advertisement PA/02/Estt/Rect/2026 dated 30/10/2026.

About IFFCO Phulpur Unit

The notice is issued by the Phulpur Unit of Indian Farmers Fertiliser Cooperative Limited, located at P.O. Ghiyanagar, Phulpur, Prayagraj, Uttar Pradesh. It is signed by the Dy. General Manager (Personnel & Administration), IFFCO – Phulpur Unit. The place of posting is IFFCO Phulpur Unit.

Who Can Apply

Full-time regular Diploma (Chemical) from AICTE recognized institutions, with minimum 60% marks for General/OBC candidates and minimum 55% marks for SC/ST candidates. Apprenticeship done in the relevant trade, preferably from any Chemical/Fertiliser Plant. A certificate of completion of apprenticeship issued by the Apprenticeship Board (BOAT). Candidates admitted directly to the second year of Diploma through a recognized lateral entry scheme (Class XII Science/ITI) are also eligible, subject to the prescribed minimum percentage of marks based on the aggregate of four semesters of the Diploma course. The age limit is Minimum 18 years and Maximum 27 Years as on 01.11.2026. Candidates holding a qualification acquired through part-time / correspondence / distance education mode are not eligible. Stipend and Training Period

The training period is one year, on a monthly consolidated stipend of Rs 31,000/-pm plus applicable allowances as per rules. After successful completion of training, suitable candidates may be absorbed in the regular pay scale of Rs. 34000-64000 along with other allowances as per rules of IFFCO. At the time of joining, trainees have to furnish a bond to serve IFFCO for a minimum period of three years after the training period. The bond is Rs. 25,000/- for General/OBC candidates and Rs.6500/- for SC/ST candidates. The place of posting is IFFCO Phulpur Unit. During the training period, the candidate may be posted or deployed at any of the Plants of IFFCO for training, as per requirement of Management.

IFFCO Trainee- Diploma Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

The selection stages, in order, are Online Test, Interview, Document Verification and Medical Examination. The exact date, time and venue of the online examination and the admit card will be informed through Email/SMS. The notice does not give the exam pattern, syllabus or marks.
