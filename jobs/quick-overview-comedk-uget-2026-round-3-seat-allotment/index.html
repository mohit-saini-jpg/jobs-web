Quick Overview - COMEDK UGET 2026 Round 3 Seat Allotment : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 19/08/2026

COMEDK UGET 2026 Round 3 seat allotment result has been declared by the Consortium of Medical, Engineering and Dental Colleges of Karnataka (COMEDK) on 18 August 2026 at 4 PM on the official website, comedk.org. Candidates who took part in Round 3 choice filling can log in with their application number and password to check their allotted engineering college and branch, download the allotment letter, and complete decision-making (Accept/Freeze) and fee payment within the schedule below.

How to Check COMEDK UGET 2026 Round 3 Seat Allotment Result

  • Visit the official COMEDK website, comedk.org.
  • Click on "Login" > "Engineering Login" on the homepage.
  • Enter your application number and password to log in.
  • Navigate to the Round 3 seat allotment/result section on your dashboard.
  • View your allotted college, engineering branch, and category details.
  • Download and save the allotment letter for reference and college reporting.

Options After Round 3 Allotment: Accept, Freeze, or Cancel

Candidates allotted a seat in Round 3 must exercise one of the following options within the decision-making window (18 August, 4 PM to 21 August, 4 PM):

  • Accept and Freeze: Confirms the allotted seat as final; the candidate must report to the college and pay fees within the reporting deadline. Only candidates who choose Accept & Freeze should report to college, strictly as per the schedule, or they may lose the allotted seat.
  • Seat cancellation: Candidates who were allotted a seat in Round 1 or Round 2 (KKR) also have the option to cancel their seat during the Round 3 cancellation window (18 August, 4 PM to 24 August, 3 PM).

Candidates planning to report to college in person are advised to make travel arrangements in advance so they can reach before the last reporting date.

Who Was Eligible for COMEDK UGET 2026 Round 3 Counselling

  • Candidates who appeared for COMEDK UGET 2026 and are on the official merit/rank list.
  • Candidates who completed counselling registration and document verification.
  • Candidates who submitted their college and branch preferences during the Round 3 choice filling window (14–16 August).
  • Candidates who selected "Upgrade" after Round 1 or Round 2 remain eligible for consideration in Round 3, retaining their earlier seat as a fallback until a new one is confirmed.
  • Candidates who chose "Freeze" in an earlier round are not part of further rounds, as their admission is already finalised.

What Happens After COMEDK Round 3: Is There a Round 4?

COMEDK UGET counselling for engineering admissions is conducted in multiple rounds. Candidates who do not accept a seat, or whose seat remains vacant after Round 3, may be considered in the subsequent round, subject to seat availability. For the exact schedule and eligibility conditions of any round after Round 3, candidates should check the official notifications section on comedk.org, since this had not been separately confirmed at the time of writing.

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Only candidates who select "Accept & Freeze" should report to the allotted college, strictly within the reporting window, or they risk losing the seat.
  • Reporting timings on Saturdays and public holidays may differ by college - confirm directly with the institution.
  • Keep the allotment letter, fee receipt, and all original documents ready before travelling to the college.
  • Candidates who accepted a seat in Round 1 or Round 2 and wish to cancel it must do so within the Round 3 cancellation window.
  • www.comedk.org is the only official COMEDK website - COMEDK has stated it is not responsible for information on any other website.
