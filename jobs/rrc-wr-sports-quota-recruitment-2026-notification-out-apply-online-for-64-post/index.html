Western Railway Sports Quota Selection Procedure

All eligible candidates are called for a Selection Trial. Only candidates securing 25 or more out of 40 marks for game skill, physical fitness, and coach's observations are declared FIT and assessed further by the Recruitment Committee. Candidates declared NOT FIT are not assessed further. The offer of appointment is purely on merit. If two or more sportspersons score the same marks, preference is given to the younger candidate. No TA/DA/accommodation is provided for the trial; candidates must bring their own kit and four passport-size photographs, and may need to stay 2-3 days at their own arrangement.

RRC WR Sports Quota Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many posts are available under Western Railway Sports Quota 2026?
A
A total of 64 posts - 21 in Level 5/4 & 3/2 (Group 'C') and 43 in Level 1 (erstwhile Group 'D') - are notified under Employment Notice No. RRC/WR/01/2026.
Q2What is the last date to apply?
A
Online applications close on 30 September 2026 at 18:00 Hrs.
Q3What is the age limit?
A
Minimum 18 years, maximum 25 years as on 01-01-2027 (born between 01-01-2002 and 01-01-2009).
Q4Is there reservation for SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PwBD in this recruitment?
A
No, there is no reservation for SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PwBD & Ex-Servicemen in this Sports Quota drive.
Q5What is the application fee?
A
₹500 (₹400 refundable) for general candidates; ₹250 (fully refundable) for SC/ST/Ex-Servicemen/PwD/Women/Minorities/EBC candidates, refundable to those found eligible who appear in the trial.
Q6What is the selection process?
A
A Selection Trial assessing game skill, physical fitness, and coach's observation (40 marks), sports achievements (50 marks), and educational qualification (10 marks), with level-wise minimum qualifying marks of 60-70 out of 100.
Q7Can I apply for more than one discipline or pay level?
A
Yes, but a separate application with separate fee is required for each discipline and each pay level.
