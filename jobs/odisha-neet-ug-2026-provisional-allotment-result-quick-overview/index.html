How to Check Odisha NEET UG 2026 Round 1 Provisional Allotment Result

  • Visit the official OJEE portal at odishajee.com or ojee.nic.in.
  • Look for the link related to "MBBS/BDS Counselling 2026: Round 1 Provisional Seat Allotment."
  • Either open the published PDF list directly, or log in using your Application Number/NEET Roll Number and Password.
  • Use the Ctrl + F search function (if viewing a PDF) or the search field on the login dashboard to locate your Application Number or Roll Number.
  • Check the allotted institute, course (MBBS/BDS), and quota category against your entry.
  • Download and save the provisional allotment PDF or summary sheet for reference during the query/objection window.

Important Instructions

  • The Round 1 result released on August 20 is a provisional list meant for checking discrepancies - it is not the final allotment letter, and physical reporting is not required on this date.
  • Formal, individually downloadable Provisional Allotment Letters (with reporting instructions) will be available on candidate dashboards only after the final allotment is published on August 22 at 11:30 AM.
  • Any errors related to category, sub-quota, or merit position noticed in the provisional list can be raised through the online query/objection window opening August 21 at 2:00 PM.
  • Choice-filling preferences were locked on August 18 at 5:00 PM and cannot be edited before the provisional result.
  • During physical reporting (August 24- 29), candidates can opt for the "Float" option after paying the admission fee - this retains the Round 1 allotted seat while allowing an upgrade attempt in Round 2.
  • Candidates allotted a BDS seat in Round 1 can still participate in Round 2 for a possible MBBS upgrade.
  • The compulsory rural service bond applies specifically to candidates allotted seats in Government Medical and Dental Colleges; private college seats follow institute-specific fee and bond terms.
  • Odisha state-quota reporting is centralised - all allotted candidates report physically to the OJEE Cell, OJEE Campus, Gandamunda, Bhubaneswar, rather than to individual colleges.
  • Candidates who remain unallotted in Round 1 do not need to register again for Round 2; only candidates who missed Round 1 registration entirely must register fresh.
