MCF Hassan Apprentice Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must meet the following essential requirements to be considered eligible. Only Indian nationals are eligible to apply. Only candidates who passed their qualifying examination as regular students are eligible. Candidates who completed their Degree/Diploma during 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, or 2026 are eligible, provided they join within 5 years of acquiring the degree (calculated from the month and year of passing as stated on the degree certificate). Candidates who passed before 2022 are not eligible. If this 5-year window lapses by the time of offer, the candidate will not be offered training even if empanelled or waitlisted. Ex-apprentices, or candidates currently undergoing apprenticeship, or those with one year or more of job experience in the government/public/private sector as on the date of joining, are not eligible. MCF Hassan Apprentice Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

All applications received will be screened, and selection panels will be prepared based on the candidates' academic scores at Degree/Diploma level. Induction of apprentices against vacant training positions will be made strictly based on their position in the merit panel. Only selected candidates will be informed through an offer letter sent by email. Only shortlisted/empanelled candidates will be called for document verification, subject to the availability of vacant apprenticeship positions. Candidates shortlisted for document verification are not eligible for Travelling Allowance.
