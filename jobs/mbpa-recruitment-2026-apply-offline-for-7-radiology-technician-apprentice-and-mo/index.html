MbPA Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Mumbai Port Authority (MbPA), Medical Department, has 7 apprenticeship seats for the year 2026-27 under the Apprentices Act, 1961: 3 for Medico Laboratory Technician Apprentice and 4 for Radiology Technician Apprentice, each with 12 months of training and a stipend of Rs.10,900/- or Rs.12,300/- per month. Applications are offline only, and the filled form must reach the Establishment section, Medical Department, Mumbai Port Authority Hospital, Wadala (East), Mumbai – 400037 by hand or by post on or before 18 December 2026, up to 5:00 pm.

About MbPA

Mumbai Port Authority (MbPA) is the port authority in Mumbai, and this apprenticeship notice is issued by its Medical Department. Applications are handled by the Establishment section of the Medical Department at the Mumbai Port Authority Hospital, Wadala (East), Mumbai. The notice states that all apprentices will be given practical training at the respective faculties of MbPA.

MbPA Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection for the MbPA apprenticeship is through a merit list, followed by verification of original documents and the selection procedure, and a physical fitness test for selected candidates. The merit list will be prepared based on the percentage of total marks obtained in the respective faculty. Preference will be given to candidates with a higher qualification in addition to the minimum educational qualification, in descending order: Post Graduate, Graduate, 3 Years Diploma. If two candidates have secured the same marks, the elder candidate will be placed above in the merit list. Preference will be given to wards of MbPA employees, and separate merit lists will be prepared for wards of MbPA employees and other candidates. The merit list(s) will be displayed on the notice board at the office of the Establishment section, Medical Department, Mumbai Port Authority Hospital, Wadala (East)

MbPA Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

After selection, the candidate must enter into a Contract of Apprenticeship in the prescribed form under the Apprentices Act, 1961, and final selection is subject to registration of the contract with the BOAT (WR). An apprentice who abandons the training before completion will be liable to pay the cost of training (maximum for an amount of three month's stipend) or an amount decided by the BOAT (WR); legal action will be initiated if this is not paid. MbPA has no obligation to offer employment to apprentices during or after the apprenticeship, and apprentices cannot claim employment in MbPA on completing the apprenticeship. Apprentices must observe all conduct rules applicable to workmen in the Port Authority; any dispute arising out of the contract will be referred to the BOAT (WR).
