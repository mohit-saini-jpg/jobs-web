Bank of India Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Educational qualification is broadly B.E./B.Tech/MCA/M.Sc (Computer Science or Information Technology) for the IT and digital-banking posts, LLB for Law Officer, a recognised Fire/Safety Engineering degree for Fire Officer, and a B.E./B.Tech in Civil Engineering for Civil Engineer. Post-qualification work experience requirements range from 1 year (Scale I) to a minimum of 8 years with domain-specific experience (Scale IV). Each of the 48 posts also carries its own mandatory/preferred professional certifications (examples: OCP Java, CISA/CISM/CISSP, PMP, TOGAF, AWS/Azure certifications, CKA, ITIL) - these are post-specific and detailed in full in Annexure-I to the Notice.

BOI Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Online Examination: English Language (25 marks, qualifying only) + Professional Knowledge relevant to the post (100 marks), composite duration 100 minutes Minimum qualifying marks: 35% for General/EWS (30% for SC/ST/OBC/PWD) Personal Interview: Maximum 100 marks; qualifying 50% for General/EWS, 45% for SC/ST/OBC/PWD; candidates called at approximately 1:3 ratio to vacancies Final weightage: Online Examination and Interview combined at 60:40 Bank may, at its discretion, select solely through Interview without an online exam
