Naval Dockyard Karwar Trade Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Indian Navy has invited applications for 210 apprenticeship seats for the training year 2027–28/29: 180 at Naval Dockyard, Naval Base, Karwar (Karnataka) across 27 trades and 30 at Naval Aircraft Yard (Goa), Dabolim across 13 trades. ITI certificate holders receive a stipend of Rs. 9,600 per month; three non-ITI trades are open to 10th pass candidates and one (Rigger) to 8th pass, on lower stipend slabs. There is no upper age limit and no fee.

Eligibility Criteria

Where the school board awarded grades or CGPA, the equivalent percentage as per that board's norms must be stated; without it the candidature is not considered. Candidates with a grade-point system must get the conversion done by the appropriate authority before applying. There is no upper age limit (MSDE O.M. dated 20 December 2021). The minimum age is reckoned on the training commencement date of 12 April 2027 : 14 years for non-hazardous trades (born on or before 12 April 2013 ) and 18 years for hazardous trades (born on or before 12 April 2009 ). The notice adds a caution: the Recruitment Rules for Tradesman Skilled posts in naval establishments do carry an upper age limit, which will apply to ex-apprentices of the Dockyard Apprentice School who later apply for those posts. Naval Dockyard Karwar Trade Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Valid applications are placed in a trade- and category-wise preliminary merit list. For ITI trades the ranking is on the aggregate percentage of marks in ITI and 10th ; for non-ITI trades on the percentage in 8th (Rigger) or 10th (Crane Operator Overhead, Forger & Heat Treater). Ties are broken by age (older first), then alphabetical order of first, middle and surname. Candidates are called in the ratio of 1:20 for every vacancy in each trade and category ; the notice's own example is 120 candidates for 6 UR Electrician seats. E-admit cards are sent by email only. The exam is at Karwar in January 2027 . OMR-based multiple-choice paper, one mark per question, two hours , bilingual (English and Hindi). No negative marking. Naval Dockyard Karwar Trade Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Application has two parts: online registration on the national apprenticeship portal, then a printed profile sent by post. Both are required.
  • 2Before you start, make sure your name, date of birth and father's name on your Aadhaar card match your 10th certificate (8th certificate for Rigger). If they differ, correct Aadhaar first; mismatches are a listed ground for rejection.
  • 3Open apprenticeshipindia.gov.in, choose Candidate Login/Register, fill in personal and contact details and submit. Activate your profile from the email you receive.
  • 4Log in with your registered email; an OTP goes to your mobile.
  • 5Complete the profile: date of birth (upload only the 10th mark sheet or a birth certificate from a competent authority as proof), father's and mother's names, address, educational qualifications (10th and ITI only), trade preference, bank account details, and validate your Aadhaar number.
  • 6Open Apprenticeship Opportunities from the top-right dropdown, enter "Naval Dockyard, Karwar" under Search by Establishment Name, click Apply against your eligible trade, select "Uttara Kannada", and click OK.
  • 7Download and print the Candidate Profile from the portal.
  • 8Attach self-attested copies of: 10th mark sheet (8th for Rigger); ITI mark sheets, semester-wise and consolidated; category certificate (OBC/SC/ST); disability certificate (PwBD); Aadhaar card.
  • 9Add the certificate of undertaking in the notice's proforma, stating whether you are applying for Naval Dockyard, Karwar or Naval Aircraft Yard (Goa), Dabolim. Tick one and strike out the other; sign with date and place.
  • 10Send by speed post or registered post to: The Officer-in-Charge, Dockyard Apprentice School, Naval Dockyard, Naval Base, Karwar, Karnataka – 581 308
  • 11It must reach within 30 days of the 10 October 2026 publication. Allow for transit time; post by the first days of November at the latest.
  • 12One application only. Double or multiple applications are rejected, and the establishment choice is made once in the undertaking. Separate merit lists are drawn for Karwar and Goa.
  • 13Apply only for a trade your ITI certificate matches; irrelevance between the applied trade and the ITI trade is a listed rejection ground.
  • 14No money is payable to anyone. Report any tout or agent demanding payment to [email protected] with proof; the notice promises confidentiality.
  • 15Give a valid mobile number and email; all notices, the admit card and the joining letter come only by email.
