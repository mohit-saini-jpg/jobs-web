Panchayati Raj Ghazipur Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

The notice states that the whole selection will be conducted under the provisions of Government Order No. 42/2021/1235/33-3-2021-989 dated 25.07.2021. The stages are: The District Panchayat Raj Officer's office and the Block Development Office pass the applications they receive to the concerned Gram Panchayat. The Gram Panchayat prepares a merit list (shreshtata suchi) of the applications received. The merit list is placed before the Administrative Committee of the Gram Panchayat for consideration. The merit list approved by that committee goes to the member secretary of the district level committee (the District Panchayat Raj Officer). The committee formed under the chairmanship of the District Magistrate examines it and makes its recommendation. The Gram Panchayat issues the contract letter.
