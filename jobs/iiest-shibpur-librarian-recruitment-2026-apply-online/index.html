IIEST Shibpur Librarian (1 Posts) Preparation Tips

1. Library automation software (Koha, LIBSYS) ka basic knowledge revise karein. 2. Bibliographic classification (Dewey, AACR2) aur digital archiving concepts par focus karein. 3. Interview me communication skills aur problem‑solving examples ready rakhein.

IIEST Shibpur Librarian (1 Posts) Job Profile

Librarian ka kaam books cataloging, digital resource management, user assistance aur library policies maintain karna hota hai. Day‑to‑day tasks me acquisition, circulation, aur training sessions shamil hain, jo career me higher administrative roles tak le ja sakte hain.

IIEST Shibpur Librarian (1 Posts) Selection Strategy
