ESIC Job Description

Senior Resident engaged at ESIC Medical College & Hospital may be assigned duties such as attending emergency, floor duty and other clinical duty as assigned by the Competent Authority, and performing duties in other departments as assigned by the competent authority.

Pay and Terms of Engagement

Tenure of 3 years subject to satisfactory annual performance evaluation; no extension beyond 3 years Contractual agreement on a stamp paper of Rs.100/- Security deposit of Rs. 30,000/- by DD at joining, forfeited if the candidate leaves within one year Contract can be ended with one month's notice or one month's emoluments No right or preference for regular appointment; no PF, pension or other service benefits

How Will ESIC Select Candidates?

Scrutiny of applications and document verification Screening test (MCQ) if turnout exceeds 1:10 per post Walk-in interview before the Selection Board Applications scrutinised by the scrutiny committee; only eligible candidates attend the interview If turnout exceeds 1:10 per post, a screening test of multiple choice questions in the concerned discipline may be held Top five from the screening test will be called for the interview Screening test marks are not linked to final selection Selection solely on performance in the interview before the Selection Board

Documents to Enclose

Identity proof and address proof Date of birth certificate (10th Certificate/Birth Certificate) and Class 10th & 12th mark sheets MBBS, MD/MS/DNB and experience certificates as applicable; internship completion certificate Registration with NMC / State Medical Council Reservation category certificate in Government of India format; NOC if applicable Two recent passport size photographs and Demand Draft for Rs.500/-

Other Points to Note

Candidates may apply for only one post, except Emergency Medicine, Physical Medicine & Rehabilitation, ICU and super-speciality departments No candidate is entertained for document verification after 11.00 AM Change in interview date, if any, will be intimated on the website only No TA/DA for interview or joining Candidates already working in the institution must submit prior intimation and not attend the interview while on duty Result will be published on www.esic.gov.in/recruitments

ESIC Chennai Senior Resident Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Fill the prescribed application form (Annexure C) attached to the notice; other formats or incomplete forms are rejected.
  • 2Paste a self-attested passport size photograph on the form.
  • 3Get a Demand Draft for Rs. 500/- in favour of 'ESI Fund Account No.1' payable at Chennai, if applicable, issued after the date of the advertisement.
  • 4Report for document verification on 17.11.2026 between 09.00 AM and 11.00 AM with the filled form, DD, original certificates and one set of self-attested photocopies.
  • 5Candidates whose documents are verified attend the walk-in interview.
  • 6A separate form and DD are needed for each additional department where more than one is allowed.
