NPCC Selection Process 2026

As per the notification, the selection procedure will be decided by NPCC depending on the response received against the above posts. The selection criteria may be by way of a Personal Interview or by any other appropriate method, at the discretion of NPCC. The final selection will be based on performance in the interview (or the alternate method used), after which the "Offer of Appointment" will be issued. The selected candidate will be on probation for one year. The notification does not specify a separate written exam or skill test stage.
