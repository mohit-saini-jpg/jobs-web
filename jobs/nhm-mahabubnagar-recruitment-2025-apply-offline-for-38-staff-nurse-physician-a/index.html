NHM Mahabubnagar Recruitment 2026 Notification PDF

The District Selection Committee, Mahabubnagar, functioning under the Office of the District Medical & Health Officer (DM&HO), Mahabubnagar District, Telangana, has released NHM Mahabubnagar Recruitment 2026 Notification No. 02/2026 for recruitment to 9 different post categories under the National Health Mission (NHM) programme. A total of 38 vacancies have been announced, including 15 vacancies for MLHP, 10 vacancies for Staff Nurse and 7 vacancies for Lab Technician, while the remaining posts have 1 vacancy each. The applications are invited through offline mode, and eligible candidates can submit their applications from 22 September 2026 to 26 September 2026 up to 05:00 PM. NHM Mahabubnagar Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection is made by the District Selection Committee (DSC) on the following basis: Selection is done on merit, based on aggregate marks obtained in the qualifying examination, as per educational guidelines. The Rule of Reservation is followed as per Government Orders in force from time to time. Presidential Order 2018 is followed. A roster is followed for posts with more than one vacancy; the existing roster is continued. Total marks are 100: 90 marks for marks obtained in the qualifying examination, and 10 marks for age (0.5 mark per completed year of age from 18 years, up to a maximum of 10 marks). A merit list is prepared and displayed on the official website of the District Collector, for transparency and for inviting objections.
