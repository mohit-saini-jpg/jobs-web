Desirable: experience of working in a school, knowledge of computer applications, and working knowledge of English (and Kannada for the TGT Kannada post).
Craft InstructorEssential: Senior Secondary/Intermediate with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks, and a Degree or Diploma in the craft field from a recognized university/institution. Desirable: ability to teach and communicate fluently in both Hindi and English, and basic knowledge of computer applications for school-related tasks.
Nursing Assistant (Male only)Three-year Diploma in General Nursing or B.Sc. in Nursing from a recognized institution with valid Registration, along with 5 years' experience or ex-servicemen of Medical Assistant trade with at least 5 years' service after training. Proficiency in Hindi and English languages is required.
General Employee (MTS)10th Pass from a recognized Board. Multi-skill knowledge is preferred, such as Bakery, Plumbing, Carpentry, Horse Care, Housekeeping, and office equipment handling; experience in a Boarding School is preferred.
