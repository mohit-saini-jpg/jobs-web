BHEL Project Engineer Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must hold a Full-Time Degree in Electronics / Electrical / Instrumentation / Computer Science with at least 60% marks (50% for SC/ST candidates) or equivalent CGPA in aggregate of all years/semesters from a recognized University/Institution. In addition, candidates must have a minimum of 12 months of relevant post-qualification work experience, exclusive of any Trainee/Apprenticeship period. The notification lists the following essential skill areas: Basic knowledge of Control Systems Familiarity with various communication protocols Testing & commissioning of Control Systems Minimum 12 months of experience in Erection & Commissioning supervision Ability to read Drawings, Electrical Schematics, Wiring Charts, and Hook-up Drawings Ability to handle test instruments such as Calibrator, Analog & Digital Multimeter Willingness to work on-board Naval ships in staggered shifts, Pan-I

BHEL Project Engineer Application Fee

A non-refundable processing fee of Rs. 236/- is payable by General, OBC, and EWS candidates. SC/ST candidates and Persons with Disability are exempted from paying the processing fee. The fee must be paid online through SBI Collect, using the link provided on the official website. Payment through Demand Draft, Money Order, Postal Order, Cheque, or any other offline mode is not accepted.

BHEL Project Engineer Selection Process

The selection process for BHEL Project Engineer Recruitment 2026 is as follows: If the number of eligible applicants for a post is up to 1:10 of the number of vacancies, Personal Interviews will be conducted for all eligible candidates. If the number of eligible applicants exceeds the 1:10 ratio, the number of candidates called for Personal Interview will be restricted based on the aggregate marks obtained in the qualifying examination (Degree). The engagement is purely on a Fixed Tenure Basis and is not against any permanent vacancy. It does not entitle any candidate to claim regular or permanent employment with BHEL in the future.
