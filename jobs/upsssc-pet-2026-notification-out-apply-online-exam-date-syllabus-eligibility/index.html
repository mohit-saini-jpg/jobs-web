UPSSSC PET 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Candidates who hold High School (10th) or an equivalent qualification are eligible to apply for PET-2026. All candidates who have obtained this qualification before the last date of application submission may apply.

UPSSSC PET 2026 Age Limit

Candidates applying for PET-2026 must have completed a minimum of 18 years of age and must not have exceeded 40 years of age as on 01 July 2026 .

UPSSSC PET 2026 Exam Pattern

PET-2026 will consist of 100 objective-type multiple-choice questions, carrying 100 marks, to be answered in a duration of 2 hours. There is negative marking of ¼ mark for each wrong answer. The difficulty level of academic subjects (Sl. No. 1 to 9 below) will correspond to the Secondary/Senior Secondary standard prescribed by NCERT.

UPSSSC PET 2026 Score Validity & Normalization

If the number of applications received for PET-2026 is very large and it is not convenient to conduct the exam for all candidates in a single shift/day, the exam may be conducted across multiple shifts/days. In such cases, a score normalization process will be applied for comparative evaluation.

UPSSSC PET 2026 Application Correction

After final submission, candidates can modify permissible details in their application (such as email, Intermediate qualification, marital status, EWS status, UP state employee status, freedom fighter dependent status, ex-serviceman status, outstanding sportsperson status, disability category, and additional age relaxation details) via the "Modify Submitted Application" option on the UPSSSC website homepage, using OTP verification. This correction window is open from 10.08.2026 to 08.09.2026 . Name, Date of Birth, and Mobile Number entered via OTR cannot be changed once the PET-2026 registration number has been generated. No correction requests will be entertained after the correction deadline.

UPSSSC PET 2026 Important Instructions

Reserved-category candidates must indicate their category correctly in the application; the Commission's decision on eligibility is final. The date of birth recorded on the High School or equivalent certificate will be the only valid proof of age; no other document will be accepted for this purpose. Candidates may be asked to submit self-attested copies of mark sheets/certificates/degrees to confirm age and educational qualification. Exam date, time, district, and centre details will be communicated via admit card with a roll number; no change in exam centre, date, or time will be permitted under any circumstances. Submitting incorrect information that cannot be verified through certificates may lead to debarment from the PET as well as all other Commission examinations and selections. UPSSSC PET 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is UPSSSC PET 2026?
A
UPSSSC PET 2026 (Preliminary Eligibility Test) is a qualifying screening exam released via Advertisement No. 16-Exam/2026, dated 01.08.2026. Under UPSSSC's two-tier selection system, candidates must clear PET to be eligible to apply for Main Examinations for various Group 'C' posts under the Commission.
Q2What is the last date to apply for UPSSSC PET 2026?
A
The last date for online registration, fee payment, and application submission is 01.09.2026. Application correction can be done until 08.09.2026.
Q3What is the eligibility for UPSSSC PET 2026?
A
Candidates must hold a High School (10th) or equivalent qualification, obtained before the last date of application submission, to be eligible for PET-2026.
Q4What is the age limit for UPSSSC PET 2026?
A
Candidates must be between 18 and 40 years of age as on 01.07.2026, with age relaxation applicable for SC/ST/OBC, sportspersons, Ex-Servicemen, PwBD, and UP state government employees as per government rules.
Q5What is the application fee for UPSSSC PET 2026?
A
The total application fee is Rs. 185 for UR/OBC candidates, Rs. 95 for SC/ST candidates, and Rs. 25 (processing fee only) for PwBD candidates.
Q6How can I apply online for UPSSSC PET 2026?
A
Candidates must first complete One Time Registration (OTR) on upsssc.gov.in, then log in using their OTR number/registered mobile number to complete the PET-2026 application, pay the fee online, and download/print the submitted form.
Q7What is the exam pattern for UPSSSC PET 2026?
A
PET-2026 consists of 100 objective MCQs worth 100 marks, to be completed in 2 hours, with ¼ mark negative marking for each wrong answer.
Q8How long is the UPSSSC PET 2026 score valid?
A
The PET-2026 score remains valid for 3 years from the date the result is declared or uploaded on the official UPSSSC website.
Q9Does PET-2026 guarantee selection to a specific post?
A
No. Merely meeting the eligibility criteria or applying for/appearing in PET-2026 does not entitle a candidate to selection for any specific post. PET is only a qualifying score used for shortlisting to separate Main Examinations, which are advertised individually by UPSSSC for specific Group 'C' posts.
Q10Is One Time Registration (OTR) mandatory for UPSSSC PET 2026?
A
Yes. OTR has been effective on the UPSSSC website since 14.07.2026, and candidates must complete OTR registration before applying online for PET-2026.
