BCECEB Senior Resident / Tutor Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1Is there an interview for these posts?
A
No. The interview provision has been removed, and every candidate receives a uniform 6 marks under that head.
Q2What score do I need to qualify?
A
The minimum qualifying score for appointment is 16 marks.
Q3Can a diploma holder apply?
A
A diploma is accepted only when suitable candidates are short. Diploma holders get 10 marks like PG degree holders, but are placed below them in the merit list.
Q4Is a DNB accepted?
A
Yes. DNB / DNB Super Speciality is treated as a valid qualification under the Medical Institutions (Qualification of Faculty) Regulation, 2025.
Q5I have already done a Senior Residency. Can I apply?
A
Not in the same subject if you completed the three-year tenure. You can apply in a different subject, and your earlier work in Bihar's government or semi-government colleges earns experience marks.
Q6I have PG degrees in two subjects. How should I apply?
A
Submit a separate application for each department and mention this in the online form. Once selected in one subject, you cannot be selected in another.
Q7Can candidates from outside Bihar apply?
A
Yes, 20% of the posts are for doctors with a PG degree from recognised institutions outside the state. However, applicants who are not natives of Bihar get no reservation benefit.
Q8Can I change my details after submitting the form?
A
You can correct entries only during the editing period ending at 11.59 PM on 24 November 2026, and only if you paid on time. Your verified mobile number and email cannot be changed.
Q9I am in a three-year bond. Can I apply?
A
Yes, if you were not posted as Senior Resident / Tutor, but you must bring a departmental NOC. Your time on the post counts towards the bond.
