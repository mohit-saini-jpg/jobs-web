About JPPO Dumka फसल रक्षक

The Office of the Junior Plant Protection Officer (JPPO), Dumka, is on Bhagalpur Road in Dumka, Jharkhand. It is a district office of the Agriculture Division of the Jharkhand Government's Department of Agriculture, Animal Husbandry and Cooperative. The notice says the Crop Protectors are hired under the Crop Protection Programme scheme (फसल सुरक्षा कार्यक्रम योजना) for the financial year 2026-27. The aim is to run crop protection and plant protection work well at the local level.

DPPO Dumka Fasal Rakshak (Crop Protector) Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many Crop Protector vacancies are in JPPO Dumka Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 11 vacancies: 05 UR, 05 ST and 01 EWS. There is one post at each of 11 Plant Protection Centres in Dumka district.
Q2What is the last date for the Dumka Fasal Rakshak application?
A
Applications are accepted from 26 October 2026 to 14 November 2026. Applications received after the last date will not be accepted.
Q3What qualification is required for Crop Protector?
A
You need at least Matric/Secondary (10th) pass from a recognised institution and at least one 5-day training from the Directorate of Agriculture or Horticulture. You also need knowledge of the local language and the ability to ride a bicycle and climb trees.
Q4What is the age limit for JPPO Dumka Crop Protector?
A
The minimum age is 18 years. The maximum is 35 years for UR and EWS and 40 years for ST, counted as on 30 September 2026 from the date of birth on the Matric certificate.
Q5What is the salary of a Crop Protector in Dumka?
A
The monthly honorarium is Rs. 11851/-.
Q6How do I apply for the Dumka Fasal Rakshak post?
A
Fill in the application form, attach self-attested copies of the required documents, and send it by registered post. The address is the Office of the Junior Plant Protection Officer, Dumka, Bhagalpur Road, Dumka, Near Tata Showroom, Dumka, PIN 814101.
Q7How will Crop Protectors be selected?
A
Selection is by a merit list out of 100 marks: up to 60 for academic marks, 10 for being a local resident of the block, and up to 30 for training. Original documents are checked before final selection. A practical test of bicycle riding and tree climbing may be held.
Q8Can I apply for a centre outside my block?
A
No. Only local residents of a block can apply for that block's centre. Residents of Jarmundi block are eligible for both the Jarmundi and Nonihat centres.
Q9Is the Crop Protector job permanent?
A
No. It is a temporary job paid by honorarium, for 11 months or until a regular appointment is made, whichever comes first. It gives no claim to a regular appointment or to permanency.
Q10Which training certificates are accepted for Crop Protector?
A
Only certificates from the Directorate of Agriculture and Horticulture under the Agriculture Department, the Krishi Vigyan Kendras of Birsa Agricultural University, and the ATMA office are accepted.
