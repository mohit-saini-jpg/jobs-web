KEA Pharmacy Officer & Nursing Officer Eligibility Criteria

Pharmacy Officer: Must possess a Diploma in Pharmacy from an institution recognized by the Government of Karnataka. Must be registered with the Karnataka Pharmacy Council. Nursing Officer (RPC notification and ESI department under KK notification): Must possess a Diploma in General Nursing issued by an authority authorized by the Government of Karnataka. Must be registered with the Karnataka Nursing Council. Nursing Officer (Karnataka Residential Educational Institutions Society post under KK notification): Must have passed a B.Sc Nursing degree from a recognized university and institution recognized by the Indian Nursing Council/Karnataka State Nursing Council, OR Must have passed PUC or an equivalent examination along with General Nursing and Midwifery (GNM) courses from an institution recognized by the Indian Nursing Council/Karnataka State Nursing Council. KEA Pharmacy Officer & Nursing Officer Selection Process

Selection is based on a competitive written examination conducted in online (CBT) or offline (OMR) mode, as decided by KEA. The exam consists of two papers: Each question carries 4 alternative answers; candidates must mark only one correct answer. Negative marking applies: 0.25 marks deducted for each wrong answer. Candidates must score a minimum of 35% marks in the competitive examination to qualify. Candidates scoring below the minimum marks will be disqualified. A Kannada language test (SSLC-standard, 150 objective-type marks, minimum 50 marks required to pass) applies to candidates who have not studied Kannada as a first or second language up to SSLC/higher level, or who have not already cleared the "Kadaya Kannada" test conducted earlier. Candidates who fail to secure the minimum required marks or remain absent are disqualified from the recruitment.

Q1What is KEA Pharmacy Officer & Nursing Officer Recruitment 2026?
A
It is a recruitment drive by the Karnataka Examinations Authority to fill Group-C posts of Pharmacy Officer and Nursing Officer across the ESI Scheme Medical Services Department and, under a separate Kalyana-Karnataka notification, allied departments.
Q2How many vacancies are available?
A
A total of 233 vacancies are notified - 188 under the RPC notification and 45 under the Kalyana-Karnataka notification.
Q3What is the last date to apply?
A
The online application can be submitted until 30.08.2026, and the fee can be paid until 31.08.2026
Q4What is the qualification required?
A
A Diploma in Pharmacy registered with the Karnataka Pharmacy Council is required for Pharmacy Officer. A Diploma in General Nursing (or B.Sc Nursing/PUC+GNM for the KREIS post) registered with the Karnataka Nursing Council is required for Nursing Officer.
Q5What is the age limit?
A
The general age limit is 18 to 40 years, with relaxation up to 43 years for Category 2A/2B/3A/3B and up to 45 years for SC/ST/Category-1 candidates, as of the last date of application.

₹750 for general and other categories, ₹500 for SC/ST/Category-1/Ex-servicemen/Third gender, and ₹250 for PWD candidates.

Applications must be submitted only through the KEA official website, https://cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea/, between 07.08.2026 and 30.08.2026.
