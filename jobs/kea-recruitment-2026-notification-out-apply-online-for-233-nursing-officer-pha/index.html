KEA Pharmacy Officer & Nursing Officer Selection Process

Selection will be made through a competitive examination conducted by KEA, either in Online (CBT) mode or Offline-OMR mode. The examination centre allotted will not be changed for any reason. Candidates who have studied Kannada as a first or second language subject in SSLC or a higher-level examination conducted by the Karnataka Secondary Education Examination Board or an equivalent examination recognized by the State Government, or who have passed a "Kadaya Kannada" test previously conducted by any authority for equivalent posts, are exempted from the Kannada Language Test. Other candidates must appear for the Kannada Language Test, which is objective type, carries 150 marks, and requires a minimum of 50 marks to qualify.
