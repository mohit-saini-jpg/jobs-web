Zilla Panchayat Raichur Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

The educational qualification is common for both the Clerk-cum-Data Entry Operator and Tax Collector posts: Must have passed PUC or an examination considered equivalent, with Kannada as one of the subjects studied. Must hold a minimum 3-month computer training course certificate from an institution recognized by the Central or State Government. The notification also clarifies that the following are treated as equivalent to PUC: a CBSE/ICSE Class 12 pass, a Class 12 pass conducted by another state's board, a higher secondary certificate from the National Institute of Open Schooling (NIOS), a two-year ITI course combined with an NIOS pass in one language and one subject, or a pre-degree examination pass in one language and one subject. Zilla Panchayat Raichur Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection for both posts will be made purely on merit, based on the marks obtained by the candidate in the qualifying educational examination (PUC or its recognized equivalent). No separate written examination or interview is mentioned in the provided notification. A provisional merit list will be published, and objections/claims regarding merit can be raised within the specified time, after which the final merit list will be published.

Zilla Panchayat Raichur Recruitment 2026 Important Instructions

Only candidates residing in the villages under the concerned Grama Panchayat of Raichur district are eligible to apply for that Grama Panchayat's vacancies; candidates from other districts, taluks, or villages will not be considered. Applications can be submitted only through the online mode; offline or postal applications will not be accepted. Candidates must upload only their original documents in a clear, legible format; incomplete applications will be rejected. The recruiting authority's decision on eligibility, category claims, and merit will be final. Providing false information, forged documents, or impersonation will lead to cancellation of candidature and may result in criminal and disciplinary action.
