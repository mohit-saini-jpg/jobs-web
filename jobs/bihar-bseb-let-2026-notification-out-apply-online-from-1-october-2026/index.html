BSEB School Librarian Eligibility Test 2026 Eligibility

The applicant must be a citizen of India and a resident of Bihar Educational qualification: a Bachelor's degree in Library Science from a recognised university, with at least 45% marks (or equivalent grade), as prescribed under the Bihar School Librarian Rules, 2025 A relaxation of 5% in the minimum marks is available to reserved/special categories as stated in the notice (see note below) Minimum age: 21 years as on 01.08.2026 Verify before publishing: The newspaper scan is low-resolution. BSEB School Librarian Eligibility Test 2026 Exam Pattern & Qualifying Marks

The eligibility test is a single-stage CBT. There is no interview. All questions are multiple-choice (objective) type Each question carries 1 mark No negative marking Duration: 150 minutes (2 hours 30 minutes), Computer Based Test Because the CBT may be held in multiple sessions on different dates, question difficulty can differ between sessions. BSEB will therefore apply a normalization process : each candidate's raw score is converted into a normalised score, and the qualifying marks above are applied to the normalised score , not the raw score. The notice explains the method with a worked example; candidates can read it in the official notice. Candidates who qualify the School Librarian Eligibility Test 2026 will be issued an eligibility certificate. Bihar BSEB LET 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is the BSEB School Librarian Eligibility Test 2026?
A
It is an eligibility test (Vidyalaya Pustakalayadhyaksh Patrata Pariksha) conducted by the Bihar School Examination Committee for candidates holding a Library Science degree. Qualifying candidates receive an eligibility certificate
Q2What are the online application dates?
A
Online applications are accepted from 1 October 2026 to 21 October 2026.
Q3What is the exam pattern?
A
A 150-minute Computer Based Test of 150 marks, with 100 marks for Library Science and 50 marks for General Knowledge. All questions are MCQs of 1 mark each, with no negative marking.
Q4What is the qualification required?
A
A Bachelor's degree in Library Science from a recognised university with at least 45% marks, and the applicant must be an Indian citizen and a resident of Bihar.
Q5What is the age limit?
A
Minimum 21 years and maximum 37 years for unreserved men, 40 years for unreserved women, BC and EBC, and 42 years for SC/ST, as on 01.08.2026, with further relaxation for Divyang candidates and ex-servicemen.
Q6What is the exam fee?
A
₹960 for General/BC/EBC candidates and ₹760 for SC/ST/Divyang candidates, payable online only.
Q7What are the qualifying marks?
A
50% for General, 45.5% for Backward Class, 42.5% for Extremely Backward Class, and 40% for SC/ST, Divyang and Women candidates, applied on the normalised score.
