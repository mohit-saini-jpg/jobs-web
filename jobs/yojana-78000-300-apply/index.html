पीएम सूर्य घर Yojana 78,000 रुपये सब्सिडी, 300 यूनिट फ्री बि Overview

PM Surya Ghar Yojana ek flagship scheme hai jo low‑income families ko solar rooftop install karne par Rs 78,000 tak ki subsidy aur 300 units free electricity deti hai. Aspirants ko bas eligibility check karni hai aur online portal se apply karna hai.

पीएम सूर्य घर Yojana 78,000 रुपये सब्सिडी, 300 यूनिट फ्री बि Expert Analysis

Yeh scheme renewable energy ko promote karte hue electricity bill mein significant cut laati hai, aur subsidy se upfront cost bahut kam ho jati hai. Government ka disbursement transparent hai, isliye eligible applicants ko jaldi benefit milta hai.

Who Is Eligible: पीएम सूर्य घर Yojana 78,000 रुपये सब्सिडी, 300 यूनिट फ्री बि

Scheme un households ke liye hai jinki annual income ₹2 lakh se ₹5 lakh ke beech hai aur jinke paas suitable rooftop space hai.

पीएम सूर्य घर Yojana 78,000 रुपये सब्सिडी, 300 यूनिट फ्री बि Application Process

1. Aadhaar, income certificate aur electricity bill ready rakhein. 2. Solar provider se pre‑approval letter lein. 3. Online portal par sabhi documents clear scan karke upload karein.

पीएम सूर्य घर Yojana 78,000 रुपये सब्सिडी, 300 यूनिट फ्री बि Benefits Details

Rs 78,000 ki subsidy aur 300 free units ka annual saving approx. ₹12,000‑₹15,000 hota hai, jo low‑income families ke liye kaafi financial relief provide karta hai.

How पीएम सूर्य घर Yojana 78,000 रुपये सब्सिडी, 300 यूनिट फ्री बि Works

Scheme ke tahat state nodal agency solar vendors ko approve karti hai, installation ke baad subsidy direct bank transfer hoti hai aur free electricity meter se automatically credit hoti hai.

पीएम सूर्य घर Yojana 78,000 रुपये सब्सिडी, 300 यूनिट फ्री बि Application Tips

Application form ko dhyan se fill karein, sabhi documents latest version ke hon, aur portal par status regularly check karein. Common rejection reasons: mismatched income proof, incomplete address details, ya missing vendor approval.

पीएम सूर्य घर Yojana 78,000 रुपये सब्सिडी, 300 यूनिट फ्री बि FAQs

Q1Yojana ke liye kaun eligible hai?
A
Annual income ₹2‑5 lakh, valid Aadhaar, aur 10‑15 sq.m. rooftop space required hai.
Q2Subsidy ka amount kaise milta hai?
A
Installation ke baad approved vendor se invoice submit karne par, subsidy bank account me direct transfer hoti hai within 30 days.
Q3Free electricity ka benefit kaise claim karte hain?
A
Solar system install hone ke baad, utility company ko meter number dene par 300 units har mahine automatically free credit ho jati hai.
