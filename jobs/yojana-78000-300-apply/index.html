Ye scheme unke liye hai jo apne ghar ki electricity bill mein bachat chahte hain. Sirf Rs 78,000 ki subsidy aur 300 units free bijli milti hai, bas kuch documents ke saath apply karein.

पीएम सूर्य घर Yojana 78,000 रुपये सब्सिडी, 300 यूनिट फ्री बि Expert Analysis

Yojana ka maksad low‑income families ko energy cost se relief dena hai, aur renewable energy adoption ko boost karna. Disbursement direct bank transfer se hota hai, isliye transparency bani rehti hai.

Who Is Eligible: पीएम सूर्य घर Yojana 78,000 रुपये सब्सिडी, 300 यूनिट फ्री बि

Aap eligible hain agar aapka annual household income Rs 2 lakh se kam hai aur aapka ghar urban ya semi‑urban area mein registered hai.

1. Aadhaar, income certificate aur electricity bill ready rakhein. 2. Online portal ya nearest CSC centre par jaake form fill karein. 3. Application submit karne ke baad status ko weekly check karte rahen.
