पीएम सूर्य घर Yojana 78,000 रुपये सब्सिडी, 300 यूनिट फ्री बि Overview

PM Surya Ghar Yojana ka maksad low‑income families ko affordable electricity dena hai. Scheme ke tehat eligible ghar ko Rs 78,000 ki direct subsidy milti hai aur pehle 300 units bilkul free milte hain.

पीएम सूर्य घर Yojana 78,000 रुपये सब्सिडी, 300 यूनिट फ्री बि Expert Analysis

Yeh yojana power bill ko dramatically kam karti hai, especially rural aur semi‑urban areas mein. Subsidy aur free units dono milne se household savings kaafi badh jati hai.

Who Is Eligible: पीएम सूर्य घर Yojana 78,000 रुपये सब्सिडी, 300 यूनिट फ्री बि

Jinke annual income ₹2 lakh se ₹5 lakh ke beech hai, aur electricity connection existing ya naya ho, wo eligible hain.

पीएम सूर्य घर Yojana 78,000 रुपये सब्सिडी, 300 यूनिट फ्री बि Application Process

1. Aadhaar, electricity bill aur income proof (salary slip/ITR) ready rakhein. 2. Online portal ya nearest DISCOM office se application form download karen. 3. Form ko accurately fill kar ke required documents attach kar ke submit karen.

पीएम सूर्य घर Yojana 78,000 रुपये सब्सिडी, 300 यूनिट फ्री बि Benefits Details

Rs 78,000 ki subsidy ek lump sum amount hai jo directly bank account me transfer hoti hai, aur 300 free units ka annual saving approx ₹2,500‑₹3,000 ho sakta hai.

How पीएम सूर्य घर Yojana 78,000 रुपये सब्सिडी, 300 यूनिट फ्री बि Works

Application approve hone ke baad DISCOM subsidy ko beneficiary ke bank account me credit karta hai, aur free units ko meter par auto‑adjust kiya jata hai. Monitoring quarterly hoti hai.

पीएम सूर्य घर Yojana 78,000 रुपये सब्सिडी, 300 यूनिट फ्री बि Application Tips

Form me sabhi details bilkul match karein, especially Aadhaar‑electricity connection linkage. Incomplete docs ya wrong income details se rejection ho sakta hai, isliye double‑check karen.

पीएम सूर्य घर Yojana 78,000 रुपये सब्सिडी, 300 यूनिट फ्री बि FAQs

Q1Yojana ke liye kaun kaun se documents chahiye?
A
Aadhaar card, latest electricity bill, income proof (salary slip, pension slip ya IT return), aur bank passbook ya IFSC details.
Q2Subsidy kitni der me milti hai?
A
Application approve hone ke baad 30‑45 din ke andar subsidy beneficiary ke bank account me transfer ho jati hai.
Q3Free 300 units ka benefit kaise track karun?
A
DISCOM aapke meter par free units ko alag se show karta hai; aap monthly bill me ‘Free Units’ ka column dekh sakte hain.
