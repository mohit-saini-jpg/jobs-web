IOB Recruitment 2026 Vacancy Details

IOB Vacancy 2026 covers a total of 291 posts across Generalist and Specialist Officer cadres in JMGS I, MMGS II, and MMGS III. Post Code 01 (Manager - Branch Management) is the Generalist Officer post; all other posts are Specialist Officer posts. The number of vacancies and reserved vacancies is provisional and may vary as per the Bank's actual requirements. Out of the 291 total posts, 12 vacancies are reserved for Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PWBD) on a horizontal basis: 4 for Hearing Impaired (HI), 5 for Orthopedically Challenged (OC), 2 for Visually Impaired (VI), and 1 for Intellectual Disability (ID).

IOB Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection is through an Online Examination followed by a Personal Interview. For Post Code 01 (Manager – Branch Management): the Online Examination has 100 questions/100 marks in 2 hours, covering English Language (25Q, 30 minutes) and Professional Knowledge – Banking Law & Practice, Indian Economy & Financial System, Principles & Practices of Banking, Accounting & Financial Management (75Q, 90 minutes). For all other posts: the Online Examination has 100 questions/100 marks in 2 hours, covering English Language (25Q, 30 minutes), General Awareness with special reference to Banking Industry (25Q, 30 minutes), and Professional Knowledge, which varies post-wise (50Q, 60 minutes). There is no minimum qualifying mark for General Awareness and English Language; candidates must secure minimum qualifying marks in Professional Knowledge.
