About Kalakshetra

Kalakshetra Foundation describes itself as an Institution of National Importance and an autonomous organisation under the Ministry of Culture, Govt. of India. It is located at Thiruvanmiyur, Chennai. The Registrar post advertised here belongs to its Rukmini Devi College of Fine Arts (RDCFA).

Who Can Apply

The notice lists the following types of applicants for the post of Registrar, RDCFA: Eminent artists/private persons who meet the essential qualifications and also have the desirable qualifications, as applicable to a Registrar / Lecturer (Bharatanatyam or Carnatic Music) level appointee in Central/State Government institutions. Officers holding analogous posts in Central/State Government institutions. A Lecturer in Bharatanatyam or Carnatic Music with 8 years' experience in a reputed institution and the qualification required for the post of Lecturer, i.e. Pay Level 15 of Tamil Nadu Government State Pay Scales (Rs 36,200-1,14,800), or an equivalent level. (a) Class X passed (b) Diploma or Post Diploma in Bharatanatyam or Carnatic Music from a recognised institution in the particular field (c) A minimum of 8 years' experience in the field Knowledge of South Indian languages and Hindi The

Pay and Terms

Pay: Pay Level 7 in 7th CPC pay matrix (Rs.44,900 – Rs.1,42,400). Group: Group - B. Mode of appointment: Deputation / short term contract. Applicants choose either "On Deputation" or "On Short Term Contract" in the application form. Extension: The contract can be extended on a year-to-year basis, subject to the candidate's satisfactory performance during the contract. Nature of appointment: The declaration in the application form says that the appointment, if the candidate is selected, is purely temporary, with no claim to any regular appointment. The notice does not mention HRA or other allowances.
