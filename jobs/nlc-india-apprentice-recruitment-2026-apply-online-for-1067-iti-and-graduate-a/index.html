NLC India Apprentice Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must possess the qualification required for their apprenticeship category. Graduate (Non-Engineering) candidates must also meet the prescribed passing-year restriction. Candidates must have passed ITI in the relevant trade from a recognized institute . The notification does not prescribe a qualifying-examination passing-year restriction for ITI candidates. Applicants must possess a regular full-time Bachelor's degree in the relevant discipline from a UGC-approved university . Eligible disciplines include Commerce, Business Administration, Computer Science, Computer Application, Geology, Microbiology, Chemistry, Pharmacy and Nursing, as listed in the vacancy table. Passing Year Requirement: Graduate (Non-Engineering) candidates must have passed their qualifying examination in 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 or 2026 . NLC India Apprentice Stipend 2026

Selected apprentices will receive a monthly stipend between Rs. 12,000 and Rs. 16,678 , depending on their training category and discipline. For Graduate Apprentices, the notified stipend includes Rs. 6,150 payable by the Government through the Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) scheme . The stipend is paid during the prescribed 12-month apprenticeship training period. These are training stipends, not regular employee salary scales.

NLC India Apprentice Selection Process 2026

Selection will be based on marks obtained in the relevant qualifying examination . The notification does not prescribe a separate written examination for these apprenticeship slots. Trade Apprentices will be selected based on the percentage of marks obtained in the qualifying ITI examination. Graduate Apprentices will be assessed based on the marks obtained in their Bachelor's degree, following the percentage-calculation procedure of the awarding university or institution. Where the university has an established CGPA or grade conversion procedure, that procedure will be followed. If no established conversion system is available, the CGPA or grade will be multiplied by 10 to obtain the equivalent percentage.
