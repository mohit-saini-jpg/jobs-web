ICMR NITHR Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1When is the ICMR-NITHR walk-in interview 2026?
A
The ICMR-NITHR walk-in interview is on 5 November 2026 for Project Research Scientist-II, Project Research Scientist-I, Consultant (Medical), Project Nurse-IV and Project Nurse-III. It is on 6 November 2026 for Project Field Officer, Project Technical Support-III, Project Assistant Multipurpose (Admin), Project Technical Support-II and Project Lab Technician-2.
Q2What is the reporting time and venue for the ICMR-NITHR walk-in?
A
Applications are accepted between 9 AM and 11 AM only, and nobody is allowed into the venue after 11 AM. The venue is ICMR-National Institute for Tribal Health Research, Nagpur Road, Post Garha, Jabalpur Pin-482003.
Q3How many vacancies are there in ICMR-NITHR Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 25 vacancies across 14 project positions under Advt. No.- 06/2026-27. Project Nurse-III has the most posts, with 10, followed by 2 each for Project Nurse-IV and Project Technical Support-II.
Q4What is the qualification for ICMR-NITHR Project Nurse-III?
A
The essential qualification for Project Nurse-III is a Four year Nursing course. Experience in clinical assessments, participant interviews, field-based data collection and NCD Screening is desirable.
Q5What is the age limit for ICMR-NITHR project posts?
A
The age limit is 40 years for Project Research Scientist-II, 35 years for Project Research Scientist-I, Project Nurse-IV, Project Field Officer and Project Technical Support-III, and 30 years for Project Nurse-III, Project Assistant Multipurpose (Admin), Project Technical Support-II and Project Lab Technician-2. Consultant (Medical) must not exceed 70 years. Age is counted as on the date of the walk-in interview.
Q6What is the salary in ICMR-NITHR Recruitment 2026?
A
The consolidated monthly salary ranges from Rs. 20,000/- for Project Technical Support-II and Project Lab Technician-2 up to 67000/- for Project Research Scientist-II. Consultant (Medical) gets Rs. 64,000/- p.m. and Project Field Officer gets 28933/- p.m.
Q7Is HRA paid on top of the ICMR-NITHR salary?
A
Yes, HRA is added to the consolidated salary for most posts. It is 20% for some posts and "as admissible" for others. The notice lists no HRA for Consultant (Medical) (Rs. 64,000/- p.m.) or Project Field Officer (28933/- p.m.).
Q8What documents should I bring to the ICMR-NITHR walk-in interview?
A
Bring the filled application in the format from www.nirth.res.in or www.main.icmr.nic.in, with copies of your certificates attached. The application must include your full address with Pin Code, mobile number, e-mail ID and details of two references.
Q9How is the selection made in ICMR-NITHR Recruitment 2026?
A
Selection is by walk-in interview. Only eligible candidates are allowed to take the interview, and appointments follow the selection committee's recommendations. Preference goes to local candidates and those with higher qualification and work experience.
Q10Is the ICMR-NITHR project job permanent?
A
No. The posts are temporary and contractual, for three months or until the Manpower Outsourcing GeM Contract is finalised, whichever is earlier, and are co-terminable with the project. Selected candidates have no claim to a regular appointment in ICMR-NITHR or ICMR.
