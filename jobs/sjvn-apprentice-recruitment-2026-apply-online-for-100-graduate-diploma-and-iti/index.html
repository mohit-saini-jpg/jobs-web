SJVN Apprentice Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must possess the prescribed educational qualification for their selected apprenticeship discipline. Graduate Apprentices require a relevant Engineering Degree, Diploma Apprentices require an Engineering Diploma, and ITI Apprentices need Matriculation with the specified ITI qualification. Candidates must be eligible in age and qualification as on the closing date and can apply for only one trade or discipline; if a candidate applies more than once, only the latest application will be considered. Result-awaited candidates, candidates who have already undergone apprenticeship training in SJVN, candidates already registered and trained under BOAT/NAPS, and candidates with one year or more of training or job experience after completing their education are not eligible. Candidates whose result was declared more than five years before training starts are also not eligible. Candidates

SJVN Apprentice Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

There will be no interview . Eligible candidates will be shortlisted on the basis of a merit list formed from the marks secured in Matriculation (10th), 12th and the ITI Course, Diploma or Engineering Degree, with the following weightage: In case of a tie in total marks, the candidate older in age will be considered senior. Shortlisted candidates must produce all original certificates at the time of verification and a certificate of medical fitness before joining. Lists of shortlisted and finally selected candidates will be published on www.sjvn.nic.in, and call letters will be sent only by email to the registered email ID.

SJVN Apprentice Recruitment 2026 Himachal Pradesh Domicile Requirement

The recruitment is restricted to candidates who are domiciles of Himachal Pradesh. Only eligible Himachal Pradesh domicile candidates can apply for the notified Graduate, Diploma and ITI apprenticeship seats.

SJVN Apprentice Recruitment 2026 Reservation

Reservation of seats for SC, ST, OBC Non-Creamy Layer, EWS and Persons with Benchmark Disabilities will be implemented according to the applicable provisions of the Apprentices Act, 1961, and subsequent amendments. Applicants claiming reservation must possess valid category or disability certificates as required by the recruiting organization.
