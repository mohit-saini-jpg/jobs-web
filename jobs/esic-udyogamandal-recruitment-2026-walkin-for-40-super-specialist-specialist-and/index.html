ESIC Udyogamandal Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

ESIC Hospital, Udyogamandal, Ernakulam District, Kerala, will hold a walk-in interview for doctors (allopathy) on 2 December 2026. Reporting time is 9:00 AM and registration runs from 9:00 AM to 10:00 AM. The Medical Superintendent has invited candidates for 2 Full Time/Part Time Super Specialist, 11 Full Time/Part Time Specialist and 27 Senior Resident (1 year) posts on contract, with consolidated remuneration from Rs 60,000 to Rs 2,40,000 a month depending on post and level.

About ESIC Udyogamandal

The notice is issued by the Medical Superintendent of the Employees' State Insurance Corporation (ESIC) Hospital at Udyogamandal in Ernakulam, Kerala. The hospital proposes to conduct the walk-in interview for contract engagement of doctors in the allopathy stream. The Medical Superintendent reserves the right to cancel the recruitment and to increase or decrease vacancies as per actual requirement on the date of interview.

Posts and Eligibility

Experience: FTSS entry level – Nil. FTSS senior level – 5 years experience in respective specialty after PG. Age: not exceeding 69 years as on the date of interview; relaxation for reserved categories as per rules. Tenure beyond 70 years is not permitted. Note for Oncology: if no eligible candidate with super specialty qualification in oncology applies, a specialist with radiotherapy qualification (MDRT/DNB RT or Diploma in RT), with 3 years experience for a degree holder and 5 years for a diploma holder, may be considered in specialist scale. Part time remuneration: fixed amount for 4 days x 4 hours a week; emergency visit charges Rs 20,000/- if willing; additional Rs 1,200/- (entry) or Rs 1,500/- (senior) for every hour beyond 16 hours a week, which the Medical Superintendent must justify. What Happens on the Day

Registration is from 09:00 AM to 10:00 AM on 02.12.2026; reporting time is 09:00 AM. Candidates arriving after 9:15 AM will not be permitted to participate in the interview process. The notice names a walk-in interview as the selection route. For Senior Resident, candidates who completed 3 years Senior Residency from any Govt. Institute need not appear for interview. Vacancies may change as per actual requirement on the date of interview.

Terms of Engagement

FTSS and FTS: one year on contract or till the regular incumbent joins, whichever is earlier; extendable for a maximum of 3 years based on satisfactory performance. PTSS and PTS: one year on contract or till the regular incumbent joins, whichever is earlier. Full time contract specialists: recruited for 3 years with annual evaluation, with the term renewed every year based on satisfactory performance. Private practice is not allowed; leave is 4 days for each month of work on pro rata basis, encashable up to 30 days a year if not availed. Part time contract specialists: contract of one year from the date of joining; not entitled to any kind of leave or benefits. ESIC Udyogamandal Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1When is the ESIC Udyogamandal walk-in interview 2026?
A
The walk-in interview is on 02.12.2026 (2 December 2026). Reporting time is 09:00 AM and registration runs from 09:00 AM to 10:00 AM.
Q2Where is the ESIC Udyogamandal walk-in interview held?
A
The venue is ESIC Hospital, Udyogamandal, Ernakulam District, Kerala. The notice prints no PIN code.
Q3How many vacancies are there in ESIC Hospital Udyogamandal?
A
The notice lists 40 vacancies: 2 Full Time/Part Time Super Specialist, 11 Full Time/Part Time Specialist and 27 Senior Resident (1 year) posts. Vacancies may change on the interview date.
Q4What is the qualification for Senior Resident at ESIC Udyogamandal?
A
A PG Degree or PG Diploma in the concerned specialty is required. If PG candidates are not available, candidates with 2 years experience after MBBS, including one year in the concerned specialty in a government or reputed private hospital, will be considered.
Q5What is the age limit for ESIC Udyogamandal posts?
A
Super Specialist and Specialist: not exceeding 69 years as on the date of interview. Senior Resident: not exceeding 45 years as on the date of interview. Reserved categories get relaxation as per rules.
Q6What is the salary for ESIC Udyogamandal Specialist posts?
A
Full Time Specialist gets a consolidated Rs 1,55,044/- (entry level) or Rs 1,76,541/- (senior level). Part Time Specialist gets Rs 60,000/- fixed. Super Specialist pay is Rs 2,00,000/- to Rs 2,40,000/- (full time) and Rs 1,00,000/- to Rs 1,50,000/- (part time).
Q7What is the application fee for the ESIC Udyogamandal walk-in?
A
General/OBC candidates pay Rs 250/- and SC/ST candidates pay Rs 50/-, by Demand Draft in favour of ESI Fund Account No.1 payable at SBI, Kalamassery. PWD and female applicants are exempted.
Q8Which documents should I carry to the ESIC Udyogamandal walk-in interview?
A
Carry the application in the prescribed proforma with a recent passport size photograph affixed, all original documents and one set of photocopies. SC/ST/OBC/EWS candidates need a caste certificate, and retired government doctors need the Pension Payment Order and last pay slip.
Q9Are these ESIC Udyogamandal posts permanent?
A
No, they are contract posts. FTSS and FTS are for one year or till the regular incumbent joins, whichever is earlier, extendable up to a maximum of 3 years on satisfactory performance. PTSS and PTS are for one year or till the regular incumbent joins.
Q10Will late arrivals be allowed at the ESIC Udyogamandal walk-in?
A
No. Candidates arriving after 9:15 AM will not be permitted to participate in the interview process.
