NITT Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering at the National Institute of Technology, Tiruchirappalli (NITT) has invited Indian nationals to apply for one Junior Research Fellow (JRF) / Project Associate-I (PA-I) position in the ISRO-RESPOND funded project on AI-enabled data association and intelligent radar scheduling. The tenure is 2 years, with JRF pay of 37,000 + 20% HRA and PA-I pay of 31000 + 20% HRA. Applications in the prescribed format must be emailed to [email protected] with the subject "JRF - ISRO-RESPOND Programme" by 30.11.2026 at 5 P.M.
