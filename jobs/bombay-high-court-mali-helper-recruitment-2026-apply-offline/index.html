Bombay High Court Mali/ Helper Recruitment 2026 - Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must fulfill all of the following conditions as on the last date of application. The candidate must be a citizen of India. Candidate must have passed at least the 4th standard (चौथी). Candidate must be able to read, write and speak Marathi. Candidate must have a minimum of 3 years of experience in maintaining gardens, plants, trees and greenery in proper condition.
