About MGU

Mahatma Gandhi University is located at Priyadarsini Hills P.O., Kottayam, Kerala - 686 560. Its letterhead states that it was established by the Kerala State Legislature by Notification No. 3431/Leg. C1/85/Law, dated 17th April 1985. In the notice, the University says that it is implementing integrated undergraduate programmes and strengthening advanced and interdisciplinary research in the areas of Sciences, Humanities and Social Sciences. The University had earlier invited applications for Associate Professor in various Departments/Schools under notifications 67435/AD A2-1/2024/AD A2(2), dated 01.11.2025, 67435/AD A2-1/2024/AD A2, dated 28.11.2025 (extension of time for submission of applications) and 67435/AD A2-1/2024/AD A 2 (PART-1)(2), dated 27.02.2026. Qualification and Experience

The notice states that the qualifications are as per UGC Regulations, 2018, and gives the same qualification and experience for all five Associate Professor posts. The full experience condition reads: a minimum of eight years of experience of teaching and/or research in an academic/research position equivalent to that of Assistant Professor in a University, College or Accredited Research Institution/industry, with a minimum of seven publications in the peer-reviewed or UGC-listed journals and a total research score of Seventy five (75) as per the criteria given in Appendix II, Table 2 of the UGC Regulations, 2018. MGU Associate Professor Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Applications are made online first, and then two hard copies of the submitted application are sent to the University. The steps given in the notice are:
  • 2Submit the online application through the link on the Mahatma Gandhi University website www.facultyrecruitment.mgu.ac.in, from 18 November 2026 onwards and on or before 17 December 2026.
  • 3Pay the Registration Fee only through the online payment facility provided in the online application form.
  • 4Prepare two hard copies of the duly completed online application, with self-attested true copies of all certificates and documents and a copy of the fee receipt.
  • 5If you work in a University, Government or Aided College, attach a No Objection Certificate from your employer to the hard copy.
  • 6Send the hard copies so that they reach the office of the Deputy Registrar-II (Admn.), Mahatma Gandhi University, Priyadarsini Hills P.O, Kottayam-686 560 on or before 31 December 2026.
  • 7Teachers working in private colleges under the direct payment system must have their applications forwarded by the Principal to the respective Zonal Deputy Director of Collegiate Education, who will forward them to the University. The notice asks candidates to submit the online application early enough for the hard copy to reach the University well within the time limit. The notice does not specify the mode of sending the hard copies (post, courier or by hand).
  • 8The fee must be paid only online, through the facility in the online application form. A copy of the receipt must go with the hard copy of the application.

MGU (Associate Professor Posts) Job Profile

Q1How many vacancies are there in MGU Recruitment 2026?

Q2What is the last date to apply for the MGU Associate Professor posts?
A
The online application must be submitted by 17 December 2026, and the two hard copies must reach the Deputy Registrar-II (Admn.) by 31 December 2026.

A
Candidates need a good academic record with a Ph.D. in the concerned/allied/relevant disciplines, a Master's Degree with at least 55% marks (or equivalent grade), and at least eight years of teaching and/or research experience with seven publications and a research score of 75, as per UGC Regulations, 2018.
Q4What is the age limit for MGU Associate Professor Recruitment 2026?
A
Candidates must be not above 60 years as on 01.01.2026.
Q5What is the salary of MGU Associate Professor?
A
The post is in Academic Level 13 A with a Rationalised Entry Pay of ₹1,31,400/-.
Q6What is the application fee for MGU Associate Professor posts?
A
The Registration Fee is ₹5000/-, and ₹2500/- for SC/ST candidates, paid only online through the application form.

Apply online on www.facultyrecruitment.mgu.ac.in by 17 December 2026, then send two hard copies with self-attested certificates and the fee receipt so they reach the Deputy Registrar-II (Admn.), Mahatma Gandhi University, Priyadarsini Hills P.O, Kottayam-686 560 by 31 December 2026.
Q8Do candidates who applied under the earlier notifications need to apply again?

Q9Can candidates from outside Kerala apply for the reserved posts?
