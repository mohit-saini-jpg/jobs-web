AIIMS Rewari Faculty (Group-A) Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must be Indian citizens or persons registered as Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) cardholders under Section 7A of the Citizenship Act, 1955. Note: The essential qualification for the post of Associate Professor remains the same as for the Assistant Professor of the respective discipline, along with required additional teaching/research experience.

AIIMS Rewari Important Instructions

Private practice of any kind is strictly prohibited for all posts. The period of probation for all faculty posts is two years. Employees will be governed by the New Pension Scheme. Candidates must appear in person for the interview; no online interviews or TA/DA will be provided. If applying for more than one post, candidates must submit separate application forms and fees for each post. Failure to submit the hard copy by 7 November 2026 will lead to cancellation of candidature. NOC is mandatory for regular and contractual candidates working in Government, Semi-Government, PSUs, or autonomous bodies.
