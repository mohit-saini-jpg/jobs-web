ESIC MH Baddi Recruitment 2026 - Eligibility Criteria

Full Time/Part Time Specialist: PG Degree or Diploma in the concerned specialty from a recognised university, with 3 years' experience after acquiring the PG Degree, or 5 years' experience after the Diploma. Senior Resident: PG Degree or Diploma in the concerned specialty from a recognised university. Non-PG candidates need 2 years of working experience in the concerned department. Super Specialist (Cardiology): Post-Graduation qualification in the concerned specialty, registered with the Medical Council of India or a State Medical Council, along with a DM/M.Ch in the concerned super specialty. Senior Resident (Against GDMO): PG Degree/Diploma or an MBBS Degree from a recognised university, with at least 2 years of working experience in the same discipline in which the candidate is proposed to be engaged. ESIC MH Baddi Recruitment 2026 - Selection Process

Selection is made through a walk-in interview conducted by an Interview Board at the Office of the Medical Superintendent, ESIC Model Hospital, Katha, Baddi. The decision of the Interview Board is final on all aspects of selection, and no further correspondence will be entertained. Selection is also subject to the candidate proving medical fitness and successful verification of character and antecedents.

Documents Required for ESIC MH Baddi Walk-in Interview

Candidates must bring original certificates along with one set of self-attested photocopies of the following documents at the time of the interview: Age proof MBBS Certificate Two recent passport-size photographs Caste Certificate for SC/ST/OBC candidates (OBC candidates must submit the latest Central Government format certificate, not more than one year old, from the date of interview) PG Degree/Diploma Certificate/DM/M.Ch Certificate, as applicable State Medical Council/MCI Registration Certificate with MS/MD/DNB qualification (for Senior Resident and Specialist posts) or DM qualification (for the Super Specialist post) No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the present employer, if applicable Experience Certificate, if applicable Income and Asset Certificate for EWS category candidates, as per DOPT letter No. 36039/1/2019-Estt.(Res) dated 31 January 2019
