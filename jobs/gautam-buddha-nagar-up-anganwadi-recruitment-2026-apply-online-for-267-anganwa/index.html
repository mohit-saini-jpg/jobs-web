Gautam Buddha Nagar UP Anganwadi Bharti 2026- Eligibility Criteria

Only women candidates are eligible to apply for these posts. For rural area vacancies, the applicant must be a resident of the concerned Gram Sabha; for urban area vacancies, the applicant must reside in the same ward. If no eligible candidate is available in a Gram Sabha, selection will be made from the concerned Nyaya Panchayat as per seniority. A widow candidate must submit a death certificate and an attested copy of the family register issued by the Gram Panchayat Officer or the concerned Nagar Nikay (Municipal Body). A divorced or deserted woman must submit a valid legal order issued by a court. Selection under the Below Poverty Line (BPL) category will be given priority as per seniority within the prescribed age limit.
