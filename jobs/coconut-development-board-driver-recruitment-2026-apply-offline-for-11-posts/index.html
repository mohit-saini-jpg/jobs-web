Coconut Development Board Driver Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Coconut Development Board (Nariyal Vikas Board), Kochi, is filling 11 Staff Car Driver posts on deputation basis: 7 posts of Driver Grade I and 4 posts of Driver Grade II. Only Staff Car Drivers already working in government, university, research institute, public sector, semi-government, statutory or autonomous organisations can apply. Applications must go through proper channel to the Chief Executive Officer, Coconut Development Board, Kera Bhavan, SRV Road, Kochi – 682 011. They must reach that address within 60 days from the date the advertisement appears in the Employment News.

Coconut Development Board Driver Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in CDB Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 11 vacancies: 7 Driver Grade I and 4 Driver Grade II. One more Driver Grade I vacancy is anticipated on 1.11.2026, and the notice says the vacancies are subject to change.
Q2What is the last date to apply for the CDB Driver posts?
A
The complete application must reach the Coconut Development Board, Kochi, within 60 days from the date the advertisement appears in the Employment News. Applications received after the due date will not be considered.
Q3Who can apply for CDB Driver Grade I and Driver Grade II?
A
Staff Car Drivers working in Central or State Governments, Universities, recognized research institutes, public sector undertakings, or semi-Government, statutory or autonomous organizations can apply. They must hold an analogous post on a regular basis or have the required regular service, and they are subject to passing a trade test.
Q4What service is required for Driver Grade I and Driver Grade II?
A
Driver Grade I needs an analogous regular post or 6 years' regular service in Level 4 (₹25,500-81,100). Driver Grade II needs an analogous regular post or 9 years' regular service in Level 2 (₹19,900-63,200), or equivalent in the parent cadre or department.
Q5What is the age limit for CDB Driver deputation?
A
The maximum age limit for appointment by deputation is not exceeding 56 years as on the closing date of receipt of applications, for both posts.
Q6What is the salary of CDB Driver Grade I and Grade II?
A
Driver Grade I is in Level 5 of the pay matrix (₹29,200-92,300), and Driver Grade II is in Level 4 (₹25,500-81,100). A selected official can either keep existing basic pay plus deputation (duty) allowance or have pay fixed in the scale of the post under DoPT OM No. 6/8/2009-Estt. (Pay-II), dated 17.06.2010.
Q7How do I apply for CDB Recruitment 2026?
A
Send the Annexure-I proforma in triplicate through proper channel to the Chief Executive Officer, Coconut Development Board, Kera Bhavan, SRV Road, Kochi – 682 011. Include attested APARs for the last 5 years, the Integrity Certificate and the Vigilance Clearance Certificate.
Q8How long is the deputation period at CDB?
A
The deputation period shall ordinarily not exceed three years. This includes any deputation in another ex-cadre post held immediately before this appointment.
Q9Is there a trade test for the CDB Driver posts?
A
Yes. For both Driver Grade I and Driver Grade II, eligibility is subject to passing a trade test of appropriate standard specified by the Central Government.
