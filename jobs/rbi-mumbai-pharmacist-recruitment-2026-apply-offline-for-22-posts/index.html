Where and How to Send

Applications must be sent in a sealed cover super-scribed as ‘Application for the post of Pharmacist on contract basis with fixed hourly remuneration, Mumbai’ to the following postal address: The Regional Director, Human Resource Management Department, Recruitment Section, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Regional Office, Shahid Bhagat Singh Road, Fort, Mumbai – 400001 Candidates must use the prescribed application format given in Annex-I of the notification. Incomplete applications, applications not in the prescribed format, or applications unaccompanied by copies of requisite documents and certificates will be summarily rejected. Applicants must also ensure that the requisite details are filled in through the official link provided by the Bank.

Posts, Eligibility and Pay

Matriculation or its equivalent examination. Minimum qualification of Diploma in Pharmacy from a recognized University registered under the Pharmacy Act, 1948, OR Bachelor’s Degree (B. Pharm) in Pharmacy. Must be registered with the Maharashtra State Pharmacy Council. Minimum experience of two (02) years as a Pharmacist. Experience with any PSBs, PSUs, or Government Organizations will be given preference. Basic working knowledge of computers is required. Not specified in the notification.

RBI Mumbai Pharmacist Job Description

To work in any of the Bank’s dispensaries located within the jurisdiction of Mumbai and Navi Mumbai during working hours. To attend dispensaries for a maximum period of five (05) hours per day as per the requirements of the Bank. To maintain strict secrecy regarding the Bank's affairs and serve the Bank honestly and loyally with high integrity. To comply with and obey all orders and directions issued by authorised Bank employees.

Enclosures

Self-attested photocopies of Professional, Academic, and Other qualifications. Caste certificate in the prescribed proforma ONLY, meant for appointment under the Government of India (if seeking reservation as SC, ST, or OBC). For OBC candidates, certificate must be issued after April 01, 2026, with Non-Creamy Layer clause. For EWS candidates, an Income and Asset Certificate for Financial Year 2025-26 valid for Year 2026-27. Experience certificate(s). Age proof (such as Matriculation certificate). Copy of registration certificate issued by the Maharashtra State Pharmacy Council.

RBI Mumbai Pharmacist Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Candidates will be shortlisted on the basis of overall educational qualifications (PG, Degree, Diploma), distance of residence from various Bank’s dispensaries, and experience with PSBs, PSUs, Government organizations, or RBI. Shortlisted candidates will be called for an interview. Candidates shortlisted after the interview will be subjected to a medical examination and document verification process before being placed on a panel for engagement as and when requirements arise.

RBI Mumbai Pharmacist Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions
