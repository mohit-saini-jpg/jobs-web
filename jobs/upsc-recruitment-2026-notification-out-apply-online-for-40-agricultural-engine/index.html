UPSC Recruitment 2026: Selection Process

Selection to these posts is made through Recruitment Test (RT), wherever applicable, followed by an Interview, or by Interview alone. The minimum level of suitability in the interview is 50 marks for UR, 45 marks for OBC, and 40 marks for SC/ST/PwBD candidates out of 100 total marks. Candidates must meet the minimum suitability criteria at both the RT and Interview stages, wherever an RT is conducted.
