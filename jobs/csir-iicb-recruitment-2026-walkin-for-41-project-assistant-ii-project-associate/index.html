CSIR IICB Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

CSIR-Indian Institute of Chemical Biology (Council of Scientific & Industrial Research) will hold a walk-in interview on 3 November 2026 (Tuesday). Candidates must report by 08:45 A.M. at CSIR–Indian Institute of Chemical Biology (CSIR IICB), 4, Raja S.C. Mullick Road, Jadavpur, Kolkata – 700032. Under Advertisement No. R&C/592/2026 dated 17.10.2026, around 41 candidates will be selected for temporary engagement as Senior Project Associate, Project Associate II, Project Associate I and Project Assistant II under different projects. Pay ranges from ₹20,000/- p.m. + HRA to ₹42,000/- p.m. + HRA.

CSIR IICB Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1When is the CSIR-IICB walk-in interview for 2026?
A
The CSIR-IICB walk-in interview under Advertisement No. R&C/592/2026 will be held on 3 November 2026 (Tuesday).
Q2What is the reporting time and venue for the CSIR-IICB walk-in?
A
The reporting time is 08:45 A.M. and the entry gate closes at 10:00 A.M. The venue is CSIR–Indian Institute of Chemical Biology (CSIR-IICB), 4, Raja S.C. Mullick Road, Jadavpur, Kolkata – 700032.
Q3How many vacancies are there in CSIR-IICB Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 41 vacancies: 12 Senior Project Associate, 12 Project Associate-II, 16 Project Associate-I and 1 Project Assistant-II. The notice says the number may increase or decrease.
Q4What qualification is needed for CSIR-IICB Recruitment 2026?
A
The qualification differs for each vacancy code. For example, Project Assistant-II (5922609) needs a B.Sc./3 years Diploma in Engineering/Technology, while Project Associate-I codes 5922633 to 5922636 need an M.Sc in Chemistry.
Q5What is the age limit for CSIR-IICB project posts?
A
The age limit is 40 years for Senior Project Associate and 35 years for the other posts, reckoned as on the date of interview. SC/ST/PwBD/Women get 05 years of relaxation and OBC candidates get 03 years.
Q6What is the salary for CSIR-IICB Project Associate posts?
A
Project Associate-I gets Rs. 31,000/- p.m. + HRA with NET/GATE or equivalent, or Rs. 25,000/- + HRA otherwise. Project Associate-II gets Rs. 35,000/- or Rs. 28,000/- p.m. + HRA on the same basis, except code 5922639, which pays ₹28,000/- p.m. + HRA.
Q7What documents should I bring to the CSIR-IICB walk-in?
A
Bring the filled Application Form along with the originals and self-attested copies of your date-of-birth proof, all mark-sheets and certificates since Matriculation, and your final degree mark sheet or Ph.D award certificate. Also bring NET/GATE proof, an NOC if you are employed, one passport size photograph and a caste certificate where applicable.
Q8Can I apply for more than one vacancy code at CSIR-IICB?
A
Yes. If the vacancy codes fall under different Selection Committees, submit a separate application form for each Committee and write all the relevant vacancy codes on each form.
Q9Is the CSIR-IICB project job permanent?
A
No. The engagement is purely temporary and co-terminus with the project, and it gives no claim to regularization or absorption. The total tenure cannot exceed six years, including engagement in all CSIR labs.
Q10Can final-semester students attend the CSIR-IICB walk-in?
A
No. Candidates who are in the final semester or whose results are awaited are not eligible for these positions.
