BSIP Scientist B Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

The selection process for BSIP Scientist 'B' posts involves the following stages: All applications received within the due date will be screened by a Screening Committee. Only candidates recommended by the Screening Committee, after approval of the Competent Authority, will be called for a personal interview. Meeting the minimum advertised qualification and experience does not automatically guarantee a call for an interview. The decision of the Institute regarding shortlisting is final, and no correspondence will be entertained in this regard.
