NICL AO Selection Process 2026

The NICL AO selection process consists of the following stages: Phase-I Preliminary Examination Phase-II Main Examination Interview Final Merit List Pre-Employment Medical Examination The Phase-I Preliminary Examination will be conducted online for 100 marks with a duration of 60 minutes . Each section has a separate time limit. Candidates must qualify in each section by securing the cut-off marks decided by NICL. Approximately 15 times the number of vacancies, subject to availability, may be shortlisted for the Main Examination. The Main Examination also includes a 30-mark English Descriptive Test of 30 minutes . The Descriptive Test is qualifying in nature. Its marks will not be counted for shortlisting candidates for the Interview or for final selection. There is a penalty for wrong answers in the objective tests of both Phase-I and Phase-II.
