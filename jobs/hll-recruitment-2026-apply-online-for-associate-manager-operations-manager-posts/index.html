HLL Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

HLL Lifecare Limited, a Government of India enterprise under the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, is filling one Executive – Fixed Term Contract position of Associate Manager / Operations Manager (Civil/Structural) in the HCD (Projects & Services – Division), with posting at Faridkot. Basic pay is Rs. 25,000 – 45,000 per month for Associate Manager and Rs. 30,000 – 50,000 per month for Operations Manager. Applicants need BE / B.Tech (Civil) or Diploma in Engineering (Civil) with the stated experience and apply through a Google Form from 3 November 2026 to 18 November 2026.

About HLL

HLL Lifecare Limited is a Government of India enterprise under the administrative control of the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare. The notice says it commenced operations on 5 April 1969 and now works across healthcare manufacturing, retail pharmacy, diagnostics, imaging services, healthcare infrastructure development, procurement consultancy and hospital management. It states an annual business turnover of approximately ₹6,500 crore.

Who Can Apply

The same qualification applies to both designations. The experience required depends on the designation and on whether the candidate holds a degree or a diploma. The experience must be in the planning, design, structural engineering, execution, supervision, quality control, testing and maintenance of civil and structural infrastructure for hospitals, medical colleges, super-specialty healthcare facilities, diagnostic centres, laboratories and allied healthcare projects. The maximum age is counted as on 01.11.2026. The notice states that the crucial date for the age limit is the same as the cut-off date for calculating qualification and post-qualification experience. SC/ST/OBC/PwD candidates are eligible for relaxation as per Government of India directives. Only Indian Nationals are eligible to apply. The notice does not mention a minimum age.

HLL Recruitment 2026 Job Description

Supervise civil / structural works at project sites, ensuring adherence to drawings, specifications, quality standards and timelines. Review structural drawings, designs, estimates and BOQs; verify measurements and contractor bills. Coordinate with contractors, structural consultants, architects and client authorities for smooth execution of projects. Ensure compliance with safety, statutory and quality requirements at site. Prepare progress reports and project documentation; support project handing over. Any other work assigned by the management from time to time.
