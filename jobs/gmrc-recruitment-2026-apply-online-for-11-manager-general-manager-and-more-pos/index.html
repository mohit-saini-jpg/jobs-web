PostExperience Requirement

Asst. Manager (Rolling Stock)Minimum 7 years relevant post-qualification experience at Supervisory grade in a reputed private organization, OR 5 years at Supervisory grade in Government/PSU, related to Rolling Stock systems

Engineer - Jr. Grade (Rolling Stock)Minimum 2 years relevant experience in planning, technical specification, contract administration, installation, testing, commissioning, statutory approvals, or maintenance of Rolling Stock systems

GMRC Recruitment 2026 - Selection Process

The selection process comprises Personal Interviews, followed by Document Verification and Medical Examination as per the category prescribed for the post. Preference is given to candidates with experience in the same grade or immediate lower grade with 2 years' experience in that grade. If sufficient suitable candidates are not available, candidates from a lower pay scale meeting the prescribed qualifications, total experience, and age criteria may be considered. The selection process evaluates knowledge, skills, experience, expertise, aptitude, and physical fitness. The venue, date, and time of the interview will be informed in advance; requests for change in date or venue will not be entertained. Document verification of candidates will be done before the interview. Candidates must carry original certificates; failing this, they will not be allowed to attend the interview.
