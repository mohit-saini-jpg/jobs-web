Kerala Public Enterprises Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

The KPESRB notifications provide for selection through the written examination, skill test or interview, as applicable. However, they do not prescribe one identical selection procedure for all 17 categories. The Board will communicate recruitment-stage details to eligible candidates through the registered email address, mobile number and official website. Applications and participation in subsequent selection stages will remain provisional until the Board verifies eligibility and credentials. Important: The supplied notifications do not provide a common written examination date, syllabus, examination pattern or interview schedule for these 33 vacancies. Candidates should follow the category-specific selection announcements.
