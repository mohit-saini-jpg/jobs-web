SRCC Teaching Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Candidates applying for SRCC Assistant Professor and Director Physical Education posts must satisfy the educational qualifications and other eligibility requirements prescribed by the University of Delhi and UGC.

SRCC Assistant Professor Qualification 2026

Master's Degree with at least 55% marks or equivalent grade from a recognized university. Candidates must fulfil UGC eligibility requirements. NET conducted by UGC/CSIR is required unless exempted according to applicable UGC rules. Ph.D. holders fulfilling UGC regulations may also be considered as per applicable provisions.

SRCC Director Physical Education Qualification 2026

Master's Degree in Physical Education and Sports or Physical Education or Sports Science with 55% marks. Record of representing University/College at inter-university/inter-collegiate competitions or State/National championships. NET qualification as applicable. Must have passed the prescribed physical fitness test. Alternatively, candidates who are Asian Games or Commonwealth Games medal winners with a degree at least at Post-Graduate level may also be considered.
