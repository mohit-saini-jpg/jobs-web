Punjabi University Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Obtain the prescribed form by hand for Rs 100/- from the in-charge of the University Enquiry and Information Centre at the University main gate.
  • 2Or obtain it by post by sending a demand draft for the form price in favour of Registrar, Punjabi University, Patiala with a 25x10 cm envelope bearing Rs 65/- postage stamps and the post name.
  • 3Or download the form from punjabiuniversity.ac.in and send it with a demand draft of Rs 100 as its price.
  • 4Submit a separate application with a separate fee for each post/subject.
  • 5Send two self-addressed 9"x4" envelopes, each with Rs 65 postage stamps, with the form.
  • 6Applications must reach the office of the Deputy Registrar (Establishment) by 26.11.2026.
