HCL FTE Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Experience is reckoned only from the date of obtaining the basic/essential qualification prescribed for the post (i.e., from the date of result publication of that qualification). Experience is counted only if in the relevant field – working experience in the advertised cadre/discipline in a Mining/Metal/Manufacturing industry, or in any other industry subject to submission of an Experience Certificate meeting HCL’s domain requirement (assessed at document verification). Applicants must upload at least 2 of the following for each employment: Appointment Letter, Pay Slip, Form-16, PF Statement, or Bank Statement (towards salary credit). Failure to do so leads to cancellation of candidature. Experience gained through Training, Consultancy, Teaching, Fellowship, Internship, Apprenticeship, or Academic Project is NOT counted as experience.

HCL FTE Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection is based entirely on performance in the Personal Interview (PI) – 100% weightage . There is no written exam by default, though HCL reserves the right to conduct a written screening test to shortlist candidates for the interview, if required. Candidates are called for interview in the ratio of 1:5 (5 candidates per advertised vacancy); for a single vacancy, 10 candidates are shortlisted. If fewer than 5 applications are received for a post, the Selection Committee will assess and state the suitability of the interviewed candidates directly. Further shortlisting may be done from the eligible pool if posts remain vacant due to low turnout or an exhausted waitlist.
