About Kalaburagi District Court

The notice is issued by the Court of the Principal District and Sessions Judge, Kalaburagi, and signed by G.L. Lakshminarayana, Principal District and Sessions Judge, Kalaburagi. The four Peon posts are vacancies in the courts of Kalaburagi district under the Kalyana Karnataka residual cadre, and recruitment is made under the Karnataka Subordinate Courts (Ministerial and Other Posts) (Recruitment) Rules, 1982 and the amendment rules of 2007 and 2021.

Vacancy Details

The notice lists 04 Peon (Jawan) posts with the following reservation break-up. As per Government Order No. CASUE 02 SEHIMA 2026 dated 13.05.2026, reservation has been kept separately for Scheduled Caste categories A, B and C. If eligible candidates are not available for the women reservation, those posts will be filled by other candidates of the respective categories (G.O No. DPAR 34 Seneni 95, Bengaluru dated 15th July, 1996).

Who Can Apply

Must have passed the SSLC examination conducted by the Karnataka Secondary Education Examination Board, Government of Karnataka, or hold an equivalent qualification, on the last date fixed for filling applications. Must be able to read and write Kannada. Candidates whose 10th marks are in grades must have them converted into marks by the head of the concerned school and enter the total percentage correctly out of 100 in the application; applications with discrepancies will be rejected. Candidates are directed to the official notification for the full eligibility conditions of the post. Age is counted as on the last date fixed for receiving applications. Pay and Tenure

The Peon post carries the pay scale 27000-650-29600-725-32500-800-35700-900-39300-1000-43300-1125-46675 and other allowances. Selected candidates will be on probation for 2 years under the Karnataka Civil Services (Probation) Rules, 1977. Candidates already in government service must follow Rule 11 of the Karnataka Civil Services (General Recruitment) Rules, 1977, as amended.

Application Fee

The fee is paid through the link on the court website by online payment through net banking, Credit Card, Debit Card or UPI. Challan, demand draft, postal order or money order is not accepted, and applications without the fee will be rejected. The last date for fee payment is 17 December 2026, within State Bank of India branch transaction hours; if that day is a holiday, the next bank working day is the last date. Fee once paid will not be refunded under any circumstances.

Selection and Interview

Candidates will be called for interview in the ratio 1:10 (10 candidates per post) on the basis of the overall percentage of marks in the qualifying examination, according to the posts announced. Selection will be made on the marks obtained in the interview, which carries 10 marks. Eligible candidates will be informed by SMS and e-mail, and the list of candidates eligible for interview will be published on the court website; candidates must download this list and produce it when they attend the interview.
