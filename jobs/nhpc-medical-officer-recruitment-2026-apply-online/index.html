About NHPC

NHPC Limited describes itself as the largest hydropower development organization in India, with capabilities from conceptualization to commissioning of hydro projects. According to the notice, NHPC has also diversified into Solar and Wind energy development. These Medical Officer posts are for its Kishanganga Power Station and Sawalkote HEP in Jammu and Kashmir.

Department-wise Vacancies

NHPC Limited has notified 02 Medical Officer vacancies in total. The notice does not name a department; the posts are listed by posting location. Candidates may apply for either one location or both locations, as per their preference. Separate merit lists are prepared for each location, based on performance in the Personal Interview. NHPC reserves the right to increase or decrease the vacancies as per requirement.

Q1How many Medical Officer vacancies has NHPC announced?

Q2What is the last date to apply for NHPC Medical Officer?
A
The application form must be emailed on or before 18 November 2026. The Personal Interview is on 19 November 2026, with reporting time 8 AM.
Q3What is the qualification for NHPC Medical Officer?
A
An MBBS Degree with valid registration is required; no percentage is fixed and pass candidates are eligible. Two years' post-internship experience as a Doctor in Govt./PSU or reputed/leading hospitals/organizations/industrial establishments is also required.

The maximum age is 35 years as on the date of the interview, and candidates must be Indian Nationals aged 18 years or above. No age relaxation applies to any category.

The fixed monthly consolidated amount is Rs. 1,00,000/-. Conveyance reimbursement, communication expenses, travelling allowance, medical benefits and leaves are also admissible as per NHPC Rules & Procedures for Contract Employment on Fixed Tenure Basis.
Q6How do I apply for the NHPC Medical Officer post?
A
Email the duly filled application form in the prescribed format to [email protected] on or before 18.11.2026, then report for the interview on 19.11.2026 at NHPC Regional Office, JDA Commercial Complex, Plot No 1, Narwal, Jammu.

A
Selection is by Personal Interview only, after document verification on the interview day. The merit panel is based solely on interview performance, with minimum qualifying marks of 40% out of 100 for all candidates.
Q8Is the NHPC Medical Officer job permanent?

Q9Can I apply for both Gurez and Sawalkote locations?
