JKSSB Junior Engineer Mechanical Selection Process

The selection for the post of Junior Engineer (Mechanical) will be made on the basis of merit obtained in the written/OMR examination only. Shortlisted candidates are then called for Document Verification. The examination consists of Objective Type, Multiple Choice Questions, set in English only. There is negative marking of one-fourth of the marks assigned to each question for every wrong answer. The syllabus will be notified separately on the official JKSSB website. The date and centres of the examination will be notified separately on www.jkssb.nic.in. No request for a change of centre is entertained. The number of questions, total marks and duration of the examination are not specified in the notification. JKSSB JE Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

Only one online application is allowed for the same item number. If more than one is detected, the latest application is considered. Appearing in the examination more than once for the same item number leads to cancellation and debarment. No change or correction in the application form is entertained after submission, including preferences for cadre and department. Applications with a blurred or illegible photograph or signature are summarily rejected. Candidature remains provisional until documents are verified by the Appointing Authority. Ineligible candidates will not be allowed in later stages. A claim for reservation based on an invalid, false or forged certificate leads to debarment from JKSSB examinations and penal action. Reserved category applicants using a certificate format other than the prescribed one will not be entertained. PwBD candidates can avail a scribe and/or compensatory time of 20 minutes per hour of examination on production of the Annexure-B certificate and the Annexure-C undertaking. The candidate must arrange their own scribe, and the scribe must not be a candidate for the same examination or a relative. Mobile phones, smart watches, smart glasses, AI-enabled devices, Bluetooth devices and similar electronic equipment, even if switched off, are prohibited in the examination hall. Roll number slips are not issued by post. Candidates must visit the JKSSB website regularly for updates, and carry two recent passport size photographs and an original photo-ID proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Driving License, PAN Card, Passport, School/College/University I-Card or Employer ID Card) to the examination. Candidates should apply well before the last date to avoid website overload during the closing days. Submission of the form implies consent to security screening, including physical frisking, metal detector scanning, biometric capture, facial recognition and Aadhaar authentication. The decision of the Board on eligibility, acceptance or rejection of applications, conduct of examinations and the merit list is final and binding.
