BCCL Full-time Advisor (Excavation) Job Description

The broad scope of work includes: Advise on excavation planning and optimum deployment of HEMM. Assist in assessment of HEMM fleet requirements, capacity and replacement needs. Advise on fleet optimization, mechanization and equipment standardization. Review availability, utilization and productivity of major HEMM. Identify critical issues affecting equipment performance and suggest corrective measures. Advise on measures for improving equipment productivity and utilization. Provide expert inputs on procurement/replacement of major HEMM. Review technical specifications, equipment capacity and suitability wherever required. Advise on life-cycle considerations and optimum utilization of capital equipment.
