RTMNU Assistant Professor Salary 2026

Selected candidates will receive a consolidated salary of Rs. 40,000/- per month. The appointment is purely contractual, valid only from the date of joining until 30th April 2027, with no enforceable right to continuation in the succeeding academic session. Selected candidates are permitted only 8 casual leaves during the academic session, with no other leave or allowances admissible.

RTMNU Assistant Professor Recruitment Expert Analysis

This recruitment notification is a great opportunity for candidates who want to join the prestigious RTMNU as Assistant Professors. The university is known for its academic excellence and research opportunities.

Who Should Apply RTMNU Assistant Professor Recruitment

Ideal candidates for this post are Ph.D. holders in relevant subjects with a good academic record and teaching experience. They should be well-versed in their subject matter and have excellent communication skills.

RTMNU Assistant Professor Recruitment Preparation Tips

To increase their chances of selection, candidates should focus on improving their teaching skills, preparing for common interview questions, and practicing their presentation skills.

RTMNU Assistant Professor Recruitment Salary Insights

The salary for RTMNU Assistant Professors is between ₹40,000 to ₹80,000 per month. This is a competitive salary compared to other government jobs in Maharashtra. With experience and performance, candidates can expect a salary growth of up to ₹1 lakh per month.

RTMNU Assistant Professor Recruitment Job Profile

As an Assistant Professor at RTMNU, the selected candidate will be responsible for teaching undergraduate and postgraduate students, conducting research, and publishing papers in reputed journals. They will also be involved in curriculum development and academic administration.

RTMNU Assistant Professor Recruitment Selection Strategy

To get selected, candidates should focus on their teaching skills, subject matter expertise, and communication skills. They should also be prepared to answer common interview questions and provide examples of their teaching experience.
