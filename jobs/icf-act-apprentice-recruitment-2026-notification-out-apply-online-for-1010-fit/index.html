ICF Act Apprentice Eligibility Criteria

Candidates with higher qualifications than 10th/Matric/SSLC, such as Degree, Diploma, Engineering Degree, or Course Completed Act Apprentices, are not eligible to apply for Act Apprenticeship, except for MLT where the qualification is 12th/HSC. Applicants must be resident and/or registered with a local Employment Exchange within Tamil Nadu, with registration date not beyond the application's last date, except wards of Railway employees. Wards of Railway employees must submit the Railway employee's Identity Card/Service Certificate (attested by a Gazetted Officer), along with UMID card and PPO copy as proof. Vacancies for Persons with Benchmark Disabilities are reserved trade-wise, subject to permissible disability categories and a minimum of 40% relevant disability; a disability certificate from the Central/State Government is required to claim this benefit.

ICF Act Apprentice Age Limit

Age will be reckoned as on 7 September 2026. ITI candidates: must have completed 15 years and not completed 24 years of age. Non-ITI candidates: must have completed 15 years and not completed 22 years of age. Upper age relaxation: 5 years for SC/ST candidates, 3 years for OBC candidates. Upper age relaxation for Persons with Disability: 10 years. SC/ST candidates must produce a caste certificate, and OBC candidates must produce an OBC Non-Creamy Layer certificate, at the time of Document Verification. Candidates are advised to check whether they satisfy the prescribed age limit before applying. Use our online Age Calculator to calculate your exact age instantly.

ICF Act Apprentice Stipend

The prescribed minimum stipend increases by 10 percent during the second year of apprenticeship training. For NCVT/SCVT certificate holders, any training period already undergone is factored into determining the minimum payable stipend.

ICF Act Apprentice Application Fee

A processing fee of ₹100 plus applicable service charges (non-refundable) must be paid online. No application fee is required for SC, ST, PwBD, and Women candidates. No request for refund of fee once remitted will be entertained under any circumstances. After successful payment (for candidates other than SC/ST/PwBD/Women), a unique Transaction ID starting with "DU" is sent to the registered mobile number and should be preserved for future reference.

ICF Act Apprentice Selection Process

The merit list is prepared based on marks obtained in Std X. For COVID-pass candidates in Std X, proof of the IX std mark sheet or X std half-yearly mark sheet duly signed by the Principal will be considered. If two candidates have equal marks, the candidate with older age is preferred; if dates of birth are also the same, the candidate who passed Std X earlier is considered first. Freshers exercise trade options, and trades are allotted based on merit order and vacancy availability; once allotted, requests for change of trade are not entertained. Candidates selected against benchmark disabilities are allotted only the trade permissible to them. Ex-ITI candidates are trained in the trade for which they hold an ITI certificate. ICF Act Apprentice Important Instructions

This notification is purely for apprenticeship training and not for employment; no employment is guaranteed to apprentices on completion of training. Imparting training at ICF confers no right to absorption in Railways after successful completion, as per the Apprenticeship Rules, 1991. Canvassing in any form will disqualify the candidature, and no correspondence will be entertained in this regard. If any declaration furnished by a candidate is found false, they will be disqualified or terminated at any stage of training without notice. Accommodation must be arranged at the candidate's own cost; ICF will not provide food or accommodation. Candidates selected for training will not be allowed to withdraw except for reasons beyond their control; those who wilfully remain absent are liable to refund the full training cost and stipend paid.
