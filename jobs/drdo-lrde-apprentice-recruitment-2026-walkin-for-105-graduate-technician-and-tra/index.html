DRDO LRDE Apprentice Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

LRDE will select candidates based on the number of vacancies, applications and eligible applicants in the respective categories. The notification provides for the following assessment methods, as required: Verification of NATS or NAPS registration. Scrutiny of educational qualifications and eligibility. Shortlisting based on academic merit. Written test, where required. Interview, where required. Verification of original certificates. Character and antecedent verification. Medical fitness certification. Execution of the apprenticeship contract. Commencement of one-year training. Important: The notification does not prescribe a fixed written examination pattern, syllabus or mandatory written test for every category. LRDE may use academic merit, a written test or an interview according to its recruitment requirements.
