NMDC Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Trade Apprentice candidates register and get approval in advance on the NAPS portal (https://www.apprenticeshipindia.gov.in).
  • 2Graduate and Technician (Diploma) Apprentice candidates register and get approval in advance on the NATS portal (https://nats.education.gov.in).
  • 3Reach Training Institute, B.I.O.M, Kirandul Complex, Kirandul, Dantewada on the walk-in date for your category before 01:00 PM.
  • 4Register at the interview help desk and fill the Bio-data form provided there with one passport size photograph.
  • 5Fill the online Google Form provided at the Help Desk on the date of interview.
  • 6Bring portal profile proof, age proof copy, original qualification and caste certificates.
  • 7Attend on 14 December 2026.

NMDC Apprentice Recruitment (247 Posts) Expert Analysis

NMDC’s walk-in recruitment is a quick way to secure a stable government job. The salary band is competitive compared to other state government apprenticeships, and the roles offer hands‑on experience in heavy industry.

Who Should Apply NMDC Apprentice Recruitment (247 Posts)

Ideal candidates are engineering graduates, diploma holders in machining, fitting or welding, with a keen interest in industrial work and a willingness to relocate to Chhattisgarh.

NMDC Apprentice Recruitment (247 Posts) Preparation Tips

1. Brush up on basic mechanical and electrical concepts. 2. Practice quick problem solving and technical questions. 3. Arrive early, carry all required documents, and dress formally.

NMDC Apprentice Recruitment (247 Posts) Salary Insights

Starting salary ₹18,000 is on par with other government apprenticeships, while the top end ₹92,300 matches senior trade roles in public sector units. Career growth includes promotions to senior technician and managerial positions.

NMDC Apprentice Recruitment (247 Posts) Job Profile

As a Trade Apprentice, you’ll work on machine maintenance, fitting, and welding tasks under supervision. Daily duties involve tool handling, quality checks, and safety compliance. Long term, you can advance to senior technician, project engineer, or supervisory roles.

NMDC Apprentice Recruitment (247 Posts) Selection Strategy

Focus on technical knowledge, practical skills, and punctuality. Demonstrate teamwork, safety awareness, and a willingness to learn. Highlight any internship or project experience related to machining or welding.
