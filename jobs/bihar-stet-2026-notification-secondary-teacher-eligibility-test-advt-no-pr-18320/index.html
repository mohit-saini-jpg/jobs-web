Bihar STET 2026 Papers & Subjects

STET 2026 is held for two separate papers based on teaching level. Candidates may apply for either paper or both, subject to eligibility. A separate category for Special School Teacher (Divyang/Special Education) is also notified under Paper-I, with distinct qualification norms as per National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE) and Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI) guidelines. Note: Multi Media & Web Technology under Paper-II is applicable only where this subject is taught at the school level; candidates need not qualify separately in it for general eligibility.

Bihar STET 2026 Eligibility

Separate detailed qualification norms (Parishisht-1 for Paper-I and Parishisht-2 for Paper-II) apply for Physical Education, Music, Fine Arts, Dance, and Special School Teacher categories, including sports-quota and NCC-linked pathways. Candidates should verify the exact clause applicable to their subject in the official notification. The candidate must be a citizen of India. Important: Any dispute regarding recognition of a qualification/institution, or equivalence of a degree, will be decided by the Department in consultation with the National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE), and that decision will be final.
PaperMinimum Requirement General Subjects
Paper-I (Secondary)Graduation with at least 50% marks in the relevant subject/subject group from a recognized university, along with a B.Ed. (or equivalent) from an NCTE-recognized institution. Relaxed norms (45% + B.Ed.) apply where prescribed under NCTE Regulations 2002/2007.
Paper-II (Higher Secondary)Post-graduation with at least 50% marks in the relevant subject from a recognized university, along with a B.Ed. (or equivalent) from an NCTE-recognized institution. Relaxed norms (45% + B.Ed., or 55% + integrated B.Ed.-M.Ed.) apply as prescribed.

Bihar STET 2026 Exam Fee & Qualifying Marks

Fee is payable online via Debit Card, Credit Card, Net Banking, or UPI. No other mode of fee payment is accepted. Candidates domiciled outside Bihar will be treated as General category (male/female) and must pay the fee applicable to the General category. Bihar STET 2026 Exam Pattern

Exam duration: 2 hours 30 minutes per paper. Question type: Objective/Multiple-choice, delivered via Computer Based Test (CBT). No negative marking for wrong answers. Since the exam is held across multiple shifts/sittings, a Normalization method is applied to the raw score to ensure candidates are not disadvantaged or advantaged by shift-wise difficulty variation; the final result is based on the Normalized Score, not the Raw Score.

Important Conditions

Candidates should download the application draft first, fill it carefully with a pen, then transcribe the details online, to avoid entry errors. The admit card will be generated strictly based on the details filled in the online application; no correction request will be entertained once the admit card is issued. Candidates must keep their User ID and Password confidential and not share them with anyone, including cyber café operators. If the qualification/subject group mentioned in the application does not match the notified eligibility, the candidature and result may be cancelled at any stage. Candidates applying for the EWS category for STET 2026 must correctly mark "General" as their category in the online application (a common error was noted where EBC candidates mistakenly selected EWS). Result and admit card will be prepared based on the reservation category marked by the candidat
