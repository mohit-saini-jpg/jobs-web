Pay and Terms

Remuneration & Perquisites: Negotiable and not a limiting factor for deserving candidate. Period of Engagement: Fixed Term Employment (FTE) on contractual basis for an initial period of 3 years, extendable upto 5 years; to be renewed yearly or in less intervals subject to performance review, needs and requirements of the Bank. Leave: 30 days per calendar year on pro-rata basis. Leave balance will not be carried forward to subsequent years and no encashment of leave is permitted. Place of work: Chennai or as decided by the Bank. Termination of Contract: Three months' notice or three month's fixed component of the compensation in lieu thereof from either of the parties, at the sole discretion of the Bank. The engagement is governed by the contract and is not to be construed as an employment or a regular employment in the Bank; it ends automatically on completion of the contractual period.

Indian Bank Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection will be by (1) scrutiny of applications by a Screening Committee and (2) interview of shortlisted candidates by a Selection Committee. If the number of applicants is substantially large, a Written Test / Group Discussion / Preliminary interview process, or a combination of these, may be adopted. Only candidates who fulfil the eligibility criteria will be called for interview and informed by email. If a written test is held, the notice's scribe and compensatory-time guidelines for Persons with Benchmark Disabilities apply, including 20 minutes per hour of compensatory time for eligible candidates and the candidate arranging his / her own scribe at his/her own cost.
