Dr. M.G.R. Medical University MBBS Time Table 2026 : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 11/09/2026

The Tamil Nadu Dr. M.G.R. Medical University (TNMGRMU), Chennai, has released the official time table for the M.B.B.S. Degree Examinations - September 2026, First Year & First Professional (CBME) Supplementary Examinations. The notification, signed by the Controller of Examinations, covers two separate candidate groups with their own subject-wise schedules.

Important Dates

All examinations in both groups are held on the same set of dates, with a 3-hour window from 9.30 AM to 12.30 PM.

  • 21 September 2026 (Monday): Anatomy Paper I
  • 22 September 2026 (Tuesday): Anatomy Paper II
  • 24 September 2026 (Thursday): Physiology Paper I
  • 25 September 2026 (Friday): Physiology Paper II
  • 28 September 2026 (Monday): Biochemistry Paper I
  • 29 September 2026 (Tuesday): Biochemistry Paper II

How to Download the Dr. M.G.R. Medical University MBBS Supplementary Time Table PDF

You can download the official time table either directly or by navigating the Dr. M.G.R. Medical University website. Both methods are given below.

Method 1: Direct Download (Fastest)

  • Click on the official PDF link: MBBS First Year & First Professional (CBME) Supplementary Exam Time Table - September 2026.
  • The PDF will open in your browser.
  • Use your browser's download or save option to save the file to your device.

Method 2: Via the Official Dr. M.G.R. Medical University Website

  • Visit the official website: www.tnmgrmu.ac.in.
  • Go to the "Examinations" or "Notifications" section on the website.
  • Look for the M.B.B.S. Degree Examinations September 2026 - First Year & First Professional (CBME) Supplementary Examinations notification.
  • Click on the notification link to open or download the time table PDF.
  • Save or print the PDF for reference before the exam dates.

Important Instructions

  • The exam timing is uniform for both groups: 9.30 AM to 12.30 PM.
  • Candidates must appear for the subject and Q.P. code applicable to their batch/admission year, as listed in the tables above.
  • Any changes or corrigenda to this time table would be issued separately by the Controller of Examinations; always cross-check with the official website before the exam date.
  • This notification does not mention exam centre locations, reporting time, or seating arrangements - candidates should follow instructions from their respective colleges.
