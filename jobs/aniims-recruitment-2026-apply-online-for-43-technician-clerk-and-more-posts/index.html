ANIIMS Recruitment 2026 - Eligibility Criteria

Eligibility differs for each post. Key requirements include: Public Health Nurse/Rehabilitation Nurse (PHN-01): BSc. (Hons) Nursing from a recognized institute/university with 2 years of relevant experience. Statistician (STAT-02): Master's degree in statistics with familiarity in standard electronic statistical software; 2 years of health statistics experience is desirable. Occupational Therapist (OT-03): 10+2 in science (PCB) plus a Bachelor's degree in Occupational Therapy with 2 years of experience. Prosthetic and Orthotic Tech (POT-04): Bachelor's degree in Orthotics and Prosthetics, or a diploma in the same field with 2 years of workshop experience. Speech Therapist (ST-05) and Audiometry Technician (AT-06): BSc in Speech and Hearing; an MSc and clinical hospital experience are desirable. Health Educator (HE-07): Bachelor's degree in Botany/Zoology/Home Science/Nutrition/Chemistry/
