About Shillong Medical College

Shillong Medical College is a Government of Meghalaya institution under the Health & Family Welfare Department, located at Pasteur Hills, Lawmali Road, Shillong, Meghalaya – 793001. The notice invites applications for Professor and Associate Professor posts on a contractual basis. The Director, Shillong Medical College, is the authority named in the notice for queries and for any cancellation or postponement of the process.

Department-wise Vacancies

The notice lists 7 Professor posts and 4 Associate Professor posts, one in each department shown. Vacancies are provisional and may increase or decrease as per the actual requirement for teaching and patient care. The notice prints no category-wise break-up.

Pay and Terms

The notice states that the maximum tenure is till 70 years of age or the years of contract mentioned in the ToR, whichever is earlier, provided other rules and regulations are satisfied. The posts are full-time and private practice of any kind is strictly prohibited. Selected candidates receive an appointment letter. Appointment is subject to being declared medically fit by the Medical Board constituted, and to verification of documents, declaration and antecedents. No TA/DA is provided for attending the interview.

Shillong Medical College Faculty Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Applications are accepted by email only. Eligible candidates who wish to attend the interview must submit the scanned application form (Annexure-I) with supporting documents to [email protected] by 05:00 PM on 20 November 2026.
  • 2Fill in the application form given as Annexure-I in the notice and affix a self-photograph.
  • 3Attach self-attested copies of the documents listed below.
  • 4Combine the form and all documents into one single PDF. Multiple submissions will not be considered.
  • 5Email the PDF to [email protected] with the subject line: Application for the Position of Professor and Associate Professor at Shillong Medical College, Shillong.
  • 6Send it so that it reaches by 05:00 PM on 20 November 2026.
  • 7Submit a separate application for each post, in the prescribed format only; other formats or incomplete applications will be summarily rejected.
  • 8Applicants already employed in Central/State Government, autonomous institutions, statutory organisations or PSUs under Central/State Governments should route applications through proper channel. If that is not immediately available, a copy of "Applied for NOC" is to be submitted at the interview. The application form asks for post and department applied for, personal details, educational qualifications, medical council registration, present designation, academic records, previous teaching experience, number of publications, and completion of the Medical Education and Biomedical Research courses.
  • 9Selection is on the basis of academic credentials and performance in the interview before the Selection Board. The interview mode is offline. Candidates will be told by email of any additional documents needed and of the interview date at least 7 days before the scheduled date. Dates of interview for later cut-off applicants under the rolling advertisement will be notified in due course. Screening, communication, interviews and recruitment will be conducted in phases, depending on availability of experts, receipt of complete documents and the requirements of the position. Results will be displayed on https://shillongmedicalcollege.co.in and https://meghealth.gov.in, and the offer of appointment will be sent by email.
