SKAU Kurukshetra Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

The selection process varies by post and is generally conducted in two or three phases, based on a total merit of 100 marks. A summary of the selection process for each post is given below. For posts with negative marking (Assistant Registrar and SDE Civil), 0.25 marks are deducted for each incorrect answer, and no marks are awarded or deducted for an unanswered question. For all other posts, no marks are deducted for an incorrect or unanswered question. Shortlisting for Physical Document Verification and subsequent stages is generally done in a post-wise/category-wise ratio of 1:5, based on merit determined from the Written Test and provisional Academic Achievement marks taken together. In case of a tie, candidates are ranked first by age (older candidate senior), then by alphabetical order of name, and finally by alphabetical order of father's name.
