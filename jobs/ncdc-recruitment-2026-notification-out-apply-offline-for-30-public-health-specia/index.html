NCDC Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection will be conducted through screening of applications followed by an interview for eligible candidates. Scrutiny of submitted applications and supporting documents. Shortlisting based on the prescribed eligibility criteria and Terms of Reference (ToR). Interview of shortlisted eligible candidates. Final selection and contractual engagement, subject to applicable conditions. Only eligible shortlisted candidates will receive an email informing them of the interview date and time. The notification does not prescribe a separate written examination.

NCDC Recruitment 2026 Apply Offline

Eligible candidates must submit the prescribed application form through Speed Post or hand delivery to the General Services Section of NCDC, Delhi. Applications must reach the office on or before 9 November 2026 . General Services Section Room No. 202, National Centre for Disease Control (NCDC), 22, Sham Nath Marg, Delhi - 110054. Office Submission Hours: 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM on working days. Important: Applications received after 9 November 2026 will not be accepted. The date of dispatch is not a substitute for timely receipt by NCDC.

Documents Required for NCDC Recruitment 2026

Candidates should submit the supporting documents relevant to their qualifications and experience along with the prescribed application form. Completed application form. Self-attested photograph. Degree, diploma and professional qualification certificates. Relevant experience certificates. Age-proof document. Supporting documents for other claims made in the application. The application form also asks for reference details, training or short courses attended, awards or achievements, mailing address, permanent address, phone number and email ID.

NCDC Recruitment 2026 Contract Terms and Conditions

The initial contractual engagement is for one year. Continuation will depend on yearly requirements. Selected candidates must sign a Contractual Service Agreement at the time of joining. Contractual personnel will not be treated as regular NCDC employees. Contractual engagement does not provide any claim for permanent government appointment. No TA will be paid for attending the interview. No separate medical facility or additional financial allowance is provided beyond the consolidated remuneration under the stated conditions. Annual leave of 30 days and 10 days of casual or sick leave are provided as specified in the notice. Either party may terminate the service by giving one month's notice.
