VKSU Semester 5 Admit Card 2026 Released

Veer Kunwar Singh University, Ara, has activated the UG Semester V admit card download facility for students enrolled in the CBCS 2024-28 academic session.

The official examination portal published a notice dated 21 October 2026 stating that UG Semester V CBCS students of session 2024-28 should download their examination admit cards by logging in to their accounts.

The admit card is an important document for students appearing in the Semester 5 theory examination. It contains the candidate's examination information, including the allotted examination centre and applicable paper details.

Students should download the latest available admit card before the examination and verify all particulars carefully.

How to Download VKSU Semester 5 Admit Card 2026?

Eligible students can download their Semester 5 hall tickets by following these steps.

  • Visit the official VKSU examination website at vksuexams.com.
  • Locate the student login section on the homepage.
  • Enter the User ID or Registration Number.
  • Enter the registered password.
  • Complete the captcha verification shown on the screen.
  • Click the login button to access the student dashboard.
  • Open the Admit Card section for UG Semester V, session 2024-28.
  • Download the available examination admit card.
  • Check the student's personal details, subject information, examination schedule and centre.
  • Save the admit card and take a clear printout for examination use.

Students should follow the login fields displayed by the official portal. The current VKSU homepage requires a registration number or user ID and password, along with captcha verification.

VKSU Semester 5 Exam 2026: Earlier Schedule Postponed

VKSU had earlier scheduled the UG Semester V (CBCS) examinations for session 2024-28 to begin on 12 October 2026.

However, the university issued a postponement notification dated 7 October 2026, stating that the examination programme was postponed due to unavoidable reasons and that revised dates would be communicated later.

Students should therefore avoid relying on older examination schedules and download the latest hall ticket provided by the examination department.

VKSU Semester 5 Practical Exam Date 2026

Published revised-examination information indicates that practical examinations for UG Semester V are scheduled from 2 to 21 November 2026.

Practical examinations are expected to be organised at the relevant constituent or affiliated colleges according to their subject-wise arrangements.

Students should check their college noticeboard or contact their examination department for the practical schedule applicable to their subjects.

The exact practical examination dates and timings for individual colleges were not independently confirmed from a current university-issued timetable.

What to Do if VKSU Semester 5 Admit Card Is Not Downloading?

Students who are unable to access their admit cards should first check whether they are using the correct examination portal and valid login credentials.

  • Open the official VKSU examination website.
  • Check the registration number or user ID carefully.
  • Re-enter the registered password and captcha.
  • Use the Forgot Password or Reset Password facility if necessary.
  • Check whether the Semester V admit-card option is available in the student dashboard.
  • Contact the college examination office if the admit card is missing or displays incorrect details.
  • Use the university's examination helpline for additional assistance.

VKSU Semester 5 Admit Card 2026 Forgot Password

The official VKSU examination portal provides Forgot Password and Reset Password services.

Students who cannot remember their passwords should use the relevant recovery option shown on the login page and complete the verification requested by the portal.

After recovering access, students can sign in and check the Semester V admit-card section.

If recovery is unsuccessful, students should contact the university examination support team or their college.

VKSU Semester 5 Examination Centre 2026

The individual examination centre assigned to a student should be checked on the downloaded admit card.

VKSU students should verify the examination centre name, location and reporting instructions before the scheduled examination date.

The university may assign examination centres according to its administrative arrangements. Students should not assume that their regular college will necessarily be their theory examination centre.

VKSU Semester 5 Exam Day Instructions

  • Download and print the latest UG Semester V admit card.
  • Check the examination date, shift and centre before travelling.
  • Carry the documents required by the university's examination instructions.
  • Reach the examination centre within the prescribed reporting time.
  • Check the correct major or minor paper before entering the examination hall.
  • Follow all instructions issued by the examination invigilators.
  • Do not rely on the postponed examination programme if a revised timetable applies.

VKSU Semester 5 Examination Form and Eligibility

The official VKSU examination portal also provides information about Semester V examination form submission for the 2024-28 session.

An examination form notice dated 10 October 2026 extended the online form-filling period until 14 October 2026 with a late fee of Rs. 500.

The university specified that UG Semester V students must meet the applicable credit-score and previous-semester requirements for examination form activation.

Students who face an admit-card issue related to their examination form should contact their college or the examination department for verification.
