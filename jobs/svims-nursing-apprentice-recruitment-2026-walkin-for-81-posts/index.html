SVIMS Nursing Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The walk-in interview for Nursing Apprentices under the National Apprenticeship Training Scheme (NATS) is on 23 November 2026, with reporting from 09:00 AM to 11:30 AM at the 2nd floor, Sri Padmavathi Auditorium, SVIMS OPD, SVIMS, Alipiri Road, Tirupati–517507. Sri Venkateswara Institute of Medical Sciences, Tirupati (SVIMS) will engage 81 apprentices on a one-year training basis with a stipend of Rs.21,500 per month. The notice says persons professing the Hindu religion shall only apply.

About SVIMS

Sri Venkateswara Institute of Medical Sciences is based in Tirupati, with its OPD on Alipiri Road. This notice is issued by its Director-cum-VC under the heading "Walk-In Interviews" and applies the condition for persons professing the Hindu religion as provided for TTD institutions.

Posts and Eligibility

SVIMS invites eligible candidates for engagement of Nursing Apprentices under NATS on training basis for a period of one year. The 81 vacancies are tentative and may increase or decrease at the discretion of the management on reservation at the time of engagement. Completed B.Sc. Nursing / B.Sc. Hons. Nursing / Post Basic B.Sc. Nursing. Registered Nurse and Registered Midwife or equivalent with State Nursing Registration Council. B.Sc. Nursing Degree passed through regular mode within the last five years, that is on or after 2022 only. Registered on the NATS portal and applying against this notification. Medically fit. Qualification and age are counted as on the reckoning date, 30.06.2026. A candidate with a qualification equivalent to the prescribed one must enclose a copy of the Government orders to that effect with the application, failing which the application will be rejected. What to Bring

Original certificates of qualifications, with one set of Photostat copies as mentioned in the application form. NATS Registration form. Latest Caste certificate, Age proof and Aadhaar copy as Identity Proof. Two passport size photos. Copy of the Government orders, if the qualification held is equivalent to the one prescribed. Any other relevant certificates, whichever applicable. The application form and other details are available on the SVIMS website, https://svimstpt.ap.nic.in .

What Happens on the Day

Candidates report on 23-11-2026 between 09:00 AM and 11:30 AM; candidates arriving after the stipulated reporting time will not be allowed. Applications submitted at the walk-in interview are verified there and then to ascertain eligibility. The interview may be extended to the next day, so candidates should be available for the next day also, if required. On selection, the candidate has to join immediately.

Before You Attend: NATS Portal Requirements

Register on the NATS portal and ensure 100 percent updation of the profile; keep the registration/enrolment number. Register as an Apprentice in the category you are applying for. Keep an active email ID and mobile number valid for at least 1 year; document verification and offer letter communications go only through email/SMS alerts. Keep scanned copies of self-attested photocopies of the documents required for enrolment in the NATS portal, as applicable. Check all information before final submission of the enrolment form; no changes are entertained by SVIMS after final submission of the online application form. Hold a PAN Card, an Aadhaar Card, and a bank account with cheque book bearing your name.

SVIMS Nursing Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

The bank account must be seeded to Aadhaar and enabled for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT); if not, approach the bank branch to enable both. Aadhaar seeding can be checked at https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in/ and DBT status through the NPCI website, npci.org.in, under the consumer tab. Candidates who have already completed or are currently undergoing apprenticeship training are strictly ineligible to register or apply for another NATS Apprenticeship. No TA/DA will be paid for attending the walk-in interview. A selected candidate must complete the one-year training; leaving before completion means no Training Certificate from SVIMS or NATS. A selected candidate may be transferred and posted to any location as per requirement and necessity. The Institute has the right to modify or cancel the engagement notification at any time without assigning reasons.

SVIMS Nursing Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is the date of the SVIMS Nursing Apprentice walk-in interview?
A
The walk-in interview is on 23 November 2026. The notice says it may be extended to the next day.
Q2What is the reporting time and venue?
A
Reporting is from 09:00 AM to 11:30 AM at the 2nd floor, Sri Padmavathi Auditorium, SVIMS OPD, SVIMS, Alipiri Road, Tirupati–517507. Candidates arriving after the stipulated time will not be allowed.
Q3How many Nursing Apprentice vacancies are there?
A
There are 81 vacancies, which the notice calls tentative. They may increase or decrease at the discretion of the management on reservation at the time of engagement.
Q4What is the qualification for SVIMS Nursing Apprentices?
A
Candidates need B.Sc. Nursing / B.Sc. Hons. Nursing / Post Basic B.Sc. Nursing, passed through regular mode on or after 2022. They must also be a Registered Nurse and Registered Midwife or equivalent with the State Nursing Registration Council.
Q5What is the age limit?
A
The age limit is minimum 21 years and max. 27 years as on 30.06.2026. SC/ST get 5 years (up to 32 years), BC categories 3 years (up to 30 years), and PwBD up to 10 years (up to 15 for SC/ST, 13 for BC).
Q6What is the stipend?
A
The stipend is Rs.21,500 per month. Of this, Rs.15,350 is paid by SVIMS and Rs.6,150 by NATS, subject to statutory deductions.
Q7What documents should I bring to the walk-in interview?
A
Bring original qualification certificates with one set of photocopies, the NATS Registration form, latest Caste, Age and Aadhaar copy as identity proof, two passport size photos, and any other relevant certificates.
Q8Who can apply for this SVIMS recruitment?
A
Only persons professing the Hindu religion may apply, as per the provisions applicable to TTD institutions. Candidates must also be registered on the NATS portal and medically fit.
Q9Is this a permanent job?
A
No, it is an engagement on training basis for a period of one year under the National Apprenticeship Training Scheme. A candidate who leaves before completion does not receive the Training Certificate from SVIMS or NATS.
Q10Will TA/DA be paid for attending the interview?

No TA/DA will be paid to candidates for attending the walk-in interview, as per the general instructions of the notice.
