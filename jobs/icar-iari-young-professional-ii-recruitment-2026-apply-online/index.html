ICAR IARI Young Professional II Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Division of Agricultural Economics, ICAR-Indian Agricultural Research Institute (IARI), New Delhi is filling one Young Professional-II (YP II) post on contract. The post is for the project "Economic survey on stakeholder/s preferences in target crops under ICAR-Network Project on Translational Genomics in Crop Plants (TGCP)". The engagement lasts up to 31 March 2027 and pays Rs. 42,000 a month, fixed, with no HRA. Candidates need a Post Graduate Degree (M. Sc.) in one of the subjects the notice lists, with thesis work. About ICAR-IARI

The ICAR-Indian Agricultural Research Institute (IARI) is an institute of the Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) in New Delhi. This post is in the Institute's Division of Agricultural Economics. The work is an economic survey of stakeholder preferences in target crops under the ICAR-Network Project on Translational Genomics in Crop Plants (TGCP).

Who Can Apply

Post Graduate Degree (M. Sc.) in Agricultural Economics/ Dairy Economics/ Fisheries Economics/ Agricultural Statistics/ Economics (with thesis work). Only candidates who have the essential qualifications will be called for the online interview. Experience in data collection and data analysis of primary and secondary data Handling of software like MS Excel, R and STATA Good scientific writing skills The minimum age is 21 years and the maximum age is 45 years as on the date of interview (25 November 2026). If you are currently working, you need a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from your present employer. Check the official notification for the full eligibility details of the post. Pay and Tenure

Pay: Rs. 42,000/- per month, fixed. No HRA is paid. The post is purely temporary and contractual. It is filled at first for up to 31 March 2027 or until the project is completed, whichever is earlier. The post ends with the project (co-terminus). The selected candidate cannot claim a regular appointment at the Institute.

Selection and Interview

Selection is through an online interview on 25 November 2026 from 10:00 AM. Only eligible candidates who have the essential qualifications will be called. Original documents will be checked at the final selection stage. The selected candidate must produce all original documents and medical certificates at the time of joining.
