Civil Court Muzaffarpur Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Retired employees of the Civil Courts can apply for 3 contractual support-staff posts in the Evening Court, Muzaffarpur: 1 Stenographer, 1 Clerk and 1 Peon/ Orderly. The Office of the Principal District & Sessions Judge, Muzaffarpur has invited the applications under Memo No. 7466-7505/E dated 17 November 2026. Applications must reach the Office of the Principal District & Sessions Judge, Patna on or before 16 December 2026, either personally or through registered post.

About Muzaffarpur Civil Court Recruitment 2026

The notice is issued by the Principal District & Sessions Judge, Muzaffarpur, Bihar, for engaging support staff in the Evening Court of Muzaffarpur Judgeship. The engagement is governed by the guidelines and directions of the Hon'ble High Court, Patna. The notice has been forwarded to all the Principal District & Sessions Judges of Bihar to be published on the notice boards of their judgeships.

Where and How to Send

Applications must reach the Office of the Principal District & Sessions Judge, Patna on or before 16 December 2026. They can be handed in personally or sent through registered post. Late receipt is not covered by the notice, as the application must reach the office by that date. The notice does not prescribe an application form, an envelope superscription or a list of enclosures. It also prints no street address or PIN code for the receiving office.

Posts, Eligibility and Pay

The applicant must be a retired employee of the Civil Courts. Only retired court employees who have retired within the last three years, prior to the period as per guidelines issued by the Hon'ble High Court, Patna, can apply. The applicant must be physically and mentally fit to discharge the duties assigned to the respective post. The applicant must possess satisfactory service records and should not have been subjected to any major disciplinary punishment during service. The notice does not mention an educational qualification or an age limit for these posts. Candidates should check the official notification for the post-specific eligibility of each post. The engagement shall be purely contractual in nature and can be terminated at any time, as per the directions of the Hon'ble High Court Patna.
