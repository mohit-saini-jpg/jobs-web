About MPKV

Mahatma Phule Krishi Vidyapeeth is based at Rahuri in Ahillyanagar district, Maharashtra, and the notice is issued on the letterhead of the Government of Maharashtra. This post sits in the Department of Soil & Water Conservation Engineering of Dr. A. S. College of Agricultural Engineering & Technology, which runs the ad-hoc research project on sediment loss and carbon loss from the untreated part of the watershed of the university and Daithane Gunjal. The post is under the administrative control of the concerned Co-Principal Investigators (Co-PI) and the Principal Investigator (PI).

Only one post is advertised. Each condition below applies to the Agriculture Assistant / Field Assistant post. Degree in Agriculture or Agricultural Engineering.
