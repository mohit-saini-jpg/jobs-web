SCL Recruitment 2026 Application Fee

The SCL examination fee includes a non-refundable processing fee of Rs. 600 and a post-wise application fee. Eligible women, SC, ST, PwBD and Ex-Servicemen candidates may receive a refund of the application fee component after appearing in the CBT, subject to the notification conditions. All applicants must initially pay the applicable examination fee. The refundable application-fee component is Rs. 900 for Group A, Rs. 700 for Group B and Rs. 600 for Group C. The processing fee of Rs. 600 is non-refundable.
