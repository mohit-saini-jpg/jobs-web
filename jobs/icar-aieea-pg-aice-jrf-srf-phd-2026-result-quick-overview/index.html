How to Download the ICAR AIEEA PG / AICE JRF-SRF PhD 2026 Scorecard

  • Visit the official NTA-ICAR result portal: exams.nta.nic.in/ icar
  • Under "Candidate Activity," click the relevant link - "ICAR 2026 Score card - PG" for AIEEA (PG), or "ICAR 2026 Score card - PhD" for AICE-JRF/ SRF (PhD)
  • Enter the required login credentials, such as your application number and date of birth (follow the exact fields shown on the login screen)
  • Submit the details to access your scorecard
  • Check your result, then download and save the scorecard as a PDF
  • Take a printout or keep a saved copy for the admission and verification process

What Happens After the Result: Verification and Admission

NTA has clarified that its role is limited to inviting applications, conducting the entrance examination, declaring the result, and providing the result to ICAR. NTA does not conduct eligibility verification.

  • Candidates who qualify will have their eligibility, self-declaration, and other required documents verified at later stages of the admission process
  • This verification will be carried out as per the norms of ICAR and the specific institution where the candidate seeks admission

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Download and save your scorecard immediately once available; keep both a digital copy and a printout
  • Double-check that the name, application number, and other personal details on the scorecard are correct
  • Do not share your login credentials (application number, date of birth) with anyone
  • For any discrepancy in the scorecard or result, contact the NTA helpdesk through the official portal rather than third-party sources
  • Continue monitoring exams.nta.nic.in/ icar for counselling and further admission-related notices
