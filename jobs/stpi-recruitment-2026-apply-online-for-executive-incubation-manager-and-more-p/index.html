STPI Recruitment 2026 - Eligibility Criteria

Chief Operating Officer (COO): Bachelor's in Technology/Engineering (or equivalent degree) or Master in Technology/ Engineering/ Science or MBA from a recognized, accredited and reputed institute/university. Minimum 8 years of overall experience in the IT industry / start-up ecosystem as co-founder/founder/CxO and/or as an enabler of Start-up, Innovation & entrepreneurship ecosystem. Incubation Manager (IM): Graduate from a recognized university. Minimum 3 years of experience in incubation management & the startup/incubation ecosystem or the Information Technology (IT) sector. Assistant Incubation Manager (AIM): Graduate from a recognized university. Minimum 4 years of work experience including minimum 2 years of experience in incubation management & Incubation Ecosystem. Executive (Finance & Admin): MBA (Finance) and/or Graduate in Science/Commerce from a recognized university. Minimum
