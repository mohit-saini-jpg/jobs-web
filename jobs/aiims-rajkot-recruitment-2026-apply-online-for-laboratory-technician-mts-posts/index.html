About AIIMS Rajkot

The All India Institute of Medical Sciences, Rajkot is a Government of India institute in Rajkot, Gujarat. Its letterhead calls it an Institute of National Importance under PMSSY, MoHFW. These posts are in its Viral Research and Diagnostic Laboratory (VRDL) at Village Para Pipaliya, Khanderi, Rajkot, Gujarat 360110. The Principal Investigator of the project is Dr. (Col) Ashwini Agarwal of the Department of Microbiology.

Vacancy Details

The notice advertises 2 posts in total. According to the notice, the number of posts may be increased or decreased as per the directions of the funding agency and the requirements of the project. Selection and Interview

For both posts, selection has three stages: screening of the applications, an MCQ-based written test, and an interview. Selection is based purely on merit. Screening: A screening committee will check all the applications received. Written test: An MCQ-based written test. Candidates with the highest marks are called to the interview, up to six (6) candidates per available post. Interview: The final merit is worked out with a weightage of 80% for the written exam and 20% for the interview. Date and reporting: 10 December 2026, reporting time 9 AM, at Research Cell, Academic block, 3rd Floor, AIIMS Rajkot. What to bring: The documents listed above, in original. Persons working in a Government or Public Sector undertaking, or in any other research project at AIIMS Rajkot, should produce a "No Objection Certificate" at the interview.
