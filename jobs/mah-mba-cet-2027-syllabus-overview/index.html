MAH MBA CET 2027 Section-Wise Syllabus

The entrance examination tests candidates in four areas: Logical Reasoning, Abstract Reasoning, Quantitative Aptitude, and Verbal Ability and Reading Comprehension. Each section assesses a different set of reasoning, numerical or language skills.

MAH MBA CET Logical Reasoning Syllabus 2027

Logical Reasoning is the highest-weighted section of MAH MBA CET 2027, with 60 questions carrying 60 marks. The official syllabus states that this area evaluates how quickly and accurately candidates can think, including verbal reasoning questions.

Candidates should prepare for reasoning questions involving information, relationships, statements and logical situations. The official syllabus describes the skills being assessed but does not prescribe a separate exhaustive list of named reasoning chapters.

MAH MBA CET Abstract Reasoning Syllabus 2027

Abstract Reasoning contains 10 questions carrying 10 marks. The examination tests reasoning ability through figures, diagrams and other visual information.

Candidates should practise identifying patterns, relationships and logical changes in figures. This section focuses on understanding visual information and selecting the most appropriate answer from the available options.

MAH MBA CET Quantitative Aptitude Syllabus 2027

The Quantitative Aptitude section includes 40 questions for 40 marks. According to the official syllabus, it assesses numerical calculation skills, arithmetic knowledge, quantitative reasoning and the ability to interpret information presented in tables and graphs.

The officially mentioned topics and skills include:

  • Numerical Calculations
  • Arithmetic Problems
  • Ratio and Proportion
  • Percentages
  • Quantitative Reasoning
  • Interpretation of Tables
  • Interpretation of Graphs and Charts

Candidates should practise solving numerical questions accurately within the examination time limit.

MAH MBA CET Verbal Ability and Reading Comprehension Syllabus 2027

The Verbal Ability and Reading Comprehension section contains 40 questions carrying 40 marks. It evaluates candidates' understanding of English, vocabulary, grammar, reading comprehension and appropriate language usage.

The official syllabus identifies the following areas:

  • Reading Comprehension Passages
  • Questions Based on Passage Content
  • English Grammar
  • Vocabulary
  • Sentence Completion
  • Synonyms
  • Antonyms
  • Choice of Appropriate Words
  • Choice of Phrases and Expressions
  • Other Similar English Language Skills

Candidates should develop reading accuracy, vocabulary knowledge and the ability to understand information presented in written passages.

How to Prepare for MAH MBA CET 2027?

Candidates should prepare using the revised 150-question pattern and practise the reasoning, numerical and English language skills described in the official syllabus.

  • Download and review the official MAH MBA CET 2027 syllabus.
  • Give sufficient attention to Logical Reasoning, which carries 60 marks.
  • Practise figure-based and diagram-based Abstract Reasoning questions.
  • Revise numerical calculations, percentages, ratios and data interpretation.
  • Improve English grammar, vocabulary and reading comprehension.
  • Attempt practice papers using the revised 120-minute duration.
  • Review mistakes and practise solving questions accurately.

MAH MBA CET 2027 Exam Date

The Maharashtra CET Cell has released a tentative examination calendar for the 2027 admission year. Separate reporting indicates that the MAH MBA CET 2027 examination is planned in two sessions, from 28 to 30 January 2027 and from 1 to 3 April 2027.

These dates are reported as part of the tentative examination calendar and are not specified in the official syllabus PDF. Candidates should confirm the examination dates and registration schedule through the CET Cell's latest examination notices.
