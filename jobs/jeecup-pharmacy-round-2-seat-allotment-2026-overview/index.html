JEECUP Pharmacy Round 4 Freeze and Float Options

After receiving a provisional seat allotment, candidates must select their willingness option through the JEECUP candidate dashboard.

OptionMeaning
FreezeAccept the currently allotted seat without seeking an upgrade.
FloatRetain the current allotment while requesting consideration for a higher-preference seat in a subsequent round, subject to counselling rules.

The official counselling instructions describe Float as Yes-Upgradation and Freeze as No-Upgradation.

Candidates should select their willingness carefully because it affects participation in further allotment rounds.

JEECUP Pharmacy Round 4 Security Fee Payment Last Date

Allotted candidates must pay the applicable seat acceptance or security fee through the official counselling system.

The scheduled payment period is 22-24 October 2026, with the Round 4 deadline at 5 PM on 24 October.

The official JEECUP allotment instructions state that failure to pay the prescribed seat acceptance fee can lead to cancellation of the allotted seat.

The exact payable amount should be checked in the candidate's counselling dashboard and current official fee instructions. Candidates should not assume that the registration fee, counselling fee and security fee are identical.

JEECUP Pharmacy Round 4 Document Verification 2026

Document verification for Round 4 Freeze candidates is scheduled from 22 to 24 October 2026 at designated district help centres.

Candidates must complete the prescribed fee and willingness procedures before attending verification, according to the counselling instructions applicable to their allotment.

Students should verify their allotted help centre and reporting requirements through the official dashboard.

Document verification confirms academic eligibility, category claims and other admission details before the admission process is completed.

What Happens After JEECUP Pharmacy Round 4 Seat Allotment?

After checking their Round 4 result, candidates should complete the relevant procedures without missing the scheduled deadlines.

  • Download the provisional allotment letter.
  • Check the allotted pharmacy institute and course.
  • Select Freeze or Float according to the counselling rules.
  • Pay the prescribed security or seat acceptance fee.
  • Complete document verification if required under the selected option.
  • Check the available admission or reporting instructions.
  • Report to the participating institution within the applicable schedule.
  • Retain the admission and payment acknowledgements.

Seat allotment is provisional until candidates complete the prescribed admission formalities.
