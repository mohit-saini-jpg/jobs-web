Qualification and Experience

All applicants must be permanent residents of West Bengal and must know the local languages. Every essential qualification must be completed on or before the first date of online application. Selection Process and Scale of Scoring

Candidates are ranked by marks under each post's Scale of Scoring. Academic marks are proportionate to the percentage obtained. Some posts also carry a computer test, marks for experience or an interview. Proportionate marking means the percentage obtained in the exam multiplied by the maximum marks for that item, divided by 100. For example, class 10 marks for a Medical Officer are % obtained × 20/100. All marks are calculated excluding marks of Additional Subject. Proportionate marking is considered up to 2 decimal points. Original testimonials are verified. Candidates without the registration form will not be entertained for this verification. Candidates must go through all stages of recruitment. A candidate who skips any stage will be disqualified immediately. The date, time and venue of interview and the short listing of candidates will be published on www.wbhealth.gov.in and www.pu

DHFWS Purba Medinipur Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in Purba Medinipur NHM Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 67 vacancies across 10 posts. The largest are Pharmacist (17), Community Health Assistant (Urban) (12) and Multi Rehabilitation Worker under NPHCE (12).
Q2What is the last date to apply?
A
The last date of registration and fee payment is 25 November 2026. Full submission of the application form must be done by midnight of 26 November 2026.
Q3What qualification is needed for Medical Officer-AYUSH(MHT)?
A
A BHMS/BAMS/BUMS degree from an institution recognized by the respective Council is required, along with completed compulsory internship and council registration. Candidates holding an MD in Homeopathy/Ayurvedic/Unani get additional marks.
Q4What is the age limit?
A
The maximum age is 40 years as on 01.01.2026 for all posts. The minimum age is 18 years for Pharmacist and 21 years for Public Health Manager, Block Data Manager, Multi Rehabilitation Worker, STLS and STS. Relaxation is as per Govt. Norms for all posts except STLS and STS.
Q5What is the salary?
A
Monthly remuneration ranges from Rs.13,000.00 (CHA (U)) to Rs.35,000.00 (Public Health Manager). Medical Officer-AYUSH(MHT) gets Rs.32,000.00 per month.
Q6What is the application fee?
A
The fee is Rs.100/- for General categories and Rs.50/- for Reserve categories. It is paid online only and is non-refundable.
Q7How do I apply?
A
Apply online in the prescribed format on the Department's website between 4 November 2026 and midnight of 26 November 2026. Afterwards, download the print copy from http://hr.wbhealth.gov.in/Index.aspx under "Application Print" and keep it for document verification.
Q8How are candidates selected?
A
Selection is by marks under each post's Scale of Scoring, mainly proportionate academic marks. Public Health Manager, Block Data Manager, STLS and STS also have a computer test. Multi Rehabilitation Worker has 5 marks for interview.
Q9Are these permanent jobs?
A
No. The posts are purely contractual, with an initial period up to 31.03.2027. The contract is likely to be extended on satisfactory performance and approval of Govt.
Q10Can male candidates apply for Community Health Assistant (Urban)?
A
No. Only female candidates may apply for CHA (U). They must be permanent residents of Purba Medinipur, proficient in Bengali, and hold an ANM or GNM course registered with WBNC.
