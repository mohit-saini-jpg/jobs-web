AIIMS Raipur Senior Resident Recruitment 2026 Eligibility

A postgraduate medical degree, viz. MD/MS/DNB/Diploma, in the respective discipline from a recognised University/Institute. For School of Public Health: A postgraduate medical degree, viz. MD/DNB in Community Medicine/PSM, from a recognised University/Institute. For Dentistry: A postgraduate degree, i.e., MDS, in the concerned speciality. Candidates must be Indian Nationals. Age and other qualifications/experience will be counted as on the date of interview. Only candidates who have been declared successful in their qualifying degree exam, and who will be completing or are likely to complete their tenure on or before 31.08.2026, will be eligible. If the qualifying exam result is declared after the interview, the candidature will stand cancelled and no claim for selection will be considered.
