5How to Check MP B.Tech Round 2 Merit List 2026 at dte.mponline.gov.in
Sr.TypeTextStyleItems
1paragraphCandidates can follow these steps to view and download their Round 2 rank:
2listordered
  • Visit the official DTE MP counselling portal at dte.mponline.gov.in.
  • Click on 'Bachelor of Technology' under the course counselling section.
  • Select either "Common Merit List (TFW and General Pool Second Round)" or "Common Merit List (Based on Qualifying Exam)", depending on your category.
  • Enter your application details (such as application/roll number and date of birth) to view your rank.
  • Download and save the merit list PDF for reference.
6What Happens After the Merit List: Seat Allotment Process
Sr.TypeTextStyleItems
1paragraphOnce the Round 2 merit list is out, DTE MP will proceed with the next stages of counselling:
2listunordered
  • Seat Allotment Result: To be released on July 27, 2026.
  • Allotment Letter Printing: Candidates can print their allotment letters from July 27 to July 30, 2026 (till 6:30 PM).
  • Reporting and Document Verification: Candidates allotted a seat must report to their allotted institute for document verification between July 27 and July 30, 2026.
  • Internal Branch Change: Candidates who wish to change their branch within the same college can fill choices from July 31 to August 1, 2026, with the internal branch change allotment letter issued on August 3, 2026.
3paragraphCandidates must secure their allotted seat by reporting to the institute within the specified window; failure to do so within the deadline can result in the seat being forfeited or moved to the next round, as per standard DTE MP counselling practice.
