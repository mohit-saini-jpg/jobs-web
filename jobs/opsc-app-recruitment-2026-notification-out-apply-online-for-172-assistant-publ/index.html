OPSC Assistant Public Prosecutor Vacancy 2026

A total of 172 vacancies (58 reserved for women) have been notified for the post of Assistant Public Prosecutor, as furnished by the Home Department, Government of Odisha. Out of the total 172 vacancies, horizontal reservation is also provided for Ex-Servicemen, Sports Persons and Persons with Disabilities (PwD), who will be adjusted against the categories to which they belong. The PwD reservation of 8 posts is further broken down by disability category as follows: Cat-I (Blind/Low Vision) – 1 post; Cat-II (Hard of Hearing) – 2 posts; Cat-III (Locomotor disabilities including One Leg, One Arm, Both Arms, Both Legs, Leprosy Cured, Dwarfism, Acid Attack Victims, etc.) – 2 posts; Cat-IV (Specific Learning Disability/Multiple Disability) – 3 posts. Persons with temporary disability are not eligible for this reservation.

OPSC Assistant Public Prosecutor Selection Process

Selection to the post of Assistant Public Prosecutor is made through a competitive examination conducted in three stages: The Preliminary Examination is only a screening test to shortlist candidates for the Main Examination and its marks are not counted in the final merit list. It comprises one paper of 100 Objective Type (MCQ) questions carrying 100 marks, with a duration of 2 hours. Each correct answer carries 1 mark, and 25% of the marks secured for a correct answer is deducted for each wrong response (negative marking); unattempted questions carry zero marks. Candidates securing the Commission-decided qualifying marks will be admitted to the Main Examination, in a ratio of up to 10 times the number of vacancies per category.
