1 & 2. B.A LLB (5-Year) 3rd and 6th Semester Result 2026

Two separate results were released for the 5-Year B.A LLB programme - one for 3rd Semester and one for 6th Semester - both for Regular and Private candidates, for the examination conducted in February/March 2026.

3 & 4. MBBS Result 2026 (First and Third Professional)

Two MBBS results were declared: the Third Professional Part-I exam (Subsequent Session), held in April 2026, and the First Professional exam (Subsequent Session), held in March 2026. Both are subsequent-session results, typically meant for students appearing again after an earlier attempt.

5. UG Part III Exam 2016 (Result Notification No. 295)

This is an older, delayed result - Notification No. 295 - for the UG Part III examination held in 2016. The notification does not state the reason for the delay in declaration.

6. UG 6th Semester Result (Multiple Notification Numbers, Multiple Years)

This single listing bundles nine notification numbers (115, 84, 48, 130, 09, 29, 15, 04, 03) covering UG 6th Semester exams from several sessions: 2018, 2022, 2023, 2024-25, 2025, and 2025-26. This means students from different batches and different exam years should check the specific notification number that applies to their session.

7. B.A./B.Sc./B.Com (DDE/Private) 6th Semester Non-CBCS Result 2026

This result is for private/DDE (Directorate of Distance Education) candidates enrolled in the Three-Year Degree (General) Course under the Non-CBCS scheme, for the 6th Semester exam held in May-June 2026.

8. FYUGP NEP-2020 Semester-III Re-evaluation Result

This is a re-evaluation result (not a fresh result) for FYUGP (Four Year Undergraduate Programme) students under NEP-2020, Semester-III, for the 2025-26 session.

How to Check Your Jammu University Result Online

  • Go to the official Controller of Examination website: coeju.com/notices.aspx.
  • Look under the "Result Notifications" panel on the homepage/ notices page.
  • Find the entry matching your course, semester, and exam session (use the notification number if your result is listed under a multi-notification entry, as in Notification No. 6 above).
  • Click the result link to open the notification, which typically links to a PDF gazette or a roll-number-wise result search tool.
  • If a roll-number search is provided, enter your roll number/registration number to view your individual result.

Important Instructions for Students

  • Match your notification number carefully if your semester/session falls under a bundled notification (see Notification No. 6).
  • Subsequent-session MBBS results (Notifications 3 and 4) apply to students appearing after an earlier attempt - confirm with your college if you're unsure which session applies to you.
  • Keep your roll number/ registration number ready before checking the result.
  • For re-evaluation results, check with your department on the next steps for marksheet issuance.
