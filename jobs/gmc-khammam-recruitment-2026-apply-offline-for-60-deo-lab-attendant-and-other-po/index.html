GMC Khammam Selection Process 2026

Selection for GMC Khammam Recruitment 2026 will be based on a merit system carrying 100 marks. Of these, 90 marks are assigned based on the qualifying examination, and 10 marks are assigned based on age. A provisional merit list will be published for objections before the final selection list is prepared. Age weightage is calculated from 18 years of age at 0.5 marks for each completed year, up to a maximum of 10 marks. For this purpose, age is reckoned as on 1 July 2026. 20 years of age: 1 mark 30 years of age: 6 marks 40 years of age: 10 marks The merit list will be prepared using the notified criteria, and the final selection list will follow the applicable reservation rules and Presidential Order 2018.
