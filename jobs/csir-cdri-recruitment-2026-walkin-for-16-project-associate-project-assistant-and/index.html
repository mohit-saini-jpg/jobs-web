About CSIR-CDRI

CSIR - Central Drug Research Institute is a laboratory of the Council of Scientific & Industrial Research, located at Sector 10, Janakipuram Extension, Sitapur Road, Lucknow - 226 031. Through this walk-in, the institute says it wants to expand and strengthen the areas of Neurobiology, Virology, Parasite Biology, Endocrinology, Human Genetics, Analytical Biochemistry, Computational Chemistry, Medicinal Process Chemistry, Statistics, IPR and HR Management. It is looking for young professionals with a strong academic record and scientific achievements to conduct research as per the institute's mandate aligned with National Missions.

All 16 positions are project staff positions under different funded projects at CSIR-CDRI, Lucknow. CSIR-CDRI Recruitment 2026 : Age Limit

The upper age limit is 28 Years for Project Technical Support-I (codes 003, 004, 010), 35 Years for Project Associate (PAT-I), Project Associate-I, Project Technical Support-III, Project Associate-II and Project Assistant-II (PA-II), and 40 Years for Research Associate-III and Senior-PAT. Age is counted on the date of interview. For Junior Research Assistant (code 006), the limit is 28 Years as on April 2026, with no age relaxation. The notice says the relaxation is as above "or as per Govt. of India instructions issued in this regard from time to time".

What to Bring

Duly filled application in the notice's Application Format for Walk-In-Interview, filled in BLOCK LETTERS, with a recent passport size photograph pasted. The signed Undertaking stating whether you were engaged earlier as Project Staff in CSIR-CDRI or any other CSIR Lab/Institute, and the total period of such engagement. Original mark sheets/certificates. Self-attested copies of mark sheets/certificates etc. A recent passport size photograph.

What Happens on the Day

Report at the venue from 09:00 am on the date fixed for your position code; candidates reporting after 10:00 A.M will not be entertained. Candidates who fulfil the requisite qualification and age will be allowed to appear before the Selection Committee for interview. Depending on the number of candidates, a written test in the subject area will be held, if required, on the same day to shortlist candidates for the interview. The interview may continue on the subsequent days, so candidates coming from outstations should make their own arrangements. No TA/DA will be paid to candidates for attending the interview. Suitable candidates may be empanelled for future requirement, if any, under different projects.

CSIR-CDRI Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1When is the CSIR-CDRI walk-in interview 2026?
A
The walk-in is on 1 December 2026 for position codes 001 to 006 (Chemical and Pharma area) and on 2 December 2026 for position codes 007 to 015 (Life Sciences and S & T Management area).
Q2What is the reporting time and venue for the CSIR-CDRI walk-in?
A
Candidates report from 09:00 am at CSIR-Central Drug Research Institute, Sector 10, Jankipuram Extension, Sitapur Road, Lucknow-226031. Candidates reporting after 10:00 A.M will not be entertained.
Q3How many vacancies are in CSIR-CDRI Advertisement No. 11/2026?
A
There are 16 positions under 15 position codes; code 008 (Project Technical Support-III) has 2 positions and every other code has 1. The number may vary at the time of selection.
Q4What is the qualification for Project Technical Support-I at CSIR-CDRI?
A
It is 10th + Diploma (MLT/DMLT or equivalent) + two years' experience in the relevant subject/field, or a three years Graduate Degree in the subjects named for codes 003, 004 or 010 + one year of experience; a Post Graduate degree is considered as experience.
Q5What is the age limit for the CSIR-CDRI project positions?
A
The upper age limit is 28, 35 or 40 Years depending on the position, counted on the date of interview. Junior Research Assistant has 28 Years as on April 2026 with no age relaxation.
Q6What is the salary for CSIR-CDRI project staff in 2026?
A
Pay ranges from Rs. 18,000 + 20% HRA per month (Project Technical Support-I) to Rs. 54,000 + 16% HRA per month (Research Associate-III), with Senior-PAT at Rs. 42,000 + 20% HRA.
Q7Is HRA paid on top of the pay?
A
Yes, for most positions the notice prints pay plus 20% HRA, and Research Associate-III gets Rs. 54000 + 16% HRA. For Junior Research Assistant, the notice prints Rs. 25000 p.m. for the first 2 years and Rs. 28000 p.m. for the 3rd year, without HRA.
Q8Who gets age relaxation in the CSIR-CDRI walk-in?
A
SC/ST/PwBD/Women candidates get 05 years and OBC candidates get 03 years, or as per Government of India instructions. There is no age relaxation for Junior Research Assistant (code 006).
Q9What documents should I bring to the CSIR-CDRI walk-in?
A
Bring the duly filled application format and undertaking, original and self-attested copies of mark sheets/certificates, and a recent passport size photograph. Tentative mark sheets and "result awaited" cases will not be considered.
Q10Are the CSIR-CDRI project positions permanent?
A
No. The positions are purely temporary and co-terminus with the projects, with tenures ending between 31.03.2027 and 31.03.2031, and they give no right to regularisation against any CSIR-CDRI post.
