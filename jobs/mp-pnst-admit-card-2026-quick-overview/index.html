Notification DatePost Date: 08/08/2026

The Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) conducted the Pre Nursing Selection Test (PNST) 2026 for admission to the 4-year B.Sc. Nursing course in government, autonomous, and private nursing institutes across Madhya Pradesh. The MP PNST 2026 exam was held from 10 July 2026 to 13 July 2026 at notified centres across the state. The result download link has been activated on the official MPESB portal, esb.mp.gov.in. Candidates who appeared for the exam can check and download their result from the official website using the direct link provided below.

How to Download MP PNST Result 2026

Candidates can follow these steps to check and download the MP PNST Result 2026:

  • Visit the official MPESB website at esb.mp.gov.in.
  • Go to the Results section on the homepage.
  • Click on the link for Pre Nursing Selection Test (PNST) 2026 Result.
  • You will be redirected to the result page.
  • Enter your login/application details as requested on the result page.
  • View and download your MP PNST Result 2026.
  • Take a printout and keep it safe for future reference and counselling purposes.

Note: The specific login fields required on the result page (such as application number, date of birth, or other credentials) are not confirmed in the provided source. For admit card download, MPESB required the Application Number, Date of Birth, first 2 letters of Mother's Name, and the last 4 digits of the Aadhar Number — the result page may follow a similar login pattern, but candidates should verify the exact fields directly on the official website.

MP PNST 2026 Important Instructions

  • Admit cards and results are not dispatched by post, fax, or email; candidates must download them from the official website.
  • Candidates must appear at the examination centre allotted to them, which may differ from their preferred choice depending on availability.
  • Keep the application number and other login credentials safe for downloading the admit card and result.
  • For any queries or discrepancies, contact MPESB through the helpline number or email provided above.
