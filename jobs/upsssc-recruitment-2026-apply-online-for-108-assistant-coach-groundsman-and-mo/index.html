UPSSSC Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1The notification allows only the online application system. Applications sent by any other means are not accepted. Candidates should read the full advertisement before applying.
  • 2Visit the Commission's website upsssc.gov.in and, on the homepage under the Live Advertisement Segment, click the advertisement for Sports Directorate Main Examination (PET-2025)/22 to view or download it.
  • 3Log in using your PET-2025 registration number. An OTP is sent to the mobile number or e-mail registered in PET-2025.
  • 4Your personal details recorded in PET-2025 (name, parents' names, domicile, category, EWS, horizontal reservation details, date of birth, gender, marital status, mobile number, e-mail) are displayed automatically.
  • 5Select the posts you want to apply for. A post not selected is not considered.
  • 6For each selected post, choose Yes or No for the required educational qualification and enter the board or university name, year of passing, certificate or roll number, certificate issue date and marks details.
  • 7Check the photo and signature uploaded in PET-2025, which are displayed automatically and cannot be changed. Then enter whether you hold any preferential qualification, and check your permanent and correspondence address.
  • 8Read the declaration, tick all checkboxes, enter the verification code and click "Save and Submit".
  • 9Pay the fee through credit card, debit card, internet banking, UPI or SBI e-challan. The application is submitted automatically once the fee is paid and reconciled.
  • 10Download and print the application. Keep a copy safe, as it may be required during record verification.
  • 11If the application does not appear after payment, the fee has not been reconciled. Log in with the registration number, use "Update Your Transaction ID by Double Verification" in the Applicant Segment, and click "Verify Transaction ID".
  • 12If payment fails, a Payment Acknowledgment Receipt (PAR) appears instead of the application. Use the same option and then the "Application Fee Deposition" option to pay.
  • 13For SBI e-challan, download the challan, pay at any SBI branch, then enter the challan details through the "Update Your Transaction ID by Double Verification" option.
  • 14After the application is finally submitted, candidates can make corrections until 11-11-2026 by logging in through the Applicant's Dashboard, clicking "Modify Application" and entering the verification code and OTP. Only the following details can be corrected:
  • 15Correspondence address.
  • 16Personal details such as EWS and horizontal reservation category (Dependant of Freedom Fighter, Disabled, Ex-Servicemen, Gender, Outstanding Sportsperson, etc.).
  • 17Details of mandatory or preferential qualification entered in the application.
  • 18Marital status.
  • 19No correction is possible after the last date for correction, and the Commission does not accept separate representations for corrections.
