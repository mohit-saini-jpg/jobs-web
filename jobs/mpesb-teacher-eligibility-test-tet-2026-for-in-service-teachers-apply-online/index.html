MP ESB TET 2026 Important Dates

Candidates should note the following dates for the application, correction, and exam process. The exam will be conducted in two shifts. In the first shift, candidates report between 8:00 AM and 9:00 AM, important instructions are read from 9:50 AM to 10:00 AM (10 minutes), and the exam runs from 10:00 AM to 12:30 PM (2 hours 30 minutes). In the second shift, candidates report between 1:00 PM and 2:00 PM, instructions are read from 2:50 PM to 3:00 PM, and the exam runs from 3:00 PM to 5:30 PM.

MP ESB TET 2026 Eligibility Criteria

This exam is open only to in-service (currently employed) teachers - it is not a recruitment exam and is not open to fresh candidates seeking a new government teaching job. All in-service primary-level teachers in government schools - Sahayak Shikshak (Assistant Teacher), Prathmik Shikshak, Sahayak Adhyapak, Samvida Shala Shikshak Varg 3 (Contract School Teacher Class 3), Prayogshala Sahayak (Laboratory Assistant), Prathmik Shikshak Vigyan, and Sahayak Shikshak Vigyan - who have not cleared the eligibility test prescribed under the Right to Education Act are eligible to appear. In-service teachers at the primary level in non-government schools who have not cleared TET may also appear. MP ESB TET 2026 Exam Pattern

Both the Primary and Secondary Teacher Eligibility Tests are conducted as a single online (computer-based), objective-type (MCQ) paper of 150 marks, with a duration of 2 hours 30 minutes and no negative marking. Language-1 questions are based on proficiency in the language chosen by the candidate in the application form. Language-2 must be different from Language-1, and candidates can choose from Hindi, English, Sanskrit, and Urdu. The question paper level and content are based on the topics of the prevalent Madhya Pradesh Class 1–5 syllabus/textbooks, though the difficulty level and linkage may extend up to high-school standard. The paper has two parts. Part A is compulsory for all candidates. MP ESB TET 2026 Syllabus Highlights

The detailed syllabus is set out separately for each subject and paper in the rule book. Key syllabus areas include: Child Development & Pedagogy (Primary): concept of child development, Piaget/Pavlov/Kohler/Thorndike theories, child-centred and progressive education, concept and measurement of intelligence, personality, inclusive education, understanding children with special needs, learning and pedagogy, cognition and emotion, motivation, memory and forgetting. Languages (Hindi, English, Sanskrit, Urdu): unseen passage comprehension, grammar, pedagogy of language development including principles of language teaching, listening/speaking/reading/writing skills, teaching-learning materials. Mathematics (Primary): number system, place value, four basic operations, fractions, geometry basics, measurement, patterns; plus pedagogical issues like development of logical thinking through mathema

MPESB Teacher Eligibility Test (TET) 2026 for In-Service : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is the MP ESB Teacher Eligibility Test 2026?
A
It is a special eligibility test conducted by MP ESB exclusively for currently in-service primary and secondary school teachers who have not yet cleared TET, following a Supreme Court order dated 01.09.2025.
Q2Who can apply for MP ESB TET 2026?
A
Only in-service (currently employed) government and non-government school teachers at the primary or secondary level who have not previously cleared TET are eligible. It is not open to fresh candidates seeking new recruitment.
Q3What is the last date to apply for MP ESB TET 2026?
A
The online application closes on 04.09.2026. A correction window remains open until 09.09.2026.
Q4What is the exam date for MP ESB TET 2026?
A
The exam is scheduled to begin from 12.10.2026 (Monday), conducted in two shifts per day.
Q5What is the application fee for MP ESB TET 2026?
A
The exam fee is ₹1,000 per paper for all candidates, regardless of category, plus an MP Online portal fee of ₹60 (kiosk) or ₹20 (registered login).
Q6What is the exam pattern for MP ESB TET 2026?
A
Both the Primary and Secondary Teacher Eligibility Tests are a single 150-mark MCQ paper of 2 hours 30 minutes, with no negative marking. The Secondary test has a compulsory Part A (30 marks) and a subject-choice Part B (120 marks).
Q7What are the qualifying marks for MP ESB TET 2026?
A
General/Unreserved candidates need 60% marks; SC/ST/OBC/Divyangjan/EWS candidates need 50% marks to qualify.
Q8Is the MP ESB TET 2026 result valid for new government teacher recruitment?
A
No. This exam's eligibility/result is valid only for the continued service eligibility of existing in-service teachers and will not be recognized for future direct recruitment to government teacher posts.
Q9How can I apply online for MP ESB TET 2026?
A
Eligible in-service teachers can apply online at www.esb.mp.gov.in or via the MP Online portal between 21.08.2026 and 04.09.2026, uploading their appointment order, DISE Code, and other required details.
Q10By when must in-service teachers qualify TET as per the Supreme Court order?
A
In-service teachers recruited before the RTE Act, with more than 5 years of service remaining, must qualify TET by 31st August 2028. Teachers with less than 5 years of service left may continue without qualifying TET, but cannot be considered for promotion without it.
