NWR Act Apprentice Eligibility Criteria

To meet the NWR Act Apprentice eligibility, candidates must satisfy the qualification and age conditions given below. Before applying, candidates should ensure that they fulfil all the eligibility norms and hold the requisite educational and technical qualifications from a recognized Board/Government University/Institute as on the date of submission of the application. Those awaiting results of the final exams need not apply. Candidates must choose only one unit and should check the available training slots against their ITI trade before selecting the unit. Candidates should hold an Aadhaar Card, as the 12-digit Aadhaar number has to be entered at the time of registration. Candidates seeking reservation must produce the relevant certificates in the prescribed format at the time of Document Verification (see the documents section). NWR Act Apprentice Stipend and Training Details

The notification does not state a fixed stipend amount. The training period and stipend will be as per the extant rules and directives issued by the Railway Board from time to time. No hostel accommodation is provided. Selected candidates have to make their own arrangement during the training, as per the Apprentices Act 1961, and are released on completion of the training. Before the training begins, the selected candidate (or the guardian, if the candidate is a minor) has to enter into a Contract of Apprenticeship with the employer. Training in the Railway does not give any right to absorption in the Railway after completing the training.

The NWR Act Apprentice application fee is Rs. 100 for all candidates except those exempted. SC/ST, Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD) and Women candidates do not have to pay any fee. NWR Act Apprentice Selection Process

The NWR Act Apprentice selection process is based on a merit list. The notification does not mention any written examination or interview. The merit list is prepared for all candidates who apply, on the basis of the percentage of marks in Matriculation (with minimum 50% aggregate marks) and the ITI marks in the trade in which the apprenticeship is to be done. The panel is prepared on the basis of the simple average of the marks in Matriculation and ITI. For the Matriculation percentage, the marks obtained in all subjects are counted, not the marks of any subject or group of subjects. For the ITI percentage, the marks mentioned in the provisional/final certificate are counted. If two candidates have the same marks, the older candidate is preferred.
