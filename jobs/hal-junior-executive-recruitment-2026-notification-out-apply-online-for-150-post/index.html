HAL Junior Executive Age Limit

The upper age limit is not above 28 years as on 17 October 2026 for UR and EWS category candidates. Candidates from OBC-NCL / SC / ST / EWS categories applying for a UR post will be treated on par with UR criteria. Age will be reckoned as on the last date for receipt of applications only. For SC / ST and OBC-NCL candidates, the caste / tribe name must appear in the Central List notified by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, Govt. of India. The certificate must contain the date of issue, and the name must be spelled exactly as in the central list. For EWS candidates, the Income and Asset Certificate must be valid for the financial year 2026-2027 and prepared on the basis of income and asset verification for the financial year 2025-26.

HAL Junior Executive Salary

Personnel engaged on Short Term Basis will be inducted in the E0 Pay Scale (2017) of DPE, i.e. Rs. 30,000 – 1,20,000. The basic pay is Rs. 30,000, and an annual increment of 3% on the basic pay is admissible in the second and third year. Maximum Rs. 25,000/- per annum towards reimbursement of Medical Insurance Coverage up to Rs. 10 Lakhs. Proof of the insurance policy must be submitted within three months of engagement. These personnel are not entitled to any other medical benefits in the Company's IHCs / Dispensaries. Casual Leave, Vacation Leave, Compensatory Off as applicable to regular Officers, and Vacation Leave Encashment (on completion / termination of the Tenure). Uniforms, Stitching Charges and Shoe Allowance as applicable to regular Officers. Maternity Benefits, Creche facilities and Paternity Leave as applicable to regular officers. Provident Fund contributions as per rules.

HAL Junior Executive Selection Process

The selection of candidates will be done through Interview only. There is no written test mentioned in the notification. Shortlisting: Candidates who fulfil the prescribed eligibility criteria are considered for shortlisting. They are shortlisted on the basis of aggregate marks scored in the qualifying course / examination, in order of merit, in the ratio of 1:10 (maximum) to the number of vacancies in each discipline and category. Interview: The suitability of shortlisted candidates is assessed by a Selection Committee by way of Interview. The date, time and venue will be intimated to shortlisted candidates only via e-mail, and the list of shortlisted candidates will be hosted on the HAL website.
