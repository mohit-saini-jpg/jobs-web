Jharkhand Home Guard Eligibility Criteria 2026

Candidates must satisfy the educational qualification, local residence, age and physical standards applicable to Rural or Urban Home Guard positions. Rural Home Guard applicants must be permanent residents of the block for which they apply. Urban Home Guard candidates must be ordinary residents of the notified urban area. All applicants must submit the required residential certificate issued by the competent authority. Applicants must provide the relevant technical qualification or experience certificate to support their eligibility.

Jharkhand Home Guard Selection Process 2026

The selection procedure includes physical measurement, Physical Efficiency Test, Hindi Writing Ability Test and a Technical Skill Test for Urban Technical Home Guard applicants. Jharkhand Home Guard Physical Test 2026

The physical test requirements differ between Rural/Urban Non-Technical and Urban Technical Home Guard applicants. Candidates exceeding six minutes for men or ten minutes for women in the one-mile run will be considered ineligible under the prescribed running standard. The notification provides additional performance-based marks for higher achievements in jumping and shot put events.
