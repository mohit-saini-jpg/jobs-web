Odisha Combined Uniformed Services Selection Process 2026

The recruitment process includes a written examination followed by the applicable physical, driving, medical and verification stages. Written Examination. Odisha Police Constable Exam Pattern 2026

The Common Written Examination is prescribed for all posts and consists of 100 multiple-choice questions for 100 marks , with a duration of 120 minutes. The examination carries negative marking of 0.25 marks for each incorrect answer . No marks will be awarded or deducted for unattended questions. Candidates applying for Constable (Communication) must appear in an additional examination based on +2 Science standards. A separate objective examination is prescribed for Firefighting Driver and Driver PMT posts.

Candidates who qualify in Physical Measurement must complete the prescribed Physical Efficiency Test (PET).
