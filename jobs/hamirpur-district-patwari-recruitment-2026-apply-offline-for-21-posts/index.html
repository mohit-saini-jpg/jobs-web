Hamirpur DC Patwari Recruitment 2026 Notification PDF

The Office of the Deputy Commissioner, Hamirpur, Himachal Pradesh, has officially released the Hamirpur DC Patwari Recruitment 2026 notification dated 21.07.2026 for the re-engagement of 21 retired Patwaris/Kanungos on a temporary remuneration basis against vacant Patwar Circles in the district. The notification contains complete details regarding vacancies, eligibility criteria, age limit, remuneration, terms and conditions of engagement, selection procedure, required documents, and the offline application process. Hamirpur DC Patwari Vacancy Details 2026

Hamirpur DC Patwari 2026 Salary

Retired Patwaris re-engaged against these posts will be paid a monthly remuneration of Rs. 40,000/- . As per the terms and conditions, they will also be entitled to: One day's casual leave after completing one month of service (no other leave is admissible). TA/DA for official tour duty, at the same rate as applicable to regular counterpart officials at the minimum of the pay scale. Continuation of medical facilities and reimbursement of medical expenses as admissible to them as a retiree. The period of engagement will not be counted for any service benefits earned before superannuation.

Hamirpur DC Patwari Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Eligible retired Patwaris/Kanungos will be considered for the post on the recommendation of a committee constituted vide office order No. DRO/Estt/Re-engagement-8234-39 dated 28/12/2023, headed by the Additional Deputy Commissioner, Hamirpur. If applications received exceed the number of vacancies advertised, candidates junior in age will be given preference for engagement against such posts.

Hamirpur DC Patwari Recruitment 2026 Required Documents

Duly filled application form Proof of date of birth and date of retirement Educational qualification certificates (10th, 10+2, Graduate/Post Graduate, and any other, as applicable) Proof of Patwari exam and Kanungo exam passing year (where applicable) Category certificate (General/SC/ST/OBC), if applicable Proof of total service rendered in the Revenue Department Fitness certificate from a Government Hospital Signed declaration confirming age does not exceed 65 years and no disciplinary proceeding is pending
