Murud Raigad District Revenue Servant (Kotwal) : Application Fee Details

The application fee must be paid in cash at the Tahsildar Office Murud at the time of submitting the application. An additional Rs. 50/- form fee is charged in cash. The application fee is non-refundable and non-transferable.

Selection Process and Exam Pattern

The selection process comprises a 100-marks written examination followed by document verification and a medical examination conducted by the Civil Surgeon, Raigad-Murud. The written examination consists of objective multiple-choice questions totaling 100 marks. Each correct answer carries 1 mark, and there is no negative marking for incorrect answers.
