ICAR CIBA Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

ICAR-Central Institute of Brackishwater Aquaculture (ICAR-CIBA), Chennai, has invited applications for four purely temporary project posts: two Young Professional-II, one Young Professional-I and one Field Assistant. The posts are under the NFDB-funded project "Super-Intensive Precision and Natural Shrimp Farming (SIPNSF): Upscaling and Demonstration for Commercial Adoptions", for one year initially, with consolidated pay of ₹42,000, ₹30,000 and ₹22,000 / ₹18,000 per month. About the Institute and Project

The notice is issued by ICAR-CIBA on its letterhead, and the interview is to be held at ICAR-CIBA, Chennai. The posts serve the SIPNSF project, funded by the National Fisheries Development Board (NFDB), Hyderabad. The work is at shrimp farms in Muttukadu (Chennai, Tamil Nadu) and Moolapalem (Srikakulam, Andhra Pradesh).

Pay and Tenure

The emoluments are consolidated and stated per month: Young Professional-II ₹42,000, Young Professional-I ₹30,000, and Field Assistant ₹22,000 (Graduate/Diploma) or ₹18,000 (Higher Secondary 10+2). The notice mentions no HRA or other allowance. The posts are filled on a purely temporary basis in the institute. The duration is one year initially and may be extended based on satisfactory performance.

Selection and Interview

Communication will be sent only to shortlisted candidates. The date and time of the interview will be intimated to the shortlisted candidate by mail. The mode of interview is physical mode or a suitable online platform. Candidates who have received the mail should appear for an online/offline interview at ICAR-CIBA, Chennai, with the required documents. ICAR-CIBA does not enter into correspondence with applicants who are not called for an interview. The Director, CIBA reserves the right to accept or reject any applications. The notice does not describe marking or any other selection stage.
