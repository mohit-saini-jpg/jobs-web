About WAPCOS

WAPCOS Limited is a public sector enterprise of the Government of India. It keeps an Expert Databank through its portal rep.wapcos.co.in. Experts in the databank may be considered for WAPCOS's future requirements. When applicants sign the CV certification, they also consent to their CV being included in bids that WAPCOS is submitting.

Vacancy Details

Annexure-I, "Categories of Expert Required For Empanelment (Fixed/ Short Term Basis)", lists 56 specialisations. It does not give a number of vacancies for any of them. Each specialisation is counted as 1 here. Instrumentation Specialist appears in both the Water Resources Sector and the Power Sector, with the same qualification. It is counted once in each sector. WAPCOS Recruitment 2027 : Important Instructions

By signing the CV certification, you certify that the CV correctly describes you, your qualifications and your experience. A wilful misstatement may lead to disqualification, or to dismissal if you have been engaged. The certification also records your agreement to work for WAPCOS and your consent to WAPCOS including your CV in bids it is submitting. The CV must name the specialisation you are applying for.

WAPCOS Recruitment 2027 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Applications are made online on the WAPCOS portal at rep.wapcos.co.in until 31 March 2027. The notice describes these steps:
  • 2Open the "Empanelment/Data Bank of experts in various fields" page (EXPERT-DATABANK-2026) on rep.wapcos.co.in and select "Apply now", or select "Apply for this specialisation" next to a specialisation.
  • 3If you are new to the portal, create your account. If you are already registered, sign in with your mobile OTP.
  • 4Choose your sector and your specialisations. The application allows you to select more than one.
  • 5Complete your details in the online application.
  • 6Download the "Prescribed CV format" (Annexure-II) from the "Advertisement and CV format" box on the same page and fill it in.
  • 7Upload the filled CV with your online application.
  • 8Upload an updated CV in the prescribed "Detailed Curriculum Vitae" format (Annexure-II). The format asks for:
  • 9Name of Expert, and Specialisation/Applied For
  • 10Category (GEN/OBC/SC/ST)
  • 11Full address, including phone and e-mail
  • 12Date of birth and nationality
  • 13Education: college or university and other specialised education, with names of institutions, degrees obtained and dates of obtainment
  • 14Membership of professional associations
  • 15Other training: significant training since the degrees listed under Education
  • 16Countries of work experience
  • 17Languages, with your proficiency (Good, Fair or Poor) in speaking, reading and writing
  • 18Employment record: every job since graduation in reverse order, starting with your present position, with dates (from and to), employer and position held
  • 19Work undertaken that best illustrates your capability to handle the position: up to five assignments, each with the name of the assignment or project, year, location, client, main project features, positions held and activities performed
  • 20Certification, with your signature, the date (Date/Month/Year) and your full name
  • 21You may attach separate sheets if the space in the CV format is not enough.
