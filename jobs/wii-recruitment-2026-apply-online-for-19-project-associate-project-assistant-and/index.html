Selection and Interview

Shortlisting of applicants for online interviews will be based on essential qualifications, age limit, percentage of marks in Bachelor’s/Master’s degrees, specialization relevance, research experience, and publications. Based on screening criteria, the top ten (10) short-listed candidates against each advertised project/position will be called for interviews. The selection criteria marks are allotted as follows: Field/Lab/Project Assistant category: Bachelor's Degree percentage (25 Marks) and Specialization relevance via internship/volunteering/experience (25 Marks) — Total 50 Marks. Project Associate - I category: Master's Degree percentage (25 Marks) and Specialization relevance via internship/volunteering/experience (25 Marks) — Total 50 Marks. Project Associate – II/ Senior Project Associate categories: UGC/CSIR/ICAR NET with JRFship or WII-NET passed (5 Marks), Post Graduation marks
