About ORGI

The Office of the Registrar General, India (ORGI) is an office of the Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India. It is located at Janganana Bhawan, Tower-A, 2/A, Man Singh Road, New Delhi-110011. Its head is the Registrar General & Census Commissioner, India, and its offices named in the circular include the Map Division, the Language Division and the Directorates of Census Operations.

Who Can Apply

All the posts are open to officers of the Central Government, State Governments, Union Territory Administrations, recognized universities, recognized research institutions, public sector undertakings and statutory or autonomous bodies or organisations. Officers of Semi Government organisations may also apply, but only for Research Officer (Language)/Linguist. The application proforma states that only officers under the Central or State Government are eligible for deputation, and that candidates of non-Government organisations are eligible only for short-term contract. Each post needs two things: (a) the service condition for that post in the parent cadre or department, and (b) the essential educational qualification and experience. Pay and Tenure

The pay of the selected officer will be regulated under the Department of Personnel and Training's O.M.No.16/8/2009-Estt.(Pay-II) dated 17 June 2010, as amended from time to time. Posts are filled by deputation (including short term contract) initially for 3 (three) years or until further orders, whichever is earlier. The period of deputation (including short-term contract) also counts any deputation in another ex-cadre post held immediately before this appointment, in the same or another organisation or department of the Central Government. For Research Officer (Map), Research Officer (Drawing), Research Officer (Language)/Linguist and Map Analyst, this total shall ordinarily not exceed three years. For Assistant Registrar General (Map), Annexure-I says it shall ordinarily not exceed four years.

  • 1Get the Bio-data/Curriculum Vitae proforma (Annexure-II) from the circular. The circular says it is available on the website www.censusindia.gov.in.
  • 2Write the post you are applying for at the top. Fill in all items, including the eligibility and qualifications of the post set against what you hold (items 5(A) and 5(B)), your employment history and your present pay. Then sign it.
  • 3Attach self-attested certificates and documents supporting the qualification and experience you claim, and the certificates listed below.
  • 4Have the application certified by your Controlling Officer, and have the Employer/Head of Office/Forwarding Authority/Cadre Controlling Authority complete the certificate at the end of the proforma.
  • 5Have the application, in duplicate, forwarded through proper channel to: The Under Secretary, Admin-III Section, Office of the Registrar General & Census Commissioner, India, Ministry of Home Affairs, Janganana Bhawan, 3rd Floor, 2-A Mansingh Road, New Delhi-110011.
  • 6Make sure the application reaches within 60 days from the date the circular is published in Rozgar Samachar/Employment News.
  • 7Bio-data in duplicate as per the proforma in Annexure-II.
  • 8Supporting self-attested certificates/documents in support of the qualification and experience claimed, including supporting documents for items 5(A) and 5(B) of the proforma.
  • 9Cadre clearance certificate.
  • 10Statement giving the details of major or minor penalties imposed on the officer, if any, during the last ten (10) years.
  • 11Vigilance clearance/Integrity Certificate.
  • 12Photocopies of APARs for the past five years (2020-21 to 2024-25), attested on each page by an officer not below the level of Under Secretary or equivalent, or the CR Dossiers in original.
  • 13The certificate by the Employer/Head of Office/Forwarding Authority/Cadre Controlling Authority. It confirms that the details are correct and that the officer will be relieved immediately if selected. It also certifies that no vigilance or disciplinary case is pending or contemplated, certifies the officer's integrity, and states whether any major or minor penalty was imposed in the last 10 years.
  • 14As the proforma's note puts it, documents related to eligibility conditions "such as Bachelor's Degree, Experience Certificate (duly attested) and APARs for the period 2020-21 to 2024-25".
  • 15If your organisation does not follow Central Government pay scales: the latest salary slip, showing basic pay with the scale of pay and rate of increment, dearness pay/interim relief/other allowances with break-up, and total emoluments.
  • 16Officers already on deputation: the application must be forwarded by the parent cadre/Department along with cadre clearance, vigilance clearance and integrity certificate.
