How to Download the RMTMU Time Table December 2026 PDF

The notice does not say where it is published or how to download it. The steps below are general guidance based on the university's official website. Menu names may differ.

  • Open the official website of Raja Mansingh Tomar Music & Arts University: rmtmusicandartsuniversity.com.
  • Look for the Notices, Examination or Time Table section on the home page or menu.
  • Find the notice titled "Time Table for the Post-Graduate First Semester Examination (Regular/Private/ATKT) for the Session December-2026" or the matching Third Semester notice. Choose the one marked revised (21-11-2026).
  • Click the notice to open the PDF.
  • Download or save the PDF and print it if needed.

Important Instructions from the Notice

  • The main practical examinations will be held from 5 December 2026 to 15 December 2026.
  • For private (Swadhyayi) students, internal examinations (practical and theory) may be conducted before the main theory exam, either between 9:00 AM and 11:00 AM or between 3:00 PM and 5:00 PM, so that these students can attend conveniently.
  • If the time table has any typing error, the concerned college or university must be informed by email within 2 days. The Head of the SOS department will then correct the error and submit it promptly.
