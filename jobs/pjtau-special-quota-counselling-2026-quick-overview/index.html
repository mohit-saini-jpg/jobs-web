How to Attend PJTAU Special Quota Counselling 2026

PJTAU Special Quota counselling is manual, not online. There is no web-options or online seat-selection process for this round. To participate:

  • Check your category, applicable quota (Non-Farmers/Farmers'/Agriculture Labourers'), and TG EAPCET-2026 rank against the schedule table above to identify your date and time slot.
  • Visit www.pjtau.edu.in beforehand to check the latest seat availability position.
  • Report at the University Auditorium, PJTAU, Rajendranagar, Hyderabad, at least one hour before your scheduled time.
  • Carry all original certificates listed above, along with two Xerox copies of relevant certificates if you belong to EWS, BC, SC, or ST categories.
  • Only one parent/guardian is permitted to accompany the candidate during the counselling process.
  • Candidates who secure a seat must submit certificates and pay the prescribed fee on the spot to confirm admission.

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Counselling is held manually; it is not conducted online.
  • Selection under EWS, BC, SC, and ST quotas is subject to clearance of social status by the respective Telangana Government Directorates.
  • If a candidate attends counselling late, they must submit a letter to the counselling authorities indicating their actual time of arrival.
  • Candidates who fail to attend on the scheduled date and time cannot claim a seat as per their merit later.
  • If information or certificates furnished are found to be false, the University will take appropriate legal action.
  • Candidates/parents should not rely on middlemen or consultants; all seats are filled only through the University's combined counselling.
  • Candidates may take original certificates back temporarily (by furnishing an undertaking) if they need to attend another counselling, and must return them by the stipulated day, failing which the admission stands cancelled.
