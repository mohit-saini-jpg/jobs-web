HLL Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

HLL Lifecare Limited, the Government of India enterprise under the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, has opened applications for 55 vacancies across 20 posts, from Quality Assurance Officer and Junior Manager to Chief Manager and Deputy General Manager. Nineteen posts are on executive or non-executive fixed-term contract; one, Deputy General Manager (QA & QC) at Kanagala, is permanent. Postings are in Chhattisgarh, Kerala (Thiruvananthapuram, Ernakulam, Kozhikode, Thrissur), Karnataka (Bangalore, Kanagala), Goa, Gujarat, Chennai, and "anywhere in India" for three posts.

Who Can Apply

Indian nationals only. Age, qualification and experience are all reckoned as on 1 October 2026. The maximum age is set per post (37, 40, 45 or 47) and is listed with each post below. SC/ST/OBC/PwD candidates get relaxation as per Government of India directives; SC/ST and OBC (non-creamy layer) certificates must be in the format prescribed for Central Government posts. Post-qualification experience is counted after the qualifying degree; several posts set different experience for postgraduates and graduates. Candidates working in Government, semi-Government, PSU or autonomous bodies must produce a No Objection Certificate at the interview . For the permanent DGM post, candidates from Central PSUs must have held a post in the scale of Rs. 60,000–1,80,000 (2017) or Rs. 24,900–50,500 (2007) for two years.

Pay Structure Explained

HLL quotes three figures for each executive post: the basic pay range of the scale, the gross monthly salary at the minimum of the scale , and the annual CTC at the minimum . The gross and CTC are approximate and include allowances; they differ between Metro (Bangalore) and Non-Metro postings. Candidates placed above the minimum would earn more than the quoted gross. Contract tenure is not stated on the position pages; the posts are described only as fixed-term contract.

HLL Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Open the post's page on the HLL careers site and click the Google form link ("Click here to Apply").
  • 2Download the prescribed job application form from the link in the Google form or from the HLL website (the careers page lists it as "Blank Application Format").
  • 3Fill the form digitally and save it as PDF or Word. Handwritten forms are not accepted. Write the job title and reference code (for example, HLL/HR/092/2026) clearly on the form.
  • 4Upload the completed form in the Google form's file-upload field; maximum 10 MB per file.
  • 5Attach supporting documents in PDF or Word, each under 10 MB: CV, educational and experience certificates, and your latest salary slip.
  • 6Select the correct post in the form's "post applied for" field so the application is routed properly.
  • 7Submit on or before 21 October 2026. Late submissions are not considered.
  • 8Applications not in the prescribed format are rejected without any further communication, so use HLL's form, not a CV alone.
  • 9Check the post's experience rule for your qualification level; most posts require more years from graduates than from postgraduates.
  • 10If you work in a Government body or PSU, arrange the NOC now; it is required at the interview.
  • 11Everything stated in the form is verified against originals at the interview; a mismatch cancels the candidature at any stage.
  • 12Canvassing in any form disqualifies.

HLL Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1Is there a written test?
A
No. The position pages refer only to an interview, with documents verified at that stage.
Q2Are these permanent jobs?
A
Nineteen of the twenty posts are fixed-term contract (executive, or non-executive for QA Officer). Only Deputy General Manager (QA & QC) at Kanagala is permanent.
Q3What is the age cut-off date?
A
1 October 2026 for every post. The maximum age differs by post: 37 for most Junior and Associate Manager posts, 40 for Operations Manager and Business Manager (Lab In-Charge), 45 for Senior Operations Manager, Senior Business Manager and DGM, and 47 for Chief Manager.
Q4Can freshers apply?
A
Only for Junior Manager (Diagnostics) in Goa, where graduates may apply without experience. Every other post requires post-qualification experience.
Q5What does "gross salary at the minimum of the scale" mean?
A
It is HLL's approximate monthly gross, including allowances, for a candidate placed at the lowest basic pay of the scale. Bangalore postings use the Metro figure; all others the Non-Metro figure.
Q6Can I submit a handwritten application form?
A
No. The form must be filled digitally and uploaded as a PDF or Word file through the Google form.
Q7I work in a Government hospital or PSU. What do I need?
A
A No Objection Certificate from your employer, to be produced at the interview.
