BOB Local Bank Officer Vacancy 2026

Bank of Baroda has announced a total of 2482 vacancies for the Local Bank Officer post, distributed state-wise across 20 states/UTs. Vacancies include both "New Vacancies" and "Unfilled Vacancies" carried forward from previous LBO recruitment exercises. Candidates can apply for only one state, and the merit list will be drawn state-wise and category-wise. *For Arunachal Pradesh and Nagaland, English is accepted as the local language only for candidates who have studied and passed English as a subject from a school located in the respective state. Out of the total vacancies, 42 posts are reserved for Locomotor Disability (LD), 37 for Hearing Impaired (HI), 26 for Visually Impaired (VI), and 25 for the "d&e" PwBD category. The Bank may modify the number of vacancies depending on its requirement.

BOB Local Bank Officer Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must fulfil the following eligibility conditions, assessed as on the cut-off date of 01.08.2026, to apply for BOB Local Bank Officer Recruitment 2026. Candidates who have resigned from an officer-grade position in Bank of Baroda, or exited the Bank while on contract within the last three years as on the cut-off date, are not eligible to apply. Employees currently working in Bank of Baroda in the officer cadre or on contract basis are also not eligible.

Candidates can apply for only one state; multiple applications will result in forfeiture of fees for extra registrations. No change of category is permitted at any stage after registration of the online application. Candidates must possess a valid personal email ID and mobile number, kept active till completion of the recruitment process. Mobile phones, pagers, calculators, or similar devices are not allowed inside the examination premises. Selected candidates will be on probation for 12 months from the date of joining. Selected candidates must execute a Service Bond to serve a minimum of 3 years, or pay an amount equivalent to three months' gross salary (not exceeding Rs. 2 Lakhs) if they leave before completing three years of service. Any legal disputes arising from this recruitment fall under the exclusive jurisdiction of courts/tribunals in Mumbai.
