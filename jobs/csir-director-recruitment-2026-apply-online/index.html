CSIR Director Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR), under the Department of Scientific and Industrial Research, Ministry of Science and Technology, is seeking a Director for CSIR-Central Glass and Ceramic Research Institute (CSIR-CGCRI), Kolkata, in Level 15 of the Pay Matrix (Rs.1,82,200 – 2,24,100) with allowances as admissible. The post requires at least 16 years of R&D experience and is for a tenure of six years or till superannuation, whichever is earlier. Who Can Apply

CSIR's covering letter says the Director must be an accomplished scientist/technologist, a thinker and a visionary with a passion to serve society through high science and technological innovation, with the required R&D experience in the areas of the institute's activities and demonstrated leadership. Ph.D. in Natural Sciences, or Master's Degree in Engineering (for Engineering, Ph.D. is desirable). At least 16 years of experience in Research and Development (with focus on translational research) in the areas of activities of the laboratory/Institutes/Centre, and demonstrated excellence in leadership therein. Years of experience are computed from the beginning of the candidate's research career. Ph.D. for candidates applying with a Master's Degree in Engineering. A creative, innovative and well-recognised scientist/technologist with a demonstrated ability to manage multidisciplinary R&D

Pay and Terms

Pay: Level 15 of Pay Matrix (Rs.1,82,200 – 2,24,100) with allowances as admissible. Tenure: Six years or till superannuation, whichever is earlier. Renewal: The tenure period will be renewable only in exceptional cases. Absorption: The Director can be considered for absorption/placement in CSIR as Director's Grade Scientist, i.e. Scientist 'H'/Outstanding Scientist, as per rules. Share of external earnings: The provision to share money realised from external contract R&D, consultancy and rendering of S&T services is available as per extant rules. Housing and transport: Residential accommodation and transport are provided as per rules. Medical and other facilities: Provided as per CSIR rules.

CSIR Recruitment 2026 Job Description

The Director shall supervise and exercise administrative control on the staff of the Institute and shall be responsible for: realising the mission of the Institute; and creating an environment conducive to nurture innovation and high-class R&D and other S&T activities of the Laboratory/Institute in keeping with societal/industrial priorities.

CSIR Director Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

CVs received are sent for consideration of the Search-cum-Selection Committee. The notice does not describe further stages.
