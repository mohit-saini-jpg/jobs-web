Senior Research Fellow (Ayurveda): Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery (BAMS) degree from a recognized university/institute. Project Technical support -1 (Lab Technician): 10th pass with Diploma in Medical Laboratory Technology (DMLT/MLT) from a recognized institution with two years of work experience OR Three-year Graduate degree in B.Sc MLT from a recognized university with one year of work experience.
