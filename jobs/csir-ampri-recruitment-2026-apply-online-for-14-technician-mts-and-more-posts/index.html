CSIR AMPRI Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

The selection procedure depends on the post applied for and may include screening, trade tests, physical tests, skill assessments and competitive written examinations. Technical Officer: Screening, qualifying Trade Test and Competitive Written Examination comprising Paper I (Mental Ability), Paper II (General Awareness and English Language) and Paper III (Concerned Subject). Final merit is based on the prescribed written examination papers. Security Officer: Screening, physical standards and physical tests, followed by written examination covering mental ability, personality assessment and security-related subjects. Security Assistant: Screening, qualifying skill / physical and personality assessment tests, followed by a competitive written examination. CSIR AMPRI Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is the last date to apply for CSIR AMPRI Recruitment 2026?
A
The last date to apply online for CSIR AMPRI Recruitment 2026 is 13 November 2026 for Technical Officer and Technician (1) posts, and 23 November 2026 for Security Officer, Security Assistant, and Multi-Tasking Staff (MTS) posts. Candidates must submit their applications by 5:30 PM on the respective closing dates through the official website, ampri.res.in.
Q2How many total vacancies are announced in this notification?
A
A total of 14 vacancies have been announced across Technical Officer, Security Officer, Security Assistant, Technician (1), and Multi-Tasking Staff posts.
Q3What is the application fee for CSIR AMPRI Recruitment?
A
The application fee is ₹500/- for General, OBC, and EWS candidates. SC, ST, PwBD, women, and Ex-Servicemen candidates are exempted from paying the fee upon uploading valid certificates.
Q4What is the educational qualification required for Technician (1) posts?
A
Candidates need an SSC / 10th Standard or equivalent with Science subjects (minimum 55% marks) along with an ITI certificate in the respective trade (Instrument Mechanic, Fitter, Machinist, or Laboratory Assistant Chemical Plant) or relevant apprenticeship/work experience.
Q5Is there any negative marking in the written examination?
A
Yes, negative marking applies to papers specified in the notification (generally one negative mark for every wrong answer in objective tests, while Paper I for Technical Officer and Technician (1) has no negative marking).
Q6Can I apply for multiple post codes?
A
Yes, candidates can apply for multiple post codes provided they satisfy eligibility criteria, but they must submit a separate application and pay the required fee for each post code.
Q7Where will the examinations be conducted?
A
Examinations and tests will ordinarily be held at CSIR-AMPRI, Bhopal, or other designated centers as specified by the institute.
Q8Do working professionals need to submit an NOC?
A
Yes, regular employees working in CSIR, Government departments, autonomous bodies, statutory bodies, universities, or PSUs must upload a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from their employer.
