IISc Junior Security Assistant Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must have passed SSLC/Matriculation or an equivalent examination and satisfy the prescribed military, Agniveer or CAPF service-background requirements. A valid two-wheeler driving licence is also compulsory. Important Agniveer Provision: The four-year service and discharge requirements are subject to the expressly notified exception for first-batch Combatant Agniveers who have not yet completed their engagement. Candidates applying under this provision should follow the supporting-document requirements of the application portal. Applicants must possess a valid two-wheeler driving licence. IISc specifically instructs candidates to upload the licence in the "Any Other Relevant Certificates" field of the online application portal.

IISc Junior Security Assistant Contract Duration 2026

The engagement is initially for one year on a contractual basis and may be renewed annually up to a maximum of five years, depending on satisfactory performance and IISc requirements. IISc Junior Security Assistant Selection Process 2026

The selection process comprises a Physical Efficiency Test and Trade Test, followed by an Interview for shortlisted candidates. Application Screening: IISc will examine applications against the prescribed qualification, service background, age and other requirements. Physical Efficiency Test: Candidates will undergo the prescribed physical assessment. Trade Test: Candidates will be assessed in the relevant security-related skills. Interview: Shortlisted candidates will be called for an interview. Medical Fitness: Contractual engagement will be subject to medical fitness and verification of eligibility. The selection process will be conducted at IISc Bengaluru on dates to be announced separately. Shortlisted candidates will be informed through their registered email addresses.

IISc Junior Security Assistant Job Responsibilities

Selected candidates will be responsible for campus security and associated operational duties in the Security and Fire Fighting Department. Control entry and exit of personnel, visitors and vehicles at designated campus gates. Conduct regular campus security patrols. Monitor CCTV cameras, alarms and other security systems. Report suspicious activities and security incidents promptly. Respond to fire, medical and other emergencies in coordination with the department. Assist with crowd management and traffic regulation during institute events and VIP visits. Maintain security logs, visitor records and incident reports. Operate security and communication equipment. Perform assigned security duties during rotational shifts, including night shifts.
