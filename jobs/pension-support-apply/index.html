Who Is Eligible: विधवा के बच्चो की Pension निराश्रित बच्चों को वित्तीय Suppor

Widowed mothers ke dependent bachche (0-18 years) jo financially dependent hain, unhe is pension ke liye apply karna chahiye.

Pension ki rakam Rs. 1,000 per month per child hoti hai, jo annually Rs. 12,000 tak ja sakti hai. Ye amount education fees, medical bills aur daily needs me help karta hai.

How विधवा के बच्चो की Pension निराश्रित बच्चों को वित्तीय Suppor Works

Application online portal ya nearest CSC se submit hoti hai. Verification ke baad, pension bank account me direct transfer hoti hai har mahine, usually 5th ya 15th ko.

Q1Is pension sirf widowed mothers ke bachcho ke liye hi hai?
A
Haan, ye scheme specifically widowed mothers ke dependent bachcho ke liye design ki gayi hai.
Q2Pension kitni der tak milti rahegi?
