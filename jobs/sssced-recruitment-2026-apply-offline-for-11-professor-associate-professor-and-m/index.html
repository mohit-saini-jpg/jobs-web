About Sahayog Sevabhavi Sanstha College of Education

Sahayog Sevabhavi Sanstha College of Education is a college of education at Vishnupuri, Nanded, described in the notice as Permanent Non-Granted. The notice offers the M.Ed. and B.Ed. faculty posts listed here, and the application form is on the website of Swami Ramanand Teerth Marathwada University, Nanded (srtmun.ac.in).

Department-wise Vacancies

The notice lists 1 Professor, 1 Associate Professor and 9 Assistant Professor posts, all of regular nature. The 9 Assistant Professor posts carry the category break-up below, which adds up to 9. As per the Government Decision dated 25.01.2024, parallel reservation in recruitment will be strictly implemented. The parallel reservation printed is: Assistant Professor Female 05, Divyang 01, Sportsperson 01. The notice also states 3% reservation for handicapped and 30% for women candidates.
