NBEMS Diploma Final Theory Result 2026 - Overview : Result Overview

The National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS) has declared the NBEMS Diploma Final Theory Examination October 2026 Session Result. Candidates who appeared in the theory examinations conducted on 11, 12 and 13 September 2026 can check their qualifying status through the official result PDF available at natboard.edu.in.

How to Check NBEMS Diploma Final Theory Result 2026?

Candidates can follow these steps to check their NBEMS Diploma Final Theory Examination result:

  • Visit the official NBEMS website at natboard.edu.in.
  • Open the Examinations section.
  • Select the NBEMS Diploma Final Examination.
  • Choose the October 2026 session.
  • Click on the Theory Results link.
  • Open the official result PDF.
  • Use the PDF search option to enter your Roll Number or Application Sequence Number.
  • Check the result status displayed against your details.
  • Download or save the result PDF for reference.

NBEMS Diploma Final Theory Scorecard 2026

NBEMS has stated that individual scorecards containing the marks obtained in each theory paper and the grand total will be made available through candidate login under the relevant examination application link.

As of 20 November 2026, the available result report describes the scorecard as forthcoming and does not confirm a specific scorecard release date.

Candidate Portal: NBEMS Diploma Examination Login

Candidates should check the official website for the activation of the individual scorecard download facility.

How to Download NBEMS Diploma Final Theory Scorecard 2026?

Once the individual scorecard facility is activated, candidates can follow the steps below:

  • Visit the NBEMS Diploma Final Examination portal.
  • Select the October 2026 session.
  • Open the relevant Application Link or Candidate Login.
  • Enter the registered login credentials requested by the portal.
  • Access the candidate dashboard.
  • Open the scorecard download facility when available.
  • Check the paper-wise marks and grand total.
  • Download and save the scorecard.

NBEMS Diploma Answer Script Copy Request 2026

NBEMS allows candidates who have not qualified in the Diploma Final Theory Examination to request digital copies of their evaluated answer scripts.

The request must be submitted through the official Miscellaneous Fee Payment Online Portal by 25 November 2026.

NBEMS has stated that requests submitted after the prescribed deadline will not be entertained.

How to Request NBEMS Diploma Answer Script Copies?

  • Visit NBEMS Miscellaneous Fee Payment Portal.
  • Find the applicable option for requesting digital copies of answer scripts.
  • Enter the examination and candidate details requested by the portal.
  • Select the relevant question paper or papers.
  • Review the applicable fee of Rs. 100 plus GST per paper.
  • Complete the online request and payment process.
  • Save the payment acknowledgement for reference.

The request facility is intended for eligible unsuccessful candidates and must be used according to the instructions in the official result notification.

NBEMS Diploma Result 2026 Re-evaluation Rules

NBEMS has provided a limited facility for re-evaluation of unassessed answers in cases where an answer has incorrectly been marked as "NOT ATTEMPTED" by the assessor.

This facility is not a general rechecking or reassessment of already evaluated answers.

ParticularsDetails
Re-evaluation TypeUnassessed Answers Only
Eligible ConditionAnswer Incorrectly Marked as NOT ATTEMPTED
Last Date to Apply28 November 2026

Already assessed answers are not covered by this limited re-evaluation facility. Candidates should read the official result notification and check the applicable charges before submitting a request.

NBEMS Diploma Final Practical Examination 2026

Candidates who qualify in the Diploma Final Theory Examination become eligible to proceed to the practical examination, subject to the applicable NBEMS examination rules.

According to examination-related reports, NBEMS has also announced a tentative speciality-wise practical examination schedule extending from 8 December 2026 to 20 January 2027.

NBEMS Diploma Practical Admit Card 2026

Qualified candidates should monitor the NBEMS website and their OEEP accounts for practical examination centre allotment and admit card updates.

The practical examination date, time and venue will be communicated through the relevant official candidate facility. Candidates should not assume that the publication of the theory result means the practical admit card has already been released.
