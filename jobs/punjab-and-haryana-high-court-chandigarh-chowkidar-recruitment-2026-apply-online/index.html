Applicants of all categories should be not less than 18 years and not more than 35 years as on 21 November 2026. Punjab and Haryana High Court Chandigarh Chowkidar : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1Can a graduate apply for the Chowkidar post?
A
No. The maximum qualification is 10+2, and applicants with anything higher are not eligible.
Q2How many Chowkidar posts are open to General Category candidates?
A
19 of the 22 posts are in the General Category, though the total may change by the time the merit list is prepared.
Q3I am an SC candidate from outside Punjab, Haryana and Chandigarh. Which category do I choose?
A
You must apply under General Category and pay the General fee of Rs. 700.
Q4What is the interview marked on?
A
Half of the 50 marks are for suitability to the job and half are for etiquettes/manners.
Q5How much age relaxation do Ex-Servicemen get?
A
Their upper age limit rises by their years of defence service plus 03 years.
Q6Can I correct my application after submitting it?
A
No. Particulars, including the category, cannot be changed once filled, so check them before paying because the fee is not refunded.
Q7Will the High Court post my admit card?
A
No. You download the E-Admit Card from the High Court website after the link is uploaded and you receive an email or SMS about it.
Q8I work in a government department. What must I submit?
A
You need a Forwarding Letter/NOC in the Annexure II format from your Head of Department. The High Court may also seek a fresh NOC when giving the offer of appointment.
Q9Which version applies if a newspaper advertisement differs?
A
The contents on the official High Court website prevail over any advertisement published in newspapers.
