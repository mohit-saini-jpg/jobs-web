Overview

Haryana Ambedkar Scholarship 2026-27 : The Welfare of Scheduled Caste & Backward Classes Department, Government of Haryana, has released the Haryana Ambedkar Scholarship Yojana 2026 notification for students belonging to the SC, BC, and General categories. Students who meet the prescribed eligibility criteria can apply for the scholarship under the Dr. Ambedkar Medhavi Chhattar Sanshodhit Yojana. Eligible students can submit their application form online after checking all the eligibility conditions and required documents. Before applying, candidates are advised to read the complete Haryana Ambedkar Scholarship Yojana 2026 notification carefully to understand the eligibility criteria, application process, important dates, and other requirements.

Eligibility Details

  • 10th / 12th / Graduation Passed (Required Percentage).
  • Family Annual Income is Less Than 04 Lakhs.
  • Haryana Domicile and Other Required Documents.
  • UR / BC Can Apply Only For 10th Based Scholarship.

Important Documents

  • Family ID (Parivar Pehchan Patra, PPP)
  • Latest Photo & Signature
  • 10th / 12th / UG Mark Sheet Copy
  • Aadhar Card & Ration Card
  • Bank Account Copy
  • Income Certificate (Under 04 Lakhs / Year)
  • Domicile & Caste Certificate
  • Permanent Mobile Number & Mail ID
  • Present Educational Class ID Card
  • Father Death Certificate (If Father Has Died)
