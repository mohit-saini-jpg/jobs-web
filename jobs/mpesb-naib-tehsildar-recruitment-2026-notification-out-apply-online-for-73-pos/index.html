MPESB Naib Tehsildar Vacancy Details 2026

The 73 Naib Tehsildar posts are divided between two separate feeder cadres - candidates from the clerical service cadre compete for one set of posts, while candidates from the Patwari/Revenue Inspector cadre compete for a separate set. Each cadre also has its own backlog posts. Within the 32 direct-recruitment clerical-cadre posts, 1 post is reserved for persons with locomotor disability (as part of the overall PwD reservation policy), and 35% of posts across categories are horizontally reserved for women. As per the notification, 35% of posts are horizontally reserved for women in each category, and this reservation is applied both segment-wise and department-wise. No unfilled women's-quota post is carried forward - such posts are filled by male candidates of the same category instead.

MPESB Naib Tehsildar Eligibility Criteria

This recruitment is open only to current employees of the specified MP Revenue Department establishments who meet all of the following conditions: Currently working on a regular or officiating basis for at least 5 years in a clerical-cadre post, or as a Patwari/Revenue Inspector, in the Revenue Mandal, Commissioner Land Records, Principal Revenue Commissioner, Divisional Commissioner, or Collector establishments (including Sub-Divisional Officer (Revenue) and Tehsil offices under Collector establishments) Holds a Bachelor's degree in Arts, Science (including Technical and Engineering), Commerce, or Agriculture from a recognised university Maximum age as on 1 January 2026 should not exceed 55 years A candidate cannot appear in this exam under this scheme more than three times Candidates must possess the minimum educational qualification on the date of submitting the application; qualifica

MPESB Naib Tehsildar Age Limit

The maximum age limit for this departmental limited competitive exam is 55 years as on 1 January 2026. Since this is a departmental recruitment restricted to existing employees, the usual category-wise age relaxations applicable to open recruitment do not apply here - the 55-year cap itself is the eligibility ceiling. MPESB Naib Tehsildar Salary

Selected candidates will be placed at Pay Level-9 of the Madhya Pradesh Pay Revision Rules, 2017 pay matrix, with a starting basic pay of ₹36,200 plus applicable Dearness Allowance. The corresponding pay band under the earlier (6th CPC-based) structure was ₹9,300–₹34,800 with Grade Pay ₹3,600. The overall pay range for the post is ₹36,200–₹1,14,800 (Level-9).

MPESB Naib Tehsildar Selection Process

Selection is based purely on a written examination consisting of two objective-type papers, each carrying 100 marks and lasting 2 hours. Candidates need a minimum of 55% marks to qualify; SC and ST candidates need a minimum of 40% marks. Each correct answer carries 1 mark, and 0.25 marks are deducted for every wrong answer. Where the exam is conducted across multiple shifts, MPESB uses a Normalised Equi-Percentile scaling method to prepare the merit list.
