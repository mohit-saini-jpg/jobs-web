About CUS

Cluster University of Srinagar is based at Gogji-Bagh, Srinagar, Jammu & Kashmir – 190008. Its letterhead says it was established under J&K State Legislature Act-III, 2016. Appointments are governed by the Srinagar and Jammu Cluster Universities Act, 2016, and the Statutes and Regulations made under it.

CUS Faculty Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Applicants will be screened using the shortlisting criteria in the UGC Regulations 2018/University norms. Screening is based on what you enter in the online form. If any information turns out to be false or wrong at any stage, your candidature will be rejected. Each Academic/Research (API) score must be backed by documentary evidence in the Performance Based Appraisal System (PBAS) Proforma. Claims without evidence will not be counted. Shortlisted candidates attend an interview and must produce original documents on age, qualifications, experience, fitness and any other claims. No TA/DA is paid for attending.

Service Conditions

Selected candidates will be on probation for the period set by UGC norms. Terms of appointment follow the Srinagar and Jammu Cluster Universities Act, 2016, its Statutes and Regulations, and the rules of the UGC and the Government of Jammu and Kashmir, where applicable. Where the university has no service rules of its own, the rules of the Government of the UT of Jammu & Kashmir apply. The university may verify your character, antecedents and documents at any time, including during service. Services will be terminated at once if documents are found to be fake or a background has been hidden.

CUS Faculty Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Visit www.cusrinagar.edu.in, click the Jobs/Recruitment link and read the advertisement in full.
  • 2Click "Register as an Applicant" and register with a valid email ID and mobile number. Keep your username and password.
  • 3Log in with your username and password.
  • 4Enter your personal details, educational qualifications and experience, then preview the form. Correct anything that needs changing, then submit. You cannot edit the form after it is submitted. Print it and note the Form No.
  • 5Pay the application fee online.
  • 6Send the printout of the online form, self-attested copies of all documents and the fee receipt, in person or by Speed Post/Registered Post, to: Assistant Registrar (Establishment & Recruitment), Cluster University of Srinagar, Gogji-Bagh, Srinagar – 190008.
  • 7Documents and other rules:
  • 8Self-attest every enclosure and attach them in the order of the serial numbers generated in the form. Mark page numbers on all supporting documents, matching the application form.
  • 9Fill the PBAS proforma with each publication's details: title, journal/book, volume, page numbers, year, ISSN/ISBN, authorship, refereed/indexed status and local/national/international status. Back each entry with evidence such as copies of publications, project sanction letters, utilisation and completion certificates, patent letters and students' Ph.D. award letters.
  • 10If you claim a reserved category, include a valid category certificate from the Competent Authority.
  • 11Include an AIU Equivalence Certificate if your degree is from a foreign university.
  • 12Include a Relation certificate if any relative works in the university, giving their name, designation and department.
  • 13Write your name exactly as it appears on your Matriculation/Higher Secondary certificate.
  • 14If you are already employed, apply through proper channel. You should still send an advance copy with documents. The forwarded form must reach the university at least ten days before the interview, or you will not be called.
  • 15To apply in more than one category, submit a separate application for each.
  • 16Late or incomplete applications will not be accepted. Canvassing in any form disqualifies you.

CUS Faculty Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many Associate Professor and Professor posts are there in each department?
A
Each listed department has 1 post per position. Six departments have both an Associate Professor and a Professor post: Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, Botany, Chemistry, Data Science, Environmental Sciences and Zoology.
Q2What is the basic pay for a Professor at CUS?
A
A Professor is placed in Academic Level 14 of the 7th CPC, with basic pay from Rs. 1,44,200 to Rs. 2,18,200, plus admissible allowances.
Q3How many publications does an Associate Professor applicant need?
A
At least seven publications in peer-reviewed/UGC-listed journals, and a total academic/research score of 75 under the UGC Regulations, 2018.
Q4Can I apply for Professor without a teaching background?
A
Yes, under option B. You need a PhD in the relevant discipline, ten years' experience, and documented significant contributions to knowledge in the discipline from an academic institution or industry.
Q5Is there any relaxation in the 55% Master's marks?
A
Yes. PhD holders who obtained their Master's Degree before 19 September 1991 may be allowed 50% instead of 55%.
Q6Where do I send the hard copy of the application?
A
To the Assistant Registrar (Establishment & Recruitment), Cluster University of Srinagar, Gogji-Bagh, Srinagar – 190008. You can deliver it in person or send it by Speed Post/Registered Post, so that it arrives by 4:30 PM on 27 November 2026.
Q7Can a UR candidate apply for the Education post reserved for EWS?
A
Yes, at their own risk. A UR candidate will be considered only if no suitable EWS candidate is available.
Q8I am already in service. What extra step do I need to take?
A
Apply through proper channel and send an advance copy with documents. The form forwarded by your employer must reach the university at least ten days before the interview.
Q9Will travel costs for the interview be paid?
A
No. The notice says no TA/DA will be paid to candidates for appearing in the interview.
Q10Is the application fee refundable?
A
No. The Rs. 2000/- (UR/OM) or Rs. 1500/- (reserved categories) fee cannot be refunded once paid.
