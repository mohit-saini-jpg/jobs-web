BEML Apprentice Eligibility Criteria

Candidates who possess the required educational qualification and have passed their final examination within three years from the closing date of application are eligible to apply. Candidates are NOT eligible to apply in the following cases: Awaiting the results of their final examination. Not completed 17 years of age. Have undergone or are undergoing apprenticeship training in any organization for any duration. Please note: Candidates must possess a full-time regular degree/diploma qualification from a recognized institute/university. Qualifications acquired through part-time/correspondence/distance education/online mode are not eligible. Candidates should have passed out not before three years from the closing date of application; the date of result declaration should be clearly mentioned in the mark sheet, or supported by a certificate from the college/university. BEML Apprentice Stipend 2026

Selected apprentices will receive a monthly stipend based on their qualification category, subject to satisfactory performance and attendance. Poor performance or unauthorised absence may result in termination from the apprenticeship program. The total tenure of apprenticeship training is one year from the date of joining, co-terminated with completion of the one-year duration; no further extension is permitted.

BEML Apprentice Application Fee

There is no application fee for BEML Apprentice Recruitment 2026. There is also no written examination or interview process for applicants.

BEML Apprentice Age Limit

Applicants must be Indian citizens and 17 years old or above at the time of application. Candidates who have not completed 17 years of age are not eligible to apply. No upper age limit is specified in the notification. Before submitting the application, candidates are advised to verify that they meet the required age criteria. Use our Age Calculator to calculate your exact age as per the official cut-off date.

BEML Apprentice Selection Process

Applications will be screened as per eligibility criteria and prescribed qualification. A category-wise merit list will be prepared for each trade at each location, in the ratio of 1:5 times the number of training slots notified. Incomplete or ineligible applications will be summarily rejected without any intimation. Reservation is applicable for SC/ST/OBC(NCL) as per the Apprentices Act 1961 and Apprenticeship Rule 1992, as amended from time to time. Shortlisted candidates will be called for document/certificate verification in order of merit, in a ratio of 1:5 with respect to the training slots notified/available. Communication will be through the candidate's registered email. No TA/DA will be paid for the document verification process. BEML Apprentices Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is BEML Apprentice Recruitment 2026?
A
It is BEML Limited's engagement of apprentices under the Apprentices Act, 1961, for one-year training at its Bangalore, Mysore, KGF, and Palakkad Complexes, under Advertisement No. L&D/01/2026.
Q2How many training slots are available in BEML Apprentice Recruitment 2026?
A
A total of 1346 training slots are available - Bangalore Complex (400), Mysore Complex (227), KGF Complex (608), and Palakkad Complex (111).
Q3What is the last date to apply for BEML Apprentice Recruitment 2026?
A
Online applications can be submitted from 18.08.2026 to 08.09.2026, with the registration site available till 23:00 hrs on the closing date.
Q4What is the educational qualification for BEML Apprentice Recruitment 2026?
A
It varies by trade category: 4-year Engineering Graduate, 3-year General Graduate (B.Sc/B.Com/BCA/BBA), 3-year Diploma, or ITI (2 year/1 year based on trade), in the relevant discipline listed for each trade.
Q5What is the age limit for BEML Apprentice Recruitment 2026?
A
Applicants must be 17 years of age or above at the time of application. No upper age limit is specified in the notification.
Q6What is the application fee for BEML Apprentice Recruitment 2026?
A
There is no application fee. There is also no written examination or interview process.
Q7What is the stipend for BEML Apprentice Recruitment 2026?
A
Stipend ranges from Rs. 9,600/- to Rs. 12,300/- per month depending on the qualification category — Rs. 12,300/- for Graduate (Technical) and Graduate (General), Rs. 10,900/- for Diploma, and Rs. 9,600/- or Rs. 10,560/- for ITI trades.
Q8How can I apply online for BEML Apprentice Recruitment 2026?
A
Candidates must first register on NAPS (for ITI) or NATS (for Degree/Diploma), then apply on the BEML website at https://recruitment.bemlindia.in under Apprentices → Login/Registration.
Q9What is the selection process for BEML Apprentice Recruitment 2026?
A
Selection is merit-based (percentage of marks in the final qualifying examination), followed by document verification in a 1:5 ratio against training slots, and Offer of Engagement to eligible candidates, with the remainder placed on a Reserved Waiting Panel.
Q10Can I apply for more than one trade or location in BEML Apprentice Recruitment 2026?
A
No. Candidates are permitted to submit only one application in a particular trade at a particular location. Multiple applications will be summarily rejected.
