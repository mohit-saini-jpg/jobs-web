AP DSC Eligibility Criteria 2026

The educational and professional qualifications for AP DSC Recruitment 2026 will be specified in the detailed notification. The brief notifications cover different teaching posts but do not provide the final post-wise eligibility conditions. Candidates should check the detailed notification for their preferred post before submitting an online application. The applicable recruitment rules will be those prevailing on the date of the detailed notification.

AP DSC Age Limit 2026

The age limits, eligibility relaxations and related conditions for AP DSC Recruitment 2026 will be provided in the detailed notification. Candidates should check the official notification for the age requirements applicable to their post and category.

AP DSC Selection Process 2026

The AP DSC 2026 recruitment examinations will be conducted through a Computer Based Test (CBT) with objective-type questions . The examination dates will be announced in the detailed notification. The AP DSC 2026 syllabus is hosted on the official recruitment website. Candidates should refer to the official syllabus and subsequent recruitment instructions for their respective posts.
