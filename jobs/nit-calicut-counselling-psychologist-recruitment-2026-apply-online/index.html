Who Can Apply

Candidates must meet the following educational qualifications, experience, and age criteria to be eligible for the NIT Calicut Counselling Psychologist position. Master’s degree in Psychology – M.A / M.Sc. in Psychology, Clinical Psychology, Counselling Psychology, or Applied Psychology; OR Master of Social Work (MSW) with specialisation in Psychiatry / Mental Health. RCI recognised professional qualification and valid RCI/CRR registration, such as: Master of Philosophy (M.Phil.) in Clinical Psychology Professional Diploma in Clinical Psychology (P.D. Cl. Psy.) Post Graduate Diploma in Rehabilitation Psychology (P.G.D.R.P.) Two years of experience in counselling is required. Candidates should preferably be below 50 years of age. Pay and Tenure

Selected candidates will receive a consolidated pay ranging from ₹40,000/- to ₹60,000/- per month, commensurate with their experience and qualification. The appointment is on a full-time contractual basis for an initial period of one year, which is extendable based on performance and institute requirements.

NIT Calicut Counselling Psychologist Job Description

The engaged Counselling Psychologists will perform the following roles and responsibilities: Counselling students. Coordinate with online wellness partner. Coordinate wellness events on campus and work with Students Guidance Cell volunteers. Submit a weekly report to the Dean (Students Welfare) Office on student caseload and well-being. Maintain professional boundaries with students and uphold confidentiality in consultations and diagnoses. Ensure information gathered during consultations is not disclosed, made public, or utilized in any way except for offering assistance to students. Conduct scheduled rotational visits across various departments, hostels, and academic facilities on campus to deliver specialized sessions.

Documents Required

Applicants must email and produce the following documents during the recruitment process: Detailed Curriculum Vitae (CV) Diploma or Degree certificates with mark sheets of all qualifying examinations Experience certificates and testimonials Proof of date of birth ID proof Community or category certificate issued by the Competent Authority (if belonging to SC, ST, OBC-NCL, or PwBD claiming concessions)

Selection and Interview

Candidates will be evaluated based on their qualifications and experience. The selection committee may conduct a skill test and/or trade test. Shortlisted candidates will be informed by email or mobile, and selected individuals are expected to join immediately.
