NeGD Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

About NeGD

NeGD is an independent business division under the Digital India Corporation, Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, at Electronics Niketan, 6, CGO Complex, Lodhi Road, New Delhi - 110003. The notice says it supports MeitY in programme management and implementation of e-governance projects of various Ministries and Departments at the Central and State levels. Initiatives named in the notice include DigiLocker, UMANG, Poshan Tracker, OpenForge Platform, API Setu, National Academic Depository, Academic Bank of Credits and Learning Management System.

NeGD Recruitment 2026 Job Description
