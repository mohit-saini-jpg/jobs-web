Satavahana University Part-Time Faculty Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

A selection committee will scrutinise the applications on the basis of educational qualification, a demo and interview, and the Rule of Reservation. Educational Qualification: Applications are scrutinised on qualifications. Only shortlisted candidates will be called for interviews. Demo & Interview: Shortlisted candidates give a demo and attend an interview. The date of the interview will be announced later. Rule of Reservation: The Rule of Reservation is followed, using the roster points shown in the vacancy table. The candidate scoring high marks will be considered for appointment.
