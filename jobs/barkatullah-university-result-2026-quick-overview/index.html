Barkatullah University Result 2026 - Direct Link

Barkatullah University (BU), Bhopal has declared the BA 3rd Final Year (NEP) Result 2026 and the B.Sc B.Ed / B.A B.Ed Secondary (RIE) Semester 1, 3, 5 Result 2026. Students who appeared in these examinations can check their results online through the official result portals linked by Barkatullah University.

The BA 3rd Final Year NEP result is available through the MPOnline result portal, while the B.Sc B.Ed and B.A B.Ed Secondary RIE Semester 1, 3 and 5 results are available through the university's ICRP ERP portal. Students should keep their Roll Number or Enrollment Number ready before opening the result link.

B A 3rd Final Year NEP

This result is for students of the BA (Bachelor of Arts) 3rd and final year under the National Education Policy (NEP) pattern. It is published on the MPOnline-hosted NEP result portal linked to Barkatullah University.

B.Sc B.Ed and B.A B.Ed Secondary (RIE) Semester 1, 3, 5

This result covers the integrated B.Sc B.Ed and B.A B.Ed (Secondary) programmes offered through the Regional Institute of Education (RIE) stream affiliated with Barkatullah University, specifically for Semester 1, Semester 3, and Semester 5. It is published on the ICRP ERP student result portal used for RIE-affiliated programmes.

Specific details such as exam session (e.g., which month/year's examination this result corresponds to), pass percentage, and number of students covered are not available in the official notification for these two entries. Please refer to the result displayed on the respective portal after entering your details for entry-specific information.

Required Details to Check the Result

Based on how these two portals are generally structured on the official BU Bhopal result page, students are typically asked for the following. Exact field requirements may vary slightly by portal and are confirmed only once you open the result page.

  • Roll Number / Enrollment Number
  • Date of Birth (format generally DD/MM/YYYY, where applicable)
  • Captcha code, where shown on screen

How to Check BA 3rd Final Year NEP Result

  • Visit the official Barkatullah University result page: bubhopal.ac.in/1594/Online-Result.
  • In the "List of Result" table, locate the entry titled "B A 3RD FINAL YEAR NEP".
  • Click the result link. You will be taken to the MPOnline-hosted NEP result portal at bubhopal.mponline.gov.in.
  • Enter your Roll Number and other requested details (such as Date of Birth) exactly as asked on the portal.
  • Submit the form to view your result on screen.
  • Download or take a screenshot/printout of the result for your records.

How to Check B.Sc B.Ed / B.A B.Ed Secondary (RIE) Semester 1, 3, 5 Result

  • Visit the official Barkatullah University result page: bubhopal.ac.in/1594/Online-Result.
  • In the "List of Result" table, locate the entry titled "B Sc B Ed and B A B Ed Secondary RIE Semester 1 3 5".
  • Click the result link. You will be redirected to the ICRP ERP student result portal at bubhopal.icrp.in/erp.
  • Enter your Roll Number/Enrollment Number and any other requested details on the form.
  • Click Search Result / Submit to view your result.
  • Download or print the result for future reference.

Important Instructions

  • The result published online is provisional and meant for immediate reference only; it is not a substitute for the original marksheet, which is issued separately by the university.
  • Cross-check your name, roll number, and marks carefully after the result is displayed.
  • Specific instructions on revaluation, retotaling, or grievance redressal for these two particular results are not available in the official notification. Check the "Important Instructions" / notifications section on the official BU Bhopal website for updates on these processes.
  • Keep a downloaded copy or printout of your result safe, as it may be needed for admission, employment, or scholarship purposes.
  • Always verify your result only on the official Barkatullah University website or the linked official result portals mentioned in this article.
