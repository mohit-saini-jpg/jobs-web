About APPSC

The Andhra Pradesh Public Service Commission conducts recruitment and selection for State posts, with its office in Vijayawada. The notice states that the Commission acts under Articles 315 to 320 of the Constitution of India and follows the principle of order of merit. The post in this notification serves the Endowments Service for A.P. charitable and Hindu religious institutions.

APPSC Selection Process 2026

Selection is based on merit in the Written Examination, subject to the candidate qualifying the Computer Proficiency Test (CPT). Appointment is subject to medical fitness. Written Examination: objective type, degree standard, conducted by the Commission in online Computer Based Recruitment Test (CBRT) mode. The date will be intimated later. Appearance in all papers is compulsory; absence in any paper disqualifies the candidature. Computer Proficiency Test (CPT): candidates provisionally shortlisted after the written examination, in the ratio prescribed by the Commission, have to qualify the CPT. Minimum qualifying marks on aggregate: OC and EWS 40%, Backward Class 35%, SCs, STs and PBDs 30%. Securing the minimum marks does not confer any right to be considered for selection. If SC and ST candidates do not come up for selection with the minimum marks, their selection is considered on rank

APPSC Exam Pattern 2026

Each wrong answer is penalised with 1/3rd of the marks prescribed for the question. The General Studies and Mental Ability paper is set in English and translated into Telugu; the English version is authentic. Subject papers are in English only. If questions are deleted from a paper, scaling is done for that part of the paper and marks are rounded off to 2 decimals. If the Computer Based Tests are held in multiple sessions, the Commission will normalise scores and announce results on a percentile basis. The test is held at four centres: Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada, Tirupathi and Ananthapuramu. Candidates give three preferences, but the Commission may create any centre or allot any centre. APPSC Application Correction 2026

Corrections in the application are enabled after the last date of submission and allowed up to 7 days from the last date of applications.
