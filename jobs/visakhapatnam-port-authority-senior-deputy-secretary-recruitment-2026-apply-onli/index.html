About VPA Senior Deputy Secretary Recruitment

Visakhapatnam Port Authority is operating under the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways. The administration has issued a re-circulation notification to fill one vacant post of Senior Deputy Secretary in the General Administration Department. The selection is conducted by merit through composite method from eligible officers of Major Port Authorities.

Vacancy Details

There is a total of 1 post available for recruitment under the General Administration Department of Visakhapatnam Port Authority.

Who Can Apply

For absorption through composite method, officers holding analogous posts or the post of Deputy Secretary and equivalent posts in the respective disciplines of General Administration Department (such as CPRO/Deputy Estate Manager/Deputy Chief Law Officer/Personnel Officer) in the scale of pay of Rs. 60000 - 180000 (Pre-revised Rs. 13000 - 18250) with three years regular service in the grade in a Major Port Authority or Deputy Secretary and equivalent posts in the respective discipline of General Administration Department with two years regular service in the grade and a combined regular service of seven years in the scales of pay of Rs. 50000 - 160000 (Pre-revised Rs. 10750 - 16750) and Rs. 60000 - 180000 (Pre-revised Rs. 13000 - 18250) in the respective discipline of General Administration Department in a Major Port Authority will be eligible. Visakhapatnam Port Authority Senior Deputy Secretary : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Access the Online Application Portal (OAP) of the Ministry of Ports, Shipping & Waterways at http://onlinevacancy.shipmin.nic.in.
  • 2Submit the online application between 22.10.2026 and 21.11.2026.
  • 3Take a print-out of the online application.
  • 4Attach copies of educational qualifications and experience, a self-attested passport size photograph, an undertaking that candidature will not be withdrawn (Annexure - IV), Administrative Clearance (Annexure - II), No Objection Certificate (Annexure - V), Vigilance/Administrative clearance proforma (Annexure - III), service particulars statement (Annexure - VI), and ACRs/APARs for the last 5 years (2021-2022 to 2025-2026) along with grading statement, duly attested by an officer not below the rank of Deputy HoD on each page (or non-availability certificate).
  • 5Super-scribe the envelope with "Application for the post of Sr. Deputy Secretary (Class - I) in Visakhapatnam Port Authority".
  • 6Send the application through proper channel so as to reach the SECRETARY, Visakhapatnam Port Authority, Administrative Office Building, Port Area, Visakhapatnam - 530035 on or before 07.12.2026.
