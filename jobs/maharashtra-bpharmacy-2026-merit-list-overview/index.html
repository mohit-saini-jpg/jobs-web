How to Check the Maharashtra B.Pharmacy 2026 Merit List

Maharashtra B.Pharmacy 2026 Merit List: Grievance Process

  • Log in to your candidate account on the CAP portal.
  • Submit your grievance between August 28 and 31, 2026.
  • Attach relevant supporting documents for the claim or correction being requested.
  • Regularly check your login for acceptance, rejection, or further instructions on the grievance.

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Do not share your CAP login credentials publicly; use only the official CET Cell website.
  • Use the waiting period before merit-list release to prepare a tentative college preference list, without finalizing it until the official seat matrix is out.
  • Do not compare your merit position with another candidate's without accounting for differences in candidature type, category, and applicable seat pool.
  • Treat previous years' closing ranks only as general guidance, not as a guarantee for 2026 admissions, since seat availability and competition can vary each year.
  • Ensure your CAP application was successfully submitted and verified before interpreting your merit position.
