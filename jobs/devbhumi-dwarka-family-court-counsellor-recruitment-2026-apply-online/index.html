About Devbhumi Dwarka Family Court Counsellors

Family Court, Devbhumi Dwarka is preparing a panel of Counsellors for the Family Courts in Devbhumi Dwarka district, located at Khambhaliya, Okha-Mandal and Kalyanpur. The empanelment is made under the Gujarat Family Court (Amendment) Rules, 2023. The Family Court functions from the New District Court Building, Salaya Road, Khambhaliya.

Who Can Apply

An applicant must possess the following qualifications as on the last date of application: Degree of a recognized University, preferably with Social Science or psychology as one of the subjects. Minimum experience of 02 (Two) years in Social Work, Child Psychiatry or Family Counselling. Impeccable integrity. The application form asks for educational qualifications from the Bachelor's degree onwards, with certificates attached, and for experience in the field of social and family welfare with certification as required under Rule 5(B) of the Gujarat Family Court (Amendment) Rules, 2023. Where other things are equal, preference in appointment shall be given to a woman candidate having the requisite qualification. Pay and Tenure

A candidate appointed as Counsellor, Family Court shall be paid an honorarium or fee as may be determined and notified by the State Government from time to time. The notice does not print an amount. The appointment of a Counsellor may be terminated on recommendation of the High Court, without assigning any reason, at any time before the expiry of his or her term, on the recommendation of the concerned Principal Judge, Family Court. The length of the term is not specified in the notice.

Devbhumi Dwarka Family Court Counsellors Job Description

The Counsellor works in association with the Family Court to promote conciliation and secure speedy settlement of disputes relating to marriage and family affairs and connected matters. The Counsellor shall not act or plead for a party to a case or proceeding, but shall generally assist the Court in reaching a peaceful and amicable settlement of the dispute. A Counsellor entrusted with any petition shall: attend the Court as and when required by the Judge of the Family Court; aid and advise the parties regarding settlement of the subject-matter of dispute or any other part thereof; help the parties in reconciliation; submit a report or interim report, as required by the Court to which he or she is attached; perform such other functions as may be assigned by the Family Court to which he or she is attached, from time to time.

Selection and Interview

Applications will be scrutinised and eligible candidates will be called for interview. The list of candidates called for interview will be placed on the official website of District Court, Devbhumi Dwarka, and candidates must check it on the website on 23 November 2026. Interviews will be held from 26 November 2026 to 27 November 2026. The selection list will then be sent to the Hon'ble Gujarat High Court for consideration and approval.
