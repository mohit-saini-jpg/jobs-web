RCF Management Trainee Salary

Selected candidates undergo a one-year training period and are paid a lump sum stipend of ₹60,000 per month during this period. Free hostel accommodation on a sharing basis is provided at Trombay/Thal, along with medical facility at the RCF Hospital. On successful completion of training, trainees are absorbed in the E1 Grade with a pay scale of ₹40,000–₹140,000. The minimum total monthly gross emoluments (Basic Pay + VDA at 51.80% + Perks at 34% + HRA at 30% and other allowances) work out to approximately ₹86,320. Management Trainees posted outside Trombay and Thal (such as at Marketing Area Offices) additionally receive a fixed allowance of ₹5,000 per month towards boarding, lodging, and local travel, plus TA/DA on tours. RCF Management Trainee Selection Process

The RCF Management Trainee Selection Process consists of an Online Test followed by a Personal Interview. Computer-based objective test of 90 minutes, with 100 questions in total. 50 questions (2 marks each) are from the course curriculum of the relevant discipline/qualifying degree. 50 questions (1 mark each) cover General English, Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning, and General Knowledge/Awareness. There is negative marking for wrong answers. The test is conducted in Hindi (Rajbhasha) and English, at centres in Bhopal, Delhi/Delhi NCR, Lucknow, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Chennai, Guwahati, Kolkata, Mumbai/Navi Mumbai/Thane/MMR region, and Nagpur. Based on online test performance and document verification, eligible candidates are provisionally called for interview in a ratio of approximately 1:7 (7 candidates per post). Candidates must score a minimum average of 50% in the interview to qualify
