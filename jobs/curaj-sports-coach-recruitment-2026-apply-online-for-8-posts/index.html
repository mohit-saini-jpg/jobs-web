CURAJ Sports Coach Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Central University of Rajasthan (राजस्थान केन्द्रीय विश्वविद्यालय) is inviting online applications for 8 posts of Sports Coaches, one each in Badminton, Basketball, Cricket, Football, Volleyball, Boxing, Table Tennis and Wushu. The posts are purely on contractual basis for a period of one year or till further orders, whichever is earlier, at a consolidated monthly salary of Rs. 55,000/-. The online application closes on 21 November 2026 (11:59 PM), and the hard copy with self-attested documents must reach the University by 26 November 2026 (05:00 PM).

About CURAJ

Central University of Rajasthan (CURAJ) is a central university at NH-8, Bandarsindri, Kishangarh, in Ajmer district, Rajasthan. Its letterhead states that it has Category-I University status from the UGC. The Registrar issued this recruitment through the University's Recruitment Cell.

Who Can Apply

All eight Sports Coach posts have the same essential and desirable qualifications. For each post, the qualification and experience must be in that post's own sport. Candidates are responsible for checking their own eligibility for the post they apply for. The notice does not mention an age limit for these posts. For the discipline-specific eligibility of each post, see the official notification.

Pay and Tenure

Sports Coaches are eligible for a consolidated monthly salary of Rs. 55,000/- per month. However, the salary will be commensurate to the qualification and experience of the candidates. The appointment is purely on contractual basis, for a period of one year or till further orders, whichever is earlier. The candidate will not be entitled to any other benefits that are admissible to the regular employees of the Central University of Rajasthan. The engagement does not give any right to claim a regular appointment in the University or continued service in the University. When the engagement period in the offer ends, the candidate will not need to report for duty unless the University gives a further extension. The University reserves the right to end the engagement at any time, even before the stipulated period, for unsatisfactory performance, misconduct or any other administrative reasons/c

Selection and Interview

Candidates must report at the University for an interview on the prescribed date and time. The University will publish the interview schedule separately on its website, www.curaj.ac.in. If any dispute or ambiguity arises in the selection process, the University's decision is final.
