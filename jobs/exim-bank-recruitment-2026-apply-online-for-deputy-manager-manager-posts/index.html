Exim Bank Deputy Manager & Manager Selection Process

Selection comprises a Written Test followed by a Personal Interview. There is no sectional cut-off; the final list for interviews is drawn based on the candidate's overall performance in the written test. Final selection is based on combined performance in the Written Examination (out of 100, with 70% weightage) and the Interview (out of 100, with 30% weightage). A pre-recruitment training will be organised for candidates from reserved categories who opt for it through the online application form.

Exim Bank Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is Exim Bank Recruitment 2026?
A
It is a Special Recruitment Drive (SRD) by the Export-Import Bank of India to fill 8 backlog vacancies for Deputy Manager (Grade JM I) and Manager (Grade MM II) posts, under Advertisement No. HRM/DM & M/SRD/2026-27/04.
Q2How many vacancies are available in Exim Bank Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 8 vacancies in total - 6 for Deputy Manager (Grade/Scale JM I) and 2 for Manager (Grade/Scale MM II), against SC, ST, and OBC backlog categories.
Q3What is the last date to apply for Exim Bank Recruitment 2026?
A
The online application window is open from 15 September 2026 to 10 October 2026.
Q4What is the qualification required for Exim Bank Deputy Manager and Manager posts?
A
Both posts require a minimum 50% graduation (3-year full-time) plus a Post Graduation (MBA/PGDBA/PGDBM/MMS) with specialization in Finance/International Business/Foreign Trade, or a Chartered Accountant qualification with ICAI membership, along with relevant banking experience.
Q5What is the age limit for Exim Bank Recruitment 2026?
A
As on 31-08-2026, the maximum age (including relaxation) is 33 years (SC/ST) and 31 years (OBC-NCL) for Deputy Manager, and 35 years (SC/ST) and 33 years (OBC-NCL) for Manager. PwD candidates get a further 10 years' relaxation, and Ex-servicemen get 5 years.
Q6What is the application fee for Exim Bank Recruitment 2026?
A
The fee is ₹600/- for OBC candidates and ₹100/- (intimation charges) for SC/ST/PwBD and Female candidates, payable online and non-refundable.
Q7What is the selection process for Exim Bank Recruitment 2026?
A
Selection is based on a Written Test (Professional Knowledge – Subjective, 100 marks) followed by a Personal Interview, with 70% weightage to the written test and 30% to the interview in the final merit.
