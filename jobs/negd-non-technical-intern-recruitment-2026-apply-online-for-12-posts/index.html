NeGD Non-Technical Intern Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The National e-Governance Division (NeGD), Digital India Corporation (DIC), Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India, has invited applications for 12 non-technical interns under its Winter Programme 2026. The token remuneration is Rs. 15,000 per month. The minimum duration is two months, extendable by up to one month. Eligible students apply through the online application form, sponsored or forwarded by their institution, by 20 November 2026 (Friday).

About NeGD

The notice is issued by the National e-Governance Division (NeGD) of Digital India Corporation (DIC) under the Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India. The Internship Programme is conducted twice a year, as a Summer Internship Programme and a Winter Internship Programme. This notice covers the Winter Programme 2026.

NeGD has listed 12 intern positions in five interest areas: 5 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 1 = 12. Each area contains one or more roles, described under Job Description below.

Who Can Apply

The qualification below applies to the non-technical internship as a whole. The notice does not name a separate qualification for each interest area. Eligible applicants are students from recognised universities in India who meet any one of these conditions: They have secured at least 60% marks in the last held degree or certificate examination and have completed education under the 10+2+3 pattern, that is, 15 years of formal education; OR They are pursuing the pre-final or final year of Engineering in the 10+2+4 pattern of education; OR They are pursuing the pre-final or final year of a Diploma/Certificate course; OR They are pursuing the pre-final or final year of an integrated degree course or dual degree of the 10+2+5 pattern of education. For the discipline-specific eligibility of each role, candidates should refer to the official notification.

Pay and Tenure

A token remuneration of Rs. 15,000/- per month is paid to an intern, subject to satisfactory performance duly certified by the Mentor. It is paid after the complete tenure of the internship, on submission of a Report duly accepted by NeGD. The notice prints no HRA or other allowance. The minimum duration of the internship is two months, extendable up to one month (total three months), depending on the candidate's performance, NeGD's requirements and the time the intern is willing to spend with NeGD. Every batch of interns or individual interns has a Mentor from NeGD, preferably the HOD of the Division/Project. NeGD issues certificates on completion of the internship and submission of the Report, duly countersigned and accepted by the Competent Authority.

Selection and Interview

Interns are shortlisted based on marks in the qualifying degree. They are selected for the internship through interviews, or based on the judgement of the internal selection committee, as per NeGD's requirements. Weightage is given to the top 100 colleges per year, as per the ranking of the National Institutional Ranking Framework by the Ministry of Education. The selection of the candidate is based on the prescribed weightage formula. The notice does not print the weightage formula or the interview mode.
