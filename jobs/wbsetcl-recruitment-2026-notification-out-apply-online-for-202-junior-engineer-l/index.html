How Will WBSETCL Select Candidates?

Computer Based Test (CBT) Personal Interview (15 marks) Pre-employment Medical Examination Police Verification and Caste/other certificate verification Shortlisting for Interview in 1:3 ratio in each category based on CBT performance Personal Interview carries 15 marks Final selection on combined CBT and Interview performance, medical fitness, vacancies and reservation rules CBT and Personal Interview to be held in Kolkata; CBT may be held in multiple cities of West Bengal
