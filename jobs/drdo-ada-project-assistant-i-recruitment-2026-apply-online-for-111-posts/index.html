ADA Project Assistant-I Tenure

The engagement is initially for a period of two years, which may be extended for a further period of up to two years (one year at a time), subject to satisfactory performance as assessed by a Review Committee. The engagement is on a tenure basis and confers no right, implicit or explicit, for continuation beyond the tenure, absorption, or regularisation in ADA.

ADA Project Assistant-I Selection Process

The selection method depends on the qualification pathway a candidate falls under, as shown in the Qualification table above: Interview Only: Applicable to candidates with a valid GATE score, M.E/M.Tech from an IIT/NIT, or (for Computer Science and Electronics & Communication Engineering) B.Sc + M.Sc with a valid GATE/NET score. Shortlisting by Written Test followed by Interview: Applicable to candidates qualifying through other routes, such as B.E/B.Tech with M.E/M.Tech, B.E/B.Tech with 2 years of relevant experience, or B.E/B.Tech alone. The written test will follow the same syllabus as GATE for the relevant discipline and will have negative marking for wrong answers. Candidates are advised to bring a scientific calculator to the written test.
