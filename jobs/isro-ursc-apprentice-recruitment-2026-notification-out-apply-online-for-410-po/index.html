URSC Apprentice Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Only candidates who have passed out in the years 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, or 2026 are eligible to apply. Candidates who cleared their qualifying examination before 2021 are not eligible. Only those who completed the qualifying examination as regular candidates can apply - candidates with sandwich course, certificate, private college degree, or short-term/certificate course qualifications are not acceptable, and candidates currently pursuing higher studies are also not eligible.

URSC Apprentice 2026 Salary Structure

Selected apprentices will receive a fixed monthly stipend as per their stream: ₹12,300/- per month for Graduate Apprentices (B.E./B.Tech.), and ₹10,900/- per month for both Technician Apprentices (Diploma) and Commercial Practice Apprentices.

URSC Apprentice Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Candidates will be shortlisted based on their percentage of marks in the respective discipline Shortlisted candidates will be called for Document Verification On successful completion of document verification, a panel will be drawn based on academic scores in the Degree/Diploma Induction of apprentices against vacant positions will be made strictly based on the candidate's position in the panel Selected candidates will be informed only through email Candidates shortlisted for document verification are not eligible for Travelling Allowance

ISRO URSC Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Visit the National Apprenticeship Training Scheme (NATS) portal at https://nats.education.gov.in and register/enroll as a candidate
  • 2Upload the Degree/Diploma certificate (as applicable) and a valid government-issued ID during enrolment
  • 3Note the Enrolment Number/Apprenticeship Registration Number generated after successful registration on the NATS portal
  • 4Visit the UMANG portal at https://web.umang.gov.in/landing/ and register/log in
  • 5Search for "URSC Apprenticeship" on the UMANG portal
  • 6Click on Apply for Position and fill in all the required details, compulsorily including the NATS Enrolment Number/Apprenticeship Registration Number
  • 7Upload the required documents (photograph, signature, certificates, caste/EWS/disability certificate as applicable)
  • 8Review all entered details carefully, since no changes are allowed once the application is submitted
  • 9Submit the application before the closing date, 28 August 2026 (11:59 Hrs)
