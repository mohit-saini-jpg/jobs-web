GSL Assistant Superintendent Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Goa Shipyard Limited (GSL), a Government of India Undertaking under the Ministry of Defence, will fill 4 regular non-executive posts under Advt. No. 10/2026: 2 Assistant Superintendent (Personal Secretary) and 2 Assistant Superintendent (Stores/Commercial), on pay scale 21000-3%-70000 (S-1). Online applications open at 00.00 hrs on 25 November 2026 and close at 17.00 hrs on 24 December 2026 at www.goashipyard.in. The application fee is Rs. 200; SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen and internal candidates are exempted.

About GSL

Goa Shipyard Limited is a Government of India Undertaking under the Ministry of Defence, located at Vaddem, Vasco-da-Gama, Goa. The notice describes it as a Schedule 'A' Mini Ratna Category I Company engaged in designing and building ships for the Indian Navy, the Indian Coast Guard and other customers, including friendly foreign countries.

Vacancy Details

The notice lists 4 vacancies in total. It states that they include backlog reserved vacancies. Reserved category candidates who meet the eligibility criteria may also apply for the posts earmarked for UR, as per Government rules. A reserved category candidate applying for a post with no vacancies in their own category is treated as a General candidate, and no relaxation in any criteria applies to such candidates.

Pay and Tenure

Both posts carry the pay scale 21000-3%-70000 (S-1). The posts are filled on regular basis. The notice does not mention allowances.

  • 1Applications are accepted only through the online system. The online window opens on 25 November 2026 and closes on 24 December 2026 at 17.00 hrs.
  • 2Visit www.goashipyard.in and, under the link Notice Board – 'careers', click 'GSL Careers'.
  • 3Click the 'Apply Now' button and select the post you wish to apply for.
  • 4Keep scanned copies of the listed documents ready before starting the form.
  • 5After completing personal information, fill the payment details and pay the Rs. 200 fee online through SBI e-pay, where applicable.
  • 6Wait for the server's intimation and do not press back or refresh, to avoid double charges.
  • 7Download and print the fee receipt. If no fee receipt is generated, the payment has failed; log in again and repeat the payment.
  • 8Take a print-out of the auto-generated application form, which must be produced at the subsequent selection process.
  • 9Passport size colour photograph with light background (preferably white), face and eyes unobstructed; file below 450 kb.
  • 10Signature on plain white paper with black ink, scanned from your own signature (not digitalized); file below 450 kb.
  • 11SSC certificate as proof of date of birth: not more than 500 kb.

  • 13Caste / Disability / Ex-Serviceman certificate: not more than 500 kb.
  • 14Experience certificates: not more than 1 Mb.
  • 15Online transaction receipt and Aadhar Card: each not more than 500 kb.
  • 16Reserved category candidates must submit the caste certificate in the prescribed proforma for "Appointment to posts under the Government of India". GSL Assistant Superintendent Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in GSL Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 4 vacancies: 2 for Assistant Superintendent (Personal Secretary), reserved UR, and 2 for Assistant Superintendent (Stores/Commercial), reserved ST. They include backlog reserved vacancies.
Q2What is the last date to apply for GSL Advt. No. 10/2026?
A
The online application closes at 17.00 hrs on 24 December 2026. It opens at 00.00 hrs on 25 November 2026.
Q3What is the qualification for the Personal Secretary post?
A
Graduate in any discipline with computer typing speed of 30 w.p.m., plus 5 years' relevant post-qualification (after graduation) experience in Secretarial functions.
Q4What is the qualification for the Stores/Commercial post?
A
Bachelors of Business Administration (BBA) OR a graduate in any discipline with a Diploma in Commercial / Supply Chain Management / Materials management / Logistics management or an equivalent qualification, with minimum 2 years' relevant experience.

Q7What is the application fee and who is exempted?

The fee is Rs. 200 (non-refundable), paid online through SBI e-pay. SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen candidates are exempted, and internal candidates pay no fee.
Q8How do I apply?

Q9What is the selection process?

Selection consists of a written test, Document Verification and a Skill/Trade test. Written test weightage is 75% and skill test 25%.
