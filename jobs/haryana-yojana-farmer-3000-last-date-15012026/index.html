Haryana पावर स्प्रे पंप सब्सिडी Yojana.. Farmer को मिलेगी स् Overview

Haryana sarkar ne Power Spray Pump machines ke liye 3000 rupaye ki subsidy launch ki hai. Kisan is scheme ka fayda utha sakte hain, bas last date 15 Jan 2026 tak apply karna hoga.

Haryana पावर स्प्रे पंप सब्सिडी Yojana.. Farmer को मिलेगी स् Expert Analysis

Yeh subsidy kisanon ko modern sprayer technology adopt karne mein madad karti hai, jis se pesticide use kam hota hai aur productivity badhti hai. Scheme simple hai, bas required documents ready rakhein.

Who Is Eligible: Haryana पावर स्प्रे पंप सब्सिडी Yojana.. Farmer को मिलेगी स्

Scheme un sabhi kisanon ke liye hai jo Haryana mein registered land holder hain aur spray pump kharidna chahte hain.

Haryana पावर स्प्रे पंप सब्सिडी Yojana.. Farmer को मिलेगी स् Application Process

1. Apna land record (Patta) aur Aadhar card ready rakhein. 2. Purchase invoice ya proforma of spray pump ki copy attach karein. 3. Online portal ya nearest Krishi Vibhag office se form fill karein.

Haryana पावर स्प्रे पंप सब्सिडी Yojana.. Farmer को मिलेगी स् Benefits Details

Beneficiary ko 3000 Rs ki direct cash subsidy milti hai, jo pump ki total cost ka 10-15% ho sakti hai. Is amount se kisan seed, maintenance ya extra equipment kharid sakta hai, isse overall farm cost kam hoti hai.

How Haryana पावर स्प्रे पंप सब्सिडी Yojana.. Farmer को मिलेगी स् Works

Scheme ka implementation Krishi Vibhag ke through hota hai. Kisan online portal ya offline form submit karta hai, verification ke baad subsidy bank account me direct transfer hoti hai. Disbursement usually 2-3 weeks me hoti hai.

Haryana पावर स्प्रे पंप सब्सिडी Yojana.. Farmer को मिलेगी स् Application Tips

Form fill karte waqt sabhi fields accurately bharain, especially Aadhar aur land details. Invoice ka original copy attach karein, warna application reject ho sakti hai. Time limit ka dhyan rakhein, late submission se benefit miss ho jayega.

Haryana पावर स्प्रे पंप सब्सिडी Yojana.. Farmer को मिलेगी स् FAQs

Q1Is scheme ke liye koi income limit hai?
A
Nahi, Haryana ki koi income ceiling nahi hai. Sabhi registered kisan eligible hain, chahe unka farm size chhota ho ya bada.
Q2Subsidy ka payment method kya hai?
A
Subsidy directly kisan ke bank account me Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) ke through transfer hoti hai, jo Aadhar se linked hota hai.
Q3Agar pump ki cost 20,000 Rs ho, to kitna paisa milta hai?
A
Aapko 3000 Rs ki fixed subsidy milti hai, baaki cost aapko khud bear karni hogi. Isse aapko pump ki total cost me 15% tak ki saving milti hai.
