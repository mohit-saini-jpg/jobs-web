About CMO Baghpat

The Office of the Chief Medical Officer, Baghpat is the district health office of Baghpat district in Uttar Pradesh. This recruitment follows letter no. 179/एस.पी.एम.यू./एन.एच.एम./डी0डब्ल्यू/2026-27/3452 dated 15.09.2026 of the Mission Director, National Health Mission, Uttar Pradesh. The 23 posts are those that remained vacant after a walk-in interview held earlier for Medical Officer (MBBS) under the various NHM programmes run in the district.

Department-wise Vacancies

The notice lists 23 vacancies across nine programmes, with the facility of posting and the category-wise reservation roster for each. The figures below are copied from the notice and add up to 23 (SC 12, UR 8, OBC 3). Selection will follow the reservation policy in force of the Uttar Pradesh Government. The number of posts may decrease or increase, and may change at any stage of the recruitment process; if it does, reservation will apply according to the Government Order and category table.

Pay and Terms

The notice gives the approved honorarium against each vacancy in rupees per month: The posts are purely on honorarium under contract terms, and no claim to permanency will be accepted. Appointment will be valid up to one financial year and will be subject to the guidelines issued by the Mission Director, National Health Mission, Uttar Pradesh. If any adverse fact is found, the District Health Society / National Health Mission, Uttar Pradesh will have the right to end the selected candidate's contract or service by giving one month's notice or an amount equal to one month's pay in lieu of notice.

CMO Baghpat Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Applications are accepted from the date of publication of the advertisement until 05:00 PM on 3 December 2026, only by registered post, and must be received at the Office of the Chief Medical Officer, Baghpat by then. No application will be considered or accepted after 05:00 PM on 3 December 2026.
  • 2At the top of the application, write the name of the post applied for and the name of the programme (for example SNCU, NUHM or CHC NCD Clinic).
  • 3Below it, give your name, husband's/father's name, date of birth, gender, mobile number and residential address.
  • 4Give all educational qualifications with year of passing/university, subject, maximum marks, marks obtained and percentage, along with your UP Medical Council registration, caste and experience details.
  • 5Sign at the bottom and paste a latest passport size photograph at the top right.
  • 6Enclose self-attested photocopies of the certificates listed under Documents Required, and a self-addressed envelope with a postage stamp for information about the interview.
  • 7Write the name of the post applied for and the name of the programme in big letters on the envelope.
  • 8Send the application by registered post to the Office of the Chief Medical Officer, Baghpat so that it reaches by 05:00 PM on 3 December 2026.
  • 9A separate application must be sent for each post if you apply for more than one.
  • 10Applicants will go through a Document Verification Process (DVP) and an interview. The dates of the DVP and the interview will be informed separately. Candidates must attend with a latest biodata, all required records, registration, two latest colour photographs and 01 set of self-attested copies of the required certificates. No travelling allowance of any kind will be paid for the interview.
