GADVASU Group C Selection Process

All three posts share a common first stage - a qualifying Punjabi Language Test - after which the process diverges by post. In every case, only candidates qualifying all prescribed stages are considered for selection; mere appearance in an examination or document verification does not confer any right to selection; and the University's decision regarding eligibility, conduct of examination, evaluation, and selection is final. Marks are not counted towards the final merit. Candidates qualifying Exam-I are called to appear for Exam-II. Consists of PART-A and PART-B; marks count towards the final merit. GADVASU Non Teaching Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is GADVASU Group C Recruitment 2026?
A
It is a recruitment process by Guru Angad Dev Veterinary and Animal Sciences University (GADVASU) for three Group C posts - Storekeeper, Clerk, and Steno-Typist - each with a distinct multi-stage selection procedure after a common qualifying Punjabi Language Test.
Q2What is the qualification required for GADVASU Group C posts?
A
Storekeeper requires a Bachelor's degree with Economics/B.Com/BBA; Clerk and Steno-Typist require any Bachelor's degree. All three additionally require a certified 120-hour computer course (or equivalent DOEACC "O" level certificate) and a pass in Matriculation-level Punjabi.
Q3What is the GADVASU Group C selection process?
A
All three posts start with a qualifying Punjabi Language Test, followed by a Main Examination. Storekeeper candidates then undergo document verification directly; Clerk candidates must additionally clear a qualifying Typing Proficiency Test; and Steno-Typist candidates must clear a Shorthand Proficiency Test whose marks count towards the final merit — see the Selection Process section above for full details.
Q4How many vacancies are announced for GADVASU Non-Teaching Recruitment 2026?
A
A total of 45 vacancies have been announced – 15 Steno Typist, 20 Clerk, and 10 Storekeeper.
Q5What is the selection process for GADVASU Non-Teaching posts?
A
The selection process includes a Punjabi Language Test (Qualifying), Main Examination, and Skill/Proficiency Test (Typing for Clerk, Shorthand for Steno Typist).
Q6Which posts are available under GADVASU Group C Recruitment 2026?
A
The available posts are Steno Typist, Clerk, and Storekeeper under Advertisement No. 03/2026.
Q7What is the last date to apply for GADVASU Group C Recruitment 2026?
A
The last date to submit the application is 8 October 2026 up to 4:00 PM.
Q8Is Punjabi language qualification required for GADVASU Group C Recruitment?
A
Yes, candidates must have passed Matriculation with Punjabi Language from a recognised board or possess an equivalent Punjabi qualification as specified by GADVASU.
