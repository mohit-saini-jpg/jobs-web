NIA Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Assistant Sub-Inspector: Graduation from a recognized university. Experience of at least 02 years in investigation of criminal cases, intelligence work or operations or information technology management. Head Constable: 12th class pass or equivalent from any recognized Board of University. Experience in intelligence work. Data Entry Operator: 'O' or 'A' level certificate for Information Technology from an Institute recognised by the Government. Holding analogous posts on regular basis in parent cadre or department.
