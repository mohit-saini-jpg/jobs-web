HPPSC Medical Officer Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Recognised Medical Qualification included in the First or Second Schedule, or Part-II of the Third Schedule (other than licentiate qualifications), to the Indian Medical Council Act, 1956. Holders of a qualification under Part-II of the Third Schedule must also fulfil the conditions in sub-section (3) of Section 13 of the Indian Medical Council Act, 1956. Completion of compulsory rotating internship. Eligibility in terms of qualification and experience is reckoned as on the closing date for submission of the Online Recruitment Application. The onus of proving that a candidate has acquired the requisite qualification by the stipulated date lies on the candidate; no extra opportunity is given to produce certificates at the time of the Personality Test.

HPPSC Medical Officer Recruitment 2026 Age Limit

Candidates must be between 18 and 45 years of age, reckoned as on 01.01.2026. Age relaxation: A 5-year relaxation in the upper age limit is admissible only to Bonafide SC of HP / ST of HP / OBC of HP / WFF of HP / Persons with Disabilities of Himachal Pradesh, and only where a post is reserved for that category. For HP Government employees and Ex-Servicemen of HP, age relaxation is as per Government instructions issued from time to time. Only the date of birth recorded in the Matriculation/Secondary Examination Certificate (or equivalent) as on the date of application submission is accepted — no subsequent request for change of date of birth will be considered or granted under any circumstances.

HPPSC Medical Officer Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection is a two-stage process: a Screening Test/Subject Aptitude Test, followed by a Personality Test. Duration: 1 hour. Objective type Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ), with negative marking. No candidate securing zero or negative marks in Paper-I forms part of the merit list. Both papers are conducted on the same day, in morning and evening sessions. Based on merit in Paper-I, answer sheets of Paper-II are evaluated for a limited number of candidates (20 times the vacancies advertised for up to 25 posts; 500 + 5 additional sheets per subsequent post for 26 and above). 0.25 marks (1/4th) deducted for each wrong answer. Multiple answers to a question are treated as wrong, with the same 0.25-mark penalty.
