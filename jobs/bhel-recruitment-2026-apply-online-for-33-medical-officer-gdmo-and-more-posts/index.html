BHEL Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Bharat Heavy Electricals Limited (BHEL), a Govt. of India Undertaking, has invited Indian nationals to apply for 33 posts (tentative) of General Duty Medical Officer (GDMO, E1 Grade, Rs 60000-3%-180000) and Sr. Medical Officer (Specialist, E2 Grade, Rs 70000-3%-200000) in its hospitals and dispensaries at Trichy, Bhopal, Haridwar, Hyderabad, Jhansi, Ranipet, Chennai and Nagpur. Applications under Advertisement No. 04/2026 are online only at careers.bhel.in, from 1 December 2026, 10:00 AM to 31 December 2026, 11:45 PM. Selection is by personal interview.

About BHEL

The notice describes BHEL as a Public Sector Undertaking incorporated in 1964, backed by 15 manufacturing units, 08 service centres, 15 Regional Marketing Centres, 04 Regional Offices and 02 Repair Units. It serves sectors such as power, transportation, defence, aerospace, oil and gas, and process industries. This recruitment is for its hospitals and dispensaries at the units and offices named above.

Qualification and Experience

Qualification and experience are counted as on 01.01.2027. Only Indian nationals may apply. The MBBS / PG degree should be recognized by Medical Council of India, and candidates should be registered with either Medical Council of India or a State Medical Council. Internship training is not considered as work experience or practice. For candidates in private medical practice, experience is considered on copies of the first and last prescription issued over the claimed period, with a self-certification confirming the period of practice. Candidates whose MBBS degree is from a foreign university or institute must upload a degree equivalence certificate or passing certificate from NBE to establish recognition in India. Pay and Terms

Selected candidates will normally be placed at the minimum of the pay scale and will be on probation for one year. Besides basic pay: a cafeteria of perks limited to 31% of basic pay, Industrial Dearness Allowance (currently 59.1% of basic pay) and Non-Practicing Allowance (20% of the basic pay). These are not considered pay for calculating other benefits. Leave, medical facilities for self and dependent family members, leave encashment, Provident Fund, gratuity, BHEL pension, group insurance, performance related payment, uniform, canteen facilities, and company accommodation or HRA are admissible as per Company Rules as applicable from time to time. Candidates should possess sound health. Before joining, they must undergo a medical examination by the Company's Authorized Medical Officer, and the appointment is subject to the Company's health standards, with no relaxation.

BHEL Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

All candidates fulfilling the eligibility conditions would be called for a Personal Interview. Candidates invited will be reimbursed to-and-fro Two-Tier AC train fare from the mailing address or the starting station, whichever is nearer to the place of interview, by the shortest route, on production of proof of journey. The interview date and venue are not specified in the notice. The notice says the candidature is provisional at all stages, and BHEL verifies eligibility against original documents again at the interview. Mere shortlisting does not entail the right to an interview, and BHEL reserves the right to invite suitable candidates depending on their credentials and declarations.

BHEL Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Keep certificates and documents ready, then fill every section of the online application form at https://careers.bhel.in. Fields marked (*) are mandatory.
  • 2Click SUBMIT. An APPLICATION NUMBER is generated.
  • 3Upload the scanned photograph, signature and other applicable documents. Retry until a GREEN TICK appears against each document.
  • 4Check the uploaded files with the "View File" button, then click "Submit Documents". This leads to the payment options.
  • 5Pay the applicable fee through SBI Collect, note the SBI Collect Reference Number (UTR Number), enter it in the application and upload the PDF of the e-Receipt.
  • 6The application is complete only on successful remittance of the fee. A printout of the online form and documents need not be sent to the BHEL office. If an applicant submits more than once, only the last eligible application is the final submission.

BHEL Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in BHEL Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 33 tentative vacancies: 19 Sr. Medical Officer (Specialist) E2 posts and 14 General Duty Medical Officer E1 posts. The notice says the number may vary on further assessment.
Q2What is the last date to apply for BHEL Medical Officer posts?
A
The last date is 31 December 2026, 11:45 PM. Online applications open on 1 December 2026 at 10:00 AM.
Q3What is the qualification for BHEL GDMO and Specialist posts?
A
GDMO needs an MBBS degree with one year of relevant post-qualification experience or practice. Sr. Medical Officer (Specialist) needs MBBS with a Post Graduate Degree in the specialty concerned and one year of such experience. Registration with Medical Council of India or a State Medical Council is required.
Q4What is the age limit for BHEL Recruitment 2026?
A
The upper age limit is 32 years for GDMO and 37 years for Sr. Medical Officer (Specialist), as on 01.01.2027. Relaxation is 5 years for SC/ST and 3 years for OBC (Non-creamy Layer), with more for PwD candidates.
Q5What is the salary of BHEL Medical Officers?
A
GDMO is in E1 Grade, Rs 60000-3%-180000, and Sr. Medical Officer is in E2 Grade, Rs 70000-3%-200000. Selected candidates are normally placed at the minimum of the scale, plus perks, Industrial Dearness Allowance and Non-Practicing Allowance.
Q6What is the application fee?
A
The non-refundable processing fee is Rs.400/- + 18% GST, i.e. Rs.472/-. SC/ST, PwD and Ex-Servicemen candidates are exempted.
Q7How can I apply for BHEL Recruitment 2026?
A
Apply online only at careers.bhel.in, upload the documents and pay the fee through SBI Collect. No printout needs to be sent to BHEL.
Q8How are candidates selected?
A
All candidates fulfilling the eligibility conditions would be called for a Personal Interview. Two-Tier AC train fare is reimbursed on proof of journey.
Q9Are these permanent posts?
A
The notice says selected candidates will be on probation for one year. It does not say whether the posts are permanent or on contract.
Q10Can candidates working in government or PSUs apply?
A
Yes, those employed in Govt. Departments, PSUs and Autonomous Bodies must apply through proper channel and produce a No Objection Certificate at the interview.
