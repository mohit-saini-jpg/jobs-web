MP Police Constable (GD) Eligibility Criteria

The applicant must be a citizen of India. As per government orders, an applicant with more than 2 living children, where one child was born on or after 26 January 2001, is not eligible for appointment (with an exception if one child is living and 2 or more children are born in a subsequent single delivery). MP Police Constable (GD) Salary

As per Madhya Pradesh General Administration Department circular dated 12 December 2019, selected candidates will draw a stipend of 70% of the minimum pay in the first year, 80% in the second year, and 90% in the third year, in addition to other allowances and perquisites sanctioned by the government from time to time. A Defined Contribution Pension Scheme (NPS) applies to employees appointed on or after 1 January 2005.

MP Police Constable (GD) Selection Process

Selection is conducted in two stages: Stage 1 – Written Online Examination (conducted by MPESB): An objective-type (MCQ) computer-based exam in Hindi/English, of 100 marks and 2 hours' duration, with 100 questions. One mark is awarded for each correct answer; there is no negative marking for wrong or unattempted questions. The paper covers: General Knowledge & Reasoning (40 marks), Intellectual Ability & Mental Aptitude (30 marks), and Science & Simple Arithmetic (30 marks), at a difficulty level equivalent to Class 8 of the MP Secondary Education Board. Stage 2 – Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Document Verification (conducted by the Police Department): Based on the written exam cut-off percentile for each vertical category, candidates numbering up to 7 times the advertised vacancies in that category are called for the PET. MP Police Constable Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

Aadhaar registration is mandatory for all candidates, and biometric verification (with Aadhaar unlocked) is compulsory both at the time of exam entry and during the exam. An original photo identity document (Voter ID, PAN card, Aadhaar card, Driving Licence, or Passport) must be carried to the exam centre; an e-Aadhaar printout is valid only if UIDAI-verified. Candidates will only be permitted entry to the exam centre up to the reporting time; late arrivals will not be allowed to enter. Electronic devices such as mobile phones, calculators, log tables, digital watches, sunglasses, and unfair-means material are strictly prohibited inside the exam hall. A black ball-point pen and the admit card downloaded from the MPESB website are mandatory items to carry to the exam hall; admit cards are not sent by post. No candidate is permitted to leave the exam room before the exam concludes, once it has started. Candidate certificates are not verified by MPESB at the application stage; eligibility for the computer-based online exam is entirely provisional and subject to verification by the concerned department during the recruitment process. All candidates must have live registration at a Madhya Pradesh Employment Office for these posts. There is no provision for recounting or re-evaluation of the exam result once declared. Selected candidates will be appointed on 3 years' probation and must undergo mandatory training with no provision for exemption; candidates unsuccessful in training get one further opportunity to clear it. Selected candidates will not be posted in their home district and must complete a minimum of 5 years' service in their allotted unit before becoming eligible for transfer. A waiting list of 15% of the advertised posts will be prepared, valid for one year or until the next exam result is declared, whichever is earlier. Character verification will be conducted as per the guidelines laid down by the Supreme Court in Mehar Singh vs. Commissioner of Police. Any candidate who conceals facts or gives false information in the character verification form will be declared ineligible for the service, even after appointment.
