GMC Mahabubnagar Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Multi-Disciplinary Research Unit (MRU) at Government Medical College, Mahabubnagar (Government of Telangana) is filling 5 posts on contract basis for one year: Research Scientist-II (Scientist-‘C’), Research Scientist-I (Scientist ‘B’), Lab Technician (2 posts) and Lab Assistant Cum DEO (Grade ‘A’). The pay is Rs. 67,000/- + HRA, Rs. 56,000/- + HRA, Rs. 20,000/- + HRA and Rs. 20,000/- (Consolidated) a month respectively. About GMC Mahabubnagar

Government Medical College, Mahabubnagar is a Government of Telangana medical college in Mahabubnagar. The posts are in its Multi-Disciplinary Research Unit (MRU) and work under the control of the Director, Govt. Medical College, Mahabubnagar. The notice is signed by the Nodal Officer, Multi-Disciplinary Research Unit, and the Director, Government Medical College, Mahabubnagar. The posts are restricted only to the MRU at Govt. Medical College, Mahabubnagar.

Pay and Tenure

HRA is paid on top of the monthly remuneration for Research Scientist-II, Research Scientist-I and Lab Technician. The Lab Assistant Cum DEO post carries a consolidated Rs. 20,000/-. The notice does not mention the HRA amount or rate. All posts are recruited on contract basis only for one year, which may be extended based on performance and sanction of funds. The posts will not be considered as permanent at any point of time. Pension/GPF/PF/Leave Benefits will not be applicable. The selected staff will be eligible for fifteen (15) days leave in a calendar year. The contract may be terminated at any point of time if the performance is not satisfactory, with 1 week notice.

Selection and Interview

Selection will be done as per the guidelines of the Department of Health Research (DHR), Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Govt. of India. After scrutinising the applications received, eligible candidates will be called for interview. List of eligible candidates: to be displayed on 17-11-2026. Interview of eligible candidates: 18-11-2026. The notice does not give the interview venue or time. Candidates must bring all original certificates at the time of interview. No TA/DA will be paid for attending interviews.
