ICDS Maharashtra Supervisor Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Integrated Child Development Service Scheme Commissionerate (ICDS Maharashtra), Navi Mumbai, has notified revised eligibility norms for the departmental promotion exam that fills the post of Mukhyasevika (Head Worker/Supervisor) from among serving Anganwadi Sevikas. This is a promotion-based recruitment restricted to existing Anganwadi Sevikas across Maharashtra's Konkan, Pune, Nashik, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Amravati and Nagpur divisions - it is not open to candidates outside the department. ICDS Maharashtra Mukhyasevika Eligibility Criteria 2026

Only currently serving Anganwadi Sevikas who meet all of the following conditions are eligible: Completed 10 years of continuous, unbroken regular service as an Anganwadi Sevika as on 1 January of the exam year, i.e., as on 01.01.2025. Hold a degree from a statutory university. Age not exceeding 50 years as on 01.01.2025 (i.e., born after 01/01/1975), as per the relaxation approved in the Government Decision dated 03.07.2026.

ICDS Maharashtra Mukhyasevika Qualification 2026

Candidates must hold a degree from a statutory university. After appointment as Mukhyasevika, the candidate must pass a computer proficiency certificate exam and the Marathi language exam within two years of joining, and the Hindi language exam within three years of joining. Annual increment and further promotion are withheld until these post-appointment qualifications are completed.

ICDS Maharashtra Mukhyasevika Age Limit 2026

As on 01.01.2025, the candidate's age should not exceed 50 years - i.e., the candidate should be born after 01/01/1975. This revised limit replaces the earlier age cap of 45 years that applied under the recruitment rule 7(1)(क) of the notification dated 04.06.2021, which was set aside by the Nagpur Bench of the High Court in its order dated 08.05.2026. Candidates are advised to check whether they satisfy the prescribed age limit before applying. Use our online Age Calculator to calculate your exact age instantly.

ICDS Maharashtra Mukhyasevika Selection Process 2026

Selection is through a departmental promotion exam for eligible Anganwadi Sevikas within the promotion quota. The detailed exam pattern, syllabus, and further selection stages are not specified in the provided notification; candidates should refer to the Commissionerate's earlier general instruction letter (Reference Letter No. 7) for procedural details.

ICDS Maharashtra Supervisor Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions
