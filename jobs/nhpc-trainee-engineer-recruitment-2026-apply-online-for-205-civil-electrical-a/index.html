NHPC Trainee Engineer Salary 2026

The NHPC Trainee Engineer Salary 2026 is based on E2 Grade with a pay scale of Rs.50,000–1,60,000 under the IDA pattern. Selected candidates will receive this pay scale during the one-year training period. On successful completion of training, they will be absorbed as Engineers in the same pay scale, with a probation period of one year. The approximate annual emoluments for a new employee are Rs.16–17 lakh. In addition to basic pay, selected employees will be eligible for Dearness Allowance, House Rent Allowance, Cafeteria Allowance, Performance Related Pay, medical facilities and social security benefits such as Provident Fund, pension and gratuity according to the applicable company rules. Note: The approximate annual emoluments of Rs.16–17 lakh are not the same as the monthly in-hand salary. Actual take-home pay will depend on applicable allowances and deductions.

NHPC Trainee Engineer Selection Process 2026

The NHPC Trainee Engineer Selection Process 2026 consists of shortlisting through GATE 2025 or GATE 2026 scores, followed by Group Discussion and Personal Interview. Candidates with a valid score in the corresponding GATE paper will be considered for shortlisting based on the normalised marks out of 100. The final merit will be prepared using the prescribed weightage for GATE, Group Discussion and Personal Interview. NHPC will shortlist candidates based on the best valid normalised marks obtained in GATE 2025 or GATE 2026. Ordinarily, candidates up to five times the number of advertised vacancies in each category and discipline may be called for the next selection stages. If candidates have equal marks at the shortlisting boundary, all such candidates may be called according to the notification. Shortlisted candidates will be invited for Group Discussion. NHPC Trainee Engineer Selection Weightage 2026

NHPC has prescribed a combined selection weightage of 75% for GATE 2025 or GATE 2026 normalised marks, 5% for Group Discussion and 20% for Personal Interview. The final score will be calculated out of 100 using these components. Candidates must obtain the prescribed minimum qualifying percentage in the combined score of Group Discussion and Personal Interview. There is no separate minimum qualifying mark for Group Discussion alone. Candidates who do not qualify in the combined Group Discussion and Personal Interview score will not be considered for final empanelment, regardless of their GATE marks.

NHPC Trainee Engineer Training and Job Location 2026

Selected Trainee Engineers will undergo one year of training in E2 Grade with a pay scale of Rs.50,000–1,60,000. After successful completion of training, they will be absorbed as Engineers in the same pay scale and placed on probation for one year. Selected candidates may be posted at NHPC or NHDC projects, power stations, offices, joint ventures or subsidiary companies in different parts of India or abroad, according to organisational requirements.
