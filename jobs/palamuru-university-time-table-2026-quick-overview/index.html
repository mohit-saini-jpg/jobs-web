How to Download the Palamuru University Time Table

  • Open the official Notifications page of Palamuru University.
  • Find the December 2026 timetable entries for Pharm D or B.Pharmacy.
  • Click the PDF link for your course.
  • Check your semester or year, subject, date and time.
  • Save or print the PDF for reference.

Important Instructions for Candidates

The B.Pharmacy timetables carry the following instructions from the Controller of Examinations:

  • Candidates whose forms are rejected by the Examination Branch will not be examined. If a candidate is found not eligible at a later stage, the registration for this examination will be cancelled.
  • Candidates must write the examination only at their allotted centres. Other-centre candidates are not to be allowed under any circumstances.
  • Cell phones and programmable calculators are strictly not allowed in the examination hall.
  • Candidates are responsible for collecting the correct question paper as per their hall ticket from the invigilator. Answering a wrong question paper may lead to cancellation of results.
  • Any discrepancy in the timetable should be brought to the notice of the Controller of Examinations within 3 days.

B.P.Ed exam me 100 marks, MCQ + practical; B.Ed me 150 marks, theory + internal; LL.B me 200 marks, objective + essay. Har section ka negative marking 0.25 hai, isliye guess karte waqt cautious rahen.

Palamuru University (Telangana Posts): Exam Center Guidelines

Exam centre me entry ke liye photo ID (Aadhaar/Passport) aur admit card mandatory hai; mobile, smart watch, notes, aur food items allowed nahi hain. Center ke andar photography ya recording bilkul prohibited hai.

Palamuru University (Telangana Posts) Last-Minute Strategy
