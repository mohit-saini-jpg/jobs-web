Gujarat Police Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

The Gujarat Police eligibility requirements differ according to the recruitment cadre. Lokrakshak posts require a Class 12 qualification, PSI Cadre posts require graduation, and Technical Cadre positions require Class 12 together with the prescribed Motor Mechanic Vehicle Trade and apprenticeship qualifications. Candidates applying for Head Constable Driver Mechanic or Motor Transport Supervisor posts must have passed Class 12 or an equivalent examination. In addition, they must possess either of the following prescribed technical qualifications: Motor Mechanic Vehicle Trade Certificate with one year of apprenticeship from the prescribed Government-recognized industrial training institution. Gujarat Police Recruitment 2026 Salary

Selected Gujarat Police candidates will initially receive fixed monthly remuneration under the applicable contractual appointment conditions. The Lokrakshak and Technical cadres carry a monthly fixed salary of Rs. 26,000, while the PSI Cadre carries Rs. 49,600 per month. The notifications provide for initial fixed-pay appointments under the applicable Government orders, generally for a five-year contractual period. Regular appointment in the applicable pay scale is subject to satisfactory completion of training, service and other prescribed conditions. The advertised amounts represent initial consolidated monthly pay rather than guaranteed in-hand salary under a regular pay scale.

Gujarat Police Physical Efficiency Test 2026 – Running Time

The Physical Efficiency Test (PET) for Lokrakshak and PSI cadres is qualifying in nature. Candidates must complete the prescribed running distance within the permitted time to qualify for the subsequent Physical Standards Test. Candidates qualifying in the running test will undergo the prescribed physical measurement examination. Only candidates who qualify in both the Physical Efficiency Test and Physical Standards Test will progress to the written examination for the relevant cadre. The same running test should not automatically be assumed for Technical Cadre recruitment, which follows a different examination sequence.

Gujarat Police Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

GPRB has prescribed different selection procedures for the Lokrakshak, PSI and Technical cadres. The Lokrakshak and PSI cadres begin with qualifying physical tests, while Technical Cadre recruitment begins with a written competitive examination. Final appointment is subject to the applicable merit, reservation, document verification, medical fitness and service-related requirements.

Gujarat Police PSI Exam Pattern 2026

The Gujarat Police PSI Main Examination carries 300 marks and consists of two papers. Paper 1 is an objective General Studies examination, while Paper 2 assesses Gujarati and English language skills through descriptive questions. Candidates must obtain at least 40% marks separately in Part A and Part B of Paper 1 and at least 40% marks in the descriptive Paper 2. Evaluation of the descriptive paper is subject to the qualifying requirements of Paper 1.
