AAI Apprentice Eligibility 2026-27

Only Indian Nationals who are domiciles of the following states/UTs may apply: Chandigarh, Delhi, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu & Kashmir, Leh Ladakh, Madhya Pradesh, Punjab, Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, and Uttarakhand. Candidates must have passed their degree/diploma/ITI in 2024 or after 2024 . Candidates who have already completed apprenticeship, terminated it midway, or are currently pursuing apprenticeship elsewhere (or in AAI at the same qualification level) are not eligible . Candidates with 1 year or more of apprenticeship/job experience after attaining the essential qualification are not eligible , as per the Apprentices Act, 1961. Important: A candidate applying for more than one discipline will not be considered, and such applications will be rejected.

Important: The allocated Airport/Station/Unit is final; no change in place of training will be entertained at a later stage. Selected candidates will preferably be posted at locations based on their registration location on the portal.

AAI Apprentice Selection Process

Selection is merit-based , with no written examination. The process comprises: Provisional selection based on the percentage of marks obtained in the qualifying examination (CGPA converted to percentage per the concerned Board/University norms). In case of a tie, date of birth and thereafter date of passing determine seniority. Document Verification at the establishment office, intimated only through the candidate's registered email ID. Interview / Certificate Verification , with candidature remaining provisional throughout. Medical Fitness Certificate (from a Government Medical Officer or Medical Officer of a Government Undertaking only), to be submitted at the time of joining. Final selection and issuance of the apprentice engagement letter follow successful document verification and medical fitness clearance. Note: Merely being shortlisted for document verification or the medical exam

Important Conditions

No Application Fee is charged for this recruitment. No TA/DA will be provided for attending interviews or document verification. Candidates must sign and accept the Apprenticeship Contract on the NATS/NAPS portal immediately after joining AAI. The Apprenticeship Contract automatically ceases after the stipulated 12-month period; any act of indiscipline during training can lead to cancellation of the contract. During apprenticeship, candidates are governed by the Apprentices Act, 1961 (as amended) and AAI's applicable policies, rules, conduct, and discipline requirements. It is the candidate's sole responsibility to get their profile verified from the respective Regional Board. AAI does not take responsibility for replying to candidates regarding non-selection or non-issuance of a call letter. Furnishing false or incorrect information at any stage renders the candidature/engagement liable

AAI NR Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Visit www.nats.education.gov.in and click the Student button, then select General Student (for Diploma/Graduate pass-outs).
  • 2Click Student Registration and keep ready: a valid personal email, mobile number, Aadhaar number, qualifying/provisional certificate (PDF, under 1 MB), and APAAR ID.
  • 3Verify your mobile number and email via OTP, then complete Aadhaar e-KYC verification.
  • 4Fill the registration form (basic details auto-populate from UIDAI); add educational details and set a password, then submit.
  • 5Verify your account via the email link sent to you, then log in and complete your profile: Basic Details, Educational Details (upload/fetch certificate via DigiLocker/APAAR), Communication Details, and Training Preferences.
  • 6Preview your profile and submit it finally to receive your Student Enrolment ID.
  • 7Search for establishment Airports Authority of India – RHQ NR, New Delhi (NDLSWC000002) and click Apply for the apprenticeship opportunity of your choice.
  • 8Visit www.apprenticeshipindia.org and click Register, then select Candidate.
  • 9Fill the Basic, Family, and Contact details in the registration form and submit.
  • 10Activate your account via the link sent to your registered email, then log in with your Registration Number/Email and password.
  • 11Click Complete Your Profile and add your "About Me" and contact details — this is mandatory before applying to any opportunity.
  • 12Search for opportunities using establishment code E05200700101 (Airports Authority of India – RHQ NR) and click Apply.
  • 13Track invitations, applications, and contracts from your candidate dashboard; if offered a contract, review the details and click Sign to accept.
