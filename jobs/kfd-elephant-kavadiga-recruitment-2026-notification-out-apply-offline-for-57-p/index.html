KFD Elephant Kavadiga Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Karnataka Forest Department (KFD) has released separate notifications for KFD Elephant Kavadiga Recruitment 2026, inviting applications for Group 'D' Elephant Kavadiga (Ane Kavadiga) posts across five forest circles: Kanara (Shirsi), Kodagu (Madikeri), Mysuru, Chamarajanagar, and Shivamoga. All five notifications share the same application window and pay scale, and are being treated here as one combined recruitment drive. KFD Elephant Kavadiga Recruitment 2026 Notification Out

The Karnataka Forest Department has issued five separate notifications - one for each circle - for direct recruitment to Group 'D' Elephant Kavadiga posts for the year 2026-27, under the Karnataka Forest Department Services (Recruitment) (Amendment) Rules, 2009. All five notifications are dated 27 July 2026 and follow common eligibility, age, and selection rules, with the same application period. KFD Elephant Kavadiga Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must hold Indian citizenship (or fall under specific categories of persons of Indian origin who migrated with the intention of permanently settling in India, as defined in the notification). Candidates must be residing within a notified forest area under the jurisdiction of the Karnataka Forest Department, within Karnataka state. Candidates must be able to speak and understand Kannada. Experience in elephant management/handling is mandatory for this post. The notifications do not specify a separate minimum educational qualification; the application form itself only asks candidates to declare their number of years of experience in handling elephants - it does not require submission of educational certificates. KFD Elephant Kavadiga Age Limit

Candidates must have completed a minimum of 18 years of age as on the last date for submission of applications. Age is reckoned as on the last date of application, i.e., 03 September 2026. A one-time additional relaxation of 5 years in the maximum age limit has been extended to candidates of all categories participating in backlog recruitment notified up to 31 December 2027, per Government Order No. CiaSau 262 Senani 2025, dated 29 January 2026. Ex-servicemen are entitled to age relaxation equal to the number of years of armed forces service actually rendered, plus 3 years, subject to their overall eligibility for the post under their category.

KFD Elephant Kavadiga Reservation (Vertical & Horizontal)

Vacancies in each circle are reserved category-wise (Vertical Reservation: General/SC/ST/Category-I/Category-IIA/Category-IIB/Category-IIIA/Category-IIIB) as well as reservation-type-wise (Horizontal Reservation: Ex-servicemen, Women, Persons with Disability, Project-Displaced persons, Kannada-medium candidates, Rural candidates, and Third Gender candidates). Persons with Disability reservation has not been extended for this post specifically, since the Elephant Kavadiga role is not identified as suitable for PwD candidates as per Government Notification No. DPAR/52/SRR/99, dated 08.12.2002; the PwD reservation point is instead adjusted into the General (Others) category under horizontal reservation.

KFD Elephant Kavadiga Application Fee

There is no application fee for this recruitment. The application form itself states that no fee is payable and that no supporting documents need to be enclosed at the time of initial application submission.

KFD Elephant Kavadiga Selection Process

Selection is made purely through an interview and a performance assessment - there is no written examination for this post. Candidates are assessed on their ability to speak and comprehend Kannada, and on their practical experience and performance capability in elephant handling. The interview and performance assessment together carry a maximum of 100 marks. Based on the marks obtained and the category-wise vacancy position, a provisional select list is prepared and published on the official website, followed by a final select list after considering any objections. Candidates not present on the specified date and time for the interview/performance test will not be permitted to appear later, and the scheduled date will not be changed for any candidate.
