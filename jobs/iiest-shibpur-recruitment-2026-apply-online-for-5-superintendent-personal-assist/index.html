IIEST Shibpur Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Graduates up to 30 years of age can apply for 3 Superintendent and 2 Personal Assistant posts (Group B, Pay Level 06) at the Indian Institute of Engineering Science and Technology (IIEST), Shibpur, Howrah, an Institute of National Importance under the Ministry of Education, Government of India. Applications are made online on the SAMARTH portal from 9 November 2026 to 9 December 2026 (11:55 pm), followed by a Google Form submission by 14 December 2026 (11:55 pm).

IIEST Shibpur Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection is through Stage-I (Written Test) and Stage-II (Skill Test). The final merit list is prepared solely on the performance in the Written Test, subject to qualifying in the Skill Test and the recommendation of the Selection Committee. Only the marks obtained in the Written Test are considered for preparing the merit list. If two or more candidates get the same marks, the tie is broken in this order: the candidate with more positive (correct) marks in the Written Test; then the candidate with higher age by date of birth; then the candidate with a higher percentage of marks in the examination for the minimum academic qualification of the post. Superintendent: knowledge in Computer applications viz., Word Processing and Spreadsheet. Personal Assistant: a minimum speed of 100 w.p.m. in stenography.
