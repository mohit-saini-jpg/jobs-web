Shatipurti Portal is a government initiative to provide compensation to farmers whose crops are damaged due to various reasons. The portal aims to make the process of filing claims easier and faster. Eligible farmers can now submit their claims online and receive timely compensation.

ई-क्षतिपूर्ति पोर्टल Crop का खराबा का Form भरवाए Shatipurti Expert Analysis

This scheme is a significant step towards supporting farmers who are vulnerable to crop damage. The online portal ensures transparency and efficiency in the process, reducing the burden on farmers. It's a welcome move by the government to provide relief to affected farmers.

Who Is Eligible — ई-क्षतिपूर्ति पोर्टल Crop का खराबा का Form भरवाए Shatipurti

Farmers who have suffered crop damage due to natural calamities, pests, or diseases can apply for compensation through the Shatipurti Portal.

To apply successfully, farmers need to register on the portal, upload required documents, and submit their claims within the specified timeframe. Ensure you have all necessary documents, including crop damage reports and identification proof.

The compensation amount varies depending on the extent of crop damage. Farmers can receive up to ₹50,000 per hectare for total crop loss. This financial support helps farmers recover from losses and invest in their next crop cycle.

The Shatipurti Portal is implemented by the state government in collaboration with the agriculture department. The process involves farmers registering on the portal, submitting claims, and receiving compensation. The government ensures timely disbursement of funds to eligible farmers.
