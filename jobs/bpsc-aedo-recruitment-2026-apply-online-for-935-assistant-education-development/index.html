BPSC AEDO Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

The Bihar Public Service Commission has prescribed a two-stage written examination for Assistant Education Development Officer recruitment. Online application and eligibility scrutiny. Preliminary Competitive Examination. Shortlisting for the Main Examination. Main Written Examination. Preparation of the merit list based on Main Examination marks. Original document verification. Final recommendation for appointment. Important: The notification does not prescribe a personal interview for the AEDO recruitment. The Preliminary Examination is used for screening, while final merit is based on the Main Examination under the prescribed rules.

BPSC AEDO Preliminary Exam Pattern 2026

The Preliminary Examination will consist of one General Studies paper with objective-type multiple-choice questions. The General Studies paper will assess candidates' knowledge of the following areas: General Science. Events of national and international importance. History of India and important features of the history of Bihar. Geography of India and Bihar. Major rivers and geographical features of Bihar. Indian polity and governance. Indian economy and economic developments. Changes in Bihar's economy after Independence. Indian National Movement and Bihar's contribution. General mental ability. The official notification specifies one-third negative marking for incorrect answers. Each question has five options, including the prescribed option for leaving the question unanswered. Candidates who do not mark any of the five options may also face the notified one-third deduction. Important

BPSC AEDO Main Exam Pattern 2026

Candidates shortlisted from the Preliminary Examination will be eligible for the Main Written Examination. The Main Examination contains three objective-type papers: General Language, General Studies and General Aptitude. Qualifying Requirement: Candidates must obtain at least 30% marks in both English and Hindi separately. The General Language paper is qualifying in nature. Its marks are not included in the final merit calculation. The Main Examination General Studies paper covers General Science, national and international events, Indian and Bihar history, geography, polity, economy, Indian National Movement and general mental ability. The General Aptitude paper covers the following areas: Number systems and numerical reasoning. Decimal fractions and simplification. Relationships between numbers. Basic arithmetic operations.
