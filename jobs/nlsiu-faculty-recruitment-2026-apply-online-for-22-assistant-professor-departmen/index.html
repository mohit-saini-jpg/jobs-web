About NLSIU

As per the notifications, the National Law School of India University (NLSIU) was established in 1987 to be a pioneer in legal education. Over the last three decades, the University has evolved as an innovative leader in law, social sciences, humanities, and public policy education and research in India. It has also been ranked first among the law universities in the National Institute Ranking Framework (NIRF) for the last eight years. The University invites applications from exceptionally well-qualified individuals dedicated to academic teaching, scholarship, and institutional service.

NLSIU Assistant Professor Eligibility Criteria

Eligibility is defined through Appendix A of each notification, titled “Minimum Qualifications for Recruitment to Regular Posts of Teachers in the University (per UGC Regulations, 2018)”. All six notifications require the Essential Qualifications in Section I. The two permanent-post notifications (Nos. 20/2026 and 21/2026) additionally refer to Section II.1. The notifications also state that the prescribed essential qualifications/experience are the bare minimum, and that merely possessing them will not entitle a candidate to be called for an interview. The qualification should have been obtained from recognized Universities/Institutions.

NLSIU Assistant Professor Qualification

A Master’s Degree with at least 55% marks (or an equivalent grade in a point-scale, wherever the grading system is followed). A relaxation of 5% may be provided at the graduate and master’s level for the Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe and Differently abled categories for the purpose of eligibility and for assessing good academic record during direct recruitment to teaching positions. A relaxation of 5% may be provided to the Ph.D Degree holders who obtained their Master’s Degree prior to 19 September 1991. The eligibility marks of 55% marks or an equivalent grade in a point scale prescribed by these regulations and the relaxation of 5% to the categories mentioned above are permissible, based on only the qualifying marks without including any grace mark procedures. NLSIU Assistant Professor Selection Process

The notifications do not describe the selection stages in detail. They refer to an interview and state the following: Meeting the minimum qualification/experience does not entitle a candidate to be called for an interview. If a large number of applications is received, NLSIU reserves the right to restrict the number of candidates called for interview to a reasonable limit. Shortlisted candidates will be intimated through email. Candidates serving in Government/Public Sector Undertakings (including Boards/Autonomous Bodies) must submit a No Objection Certificate from their current employer at the time of interview, if not submitted earlier. No correspondence will be entertained regarding the conduct/result of the interview or the reasons for not being called for an interview. The notifications do not mention a written test or any other stage.
