About MHI Consultant Grade-II

The Ministry of Heavy Industries is a ministry of the Government of India. Its HEI Division at GPOA - 3, New Delhi, issued this notice to engage one Consultant Grade-II for specific and time-bound tasks of the Ministry. The work centres on the Electrical Equipment Quality Control Order (QCO) for Low Voltage Switchgears and Controlgears, electrical equipment standardization and trade-related technical matters. The general terms of the engagement are those in the MHI guidelines No.A-45011/9/2019-Admn., dated 27.08.2024, which are attached to the notice.

MHI Consultant Grade-II Job Description

Oversee and manage all activities related to the implementation of the Electrical Equipment Quality Control Order (QCO) for Low Voltage Switchgears and Controlgears. Coordinate with industry stakeholders, BIS, testing labs, departments/ministries and other agencies to ensure effective implementation of regulations and policies. Participate, examine and prepare inputs/proposals for BIS Sectoral Committees for electrical equipment standardization. Prepare comprehensive procedures for clarifying QCO product classifications with customs. Examine and prepare proposals or suggest initiatives to address and mitigate the impacts of supply chain constraints. Develop concept notes for initiatives aimed at enhancing the electrical equipment sector. Ministry of Heavy Industries Consultant Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Short-listed candidates who meet the qualification/experience criteria for the post will be called for interview through online or offline mode. Under the attached MHI guidelines, a Screening Committee of the concerned Division (Director/Deputy Secretary as Chairman, with the Under Secretary and SO as members) shortlists eligible applicants and recommends a panel. The shortlisted applications go before a Selection Committee chaired by an Additional Secretary/JS or equivalent officer, which may include outside experts and may devise its own method of selection. The Selection Committee recommends the selected candidate along with a list of reserve candidates valid for one year from the date of finalization of the panel.

Ministry of Heavy Industries Consultant Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

The initial tenure is 02 (Two) years, subject to extension up to 01 (One) more year on the basis of performance evaluated every year. Under the guidelines, no extension will be given beyond the age of 65 years. Prior to appointment, the selected person must submit a statement of Good Health from a recognized physician; no medical benefits from the Ministry are available during the tenure. Police verification is mandatory; if it is negative, the contract ceases immediately without notice. Normal working hours are 09:00 AM to 05:30 PM; the Consultant may have to sit late or attend office on holidays/weekends for time-bound work. Leave is one day for each completed month, with no accumulation beyond a calendar year. The Consultant shall not take up any other assignment during the contract. No residential telephone/mobile bill reimbursement, no use of Staff Car for private purposes or residence-office journeys, and no residential accommodation from the Central Government Pool. Income Tax is deducted at source; GST, as applicable, shall be admissible. MHI can terminate the contract at any time; in the normal course it gives one month's notice, and the Consultant can also seek termination with one month's notice.
