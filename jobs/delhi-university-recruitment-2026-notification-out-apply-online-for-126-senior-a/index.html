Delhi University Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Both posts follow a two-stage recruitment framework consisting of preliminary shortlisting and final assessment. Stage 1: Preliminary MCQ Examination for shortlisting candidates. Stage 2: Descriptive Main Examination followed by a qualifying Skill Test. Verification of educational qualifications, experience, reservation claims and other supporting documents. Final merit preparation according to Main Examination performance, subject to qualifying the Skill Test and prescribed rules. Merit Clarification: The Preliminary Examination is used only for shortlisting and does not carry weightage in the final assessment. The Skill Test is qualifying in nature.

Delhi University Recruitment 2026 Examination Rules

The following rules apply to the preliminary examination and final selection process. The Preliminary Examination is conducted in English and Hindi. Candidates may answer in either language but must use the same language throughout. Each preliminary question carries two marks, with a deduction of 0.5 mark for every incorrect answer. The preliminary qualifying cut-off is calculated using 50% of the average score of the top 5% of candidates, subject to the alternative calculation specified where fewer than two candidates qualify for that group. The notification allows 5% relaxation in the qualifying cut-off for SC/ST and 10% for PwBD candidates. Shortlisting for the Main Examination follows the prescribed vacancy-based ratio, with tied candidates at the cut-off also shortlisted. Final merit is based on the Main Examination, subject to qualifying the Skill Test and eligibility verification.
