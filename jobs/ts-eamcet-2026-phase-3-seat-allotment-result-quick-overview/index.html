The Telangana State Council of Higher Education (TGCHE) has released the TS EAMCET 2026 Phase 3 (final phase / Round 3) seat allotment result on the official counselling portal, tgeapcet.nic.in. This is the last regular round of TG EAPCET counselling for the year, and candidates who registered and submitted web options in Phase 3 can now log in to check whether they have been allotted a seat.

Who Should Check the TS EAMCET 2026 Phase 3 Result

The Phase 3 allotment result applies to candidates who:

  • Completed registration and certificate verification for TS EAMCET 2026 counselling
  • Exercised fresh web options during the Phase 3 window (options from Phase 1 and Phase 2 are not carried forward automatically)
  • Had not secured a seat in Phase 1 or Phase 2, or chose to try for a better seat in Phase 3

How to Check and Download the TS EAMCET 2026 Phase 3 Allotment Order

Follow these steps to check your result and download the provisional allotment order:

  • Go to the official website: tgeapcet.nic.in
  • Click on the "Candidate Login" link on the homepage
  • Enter your login credentials - TG EAPCET Hall Ticket Number, Password, and Date of Birth
  • Submit the login form to view your Phase 3 seat allotment status
  • If a seat has been allotted, the system will display details of the allotted institute, branch, and the fee to be paid
  • Download and save the Provisional Allotment Order
  • Take a printout - you will need it for fee payment, self-reporting, and physical reporting at the college

Basis of TS EAMCET 2026 Seat Allotment

The Phase 3 allotment is prepared based on the following factors:

  • Candidate's TS EAMCET 2026 rank
  • Reservation category
  • Local area status
  • Web options filled during the Phase 3 counselling window
  • Availability of seats remaining after Phase 1 and Phase 2

Important Instructions

  • Use only your registered login credentials (Hall Ticket Number, Password, Date of Birth) to access the counselling portal - do not share these with third parties.
  • Keep a printed and digital copy of your Provisional Allotment Order for all future reference.
  • Regularly check tgeapcet.nic.in for updates, since exact reporting dates and sliding/spot round eligibility criteria are announced directly on the portal.
  • Missing the tuition fee payment or self-reporting deadline may result in cancellation of the allotted seat.
