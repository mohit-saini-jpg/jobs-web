IIT (BHU) Junior Research Fellow Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

IIT BHU Recruitment 2026 has one Junior Research Fellow (JRF) post in the Department of Mathematical Sciences, IIT (BHU) Varanasi. The post is in an ANRF-IRG project, and ANRF is the funding agency. The duration is 2.3 years, and the post is purely temporary and co-terminus with the project. The pay is Rs. 37,000 per month + HRA for the first two years and Rs. 42,000 per month + HRA for the third year. You need post-graduation in Mathematics with 55% or above marks (equivalent grade) and NET/GATE or equivalent. Applications go by email to the Principal Investigator within 21 days of the advert

About IIT BHU

IIT (BHU) Varanasi is an Indian Institute of Technology in Varanasi (PIN 221005). This post is in its Department of Mathematical Sciences. It serves a research project sanctioned for 36 months under the ANRF-IRG scheme. The Principal Investigator is Prof. Rajeev.

Who Can Apply

NET/GATE is part of the minimum essential qualification. The application form asks whether you have qualified in UGC/CSIR/NET/GATE (or equivalent) and asks for the details if you have. You can also list research or work experience on the form, but experience is not part of the minimum qualification. The notice gives the age limit for the Junior Research Fellow post as 28 years. Age relaxation will be as per GoI norms. For the full eligibility conditions of the post, see the official notification.
PostMinimum Essential Qualifications
Junior Research FellowPost-graduation in Mathematics with 55% or above marks (equivalent grade) and NET/GATE or equivalent as per specifications of the funding agency (ANRF).

Pay and Tenure

HRA is paid on top of the monthly emoluments in both periods. The project is sanctioned for a total duration of 36 months. The duration of the post is 2.3 years. The first appointment is for one year and can be continued for the next year depending on performance. The post is purely temporary and co-terminus with the project.

First two yearsRs. 37,000 per month + HRA
Third yearRs. 42,000 per month + HRA

Selection and Interview

The interview is held at the Department of Mathematical Sciences, IIT (BHU), Varanasi- 221005. Shortlisted candidates must bring: The application form All documents in original A valid ID proof recognised by the Government of India One set of self-attested copies of proof of date of birth, mark sheets, certificates, NET Certificate, GATE Certificate, experience certificate(s) and other documents No TA/DA will be paid to candidates called for the interview. The decision of the Selection Committee will be final.
