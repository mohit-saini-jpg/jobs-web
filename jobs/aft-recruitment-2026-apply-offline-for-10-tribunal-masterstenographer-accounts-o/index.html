AFT Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Armed Forces Tribunal, Principal Bench, New Delhi, is filling 10 posts on deputation basis for a period of three years. About AFT Recruitment 2026

The Armed Forces Tribunal, Principal Bench, functions from West Block-8, Sector-1, R.K. Puram, New Delhi (Tel: 011-26105366, Fax: 011-26105361, as printed on the letterhead). This circular was issued by Sunil Kumar Sharma, Deputy Registrar, and was circulated to the Supreme Court of India, all High Courts, the Central Administrative Tribunal, the Controller General of Defence Accounts, the Controller General of Accounts, the Comptroller and Auditor General of India, Delhi universities, the Rajya Sabha and Lok Sabha Secretariats and other offices so that eligible officers can apply.

AFT Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in AFT Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 10 posts: Financial Adviser and Chief Accounts Officer (1), Deputy Controller of Accounts (1), Private Secretary (1), Tribunal Master/Stenographer Grade-I (4), Accounts Officer (2) and Junior Accounts Officer (1).
Q2What is the last date for AFT Recruitment 2026?
A
The application may be forwarded by the Department to the Principal Registrar, Armed Forces Tribunal, Principal Bench, New Delhi by 28 December 2026.
Q3Who can apply for the AFT deputation posts?
A
Serving officers only: officers of the organised Accounts Cadre of the Central Government for the accounts posts, and Stenographers of Central/State Government, courts or statutory/autonomous bodies with pensionary benefits for Private Secretary and Tribunal Master/Stenographer Grade-I, holding analogous posts or the prescribed regular service.
Q4What is the age limit for AFT Recruitment 2026?
A
The maximum age limit for appointment by deputation shall not exceed 56 years, with a residual service of four years on the closing date of receipt of applications.
Q5What is the salary in AFT Recruitment 2026?
A
Pay ranges from Pay Matrix Level-6 (Rs 35400-112400) for Tribunal Master/Stenographer Grade-I and Junior Accounts Officer to Level-13 (Rs 123100-215900) for Financial Adviser and Chief Accounts Officer, governed by DoP&T O.M. No. 6/8/2009-Estt (Pay-II) dated 17.06.2010.
Q6How to apply for AFT Recruitment 2026?
A
Fill the Annexure-I pro forma, attach attested ACR photocopies for the last five years and the Vigilance Clearance Certificate, and have the Department forward it to the Principal Registrar, Armed Forces Tribunal, Principal Bench, West Block-VIII, Sector-I, R.K. Puram, New Delhi - 110 066.
Q7For how long is the AFT deputation?
A
The posts are on deputation basis for a period of three years. Total deputation including an immediately preceding ex-cadre deputation shall not ordinarily exceed five years for Financial Adviser and Chief Accounts Officer and three years for the other posts.
Q8Do I need to apply again if I applied against the 2025 circular?
A
No, if your application against Vacancy Circular dated 14.11.2025 was received through the Cadre Controlling Authority and was complete in all respects, you need not apply again.
Q9Can feeder-grade officers of AFT apply on deputation?
A
No. Departmental officers in the feeder grade who are in direct line of promotion shall not be eligible for consideration for appointment on deputation.
Q10Will government accommodation be provided to selected officers?
A
The circular states that the office is not yet enlisted in the Directorate of Estate for allotment of General Pool Residential Accommodation.
