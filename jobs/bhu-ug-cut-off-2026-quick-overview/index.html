How the CUET Cut Off for BHU 2026 Is Prepared

The BHU UG cutoff is calculated based on several factors rather than a single fixed formula. It is determined after each round using:

  • Total number of CUET UG candidates who applied to BHU
  • Total number of seats available in each programme and college
  • Candidate's category (General, OBC, SC, ST, EWS, PwBD)
  • Overall difficulty level of the CUET UG examination that year
  • Performance level of candidates in CUET UG

How to Check BHU UG Cut Off 2026

  • Visit the official BHU admission portal at admission.bhu.ac.in.
  • Log in using your registered CUET UG login credentials.
  • Navigate to the UG cutoff/merit list section for 2026.
  • Check your category-wise and course-wise cutoff status.
  • Download or save the cutoff/merit details for reference.

What Happens After the BHU UG Cutoff 2026?

Candidates whose scores meet or exceed the cutoff for a given round are invited to proceed in the BHU UG counselling process. Those who do not clear a round may still be considered in subsequent rounds if their score matches the cutoff released later. The typical next steps are:

  • Download the counselling call letter from the portal using roll number and date of birth.
  • Accept the allotted seat and complete the online fee payment within the specified window.
  • Report to the respective faculty/college on the scheduled reporting dates to complete admission formalities.

Seats that remain vacant after the regular cutoff rounds are declared on August 20, 2026, and are filled through the GD-PI round and the subsequent spot rounds (August 25 and August 29, 2026).

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Complete fee payment within the deadline given for your cutoff round; missing the deadline can lead to seat cancellation.
  • Keep login credentials (CUET application number and password/date of birth) ready to access the cutoff and merit list.
  • Candidates opting for a paid seat instead of a regular merit seat will be allotted accordingly based on preference order and merit score.
  • Recheck the vacant seat list before registering for the spot round, since spot round cutoffs typically differ from regular round cutoffs.
  • Carry the provisional admission letter and required documents while reporting to the faculty/college.
