ESIC Faridabad Senior Resident Vacancy 2026

The notification lists vacancies for Senior Resident across three departments, category-wise. Adding these figures gives 16 posts in ICU, 15 in NICU and 12 in PICU, or 43 in all. The grand total is calculated from the table and is not printed separately in the notification. In the ICU department, if candidates with a postgraduate qualification do not appear, MBBS doctors without a PG degree may be considered for appointment for 01 year as Non-PG Resident against a Senior Resident post on contract basis. They need 02 years of experience, of which one year must be in the concerned speciality (ICU/General Medicine/Emergency Medicine/Casualty/Anesthesia) from a government or private hospital (not a private clinic).

ESIC Faridabad Senior Resident Salary

The notification does not specify whether this amount is monthly or annual. The tenure is 01 (One) year, extendable up to 03 years, subject to satisfactory performance, willingness, conduct of the candidate and requirements of the institution. The contract will be terminated in case of appointment/joining of a bonded Senior Resident on the same post. The competent authority can terminate services at any time in the public interest without prior notice or reason. To resign, the Senior Resident must submit one-month advance notice or deposit an amount equivalent to one month's salary. Failure to do so will result in forfeiture of both the one-month salary and the security deposit, and may lead to legal proceedings. ESIC Faridabad Senior Resident Selection Process

Candidates appear before the Document Verification Committee on the interview date with their offline application form and self-attested copies of documents. Only candidates whose documents are successfully verified are allowed before the Interview Board. Selection is made on the basis of acquired academic credentials and performance in the interview, conducted by a duly constituted selection board. The result will be published only in the Recruitments section of the website www.esic.gov.in. The offer of appointment will be sent through E-mail only. Selected candidates must join as per the instructions in the offer of appointment.
