Stipend and Training Period

Selected Graduate Apprentices will receive a stipend of ₹ 14,000/- Per Month, and Technician (Diploma) Holders will receive ₹ 12,000/- Per Month. Under the National Apprenticeship Training Scheme (NATS), 50% of the minimum prescribed rate is borne by the Govt. of India and disbursed via Direct Benefit Transfer into the Aadhar-seeded savings bank account of the apprentice. The training duration is strictly One Year, and extension after completion is not admissible.

IRCON to recruit 54 Apprentices Posts : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

There will be no interviews. Eligible candidates will be shortlisted based on a merit list formed from the marks secured in essential educational qualifications.

IRCON to recruit 54 Apprentices Posts : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Mandatorily enroll and register yourself as a "Student" on the government website www.mhrdnats.gov.in and obtain the unique registration number.
  • 2Visit the IRCON website at www.ircon.org and navigate to Careers -> HR & Career -> Engagement of Apprentices under Apprentices Act, 1961.
  • 3Register on the IRCON portal using a personal email ID and mobile number to obtain login credentials.
  • 4Log in with your registered email ID and password, read the instructions carefully, and fill out the online application form with post selection, personal details, education details, and experience details.
  • 5Upload scanned copies of required documents, photograph, and signature in PDF or JPEG format (maximum size 2 MB each).
  • 6Preview the details carefully, submit the online application form, and take a printout of the submitted application form generated in PDF format.
  • 7Duly sign the printed application form physically at the bottom right corner under the declaration.
  • 8Send the hard copy of the application form along with self-attested photocopies of required documents in an envelope superscribed as "Application for the Engagement of Apprentices as per Apprentices Act 1961 –Advt No. A01/2026" to Manager/ HRM, IRCON INTERNATIONAL LIMITED, C- 4, District Centre, Saket, New Delhi-110017 so that it reaches on or before 17.11.2026.
