OAV Bapanpalli Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

OAV Bapanpalli Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

According to the staff position table in the notice, the Warden, Chowkidar-cum-sweeper, Head Cook and Assistant Cook are to be selected by the District as per Govt. guideline. Head Cook: the committee staff conduct physical cooking of some items and evaluate the deliciousness, the time taken to prepare the dishes and the hygienic condition of cooking, and select the best. Warden: preference is given to the local candidate, preferably from the block area, and then from the adjacent block of the district. Head Cook: candidates trained in cookery get preference and due weightage.

OAV Bapanpalli Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in OAV Bapanpalli Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 5 vacancies in the Boys' Hostel: Warden 1, Chowkidar-cum-sweeper 1, Head Cook 1 and Assistant Cook 2.
Q2What is the last date to apply?
A
Applications must be sent by Speed Post to the Principal of OAV Bapanpalli by 26 November 2026; forms received after the due date shall be summarily rejected.
Q3What qualification is needed for the Warden post?
A
The Warden needs a B.A/B.Sc from any recognized University and at least 02 years of experience in hostel management.
Q4What is the qualification for Chowkidar-cum-sweeper?
A
The Chowkidar-cum-sweeper must have passed the HSC Examination; the post is for male candidates aged 21 to 32 years.
Q5What is the age limit for each post?
A
As on the date of advertisement, the age limit is 30-60 years for Warden, 21-32 years for Chowkidar-cum-sweeper, 30-40 years for Head Cook and 21-32 years for Assistant Cook.
Q6What is the salary of OAV Bapanpalli hostel staff?
A
The monthly remuneration is Rs. 21,175 for Warden, Rs. 7,500 for Chowkidar-cum-sweeper, Rs. 9,000 for Head Cook and Rs. 6,750 for Assistant Cook, each including the EPF share of Employee & Employer.
Q7How do I apply for OAV Bapanpalli Recruitment 2026?
A
Download the form from https://malkangiri.odisha.gov.in or https://oav.edu.in/OAVBAP, fill it in capital letters, attach self-attested documents and send it by Speed Post only to Principal, Odisha Adarsha Vidyalaya Bapanpalli, At: Bapanpalli, PO: Padia, Dist: Malkangiri, PIN: 764047, State: Odisha.
Q8How is the Head Cook selected?
A
The committee conducts physical cooking of some items, evaluates taste, preparation time and hygiene, and selects the best; candidates trained in cookery get preference and due weightage.
Q9Do the Warden and cooks have to stay in the hostel?
A
Yes, the Warden, Head Cook and Assistant Cook have to stay in the hostel 24x7.
Q10Can retired teachers apply for the Warden post?
A
Retired teachers, if willing and physically and mentally sound within the age limit of 62 years as on the date of engagement, may be engaged as Warden.
