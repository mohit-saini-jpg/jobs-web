TNSTC MTC SETC Apprentice Recruitment 2026-27 Notification Out

Tamil Nadu State Transport Corporation, Metropolitan Transport Corporation Chennai, and State Express Transport Corporation Tamil Nadu have jointly released the official notification for engagement of Apprentices for 2026-27 under the Apprenticeship (Amendment) Act, 1973. The notification includes complete details about region-wise training slots, eligibility criteria, stipend, selection procedure, and important dates. Eligible candidates can apply online through the NATS portal until 28-08-2026. Candidates can use the direct link below to download the official notification PDF and read all recruitment details. TNSTC MTC SETC Apprentice Recruitment 2026-27 Eligibility Criteria

Qualifying degree/diploma must have been passed during 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, or May 2026, and candidates must hail from Tamil Nadu. Not Eligible: Candidates who have already completed, or are currently undergoing, apprenticeship under the Apprenticeship (Amendment) Act 1973, or who have one year or more of relevant experience, are not eligible to apply.

TNSTC MTC SETC Apprentice Recruitment 2026-27 Age Limit

The age limit will be followed as per the Apprenticeship Rules. Specific minimum/maximum age figures are not specified in the notification.

TNSTC MTC SETC Apprentice 2026-27 Stipend/Salary Structure

Graduate Apprentices (Engineering and Non-Engineering): Rs. 12,300 per month Diploma in Engineering (Technician) Apprentices: Rs. 10,900 per month

TNSTC MTC SETC Apprentice Recruitment 2026-27 Selection Process

Shortlisting will be carried out by the Board of Apprenticeship Training (Southern Region) based on the percentage of marks obtained in the qualifying/basic prescribed qualification relevant to each discipline. Shortlisted candidates will be notified through their registered email ID. Shortlisted candidates must appear for Certificate Verification at the TNSTC Trichy Region office (tentatively scheduled for the fourth week of September 2026). The list of shortlisted candidates will be published on the NATS portal (Home page → Regional Events → BOAT Southern Region → Apprenticeship News).

TNSTC MTC SETC Apprentice Recruitment 2026-27 Documents Required

B.E./B.Tech/Diploma/BA/BSc/BCom/BBA/BCA (or equivalent) Provisional Certificate or Consolidated Mark Sheet Percentage of marks or CGPA x 10 (mandatory entry on the NATS portal; candidature will not be considered if not furnished) Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC-NCL/PwD), if claiming reservation

TNSTC Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Applications must be submitted online through the National Apprenticeship Training Scheme (NATS) portal at nats.education.gov.in. The process differs based on whether the candidate is already enrolled on the portal.
  • 2Visit https://nats.education.gov.in and click Student, then Student Login.
  • 3Click Forgot Password and enter your registered email ID to receive an activation link and reset your password.
  • 4Log in with your registered email ID and new password, and complete the application form.
  • 5Under "Apply against advertised vacancies," search for the relevant organization name (e.g., "TAMILNADU STATE TRANSPORT CORPORATION VILLUPURAM LTD," "TNSTC KUMBAKONAM," "TNSTC SALEM SALEM REGION," "TAMILNADU STATE TRANSPORT CORPORATION MADURAI LTD MADURAI REGION," "TAMIL NADU STATE TRANSPORT CORPORATION LTD TIRUNELVELI REGION," "METROPOLITAN TRANSPORT CORPORATION CHENNAI LTD," or "STATE EXPRESS TRANSPORT CORPORATION TN LTD").
  • 6Click Apply. The application status will show as "Applied."
  • 7Visit https://nats.education.gov.in, click Student, then Student Register.
  • 8Complete the application form to generate a unique 12-digit Enrolment Number (ATN...).
  • 9Log in with your registered email ID and password.
  • 10Under "Apply against advertised vacancies," search for the relevant organization name as listed above.
  • 11Click Apply, and upload the required Provisional Certificate/Consolidated Mark Sheet along with the percentage of marks or CGPA x 10.
  • 12Confirm the application status shows as "Applied."
