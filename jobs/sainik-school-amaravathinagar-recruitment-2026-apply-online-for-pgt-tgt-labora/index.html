Sainik School Amaravathinagar Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

The selection process consists of three phases, along with weightage for higher qualification and experience. A candidate must score a minimum of 33% marks to qualify for the next step (skill/trade test). However, the Board of Officers may increase this minimum qualifying mark up to 50% to shortlist candidates for the next stage of selection. Shortlisted candidates will be required to demonstrate their classroom teaching skills in a live classroom environment on a topic assigned by the Selection Board. The demonstration must be supported by a well-prepared lesson plan and appropriate teaching aids. Candidates will appear before the Selection Board for an interview to assess subject knowledge, teaching aptitude, communication skills, and overall suitability for the post.
