UPPSC Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

The selection process for UPPSC Recruitment 2026 depends on the post applied for. The Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission will conduct separate examinations for the advertised posts and select suitable candidates based on the prescribed selection procedure. For the posts of Architectural Assistant, Estate Manager and Microbiologist, UPPSC will conduct separate examinations at various examination centres in Uttar Pradesh. The selection will be made on the basis of marks obtained by candidates in the examination. For Manager (System), Senior Programmer and Programmer Grade-2 posts, selection will be based on marks obtained by candidates in the examination. For Computer Operator Grade-B, selection will be based on total marks obtained in the examination along with Hindi and English type test.
