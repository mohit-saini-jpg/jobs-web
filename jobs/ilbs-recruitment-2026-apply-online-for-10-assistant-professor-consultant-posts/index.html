ILBS Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Institute of Liver and Biliary Sciences (ILBS), New Delhi, has invited applications for 10 vacancies in 8 faculty and consultant posts, from Assistant Professor (Radiology) to Consultant (GEN AI Developer – Healthcare Software) Grade IV. The appointments are on contract for one year, with remuneration at PB - IV, GP - 8700 (GP - 9500 for one post). Applications are sent by email to [email protected] latest by 30 November 2026.

About ILBS

The Institute of Liver and Biliary Sciences is an autonomous super-specialty institute under GNCT of Delhi and a Deemed-to-be-University by the UGC. Its stated vision is to be a global leader in patient care, teaching and research in the field of liver, gall bladder and pancreatobiliary diseases. Its address is D - 1, Vasant Kunj, Delhi - 110070.

Department-wise Vacancies

ILBS has listed 10 vacancies in 8 posts. The vacancies are tentative: the notice says they are subject to change at any time and that the Institute reserves the right whether or not to fill any post. The vacancy may be removed from the ILBS website once a post is filled. A post that falls vacant can also be added at the discretion of ILBS.

Pay and Terms

The pay package is based on the 7th CPC at the pay level applicable for the Institute. The notice does not name any allowances. All 8 posts are on "Contract Basis" for a period of one year. Either side can end the contract by giving the other party not less than three months notice in writing, or salary in lieu of the notice period. The date of joining of selected candidates and other conditions will be as per the HR policy of the Institute.
