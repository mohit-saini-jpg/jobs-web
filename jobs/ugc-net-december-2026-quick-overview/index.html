How to Download the UGC NET December 2026 Exam Schedule PDF

The subject-wise schedule is published as a public notice dated 19 November 2026 on the official NTA UGC NET website. Follow these steps to find and save it:

  • Open the official website: https://ugcnet.nta.nic.in/.
  • Look for the public notice titled Subject-wise Schedule of UGC-NET December 2026 examination, dated 19 November 2026, in the notices or latest updates section.
  • Click the notice to open the PDF. The schedule is on the pages titled Annexure-I.
  • Use the download or save option in your browser or PDF viewer to keep a copy.
  • Find your subject name or code in the table and note your date and shift.

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Visit the official NTA UGC NET website regularly for updates, instructions and notices.
  • Note your subject code, exam date and shift from Annexure-I and confirm them against your admit card when it is released.
  • Check the exam city intimation on the NTA website 21 days before your exam date.
  • Rely only on official NTA communication for any clarification.
