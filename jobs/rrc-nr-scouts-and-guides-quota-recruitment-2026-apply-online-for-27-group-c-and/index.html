RRC NR Scouts and Guides Quota Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Scouts and Guides who hold the President Scout/Guide/Rover/Ranger award or the Himalaya Wood Badge can apply for 27 posts in Northern Railway under the Scouts & Guides quota for 2026-27: 5 Group 'C' posts in Level 2 and 22 Group 'D' (Erstwhile) posts in Level 1 of the 7th CPC Pay Matrix. Railway Recruitment Cell, Northern Railway (RRC/NR) has issued Employment Notice No. RRC/NR 01/2026/S&G; the posts are open to all communities with no reservation. Applications are accepted online on www.rrcnr.org from 6 November 2026 to 6 December 2026, with a fee of Rs. 500 (Rs. 250 for specified categories)

About RRC NR

The Railway Recruitment Cell, Northern Railway conducts this recruitment for posts in Northern Railway against the Scouts & Guides quota for the year 2026-27. The notice is signed by the Chairman, Railway Recruitment Cell, Northern Railway, and the recruitment follows the Railway Board's policy in Master Circular No. 07 dated 13/12/2019, read with later amendments. The RRC website www.rrcnr.org is named as the main source of information for candidates during the selection.

Vacancy Details

The notice gives 27 posts in total. No post is reserved for SC, ST or OBC; candidates of these categories get only the age relaxation admissible in open market recruitment. A candidate may apply for both Level 1 and Level 2 if eligible, but must submit a separate application form with a separate examination fee for each. Separate examinations will be held for the two levels, tentatively on the same day in two shifts.

Selection and Interview

Eligible candidates will be called for a written examination (Computer Based Test/Tablet Based Test); those who qualify undergo Document Verification, and the panel is formed on merit of the written examination and the marks on certificates. Candidates recommended for appointment must pass the prescribed medical fitness standards. The examination is in Hindi or English. Negative marking: 1/3rd of the marks for the question deducted for every wrong objective answer. Minimum 40% marks in the written examination are needed for the next stage; relaxation for reserved categories as per Railway Board's rules. Candidates equal to 5 times the notified vacancies will be called for Document Verification on merit. Written Examination and Document Verification will be held in Delhi only. RRC NR Scouts and Guides Quota Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in RRC NR Scouts & Guides quota 2026?
A
There are 27 vacancies: 05 Group 'C' (Level 2) and 22 Group 'D' (Erstwhile) (Level 1), with no reservation for any community.
Q2What is the last date to apply?
A
The online application opens on 6 November 2026 and closes on 6 December 2026.
Q3What qualification is needed for Group 'D' (Erstwhile)?
A
10th pass with NAC granted by NCVT, or 10th pass with ITI, or 10th pass or ITI or equivalent or NAC, plus the scouting qualification (President Scout/Guide/Rover/Ranger or HWB, 5 years' active membership and the required events).
Q4What is the age limit?
A
As on 01.01.2027, Group 'C' (Level 2) is 18 to 30 years for UR, 18 to 33 for OBC and 18 to 35 for SC/ST; Group 'D' (Erstwhile) (Level 1) is 18 to 33 for UR, 18 to 36 for OBC and 18 to 38 for SC/ST.
Q5What is the salary?
A
Group 'C' is in Level 2 and Group 'D' (Erstwhile) in Level 1 of the 7th CPC Pay Matrix, with appointment at the minimum of the scale.
Q6What is the application fee?
A
Rs. 500 for general candidates, of which Rs. 400 is refunded on appearing in the exam, and Rs. 250 for SC/ST/Ex-Serviceman/PwBD/Transgender/Women/Minorities/Economically Backward Classes, refunded on appearing, less bank charges in both cases.
Q7How is the selection made?
A
Through a 60-mark written test (CBT/TBT) and 40 marks on Scouts & Guides certificates, followed by Document Verification and a medical examination; a minimum of 40% in the written test is needed.
Q8Is there negative marking?
A
Yes, 1/3rd of the marks of a question is deducted for every wrong answer in the objective questions.
Q9Can I apply for both Level 1 and Level 2?
A
Yes, if eligible, but a separate application and fee is needed for each; the two exams are tentatively on the same day in two shifts.
Q10Where will the exam be held?
A
The Written Examination and Document Verification will be held in Delhi only, with the exam expected in January 2027.
