DCPW Recruitment 2027 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in DCPW Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 12 vacancies: 05 Assistant Section Officer posts and 07 Assistant Communication Officer posts.
Q2What is the last date for DCPW Recruitment 2026?
A
Applications must reach DCPW within 60 days from the circular's publication in Employment News or its date of issue, whichever is later; counted from the date of issue (23 November 2026), this is about 22 January 2027.
Q3What is the qualification for DCPW Assistant Section Officer?
A
A Bachelor Degree in Science in Electronics or Electronics and Communication or Physics as one of the subjects, plus two years experience of General Administration/Store/Procurement with computer proficiency, along with the regular-service condition in the parent cadre.
Q4What is the qualification for DCPW Assistant Communication Officer?
A
A B.Tech/B.E./B.Sc (Engineering) degree in Electronics, Electronics and Communication, Information Technology, Computer Science or Information and Communication Technology, or a Bachelor's Degree in science with one of the listed subjects plus one year experience in Radio, Computer or Satellite Communication or Cyber Security.
Q5What is the age limit for DCPW deputation posts?
A
Where prescribed by the Recruitment Rules, the maximum age for appointment by deputation is 56 years as on the closing date of receipt of applications.
Q6What is the salary of DCPW Assistant Section Officer and Assistant Communication Officer?
A
Assistant Section Officer is in Level 7 (₹44,900–1,42,400) and Assistant Communication Officer is in Level 6 (₹35,400–1,12,400) of the pay matrix.
Q7How do I apply for DCPW Recruitment 2026?
A
Fill in the Annexure-II proforma and send it with the required documents through the proper channel to the Joint Director (Admn.), Directorate of Coordination Police Wireless, Ministry of Home Affairs, Block No. 9, CGO Complex, Lodhi Road, New Delhi-110003.
Q8Who can apply for the DCPW Assistant Communication Officer post?
A
Officers of Central Police Organisations, Central Armed Police Forces, Defence Organisations or State/UT Police Organisations who meet the service and qualification conditions; Armed Forces personnel due to retire or be transferred to reserve within one year may also be considered.
Q9How long is the deputation period in DCPW?
A
The period of deputation, including any deputation in another ex-cadre post held immediately before, shall ordinarily not exceed three years.
Q10Can departmental feeder-grade officers apply for DCPW Assistant Section Officer on deputation?
A
No. Departmental officers in the feeder category who are in the direct line of promotion are not eligible for appointment on deputation to the Assistant Section Officer post.
