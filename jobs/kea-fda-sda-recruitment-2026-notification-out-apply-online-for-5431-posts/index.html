What is the Full Form of FDA and SDA in KEA Recruitment 2026?

In the KEA SDA FDA Recruitment 2026 , FDA stands for First Division Assistant , while SDA stands for Second Division Assistant . Both are administrative and clerical positions in various Karnataka government departments and corporations. FDA generally involves higher-level administrative responsibilities, whereas SDA focuses on routine clerical work, record maintenance, data entry and office support.

Difference Between FDA and SDA in Karnataka Government Jobs

Key Difference: FDA is generally a higher-level administrative post requiring a Bachelor's degree, while SDA is a lower-level clerical post generally requiring 2nd PUC (12th Pass). FDA positions typically offer higher pay and greater responsibilities than SDA positions. However, the exact qualifications, salary scales and duties depend on the department and specific post mentioned in the KEA SDA FDA Recruitment 2026 notification.
