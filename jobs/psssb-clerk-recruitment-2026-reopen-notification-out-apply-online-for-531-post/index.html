PSSSB Clerk Age Limit

Age is reckoned as on 01.01.2026: Minimum age: 18 years for all categories General category: maximum 37 years SC and BC candidates who are residents of Punjab: maximum 42 years State and Central Government employees: maximum 45 years Ex-servicemen (residents of Punjab): as per the Punjab Recruitment of Ex-Servicemen Rules, 1982, as amended from time to time Physically Handicapped candidates (residents of Punjab): maximum 47 years (10 years' relaxation) Widows, divorcees, and certain other categories of married women: relaxation up to 40 years Candidates should ensure that they meet the prescribed age limit as on the specified cut-off date. PSSSB Clerk Selection Process

An objective-type (Multiple Choice Questions) written examination will be held in two parts — Part A and Part B Part A: Matriculation-level Punjabi Language paper, of qualifying nature only; a minimum of 50% marks is required to qualify Part B: The merit list is prepared based only on marks obtained in Part B; each question carries 1 mark, and 1/4 mark is deducted for each wrong answer A Typing Test in English and Punjabi at a speed of 30 words per minute will be conducted on computer for candidates called as per the competent authority's decision; the Punjabi typing test will be in Unicode-compliant Raavi font The typing test is of qualifying nature only and must be qualified The Common Merit List is prepared based on Part-B marks of candidates who qualify the typing test In case of a tie in merit, preference is given first by date of birth (older candidate ranked higher), then by perce
