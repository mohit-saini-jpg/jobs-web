About UPSC Recruitment 2026

The Union Public Service Commission invites these applications through its online recruitment application portal. The Dental Surgeon post serves in Safdarjung Hospital, Dr. RML Hospital and RHTC Najafgarh. The Ayurveda and Homeopathic Physician posts are in the AYUSH Cadre of the New Delhi Municipal Council. The Scientist 'B' post is at the Botanical Survey of India, CGO Complex, Salt Lake City, Kolkata.

UPSC Recruitment 2026 Important Dates

Applications start on 14 November 2026. The date for determining the eligibility of all candidates in every respect is the closing date for online applications. For Scientist 'B', the notice says the original closing date, 03.07.2026, prevails for age and similar eligibility conditions.

UPSC Vacancy 2026

The notice carries 13 vacancies in total: 5 + 2 + 1 + 5. Of the five Dental Surgeon vacancies, one is reserved for persons with benchmark disability (PwBD) in the Deaf and Hard of Hearing category. Ayurveda Physician, Homeopathic Physician and Scientist 'B' are not reserved for PwBD but are identified as suitable for certain disability types. The disability-type suitability tables for each post are in the official notification. The five Scientist 'B' vacancies may be filled by candidates with these disciplines: MSc Botany - 3; MSc Horticulture - 1; MSc Chemistry (Organic) - 1.

UPSC Eligibility Criteria 2026

A recognised Dental qualification obtained upto 01.11.1972 under Part-II of the Schedule to the Dentists Act, 1948 (16 of 1948). OR a recognised Dental qualification granted by an authority or institution outside India, only when granted to a citizen of India upto 13.08.2009 under Part-III of the Schedule to the Dentists Act, 1948. OR a citizen of India with a foreign qualification who has appeared and passed the Screening Test Examination as per Screening Test Regulation 2009 w.e.f. 13.08.2009. Candidates who have not completed CRI (compulsory rotating internship) may appear for selection, but if selected must complete it satisfactorily before appointment. Citizenship: a citizen of India, a subject of Nepal or Bhutan, a Tibetan refugee who came to India before 1 January 1962 intending to settle permanently, or a person of Indian origin who migrated from the countries named in the notice
PostQualification Required
Dental SurgeonA recognised Bachelor of Dental Surgery degree included in part-1 of the Dentists Act, 1948 (16 of 1948), or one of the alternatives listed below; registration with any State Dental Council or Tribunal in India; completion of CRI.

UPSC Selection Process 2026

Candidates are shortlisted for interview on the basis of the information in their online applications and their performance in the Recruitment Test (RT), if held. The stages in order are: Shortlisting based on the online application, with a Recruitment Test if held. Interview. If applications are large, the Commission may restrict the number called for interview by desirable qualification, higher qualification, higher experience, counting experience before or after the essential qualification, invoking experience even where none is prescribed, or by holding a Recruitment Test. Minimum suitability in the interview, out of 100 marks: UR-50, OBC-45, SC/ST/PH-40. Where an RT is followed by an interview, a candidate must reach the minimum suitability for the category at both stages. The RT minimum is decided by the Commission case to case. UPSC Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Go to the Commission's website https://upsconline.nic.in and read the General instructions, Profile/Module-wise instructions and instructions for uploading documents on the menu bar of the home page.
  • 2Register on the portal to get a Universal Registration Number (URN), giving the details of one Photo ID Card (Aadhaar is strongly advised) in the URN profile.
  • 3Fill the Common Application Form (CAF), uploading a photograph and capturing a live photograph, and uploading three signatures made one below the other in black ink on plain white paper.
  • 4Fill the post/vacancy-specific module for the advertised post and upload, in pdf, the documents supporting each claim (date of birth, category, disability, qualification, experience, age relaxation).
  • 5Pay the fee of Rs. 25/- by card, UPI or net banking if not exempted.
  • 6Submit the application by 1800 hrs on 04-12-2026 and take a print-out of the finally submitted application. An Application Number is generated; keep it with the URN for communication.
  • 7Failure to provide the required information/documents along with the CAF cancels candidature for the post.
  • 8Candidates are not required to send printouts or documents by post or by hand. They bring them if called for interview.
  • 9Candidates are advised to apply well in advance of the closing date.
