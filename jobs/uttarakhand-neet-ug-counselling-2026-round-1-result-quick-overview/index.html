Uttarakhand NEET UG Counselling 2026 Round 1 Result : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 28/08/2026

Uttarakhand NEET UG Counselling 2026 Round 1 Result: The Uttarakhand State Centralised Counselling Board, under the Hemwati Nandan Bahuguna Uttarakhand Medical Education University (HNBUMU), has declared the Round 1 seat allotment result for Uttarakhand NEET UG Counselling 2026 on August 27, 2026. Candidates who registered and filled choices for MBBS and BDS seats under the state's 85% quota can check their allotment status on the official website, hnbumu.ac.in, or the counselling portal meta-secure.com/HNBUMU_NEETUG. Candidates allotted a seat must complete document verification and report to their allotted college by September 5, 2026.

Uttarakhand NEET UG Counselling 2026 Important Dates

The counselling board revised the original Round 1 schedule through an official notice. The table below shows both the earlier and the revised dates.

EventEarlier ScheduleRevised Schedule
Registration and choice fillingAugust 13 to 19, 2026August 13 to 24, 2026
Data processingAugust 20 and 21, 2026August 25 and 26, 2026
Round 1 seat allotment resultAugust 22, 2026August 27, 2026
Last date to report at allotted collegeAugust 28, 2026September 5, 2026

The board has clarified that this schedule is indicative and may be revised further based on directions from the Government of India, the Medical Counselling Committee (MCC), the National Medical Commission (NMC), the Dental Council of India, and the Uttarakhand government. No individual communication is sent for schedule changes, so candidates should check the official website regularly.

How to Check Uttarakhand NEET UG Counselling 2026 Round 1 Result

  • Visit the official website, hnbumu.ac.in, or the counselling portal, meta-secure.com/HNBUMU_NEETUG.
  • Open the "NEET UG 2026 Uttarakhand State Centralized Counselling" section on the homepage.
  • Go to the "Announcements" section.
  • Click on "Provisional Complete Broad Sheet (NEET UG) Round-1."
  • The Round 1 seat allotment PDF will open on the screen.
  • Use Ctrl+F (or the search function) to look up your NEET UG roll number and check the allotted college and course.
  • Download and save the PDF for future reference and college reporting.

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Verify all details on the allotment letter carefully - name, roll number, category, allotted college, and course - immediately after downloading.
  • Report to the allotted college by September 5, 2026, along with original documents and the prescribed reporting fee to confirm admission.
  • Candidates who wish to use the free exit facility should do so within the timeline specified in the official notice, to remain eligible for later rounds.
  • Keep checking hnbumu.ac.in regularly, as the counselling board does not send individual notifications for schedule updates.
  • Schedules and procedures for Round 2, Round 3, and the stray vacancy round will be announced separately after Round 1 concludes.

What Happens After Round 1?

Candidates who do not receive a seat in Round 1, or who use the free exit facility, remain eligible for Round 2, Round 3, and the stray vacancy round. The counselling board has indicated that detailed schedules for these subsequent rounds will be notified separately on the official website once Round 1 concludes.
