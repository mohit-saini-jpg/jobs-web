Ratnagiri District Mahsul Sevak Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

Reservation applies as per the category-wise posts listed above. The notification sets these conditions for candidates applying from reserved categories.
A candidate claiming a reserved post must attach attested copies of the caste/category certificate issued by the competent authority and, where applicable, the non-creamy layer certificate. Backward-class candidates must produce the original caste certificate and original caste validity certificate at the time of verification. If a selected backward-class candidate cannot produce the caste validity certificate, a temporary appointment may be given after the candidate submits a bond or affidavit promising to submit it within six months. The notification cites the General Administration Department Government Resolution dated 12/12/2011 for this. If the certificate is not submitted within the prescribed period of at most six months after appointment, the service will be terminated. Backward-class candidates other than Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes must attach an attested copy of a valid non-creamy layer certificate showing that they are not in the creamy layer. EWS candidates must attach an attested copy of a valid EWS certificate, issued as prescribed by the General Administration Department Government Resolutions dated 12/02/2019 and 31/05/2021. SEBC candidates must attach attested copies of the caste certificate and the non-creamy layer certificate, as prescribed by the General Administration Department Government Resolution dated 29 January 2025 (or the equivalent resolution in force at the time). Creamy layer candidates from other backward classes will not get the reservation of that class. The reserved category must be clearly mentioned in the application. Otherwise the application will be rejected. The application will be considered only under the category for which the candidate has applied, and no complaint on this point will be entertained later. Women candidates must submit the application in their maiden name (name by birth), in the prescribed format.
