BRIC-THSTI Recruitment 2027 : Job Overview

The posts carry Pay Level 15 to 13, with monthly emoluments of ₹2,71,542 to ₹3,99,092 at present rates. About the Institute

BRIC-THSTI works with academic and industry partners to turn biomedical discoveries into public-health innovations. Its campus is at the NCR Biotech Science Cluster, Faridabad-Gurugram Expressway, Faridabad. The notice names its Medical Research Centre (MRC), which supports advanced clinical research, and the APEX Centre, an integrated pre-product-development facility that is being operationalised.

Vacancy Details

The notice lists 10 posts in total. Scientist-E is the only post with reserved vacancies. The notice says none of the posts is reserved for Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD), but they are identified as suitable for locomotor disability (OA, OL, OAL including leprosy cured, dwarfism and acid attack victims), hard of hearing, low vision, and multiple disabilities from among these categories (excluding deaf-blindness). UR means Unreserved, OBC means Other Backward Classes (Non-Creamy layer) and SC means Scheduled Caste. The monthly emoluments are at present rates of initial Basic Pay + DA + HRA + TA + Employer's NPS.

Selection and Interview

The posts are filled through Direct Recruitment in the first instance. Deputation/Absorption applicants are considered only if no suitable candidate is empanelled through Direct Recruitment. The notice mentions a written test/skill test/interview and does not give their dates or marks. If many applications are received, screening limits the candidates to those with higher/relevant qualifications, concept notes and experience. Only shortlisted candidates are called for the written test/skill test/interview. Meeting the essential qualifications does not entitle a candidate to be called. Applicants will be asked to explain and defend their concept note during the selection process. Future communications about selection are made only by email. Results and notifications are published only on the BRIC-THSTI website.
