About IIITD

IIITD is the Indraprastha Institute of Information Technology Delhi, a State University established by an Act of the Govt. of NCT of Delhi (IIIT-Delhi Act 2007). The notice is signed by the Registrar. The post sits in the Placements Division and works with the Head Career Services and the placement and student teams.

Vacancy and Who Can Apply

The notice lists one post, Deputy Administrative Officer, under post code Recruitment/42/2026-01, in the UR category. Post Graduate degree in the relevant domain with good academic record (minimum 55% marks); or Equivalent and total 8 years of relevant experience out of which 6 years at Level-6; or Equivalent experience for those working in private organizations. Minimum of 4 to 5 years of relevant work experience in campus placement in an educational/research institutes/University, AND Established network and live connections with the industry. The prescribed qualifications are minimum; possessing them does not entitle a candidate to be called for the selection process. Candidates should check the official notification for the full eligibility rules.

Pay and Tenure

The post carries Level-8 (Rs. 47600 – Rs.151100) as per 7th CPC. The notice says the position is associated with House Rent Allowance (HRA), Dearness Allowance (DA), Travel Allowance (TA), Medical Allowance, National Pension System (NPS), mobile expenses reimbursement, data card reimbursement, LTS reimbursement, Professional Development Allowance (PDA), Group Term Insurance, Group Health Insurance, Children's Education Allowance and NPL Bonus. Deserving candidates may be given higher starting salary, additional increments or pay protection. The notice states that all posts are contractual, initially for five years or as recommended by the Selection Committee, extendable as per Institute norms and the provisions of the Recruitment and Promotion Rules for Non-Teaching Staff – 2024.
