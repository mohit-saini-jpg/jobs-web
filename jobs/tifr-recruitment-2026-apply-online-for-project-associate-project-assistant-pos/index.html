TIFR Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Tata Institute of Fundamental Research (TIFR), Mumbai has released Advertisement No. 2026/22, inviting applications for one post each of Project Associate and Project Assistant in the Department of Biological Sciences. Interested and eligible candidates with a BSc or MSc in Biological Sciences can apply online through the prescribed Google Form on or before 10 August 2026. Both positions are purely temporary, for a 10-month duration from 1 October 2026 to 31 July 2027.

Who Can Apply – Eligibility Criteria

The selected Project Associate will work on projects involving microdissection, tissue culture, imaging, and histological analysis. The selected Project Assistant will provide genotyping support across multiple lab projects and will also perform histology and imaging.
Post NameEducational QualificationDesirable Experience
Project AssociateMSc in Biological SciencesExperience in imaging histological slides, microdissection of embryonic brain tissue, and tissue culture will be advantageous
Project AssistantBSc or MSc in Biological SciencesExperience in genotyping of mice and associated techniques, and tissue sectioning/histology will be advantageous

Nature and Period of Post

Both appointments are temporary, for a period of 10 months (1 October 2026 to 31 July 2027), and are non-pensionable in nature.
