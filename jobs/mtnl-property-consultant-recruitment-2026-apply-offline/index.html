About MTNL property Consultant

According to the notice, Mahanagar Telephone Nigam Limited is a Navratna PSU under the Ministry of Telecommunication, providing a wide array of Telecom and IT services in the metro cities of Delhi and Mumbai. The property Consultant will work in MTNL Delhi and is expected to bring knowledge of prevailing land laws/ rules of costing, disposal, planning/ engineering of land management. The engagement is made under MTNL's Terms of Engagement (Annexure-II), "as per Company policy as amended from time to time".

Who Can Apply

The notice is open only to retired officers. An applicant must meet one of the two options below. Option 1: The applicant should have retired from Group 'A' services of any CPSE/ Government Department, preferably from DDA/ L&DO/ Noida Authority, having minimum three years experience & expertise in dealing with land, planning, engineering, and is supposed to coordinate with DDA, L&DO, MCD, DUSIB, Delhi Government, DSIIDC, Gram panchayt, Noida Authority in land matters. OR Option 2: The applicant should have retired from PSU/Autonomous body/ Govt. from the post of DGM and above (regular E-6 or above)/JAG, having mandatory expertise for the job. Retirement from DDA, L&DO or Noida Authority is a preference under Option 1, not a requirement. Pay and Tenure

Consolidated fee: the difference between last pay drawn plus DA and Pension plus DA, or a lumpsum amount of Rs.51,000/-, whichever is less. Mobile: CMD, MTNL has power to consider a special request for mobile for discharge of official duties. No other charges: no other charges shall be admissible. Tax: Income Tax or any other tax liable to be deducted under prevailing rules will be deducted at source, and MTNL will issue a TDS certificate. Leave: Casual Leave on pro-rata basis is admissible for the period of contract, up to a maximum of 12 in a year. Tenure: purely on a short term contract basis, initially for six months, which can be further extended based on requirement and performance. Either party may terminate the consultancy/contract by giving 30 days prior written notice.

MTNL property Consultant Job Description

The scope of work printed in the notice is to handle asset monetization related work in the Company and handling/ liaisoning for land disposal related matters, as under: Resolving issues pertaining to property documents/ monetization with various local bodies like DDA, L&DO, MCD, DUSIB, Delhi Government, DSIIDC, Gram panchayt, Noida Authority etc.

Selection and Interview

Selection shall be based on the qualifications/ experience, on the recommendation of a committee to be constituted for the purpose. The notice does not mention a written test or an interview date.
