KSRTC SWIFT Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

KSRTC SWIFT Limited is hiring for 5 contract posts at its terminal at Anayara, Thiruvananthapuram. The posts are 1 Front Office Manager Cum Multi-Tasking-Technical Supervisor (Rs. 30,000 per month; Plus Two/ITI/Equivalent in any discipline with 15 years' experience in a similar field), 1 Housekeeping Staff and 3 Security Staff (both need a 7th class pass). Applications must be submitted online at www.cmd.kerala.gov.in before 5 pm on 2 November 2026.

About KSRTC SWIFT Recruitment 2026

KSRTC SWIFT Limited is a company that runs Swift buses, which are deployed across the bus depots and units of KSRTC. This recruitment fills existing vacancies at the company's terminal at Anayara, Thiruvananthapuram. The notification is signed by the Chairman & Managing Director.

Who Can Apply

The age limit is the same for all three posts: 21 to 55 years as on the last date for submitting applications. Selection and Interview

Applications will be checked closely and a shortlist prepared. For all three posts, shortlisted candidates must pass an interview held by a selection committee set up for the appointment. KSRTC SWIFT Limited reserves the full right to shortlist only a limited number of candidates for a written test, group discussion, proficiency test or interview, as suits the post. Shortlisting is based on marks in the qualifying examination and/or relevant years of work experience. Admission to any stage of the selection process is provisional only. Applications and certificates will be verified in detail before the interview or appointment. If any discrepancy or false information is found, the candidature will be rejected. No travelling allowance or daily allowance (TA/DA) will be paid for attending the written test, proficiency test or interview.
