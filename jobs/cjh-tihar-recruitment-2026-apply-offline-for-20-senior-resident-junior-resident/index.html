CJH Tihar Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Central Jail Hospital, Tihar is filling 20 posts of doctors in Delhi Prisons on an ad-hoc basis: 7 Sr. Residents and 13 Jr. Residents (MBBS). Forms by hand are taken between 10:00 AM and 05:00 PM, Monday to Friday. The Sr. Resident interviews are on 2 November 2026 and the Jr. Resident interviews on 3 November 2026.

CJH Tihar Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Candidates are selected by interview. Both interviews are held at the Conference Hall, Prison Head Quarter, Near Lajwanti Garden Chowk, Janak Puri, New Delhi-110058. Reporting: eligible candidates should report at the Conference Hall between 10:00 AM and 11:00 AM to register for the interview. No candidate who reports after 11:00 AM will be allowed into the interview in any circumstances. Merit: the interview marks set the order of merit. If services have to end, they end in ascending order of interview merit, so the candidate with the lowest marks is terminated first. Before appointment: medical fitness and verification of certificates. Joining: selected candidates must join within 07 days of the offer letter being issued, or the offer is automatically cancelled.

CJH Tihar Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in CJH Tihar Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 20 vacancies: 07 Sr. Residents (Medicine 03, Orthopedics 01, Psychiatry 01, Ophthalmology 01, Pathology 01) and 13 Jr. Residents (MBBS). The number of vacancies may vary at any stage.
Q2What is the last date to apply?
A
The application form must be submitted by Speed Post or by hand latest by 29 October 2026. Forms by hand are accepted between 10:00 AM and 05:00 PM, Monday to Friday.
Q3What qualification is needed for the Sr. Resident post?
A
A Post Graduate Degree (MD/MS/DNB)/Diploma in the concerned specialty. If no PG candidates are available, MBBS graduates with at least 02 years' experience from a Govt./Govt. Empanelled Hospital, 01 year of it in the concerned specialty, may be considered.
Q4Who is eligible for the Jr. Resident (MBBS) post?
A
Candidates with MBBS and a 01-year internship completed on or after 29 October 2024 are eligible. FMGs with a foreign internship qualify based on their date of passing the FMGE. A DMC registration certificate is required when applying, though a valid DMC acknowledgement receipt is accepted for the interview.
Q5What is the age limit for CJH Tihar Recruitment 2026?
A
The upper age limit is 45 years for Sr. Residents and 30 years for Jr. Residents of the General/EWS category, both as on 29 October 2026. There is a relaxation of 5 years for SC/ST candidates, 03 years for OBC (Delhi only) and 5 years for qualified Ex-Service personnel with at least 5 years' continuous service.
Q6What is the salary of Sr. and Jr. Residents at Tihar Jail Hospital?
A
Sr. Residents get Pay Matrix Level-11 (Rs. 67,700-208700) plus other admissible allowances per month under the residency scheme. Jr. Residents get Pay level 10 (Rs. 56100-Rs. 177500/-) plus usual allowances as per rules/residency rules.
Q7Are allowances paid on top of the pay?
A
Yes. Sr. Residents get other admissible allowances under the residency scheme, and Jr. Residents get the usual allowances as per rules/residency rules. No TA/DA is paid for attending the interview.
Q8Is there an application fee?
A
No. The notice states that there is no fee for appearing in the interview.
Q9How will candidates be selected, and when are the interviews?
A
Selection is by interview at the Conference Hall, Prison Head Quarter, Janak Puri, New Delhi-110058. Sr. Residents are interviewed on 2 November 2026 and Jr. Residents on 3 November 2026. Candidates must report between 10:00 AM and 11:00 AM, and no one is allowed in after 11:00 AM.
Q10Is this a permanent job?
A
No. The posts are ad-hoc, initially for 44 days or until the Centralized Recruitment Committee under the Dean, MAMC makes its selection, whichever is earlier. The term may be extended by 44 days at a time, up to 03 years for Sr. Residents and 01 year for Jr. Residents.
