About IPRCL

Indian Port Rail & Ropeway Corporation Limited is a Joint Venture Company between 11 Major Ports under the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways, which hold 90% of the equity, and Rail Vikas Nigam Limited under the Ministry of Railways, which holds 10%. It was set up to provide efficient rail evacuation systems to Major Ports and to raise their capacity and throughput. It was registered on 10th July 2015 as a Public Limited Company under the Companies Act, 2013. Its Corporate Office is in Mazgaon, Mumbai, and its Registered Office is in Lodhi Road, New Delhi.

Selection involves a Personal Interview. Suitable candidates will be shortlisted on their eligibility/experience in the relevant field and may be called for interview. Interview intimation will be sent through e-mail/IPRCL website; candidates should keep checking the website rather than wait for e-mail.
