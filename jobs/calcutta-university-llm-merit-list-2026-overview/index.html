Calcutta University LL.M Merit List 2026 - Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 11/09/2026

The Calcutta University has released the Provisional Merit List (Part-B) for the 2-Year LL.M. programme under Group No. 502, for the Unreserved (UR) category, for the 2026-27 academic session. This list ranks candidates who applied under the Part-B route (based on the LL.M. entrance test) and is published on the official Calcutta University LL.M. admission portal.

How to Check Your Name in the Calcutta LL.M. Part-B Merit List (Group 502, UR)

  • Open the official PDF at the link provided above.
  • Use your PDF viewer's search/find function (Ctrl+F or Cmd+F) and type your full name or your Hall Ticket Number.
  • Cross-check your Form Number, Hall Ticket Number, HS percentage, entrance test marks, and Final Score against your own records.
  • If any of your requested corrections have not been reflected, or if your name is missing entirely, email [email protected] immediately, as instructed in the notification.

Important Instructions

  • Candidates must verify that all corrections they had requested on the draft merit list have actually been made in this provisional list.
  • Any omission of a name or an uncorrected error must be reported by email immediately.
  • If any information submitted by a candidate is later found to mismatch original documents - even after admission, at the stage of physical document verification - the admission will be treated as cancelled.
  • Meeting the eligibility criteria does not guarantee admission to the programme.
  • Candidates are responsible for satisfying themselves about their own eligibility; overlooking any information will not be accepted as grounds for a claim later.
  • If a candidate is inadvertently given provisional admission despite not meeting minimum eligibility, they cannot later claim a right to that admission - it will be cancelled.
