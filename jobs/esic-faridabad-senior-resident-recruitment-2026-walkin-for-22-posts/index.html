Pay and Terms

The tenure of the contract will be for 01 (One) year, which may be extendable up to 03 years, subject to satisfactory performance, willingness and conduct of the candidate and the requirements of the institution. The contract will be terminated on the appointment/joining of a bonded Senior Resident on the same post. The competent authority reserves the right to terminate the services at any time in the public interest, without prior notice or providing any reason. To resign, the Senior Resident must submit one-month advance notice or deposit an amount equivalent to one month's salary. Failure to do so will result in forfeiture of both the one-month salary and the security deposit and may also lead to legal proceedings. ESIC Faridabad Senior Resident Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection has two stages: document verification, then an interview, both on 27 November 2026. Document verification: candidates must first appear before the Document Verification Committee. Only those whose documents are successfully verified will be allowed to appear before the Interview Board. Interview: selection will be made on the basis of acquired academic credentials and performance in the interview, conducted by the duly constituted selection board. The interview opportunity is provisional, subject to detailed scrutiny of the eligibility criteria. The result will be published only in the Recruitments section of www.esic.gov.in , and the Offer of Appointment will be sent through e-mail only. Selected candidates must join as per the instructions in the offer of appointment.

If any applicable document is not verified by the Document Verification Committee, the candidate will not be allowed in the interview.
