About JIPMER

The Jawaharlal Institute of Postgraduate Medical Education and Research (JIPMER) is a medical education and research institute in Puducherry. These posts are part of a research project run by an outside funder, ICMR. The project is the PReMI Study (Preventing Premature Myocardial Infarction), a community-digital intervention to reduce cardiometabolic risk among young high-risk adults in South India. The Project Investigator is Dr Venkatachalam J of the Department of Preventive and Social Medicine.

Vacancy Details

The notice lists 7 posts in total, across three post types. All of them are for the same project, with the same funding agency, Project Investigator and likely duration. Who Can Apply

The notice gives each post its own essential and desirable requirements, listed below. All three posts have the same age rule: a maximum of 40 years as on the last date of receipt of applications (6 November 2026), with relaxation as per GoI guidelines. Two rules apply to every post. The qualification and experience must be in a relevant discipline/field and from a reputed institution/organization. The experience must have been gained after getting the essential qualification. Other essential requirements: Excellent communication skills in Tamil & English. Two-wheeler with DL (Driving License). Desirable: Experience in community-based public health research.
