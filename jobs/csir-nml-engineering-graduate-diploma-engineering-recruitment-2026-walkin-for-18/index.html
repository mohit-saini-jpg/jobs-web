About CSIR-NML

CSIR-National Metallurgical Laboratory is a laboratory of the Council of Scientific & Industrial Research located at Burmamines, Jamshedpur. The notice describes it as one of the global leaders in metallurgy and materials research, with research publications in peer reviewed journals and intellectual property in metallurgical and materials science and technology. The Director, CSIR-NML is engaging these apprentices under the scheme provided by the Apprenticeship Act, 1961 and the Apprenticeship Rules, 1992 of the Government of India.

What Happens on the Day

Reporting starts at 09:30 AM on 16 December 2026, and entry closes at 11:00 AM. Candidates will be selected for apprenticeship training based on their performance in the walk-in interview. The engagement letter will be sent to selected candidates by email; they must submit acceptance within 03 days and join within 07 days of receiving it, failing which the selection will be treated as cancelled. Original documents of selected candidates will be verified at the time of reporting, and the original College Leaving Certificate is submitted at joining and retained during the apprenticeship. No TA/DA will be paid for attending the walk-in interview.

CSIR-NML Engineering Graduate &amp; Diploma Engineering : Important Instructions

The training period is 12 months (one year) for all branches, at CSIR-NML, Jamshedpur. The engagement is purely temporary for one year and not for regular employment in CSIR-NML; there is no obligation to offer employment after the apprenticeship, and no claim to employment arises from it. No accommodation will be provided; selected candidates must arrange their own stay. Candidates should be medically fit as per the standard required for the concerned discipline, failing which candidature is liable to be cancelled. Candidature is provisional; false information or suppression of facts will cancel candidature, and engagement is liable to be terminated without notice if detected later. CSIR-NML Engineering Graduate & Diploma Engineering : Important Instructions

The training period is 12 months (one year) for all branches, at CSIR-NML, Jamshedpur. Candidates should be medically fit as per the standard required for the concerned discipline, failing which candidature is liable to be cancelled. CSIR-NML Engineering Graduate & Diploma Engineering : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1When and where is the CSIR-NML apprentice walk-in interview?
A
The walk-in interview is on 16 December 2026 (Wednesday) from 09:30 AM at CSIR-National Metallurgical Laboratory, Burmamines, Jamshedpur (Jharkhand) – 831007, with no entry after 11:00 AM.
Q2How many apprentices will CSIR-NML engage?
A
CSIR-NML will engage 18 apprentices: 7 Engineering Graduate (B.E/ B. Tech.) and 11 Diploma Engineering apprentices; the number of seats is tentative.
Q3What qualification is required?
A
Graduate apprentices need a regular full-time Degree in Engineering/Technology and Technician apprentices a three-year regular full-time Diploma in Engineering/Technology in the relevant area, passed during 2022 to 2026.
Q4What is the age limit for CSIR-NML apprentices?
A
The age limit is minimum 21 and maximum 26 years for Graduate apprentices and minimum 18 and maximum 24 years for Technician (Diploma) apprentices, as on the date of interview.
Q5What is the stipend?
A
The stipend is Rs.12,300/- per month for Graduate apprentices and Rs.10,900/- per month for Diploma apprentices, half paid by CSIR-NML and half by the Government through DBT; no other allowance is admissible.
Q6Who gets age relaxation?
A
SC/ST candidates get 5 years and OBC candidates 3 years for the posts reserved for them; PwBD candidates get up to 10 years (up to 15 years for SC/ST and up to 13 years for OBC Non-Creamy Layer).
Q7Which documents must be brought to the walk-in?
A
Originals with one set of self-attested photocopies of the 10th certificate, degree or diploma certificate and marks sheet, original College Leaving Certificate, caste and PwBD certificates if applicable, two passport size colour photographs and a Government photo ID.
Q8How will candidates be selected?
A
Candidates will be selected based on their performance in the walk-in interview, and the engagement letter will be sent by email.
Q9Is NATS registration compulsory?
A
Yes, each candidate must have a verified NATS ID and APAAR ID, and only candidates registered on the NATS/NAPS portal will be considered for the walk-in interview.
Q10Is this a permanent job at CSIR-NML?
A
No, the engagement is purely temporary for one year and not for regular employment, and CSIR-NML has no obligation to offer employment after the apprenticeship.

A
