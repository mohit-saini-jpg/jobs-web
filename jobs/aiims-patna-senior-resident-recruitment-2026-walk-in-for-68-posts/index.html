AIIMS Patna Senior Resident 2026 Salary Structure

Selected Senior Residents will be paid as per the 7th Central Pay Commission (CPC), Level-11 of the pay matrix (pre-revised PB-3), with an entry pay of Rs. 67,700 per month, plus Non-Practicing Allowance (NPA) and other allowances as admissible under Government of India rules.

AIIMS Patna Senior Resident Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Verification of eligibility and original documents. Selection based on performance in the walk-in interview, subject to fulfilment of prescribed eligibility criteria, document verification and applicable reservation rules. In case two or more candidates secure equal marks, the candidate older in age (earlier date of birth) will be placed higher in the merit list. Performance in the interview and/or any other selection process, as decided by the Competent Authority. The list of selected candidates will be uploaded on the official AIIMS Patna website. The decision of the Competent Authority/Selection Committee is final and binding.
