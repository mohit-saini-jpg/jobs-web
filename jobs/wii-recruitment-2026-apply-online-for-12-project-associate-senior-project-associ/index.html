About WII

The Wildlife Institute of India is an autonomous institution under the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC), Government of India. It is based in Chandrabani, Dehradun, Uttarakhand. It runs training programmes and academic courses and gives advice on wildlife research and management. This recruitment is run by its Recruitment and Placement Cell.

Projects, Duration and Duties

The full title is "Study the Impacts of Morand-Ganjal Irrigation Project on Wildlife and Preparation of Wildlife Mitigation Plan". All three posts on this project last six months. Senior Project Associate: helps manage the team, plans and runs field research and surveys, and does data analysis and report writing. Project Associate II (RS & GIS): does data analysis and report writing. Project Associate I: supports field teams and does field research, surveys, data analysis and report writing. This project is a conservation plan for the critically endangered White Bellied Heron (Ardea insignis) and its habitats in the Lohit River and Noa-Dehing River Basins. The Project Associate I post lasts one year. The work covers field teams, field research, surveys, data analysis and report writing. This project prepares a site-specific wildlife management plan for the Sijimali Bauxite Mines in Rayag

WII Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

WII shortlists candidates for an online interview. Shortlisting looks at the essential qualification, the age limit, your percentage of marks in the Bachelor's or Master's degree, how relevant your Master's specialisation is, your research experience, and your publications (journal papers, books, and papers presented at workshops, seminars, symposiums and conferences). The top ten (10) shortlisted candidates for each project or position are called for the interview. A duly constituted Selection Committee runs the process. Meeting the minimum qualification and experience does not by itself get you an interview call. The screening marks are listed below. A candidate who is not found suitable for the post applied for may be considered for a lower-grade position if the Interview Committee recommends it. Service Conditions

The positions are purely temporary, for the project period given for each post, and will be abolished when the project ends. The Institute's rules and regulations govern the tenure and the code of conduct of selected candidates. Selected candidates must join within the time given in the offer letter and produce all original certificates at joining. Any candidate found to have concealed or misrepresented facts, or to have canvassed, will be rejected or terminated. Canvassing in any form can lead to rejection. The Director, WII, may reject any application without giving a reason.

WII Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How much does a Project Associate I earn at WII?
A
Rs. 37,000 + HRA a month if you have qualified NET or GATE, and Rs. 30,000 + HRA if you have not.
Q2Which post pays the most?
A
The Principal Project Associate on the Yettinahole project in Karnataka, at Rs. 67,000/- + HRA a month.
Q3Can a graduate with no research background apply?
A
Yes, for the Administrative Assistant post on the Sijimali Bauxite Mines project. It needs a graduate degree in any discipline, has an age limit of 50 years and pays Rs. 24,000 + HRA.
Q4Can I apply with a 4-year BSc?
A
Yes. The Project Associate I posts on Projects 02, 04 and 05 (Vegetation and Entomology) accept a Four Years Bachelor's Degree as per the National Education Policy in the listed subjects.
Q5How many posts can I apply for?
A
Up to two. Each needs its own online form, its own documents and its own fee.
Q6What do reserved-category candidates pay?
A
SC, ST, OBC, EWS and PC candidates do not pay the Rs. 500/- application fee, but they do pay a Rs. 100/- processing fee.
Q7How many candidates will be interviewed?
A
The top ten shortlisted candidates for each project or position are called for an online interview.
Q8Are these permanent jobs?
A
No. The posts are purely temporary for the project period and end with the project. The Saranda posts are one year, likely to be extended based on funding.
Q9What age relaxation is given?
A
Up to 5 years for SC and ST candidates and up to 3 years for OBC candidates, under DoPT OM dated 27.03.2012.
Q10I currently work on a WII project. What extra document do I need?
A
A No Objection Certificate from your Project Investigator, Supervisor or Nodal Officer, issued no more than one month before the last date of application.
