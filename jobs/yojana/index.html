Yeh yojana un logon ke liye hai jo apne purane ya kharab ghar ko renovate karna chahte hain. Zila Kalyan Karyalay ke through financial assistance milti hai, bas kuch simple documents ki zaroorat hoti hai.

Scheme ka maksad low‑income families ko safe aur hygienic rehne ki suvidha dena hai. Disbursement direct bank transfer hoti hai, isliye transparency aur speed dono barqarar rehti hai.

Jo bhi citizen apne ghar ki structural ya sanitary condition improve karna chahta hai, aur income criteria meet karta hai, woh eligible hai.

डॉ बीआर अम्बेडकर अवस नविनिकरण Yojana (जिला कल्याण कार्यालय) Application Process

1. Apna latest income certificate aur property documents ready rakhein. 2. Zila Kalyan Karyalay ki official website se application form download karen. 3. Required photographs aur bank passbook copy attach karna na bhoolen.

डॉ बीआर अम्बेडकर अवस नविनिकरण Yojana (जिला कल्याण कार्यालय) Benefits Details

Scheme ke tahat ₹1 lakh tak ka renovation grant mil sakta hai, jo plaster, paint, sanitary fittings aur basic structural repairs cover karta hai. Yeh amount directly beneficiary ke bank account me transfer hota hai.

How डॉ बीआर अम्बेडकर अवस नविनिकरण Yojana (जिला कल्याण कार्यालय) Works

Application submit karne ke baad, local inspection team property ki survey karti hai. Approval milte hi, funds ko 2‑3 weeks ke andar beneficiary ke bank account me release kar diya jata hai.

डॉ बीआर अम्बेडकर अवस नविनिकरण Yojana (जिला कल्याण कार्यालय) Application Tips

Q1Yojana ke liye income limit kya hai?
A
Generally, annual household income ₹2 lakh se ₹5 lakh ke beech honi chahiye, lekin exact limit state guidelines par depend karti hai.
Q2Funds kitne samay me milte hain?
A
Approval ke baad, usually 15‑20 din ke andar bank transfer ke through beneficiary ko mil jate hain.
Q3Kya existing loan holders bhi apply kar sakte hain?
A
Haan, lekin agar existing home loan ka outstanding amount grant amount se zyada ho, to scheme ka benefit limited ho sakta hai.
