डॉ बीआर अम्बेडकर अवस नविनिकरण Yojana (जिला कल्याण कार्यालय) Application Process

1. Apna latest property tax receipt aur ownership document ready rakhein. 2. Income certificate aur Aadhar card ka scanned copy zaroor attach karein. 3. Application form ko online portal par fill karte waqt sabhi fields accurately bharain.

डॉ बीआर अम्बेडकर अवस नविनिकरण Yojana (जिला कल्याण कार्यालय) Benefits Details

Scheme ke tahat har eligible household ko ₹1.5 lakh tak ka renovation grant mil sakta hai, jo ki plaster, paint, roof repair jaise kaam ke liye kaafi hota hai. Ye amount directly beneficiary ke bank account mein transfer hota hai.

Application online submit hoti hai, phir Zila Kalyan Office site inspection karta hai. Inspection ke baad approval milne par fund disbursement hota hai, jo 30 din ke andar beneficiary ke account mein credit ho jata hai.

डॉ बीआर अम्बेडकर अवस नविनिकरण Yojana (जिला कल्याण कार्यालय) FAQs

Q1Is scheme ke liye income limit hai?
A
Haan, annual household income ₹5 lakh se kam honi chahiye, lekin exact limit state‑wise thodi vary kar sakti hai.
Q2Fund kitne din mein milta hai?
A
Approval ke baad 30 din ke andar bank transfer ke through fund beneficiary ke account mein aata hai.
Q3Kya existing loan ke saath bhi apply kar sakte hain?
A
Agar existing home loan ka outstanding amount renovation cost se zyada nahi hai, to aap apply kar sakte hain, lekin loan details disclose karna zaroori hai.
