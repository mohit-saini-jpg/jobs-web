DU PG Admission 2026 - Quick Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 22/08/2026

The University of Delhi (DU) has declared the Minimum Allocation Scores for DU PG Admission 2026 Spot Round 2 through the Common Seat Allocation System for Postgraduate programmes (CSAS-PG 2026-27). Candidates who applied for the spot round can check their programme-wise minimum allocation score on the official admission portal, admission.uod.ac.in. This article explains the important dates, how to check your score, the seat acceptance process, and what to do next.

DU PG Spot Round 2 Minimum Allocation Score: How to Check

The programme-wise, college-wise minimum allocation scores for Spot Round 2 are published as an official PDF on the admission portal. Exact numeric cutoffs vary by programme, college/department, and category, so they are not reproduced here - always refer to the live official document to avoid relying on outdated or third-party figures. Use the steps and link table below to reach the correct source.

Steps to Check Your Allocation Score/Result

  • Visit admission.uod.ac.in or pgadmission.uod.ac.in.
  • Log in using your CUET (PG) 2026 application number and password/date of birth as created during CSAS-PG registration.
  • Open the Spot Round 2 section on your dashboard to view your individual allocation status.
  • Separately, open the official "Minimum Allocation Score - Spot Round 2" notice/PDF to compare your CUET PG score against the programme's cutoff.
  • Download and save the allocation letter (if allotted) for verification and fee payment.

Eligibility for DU PG Spot Round 2

  • The candidate must have registered for CSAS-PG 2026 and appeared in CUET (PG) 2026 conducted by NTA.
  • The candidate must satisfy the programme-specific eligibility criteria published in the PG Bulletin of Information (BoI-PG) 2026.
  • Candidates who were allotted a seat in Spot Round 1 but did not accept it or did not pay the admission fee are not eligible to participate in Spot Round 2 for that same programme.
  • Candidates who did not receive any allocation in earlier rounds (Round 1, Round 2, Round 3, Spot Round 1) are the primary eligible group for Spot Round 2.

How to Accept Your Seat (Spot Round 2)

  • Log in to your CSAS-PG dashboard at pgadmission.uod.ac.in.
  • Check the allotted programme, college/department, and category under the Spot Round 2 section.
  • Click "Accept Seat" within the seat acceptance window (by August 20, 2026, before the dashboard freeze).
  • Upload the required documents for verification by the allotted college.
  • Pay the admission fee online once the payment link is activated on your dashboard, before the last date specified in your allotment letter.
  • Download and retain the final admission confirmation/receipt for future reference.

Important Instructions

  • There is no option to upgrade or withdraw a seat accepted during the Spot Admission rounds.
  • Minimum allocation scores can vary due to reconciliations, withdrawals, cancellations, or grievance redressal - always refer to the latest official PDF rather than a cached or third-party copy.
  • The final decision on all admissions rests solely with the University of Delhi and is subject to document verification and compliance.
  • DU does not entertain admission-related requests sent via post, fax, WhatsApp, email, in person, or phone call - all actions must be completed through the CSAS-PG portal.
  • Keep checking your dashboard, registered email, and the official admission website regularly, as DU is not responsible for a candidate's lack of awareness of schedule changes.
