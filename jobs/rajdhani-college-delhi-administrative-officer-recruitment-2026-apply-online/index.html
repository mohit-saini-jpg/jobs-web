Rajdhani College Delhi Administrative Officer Recruitment : Job Overview

Rajdhani College, Raja Garden, New Delhi (University of Delhi) has one permanent Administrative Officer post. The pay is Pay Level 10 (as per the 7th Pay Commission). The single post is reserved for a PwBD candidate with Locomotor Disability (LD). You need a Master's Degree with at least 55% marks or an equivalent grade, and the upper age limit is 40 years. You apply online at dunt.uod.ac.in under Advertisement No. RC/RG/152/Non-Teaching/2026/01, dated 17 October 2026. The last date is 21 days after the advertisement is published in the Employment News.

About Rajdhani College

Rajdhani College is a college of the University of Delhi, located at Raja Garden, New Delhi – 110015. The college is filling this Administrative Officer post by direct recruitment, using the University of Delhi's Scheme of Examination for Assistant Registrar, Assistant Controller of Examination and Administrative Officer (In Colleges).

Who Can Apply

The one Administrative Officer post is reserved for Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD), Locomotor Disability (LD). No vacancy is shown under UR, OBC, SC, ST or EWS. If you want the PwBD reservation, you must upload a document that supports your claim, as per Govt. of India lists/rules/norms. The certificate must be in the format prescribed by the Union Government and must be digitally verifiable. Your disability must be at least 40 per cent. You must upload a digitally verifiable, valid Disability Certificate as proof with your application. PwBD applicants must state in the application their main category (UR/SC/ST/OBC), which is used for computing the reservation. The notice says the post will be filled by direct recruitment through an All India Open Competition, with a written test and an interview. The upper age limit is 40 years. Selection and Interview

Selection follows the University of Delhi's two-stage scheme. Stage 1 is a preliminary MCQ test for shortlisting. Stage 2 is a descriptive Examination (Main) followed by an Interview/Personality Test. The final merit list is based only on Stage 2. Meeting the minimum eligibility conditions does not by itself mean you will be called for the written test. Applications will be screened under the University's guidelines to limit the number of candidates. All questions are compulsory. Each question carries 04 marks, and each wrong answer loses 01 mark. The qualifying cut-off is 50% marks of the average score of the top 05% of candidates. For deciding the category cut-off, SC and ST candidates get a relaxation of 05% marks and PwBD candidates get 10%. For one vacant post, 30 candidates are shortlisted for the Examination (Main).
