SCCL Apprentice Recruitment 2026-27 Eligibility Criteria

Engineering Graduates, Non-Engineering Graduates, and Diploma holders who have passed their qualifying examination during the last five years – i.e., pass-out years 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, or 2026 only – are eligible to apply. Candidates who passed out in any other year will not be considered and will be rejected during scrutiny. Candidates must select the branch matching their qualification, as listed in the Apprenticeship Slots section above (Mining, EEE, ECE, CSE, IT, Mechanical, Civil for Engineering Graduates; BA, B.Sc, B.Com, BBA, BCA, B.Pharmacy for Non-Engineering Graduates; Mining, EEE, Mechanical, Civil for Diploma Holders). Candidates must not have already undergone, or be currently registered for, training under the Apprentices Act, 1961, in SCCL or any other organisation; an undertaking to this effect must be submitted with the application. SCCL Apprentice Recruitment 2026-27 Stipend

The stipend is shared equally: 50% by SCCL and 50% by BOAT (Southern Region)/NATS 2.0, Ministry of Education, Government of India, credited directly into the apprentice's Aadhaar-enabled bank account under the Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) system. Selected candidates must complete e-KYC and ensure their Union Bank of India (UBI) account is Aadhaar-seeded, as SCCL will not be responsible for non-payment of stipend if Aadhaar is not linked to the bank account. Apprentices are not eligible for any other financial or service benefit apart from this monthly stipend during the training period, as per the provisions of the Apprentices Act, 1961.

SCCL Apprentice Recruitment 2026-27 Selection Process

There is no written examination for this apprenticeship. Selection in SCCL will be based on inter-se seniority with reference to the year of passing, and merit within that year. Priority will be given to "SCCL/Retired Employee Children" and "PAF/PDF" candidates as per prevailing practice, and Local, Caste, and EWS reservations will be followed as per SCCL's extant policy, with vacant reserved slots diverted to the open category. The selection process for the first batch will begin only from the first week of September 2026, after scrutiny of applications at the MVTCs. The MVTC where a candidate submits the application will not be treated as the fixed posting area. Candidates must select their area posting preferences as Option-01, Option-02, Option-03, etc., in the application; final posting will be based on vacancies and the preferences chosen. SCCL Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Register on the NATS 2.0 web portal at www.nats.education.gov.in, filling in all required fields and uploading the required certificate copies, to obtain a Registration Number.
  • 2Enter all required information, and upload your recent photograph (JPEG format) along with all required original certificates (as listed in the Documents Required section above).
  • 3Take a printout of the duly signed portal-generated application form, along with the NATS 2.0 registration form, certificate copies, and the first page of your UBI bank account passbook.
  • 4Submit these hard copies at your nearest Area Group/Mines Vocational Training Centre (GVTC/MVTC – full address list given below), within the same window: 06 August 2026, 12:00 PM to 16 August 2026, 5:00 PM.
  • 5Candidates applying for apprenticeship in SCCL must have an account in Union Bank of India (UBI), in one of the SCCL salary disbursing branches (list given below), with Aadhaar linking, e-KYC, and Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) enabled, since 50% of the stipend is transferred through the DBT process. Candidates will be given time to submit the first page of their passbook before joining the apprenticeship.

SCCL Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is SCCL Apprentice Recruitment 2026-27?
A
It is a circular issued by The Singareni Collieries Company Limited (SCCL) HRD Department, inviting applications for 300 Graduate (Engineering & Non-Engineering) and Diploma Apprenticeship slots for the year 2026-2027, under the Apprentices Act, 1961.
Q2How many apprenticeship slots are available at SCCL?
A
A total of 300 slots are available: 150 for Engineering Graduates, 100 for Non-Engineering Graduates, and 50 for Diploma Holders, across the branches listed in the Apprenticeship Slots section.
Q3What is the last date to apply for SCCL Apprentice Recruitment 2026-27?
A
The SCCL portal is open from 06 August 2026, 12:00 PM to 16 August 2026, 5:00 PM. Hard copies must also be submitted at the Area MVTC/GVTC within the same window.
Q4Who is eligible for SCCL Apprentice Recruitment 2026-27?
A
Engineering Graduates, Non-Engineering Graduates, and Diploma holders who passed their qualifying examination in the last five years – pass-out years 2022 to 2026 only.
Q5What is the age limit for SCCL Apprentice Recruitment 2026-27?
A
The minimum age is 18 years and the maximum is 28 years as on 31 July 2026, with a 5-year age relaxation (up to 33 years) for SC, ST, and BC candidates.
Q6Is there an application fee for SCCL Apprentice Recruitment 2026-27?
A
No. There is no registration or application fee for applying on the SCCL web portal, or for submitting the application at the Area MVTC counter.
Q7How can I apply for SCCL Apprentice Recruitment 2026-27?
A
Candidates must first register on the NATS 2.0 portal (www.nats.education.gov.in), then apply through the official SCCL portal (www.scclmines.com/apprenticeship), and finally submit hard copies of the application and required certificates at their nearest Area MVTC/GVTC.
Q8What is the selection process for SCCL Apprentice Recruitment 2026-27?
A
There is no written exam. Selection is based on inter-se seniority by year of passing and merit within that year, with priority for SCCL/Retired Employee Children and PAF/PDF candidates, and reservation followed as per SCCL policy.
Q9What is the stipend for SCCL apprentices?
A
Rs. 12,300/- per month for Engineering and Non-Engineering Graduates, and Rs. 10,900/- per month for Diploma Holders, shared 50:50 between SCCL and BOAT (Southern Region)/NATS 2.0, MoE, GoI, and credited via DBT.
Q10Will I get a regular job at SCCL after completing the apprenticeship?
A
No. The offer of Apprenticeship does not confer any right of employment in SCCL, and there is no quota or preference for SCCL apprentices in selection for regular jobs.
