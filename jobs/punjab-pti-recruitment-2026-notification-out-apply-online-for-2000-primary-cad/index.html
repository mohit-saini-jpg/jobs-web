ERD Punjab PTI Eligibility Criteria

As per Appendix 'B' of the Punjab State Elementary Education (Physical Training Instructor) Service Rules, 2023 (as amended in 2025), candidates must have passed 10+2 along with at least a two-year diploma or certificate in Physical Education , referred to as Diploma in Physical Education or Certificate in Physical Education (D.P.Ed/C.P.Ed) or an equivalent course under any other nomenclature. ERD Punjab PTI Selection Process

Selection for the PTI (Primary Cadre) posts will be based on a combined merit prepared from a written test and sports achievement weightage, as per the following distribution out of a total of 100 marks. The written test will be a single objective multiple-choice paper of 70 marks conducted at the state level. Candidates must also clear the Physical Eligibility Test (PET), which is qualifying in nature and does not carry marks in the merit calculation. Sports achievement weightage does not apply to candidates recruited under the sports quota; for these candidates, the Punjab Sportsmen Recruitment Rules, 1988, remain applicable. In case of multiple gradation certificates, a candidate can claim weightage for only one gradation certificate issued as per the revised Gradation Policy, 2023, of the Department of Sports and Youth Affairs, Punjab.
