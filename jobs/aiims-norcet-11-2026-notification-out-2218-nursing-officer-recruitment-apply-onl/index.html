AIIMS NORCET 11 Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection is made through a two-stage Computer-Based Test (CBT): Stage I – NORCET Preliminary: A 90-minute qualifying exam with 100 MCQs (100 marks) - 20 questions on General Knowledge & Aptitude and 80 on Nursing subjects. There is negative marking of 1/3 mark per wrong answer. This stage only qualifies candidates for Stage II and is not used for final merit. Stage II – NORCET Mains: A 180-minute exam with 160 case-scenario-based MCQs (160 marks) covering the nursing syllabus, testing clinical skill competency. Negative marking of 1/3 mark per wrong answer applies. Final merit is based solely on Stage II performance. Qualifying marks for Stage I are 50 percentile (UR/EWS), 45 percentile (OBC), and 40 percentile (SC/ST), with a further 5 percentile relaxation for PWBD candidates. Qualifying marks for Stage II are 50% (UR/EWS), 45% (OBC), and 40% (SC/ST), with a similar 5% PWBD relaxation
