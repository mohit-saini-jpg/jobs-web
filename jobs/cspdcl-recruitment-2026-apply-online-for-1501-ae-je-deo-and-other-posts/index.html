CSPDCL Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is the total number of vacancies announced in CSPTCL Recruitment 2026?
A
A total of 1501 vacancies have been announced for various posts including Assistant Engineer, Junior Engineer, Data Entry Operator, and other positions across departments in Chhattisgarh State Power Transmission Company Ltd.
Q2When does the online application process start and end?
A
The online application process begins on 6 November 2026 and concludes on 26 November 2026. Candidates must complete their application and fee payment within this specified timeframe.
Q3What is the educational qualification required for CSPTCL posts?
A
The qualification varies depending on the post, ranging from 10th pass, ITI, Diploma, Bachelor's Degree in Engineering, Master's degree, MBBS, to CA/CMA for different Class-II, III, and IV positions.
Q4What is the age limit to apply for CSPTCL Recruitment 2026?
A
The age limit ranges from a minimum of 18 or 21 years up to a maximum of 45 years depending on the post category and domicile, calculated as of 1 January 2026.
Q5Are candidates from other states eligible for Class-III and IV posts?
A
No. For Class-III and IV posts, only candidates possessing a domicile of Chhattisgarh are eligible to apply as per the specified recruitment conditions.
Q6What is the application fee for UR and OBC candidates?
A
The application fee is Rs. 800/- or Rs. 400/- for UR candidates and Rs. 700/- or Rs. 300/- for OBC candidates, depending on the specific post applied for.
Q7How will candidates be selected for CSPTCL jobs?
A
The selection process comprises an Online Computer Based Test (CBT), followed by a Physical/Skill Test, Interview where applicable, and final Document Verification.
Q8What is the exam pattern for the Online CBT?
A
The Online CBT consists of 100 Multiple Choice Questions carrying 100 marks with a duration of 2 hours, divided into Professional Subject (80 questions) and General Knowledge, English, Hindi, Reasoning & Quantitative Abilities (20 questions).
Q9Is there any negative marking in the Online Computer Based Test?
A
Yes, 1 mark is awarded for every correct answer, while 0.25 marks are deducted for every incorrect answer attempted in the Online CBT.
Q10Where should candidates visit to submit their online application?
A
Candidates can visit the official Power Companies website at www.cspc.co.in to fill out the online application form and complete the payment process securely.
