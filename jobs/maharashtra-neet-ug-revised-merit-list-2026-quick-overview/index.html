What Is the Maharashtra NEET UG Revised Merit List?

The revised merit list is an updated version of the provisional merit list. After the provisional list is published, registered candidates get a window to raise grievances if they spot errors in their name, category, gender, marks, NEET details, or reservation status. Once the CET Cell reviews these grievances and makes approved corrections, it publishes the revised (and eventually the final) merit list. This revised rank is what will actually be used going forward in the counselling process, so candidates should check it carefully rather than relying on their provisional rank.

Who Is Covered in This Merit List?

The Maharashtra NEET UG merit list includes only those candidates who successfully completed registration for Maharashtra's state counselling process. It is used for admission to:

  • 85% state quota seats in government and municipal medical/dental colleges
  • Government-aided medical/dental colleges
  • All-quota seats in private and minority medical/dental colleges in Maharashtra

Ranking in this list is based primarily on the candidate's NEET UG 2026 score/ All India Rank, along with category and reservation status as declared during registration.

Details Mentioned in the Merit List

The merit list PDF typically displays the following details for each registered candidate:

  • Candidate's name
  • Merit number / state rank
  • NEET UG All India Rank (AIR) and roll number
  • CET Cell application/form number
  • Category and category-wise rank
  • Gender
  • Reservation status (including NRI and special reservation categories, where applicable)
  • Marks obtained in NEET UG 2026

How to Download the Maharashtra NEET UG Revised Merit List 2026

  • Visit the official Maharashtra CET Cell medical admissions counselling website.
  • Go to the NEET UG 2026 counselling section on the homepage.
  • Click on the link for the "Revised Merit List" (Group A - MBBS/BDS).
  • Depending on the format provided, either log in with your Application ID and password, or open the published PDF directly.
  • Locate your name, application number, or NEET roll number in the list.
  • Note your revised merit number/rank and download or save the list for reference.

How to Correct Errors or Raise a Grievance

If a candidate notices a genuine error in their details on the merit list, the CET Cell allows corrections through a structured grievance process:

  • Grievances must be submitted only through the candidate login on the official portal - email requests are not accepted.
  • A candidate can submit only one grievance, but it can include multiple corrections in a single submission.
  • Once submitted, a grievance cannot be edited or deleted, so details should be checked carefully before submitting.
  • Supporting documents must be uploaded to justify each requested correction; no changes are made without valid proof.

Since the revised list follows the resolution of grievances from the provisional stage, check whether your reported corrections have actually been reflected before proceeding to choice filling.

What Happens After the Revised Merit List?

Based on the currently available schedule, the process after the revised merit list moves in this order:

  • CET Cell finalises and publishes the final merit list and seat matrix for Group A courses.
  • Eligible candidates proceed to choice filling, where they select and lock their preferred colleges and courses in order of preference.
  • The CET Cell processes Round 1 seat allotment based on merit rank, category, and choices filled.
  • Allotted candidates report to their allotted college with original documents within the notified reporting period.

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Check your revised rank, category, and personal details carefully - do not assume the provisional list details automatically carried over correctly.
  • Keep your login credentials (Application ID and password) ready and secure, as they are needed at every stage of counselling.
  • Complete choice filling within the notified window; missing the deadline can affect your seat allotment chances.
  • Keep scanned copies of all original documents ready in advance for the reporting stage.
  • Rely only on the official CET Cell portal for dates and updates, since this schedule has been revised multiple times already.
