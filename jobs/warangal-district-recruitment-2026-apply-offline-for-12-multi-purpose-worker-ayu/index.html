About Warangal District

This recruitment is run by the Office of the District Collector and Magistrate, Warangal District, under the Government of Telangana. The Director, Department of AYUSH, T.G., Secunderabad, asked the Collector & District Magistrate to fill 12 posts in 6 categories sanctioned in Warangal District. The posts are meant only for the AYUSH Department under the Public Health Programme of the National AYUSH Mission. The posts follow Government permission under G.O.Rt.No.1508, Fin (HRM.VII) Dept., Dt:21/08/2026, which covers 333 services statewide (38 on contract and 295 on outsourcing basis). The people selected will work under the administrative control of the Director, Department of AYUSH, T.G., Secunderabad.

The Warangal District Recruitment 2026 notification has 12 posts. The table below shows the roster point for each post, as printed. For both the AYUSH Medical Officer and the Multi Purpose Worker (MPW), the 4 posts are 2 OC, 1 SC (Group II) and 1 BC-A. The other four posts have 1 OC post each. The rules of reservation for community, disability and ex-servicemen will be strictly followed as per the Telangana State and Subordinate Service Rules, 1996, read with the relevant Specific Rules. Reservation for local candidates applies as per the Presidential Order. Warangal District Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

The District Selection Committee of the District Health Society makes the selection. Candidates are marked out of 100 marks, a merit list is prepared, and a selection list is drawn from the merit list following the rule of reservation and the Presidential Order. For all posts, 10 marks are given for age. The age weightage counts from 18 years of age: 0.5 mark is given for each completed year, up to a maximum of 10 marks. Age is reckoned as on 1.7.2026. The notice gives these examples: A candidate who completes 20 years as on 1.7.2026 gets 1.0 mark. A candidate who completes 30 years as on 1.7.2026 gets 6.0 marks. A candidate who completes 40 years as on 1.7.2026 gets 10.0 marks. The merit list will be prepared from the marks under the above criteria and displayed on the website for transparency.   • 1Applications for the Warangal District Recruitment 2026 are offline only. Each post has its own application form, attached to the notification. The form is addressed to The District Collector & Magistrate / Chairman, District Selection Committee, Warangal District.

  • 3Fill in the form. It asks for your name and your father's, mother's or spouse's name as per the Aadhaar Card, date of birth, age as on 01/07/2026, local status/district, community and sub-caste, PwD/PH/VH status and educational details.
  • 4Paste 1 photograph on the application form.
  • 5Enclose copies of the certificates listed below. The notification asks for self-attested copies, and the application form says all certificates should be attested by a Gazetted Officer.
  • 6Enclose the acknowledgment card.
  • 7Submit the filled form in person or through Registered Post at: O/o District Medical & Health Officer, Warangal District.
  • 8Applications are received from 27 October 2026 to 31 October 2026, between 10:30 AM and 05:00 PM, on working days only.
  • 9Applications must reach the office by 31 October 2026. Applications received after that date will be summarily rejected. The District Selection Committee is not responsible for postal delays.

Warangal District Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in the Warangal District Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 12 posts: 1 AYUSH Doctor with PG Qualification, 4 AYUSH Medical Officer, 1 Dietician, 1 Psychosocial Counselor, 1 Yoga Professional and 4 Multi Purpose Worker (MPW).
Q2What is the last date to apply?
A
The last date is 31 October 2026. Applications are received from 27 October 2026 between 10:30 AM and 05:00 PM on working days, and those received after 31 October 2026 will be summarily rejected.
Q3What qualification is needed for the AYUSH Medical Officer post?
A
A Degree in Ayurveda or Unani or Homoeopathy or Naturopathy system, after a course of not less than 4½ years, with one year compulsory internship. The degree must come from a recognized university or institution.
Q4What is the qualification for the Multi Purpose Worker (MPW) post?
A
The candidate must have passed the VII Class examination and must be able to ride a Bicycle / Motorcycle.
Q5What is the age limit?
A
The age limit is 18 to 44 years as on 01/07/2026. The maximum age is relaxed by 5 years for SC, ST, BC and EWS candidates, 10 years for disabled persons, and 3 years plus the length of armed forces service for ex-servicemen.
Q6What is the salary for the Warangal AYUSH posts?
A
The consolidated monthly pay is Rs.60,000 for the AYUSH Doctor with PG Qualification and Rs.40,000 for the AYUSH Medical Officer. It is Rs.28,000 for the Yoga Professional, Rs.20,000 for the Dietician and Rs.15,000 for the MPW. The Psychosocial Counselor gets Rs.500 per session, with at least 200 visits a year (on call).
Q7How do I apply for the Warangal District Recruitment 2026?
A
Fill in the application form for your post and enclose the certificates and the acknowledgment card. Submit it in person or by Registered Post at the O/o District Medical & Health Officer, Warangal District, by 31 October 2026.
Q8How will candidates be selected?
A
Candidates are marked out of 100. For five posts, 90 marks come from the qualifying examination marks; for the MPW, 90 marks come from a written test. All posts get up to 10 marks for age, at 0.5 mark per completed year from 18 years.
Q9Is this a permanent job?
A
No. The posts are on an outsourcing basis up to 31.03.2027. After that, services will be as per need and also subject to satisfactory performance.
Q10How is the Psychosocial Counselor engaged?
A
A panel of eligible candidates may be empaneled for the Psychosocial Counselor post. Those on the panel are engaged on a call basis, as and when their services are needed, at Rs.500 per session.
