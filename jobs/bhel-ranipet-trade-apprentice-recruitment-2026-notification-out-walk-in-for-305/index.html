BHEL Ranipet Trade Apprentice Eligibility Criteria / Qualification

The qualification required for all trades under this recruitment is ITI (Industrial Training Institute) in the relevant trade. Candidates attending the walk-in interview must bring the following documents: VIII or X Standard Marksheets National Trade Certificate with Marksheets/Hall Tickets - for candidates whose Final Exams were in June 2026 and whose result is awaited, the Hall Ticket is acceptable in place of the certificate Aadhaar Card Community Certificate (OBC/SC/ST/EWS), if applicable Disability Certificate, in case of PwD candidates The notification does not specify a minimum percentage of marks or any other qualifying criteria beyond possession of the ITI qualification and the documents listed above.

BHEL Ranipet Trade Apprentice Selection Process

Selection is conducted through a Walk-in Interview at BHEL, Boiler Auxiliaries Plant, Ranipet. Interested and eligible candidates must report in person at the venue on any of the specified dates, along with all the original documents listed in the eligibility section, for document verification and interview. The notification does not provide further details of a separate written test or additional selection stages beyond the walk-in interview and document verification.

BHEL Ranipet Trade Apprentice Walk-in Interview Details

There is no online application process for this recruitment. Interested and eligible candidates should directly attend the walk-in interview as per the details below. Check that you hold the ITI qualification in one of the eligible trades (Fitter, Welder, Turner, MMV, Plumber, Electrician, Refrigerator & AC Mechanic, Carpenter, Machinist/OAMT, or Instrument Mechanic). Gather all required original documents: VIII or X Standard Marksheets, National Trade Certificate with Marksheets/Hall Tickets (Hall Ticket accepted only for June 2026 result-awaited candidates), Aadhaar Card, Community Certificate (if applicable), and Disability Certificate (if applicable, for PwD candidates). Report to the HRM Conference Hall, Admin. Building, BHEL, Ranipet – 632406, on any date between 18th and 21st August 2026, from 09:00 Hrs onwards. Carry your documents for verification at the venue.
