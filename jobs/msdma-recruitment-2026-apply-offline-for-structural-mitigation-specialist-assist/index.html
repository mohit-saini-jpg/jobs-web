About MSDMA

The Meghalaya State Disaster Management Authority is part of the Government of Meghalaya and works under the Revenue and Disaster Management Department. Its office is at Lower Lachaumiere, Shillong. Both posts are in its Mitigation Project Management Division (MPMD), which deals with structural mitigation projects funded under NDMF/SDMF.

MSDMA Recruitment 2026 Job Description

Provide technical expertise in the planning, design, and implementation of structural mitigation projects funded under NDMF/SDMF. Preparation of Project Pre-Feasibility Reports including cost estimates. Conducting technical assessments, including geotechnical and hazard-specific studies, and recommend safe, resilient design solutions. Design of appropriate mitigation measures. Prepare/review/vet DPRs, drawings, and technical specifications for compliance with BIS/NBC/NDMA standards. Support quality control and monitoring during project execution. Document best practices, case studies, and technical reports for resilient infrastructure. Provide technical inputs for capacity-building workshops on structural mitigation and resilient construction practices. Any other matters as required by SDMA.
