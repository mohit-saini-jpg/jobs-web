Jamnagar Mahanagarpalika Divyang Recruitment 2026 Notification Out

The recruitment is being carried out under the Special Recruitment Drive for Divyang candidates, in line with General Board Resolution No. 46 dated 25/01/2024 and the provisions of the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016. Only candidates with a benchmark disability of 40% or more, falling under the specified disability categories for each post, are eligible to apply under this notification. JMC Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Only candidates with a benchmark disability (40% or more) in the categories specified below for each post are eligible to apply. Candidates must also be an Indian citizen, or a subject of Nepal/Bhutan, or a Tibetan refugee/person of Indian origin from specified countries who has settled in India with the intention of permanent residence (subject to an eligibility certificate from the State Government where applicable). Candidates must have sufficient knowledge of Gujarati, Hindi, and English, with command over Gujarati.

JMC Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection will be made through a competitive written examination conducted by the JMC Staff Selection Committee Selected candidates will be appointed on a 3-year probationary basis During probation, candidates must pass the applicable departmental exams, computer proficiency exams, and pre-service training exams as per the recruitment rules for the respective post A provisional/waiting list is not the same as a final selection list
