TGPSC HMWSSB Manager Salary

Selected candidates appointed as Manager (Engineering) will receive a pay scale of Rs. 54,220 to Rs. 1,33,630 .

Local Reservation

The post is confined to Multi Zone II. Local reservation applies to 95% of the notified posts. Candidates belonging to other states may apply but are not entitled to Telangana reservation benefits.

Application and Examination Fee

All unemployed applicants are exempted from paying the examination fee of Rs. 120. Employees of the Central Government, State Government, PSUs, corporations and other government-sector organizations must pay the examination fee. The application processing fee is compulsory for all applicants according to their category. Fees must be paid online through Net Banking, debit card, credit card or UPI. Fees once paid will not be refunded or adjusted.

TGPSC HMWSSB Manager Selection Process

Candidates will be selected through an objective written examination conducted in CBRT or OMR mode. Selection will be based on the marks secured in the examination and the applicable reservation rules. Securing the minimum qualifying marks does not automatically provide a right to selection. Final selection will be based on merit, branch-wise vacancies and eligibility verification.
