ICFRE-RFRI Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The ICFRE-Rain Forest Research Institute holds a walk-in interview on 10 November 2026 from 9.30 am onwards at the institute in Jorhat (Deovan, Sotai, Jorhat-785010, Assam). Candidates must report between 9.30 am and 10.30 am. The institute will engage 4 Junior Project Fellows (JPF) at Rs. 24,000/- + HRA as admissible per month and 4 Project Assistants (PA) at Rs. 22,000/- per month (Fixed), on a purely contractual basis under a consultancy project.

About the Institute

The ICFRE-Rain Forest Research Institute is a unit of the Indian Council of Forestry Research and Education under the Ministry of Environment, Forest & Climate Change, Government of India, located at Deovan, Sotai, Jorhat in Assam. This is a government institute. The posts are for a consultancy project funded by the National Highways & Infrastructure Development Corporation Ltd. (NHIDCL).

Posts and Eligibility

Both posts are for the project "Enumeration and Economic Valuation of Standing Vegetation within the Silchar NHIDCL Project Area for Compensation against Land Acquisition". The project is described as "Initially for two months". Desirable for JPF: working experiences in forest and plant identification. No desirable qualification is printed for the Project Assistant post. For the full discipline-specific eligibility of each post, candidates should refer to the official notification.
