DTU Research Score Requirements 2026

Applicants must meet the minimum research score specified for their post and provide evidence supporting all claimed research achievements. The notification identifies the following activities for calculating academic and research scores. Research papers published in eligible indexed or approved journals. Books authored and eligible translation works. Innovative teaching pedagogy, curricula, MOOCs and electronic content. Research guidance and completed or ongoing research projects. Consultancy activities. Patents, policy documents, awards and fellowships. Invited lectures, seminars, conferences and eligible conference publications. The detailed score must be calculated according to Annexure-II and the criteria referenced from the UGC Notification dated 18 July 2018.

DTU Academic and Research Screening Score 2026

The university evaluates candidates using Academic Record and Experience and Research Performance scores for shortlisting. The academic record is initially assessed using a 50-point schedule and scaled down to 20 points. Experience and research performance are initially assessed using a separate 50-point schedule and scaled down to 40 points. Important: The detailed marks awarded for publications, patents, research projects and related achievements depend on authorship, publication category, project value and other criteria in the notification. Candidates must calculate the score using the prescribed table rather than claiming the maximum points automatically.
