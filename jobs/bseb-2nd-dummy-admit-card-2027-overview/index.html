BSEB 2nd Dummy Admit Card 2027 - Overview : Admit Card Overview

The Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB) has released the BSEB 2nd Dummy Admit Card 2027 for students registered for the Class 10 (Matric) and Class 12 (Intermediate) Annual Examinations 2027. The second dummy admit cards are available from 21 November to 26 November 2026.

BSEB 2nd Dummy Admit Card 2027 - Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 23/11/2026

Students can download their documents through the respective official Bihar Board examination portals and check the information submitted during registration. The last date to download the second dummy admit card and request corrections is 26 November 2026. Students must verify their names, parents' names, date of birth, subject details, photograph, signature and other relevant information. Any discrepancies must be reported to the school or college authorities within the correction period. The second dummy admit card is issued for verification and is not the final examination admit card.

BSEB Class 10 2nd Dummy Admit Card 2027

The Bihar Board Matric Second Dummy Admit Card 2027 is available for students registered for the Annual Secondary Examination 2027. The board's official notification dated 20 November 2026 confirms the download and correction window from 21 to 26 November 2026.

Class 10 Download Link: BSEB Matric 2nd Dummy Admit Card 2027

How to Download Bihar Board Matric 2nd Dummy Admit Card 2027?

  • Visit exam.biharboardonline.org.
  • Find the "CLICK HERE TO DOWNLOAD DUMMY ADMIT CARD (STUDENT LOGIN)" option.
  • Open the student login facility.
  • Enter your registration number.
  • Enter your date of birth.
  • Submit the details to retrieve the dummy admit card.
  • Check the personal and examination information.
  • Download or print the document for verification.

Students can also contact their school heads to obtain copies of their second dummy admit cards. The board has instructed school heads to download and distribute the documents to registered students.

BSEB Class 12 2nd Dummy Admit Card 2027

The Bihar Board Intermediate Second Dummy Admit Card 2027 has also been released for students registered for the 2027 Class 12 annual examinations. The Intermediate portal provides a separate student login facility.

Class 12 Direct Download Link: BSEB Inter 2nd Dummy Admit Card 2027

How to Download Bihar Board Inter 2nd Dummy Admit Card 2027?

  • Visit intermediate.biharboardonline.com.
  • Click on "Second Dummy Admit Card Year 2027 Student Login".
  • Enter the College Code.
  • Enter the Registration Number.
  • Select the applicable Faculty.
  • Complete the verification question displayed on the screen.
  • Submit the form to access the dummy admit card.
  • Review the details and download or print the document.

The verified Intermediate student login uses college code, registration number, faculty and a verification question. Students should follow the fields displayed on the actual portal.

Details to Check on BSEB 2nd Dummy Admit Card 2027

Students should carefully compare their second dummy admit cards with their registration records and supporting documents.

  • Student's full name and spelling
  • Father's and mother's names
  • Date of birth
  • Gender
  • Category and other applicable classification details
  • Registration number
  • Photograph
  • Signature
  • Subject names and subject combinations
  • Aadhaar number, where displayed
  • Other relevant registration information

If any detail is incorrect, students should report the discrepancy to their school or college head before the correction deadline.

BSEB 2nd Dummy Admit Card Correction 2027

Bihar Board has provided another opportunity for students to correct errors in their dummy admit cards before the final admit cards are prepared. The correction window remains open until 26 November 2026.

According to the official Class 10 notification, corrections are divided into two categories depending on the nature of the error.

Important: BSEB has stated that complete changes to the student's name, parents' names or date of birth are not permitted. Requests for correcting errors in these particulars must follow the prescribed evidence-based process.

How to Correct BSEB 2nd Dummy Admit Card 2027?

Students who discover errors should follow the correction process through their school or college authorities.

  • Download or obtain a copy of the second dummy admit card.
  • Carefully check all personal and examination details.
  • Identify the incorrect entries.
  • Mark the required corrections and prepare the supporting documents.
  • Sign the corrected dummy admit card copy.
  • Submit the signed copy to the school or college head.
  • Request the institution to complete the applicable online correction or submit the required report to BSEB.
  • Keep an acknowledged copy for future reference.
  • Ensure the correction request is completed by 26 November 2026.

For Matric students, BSEB specifically instructs candidates to retain a copy bearing the school head's signature and seal after submitting the correction request.

BSEB 2nd Dummy Admit Card 2027 Name and DOB Correction Rules

The official Matric notification prescribes a separate procedure for errors involving the student's name, parents' names and date of birth.

The school head must prepare a report on the school's letterhead with the necessary reference number, date, signature and seal, along with supporting evidence. The report must reach the designated BSEB email address by 26 November 2026.

BSEB 2nd Dummy Admit Card 2027 School Login

The board has also provided a facility for school heads to download dummy admit cards and make permitted corrections.

For Class 10, the school head must log in to the official examination portal using the institution's user ID and password. The Dummy Admit Card menu displays the student list, with options to view and edit applicable records.

School authorities must verify student details and complete the necessary corrections within the prescribed deadline.
