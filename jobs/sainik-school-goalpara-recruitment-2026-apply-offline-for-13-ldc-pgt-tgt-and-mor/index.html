Who Can Apply

Applicants must be Indian citizens. Candidates must not be below 18 years and above 50 years of age as on 12 Nov 2026 for Quarter Master, Band Master, PTI Cum Matron (Female), Mess Manager, LDC, Ward Boy, Office Superintendent, System Administrator, and Horse Riding Instructor. For PGT and TGT posts, candidates must not be below 21 years and above 50 years of age as on 12 Nov 2026.

Selection Process and Exam Pattern

Selection will be made based on an individual’s performance in a Written Test, Skill Test / Class Demonstration, and Interview (as applicable). Shortlisted candidates meeting eligibility criteria will be called for the written test. Written Test Pattern: The written test will be of one hour duration comprising General Knowledge, General English, Basic Mathematics, and a Subject Test of appropriate level.
