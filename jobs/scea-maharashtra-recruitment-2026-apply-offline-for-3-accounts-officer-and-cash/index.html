About SCEA

The State Cooperative Election Authority, Maharashtra State, Pune is an independent and autonomous authority set up in Pune through a notification dated 30/03/2013. Its office is at Old Central Building, Ground Floor, Pune-411001 (phone: (020) 26050641, email: [email protected] ). This recruitment is made on contract under the provisions of the Maharashtra Government General Administration Department Government Resolution (GR) No. CBE 1525/Pr.Kr.37/Asthamam(Ka.13) dated 10 June 2025.

Where and How to Send

Use the prescribed application form (Vihit Namuna Arj). It is printed with the notice and can be downloaded from www.maharashtra.gov.in , https://sahakarayukta.maharashtra.gov.in , www.mahasugar.in and https://scea.maharashtra.gov.in . Forms are also available at the Authority's office. Download the prescribed application form from one of the websites above, or collect it from the Authority's office. Write your date of birth, date of retirement, correspondence address, telephone/mobile number and email ID clearly and correctly in the form. Attach a true photocopy of your Pension Payment Order (PPO) and the other enclosures listed below. Send the application by post to: Member Secretary, State Cooperative Election Authority, Maharashtra State, Pune, Old Central Building, Ground Floor, Pune 411001. Or hand it in at the same office, in the prescribed form and within the prescribed period. O

Posts, Eligibility and Pay

Only officers/employees who have retired from the relevant pay scale in any department of the State Government may apply. Applicants must have a minimum of 3 years' experience in the proposed pay scale for the post, or the special qualification required for the specific work. The official notification does not specify the minimum educational qualification. The application form asks for details of SSC, HSC, diploma (if any), degree, postgraduate degree, GDC&A, MS-CIT/other computer and typing qualifications. Officers/employees retired from government service who have not completed 65 years of age are eligible for contract appointment. Those appointed can work only up to the age of 65. The notice gives no "as on" date for age. For both posts, remuneration is payable as stated in GAD GR No. CBE 1525/Pr.Kr.37/Asthamam(Ka.13) dated 10 June 2025. SCEA Maharashtra Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

The contract appointment is for a maximum of one year at a time. The contract may be renewed every year for a further period, based on a performance evaluation report/criteria and the needs of the work, if the Authority needs it. The total period will not exceed three years. Service conditions of the contract staff will be as per GAD GR No. CBE 1525/Pr.Kr.37/Asthamam(Ka.13) dated 10 June 2025. The Authority has reserved the right to reduce the number of posts or not to fill any of the posts. If any information is found false or incorrect at any stage of selection or after appointment, the selection/appointment is liable to be cancelled without notice, and legal action may follow.
