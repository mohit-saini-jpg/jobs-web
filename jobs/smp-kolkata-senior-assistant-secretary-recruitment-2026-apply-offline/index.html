About SMP Kolkata

Syama Prasad Mookerjee Port, Kolkata, formerly Kolkata Port Trust, is a Major Port Authority with its office at 15, Strand Road, Kolkata-700 001. The two vacancies are in the General Administration Department of its Kolkata Dock System. The circular was sent to the Secretaries of all Major Ports.

SMP Kolkata Senior Assistant Secretary Eligibility 2026

Deputation or absorption is open to officers of a Major Port Authority who hold one of the following: an analogous post, or the post of Assistant Secretary Grade I / Assistant Secretary (Public Relations) Grade-I in the scale of Rs. 50,000 to Rs. 1,60,000 (pre-revised Rs. 9,100 to Rs. 15,100), with 5 years of regular service in the grade. Eligibility is checked as on 30 October 2026, the last date for applications. The Recruitment Rules set an upper age limit of 35 years for direct recruitment. They state that this age limit does not apply to promotion, absorption or deputation. Essential: a degree in any discipline from a recognized University. The Recruitment Rules state that this qualification also applies to candidates coming on deputation or absorption. Pay and Allowances on Deputation

Within one month of joining the Kolkata Dock System, the officer chooses one of two options. The first is pay in the scale of the post at the Port. The second is basic pay in the parent organisation's scale plus deputation allowance. The deputation allowance ceilings rise by a further 25% each time Dearness Allowance goes up by 50%. If the officer is covered under NPS, SMPK pays the employer's contribution. SMPA also bears a gratuity contribution of 4% of basic pay plus applicable DA. An officer who keeps the parent pay scale gets LTC under the parent organisation's rules, and SMPK pays for it.

Service Conditions

Deputation is for three (03) years from the date the officer takes charge, and it can be extended by up to two (02) more years. The Recruitment Rules give a probation period of 2 years for the post. Leave rules of the parent organisation continue to apply, but SMPK keeps the leave account and sanctions the leave. If a departmental inquiry is started during deputation, SMPK may send the officer back to the parent organisation. SMPK can end the deputation early after giving three months' notice. In exceptional cases it can do so without notice. Any matter not covered by the terms is governed by SMPK's rules. Any relaxation of the terms needs SMPK's prior approval.

SMP Kolkata Senior Assistant Secretary Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1Can a fresh graduate apply for this post?
A
No. The posts are filled by deputation or absorption from officers of Major Port Authorities who hold an analogous post or the post of Assistant Secretary Grade I / Assistant Secretary (Public Relations) Grade-I with 5 years of regular service in the grade.
Q2Where will the selected officers work?
A
They will work in the General Administration Department of the Kolkata Dock System, Syama Prasad Mookerjee Port, Kolkata.
Q3How long is the deputation?
A
The deputation is for three years from the date the officer takes charge, and it can be extended by a maximum of two years.
Q4What qualification is needed?
A
The essential qualification is a degree in any discipline from a recognized University. A PG degree or diploma in Personnel Management, Industrial Relations, Social Work, Labour Welfare or a Law degree is desirable.
Q5Is there an upper age limit for deputationists?
A
The 35-year upper age limit in the Recruitment Rules is for direct recruitment. The rules say it does not apply to deputation or absorption.
Q6How much deputation allowance is paid?
A
An officer who keeps the parent pay scale gets 5% of basic pay, up to Rs. 4500 a month, if the station is the same. With a change of station, the allowance is 10% of basic pay, up to Rs. 9000 a month.
Q7What should be written on the envelope?
A
Write "Application for the post of Sr. Asst. Secretary on deputation / absorption basis" and address it to the Secretary, Syama Prasad Mookerjee Port Kolkata, 15, Strand Road, Kolkata-700001.
Q8I sent an advance copy. What is my deadline for the proper-channel application?
A
The application through proper channel must arrive within 15 days of the last date, that is, by 14 November 2026. Otherwise your candidature will not be considered.
Q9Which years' APARs must be enclosed?
A
Annexure-IV lists the APARs for the last 5 years, from 2020-2021 to 2024-25. Each page must be attested by an officer not below the rank of Dy. HOD.
