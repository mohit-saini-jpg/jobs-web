SAIL Young Professional Important Dates

The online application window is short, running from 30 September 2026 to 15 October 2026. The notification advises candidates to apply well before the closing date. The last date to apply online is 15 October 2026. Applications received after this date will not be considered.

SAIL Young Professional Vacancy 2026

SAIL has notified 11 engagements in total. Each engagement code has one vacancy, and the grade decides the upper age limit, minimum experience and monthly retainership fee. Category-wise vacancy distribution (such as UR, OBC, SC, ST, EWS or PwBD) is not specified in the notification.

SAIL Young Professional Eligibility Criteria

All Indian nationals who have completed 23 years of age and possess the required educational or professional qualifications and work experience can apply. The notification states that the minimum age for this engagement is 23 years. Essential qualifications must be Regular/Full-time in the respective stream from a University/Institution recognised by UGC/AICTE/ICAI/ICMAI. The experience must be from a PSU, a Govt. organisation, a Public Limited Company, a JV of PSUs, or a private firm with a valid CIN. The upper age limit, essential qualification and post-qualification experience are reckoned as on 15.10.2026. SAIL Young Professional Age Limit

The age limit depends on the grade. The upper age limit is 35 years for YP-1, 38 years for YP-2 and 42 years for YP-3, reckoned as on 15.10.2026. The minimum age is 23 years. Age relaxation for reserved categories is not specified in the notification.

SAIL Young Professional Qualification and Experience

Each engagement has its own essential qualification, desirable qualification and work experience requirement. Check the row for the engagement code you plan to apply for.

SAIL Young Professional Application Fee

The registration and processing fee is Rs. 500/- for all Young Professional categories (YP-1, YP-2 and YP-3). The fee is paid online after the application is completed. Candidates must also bear the payment charges in addition to the fee, and fees once paid cannot be refunded under any circumstances. Fee concessions or exemptions for any category are not specified in the notification.

SAIL Young Professional Salary

Young Professionals are paid a monthly consolidated retainership fee based on their grade. An annual increment of 4%, 7% or 10% of the monthly consolidated retainership fee is given based on performance during the year. Outstation Young Professionals will be provided company accommodation, subject to availability: 2-bedroom accommodation, or an equivalent monthly rent of Rs. 6,000 at city location. YP-3 may also be considered for 3-bedroom accommodation, or an equivalent monthly rent of Rs. 10,000 at city location. Charges towards electricity, water, maintenance etc. will be applicable as per actuals. No HRA will be payable. During the initial three (3) months, outstation Young Professionals may be allowed to stay at the SAIL Guest House/Transit House on payment of Rs. 200/- per day, subject to availability. SAIL Young Professional Selection Process

The notification describes a scrutiny stage followed by an interview, with a test in some cases, depending on how many applications are received. Applications received by the closing date are scrutinised for fulfilment of the eligibility criteria. If the applications against an engagement are more than five (5) times the requirement, a Computer-based/Aptitude/General Management/Psychometric Test may be organised, or applicants may be asked to appear in any standard test/exam. Candidates are then shortlisted for personal interaction/interview in the ratio of 1:3 based on performance in the prescribed test. If more than one candidate obtains the cut-off marks, all of them will be called for interview. If fewer than five (5) times the requirement is received, all candidates may be called for personal interview. Candidates selected for engagement must join as indicated in the Offer of Engage
