PRL Recruitment 2026 Application Fee

The application fee is ₹250 for all posts. Initially, all applicants - including those from fee-exempt categories (Women/SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen) - must uniformly pay ₹750 as application fee. Candidates from fee-exemption categories (Women/SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen) will get a full refund of the application fee if they attend the written test. UR/EWS/OBC candidates will get a refund of ₹500 if they attend the written test. The application fee will not be refunded to candidates who fail to attend the written test. Correct bank account details with IFSC code must be furnished while filling the online application in order to receive the refund. All fee-exempted categories except women must upload the relevant certificate (SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen) to get the refund. PRL Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection is through a Written Test followed by an Interview . The written test will be conducted only at Ahmedabad (PRL reserves the right to change the venue). To qualify the written test, candidates must secure at least 40% marks in each part of the objective-type test and a minimum aggregate of 50% in the objective test, plus a minimum of 50% marks in the descriptive-type test. Part D (descriptive) will be evaluated manually only for candidates who have qualified in the other three parts. Based on written test performance, candidates will be shortlisted for interview in a ratio of 1:5 (minimum 10 candidates) against the category-wise number of vacancies. Qualifying the written test alone does not guarantee an interview call. Weightage: Written test - 60% , Interview - 40% .
