NAM Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Nine contract posts in AYUSH institutions across Kottayam District are open, with a monthly consolidated remuneration of Rs. 10,500 to Rs. 15,000 depending on the post. The District Programme Management & Supporting Unit of National AYUSH Mission, Kottayam District, under advertisement No. NAM/EST/DPMSU/KTM/115/2026 dated 20 November 2026, invites applications for Ayurveda Nurse, Therapist (Male & Female), Attender, Data Entry Operator, Nursing Assistant, Multipurpose Worker (Drishti Project), Multipurpose Worker (Harsham Project), Multipurpose Health Worker and Yoga Instructor. About NAM Kottayam

The recruitment is conducted by the District Programme Management & Supporting Unit of National AYUSH Mission (NAM), Kottayam District, Kerala. The unit's office is inside the District Ayurveda Hospital, Palace Road, Vayaskara, Kottayam, 686001, and the notice is signed by Dr. Jewel Jose, District Programme Manager, National AYUSH Mission, Kottayam. The selected candidates will work under various AYUSH institutions in Kottayam District; two Multipurpose Worker posts are attached to the Drishti Project and the Harsham Project.

Vacancy Details

The notice lists nine posts. For every post the vacancy column says "Anticipatory", so no fixed number of posts is printed. The remuneration is a monthly consolidated amount. The notice does not mention HRA or any other allowance on top of it.

Who Can Apply

The notice accepts any one of the following for the Yoga Instructor post: BNYS (Bachelor of Naturopathy and Yogic Sciences) BAMS MSc (Yoga) MPhil (Yoga) Post Graduate Diploma in Yoga (Minimum one year duration) from a recognized University Approved Certificate Course in Yoga (one year duration) from a recognized University or Government Department YCB (Yoga Certification Board) Certificate Yoga Teacher Training Course of at least one year's duration from the State Resource Centre (SRC) Diploma in Yogic Science & Sports Yoga conducted by SCOLE Kerala Certificate Course in Yoga (one year duration) The notice prints no experience requirement or desirable qualification for any post. The Therapist post is open to both male and female candidates. Candidates should check the official notification for the exact eligibility wording of each post. Selection and Interview

According to the notice, the selection procedure includes a Skill Test/Written Test and Interview, "as the case may be". The notice does not say which posts will have a skill test or a written test, and it does not give a date or venue for any test or interview. At the Interview, candidates produce their original documents. If the particulars furnished in the application form do not tally with these original documents, the candidature will be rejected.
