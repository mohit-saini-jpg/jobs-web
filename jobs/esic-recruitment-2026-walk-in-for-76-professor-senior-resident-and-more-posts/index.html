ESIC Andheri Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection is based on the candidate's acquired academic credentials and performance in the interview before the Selection Board. The result will be displayed in the Recruitment section of www.esic.gov.in. The Offer of Appointment will be sent through e-mail only. Selected candidates must join as per the instructions in the offer of appointment; if a selected candidate does not join within the prescribed time, the offer will be issued to the waitlisted candidate. The engagement is for 1 (one) year, extendable up to 3 (three) years, subject to satisfactory performance, or till attainment of 68 years of age, whichever is earlier. The contract may be terminated from either side with one month's prior notice; in case of spot resignation, one month's salary must be deposited to the ESI account.
