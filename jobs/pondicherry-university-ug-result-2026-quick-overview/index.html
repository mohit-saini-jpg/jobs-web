Notification DatePost Date: 13/08/2026

Pondicherry University has released provisional results for multiple undergraduate programmes across several semesters. The results were declared on 12 August 2026 and cover courses including B.F.A (Visual Arts), B.P.A (Music), B.P.A (Hindustani Vocal & Instrumental), B.P.A (Dance), B.A. Malayalam, B.A. Political Science, B.A. Public Administration, B.Voc (Fashion Technology), B.Voc (Office Administration and Secretarial Assistance), and B.A. Hindi. Students can check their results using their Register/Roll Number on the official results portal.

How to Check Pondicherry University Result 2026 Online

  • Visit the official result portal: exam.pondiuni.edu.in/results
  • Enter your Register/Roll Number in the field provided.
  • Select the correct Year/Semester (First, Second, Third, and so on) from the dropdown menu.
  • Click the "Go" button to view the result.
  • Check the details displayed and download or save a copy for your records.

Important Instructions for Students

  • The result declared is provisional. Always refer to the original marksheet/grade card issued by the university's Controller of Examinations for official use.
  • New or updated results are hosted only after 6:00 PM, as per the official announcement on the results portal.
  • Keep your Register/Roll Number handy, as it is mandatory to view the result.
  • For discrepancies in marks or grades, contact the Pondicherry University Examination Section directly; the process for correction/revaluation is not detailed in this notification.
  • Bookmark the official portal and avoid third-party websites claiming to show university results, as they can be unreliable or unsafe.
