About NIT Raipur

National Institute of Technology Raipur is an Institute of National Importance under the Ministry of Education, Govt. of India, in Raipur, Chhattisgarh. These posts are for an ICSSR-funded research project on the language, culture and survival of the Birhor PVTGs across Indian States. Interviews will be held in the Dept. of Humanities & Social Sciences, NIT Raipur.

Vacancy Details

The notice advertises 2 posts in total, 1 for each post. Both posts are temporary and co-terminus with the project.

Who Can Apply

Candidates with a Ph.D. degree and prior experience of working in research projects, conducting primary qualitative and quantitative survey on tribal language and culture. Candidate should be bilingual or multilingual proficient in any Austro-Asiatic family languages and English. The project is field-based, hence willingness to travel is mandatory across Bengal and Bihar. Pay and Tenure
