About AP Mahesh Bank

Andhra Pradesh Mahesh Co-operative Urban Bank Ltd. describes itself in the notice as a leading Multi State Scheduled Co-operative Bank based at Hyderabad, with a network of 45 branches spread across 4 states: Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra and Rajasthan. Its Head Office is at 8-2-680/1&2, Road No.12, Banjara Hills, Hyderabad – 500 034, and the recruitment notice is issued by the bank's Deputy General Manager (HR).

Who Can Apply

The candidate shall be a Graduate/ Post Graduate. The candidate shall have a good understanding of the Banking industry (Urban Co-operative Bank) and Compliance of RBI regulations and guidelines. The candidate shall have knowledge of regulations, legal requirement, audit & inspection functions, and have sensitivity to Supervisory expectations. The candidate must have experience of at least 15 years at the senior management level in the Banking sector. Knowledge of statutory/regulatory prescriptions law, accountancy, risk management, information technology, etc., is desirable. Candidates with MBA/CFA/ICWA/CA/CS/CAIIB will be given preference. Knowledge of regional language may be considered as an advantage. The age limit for the Chief Compliance Officer (CCO) in DGM Cadre post is not more than 65 years. The notice does not mention a cut-off date for counting age or any age relaxation. Und
