About TISS Recruitment 2026

Tata Institute of Social Sciences, located at V.N. Purav Marg, Deonar, Mumbai 400 088, is declared as a Deemed to be University under Section 3 of the UGC Act 1956 and functions as a grant-in-aid Institute under the Ministry of Education, Government of India. "Prayas" is a field action project initiated in the year 1990 under the Centre for Criminology and Justice, School of Social Work, TISS, Mumbai. It aims at the socio-legal and economic rehabilitation of persons who are at risk of being criminalized, exploited for commercial sex, and those in destitute states.
