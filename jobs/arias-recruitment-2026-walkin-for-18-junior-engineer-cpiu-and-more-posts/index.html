Eligibility Criteria

Rural Finance Specialist, PIU: Graduate degree, preferably post-graduate degree in rural finance, economics, business administration, development studies, or related field with Minimum 10 years of relevant professional experience, including 5 years in planning and implementing microfinance, agribusiness or rural development projects. Livelihood Coordinator, CPIU: Masters/Post-graduate degree in Livelihoods, Entrepreneurship Development, Food Security, Agriculture, Agribusiness, value chain Development, Rural Development, Social sciences or related field with Minimum 5 years of professional experience in promoting livelihoods interventions. ARIAS Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is the walk-in date for ARIAS Recruitment 2026?
A
The interviews are held on seven dates, one per post: 01.12.2026 (Rural Finance Specialist), 03.12.2026 (Livelihood Coordinator), 05.12.2026 (Hydrology Specialist), 07.12.2026 (GIS and MIS Executive), 09.12.2026 (Financial Management Specialist), 11.12.2026 (Junior Engineer (Civil)) and 15.12.2026 (Accountant).
Q2Where will the ARIAS walk-in interview be held?
A
The interviews are at the office of the State Project Director, ARIASS, Agriculture Campus, Six-mile, Guwahati-781022.
Q3How many vacancies are there in ARIAS SWIFT recruitment?
A
There are 18 positions in total: 1 Rural Finance Specialist, 4 Livelihood Coordinator, 1 Hydrology Specialist, 4 GIS and MIS Executive, 1 Financial Management Specialist, 5 Junior Engineer (Civil) and 2 Accountant.
Q4Are these ARIAS posts permanent?
A
No. The notice says the positions are to be filled on a purely contractual basis for the SWIFT Project.
Q5Which project are the ARIAS posts for?
A
The posts are for the Assam Sustainable Wetland and Integrated Fisheries Transformation (SWIFT) Project, with financial and technical support from the Asian Development Bank (ADB).
Q6Where can I find the Terms of Reference for the ARIAS posts?
A
The notice advises candidates to visit https://www.arias.in/ for further details of the advertisement and the relevant Terms of Reference (ToR).
Q7When is the interview for Junior Engineer (Civil) in ARIAS?
A
The interview for the 5 Junior Engineer (Civil), CPIU positions is on 11.12.2026.
Q8When is the interview for Accountant in ARIAS?
A
The interview for the 2 Accountant, CPIU positions is on 15.12.2026.
