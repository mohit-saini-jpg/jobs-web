About Govt. Higher Secondary School, Majhidhanua

Govt. Higher Secondary School, Majhidhanua is a government higher secondary school in Kosagumuda Block of Nabarangpur district, Odisha, with +2 classes in the Arts Stream. This engagement of Guest Faculty is made pursuant to the Govt. in School & Mass Education Department Approved Letter No. 14101/SME Dtd. 01.06.2026, as communicated by the Director, Higher Secondary Education, Odisha vide letter No. 13112 Dtd. 03.11.2026.

Qualification and Experience

Two groups are invited: Retired Teachers of Govt. and Aided College/HSS below 65 years of age, and Fresh Eligible Candidates. The notice states that in case of non-availability of such teachers, fresh candidates will be selected. Candidates should check the official notification for the subject-specific eligibility of each post.

GHSS Majhidhanua Guest Faculty Recruitment 2026 : Age Limit

Retired Teachers of Govt. and Aided College/HSS must be below 65 years of age. The age limit for fresh eligible candidates is not specified in the notice, and no age relaxation is printed.

Pay and Terms

The engagement is purely temporary in nature, for the session 2026-27. It will continue till closure of schools on summer vacation in 2027 or posting of PGT in the school, whichever is earlier. The Principal reserves the right of disengagement by giving one month notice if performance is not satisfactory.
PostPay
Guest FacultyRs 400/- per class/lecturer of 45 minutes duration, subject to a ceiling of maximum 50 classes with Rs. 20,000/- per month

GHSS Majhidhanua Guest Faculty Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

The selection shall be made through career assessment (printed as "carrier assessment"), teaching experience and basic experience in computer application, followed by document verification. Career assessment: the candidate's academic record. Teaching experience: supported by the experience certificate. Basic experience in computer application: supported by the Certificate of Computer Knowledge. Document verification: on 12.11.2026 in the office of the Principal, Govt. Higher Secondary School, Majhidhanua. No TA/DA shall be admissible for document verification.

Documents Required

Self-attested documents to be attached with the application form: Bio-Data duly filled up. HSC or Equivalent Certificate and Mark sheet. +2 or Equivalent Certificate and Mark sheet. Bachelor Degree or Equivalent Certificate and Mark sheet. Master Degree or Equivalent Certificate and Mark sheet. B.Ed. Degree or Equivalent certificate and Mark sheet. Certificate of Computer Knowledge. Experience certificate. Id proof/Address Proof. Any other.
