ESIC Indore Teaching Faculty Eligibility Criteria

Candidates should check their own eligibility before reporting for the interview. Eligibility for the qualifying degree and experience is counted as on the 15th of every month. Interview opportunity is provisional, subject to detailed scrutiny of eligibility. Candidates must hold an updated Medical Registration (NMC or State Medical Council) with all post-MBBS, MD or MS qualifications. If the registration is not updated, candidates must show and submit a copy of the "Applied for" document at the time of interview. Otherwise they may not be interviewed, or their result may be withheld. Selected candidates must submit the updated Medical Registration Certificate when reporting to join. The educational qualification is as per National Medical Commission (NMC, erstwhile MCI) guidelines. ESIC Indore Teaching Faculty Application Fee

The fee, once paid, will not be refunded under any circumstances. The Demand Draft is described as the interview fee "if applicable". The scanned copy of the DD is sent with the email application, and the original DD must reach the Dean by post.

ESIC Indore Teaching Faculty Salary

The consolidated remuneration is as per Medical Administration, HQRS Office Memoranda No. Z-17/11/1/2007/Med.IV (Pt. File) dated 13.04.2022 (other than retired Army Medical Teachers) and Z-11012/51/2022-MED-VI dated 08.12.2022. It may change as per Headquarters instructions issued from time to time. Salary and other allowances are paid as: NPA at 20% (wherever applicable and admissible), DA at 60%, HRA at 20%, and TA at Rs. 7,200 plus DA thereon. The remuneration is revised automatically when the Dearness Allowance rate is revised, effective from the date of notification of Government orders. Arrears are not admissible for retrospective rate revisions.

ESIC Indore Teaching Faculty Terms of Contract

Tenure is 1 year, or until a regular incumbent joins, or until 70 years of age, whichever is earlier. Either side may end the contract with one month's prior notice without assigning a reason. In case of spot resignation, one month's salary is to be deposited to the ESI Account through challan. No service benefits such as PF, Pension, Gratuity, Medical Allowance, Medical Benefits, Seniority or Promotion are admissible. Absence beyond 7 days without proper permission counts as voluntary abandonment and leads to automatic termination. Attendance must be marked manually and on the Aadhaar Enabled Bio-metric Attendance System (AEBAS) daily on scheduled working days. Leave of 2.5 days is credited for each completed month. ESIC Indore Teaching Faculty Selection Process

Selection is based on acquired academic credentials and performance in the interview before the Selection Board. The mode of interview is offline (walk-in). The result will be displayed in the Recruitments section of www.esic.gov.in. The offer of appointment will be sent through email only. Selected candidates must join as per the instructions in the offer of appointment. Any amendment to the notification will be displayed on www.esic.gov.in/recruitments. The notification states that the appointment letter will be issued on the day the results are announced.

ESIC Indore Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1The notification does not provide an online application form or portal. Candidates apply by emailing the scanned application and then sending the original documents by post. The official page for notices is www.esic.gov.in/recruitments.
  • 2Visit www.esic.gov.in/recruitments between the 1st and 7th of the month and check the vacancy notification.
  • 3Fill in the prescribed application format in black or blue ink in BLOCK letters, with a recent self-attested passport-size photograph. File a separate form for each post.
  • 4Scan the application form, the Demand Draft (where applicable) and all self-attested supporting documents, including research publications and conference papers, if any. Arrange them in the checklist order as a PDF only.
  • 5Email them to [email protected] by 4:00 PM on the 15th of the month (or the next working day if it is a holiday). Write the Name, Post and Department in the email subject. Emails after the deadline are not accepted.
  • 6Send the original application form with self-attested documents and the original Demand Draft (if applicable) to The Dean, ESIC Medical College & Hospital, Nanda Nagar, Indore (M.P.) - 452011, to reach on or before the interview date (the 17th of every month, as written in the notification).
  • 7Write "Recruitment of Teaching Faculty/Senior Residents on contract basis" with the Name of Post and Department on the envelope.
  • 8Report for the walk-in interview at 9:00 AM with original documents for verification. For October 2026, the interview is on 19/10/2026.
  • 9Self-attested copies of the following must be attached with the application. Items (i) to (xi) are verified against the originals before the interview.
  • 10Class 10th certificate (for date of birth)
  • 11All marks sheets, attempt details and degree certificate of MBBS
  • 12All marks sheets, attempt details and degree certificate of MD/MS/DNB or other examination
  • 13Valid EWS/OBC/SC/ST/PwD certificate in the Central Government job format
  • 14Updated State/NMC Medical Registration Certificate
  • 15Proof of publication(s)
  • 16Experience certificate
  • 17Recent passport-size photograph
  • 18Relieving/NOC certificate from previous or current employer, if applicable
  • 19Aadhaar card
  • 20Completion certificate of the ICMR/NMC-conducted bio-medical research examination, if any
  • 21NMC-approved Revised/Basic Medical Education Workshop completion certificate, if any
  • 22The application format asks for: post applied for, department, name in block letters, father's/husband's name, date of birth, age as on the 15th of the month, category and caste, post notified under category, mode of interview (offline), Demand Draft details (bank name, DD number, date), qualifications table (qualification, board/university, year of passing, marks, division, attempts), experience with certificates, MCI/State registration number, telephone and mobile numbers, email, permanent and present addresses, marital status, nationality and mother tongue, Aadhaar number, identification mark, and a signed declaration.
  • 23The enclosure checklist covers: date of birth certificate (matriculation), proof of identity (Aadhaar/PAN/driving licence/other), UG certificate, Diploma/PG certificate, MCI/State registration certificate, experience certificate/NOC, research publications, caste/EWS/PwD certificate, and residential address proof.
  • 24Fill only one form for each post applied for, in clear and bold letters.
  • 25The photograph must show the face and both ears clearly and be signed across.

ESIC Indore Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is ESIC Indore Recruitment 2026?
A
ESIC Medical College & Hospital, Indore has released Rolling Advertisement No. 08 of 2026 to recruit 86 Teaching Faculty (Professor, Associate Professor, Assistant Professor) and Senior Residents on a contractual basis through monthly walk-in interviews.
Q2How many vacancies are available under this recruitment?
A
A total of 86 vacancies are available across various clinical and non-clinical departments for Professor, Associate Professor, Assistant Professor, and Senior Resident posts.
Q3What is the last date to submit the application?
A
Candidates must submit scanned applications via email by 04:00 PM on the 15th of every month. For October 2026, the application deadline is 15-10-2026.
Q4When and where are the walk-in interviews conducted?
A
Walk-in interviews are conducted offline on the 18th of every month (or the next working day if the 18th is a holiday) at ESIC Medical College & Hospital, Indore. For October 2026, the interview is on 19/10/2026 with reporting time at 09:00 AM.
Q5What is the educational qualification required for Teaching Faculty?
A
Candidates must possess qualifications as per National Medical Commission (NMC erstwhile MCI) guidelines along with an updated Medical Registration certificate from the NMC or State Medical Council.
Q6What is the upper age limit for Senior Residents?
A
The upper age limit for Senior Residents is not exceeding 45 years as on the 15th of every month, with age relaxations as per Government of India norms.
Q7Is there any application fee?
A
Yes, the application fee is ₹500/- for all other categories. SC, ST, ESIC regular employees, defence ex-servicemen, and PwD candidates are exempt from paying the fee (Nil fee).
Q8How should the application fee be paid?
A
The application fee must be paid through a Demand Draft drawn in favour of 'ESI Fund A/c No.-1, payable at Indore'.
Q9What is the salary for Assistant Professor posts?
A
Assistant Professors receive a fixed basic pay of ₹67,700.00 at Pay Level 11 under the 7th CPC, along with admissible allowances such as NPA, DA, HRA, and TA.
Q10Can I apply for more than one post?
A
Yes, candidates must submit a separate application form and fee for each post applied for.
