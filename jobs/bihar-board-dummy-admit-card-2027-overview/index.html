Bihar Board Dummy Admit Card 2027 - Overview : Admit Card Overview

The Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB) has released the Bihar Board Dummy Admit Card 2027 for students registered for the Class 10 (Matric) and Class 12 (Intermediate) annual examinations. The dummy admit cards were made available from 1 November 2026 through the official websites, exam.biharboardonline.org and intermediate.biharboardonline.com.

Bihar Board 10th Dummy Admit Card 2027 Download Link

The BSEB Class 10 Dummy Admit Card 2027 was issued for students registered for the Annual Secondary Examination 2027. The board uploaded the provisional admit cards to its secondary examination portal based on the submitted registration and examination application details.

Class 10 Official Portal: BSEB 10th Dummy Admit Card 2027

The announced download and correction period was 1 to 7 November 2026. Students who have already downloaded their dummy admit cards should retain them for reference. The availability of the download facility after the stated deadline should be checked directly on the official portal.

Bihar Board 12th Dummy Admit Card 2027 Download Link

How to Download Bihar Board Class 10 Dummy Admit Card 2027?

The supplied examination update provides the following procedure for accessing the BSEB Class 10 Dummy Admit Card 2027. These steps describe the announced download facility, whose original window has ended.

  • Visit the official BSEB Secondary examination portal at exam.biharboardonline.org.
  • Find the Dummy Admit Card option for the Annual Secondary Examination 2027.
  • Open the student dummy admit card page.
  • Enter the registration number.
  • Enter the date of birth.
  • Select the Search option to access the dummy admit card.
  • Check the personal and examination details.
  • Save a copy of the admit card for reference if the download facility remains available.

How to Download Bihar Board Class 12 Dummy Admit Card 2027?

Intermediate students were provided with a separate download facility through the official Class 12 examination portal.

  • Visit intermediate.biharboardonline.com.
  • Open the student dummy admit card download option for the 2027 annual examination.
  • Enter the registration number.
  • Enter the date of birth as recorded during registration.
  • Select the Download option.
  • Review the information displayed on the provisional admit card.
  • Save the document if the portal still permits downloading.

Details to Check on BSEB Dummy Admit Card 2027

The purpose of the Bihar Board dummy admit card is to allow students to verify their registered details before the final examination admit card is issued. Students should examine the following information carefully:

  • Student's name and spelling
  • Father's name
  • Mother's name
  • Date of birth
  • Gender
  • Category
  • Aadhaar details, where applicable
  • Student's photograph
  • Student's signature
  • Registered examination subjects
  • Other examination application details

The board's correction notices specify that complete changes to a student's name or their parents' names are not permitted. Corrections must remain within the conditions prescribed by BSEB.

Bihar Board Dummy Admit Card Correction 2027

The Bihar Board permitted eligible students to request corrections to errors in their Class 10 and Class 12 dummy admit cards between 1 and 7 November 2026. The process was intended to prevent incorrect information from appearing on the final examination admit cards.

Students finding discrepancies were instructed to contact their respective school or college principals. The concerned institution was responsible for completing permitted corrections online through the board's examination system.

How Was the Dummy Admit Card Correction Process Conducted?

  • Download and examine the dummy admit card.
  • Identify the incorrect personal or examination details.
  • Mark the permitted corrections on the dummy admit card.
  • Submit the corrected and signed copy to the school or college head.
  • Request the institution to complete the prescribed online correction.
  • Retain the acknowledged copy for reference.

The above procedure applied during the announced correction window, which closed on 7 November 2026. It should not be interpreted as confirmation that online corrections remain available after that date.

What to Do After the BSEB Dummy Admit Card Correction Deadline?

As of 12 November 2026, the announced correction deadline for the Bihar Board Class 10 and Class 12 Dummy Admit Card 2027 has passed.

Students who submitted correction requests within the prescribed period should retain their acknowledged documents and follow up with their schools or colleges if necessary. Students who missed the deadline or still find incorrect details should contact their institution for guidance. The available notices do not confirm a further correction window.
