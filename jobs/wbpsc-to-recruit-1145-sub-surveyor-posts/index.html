WBPSC Sub Surveyor Age Limit

The WBPSC Sub Surveyor age limit is a minimum of 21 years and a maximum of 45 years as on 01.01.2024, in terms of Finance Department Notification No. 1707-F(P2) dated 16.05.2026 read with Notification No. 2619-F(P2) dated 20.07.2026. The age recorded only in the Madhyamik or equivalent certificate is accepted. No relaxation is applicable to SC/ST/OBC candidates of other states, who are treated as General candidates. The Ex-Servicemen relaxation is for those who have put in not less than 6 months' continuous service in any rank (combatant or non-combatant) in the Armed Forces of the Union. The provisions of the West Bengal Services (Raising of Age Limit) Rules, 1981 are not applicable.

WBPSC Sub Surveyor Selection Process

The WBPSC Sub Surveyor selection process is a written examination of two papers. The final merit list is prepared on the basis of the marks obtained in Paper-II only. The notification also refers to Document Verification, but does not give its schedule. Qualifying marks are fixed at 30% of the marks allotted for Paper-I. The Commission has the discretion to fix the qualifying marks in Paper-II. Marks in Paper-II are taken into consideration only for candidates who attain 30% marks in Paper-I. There is negative marking of 0.25 mark for each wrong answer in the MCQ-type Paper-II. If two or more candidates get equal marks in Paper-II, preference is given to the senior candidate by date of birth.
