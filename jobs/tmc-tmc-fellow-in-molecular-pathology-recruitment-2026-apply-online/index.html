Who Can Apply

The fellowship is open to Indian Nationals who meet the following requirements. All undergraduate, postgraduate and super-specialty degrees must be recognised by the National Medical Commission (NMC). Experience is counted as on 15 November 2026. Candidates whose results are awaited may apply, where applicable, but must produce proof of having passed the required degree at the time of the MCQ/Interview. Those who cannot produce this proof will not be permitted to appear for the MCQ/Interview. The maximum age limit is 45 years, reckoned as on 15 November 2026. The age limit is relaxable as per applicable rules: Candidates should check the official notification for the full eligibility conditions of this post.

Pay and Tenure

The Fellow will be offered a monthly consolidated salary of Rs. 1,33,623/- (Rupees One Lakh Thirty-Three Thousand Six Hundred Twenty-Three only). The post is a tenured position for a fixed period up to one year. The notice also prescribes a Security/Caution Deposit of Rs. 1,33,623/- for the Fellow. All candidates selected and appointed to HBCH/MPMMCC, Varanasi, must be registered with the Uttar Pradesh Medical Council, as applicable.
