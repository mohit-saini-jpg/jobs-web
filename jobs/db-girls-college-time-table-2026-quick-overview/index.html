How to Check the DB Girls College Raipur Time Table

  • Find your semester (I, III or V) and course (B.A., B.Com., B.Sc. or B.Sc. Home Sc.).
  • Go to the column for your course in the tables above.
  • Note the date and weekday against each subject.
  • Check the VAC table separately for your VAC paper date.
  • Compare with the official notice from the college before you finalise your preparation plan.

Important Instructions

  • All semester papers run from 1:00 PM to 4:00 PM.
  • Subjects separated by a slash (for example, Hindi Lit. / Sanskrit Lit.) appear against the same date. The notice does not explain this further, so confirm your subject's paper with the college if you are unsure.
  • Rows marked with a dash in the original notice are shown here as "No exam listed".
  • For GE/DSE papers in III and V Semester, the notice does not list individual subjects.
  • The notice is signed by the Controller of Autonomous Examination and the Principal and is dated 3 November 2026.
