Pay and Tenure

Senior Research Fellow: consolidated pay of Rs. 37000 per month plus HRA. Young Professional-1: Rs. 30000/- fixed per month. Tenure: up to 31 March 2027 or till termination of the project, whichever is earlier. Both positions are purely temporary, filled on contractual basis, co-terminus with the project, and of a time-bound, non-regular nature. Selected candidates will not be entitled to claim regular appointment or absorption in the University or the State Government.

Selection and Interview

Applications will be screened and shortlisted candidates will be informed by email, WhatsApp or a personal phone call to appear for the interview. The interview date and venue are not specified in the notice. Original documents will be verified at the time of interview. Shortlisted candidates must carry their recent bio-data in the enclosed formats along with the originals of the relevant documents. No TA/DA will be borne by the Institute for attending the interview.
