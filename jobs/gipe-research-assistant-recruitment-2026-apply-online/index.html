GIPE Research Assistant Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Population Research Centre (PRC), Pune, funded by the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, New Delhi, has two contractual Research Assistant posts open at the Gokhale Institute of Politics and Economics (GIPE): one Research Assistant – I (Unreserved) on consolidated pay of Rs. 28,000 per month and one Research Assistant - II (OBC) on Rs. 25,000 per month. Both need a 2nd Class Post Graduate degree in a listed subject. Applications are online and the last date is 6 December 2026, 6.00 p.m.

About GIPE

The notice describes the Gokhale Institute of Politics and Economics as one of the oldest research institutes in the country, established in 1930 under the aegis of Servants of India Society. It has been declared a Deemed to be University under Section 3 of the UGC Act, 1956. The Institute is based at 846, Shivajinagar, Pune – 411 004.

Vacancy Details

The notice advertises 2 non-faculty contractual positions in total.

Who Can Apply

Accepted subjects for the Post Graduate degree, from a recognized Institution / University: Demography / Population Studies / Statistics / Biostatistics / Economics / Mathematics / Sociology / Social work / Psychology / Anthropology / Geography / Public Health. Candidates having a degree from a foreign university must produce an equivalence certificate issued by the Association of Indian University (AIU) while applying. Candidates already in service must apply through proper channel with a no objection certificate from the current employer.
PostEssential Qualification And Experience
Research Assistant – I (Unreserved)2nd Class Post Graduate degree (minimum) in the subjects below. Four years of experience post qualification (i.e. after Post Graduation) /Ph.D. Relaxable in deserving cases.
Research Assistant - II (OBC)2nd Class Post Graduate degree (minimum) in the subjects below. One year of experience post qualification (i.e. after Post Graduation). Relaxable in deserving cases.

Pay and Tenure

Research Assistant – I receives consolidated pay of Rs. 28,000/- per month and Research Assistant - II Rs. 25,000/- per month. Hiring is purely on a contractual basis, and the contract is renewed at least every year based on the performance of the Research Assistant. PRC has no liability for regularizing the service rendered or offering regular appointment in the PRC. No TA/DA is admissible for attending the interview or for joining the post.

Selection and Interview

The Institute may short-list candidates for interview, as necessary. A Screening Committee appointed for the purpose short-lists candidates from the applications received, and its decision is final and binding. Meeting the minimum qualifications does not entitle a candidate to be called for interview. The call letter is sent only to short-listed candidates, to their email only. No correspondence is entertained from applicants who are not short-listed. The notice does not give an interview date.
