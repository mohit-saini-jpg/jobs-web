Who Can Apply

Candidates must possess the specific educational qualification corresponding to the designated trade as listed in the vacancy table. Qualifications acquired through Distance Learning Mode, Part Time Mode, or Correspondence Mode are not considered. The candidate must be a minimum of 18 years and maximum 25 years of age as on the last date of application. Age relaxation is applicable up to 5 years for SC/ST categories, up to 3 years for OBC (NCL) categories, and up to 10 years for PWD categories. Candidates who have already undergone or are currently undergoing Apprenticeship training under the Apprenticeship Act, 1961 and rules made thereunder in any Govt. / Public Sector / Private Industrial Organization are NOT eligible. Reservation for SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PWD is applicable as per the prescribed percentage applicable to the State of Kerala, along with relevant Government guidelines.

Stipend and Training Period

The rate of stipend for the apprentices is as prescribed under the Apprenticeship Rules, 1992 and notified by the Government from time to time. During the period of training, IREL (India) Limited will not provide any other benefit or financial assistance.

IREL Apprentices Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection of apprentices is based on a merit list drawn from marks scored in the specified educational qualification, subject to being found "Medically Fit" by IREL (India) Limited's Medical Officer. In case of a tie in marks, the higher marks of specified educational qualification will be considered, and if still tied, seniority in date of birth will be taken. Priority for selection is given in the following order: Candidates belonging to Land Affected Category (A Certificate issued by Resources Department). Candidates coming under the Mining area and from peripheral villages situated within 10 KM radius of IREL (India) Limited, Chavara unit. Wards of serving employees / Forum members. Belonging to Kollam District. Belonging to Kerala State. Others.

IREL Apprentices Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Register on the portal of Ministry of Skill Development And Entrepreneurship (apprenticeshipindia.org) for Trade / Vocational apprentices or on the National Apprenticeship Training Scheme (NATS) of MHRD (nats.education.gov.in) for Graduate / Technician Apprentices, using establishment registration number E08153200007.
  • 2Obtain the application proforma provided in the advertisement and type it on A4 size paper.
  • 3Superscribe the outer cover with "APPLICATION FOR ENGAGEMENT OF APPRENTICE AGAINST NOTIFICATION NO. IREL(India) Limited / Chavara/Apprentices Engagement/2026-27 dated 12-10-2026".
  • 4Enclose self-attested photocopies of certificates including 10th pass/Matriculation certificate, 10th/HSC mark sheet, category certificates (SC/ST/OBC(NCL)/EWS/Disability), Income and Assets Certificate for EWS, semester-wise/year-wise mark sheets and pass certificate, documentary proof for Land Affected Category / Mining areas / peripheral villages within 10 KM radius, Nativity Certificate, and Aadhaar Card.
  • 5Affix a passport size photograph on the space provided in the filled application form along with the documents.
  • 6Send the completed application by post, preferably through speed post, to reach The DGM (HRM), IREL (India) Limited, Chavara, Kollam District, Kerala - 691583 on or before 31st October 2026.

IREL Apprentices Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1Who cannot apply for the IREL apprentice positions?
A
Candidates who have already undergone or are currently undergoing Apprenticeship training under the Apprenticeship Act, 1961 in any Government, Public Sector, or Private Industrial Organization are not eligible.
Q2Who is eligible for age relaxations?
A
Age relaxation is provided up to 5 years for SC/ST candidates, up to 3 years for OBC (NCL) candidates, and up to 10 years for PWD candidates.
Q3Which documents need to be enclosed with the application?
A
Candidates must enclose self-attested copies of 10th pass certificate, 10th/HSC mark sheet, caste/disability certificates, EWS income certificate, semester/year-wise mark sheets and pass certificates, land affected or mining area documentary proof, nativity certificate, Aadhaar card, and a passport-size photograph.
Q4Is the job permanent after completion of apprenticeship training?
A
No, IREL (India) Limited has no obligation to offer regular employment to apprentices during or after the completion of the apprenticeship period, and candidates shall be relieved from training upon completion.
