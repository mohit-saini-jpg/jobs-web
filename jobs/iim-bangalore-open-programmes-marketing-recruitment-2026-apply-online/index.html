About IIMB

The notice describes the Indian Institute of Management Bangalore (IIMB) as a leading management school in Asia. Under the IIM Act of 2017, IIMB is an Institute of National Importance. Its sector is listed as Higher Education. This post sits in the Executive Education (EEP) open programmes team.

Role and Responsibilities

Open Enrolment programmes are either Short-duration Programmes (SDPs) of 3-6 days or Long-duration Programmes (LDPs) that run over 6 months to 1 year. This is an individual contributor Sales role in a B2C and B2B context. The job is to generate leads (aspirants), engage with them and turn them into paid participants for each cohort. The duties are: Handle all inbound and outbound leads and close each sale when the finalised candidate pays. Follow up with direct aspirants and sponsoring organisations by phone, email and in person. Provide data analytics and feedback on what is and is not working, to fine-tune Digital Marketing campaigns. Understand how social media campaigns are run and what goes into social media posts. Work with the DM team to design marketing collaterals, such as videos, graphic artwork and text for webpages, brochures and testimonials, across different media platforms

IIMB Open Programmes Marketing – EEP Eligibility 2026

The candidate should be a graduate. At least 3 years of experience in Sales and Marketing in a B2B or B2C environment. An MBA or postgraduate degree in a relevant discipline from a reputed institution. Prior sales experience in the higher education sector (an added advantage).
