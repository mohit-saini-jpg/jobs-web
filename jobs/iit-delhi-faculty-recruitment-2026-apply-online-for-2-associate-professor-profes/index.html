Qualification and Experience

For both posts, the qualifying experience must be in IITs or similar institutes of national importance, or at an equivalent level in any other Indian or foreign institution of comparable standards. Both posts also require the following: specialization in the appropriate area/branch, with a consistently good academic record throughout; good communication skills; a commitment to high quality undergraduate and postgraduate education; a demonstrated ability to carry out original and creative research. Pay and Terms

Both posts carry Dearness Allowance, House Rent Allowance, Transport Allowance and telephone charges reimbursement as per Institute rules. LTC, Children Education Allowance and contribution towards the New Pension Scheme (NPS) are permissible as per GoI rules. Annexure-II gives one description of the nature of work for Professor, Associate Professor and other academic positions: Teach UG/PG students in Science/Engineering/Humanities and Social Science/Management/Policy and Design. Who Should Apply II (Associate Professor; Professor Posts)

Associate Professor/Professor ko lectures dene, research projects lead karne, Ph.D. students supervise karne aur industry collaboration me involve rehna hota hai. Seniority ke saath administrative roles bhi milte hain.

Application form me sabhi qualifications aur publications accurately fill karen, reference letters attach karen. Written test (agar ho) me conceptual clarity aur research methodology pe focus karen, interview me apne contributions ko confidently present karen.

IIT Delhi Faculty Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is the last date to apply for IIT Delhi Recruitment 2026?
A
The last date is 31 December 2026, 23:59 PM (IST), under Rolling Advertisement No. IITD/2026/PROF-2.
Q2Which posts are offered in IIT Delhi Recruitment 2026?
A
Associate Professor and Professor posts are offered in 19 academic units. The Biochemical Engineering & Biotechnology and Humanities and Social Sciences departments are marked "Not Hiring" for Professor.
Q3What qualification is needed for IIT Delhi Associate Professor and Professor posts?
A
Both posts need a Ph.D., with first class or equivalent in the preceding degree, and a consistently good academic record. Academic units add their own criteria in Annexure-I.
Q4How much experience is required?
A
Associate Professor needs at least 6 years, with 3 at Assistant Professor level or equivalent. Professor needs at least 10 years, with 4 at Associate Professor level or equivalent.
Q5What is the salary of IIT Delhi Associate Professor and Professor?
A
Associate Professor: ₹ 1,39,600 – 2,11,300/- in Academic Pay Level 13A2. Professor: ₹ 1,59,100 – 2,20,200/- in Academic Pay Level 14A. DA, HRA and TA are paid as per Institute rules.
Q6How to apply for IIT Delhi faculty posts?
A
Apply online at home.iitd.ac.in/jobs-iitd/index.php and upload the Academic Profile and certificates by 31 December 2026. Retain a copy of the submitted application.
Q7How are candidates selected?
A
Candidates are shortlisted on qualification, experience, research, publication records and the academic unit's requirements. Shortlisted candidates are called for a personal interview.
Q8Can I apply to more than one department?
A
Yes. A separate online application must be filled for each academic unit, and candidates with overlapping interests may be considered for joint appointment.
Q9Are foreign nationals appointed on a regular basis?
A
No. Foreign nationals are appointed on contract basis, initially for 5 years, and the contract can be renewed after that tenure. A Government of India work visa is needed.
Q10Is travel fare paid for the interview?
A
Yes. Outstation candidates get 2-Tier AC railway or economy airfare within India. Candidates coming from abroad get up to Rs.40,000/- or the actual fare, whichever is less.
