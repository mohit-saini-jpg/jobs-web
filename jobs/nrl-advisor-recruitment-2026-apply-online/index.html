About NRL Recruitment 2026

Numaligarh Refinery Limited (NRL) is a Navaratna Public Sector Undertaking under the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas engaged in refining and marketing petroleum products. The company is currently executing a mega refinery expansion project to ramp up refining capacity from 3 MMTPA to 9 MMTPA, which includes crude oil importation at Paradip Port and laying a cross-country pipeline from Paradip to Numaligarh. This recruitment engages experienced retired or serving professionals to support critical refinery operations during this expansion.

Pay and Tenure

The engagement is purely contractual for a maximum duration of up to 12 months from the date of joining. Remuneration is an all-inclusive fixed monthly lumpsum with no additional allowances. Statutory deductions such as TDS will be deducted from the monthly remuneration, against which necessary certificates will be issued. Outstation tour travel will be compensated with TA/DA as per the applicable NRL equivalent grade rate. Appointees will work in line with the regular officers of the company, observe normal office timings, and may be called upon to attend office on weekly off days or any holiday in case of urgency. During their engagement they shall not be allowed to take any other assignment. Advisors are entitled to 20 days of paid leave for every 12 months on a pro-rata basis, and normal company holidays of NRL will be applicable.
