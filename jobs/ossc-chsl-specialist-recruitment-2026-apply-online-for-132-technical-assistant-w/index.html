How Will OSSC Select Candidates?

Stage-I: Preliminary Examination (OMR/CBRE), 150 marks, MCQ Stage-II: Main Written Examination (OMR/CBRE), Technical Paper, 200 marks Stage-III: Certificate Verification Preliminary: one 150-mark MCQ paper; minimum 5 times the vacancies category-wise shortlisted for the Main Main Written: Technical Paper of 200 marks as per the post's qualification 2 times the vacancies (post-wise and category-wise) shortlisted for Certificate Verification Merit list prepared category-wise on Main Written marks and post options Ties resolved by Preliminary marks, then older age, then alphabetical order

OSSC CHSL Specialist Exam Pattern 2026

Negative marking of 0.25 marks for each wrong answer in the Preliminary MCQ paper Commission may fix minimum qualifying marks by category and post Preliminary and Main examinations conducted in both Odia and English; medium option once given cannot be changed No re-evaluation or re-checking of scores Normalisation formula (Notice No.2444/OSSC dated 02.09.2021) applies if exam is held in multiple batches
