OPSC AEE Civil Salary

The post of Assistant Executive Engineer (Civil) carries the scale of pay in Level-12 of the pay matrix provided under Rule-3 of the ORSP Rules-2017, corresponding to the pre-revised scale of Pay Band-3 (Rs. 15,600 – Rs. 39,100) with Grade Pay of Rs. 5,400, along with usual dearness allowance and other allowances as may be sanctioned by the Government of Odisha from time to time.

OPSC AEE Civil Selection Process

Selection for OPSC AEE Civil Recruitment 2026 is made through a competitive examination conducted in three stages: Preliminary Examination, Main Examination, and Interview. The Preliminary Examination is a screening test for shortlisting candidates for the Main Examination. It consists of one paper in Civil Engineering with Objective Type Multiple Choice Questions, conducted through Optical Mark Recognition (OMR) or Computer-Based Test (CBT), as decided by the Commission. Minimum qualifying marks for the Preliminary Examination are determined separately by the Commission for each category. Candidates securing the qualifying marks are shortlisted for the Main Examination in order of merit, subject to a maximum of approximately 10 times the number of vacancies notified for each category. OPSC AEE Civil Recruitment 2026 Syllabus

The Preliminary Examination paper covers: Engineering Mechanics, Soil Mechanics, Structural Analysis, Concrete Structure, Steel Structure, Solid Mechanics, Fluid Mechanics, Irrigation, Transportation Engineering, and Survey.
