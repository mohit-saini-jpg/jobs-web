SINP Recruitment 2026 Vacancy Details

SINP has announced a total of 62 vacancies across three categories - Scientific, Technical, and Administrative & Auxiliary (including Canteen). Category-wise reservation (UR/SC/OBC-NCL/EWS) is shown in parentheses against each post. All 5 Scientist 'C' posts together are reserved for Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD) on a horizontal basis: 1 post for blindness/low vision, 1 for deaf and hard of hearing, 2 for locomotor disability, and 1 for multiple disabilities/autism/intellectual disability, as notified under DoPT O.M. No. 36035/02/2017-Estt (Res) dated 15.01.2018. Scientific Assistant 'B' posts total 8 vacancies, of which 1 is reserved for PwBD (category c: locomotor disability). Technician 'B' posts total 18 vacancies. Out of the 7 LDC vacancies, 1 is reserved for PwBD. Out of the 8 Security Guard A vacancies, 4 are reserved for Ex-Servicemen.

SINP Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

The selection process for SINP Recruitment 2026 differs by post category: Scientist 'C' (all posts): Interview; a qualifying-nature written test may also be conducted. Final merit list is prepared from Interview marks only. Scientific Assistant 'B' and Technician 'B' (all posts): Phase I - Written Test (qualifying); Phase II - Trade Test. Final merit list is prepared from Trade Test marks only. Assistant Administrative Officer-I: Written exam and perusal of APAR. Lower Division Clerk: Phase I - Written Test; Phase II - Typing Skill Test (qualifying). Final merit list is prepared from Written Test marks only, among candidates who qualify Phase II. Security Guard A: Phase I - Physical Fitness Test (qualifying); Phase II - Written Test. Final merit list is prepared from Written Test marks only. Work Assistant 'A': MCQ-based Written Test. Merit list prepared from Written Test marks. Halwai-c

A
A total of 62 vacancies are available: 5 Scientist 'C', 8 Scientific Assistant 'B', 18 Technician 'B', 1 AAO-I, 7 LDC, 8 Security Guard A, 11 Work Assistant 'A', and 4 Halwai-cum-Cook.

Qualification varies by post, ranging from 10th pass with a Diploma in Catering (Halwai-cum-Cook) to an M.Sc in Physics/Applied Physics/Optics/Electronics (Scientist 'C'). See the Eligibility Criteria section above for post-wise details.
Q5What is the age limit for SINP Recruitment 2026?

The upper age limit ranges from 25 years (Technician 'B', Halwai-cum-Cook) to 33 years (Scientist 'C'), with age relaxation applicable for SC/ST/OBC/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen candidates as per government norms.

The application fee is Rs. 500 for Scientist 'C' and Scientific Assistant 'B' posts, and Rs. 300 for all other posts. Women, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen, and Deputation candidates are exempted from paying the fee.
