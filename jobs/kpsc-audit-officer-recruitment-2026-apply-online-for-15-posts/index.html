KPSC Audit Officer Eligibility 2026

Candidates who have appeared for an examination whose passing would render them educationally qualified for the Preliminary Examination, but whose results have not yet been declared, are also eligible for admission to the Preliminary Examination. However, all candidates declared qualified for the Main Examination must produce proof of passing the requisite qualifying examination along with their Main Examination application, failing which they will not be admitted to the Main Examination.

KPSC Audit Officer Exam Pattern & Selection Procedure

Selection follows the two-stage process prescribed under Section-1 of Schedule-2 of the Karnataka State Public Services (Recruitment) Rules, 2011: a Preliminary Examination (objective, used to screen candidates for the Main Examination) followed by a Main Examination (written, descriptive), conducted for Group 'B' posts of the Karnataka State Accounts Inspection and Accounts Department. Question papers are set in both Kannada and English. Preliminary exam marks are used only for ranking/shortlisting for the Main Examination and are not added to the final merit. Candidates are admitted to the Main Examination based on the marks obtained in the Preliminary Examination, in the ratio of 1:20 against the number of vacancies notified for each post.   • 5OTR needs to be completed only once; it will be reused for all future KPSC notifications, so fill it in with utmost care.

  • 7Click the "Apply Post" link against this notification; the system will check your eligibility per the criteria in the notification.
  • 8Only candidates found eligible are permitted to proceed with the application; ineligible candidates will see an alert message.
  • 9Read the notification fully, satisfy yourself of your eligibility, and then proceed to submit the application.
  • 10Upload a recent passport-size photograph (under 200 KB) and signature (under 70 KB), both in JPEG/JPG format only.
  • 11Pay the applicable fee via net banking, debit card, credit card, or UPI, and confirm the payment.
