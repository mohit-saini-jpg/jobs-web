How to Check Calicut University Result 2026 Online

  • Visit the official Calicut University result portal: results.uoc.ac.in
  • Look for the link related to "Ninth Semester B.Com., LLB. (Hons.) Regular & Supplementary Examination, 10/2025" on the homepage.
  • Click on the relevant result link.
  • Enter your Registration Number and the security code/captcha displayed on screen.
  • Click the Submit or Get Result button.
  • Your result, showing subject-wise marks and overall status, will be displayed on the screen.
  • Download and save the result page or take a printout for future reference.

What If Your Result Is Not Showing?

  • Double-check that you are selecting the correct examination link - "Ninth Semester B.Com., LLB. (Hons.) Regular & Supplementary Examination, 10/2025" - and not a different semester or session.
  • Verify your Registration Number is entered correctly, without extra spaces.
  • Try refreshing the page or checking again after some time, as result servers can be slow immediately after release due to heavy traffic.
  • If the issue persists, contact the University of Calicut examination section (Pareeksha Bhavan) directly.
