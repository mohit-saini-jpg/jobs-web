About APPSC

The Andhra Pradesh Public Service Commission is based in Vijayawada and conducts examinations for appointment to State posts. APPSC Selection Process 2026

Selection is based on the Written Examination in online Computer based Recruitment Test (CBRT) mode, followed by a Computer Proficiency Test (CPT) that shortlisted candidates must qualify. Written Examination (objective type, degree standard), conducted by the Commission in CBRT mode. The date will be intimated later. After the written examination, candidates provisionally shortlisted in the ratio prescribed by the Commission have to qualify the CPT. Selection is post/post code wise, on merit in the written examination in the concerned subject, subject to qualifying the CPT. Minimum qualifying marks on aggregate: OC & EWS 40%, Backward Class 35%, SCs and STs 30%. Securing the minimum does not confer any right to selection. Appointment is subject to the selected candidate being found medically fit. Where candidates get equal marks, they are bracketed and ranked by age, the oldest first. I

APPSC Exam Pattern 2026

The Written (objective type) Examination is of degree standard, with Paper I common to all posts and Paper II in the subject of the post. The total for each post is 300 questions and 450 marks. Each wrong answer is penalised with 1/3rd of the marks prescribed for the question. Appearance in all papers is compulsory, and absence in any paper disqualifies the candidature. Paper I is set in English and translated into Telugu, with English as the authentic version. Subject papers are in English medium only. The test will be held at Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada, Tirupati and Ananthapuramu, and applicants choose three centre preferences; the Commission may allot any centre. If the CBRT is held in multiple sessions, scores will be normalised and results announced on percentile basis. If questions are deleted, scaling is done for that part of the paper. APPSC Application Correction 2026

Corrections are enabled after the last date of submission and allowed for up to 7 days only from the last date of applications. Each correction costs Rs. 100, and corrections are made in the application form itself. Fields that affect the Name, fee and age relaxation are not enabled for corrections, and no manual application for corrections is entertained. After this period, no change such as community or reservation category is entertained.

APPSC Assistant Director Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Log in to the Commission's website https://psc.ap.gov.in with the registered OTPR number. First-time applicants for any APPSC notification must first fill the One Time Profile Registration (OTPR) to obtain an OTPR ID, which is sent to the registered mobile number and email ID. OTPR alone is not an application.
  • 2Log in with the User Name (OTPR ID) and the password set. Click the "Online Application Submission" link at the bottom right corner of the website, then click "Fill Application" against the notification number to be applied for.
  • 3Check the OTPR data. Fill the application-specific data such as Local/Non-Local status and White card details, which are used to calculate the fee. To change any detail, use the modify OTPR link, save it and click online application submission again.
  • 4Fill the qualification details, post preferences and examination centre, then click "Save & Submit". Only then is the payment link generated; "Save" alone only partly saves the application.
  • 5Pay the fee through the payment gateway. The application is finally submitted on successful payment, and a payment reference ID is generated with an SMS to the registered mobile number.
  • 6If the payment fails, start a fresh payment process as in step 2. The filled application can be downloaded as a PDF for reference.
