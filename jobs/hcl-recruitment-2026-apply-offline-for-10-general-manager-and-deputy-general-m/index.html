HCL GM & DGM Age Limit and Post-Qualification Experience

Age relaxation of 5 years is admissible for SC/ST candidates, 3 years for OBC candidates, 5 years for Ex-Servicemen, and as per Government of India guidelines for PwD candidates - applicable to external applicants, up to the extent that leaves a minimum residual service of 3 years before superannuation. Internal HCL employees applying on a regular basis do not have a maximum age limit, but must have completed at least one year of service in the next-below scale of pay and must meet the prescribed post-qualification experience. Experience is counted only from the date of obtaining the essential qualification, must be in the relevant field (Mining/Metal/Manufacturing industry, or another industry with a supporting experience certificate and job description), and does not include teaching, research fellowship, internship, apprenticeship, consultancy, or academic project experience. HCL GM & DGM Selection Process

Selection for HCL Recruitment 2026 is based solely on performance in the Personal Interview. There is no written examination mentioned in the notification. Candidates are shortlisted for Personal Interview in a ratio of 1:5, subject to a minimum of 10 candidates, or as decided by HCL. If applications received exceed the zone of consideration, shortlisting is done by awarding marks: 1 mark for every completed year of relevant experience beyond the minimum requirement, and up to 2 marks for additional higher qualification in the relevant discipline. In case of a tie, candidates from organisations with higher turnover are ranked higher. HCL reserves the right to interview more or fewer candidates than the standard ratio, and to add further shortlisting criteria if required.

Only Indian Nationals aged 18 years and above are eligible to apply. Appointment is subject to medical fitness and verification of character, antecedents, and caste/category certificates. Q1What is HCL General Manager & Deputy General Manager Recruitment 2026?
A
It is a recruitment drive by Hindustan Copper Limited, under Notification No. Estt./1/2035/2026-27 dated 09/09/2026, to fill 10 posts of General Manager (E8) and Deputy General Manager (E7) in Mining, HR, Finance, and Materials & Contracts disciplines.
Q2How many vacancies are available in HCL GM & DGM Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 10 vacancies in total: 4 General Manager posts (3 Mining, 1 HR) and 6 Deputy General Manager posts (2 Mining, 2 Finance, 1 HR, 1 Materials & Contracts).
Q3What is the last date to apply for HCL Recruitment 2026?
