TSU Selection Process

The University will conduct a Screening Test to shortlist candidates as per requirements; call letters for the screening test/interview will be sent only to shortlisted candidates by email. Interviews will be conducted only in physical mode. Selection for all posts is based on the candidate’s overall record and performance in the interview. The University may restrict the number of candidates called for interview based on the screening test and/or experience higher than the minimum prescribed, as decided by the Scrutiny Committee. Meeting the minimum prescribed qualifications does not, by itself, entitle a candidate to be called for a screening test or interview. No correspondence will be entertained with candidates who are not shortlisted for interview.
