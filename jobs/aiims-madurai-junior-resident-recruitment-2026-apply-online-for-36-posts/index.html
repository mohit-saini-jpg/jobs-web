AIIMS Madurai Junior Resident Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS), Madurai, Tamil Nadu, under the PMSSY Division of the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, invites applications for 36 posts of Junior Resident (Non-Academic) on a temporary basis for 1 year, at Pay Level 10 (56100 – 177500) of the 7th CPC plus usual allowances including NPA (if applicable). Applications are made through a Google Form, which opened on 13 November 2026 and closes on 3 December 2026 at 5.00 PM. Selection is on merit assessed through interview.

About the Institute

AIIMS Madurai is a healthcare Institute of National Importance, established by the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Government of India, under the Pradhan Mantri Swasthya Suraksha Yojna (PMSSY). Its letterhead names JIPMER, Puducherry as the Mentor Institute. Applications are invited by the Executive Director, AIIMS Madurai. The notice says the post will be rolled over for walk in interview, if not filled.

Department-wise Vacancies

The notice lists 36 vacancies for one post, with the following category-wise break-up (16 + 9 + 5 + 2 + 4 = 36). 4% reservation for PwBD on a horizontal basis applies as per Government Rules. Vacancies may increase or decrease at the time of selection. The numbers are provisional and subject to change without any notice. If no application is received under the EWS category, the post may be filled under the UR category. Reservation for ST / SC / OBC / EWS / PwBD candidates is as per Central Govt. Rules.

Pay and Terms

Pay is Pay Level 10 (56100 – 177500) of the 7th CPC plus usual allowances including NPA (if applicable). The appointment is purely temporary and initially for 1 year. It gives no right to regular appointment or permanent absorption in the Institute. The contract expires automatically after 1 year (1 term) unless renewed for another term, up to a maximum of 3 terms, on the recommendation of the concerned HoD and if the service is required by the Institute. The Institute can terminate the appointment at any time without assigning any reason. The employee can leave by giving 01 (one) month notice or salary in lieu thereof. A candidate cannot resign or leave within 03 months from the date of joining.
