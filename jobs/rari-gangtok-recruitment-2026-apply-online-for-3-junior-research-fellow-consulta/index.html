About RARI Gangtok

The Regional Ayurveda Research Institute is located at Tadong, Gangtok, Sikkim - 737102, and works under the Central Council for Research in Ayurvedic Sciences, Ministry of AYUSH, Govt. of India. The institute runs a Peripheral Pharmacovigilance Centre (PPvC), for which the JRF post is advertised for the year 2026-27. The notice is signed for the Assistant Director (Ay) In-Charge.

Pay and Tenure

Consultant (Ayurveda): ₹ 50,000/- per month (consolidated), with no HRA mentioned. Senior Research Fellow (Ayurveda): Rs.42,000/- + HRA (as per rule). Junior Research Fellow (J.R.F): Rs.31,000/- + HRA per month. The HRA rate is not specified in the notice. All three posts will be filled initially for a period of 6 months, which may be curtailed or extended at the discretion of the competent authority, and the engagement will be co-terminus. The engagement is purely on contractual basis, with no right to claim continuation or regular appointment. Leave: maximum 12 days leave, proportionately, in a calendar year.

Selection and Interview

Selection will be through an online/offline written test followed by an interview, and candidates have to appear for both. The walk-in will be held online and offline on 20 November 2026 from 10.00 am at the Conference hall of Regional Ayurveda Research Institute, Gangtok. Candidates reporting for the walk-in interview after 10.30 am shall not be allowed. The selection committee has the right to select or reject the application of a candidate.
