Madras High Court DEO Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

Recruitment is made purely on merit basis. Candidates are cautioned against touts and agents who may indulge in cheating by making false promises of securing appointment through unfair means; the Madras High Court/District Court will not be responsible for any loss arising from such activity. Candidates indulging in unfair means, or approaching the Recruitment Body in person or through anyone, will be disqualified from appointment. Candidates are solely responsible for their claims made in the online application, and cannot blame internet café/browsing centre/Common Service Centre staff for mistakes made while applying. Candidates should not be a party to any kind of canvassing, should not have been debarred/disqualified by the Madras High Court or any other recruiting agency, and should not be a dismissed Government employee. No individual communication regarding the date and time of the Written-cum-Skill Test, Viva Voce, and certificate verification will be issued - candidates must regularly check https://www.mhc.tn.gov.in/esk_rec and the respective District Court website. The post is purely on contractual basis; candidates should not claim any permanency in the future, and Madras High Court can terminate the employment of a selected candidate at any time without assigning any reason. Selected candidates are eligible for only 12 days of casual leave per year, with no sandwich leave permitted (e.g., if a national holiday falls on Thursday or Tuesday, leave cannot be taken on the adjoining Friday or Monday). Salary will be deducted for any absence or leave beyond the permissible leave. Candidates should not claim any transfer from one District to another, and must attend the office irrespective of Court hours, including odd hours and Court holidays, if required. No TA/DA is payable for attending the interview, or for attending an additional e-Sewa Kendra beyond the candidate's allotted one. The Written-cum-Skill Test and Viva Voce will be conducted at the campus of the Principal District Court of the concerned district, or any other place notified by the District Court - requests for change of venue will not be entertained.
