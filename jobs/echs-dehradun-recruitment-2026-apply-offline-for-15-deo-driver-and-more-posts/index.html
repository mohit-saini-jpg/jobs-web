ECHS Dehradun Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Ex-Servicemen Contributory Health Scheme (ECHS), Dehradun has released a recruitment notification for 15 vacancies of Medical, Para Medical, and Non-Medical Staff posts on a contractual basis. The recruitment includes posts such as Officer In Charge, Medical Specialist, Medical Officer, Lab Technician, Lab Assistant, Pharmacist, Data Entry Operator, Driver, and House Keeper. Eligible candidates possessing qualifications ranging from Literate and Class 8 to Graduate, MBBS, MD/MS/DNB, and B.Pharmacy can apply for the respective posts.
