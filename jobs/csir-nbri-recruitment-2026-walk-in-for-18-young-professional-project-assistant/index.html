CSIR NBRI Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection is through a walk-in interview. However, if a large number of candidates turn up, a screening test comprising multiple-choice objective questions may be conducted to shortlist candidates for the interview. Shortlisted candidates will be interviewed by the Selection Committee, and a panel of recommended candidates will be formed based on interview performance. This panel will be utilised as and when requirements arise in the relevant field/project. Candidates have the option to interact with the committee in Hindi or English. If a large number of candidates attend, the interview may continue into the next day.

CSIR NBRI Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1There is no online application for this recruitment. Eligible candidates must attend the walk-in interview in person on the date allotted to their area of interest.
  • 2Check the eligibility criteria for the relevant post and area carefully before proceeding.
  • 3Download the application form in advance from www.nbri.res.in, or collect it at the venue on the day of the interview.
  • 4Reach the venue - K. N. Kaul Block, CSIR-NBRI, Rana Pratap Marg, Lucknow - 226001 - at least one hour before the scheduled time (11:00 AM) on the date applicable to the chosen area.
  • 5Carry one latest photograph, original documents, self-attested copies of all certificates from matriculation onwards, date of birth proof, and experience certificates.
  • 6Note that tentative mark sheets/certificates and "result awaited" documents will not be accepted.
  • 7Appear for document verification, followed by a screening test (if applicable) and/or the interview.
  • 8Only candidates who strictly fulfil the eligibility criteria and hold the required degree/mark sheet in the prescribed subjects should attend the walk-in interview. No interim queries will be entertained by CSIR-NBRI regarding this recruitment.
