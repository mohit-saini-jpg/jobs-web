NGEL Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

NGEL may conduct a multi-stage selection process comprising application shortlisting/screening, a Computer-Based Test (CBT), Group Discussion and/or Interview, or a combination of these, and reserves the right to raise the minimum eligibility standards if required. The CBT is bilingual with multiple-choice questions and has two sections: Section 1 – Subject Knowledge Test (SKT) (discipline-specific questions) and Section 2 – Aptitude Test (AT) (numerical ability, reading comprehension, reasoning, general awareness, data interpretation). The test has 100 questions and a duration of 2 hours . There is negative marking : ¼ of the marks allotted to a question is deducted for every wrong answer. Online CBT centres: Kolkata, Chennai, Delhi, Mumbai, Vishakhapatnam, Gandhinagar/Ahmedabad, Lucknow, Jaipur, Bhopal and Raipur. Shortlisted candidates from the CBT are called for an interview.
