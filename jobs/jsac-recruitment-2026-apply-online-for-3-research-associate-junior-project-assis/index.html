JSAC Eligibility Criteria

The candidate must be a Citizen of India. All candidates must fulfill eligibility criteria on or before 01.10.2026.
PostQualification Required
Research Associate (RA)Minimum Essential: Ph.D in Remote Sensing & GIS / Geo-informatics / Geology / Forestry / Geophysics / Earth & Atmospheric Sciences / Agriculture / Water Engineering and Management / Civil Engineering / Environmental Science or equivalent from recognized university OR having 3 years of research, teaching and design and development experience after M.Sc / ME / M.Tech with first class in above mentioned discipline. Additional Requirement: One research paper in Science Citation Indexed (SCI) journal. Desirable: Proficiency in image processing & GIS software and DGPS Survey; Experience in Data Collection through drone survey and DGPS and processing of data; DGCA approved Remote Pilot Certificate (RPC) holder for flying of drone will be preferred.
Junior Project Assistant (JPA)Minimum Essential: M.Sc or M.Tech in Remote Sensing & GIS / Geo-informatics / Geology / Forestry / Geophysics/Earth & Atmospheric Sciences / Agriculture / Water Engineering and Management / Civil Engineering / Environmental Science or equivalent with first class from recognized university. Desirable: Working experience in image processing and GIS software.

JSAC Important Instructions

The appointment will be purely contractual on project basis for a minimum period of one year and may be extended as per project guidelines. Reservation rules of the Government of Jharkhand apply only to BC-I/BC-II, SC, and ST candidates of Jharkhand State. Reserved candidates from other states are treated as General candidates. If applying for more than one post, candidates must submit a separate application and pay the prescribed fee separately for each post. Incomplete applications, applications without required attachments, and those received after the due date will be summarily rejected. No TA/DA will be admissible for attending the written test or interview. The place of posting is Ranchi, but the incumbent is liable to be posted anywhere in Jharkhand State as and when required.
