BHEL Trichy Apprentice Recruitment 2026 Notification Out

BHEL Trichy has issued the Apprentice Recruitment 2026-27 Batch 2 notification on 21 November 2026 for 214 apprenticeship training vacancies. BHEL Trichy Apprentice Stipend 2026

Selected candidates under BHEL Trichy Apprentice Recruitment 2026 will receive a monthly stipend according to their apprentice category and trade. The highest stipend is Rs.12,300 per month for Graduate Apprentices, followed by Rs.10,900 for Technician Apprentices. Trade Apprentices will receive Rs.10,560 or Rs.11,040, depending on the trade. Note: These amounts are monthly apprenticeship stipends, not permanent employee salaries. The apprenticeship engagement is for one year, and completion of training does not guarantee employment with BHEL.
