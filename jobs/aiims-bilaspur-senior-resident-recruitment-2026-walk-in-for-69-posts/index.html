AIIMS Bilaspur Senior Resident Vacancy 2026

A total of 69 Senior Resident (Non-Academic) posts are vacant, divided into Broad Specialty (42 posts) and Super Specialty (27 posts) departments. In case of non-availability of EWS candidates, the post shall be filled under the UR category. Unreserved category candidates can also apply against reserved posts; if considered due to unavailability of a reserved-category candidate, their engagement in such cases will be for a period of 44 days only from the date of joining. 4% of the total posts are reserved for Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD) (non-surgical jobs only) among Senior Resident posts.

AIIMS Bilaspur Senior Resident Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must fulfil the following essential qualifications to apply for the AIIMS Bilaspur Senior Resident (Non-Academic) walk-in interview. A medical qualification included in the first or second schedule, or Part II of the third schedule, of the Indian Medical Council Act 1956 (candidates with Part II third schedule qualifications must also fulfil the conditions of Section 13(3) of the Act). Must be registered with the Central/State Medical Council. A postgraduate degree, i.e., MD/MS/DNB as per MCI rule in the specialty concerned, or its equivalent from a recognized University/Institute. Candidates must hold a valid qualification (pass certificate) as on the last date for receipt of applications. AIIMS Bilaspur Senior Resident Age Limit

Upper Age Limit: 45 years (age as on crucial date) Note: The notification states the upper age limit is determined as on the last date of submission of online applications; however, this recruitment is conducted via walk-in interview with no online application form specified, so candidates should confirm the exact crucial date with AIIMS Bilaspur. Date of Birth as recorded in the Matriculation/Secondary Examination Certificate will be accepted for determining age, and no subsequent request for change will be considered. No age relaxation is available to reserved category candidates applying against unreserved vacancies. Permissible age relaxation as per Government orders will be allowed subject to submission of documentary proof of the applicable category.

AIIMS Bilaspur Senior Resident Selection Process

Walk-in Interview will be held at the Administrative Block, 3rd Floor, AIIMS Bilaspur, Kothipura, Himachal Pradesh-174037. Candidates are required to report on or before 09:00 AM on 21.08.2026. Candidates must appear physically before the interview board; no request for an online interview will be entertained. Based on verification of original documents, the Screening Committee may shortlist candidates for interview. Only original certificates of candidates selected for the post of Senior Resident will be verified, including MBBS degree certificate, Internship Completion Certificate, qualifying degree certificate, Medical Registration Certificate, Date of Birth Certificate/10th Standard Certificate, Caste Certificate, and other relevant certificates.

AIIMS Bilaspur Senior Resident Walk-in Interview Process

Since this is a walk-in recruitment, candidates are NOT required to fill any online application form. The process to be followed is: Prepare a duly filled and signed original application form. Fill and keep ready Annexure-II (Proforma/Checklist for the post of Senior Resident), to be submitted during document verification before the interview. Carry one set of self-attested photocopies of Degrees, Certificates, Mark sheets, Age proof, Caste certificates, PwBD certificate (if applicable), and Publications (Pub-Med, Non-PubMed)/Awards, if any. Carry all original documents for verification at the time of the interview. Pay the application fee (if applicable) via NEFT and carry proof of payment. Report in person at the Recruitment Cell, 3rd Floor, Administrative Block, AIIMS Bilaspur, Kothipura, Himachal Pradesh, on or before 09:00 AM on 21.08.2026.
