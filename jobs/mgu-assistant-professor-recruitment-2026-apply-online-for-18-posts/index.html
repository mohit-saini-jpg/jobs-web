About MGU Recruitment 2026

Mahatma Gandhi University is located at Priyadarsini Hills P.O., Kottayam, Kerala - 686 560, and its letterhead states that it was established by the Kerala State Legislature by Notification No. 3431/Leg. C1/85/Law, dated 17th April 1985. According to the notice, the University is implementing integrated undergraduate programmes and strengthening advanced and interdisciplinary research in the Sciences, Humanities and Social Sciences. The University had earlier invited applications for Assistant Professor through notifications 67435/AD A2-1/2024/AD A 2(3) dated 01.11.2025, 67435/AD A2-1/2024/AD A 2 dated 28.11.2025 (extension of time for submission of applications) and 67435/AD A2-1/2024/AD A 2(PART-1)(1) dated 27.02.2026. To ensure wider participation of eligible and academically qualified candidates, the recruitment has now been re-notified.

Qualification and Experience

Qualifications are as per UGC Regulations, 2018. The notice prints the following qualification for the Assistant Professor posts: Option A OR Option B The Ph.D. degree has been obtained from a foreign university/institution with a ranking among top 500 in the World University Ranking (at any time) by any one of: (i) Quacquarelli Symonds (QS), (ii) the Times Higher Education (THE) or (iii) the Academic Ranking of World Universities (ARWU) of the Shanghai Jiao Tong University (Shanghai). Applicants who have obtained degrees from Universities/Institutions outside the State of Keralam must submit an equivalence certificate/certificate of eligibility issued by Mahatma Gandhi University, Kottayam. Applicants who earn additional qualifications may submit them as hard copy before the last date of submission. The notice does not mention any teaching or research experience requirement. Candidates

Pay and Terms

The notice does not mention allowances, the nature of appointment or a probation period. MGU Assistant Professor Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Submit the online application through the link on the Mahatma Gandhi University website www.www.mgu.ac.in from 18 November 2026 onwards, on or before 17 December 2026.
  • 2Pay the registration fee online through the facility provided in the online application form.
  • 3Prepare two hard copies of the duly completed online application.
  • 4Attach self-attested true copies of all certificates and documents in proof of qualification, age, community, reservation (if any), experience, NOC, equivalency and eligibility, along with the copy of the fee receipt.
  • 5Send the hard copies to: Deputy Registrar-II (Admn.), Mahatma Gandhi University, Priyadarsini Hills P.O, Kottayam-686 560. They must reach this office on or before 31 December 2026.
  • 6Teachers working in private colleges under the direct payment system must have their applications forwarded by the Principal to the respective Zonal Deputy Director of Collegiate Education, who will forward them to the University.
  • 7The notice asks candidates to submit the online application early enough that the hard copy reaches the University well within the time limit. Late and defective applications will be summarily rejected.

MGU Assistant Professor Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in MGU Recruitment 2026?
A
Mahatma Gandhi University, Kottayam has re-notified 18 Assistant Professor posts across nine Schools, one post in each listed row.
Q2What is the last date to apply for the MGU Assistant Professor posts?
A
The online application must be submitted by 17 December 2026, and two hard copies must reach the Deputy Registrar-II (Admn.) by 31 December 2026.
Q3What is the qualification for MGU Assistant Professor?
A
As per UGC Regulations, 2018: a Master's degree with 55% marks in a concerned/relevant/allied subject plus NET/SLET/SET (with Ph.D. exemptions), or a Ph.D. from a foreign university ranked among the top 500 by QS, THE or ARWU.
Q4What is the age limit for MGU Assistant Professor 2026?
A
Candidates must be not more than 50 years as on 01.01.2026, with usual relaxation as per rules and 3 years' relaxation for regular teachers presently employed in Universities, Aided and Government Colleges in Keralam.
Q5What is the salary of an MGU Assistant Professor?
A
The post is in Academic Level 10 with a Rationalised Entry pay of ₹57,700/-.
Q6What is the application fee for MGU Recruitment 2026?
A
The registration fee is ₹2000/-, and ₹1000/- for SC/ST candidates, payable only online through the online application form.
Q7How do I apply for MGU Assistant Professor posts?
A
Apply online at www.facultyrecruitment.mgu.ac.in by 17 December 2026, then send two hard copies with self-attested certificates and the fee receipt to the Deputy Registrar-II (Admn.), Mahatma Gandhi University, Priyadarsini Hills P.O, Kottayam-686 560, to reach by 31 December 2026.
Q8Do candidates who applied under the earlier notifications need to apply again?
A
No. Those who applied under the notifications dated 01.11.2025, 28.11.2025 and 27.02.2026 need not apply again, and may submit additional qualifications as hard copy before the last date.
Q9Can OC candidates apply for the OC/EWS Physics post?
A
Yes. If no eligible OC-EWS candidate is available, OC (Open Competition) candidates will be considered, so OC candidates may also apply for that post.
Q10Can candidates from outside Kerala apply for community-reserved posts?
A
No. Natives of States other than Kerala State are not eligible for posts notified exclusively under the community reservation quota.
