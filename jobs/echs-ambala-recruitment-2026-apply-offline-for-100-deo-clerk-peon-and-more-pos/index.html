ECHS Ambala Cantt Eligibility Criteria

Minimum qualification differs by post - ranging from a basic literacy/8th-pass requirement for posts like Safaiwala and Chowkidar, to professional degrees such as MBBS for Medical Officer, BDS for Dental Officer, and MD/MS/DNB for Medical Specialist. Most Para-Medical and Non-Medical posts also require a specific Armed Forces trade course or equivalent civilian diploma along with a minimum number of years of relevant experience. Full post-wise qualification details are listed in the vacancy table above. Each post carries two age figures in the notification: the age limit "for employment" and a higher age limit "for contractual service." Age limits range from 53-55 years (Driver, Clerk, Data Entry Operator, Receptionist, Peon, Chowkidar, Safaiwala) to 68-70 years (Medical Specialist, Gynaecologist, Radiologist). ECHS Ambala Cantt Selection Process

The selection process, as per the notification, involves the following stages: Screening of applications against the minimum qualification, age and experience criteria for each post. Only screened candidates meeting the qualitative requirements will be called telephonically for an interview. Candidates must appear for the interview with all original certificates, mark sheets and documents. No TA/DA (travelling/dearness allowance) is admissible for attending the interview. Selected candidates for the posts of Medical Officer, Radiologist, Gynaecologist and Dental Officer will be required to undergo 10 days of On-the-Job Training (OJT)/handing-taking over and obtain a certificate from a Medical Officer of the ECHS Polyclinic before signing the contract.
