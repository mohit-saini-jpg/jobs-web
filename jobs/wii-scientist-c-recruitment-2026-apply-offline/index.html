WII Scientist-C Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Holders of a Ph.D. awarded in Wildlife Sciences can apply for 03 Scientist-C posts at the Wildlife Institute of India (WII), Dehradun, an autonomous institution of the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, Government of India. The posts are filled on a Direct Recruitment (D.R.) basis at Pay Level-11 (₹ 67,700 – 2,08,700), and the application, in the Annexure-3 format, must be sent by Registered/Speed Post so that it is received by 02 December 2026 up to 1700hrs (09 December 2026 for applicants from abroad and the listed remote areas).

Who Can Apply

Applications are invited from eligible Indian citizens. Area 1: Assessment of wildlife habitats (terrestrial/aquatic), including use of RS & GIS, with at least 02 publications in peer-reviewed journals. Area 2: Ecology or conservation of endangered species, with at least 02 publications in peer-reviewed journals. Area 3: Humanities, Indian Knowledge Systems, or biodiversity conservation, including experience in human-protected area interface & implementation of livelihood extension, with at least 02 publications in peer-reviewed journals. Professional experience in the requisite discipline/area will be counted only after acquiring the mandatory minimum educational qualification and must be supported by credible documentary evidence. Pay and Tenure

Pay: Level-11 (₹ 67,700 – 2,08,700). Selected candidates will be placed on probation for a period of two years; satisfactory completion of probation is mandatory for confirmation. Candidates holding a degree in Veterinary Science are not entitled to Non-Practicing Allowance (NPA). The headquarters for the post is WII, Dehradun; however, selected candidates are liable to serve anywhere in India as per functional requirements.

WII Scientist-C Job Description

Teaching and training. Extensive field research requiring travel to remote forest interiors, high-altitude areas and islands.

Selection and Interview

Selection is through screening and shortlisting, a written examination and a personal interview, with a final merit list based on aggregated performance. Applications will be evaluated by a Screening Committee. Fulfilling the minimum eligibility criteria does not automatically entitle an applicant to be called for the test/interview. Shortlisting for interviews will maintain a 1:5 ratio (05 candidates per vacancy), and the institute may at any time increase or decrease this ratio. The written examination comprises subject-specific Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) with 1/3rd negative marking for incorrect answers. Minimum qualifying marks are 50% for UR/EWS/OBC and 40% for SC/ST/PwBD.
