About BRIC-inStem

The Institute for Stem Cell Science and Regenerative Medicine (BRIC-inStem), located at the GKVK Campus, Bellary Road, Bangalore, is an autonomous institute under the Biotechnology Research and Innovation Council of the Department of Biotechnology. Its mandate is to establish collaborative research programmes in stem cell biology, regeneration and repair, in development and disease, with translational emphasis. The posts are at its Centre for Research, Application and Training in Embryology (CReATE), which is dedicated to fundamental and translational research in early development, using human stem cell-based models and allied approaches for applications in reproductive health, congenital anomaly prevention and regenerative medicine. The institute invites candidates in Human Embryology, Reproductive Biology, Stem Cell Biology and Regenerative Medicine.

Vacancy Details

BRIC-inStem has advertised 4 posts in total, filled in the scientific cadre on Direct Recruitment, failing which by Deputation (Including Short Term Contract) basis. The institute reserves the right to withdraw any or all posts, to fill or not to fill them, and to increase or decrease the number of posts.

Pay and Tenure

Scientist-E carries Pay Level 13 and Scientist-D carries Pay Level 12 in the 7th CPC Pay Matrix (Entry Level). Other benefits named in the notice are Dearness Allowance, House Rent Allowance (in lieu of accommodation), Transport Allowance, Children Education Allowance, Leave Travel Concession and Medical Facilities, as applicable to the institute from time to time. The selected candidate will be covered under the National Pension System (NPS). There is a probation period of 2 years for the posts. The notice states that generous start-up support is provided, and extramural funding is encouraged. Selected candidates may be posted and transferred anywhere in India as per the requirement of the institute.

BRIC-inStem Recruitment 2026 Job Description

The notice gives one set of expectations for all faculty posts (Scientist-E and Scientist-D): Undertake high-quality research and contribute to the mandate of the institute. Mentor Ph.D. students and postdoctoral fellows. Contribute to the teaching and outreach activities of BRIC-inStem.

Selection and Interview

Merely meeting the minimum eligibility does not entitle a candidate to be called for assessment. Shortlisted candidates will be invited for a seminar, interaction and an interview. The institute reserves the right to determine the mode of screening and evaluation for shortlisting and selection. Candidates shortlisted for interview must produce original documents for verification of reservation category, educational qualification, experience and other claims. Candidates employed in Government/PSUs/State Government/Autonomous Organizations, and Departmental Candidates, must produce a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the present employer at the time of interview, failing which they will not be allowed to appear in the interview.
