About CSIR-SERC

CSIR-Structural Engineering Research Centre is a National Laboratory under the aegis of the Council of Scientific & Industrial Research (CSIR). The notice describes it as one of the world's premier institutes engaged in Research & Development in Structural Engineering. It is located at CSIR Road, Taramani, Chennai 600 113, and the Project Associates will work in various projects at SERC, Chennai.

Who Can Apply

The upper age limit for every position is 35 years as on the date of interview. The upper age limit can be relaxed by five years for SC/ST/Women/Persons with Disabilities (PWD) and by 3 years for OBC. Prescribed qualifications must come from recognized Universities/institutions. Candidates must meet the essential qualifications and other conditions as on the date of interview. Each position has its own essential and desirable qualifications, listed below in the notice's order. Essential: B.E/B.Tech (Civil/Mechanical Engineering or its equivalent) from a recognized University/Institute and 2 years of Experience in Research and Development in Industrial and Academic Institutions or Science and Technology Organisations and Scientific activities and services. Desirable: M.E/M.Tech/M.S (Structural Engineering/Railway Engineering/Structural Dynamics or its equivalent) from a recognized Univers

Pay and Tenure

HRA is paid on top of the monthly emoluments for every position. All positions are purely temporary, on a contract/temporary basis, and co-terminus with the completion of the project. The tenure of each position runs up to March 2027 and is extendable up to the date shown in the vacancy table. The tenure may be curtailed or extended. It ends with the project, or when the requirement for Project Associate-I & II ends, whichever is earlier. Under CSIR letter No. 5-1(342)/2017-PD dated 02.09.2024, the total engagement of a Project Staff in different projects of CSIR Labs/Instts. cannot exceed 6 years. Candidates who have already completed 6 years as Project Staff in any CSIR Lab/Instt. are not eligible to attend the walk-in interview. Q4What qualification is needed for CSIR-SERC Project Associate posts?
