About AIIMS Kalyani Senior Resident

AIIMS Kalyani is a statutory body under the aegis of the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India. It is located at NH-34 Connector, Basantapur, Saguna, Kalyani, District Nadia, West Bengal 741245. The notice describes it as an Institute of National Importance and one of the new AIIMS set up under the Pradhan Mantri Swasthya Suraksha Yojana (PMSSY) to correct regional imbalance in tertiary healthcare and to reach self-sufficiency in graduate, postgraduate and higher medical education and training.

Department-wise Vacancies

AIIMS Kalyani has 12 Senior Resident (Non-Academic) vacancies across five departments. The notice gives the category break-up only for the total, not for each department: 4% of the total vacancies are horizontally reserved for Persons with Disabilities (PwD)-OPH (OL) category candidates, as permissible under the Rules. The physical requirements for these posts are S (Sitting), ST (Standing), W (Walking), MF (Manipulation by Fingers) and RW (Reading and Writing). Reservation will be as per Government of India Policy. The Competent Authority of AIIMS Kalyani reserves the right to vary the vacancies, including reserved vacancies, and AIIMS reserves the right to increase or decrease the number of vacancies. Pay and Terms

Allowances and perks are admissible as per the rules, in addition to Pay and NPA (for medical candidates only). All the posts carry the usual allowances admissible to Central Government Employees of similar status at Kalyani (West Bengal). AIIMS Kalyani will try to provide residential accommodation, subject to availability, but campus accommodation is limited and may not be possible in every case. Where no accommodation is provided, HRA as admissible to Central Government Servants of similar status stationed at Kalyani, West Bengal will be provided. Nature of appointment: Senior Resident (Non-Academic), a tenure post on contract for a maximum period of 3 years as per the Central Residency Scheme. Rules: The post is regulated by the provisions of the Central Residency Scheme and governed by the CCS (Temporary Service) Rules. Service under the Institute is governed by the Act of Parliament

AIIMS Kalyani Senior Resident Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many Senior Resident vacancies are there at AIIMS Kalyani in 2026?
A
There are 12 vacancies: Dermatology 2, Microbiology 1, General Surgery 4, Pharmacology 2 and Pathology 3. By category they are EWS - 1, OBC - 9 and ST - 2.
Q2What is the last date to apply for the AIIMS Kalyani Senior Resident posts?
A
The online application form is available up to 9 November 2026, 05:00 PM. No hard copy of the application will be accepted.
Q3What qualification is required for AIIMS Kalyani Senior Resident?
A
The notice requires a Post Graduation Medical Degree (MD/MS/DNB) or Equivalent in the respective discipline, and also lists DM/MCh /DNB or Equivalent in the respective discipline, in each case from a recognized University/Institute as provided under the Residency Scheme.
Q4What is the age limit for AIIMS Kalyani Senior Resident?
A
The age limit is not exceeding 45 years as on the date of publishing the notification on the Institute website. The relaxation is 05 years for SC/ST, 03 years for OBC and 10 years for PwD (OPH).
Q5What is the salary of an AIIMS Kalyani Senior Resident?
A
The pay is Rs.15,600-39,100 + GP 6,600 (6th CPC), which is Level 11 of the 7th CPC Pay Matrix, plus NPA for Medical Graduates. Allowances and perks are paid as per the rules.
Q6What is the application fee for AIIMS Kalyani Senior Resident?
A
The fee is Rs.1000/- for Unreserved/OBC candidates and Rs.500/- for SC/ST/EWS candidates. PWBD candidates are exempted. The fee is paid by NEFT and is not refundable.
Q7How is the AIIMS Kalyani Senior Resident selection done?
A
Selection is based only on performance in the interview, which is held after the applications and documents are verified. A merit list with selected and waiting candidates is prepared from the personal interview.
Q8When and where is the AIIMS Kalyani Senior Resident interview?
A
The interview is from 19 November 2026 to 20 November 2026, 09.30 AM onward, in hybrid mode. Offline candidates go to Academic Block -1, Ground Floor, Academic Section of AIIMS, Kalyani, Pin -741245. Online candidates receive a joining link.
Q9Is the AIIMS Kalyani Senior Resident job permanent?
A
No. It is a tenure post on contract for a maximum period of 3 years as per the Central Residency Scheme, governed by the CCS (Temporary Service) Rules.
Q10Can I apply for more than one department at AIIMS Kalyani?
A
Yes. A candidate can apply in more than one of the listed departments, but must fill a separate form and pay a separate application fee for each.
