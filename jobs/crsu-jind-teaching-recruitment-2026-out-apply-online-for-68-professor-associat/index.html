CRSU Teaching Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Candidates applying for CRSU teaching positions must satisfy the qualifications prescribed for the relevant post and discipline. The official bulletins specify different academic and professional requirements for Assistant Professor, Associate Professor and Professor posts. Under the general eligibility route specified in the official bulletin, candidates must have a master's degree with at least 55% marks or an equivalent grade in the concerned, relevant or allied subject from a recognized university. Applicants must also have qualified the UGC NET, CSIR NET or another recognized eligibility test such as SET/SLET, unless they qualify for an exemption under the applicable UGC Ph.D. regulations. The bulletin also provides an alternative eligibility route for candidates holding a Ph.D. from a foreign university or institution ranked among the world's top 500 by a recognized ranking organiz

CRSU Teaching Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection for CRSU teaching posts will involve scrutiny of applications, verification of eligibility and assessment through the prescribed interview process. According to the recruitment bulletin, eligible candidates will be evaluated during interviews on subject knowledge, communication skills, teaching abilities and strategies, research potential and scholarly contributions. Final selection decisions are based on candidates' interview performance. The university states that the mere possession of minimum qualifications does not guarantee an interview call. Candidates must satisfy the relevant eligibility, screening and document-verification requirements.

CRSU Teaching Recruitment 2026 NOC Requirements

Candidates who are already employed must apply through the proper channel and upload a No Objection Certificate signed by their employer in the format prescribed in Annexure-F. The university allows candidates to submit an advance copy in the circumstances specified in the bulletin. However, such candidates must produce the required NOC before the interview. Applicants should read the official NOC instructions carefully because failure to follow the proper procedure may affect recognition of previous employment benefits.
