TCGL Recruitment 2026 - General Terms and Conditions

The engagement shall be purely on an outsource basis. TCGL/Ethos HR Management & Projects Limited reserves the right to accept or reject any application, modify eligibility criteria, increase or decrease vacancies, or cancel the recruitment process without assigning any reason. TCGL Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is TCGL Recruitment 2026?
A
It is a walk-in interview recruitment drive by Tourism Corporation of Gujarat Limited (TCGL), a Government of Gujarat undertaking, for 47 outsourced posts, conducted through Ethos HR Management & Projects Limited.
Q2How many vacancies are there in TCGL Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 47 vacancies across 29 posts and 15 branches, including Audit, Commercial, Event, Finance, HR & Admin, IT, Land & Legal, Market Research, Project Branch, and Skill Development.
Q3When is the TCGL walk-in interview?
A
Technical posts will be interviewed on 07-09-2026, and Administrative posts on 08-09-2026, both at Gandhinagar. The Project Engineer (Ayodhya) post has a separate walk-in on 07-09-2026 at Ayodhya.
Q4Where is the TCGL walk-in interview venue?
A
Most posts will be interviewed at Tourism Corporation of Gujarat Limited, Block No. 16/17, 4th Floor, Udhyogbhavan, Sector 11, Gandhinagar. The Project Engineer (Ayodhya) post will be interviewed at the Tourist Information Bureau, Ayodhya Cantt.
Q5What is the qualification required for TCGL Recruitment 2026?
A
Qualification varies by post, ranging from a Bachelor's degree in any discipline for Office Assistant posts to MBA/PG Diploma, LLB/LLM, or B.E./B.Tech qualifications for Executive, Sr. Executive, and Engineering posts, each with a specified number of years of relevant experience.
Q6What is the remuneration for TCGL Recruitment 2026 posts?
A
Remuneration ranges from ₹18,000 per month for Office Assistant posts to ₹55,000 per month for the Project Engineer (Ayodhya) post.
Q7What is the selection process for TCGL Recruitment 2026?
A
Selection is through a walk-in interview with document verification. Mandatory registration at the venue is required, and mere attendance at the interview does not guarantee selection.
Q8Is the TCGL recruitment for permanent government employment?
A
No. The notification specifies that engagement is purely on an outsource basis, to be deputed at Tourism Corporation of Gujarat Limited.
