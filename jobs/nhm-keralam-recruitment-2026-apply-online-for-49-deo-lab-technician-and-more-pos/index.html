National Health Mission, Keralam has 49 contract vacancies open until 5 pm on 7 November 2026. About NHM

This recruitment comes from National Health Mission, Keralam, through its State Programme Monitoring & Support Unit at NHM Building, General Hospital Junction, Thiruvananthapuram. The State Health & Family Welfare Society (Arogyakeralam) handles the contracts and interviews, and the notice is issued by the State Mission Director (NHM).

NHM Keralam Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection is based on qualification, experience and performance in the interview. Incomplete applications are not considered for short listing. The posts are for immediate requirement, and the Society may prioritize interviews for different categories of posts based on its needs. On the recommendation of a selection committee, the Society may also ask candidates to demonstrate skills in the area or specialization concerned. Originals of documents may be required at the interview. Providing a reference, especially the names of superior officers from previous organisations, is described as ideal.

Service Conditions

Appointment is on contract, initially for three months. The Society may extend it at its discretion, based on performance and integrity. The successful candidate signs a contract with the Society in the format of Annexure-I, uploaded on the website. Posts are temporary during the period of operation of the Mission, with no claim for permanency of job or labour claims against NHM, DHS or the Government. Candidates must be willing to relocate anywhere in the State and to work in any District of Kerala decided by the Society. All selected candidates are governed by the NHM rules and regulations in force from time to time. Opportunities for advanced training will be available. Canvassing in any form leads to automatic disqualification.

NHM Keralam Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1Which post has the lowest educational requirement?
A
The Secretarial Assistant post asks for a Matriculate pass, along with shorthand at 80 wpm, typing at 40 wpm and 2-3 years of experience as an office assistant.
Q2Can a Plus two pass candidate apply as Lab Technician?
A
Plus two alone is not enough. The 4 Lab Technician posts also require a Diploma or certified course in Medical Laboratory Technology or equivalent.
Q3Which posts pay Rs. 78,000 a month?
A
Assistant Programme Officer/Epidemiologist (TB) pays Rs. 78,000. Microbiologist - IRL pays the same to an MD Microbiologist, but Rs. 30,000 to a non medical candidate.
Q4Do I need to post a hard copy of my application?
A
No. The scanned documents go by email as one PDF file, and no physical copy needs to reach the NHM State office.
Q5Can I pay the fee by demand draft?
A
No. The Rs. 250/- must be deposited in the ICICI Bank account, and a demand draft will not be accepted.
Q6How long is the contract?
A
The first contract runs for three months. Any extension depends on the Society's decision, based on performance and integrity.
Q7Will I be posted in my home district?
A
The notice makes no such promise. Candidates must be willing to work in any District of Kerala as decided by the Society.
Q8How will I get the interview call letter?
A
The call letter and all other communication from the Society come only through email. Updates and announcements appear on the website, which should be checked regularly.
Q9Is travel allowance paid for attending the interview?
A
No TA/DA will be paid for the interview.
Q10Can I call the office with questions?
A
The notice asks candidates to avoid phoning the head office as far as possible. The Society answers queries raised by email.
