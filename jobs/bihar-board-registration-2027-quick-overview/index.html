Bihar Board Registration 2027 - Quick Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 01/10/2026

The Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB) has extended the deadline to complete the registration and examination form process for the Class 10 (Matric) and Class 12 (Intermediate) annual exams 2027. The new last date is October 15, 2026. Students who missed earlier registration can also apply during a special window from October 1 to October 6, 2026 by paying the prescribed late fee.

How to Complete Bihar Board 2027 Registration and Exam Form

Students do not fill the form on their own. The online process is carried out by the head of the school or college. Here is what students should do:

  • Contact your school or college head or office immediately.
  • Check that your name, date of birth, parents' names, subjects and category are correct in the details the school will enter.
  • Pay the fee amount your school asks for, and keep the receipt.
  • Ask the school to submit the form on the relevant portal before the deadline.
  • If you missed the earlier registration, ask the school to use the special registration window between October 1 and 6, 2026.
  • Collect and keep a copy of the submitted form or confirmation from the school.

Important Instructions for Students

  • Do not wait for the last day. Schools handle many forms, and portals can slow down near deadlines.
  • Check every detail before submission. Corrections may not be easy after the form is submitted.
  • Pay fees only through the channel your school or the official portal specifies.
  • Keep fee receipts and form confirmation safe.
  • Rely only on notices from the official BSEB website and your school.
