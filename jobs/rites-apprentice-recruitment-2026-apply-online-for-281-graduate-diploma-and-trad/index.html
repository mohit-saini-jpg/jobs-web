RITES Apprentice OBC and EWS Certificate Requirements

OBC Non-Creamy Layer candidates must submit a valid caste certificate in the prescribed Government of India Central format, issued on or after 5 November 2025 . EWS candidates must provide the latest applicable EWS certificate in the prescribed format. Candidates claiming SC, ST, OBC-NCL, EWS or PwBD reservation must submit valid documents supporting their claim.

RITES Apprentice PwBD Reservation 2026

The notification includes four PwBD reservation positions on a horizontal basis. Applicants seeking PwBD benefits must possess a certificate issued by the competent authority. Candidates must have at least 40% of the relevant disability and satisfy the physical requirements and functional classifications applicable to the selected apprenticeship position. PwBD reservation is included within the advertised vacancies and must not be added separately.

RITES Apprentice Posting Location 2026

The apprenticeship opportunities are available across India. Candidates from all states are eligible to apply, subject to the prescribed qualifications and other requirements. Applicants may provide their preferred location through the RITES application form. However, the final posting will be determined by the company's operational requirements. Important: A preferred location does not guarantee posting at that location. RITES will not entertain subsequent requests to change the posting preference after the prescribed process.

RITES Apprentice Merit List 2026

RITES will prepare the merit list after examining apprenticeship applications submitted through NATS or NAPS and verifying documents submitted through the RITES form. Shortlisted candidates will receive instructions through email from [email protected] . The selected candidates list will also be uploaded on the official RITES website. Candidates must bring the original documents at the time of joining for further verification. Vacancies remaining unfilled after the initial shortlisting process may be filled from subsequent merit lists of eligible candidates.
