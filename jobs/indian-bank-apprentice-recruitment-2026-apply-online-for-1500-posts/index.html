Indian Bank Apprentice Training Duration 2026

The apprenticeship duration is 12 months of on-the-job training under the Apprentices Act, 1961. Apprentices are entitled to bank holidays and one casual leave after completing each month of training. A maximum of four casual leaves may be taken at one time. The notification requires more than 338 paid days during the 12-month period to become eligible for the Certificate of Proficiency, subject to the prescribed assessment and completion conditions. After successful training and assessment, the Board of Apprenticeship Training (Southern Region) will issue a digital Certificate of Proficiency through NATS 2.0. No. Apprenticeship is a fixed-duration training arrangement. Indian Bank has no obligation to provide regular or contractual employment after completion of training, and apprentices do not have a right to claim employment with the bank.

Indian Bank Apprentice Exam Pattern 2026

The online examination consists of 100 multiple-choice questions carrying 100 marks , with a composite duration of 60 minutes. Indian Bank will determine the minimum qualifying marks on the aggregate examination score. There are no separate minimum qualifying marks for individual sections. SC, ST, OBC and PwBD candidates receive a 5% relaxation in the prescribed aggregate qualifying percentage. The bank will prepare merit lists State/UT-wise and category-wise according to online examination performance. Candidates will be ranked in descending order of aggregate marks. If candidates have identical marks at the cut-off, the older candidate will receive preference. Where examinations are conducted in multiple sessions, scores will be normalized using the equi-percentile method. A State/UT-wise and category-wise waiting list may also be prepared to fill unoccupied apprenticeship seats.

Indian Bank Apprentice Local Language Test 2026

Candidates must be able to read, write, speak and understand one of the specified local languages of their selected State or Union Territory. The Local Language Proficiency Test will be conducted after the online examination. Candidates who fail to qualify will not be engaged as apprentices. Candidates who provide Class 8, Class 10 or Class 12 marksheets or certificates proving they studied their selected local language are exempt from appearing in the Local Language Proficiency Test. Important: Candidates without the prescribed language-study evidence must qualify in the LLPT. The test will be held at a centre decided by the bank in the relevant State/UT, and candidates must bear their own travel expenses.

Indian Bank Apprentice State and District Preference 2026

Applicants can apply for only one State or Union Territory . During registration, candidates may choose three preferred districts within the selected State/UT. The bank gives the applicant's resident district the first preference in the applicable merit arrangement, while candidates may select two additional neighbouring districts. Important: District preferences do not guarantee posting in a particular branch or district. Indian Bank may allot candidates to any district within their selected State/UT according to availability and operational requirements.

Indian Bank Apprentice Certificate and Contract Conditions

Selected candidates will receive a digital apprenticeship engagement offer through NATS 2.0. The apprenticeship contract is normally for 12 months and terminates automatically after completion of the prescribed training period. Candidates must report at the allotted bank branch or training venue on the specified joining date. Failure to report may lead to termination of the apprenticeship contract. A candidate who voluntarily leaves the training before completing the contract may be required to pay an amount equivalent to one month's stipend, under the notified conditions. Separate provisions apply to termination after securing gainful employment or due to medical grounds. Candidates should read the full contractual conditions before accepting the apprenticeship.

Indian Bank Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many apprentice seats are in the Indian Bank Recruitment 2026?
A
Indian Bank has notified 1500 tentative apprentice training seats across 30 States/UTs, with the highest numbers in Uttar Pradesh (285) and Tamil Nadu (270).
Q2What is the last date to apply for the Indian Bank apprentice posts?
A
The last date is 25 November 2026. Online registration and fee payment are open from 5 November 2026 to 25 November 2026, both days inclusive.
Q3What qualification is needed for the Indian Bank apprentice 2026?
A
A graduate degree in any discipline from a recognized University, passed on or after 01.04.2022 and not later than 01.11.2026, with the result declared on or before 01.11.2026.
Q4What is the age limit for the Indian Bank apprentice 2026?
A
Minimum 20 years and maximum 28 years as on 01.11.2026. SC/ST get 5 years' relaxation, OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) 3 years and PwBD 10 years.
Q5What stipend do Indian Bank apprentices get?
A
The total stipend is ₹15,000/- per month at metro/urban branches and ₹12,300/- per month at rural/semi-urban branches. Apprentices are not eligible for any other allowances or benefits.
Q6What is the application fee for the Indian Bank apprentice 2026?
A
The non-refundable fee is ₹800/- plus GST for General/OBC/EWS candidates and ₹175/- plus GST for SC/ST/PwBD candidates, paid online only.
Q7How do I apply for the Indian Bank apprentice 2026?
A
Register with a 100% complete profile on the NATS 2.0 Portal (www.nats.education.gov.in), then apply through the Indian Bank careers page or the NATS portal link "Engagement of Apprentices in Indian Bank – 2026" and pay the fee online.
Q8What is the selection process for Indian Bank apprentices?
A
Selection is through a 100-mark online objective test of 60 minutes and a Local Language Proficiency Test. The Bank may conduct an interview or dispense with it at its discretion.
Q9Can I apply for more than one State/UT?
A
No. A candidate can apply for only one State/UT and submit only one application; with multiple applications only the latest valid one is kept and other fees are forfeited.
Q10Who is not eligible for the Indian Bank apprenticeship?
A
Candidates who have already done or are doing apprenticeship, who have 01 year or more of job experience after their qualification, and Ex-servicemen / Disabled Ex-servicemen are not eligible.
