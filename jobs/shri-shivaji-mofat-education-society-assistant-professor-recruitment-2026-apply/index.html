Shri Shivaji Mofat Education Society Assistant Professor : Job Overview

Shri Shivaji Mofat Education Society, Tq. Kandhar, Dist. Nanded (M.S.) has invited applications for 27 Assistant Professor posts in 13 subjects, to be filled on 100% granted basis in Shri Shivaji College of Arts, Commerce & Science, Kandhar, Shri Sant Gadge Maharaj Mahavidyalaya, Loha, and Shri Shivaji Law College, Kandhar. Applications go by email to [email protected], by Registered Post or in person within fifteen days from the date of publication of the advertisement. Pay is as per the rules of UGC, the Maharashtra Government and Swami Ramanand Teerth Marathwada University, Nanded.

About the Society

Shri Shivaji Mofat Education Society, Kandhar, Dist. Nanded, runs three institutions named in the notice: Shri Shivaji College of Arts, Commerce & Science, Kandhar; Shri Sant Gadge Maharaj Mahavidyalaya, Loha, Tq. Loha; and Shri Shivaji Law College, Kandhar. The notice names Hon'ble Dr. Keshavraoji Dhondge Saheb (Veteran Freedom Fighter, Ex. M.P. and M.L.A.) as founder and administrator. This is a recruitment by a private education society for posts on 100% granted basis.

Department-wise Vacancies

The notice lists 27 Assistant Professor posts across 13 subjects. The posts are permitted as per NOC No. JDHE Nanded / NOC / 2026 / 00023 dated 28/10/2026. The notice does not say which subject belongs to which of the three institutions. The notice prints one reservation break-up for all 27 posts together, including parallel reservation posts, not subject by subject. Persons with disability: 2 posts in total, for (A) Blindness and Low Vision and (B) Deaf and Hard of Hearing. The notice also states that, according to Govt. rules, 30% and 4% of seats are reserved for women and differently abled persons respectively.

Pay and Terms

Pay scale is given from time to time as per the rules of UGC, the State Government of Maharashtra and Swami Ramanand Teerth Marathwada University, Nanded. The notice prints no pay amount. The posts are to be filled on 100% granted basis. No TA/DA will be paid for attending the interview.
