About the Recruiting University

Tamil University is located in Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu (Thanjavur – 613 010, as printed on its notice). This recruitment is for its Music Department, which has advertised the two posts on a self-finance basis. The notice is issued by the Registrar (i/c) of the University.

Department-wise Vacancies

The notice invites applications for 2 posts of Honorary Assistant Professor (Self-Finance), one in each subject listed below. The notice gives no category-wise break-up for the two posts. It states that the Tamil Nadu Government 200-point reservation roster will be followed.

Qualification and Experience

The qualifications are subject to the regulations of the University Grants Commission (UGC Regulations). Only candidates who have NET/SLET/Ph.D. (Awarded) in the concerned subjects may apply. Work experience is not a stated requirement, but the application form has a column for work experience. Candidates should check the official notification for the subject-specific eligibility of each post.
PostQualification
Honorary Assistant Professor (Self-Finance) – Vocal1) Postgraduate degree (Muthukalai) in Music with 55% marks. 2) NET or SET pass. /OR/ 3) Ph.D. in Music.
Honorary Assistant Professor (Self-Finance) – Bharatanatyam1) Postgraduate degree (Muthukalai) in Bharatanatyam with 55% marks. 2) NET or SET pass. /OR/ 3) Ph.D. in Bharatanatyam.

Pay and Terms

The monthly consolidated pay of an Honorary Assistant Professor is Rs.30,000/- only. The notice names no allowances. The appointment of Honorary Assistant Professor is entirely temporary. No claim to a permanent appointment can be made on the basis of this appointment. If the work of the Honorary Assistant Professor is not satisfactory, the person may be relieved from service at any time. The appointment and the interview are subject to the rules of the University.
