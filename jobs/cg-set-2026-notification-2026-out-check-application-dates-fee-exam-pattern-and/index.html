CG SET 2026 Notification 2026 Out - Check Application : Job Overview

The Chhattisgarh Karamchari Chayan Mandal (CGSSB), formerly known as Vyapam, has released the exam notification for the Chhattisgarh State Eligibility Test (SET) 2026. Conducted under the Directorate of Higher Education, CGSSB SET 2026 determines eligibility for the post of Assistant Professor in Chhattisgarh. Online applications open on 23 July 2026, and the exam is provisionally scheduled for 4 October 2026. This article covers all the important dates, application fee, exam pattern, admit card details, exam centres, and the qualifying criteria as given in the official notification.

CGSSB SET 2026 Exam Pattern

The SET consists of two papers, both containing only objective (multiple-choice) questions, and there is no negative marking in either paper. Paper I is compulsory for all candidates and is of a general nature, assessing teaching aptitude, research aptitude, reasoning ability, comprehension, divergent thinking and general awareness. Paper II is based entirely on the subject chosen by the candidate, covering the complete syllabus of that subject. Each question in both papers carries 2 marks. The detailed subject-wise syllabus for SET 2026 is available on the "Syllabus" link on the CGSSB website.

CGSSB SET 2026 Qualifying Criteria and Result

As per the official notification, the top 6% of candidates who appear in both Paper I and Paper II, and who secure at least 40% aggregate marks (for General category candidates) or at least 35% aggregate marks (for SC, ST, OBC-Non Creamy Layer, PWD and Transgender candidates) across both papers combined, will be declared qualified for Assistant Professor eligibility, following the reservation policy of the Chhattisgarh Government. This qualifying criterion is stated to be final and binding. The result will be published on the official website, https://cgssb.cgstate.gov.in , where candidates can view and print it. As answer sheets are evaluated through an OMR machine, there is no provision for rechecking or re-evaluation.

CGSSB SET 2026 Answer Key and Objection Process

Objections to the model answer key must be submitted online by logging in with the registration number (Profile ID) and date of birth. A fee of Rs. 50 per question is charged for raising an objection, payable only online. Objections sent by post, email, or submitted in person are not accepted. Each question must be objected to separately; combining multiple questions in a single objection will result in rejection. Supporting proof must be uploaded with the objection - a guide book is not accepted as valid proof. Objections without proof will be rejected. Objections are reviewed by subject experts, and their decision is final and binding. The final answer key is prepared based on this review, and no further objections are accepted once the final answer key is released with the result.
