HBSE Exam Dates 2027 - Overview : Job Overview

The HBSE Exam Dates 2027 have been announced by the Board of School Education Haryana (BSEH), Bhiwani, for the annual Secondary and Senior Secondary examinations. According to the official notification dated 25 November 2026, the Haryana Board Class 10 examinations will be conducted from 4 March to 26 March 2027, while the Class 12 examinations will take place from 3 March to 8 April 2027.

HBSE Practical Exam Dates 2027

The Haryana Board's 25 November notification announces the overall Secondary and Senior Secondary annual examination periods. It does not provide a separate practical examination timetable.

Students with practical components should follow the arrangements communicated by their schools and any subsequent notices issued by BSEH.

The dates of practical examinations should not be inferred from the first or last day of the theory examination period.

HBSE Board Exam 2027 Preparation Tips

With the examination periods confirmed, students can organise their preparation using the relevant Haryana Board syllabus and the remaining time before March 2027.

  • Complete the prescribed syllabus for each subject.
  • Review the latest examination pattern and syllabus issued by BSEH.
  • Revise important chapters and formulas regularly.
  • Practise previous-year papers and sample questions where available.
  • Identify weaker topics and allocate additional revision time.
  • Take timed practice tests to improve answer-writing speed.
  • Monitor the official website for the subject-wise date sheet.
  • Adjust the revision plan after individual paper dates are announced.

The announced examination windows can help students plan their revision, but a final subject-by-subject study timetable should be based on the official detailed date sheet.
