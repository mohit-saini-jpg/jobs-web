IRCTC SCZ Selection Process 2026

Selection for both Hospitality Monitors and Tourism Monitors is through Walk-in Interview, following the same broad process: Candidates must fill in the application form (attached with the respective notification), completing it in all respects. The completed application form must be submitted at the venue of the interview for verification, along with original documents, one set of attested copies of requisite documents, and two recent passport-size photographs. An interview will be conducted, and candidates will be shortlisted based on their credentials and performance in the personal interview. The offer of engagement will be issued to suitable candidates in order of merit and based on the number of vacancies, subject to verification of antecedents. IRCTC Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is IRCTC SCZ Hospitality Monitors and Tourism Monitors Recruitment 2026?
A
These are two walk-in interview notifications, both dated 16.09.2026, issued by IRCTC South Central Zone for engagement of Hospitality Monitors (22 posts) and Tourism Monitors (6 posts) on a fixed-term/contractual basis.
Q2How many vacancies are available in IRCTC SCZ Recruitment 2026?
A
A total of 28 posts are available: 22 Hospitality Monitor posts (including 4 OBC backlog vacancies) and 6 Tourism Monitor posts.
Q3What is the walk-in interview date for IRCTC SCZ Recruitment 2026?
A
Hospitality Monitors interviews are on 05.10.2026 (Bhubaneswar) and 08.10.2026 (Hyderabad). Tourism Monitors interviews are on 06.10.2026 (Bhubaneswar) and 09.10.2026 (Hyderabad), each from 10:30 hrs to 17:30 hrs.
Q4What is the qualification required for IRCTC Hospitality Monitors?
A
Candidates need a B.Sc. in Hospitality and Hotel Administration, BBA/MBA (Culinary Arts), B.Sc. Hotel Management and Catering Science, or M.B.A (Tourism and Hotel Management) from a recognized institute/university, with age-linked experience requirements (no experience up to 28 years, 1 year for 28–30 years, 2 years for 30–35 years).
Q5What is the age limit for IRCTC SCZ Recruitment 2026?
A
For Hospitality Monitors, the upper age limit ranges up to 28, 30, or 35 years depending on experience (35 years for UR). For Tourism Monitors, the upper age limit is 35 years for UR. Age relaxation applies for SC/ST (5 years), OBC (3 years), PwBD (10 years), and Ex-Servicemen (service rendered plus 3 years).
Q6What is the qualification required for IRCTC Tourism Monitors?
A
Candidates need a 3-year Bachelor's degree in Tourism (or any stream plus a 1-year Travel & Tourism diploma) with minimum 1 year of relevant experience, or a Bachelor's degree plus a 2-year PG degree/diploma in Travel & Tourism with minimum 2 years of relevant experience.
Q7What is the remuneration for IRCTC Hospitality Monitors and Tourism Monitors?
A
Hospitality Monitors receive a CTC of Rs. 30,000/- per month. Tourism Monitors receive a CTC of Rs. 30,000/- to Rs. 35,000/- per month, based on qualification and experience. Both posts carry additional allowances such as Daily Allowance, Lodging Charges, National Holiday Allowance, and Medical Insurance.
Q8How can I apply for IRCTC SCZ Hospitality Monitors and Tourism Monitors Recruitment 2026?
A
There is no online application; eligible candidates must fill the prescribed application proforma and appear directly at the walk-in interview venue on the scheduled date and time, with original documents, attested copies, and photographs.
