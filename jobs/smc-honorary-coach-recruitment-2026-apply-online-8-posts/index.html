SMC Honorary Coach Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Surat Municipal Corporation (SMC) has announced 8 vacancies for the Honorary Coach post on a contractual basis for a tenure of 11 months. The posts include Table Tennis Coach, Badminton Coach, Volleyball Coach, Kho-Kho Coach, Kabaddi Coach, Judo Coach, Karate Coach, and Gym Coach. Qualifications vary by post as specified in the official notification, with a maximum age limit of 50 years and a fixed monthly salary of Rs. 10,000 for applicable posts with no other allowances. Who Can Apply

Candidates must possess the requisite qualifications and experience specified for the respective coaching discipline: Sports Qualification: NIS diploma or its equivalent degree, or Sports Authority of India (SAI) 6-month certificate course and at least one year of coaching experience. Alternatively, C.P.Ed./D.P.Ed./M.P.Ed. qualification and physical fitness as a physical training instructor. Alternatively, H.S.C. pass with participation at national/state level in the relevant sport or one year of coaching experience. Preference: Candidates who have won medals in national or state level tournaments for the concerned sport will be given preference during selection.

Pay and Tenure

Selected candidates will receive a fixed consolidated monthly remuneration of Rs. 10,000/- (Ten Thousand Rupees only) . No other allowances, perks, or financial benefits of any other kind will be payable. The engagement is made on a contractual basis through the Standing Committee resolution for a fixed duration of 11 months.

SMC Honorary Coach Job Description

Coaches appointed under this recruitment will be responsible for conducting training sessions, imparting structured sports coaching, maintaining training discipline, and preparing teams for competitions at the newly constructed sports complex located at Dindoli in the South Zone of Surat Municipal Corporation.

Selection and Interview

The selection process is based on scrutiny of registered applications, verification of original documents, and meeting the required eligibility criteria and qualifications. Eligible candidates will be called for document verification and walk-in interview sessions. The exact schedule, date, and venue for document verification will be communicated via SMS to the candidate's registered mobile number.
