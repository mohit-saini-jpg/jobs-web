About AIIMS Bathinda

The notice describes AIIMS Bathinda as one of the new AIIMS, set up by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India, under the Pradhan Mantri Swasthya Suraksha Yojana (PMSSY) under an Act of Parliament. Its stated aim is to correct regional imbalances in quality tertiary healthcare and to attain self-sufficiency in graduate and postgraduate medical education. Applications are invited by the Executive Director, and the Institute is located at Jodhpur Romana, Mandi Dabwali Road, Bathinda, Punjab, 151001.

Department-wise Vacancies

The notice lists 55 posts in four tables: Professor 17, Additional Professor 4, Associate Professor 16 and Assistant Professor 18. The vacancies are provisional and subject to variation. The notice marks vacancies reserved for Persons with Benchmark Disabilities as (PwBD) within a category. If suitable candidates are not found for the Additional Professor posts, these posts can be filled by the lower cadre in the respective category in all the disciplines as advertised. The notice gives the example of the Additional Professor (SC) post in Nuclear Medicine, which can be filled by an Associate Professor (SC) or Assistant Professor (SC) candidate. Candidates eligible for the lower cadre may therefore also apply in departments where the higher post is advertised. Qualification and Experience

The official notification does not specify the minimum educational qualification or experience for each post. It says: "Essential Eligibility Criteria: As per Annexure-A uploaded on Institute website separately." Pay and Terms

For every post the notice adds "plus usual allowances including NPA (if applicable)". It does not list the other allowances. The posts are full time and private practice of any kind is prohibited. Selected candidates are governed by the New Pension Scheme under Ministry of Finance Notification No.5/7/2003-ECB&PR dated 22.12.2003. The appointment is subject to review and probation of two years. If an appointee wishes to apply elsewhere in the first two years, a No Objection Certificate is provided as per AIIMS Bathinda rules. The appointee is on a whole-time appointment and may not accept any other assignment, paid or otherwise. Joining time is 30 days from the issue of the appointment letter; extension is considered only in exceptional cases with the approval of the Competent Authority. AIIMS Bathinda Faculty Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Applications are in two steps. The notice states that candidates must fulfil all eligibility criteria on or before 30 November 2026, failing which the application will be rejected.
  • 2Fill the mandatory online Google Form at https://forms.gle/gpUWffmmgxHiuJgY9 by 30 November 2026, 05.00 PM IST. The link is disabled after that.
  • 3Download the application form from www.aiimsbathinda.edu.in, fill it in MS Word format as per the proforma and take a printout.
  • 4Pay the application fee online only, through the payment gateway link given in the notice (see the Important Links table). Write the transaction number in the space provided in the application form.
  • 5Send the duly typed application form, the fee transaction receipt and self-attested certificates by Registered post, Speed post or by hand to: The Recruitment Cell, Administrative Block, Medical College Building, Mandi Dabwali Road, AIIMS, Bathinda-151001, Punjab.
  • 6Write on the envelope: "APPLICATION FOR THE POST OF ... DEPTT. OF ...".
  • 7Make sure the hard copy reaches by 03 December 2026, 05:00 PM IST. Late hard copies are not accepted and AIIMS Bathinda is not responsible for postal delay.
  • 8Candidates applying for more than one post must apply separately for each post, pay the fee separately and submit hard copies with supporting documents separately.
  • 9Candidates must keep a valid personal e-mail ID and mobile number active, as information will be shared through email only.
  • 10Interview date: to be notified later. The interview notice goes to the registered email and the schedule is published on www.aiimsbathinda.edu.in.
  • 11For help, write to [email protected], mentioning the post applied and the discipline in the subject line.
  • 12The fee is paid through online mode only. No request for refund is entertained after submission of the application form, and applications without the prescribed fee are summarily rejected.
  • 13The stages are scrutiny, an optional shortlisting step and a personal interview, followed by appointment subject to medical fitness.
  • 14Applications received within the date and time with the fee are scrutinised by the Institute's scrutiny committee. Only eligible candidates are called for personal interview before the Standing Selection Committee.
  • 15If applicants are many, the selection committee may devise shortlisting criteria, which may include a written examination.
  • 16Eligibility and shortlisting information is published on the AIIMS Bathinda website only. Calls are provisional, subject to fulfilment of all requirements.
  • 17The interview is held at AIIMS Bathinda only, in person. Online interviews are not accepted and no TA/DA is paid.
  • 18Selected candidates receive appointment letters, subject to being declared medically fit by the competent authority.

AIIMS Bathinda Faculty Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many faculty vacancies are there in AIIMS Bathinda Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 55 provisional vacancies: 17 Professor, 4 Additional Professor, 16 Associate Professor and 18 Assistant Professor posts, subject to variation.
Q2What is the last date to apply?
A
The online Google Form closes on 30 November 2026 at 05.00 PM IST. The hard copy must reach the Recruitment Cell by 03 December 2026 at 05:00 PM IST.
Q3What is the age limit?
A
The upper age limit must not exceed 58 years for Professor and Additional Professor, and 50 years for Associate Professor and Assistant Professor, as on 30 November 2026. SC/ST get 5 years and OBC 3 years of relaxation; EWS get none.
Q4What is the application fee?
A
General/OBC/EWS candidates pay Rs. 2360/- and SC/ST candidates pay Rs. 1180/-, both including 18% GST. The fee is NIL for persons with benchmark disabilities and is paid online only.
Q5What is the salary for AIIMS Bathinda faculty posts?
A
Professor: Level-14-A (Rs. 1,68,900-2,20,400); Additional Professor: Level-13-A2+ (Rs. 1,48,200-2,11,400); Associate Professor: Level-13-A1+ (Rs. 1,38,300-2,09,200); Assistant Professor: Level-12 (Rs. 1,01,500-1,67,400), all of the 7th CPC.
Q6Are allowances paid on top of the pay?
A
Yes. The notice says the pay is plus usual allowances including NPA (if applicable). It does not list any other allowance.
Q7How do I apply for AIIMS Bathinda faculty posts?
A
Fill the online Google Form, download and fill the application form from www.aiimsbathinda.edu.in, pay the fee online, and send the printed form with documents to the Recruitment Cell, AIIMS Bathinda, to reach by 03 December 2026.
Q8How are candidates selected?
A
Eligible candidates are called for a personal interview before the Standing Selection Committee. If applicants are many, the committee may set shortlisting criteria, which may include a written examination.
Q9Can I apply for more than one post?
A
Yes. Apply separately for each post, pay the fee separately for each and submit separate hard copies with supporting documents.
Q10Can lower-cadre candidates apply for Additional Professor or Associate Professor posts?
A
Yes. If suitable candidates are not found, those posts can be filled by the lower cadre in the same category. Candidates eligible for the lower cadre may apply where the higher post is advertised.
