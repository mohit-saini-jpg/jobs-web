Uttara Kannada District Court Process Server and Peon Eligibility Criteria

Uttara Kannada District Court Process Server and Peon Selection Process

Selection to both posts will be made as per the Karnataka Civil Courts (Clerical and Other Posts) (Recruitment) Rules, 1982, and its amendment rules of 2007 and 2021. Process Server: Candidates will be shortlisted for interview based on the percentage of marks obtained in the qualifying educational examination, in a ratio of 1:25 per post as per the reservation of notified posts. The interview carries a total of 10 marks, and final selection is based on marks obtained in the interview. Peon: Candidates will be shortlisted for interview based on the percentage of marks obtained in the SSLC examination, in a ratio of 1:10 per post. The interview carries a total of 10 marks, and final selection is based on marks obtained in the interview. If two or more candidates secure equal marks, the order of merit will be decided on the basis of age, with the older candidate placed higher in the merit
