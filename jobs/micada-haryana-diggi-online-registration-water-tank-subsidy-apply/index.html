Yeh scheme Haryana government ki water conservation aur irrigation efficiency ko boost karne ke liye launch ki gayi hai. Subsidy se kisan ko initial cost ka kaafi hissa cover milta hai, isliye adoption rate badhne ki sambhavna hai.

Who Is Eligible: micada haryana Diggi online registration | Water Tank Subsid

Scheme un sabhi kisanon ke liye hai jo Haryana ke Diggi area mein registered landholders hain aur paani ki tanki lagwana chahte hain.

micada haryana Diggi online registration | Water Tank Subsid Application Process

1. Apna land record (Patta) aur water tank ka layout ready rakhein. 2. Aadhaar, bank account details aur recent electricity bill upload karen. 3. Online portal par sabhi fields dhyan se fill karen, koi bhi galti rejection ka reason ban sakti hai.

micada haryana Diggi online registration | Water Tank Subsid Benefits Details

Subsidy ki rakam total tank cost ka 50% tak ho sakti hai, maximum ₹1.5 lakh tak. Isse kisan ko capital outlay kam karne mein madad milti hai aur irrigation ke liye paani ki uplabdhata badhti hai.

How micada haryana Diggi online registration | Water Tank Subsid Works

Applicant portal par registration ke baad, documents verify hote hain, phir scheme officer site visit karta hai. Approval milne ke baad, subsidy amount directly beneficiary ke bank account mein transfer hoti hai within 30-45 din.

micada haryana Diggi online registration | Water Tank Subsid Application Tips

Form fill karte waqt sabhi required fields ko mandatory mark karen, scanned copies clear honi chahiye. Common rejection reasons: mismatched land record, incomplete bank details, ya outdated Aadhaar. In sabko double-check karen before submit.
