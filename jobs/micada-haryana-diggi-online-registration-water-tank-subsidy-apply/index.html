micada haryana Diggi online registration | Water Tank Subsid Overview

Micada Haryana Diggi yojana se kisan apne khet mein diggi banane ke liye water tank ke liye subsidy le sakte hain. Online portal par registration bahut simple hai, bas kuch documents upload karne hain.

micada haryana Diggi online registration | Water Tank Subsid Expert Analysis

Yeh scheme kisanon ko paani ki uplabdhata badhane aur irrigation cost kam karne mein madad karti hai. Subsidy seedhi bank account mein transfer hoti hai, isliye transparency bhi bani rehti hai.

Who Is Eligible: micada haryana Diggi online registration | Water Tank Subsid

Scheme un sabhi kisanon ke liye hai jo Haryana ke Micada block mein registered hain aur apne khet mein diggi banana chahte hain.

micada haryana Diggi online registration | Water Tank Subsid Application Process

1. Apna Aadhaar, land record aur bank passbook ready rakhein. 2. Online portal par apna account banaye aur sabhi fields accurately fill karein. 3. Required documents PDF format mein compress karke upload karein.

Subsidy ki rakam ₹30,000 tak ho sakti hai, jo water tank ki poori cost ka 50% tak cover karti hai. Isse kisan ko initial investment ka bojh kam hota hai aur irrigation efficiency badhti hai.

How micada haryana Diggi online registration | Water Tank Subsid Works

Kisan portal par application submit karta hai, phir district level officer verification karta hai. Verification ke baad subsidy amount directly kisan ke bank account mein transfer hoti hai, usually 30-45 din ke andar.

Form fill karte waqt sabhi details double-check karein, especially land record number. Common rejection ka reason incomplete documents ya mismatched Aadhaar hai, isliye sabhi PDFs clear aur readable honi chahiye.
