micada haryana Diggi online registration | Water Tank Subsid Overview

Micada Haryana ki Diggi Yojana ab water tank subsidy ke saath aayi hai. Kisan apne kheton ke liye online registration karke ₹15,000 tak ka financial support le sakte hain.

micada haryana Diggi online registration | Water Tank Subsid Expert Analysis

Scheme ka maksad irrigation efficiency badhana aur paani ki kami ko door karna hai. Online portal se process transparent aur tezi se hoti hai, isliye eligible farmers ko turant fayda milta hai.

Who Is Eligible: micada haryana Diggi online registration | Water Tank Subsid

Ye yojana un sabhi kisanon ke liye hai jo Haryana mein Diggi (pond) banane ya renovate karne ki soch rahe hain aur water tank install karna chahte hain.

micada haryana Diggi online registration | Water Tank Subsid Application Process

1. Apna Aadhaar, land record aur latest water tank invoice ready rakhein. 2. Portal par sabhi fields ko dhyan se fill karein, especially bank account details. 3. Application submit karne ke baad receipt ka screenshot save kar lein.

micada haryana Diggi online registration | Water Tank Subsid Benefits Details

Scheme ke tahat eligible farmer ko ₹15,000 tak ka subsidy milta hai, jo tank ki purchase aur installation cost ka 50% tak cover karta hai. Isse paani ki availability badhti hai aur fasal ki productivity improve hoti hai.

How micada haryana Diggi online registration | Water Tank Subsid Works

Online portal par farmer apni details daalta hai, verification ke baad water tank supplier se direct payment hoti hai. Disbursement bank transfer ke through hoti hai, jo 2-3 weeks ke andar complete ho jati hai.

micada haryana Diggi online registration | Water Tank Subsid Application Tips

Application form mein sabhi mandatory fields ko fill karein, correct bank IFSC code dalein, aur required documents ka scanned copy upload karein. Common rejection ka reason incomplete documents ya mismatched land details hota hai, isliye double-check zaroor karein.

micada haryana Diggi online registration | Water Tank Subsid FAQs

Q1Is scheme ke liye kaun kaun se documents chahiye?
A
Aadhaar card, land record (Patta), water tank invoice, bank passbook aur recent passport size photo.
Q2Subsidy kitni der mein milti hai?
A
Verification ke baad 2-3 weeks ke andar bank transfer ke through subsidy directly farmer ke account mein aati hai.
Q3Kya existing Diggi ke liye bhi apply kar sakte hain?
A
Haan, agar existing Diggi ko renovate kar rahe hain aur naya water tank install kar rahe hain to bhi eligible hain.
