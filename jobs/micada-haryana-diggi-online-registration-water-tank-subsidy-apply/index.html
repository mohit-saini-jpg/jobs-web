micada haryana Diggi online registration | Water Tank Subsid Overview

Micada Haryana Diggi scheme ab online ho gaya! Kisan apne kheton ke liye water tank banwane par ₹20,000 tak subsidy le sakte hain. Registration simple hai, bas required documents upload karein.

micada haryana Diggi online registration | Water Tank Subsid Expert Analysis

Yeh yojana especially un small and marginal farmers ke liye design ki gayi hai jinko irrigation ke liye reliable water storage ki zaroorat hai. Subsidy se unka capital burden kam hota hai aur crop yield improve hoti hai.

Who Is Eligible: micada haryana Diggi online registration | Water Tank Subsid

Kisi bhi Haryana resident farmer jo apne farm ke liye water tank install karna chahta hai, aur land ownership ya lease proof de sakta hai, eligible hai.

micada haryana Diggi online registration | Water Tank Subsid Application Process

1. Apna Aadhaar, land record (Patta) aur bank account details ready rakhein. 2. Recent electricity bill ya water bill ko proof ke roop mein upload karein. 3. Application deadline se pehle sabhi fields ko double-check karen.

micada haryana Diggi online registration | Water Tank Subsid Benefits Details

Scheme ke tahat ₹20,000 tak ki subsidy milti hai, jo tank ki total cost ka 50% tak cover kar sakti hai. Isse farmer ko upfront investment kam karne ka mauka milta hai aur irrigation ka cost-effective solution milta hai.

How micada haryana Diggi online registration | Water Tank Subsid Works

Apply karne ke baad, state agriculture department verification karta hai, phir subsidy amount directly farmer ke bank account mein transfer hoti hai. Implementation agencies local water tank manufacturers ke through hoti hain, jo quality standards follow karte hain.

micada haryana Diggi online registration | Water Tank Subsid Application Tips

Application form ko accurately fill karein, sabhi mandatory documents attach karein, aur photo upload guidelines follow karein. Common rejections tab aate hain jab land proof ya bank details mismatch hoti hain, isliye double verification zaroori hai.

micada haryana Diggi online registration | Water Tank Subsid FAQs

Q1Yeh subsidy kaise claim kar sakte hain?
A
Official Micada portal par jaake online registration form fill karein, required documents upload karein, aur verification ke baad subsidy aapke bank account mein transfer ho jayegi.
Q2Kya existing water tanks ke liye bhi subsidy milti hai?
A
Nahi, yeh subsidy sirf naya water tank install karne ke liye hi applicable hai. Existing tanks ke liye koi financial assistance nahi milti.
Q3Application deadline kab tak hai?
A
Har financial year ke 30 June tak online applications accept kiye jaate hain. Late submission se aapka application reject ho sakta hai.
