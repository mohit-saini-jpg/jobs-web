MGU MArch Exam Time Table 2026 - Quick Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 24/07/2026

Mahatma Gandhi University (MGU), Kottayam, Kerala has released the official time table for the I Semester M.Arch degree examinations covering 2025 admission regular candidates and 2024 supplementary candidates. The exams are scheduled to begin on 14 August 2026. This article covers the complete exam schedule, subject-wise dates, exam fee structure, and application deadlines as per the official notification.

MGU M.Arch I Semester Exam Time Table 2026

The examinations will be held from 1:30 pm to 4:30 pm on all days, except on Friday, when the timing is 2:00 pm to 5:00 pm. Below is the subject-wise schedule as per the official notification.

MGU M.Arch Exam Fee Details 2026

Candidates applying for the I Semester M.Arch examination need to pay the prescribed examination fee along with an additional camp fee, as per the fine structure below.

Application CategoryLast DateFine Amount
Without fine01 August 2026Nil
With fine03 August 2026₹685/-
With superfine04 August 2026₹1,355/-

In addition to the prescribed examination fee, candidates must remit ₹70/- per paper towards the C.V. Camp fee.

How to Apply for the MGU M.Arch Exam

The notification does not specify a step-by-step application procedure. Candidates are advised to submit their applications through the concerned college as per the standard MGU examination application process, ensuring submission before the applicable deadline (with or without fine, as listed above) to avoid rejection.

Important Instructions

  • Applications must be submitted at the University office within the specified deadlines.
  • Fee amounts increase progressively after the "without fine" deadline, so candidates should apply early wherever possible.
  • An additional camp fee of ₹70/- per paper applies over and above the standard examination fee.
  • The document is digitally approved by the Controller of Examinations, and no physical signature is required.
