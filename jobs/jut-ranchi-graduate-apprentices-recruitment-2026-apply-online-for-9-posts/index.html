Who Can Apply

Candidates who have passed B.Tech. / B.E / BCA / B.Sc. (IT) from any recognized University/College of Jharkhand State during the years 2022 to 2026 are eligible to apply. A degree from a University/College outside Jharkhand State, or one passed outside the 2022 to 2026 window, does not meet this condition as printed. Shortlisting is based on the aggregate percentage of all subjects of the undergraduate degree. When a percentage is not available, the CGPA conversion certificate issued by the University/Higher Education Institution must be uploaded in the application form. Candidates should check the official notification for the full eligibility conditions of the Graduate Apprentice position.

Stipend and Training Period

Each Graduate Apprentice gets a monthly stipend of Rs. 16650/- . JUT is responsible only for payment of its own share of the stipend and bears no responsibility for payment of the BoPT (Board of Practical Training) share of the monthly stipend. The engagement is valid for one (01) year only, counted from the date of generation of the Apprenticeship Contract. On successful completion of one year of apprenticeship training, the Board of Practical Training, Eastern Region awards a Certificate of Proficiency (CoP). The engagement is purely temporary and gives no right or claim to regular appointment in any post of JUT.

JUT Ranchi Graduate Apprentices Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection has two stages: shortlisting on degree marks, followed by a Personal Interview. The final selection list combines both. Candidates will be shortlisted on the aggregate percentage obtained in all subjects of the Undergraduate Degree, in the ratio of 1:5 for the total available posts. The list of shortlisted applicants is scheduled for 21 December 2026. Shortlisted candidates will be called for a Personal Interview on 28 December 2026 at a place to be communicated in due course of time. The interview carries a maximum of 100 marks. Shortlisted candidates must produce all their original certificates at the interview. The final selection is based on a cumulative score combining the percentage of marks in the qualifying examination and the Personal Interview in the ratio of 70:30 respectively. The final selection list is scheduled for 30 December 2026.
