ICAR-NBSS&LUP Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The ICAR-National Bureau of Soil Survey and Land Use Planning Regional Centre, Kolkata is engaging 1 Sr. Project Associate (RS & GIS) at Rs. 45,000/- consolidated and 2 Jr. Project Associate (RS & GIS) at Rs. 35,000/- under the project "National Soil Mapping Programme (NSMP), 1:10K". The Sr. post needs a Post-Graduate degree with at least 3 years' experience and the Jr. post a Graduate degree with at least 2 years' experience. Applications are sent by email to [email protected] in the prescribed format, and the interview is on 9 December 2026 from 10:00 a.m. onwards.

Pay and Tenure

The Sr. Project Associate gets Rs. 45,000/- consolidated and the Jr. Project Associate gets Rs.35, 000/-. For both posts the job duration is 24 months or until termination of the project, whichever is earlier, and the position is to be renewed every year based on performance. The initial appointment will be for one year or earlier, whichever is less, and may be extended based on satisfactory performance and the requirement of the project. Selected candidates will not be liable to bear any expenditure on pension contribution, leave salary contribution, Provident Fund, ESI etc. No TA/DA will be paid for appearing in the interview.

ICAR-NBSS&amp;LUP Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

The application must be submitted strictly in the prescribed format. Applications received after the stipulated last date of submission will not be entertained. Selected candidates will not be entitled to any claim for regular appointment/absorption in the Institute or in ICAR, nor to employment or engagement in ICAR at the end of the contract period.
