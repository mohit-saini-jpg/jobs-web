RailTel Advisor Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

RailTel Corporation of India Ltd., a Navratna Central Public Sector Enterprise under the Government of India (Ministry of Railways), is inviting applications for 1 post of Advisor (Signalling) – Northern Region/ Delhi NCR on contract under Vacancy Notice No. 20/2026. About RailTel

RailTel is a "Navratna" Central Public Sector Enterprise and an ICT provider. The notice describes it as one of the largest neutral telecom infrastructure providers in the country, owning a Pan-India optic fiber network laid along railway tracks, and as a listed company aiming to modernise the telecom system for train control, operation and safety. Its registered and corporate office is at East Kidwai Nagar, New Delhi. Through its strategic relationship with the Indian Railways, RailTel undertakes projects such as IP based video surveillance at stations, 'e-Office' services and short haul and long haul connectivity, and also provides passenger services including content on demand and Wi-Fi at major railway stations.
