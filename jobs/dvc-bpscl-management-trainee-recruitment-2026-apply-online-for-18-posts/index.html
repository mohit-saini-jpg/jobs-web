DVC BPSCL Management Trainee Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Damodar Valley Corporation (DVC), under the Ministry of Power, Government of India, has invited online applications on behalf of Bokaro Power Supply Company (P) Limited (BPSCL) for 18 Management Trainee (Technical) posts in Mechanical, Electrical, Civil and C&I, to be filled on GATE-2025 marks, in the pay scale of Rs 50,000–1,60,000. Applications are taken only on the DVC website www.dvc.gov.in (Career Section, Recruitment Notices) from 6 November 2026 until 23:59 hrs on 6 December 2026. Who Can Apply

For all four posts the essential qualification is a full-time Bachelor's Degree in Engineering or Technology from a university/institution approved by AICTE/appropriate statutory authority. Marks must be not less than 65% for Gen/OBC (NCL)/EWS and 55% for SC/ST/PwD, in aggregate of all years/semesters, as per the conversion formula of the concerned university/institution. The discipline and the GATE-2025 paper differ by post: The candidate must have appeared in the corresponding GATE-2025 paper and qualified in it. Only GATE-2025 marks (out of 100) are valid; GATE marks of 2024 or earlier are not valid. Candidates who appeared in the final semester (2025) of their engineering course and are awaiting results may apply, but must submit the final year mark sheet and provisional degree certificate/degree certificate at Document Verification. DVC BPSCL Management Trainee Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection rests on GATE-2025 marks, followed by Document Verification and a pre-employment medical examination. The selection consists only of marks obtained (out of 100) in the corresponding GATE-2025 paper. Qualifying marks are as decided by the GATE-2025 conducting authority. Candidates are shortlisted in order of merit in the ratio of 1:5 or more to the category-wise vacancies in each discipline. If two or more candidates in the same discipline and category have equal marks and the ratio is exceeded, all of them are called. BPSCL may increase or decrease the ratio, and being GATE-qualified gives no right to be called. Date, time and venue are posted on the DVC website only, and no hard copy call letter is sent by post. Candidates must show the GATE-2025 admit card and original scorecard as identity proof; a hazy photo may be corroborated with Aadhaar, Voter Card, PAN, Driving Licence

DVC BPSCL Management Trainee Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many Management Trainee posts are there in DVC BPSCL Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 18 projected vacancies: 10 Mechanical, 4 Electrical, 1 Civil and 3 C&I. The notice says the numbers may increase or decrease at BPSCL's discretion.
Q2What is the last date to apply?
A
The last date of online application is 6 December 2026, till 23:59 hrs. The last date for paying the application fee is also 6 December 2026.
Q3What is the qualification required?
A
A full-time Bachelor's Degree in Engineering or Technology in the advertised discipline from an AICTE/appropriate statutory authority approved institution, with not less than 65% marks (Gen/OBC (NCL)/EWS) or 55% (SC/ST/PwD) in aggregate. A valid GATE-2025 score in the corresponding paper is also required.
Q4What is the age limit?
A
The upper age limit is 28 years for unreserved candidates as on the closing date of online application. For BPSCL departmental candidates it is 45 years, and relaxations are 3 years for OBC (NCL) and 5 years for SC/ST.
Q5What is the salary of a DVC BPSCL Management Trainee?
A
The appointment is in the pay scale of Rs 50,000–1,60,000, with initial Basic Pay of Rs 50,000 during one year of training. After training the pay scale is Rs 60,000-3%-1,80,000 on one year probation as Assistant Manager (E-1 Grade).
Q6What is the application fee?
A
General, OBC (NCL) and EWS candidates pay a non-refundable Rs 300 online. SC, ST, PwBD, Ex-SM and BPSCL departmental candidates are exempted.
Q7How do I apply for DVC BPSCL Management Trainee?
A
Apply online only on www.dvc.gov.in under Career Section, Recruitment Notices, then use the fee payment link if you are not exempted. Keep your GATE-2025 scorecard, photograph, signature and PDF certificates (maximum 2 MB each) ready.
Q8How are candidates selected?
A
Selection is based only on marks (out of 100) in the corresponding GATE-2025 paper. Candidates are shortlisted for Document Verification in the ratio of 1:5 or more to category-wise vacancies, followed by a pre-employment medical examination before joining.
Q9Can I apply for more than one post?
A
No, a candidate can apply for one post/discipline only. The GATE-2025 paper must match the post: ME for Mechanical, EE for Electrical, CE for Civil and IN for C&I.
Q10Are GATE 2024 marks valid for this recruitment?
A
No, only GATE-2025 marks (out of 100) are valid. GATE marks of 2024 or earlier are not valid for this recruitment.
