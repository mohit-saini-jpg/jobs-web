How to Download Calicut University Time Table 2026

  • Go to the Pareeksha Bhavan website of the Calicut University: pareekshabhavan.uoc.ac.in.
  • Open the Time Table page from the Examination menu. The Notifications page also lists announcements.
  • Look for the entry that matches your programme, semester and scheme, for example Third Semester MCA or Third Semester M.Sc. Computer Science.
  • Check the publication date (18 November 2026) so you do not open an older or superseded notice.
  • Click the PDF link to open the time table.
  • Download the PDF and save a copy. Take a printout if you need one.

What to Check in the Time Table PDF

  • Programme name, semester and scheme (for example 2020 Scheme).
  • Admission year range the notice applies to.
  • Whether the notice is for regular, supplementary or improvement candidates.
  • Whether it applies to a specific campus or centre, such as CCSIT or the School of Health Science.
  • Examination dates, session and paper-wise schedule.
  • Any instruction or correction added by Pareeksha Bhavan.

Important Instructions for Students

  • Refer only to the official Calicut University website for the final schedule.
  • Match your scheme and admission year with the notice before relying on it.
  • Campus-specific notices (CCSIT, School of Health Science) apply only to those centres.
  • Check the website regularly, because universities can revise or postpone schedules.
  • For queries, contact Pareeksha Bhavan, Calicut University, on 0494 240 7200 or at [email protected].
