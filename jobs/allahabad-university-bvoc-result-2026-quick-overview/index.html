How to Check Allahabad University B.Voc Result 2026 Online

Students can follow these steps to check their B.Voc result on the official Allahabad University result portal:

  • Visit the official result page at coe.allduniv.ac.in/result.
  • Look for the "List of declared results for session 2025-26" table on the page.
  • Find and click on your relevant course entry - for example, "B Voc FPT II Sem," "B Voc FTI II Sem," "B Voc SD II Sem," or "B Voc Automobile VI Sem."
  • You will be redirected to the result search page for that course.
  • Enter your roll number (and any other login details asked, such as enrollment number, as required by the portal).
  • Submit the details to view your result.
  • Download or take a screenshot/printout of the result for future reference.

Details Usually Shown on the AU B.Voc Result

While the exact result format may vary by course, results published on the Allahabad University CoE portal typically display the following details:

  • Student's name
  • Roll number / enrollment number
  • Course and semester name
  • Subject-wise marks or grades
  • Overall result status (Pass/Fail/RL, etc.)

Note: The exact fields shown on the result (for example, whether SGPA/CGPA is displayed, or whether it is marks-based or grade-based) are not confirmed in the source information provided and may vary. Students should refer to their actual result page for exact details.

What to Do After Checking the Result

If you have concerns about your B.Voc result, such as a discrepancy in marks or a request for revaluation/scrutiny, you should check the official Allahabad University website or contact the Controller of Examinations office directly, as specific revaluation dates, fees, and procedures are not available in the official notification referenced for this article.
