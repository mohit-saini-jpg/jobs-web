KSRLPS Eligibility Criteria 2026

Block Program Officer – SVEP: Post Graduate / Graduate in any subject with Mathematics as a subject till class. Preferably from a Management, Commerce, or Engineering background. Strong numeracy and business skills required. Minimum 2 years of work experience in non-farm livelihoods sector. Cluster Supervisor: Graduate. Minimum 2 years post qualification relevant experience. Reading, writing & speaking skills in Kannada & English. Knowledge of working with computers. Block Manager – Non-Farm Livelihood: Post-Graduate. 3+ Years of Experience in relevant field. Taluk Program Manager: Full time Post Graduate Degree (2 Years) / PG Diploma. Minimum 5 years post qualification relevant experience. Preference to candidates with minimum 1 year experience as Taluk Mission Manager or 2 years as Cluster Supervisor. Reading, writing (including typing) & speaking skills in Kannada & English.
