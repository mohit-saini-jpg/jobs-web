About the Centre

The notice is issued by the Head of the Project, CoE-UPD, SPA Delhi, for engagement of non-technical manpower for the Centre of Excellence in Urban Planning and Design, MoHUA, Govt. of India. The engagement is on contract basis and is project-based.

Where and How to Send

Candidates must apply in the prescribed application form for Non-Technical Positions. The form can be downloaded from the SPA Delhi application form (PDF) . Download the prescribed application form from the link given in the notice. Fill in the form completely, as incomplete applications are summarily rejected. Prepare a proper scanned copy of the complete application form along with self-attested copies of certificates, marks sheets, testimonials, etc. Email the scanned set to both [email protected] and [email protected] . Make sure the email is sent latest by 30 November 2026; applications received after the last date are summarily rejected. The notice does not give an email subject line or a postal address.

Posts, Eligibility and Pay

Essential: B.E/B.Tech in a relevant field in relevant subject from a recognized university with at least 50% marks or equivalent grade. Desirable: Proficiency in Soft Computing and Data Management. Essential: Senior Secondary Examination (12th Standard) or equivalent qualification from a recognized University/Board. Essential: Typewriting speed of 35 words per minute in English or 30 words per minute in Hindi on computer. Desirable: Working knowledge of MS Office and typing speed on computer. Desirable: Experience of handling processing/management/data entry, etc. Candidates should check the official notification for the discipline-specific eligibility of each post. The remuneration is paid as stated above. No other allowance such as TA, DA, HRA or Medical is admissible. Terms of Engagement

Selected candidates must enter into a contract with CoE-UPD, including a confidentiality clause, before being assigned any work. The initial tenure is 11 months from the date of engagement, which may be extended based on satisfactory performance and project requirements. Either party may terminate the engagement by giving one month's notice. The engagement is purely contractual and project-based; selected candidates have no right to claim regularization or permanent employment in the School of Planning and Architecture or any other authority. Candidates are entitled to 08 days of Casual Leave during the period of engagement, as per applicable rules.

Enclosures

Complete application form (scanned copy). Self-attested copies of certificates, marks sheets, testimonials, etc. Original documents, to be produced at the time of verification if shortlisted.

SPA Delhi Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Applications are scrutinized and eligible candidates are shortlisted for the selection process. For Data Entry Operator, a Skill Test (Typing Test) is conducted for shortlisted candidates to assess typing speed and computer proficiency. The prescribed speed is 35 words per minute in English or 30 words per minute in Hindi on a computer. Shortlisted candidates may also be called for personal interaction and verification of documents, and must produce original documents at verification. The final selection is based on the prescribed eligibility criteria, educational qualifications, relevant experience, performance in the Skill Test (where applicable) and personal interaction.
