IIT Indore JRF Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The School of Humanities and Social Sciences at IIT Indore has one JRF post in the ANRF-funded project "Exploring Sleep-Dependent Memory Consolidation: A Study on Destination Memory", for 6 months at ₹ 37,000 + 20% HRA. Candidates need a PhD (submitted/defended) and a Master's degree in psychology, Cognitive Science, Neuroscience, Life Sciences, or a related discipline, with at least 60% marks. Applications go by email to [email protected] by 15 November 2026 (23:59), and the online interview is on 18 November 2026.

About IIT Indore

The notice is issued by the Research & Development Section of the Indian Institute of Technology Indore, Khandwa Road, Simrol, Indore 453552. The post sits in the School of Humanities and Social Sciences. The research project focuses on sleep and memory and is funded by ANRF.

Who Can Apply

PhD (submitted/defended) & Master's degree in psychology, Cognitive Science, Neuroscience, Life Sciences, or a related discipline, with at least 60% marks (50% for SC/ST/OBC-NCL/PwD, as applicable). Strong interest in experimental research, cognitive psychology, and sleep research. Research experience in cognitive psychology, sleep research, and neuroscience. Familiarity with experimental methods, behavioural data collection, and statistical analysis (SPSS, R, or Python). Knowledge of EEG/PSG, sleep assessment, or psychophysiological techniques will be an added advantage. Good scientific writing, communication, and interpersonal skills. The age limit is 22 to 32 years. The notice prints no age relaxation and no date for counting age. Candidates should check the official notification for the exact eligibility conditions of the post.

Pay and Tenure

The fellowship is ₹ 37,000 + 20% HRA. The appointment lasts 6 months. The selected candidate will be encouraged to register for the PhD program at IIT Indore as per institute norms. If selected for the PhD program, a fellowship will be extended for the PhD duration as per institute norms.

IIT Indore JRF Job Description

The selected candidate will work on an ANRF-funded research project focusing on Sleep and Memory. Responsibilities will include conducting experimental research, recruiting participants, collecting and analysing behavioural and sleep-related data, and assisting in research publications. The candidate will be expected to contribute to experimental design, data management, statistical analysis, and other project-related activities.

Selection and Interview

Only shortlisted candidates will be called for an interview, which is online on 18 November 2026. Selected candidates will be informed by email. The notice does not describe how shortlisting is done.
