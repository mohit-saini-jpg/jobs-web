CMRL General Manager Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The one post of General Manager (Operations) at Chennai Metro Rail Limited (CMRL), a Joint Venture of Government of India and Government of Tamil Nadu, is on Regular basis with IDA-pattern pay of Rs.1,20,000 - 2,80,000 a month, plus DA, HRA (30% of Basic Pay) and Cafeteria Allowance (31.5% of Basic Pay). Applicants must be a B.E / B.Tech or any Graduate, and the experience required depends on whether they come from a Government organisation, a Metro / PSU or the private sector. Applications are accepted only online at careers.chennaimetrorail.org on or before 15-12-2026, under Employment Notif

About CMRL

Chennai Metro Rail Limited, with its office at Anna Salai, Nandanam, Chennai - 600 035, is a Joint Venture of Government of India and Government of Tamil Nadu. In the notice it is described as a special purpose vehicle (SPV) entrusted with implementing the Metro Rail Project in the city of Chennai.

Who Can Apply

Educational qualification: B. E / B. Tech or any Graduate from a Govt recognized Institute / University, approved by AICTE / UGC. Only Indian Nationals need to apply. Experience required, by type of applicant: Applicant from Govt Organization: Group A Officer from Railways working in Senior Administrative Grade (SAG) / Non-Functional Senior Administrative Grade (NF-SAG) / Equivalent in GoI, having sufficient experience in Train operations. Applicant from Metros / PSUs Organization: minimum 20 years of post qualification executive experience and at least 3 years as AGM in the IDA pay scale of Rs. 100000 - 260000 or equivalent pay. Applicant from Private Sector: minimum 20 years of post qualification executive experience and at least 03 years in top management positions (equivalent to AGM in PSU), reporting directly to a Director or Board. Pay and Terms

The scale of pay (IDA pattern) is Rs.1,20,000 - 2,80,000 per month. Apart from the Basic Pay, DA (IDA pattern), HRA (30% of Basic Pay), Cafeteria Allowance (31.5% of Basic Pay) and other benefits are paid. These include Perks, Medical benefit, EPF, Gratuity and insurance etc., as per extant rules of HR Manual, CMRL. The post is on Regular basis. Selected candidates are on probation for one year. Confirmation is solely based on performance and successful completion of probation. CMRL can terminate service during probation if performance is found unsatisfactory, in accordance with the terms of the offer of appointment. One year relaxation in the residency period in next below scale will be considered for internal candidates.

CMRL General Manager Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

The selection is a two-stage process: interview followed by a medical examination. It judges candidates on knowledge, skills, comprehension, attitude, aptitude and physical fitness. Scrutiny of applications for shortlisting is based solely on the documents and certificates uploaded online. Merely meeting the qualifications and experience does not entitle a candidate to be selected for interview. Only short-listed candidates will be notified for interview, and CMRL reserves the right to shortlist. Eligibility is provisional and is verified only for shortlisted candidates. Expenses for the first-time medical examination are borne by CMRL. If a candidate seeks extension for joining, the second medical examination is at the candidate's expense. To and fro travel expenses for the medical test are borne by the candidate. A candidate who fails the prescribed medical test will not be given alter
