IREL Tradesman Trainee Selection Process 2026

The selection process consists of a Computer Based Test (CBT) followed by a qualifying Skill Test or Trade Test and document verification. Computer Based Written Examination. Shortlisting for the Skill Test in the ratio of 1:5 based on CBT merit. Qualifying Skill Test / Trade Test or Computer Proficiency Test, as applicable. Preparation of unit-wise, trade-wise and category-wise merit lists using CBT marks. Document Verification. Pre-employment Medical Examination and other prescribed verification. Final Merit Rule: Only candidates qualifying in the Skill Test will be considered for final selection. Final merit will be determined using CBT marks only. Skill Test marks will not be added to the final merit.

IREL Tradesman Trainee Exam Pattern 2026

The written examination will be a Computer Based Test containing 150 multiple-choice questions for 150 marks . The examination duration is two hours, and there is no negative marking. Important Exam Rule: Candidates must secure the prescribed minimum qualifying percentage separately in Section I and Section II. Obtaining the required percentage in the combined total alone is not sufficient.
