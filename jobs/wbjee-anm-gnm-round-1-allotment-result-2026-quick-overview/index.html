WBJEE ANM & GNM Round 2 Allotment 2026 - Quick Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 26/08/2026

The West Bengal Joint Entrance Examinations Board (WBJEEB) will declare the WBJEE ANM(R) & GNM Round 2 seat allotment result 2026 on August 26, 2026 today. Candidates who registered and filled choices during the Round 2 window (August 22 to 24, 2026) can now check their allotment status on the official counselling portals, wbjeeb.nic.in and wbjeeb.in. This round covers admission to the 2-year Auxiliary Nursing & Midwifery (Revised) - ANM(R) course and the 3-year General Nursing & Midwifery (GNM) course for the 2026-27 academic session.

How to Check the WBJEE ANM & GNM Round 2 Seat Allotment Result 2026

  • Visit the official WBJEEB counselling portal at wbjeeb.nic.in or wbjeeb.in.
  • Click on the ANM & GNM 2026 Round 2 seat allotment result link on the homepage.
  • Log in using your ANM & GNM-2026 application/roll number and password.
  • Your Round 2 allotment status - allotted institute, course, and category - will be displayed on screen.
  • Download and save the Provisional Seat Allotment Letter for further admission formalities.
  • If allotted a seat, proceed to pay the seat acceptance fee and complete document verification within the window of August 26 to 29, 2026.

Important Instructions for Round 2 Candidates

  • Candidates who did not submit any choice in Round 2 were not considered for allotment in this round.
  • Candidates who opted "Yes" for upgradation after a Round 1 allotment automatically remained in contention for Round 2 but still need to track their status and complete formalities as instructed.
  • Candidates who fail to complete document verification and admission within the stipulated window (August 26 to 29, 2026) may be debarred from the entire counselling process.
  • Only female candidates are eligible for the ANM course; male candidates may apply only for GNM, and only in designated Male institutes.
  • All matters relating to the ANM & GNM-2026 e-counselling process fall under the exclusive jurisdiction of the Hon'ble High Court at Calcutta.
  • Candidates not satisfied with their Round 2 allotment, or not allotted a seat, can participate in the Round 3 (Mop-Up) round, scheduled for August 31 to September 1, 2026 (registration/choice filling) with the result on September 3, 2026.
