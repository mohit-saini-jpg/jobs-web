ICF Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Integral Coach Factory, Chennai (Ministry of Railways) is re-engaging retired Non-Gazetted Railway Accounts staff against 8 posts: 1 Sr.Section officer(A), 3 Sr.Stock Verifier / Stock Verifier and 4 Sr.Accountant (erstwhile Accounts Assistant). Only retired Railway staff of the Accounts Department of Zonal Railways/PUs may apply. Applications in the prescribed form go by ordinary post only to DY.FA&CAO/GENERAL, Integral Coach Factory, Chennai and must be received by 02.12.2026 at 17:00 hrs.

About ICF

Integral Coach Factory (ICF), Chennai-38 functions under the Ministry of Railways. This notification is issued by the Dy.FA&CAO/General of its Accounts Department, Shell Division, under Railway Board's letters No.E(NG)II/2024/RC-4/9 dated 15/10/2024 (RBE 96/2024), 31/12/2024 (RBE 115/2024) and 22/09/2026 (RBE 73/2026), which govern the re-engagement of retired Non-Gazetted staff. The re-engagement scheme is valid up to 15.10.2028.

Where and How to Send

Applications must be sent through ordinary post only and must reach on or before the closing date and time, 02.12.2026 at 17:00 hrs. The application is addressed as printed on the form: DY.FA&CAO/ GENERAL, ACCOUNTS DEPARTMENT, SHELL DIVISION, INTEGRAL COACH FACTORY, CHENNAI – 600 038. Download the application format (Annexure I and Annexure II) from the ACCOUNTS WEBSITE/ICF, or use the format enclosed with the notification. Fill in all columns of Annexure I in your own handwriting; all columns are mandatory. Write the post applied for at the top. Fill in Annexure II with the details of postings held during the service period (office, section, designation and dates of joining and relieving). Affix a recent passport size photograph in the space provided and sign legibly in the space provided. Enclose self-attested photocopies of the documents listed under Enclosures. ICF Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

The suitability/competency of applicants will be adjudged by a Committee, and the integrity aspect will also be checked before re-engagement is permitted. Eligible candidates will be intimated through SMS only, and all SMS communications will come from ACCOUNTS/ICF. Applicants called for document verification should bring all original certificates. The Administration reserves the right to conduct a viva voce in case of necessity. The decision of ICF on eligibility, acceptance or rejection of applications, selection and allotment of posts is final.

ICF Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

Re-engagement is for 1 year or till the validity of the scheme or till further orders, whichever is earlier. On satisfactory performance it can be extended for one more year or till the continuation of the scheme or till attainment of 65 years of age, whichever is earlier. Re-engaged staff shall be discharged immediately on joining of selected candidates from RRBs, CGA, GDCE(RRC) or through Departmental Selection, or for other reasons of administrative convenience on the recommendation of the PHOD/CHOD concerned. The Administration reserves the right to terminate the services at any time if work or conduct is found unsatisfactory or in administrative exigencies. Duty hours will be as prescribed by the Competent Authority as per the working conditions of the category, with a weekly off for one day. Re-engaged staff will not be given financial and D&AR powers and cannot issue any safety certificate, but may recommend suggestions to regular staff/Officers. The retired employee must execute an agreement to abide by all terms and conditions of the re-engagement. The mobile number and e-mail id should not be changed till completion of selection; the administration is not responsible for non-receipt of communication otherwise. ICF reserves the right to reject incomplete or illegible applications and applications without requisite enclosures, signature or latest photograph; no correspondence will be entertained.

ICF Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in ICF Recruitment 2026 for retired Accounts staff?
A
There are 8 posts: 01 Sr.Section officer(A), 03 Sr.Stock Verifier / Stock Verifier and 04 Sr.Accountant (erstwhile Accounts Assistant).
Q2What is the last date for the ICF re-engagement application?
A
Applications must be received by 02.12.2026 at 17:00 hrs, sent through ordinary post only.
Q3Who can apply for ICF re-engagement 2026?
A
Retired Railway staff who worked in the same post in the Accounts Department of Zonal Railways/PUs can apply; volunteers retired from posts up to three levels higher in the same cadre/category may also be considered.
Q4What is the age limit?
A
The age limit is upto 65 years of age as on the date of notification, 19.11.2026.
Q5What salary will re-engaged staff get?
A
A fixed monthly remuneration equal to the Pay drawn at retirement minus the Basic Pension, under RBE No.96/2024; no Annual Increment or Dearness Allowance is allowed.
Q6How do I apply for ICF Recruitment 2026?
A
Fill in Annexure I and II in your own handwriting, affix a photograph, sign, enclose self-attested copies of the Service Certificate, Pensioner Identity Card and PPO, and send it by ordinary post to DY.FA&CAO/GENERAL, Integral Coach Factory, Chennai-600038.
Q7How will candidates be selected?
A
A Committee will adjudge suitability/competency and check integrity, applicants bring original certificates for document verification, and a viva voce may be conducted in case of necessity.
Q8How long is the re-engagement?
A
It is for 1 year or till the validity of the scheme (upto 15.10.2028) or till further orders, whichever is earlier, extendable by one more year on satisfactory performance up to age 65.
Q9Who is not eligible for re-engagement?
A
Retired employees covered under the Safety Related Retirement Scheme/LARSGESS, and those with DAR/Vigilance cases or a criminal/Police case pending till the application date, are not eligible.
