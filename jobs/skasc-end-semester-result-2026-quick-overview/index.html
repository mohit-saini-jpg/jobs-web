Who Can Check This Result?

The notice covers the following students who appeared in the October/November 2026 end semester examinations:

  • Undergraduate (UG) students of the 2024, 2023 and 2022 batches
  • M.Sc. SS students of the 2024, 2023 and 2022 batches

How to Check SKASC End Semester Result 2026

  • Open the official SKASC Results page at skasc.ac.in/examinations/results.
  • Click the link titled "17 Nov 2026 - END SEMESTER EXAMINATIONS RESULT (UG & M.Sc. SS (2024/2023/2022) - OCT /NOV 2026".
  • The result portal will open. Follow the on-screen instructions to sign in.
  • View your result on the screen.
  • Save or print a copy for your records.

What to Do After the Result

  • Check your result carefully, including subject codes and your personal details.
  • If you find a mistake or have a query, contact the Controller of Examinations office.
  • For revaluation, re-totalling, arrear or supplementary exams, watch the official Examination News and Online Applications pages. No such details are given in the current result notice.
  • Keep a copy of your result for future reference.

Important Instructions for Students

  • Use only the official SKASC website to reach the result link.
  • Do not share your login details with anyone.
  • If the portal is slow, try again after some time.
  • Passing criteria, grading rules and revaluation rules are not given in the result notice. Refer to the college's examination regulations or ask the examination office.
