UCMS Recruitment 2026 Reservation and Certificate Requirements

Reservation for SC, ST, OBC-NCL, EWS and Persons with Benchmark Disabilities is governed by applicable UGC and Central Government rules. Candidates must upload valid, digitally verifiable reservation certificates in the prescribed Government of India format. OBC-NCL certificates must satisfy the current financial year's requirements and correspond to communities included in the Central List of Other Backward Classes. For PwBD reservation, candidates must possess a valid certificate showing at least 40% benchmark disability . Important: The advertised PwBD vacancies are specified for the Locomotor Disability category under the notification, including the disability conditions described in the original vacancy table.
