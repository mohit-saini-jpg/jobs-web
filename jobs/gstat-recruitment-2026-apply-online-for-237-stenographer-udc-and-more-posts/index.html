GSTAT Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Department of Revenue has issued the Vacancy Circular for Appointment of Officers in GSTAT. It continues the department's earlier vacancy circulars dated 14.09.2024 and 14.08.2025 and invites fresh applications for posts in GSTAT benches. The official notification does not specify the application fee.

About GSTAT

The Goods and Services Tax Appellate Tribunal (GSTAT) functions under the Department of Revenue, Ministry of Finance, Government of India, and has benches across which these posts are spread. The vacancy circular was issued from 14108, C Wing, 4th Floor, Kartavya Bhawan – 1, New Delhi, and was signed by Tarandeep Singh, Under Secretary to the Government of India (Tele: 011-24012544). It is hosted on the Department of Revenue website and is also posted on the CBIC website ( www.cbic.gov.in ) and the CBDT site ( www.irsofficersonline.gov.in ).

GSTAT Selection Process 2026

Positions will be filled based on merit cum first cum first receipt basis. Applications received and found complete in all respects will be considered for selection periodically for the remaining available vacancies. The Department of Revenue reserves the right to post suitable applicants in any bench out of the three preferences given by the applicant, subject to the availability of vacancy in the posts applied for.

GSTAT Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Open the online application portal at https://gstn.org.in/gstat-application. The link will be active from 15 November 2026. The vacancy circular, proforma and application link are also hosted at dor.gov.in/gstat-recruitment.
  • 2Fill in the application in the proforma given in Annexure 2 and Annexure 3, with the requisite attachments.
  • 3Choose three bench preferences in the "Location applying for" field.
  • 4Submit the application through the online portal within 30 days from 15 November 2026, i.e. before 15 December 2026.
  • 5Send a copy of your application to your Cadre Controlling Authority.
  • 6The Cadre Controlling Authority forwards the application in the Annexure 3 proforma, with photocopies of APARs/ACRs of the last five years attested by an officer not below the rank of Under Secretary to the Government of India, to the Under Secretary, Ad.1C Branch, Department of Revenue, Ministry of Finance, Kartavya Bhawan-1, New Delhi-110001 (also at [email protected]). This must be done within 15 days from the date it receives the application.
  • 7Officers already on deputation must have their application forwarded by the parent cadre or department, along with Cadre Clearance, Vigilance Clearance and an integrity certificate.
  • 8A photograph and three bench preferences, chosen from a drop-down list.
  • 9The date of entry into government service and the date of retirement as per parent cadre rules.
  • 10A clear statement on whether you meet the requisite essential qualification and work experience.
  • 11Employment details in chronological order (office, post held, from, to, scale of pay, nature of appointment). Attach a separate sheet if needed.
  • 12The nature of present employment (ad-hoc, temporary, quasi-permanent or permanent), deputation or contract details where applicable, any past deputation with the date of return, and whether you work under the Central Government, a State Government or another employer.

  • 14Photograph on the application form.

GSTAT Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in GSTAT Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 237 posts across 13 grades, the largest being Stenographer Grade I (59) and Court Officer (56). All counts are tentative and subject to variation dependent on workload.
Q2When does the GSTAT 2026 online application start?
A
The online application link at gstn.org.in/gstat-application will be active from 15 November 2026.
Q3What is the last date to apply for GSTAT Recruitment 2026?
A
Applications must be submitted within 30 days from 15 November 2026, i.e. before 15 December 2026. The cut-off date for eligibility is 15 December 2026.
Q4What is the age limit for GSTAT deputation posts?
A
The maximum age is fifty-six years for posts at Sl. No. 1 to 12 and fifty-eight years for Upper Division Clerk, as on the closing date of receipt of applications.
Q5What qualification is required for GSTAT Recruitment 2026?
A
Most posts need a Bachelor's degree with experience; for example, Court Officer needs a Bachelors Degree and two years experience in administration or establishment or Court matters. Senior Private Secretary, Private Secretary, Stenographer Grade I and Upper Division Clerk have only service conditions.
Q6What is the salary for GSTAT posts?
A
Pay ranges from Level-4 (Rs.25500 - 81100) for Upper Division Clerk to Level-13 (Rs.123100 - 215900) for Financial Advisor. Court Officer and Senior Private Secretary are at Level-8 (Rs.47600 - 151100).
Q7How will candidates be selected for GSTAT 2026?
A
Positions will be filled based on merit cum first cum first receipt basis. Complete applications are considered periodically for the remaining available vacancies.
Q8Who can apply for GSTAT Recruitment 2026?
A
Only serving officers and officials of the Central Government, State Governments, Union Territories or Tribunals can apply, along with other bodies listed for some posts. Each applicant must hold an analogous post or have the prescribed regular service in a lower pay level.
Q9How long is the deputation period in GSTAT?
A
The deputation period is ordinarily not more than three years for most posts, four years for Deputy Registrar and Principal Private Secretary, and five years for Financial Advisor and Upper Division Clerk. It may be curtailed, or extended or curtailed in administrative exigencies.
Q10Can a selected candidate withdraw from GSTAT deputation?
A
No. Candidates once selected will not be allowed to withdraw their candidature subsequently on any ground.

A
