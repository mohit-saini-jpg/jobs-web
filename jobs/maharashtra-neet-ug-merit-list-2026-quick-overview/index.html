Maharashtra NEET UG Merit List 2026 - Quick Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 29/08/2026

The State Common Entrance Test (CET) Cell, Government of Maharashtra, has published the Revised Final Merit List of registered candidates for MBBS/ BDS courses (Group A) of NEET-UG-2026, dated 28 August 2026. This list is part of the Centralised Admission Process (CAP) that governs admission to 85% state-quota seats in MBBS and BDS courses at government, government-aided, municipal, and private unaided medical and dental colleges in Maharashtra.

How to Check Your Name and Rank in the Merit List

  • Go to the official State CET Cell portal at cetcell.mahacet.org (or the round-specific counselling site indicated on the portal).
  • Open the "UG Medical Admissions" or NEET UG 2026 CAP section.
  • Look for the link titled "Revised Final Merit List (Group A)" or "NEET UG Revised Candidate List for Group A."
  • Open the PDF - it will typically run into several hundred pages.
  • Use Ctrl+F (or Cmd+F on Mac) and search for your NEET Roll Number, CET Cell Form Number, or full name to locate your entry quickly.
  • Note your Sr. No., NEET All India Rank, category, and any remark shown against your name.
  • Download and save the PDF, or the relevant page, for your records before choice filling.

Reservation and Category Notes (As Stated in the Official List)

  • NRI candidature is subject to approval from the Admission Regulating Authority (ARA).
  • PWD (Persons with Disability) candidature is subject to eligibility confirmation from the Medical Board.
  • Constitutional and specified reservation benefits are admitted subject to submission and verification of the requisite supporting documents.

Next Steps After This Merit List

  • Choice Filling: Eligible candidates on the merit list fill their college and course preferences on the CAP counselling portal.
  • Seat Allotment: Based on rank, category, choices filled, and seat availability, a provisional seat allotment is declared for CAP Round 1.
  • Reporting: Candidates allotted a seat must report to the allotted college within the specified window, carrying original documents and any required fee instrument.
