About BSA Hardoi ARP

The District Basic Education Officer, Hardoi heads basic education in Hardoi district, Uttar Pradesh. Under the Government Order, every development block has 06 Academic Resource Persons: 05 are chosen through this selection process and 01 DIET mentor nominated for the block also works as an ARP. Each urban resource centre also has 05 ARPs.

BSA Hardoi ARP Salary 2026

No honorarium of any other kind is payable for work as an ARP, apart from the travel allowance and TLM. The mobility allowance is paid for supportive supervision and teacher-training work, only after evidence is submitted that supervision or training as a resource person was done as per the set target. If not, the district project office will deduct the allowance at proportional rates as per rules. The TLM grant of Rs. 500/- per month is released for preparing self-made teaching-learning material. The training, educational tour, mobility allowance and TLM funds are provided in the Samagra Shiksha annual work plan and budget, excluding the summer and winter vacations.

BSA Hardoi ARP Selection Process 2026

Scrutiny of applications to decide eligibility or ineligibility. Written examination of 70 marks. Interview of 30 marks for candidates who score 60% or more in the written examination. Merit list prepared on the marks of the written examination and the interview. The selection is made by a district-level selection committee chaired by the Chief Development Officer. Its members are the Principal of the District Institute of Education and Training, the District Basic Education Officer, the District Coordinator (NIPUN Bharat Mission / Training) and the senior-most Block Education Officer. The committee obtains the District Magistrate's approval for the ARP selection. An evaluation committee of teachers of Government Inter Colleges / Government Girls' Inter Colleges and a DIET lecturer will set the question papers and evaluate the answer sheets. BSA Hardoi ARP Job Description 2026

ARP working hours are mainly school hours; the ARP can go to the block resource centre only after school hours, as work requires. Supportive supervision of at least 30 unique schools each month (KGBV at most 02 times) through the Prerna Gunvatta app, with actual checklist data filled on the app. Observing teaching-learning and student participation, spot assessment of 05 random students, and demonstrating TLM and running model classes. Observing school activities (prayer assembly, seating, academic calendar, timetable, syllabus progress, TLM, 'Talika' and NIPUN+ app use, library, sports) and giving academic support. Taking part as a resource person in trainings, as directed by the State Project Office. Sending the next month's work plan and tour programme to the DBEO and DIET Principal by the 25th of each month, and an achievement report by the 5th of the following month. Discussing gend

BSA Hardoi Academic Resource Person Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in BSA Hardoi ARP Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 36 vacant Academic Resource Person posts. Hardoi district has 100 ARP posts in all, of which 64 are filled.
Q2What is the last date for the Hardoi ARP application?
A
The application must reach the Office of the District Basic Education Officer, Hardoi by registered post by 05:00 PM on 30 November 2026.
Q3Who can apply for the Hardoi ARP post?
A
Teachers and female teachers working in primary and upper primary schools under the Basic Shiksha Parishad, Uttar Pradesh can apply. They need at least 05 years of teaching experience and at least 05 years of service left.
Q4What qualification is required for the ARP post?
A
A graduate degree with a teacher training qualification is required. Applicants must also have no pending enquiry and no proven disciplinary action.
Q5What is the salary of an ARP in Hardoi?
A
The ARP's pay continues from the original working school with no other increase. A mobility allowance of up to Rs. 4500/- per month and a TLM grant of Rs. 500/- per month are paid.
Q6What is the selection process for the Hardoi ARP post?
A
Selection is by a 70-mark written examination and a 30-mark interview. The merit list is based on the marks of both.
Q7What are the qualifying marks in the ARP written exam?
A
Only candidates who score 60% or more in the written examination qualify for the interview.
Q8Is the ARP post permanent?
A
No. The ARP tenure is 01 year, renewable each year on satisfactory performance, up to a maximum of 03 years. In the declaration, the applicant accepts that there is no permanent claim to the post.
Q9Can an ARP who has completed 03 years apply again?
A
Such ARPs are ineligible for future selection. However, they may apply again if fewer applications than posts are received.
Q10Where can I get the Hardoi ARP application form?
A
The form is available on www.hardoi.nic.in, at the District Basic Education Officer office, and at all Block Resource Centre / Nagar Sansadhan Kendra offices.
