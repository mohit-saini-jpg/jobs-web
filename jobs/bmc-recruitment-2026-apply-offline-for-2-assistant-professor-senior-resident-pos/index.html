Qualification and Experience

The notice lists these educational requirements: Qualifications as laid down by the National Medical Commission in the Minimum Qualifications for Teachers in Medical Institutions Regulations, 1998, as amended up to 14 February 2022. The candidate must be registered with the Maharashtra Medical Council. The candidate must have an M.D degree or DNB or M.S degree from a recognized University. Self-attested xerox copies of all the required educational qualifications (marksheets and certificates) must be attached to the filled application form. For both posts, the candidate should be not more than 38 years and not less than 18 years old. The notice does not give the date on which age will be counted. For the discipline-specific eligibility of each post, candidates should check the official notification.

Pay and Terms

The appointment is purely on contract basis, for up to 3 years, and is not a regular appointment. It will not be treated as a regular appointment. A candidate appointed on contract will be discontinued at any time if not required. A candidate can be terminated without prior notice at any stage if a qualified candidate is selected by the M.M.S.S. Board. Selected candidates will not be entitled to any holidays/vacation or any kind of leave available under the Service Rules for the permanent and regular employees of MCGM. Selected candidates must mark biometric attendance both when entering and when leaving the premises. Attendance is not considered valid if they fail to do so. Selected candidates must join within 7 working days. Selected candidates will be posted at Hinduhridaysamrat Balasaheb Thackeray Medical College & Dr. R.N. Cooper Municipal General Hospital.
