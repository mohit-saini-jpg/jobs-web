BMC Trade Apprentice Selection Process

There is no written examination for this recruitment. Selection is based on: Marks obtained in the final semester of the ITI trade examination , as declared in the mela application. Applicable social reservation (SC/ST/VJ/NT-B/NT-C/NT-D/SEBC/EWS) and parallel reservation (Women 30%, Ex-servicemen 15%, Project-affected 5%, Sportspersons 5%, Earthquake-affected 2%, Orphans 1%, PwD 4%). In case of a tie in marks, preference is given to the candidate with the earlier date of birth (seniority by age). A combined merit list is prepared; high-scoring backward-class candidates are also considered against open-category posts. Selected candidates' names are published on the BMC recruitment portal, followed by document verification and an online offer. Note: Under Section 22 of the Apprentices Act, 1961, BMC is not obligated to absorb apprentices into regular service after training completion. Sele

Important Conditions

Training period is one year ; apprentices must be physically present for training and may be assigned to any of the three shifts as per BMC's requirement. Passing the apprenticeship does not confer any right to permanent appointment in BMC service. No accommodation is provided during the training period; this is the candidate's own responsibility. Submission of false information or documents at any stage will lead to cancellation of training/contract without prior notice, and no correspondence will be entertained. Any candidate convicted of a moral turpitude or criminal offence is disqualified from selection. BMC reserves the right to postpone, modify, or cancel this recruitment process at any stage for administrative or other reasons. Reserved-category posts among the Denotified/Nomadic Tribes (VJ-A, NT-B, NT-C, NT-D) are inter-transferable as per prevailing government policy if suitabl

BMC Trade Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Visit www.apprenticeshipindia.gov.in and log in (only Maharashtra ITI-passed candidates are eligible).
  • 2Under "Apprenticeship Opportunities," select MCGM (BMC) as the establishment.
  • 3On the applicable mela date for your trade (20 or 21 August 2026), report in person at Jamsaheb Mukadam Government ITI, Govandi, Mumbai, between 10:00 AM and 5:00 PM.
  • 4Fill and submit the prescribed application form (format issued with the notification) along with self-attested photocopies of required documents.
  • 5Candidates are shortlisted at the mela based on ITI final-semester marks and applicable reservation.
  • 6Selected candidates undergo document verification, followed by an online offer through the portal.
  • 7The offer must be accepted within 7 days, failing which the selection is cancelled and the next eligible candidate is considered.
  • 8On acceptance, a Model Contract of Apprenticeship Training is executed online between the candidate and BMC.
