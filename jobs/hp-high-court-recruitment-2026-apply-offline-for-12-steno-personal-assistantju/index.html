HP High Court Recruitment 2026 - Eligibility Criteria

Personal Assistant/Judgment Writer (Group-B) (Mode-C): Graduate Stenographers working in High Court with no rider of service or Junior Scale Stenographers having at least 5 years service as Junior Scale Stenographer in High Court. Personal Assistant/Judgment Writer (Group-B) (Mode-D): Graduate Senior Scale Stenographers working in District Judiciary of H.P. with at least five years experience as Senior Scale Stenographer. Stenographer (Group-C) (Mode-B): Graduate Junior Scale Stenographers working in the High Court and Graduate Junior Scale Stenographers working in the District Judiciary in H.P., with experience of one year in case of Junior Scale Stenographers of the High Court of H.P. and three years in case of Junior Scale Stenographers from the District Judiciary.
