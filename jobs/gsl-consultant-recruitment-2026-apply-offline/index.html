About GSL

Goa Shipyard Limited (GSL) is a Central Public Sector Undertaking under the Ministry of Defence and a Schedule 'A' Mini Ratna Category-I Company, based at Vaddem, Vasco-Da-Gama, Goa. It designs and builds ships for the Indian Navy, the Indian Coast Guard and other customers, including friendly foreign countries.

GSL Consultant Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in GSL Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 3 vacancies under Advt. No. 07/2026, one each for Consultant (Infrastructure Finance), Consultant (Import/Export) and Consultant (Stores). All three are on contract basis.
Q2What is the last date to apply for the GSL Consultant posts?
A
The hard copy of the application, with the relevant documents, must reach GSL by 5 November 2026.
Q3What qualification is needed for the GSL Consultant posts?
A
Consultant (Infrastructure Finance) needs a graduate degree AND qualification as a Chartered Accountant or Cost Accountant. Consultant (Import/Export) needs a B.E / B.Tech with specialization in Mechanical Engineering, and Consultant (Stores) needs a graduate degree in any discipline from a recognized institution. Each post also needs at least 15 years of post-qualification experience in its own field.
Q4What is the age limit for GSL Recruitment 2026?
A
The upper age limit for all three Consultant posts is 63 years as on 30.09.2026.
Q5What is the salary of a GSL Consultant?
A
The consolidated monthly pay is Rs. 1,10,000 for Consultant (Infrastructure Finance), Rs. 85,000 for Consultant (Import/Export) and Rs. 50,000 for Consultant (Stores).
Q6How do I apply for the Goa Shipyard Consultant posts?
A
Fill in the prescribed application form and send it, with the documents supporting your age, experience and qualification, to General Manger (HR&A), Goa Shipyard Limited, Vasco-Da-Gama, Goa – 403802. It must reach GSL by 5 November 2026.
Q7What is the selection process for the GSL Consultant posts?
A
GSL screens and shortlists the applications, and the Management reserves the right to raise the eligibility criteria for shortlisting. Shortlisted candidates receive interview call letters by email only and are called for a personal interview.
Q8Is the GSL Consultant job permanent?
A
No. The posts are on contract basis only and give no claim to permanent employment. The engagement is for one year at first and could be extended by 01 year at a time until the person turns 65, depending on the Company's needs and the person's performance.
Q9Will GSL pay TA/DA for the interview?
A
No. The notice states that no TA / DA will be paid to candidates called for the personal interview.
Q10Which documents must be attached to the GSL application form?
A
Attach identity proof, a copy of the last pay certificate, address proof, a copy of the certificate proving your educational qualification and a copy of the service verification certificate, along with all documents supporting your age, experience and qualification.
