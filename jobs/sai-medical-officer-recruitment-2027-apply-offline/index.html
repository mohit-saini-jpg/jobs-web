About Sports Authority of India

The Sports Authority of India is an autonomous organisation under the Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports (MYAS). It is registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860, and is responsible for the development and promotion of sports in the country. The notice was issued on 17 November 2026 by the Human Resources Division-II at the SAI Headquarters, J N Stadium Complex, East Gate-No. 10, Lodhi Road, New Delhi-110003.

Vacancy Details

SAI is filling a total of 3 Medical Officer posts on deputation. The notice does not give a category-wise break-up.

Qualification and Experience

The notice lists three alternative routes to the essential education and experience for the Medical Officer post. A candidate needs to meet any one of them. The post is filled only on deputation. Applicants must be officers working under the Central Government, State Governments or Union Territory Administrations who hold either of the following: An analogous post in their parent cadre/department, OR Regular service of 5 years in Pay Level-10, Rs. 56,100-1,77,500 (Pay Band-3, Rs. 15600-39100 + Rs. 5400/- Grade Pay). The age limit is not exceeding 56 years as on the opening date of receipt of application. The notice repeats this rule in its deputation terms: the maximum age limit for appointment by deputation shall not exceed 56 years as on the opening date of receipt of application. The notice does not print any age relaxation. Candidates should check the full notification for the detail

Pay and Terms

Pay: Pay Matrix Level-11, Rs. 67700-208700/- (PB-3, Rs.15600-39100+Rs. 6,600/- Grade Pay), of the 7th CPC. Tenure: Initially for a period of 3 years extendable up to 5 years. Deputation terms: Deputation to SAI is as per the standard Terms and Conditions on deputation notified by DoP&T vide OM no. 06/08/2009 Estt (Pay-(II) dated 17/06/2010, as amended from time to time. Nature of appointment: On usual deputation terms, not a direct recruitment. The notice does not mention allowances beyond the pay level, and it does not name a place of posting other than the SAI Head Office that issued it.

SAI Medical Officer Recruitment 2027 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

The SAI Medical Officer selection has two stages, in this order: Interview: Candidates who fulfil the eligibility criteria will be called for interview. Merit list: The selection will be made on the basis of the merit list prepared by the Selection Committee and approved by the Competent Authority. The declaration in the application form also states that the curriculum vitae, supported by the documents submitted, will be assessed by the Selection Committee at the time of selection. The notice does not mention a written test.
