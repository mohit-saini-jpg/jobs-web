RRC NCR GDCE Eligibility Criteria

Eligible and serving regular employees (except RPF/RPSF) having lien in North Central Railway can apply against the GDCE quota. All eligible regular and serving railway employees of NCR can fill the online application form. No minimum service condition is prescribed, as per RBE 72/2024 dated 02.08.2024. Regular and eligible NCR employees who have the prescribed educational qualification for Direct Recruitment and work in grades lower than the grade/pay scale for which GDCE is being conducted can appear for all categories. Regular and eligible employees with the prescribed educational qualification for Direct Recruitment who work in the same grade/pay scale for which GDCE is being conducted can appear for selection from Non-Safety to Safety category posts as well as Safety to Safety category posts. RRC NCR GDCE Reservation

Vertical reservation for SC, ST and OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) is admissible wherever applicable. SC/ST candidates must furnish a Caste Certificate in the format at Annexure-G, and OBC-NCL candidates in the format at Annexure-H, at the time of Document Verification. OBC-NCL candidates must also furnish a declaration in the format at Annexure-H (1) that they do not belong to the creamy layer. The certificate should not be older than one year at the time of Document Verification. If these stipulations are not complied with, the OBC-NCL claim will not be entertained and the candidate, if otherwise fulfilling the General (UR) conditions, will be considered under General (UR) vacancies only. There is no separate reservation of posts for Economically Weaker Sections (EWS), as per Railway Board's letter No. E(NG)I-2018/PM1/23 dated 02/05/2019 (RBE No. 72/2019). RRC NCR GDCE Selection Process

Only a single online application has to be submitted. The selection process involves a single-stage Computer Based Test (CBT) and Aptitude Test (wherever applicable), followed by Document Verification and Medical Examination. Selection is made strictly on merit based on these stages. The date, time and venue of all activities will be fixed by RRC and intimated to eligible candidates in due course. Requests for postponement or change of venue, date and shift will not be entertained under any circumstances. The CBT has questions and answer options only in English and Hindi. There is negative marking in the CBTs for all categories of posts: 1/3rd of the marks allotted for each question is deducted for each wrong answer. There is no supplementary exam for absentee candidates under any circumstances. Candidates absent on the scheduled date and session will be disqualified without further noti

Candidates should register well before the closing date to avoid last-minute load on the website. RRC will not bear responsibility if a candidate cannot register within the last date and time. Post preference, once opted, is treated as final and no change is permissible. Candidates with partial options are considered only for the posts/categories they opted for. Not giving an option for certain posts/categories is treated as unwillingness for them. Eligibility is considered only on the basis of information furnished in the online application. False or incorrect information or suppression of relevant information leads to rejection of candidature. Candidates should note and preserve their Registration Number for further stages. Signatures and photographs must be identical at all stages of the selection process. Candidates should keep at least 12 copies of the same photograph. Candidates must ensure they meet the medical standards for the post. Appointment is subject to medical fitness, final verification of educational and community certificates, and verification of antecedents/character. Selected employees are liable to be posted anywhere in North Central Railway, and the decision of the Railway administration is final. Selection by RRC does not confer any right of appointment. RRC only recommends names to the concerned units of North Central Railway, which issue the offer of appointment letter.

RRC North Central Railway GDCE Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is NCR GDCE Recruitment 2026?
A
NCR GDCE Recruitment 2026 is the General Departmental Competitive Examination recruitment notified by the Railway Recruitment Cell, North Central Railway, Prayagraj, under Notification No. RRC/NCR/GDCE - 01/2026 dated 28.09.2026. It invites online applications from eligible serving regular employees (except RPF/RPSF) having lien in North Central Railway for posts against the GDCE quota.
Q2How many vacancies are available in NCR GDCE 2026?
A
The post-wise vacancies in Annexure-E add up to 656: 428 for Assistant Loco Pilot, 61 for Technician posts, 60 for Junior Engineer posts and 107 for NTPC (Non-Graduate) posts. The notification does not state a separate grand total, and the vacancies are provisional.
Q3What is the last date to apply for NCR GDCE 2026?
A
The last date is 30.10.2026 at 23:59 hrs. Online applications open on 01.10.2026.
Q4Who can apply for NCR GDCE 2026?
A
Only eligible serving regular employees (except RPF/RPSF) having lien in North Central Railway can apply. No minimum service condition is prescribed as per RBE 72/2024 dated 02.08.2024. Outside candidates cannot apply under this notification.
Q5What is the qualification for NCR GDCE 2026?
A
It depends on the post. ALP and Technician posts require Matriculation/SSLC plus ITI or Course Completed Act Apprenticeship in specified trades (with Diploma or Degree options for ALP and Technician-I, and 10+2 with Physics and Maths as an option for Technician-III Sig & Tele). JE posts require a three years Diploma in the specified Engineering disciplines (B.E./B.Tech acceptable in lieu of Diploma), and the clerk posts require 12th (+2) with not less than 50% marks in the aggregate, with relaxations as stated in the notification.
Q6What is the age limit for NCR GDCE 2026?
A
The lower age limit is 18 years for all categories. The upper age limit is 42 years for UR, 45 years for OBC and 47 years for SC/ST, reckoned as on 01.01.2027.
Q7How can I apply online for NCR GDCE 2026?
A
Candidates can apply online only through the link on the official website www.rrcpryj.org. They need to complete "New Registration", log in with the registration number and password received by e-mail/SMS, fill in the details with post preference, and upload the required documents and photograph.
Q8What is the selection process for NCR GDCE 2026?
A
The selection process has a single-stage CBT, followed by an Aptitude Test for ALP (CBAT) or a Typing Skill Test for Junior Clerk cum Typist (CBTST), Document Verification and Medical Examination. Selection is strictly on merit.
Q9What is the salary for NCR GDCE 2026 posts?
A
The salary amount is not specified in the notification. The posts fall under 7th CPC Pay Levels 2 (ALP, Technician-III, Junior Clerk cum Typist), 3 (Commercial cum Ticket Clerk), 5 (Technician-I) and 6 (Junior Engineer).
Q10Is there negative marking in the NCR GDCE CBT?
A
Yes. 1/3rd of the marks allotted for each question is deducted for every wrong answer in the CBTs. There is no negative marking in the Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT).
