About IIITNR

International Institute of Information Technology (IIIT) Naya Raipur, referred to in the notice as IIITNR or IIIT-NR, is the institute inviting these applications. The posts are for coordination, registration, communication and documentation support. The institute's website named in the notice is www.iiitnr.edu.in .

Who Can Apply

The essential qualification is Any Graduate from a recognized university. Pay and Tenure

A consolidated remuneration of ₹20,000/- per month (in-hand) will be provided, with no deductions. TA, DA and HRA will not be provided separately. The engagement is temporary and lasts 03 months. The post does not confer any right or claim for regularization or absorption at IIITNR.

IIITNR Project Assistant Job Description

Coordination and assistance in organizing project-related activities. Registration and management of participant information and records. Communication and correspondence with participants and relevant stakeholders. Preparation, maintenance and management of project-related documentation. Data entry, record keeping and systematic filing of documents. Preparation of presentations, reports and other administrative materials.

Selection and Interview

Only shortlisted candidates may be contacted for further selection procedures. The Institute reserves the right to shortlist only candidates whose profiles meet its requirements. In case of a large number of candidates, a written test may be conducted. The interview is a walk-in interview on 25 November 2026 at 11:00 AM in the meeting room, New building. The date and time of the interview will be communicated only through the Institute website or email.
