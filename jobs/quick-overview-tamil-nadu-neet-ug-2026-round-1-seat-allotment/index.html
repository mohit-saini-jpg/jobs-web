How to Check and Download Tamil Nadu NEET UG 2026 Round 1 Seat Allotment Result

Candidates can follow these steps to check their Round 1 seat allotment status and download the Allotment Order:

  • Visit the official website: tnmedicalselection.net.
  • Click on the "UG Courses" tab and select the MBBS/BDS counselling section.
  • Look for the link titled "Tamil Nadu NEET UG 2026 Round 1 Seat Allotment Result" or "Allotment Order."
  • Log in using your registration credentials (registration number and date of birth, or as specified on the portal).
  • Your Round 1 allotment status, along with the allotted college and course (if applicable), will be displayed on screen.
  • Carefully check your name, allotted college, and allotted course.
  • Download and save the Allotment Order/PDF for reporting and future reference.

Important Instructions for Allotted Candidates

  • Report to the allotted college within the specified window - August 27 to September 5, 2026 (before 12:00 noon) - to confirm admission.
  • Carry all original documents along with photocopies for verification at the allotted college.
  • Failure to report and complete admission formalities within the deadline may lead to forfeiture of the allotted seat.
  • Candidates not allotted a seat in Round 1, or who wish to try for a better option, should watch for Round 2/mop-up round notifications on the same official website.
  • Cross-check all personal, category, and academic details on the Allotment Order; report any discrepancy to DME Tamil Nadu through the process specified in the prospectus.
