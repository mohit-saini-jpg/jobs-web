About AIIMS Jammu

The notice describes AIIMS Jammu as an Institute of National Importance and one of the new AIIMS under the Pradhan Mantri Swasthya Suraksha Yojana (PMSSY), set up by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India. Its stated aims are correcting regional imbalance in quality tertiary level healthcare and attaining self-sufficiency in graduate, postgraduate and higher medical education and training. Applications are invited from Indian citizens and persons registered as Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) cardholders under Section 7A of the Citizenship Act, 1955, as permissible under the applicable Act/Rules.

Qualification and Experience

Applicants must have the essential qualification and experience up to the last date of online submission (14 December 2026). Only experience gained after the essential qualification is counted, and only experience in recognised organisations or institutions is considered. Pay and Terms

Pay: Level 11 for Blood Transfusion Officer and Level 10 for Medical Officer Ayush and Child Psychologist, as per the 7th CPC. Rupee amounts are not printed. All posts carry the usual allowances admissible to Central Government employees of similar status at Samba (Vijaypur), UT of J&K. The posts are whole time, and private practice of any kind is strictly prohibited. Probation: two years for all posts. AIIMS Jammu Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection is based on performance in the Interview. A written test or screening, based on objective criteria, may be held to shortlist applicants to be called for interview. The decision of the Competent Authority on shortlisting is final and binding on all candidates. The interview date is shown as tentative and "To be notified separately". The venue is Academic Block, 6th Floor, AIIMS, Vijaypur, Jammu. The test/interview schedule will be mailed to provisionally eligible candidates on their registered e-mail ID. Shortlisted candidates called for interview must bring the printout of the online application and a set of self-attested photocopies of all documents uploaded online, along with the originals of the certificates. Failure to produce them on the day leads to cancellation of candidature and the candidate will not be allowed to appear for interview.
