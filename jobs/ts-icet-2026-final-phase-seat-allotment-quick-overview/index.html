TS ICET 2026 Final Phase Seat Allotment: Quick Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 22/08/2026

The Telangana Council of Higher Education (TGCHE) has released the TS ICET 2026 Final Phase seat allotment result on the official counselling portal, tgicet.nic.in. Candidates who completed web option entry in the final phase can log in with their hall ticket number, password, and date of birth to download their allotment order. Candidates allotted a seat must pay the tuition fee and complete self-reporting between August 22 and August 24, 2026, and report physically at the allotted college by August 25, 2026.

TS ICET 2026 Final Phase Seat Allotment Process

Seat allotment in the Final Phase works on the same principle as Phase 1. TGCHE matches candidates to seats based on:

  • The candidate's TS ICET 2026 rank
  • Web options (college and course preferences) entered by the candidate
  • Reservation category (OC, BC, SC, ST, EWS, etc.)
  • Local/non-local candidate status
  • Seat availability across participating colleges
  • Category-wise and course-wise cutoff ranks

Every allotment is provisional. It is confirmed only after the candidate pays the tuition fee online, completes self-reporting on the portal, and submits original documents for physical verification at the allotted college within the notified deadline.

Documents Required for Final Phase Reporting

Candidates should carry the original documents along with self-attested photocopies when reporting physically to the allotted college. Commonly required documents for TS ICET counselling include:

  • TS ICET 2026 hall ticket and rank card
  • Provisional seat allotment order (downloaded after login)
  • Bachelor's degree provisional certificate and consolidated marks memo
  • SSC/10th and Intermediate/12th certificates (for age and study proof)
  • Transfer certificate (TC) and study certificates, as applicable
  • Caste certificate (for reserved category candidates)
  • Income certificate, if claiming fee reimbursement
  • Local/non-local status certificate, where required
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Fee payment receipt/self-reporting confirmation

Some colleges may ask for additional documents specific to the course or category. Candidates should confirm the exact checklist with their allotted institute in advance.

How to Download the TS ICET 2026 Final Phase Seat Allotment Result

  • Visit the official TG ICET counselling website: tgicet.nic.in
  • Click on the "Candidate Login" tab on the homepage.
  • Enter your ROC Form Number/Hall Ticket Number, Password, and Date of Birth.
  • Locate and click the Final Phase "Seat Allotment Result" or "Provisional Allotment Order" link.
  • Your allotment order will display the allotted college, course, category, and applicable tuition fee.
  • Download the allotment order and save or print a copy for reporting at the college.

What to Do After Checking the Allotment Result

  • If allotted a seat and satisfied: Pay the tuition fee online and complete self-reporting on the portal between August 22 and 24, 2026, then report physically at the college with original documents by August 25, 2026.
  • If allotted a seat but seeking an upgrade: This was the Final Phase, so no further regular counselling round follows; only spot counselling for remaining vacant seats will be available after August 24, 2026.
  • If not allotted any seat: Watch for the TG ICET 2026 spot counselling notification, expected around August 24, 2026, for any seats still vacant after this round.
  • If you miss the fee payment or self-reporting deadline: The provisional allotment stands to be cancelled, and the seat can be offered to another candidate.

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Seat allotment is provisional until fee payment, online self-reporting, and physical document verification are all completed.
  • Keep your login credentials (hall ticket number, password, date of birth) private and secure.
  • Regularly check tgicet.nic.in for updates, since counselling dates can shift at short notice.
  • Non-payment of tuition fee within the notified window can lead to automatic cancellation of the allotted seat.
  • Candidates upgrading from a Phase 1 seat to a Final Phase seat permanently lose the earlier seat once the new allotment is confirmed.
