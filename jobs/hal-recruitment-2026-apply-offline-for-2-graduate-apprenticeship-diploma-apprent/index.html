HAL Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL), Transport Aircraft Division, Kanpur is engaging Graduate (Degree) and Diploma Apprentices for the year 2026-27 under the Apprentices Act-1961, in 11 degree branches and 11 diploma branches, for candidates who passed in 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 or 2026. The stipend is payable as per the Apprentices Act-1961, and selection is purely on the marks of the degree or diploma. Candidates must register on the NATS 2.0 portal and send the prescribed application form by Ordinary Post, Speed Post or Courier so that it reaches the Technical Training Institute, HAL Kanpur

Who Can Apply

Only Indian Nationals are eligible to apply. Each branch needs the degree or diploma shown against it in the tables above. In addition, the notice sets these conditions: The Diploma/Degree course must have been passed in the years 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 or 2026. The candidate should possess the Diploma/Degree Pass Certificate as on the date of application. Candidates with status "Appearing" or "Result awaited" are not eligible. Candidates with pending Back Papers, or whose Supplementary Exam results are awaited, are also not eligible. Registration on the NATS 2.0 portal ( www.nats.education.gov.in ) is required; candidates registered on the NATS 1.0 portal will not be considered. Stipend and Training Period

Stipend will be payable to Diploma/Degree Apprentices as per the Apprentices Act-1961 (as amended from time to time); the notice does not print a stipend amount. The engagement is apprenticeship training for the year 2026-27 under the Apprentices Act-1961; the length of the training is not specified in the notice.

HAL Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection of apprentices shall be made strictly on the basis of 100% merit, taking into consideration 100% weightage of the total aggregate marks secured in the Degree / Diploma examination. Provisionally selected candidates will be informed through India Post, Email or Telephone, and will then come to TTI, HAL, Kanpur for the further selection process; no TA/DA is payable for this visit. Mere submission of an application does not give a right to take part in the selection process, and the decision of HAL Kanpur on eligibility will be final. Selected candidates should possess an online Character Certificate (issued by SSP/DCP/SP) and a Medical Certificate (in the format provided by HAL) issued by the Chief Medical Officer or an authorised Medical Officer of the District Hospital, as per the address in their Domicile Certificate / Aadhar only.

HAL Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Register on the NATS 2.0 portal (www.nats.education.gov.in) to get the Apprenticeship Registration Number.
  • 2Note the registration number, registered e-mail and registered mobile number; the application form asks for all three.
  • 3Download the prescribed application form, which is available on the HAL website along with the notification. Applications in any other format will be summarily rejected.
  • 4Tick Graduate (Degree) Apprenticeship or Diploma Apprenticeship and select your branch from the list in the form.
  • 5Fill in every detail; your name, father's name, mother's name and date of birth must be as per the 10th / High School Certificate.
  • 6Give the total and obtained marks and the percentage up to 2 decimal places; CGPA must be converted to an equivalent percentage, with the conversion formula of your Board/University.
  • 7Paste a recent 3.5 x 2.5 cm photograph on the form, tick the enclosure checklist, and sign the declaration with date and place.
  • 8Enclose self-attested copies of the documents listed below.
  • 9Send the form by Ordinary Post, Speed Post or Courier Service to: The Chief Manager, Technical Training Institute, Hindustan Aeronautics Ltd., Transport Aircraft Division, Post office- Chakeri, Distt. - Kanpur, Pin code – 208008.

HAL Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is the last date for the HAL Kanpur apprentice application 2026?
A
The filled application form must reach the Technical Training Institute, HAL Transport Aircraft Division, Kanpur by 07 December 2026 (1640 hrs). No application is accepted after this date.
Q2What qualification is needed for HAL Kanpur Graduate Apprenticeship?
A
A 4 Years B.E./B.Tech. in Mechanical, Electrical, Civil, Electronics/Instrumentation, Chemical, Computer Science/IT or Aeronautical/Aerospace Engineering; 1 Years Library Science; or a 3 Years B.Com., B.Sc. or BBA degree from a recognized Board/University, passed in 2022 to 2026.
Q3What qualification is needed for HAL Kanpur Diploma Apprenticeship?
A
A 3 Years Diploma in Mechanical, Electrical, Civil, Electronics/Instrumentation, Chemical, Computer Science/IT or A.M.E., or a 2 Years Diploma in Library Science, Pharmacy, MOM & SP or Hotel Management & Catering Technology, passed in 2022 to 2026.
Q4What is the age limit for HAL Kanpur apprentice 2026?
A
The minimum age is 18 years and the maximum is 27 years as on 31 October 2026, with relaxation of 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC (NCL) and 10 years for PwD candidates.
Q5How much stipend will HAL Kanpur apprentices get?
A
The stipend is payable to Diploma/Degree Apprentices as per the Apprentices Act-1961, as amended from time to time.
Q6How do I apply for the HAL Kanpur apprenticeship?
A
Register on NATS 2.0, download the prescribed form from the HAL website, fill it, attach self-attested documents and send it by Ordinary Post, Speed Post or Courier to The Chief Manager, Technical Training Institute, HAL, Transport Aircraft Division, Post office- Chakeri, Distt. - Kanpur, Pin code – 208008.
Q7How will HAL Kanpur select apprentices?
A
Selection is strictly on 100% merit, based on 100% weightage of the total aggregate marks in the Degree / Diploma examination. Provisionally selected candidates are informed through India Post, Email or Telephone.
Q8Can I apply if I am registered only on NATS 1.0?
A
No. Registration must be on the NATS 2.0 portal only; candidates registered on the NATS 1.0 portal will not be considered.
Q9Who is not eligible for HAL Kanpur apprenticeship?
A
Candidates who have completed or are pursuing apprenticeship under the Apprenticeship Act-1961, those with one year or more of work experience, and those who are appearing, awaiting results or have pending back or supplementary papers are not eligible.
Q10Can I hand over the application form in person at HAL Kanpur?
A
No. Application forms will not be accepted by hand or individually; they must be sent by Ordinary Post, Speed Post or Courier Service.
