About NAM Kozhikode

The National AYUSH Mission's District Programme Management & Supporting Unit (DPMSU) for Kozhikode works from the District Ayurveda Hospital, Bhatt Road, Westhill, Chungam-Kozhikode-673005. Both notices are signed by Dr. Anupama Dev A, District Programme Manager, National AYUSH Mission, DPMSU, Kozhikode, Kerala. The posts are under the Mission's Indian Systems of Medicine Department and Homoeopathy Department.

NAM Kozhikode Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many posts are advertised in NAM Kozhikode Recruitment 2026?
A
Eighteen posts are advertised: 17 in notice NAM/DPMSU/KKD/NT-01/2026 and YOGA Instructor in notice NAM/DPMSU/KKD/NT-02/2026. The number of vacancies for each post is shown as "Anticipatory".
Q2What is the last date to apply for NAM Kozhikode Recruitment 2026?
A
The last date for submitting online applications is 30 November 2026, for both notices.
Q3What is the qualification for the Cook and Security posts?
A
The qualification for both the Cook and the Security posts is SSLC.
Q4What is the age limit for NAM Kozhikode Recruitment 2026?
A
The maximum age is 40 years for the 17 posts in notice NT-01/2026 and 50 years for YOGA Instructor in notice NT-02/2026. Candidates above these ages are not eligible to apply.
Q5What is the salary for NAM Kozhikode posts?
A
The salary ranges from 10,500/- per month for Cook to 32,000/- per month for Speech Therapist. Clinical Psychologist, Psychologist, Special Educator and Occupational Therapist each get 30,000/- per month.
Q6How do I apply for NAM Kozhikode Recruitment 2026?
A
Apply online through the website https://careers.nam.kerala.gov.in by 30 November 2026. Incomplete applications will be rejected.
Q7What is the selection process?
A
The selection process may include a Skill Test, Written Examination and Interview, as required. Original documents are produced at the time of the interview.
Q8Are the NAM Kozhikode posts permanent?
A
No. The posts are temporary appointments on a contract basis.
Q9What is the qualification for the Therapist posts?
A
Therapist-Male and Therapist-Female need the Ayurveda Therapist Course conducted by Govt. of Kerala/DAME, or the one-year Ayurveda Panchakarma Technician Course conducted by CCRAS Cheruthuruthy.
Q10Is RCI registration needed for the Audiologist and Clinical Psychologist posts?
A
Yes. RCI registration is compulsory for Audiologist, and Clinical Psychologist requires Rehabilitation Council of India Registration as "Clinical Psychologist".
