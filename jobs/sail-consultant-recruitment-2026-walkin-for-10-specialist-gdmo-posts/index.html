About SAIL Bokaro Steel Plant

Bokaro Steel Plant in Bokaro, Jharkhand, is a plant of Steel Authority of India Limited (SAIL). The notice describes SAIL as a Maharatna Public Sector Enterprise and India's leading steel-making company. Bokaro General Hospital (BGH), where these doctors will work, is a 910-bedded multispecialty hospital run by SAIL.

Job Description

The doctors will work in different departments of Medical and Health Services (M&HS) as needed and do the work assigned to them. The minimum is 8 hours a day, six days a week, or 48 hours a week. Doctors who work fewer hours are paid on a pro-rata basis, and the monthly rate cannot go above the maximum for their category.

SAIL Consultant Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Walk-in interview. Medical screening. The engagement starts as soon as selection is complete and engagement orders are issued. All certificates are checked only at the walk-in interview, and candidates who cannot produce them will not be interviewed. BSL may engage fewer doctors than advertised, reject any application or cancel the whole process without giving a reason.

Service Conditions

The first engagement is for one year or until the doctor turns 70, whichever comes first. The Company may extend it one year at a time, up to a maximum of 3 years under the same contract. Re-engagement is allowed, within the age limit. Performance is reviewed every year. Based on that review, the tenure may be cut short or extended. Either side can end the contract with one month's notice. The company can end it for poor performance, moral turpitude, conviction by a court, insolvency, loss of licence to practise, grave misconduct or financial irregularity, or long-term medical unfitness. The engagement is purely on a contractual basis and gives no right to regular appointment in BSL. Any dispute falls under the jurisdiction of the courts at Bokaro Steel City, Jharkhand.
