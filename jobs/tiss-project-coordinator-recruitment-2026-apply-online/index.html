Vacancy Details

There is a total of 1 vacancy available for the Project Coordinator position under the NT-DNT Youth Development Project implemented across six districts of Maharashtra.

Who Can Apply

Candidates must meet the educational and experience criteria specified by the institute to apply for the Project Coordinator position. Educational Qualification: Masters in Social Work or Social Sciences. Experience: 3-5 years of experience in managing projects working with marginalised communities in Maharashtra, with a special focus on legal aid, health, education, livelihood, citizenship entitlements, and training. Language Proficiency: Fluency in spoken Marathi, Hindi, and English languages, and good ability in written English language. Technical Skills: Familiarity with the use of MS-Office, emails, MIS, and the internet.

Pay and Tenure

The selected Project Coordinator will receive a consolidated salary of Rs. 50,000/- per month. The appointment is on a contractual basis for an initial period of one year, which is extendable up to two years.

TISS Project Coordinator Job Description

The roles and responsibilities assigned to the Project Coordinator include: Execute project implementation plan – activities. Develop activity contents and identify resources needed for execution of the same. Ensure that reports of the activities are prepared and filed for records. Organise training sessions. Manage MIS. Documentation and record keeping. Coordination with youth fellows across 6 districts of Maharashtra. Ensure that the project delivers evidence based results – quantitative and qualitative. Update project work plans to reflect a changing environment. Articulate changes in project activities (if any) under the guidance of Project Director. Work effectively in teams as well as independently. Travel across Maharashtra and other States when necessary.

Selection and Interview

Applications received will be shortlisted based on the prescribed qualifications and requisite experience. Merely possessing the qualifications does not entitle a person to be called for interview. Shortlisted candidates will be informed over email and/or phone to appear for a Written Test/Interview (offline or online). No TA/DA is payable for appearing for the interview.
