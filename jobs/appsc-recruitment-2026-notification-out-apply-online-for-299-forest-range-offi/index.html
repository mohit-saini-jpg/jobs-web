APPSC Various Posts Recruitment 2026 Notification PDF Out

The Andhra Pradesh Public Service Commission (APPSC), Vijayawada has released the Brief Notifications for various posts under different subordinate services of the Andhra Pradesh Government on 15 September 2026. The APPSC Various Posts Recruitment 2026 notification includes a total of 299 provisional vacancies for posts such as Executive Officer Grade-III, Forest Range Officer, Forest Section Officer, Forest Beat Officer, Draughtsman Grade-II, District Probation Officer Grade-II, Industrial Promotion Officer and Supervisor Grade-I. Candidates can download the APPSC Various Posts Recruitment 2026 Notification PDF from the official website of APPSC at psc.ap.gov.in. The Detailed Notification containing complete vacancy details, age limit, application fee, reservation details and other instructions will be released on or before 16 October 2026. APPSC Various Posts Selection Process

As stated in each of the notifications, selection for all 8 posts will be through a single-level examination only - no Screening Test will be conducted, irrespective of the number of applications received. The examination will be conducted in online Computer-Based Recruitment Test (CBRT) mode. Q1What is APPSC Various Posts Recruitment 2026?

It is a set of 8 Brief Notifications (Nos. 12, 13, 15, 16, 17, 18, 23, and 24 of 2026), all dated 15 September 2026, issued by the Andhra Pradesh Public Service Commission for direct recruitment to 299 posts across 8 different Andhra Pradesh subordinate services.

A total of 299 provisional vacancies: 104 Executive Officer Grade-III, 78 Industrial Promotion Officer, 29 Supervisor Grade-I, 26 Forest Section Officer, 23 Forest Beat Officer, 20 Draughtsman Grade-II, 15 District Probation Officer Grade-II, and 4 Forest Range Officer.
