Maitreyi College Non-Teaching Recruitment Selection Process

Selection follows the University of Delhi's standard Scheme of Examination for non-teaching posts, which differs by post group. All stages (except Interview) are conducted in a bilingual (English/Hindi) question paper. Preliminary Test cut-off: 50% of the average score of the top 5% of candidates, with 5% relaxation for SC/ST and 10% for PwBD; for most posts (excluding Administrative Officer) the relaxation is calculated on the cut-off derived for UR candidates Negative marking in Preliminary Test: 1 mark per wrong answer for Administrative Officer; 0.5 mark per wrong answer for all other posts Shortlisting ratio for Main: 30 candidates for one vacant post, plus 15 additional candidates for each remaining advertised vacancy (e.g., 45 for two vacancies, 60 for three) 20 minutes extra per hour is given to Visually Impaired, Cerebral Palsy, or similarly placed PwBD candidates in every stage
