Bareilly Anganwadi Sahayika Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

The Bareilly Anganwadi Sahayika Selection Process does not involve a written examination or interview. Selection is made from among eligible resident candidates as per the priority order described in the Eligibility section above (widow, legally divorced/abandoned, or below/above poverty line status), verified using supporting documents such as death certificates, court orders, income certificates and residence/caste certificates. The notification states that if any dispute arises regarding vacancies, the decision taken by the selection committee will be final. The number of vacant posts may increase or decrease, and the authority to recruit, not recruit, or cancel recruitment for the vacant posts rests with the selection committee.
