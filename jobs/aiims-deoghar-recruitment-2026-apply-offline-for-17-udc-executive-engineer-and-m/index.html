AIIMS Deoghar Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many posts are vacant in AIIMS Deoghar Recruitment 2026?

There are 17 posts in nine designations: Executive Engineer (Civil) 1, Executive Engineer (Electrical) 1, Nursing Superintendent 2, Deputy Nursing Superintendent 2, Assistant Administrative Officer 2, Assistant Accounts Officer 3, Chief Pharmacist 1, Junior Administrative Officer 1 and UDC 4. The number is tentative.
Q2What is the last date to apply for AIIMS Deoghar deputation posts?
A
The last date of receipt of application at AIIMS Deoghar is 45 days from the date of publication of the advertisement in Employment News. The advertisement also gives 30 days from that publication as the last date for submission through proper channel. The notice prints no calendar date.
Q3How do I apply for the AIIMS Deoghar deputation posts?
A
Send the application in the prescribed proforma (Annexure-I and Annexure-II), through proper channel, by Speed Post or Registered Post only, to the Dy. Director (Administration), AIIMS Deoghar, Devipur Campus, Ramsagar, Deoghar-814117, Jharkhand.
Q4What is the age limit for AIIMS Deoghar deputation?
A
The maximum age limit is 56 years as on the last date of receipt of application, for all the posts.
Q5What is the pay for AIIMS Deoghar deputation posts?
A
Pay ranges from Level 11 (Rs. 67700 – 208700) for Executive Engineers and Nursing Superintendent to Level 04 (Rs. 25500 – 81100) for UDC. The post also carries the usual allowance admissible to Central Government employees of similar status stationed at Deoghar.
Q6Who can apply for AIIMS Deoghar Recruitment 2026?
A
Officers under the Central/State/UT Government, universities, statutory/autonomous bodies or R&D organisations holding analogous posts on regular basis, or officers in the feeder grade with the regular service stated for each post. Posts such as Nursing Superintendent are open to a narrower list of bodies.
Q7What is the qualification for the UDC post?
A
An officer holding an analogous post on regular basis, OR one with 8 years regular service in the grade pay of Rs. 1900 who has a degree from a recognized University or its equivalent.
Q8How long is the deputation period?
A
The initial period of deputation is 3 years or until the appointment of a regular incumbent, whichever is earlier.
Q9Can I apply for more than one post?
A
Yes, but a separate application is required for each post.
Q10Will applications without vigilance clearance be considered?
A
No. Applications without vigilance clearance and CR Dossiers will not be considered, and only applications through proper channel will be considered.
