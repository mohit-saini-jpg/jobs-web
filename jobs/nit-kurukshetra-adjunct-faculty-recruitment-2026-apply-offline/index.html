NIT Kurukshetra Adjunct Faculty Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Short-listing of applications, using criteria that may be higher than those advertised and may vary from department to department. Presentation and interview before the Selection Committee, on a date and at a place notified separately to candidates. Verification of all original documents at the interview. Medical examination by the institute's Medical Board, Senior Medical Officer or Medical Officer, which may be undertaken before joining. Meeting the advertised requirements does not by itself entitle a candidate to an interview. The institute may restrict the number of candidates to a reasonable limit based on qualifications, experience and merit. The decision of the competent authority is final.

Service Conditions

Engagement depends on being found physically fit and sound in health for service in the institute. Selected candidates should be prepared to join duty within the specified time limit. Any dispute regarding the recruitment falls under the jurisdiction of District Kurukshetra.

NIT Kurukshetra Adjunct Faculty Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What happens if my application arrives after 6 November 2026?
A
It is not rejected for that reason alone. Because this is a rolling advertisement, an application received after the Fourth Round date is considered for the next round.
Q2Is a Ph.D. compulsory for NIT Kurukshetra Adjunct Faculty?
A
For a candidate from an industry or research organization, a Post-graduate or Ph.D. degree may not be essential, but domain knowledge should be of significant value.
Q3Can I apply with less than 10 years of experience?
A
Yes, in emerging and cutting-edge technologies, where persons with more than 5 years of experience can also be considered.
Q4How is the honorarium paid per day or per hour?
A
An Adjunct Professor receives Rs. 8,000 per day or Rs. 2,000 per hour, with a Rs. 1,80,000 monthly ceiling. An Adjunct Fellow receives Rs. 6,000 per day or Rs. 1,500 per hour, with a Rs. 1,20,000 monthly ceiling.
Q5Does private industry experience count?
A
Yes, provided the company is incorporated in the Indian Companies Act of 1956.
Q6Can I apply to more than one department?
A
Yes, but a separate application is required for each Post, Department and Discipline.
Q7Can retired professionals apply?
A
Yes. The notice covers both serving and retired scientists, engineers, physicians, academicians, officials and managers, among others.
Q8Will the institute pay for travel to the interview?
A
No. Candidates called for the presentation and interview attend at their own cost.
