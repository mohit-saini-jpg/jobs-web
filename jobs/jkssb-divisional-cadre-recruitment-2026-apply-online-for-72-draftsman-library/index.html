JKSSB Divisional Cadre Eligibility Criteria

This recruitment is restricted to a specific eligible group: only candidates who qualify as "Migrants" under SRO 412 of 2009, or as "Kashmiri Pandits" under SRO 425 of 2017, are eligible to apply. Migrant status is certified by the Relief and Rehabilitation Commissioner, J&K, while Kashmiri Pandit status is certified by the Deputy Commissioner of the concerned district. In addition, candidates must be domiciles of the UT of Jammu & Kashmir and possess a valid Domicile Certificate issued by the Competent Authority, valid on or before the cut-off date of 08.09.2026. Candidates must produce original certificates as proof of the prescribed qualification at the time of Document Verification, acquired strictly on or before the cut-off date. Candidates claiming an equivalent qualification must produce an Equivalence Certificate from the competent authority when required by the Board.

JKSSB Divisional Cadre Selection Process

Selection for all 7 posts under this notification is made purely on the basis of merit obtained in the Written/OMR Examination, followed by Document Verification for shortlisted candidates. Written/OMR Examination: Objective type, Multiple Choice Questions, set in English language only. Negative marking of one-fourth (0.25) mark for each wrong answer applies. Document Verification (DV): Shortlisted candidates must appear with original documents - Domicile Certificate, Qualification Certificate, Category Certificate, Birth Certificate, Bonafide Certificate (if applicable), Migrant/Kashmiri Pandit status certificate - along with self-attested photocopies. Failure to appear or produce required documents on the scheduled DV date will result in the candidate not being considered for selection. The date and centre of examination, along with the syllabus, will be notified separately on www.jkss

JKSSB Divisional Cadre Reservation and Horizontal Reservation

Reservation is applied as per the category-wise break-up conveyed by the Indenting Department and the Jammu & Kashmir Reservation Act/Rules. If candidates are not available from any reserved category (other than RBA), those posts may be filled from the Open Merit category. Horizontal Reservation for Ex-Servicemen (ESM) and Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD) is applicable as communicated by the Indenting Department; ESM horizontal reservation applies only to posts carrying pay up to Level-6E (35900-113500). An ESM candidate who avails Horizontal Reservation on joining a post cannot avail it again for subsequent employment.
