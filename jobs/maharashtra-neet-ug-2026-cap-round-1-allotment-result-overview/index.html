How to Check and Download the Maharashtra NEET UG 2026 CAP Round 1 Allotment Result

  • Visit the official Maharashtra CAP counselling portal used for NEET UG 2026 or the CET Cell's main site for the relevant notice and result link.
  • Locate and click the link for the CAP Round 1 Seat Allotment Result / Selection List for MBBS/BDS (Group A).
  • Log in using the credentials created at the time of CAP registration (application/registration number and password, or the login details specified on the portal).
  • View your allotted college, course, and category on screen.
  • Download and save the allotment letter as a PDF, and take a printout for physical reporting.
  • Carefully verify the college name, course, and category shown on the letter before proceeding to reporting.

What to Do After Getting the Allotment Result

Once a seat is allotted, candidates typically have to choose one of the following options at the reporting stage, based on how the CET Cell has structured this round:

  • Confirm and freeze the seat: If satisfied with the allotted college and course, pay the requisite fee, submit original documents, and complete admission at that institute.
  • Report and retain the option to upgrade (if permitted): Some candidates may be allowed to complete provisional joining while remaining in consideration for a better preference in a later round, depending on the rules notified for that particular round.

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • A Round 1 seat allotment is provisional until physical reporting and document verification are completed.
  • Reporting and fee payment must be completed within the window of August 31 to September 4, 2026 (up to 5:30 PM); this deadline is treated as final.
  • Always download the allotment result and any notices directly from the official CET Cell portal, not from screenshots or messages circulated on social media.
  • Keep both original documents and multiple self-attested photocopies ready, as colleges may retain copies during verification.
  • Candidates who do not get a seat in Round 1, or who wish to try for a better option, should watch the CET Cell's official notices for the CAP Round 2 schedule, which has not been announced yet.
  • Candidates applying for AYUSH or other allied health science courses should note that their round-wise schedule may be declared separately from the MBBS/BDS schedule.
