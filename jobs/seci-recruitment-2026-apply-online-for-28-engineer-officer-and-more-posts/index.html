SECI Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

SECI reserves the right to revise the eligibility criteria upward, or limit the number of applications called for a post, based on marks obtained in Graduation/Post-Graduation/Diploma or years of relevant experience. Depending on the number of applications received, SECI may conduct a screening test, written test, trade test, or skill test, as deemed fit. Only shortlisted candidates found eligible based on the notified specifications will be called for a Written Test/Group Discussion/Interview. Mere possession of the minimum prescribed qualification and experience does not entitle a candidate to be called for the next stage. The selection process stages will be updated on the SECI website's Careers section, and candidates are advised to check it periodically.
