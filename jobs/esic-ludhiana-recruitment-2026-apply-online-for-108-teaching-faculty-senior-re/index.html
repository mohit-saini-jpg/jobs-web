ESIC Ludhiana Terms of Contract

Tenure is initially for one year, extendable up to three years (renewed yearly based on satisfactory performance), or until the candidate attains 70 years of age, or until a regular incumbent joins - whichever is earliest. The engagement may be terminated by either side with one month's prior notice, without assigning reason. If a candidate resigns without notice, one month's salary must be deposited to the ESI Account through challan. Candidates must deposit a security amount of Rs. 1,00,000/- (by demand draft) at the time of joining, forfeited if they resign within six months. Candidates appointed for a 44-day tenure must deposit Rs. 50,000/- as security, forfeited if they resign during that 44-day period. Faculty are entitled to 2.5 leaves per month. No service benefits such as PF, pension, gratuity, medical allowance, medical benefits, or seniority-based promotion are admissible. Bei
