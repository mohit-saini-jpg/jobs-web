NAM Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The State Programme Management Unit of National Ayush Mission (NAM), Assam has invited online applications for three contractual posts in mission mode: Consultant - HR (₹50,000 per month), Account Assistant (₹22,000) and Programme Assistant (₹20,000), one vacancy each, all unreserved. Applications go through the NHM, Assam website https://nhm.assam.gov.in until 05:00 PM on 27 November 2026. The advertisement is numbered 771414/I/1846605/2026 and dated 12 November 2026, and no application fee is required.

Pay and Tenure

The three posts are engagements on a contractual basis. Remuneration is ₹50,000.00 per month for Consultant - HR, ₹22,000.00 for Account Assistant and ₹20,000.00 for Programme Assistant. The notice says detailed terms and conditions and other criteria of the recruitment process will be published in due course on https://ayush.assam.gov.in and https://nhm.assam.gov.in .

Selection and Interview

The selection process will be notified on the official website of National Ayush Mission, Assam ( https://ayush.assam.gov.in ) and on https://nhm.assam.gov.in in due course, along with the list of provisionally shortlisted candidates. No separate individual call letter for an interview or selection test will be sent, so candidates should keep visiting the websites. Merely having the requisite qualification and experience does not make an applicant eligible for short listing.
