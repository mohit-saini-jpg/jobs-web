RRC Eastern Railway Scouts & Guides Recruitment Process

Stage-I: Written Test (60 marks) Consists of 40 objective-type questions (40 marks) and 1 essay-type question (20 marks) relating to the Scouts & Guides Organization and its activities, and General Knowledge - common for both Level-1 and Level-2. Duration is 60 minutes, with a minimum qualifying percentage of 40%. Candidates are called for Stage-II in a 1:3 ratio of notified vacancies, based on merit in Stage-I. Stage-II: Marks on Certificates (40 marks) Total Marks (Stage-I + Stage-II): 100 Candidates must choose either Hindi or English for the written examination. After clearing Stage-I and Stage-II and on the Recruitment Committee's recommendation, candidates are sent for a medical examination at a Railway Hospital, in merit order, in a 1:1 ratio of notified vacancies, and must be declared medically fit for the offered post.

Syllabus for RRC Eastern Railway Scouts & Guides Written Exam

History of Scouting/Guiding; Law and Promise; Organisation at District & State level; Training Centres at District & State level; Knowledge of Books; Scouting for Boys/Guiding for Girls in India; Motto, left-hand shake, salute, prayer, flag song, National Anthem; National and Scouting flags; Social Services; Hiking; Proficiency Badges; Stages in Scouting/Guiding; Thinking Day; Progressive Training of Scouts/Guides/Rovers/Rangers; Pioneering; Handicrafts; Indian Railway Jamborette; National Jamboree; Jamboree on the Air; Organization at National level; National Training Centres; WAGGGS/WOSM; Uprastrapati Award Competition; Prime Minister Shield Competition; Community Development Programmes; Aims and Methods of Scouting; and how Scouting is useful in producing better citizens.
