Selection and Interview

Professors of Practice are selected under the "Procedure for Selection of Professor of Practice" in Ordinance-189 and the University Circular dated 15/04/2023. The Hon'ble Vice-Chancellor may invite nominations from eminent experts, with or without a recommendation from the Department. Because the position is held by persons of eminence, the Vice-Chancellor may also nominate eminent experts and approve their engagement directly. All other candidates go through the Selection Committee. The Committee has the Hon'ble Vice-Chancellor or his or her representative, the Head of the Department concerned, the Dean of the respective Faculty, two subject experts nominated by the Vice-Chancellor, and one eminent external member (non-academic) nominated by the Vice-Chancellor. VNSGU Professor of Practice Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are open in VNSGU Recruitment 2026?
A
Five posts are open: Technical Assistant (D.C.A), Computer Operator (D.C.A), Junior Clerk/Typist (3rd Attempt), Junior Clerk/Typist (2nd Attempt) and Professor of Practice. The notice does not print a number for any of them, so each counts as 1 vacancy.
Q2What is the last date for the VNSGU non-teaching posts?
A
The last date for Technical Assistant (D.C.A), Computer Operator (D.C.A) and Junior Clerk/Typist is 11-11-2026 till 6:00 pm.
Q3What is the last date for VNSGU Professor of Practice applications?
A
The First Batch closes on 30/10/2026. After that, the online form is open from the 1st to the 20th of every month up to 30/07/2027.
Q4What is the eligibility for VNSGU Professor of Practice?
A
Candidates need proven expertise in their profession or role with at least fifteen (15) years of service or experience, preferably at a senior level. A formal academic qualification is not considered essential when the candidate has exemplary professional practice instead.
Q5What is the VNSGU Professor of Practice honorarium?
A
The honorarium is ₹2,000/- per hour, under University Circular No. પીજી/મહેનતાણું/11751/2022 dated 09/06/2022. Mileage allowance is paid for travel on University work.
Q6How do I apply for VNSGU Professor of Practice?
A
Apply online only, through the Google Form at https://forms.gle/mPx6MJsWUifqFBVm8 or its QR code. Upload your supporting documents in PDF format and record your consent to serve if selected.
Q7How is the VNSGU Professor of Practice selected?
A
A Selection Committee chaired by the Hon'ble Vice-Chancellor or his or her representative recommends candidates, and the University approves the engagement. The Vice-Chancellor may also nominate eminent experts and approve their engagement directly.
Q8Is the VNSGU Professor of Practice a permanent job?
A
No. The engagement is temporary, contractual and fixed-term, at first for one year, which may be extended up to three years in all. It gives no right to regular appointment, absorption or seniority.
Q9Can retired teachers apply for VNSGU Professor of Practice?
A
No. People in the teaching profession, whether serving or retired, are excluded, and their applications will be summarily rejected.
Q10Can I apply to more than one department as Professor of Practice?
A
Yes. A candidate may apply to more than one Department or subject area by giving an order of preference, with a separate submission for each Department.
