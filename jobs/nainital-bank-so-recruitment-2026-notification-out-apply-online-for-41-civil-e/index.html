The educational qualification for every post must be full-time and regular, from a University accredited by UGC, or an AICTE-approved institute, or a Board recognized by the Government of India/approved regulatory bodies. Candidates who completed their education through Distance Mode, Open Schooling, Part-Time mode, Correspondence Courses, or Executive MBA mode are not eligible to apply. Where a university has awarded a CGPA/GPA and no percentage conversion criterion is specified, the percentage shall be calculated by dividing the total marks obtained by the candidate in all subjects across all semesters by the aggregate maximum marks in all subjects, irrespective of honours/optional/additional subjects. In case of experience, the relevant experience certificate must specifically mention experience in Banks/NBFCs/FIs/Fintech.

Nainital Bank Specialist Officer Selection Process 2026

Selection is based on an Online Written Test followed by a Personal Interview . Candidates who qualify in the online written test will be called for interview, and final selection will be based on performance in both the written test and interview, subject to the candidate being found medically fit for the assignment. The test is conducted only in English. Each question has 5 answer options. Penalty for wrong answer: Yes, 0.25 marks deducted for each wrong answer. Though the test duration is 145 minutes, candidates may need to be at the venue for about 4 hours, including time for document verification, logging in, and instructions. Candidates have to choose any three centres of their preference and convenience during registration.
