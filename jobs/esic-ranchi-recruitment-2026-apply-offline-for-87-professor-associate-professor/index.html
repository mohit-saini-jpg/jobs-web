About ESIC Ranchi

The Employees' State Insurance Corporation (ESIC) is a statutory body set up under an Act of Parliament. It works under the administrative control of the Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India. ESIC Medical College and Hospital is on Purulia Road, Namkum, Ranchi - 834010, and its Dean is recruiting faculty for the proposed medical college on behalf of ESIC.

Application Fee

Pay the fee by Demand Draft in favour of "ESIC Saving Fund A/c No.-2", payable at Namkum, and send it with the application form. The fee is not refunded under any circumstances.

ESIC Ranchi Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection depends on your academic credentials and your performance in an interview before the Selection Board. After the applications are scrutinised, the exact interview date will be published on the ESIC recruitments page with the list of candidates and departments, or emailed to candidates directly. Being called for interview is provisional and depends on a detailed check of your eligibility. The result will be shown in the Recruitment section of the ESIC website, and offers of appointment will be sent by email only.

Service Conditions

Tenure: 1 year, extendable every year up to a maximum of 3 years if performance is satisfactory, or until a regular incumbent joins, whichever is earlier. The engagement can last until you turn 70 at most. It gives no claim to regularisation or to preference for a regular post. Leave: 2.5 days of paid leave for each completed month. This is earned leave under the CCS Leave Rules, 1972, with no encashment, no half leave and no advance leave. Holidays and Sundays that fall within a leave period count as leave. Security deposit: Rs 30,000/-, interest-free, paid at joining by Demand Draft in favour of "ESIC Saving Fund Account No.2", payable at Namkum. It is refunded after the contract is completed and you produce a no-dues certificate. Notice period: Either side can end the contract with one month's notice. If you resign on the spot, you must deposit one month's salary into the ESI account

ESIC Ranchi Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1Which department has the most Senior Resident vacancies?
A
Anaesthesiology has 7 Senior Resident posts (02 SC, 01 ST, 03 OBC, 01 EWS), followed by Medicine with 6.
Q2What is the age limit for a Senior Resident in Pathology or Anatomy?
A
It is 50 years for Anatomy, Biochemistry, Physiology, Pharmacology, Microbiology, Pathology, Forensic Medicine and Community Medicine. All other departments have a 45-year limit, counted on the date of interview.
Q3Do women candidates pay the application fee?
A
No. Female, SC, ST, PH and Defence Ex-servicemen candidates pay nothing. Everyone else pays Rs. 500/- by Demand Draft payable at Namkum.
Q4Can retired doctors apply for Professor posts?
A
Yes, up to an age of 69 years. Retired employees and pensioners draw the full pay without deduction and keep receiving their pension and dearness relief.
Q5Is there any deposit to pay after selection?
A
Yes. You pay an interest-free security deposit of Rs 30,000/- by Demand Draft at joining. It is refunded after the contract is completed and you produce a no-dues certificate.
Q6How will I know my interview date?
A
The tentative dates are 5 and 6 November 2026. The exact date will be posted on the ESIC recruitments page with the list of candidates, or emailed to you after the applications are scrutinised.
Q7Can I apply for two departments or posts with one form?
A
No. You must submit a separate application for each post in the prescribed format, or it will be rejected.
Q8Is accommodation provided?
A
No, the college does not provide accommodation. Private practice and teaching at another medical college are also prohibited.
Q9How much leave do I get on this contract?
A
You earn 2.5 days of paid leave for each completed month, with no encashment. Salary is deducted for any leave taken beyond that.
