DH&FWS Birbhum Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The notification (Memo No: DMO/AYUSH/ Birbhum/461) is a contractual recruitment under the National AYUSH Mission for Yoga Instructors in AYUSH clinics of Birbhum district and for support staff at the AYUSH District Programme Management Unit (DPMU), Birbhum. The notice does not print a combined total; the figure of 52 is the sum of the post-wise numbers it prints.

About Birbhum AYUSH

The recruitment is run by the AYUSH Branch of the Office of the Chief Medical Officer of Health & Secretary, District Health and Family Welfare Samity, Birbhum, located at Old Outdoor Campus, Suri, Birbhum. The notice is signed by the District Medical Officer (AYUSH), Birbhum, and the Chief Medical Officer of Health, Birbhum. The posts are funded under the National AYUSH Mission, and the DPMU Section manages the recruitment process.

Birbhum AYUSH Eligibility Criteria 2026

The applicant must be a permanent resident of West Bengal. This applies to applicants of all categories. The applicant must have knowledge of local languages. All essential criteria and the essential qualification must be completed on or before the first date of online application. Experience will be calculated after obtaining the required essential academic qualification for the post, till the first date of online application. Caste/EWS certificates are accepted only when issued on or before the first date of online application by the competent authorities of West Bengal. The essential criteria are the minimum; merely meeting them does not entitle a candidate to claim selection. Candidates should check the official notification for the exact eligibility of each post.

Birbhum AYUSH Selection Process 2026

Yoga Instructor (Male and Female): shortlisting on marks obtained in educational qualifications, a merit list, then demonstration and interview. Accountant, LDC and Group - D: selection procedure will be as per the decision of the DLSC Committee and will be notified later. Candidates will be selected from the merit list according to vacancy. Final selection will be done based on marks obtained on merit, interview and experience, if any. For both Yoga Instructor posts, 1:5 candidates will be shortlisted as per Academic Merit, that is, the marks out of 30 for Secondary/Madhyamik and the yoga qualification. Marks are rounded off up to 2 decimal points.

DH&FWS Birbhum Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in Birbhum AYUSH Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 52 posts: 21 Yoga Instructor (Male), 27 Yoga Instructor (Female), 1 Accountant, 1 Lower Division Clerk (LDC) and 2 Group - D.
Q2When does the Birbhum AYUSH online application start?
A
Online registration, fee payment and form submission open from 11 am of 20 November 2026.
Q3What is the last date to apply for Birbhum AYUSH Recruitment 2026?
A
Online registration and fee payment close at midnight of 3 December 2026, and the final submission of applications closes at midnight of 5 December 2026.
Q4What is the application fee?
A
The fee is Rs. 100/- for general candidates and Rs. 50/- for reserved category candidates, paid online only by net banking or credit/debit card; it is non-refundable.
Q5What is the age limit for Birbhum AYUSH Yoga Instructor?
A
Yoga Instructor candidates must be a minimum of 21 years and a maximum of 40 years as on 1st January 2026 (DOB within 01/01/1976 to 01/01/2006). For the Accountant, LDC and Group - D posts the range is 60 to 62 years.
Q6What qualification is needed for the Yoga Instructor post?
A
Secondary/Madhyamik passed with a Certificate/Diploma/Degree in Yoga affiliated by the West Bengal Council of Yoga Naturopathy (WBCYN).
Q7What is the salary of a Birbhum AYUSH Yoga Instructor?
A
Yoga Instructor (Male) gets Rs. 8000/- (32 sessions x Rs. 250/- per session) and Yoga Instructor (Female) gets Rs. 5000/- (20 sessions x Rs. 250/- per session).
Q8How are Yoga Instructors selected?
A
Candidates are shortlisted 1:5 on academic merit out of 30 and then scored on demonstration (10 marks) and interview (10 marks), for full marks of 50.
Q9Can retired employees apply, and how is their pay fixed?
A
Yes. The Accountant, LDC and Group - D posts are only for retired State Government employees aged 60 to 62, at a fixed Rs. 12000/-, Rs. 10000/- and Rs. 8000/- per month respectively.
Q10Is the Birbhum AYUSH job permanent?
A
No. The posts are filled on a contractual basis under the National AYUSH Mission.
