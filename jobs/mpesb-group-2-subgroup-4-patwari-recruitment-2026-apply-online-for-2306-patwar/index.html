MPESB Recruitment 2026 Notification Out

MPESB Group 2 Subgroup 4 Vacancy 2026

All 200 Patwari posts under this notification are reserved exclusively for Divyang (persons with disabilities) candidates, distributed across districts of Madhya Pradesh. Disability categories are EH (hearing impaired), LD (locomotor disability), and MD (multiple disabilities) - each category gets 2% horizontal reservation (6% total). Visually impaired candidates are not eligible for the Patwari post, as per the Revenue Department's order dated 11 May 2009. MPESB Group 2 Subgroup 4 Eligibility & Qualification 2026

Must be an Indian citizen and a local resident (domicile) of Madhya Pradesh A graduation degree from a recognized university A valid CPCT (Computer Proficiency Certification Test) score card, including Hindi typing and computer proficiency. Candidates who have not cleared CPCT at the time of application must clear it within 3 years of the probation period after appointment, failing which the appointment will be cancelled Must hold a Divyang (disability) certificate issued by a competent authority, in the categories eligible for this post: hearing impaired (EH), locomotor disability (LD), or multiple disabilities (MD). Visually impaired candidates are not eligible for the Patwari post Must be registered with the Madhya Pradesh Employment Office (Rozgar Karyalay) Educational qualification and experience requirements vary by post and department (ranging from Class 10/12 pass to graduate/tec

MPESB Patwari & Group 2 Subgroup 4 Salary 2026

Patwari: Pay scale is ₹5,200–20,200 with Grade Pay ₹2,100. For directly recruited candidates, salary during the first three years will be paid as per Madhya Pradesh General Administration Department circular No. C.3-13/2019/3/One, dated 12 December 2019. Other Group-2 Subgroup-4 posts: Pay scale varies by post and department (e.g. Assistant Grade-3, Surveyor, Computer Operator, etc.) - not consolidated into a single figure in this notification. MPESB Group 2 Subgroup 4 Exam Pattern 2026

The written examination is an online Computer-Based Test (CBT) of 200 multiple-choice questions for 200 marks, to be completed in 3 hours (2 hours of actual answering time within a 3-hour reporting/instruction/exam window per shift). Candidates must carry Aadhaar registration proof and a valid original photo ID (Voter ID, PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Driving Licence, or Passport) to the exam centre - multi-level biometric verification is mandatory both at entry and during the exam. Electronic devices (mobile phones, calculators, log tables, digital watches, sunglasses, etc.) are strictly prohibited in the exam hall.

MPESB Group 2 Subgroup 4 Selection Process 2026

Eligibility of candidates as filled in the online application is treated as provisional and is not verified by MPESB at the application stage - actual document/certificate verification for the Patwari (Divyang) posts is done by a document verification committee constituted in the candidate's allotted district after the exam, with disability certificates further verified by the District Medical Board (and, in special cases, the Divisional Medical Board). For the other Group-2 Subgroup-4 posts, the concerned recruiting department verifies educational, technical, and professional qualification certificates of successful candidates based on the final merit list from the written exam.

Q1What is MPESB Patwari Recruitment 2026?
A
It is a special recruitment drive by the Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board for 200 Patwari posts reserved for Persons with Disabilities (PwD/Divyang category), conducted under the Group-2 Sub-Group-4 Combined Recruitment Examination 2026.
Q2How many vacancies are available in MPESB Patwari Recruitment 2026?
A
A total of 200 Patwari vacancies are available, distributed across 44 districts of Madhya Pradesh and reserved for the PwD/Divyang category.

A
The online application last date is 21 August 2026. Corrections to the submitted form can be made until 23 August 2026.
Q4What is the qualification required for MPESB Patwari Recruitment 2026?
A
Candidates need a graduate degree from a recognised university, along with a CPCT score card with Hindi typing (or the ability to clear it within 3 years of probation after appointment).
Q5What is the age limit for MPESB Patwari Recruitment 2026?
A
The age limit is 18 to 45 years as on 1 January 2026 for Madhya Pradesh permanent resident candidates applying under this PwD category recruitment.
Q6What is the application fee for MPESB Patwari Recruitment 2026?

Q7Who is eligible to apply for MPESB Patwari Recruitment 2026?
A
Only Indian citizens who are permanent residents of Madhya Pradesh, hold a graduate degree, and have a valid disability certificate in the Hearing Impairment, Locomotor Disability, or Multiple Disability/Autism/Intellectual Disability/Mental Illness category are eligible. Visually impaired candidates are not eligible for this post.

Q9What is the selection process for MPESB Patwari Recruitment 2026?
A
Selection is based on a 200-mark computer-based online exam, followed by a percentile-based merit list, district allotment through a single round of counselling, and document verification.
