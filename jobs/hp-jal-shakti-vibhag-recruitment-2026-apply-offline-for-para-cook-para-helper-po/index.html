Where and How to Send

Completed application forms on the prescribed proforma, along with all requisite self-attested documents, must be submitted either in person or by post to the following address on or before 07 November 2026 up to 5:00 PM: Office of the Executive Engineer, Jal Shakti Division Reckong Peo, Distt. Kinnaur, Himachal Pradesh The prescribed application proforma can be obtained from the office of the Executive Engineer on any working day or downloaded from the official departmental website (https://jsv.hp.nic.in/). The department will not be responsible for delay in receipt of application due to any reason. Candidates who wish to apply for more than one post must submit a separate application form for each post.

The selected incumbent will be paid honorarium at the rate of Rs. 9000/- per month for Para Cook and 5500/- per month for Para Helper. The working hours of incumbents shall be 6 hours per day. Unsatisfactory performance may result in termination of appointment by passing an order. The process of termination shall however not be completed unless at least 3 notices have been served upon the incumbent on a specific charge and there has been no improvement in service delivery, the concerned J.E would have substantially put down in written the reasons for such termination and this need to be countersigned by concerned Assistant Engineer based on record.
