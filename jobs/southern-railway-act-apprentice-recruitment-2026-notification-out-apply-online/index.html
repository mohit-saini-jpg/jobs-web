Southern Railway Act Apprentice Eligibility Criteria

Only candidates residing within the following areas (Geographical Jurisdiction of Southern Railway) are eligible to apply: Whole State of Tamil Nadu Whole Union Territory of Puducherry Whole State of Kerala Whole Union Territories of Andaman & Nicobar and Lakshadweep Islands Only two districts of Andhra Pradesh: SPSR Nellore and Chittoor Only one district of Karnataka: Dakshina Kannada Candidates must also meet the minimum standards of physical fitness under Schedule-II of the Apprenticeship Rules, 1992, verified through a medical certificate from a Government-Authorized Doctor at the time of document verification.

Southern Railway Act Apprentice Qualification

Fresher Category: Fitter, Painter, Welder - Passed 10th Class (minimum 50% aggregate) under the 10+2 system or equivalent. Medical Laboratory Technician (Radiology/Pathology/Cardiology) - Passed 12th Class (minimum 50% aggregate) with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology. Ex-ITI Category: Most trades (Fitter, Machinist, MMV, Turner, Diesel Mechanic, Carpenter, Painter, Welder(G&E), Wireman, Advance Welder, Plumber, Draughtsman-Civil, Instrument Mechanic, Mech. R&AC, Gas Cutter, MMTM, Structural Welder) - Passed 10th Class (min. 50%) + ITI course in the relevant trade with an NCVT/SCVT certificate. Electrician - Same as above, with Science as a subject in 10th. Electronics Mechanic - Same as above, with Science (Physics & Chemistry) and Mathematics. Secretarial Assistant & Stenographer (English) - Passed 12th Class + ITI course with NCVT/SCVT certificate. ICTSM - Passed 10th Class + ITI course

Southern Railway Act Apprentice Age Limit

As on the date of notification (28-08-2026): Minimum age: 15 years completed Maximum age: 22 years (Fresher/Ex-ITI) / 24 years (MLT) Upper age relaxation: 3 years for OBC-(NCL), 5 years for SC/ST, 10 years for PwBD Combined relaxation for PwBD candidates: UR/EWS + PwBD = 10 years; OBC-(NCL) + PwBD = 13 years; SC/ST + PwBD = 15 years To avoid any confusion regarding age eligibility, candidates can use our Age Calculator to calculate their exact age based on the prescribed cut-off date before applying.

Stipend increases by 10% in the second year of training and a further 15% in the third year, over the prescribed minimum.

Southern Railway Act Apprentice Application Fee

Processing fee of Rs. 100/- (non-refundable), payable online. No fee is required for SC/ST/PwBD/Women candidates.

Southern Railway Act Apprentice Selection Process

Selection is based on a Merit List, prepared as follows: Fresher Category: Matriculation marks converted to 200 (minimum 50% aggregate required, except SC/ST/PwBD). Ex-ITI Category: Matriculation marks converted to 100 + ITI marks converted to 100 = 200 total, with equal weightage to both. MLT Category: Higher Secondary marks (Physics, Chemistry, Maths & Biology) converted to 200. In case of a tie, the older candidate is preferred; if birth dates are also the same, the candidate who passed Matriculation earlier is preferred.

Southern Railway Act Apprentice Important Instructions

This notification is purely for apprenticeship training, not employment - there is no guarantee of employment after training. 20% of vacancies in Direct Recruitment to Level 1 posts may give preference to Course Completed Act Apprentices (CCAAs) holding a National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC). All official communication - notifications, document verification lists, selection and eligibility results - will be posted only on the official website link mentioned above. Candidates availing SC/ST/OBC-NCL/PwBD benefits must produce the relevant certificate at document verification, else they will be treated as UR. False declarations will lead to disqualification/termination at any stage without notice. Candidates are responsible for arranging their own accommodation during training. Ex-ITI candidates must possess the relevant ITI qualification at the time of applying.

Southern Railway Act Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is Southern Railway Act Apprentice Recruitment 2026?
A
It is Southern Railway's engagement of candidates as Act Apprentices under the Apprentices Act, 1961, for training in designated trades across its Divisions/Workshops/Units (Notification No. 01/2026).
Q2How many vacancies are available?
A
The notification states 4,471 total posts (163 Fresher + 4,308 Ex-ITI); note the category-wise figures in the source table sum to slightly different totals (162 and 4,229 respectively) – this discrepancy should be verified before publishing.
Q3What is the last date to apply?
A
27 September 2026, till 5:00 PM, online only.
Q4What is the qualification required?
A
It varies by trade and category – ranging from 10th pass with 50% marks (Fresher trades like Fitter/Painter/Welder) to 10th/12th pass plus a relevant ITI certificate (Ex-ITI trades).
Q5What is the age limit for this recruitment?
A
15 to 22 years (24 years for MLT), as on the notification date, with relaxations for OBC-NCL, SC/ST, and PwBD candidates.
Q6Is there an application fee?

Yes, Rs. 100/- non-refundable, waived for SC/ST/PwBD/Women candidates.

Q8What is the selection process?

A merit list based on Matriculation marks (plus ITI marks for Ex-ITI candidates, or Higher Secondary marks for MLT candidates).

Rs. 8,200/month (Fresher–10th std), Rs. 9,600/month (Fresher–12th std and Ex-ITI), increasing in years 2 and 3 of training.
