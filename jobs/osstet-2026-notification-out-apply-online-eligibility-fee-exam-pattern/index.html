OSSTET 2026 Eligibility Criteria

OSSTET does not have a fixed vacancy count or a specified age limit in the notification - it is an eligibility test whose pass certificate is a prerequisite for teacher recruitment. Eligibility instead depends on nationality, the ability to read and write Odia, and the academic/professional qualification required for the specific teaching category and subject a candidate is applying for. The candidate must be a citizen of India. Candidates must be able to read and write Odia. OSSTET 2026 Exam Pattern

OSSTET 2026 will be conducted in two papers depending on the category chosen: Paper-I for Category I (Education Teachers) and Paper-II for Category II (Physical Education Teacher). Each paper is of 2 hours 30 minutes duration, contains multiple-choice questions worth 1 mark each with four options, and has no negative marking. Each paper is divided into four sections. Section III varies by stream: TGT (Arts) covers Odia, English, History & Political Science, and Geography & Economics (15 marks each); TGT (Science) PCM covers Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry (20 marks each); TGT (Science) CBZ covers Chemistry, Botany, and Zoology (20 marks each); and the Classical Teacher (Sanskrit/Telugu/Urdu) and Hindi Teacher streams have 60 marks dedicated entirely to the respective language. OSSTET 2026 Qualifying Marks

The minimum pass mark in each paper is 45% (68 marks out of 150) for General category candidates, and 35% (53 marks out of 150) for SC/ST/PH/SEBC candidates. A candidate must secure at least the applicable qualifying percentage in each paper to be considered as having qualified OSSTET.

OSSTET 2026 Certificate Validity and Number of Attempts

Successful candidates are awarded the OSSTET Pass Certificate by BSE Odisha. The certificate remains valid until the candidate reaches the upper age limit prescribed for teacher recruitment under applicable rules or government instructions issued from time to time. There is no restriction on the number of attempts a candidate can make to acquire the OSSTET Pass Certificate. Qualifying OSSTET is only one of the eligibility criteria for appointment as a teacher - it does not by itself confer any right to recruitment or employment in a Government or Government-Aided school; actual recruitment eligibility is determined separately by the concerned recruiting agency or appointing authority.

OSSTET 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1OSSTET 2026 applications must be submitted online only at www.bseodisha.ac.in. The registration link is active 24×7 from 3 August 2026 to midnight of 17 August 2026.
  • 2Visit www.bseodisha.ac.in and click "Click Here for New Registration", or log in with the registered mobile number and password if already registered.
  • 3Enter Name, Password, Mobile Number, e-Mail ID, and Caste Category, then click "Submit". Caste details entered at this stage cannot be corrected later.
  • 4Log in again using the registered mobile number and password.
  • 5Enter the verification code sent to the registered mobile number and click "Verify".
  • 6Proceed to pay the application fee online.
  • 7Fill up the four sections of the application form in order: Personal Information, Eligibility Criteria, Educational Qualification, and Declaration.
  • 8Upload all required certificates, documents, and photographs in the specified format and size.
  • 9Review all filled information at the Declaration stage, make edits if necessary, and tick the declaration box.
  • 10Click "YES – FINAL SUBMIT" to complete registration; no further editing is possible after this step.
  • 11Download and preserve the print copy of the Acknowledgement Receipt. Registration is complete only after this receipt is downloaded; without it, the admit card cannot be generated and the candidate will not be allowed to appear for the examination.

OSSTET 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is OSSTET 2026?
A
OSSTET 2026 is the Odisha Secondary School Teacher Eligibility Test conducted by BSE Odisha. Passing it is a mandatory eligibility requirement for appointment as a teacher in Secondary Schools (Government/Government-Aided/Recognised Unaided) in Odisha.
Q2What is the last date to apply for OSSTET 2026?
A
The online application process closes on 17 August 2026 at midnight, and BSE Odisha has stated that this date will not be extended under any circumstances.
Q3When will the OSSTET 2026 exam be conducted?
A
The examination will be held on 30 September 2026, from 9:00 AM to 11:30 AM, for both Paper-I and Paper-II.
Q4What is the application fee for OSSTET 2026?
A
The application fee is Rs. 900 per candidate for General/OBC category, and Rs. 600 per candidate for SC/ST candidates.
Q5What is the qualification required for OSSTET 2026?
A
Qualification depends on the category and subject chosen - for example, a relevant bachelor's degree with 50% aggregate (45% for reserved categories) plus a B.Ed. or equivalent teacher training qualification for Category I streams, and a +2 pass plus a physical education diploma/degree for Category II.
Q6What is the qualifying mark for OSSTET 2026?
A
The minimum qualifying mark is 45% (68 out of 150) for General candidates and 35% (53 out of 150) for SC/ST/PH/SEBC candidates, in each paper.
Q7How many papers are there in OSSTET 2026, and what is the exam pattern?
A
There are two papers: Paper-I for Category I (Education Teachers) and Paper-II for Category II (Physical Education Teacher). Each paper has 150 MCQs across four sections, a duration of 2 hours 30 minutes, and no negative marking.
Q8How can I apply online for OSSTET 2026?
A
Candidates can register online at www.bseodisha.ac.in between 3 August 2026 and 17 August 2026, complete the four-section application form, upload documents, pay the fee, and download the acknowledgement receipt after final submission.
