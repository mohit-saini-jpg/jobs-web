About Kalakshetra Foundation

Kalakshetra Foundation describes itself as an Institution of National Importance and an autonomous organisation under the Ministry of Culture, Govt. of India, located at Thiruvanmiyur, Chennai. The post advertised here is in its Rukmini Devi College of Fine Arts (RDCFA), which teaches Diploma and Post-Diploma courses and stages Kalakshetra productions and dance dramas.
