CCRUM Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Group A Posts: Online Computer Based Test (CBT) of 70 marks followed by an Interview of 30 marks. Interview shortlisting ratio: 1:5 for 1 vacancy, 1:4 for 2 vacancies, 1:3 for 3 and above vacancies. Group B & C Posts: Selection is based only on the online CBT examination of 100 marks, followed by a qualifying Skill Test wherever applicable (LDC, Stenographer Grade-III, Staff Car Driver). The CBT will be bilingual (English and Hindi) except MTS, which has no negative marking. There is 0.25 negative marking per wrong answer for all other posts. Minimum qualifying marks in CBT: 25% for SC/ST/PwBD candidates and 30% for all other categories (UR, OBC, EWS, ESM). Skill Test for Stenographer Grade-III: dictation at 80 wpm for 10 minutes (English/Hindi), transcribed on computer; qualifying in nature. Skill Test for LDC: typing speed of 35 wpm in English or 30 wpm in Hindi on computer; qualifying

CCRUM Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Visit the official CCRUM website: www.ccrum.res.in or www.ccrum.ayush.gov.in and go to the "Advertisement/Vacancy" tab.
  • 2Step 1 - Create Account: Register using your full name, father's/mother's name, gender, date of birth, mobile number, e-mail ID, and a valid Government ID proof to generate a Login ID (Reference Number) and password.
  • 3Step 2 - Complete Profile: Log in and fill in personal details, category/sub-category, upload photograph, signature, caste/category certificate, PwBD certificate, and basic qualification certificates.
  • 4Step 3 - Apply for Post(s): Select the desired post, fill post-specific details, upload post-specific documents, and proceed to payment. This step can be repeated for each additional post.
  • 5Pay the applicable Processing Fee and Examination Fee online through Net Banking/Debit Card/Credit Card/UPI.
