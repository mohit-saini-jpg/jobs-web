Notification DatePost Date: 27/07/2026

DU Admissions 2026: The University of Delhi (DU) released the second seat allocation list for undergraduate (UG) admissions on July 25, 2026, under the Common Seat Allocation System (CSAS-UG) 2026. As per official and verified reports, 24,814 candidates received fresh seat allotments in this round, while thousands of others saw their allotments upgraded. Candidates can check their status by logging in to the official CSAS-UG portal using their CUET UG 2026 application number and password.

Quick Overview - DU UG Second Seat Allotment 2026

DU Admissions 2026 for undergraduate programmes are being conducted through CSAS-UG 2026, based entirely on CUET UG 2026 scores. The second round of seat allocation is meant for candidates who did not get a seat in Round 1, and for those who chose "Float" (instead of "Freeze") after Round 1 to seek an upgrade to a better college or course.

DU Round 2 Seat Allocation 2026: Full Statistics

Delhi University's Round 2 allocation reshuffled a large number of provisional seats. Here is a breakdown of the officially reported numbers for this round:

  • 24,814 candidates received a fresh seat allotment in Round 2.
  • 30,682 candidates received an upgraded college or course allocation compared to Round 1.
  • 15,265 candidates chose to freeze their previously allotted seat, confirming their admission preference.
  • 88,584 candidates in total participated in the Round 2 process.

Round 1 Allocation Summary (For Context)

In the first round of DU UG admissions 2026, out of 15,942,385 college-and-programme preferences considered, 93,033 candidates were offered seats - 42,019 male and 51,014 female candidates - representing an overall allocation rate of about 86.1%. This included 1,243 single girl children and 242 orphan candidates (109 male, 133 female).

Tie-Breaking Rule in Case of Equal CUET Scores

Where two or more candidates have the same CUET UG 2026 score for a programme-college combination, DU uses the following order to break the tie:

  • Higher marks in Class 12 (best of 3 subjects considered first, then best of 4, then best of 5).
  • If the tie persists, Class 10 marks are considered.
  • If still tied, the older candidate is given priority.

DU CSAS-UG 2026 Application and Admission Fee

The base CSAS-UG registration/application fee amount was not explicitly available in the sources reviewed. However, the following additional fees are confirmed on the official CSAS-UG admission portal:

  • An additional, non-refundable fee of Rs. 400 applies for candidates applying to BFA, B.Sc. (Physical Education, Health Education & Sports), or B.A. (Hons.) Music.
  • An additional fee of Rs. 100 per quota applies for candidates applying under the ECA (Extra-Curricular Activities) or Sports supernumerary quota.
  • Fee payment is accepted only online - via credit card, debit card, net banking, or UPI. There is no cash or offline payment option.

How to Check DU Second Seat Allotment 2026 and Apply/Accept Online

  • Visit the official DU admission portal at admission.uod.ac.in (also referenced as ugadmission.uod.ac.in).
  • Log in using your CUET UG 2026 application number and password.
  • Go to the seat allotment/dashboard section to view your Round 2 allotment status.
  • Choose to accept the allotted seat, and decide whether to freeze it or float for a further upgrade.
  • If accepting, complete the admission fee payment online before the deadline.
  • Download and retain the confirmation/receipt for your records.

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Candidates must accept their Round 2 allotted seat by July 26, 2026.
  • Admission fee payment for confirmed seats must be completed by July 28, 2026, 11:59 PM.
  • If a candidate takes no action on an allotted seat, DU may cancel the provisional allotment, and the candidate may not be considered in subsequent rounds.
  • Registration and preferences carry forward automatically for further rounds - candidates do not need to register again.
  • Candidates are advised not to rely on previous years' cutoffs as a prediction for 2026, since CUET scores and the seat matrix change every year.
