CVRDE Eligibility Criteria 2026

Candidates must have passed out from an ITI duly recognized by NCVT/SCVT, as per the Gazette Notification of the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship, in a trade of minimum two years' duration - except for Computer Operator & Programming Assistant (COPA), Painter, Plumber, Welder, and Food Production, which are exempt from this two-year duration requirement. Only candidates who completed the qualifying examination as regular candidates are eligible; private candidates are not eligible.

CVRDE Age Limit (as on 01-09-2026)

Minimum age: 18 years Maximum age - Unreserved (UR): 27 years Maximum age - OBC: 30 years Maximum age - SC/ST: 32 years Maximum age - PWD: 37 years Candidates are advised to check whether they satisfy the prescribed age limit before applying. CVRDE Selection Process

Candidates will be shortlisted based on marks obtained in the qualifying ITI examination, followed by a Walk-In Interview. Selected candidates will be given their joining letter on the spot at CVRDE, Avadi, Chennai-600054. It is clarified that no employment is guaranteed to apprentices on completion of their training, as selection and training are strictly under the provisions of the Apprentices Act, 1961. Additional eligibility notes: only candidates who passed the qualifying exam in 2023, 2024, 2025, or 2026 are eligible - those who passed earlier than 2023 are not eligible. Candidates whose results were not declared by 30-08-2026 will not be considered for training, though a provisional certificate is acceptable at the time of joining, with the final NCVT/SCVT certificate to be submitted later. Expired OBC certificates will not be accepted.

Documents Required for the CVRDE Walk-In Interview

10th or 12th mark sheet and certificate ITI mark sheets and NCVT/SCVT certificate Caste certificate, if applicable Aadhaar Card NCVT/SCVT MIS portal registration number (mandatory, verified at the time of walk-in interview/document verification)

How to Attend the CVRDE Walk-In Interview

Complete mandatory registration on the NCVT/SCVT MIS portal at https://ncvtmis.gov.in and note the registration number. Report to the CVRDE, Avadi, Chennai-600054 venue on 08-09-2026 or 09-09-2026, between 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM. Carry all required original documents along with photocopies for verification, including the NCVT/SCVT registration number. Candidates will be shortlisted on the spot based on ITI marks and interviewed the same day. Candidates will be informed of their selection status on the spot, and selected candidates will receive their joining letter immediately. No separate application form submission, online portal, or fee payment process is specified in the notification beyond the NCVT/SCVT registration requirement.

CVRDE Recruitment 2026 – Important Instructions

Duration of training will strictly follow the Apprentices Act, 1961, the Apprenticeship Rules, and their amendments from time to time. No employment is guaranteed to apprentices on completion of their training. If any information or document is found false or forged at any stage, the candidature or appointment will be withdrawn immediately without assigning any reason.
