About NPCIL

NPCIL is a public sector enterprise under the Department of Atomic Energy, Government of India. It handles every stage of a nuclear power reactor in India, from site selection, design and construction through operation, maintenance, life extension, waste management and decommissioning. This intake is for the Narora Atomic Power Station plant site in Narora, Bulandshahr district, Uttar Pradesh (PIN 203389).

NPCIL Apprentice Eligibility 2026

The closing date, 18 November 2026, is also the cut-off date for qualifications and every other eligibility condition. The qualification must have been earned through a full-time, regular course. Diploma and graduate candidates must have passed their qualifying exam within 5 years of 18 November 2026. This is a condition for applying on the NATS portal. Anyone who is not an Indian national over 18 years of age. Anyone who has completed, or is currently doing, an apprenticeship under the Apprentices Act, 1961 in any government body, PSU or private industrial organisation. Anyone who left an earlier apprenticeship at any organisation without completing it (absconded). Candidates must be free of any contagious or infectious disease. They must also be free of any illness that apprenticeship is likely to worsen, that would make them unfit for training or that would put public health at risk.

NPCIL Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Eligible local candidates living within 16 km of Narora Atomic Power Station get first preference, subject to the reservation rules. If there are not enough eligible local candidates in a category, candidates from outside the area are considered on merit. The shortlist is based on the percentage of marks in the respective trade or discipline. If two candidates have the same percentage, the one born earlier ranks higher. If the date of birth is also the same, the candidate with the higher Matriculation (10th) percentage ranks higher. Candidates graded in CGPA, GPA, SGPA or letter grades must submit the conversion table issued by their institute. If the institute has no conversion formula, NPCIL uses the following equivalents:

Training Conditions

Place of training: Narora Atomic Power Station, Narora, PO NAPS Township, Distt. Bulandshahr, Uttar Pradesh - 203389. Duration: a maximum of 12 months for all three types of apprentice. Each apprentice signs a contract of apprenticeship with NPCIL under the Apprentices Act, 1961 and the Apprenticeship Rules, 1992. The contract ends when the training period ends. Apprentices who stay away from training may have to refund the whole cost of their training, meaning the stipend received, as decided by the Central Apprenticeship Advisor. NAPS is a highly protected area, so apprentices are engaged only after they submit a Police Verification Certificate issued by the District Police Authorities. No hostel or transport is provided. NPCIL is not obliged to offer a job to anyone who completes the training, and apprentices are not obliged to accept one. Completing the apprenticeship gives no right

NPCIL Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many seats are there for ITI holders?
A
There are 50 Trade Apprentice seats: 25 for ITI Electrician, 16 for ITI Fitter and 09 for ITI Electronics Mechanic.
Q2What stipend does a Trade Apprentice get?
A
Rs 10,560/- a month after a one-year ITI course, and Rs 11,040/- a month after a two-year ITI course.
Q3Do local candidates get preference?
A
Yes. Eligible candidates living within 16 km of Narora Atomic Power Station are considered first, subject to reservation. They must submit proof of their permanent address.
Q4Which establishment ID should I apply under?
A
Trade Apprentices apply under E05200901473 on the NAPS portal. Diploma and Graduate Apprentices apply under NUPBUC093592 on the NATS portal.
Q5Can I apply if I completed my diploma or degree long ago?
A
Diploma and Graduate Apprentices must have passed their qualifying exam within 5 years of 18 November 2026.
Q6I have already done an apprenticeship. Can I apply?
A
No. Anyone who has done, or is doing, an apprenticeship under the Apprentices Act, 1961 in any government body, PSU or private organisation cannot apply.
Q7How is the merit list decided if two candidates have the same marks?
A
The candidate born earlier ranks higher. If the dates of birth are also the same, the higher 10th percentage decides.
Q8Is there a written exam?
A
The notice describes only a shortlist based on the percentage of marks in the respective trade or discipline, followed by document verification with originals.
Q9Will NPCIL give me a job after the apprenticeship?
A
No. Under the Apprentices Act, 1961, NPCIL is not obliged to offer a job to anyone who completes the training, and apprentices have no right to claim one.
Q10What are the photo and signature sizes for the online form?
A
The photo must be a colour JPG of no more than 50KB, between 240 x 180 and 640 x 480 pixels. The signature must be a JPG of no more than 20KB, between 80 x 200 and 160 x 560 pixels.
