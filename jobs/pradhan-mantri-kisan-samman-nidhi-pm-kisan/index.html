Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) Overview

PM-KISAN ek simple cash assistance yojana hai jo har eligible kisan ko har saal Rs 6,000 directly bank account mein bhejti hai. Aspirants ko bas apna land record verify karna hota hai aur portal par apply karna hota hai.

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) Expert Analysis

Yeh scheme financial inclusion ko boost karti hai aur crop failure ke time farmers ko thoda cushion deti hai. Direct transfer se leakage kam hoti hai, isliye sarkar isko priority deti hai.

Who Is Eligible: Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)

Eligibility hai 2 hectare se kam land holder, jo 2018-19 tak land records mein listed ho, aur koi bhi income tax ya GST registration nahi ho.

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) Application Process

1. Apna Aadhaar, bank account aur land record (Khasra) ready rakho. 2. PM-KISAN portal ya mPariksha app par login karke "Apply" button dabao. 3. Form fill karte waqt mobile number aur email sahi dalein, verification ke liye OTP aayega.

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) Benefits Details

Har eligible farmer ko Rs 6,000 per year milta hai, jo 3 installments of Rs 2,000 mein bank transfer hota hai. Yeh amount seed, fertilizer ya daily expenses ke liye use ho sakta hai, isse financial stress kam hota hai.

How Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) Works

Scheme ka implementation state agriculture department ke through hota hai. Data verification ke baad, Ministry of Finance direct benefit transfer (DBT) system se paise farmer ke bank account mein bhejti hai, usually har quarter ke end mein.

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) Application Tips

Application submit karne se pehle land record ko online portal par cross‑check karo, Aadhaar se link ensure karo, aur sabhi documents clear scan upload karo. Common rejection ka reason hota hai mismatched name or wrong bank details, isliye double‑check karo.
