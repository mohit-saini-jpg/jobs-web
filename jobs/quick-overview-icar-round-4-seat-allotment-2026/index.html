Quick Overview - ICAR Round 4 Seat Allotment 2026 : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 14/08/2026

The Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) will release the ICAR UG Counselling 2026 Round 4 seat allotment result on August 14, 2026 at the official counselling portal, icarcounseling.com. This round is meant for candidates who did not receive a seat in Round 3, or who were allotted a seat but did not accept it in time. Candidates can log in with their CUET (ICAR-UG) 2026 application number, roll number, and date of birth to check their allotted university, programme, and next steps.

How to Check ICAR Round 4 Seat Allotment Result 2026

  • Go to the official ICAR counselling portal at icar.org.in
  • Click on the "Applicant Login" tab.
  • Enter your CUET (ICAR-UG) 2026 application number, roll number, and date of birth as login credentials.
  • Submit the login form to open your candidate dashboard.
  • Check the Round 4 seat allotment status - it will show either an allotted university/programme or "not allotted."
  • If allotted, download and save the provisional allotment letter; keep multiple printed copies for document verification.

Programmes and Participating Universities

ICAR UG Counselling 2026 covers admission to undergraduate programmes including:

  • B.Sc (Hons) Agriculture
  • B.Sc (Hons) Horticulture
  • B.Sc (Hons) Forestry
  • B.F.Sc (Fisheries Science)
  • B.Sc (Hons) Sericulture
  • B.Sc (Hons) Community Science
  • B.Tech Agricultural Engineering
  • B.Tech Food Technology
  • B.Tech Dairy Technology
  • B.Tech Biotechnology

Admissions are conducted across 74 SAUs, CAUs, and ICAR Deemed Universities. Note that ICAR allots the university, not the specific college - the constituent college (which may be located away from the main campus) is decided by the allotted university itself, based on its own merit and category-based criteria.

What Happens After Round 4

  • If allotted a seat: Upload documents for verification by the allotted university and pay the seat acceptance fee within the deadline shown on your dashboard to confirm your provisional admission.
  • If documents are not verified in time, or queries raised by the university are not resolved: The provisional allotment can be cancelled, and the seat is carried forward as vacant to the Mop-Up round.
  • If not allotted a seat in Round 4: You become eligible for the Mop-Up round, which includes fresh choice filling starting August 18, 2026, for any seats that remain vacant after all four regular rounds.
  • Upgradation: Unlike Rounds 1- 3, Round 4 does not offer an upgradation option - candidates allotted a seat in Round 4 can accept it or wait for the Mop-Up round, but cannot request an upgrade within Round 4 itself.

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Always access the result only through the official domain, icarcounseling.com, and avoid third-party links or unofficial WhatsApp/Telegram forwards.
  • Keep your CUET (ICAR-UG) application number, roll number, and date of birth ready before the result window opens to avoid last-minute login issues.
  • Do not wait until the last hour to complete document verification or fee payment - Round 4 timelines, like previous rounds, are typically tight (often same-day or next-day).
  • Download and safely store multiple copies of your provisional allotment letter, as it will be required at multiple stages of admission.
  • If the documents or testimonials submitted are found to be forged or fake, the admission can be cancelled and the candidate may face legal action, as per ICAR's counselling rules.
