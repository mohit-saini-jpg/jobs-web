SSSB Punjab Recruitment 2026 Age Limit

The age limit for these posts, as on 01.01.2026 , as per Punjab Government instructions, is: No candidate of any category should be less than 18 years of age. The upper age limit for General category candidates should not exceed 37 years. The maximum upper age limit for Scheduled Caste candidates who are residents of Punjab State shall be 42 years. The maximum age limit, giving relaxation in upper age limit for State and Central Government employees, shall be 45 years. The upper age limit for widows, divorcees and certain other categories of married women, as per Punjab Government instructions vide letter No. 1/50/83-5PP(1368)/3454 dated 23.04.1984, will be relaxed up to 40 years. State and Central Government employees seeking upper age relaxation must obtain an NOC from their department and present it at the time of counseling.

SSSB Punjab Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Candidates who successfully apply for the notified posts will be called for an Objective Type (Multiple Choice Question, MCQ) written examination. The merit list will be prepared on the basis of marks obtained in the written examination. There will be negative marking in this written examination: each question carries 1 mark, and ¼ mark will be deducted for a wrong answer. In case of a tie in merit in the written examination: first, the candidate with the higher age (based on date of birth) will rank above; if the date of birth is also the same, the candidate with a higher percentage in the required educational qualification will rank above; if this is also tied, the candidate with higher marks in Matriculation will rank above. Punjab SSSB Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Candidates can apply only online, by clicking the link under "Apply Online" on the Board's website https://sssb.punjab.gov.in, from 01-10-2026 to 22-10-2026. Applications received through any other method will not be accepted and will be considered rejected.
  • 2Registration and Login: Candidates who do not have a Login/User ID must click "Registration" and fill in their details, which will generate their User ID and Password. The User ID will be the Email ID entered at the time of registration. Online application can be done only after successful registration, using the User ID (Email ID). Candidates should keep this User ID/Password safe for future use. After registration, candidates should log in with their registered Email ID and continue the online application process.
  • 3Filling the Online Application Form: Candidates first fill in their personal, educational and other required information correctly and completely, then upload a passport-size photo (or capture a live photo through the camera), along with a scanned copy of their signature. Applications sent without a photo and signature will be considered incomplete and rejected. Candidates then click "Save Draft" or "Next".
  • 4Attach Annexure: Candidates click "Attach Annexure" and upload scanned copies of their 10th class certificate, certificates related to educational qualification, and other mandatory documents, then click "Save Documents".
  • 5Make Payment: Candidates click "Make Payment" and pay the online fee.
  • 6Submit: After paying the fee, candidates click "Submit" to complete the application process. The full Application Form will then be displayed on screen; candidates should take a printout and keep it safe for future reference. The Application Form is accepted only after successful payment of the fee.
  • 7A saved draft application can be viewed and completed again via "Menu > View Status of Application > View Incomplete Application". Once an Application Form is submitted, it cannot be modified under any circumstances, and no request/demand from a candidate for a future modification will be accepted by the office. If an online fee payment transaction fails for any reason, the candidate's application will be considered not submitted, and the fee already paid will be non-refundable. Applications or fees sent/deposited through any other method will not be considered valid and will be rejected.
