Who Can Apply

The notice lists the following eligibility conditions for the Post-Doctoral Fellow (PDF): The applicant must be an Indian citizen. The applicant must possess a PhD degree from a recognized university, with first class (or equivalent grades) at all preceding academic levels and a consistently good academic record. The PhD must have been completed within the five years immediately preceding the last date for submission of the application to NERIST. The applicant must have a proven record of research, supported by peer-reviewed publications, recognitions and/or relevant research outputs in ESDM or IT/ITES. The applicant must not have obtained the PhD degree from NERIST, the proposed host institution. Applicants in regular employment are not eligible. Pay and Tenure

The fellowship is tenable in India only, at the host institution to which the PDF seat is allotted. The initial tenure is one year. Any extension, if permitted, is subject to performance review and approval by the PhD Cell, Digital India Corporation (DIC), MeitY, under the applicable scheme provisions. The travel support covers (a) visa fee and one-time travel support for eligible overseas faculty/applicants returning to India to take up the fellowship, and (b) participation in invited Visvesvaraya scheme activities/events/workshops. The contingency grant may be used for eligible minor equipment, consumables and other research-related expenditure in accordance with the scheme. The host institution is expected to provide administrative and infrastructure support. Selection and Interview

Applications will be scrutinized by a duly constituted NERIST committee in accordance with the sanctioned PDF seats and scheme guidelines. Candidates may be shortlisted on academic record, PhD research relevance, quality of publications, research proposal, proposed mentor alignment and other approved criteria. Shortlisted applicants will be informed through the official NERIST channel, ordinarily by email, and may be called for an interview/presentation of the proposed research. Shortlisted candidates must produce original documents and one set of self-attested photocopies for verification at the interview or joining stage. The selection committee shall recommend suitable candidate(s) to the competent authority, subject to scheme approvals and verification of documents.
