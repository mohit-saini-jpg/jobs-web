IIT Kharagpur Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur is filling 13 posts in Level 10 of the Pay Matrix (₹56100-177500/- plus admissible allowances): 4 Assistant Registrar, 5 Counsellor and 4 Executive Officer, including backlog vacancies, open to Indian Nationals. Applications are online only, through www.iitkgp.ac.in >> Jobs >> Staff Opening, on or before 19 December 2026, under Advertisement No. R/16/2026 dated 19 November 2026.

Who Can Apply

Only Indian Nationals may apply. Eligibility and any other shortlisting criteria are considered as on the last date of online application, 19 December 2026. The degree certificate should have been awarded by a recognized University/Institute. Essential Qualification: Master's Degree with at least 55% marks or its equivalent grade of 'B' in the UGC 7-point scale from recognised Universities/Institutes of repute and consistently good academic records. Essential Experience: At least 3 years of relevant experience at supervisory level at least in Level 6 of Pay Matrix ₹35400-112400/- (Pre-revised: PB-2, GP ₹4200/-) or equivalent in a reputed Educational Institute/University System/Research Institute/Central Government Departments; Experience in Computer System & Applications, Office Automation. Pay and Tenure

All three posts carry Level 10 of Pay Matrix ₹56100-177500/- and other allowances as admissible as per the Institute norms. The notice does not mention the in-hand salary. The posts are advertised as regular posts, but the Institute reserves the right to offer a post on contract basis instead of a regular post, depending upon the qualification, experience and/or performance in the selection process.

Application Fee

The fee is paid through the online portal (SBI Net Banking/Other Banks Net Banking/All Banks Credit Cards (Retail)/Rupay Debit Cards). The fee paid will not be refunded under any circumstances, nor can it be held in reserve for any other application, examination or selection.

Selection and Interview

The Institute may call only the requisite number of candidates for the selection process after shortlisting with reference to qualification, suitability, experience, etc. If the number of applicants for a post is large, the Institute reserves the right to evolve a screening or selection process such as a Screening Test, Written Test, Presentation, Group Discussion or Personal Interview. The test/interview schedule will be e-mailed in due course to shortlisted candidates at their registered e-mail; no separate hard-copy letter will be sent. Admission to any stage is provisional, subject to confirmation of eligibility.

IIT Kharagpur Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

The Institute reserves the right to fill or not fill any of the advertised positions, and to reject any or all applications without assigning reasons. No interim enquiries will be entertained, and no correspondence will be entertained about the selection process. Canvassing in any manner would entail disqualification. The Institute will verify antecedents and documents at appointment or during service; service will be terminated if documents are fake or antecedents have been suppressed. Updates and amendments will appear at www.iitkgp.ac.in >> Jobs >> Staff Opening. Any dispute about the selection/recruitment process is subject to courts having jurisdiction over Kolkata.

  • 1Go to www.iitkgp.ac.in >> Jobs >> Staff Opening and apply online on or before 19 December 2026.
  • 2Enter a correct and active e-mail address, because all correspondence from the Institute is by e-mail only.
  • 3Upload the certificates that apply to you (see the list below); shortlisting is done only on the documents uploaded with the online application.
  • 4Pay the application fee through the online portal.
  • 5Check every field before submitting: no change, correction or modification is allowed once the application is submitted.
  • 6Obtain a printout of the application form after successful submission; it is required at the time of document verification/test/interview.
  • 7No hard copy of the application is to be submitted. For problems during the online application, the notice gives the contact [email protected].
  • 8Reserved categories: latest Caste/Category certificate issued by the Competent Authority.
  • 9OBC (Non Creamy Layer): OBC(NCL) certificate clearly showing non-creamy layer status, issued on or after 01.04.2026 but not later than the last date of online applications; without it, the applicant is treated as unreserved.
  • 10EWS: Income and Assets certificate in the prescribed format, based on income of financial year 2025-2026, issued on or after 01.04.2026 but not later than the last date, valid for 2026-2027.
  • 11PwD: proof of disability mentioning the percentage of disability, issued by the Competent Authority.
  • 12Experience certificates on the organisation's letterhead, with date of issue, specific period of work, salary, and name, designation, signature and stamp of the issuing authority.
  • 13Persons serving in Government/Semi Government Organizations/Autonomous Bodies/Educational Institutions: No Objection Certificate (NOC) and Vigilance clearance while applying online, failing which these must be produced at the interview.
