Pay and Tenure

The Chief Manager - Accounts & Finance post is in Grade E5, with a pay scale of Rs.80,000 - 3% - 2,20,000/- under the IDA pattern. Total Emoluments & Allowances include: Basic Pay in the pay scale Dearness Allowance House Rent Allowance (at the rates that apply at the place of posting) Cafeteria Allowance, etc. Officials can also use the Group Health Schemes that the company has adopted. The appointment is on a regular basis. You must be willing to be posted anywhere in India where NSIL has an office, and to travel across India as required.

NSIL Recruitment 2026 Job Description

The key responsibilities of the Chief Manager - Accounts & Finance are: Oversee financial planning, budgeting, forecasting, and financial analysis to support strategic decision-making. Ensure compliance with government financial regulations, tax laws, and statutory requirements. Coordinate with regulatory bodies such as CAG, GST authorities, and other financial auditors. Provide leadership in investment planning and risk assessment, and lead a team so that the department functions efficiently. Develop and implement financial policies, procedures, and best practices. Support management in financial decision-making through in-depth financial analysis and reports. Liaise with banks, financial institutions, and government agencies for financial transactions.

Selection and Interview

NSIL will select through a written test and/or interview, on a schedule it will announce. Screening and selection are based on the details you give in the application form. The final selection is made in order of merit, based on your performance in the written test and/or interview. Meeting the minimum qualification and experience does not give you a right to be called for the interview. You may need to stay an additional day for the selection process. Travelling expenses/allowance for attending the written test and/or interview are paid as per the Company norms.
