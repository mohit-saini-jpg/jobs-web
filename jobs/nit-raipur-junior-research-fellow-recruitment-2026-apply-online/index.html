Who Can Apply

Candidates must hold a Graduate or Post graduate Degree in Electronics, Electronics and Telecommunication, Biomedical Engineering, Bioengineering, Medical Electronics, Biomedical Instrumentation, Signal Processing, and related fields. Applicants must be selected through a process described in either: Scholars selected through GATE and National Eligibility Tests - CSIR-UGC NET including lectureship (Assistant professorship). Selection processes through National Level Examinations conducted by central Govt. Departments and their agencies and Institutions like IIT, IISc, DST, DBT, MHRD, etc. Desirable Qualification: Knowledge of basic programming skills in Python, MATLAB, C, C++. Understanding of EEG, signal processing techniques, machine learning and deep learning techniques/algorithms.

Pay and Tenure

The selected Junior Research Fellow will receive a fellowship amount of Rs. 31,000 per month plus 16% HRA. The position is purely temporary and continuation of the fellowship is subject to satisfactory performance, review, and availability of funds under the project. The appointment is co-terminus with the project, lasting up to 11 January 2027 only.

Selection and Interview

Selection is based on qualifications, eligibility, and an interview. The interview will be conducted on 21/10/2026 at 12:30 PM in the Department of Biomedical Engineering, National Institute of Technology, Raipur. Online interviews are permissible for candidates who submit their application online by the due date. No TA/DA is permissible for appearing in the interview.
