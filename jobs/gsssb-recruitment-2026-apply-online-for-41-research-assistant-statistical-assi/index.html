Application fee must be paid online (debit card, internet banking, UPI, or wallet) after submitting the form, by 26 September 2026 (11:59 PM). A transaction fee applies in addition to the exam fee. GSSSB Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection for both Research Assistant and Statistical Assistant is through a single-stage Computer Based Response Test (CBRT)/OMR-based objective (MCQ) competitive examination, held in one paper divided into Part-A and Part-B. Part-A (90 marks, 90 questions): Reasoning & Data Interpretation (30), Quantitative Aptitude (30), Constitution of India, Current Affairs & Gujarati-English Comprehension (30) Part-B (120 marks, 120 questions): Subject-related questions based on educational qualification - Statistics (30), Official Statistics (30), Mathematics (30), Economics (30) Total: 210 questions, 210 marks, combined duration of 3 hours (180 minutes) Negative marking: 1/4 mark deducted for each wrong answer Minimum qualifying standard: 40% in Part-A and 40% in Part-B, independently, for all categories Candidates (roughly twice the category-wise vacancies) are called for document verification b

GSSSB Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1GSSSB Apply Online is available only through the OJAS portal - no offline applications are accepted.
  • 2Visit ojas.gujarat.gov.in
  • 3Go to "Online Application," click Apply, and select GSSSB
  • 4Select Advertisement No. 457/202627, Class-3, click Apply Now
  • 5Fill in Personal Details and Educational Details
  • 6Accept the declaration under "Assurance" and click Save to generate your Application Number
  • 7Upload photograph and signature in the specified size and format
  • 8Click "Confirm Application" to generate your Confirmation Number (no edits possible after this step)
  • 9Print the confirmed application form and keep it safely
  • 10Pay the application fee online by 26 September 2026 and keep the fee receipt
