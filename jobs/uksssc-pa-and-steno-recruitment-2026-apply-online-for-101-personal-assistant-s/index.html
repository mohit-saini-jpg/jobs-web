UKSSSC PA and Steno Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Uttarakhand Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UKSSSC), headquartered on Thanon Road near Maharana Pratap Sports College, Rajpur, Dehradun, conducts direct recruitment to Group 'C' posts on behalf of various Uttarakhand government departments. This recruitment drive covers Personal Assistant, Stenographer, Stenographer Grade-3, and Stenographer Grade-2 posts spread across departments such as the Commercial Tax Tribunal, Industrial Tribunal & Labour Court, Uttarakhand Forest Development Corporation, Power Transmission Corporation of Uttarakhand, Commissioner Kumaon Division, Rural Development Department, Stamps & Registration Department, Higher Education Department, Housing Department, and several others. UKSSSC Personal Assistant & Stenographer Selection Process

Selection is conducted in two stages: Written Competitive Examination: An objective-type (multiple-choice) offline exam of 100 marks and 2 hours' duration, covering the syllabus related to the educational qualification of the post. This exam determines the merit list. Qualifying Typing and Shorthand/Dictation Test: Based on merit in the written exam, candidates numbering up to 4 times the number of vacancies are shortlisted for a qualifying typing test and shorthand/dictation test. This stage is qualifying in nature only - marks from this stage are not added to the final merit. Minimum qualifying marks in the written exam are 45% for Unreserved, EWS, and OBC category candidates, and 35% for Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe category candidates; candidates scoring below this are treated as not qualified.
