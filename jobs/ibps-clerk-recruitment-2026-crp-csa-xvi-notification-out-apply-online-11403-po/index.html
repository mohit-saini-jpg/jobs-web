IBPS Clerk Selection Process

Selection for CRP CSA-XVI is a two-tier process consisting of an Online Preliminary Examination and an Online Main Examination, followed by a qualifying Local Language Proficiency Test before joining. There is a penalty of one-fourth (0.25) of the marks assigned to a question for each wrong answer in both the Preliminary and Main Examinations. No penalty applies to unattempted questions. Candidates must clear individual cut-offs in each test as well as an overall cut-off. Only marks obtained in the Main Examination are considered for the final merit list; Preliminary Exam marks are used only for shortlisting. Candidates must be proficient in the local language of the State/UT they apply for. Candidates who have studied that language up to Class 10 or above are exempt from a separate test. IBPS Clerk Recruitment 2026 (CRP CSA-XVI) : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Candidates can apply online only - no other mode of application is accepted.
  • 2Visit the official IBPS website www.ibps.in and open the "CRP CSA" link, then click "Click Here to Apply Online for CRP – Customer Service Associates (CRP-CSA-XVI)".
  • 3Click "Click Here for New Registration" and enter basic details to generate a provisional registration number and password (also sent by email/SMS).
  • 4Upload the required scanned documents: photograph, signature, left thumb impression, a handwritten declaration, SSC/SSLC/10th standard or equivalent certificate, and the certificate mentioned in Clause K(ix) if applicable - as per the specifications in Annexure IV of the notification.
  • 5Carefully fill in all sections of the online application form using the "Save and Next" facility to verify details before final submission, since most fields cannot be edited after submission.
  • 6Indicate the State/UT for which you want to be considered for provisional allotment - this choice is irrevocable once submitted.
  • 7Pay the application fee/intimation charges online through the integrated payment gateway (Debit Card/Credit Card/Internet Banking/IMPS/Wallets/UPI).
  • 8On successful payment, take a printout of the e-receipt and the completed application form for your records.
