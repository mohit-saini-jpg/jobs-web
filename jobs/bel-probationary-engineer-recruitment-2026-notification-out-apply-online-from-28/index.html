About BEL

Bharat Electronics Limited (BEL) is a Govt. of India Enterprise under the Ministry of Defence. BEL Selection Process 2026

Computer Based Test (CBT), with a weightage of 85 marks Interview, with a weightage of 15 marks Candidates who meet the qualifying criteria and whose online applications are accepted are provisionally shortlisted for the CBT. They must log on to the BEL website with their credentials to download and print the CBT call letter, and follow the instructions in it. Call letters will not be sent by e-mail or by post. Candidates will not be reimbursed the cost of travel to the CBT. Candidates are provisionally shortlisted for interview on the basis of their CBT performance, in the ratio of 1:5, discipline-wise and category-wise. The list of shortlisted candidates will be published on the BEL website through query mode. The final selection is based on performance in both the CBT and the interview. BEL Exam Pattern 2026

Each question carries 1 mark. 1/4th of a mark is deducted for each wrong answer. No marks are given for unattempted or marked-for-review questions. The question paper is bilingual (English and Hindi only). If the Hindi version has an error or variation, the English version will be treated as valid. The minimum qualifying marks are 35% for General/OBC/EWS candidates and 30% for SC/ST/PwBD candidates. Candidates should choose 5 test centres nearest to their place of correspondence. Centres are allotted based on availability, and no request to change the test centre, city or state will be allowed. The list is tentative and not exhaustive. BEL reserves the right to change the centre, city or state based on the number of candidates and the availability of centres.

BEL Probationary Engineer Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Keep these ready: a valid personal e-mail ID and mobile number (both must stay valid for the whole recruitment process), a scanned passport-size colour photograph, a scanned signature in JPG/JPEG format and the other documents required by the advertisement.
  • 2Click the "Click here" link in front of "To Register" to start the online registration for BEL.
  • 3Step-I (Registration): fill in the Personal Details and Contact Details, press "Generate OTP", and enter the two OTPs sent to your mobile number and e-mail ID.
  • 4Tick the "I Agree" box after the Declaration, click "Re-verify", check the details and press "Submit". Your Application Sequence No. (User ID) and Password will be sent to your e-mail ID and mobile number.
  • 5Step-II (Application): click "Go To Application", or log in again with your User ID and Password. Then fill in the Personal Details, Additional Details, Communication Details, Qualification Details and Declaration, and choose 5 CBT centres.
  • 6Upload the relevant documents, including your photograph and signature. Click "Preview", correct any error and then click "Submit". Details cannot be edited after submission.
  • 7On the SBI e-Pay Lite payment gateway, check the details and pay the fee if your category has to pay. You will then return to your application form. Keep the Payment Acknowledgement Slip.
  • 8Press "PRINT" and save or print the application form as a PDF. The print option will not be available after the last date of online submission.
  • 9Photograph: recent passport-size colour photo with a white background, not more than 3 months old; .jpg or .jpeg format; 100 KB to 200 KB; scanner at 200 dpi; 3.5 cm (width) x 4.5 cm (height). Photos with a cap, hat or dark glasses are not accepted. Religious headwear is allowed but must not cover the face. Use the same photograph throughout the recruitment process.
  • 10Signature: sign on white paper with a black ink pen. Only the applicant may sign. Scan only the signature area, not the whole page, in .jpg or .jpeg format, 80 KB to 150 KB.
  • 11Age proof: a Matriculation/Higher Secondary certificate showing the date of birth, or a Birth certificate from the competent authority if that certificate does not show the date of birth.
  • 12EWS: an EWS Certificate / Income and Asset Certificate based on income for FY 2025-2026 and issued on or after 01.04.2026. The applicant's photo on it must be pasted, signed and stamped by the issuing authority.
  • 13OBC (NCL): a caste certificate issued by the competent authority on or after 01.10.2025, in the latest prescribed format for posts under the Government of India/Central PSUs. It must show the date of issue and the caste name spelt exactly as in the Central List. Candidates in the "Creamy Layer" must apply as Unreserved candidates.
  • 14SC/ST: a caste certificate in the latest format prescribed by the Government of India, showing the date of issue and the caste name. The caste or tribe must appear in the Central List of SC/ST of the respective state.
  • 15PwBD: a PwBD certificate in the latest format for the relevant category of disability, showing a disability of at least 40%. Conditions "likely to improve" under the temporary disability category are not eligible for reservation.
  • 16Ex-Servicemen: Annexure-3 in the prescribed format, signed by the competent authority.
  • 17At the interview (if shortlisted): the CGPA/GPI/SGPA to percentage conversion certificate/formula, where it applies, and a NOC from the current employer for candidates working in Government Departments / PSUs / Autonomous Bodies.
  • 18At document verification: the relevant valid documents in original.
