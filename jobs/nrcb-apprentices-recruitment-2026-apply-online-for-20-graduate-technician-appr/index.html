ICAR-NRC Banana Apprentice Selection Process

Engagement of both Graduate and Technician Apprentices is made purely on merit , based on the aggregate marks obtained by the candidate in their Graduate or Diploma examination, respectively. Preference is given to children of employees of the host establishment (VPA) – specifically, children of deceased employees, medically invalidated and superannuated employees, and serving employees, respectively. The final rank list is prepared category-wise (General / SC / ST / OBC) after certificate verification, conducted jointly by the Board of Apprenticeship Training (SR) and officials of ICAR – NRC for Banana, Tiruchirappalli.

Documents Required

B.Sc./B.C.A./B.A./Diploma Provisional Certificate or Consolidated Mark Sheet, showing percentage of marks SC/ST/OBC/PWD reservation certificate, in the Government-prescribed standard format, if claiming reservation (OBC candidates must submit a certificate from the certificate-issuing Authority/Tahsildar, if authorized) Secondary (or equivalent) educational certificate showing the candidate's name exactly as it should appear in the application; if the name has since changed, legally accepted documentary proof must be submitted at the time of interview Note: If reservation-category proof is not provided, the candidate's claim for reservation will be considered as 'General' category only.
