1paragraphMaharshi Dayanand University (MDU), Rohtak has released the theory date sheet for special examinations to be held in August 2026 for B.Tech 2nd Semester (H-Scheme) and B.Tech 4th Semester (G-Scheme) students. This special date sheet is meant only for students who could not appear in the May/June 2026 examinations because of their participation in NCC (National Cadet Corps) activities. The date sheet was issued by the Controller of Examinations on 24.07.2026.

5Important Instructions for Candidates
Sr.TypeTextStyleItems
1paragraphMDU has issued the following instructions along with the date sheet:
2listordered
  • Any modification(s) in the date sheet due to a clash of papers, etc., will be notified on the university website. Students must keep themselves updated by regularly checking the official notification page.
  • Students must report at the examination centre at least one hour before the commencement of the examination.
  • No student carrying a mobile phone or any electronic gadget will be allowed to sit in the examination.
  • Before answering the question paper, candidates must ensure they have been given the correct question paper. Complaints about an incorrect question paper will not be entertained after the examination has concluded.
  • The use of a simple calculator is allowed. Exchanging a calculator, eraser, pen, pencil, etc. with other candidates during the exam is not allowed.
