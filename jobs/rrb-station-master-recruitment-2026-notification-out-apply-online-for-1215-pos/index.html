RRB Station Master Medical Standard 2026

Station Master candidates must satisfy the A-2 medical standard , including strict unaided vision requirements. LASIK Surgery Restriction: According to the supplied CEN, candidates who have undergone LASIK surgery or another surgical procedure to correct refractive error are not eligible for the Station Master post. Candidates shortlisted for CBAT must produce the prescribed original medical vision certificate as per Annexure-VI or Annexure-VIA, whichever applies. Candidates without the required certificate will not be permitted to appear in CBAT. Failure to meet the A-2 medical requirements may result in rejection, and no alternative appointment will be offered for medical unsuitability.

RRB Station Master Selection Process 2026

The selection process includes a single-stage Computer Based Test (CBT), Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT), Document Verification and Medical Examination . Computer Based Test: Eligible candidates appear in the objective examination, and normalized CBT scores are used for CBAT shortlisting. Computer Based Aptitude Test: Candidates shortlisted from the CBT must qualify in each prescribed CBAT test battery. Document Verification: Candidates are called according to combined CBT and CBAT merit, Railway preferences and vacancies. Medical Examination: Candidates must meet the prescribed A-2 medical fitness standard. CBAT shortlisting numbers may increase or decrease according to the requirements of the Railway Administration.

RRB Station Master CBAT 2026

The Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT) is mandatory for candidates shortlisted through CBT. Candidates must secure a minimum T-score of 42 in each CBAT test battery , without category-wise relaxation. Candidates must qualify in every test battery to be considered for the final merit list. There is no relaxation in the minimum T-score for SC, ST, OBC-NCL, EWS or Ex-Servicemen candidates.

RRB Station Master Recruitment 2026 Important Instructions

Only one application to one selected RRB is permitted. Candidates must possess the required educational qualification by 25 November 2026. The A-2 medical standard is mandatory for Station Master. Candidates who have undergone LASIK or other refractive-error correction surgery are ineligible under the supplied medical conditions. CBAT requires a minimum T-score of 42 in each test battery. The original prescribed vision certificate must be produced at the CBAT stage. Final merit gives 70% weightage to CBT and 30% to CBAT. Applicants should use their own active mobile number and email address. Wrong or false application details may lead to rejection or cancellation of candidature. Examination dates, city details and admit cards will be announced through the official RRB websites. The number of notified vacancies is provisional and may change according to Railway requirements.
