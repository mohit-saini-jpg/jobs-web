About ICAR-IASRI

ICAR-Indian Agricultural Statistics Research Institute is an ICAR institute at Library Avenue, Pusa, New Delhi. This notice fills positions on projects running at the Institute: a Network Project on Agricultural Bioinformatics and Computational Biology under CABin Scheme, and the DBT-funded Centre for Bioinformatics and Computational Biology in Agriculture-BIC. The appointments are contractual and have no link to regular ICAR service.

The notice lists 16 posts in total across five designations. It does not print a category-wise break-up. The notice says the total number of vacancies may vary depending on other project requirements, if any, on the date of the interview.

Pay and Tenure

The notice does not print a separate pay line for Young Professional-II(IT). Selection and Interview

Depending on the number of applications received, shortlisting of candidates may be done, and a maximum of 10 times per post will be called for interview. After scrutiny of applications, eligible candidates will be informed of the time, link and other instructions by email before the interview. The interview will be held via video conferencing on 20 November 2026 from 10:30 AM onwards. Candidates can give the interview in English or Hindi. The Institute will not be responsible for any internet or connectivity issues. Further updates about the interview will be uploaded on the Institute's website.

ICAR-IASRI Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in ICAR-IASRI Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 16 posts: Research Associate 3, Senior Research Fellow 4, Young Professional-II(IT) 3, Young Professional-II 4 and Project Associate-1 2. The total may vary depending on other project requirements on the interview date.
Q2What is the last date to apply for ICAR-IASRI posts?
A
The last date is 15 November 2026, 05:30 PM. Applications received after that time will not be considered for further processing.
Q3How do I apply for ICAR-IASRI Recruitment 2026?
A
Email your resume and all listed documents, including filled Annexure-I and Annexure-II or III, as a single PDF to [email protected] by 05:30 PM on 15 November 2026.
Q4What is the qualification for Research Associate and Senior Research Fellow?
A
Research Associate needs a Ph.D, or a Master's with 3 years of research experience and one SCI/NAAS rated (>=4.0) paper, in the listed fields. Senior Research Fellow needs a Master's with a 4 or 5 year Bachelor's, or a basic-sciences Master's with NET and 2 years of research experience, or a Ph.D.
Q5What is the age limit for ICAR-IASRI Recruitment 2026?
A
Research Associate: not more than 40 years. Senior Research Fellow and Project Associate-1: not more than 35 years. Young Professional-II: not less than 21 and not more than 45 years. Age is counted as on the date of the interview.
Q6What is the salary for ICAR-IASRI Research Associate and SRF posts?
A
Research Associate gets consolidated Rs.61,000 per month for Master's holders and Rs.67,000 for Ph.D holders, plus HRA. Senior Research Fellow gets Rs.37,000 plus HRA for the 1st and 2nd year and Rs.42,000 plus HRA from the 3rd year.
Q7What is the pay for Young Professional-II and Project Associate-1?
A
Young Professional-II gets consolidated Rs.42,000 per month. Project Associate-1 gets Rs.31,000 plus HRA for NET/GATE or national-level examination selected scholars, and Rs.25,000 plus HRA for others.
Q8Is HRA paid on top of the pay?
A
HRA is mentioned for Research Associate and Senior Research Fellow as per central government norms, and for Project Associate-1 as per DBT/funding agency norms. The notice mentions no HRA for Young Professional-II.
Q9Who gets age relaxation in ICAR-IASRI Recruitment 2026?
A
For Research Associate and Senior Research Fellow, SC/ST/women candidates get 5 years and OBC candidates get 3 years. For Young Professional-II, SC/ST candidates get 5 years and OBC candidates 3 years.
Q10Is the ICAR-IASRI job permanent, and how is the interview held?
A
No, the positions are contractual and co-terminus with the project, initially for one year or till project duration, whichever is earlier. The interview is by video conferencing on 20 November 2026 from 10:30 AM.
