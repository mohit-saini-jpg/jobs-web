WBPSC Clerkship Eligibility Criteria

The qualification for direct recruitment is as follows: Passed the Madhyamik examination of the West Bengal Board of Secondary Education or its equivalent. Elementary knowledge of computer operation, with the ability to type on a computer at 20 words per minute in English or 10 words per minute in Bengali. Candidates must choose the typing option (English or Bengali) at the time of submitting the application. The computer and typing ability is judged only after a candidate qualifies in Part-I and Part-II. The candidate must be a citizen of India (natural or by registration), or another national declared eligible by the Government of India. A candidate in the second category needs a certificate of eligibility issued by the Government; such a candidate may be admitted to the examination, but the offer of appointment is made only after the certificate is issued. WBPSC Clerkship Age Limit

The age limit for WBPSC Clerkship Recruitment 2026 is not below 18 years and not more than 45 years as on 01.01.2024. In terms of date of birth, candidates should be born not earlier than 2 January 1979 and not later than 1 January 2006. Only the age recorded in the Madhyamik or equivalent certificate is accepted, and the limits apply to all candidates, whether in Government service or not. No claim for being a member of S.C./S.T./O.B.C. (Non-Creamy Layer)/PwBD/MSP/Ex-Servicemen is entertained after the application is submitted.

WBPSC Clerkship Selection Process

The selection under WBPSC Clerkship Recruitment 2026 has two examination stages followed by a computer and typing test. Part-I (Objective Type): held first. A limited number of candidates who obtain qualifying marks are allowed to appear in Part-II. Part-II (Conventional Type – Written): held on a later date to be notified by the Commission. Test of computer operation and typing: judged only for candidates found qualified in Part-I and Part-II. The final merit list is prepared on the basis of total marks obtained in Part-I and Part-II, provided the candidate qualifies in the computer operation and typing test. A candidate who qualifies for Part-II but does not appear in it will not be considered for the final merit list. WBPSC Clerkship Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is the conducting body for the WBPSC Lower Division Assistant / Lower Division Clerk Recruitment 2026?
A
The recruitment process is administered by the Public Service Commission, West Bengal (WBPSC) for selecting eligible candidates for lower division assistant and lower division clerk posts in Kolkata and across the state.
Q2When does the online application process start for WBPSC LDA/LDC 2026?
A
The online application process officially commences on 06-10-2026. Interested candidates can submit their applications through the official website until the designated closing date.
Q3What is the last date to apply for the WBPSC Lower Division Assistant / Lower Division Clerk posts?
A
The last date for the submission of the online application form as well as the closing date for fee submission is 26-10-2026. Candidates must complete their submissions before this deadline.
Q4What is the minimum educational qualification required for this recruitment?
A
Applicants must have passed the Madhyamik examination of the West Bengal Board of Secondary Education or its equivalent. Additionally, they need computer operation knowledge and a typing speed of 20 wpm in English or 10 wpm in Bengali.
Q5What is the age limit for applying to the WBPSC LDA/LDC position?
A
Candidates must be between 18 and 45 years of age as on 01.01.2024. Age relaxations are applicable for reserved categories such as SC, ST, OBC, and PwBD as per West Bengal government rules.
Q6What is the salary structure offered for the Lower Division Clerk post?

Selected candidates will receive a salary under Level 6, ranging from Rs. 22,700/- to 58,500/- per month, along with additional allowances like Dearness Allowance (D.A.), Medical Allowance (M.A.), and House Rent Allowance (H.R.A.).
Q7Is there any application fee for the WBPSC recruitment examination?

Q8What is the selection procedure for WBPSC Lower Division Assistant / LDC?

The selection process comprises a Part-I Examination (Objective Type), a Part-II Examination (Conventional Type – Written), followed by a test to judge elementary knowledge in computer operation and typing speed.
