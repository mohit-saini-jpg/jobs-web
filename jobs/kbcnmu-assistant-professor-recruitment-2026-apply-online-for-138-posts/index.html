KBCNMU Assistant Professor Eligibility Criteria

Eligibility for the post of Assistant Professor is governed by U.G.C., State Government and AICTE rules, and the applicable rule set depends on the subject code applied for. The notification groups the subject codes as follows. For these posts the qualification stated in the notification is: A Master's degree with 55% marks (or an equivalent grade in a point-scale wherever the grading system is followed) in a concerned/relevant/allied subject from an Indian University, or an equivalent degree from an accredited foreign university. Besides fulfilling the above qualification, the candidate must have cleared the National Eligibility Test (NET) conducted by the UGC or the CSIR, or a similar test accredited by the UGC such as SET, or must have been awarded a Ph.D. degree in accordance with the University Grants Commission (Minimum Standards and Procedure for Award of M.Phil./Ph.D. Degree) Reg

The notification does not specify a minimum or maximum age limit for general applicants. KBCNMU Assistant Professor Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Applicants shall apply through online mode only, from 12.09.2026 to 28.09.2026, through the website link of the university. The online step must be followed by submission of a hard copy.
  • 2Visit the university recruitment portal link given in the notification: https//apps.nmu.ac.in/recruitment/ (printed in the notification without the colon after "https").
  • 3Complete the online application form for the post and subject code applied for.
  • 4Pay the application fee of Rs. 750/- (open category) or Rs. 375/- (reserved category) by online mode only.
  • 5Take a printout of the submitted online form.
  • 6Submit the hard copy of the online form along with testimonials in two copies and the online payment receipt, on or before 05.10.2026 up to 5.00 p.m.
  • 7An applicant who wishes to be considered for more than one post should apply separately for each post.
  • 8Applicants already employed should submit the hard copy of their application through proper channel.
  • 9Applications received after the last date, and/or not submitted through proper channel, and/or incomplete applications will not be considered.
  • 10The university will not be responsible for postal delay of the hard copy, if any.
  • 11Applicants are required to attach certificates of date of birth, educational qualifications, caste certificate, non-creamy layer certificate, caste validity certificate (if obtained), experience, research experience, etc.
  • 12A candidate furnishing incorrect or false information shall stand disqualified at any stage.
  • 13The University Authority reserves the right to fill up or not to fill up the posts, to modify/alter/cancel the advertisement, or change the number of posts. The University Authority also reserves the right to extend the services of a candidate for the next academic year after assessing performance during the previous academic year, vide Government Resolution dated 09.02.2018.
