Registration Year and Syllabus Rules

  • Regular candidates registered in 2025 and Ex-Regular candidates registered up to 2024 (except those registered in 2023) will appear in AHS Examination 2027 with the syllabus meant for students registered in 2025.
  • Students registered in 2023 will also appear with the same syllabus in all subjects, except the subjects specifically mentioned for 2023 registration in the programme.
  • Business Studies & Management (Commerce) and BFC Business Studies & Management (Vocational) have a new Unit-IV, "Consumer Protection", for students registered in 2025.
  • For BSM papers (codes 572 and 804), the Regular paper is for candidates registered in 2025 and the Ex-Regular paper is for those registered up to 2024.

Vocational Stream Trade Papers (Paper-III and Paper-IV)

Paper-III is on 12 March 2027 and Paper-IV is on 18 March 2027. Papers with a full mark of 40 run from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM, and papers with a full mark of 50 run from 10:00 AM to 12:30 PM. The notification lists 48 trade subjects with their Paper-III and Paper-IV codes in Annexure-1, for example Accounting & Auditing (574/575), Computer Technique (541/542), Dairying (505/506), Office Management (582/583), Tax Assistant (586/587) and Drone Pilot (113/114). Candidates should check Annexure-1 of the official notification for their own trade code.

Biology Exam Pattern for Science Stream

Biology Section-A (Botany) and Section-B (Zoology) are held in a single sitting on 11 March 2027 for both Regular and Ex-Regular candidates. Botany is given at 10:00 AM. After the Botany answer booklets are collected, Zoology is given at 11:50 AM. This leaves a gap of 20 minutes between the two papers.

Distance Education (Correspondence) Students

Students under Distance Education will appear in their respective subjects and papers in the sitting and on the date mentioned for the Arts and Commerce streams.

How to Download the CHSE Odisha Plus 2 Exam Routine 2027 PDF

  • Visit the official CHSE Odisha website, www.chseodisha.nic.in.
  • Look for the notice or notification section.
  • Open the link for the Annual Higher Secondary Examination 2027 programme.
  • Download and save the PDF, and check your stream, subject code and registration year.

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Reach the examination centre 30 minutes before the exam starts.
  • Enter the examination room or hall 15 minutes before the exam to take your allotted seat.
  • Check that you are sitting on the seat allotted to you before the exam begins.
  • The examinations will be conducted under CCTV surveillance, and Centre Superintendents must keep CCTV working in all halls throughout every sitting.
  • Internal Assessment, Project and Practical exams will be held as per the programme even if any date is later declared a holiday.
  • Internal Assessment question papers are prepared by the concerned subject teachers, following the CHSE notifications 1805 dated 10.05.2024 and 3416 dated 21.09.2024 for candidates registered up to 2025.
  • Project work must be handwritten by the student within 6 A4 pages on a topic from the prescribed syllabus, as assigned by the subject teacher.
  • For Commerce project evaluation and viva-voce, groups are formed with a minimum of 24 students. One group's evaluation is completed in two hours by the institution's own subject teacher, with no external examiner.
  • Report any omission or error in the programme to the Controller of Examinations immediately.
