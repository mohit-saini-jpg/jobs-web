AIIMS Bilaspur Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS), Bilaspur, Himachal Pradesh has 73 faculty posts open: 20 Professor, 7 Additional Professor, 13 Associate Professor, 30 Assistant Professor, 2 Assistant Professor (Contractual) and 1 Lecturer in Nursing (Assistant Professor). Pay runs from Entry Level Pay Matrix 14A to 11 under the 7th CPC. About AIIMS Bilaspur

AIIMS Bilaspur is described in the notice as an apex health care institute established by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India, under the Pradhan Mantri Swasthya Suraksha Yojana (PMSSY). Its stated aim is correcting regional imbalances in quality tertiary level health care and attaining self-sufficiency in graduate and postgraduate medical education and training. The Executive Director invites applications from Indian nationals and persons registered as Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) cardholders under Section 7(A) of the Citizenship Act, 1955.

Department-wise Vacancies

The total is 73 posts (20 + 7 + 13 + 30 + 2 + 1). The notice calls the vacancies provisional and subject to variation, and the institute may increase, decrease or withdraw any post. Reserved vacancies follow Government of India rules. The notice adds that if an Additional Professor or Associate Professor is not available in the concerned department, the post can be downgraded and operated at the level of Assistant Professor in the respective category. The appointment on a downgraded post will be purely contractual. For example, an advertised Additional Professor (OBC) post may be filled by an Assistant Professor (OBC) on contractual basis only.

Qualification and Experience

Those holding DNB in broad or super specialties must produce documentary evidence from the competent authority of DNB equivalence with MD/MS/DM/MCh, as per the MCI, New Delhi notification dated 31-10-2018. Those who underwent DNB training at institutions under para 2 at (a) and (b) of that notification need no extra experience after the qualifying degree. Those who trained at institutions under (c) and (d) need 01 year and 02 years extra experience respectively, i.e. total 04 and 05 years' experience from an MCI recognized/permitted institution. All eligibility criteria must be met on or before 21 December 2026, the crucial date for age limit and experience. Where experience is prescribed, it is counted after obtaining the prescribed qualification. Failure to fulfil the criteria leads to rejection. Pay and Terms

All pay is as per the 7th CPC with all usual allowances. NPA is added for candidates possessing medical qualification; for the Lecturer in Nursing, allowances apply if applicable. The notice prints no separate pay for the Assistant Professor – Contractual posts. Retired faculty are appointed on contract as consultants at a fixed consolidated monthly remuneration, arrived at by deducting basic pension from the pay drawn at retirement. The amount stays unchanged for the term of the contract, with no annual increment or percentage increase. Their other terms follow MoHFW's OM no. Z-28016/195/2017/-PMSSY-IV dated 16.11.2017. They may also be entitled to residential accommodation subject to availability, as per the institute's policy and applicable deductions. AIIMS Bilaspur Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection runs through scrutiny of applications, shortlisting where needed, and a personal interview before the Standing Selection Committee (SSC) of the institute. Applications received within the prescribed date and time, with the fee, are scrutinised by the institute's scrutiny committee. Only candidates found eligible are called for the personal interview. Meeting the eligibility criteria alone may not confer the right to be called. Eligibility is decided on experience in the immediate feeder post, experience in patient care, teaching/training and research, number and quality of publications and publication metrics, and administrative experience. If applicants are numerous, the selection committee may devise further criteria and a shortlisting procedure, which may also include a written examination if necessary. AIIMS Bilaspur Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in AIIMS Bilaspur Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 73 posts: 20 Professor, 7 Additional Professor, 13 Associate Professor, 30 Assistant Professor, 2 Assistant Professor (Contractual) and 1 Lecturer in Nursing (Assistant Professor). The notice calls them provisional and subject to variation.
Q2What is the last date to apply?
A
The hard copy of the application must reach AIIMS Bilaspur by 21 December 2026, 5:00 PM. The Google form closes earlier, on 14 December 2026 at 5:00 PM.
Q3What is the qualification for the Lecturer in Nursing post?
A
The candidate needs a Masters Degree in Nursing from a recognized Institution/University, must be a Registered Nurse Midwife, and must have five years' experience with a minimum of two years teaching experience in Nursing.
Q4What is the age limit?
A
For Professor and Additional Professor it is not exceeding 58 years in direct recruitment, 56 years for deputation and 70 years for retired faculty. For Associate Professor, Assistant Professor and Lecturer in Nursing it is not exceeding 50 years, with 70 years for retired faculty applying as Associate Professor. Age is counted on 21 December 2026.
Q5What is the salary?
A
Professor is at Pay Matrix 14A (Basic 1,68,900-2,20,400) and Assistant Professor at Level 12 (Basic 1,01,500-1,67,400). Additional Professor, Associate Professor and Lecturer in Nursing are at Levels 13A2, 13A1 and 11 respectively, with usual allowances and NPA for medical qualification holders.
Q6What is the application fee?
A
SC/ST/PwBD candidates are exempted. Other categories pay Rs. 2,000 plus 18% GST, i.e. Rs. 2,360, by online NEFT only; UPI is not accepted and the fee is non-refundable.
Q7How do I apply for AIIMS Bilaspur Recruitment 2026?
A
Pay the fee, submit the Google form by 14 December 2026, then send the signed hard copy with self-attested documents to the Deputy Director (Administration), AIIMS Bilaspur, so that it reaches by 21 December 2026, 5:00 PM.
Q8How are candidates selected?
A
Applications are scrutinised and eligible candidates are called for a personal interview, including a presentation of up to 5 minutes. If applicants are numerous, the committee may shortlist by further criteria, which may include a written examination.
Q9Can retired faculty apply, and how is their pay fixed?
A
Retired faculty can apply for Professor, Additional Professor and Associate Professor. They are engaged on contract for an initial two years, with a fixed consolidated monthly remuneration worked out by deducting basic pension from the pay drawn at retirement.
Q10Can I apply for more than one post?
A
Yes, but a separate application must be submitted for each post applied for.
