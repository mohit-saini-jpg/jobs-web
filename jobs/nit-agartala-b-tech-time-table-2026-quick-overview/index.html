NIT Agartala B.Tech Time Table 2026 - Quick Overview : Admit Card Overview

The odd semester end-term examinations 2026-27 begin on 26 November 2026. The B.Tech 1st semester exams (NIT Agartala) end on 4 December 2026, and the IIIT Agartala 3rd, 5th and 7th semester exams run until 7 December 2026. Each exam is 2 hours long, and students must report 30 minutes before the exam starts.

NIT Agartala B.Tech Time Table 2026 - Quick Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 12/11/2026

The Office of the Dean Academic, Barjala, Agartala, issued the official examination schedule on 9 November 2026. It covers B.Tech 1st semester (Group I, Group II and BT MT), BS MS Dual Degree 1st semester, and IIIT Agartala 3rd, 5th and 7th semester students. Exams are held in two shifts, 10.00 AM to 12.00 PM and 2.00 PM to 4.00 PM, depending on the semester.

Reserve Days and Campus Departure Rule

  • NIT Agartala notice: 27.11.2026, 02.12.2026 and 05.12.2026 are reserve days.
  • IIIT Agartala notice: 02.12.2026 and 05.12.2026 are reserve days.
  • Students who do not have an examination on 07.12.2026 are permitted to leave the campus on 06.12.2026.

Important Instructions

  • Report to your exam venue at least 30 minutes before the exam timing.
  • Check the shift for your semester: 10.00 AM to 12.00 PM or 2.00 PM to 4.00 PM.
  • Confirm your section and venue from the venue table above or the notice board.
  • Each exam lasts 2 hours.
