Jadavpur University Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Jadavpur University, Kolkata, will hold a walk-in interview on 18 November 2026 at the Director's Room of the School of Materials Science and Nanotechnology, Jadavpur University. The interviews are at 11.30 am for Project Technical Support - I, 1.30 pm for Project Technical Support - III and 3 pm for Project Research Scientist II (Non-Medical), one post each, with a fellowship of Rs. 18,000/-, Rs. 28,000/- and Rs. 67,000/- respectively, plus HRA (as admissible). Candidates bring the prescribed application form (price Rs. 50/-) from the University's Information Section.

About the ICMR Project

Jadavpur University is a university in Kolkata. All three posts are under the project entitled 'Optically Detected Magnetic Resonance (ODMR)-based Quantum Theranostic Platform for Rapid Detection, Antibiotic Susceptibility Testing & Therapeutic Modulation in Sepsis Management and AMR', funded by ICMR under Collaborative Research (CCR) Scheme: Year 2025-2026, Govt. of India, vide Project Id Epms. CAR-2026-04-000012. The work is under the supervision of Dr. Chandan Kumar Ghosh (PI), School of Materials Science and Nanotechnology, Jadavpur University. The three posts were notified in three separate notices, each dated 02.11.2026 and signed by the Registrar (Actg.).

Posts and Eligibility

For the full wording of each post's eligibility, candidates can check the official notification for that post. The pay is called a fellowship in the notice. Each post carries HRA (as admissible) in addition to the fixed fellowship amount. The notice states the tenure for all three posts as: "5 years on termination of the project on the basis of satisfactory performance." The notice does not mention any other allowance, and states that no TA/DA is admissible for the interview.

What to Bring

The prescribed application form (Price Rs. 50/-) is to be obtained from the Information Section of the University on all working days. At the time of the interview, candidates must produce: Duly filled application form(s) Original certificates, mark sheets and testimonials Self-attested copies of the certificates, mark sheets and testimonials

What Happens on the Day

The type of selection for all three posts is Walk-in-Interview, held on 18 November 2026 in the Director's Room of the School of Materials Science and Nanotechnology, Jadavpur University. Each post has its own time: Project Technical Support - I at 11.30 am, Project Technical Support - III at 1.30 pm and Project Research Scientist II (Non-Medical) at 3 pm. The notice does not mention a written test.

Q1When is the Jadavpur University walk-in interview for the project posts?
A
The walk-in interview is on 18 November 2026: at 11.30 am for Project Technical Support - I, 1.30 pm for Project Technical Support - III and 3 pm for Project Research Scientist II (Non-Medical).
Q2Where is the walk-in interview held?
A
All three interviews are held at the Director's Room of the School of Materials Science and Nanotechnology, Jadavpur University.
Q3How many vacancies are there?
A
There are 3 vacancies in total: One (01) Project Research Scientist II (Non-Medical), One (01) Project Technical Support - III and One (01) Project Technical Support - I.
Q4What qualification is required?
A
Project Research Scientist II (Non-Medical) needs a Ph.D in Science or Engineering, Project Technical Support - III needs an M.Sc in chemistry, and Project Technical Support - I needs a B.Sc in Zoology + One year experience.
Q5What is the age limit?
A
The age limit is not more than 40 years for Project Research Scientist II (Non-Medical) and not more than 30 years for both Project Technical Support posts, as on the date of advertisement. SC/ST/OBC/WOMEN candidates get relaxation as per Govt. of India Rules.
Q6What is the salary for these posts?
A
The fellowship is Rs. 67,000/- for Project Research Scientist II (Non-Medical), Rs. 28,000/- for Project Technical Support - III and Rs. 18,000/- for Project Technical Support - I.
Q7Is HRA paid on top of the fellowship?
A
Yes. Each post's fellowship is plus HRA (as admissible). No TA/DA is admissible for attending the interview.
Q8Where do I get the application form and what does it cost?

The prescribed application form costs Rs. 50/- and is obtained from the Information Section of the University on all working days.
Q9What documents should I bring to the interview?
A
Bring the duly filled application form(s) and the originals along with self-attested copies of certificates, mark sheets and testimonials.
Q10Is this a permanent job?

No. The posts are under an ICMR-funded project, and the notice states the tenure as "5 years on termination of the project on the basis of satisfactory performance."
