How to Check the UPMSP Class 9 and 11 Schedule

  • Visit the official UPMSP website at upmsp.edu.in.
  • Look for the "Important Information and Downloads" or notice section on the homepage.
  • Click on the notice related to the Class 9 and 11 annual exam schedule or academic calendar 2026- 27.
  • Download the PDF and check with your respective school for the confirmed local schedule.

Important Instructions

  • Since Class 9 and 11 exams are conducted at the school level, students should confirm exact subject-wise timings with their own institution.
  • Students should carry any ID or admit card required by their school for the exam.
  • Any further revisions to the schedule will be communicated by UPMSP or individual schools - always cross-check with the official website for the latest update.
