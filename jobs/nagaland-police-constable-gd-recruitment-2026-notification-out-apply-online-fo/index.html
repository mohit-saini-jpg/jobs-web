Nagaland Police Constable Medical Fitness Requirements

Candidates must satisfy the medical fitness standards prescribed for police service. Medical examination includes assessment of height, weight, chest, vision, hearing and physical fitness. The notification identifies several disqualifying conditions, including: Eyesight below the prescribed standard. Colour blindness. Deafness or serious hearing impairment. Varicose veins. Squint eyes. Amputated limbs or physical extremities. Physical deformities, including flat feet and knocked knees. Adverse drug screening or alcohol abuse test findings. Candidates must also satisfy the medical and physical requirements during the recruitment process and subsequent training.

Nagaland Police Constable Probation and Posting

Selected candidates will undergo a probation period of two years. Service confirmation will depend on satisfactory completion of probation, medical clearance and verification of antecedents under the applicable rules. Fresh recruits will initially be deployed in India Reserve (IR) Battalions and may be required to serve within or outside Nagaland according to the instructions of the Nagaland Police Department.
