ULB Haryana Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

There are 87 municipalities (11 Municipal Corporations, 25 Municipal Councils, and 51 Municipal Committees) under the supervision of the Urban Local Bodies Department in Haryana, entrusted with providing civic amenities to the state's urban population. To strengthen taxation and sanitation functions in these municipalities, the Directorate of Urban Local Bodies, Haryana, intends to engage Taxation Experts, Taxation Associates, and Sanitation Experts/Associates having the requisite qualification and experience, on contract basis, initially for one year and further extendable up to three years.
