CIHTS Recruitment 2026 Age Limit

No general age limit is specified for most CIHTS posts. Specific age conditions apply only to the following posts: Post Code 26091, BRA Research Professor (Tenure Post): Age below 65 years. Post Code 26403, Assistant Professor, Sowa-Rigpa (NCISM-2022): Age must not exceed 45 years at the time of first appointment. Relaxation in the percentage of marks for SC/ST/OBC (NCL)/PwBD candidates is provided in accordance with UGC/GOI/CIHTS rules, and the Institute reserves the right to adopt relaxation and/or upgradation of qualification/experience/age at any stage, as recommended by the Screening/Selection Committee.

CIHTS Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Merely fulfilling the minimum eligibility requirements does not entitle a candidate to be called for interview; CIHTS reserves the right to restrict the number of candidates called for interview. The list of shortlisted candidates will be displayed on the CIHTS website. Registration is provisional, as the eligibility of shortlisted candidates will be verified at the time of Interview/Written Test from original documents. A registered candidate's candidature is liable to be rejected at any stage of the recruitment process, or even after joining, if any information provided is found false or not in conformity with the eligibility criteria. In-service candidates should apply through proper channel and submit a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from their employer with the application, or at the time of interview/written test if shortlisted. CIHTS Teaching Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is CIHTS Recruitment 2026?
A
It is a recruitment drive by the Central Institute of Higher Tibetan Studies, Sarnath, Varanasi, for 59 teaching posts of Professor, Associate Professor, and Assistant Professor across 40 subject/post combinations, under Advertisement No. CIHTS/Acad./Rectt./01/2026.
Q2How many vacancies are available in CIHTS Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 59 total vacancies: 27 UR, 9 SC, 4 ST, 15 OBC, and 4 EWS, distributed across Professor, Associate Professor and Assistant Professor posts in various departments.
Q3What is the last date to apply for CIHTS Recruitment 2026?
A
The online application window is open from 08 September 2026 to 07 October 2026. The hard copy of the application must reach the Registrar within 3 days of the online submission deadline.
Q4What is the qualification for CIHTS Recruitment 2026?
A
Qualification varies by post and subject - generally a Ph.D. with research publications and teaching experience for Professor and Associate Professor posts, and a Master's degree with NET/SLET/SET (or a qualifying Ph.D.) for most Assistant Professor posts, with distinct requirements for Sowa-Rigpa, Tibetan Traditional Painting/Wood Craft, and Centre for Teacher Education posts. See the Eligibility section above for post-wise details.
Q5What is the age limit for CIHTS Recruitment 2026?
A
No general age limit is specified. The BRA Research Professor post (26091) requires candidates to be below 65 years, and the Sowa-Rigpa Assistant Professor post (26403) requires candidates not to exceed 45 years at first appointment.
Q6What is the application fee for CIHTS Recruitment 2026?
A
Rs. 1,000/- for UR/OBC/EWS candidates, payable online. Women applicants and SC/ST/PwBD candidates are exempted from the fee.
Q7How can I apply online for CIHTS Recruitment 2026?
A
Eligible candidates must apply online through cihtsrec.samarth.edu.in between 08.09.2026 and 07.10.2026, and additionally send a signed hard copy of the application with self-attested enclosures by registered post to the Registrar, CIHTS, Sarnath, Varanasi-221007, within 3 days of the online submission deadline.
Q8What is the selection process for CIHTS Recruitment 2026?
A
Selection involves screening/shortlisting followed by an Interview/Written Test, with eligibility verified from original documents at that stage. Merely meeting the minimum eligibility criteria does not guarantee an interview call.
