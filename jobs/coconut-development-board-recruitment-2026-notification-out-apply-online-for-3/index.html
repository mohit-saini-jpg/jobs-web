Coconut Development Board Application Fee 2026

The application fee for Coconut Development Board Recruitment 2026 is Rs.300 for candidates who are not eligible for fee exemption. The payment must be made through SBI Collect. Applications from eligible SC, ST, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen and women candidates are exempted from the application fee. Payment Mode: SBI Collect only. The Board will not accept application fee payments through any other method.
