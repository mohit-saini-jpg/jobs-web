About the Project

ESIC Medical College & Hospital, Faridabad has advertised staff recruitment for a research project titled "Understanding the correlates of vaccine-induced immune response: an Indian cohort study involving HPV vaccines". The post is advertised for the project at ESIC MC&H, Faridabad, and is open to eligible Indian nationals. The appointment is purely temporary/contractual and carries no claim to regular employment.

Who Can Apply

The maximum age limit is 30 years. The notice marks the age limit with an asterisk; the application form has a field "Age As on 01.12.2026". The notice prints no age relaxation. Only eligible Indian nationals may apply. For the full eligibility of the post, candidates should refer to the official notification.

Pay and Tenure

The Field Worker post carries a consolidated salary of Rs. 23,400/-. The appointment is initially for 11 months and is extendable subject to satisfactory performance, continuation of the project, availability of funds and approval of the competent authority. The project duration is 2 years. No TA/DA is paid for appearing in the interview or joining the post.

Selection and Interview

Selection will be based on screening of applications, a written test (if required) and an interview by the Selection Panel. Only shortlisted candidates will be considered for interview. The date of the interview/skill test is to be notified. Mere fulfilment of eligibility criteria does not guarantee selection.
