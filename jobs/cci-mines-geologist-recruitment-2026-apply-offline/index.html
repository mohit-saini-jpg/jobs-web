About CCI Mines Geologist

Cement Corporation of India Limited describes itself in the notice as a Central Government Public Sector Enterprise engaged in the manufacturing and marketing of cement, committed to becoming a sustainable cement company. The Mines Geologist will work at the Dillai Parbat Limestone Mines of its Bokajan Cement Factory in Karbi-Anglong, Assam, where CCI proposes to engage qualified and experienced professionals on a fixed-term contract basis.
