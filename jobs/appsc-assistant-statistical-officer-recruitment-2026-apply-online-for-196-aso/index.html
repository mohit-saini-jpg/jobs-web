APPSC Assistant Statistical Officer Selection Process 2026

Selection for APPSC ASO Recruitment 2026 will be based on merit in the written examination, subject to qualifying the Computer Proficiency Test (CPT). Computer Based Recruitment Test: Candidates must appear in the objective-type written examination. Computer Proficiency Test: Provisionally shortlisted candidates must qualify the CPT. Certificate Verification: Candidates must satisfy the applicable educational, reservation, local status and other documentary requirements.

APPSC ASO Exam Pattern 2026

The APPSC Assistant Statistical Officer written examination consists of two papers carrying 300 marks in total. Each paper contains 150 questions and has a duration of 150 minutes. Negative Marking: One-third of the marks assigned to each incorrect answer will be deducted. Obtaining the prescribed minimum marks does not guarantee selection. Final selection is subject to merit, vacancies and the applicable recruitment conditions.
