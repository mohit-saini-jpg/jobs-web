Central University of Punjab Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is the last date to apply for Central University of Punjab Recruitment 2026?
A
The last date to apply differs by post: 08.08.2026 for Contractual Faculty (Pharmacy, Law, Economics); 09.08.2026 for Part-Time Faculty in History, Political Science, Geography, and Economics; and 10.08.2026 for Part-Time Faculty in Physics, Chemistry, Zoology, and Mathematics.
Q2What is the salary for Central University of Punjab Contractual Faculty posts?
A
Contractual Faculty posts in Pharmacy, Law, and Economics carry a consolidated monthly salary of Rs. 64,000/-. Part-Time Faculty posts are paid Rs. 1,000/- per lecture.
Q3How can I apply for Central University of Punjab Recruitment 2026?
A
Applications must be submitted online through the official recruitment portal at https://cuprec.samarth.edu.in.
Q4What is the mode of selection for CUPB Recruitment 2026?
A
Selection is through a walk-in interview. No separate call letters are issued; eligible candidates who applied online must directly attend the interview on the scheduled date, time, and venue.
Q5What is the application fee for Central University of Punjab Recruitment 2026?
A
The application fee is Rs. 100/-, applicable to candidates other than SC/ST/PWD/Women, who are exempted from the fee.
Q6Where will the Central University of Punjab walk-in interview be held?
A
The interview will be held at the Conference Room, VC Office, Aryabhata Academic Block, Central University of Punjab, Badal-Bathinda Road, Village Ghudda, District Bathinda, Punjab.
Q7What is the duration of the contractual faculty engagement?
A
The engagement is purely temporary and contractual, for one semester (up to dispersal of classes, i.e., 18.12.2026), or till the posts are filled on a regular basis, whichever is earlier.
Q8What documents are required at the Central University of Punjab interview?
A
Candidates must carry the printed application form, original and self-attested educational/experience certificates, caste certificate (if applicable), last pay certificate, NOC (if required), passport-size photographs, and Aadhaar card, among other supporting documents.
