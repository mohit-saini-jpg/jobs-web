DLSA Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The District Legal Services Authority (DLSA), Yadadri-Bhuvanagiri, Government of Telangana, has invited applications for 3 posts – 1 Office Assistant and 2 Office Peon – on contract basis for the Legal Aid Defence Counsel System. Only eligible candidates of Yadadri-Bhuvanagiri District may apply. The application, in the prescribed format and in a sealed cover, goes to the DLSA office at Court Buildings, Opp. New Bus Stand, Yadadri-Bhuvanagiri-508116, and must be received by 5.00 p.m. on 3 December 2026.

About DLSA Yadadri-Bhuvanagiri

The District Legal Services Authority, Yadadri-Bhuvanagiri works under the Government of Telangana from the District Court Buildings, Yadadri-Bhuvanagiri. Its Chairman is the Principal District and Sessions Judge, who signed this notification. The posts are in the Office of the Legal Aid Defence Counsel, under the Legal Aid Defence Counsel System of the DLSA.

Where and How to Send

Applications must be made in the prescribed format enclosed with the notification (the application form for the post of Office Assistant/Office Peon). The filled form, with the documents, is sent in a sealed cover to this address: District Legal Services Authority, Court Buildings, Opp. New Bus Stand, Yadadri-Bhuvanagiri-508116 The notice asks candidates to mention the post applied for. It says the application is to be "sent in sealed cover" but does not name a postal mode such as speed post or registered post. Applications received after 5.00 p.m. on 3 December 2026 will not be accepted. The application form asks for: Name of the applicant with surname (as per certificate) and father's/husband's name. Date of birth and age as on 1.7.2026 (proof to be enclosed). Educational qualifications and technical qualifications (proof to be enclosed). Postal address with PIN code and mobile number.

Posts, Eligibility and Pay

Both posts are filled on contract basis. The notice prints the remuneration as a fixed amount and does not mention any allowance.
