About Jharkhand Finance Department

The Finance Department of the Government of Jharkhand, Ranchi, runs a Project Management Unit (PMU) for its IT projects. The notice invites highly skilled technical professionals to be engaged on contract as technical expert officers to strengthen this PMU.

Pay and Terms

The gross allowance includes Transport Allowance, House Rent Allowance, Medical Allowance etc. These figures apply to new personnel. For employees retired from the Government of India or a State Government who are re-engaged on contract in these posts, the fixed pay is set as follows: A fixed monthly honorarium/pay equal to the last basic pay drawn at retirement minus the basic pension. No dearness allowance or annual increment on this fixed pay. Pension and dearness relief continue to be paid as per rules. If no government accommodation is allotted, 75 percent of the House Rent Allowance admissible for Ranchi city, based on the pay at retirement. Jharkhand Finance Department Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Candidates will be interviewed, on the basis of the applications received by the Department, by a high-level selection committee chaired by the Development Commissioner. The selected candidate will be engaged on contract by the Department on the committee's recommendation. The committee is: Development Commissioner, Jharkhand – Chairperson Additional Chief Secretary/Principal Secretary/Secretary, Finance Department, Jharkhand – Member Additional Chief Secretary/Principal Secretary/Secretary, Information Technology and e-Governance Department, Jharkhand – Member SIO, NIC, Jharkhand – Member

Jharkhand Finance Department Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in Jharkhand Finance Department Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 2 vacancies: 1 Program Director and 1 Additional Program Director in the Project Management Unit of the Finance Department.
Q2What is the last date to apply?
A
The last date for the online application is 25 December 2026, and no application will be accepted after it.
Q3What qualification is required for the Program Director and Additional Program Director posts?
A
Both posts need a B.Tech. (Computer Science/IT)/B.E. (Computer Science/IT)/M.C.A from a Government recognized University/Institute, with MBA/PGDBM as a desirable qualification.
Q4How much experience is needed?
A
A minimum of 10 years for Program Director and 7 years for Additional Program Director in a relevant field, as a Senior Management Officer from a CMMI Level 5/Forbes Global 2000 Company/Indian Unicorn Company or Government Organization.
Q5What is the salary of the Program Director and Additional Program Director?
A
The Program Director gets a salary of Rs. 2 Lakh a month plus a gross allowance of Rs. 1.5 Lakh, and the Additional Program Director gets Rs. 1.5 Lakh plus Rs. 0.75 Lakh; the allowance includes Transport, House Rent and Medical Allowance.
Q6What is the application fee?
A
The application fee is Rs. 100, paid online through the Payment Gateway on the JAPIT Recruitment Portal.
Q7How do I apply?
A
Apply online on the JAP-IT Online Recruitment Portal (https://recruitment.jharkhand.gov.in) and upload scanned copies of your matriculation, qualification and work experience certificates; other modes are not accepted.
Q8How will candidates be selected?
A
Candidates will be interviewed by a high-level selection committee chaired by the Development Commissioner, Jharkhand, and the selected candidate will be engaged on contract on its recommendation.
Q9Is the job permanent, and for how long is the contract?
A
The job is on contract for 05 years or until age 65, whichever comes first, and may be extended by 3 years at a time (up to 65) with the Departmental Minister's approval; it gives no claim to a government post.
Q10Is there any annual increment?
A
Yes. After successfully completing one year, an Annual Increment at 8 percent of the salary is admissible at the start of each subsequent year.
