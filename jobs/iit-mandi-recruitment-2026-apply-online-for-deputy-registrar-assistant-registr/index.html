IIT Mandi Recruitment 2026 - Eligibility Criteria

Deputy Registrar: Master's degree in any discipline with at least 55% marks or its equivalent Grade 'B' in the UGC 7 point scale from a recognized University/Institute. 9 years experience as Assistant Professor in Academic Pay Level-10 and above OR comparable experience in research establishment and/or other Institutions of higher education OR 5 years of administrative experience as Assistant Registrar or equivalent in pay level 10. Assistant Registrar: Master's degree in any discipline with at least 55% marks or its equivalent Grade 'B' in the UGC 7-point scale from a recognized University/Institute. Service Experience of 5 years as Superintendent in the pay level 7 or equivalent post/pay.
