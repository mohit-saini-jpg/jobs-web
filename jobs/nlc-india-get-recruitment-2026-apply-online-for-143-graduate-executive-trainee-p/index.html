NLC India GET Salary 2026

Graduate Executive Trainees will be inducted in E-2 Grade with a basic pay of Rs. 50,000 per month during their one-year training period. The approximate annual CTC is Rs. 14.50 lakh . After successfully completing the one-year training programme, GETs will be absorbed into E-3 Grade , with a pay scale of Rs. 60,000 to Rs. 1,80,000 and an initial basic pay of Rs. 60,000 per month. Other notified benefits include medical treatment for employees and eligible dependants, group insurance, applicable annual performance-related pay and residential accommodation at standard rent, subject to availability and company rules. Salary Clarification: The CTC is an approximate annual compensation figure, not monthly take-home pay. The advertised basic pay should not be presented as the final net salary after deductions.

NLC India GET Selection Process 2026

The selection process consists of GATE 2025 score evaluation carrying 80 marks and a Personal Interview carrying 20 marks , for a total of 100 marks. Eligible candidates will be shortlisted separately for each recruitment area based on their GATE 2025 marks, generally in a ratio of 1:6 or 1:10 against the notified vacancies, as applicable. The merit list for Area-1, Area-2, Area-3 and Area-4 will be prepared independently, ensuring the applicable reservation provisions. The final merit list will be prepared using the total marks obtained out of 100, combining the weighted GATE 2025 score and Personal Interview marks. If candidates obtain equal final scores at the cut-off, the older candidate will be placed higher. If the tie continues, the alphabetical order of the candidate's first name will be considered.

NLC India GET Reservation and Certificate Requirements

Reservation and age relaxation are governed by Government of India rules. Candidates claiming reservation must upload the appropriate certificates issued by the competent authority. OBC-NCL candidates must produce a valid Non-Creamy Layer certificate obtained on or after 1 April 2026 at document verification. EWS candidates must produce a valid Income and Asset Certificate obtained on or after 1 April 2026 . PwBD candidates must possess the applicable disability certificate or UDID card. Suitability depends on the engineering discipline and the identified disability categories listed in Annexure I. The notification identifies no PwBD suitability category for Mining Engineering.

NLC India GET Document Verification and Medical Fitness

Shortlisted candidates will be called for document verification before the Personal Interview. They must produce original qualification certificates, GATE 2025 scorecard, category certificates and other applicable records. Candidates employed in Government, Central/State PSUs or qualifying organizations must submit the required No Objection Certificate. Finally selected candidates must undergo a pre-employment medical examination and be declared medically fit according to NLCIL standards before appointment.
