DRDO SSPL Internship 2026 Stipend Structure

Monthly stipend: ₹5,000/- (Fixed) , applicable only to candidates who undertake the full 6-month internship. Stipend is paid in two equal installments of ₹15,000/- each - the first after completion of 3 months, and the second after completion of 6 months - via electronic transfer. A minimum of 15 days' attendance in a month is required for that month to count toward the stipend calculation. Interns joining for a shorter duration (less than 6 months, minimum 4 weeks) are not eligible for the stipend . Expenses such as lodging, boarding, transport, and medical costs are to be borne by the candidate.
