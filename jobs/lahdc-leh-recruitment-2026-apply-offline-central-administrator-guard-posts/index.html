LAHDC Leh Eligibility Criteria

The applicant should be a permanent resident of UT Ladakh.
PostQualification Required
Central Administrator (One Stop Centre)i. Any women having a Master in Law/Social Work/Sociology/Science/Psychology with at least 5 years experience of working on women related relevant domains in an administrative set-up with a Government or Non-Government project/programme and preferably with at least 1-year experience of counselling either within or outside the same set-up. ii. She should be preferably a resident of the local community so that local human resource and expertise is utilized for effective functioning of the centre.
GuardPerson having at least 2 years experience of working as security personnel in a government or reputed organization at the district/state level. He/She should preferably be retired military/para-military personnel.
