NHM Simdega Recruitment 2026 Notification PDF

National Health Mission (NHM), Simdega, Jharkhand has released the NHM Simdega Recruitment 2026 notification for the recruitment of Staff Nurse, District PPM Coordinator, ANM, Block Data Manager, ARSH Counselor, Sanitary Attendant and Rehabilitation Worker posts. A total of 18 vacancies across 8 post codes have been announced on a contract basis, with applications invited through offline mode up to 17 October 2026, 5:00 PM. NHM Simdega Vacancy 2026: Post-wise Details

NHM Simdega Recruitment 2026 covers 8 posts with a total of 18 vacancies, including 3 regular vacant posts and 15 backlog vacancies. Reservation is applied district-wise as per the roster. NHM Simdega Selection Process

Selection is based on a 100-point marking scheme combining academic scores, qualification, and an interview/written test, applied on a pro-rata basis. The final merit list will be prepared by the District Selection Committee, Simdega, and published on the office of the Civil Surgeon-cum-Chief Medical Officer and on the Simdega district website (https://www.simdega.nic.in). A waitlist will also be prepared and remains valid for one year. Selected candidates will be informed by registered post and through the district website.

NHM Simdega Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1NHM Simdega Recruitment 2026 does not have an online application portal. Applications must be submitted offline in the prescribed format, sent only by Registered Post/Speed Post, so that it reaches the office on or before 17 October 2026, 5:00 PM.
  • 2Download/obtain the prescribed application format included with the notification.
  • 3Fill in personal details, educational and training qualifications (starting from the highest qualification down to at least 10th standard), and work experience (voluntary/unpaid work is not counted as experience).
  • 4Attach self-attested copies of all educational certificates and mark sheets. Candidates submitting CGPA scorecards must also attach the CGPA-to-percentage conversion chart.
  • 5Attach category certificate (if claiming reservation), residence certificate, matriculation certificate for date of birth, computer proficiency certificate (if applicable), and two self-attested passport-size photographs.
  • 6Attach the Demand Draft for the applicable application fee, made in favour of "District Rural Health Society, Simdega-RCH."
  • 7Enclose a declaration/affidavit that the candidate has not been convicted in any criminal case, along with a medical fitness certificate issued by a registered Civil Surgeon.
  • 8Clearly write the Advertisement Number, Post Code and Post Name on top of the envelope.
  • 9Send the sealed envelope by Registered Post/Speed Post only to the address below. Applications submitted to any other office will be automatically rejected, and those received after the deadline will not be considered.
  • 10Candidates applying for more than one post must submit a separate application form (with separate fee) for each post.
