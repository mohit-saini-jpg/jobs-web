BMC Shikshan Sevak Vacancy 2026

The 935 vacancies for Shikshan Sevak 2026 are distributed across four streams: Each stream carries its own category-wise reservation breakup (SC, ST, VJ-A, NT-B, NT-C, NT-D, SBC, OBC, EWS, SEBC, and Open), along with horizontal reservation for women (30%), ex-servicemen (15%), project-affected (5%), earthquake-affected (2%), sportspersons (5%), part-time graduates/educated unemployed (10%), orphans (1%), and persons with disabilities (4%). The notification states that the number of vacancies and the reservation break-up may increase or decrease before final recruitment.

BMC Shikshan Sevak Eligibility Criteria

Eligibility requirements differ by stream and by the class level being taught (Classes 1–5 or Classes 6–8 for Marathi/English medium), and by discipline for the Art and Music special-teacher posts. All candidates must additionally have passed Marathi as a subject (minimum 100 marks in Marathi-medium schools, minimum 50 marks in other-medium schools) at SSC/HSC or equivalent level, and must hold an MSCIT (or government-recognized equivalent) computer certificate — candidates without it at the time of application must clear MSCIT within 2 years of appointment, failing which their service will be terminated.

BMC Shikshan Sevak Application Fee

The notification does not specify an application fee amount. Fee details, if applicable, will be indicated on the Pavitra Portal at the time of application.

BMC Shikshan Sevak Salary

Selected Shikshan Sevak candidates will receive a fixed honorarium of Rs. 16,000 per month. This is an honorarium (मानधन) for the Shikshan Sevak position rather than a regular pay-scale salary; the notification does not specify a separate pay matrix.

BMC Shikshan Sevak Selection Process

Selection under this notification is based on candidates' scores in the Teacher Aptitude and Intelligence Test (TAIT) 2025, conducted between 27.05.2025 and 05.06.2025, followed by: Preparation of a combined merit list based on TAIT 2025 scores, category-wise and parallel (horizontal) reservation Document verification of original educational, caste, and other supporting certificates Police character and antecedent verification Medical examination (through BMC's medical board; candidates selected under the disability quota are examined by BMC's Medical Board, which must certify fitness before appointment) Final selection list published on the BMC portal, subject to certificate verification Selected candidates are appointed on a 3-year probationary period and must deposit a security amount equal to one month's honorarium (refundable, interest-free, after probation) before joining.
