MTI Powai Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Maritime Training Institute (MTI), Powai, Mumbai has invited applications for empanelment of 37 faculty and instructors across 16 posts: 4 on full-time contract basis (₹1,50,000 per month lumpsum for full-time contract faculty) and 33 on visiting basis (as and when required). Empanelment is for two years. Applications go to the Principal, MTI by hand or by email and must be submitted on or before 07 December 2026, 1700 hrs. Interviews are tentatively scheduled for 14 to 16 December 2026.

About MTI

Maritime Training Institute (MTI), Powai is a training institute established by The Shipping Corporation of India Limited (SCI) and owned by Shipping Corporation of India Land and Assets Limited (SCILAL). This invitation is for faculty and instructors for Nautical and Marine Engineering subjects and courses. The notice describes visiting basis as "as and when required".

Department-wise Vacancies

The notice lists 37 vacancies in total: 04 on full-time contract basis and 33 on visiting basis. The notice states that the qualification of faculty and instructors is the same whether the empanelment is full-time contract or visiting. No category-wise reservation is printed.

Pay and Terms

In the absence of a hostel warden, a Visiting Faculty or Instructor who undertakes the hostel warden job will be compensated with ₹ 1,400/- for minimum 08 hours (same remuneration as a Visiting Instructor). Honorarium rates are subject to change at the discretion of Maritime Training Institute, Powai. The notice does not print separate pay for the Hostel Warden, Medical Instructor, Physical Training Instructor, Swimming Pool Instructor, Workshop Instructor, Electro Technical/Electrical Officer and Naval Architect posts. The empanelment period is two years only and extension will not be granted. Empanelment does not confer any right to regular employment in SCI/MTI, and engagement is governed by the terms and conditions in the Empanelment/Offer Letter.
