CSL PMIS Internship 2026 Ineligibility Criteria

The following categories of candidates are not eligible to apply under this scheme: Graduates/Postgraduates from IITs, IIMs, National Law Universities, IISER, NIDs, IIITs, and IISc. Candidates holding qualifications such as CA, CMA, CS, MBBS, BDS, MD, MS, MBA or equivalent, MPhil, and PhD. Candidates already undergoing any skill, apprenticeship, internship, or training program under Central or State Government schemes. Candidates who have completed apprenticeship or training under the National Apprenticeship Training Scheme (NATS) or National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (NAPS) at any point. Candidates whose family income exceeds ₹12 lakh in the preceding financial year, as on the date of submission of application. Candidates with any family member who is a permanent/regular government employee.

CSL PMIS Internship 2026 Stipend

ITI Trades, SSLC, and HSE/ITI categories: ₹11,000 per month (including Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) by the Government of India) Diploma, Graduation, and Post Graduation categories: ₹13,000 per month (including Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) by the Government of India) No travel expenses are admissible for attending certificate verification or during the internship training period. Boarding and lodging will not be provided by CSL during the internship period.

CSL PMIS Internship 2026 Duration

The duration of the internship will be Nine (9) months, as per the Prime Minister Internship Scheme guidelines.

CSL PMIS Internship 2026 Application Fee

There is no application fee for the CSL PMIS Internship 2026. The notification explicitly states "Application Fee: Nil."

CSL PMIS Internship 2026 Selection Process

Shortlisting of candidates will be based on the percentage of marks obtained in the prescribed qualification applicable to the respective trade/discipline. In case two or more candidates secure the same percentage of marks, relative merit will be decided based on seniority in age. Shortlisted candidates will be issued an offer of Internship Training through the PMIS portal and must appear for certificate verification on the date mentioned in the offer letter. Certificate verification requires original certificates for proof of age, qualification, caste, and disability (if any), along with self-attested copies of the same. On successful completion of certificate verification, candidates will be provisionally inducted for internship training, subject to medical fitness.

CSL PMIS Internship 2026 Documents Required

Original certificates as proof of age Original educational qualification certificates relevant to the applied category Original caste certificate (if applicable) Original disability certificate (if applicable) Self-attested copies of all the above documents Equivalency Certificate, if the candidate holds a qualification equivalent to the prescribed qualification, issued by the competent authority
