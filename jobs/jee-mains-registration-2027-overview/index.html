JEE Mains Registration 2027 Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 30/10/2026

JEE Mains Registration 2027: The National Testing Agency (NTA) has started online registration for the Joint Entrance Examination (Main) 2027 Session 1. The application process began on 24 October 2026, and eligible candidates can apply until 22 November 2026 at 11:50 PM without a late fee. Candidates who miss the regular deadline can submit their applications from 23 to 26 November 2026 by paying an additional late fee of ₹500. The application link is available at jeemain.nta.nic.in.

JEE Main 2027 is conducted for admission to undergraduate engineering, architecture and planning courses. The Session 1 examination will be held on 20, 21, 22, 23, 24 and 28 January 2027. Candidates who passed Class 12 or an equivalent examination in 2025 or 2026, or who are appearing in 2027, can apply subject to the official eligibility requirements.

JEE Mains 2027 Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must check the prescribed eligibility conditions before submitting their JEE Main 2027 application form.

Educational Qualification

Candidates who passed Class 12 or an equivalent qualifying examination in 2025 or 2026, or who are appearing in 2027, are eligible to appear for JEE Main 2027, subject to the remaining examination requirements.

Candidates who passed Class 12 or an equivalent examination in 2024 or earlier are not eligible to appear in JEE Main 2027.

Age Limit

There is no age limit for appearing in JEE Main 2027. However, candidates must satisfy the applicable age and admission eligibility requirements of the institutions where they seek admission.

Subject Requirements

For admission to B.E./B.Tech programmes at participating institutions, the applicable subject requirements generally include Physics and Mathematics along with one of Chemistry, Biotechnology, Biology or a Technical Vocational subject.

B.Arch and B.Planning programmes have separate subject and admission eligibility conditions. Candidates should consult the official information bulletin and the requirements of the institution to which they intend to apply.

How to Apply Online for JEE Mains Registration 2027?

Eligible candidates can complete the JEE Main 2027 Session 1 registration by following these steps.

  • Visit the official website at jeemain.nta.nic.in.
  • Click on the Online Application Form for JEE Main 2027 Session 1 link.
  • Read the official registration instructions and information bulletin.
  • Complete the new registration process using the available identity verification and registration options.
  • Enter the required personal, contact and educational details.
  • Select the examination paper and preferred examination cities.
  • Upload the required photograph, signature and supporting documents in the prescribed format.
  • Check all application details carefully before final submission.
  • Pay the applicable examination fee using the available online payment modes.
  • Download and save the application confirmation page after successful payment.

The confirmation page is generated only after successful payment of the examination fee. Candidates should verify the submission status and retain their application number and confirmation page.

JEE Main 2027 Identity Verification Instructions

NTA has issued instructions concerning the registration and identity verification process. Candidates should use the identity verification facility provided on the official application portal.

Applicants must ensure that their personal details, including name and date of birth, match their supporting academic and identity records. Candidates using DigiLocker or other supported digital identity services should complete the verification steps requested by the portal.

Incorrect identity details may create difficulties during registration or subsequent examination verification.

JEE Main 2027 Session 1 and Session 2 Registration

JEE Main 2027 has separate application periods for its January and April sessions. Registration for Session 1 is currently open, while Session 2 registration is scheduled to begin on 5 January 2027.

Candidates who register for Session 1 and later wish to appear in Session 2 can use their existing application number and pay the applicable Session 2 examination fee during the designated registration period.

Candidates who wish to appear only in Session 2 can register when the April session application facility opens.
