SBI SCO Recruitment 2026-27 Remuneration / CTC

Annual CTC is negotiable and depends on the candidate's experience, current emoluments, and place of posting. CTC is bifurcated into Fixed Pay and Variable Pay in an 80:20 ratio, and includes an option for accommodation (and a vehicle, for SBIL posts) - where accommodation/vehicle is availed, a notional amount is deducted from the CTC. Variable pay is subject to securing a minimum qualifying score in the half-yearly performance appraisal (90% or more for full variable pay eligibility under Advt No. 20). Annual increment from the second year onward can be up to 10% of the finalized annual CTC (Advt No. 20) or up to 7% (Advt No. 22), subject to a minimum performance appraisal score of 90%.

SBI SCO Recruitment 2026-27 Selection Process

Shortlisting, followed by Interview cum Demo Session. Interview/Demo Session carries 100 marks; the qualifying marks are decided by the Bank. Shortlisting, followed by Interview. Shortlisting: A shortlisting committee constituted by the Bank decides the shortlisting parameters; merely fulfilling minimum qualification and experience does not entitle a candidate to be called for interview. The Bank's decision is final, with no correspondence entertained. CTC Negotiation: Conducted with candidates in order of merit, one by one. If a selected candidate does not agree to the negotiated CTC, they are treated as a Voluntary Exit, and the next candidate in the merit list is called for negotiation. Merit List: Prepared in descending order based on interview marks only. In case of a tie at the cut-off, candidates are ranked by age in descending order (older candidate ranked higher). Contract Period: 3 years, extendable up to a maximum of 2 further years at the Bank's discretion (total engagement not to exceed 5 years).
