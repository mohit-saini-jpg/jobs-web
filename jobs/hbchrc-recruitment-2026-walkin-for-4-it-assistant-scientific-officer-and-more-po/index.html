About HBCHRC Punjab

Homi Bhabha Cancer Hospital & Research Centre, Punjab is a cancer hospital and research centre at Medicity, New Chandigarh, Mohali. M/s. Innovsource Services Pvt. Ltd. supplies manpower services to the hospital on contract as an outsourcing agency. Anyone selected through this notice is engaged through the agency and deployed at the hospital.

HBCH&amp;RC Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection is through a walk-in interview. If too many candidates turn up, an MCQ test will be held and the eligible candidates will be shortlisted for the interview based on it. The agency may set a minimum eligibility standard or benchmark and cut-off marks (by group, stream, discipline or category) and may limit the number of candidates called for a written test, interview or skill test. It can base this on factors such as the number of vacancies, marks in graduation or post-graduation, and the applicants' qualifications and experience. Meeting the prescribed qualifications alone does not entitle a candidate to be called for a test or interview. The competent authority's decision is final, and no correspondence will be entertained.

Service Conditions

Each post is initially for a fixed tenure of one year from the date of joining. The tenure may be extended depending on satisfactory performance and institutional requirement. Selected candidates will be posted at New Chandigarh or Sangrur, as the institution requires. The engagement is on contract through M/s. Innovsource Services Pvt. Ltd. If a candidate is found ineligible at any stage, or to have given incorrect, false or incomplete information or hidden any material fact, the candidature will be cancelled. If a shortcoming comes to light after appointment, the services can be terminated immediately. Canvassing in any form will lead to disqualification.
