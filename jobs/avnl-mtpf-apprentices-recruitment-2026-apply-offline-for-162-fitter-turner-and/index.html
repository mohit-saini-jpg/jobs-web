MTPF Trade Apprentice Selection Process

Selection is on the basis of a merit list; there is no written examination or interview. Separate merit lists are prepared for the Non-ITI and Ex-ITI categories. Non-ITI: A common merit list is prepared based on the overall percentage of marks scored in Madhyamik/Matriculation (Class X or equivalent), aggregated across all subjects, irrespective of Board. Trades are allotted on a merit-cum-choice basis after selection. Ex-ITI: A trade-wise merit list is prepared based on the simple average of the percentage of marks in both Matriculation and ITI (minimum 50% aggregate required in each). Based on vacancy position, select lists are prepared; only provisionally selected candidates are called for document verification and medical examination. PH/PWD vacancies are not separate but included within the total vacancies of each trade. Interchange rule: unfilled SC training places may be filled by ST candidates (or vice versa); unfilled OBC places may be filled by non-OBC candidates, if the reserved category is unavailable. AVNL MTPF Apprentices Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is MTPF Apprentice Recruitment 2026?
A
It is a recruitment drive by Machine Tool Prototype Factory (MTPF), a unit of Armoured Vehicles Nigam Limited (AVNL), to engage the 61st Batch of Non-ITI and Ex-ITI Trade Apprentices under the Apprentices Act, 1961.
Q2How many vacancies are available in MTPF Apprentice Recruitment 2026?
A
A total of 162 vacancies are available, comprising 83 posts under Non-ITI trades and 79 posts under Ex-ITI trades.
Q3What is the educational qualification required?
A
Non-ITI candidates need Madhyamik/Class X with 50% aggregate and 40% each in Maths and Science. Ex-ITI candidates need a relevant NCVT/SCVT trade certificate plus Matriculation, with 50% aggregate in each.
Q4What stipend is paid during training?
A
Non-ITI apprentices receive ₹12,600/month (Year 1) and ₹14,400/month (Year 2). Ex-ITI apprentices receive ₹16,200/month (2-year ITI trades) or ₹14,400/month (1-year ITI trades).
Q5Is there a written exam for selection?
A
No. Selection is purely merit-list based, using Matriculation percentage (Non-ITI) or the average of Matriculation and ITI percentage (Ex-ITI).
Q6Is there an application fee?
A
The official notice does not specify any application fee for this recruitment.
Q7How do I apply for MTPF Trade Apprentice 2026?
A
There is no online portal; applications must be sent by post using the Annexure-I format to the Chief General Manager, MTPF, Ambarnath.
