Qualification and Experience

All degrees must be from an Institute/University recognized by UGC, AICTE, AIU, or GOI. Foreign degrees are accepted if from national universities or institutions ranked up to 500 in QS/THE World Rankings. For candidates with a Ph.D. from SVNIT Surat, a three-year inbreeding gap between Ph.D. notification and the application deadline applies (not applicable to existing SVNIT faculty).

Experience at the level of Associate Professor with AGP ₹9000 and/or ₹9500 (Pay Level 13A1 and/or 13A2). Pay and Terms

The post carries Pay Level 14A of the Pay Matrix, with basic pay ranging from ₹159,100 to ₹220,200 per month along with admissible allowances. All appointments are made on probation for a period of one year, wherever applicable. On successful completion and confirmation, appointees hold office until the age of superannuation specified by the Ministry of Education, Government of India. Additional benefits include Cumulative Professional Development Allowance (CPDA), provision for industrial consultancy, children's tuition fee reimbursement up to Class XII, LTC, medical facilities for self and dependent family, campus accommodation with nominal licence fee or HRA, coverage under the National Pension System (NPS-2004), and reimbursement of telephone and data charges.

SVNIT Professor Age Limit

Fresh appointment beyond the age of 60 years is discouraged except for candidates with exceptional research and ongoing or approved externally funded research projects. The date for determining eligibility in all respects, including the preferred age limit, qualification, and experience, is the closing date of the online application (02.12.2026).

SVNIT Professor Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Open the online recruitment portal at https://svnitrec.samarth.edu.in/ between 18.10.2026 and 02.12.2026 (up to 5.00 p.m.).
  • 2Complete the online application form and fill in the credit point summary sheet and details sheet.
  • 3Upload a recent colour passport-size photograph (taken within 3 months, without cap, scarf, or sunglasses) along with relevant certificates.
  • 4Pay the non-refundable processing fee of ₹1000 online, if applicable.
  • 5Submit the online form and download the completed application form along with the credit point summary sheet.
  • 6Number and sign all pages of the printed application form, and write the total number of pages on the first page.
  • 7Enclose self-attested copies of all testimonials, educational certificates, experience certificates, category proof, and fee payment proof.
  • 8Place the documents in an envelope superscribed as "Advertisement No. Estt. /2026/ Prof/1600, Dated 16.10.2026, Department of [Name of Department], Application ID no. [ID]".
  • 9Send the packet so as to reach the Deputy Registrar (Establishment), Sardar Vallabhbhai National Institute of Technology, Ichchhanath, Dumas Road, Surat – 395 007, Gujarat on or before 09.12.2026 (5:00 p.m.).
