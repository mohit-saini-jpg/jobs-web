SAMEER Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

SAMEER Recruitment 2026: Society for Applied Microwave Electronics Engineering and Research (SAMEER), an autonomous research organization under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India, has released for 140 contractual project positions at Mumbai and Navi Mumbai. The vacancies include Project Fellow, Project Scientist, Project Engineer, Project Associate, Project Scientific Assistant, Project Technical Assistant, Project Manager and Project Executive posts. SAMEER Contract Period and Employment Conditions

The posts are temporary and project-specific. The notification expressly excludes any right to regularisation, permanent absorption, continued service or automatic contract extension. Selected candidates will normally work at Mumbai/Navi Mumbai but may be required to serve at another SAMEER centre or location in India, depending on project needs. Candidates previously engaged at any SAMEER centre as Research Scientist, Project Engineer or Project Associate for an aggregate period of five years or more are not eligible for appointment as Project Engineer or Project Associate under the notified restriction.

  • 5Complete candidate registration and verify your email address.
  • 6Log in and create your candidate profile.
  • 7Enter accurate personal, educational and other required details.
  • 8Upload the supporting documents requested by the portal.
  • 9Review and lock the candidate profile according to the portal instructions.
  • 10Select Advertisement No. 10/2026 and the eligible post code.

  • 13Review the application carefully before final submission.
