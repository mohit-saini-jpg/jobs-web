About IIT Dharwad Project Associate I

According to the notice, IIT Dharwad is an Institute of National Importance created by an Act of Parliament in 2016. It is recruiting for these positions to meet the needs of a project in its Department of Computer Science and Engineering. The project is titled "Speech Technologies in Indian Languages", and its project code carries the reference MeitY-NLTM. The whole selection is described as an Online Selection Process for a temporary position.

Who Can Apply

Candidates must hold a B. Tech/M. Tech degree in Computer Science Engineering, Electrical & Communication Engineering, or a related stream from an accredited institution. They must also meet any ONE of the following additional requirements: GATE qualification, or The Selection process is through a national-level examination conducted by IIT. The qualification must come from a recognized University/Institute. Age, qualification and experience will be considered as on the date of the Selection Process. The notice says strong programming skills and knowledge in Machine Learning, Deep Learning and Signal Processing are preferable. The age must be not exceeding 35 years as on the date of the selection process. The notice does not mention any age relaxation. Candidates should check the official notification for the full eligibility conditions of the post.

Pay and Tenure

The Project Associate I position carries consolidated emoluments. The rate depends on the candidate's degree: The tenure of appointment is a maximum of 5 months. Selected candidates will have no claim on any regular position. They will also not receive the privileges available to the Institute's regular employees, such as housing, medical facility and other benefits.
