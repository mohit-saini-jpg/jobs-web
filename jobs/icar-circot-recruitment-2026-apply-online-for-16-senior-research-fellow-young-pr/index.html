About ICAR-CIRCOT

ICAR-Central Institute for Research on Cotton Technology is an institute of the Indian Council of Agricultural Research, with its office at Adenwala Road, Matunga, Mumbai - 400019. The notice invites engagement for programmes of the Mission on Cotton Productivity (Kapas Kanti) and for the Institute's Finance and Accounts Section. Program 5.1 covers post-harvest technologies including cleaning, ginning and quality profiling systems for mechanically harvested cotton. Program 5.2 covers an innovative cotton value chain to enhance farm profitability through circular economy.

ICAR-CIRCOT Recruitment 2026 Job Description

The notice prints the nature of work for only the following posts. Molecular Biology work, designing of stable short chains of deoxy-ribonucleotides (marker DNA sequences) using bioinformatics tools. PCR-based analysis and validation of marker DNA sequences for cotton identification and traceability. Encapsulation of marker DNA sequences using suitable encapsulating agents for improving its stability. Screening of microbial strains for PLA production. Optimization of culture media and fermentation conditions. Molecular characterization and genetic modification of selected strains, as required. Laboratory scale fermentation; recovery and purification of PLA. HPLC analysis of fermentation products. The candidate will be mainly required for project management and administrative work related to the project.

Selection and Interview

The notice describes an interview in hybrid mode, with the venue, date and time given against each post: GTC, Nagpur on 16 November 2026, ICAR-CIRCOT, Mumbai on 17 November 2026 and ICAR-CIRCOT, Mumbai on 23 November 2026 (see the vacancy table). Candidates choose Online or Offline mode in the application. The Director's decision is final and binding in all respects. Candidates currently employed elsewhere whose NOC is missing are liable to have the interview cancelled. The notice mentions no written test.

No T.A./D.A. will be paid for appearing in the interview. The selected SRF/YPs may be posted at all regional units.
