GPSC Goa Application Fee 2026

After filling the application form, candidates pay the application fee in one of two ways. The notice does not print the fee amounts. Online: make the online payment and download the e-receipt showing successful status. Offline: generate an e-challan and make cash payment at any SBI Branch situated in Goa only, on or before the last date. The e-challan is valid for seven days from generation, a bank stamp on it is essential, and the stamped e-challan must be produced to the Commission's office on or before the last date of submission. Applications where fee payment is made after the last date will be summarily rejected and no refund will be provided. The application form can be downloaded only after successful payment.

GPSC Goa Selection Process 2026

Shortlisting: where the number of applications received is large, the Commission will short list candidates to be called for interview, as mentioned in the Instructions on its website. Interview: the prescribed essential qualifications are minimum, and mere possession of them does not entitle a candidate to be called for interview. For the Assistant Professor in Science and Humanities (Chemistry) post, the notice says the academic score under the UGC Regulations, 2018 is considered for shortlisting for interview only, and selection is based only on performance in the interview.

GPSC Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Read the Instructions available on the Commission's website https://gpsc.goa.gov.in before filling the application.
  • 2Apply online through the Commission's website https://gpsc.goa.gov.in or the Government of Goa portal https://cbes.goa.gov.in/.
  • 3Give an email address and mobile number in the application form; the Commission sends all communications through them.
  • 4Pay the application fee online and download the e-receipt, or generate an e-challan and pay cash at an SBI Branch in Goa.
  • 5For offline payment, produce the bank-stamped e-challan at the Commission's office on or before the last date.
  • 6Download the application form after successful payment.
  • 7The last date is 27.11.2026 for all posts other than the deputation post, and 27.12.2026 for the deputation post only.
