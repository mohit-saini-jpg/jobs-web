Gujarat University Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Gujarat University's Indian Institute of Sustainability (IIS), Ahmedabad, has 3 posts for its Integrated M.B.A. Programme: 1 Research Fellow at Rs. 40,000 per month and 2 Office Assistants at Rs. 18,000 per month. Both salaries are consolidated, and the appointment is purely on a contractual basis for a period of 6 months. The Research Fellow needs a Master's or equivalent degree with 55% marks (or a CGPA of 5.5 out of 10) and 2 years of experience. The Office Assistant needs to be a graduate from a recognized university. Applications go online by email to [email protected] from 27 October 202
