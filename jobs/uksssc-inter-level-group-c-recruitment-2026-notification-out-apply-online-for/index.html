UKSSSC Eligibility Criteria & Qualification

The common baseline qualification across almost all 59 posts is an Intermediate (Class 12) pass or an equivalent exam recognised by the Uttarakhand Board of School Education or the state government, along with a minimum Hindi typing speed of 4000 Key Depressions Per Hour (KDPH) on computer. A number of posts carry additional, post-specific requirements, for example: Computer Assistant, Junior Assistant-cum-Computer Operator, Computer Operator: knowledge of MS Office is also required, and for Computer Operator posts a minimum 6-month computer course is required. UKSSSC Exam Pattern & Selection Process

Selection involves two stages: a written objective-type competitive exam, followed by a qualifying typing test for candidates shortlisted on the written exam. The General Knowledge & General Studies portion (40 marks) covers General Intelligence Test & Reasoning, History, Geography, Political Science, Economics, National & International Current Affairs, and Fundamentals of Computers; General Hindi carries 20 marks; and General Knowledge of Uttarakhand carries 40 marks. Each wrong answer attracts a negative marking of one-fourth (0.25) of the marks assigned to that question. Minimum qualifying marks in the written exam are 45% for Unreserved/EWS/OBC candidates and 35% for SC/ST candidates; candidates scoring below this are treated as not qualified. UKSSSC Inter Level Group C Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Visit the official UKSSSC website, www.sssc.uk.gov.in.
  • 2Step 1 – Registration & Personal Details: register using your Aadhaar number (one Aadhaar can be used for only one registration) and your own mobile number and email ID.
  • 3Step 2 – Educational & Other Details: fill in your educational qualification, category, and other required details.
  • 4Step 3 – Upload Photo, Signature: upload a scanned recent colour passport-size photograph (150w × 200H px) and signature (150w × 100H px), plus a left thumb impression (150w × 100H px) in JPG/JPEG format.
  • 5Step 4 – Make Payment: pay the application fee online via credit card/debit card/net banking/UPI.
  • 6Step 5 – Print Application: after successful submission, print a copy of the final application form for your own records.
  • 7Candidates should note down their registration number, since it is required for all further steps. After the last date, a one-time edit window (12.10.2026 to 14.10.2026) is provided to correct the application, but candidates cannot change their name, mother's/father's name, date of birth, or exam centre during this correction window.

UKSSSC Inter Level Group C Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q4What is the qualification for UKSSSC Group C posts?
A
Intermediate (Class 12) or an equivalent exam, along with a minimum Hindi typing speed of 4000 KDPH for most posts. A few posts require additional qualifications such as MS Office knowledge, a computer diploma, or prior work experience - see the Eligibility section above.
Q5What is the age limit for UKSSSC Recruitment 2026?

Generally 18 to 42 years as on 1 July 2026; a few specific posts (such as Junior Assistant at the Tourism Development Board or the High Court, and Store Keeper) require a minimum age of 21 years. Category-wise age relaxation is available as detailed above.
Q6What is the application fee for UKSSSC Recruitment 2026?

₹300 for Unreserved/OBC, ₹150 for SC/ST/EWS/PwBD candidates, and no fee for orphan candidates.
Q7How can I apply online for UKSSSC Recruitment 2026?

Applications are accepted only online at www.sssc.uk.gov.in from 8 September to 7 October 2026. See the "How to Apply" section above for the full step-by-step process.
Q8What is the selection process for UKSSSC Group C posts?
A
A 100-mark, 2-hour written objective exam on General Hindi, General Knowledge, and General Studies, followed by a qualifying typing test. Final merit is based on the written exam marks only.
Q9What is the salary for UKSSSC Group C posts?

Pay ranges from Level-02 (₹19,900–₹63,200) to Level-05 (₹29,200–₹92,300) depending on the post; most posts fall under Level-03 (₹21,700–₹69,100). See the Vacancy Details table above for the exact pay level of each post.

The notification does not specify a post-limit restriction in the sections reviewed here; however, a separate application and fee are required for each post applied for, and multiple applications for the same single post are not allowed.
