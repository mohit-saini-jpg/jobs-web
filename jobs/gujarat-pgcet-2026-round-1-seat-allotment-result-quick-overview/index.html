Who Should Check the Round 1 Result

  • Candidates who completed choice filling, alteration, and locking for Round 1 (July 20-22, 2026)
  • Candidates seeking admission to ME, MTech, MPharm, or MPlan programmes through Gujarat PGCET 2026
  • Candidates who reviewed the mock allotment result (July 21, 2026) and confirmed or revised their preferences

How to Check and Download the Gujarat PGCET 2026 Round 1 Seat Allotment Result

  • Visit the official ACPC counselling portal at acpc.gujarat.gov.in.
  • Click on the Gujarat PGCET / ME-MTech-MPharm-MPlan admissions 2026 counselling link.
  • Log in using your application number and password.
  • Open the Round 1 seat allotment result on your candidate dashboard.
  • Check the allotted institute, course, and category details carefully.
  • Download and save the seat allotment letter for your records.

ACPC has advised candidates to rely only on the official portal for their result and to avoid third-party or unofficial links, since login credentials are required to access individual allotment details.

After the Result: Fee Payment and Confirmation

Candidates allotted a seat in Round 1 need to pay the token tuition fee online (net banking, debit/credit card, or UPI, based on the options enabled on the portal) and download their admission letter between July 24 and July 26, 2026. The exact fee amount for the 2026 cycle has not been specified in the available official updates; candidates should confirm the applicable amount from their individual allotment/payment page on the ACPC portal. (For reference, in the 2024 PGCET cycle the token fee was reported as Rs. 2,000 this is historical information only and should not be assumed to apply to 2026.)

Candidates who are not satisfied with their Round 1 allotment and wish to be considered in the next round can cancel their seat online within the same July 24-26, 2026 window.

Required Documents (for Reporting/Verification)

The specific document checklist for Round 1 reporting or verification has not been detailed in the currently available official updates. Candidates are advised to keep the following commonly required items ready and to confirm the exact list from the official ACPC notification or admission letter:

  • Gujarat PGCET 2026 admission letter/seat allotment letter (downloaded from the portal)
  • Valid photo ID proof
  • Educational qualification certificates and mark sheets
  • Category/reservation certificate, where applicable
  • GATE scorecard, where applicable
  • Fee payment confirmation receipt

Important Instructions

  • The Round 1 allotment is provisional in nature and subject to document verification requirements as specified by ACPC.
  • Missing the fee payment deadline (July 26, 2026) may result in cancellation of the allotted seat.
  • The mock allotment released on July 21, 2026, was a trial run only and not a binding seat offer.
  • Candidates who did not revise their preferences after the mock round will have their original locked choices carried forward automatically into Round 1.
  • Always cross-check dates and processes on the official ACPC website, since counselling schedules can be revised.
