About USRLM

The Uttarakhand State Rural Livelihood Mission forms Cluster Level Federations in Rudraprayag district, and these posts are in federations funded by the Gramotthan Project, also called the Rural Enterprise Acceleration Project (REAP). The project supports community institutions through group formation, accounts, small loans, banking and livelihood work. It also links beneficiaries to value chains so that they can set up enterprises and reach markets.

USRLM Group Mobiliser Vacancy Details 2026

The notice calls the Group Mobiliser a Samuh Sugamkarta (group facilitator) in its vacancy table. Vacancies are spread equally across the two federations, both in Agastyamuni block: The number of posts may be increased or decreased according to vacancies in the district's federations, and the posts may be cancelled on any ground. USRLM Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

A written examination will be held for the vacant posts. Only candidates who pass it are placed in the merit list, which is prepared according to project norms. The notice proposes to send examination details to shortlisted candidates on the mobile number given in the application form. Class marks: Each class carries a maximum of 10 marks, so 55% in graduation gives 5.5 marks. Business Promoter counts 10th, 12th and graduation, and the other two posts count 10th and 12th. Experience marks: At least 1 year earns 3 marks, more than 1 year earns 5, and more than 2 years earns 9. Merit list: A five-member panel is formed to prepare it, and marks are displayed in order on the CLF notice board. Tie-break: If two candidates have equal marks, the older candidate gets preference.

Nature of the post: All posts are fully temporary, on a contract basis, and are for the Cluster Level Federations. Contract period: One year. It can be extended for another year with approval of the CLF executive committee. Contract letter: Selected candidates receive a contract on the CLF letter pad, signed by its office bearers. A copy is kept at the CLF office with the candidate's essential documents. Place of posting: Merit list candidates may be appointed in any CLF in Rudraprayag district. Waiting list candidates may be appointed to vacancies in other CLFs of the district after approval of the CLF concerned. Leave: Up to 20 days in a calendar year, with a maximum of 2 in a month. USRLM Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1Can I apply with only Intermediate?
A
Yes, for Group Mobiliser or Accountant cum Data Entry Operator, provided you also have the required 1 year of experience. Business Promoter requires graduation in any subject.
Q2Where do I get the application form?
A
It is available on any working day from the REAP office or USRLM office of Agastyamuni block.
Q3Where do I submit the form?
A
Submit it at the Agastyamuni block REAP office on a working day. The cut-off is 5 PM on the last date.
Q4What does the written exam contain?
A
Each post has two MCQ sections of 5 questions and 10 marks each, plus one subjective question of 5 marks. The topics differ by post.
Q5How will I know the exam details?
A
Shortlisted candidates are proposed to be informed on the mobile number given in the application form.
Q6Do group members get any advantage?
A
Yes. District group members and their sons, daughters or husbands get preference with the certificate attached. Local SHG/PG members or first relatives can also earn up to 6 merit marks.
Q7What happens if two candidates get the same merit marks?
A
The older candidate gets preference.
Q8How long is the contract?
A
The contract runs for one year and may be extended for one more year with CLF executive committee approval.
Q9Can I be posted outside the CLF I am interested in?
A
Yes. Merit list candidates may be appointed in any CLF in Rudraprayag district, depending on vacancies.
Q10Can a current CLF employee apply?
A
Yes, but staff working in a CLF in Rudraprayag district must first obtain an NOC from their CLF.
