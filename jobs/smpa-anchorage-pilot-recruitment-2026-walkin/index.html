SMPA Anchorage Pilot Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Syama Prasad Mookerjee Port Authority (SMPA) will hold a walk-in interview on 14 December 2026 (Monday). The reporting time is 10:00 A.M., and the venue is SMPA's Head Office, 15 Strand Road, Kolkata-700001. SMPA needs two (02) Anchorage Pilots, on a purely contractual basis for three (03) years, for the Marine Department of the Kolkata Dock System (KDS). The pay is Rs. 1,50,000/- consolidated per month for Master FG / First Mate (FG) and Rs. 1,00,000/- consolidated per month for Second Mate (FG). Only Indian nationals can attend.

About the Organisation

Syama Prasad Mookerjee Port Authority (SMPA) has its Head Office at 15 Strand Road, Kolkata. These Anchorage Pilot posts are in the Marine Department of the Kolkata Dock System (KDS) of SMPA. The notice is signed by the Dy. Secretary-II for the Secretary.

Posts and Eligibility

SMPA will engage two Anchorage Pilots on a purely contractual basis for a period of three (03) years. Applications are invited from eligible Indian nationals. The nationality condition is "Indian citizens". Essential educational qualification: "Must have Certificate of Competency as Master FG or First Mate (FG) or Second Mate (FG)." The notice names no other degree or experience requirement. Candidates should check the official notification for the full eligibility conditions of the post. The upper age limit is 50 years as on 01.11.2026. The notice does not mention any age relaxation.

Pay and Allowances

The consolidated monthly remuneration depends on the Certificate of Competency the candidate holds: The notice lists these allowances and benefits: Mess Allowance of Rs. 350/- per day during the stay on board the vessels at anchor. Uniform Allowance at Rs. 1125/- per month. Uniform washing allowance at Rs. 600/- per month. Indoor and outdoor medical facilities at Centenary Hospital, SMPA, for self and spouse. Medicines, tests or treatment taken outside are not reimbursed, except for treatment of an injury caused by an accident arising out of and in the course of employment. Reimbursement of 25% of the Medical Insurance premium paid to cover the employee and spouse, up to Rs. 5000/- per annum, on documentary proof of payment. Contract Terms and Posting

The engagement is provisionally for three (03) years. Renewal is subject to an annual performance review after each calendar year of service. The grading range is A – Outstanding, B – Very Good, C – Good or Satisfactory and D – Poor, and the minimum benchmark is Very Good. Selected candidates will undergo familiarisation training for 02 months, assisting the Pilots at the Anchorage. After the training they must pass an examination before they are issued dispensation to work as Anchorage Pilots (on contract). Selected candidates will first report to the office of Harbour Master (River), Subhas Bhavan, 40, Circular Garden Reach Road, Kolkata - 700043. They may be required to work at any place within the jurisdiction of SMP, Kolkata. What Happens on the Day

Candidates report at 10:00 A.M. at SMPA's Head Office and attend the walk-in interview.
