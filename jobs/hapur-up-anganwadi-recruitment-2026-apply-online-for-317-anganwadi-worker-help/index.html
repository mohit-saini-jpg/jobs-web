Hapur Anganwadi Recruitment 2026 Notification PDF

The Office of the District Programme Officer, Hapur has officially released the Hapur Anganwadi Recruitment 2026 Notification vide Notification Letter No. C-541 / District Programme Office / Anganwadi Appointment / 2026-27 dated 28 July 2026 for the recruitment of 317 Anganwadi Karyakartri and Anganwadi Sahayika posts in the rural Child Development Projects of Hapur district. The notification contains complete information regarding project-wise vacancies, eligibility criteria, age limit, educational qualifications, residency requirements, application process, and important instructions. Candidates are advised to read the official notification carefully before submitting the online application. The notification is available on the official Anganwadi recruitment portal. Hapur Anganwadi Vacancy 2026 Details (Project-wise)

The notification specifies vacancies separately for Anganwadi Karyakartri and Anganwadi Sahayika posts across four rural Child Development Projects: Garhmukteshwar, Simbhawali, Dhaulana, and Hapur. Note: The official notice states that no vacancies are reserved under the ST (Scheduled Tribe) category for any project in this recruitment cycle. Complete gram sabha-wise vacancy and reservation details are available at the Collectorate, Vikas Bhawan, Tehsil, District Programme Office (Child Development), and Block Development Office notice boards, as well as on upanganwadibharti.in.

Hapur Anganwadi Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Only female candidates are eligible to apply for the advertised posts. The candidate must be a permanent resident of the gram sabha to which the vacant post belongs. If an eligible candidate from the required category is not available in that gram sabha, a candidate who is a permanent resident of the concerned Nyaya Panchayat is eligible to apply.

Anganwadi Karyakartri Sahayika Educational Qualification 2026

Anganwadi Karyakartri: Minimum qualification - Intermediate (12th) or its equivalent; Maximum qualification - Postgraduate. Anganwadi Sahayika: Educational qualification for the Sahayika post is not specified in the official notice. Candidates are advised to refer to the detailed Government Order dated 17.09.2025 for this information.
