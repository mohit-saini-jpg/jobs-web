GSSSB CCE 2.0 Selection Process 2026

The recruitment follows a two-stage competitive examination process: a common preliminary examination and separate main examinations for Group-A and Group-B. Online Application: Candidates register and submit the application through OJAS. Preliminary Examination: A common 150-mark objective examination is conducted for Group-A and Group-B. Shortlisting for Mains: Separate Group-A and Group-B eligibility lists are prepared based on preliminary examination performance. Main Examination: Group-A candidates appear for descriptive papers, while Group-B candidates appear for an objective main examination. Merit List: Final selection lists are prepared using the applicable main examination results, reservation provisions and qualifying standards. GSSSB CCE 2.0 Group-A Main Exam Pattern 2026

The Group-A main examination is descriptive and consists of three papers carrying a combined total of 350 marks. The Gujarati descriptive paper carries 100 marks and includes essay writing, expansion of ideas, précis writing, prose comprehension, press notes, letter writing, discussion articles, reports, translation and Gujarati grammar. The English descriptive paper carries 100 marks and covers essay writing, formal letters, report writing, interpretation of visual information, formal speech, précis writing, reading comprehension, grammar and translation. The General Studies paper carries 150 marks. Its syllabus covers Indian history, Gujarat history, cultural heritage, the Constitution, governance, economy, geography, science, environment, current affairs and other subjects prescribed in Appendix-H of the official notification.

GSSSB CCE 2.0 Group-B Main Exam Pattern 2026

The Group-B main examination is an objective-type examination carrying 200 marks with a duration of 120 minutes. The notification prescribes a minimum qualifying standard of 40% for the preliminary and main examinations, including the applicable papers. The qualifying standard cannot be reduced below 40% for any category. Meeting the minimum qualifying standard does not automatically guarantee shortlisting or selection, as the competitive merit cut-off may be higher.
