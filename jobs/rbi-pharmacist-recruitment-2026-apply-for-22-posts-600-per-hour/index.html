RBI Pharmacist Eligibility Criteria 2026

Applicants must satisfy the educational qualification, professional registration, experience and computer knowledge requirements specified by RBI. Candidates must have passed Matriculation or an equivalent examination. Candidates must possess a Diploma in Pharmacy from a recognized university, as specified in the notification, or a Bachelor's Degree in Pharmacy (B.Pharm). Registration with the Maharashtra State Pharmacy Council is compulsory. Candidates must have basic working knowledge of computers. Applicants must have at least two years of experience working as a Pharmacist. RBI Pharmacist Selection Process 2026

RBI will shortlist eligible applicants for an interview. Shortlisting will consider educational qualifications, relevant experience and distance of residence from the Bank's dispensaries. The recruitment process includes: Shortlisting based on educational qualifications, residence distance and experience. Interview for shortlisted candidates. Medical examination for candidates shortlisted after the interview. Document verification before engagement. Empanelment and engagement according to the Bank's requirements. RBI may raise its shortlisting standards to limit the number of applicants called for interview. Merely meeting the minimum eligibility conditions does not guarantee an interview call. The notification does not specify a written examination. No travelling allowance or other interview-related allowance will be paid.

Is RBI Pharmacist Recruitment 2026 a Permanent Job?

No. The Pharmacist engagement is purely contractual and does not constitute regular RBI employment. The maximum engagement period is 240 days at a stretch. Pharmacists may be engaged only when required. Selection for the panel does not guarantee daily employment. The engagement does not provide a right to permanent or temporary appointment in RBI. Facilities available to regular RBI employees are not applicable. RBI may discontinue utilization of the services at any time without notice or assigning a reason. Candidates should consider these conditions before applying, particularly if they are looking for a permanent banking job with regular monthly pay.

RBI Pharmacist Job Location and Duty Hours

Selected candidates may be assigned to one or more RBI dispensaries situated in Mumbai and Navi Mumbai. RBI will decide the dispensary allocation based on its requirements. Duty hours will depend on the assigned dispensary, subject to a maximum of five hours per day under the advertised remuneration terms. Candidates must be prepared to work at the locations allocated by the Bank.
