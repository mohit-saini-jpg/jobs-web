Educational Qualification

The applicant must be a literate woman who has completed at least Class 10. The educational qualification may be relaxed if no suitable woman possessing the prescribed qualification is available in the concerned area.

Eligibility for Rural Areas

The applicant must be a woman resident of the concerned village. Preference will be given to married, widowed, divorced or separated women. The preferred age group is 25 to 45 years. The candidate should have effective communication and leadership skills. She should be able to connect with and support the local community. She should have family and social support to perform ASHA duties. The minimum educational qualification is Class 10.

Eligibility for Urban Areas

The applicant must be a woman resident of the identified slum or vulnerable cluster. She should preferably belong to the vulnerable group for which the ASHA is being selected. Preference will be given to married, widowed, divorced or separated women. The preferred age group is 25 to 45 years. The candidate should have communication and leadership skills. She should be fluent in the local language of the population she will serve. The minimum educational qualification is Class 10.

East Godavari District ASHA Recruitment 2026 : Age Limit

Candidates should preferably be between 25 and 45 years of age. The notification allows relaxation of the age and educational criteria if a suitable woman meeting the prescribed conditions is unavailable in the concerned area.

Salary

The notification does not specify the salary or honorarium payable to the selected ASHA workers.

East Godavari District ASHA Recruitment 2026 : Application Fee Details

No application fee is mentioned in the notification.

East Godavari District ASHA Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

The selection process will include the following stages: Scrutiny of applications and supporting documents Preparation of the provisional merit list Receipt and consideration of objections Publication of the final merit list Final selection and issue of permission to the concerned Medical Officers The VHNC or VHSNC resolution and approval for the applicant’s area must be enclosed with the application.

Documents Required

Class 10 or SSC certificate as proof of date of birth and qualification Residence certificate or ration card Resolution and approval of the VHNC/VHSNC of the respective area Latest caste certificate issued by the concerned Mandal Revenue Officer, if applicable Aadhaar card Latest disability certificate issued by the Medical Board through SADAREM, if applying under the PH quota Other supporting documents required in the application form

Application Submission Address

The completed application and documents must be handed over to the concerned PHC or UPHC for the notified village, ward or urban area. The application form on page 4 is addressed to: The District Medical and Health Officer East Godavari District Rajamahendravaram, Andhra Pradesh

East Godavari District ASHA Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

Only women candidates are eligible. The candidate must be a resident of the respective notified village, ward, slum or vulnerable cluster. Applications must be submitted to the concerned PHC or UPHC. The last date for receipt of applications is 25 September 2026. Candidates should attach all required certificates and the local committee resolution. Applicants without the required caste certificate will be treated as Open Category candidates. The number of vacancies may increase, decrease or be cancelled based on the District Selection Committee’s decision. Candidates should provide correct information in the application form. Providing incorrect information may result in cancellation or termination.
