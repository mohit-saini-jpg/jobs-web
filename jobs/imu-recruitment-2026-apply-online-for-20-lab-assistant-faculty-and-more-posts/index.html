About IMU Mumbai

Indian Maritime University is a Central University under the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways, Government of India. The posts are at its Mumbai Port Campus (L.B.S. CAMSAR & MERI), Hay Bunder Road, Sewri, Mumbai - 400 033, which runs pre-sea courses such as B.Tech (Marine Engineering) and PGDME and post-sea competency, modular and simulator courses, as named in the duties of the faculty posts.

IMU Mumbai Recruitment 2026 Job Description

Teaching / Training to Post-Sea Courses (Competency, Modular & Simulator). Teaching / Training to Pre-sea Courses (B.Tech [Marine Engineering] & PGDME) & Post-Sea Courses (Competency, Modular & Simulator). To instruct students / assist the Faculty in Workshop / Simulators, etc. Maintenance, augmentation and upkeep of Workshop equipment / devices. Training of Inland Vessel Crew. Project-related work. To instruct students / assist the Faculty in Workshop / Simulators, etc. Maintenance, augmentation and upkeep of Workshop equipment / devices. Project-related work. Maintenance of Gym. Training of cadets / students. Regular upkeep of Gym. Conduct of Gym-related activities.

Documents Required

Detailed CV with educational qualifications, work experience, special achievements, present address and expected salary. All relevant documents. Signed bio-data in the IMU-MPC format for the post applied for. Faculty posts: the form includes a checklist of essential and desirable qualifications, details of sailing experience at management level, teaching / training experience in Maritime Training Institute(s) in India, and a sailing and shore experience sheet (CDC period, rank and days) in chronological order. Paramedic, Workshop Instructor and Lab Assistant posts: the form asks for Aadhar No. and PAN Card No., essential qualification details, experience with total duration, and academic qualifications from Class X onwards.

IMU Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

No TA/DA will be admissible for the interview. IMU Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in IMU Mumbai Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 20 posts: 07 Faculty (Nautical Science), 05 Faculty (Marine Engineering), 01 Paramedic, 02 Workshop Instructor (Marine Engineering), 02 Workshop Instructor (Electrical Engineering), 02 Lab Assistant (Instructor) and 01 Gym Instructor.
Q2What is the last date to apply?
A
The CV must be sent by email by 18:00 hrs on 30 November 2026.
Q3What qualification is needed for Faculty (Nautical Science)?
A
Candidates need a Master (Foreign Going) Certificate of Competency issued by the Directorate General of Shipping, at least six months of sailing at Management level within the meaning of STCW Convention, and a VICT / TOTA Course Certificate or willingness to complete it before joining.
Q4What is the age limit for the posts?
A
The age limit is preferably below 62 years for both Faculty posts, below 55 years for Paramedic, below 57 years for the Workshop Instructor and Lab Assistant (Instructor) posts, and below 50 years for Gym Instructor.
Q5What is the salary for IMU Mumbai faculty posts?
A
The proposed remuneration for Faculty (Nautical Science), Faculty (Marine Engineering), Paramedic and Gym Instructor is "As per IMU Norms"; the notice prints no amount.
Q6How do I apply for IMU Mumbai Recruitment 2026?
A
Email your detailed CV, all relevant documents and the signed bio-data in the IMU-MPC format to [email protected], with the post applied for written in the subject.
Q7What is the selection process?
A
Selection is by physical interview, and candidates will be told the interview date by e-mail.
Q8Is TA/DA paid for attending the interview?
A
No. The notice states that no TA/DA will be admissible for the interview.
Q9Are these permanent posts?
A
No. The posts are advertised on contract basis, and the Gym Instructor post is on visiting basis.
Q10What qualification is needed for Workshop Instructor (Marine Engineering)?

A candidate must be a Marine Fitter from Merchant Navy, or a Petty Officer of Marine Engineering Branch from Indian Navy / Coast Guard, or a Near Coastal Voyage (NCV) Class IV Engineer.
