NICL Graduate Apprentice Stipend 2026

Selected apprentices will receive a fixed monthly stipend of Rs. 15,000 during the 12-month apprenticeship period. The Government component will be credited directly to the apprentice's bank account through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT), in accordance with applicable guidelines. Stipend Clarification: The Rs. 15,000 is an apprenticeship stipend, not a permanent employee salary. Apprentices are not entitled to other NICL employee allowances or benefits.

NICL Graduate Apprentice Training Duration 2026

The apprenticeship period is 12 months . Selected candidates will receive practical training at the NICL office allotted within their selected state. Apprentices are entitled to one day of leave for each month of training, credited at the beginning of the following month and subject to prior approval. Unused eligible leave may be carried forward. A maximum of four days of leave may be taken at one time. Unused accumulated leave expires when the contract ends. Approved leave does not result in stipend deduction. Other absences may result in proportionate stipend deductions. No. NICL clearly states that it has no obligation to offer regular employment during or after apprenticeship training. The contract ends after 12 months, and apprentices cannot claim permanent absorption on the basis of completing the programme.

NICL Graduate Apprentice Selection Process 2026

NATS Registration: Candidates register and complete their NATS student profile. Online Application: Eligible candidates apply for the National Insurance Company Limited apprenticeship opportunity. Merit-Based Shortlisting: BOPT (Eastern Region) shortlists candidates using graduation percentage marks. Email Intimation: Shortlisted candidates receive instructions through their registered email address. Document Verification: Candidates appear in person at the designated NICL office with original certificates and self-attested copies. Final Engagement: Selection is subject to eligibility verification, state-wise seat availability and approval of the apprenticeship contract in NATS. NICL will use the percentage of marks obtained in the qualifying graduation examination, calculated up to two decimal places . NICL Graduate Apprentice Document Verification 2026

Shortlisted candidates must attend document verification in person at the NICL office assigned to them. The date and location will be communicated through their registered email address. Applicants must carry original certificates and self-attested photocopies supporting their age, educational qualification, identity and other eligibility claims. Travel and Accommodation: NICL will not pay TA/DA for document verification. Boarding and lodging expenses are also not admissible. Being called for document verification does not guarantee final engagement. The company will verify the details entered in NATS against the original records before approving the apprenticeship contract.

NICL Graduate Apprentice Reservation Rules 2026

NICL will follow the applicable reservation guidelines under the Apprentices Act for SC, ST, OBC and PwBD candidates. OBC candidates claiming reservation must possess the prescribed Government of India Non-Creamy Layer certificate. The notification specifies that the certificate must be issued during the period from 1 April 2026 up to the date of engagement , if selected. OBC candidates belonging to the creamy layer are not entitled to OBC reservation benefits and should apply as General or General-PwBD, as applicable.

NICL Apprentice Contract and Service Conditions

Selected candidates must enter into a formal apprenticeship contract with NICL, approved through the NATS portal. Apprentices are trainees and will not be treated as regular employees of NICL. Employee benefits such as PF, ESI and bonus do not apply to this apprenticeship engagement as stated in the notification. The contract normally terminates automatically after 12 months. Misconduct, false information, failure to meet eligibility conditions or prolonged unauthorized absence may lead to earlier termination. If an apprentice remains absent without authorization for more than 15 consecutive business days, NICL may issue a three-day notice by email. If no satisfactory explanation is received, the company may terminate the apprenticeship contract under the notified conditions.
