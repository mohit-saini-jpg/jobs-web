UPSSSC Veterinary Pharmacist Eligibility 2026

As per Rule 9 of the UP Animal Husbandry Department Veterinary Pharmacist Service Rules, 2025, preference in direct recruitment will be given (all else being equal) to candidates who have: Served for a minimum of 2 years in the Territorial Army, OR Obtained a National Cadet Corps (NCC) 'B' Certificate Important: Candidates must possess the essential qualification and all other required eligibility as on the last date of application (05.10.2026), along with the corresponding proof/certificates.

UPSSSC Veterinary Pharmacist Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection is based solely on a written (Main) examination . Shortlisting for the Main Exam is done using the candidate's normalized PET-2025 score - category-wise, up to 15 times the number of advertised vacancies (including all candidates whose score matches the final cut-off). Total 100 objective, multiple-choice questions, 100 marks, single question paper, duration 2 hours (120 minutes) . Negative marking: ¼ (25%) of the marks for that question is deducted for each wrong answer. If the exam is held across multiple shifts/days, scores will be normalized as per the procedure notified vide Essential Information No. 42/05/Vigyapan Anu.(Eleven)/2019 dated 22 May 2019.

UPSSSC Veterinary Pharmacist Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Visit the official website upsssc.gov.in and locate the advertisement under the "Live Advertisement Segment" on the homepage.
  • 2Log in using your PET-2025 Registration Number and the OTP sent to your registered mobile number/email.
  • 3Personal details recorded in PET-2025 (name, parents' names, category, EWS, horizontal reservation details, date of birth, gender, marital status, contact details) will auto-populate.
  • 4Fill in the essential qualification (educational) details specific to this advertisement, including board/university name, year of passing, certificate number, and marks.
  • 5Enter the verification code and click Submit to generate your 11-digit application registration number; print a copy for your records.
  • 6Photo and signature uploaded during PET-2025 will display automatically (cannot be modified); click Continue.
  • 7Fill "Other Details" (preferential qualification, if any) and review the address details.
  • 8Read and accept the Declaration, tick the checkbox, enter the verification code, and click Save and Submit.
  • 9Pay the requisite fee via Credit Card / Debit Card / Internet Banking / UPI, or SBI e-Challan.
  • 10Once fee payment/reconciliation is successful, the application is auto-submitted. Download and print your final application form.
