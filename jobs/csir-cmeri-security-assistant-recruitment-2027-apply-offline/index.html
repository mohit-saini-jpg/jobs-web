CSIR-CMERI Security Assistant Recruitment 2027 : Job Overview

CSIR-Central Mechanical Engineering Research Institute (CSIR-CMERI), Durgapur, under the Council of Scientific and Industrial Research, has invited applications for one unreserved Security Assistant post (Post Code 260201) at its Centre of Excellence for Farm Machinery (CoEFM), Ludhiana. The post carries Pay Level-6 (Rs. 35,400 - 1,12,400) and is for ex-servicemen, JCOs in Army or equivalent in other Paramilitary Forces with five years' experience in security work. The signed form must be sent by Registered/Speed Post to reach Durgapur by 6.00 PM on 12 January 2027.

About the Institute

CSIR-CMERI, Durgapur is described in the notice as a premier institute under CSIR and the only national laboratory dedicated to mechanical engineering. Its mission is to develop cost-effective, value-added technologies in mechanical engineering and allied domains, and to contribute to national skill development initiatives. The advertised post is at its Centre of Excellence for Farm Machinery in Ludhiana. The notice states that CSIR encourages women candidates to apply.

Who Can Apply

The applicant must be a citizen of India. PwBD candidates are not eligible, as the notice says the job is not suitable for them. The post is unreserved. Annexure-A of the notice lists JCO ranks and pay levels and the equivalence between Armed Forces and Paramilitary Forces. Sub Inspector in Pay Level 6 is treated as equivalent to Naib Subedar in Army, and Inspector in Pay Level 7 as equivalent to Subedar. Pay is counted at the Level 6 or 7 of the post substantively held, not one granted under financial upgradation such as MACPS. The upper age limit is 28 years. Age, qualifications and experience are counted as on the last date for submission of applications, 12.01.2027, including for candidates from far flung areas. CSIR-CMERI Security Assistant Job Description

The notice gives the job requirement as responsibility for day-to-day security arrangements of the Centre, including: Office premises and campus, guest house, staff quarters and similar buildings of the Centre. Intelligence, vigilance, firefighting and safety standards of the estate, buildings, residential quarters, office premises, guest house and reception. Round-the-clock vigil and regulation of movement of persons and goods to and from the Centre campus. Liaison with local Police and other authorities, audit, and monitoring of the security contract. Supervising and controlling the regular/outsourced security personnel deployed for security duty, and maintenance of records. Any other work assigned by the Competent Authority.

Selection and Interview

Selection has three steps after scrutiny: the applications are first scrutinised prima facie on the documents sent. Candidates with the prescribed qualifications and age are then invited to a Physical and Personality Assessment Test, which is qualifying in nature. Those who qualify are invited to a Competitive Written Examination. The final merit list is prepared on written examination performance. The notice does not mention an interview.
