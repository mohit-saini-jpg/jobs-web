BDCC Bank Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must be citizens of India. Applicants must be able to read, write, and speak Kannada. Applicants can apply for only one post; if a candidate applies for more than one post, only the first submitted application is considered valid. Candidates already in service must submit a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from their current employer.

BDCC Bank Second Division Clerk Educational Qualification

Applicants must hold a Bachelor's degree (BA/B.Sc/B.Com) from a university legally established and recognized in India. Basic knowledge of computer operation and applications is required for this post; if a candidate has completed at least one computer-related course, the certificate for it may be submitted voluntarily.

BDCC Bank Gun Man Educational Qualification

Applicants must have passed SSLC/10th standard or an equivalent examination, and should either be retired armed-forces personnel with weapons training or an ex-serviceman, and must hold a valid Arm License.

BDCC Bank Peon Educational Qualification

Applicants must have passed SSLC/10th standard or an equivalent examination.

BDCC Bank Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection is through a Written Examination followed by an Interview, conducted as per the method prescribed by the Karnataka State Cooperative Societies Registrar's office. A merit list is prepared based on written exam marks. Candidates are shortlisted for interview in a 1:5 ratio against each post. The interview carries a maximum of 15 marks. The final merit list is prepared by combining written exam marks and interview marks, and candidates are selected as per the applicable reservation policy. Details of the exam admission letter, date, and exam schedule will be announced on the bank's website.
