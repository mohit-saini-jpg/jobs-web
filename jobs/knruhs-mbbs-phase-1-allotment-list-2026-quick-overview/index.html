KNRUHS MBBS Phase 1 Allotment List 2026 - Quick Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 04/09/2026

Kaloji Narayana Rao University of Health Sciences (KNRUHS), Telangana, Warangal, has released the college-wise allotment list for MBBS admissions under the Competent Authority Quota (CQ) for the Academic Year 2026-27, on 3rd September 2026 First Phase of Counselling. The list shows candidates allotted a seat, arranged college-wise by NEET-UG state merit rank, roll number, category, and admission code.

How to Download the KNRUHS MBBS Phase 1 Allotment List

  • Go to the official KNRUHS website: knruhs.telangana.gov.in
  • Open the "Notifications" section on the homepage.
  • Look for "MBBS Admissions Under Competent Authority Quota for AY 2026-27 - College Wise Allotment List - First Phase of Counselling."
  • Click the link to open or download the PDF.
  • Use Ctrl+F (or your device's search/find function) within the PDF to locate your roll number or name faster, since the list is organized college-wise and rank-wise, not alphabetically.

How to Check Your Individual Allotment Status Online

Beyond the college-wise PDF, candidates can also check their personal allotment status by logging in with their TS NEET UG registration number and password on the counselling portal.

Next Steps After Allotment: Fee Payment and Reporting

Based on recent, publicly reported KNRUHS counselling procedure (not stated in the allotment PDF itself), candidates allotted a seat are generally expected to:

  • Pay the university fee online - reported at approximately ₹12,000 - to generate the provisional seat allotment order.
  • Download and print the official Provisional Seat Allotment Order, checking the college name, course, category, and reporting deadline carefully.
  • Physically report to the Principal of the allotted medical college within the deadline mentioned on the allotment letter, carrying original documents for verification.

Important Instructions for Allotted Candidates

  • Candidates who receive a Phase 1 seat but fail to pay the university fee or report within the given timeframe risk automatic cancellation of admission and may be barred from exercising options in subsequent counselling rounds.
  • Candidates admitted to Government Medical Colleges are generally required to submit a service bond, as mandated by state health regulations, committing to compulsory post-MBBS rural service.
  • Candidates wishing to upgrade their college in a later round must first accept and report to their Phase 1 allotment before participating in Phase 2 upgradation.
