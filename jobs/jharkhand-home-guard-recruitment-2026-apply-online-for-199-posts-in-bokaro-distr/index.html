About Bokaro Home Guard Recruitment 2026

The notice states that the Home Guard is a voluntary organisation and Home Guards are its volunteer members. They are paid an allowance at the rate fixed by the government only for the working days on which they are deployed on duty as required. The notice clearly says that this is not a salaried government job or a means of livelihood, and applications are expected from candidates ready to serve the nation selflessly. The enrolment is conducted by the Home Guard New Enrolment Committee, Bokaro, chaired by the Deputy Commissioner, with the Superintendent of Police as senior member and the District Commandant as member. Under the Jharkhand Home Guard (Volunteer) Rules, 2014, Home Guards are enrolled for 4 years.

Vacancies by Project / Block

The notice states that the vacancies may decrease or increase. Of the 199 posts, 111 are for Rural Home Guards and 88 for Urban Home Guards. Chas block has the most rural vacancies (40), followed by Chandankiyari (23). In both categories, 50 percent of the posts are earmarked for women, as shown in the tables above. Of the posts earmarked for women, 5 percent are reserved for qualified divorced, abandoned or widowed women. If such candidates are not available, these posts merge back into the women's share. 50 percent of the Urban Home Guard vacancies are for technically skilled candidates. If suitable candidates are not found, the non-technical and technically skilled vacancies will be filled from each other. Government servants can be enrolled as Urban Home Guards up to a limit of 10 percent of the total urban vacancies.

Who Can Apply

Rural Home Guard: the candidate must be a permanent resident of the block of Bokaro district from which the application for Rural Home Guard is made. Urban Home Guard: the candidate must be an ordinary resident of the urban area of Bokaro district. Both must attach a self-attested copy of the online residence certificate issued by the competent authority authorised by the State Government. Candidates may apply only from their own block or urban area. The age limit for both posts is 19 to 40 years as on 01-01-2026. A self-attested copy of the date of birth certificate issued by the competent authority is required. The notice does not mention any age relaxation. Jharkhand Home Guard Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection follows the procedure fixed by the selection committee in light of the Home Department, Government of Jharkhand, Ranchi notification no. 5442 dated 20.10.2014. The stages are: physical test, Hindi writing ability test, technical proficiency test (technically skilled urban candidates only), merit list, medical examination and police character verification, and basic training. The physical test includes a 1 mile run, high jump, long jump and shot put. Candidates who pass the physical test take the Hindi writing ability test, set at the class 7 level for Rural Home Guards and the class 10 level for Urban Home Guards. It is qualifying, with full marks 100 and pass marks 30. Only technically skilled Urban Home Guard candidates who pass the physical test and the Hindi writing test take this test. Jharkhand Home Guard Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Detailed information on applying online is available at http://recruitment.Jharkhand.gov.in, the website provided by JAP IT, Ranchi.
  • 2Apply online on that website from 27 November 2026 to 11 December 2026. Rural and Urban Home Guard applications are submitted separately in the prescribed form.
  • 3Fill in every detail asked for in the online form according to the eligibility.
  • 4Give the details of one of these: voter ID card, driving licence, PAN card or Aadhaar card. Aadhaar card is desired.
