IIM Jammu Chief Administrative Officer Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Indian Institute of Management Jammu is filling one Chief Administrative Officer (CAO) post (UR, Regular/Contract) at Level 12, pay scale Rs. 78,800 - 2,09,200 with entry pay Rs. 78,800 and allowances as admissible to Central Government employees. Applicants need at least 15 years of post-qualification experience, including 10 years at Senior Administrative Officer level. Apply online by 10 December 2026 with a fee of Rs. 590, then send the printed hard copy to CAO Office, IIM Jammu Jagti, Jammu 181221.

About IIM Jammu

Indian Institute of Management Jammu is a management institute whose campus is at Jagti, Jammu. The notice lists the CAO post under its Non-Faculty Positions. The CAO provides administrative support for all the Institute's activities.

Who Can Apply

Post-graduate degree in any discipline with at least Second Class (55% marks), preferably with a post-graduate diploma in management from a reputed University/Institute, or Engineering graduates with a post-graduate diploma in management. Pay and Terms

The post is at Level 12 with pay scale Rs. 78,800 - 2,09,200 and entry pay Rs. 78,800. In addition to basic pay, the post carries allowances as admissible to Central Government employees. A higher start in the pay scale may be considered for exceptionally deserving candidates. The post is advertised as Regular/Contract. IIM Jammu Chief Administrative Officer (CAO) Job Description

Provide total administrative support for all the activities of the Institute. Lead a team of officers in the Institute and guide them in their day-to-day activities. Coordinate with the academic and administrative activities, including personnel, general administration, estate management, engineering, construction, stores and purchase, and contract services. Conduct the Board and Society meetings and follow-ups. Act as the First Appellate Authority under the RTI Act, 2005. Have thorough knowledge of the GeM portal, GFR 2017, FR&SR and relevant Central Government rules and procedures.

IIM Jammu Chief Administrative Officer Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

For the post of Chief Administrative Officer, selection is made based on personal interviews. The interview may be online or offline as decided by the Administration, and any written test will be in offline mode. Only short-listed candidates will be contacted. Meeting the minimum qualifications and experience does not give any right to be called for the interview/written/skills test. The Institute's decision to call candidates is final. Outstation candidates called for interview are paid II-AC Class Railway Fare from the present/mailing address to the place of interview and back by the shortest route on production of proof of travel. Local travel is not reimbursed and no stay is provided by IIM Jammu.
