HLL Operations Manager Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

HLL Lifecare Limited, a Government of India enterprise under the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, is recruiting one Operations Manager (Electrical) for its HCD (Projects & Services – Division) on an Executive – Fixed Term Contract, with posting at Chennai. Basic pay is Rs. 30,000 – 50,000 per month. BE / B.Tech in Electrical or Diploma in Engineering (Electrical) with experience is required. Applications open from 03.11.2026 and the last date is 18.11.2026, through a Google Form.

About HLL

HLL Lifecare Limited is a Government of India enterprise under the administrative control of the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, and commenced operations on 5 April 1969. The notice describes it as an integrated healthcare organisation with a presence in healthcare manufacturing, retail pharmacy, diagnostics, imaging services, healthcare infrastructure development, procurement consultancy, hospital management and allied services. It gives the HLL Group's annual business turnover as approximately ₹6,500 crore.

Who Can Apply

The post is open to Indian Nationals only. Candidates holding either of the two essential qualifications below may apply, each with its own experience requirement and age limit. The experience, for both degree and diploma holders, must be in the planning, design, engineering, execution, installation, testing, commissioning, operation and maintenance of electrical infrastructure for hospitals, medical colleges, super-specialty healthcare facilities, diagnostic centres, laboratories and allied healthcare projects. SC/ST/OBC/PwD candidates are eligible for relaxation as per Government of India directives. The notice says the date for determining the age limit is the same as the cut-off date for calculating qualification and post-qualification experience. HLL Operations Manager (Electrical) Job Description

Design: Prepare and review electrical designs, SLDs, load calculations, BOQs and estimates, coordinating with architects and other engineering teams. HT/LT systems: Design, supervise and commission 11/33 kV substations, transformers, panels and LT distribution, with proper earthing and IS compliance. Critical power: Ensure uninterrupted, redundant supply (DG, AMF, ATS, UPS) to OTs, ICUs, cath labs, MRI/CT, emergency and other critical areas. Building services: Coordinate power needs for HVAC, lifts, fire-fighting, labs (BSL-2/3) and ELV systems such as fire alarm, CCTV, BMS and nurse call. Execution: Supervise contractors, review submittals and drawings, run integrated testing and commissioning, and ensure handover documentation.
