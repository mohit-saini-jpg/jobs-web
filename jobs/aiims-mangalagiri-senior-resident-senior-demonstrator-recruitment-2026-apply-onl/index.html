AIIMS Mangalagiri Senior Resident / Senior Demonstrator : Job Overview

An addendum dated 10 November 2026 from the All India Institute of Medical Sciences, Mangalagiri, Andhra Pradesh (an autonomous institute under MoHFW, Govt. of India) revises the vacancy matrix for Senior Residents / Senior Demonstrators to 45 posts across 24 departments. Applications are submitted by Google Form, with the application forms (Annexure I and II) and supporting documents, by 21 November 2026 (Saturday), 5:00 PM. The fee is Rs. 1500 for General/EWS/OBC and Rs. 1000 for SC/ST; PwBD candidates pay no fee.

What the Addendum Changes

The addendum publishes a REVISED VACANCY MATRIX for the Senior Resident / Senior Demonstrator posts. In the matrix, "#" marks new vacancies. The total across all categories is 17 UR, 9 OBC, 9 SC, 5 ST and 5 EWS, which makes 45. The addendum also sets the closing date and the application procedure and fee (below).
