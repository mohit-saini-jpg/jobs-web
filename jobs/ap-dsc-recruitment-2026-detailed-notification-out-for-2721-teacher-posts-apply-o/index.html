Who Can Apply for AP DSC 2026? Eligibility Criteria

Educational qualifications are post-specific and governed by the relevant recruitment rules and Information Bulletin. Applicants should not assume that qualification for SGT also qualifies them for SA, TGT, PGT, Principal, PD, PET or Special Education posts. Check the appropriate bulletin for the required academic degree, professional teacher-training qualification, subject or medium, TET eligibility where applicable, and any special education recognition requirements. The notifications direct candidates to the Information Bulletin for the complete post-wise qualifications. Applicants must meet the prescribed qualifications by the applicable notification cutoff; check the relevant bulletin before paying the fee.

AP DSC Application Fee 2026

The application fee is ₹1,000 for each post . Candidates applying for more than one post should check the relevant application instructions and the total payable amount before submitting payment. The official payment window runs from 11 to 30 November 2026.

AP DSC Selection Process and Minimum Qualifying Marks

The selection method depends on the post. The regular SA (except Physical Education) and SGT notifications specify aggregate TRT (80%) and qualifying TET (20%) marks. The societies notification specifies TRT (80%) and TET (20%) for TGT, while Principal, PGT, PD and PET qualifying marks are based on TRT (100%). Do not apply the same weightage mechanically to every post. These are minimum qualifying thresholds, not guaranteed selection cutoffs. The societies notification also provides an English Language Proficiency screening test for Principal, non-language PGT and non-language TGT candidates: 60/100 marks for OC, BC and EWS; 50/100 for SC, ST, PwBD and ex-servicemen. The notification excludes TGT Special Education (Special Schools) from this English screening test. AP DSC Syllabus 2026 and Exam Pattern

The Government of Andhra Pradesh has released a detailed suggestive syllabus covering SGT, SGT Special Education, School Assistants, TGT, TGT Special Education, PGT, Principals and Physical Education posts. Candidates should prepare using the syllabus for their exact post and subject; the department states that the topics are indicative rather than exhaustive. The official syllabus also includes separate subject coverage for School Assistant languages, Mathematics, Physical Science, Biological Science and Social Studies; distinct TGT and PGT papers; English proficiency where applicable; Principals; PET; and SA-PE/PD.
