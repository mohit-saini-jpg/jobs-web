Pay and Tenure

Appointments are made on a contractual basis, renewable yearly or up to the duration of the project, whichever is earlier. A 5% relaxation of marks may be granted to SC/ST candidates. For retired or superannuated government employees selected for a post, salary will be fixed as per prevailing IRD norms.

Selection and Interview

IIT Delhi reserves the right to fix higher criteria for short-listing eligible candidates based on advertised qualifications and project requirements. Short-listed candidates will be displayed on the web link (http://ird.iitd.ac.in/shortlisted) along with online interview details. Only short-listed candidates will be informed for the online interview.
