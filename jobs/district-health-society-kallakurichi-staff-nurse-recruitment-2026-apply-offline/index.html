District Health Society Kallakurichi Staff Nurse : Job Overview

The District Health Society, Kallakurichi (Maavatta Nalavaazhvu Sangam, Kallakurichi Maavattam) is filling 17 Staff Nurse posts on a purely temporary contract basis under the National Health Mission. The posts are in the Comprehensive Emergency Obstetric and Newborn Care (CEmONC) centres, including SNCU and PICU, and pay a consolidated Rs. 21,000 per month. Candidates with GNM or B.Sc Nursing, registered with the Tamil Nadu Nursing Council, may submit the application form in person or by speed post from 21 November 2026; it must reach the District Health Office by 04 December 2026, 5.00 PM.

About Kallakurichi DHS Staff Nurse

The District Health Society, Kallakurichi works under the National Health Mission, and its Chairman is the District Collector of Kallakurichi. These Staff Nurse posts are in CEmONC centres, including the SNCU and PICU units. The centres are run under the control of the Director of Medical and Rural Health Services and the Director of Medical Education and Research. The posts are filled under the National Health Mission guidelines.

Who Can Apply

Registration with the Tamil Nadu Nursing Council is mandatory. Knowledge of Tamil, the official language, is mandatory. Proof is the SSLC (10th) or HSC (12th) mark sheet showing Tamil studied as a subject. Age: not exceeding 50 years as on 20.11.2026. The notice does not mention any age relaxation. For the full eligibility conditions, candidates should check the official notification.
PostEssential Qualification
Staff NurseDiploma in Nursing (GNM) or B.Sc Nursing from a Government nursing college or a Government-recognised private nursing college, recognised by the State Government.

Pay and Tenure

The Staff Nurse gets a consolidated pay of Rs. 21,000/- per month. The notice does not mention HRA or any other allowance. The post is purely temporary and on contract under the National Health Mission guidelines. Under the NHM-TN contract rules, the selected nurse must serve at least 2 years in the post. The contract is renewed after every 11 months, based on a performance appraisal. A nurse who wants to resign must give 3 months' prior notice. Selected nurses must sign a declaration that the post is purely temporary and contractual, will not be regularised for any reason, and has no chance of becoming a permanent post.

Kallakurichi DHS Staff Nurse Job Description

The notice does not list the duties. It says the duties and working hours will be fixed separately by the Mission Director, National Health Mission (State). This is done together with the Director of Public Health and Preventive Medicine, the Director of Medical and Rural Health Services, the Director of Medical Education and the Commissioner of Greater Chennai Corporation.

Selection and Interview

The District Health Society has the right to call suitable applicants for certificate verification / interview. The notice does not give the date for this.

District Health Society Kallakurichi Staff Nurse : Important Instructions

Selected candidates must join immediately or on the date given in the Offer Letter. If a nurse is found guilty of serious misconduct or dereliction of duty, the District Health Society will remove the nurse immediately, without notice or compensation. Staying away from work for 15 continuous days without the prior permission of the competent authority is treated as abandoning the job and leads to automatic removal.
