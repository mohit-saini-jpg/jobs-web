BEML Eligibility Criteria

Only Indian Nationals may apply. Candidates with Degree in Engineering from allied branches of Mechanical / Electrical / Electronics / CSE / IT can also apply, provided they possess the requisite Post Qualification Experience mentioned. Assistant Manager – T&C Rolling stock (Electrical & Mechanical): Minimum 4 years of post-qualification experience in rolling stock (Metro / Commuter Rail etc.) with at least 2 years of experience related to Testing, Commissioning & Maintenance of Rolling Stock. BEML Important Instructions

Candidates shall apply for a single position which is most suitable. First class is reckoned at 60% as an aggregate of marks of all semesters/years, relaxable by 5% for SC/ST & PwD candidates. Candidates with CGPA/Credit must provide conversion to percentage. OBC (NCL) certificate must not be older than six months as on the last date of online application submission. Experienced candidates must provide a pen picture of minimum 150 words for each experience. Eligible candidates shortlisted for assessment must undergo pre-employment medical examination and document verification. Canvassing or influencing officials will result in immediate disqualification.
