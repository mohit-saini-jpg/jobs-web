GSSSB Staff Nurse Eligibility 2026

The candidate must be a citizen of India, or possess nationality as provided under Rule-7 of the Gujarat Civil Services Classification and Recruitment (General) Rules, 1967. Important: Candidates lacking a valid computer-knowledge certificate may still apply, but must submit proof of passing a basic computer knowledge test before appointment; failure to do so will lead to cancellation of selection. Candidates without Gujarat Nursing Council registration must obtain it before appointment.

GSSSB Staff Nurse Selection Process

Selection is through a single-stage competitive written examination (MCQ), conducted in two papers as per the Health & Family Welfare Department's exam rules: Each paper has 100 multiple-choice questions of 1 mark each. Paper I may be answered in Gujarati or English; Paper II is in Gujarati only. Negative marking: ¼ mark deducted for each wrong answer. Qualifying standard: Minimum 40% separately in Paper I and in Paper II, for all categories. The merit list is prepared on the combined marks of both papers, for candidates who clear both qualifying standards. Document verification is called for approximately twice the number of vacancies per category, based on provisional merit. A provisional answer key is published after the exam, followed by a final answer key after considering objections; cancelled questions are handled on a pro-rata basis. GSSSB Staff Nurse Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Complete OTR (One Time Registration) on ojas.gujarat.gov.in if not already registered — only OTR-registered candidates can apply.
  • 2Go to "Online Application" → click Apply → select GSSSB.
  • 3Select Advertisement No. 458/2026-27, Staff Nurse, Class-3 and click Apply.
  • 4Read the "More Details" notification carefully before proceeding.
  • 5Click "Apply Now", enter your Registration No. and date of birth, and click "Apply with OTR" to open the application format; fill in Personal Details.
  • 6Fill in Educational Details in the next step.
  • 7Accept the declaration/assurance ("Yes") and click Save to generate the Application Number.
  • 8Upload photograph (5 cm × 3.6 cm, white background, dated, taken within one year before the last date) and signature (2.5 cm × 7.5 cm), in JPG format under 15 KB each.
  • 9Click "Confirm Application" after a final review — no edits are possible after confirmation; a Confirmation Number is generated.
  • 10Print and keep the confirmed application; download it before the last date as it cannot be downloaded afterwards.
  • 11Pay the requisite exam fee online by 15 October 2026, 11:59 PM and save the receipt.
