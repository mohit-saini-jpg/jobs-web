CVRDE Paid Internship Eligibility Criteria

Regular students pursuing an Engineering Degree in B.E/B.Tech (7th/8th semester) or M.E/M.Tech (1st/2nd year) full-time course in the relevant discipline from a recognised Indian University/Institute. Only regular students from AICTE-approved technical institutions or UGC-approved universities are eligible to apply. Candidates should have a minimum 8.5 CGPA (on a 10-point scale) or 85% marks, maintained continuously in all previous semesters. Students with backlogs are not eligible. The scheme is open only to students holding Indian nationality. Students already receiving a stipend or similar benefit from any other institute are not eligible for this internship programme.

CVRDE Paid Internship Qualification

Applicants must be regular full-time students of a recognised Indian University or Institute, currently pursuing B.E/B.Tech in the 7th or 8th semester (UG category) or M.E/M.Tech in the 1st or 2nd year (PG category), in one of the four disciplines listed above: Mechanical, Electronics/Electronics & Communication/Electronics & Instrumentation, Electrical, or Computer Science/AI/ML/IT. No equivalent qualification or related branch will be considered.

CVRDE Paid Internship Age Limit

Below 28 years as on 1st July 2026. To avoid any confusion regarding age eligibility, candidates can use our Age Calculator to calculate their exact age based on the prescribed cut-off date before applying.

CVRDE Paid Internship Salary / Stipend

Selected interns will receive a monthly stipend of Rs. 5,000/- for the six-month duration. The stipend is paid in two slabs of three months each, with Rs. 15,000/- credited directly to the student's bank account per slab. No boarding, lodging, or transport allowance is provided; students must make their own arrangements. A wet canteen facility is available at CVRDE for lunch and tea on a payment basis.

CVRDE Paid Internship Selection Process

Selection is entirely on a merit basis, considering the candidate's academic track record (≥85% marks or ≥8.5 CGPA in every previous semester). Shortlisted students will be intimated through their registered e-mail ID along with joining instructions. Candidates who are not selected will not receive any communication. CVRDE/DRDO reserves the right to change the vacancy positions or selection criteria at any point during the selection process without assigning any reason.
