Bastar Police Bastar Fighter Constable Selection Process 2026

Selection is carried out in several stages, in this order: Document verification Physical measurement Physical Efficiency Test (100 marks) Written examination (50 marks) Interview (20 marks) Bonus marks (up to 30) The test has five events, each worth 20 marks, for a total of 100 marks: Long jump High jump Shot put 100 metre race 800 metre race Rules for the test: Candidates get only one chance in each event. Taking part in all events is compulsory. Unreserved candidates must score 60% of the 100 marks. SC, ST and OBC candidates must score 50% of the 100 marks. A candidate who is not satisfied with the assessment may appeal to the selection committee. The appeal will be decided immediately, and the committee's decision is final.
