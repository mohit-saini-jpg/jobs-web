TNSCPS Data Entry Operator Recruitment 2026: Can 12th-Pass Candidates Apply?

Yes. Candidates who have passed Class 12 and possess a Diploma or Certificate in Computers can meet the educational qualification requirement for Assistant cum Data Entry Operator. However, the notification also requires at least one year of experience working with a Government or Non-Government organization. Therefore, passing Class 12 and having a computer certificate alone do not satisfy all the listed eligibility requirements.

Is Work Experience Mandatory for TNSCPS Data Entry Operator?

Yes. The official notification requires at least 1 year of experience working with a Government or Non-Government organization for the Assistant cum Data Entry Operator post. Applicants should provide the relevant experience details and supporting certificates in the prescribed application form.

TNSCPS Recruitment 2026 Job Responsibilities

The Assistant cum Data Entry Operator will support data management and administrative functions relating to child welfare and protection activities. The responsibilities include: Maintaining databases, archives and filing systems. Creating and exporting spreadsheets, documents and data reports. Maintaining monthly reports in coordination with District Child Protection Units. Managing registers, email records and e-office files. Checking documents and data for errors or discrepancies. Updating State and Central child-related portals. Handling scanning, printing, filing and related clerical work. Working with MS Office, Google Suite and other office-management software. The Program Officer will assist in coordinating child protection activities, training, monitoring, project implementation and related welfare activities at State and District levels.
