About PESCO

Punjab Ex-Servicemen Corporation (PESCO) is a Punjab Government Undertaking with its office at SCO 89-90, Sector 34-A, Chandigarh. The notice is issued under the heading "Government of Punjab", and in it PESCO says it requires medically fit retired Defence Services personnel on contractual basis. The Waiter post is in the Department of Punjab Ex-Servicemen Corporation at this Chandigarh address.

Who Can Apply

The single Waiter post is reserved for ex-servicemen: the notice describes it as "Only Ex-Servicemen of waiter trade" and asks for "An experienced retired Defence Services Waiter (Waiter Trade)". Candidates must meet all of the following conditions printed in the notice: Retired Defence Services personnel who are medically fit. Experienced Waiter of the Waiter Trade in the Defence Services. Working knowledge of Punjabi. Punjab Domicile. Proficiency in waiter trade. The official notification does not specify the minimum educational qualification. The bio-data format, however, asks candidates to state their civil education ("Education Civil") and the year of "Matric with Punjabi". Candidates should check the official notification for the full eligibility of the post. Candidates must be less than 55 years as on 01 Nov 2026. The notice does not mention any age relaxation.

Selection and Interview

According to the notice, only eligible and shortlisted candidates will be called for interview. The interview date will be intimated later. The notice does not mention a written test or any other stage.
