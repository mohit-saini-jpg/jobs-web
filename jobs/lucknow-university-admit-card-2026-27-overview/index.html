How to Download Lucknow University Admit Card 2026-27?

Students can follow these steps to access the Lucknow University odd-semester admit card:

  • Visit the official admit card page at exam.lkouniv.ac.in/apps/Public/AdmitCardList.
  • Open the Admit Card List for Session 2026-27.
  • Enter your Roll Number or Lucknow University Registration Number (LURN).
  • Enter your Date of Birth in DD/MM/YYYY format.
  • Enter the CAPTCHA code displayed on the screen.
  • Click the Submit button.
  • Check the examination admit card information returned by the portal.
  • Open the relevant admit card if available.
  • Verify the examination details and download or print the hall ticket.

Important: The public admit card search page requires a Roll Number or LURN, Date of Birth and CAPTCHA. A password is not required on this particular public search form.

Details to Check on Lucknow University Admit Card 2026-27

Lucknow University Admit Card 2026-27 Not Downloading?

If the admit card search does not return the expected information, students should check their login details and confirm that their examination records are available.

  • Enter the correct Roll Number or LURN Number.
  • Check the Date of Birth in DD/MM/YYYY format.
  • Enter the CAPTCHA accurately.
  • Ensure that the 2026-27 admit card portal is being used.
  • Check the relevant course examination notices.
  • Contact the department or college if the admit card remains unavailable.
  • Use the university's examination portal technical helpline for technical issues.

Students should not assume that a missing admit card automatically means cancellation of their examination registration.
