Karnataka PGCET 2026 Extended Counselling - Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 31/10/2026

The Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) has announced an extended counselling round for admission to MBA, MCA and M.Tech programmes. The fresh option-entry facility opens on 31 October 2026 at 2:00 PM, and eligible candidates must submit their college and course preferences by 4 November 2026 at 2:00 PM. The provisional seat allotment result is scheduled to be released on 5 November 2026 after 1:00 PM. Candidates can check counselling updates through the official KEA website.

Karnataka PGCET 2026 Fresh Option Entry Rules

The most important requirement of the extended round is that candidates must submit fresh choices.

  • Options submitted in earlier counselling rounds will not be considered for the extended allotment.
  • Candidates must enter their preferred colleges and courses again.
  • Choices should be arranged according to the candidate's genuine order of priority.
  • Candidates must review the latest available vacancy information before entering their preferences.
  • The option-entry facility opens at 2:00 PM on 31 October 2026.
  • Fresh options must be submitted by 2:00 PM on 4 November 2026.
  • Only eligible candidates who satisfy applicable payment and participation requirements should submit options.

How to Enter Fresh Options for Karnataka PGCET 2026?

Eligible candidates can follow these general steps to participate in the extended counselling round.

  • Visit the official KEA website at cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea/.
  • Find the Karnataka PGCET 2026 Extended Round Counselling notification.
  • Read the eligibility conditions, caution deposit instructions and option-entry schedule.
  • Open the extended-round candidate login facility.
  • Enter the registration or other login details requested by the portal.
  • Check whether payment of the caution deposit is required for your category and earlier admission status.
  • Complete the applicable caution deposit payment within the prescribed period.
  • Review the latest vacant seat matrix and available courses.
  • Enter fresh college and course options in order of preference.
  • Review all entered choices carefully.
  • Save and submit the options according to the instructions displayed on the official portal.
  • Keep the available acknowledgement or submission details for future reference.

How to Check Karnataka PGCET 2026 Extended Round Allotment?

Once KEA releases the provisional result, candidates can follow these steps.

  • Visit the official KEA website.
  • Open the Karnataka PGCET 2026 counselling updates.
  • Find the Extended Round Provisional Seat Allotment Result link.
  • Open the result or candidate login facility.
  • Enter the details requested by the portal, if applicable.
  • Check the allotted course, institute and category.
  • Read the provisional allotment and subsequent admission instructions.
  • Save the result information for reference.

Important Instructions

  • Extended counselling has been announced for MBA, MCA and M.Tech admissions.
  • Fresh option entry begins on 31 October 2026 at 2:00 PM.
  • The final deadline for submitting fresh options is 4 November 2026 at 2:00 PM.
  • Earlier counselling preferences will not carry forward.
  • The caution deposit payment period is reported as 31 October to 3 November 2026.
  • The caution deposit is ₹35,000 for General Category and ₹10,000 for SC/ST candidates.
  • Candidates who have already joined their allotted college are exempted from the additional caution deposit.
  • Eligible candidates can participate for new allotment or seat upgradation according to their previous admission status.
  • Some earlier allottees must complete reporting or cancellation by 31 October 2026 at 6:00 PM.
  • The provisional result is scheduled for 5 November 2026 after 1:00 PM.
  • Allotment, cancellation and refund conditions must be checked from the official KEA instructions.

Karnataka PGCET 2026 (Karnataka Posts) Expert Analysis

Extended counselling ka fayda ye hai ki late applicants ko bhi second chance milta hai, isliye abhi se apna rank check karke seat allocation process ko closely follow karein.

Who Should Apply Karnataka PGCET 2026 (Karnataka Posts)

Agar aapke paas Karnataka PGCET 2026 ka valid rank hai aur MBA, MCA ya M.Tech ke liye admission chahte hain, to aapko is round me apply karna chahiye.

Karnataka PGCET 2026 (Karnataka Posts) Application Tips

1. Apna rank card, 10th/12th ya graduation certificates aur ID proof ready rakhein. 2. KEA portal par login karke application form fill karein, sabhi details verify kar lein. 3. Application fee online pay karne ke baad confirmation slip save kar lein.

Karnataka PGCET 2026 (Karnataka Posts) Fee & Scholarship Details

Course fee MBA ke liye approx ₹1.5 lakh, MCA ₹80,000 aur M.Tech ₹1 lakh hai. Merit based scholarships aur fee waiver schemes KEA dwara eligible candidates ke liye available hain.

Karnataka PGCET 2026 (Karnataka Posts) Course & Career Insights

MBA se aap management consulting, marketing ya finance me leadership roles ke liye tayar ho sakte hain. MCA se software development, data analytics ya IT project management me future growth hoti hai. M.Tech se research, R&D aur specialized engineering fields me high potential career milta hai.

Karnataka PGCET 2026 (Karnataka Posts) Admission Strategy

Seat allocation merit rank ke basis par hoti hai, jo PGCET entrance test score se decide hoti hai; koi interview ya additional test nahi hota.
