GMDC Job Profile: Key Responsibilities (Post-wise)

The Boiler Expert will act as a technical advisor to industrial clients, visiting plant sites to audit boiler performance and recommend efficiency improvements. Key responsibilities include: Conducting on-site technical audits of operating boiler systems and identifying efficiency bottlenecks. Assessing compatibility of client boilers with lignite fuel blends and guiding operational adjustments. Suggesting design and engineering modifications to improve heat transfer and fuel mixing. Engaging with plant stakeholders and explaining the return on investment of recommended changes. Training sales managers and boiler operators on boiler fundamentals. This role requires being based in Gujarat or willing to relocate, with extensive travel (more than 15 days a month) to industrial zones such as Ahmedabad, Surat, Morbi, and Dahej. GMDC Recruitment 2026 Terms of Contract

Across all posts, the engagement does not confer any right to regular appointment in the organization. The contract can be terminated by either side at any time by giving one month's prior notice or pay in lieu of the notice period.
