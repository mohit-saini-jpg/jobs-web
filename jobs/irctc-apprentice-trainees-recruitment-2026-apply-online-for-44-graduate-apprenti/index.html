IRCTC Apprentice Trainees Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation (IRCTC), East Zone, Kolkata has invited applications for 44 apprentice trainees for the financial year 2026–27 under the Apprentices Act: 34 Graduate Apprentices (any degree with 50% aggregate) at a stipend of Rs. 12,300 per month, and 10 Computer Operator and Programming Assistant (COPA) apprentices (10th plus ITI with 50%) at Rs. 9,600 per month, each for 12 months. Candidates must be between 15 and 25 years on 9 October 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories.

Eligibility Criteria

Graduate Apprentice: a graduate in any discipline from a recognised university with at least 50% marks in aggregate . COPA: Matriculation and an ITI certificate in COPA affiliated to NCVT or SCVT, with at least 50% marks in aggregate from a recognised board. The qualification must be held on 9 October 2026 , the cut-off date. Candidates whose final results are not declared by then do not meet it. Because selection is purely on marks, the percentage you enter and the mark sheets you upload decide the outcome. Candidates must have completed 15 years and not completed 25 years on 9 October 2026 , which means a date of birth between 10 October 2001 and 9 October 2011 (both inclusive) for unreserved candidates. IRCTC Apprentice Trainees Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Merit list prepared on percentage of marks in the qualifying examination. There is no written test and no viva. Tie-break: the older candidate is preferred; if dates of birth are the same, the candidate who passed Matriculation earlier. Document verification: candidates bring originals and two sets of self-attested photocopies. The schedule is advised by email only, at short notice. No daily, conveyance or travelling allowance is paid. Offers are issued strictly in merit order. A stand-by list is used only to fill places of absentees and rejected candidates. Candidates are trained only in the trade for which they are selected.

Reservation Notes

Reservation follows Government of India guidelines for SC, ST, OBC, PwBD, ex-servicemen and EWS, with the age relaxations above. EWS: candidates not covered by SC/ST/OBC-NCL reservation whose family gross annual income (all sources, for the financial year before the application year) is below Rs. 8 lakh. The income and asset certificate must be issued by one of the authorities the notice lists (District Magistrate or equivalent, Presidency Magistrate, Revenue Officer not below Tehsildar, or the Sub-Divisional Officer of the area) and must be held on the closing date, 26 October 2026 . OBC: non-creamy layer certificate issued within the last one year. PwBD: disability of 40% or more.

Q1Is there a written test or interview?

No. Selection is a merit list on percentage of marks, followed by verification of original documents.
Q2How many total vacancies are available in IRCTC Recruitment 2026?

There are a total of 44 vacancies available for Graduate Apprentice and Computer Operator & Programming Assistant (COPA) positions across various zonal and regional offices.
Q3Is there an application fee?
A
The notification does not mention any fee. It also warns that IRCTC has appointed no agents and that candidates should not pay anyone.
Q4What is the age limit?
A
Completed 15 and not completed 25 years on 9 October 2026, with relaxation of 5 years for SC/ST, 3 for OBC, and 10 for ex-servicemen and PwBD candidates.
