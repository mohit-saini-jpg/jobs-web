TNDTE GTE August Result 2026 - Overview : Result Overview

The Directorate of Technical Education (DoTE), Tamil Nadu, has declared the Government Technical Examination (GTE) - TME August 2026 results on 11 November 2026. Candidates who appeared for Typewriting, Shorthand and Accountancy examinations can check their results through the official website at tndtegteonline.in.

TNDTE GTE August Result 2026 - Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 12/11/2026

The department has released a subject-wise result PDF along with an online facility for checking individual examination results. According to the official result statistics, 1,47,749 candidates appeared in the August 2026 TME examination, of whom 97,782 passed. The overall pass percentage stands at 66.18%. Among the successful candidates, 44,799 received Distinction, 36,806 secured First Class and 16,177 obtained Second Class. The department has also withheld 156 results.

Candidates can check their results online using their register number and date of birth or search the official 225-page result PDF for their examination roll number. Applications for answer-script photocopies are scheduled from 12 to 18 November 2026, while the revaluation application period is reported as 19 to 25 November 2026. Applicants should check the applicable instructions and fees on the official portal.

How to Check TNDTE GTE August Result 2026 Online?

Candidates can check their individual marks through the Government Technical Examination results page.

  • Visit the official website at tndtegteonline.in.
  • Open the Results section.
  • Select the TME examination category.
  • Choose the August 2026 examination cycle.
  • Enter the examination register number.
  • Enter the date of birth in the format requested by the portal.
  • Click the option to view the result.
  • Check the displayed examination marks and result status.
  • Download or save the result where the portal provides an option.
  • Keep a printed copy for future reference.

How to Download TNDTE GTE August Result 2026 PDF?

The Directorate has released a consolidated result PDF containing subject-wise statistics and successful candidates' roll numbers.

  • Open the official August 2026 TME result PDF.
  • Locate the section corresponding to your examination subject and grade.
  • Use Ctrl + F on a computer or the PDF search feature on a mobile device.
  • Enter your examination roll number exactly.
  • Check the heading under which your roll number appears.
  • The published categories include Distinction, First Class, Second Class and Withheld.
  • Save the PDF if required.

TNDTE GTE August 2026 Result Withheld

The official result statistics show that 156 results have been withheld across the August 2026 TME examinations.

When a result is marked Withheld, the department has not published the candidate's final regular result in the usual pass classification.

A withheld result is not necessarily the same as a failed result. Candidates should contact the concerned institute or Directorate for the reason applicable to their case.

Applicants should follow the department's instructions for any required clarification or verification.
