5How to Check Rajasthan University Result 2026 Online
Sr.TypeTextStyleItems
1paragraphFollow these steps to check your B.Com, B.Com (Hons), or BBA Part-II/Part-III result:
2listordered
  • Go to the official result portal: result.uniraj.ac.in.
  • On the homepage, locate the result link matching your exact course and part - for example, "B.Com Part-II Exam-2026" or "B.B.A. Part-III Exam-2026."
  • Click on the relevant link. You will be redirected to a result login page for that specific exam.
  • Enter your roll number and/or name exactly as printed on your admit card.
  • Submit the form to view your result on screen.
  • Check all details carefully, then download and save or print the result/marksheet for your records.
