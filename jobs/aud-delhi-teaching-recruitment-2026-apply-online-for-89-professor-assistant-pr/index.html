AUD Delhi Selection Process

A Scrutiny/Screening Committee reviews all applications and prepares a list of eligible and not-eligible candidates based on stipulated criteria. The eligible/not-eligible lists (with reasons) are published on the University website; not-eligible candidates may submit grievances with documentary evidence within a stipulated deadline. For Assistant Professor posts, shortlisting for interview uses an Academic Score matrix (Table 3A of UGC 2018 Regulations) covering Graduation, Post-Graduation, M.Phil., Ph.D., NET/SLET/SET, research publications, teaching/post-doctoral experience and awards - maximum 100 marks. This score is used only for shortlisting; final selection is based on interview performance. AUD Delhi Teaching Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Visit the official University recruitment portal (linked on aud.delhi.gov.in).
  • 2Fill the application form mandatorily in online mode within the prescribed time limit indicated in the advertisement.
  • 3Pay the applicable fee online through credit/debit card, net banking or the payment gateway provided; apply and pay separately for each discipline if applying to more than one.

AUD Delhi Teaching Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many teaching posts has AUD Delhi advertised?
A
AUD has advertised 89 posts - 24 Professor, 28 Associate Professor and 37 Assistant Professor - across 14 Schools, under Advertisement No. AUD/55-1(67)/Rect./2026/838 dated 31.07.2026.
Q2Is NET mandatory for Assistant Professor posts at AUD?
A
Candidates need NET/SLET/SET, or a Ph.D. meeting the specified UGC 2009/2016 exemption conditions, or a Ph.D. from a foreign university ranked in the global top 500 (QS/THE/ARWU).
Q3What is the application fee for AUD Delhi recruitment?
A
₹1,000 (non-refundable) for UR/OBC/EWS candidates; no fee for SC, ST, PwD and Women candidates.
Q4Is there a written exam for AUD Delhi teaching posts?
A
No. Selection is through screening/shortlisting of applications followed by an interview (and, where required, a presentation/seminar); there is no written examination.
Q5What is the pay level for Assistant Professor at AUD?
A
Assistant Professor posts carry Academic Pay Level-10 (₹57,700–₹92,500), Associate Professor Level-13A (₹1,31,400–₹2,10,800), and Professor Level-14 (₹1,44,200–₹2,18,200).
Q6Can a candidate apply for more than one discipline?
A
Yes, but each discipline requires a separate application and separate fee payment.
Q7Where should the hard copy of the application be sent?
A
To the Deputy Registrar (Recruitment & Promotion Cell), Room No. 31A, Dr. B.R. Ambedkar University Delhi, Lothian Road, Kashmere Gate Campus, Delhi – 110006, with the post/discipline name mentioned on the envelope.
