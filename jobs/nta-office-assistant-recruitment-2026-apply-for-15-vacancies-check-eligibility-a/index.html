NTA Office Assistant Job Profile and Responsibilities

Office Assistants will provide administrative and clerical support to facilitate the smooth functioning of NTA offices and assigned sections. The role involves maintaining records, handling official correspondence and assisting with routine administrative activities. Selected candidates may also be assigned additional office-related duties by the reporting officer or competent authority.
