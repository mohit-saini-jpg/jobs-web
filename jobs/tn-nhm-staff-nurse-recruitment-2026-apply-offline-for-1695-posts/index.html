TN NHM Staff Nurse Recruitment 2026 COVID-19 Pass Marks Clarification

NHM Tamil Nadu has also issued a clarification dated 24 September 2026 regarding candidates whose SSLC or HSC marksheets show only "PASS" during the COVID-19 examination period. When an SSLC marksheet contains only "PASS", the weightage may be calculated using the candidate's HSC marks according to the prescribed formula. Similarly, when an HSC marksheet contains only "PASS", the candidate's SSLC marks may be used for the corresponding weightage calculation. For example, where a candidate scored 480 out of 600 in HSC and the SSLC marksheet shows only "PASS", the same 80% may be used to determine the SSLC weightage of 16 marks and HSC weightage of 16 marks. Important: This clarification applies only where original marksheets issued by the competent examining authority record "PASS" without numerical marks. TN NHM Staff Nurse Recruitment 2026 Contract Period

The Staff Nurse engagement is purely temporary and contractual under National Health Mission Tamil Nadu. The minimum service period prescribed for the position is two years , subject to the contractual terms. The contract will be renewed after completion of every 11 months based on performance appraisal and other applicable conditions. Important: The appointment does not confer any right to permanent absorption, regularization or permanent Government employment.

TN NHM Staff Nurse Recruitment 2026 Posting and Joining

The official recruitment instructions state that the place of posting will preferably be close to the candidate's place of residence. However, the final posting will depend on vacancies, institutional requirements and the decision of the District Health Society or Greater Chennai Corporation. Selected candidates must report to the District Health Officer within three days of receiving the appointment order. At the time of joining, candidates must submit a Physical Fitness Certificate issued by an officer not below the rank of Civil Surgeon.
