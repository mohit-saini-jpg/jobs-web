Haryana Labour Divyang Help Fund Yojana Form Overview

Ye scheme specially physically challenged labourers ke liye hai, jo unko financial support deti hai. Form online ya offline milta hai, aur apply karna bahut simple hai.

Haryana Labour Divyang Help Fund Yojana Form Expert Analysis

Scheme ka maksad disabled workers ko livelihood support dena hai, taaki wo apni daily expenses aur medical costs cover kar saken. State government ne iske liye clear guidelines aur timely disbursement ka promise kiya hai.

Who Is Eligible: Haryana Labour Divyang Help Fund Yojana Form

Jo bhi Haryana ka physically disabled labourer hai, jiska annual income Rs. 1 lakh se kam hai, wo is yojana ke liye eligible hai.

Haryana Labour Divyang Help Fund Yojana Form Application Process

1. Apna disability certificate, Aadhar aur income proof ready rakhein. 2. Form ko dhyan se fill karein, sabhi fields ko correct data se bharein. 3. Required documents ko scan karke PDF format me upload karein ya physical copy ke saath submit karein.

Haryana Labour Divyang Help Fund Yojana Form Benefits Details

Yojana ke tahat eligible beneficiaries ko Rs. 10,000 se Rs. 25,000 tak ka monthly financial assistance milta hai, jo unke basic needs aur medical kharchon ko cover karta hai.

How Haryana Labour Divyang Help Fund Yojana Form Works

Application submit hone ke baad, local Labour Department verification karta hai, phir fund ko bank account me direct transfer kiya jata hai. Disbursement har mahine hota hai, jab tak eligibility criteria meet hota rahe.

Haryana Labour Divyang Help Fund Yojana Form Application Tips

Form fill karte waqt sabhi details double-check karein, galat spelling ya missing documents se rejection ho sakta hai. Aadhar aur disability certificate ka valid copy zaroor attach karein, aur deadline se pehle submit karein.

Haryana Labour Divyang Help Fund Yojana Form FAQs

Q1Is scheme ke liye online application kaise karein?
A
Official Haryana Labour Department website par jaake 'Divyang Help Fund' section me form download karein, required details fill karein, documents upload karein aur submit button dabayein.
Q2Fund kitna time me milta hai?
A
Verification ke baad, usually 15-20 din ke andar bank account me direct credit ho jata hai.
Q3Agar income increase ho jaye to kya fund band ho jayega?
A
Haan, agar annual income Rs. 1 lakh se upar ho jati hai, to aapko scheme se withdraw karna padega aur future disbursement stop ho jayega.
