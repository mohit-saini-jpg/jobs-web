AEEE 2027 Registration - Overview : Job Overview

Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham has started the online registration process for the Amrita Entrance Examination - Engineering (AEEE) 2027. Candidates seeking admission to B.Tech programmes at the university can complete their application forms through the official website, aeee.amrita.edu. The registration process commenced on 7 November 2026 and is currently open.

AEEE 2027 Registration - Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 11/11/2026

According to the university's official admission schedule, AEEE 2027 will be conducted from 15 April to 7 May 2027 in computer-based test (CBT) mode at examination centres across India. Candidates who have passed Class 12 or are appearing for an equivalent examination in 2027 can apply, provided they meet the prescribed Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics eligibility criteria.

The university has provided an online registration facility through which applicants can enter their personal and academic details, upload required documents, select their preferred examination cities and pay the application fee. The official website lists an application fee of ₹1,300 for the AEEE route. Candidates can also seek B.Tech admission through their JEE Main 2027 percentile. The last date for registration has not yet been officially announced.

How to Apply Online for AEEE 2027?

The official Amrita Online Application Portal provides registration, personal information, application and payment stages.

  • Visit the official AEEE website at aeee.amrita.edu.
  • Click the Register Now or Apply Online option.
  • Open the official application portal at aeee.amrita.edu/app/.
  • Enter a valid email address or mobile number.
  • Click Get OTP and complete the verification.
  • Enter the candidate's full name, date of birth, social status and other personal details requested by the portal.
  • Provide the required contact and address details.
  • Upload a recent photograph and digital signature.
  • Enter the parent or guardian's name, email address and mobile number.
  • Select the state from which the candidate studied Class 12.
  • Open the application section and select the AEEE examination.
  • Choose three preferred examination cities, as required by the official portal.
  • Select the JEE Main 2027 option also, if the candidate wishes to use that admission route.
  • Review the application details carefully.
  • Pay the applicable registration fee through the portal.
  • Complete the submission process and save the available confirmation details.

Candidates must complete payment to finish the registration process. They should retain their payment acknowledgement and avoid making advance payments to unauthorised admission agents.

AEEE 2027 Marking Scheme

  • Correct Answer: +3 Marks
  • Incorrect Answer: -1 Mark
  • Total Questions: 100
  • Maximum Marks: 300
  • Examination Duration: 2 Hours 30 Minutes

The test is conducted in English. Candidates should practise solving questions within the prescribed duration and account for negative marking while attempting uncertain answers.

Students should prepare Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry thoroughly and also practise Quantitative Aptitude and English.

AEEE 2027 Admission Through JEE Main

Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham also accepts JEE Main percentile scores for eligible B.Tech admissions.

Candidates may seek admission using:

  • AEEE 2027 Rank
  • JEE Main 2027 Percentile
  • Both Routes, Subject to the University's Application Rules

The official portal confirms that candidates applying through AEEE may also opt for admission consideration through JEE Main 2027.

Applicants using only JEE Main should follow the registration and fee instructions applicable to that route.

Important: AEEE is not mandatory for candidates seeking admission through an accepted JEE Main score. However, registration for Amrita's admission process is still required.

AEEE 2027 Exam City Selection

The official registration instructions require AEEE applicants to select three preferred examination cities.

Candidates should choose their city preferences carefully based on travel convenience and examination availability.

The final allotted examination centre may depend on the available slots and the university's scheduling arrangements.

Applicants should not assume that selecting a city guarantees a specific examination centre.

AEEE 2027 Slot Booking

After completing the application, registered candidates will be required to follow the slot-booking process announced by the university.

Slot booking enables candidates to select an available examination date and time within the prescribed schedule.

As of 11 November 2026, the exact AEEE 2027 slot-booking dates have not been officially confirmed.

Students should monitor the candidate portal and their registered email addresses for further instructions.

AEEE 2027 Counselling and Seat Allotment

B.Tech seats at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham are allotted through the Centralised Seat Allotment Process (CSAP).

Admission consideration is based on the candidate's AEEE rank or JEE Main percentile, along with applicable eligibility conditions, programme preferences and seat availability.

The general admission process includes:

  • Complete AEEE or JEE-based registration.
  • Appear for AEEE or submit the relevant JEE Main details.
  • Check the published examination result or admission ranking.
  • Register for CSAP when notified.
  • Submit campus and programme preferences.
  • Participate in seat allocation.
  • Complete the required admission fee payment and document verification.

What If AEEE 2027 Registration Is Not Working?

Applicants experiencing registration difficulties should first verify that they are using the official online application portal.

  • Open aeee.amrita.edu/app/.
  • Check the registered email address or mobile number.
  • Request a fresh OTP if the portal permits it.
  • Verify the OTP and continue the application.
  • Check the required document format before uploading.
  • Complete all mandatory profile and application fields.
  • Review the payment status before attempting another payment.
  • Contact the official admission support team if the problem persists.
