About NCL Recruitment 2026

Northern Coalfields Limited is a listed Public Undertaking and a subsidiary of Coal India Limited under the Ministry of Coal, Government of India, with its headquarters at Post- Singrauli Colliery, Distt: Singrauli, M.P., PIN 486889. The notice states that NCL scaled up to 140.50 MT of coal production. This internship engagement is part of the national skill development initiative and is run by the HRD Department, with the notice signed by the General Manager (HRD), CETI, NCL HQ, Singrauli.

NCL Selection Process 2026

Merit list: prepared by arranging the aggregate scores of the qualifying examinations (Degree/Diploma) in descending order for each discipline. Tie-breaking: a candidate older in age (earlier date of birth) is preferred; if a tie persists, the candidate whose name appears first alphabetically in English (Roman script) is preferred. Document verification: shortlisted candidates will be notified via the PMIS portal and the NCL official website. The notice mentions no written examination or interview for this engagement. Before joining, selected candidates must submit a Medical Fitness Certificate in the prescribed format from a Government Medical Officer.

NCL Recruitment 2026 Important Instructions

The internship training under PMIS lasts 09 months from the date of engagement. The engagement does not lead to any claim for regular employment in NCL or CIL, and interns will not be absorbed into regular service of NCL or CIL under any circumstances. Interns must make their own arrangements for accommodation, food and transport during the training period. Leave entitlements are governed by the applicable PMIS guidelines. Suppression of facts or submission of false certificates will lead to immediate cancellation of candidature.
