GSSSB Surveyor Physical Standards 2026

The Surveyor post requires minimum physical measurements. Candidates should check these standards carefully because they form part of the prescribed recruitment eligibility. Male candidates must have a minimum chest expansion of 5 cm. The recruitment rules require candidates selected through direct recruitment to complete a prescribed walking test at the appointment stage. Candidates unable to complete the walking test or absent from the test may receive one additional opportunity under the notified conditions. Failure to qualify or attend on the additional opportunity will result in cancellation of appointment. Important: The walking test is conducted by the appointing authority at the appointment stage after GSSSB recommendation. It should not be confused with the competitive written examination.

GSSSB Surveyor Selection Process 2026

Selection will be based on a single-stage competitive MCQ examination conducted through CBRT or OMR mode, followed by document verification and preparation of the selection and waiting lists. Appointed candidates must also satisfy the physical and walking-test requirements. Competitive Examination - MCQ-based CBRT or OMR examination. Provisional Result and Merit Preparation. Document Verification of eligible shortlisted candidates. Preparation of Selection List and Waiting List. Physical Eligibility and Walking Test at the appointment stage. Candidates must meet the minimum qualifying standard separately in both examination parts. Approximately twice the number of category-wise vacancies may be called for document verification, subject to the Board's recruitment procedure.

GSSSB Surveyor Exam Pattern 2026

The competitive examination consists of 210 multiple-choice questions for 210 marks , divided into Part A and Part B. The total duration is three hours. Important: Candidates must secure at least 40% separately in Part A and Part B. The minimum qualifying standard cannot be reduced below 40% for any category under the notified rules.
