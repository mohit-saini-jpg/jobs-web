KGBV Banda Recruitment 2026 Notification PDF

The Office of the District Basic Education Officer (Zila Basic Shiksha Adhikari), Banda, Uttar Pradesh, has officially released the KGBV Banda Recruitment 2026 notification dated 25.07.2026 for the recruitment of 77 vacancies under Kasturba Gandhi Balika Vidyalayas (KGBVs). The notification includes posts such as Principal, PGT Teachers, Computer Science Teacher, Care Taker, Lab Assistant, Office Superintendent/Clerk, Assistant Cook, Peon, and Watchman for the 2026–27 academic session. It contains complete details regarding post-wise vacancies, eligibility criteria, age limit, educational qualifications, salary, selection process, reservation, required documents, and the offline application procedure. KGBV Banda Recruitment 2026 - Eligibility Criteria

Only women candidates can apply; all posts are reserved for female applicants. Candidates convicted of a moral/character offence, dismissed from any service, or declared insolvent are not eligible. Candidates with physical disability, contagious disease, or serious illness are not eligible for the Care Taker, Peon, Watchman and Assistant Cook posts, since night stay at the school is compulsory for these roles. Maximum age for continued service in a Kasturba Gandhi Balika Vidyalaya is 60 years.

KGBV Banda Recruitment 2026 - Application Fee

No application fee is mentioned in the official notification. The only cost specified is for the correspondence envelope: candidates must attach a self-addressed envelope bearing a postage stamp of ₹58, along with the application, for receiving counselling/verification communication.

KGBV Banda Recruitment 2026 - Selection Process

Selection for teaching and academic posts (Principal, PGT subjects, Care Taker) is made on the basis of a merit/weightage score calculated as follows: If two or more candidates have equal merit marks, the older candidate (by age) is ranked higher. If merit marks and age are both equal, ranking is done alphabetically by name (English alphabetical order). Selection for Assistant Cook, Peon, and Watchman posts is made through an interview . All submitted certificates are verified with the concerned board/institution before final selection; original documents are kept safe at the District Project Office.

KGBV Banda Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Download the prescribed application form from the Banda district website: https://banda.nic.in, or collect it from the notice board of the BSA office.
  • 2Fill the application form clearly by hand; overwriting and cutting are not acceptable.
  • 3Fill in the candidate's name, father's/husband's name, and date of birth exactly as recorded in the High School certificate.
  • 4Attach self-attested photocopies of all required educational, training, and category certificates.
  • 5Mention a valid mobile number and email ID, since all counselling and verification communication will be sent only to these.
  • 6Submit a separate application for each post applied for.
  • 7Write the post name and subject applied for on the top of the envelope, and enclose a second, self-addressed envelope with a ₹58 postage stamp.
  • 8Send the completed application only by registered post to: Office of the District Basic Education Officer, Civil Lines, Banda, Banda District, Uttar Pradesh - PIN 210001.
  • 9Ensure the application reaches the office on or before 9 August 2026, by 5:00 PM. Applications received after the due date, or through any other medium, will not be accepted.
