MAT December 2026 Exam Date - Quick Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 28/09/2026

The MAT December 2026 Paper Based Test (PBT) is scheduled for 20 December 2026, and the Computer Based Test (CBT) is scheduled for 27 December 2026. Registration closes on 14 December for PBT and 21 December for CBT. Applications are submitted online at mat.aima.in.

The Management Aptitude Test (MAT) is held by the All India Management Association (AIMA) four times a year, in February, May, September and December. MAT December 2026 Registration

Registration is completed online at mat.aima.in. The last date to register is 14 December 2026 for PBT and 21 December 2026 for CBT. The date on which registration opened is not available in the schedule checked.

Documents needed for MAT registration

  • A valid email ID
  • Scanned photograph
  • Scanned signature
  • Online payment method for the fee

How to apply for MAT December 2026

  • Go to mat.aima.in.
  • Open the registration link for the December 2026 session once it is live.
  • Create a login with your email ID and fill in personal and academic details.
  • Choose your test mode (PBT, CBT or both) and your test cities.
  • Upload your scanned photograph and signature.
  • Pay the application fee online.
  • Submit the form and save a copy of the confirmation.

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Check the official website for the latest schedule, as dates can change.
  • Use a valid email ID that you check regularly.
  • Keep your photo and signature scans ready before starting the form.
  • Enter personal details exactly as they appear in your documents.
  • Choose your test mode and cities carefully.
  • Carry your admit card to the exam centre.
  • Do not rely on unofficial sources for dates or fees.
