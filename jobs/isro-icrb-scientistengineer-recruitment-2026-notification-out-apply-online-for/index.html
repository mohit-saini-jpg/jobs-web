ISRO Scientist Engineer SC Vacancy 2026 – Post-Wise Details

A total of 175 vacancies have been notified for Scientist/Engineer 'SC' across six post codes, covering constituent ISRO Centres and the autonomous body PRL. The number of vacancies notified is tentative, and ISRO reserves the right to increase or decrease this number, with any change notified through a corrigendum on the ISRO website. An asterisk (*) against a PwBD figure denotes a backlog vacancy. If suitable PwBD candidates are not available in a notified disability category, the vacancy may be filled by interchange among the five PwBD categories (A to E), and if no suitable PwBD candidate is available at all, the post will be filled by a candidate other than a person with disability.

ISRO Scientist Engineer SC Eligibility Criteria and Qualification

Eligibility for ISRO Scientist Engineer SC Recruitment 2026 is assessed on educational qualification, nationality, and age as on 16-09-2026. The notification clarifies that graduation must be completed within the stipulated duration of the course as prescribed by the university. Allied disciplines acceptable under each stream are listed in the FAQs published on the ISRO website (not detailed in this notification). Where a university's certificate mentions both CGPA and percentage, at least one criterion must meet ISRO's eligibility norms; where only CGPA is mentioned, that CGPA itself must meet the norm, and conversion of CGPA to percentage is not permitted for determining eligibility (and vice versa where only percentage is mentioned). Separate rules for Dual/Integrated Degree programmes are also specified in the notification. Only Indian nationals are eligible to apply.

ISRO Scientist Engineer SC Application Fee

There is a non-refundable application fee of ₹250 for all posts. However, all candidates must initially and uniformly pay ₹750 as a processing fee at the time of applying online. The application fee is collected only in online mode through the Bharatkosh e-payment gateway, using Internet Banking, Unified Payment Interface (UPI), or Domestic Debit Cards. Cash, cheque, draft, money order, or IPO payments are not accepted. Refunds will be credited to the bank account details furnished by the candidate in the application; ICRB/ISRO will not be responsible for refund issues arising from incorrect bank details entered by the candidate. Refund will not be considered if a candidate does not appear for the Written Test/CBT.

ISRO Scientist Engineer SC Selection Process

Selection for ISRO Scientist Engineer SC Recruitment 2026 is based on a Written Test/CBT followed by an Interview. The Written Test/CBT carries a total of 100 marks and will be conducted at 37 venues across India, including Agartala, Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Bhopal, Bhubaneshwar, Chandigarh/Mohali, Chennai, Dehradun, Delhi, Ernakulam, Gorakhpur, Gurugram, Guwahati, Hyderabad, Indore, Jaipur, Jammu, Jodhpur, Kanpur, Kavaratti, Kolkata, Lucknow, Mangaluru, Mumbai, Mysore, Nagpur, Patna, Port Blair, Pune, Raipur, Ranchi, Shillong, Tiruchirapalli, Thiruvananthapuram, Varanasi, Vijayawada, and Visakhapatnam. ICRB reserves the right to change or cancel a test venue and reallot candidates to another centre. Q1What is ISRO Scientist Engineer SC Recruitment 2026?
