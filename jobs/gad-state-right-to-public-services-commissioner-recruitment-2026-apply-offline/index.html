GAD State Right to Public Services Commissioner : Job Overview

The Government of Maharashtra is inviting applications for 2 State Right to Public Services Commissioner posts, one each for the Nashik and Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar Divisions. These are senior appointments for eminent persons in public life with at least 20 years of experience in the Government or a public authority. The basic salary is Rs. 2,25,000/- per month. Applications go to the General Administration Department in Mumbai by post, in person or by email.

About Maharashtra State Commission for Right to Public Services

The Commission was established by the Government of Maharashtra under the Maharashtra Right to Public Services Act, 2015, and has its headquarters in Greater Mumbai. It comprises the State Chief Commissioner and six State Commissioners for Right to Public Services. One State Commissioner is provided for each Revenue Division, excluding the areas of Mumbai City and Mumbai Suburban Districts.

Maharashtra Right to Public Services Commissioner Eligibility 2026

The applicant must be at least 45 years of age and must not exceed 62 years of age, counted as on the last date of submission of the application. The applicant must be a graduate in any discipline. The applicant must be an eminent person in public life with wide knowledge and experience in administration in the Government or a public authority, as stipulated under Section 13(5) of the Maharashtra Right to Public Services Act, 2015. A minimum of 20 years of experience in the functioning of the Government or a public authority is required. GAD State Right to Public Services Commissioner : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Applications received up to the last date are scrutinised by the Secretary-level Scrutiny Committee constituted under Government Circular No. RTS-1120/CR-55/18 (RWK), dated 23 October 2020. The shortlist prepared by this committee is submitted to the Committee constituted under Section 13(3) of the Act, chaired by the Hon'ble Chief Minister, for the final selection process.

Service Conditions

A State Commissioner holds office for five years from the date of assuming office, or until attaining the age of 65 years, whichever is earlier (Section 14(1) of the Act). A State Commissioner is not eligible for reappointment. Where a Commissioner receives pension or retirement benefits, these are adjusted in accordance with the applicable provisions (Section 14(4) of the Act).

GAD State Right to Public Services Commissioner : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1Can I apply for both the Nashik and Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar posts?
A
Yes. Item 10 of Form 'A' lets you choose Nashik, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar or both.
Q2Is any degree accepted for this post?
A
Yes, a graduate in any discipline meets the educational requirement, but the applicant must also have the required 20 years of experience.
Q3On which date is my age counted?
A
Age is counted as on the last date of submission of the application, and the permitted range is 45 to 62 years.
Q4What is the total term of the appointment?
A
The term is five years or until the age of 65, whichever comes first. There is no second term because a Commissioner cannot be reappointed.
Q5I am a serving government employee. How should I apply?
A
Applicants serving in the State Government, Central Government or any other organisation must apply through the proper channel. This means going through the concerned Administrative Ministry, Department or Competent Authority before the last date.
Q6Does the application have to reach the office by the deadline?
A
Yes. It must reach the concerned office by 6:00 p.m. on the last date, and applications received later shall not be considered.
Q7Who makes the final selection?
A
The final selection process is carried out by the committee chaired by the Hon'ble Chief Minister, which considers the shortlist prepared by the Scrutiny Committee.
Q8Which language can I use in the application?
A
Particulars can be submitted in either Marathi or English.
