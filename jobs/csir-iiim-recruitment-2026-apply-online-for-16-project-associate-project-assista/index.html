About CSIR-IIIM

CSIR-Indian Institute of Integrative Medicine is described in the notice as a premier institute working under the aegis of the Council of Scientific & Industrial Research (CSIR), an autonomous body under the Department of Scientific & Industrial Research, Ministry of Science & Technology, Government of India. Its address in the notice is Canal Road, Jammu, Jammu & Kashmir - 180001. The notice is dated 26 November 2026 and is signed by the Controller of Administration.

Vacancy Details

The notice lists 16 positions across eight position codes. Position code 02 is printed as "Project Associate-II (01 position) OR Project Associate-I (03 positions)"; the 16 counts both. The notice does not print category-wise vacancies. The number of positions may vary with project requirements at the time of interview. If no suitable candidate is found for the Project Associate-II position, the notice says it may be downgraded to Project Associate-I.

Pay and Tenure

The notice calls the pay a stipend, stated per month in rupees. Pay (a) is for candidates who have qualified CSIR-UGC / ICAR / ICMR NET including Lecturership/Assistant Professorship, or GATE, or national level examinations conducted by Central Government departments like DBT/DST or equivalent and/or their agencies or institutions. Pay (b) is for those who do not fall under (a). HRA will be paid only if institutional accommodation is not availed or provided. Engagement is initially for one year or until the project duration, whichever is earlier, and may be extended or curtailed depending on the project duration, requirements and performance or conduct. The tenure is co-terminus with the project, and the total tenure, including engagement in all CSIR labs, shall not exceed six years. Selection is on the basis of an interview. If a large number of candidates apply, the Screening Committee may revisit the screening criteria if required. The interview will be held in hybrid mode (online and offline), and candidates may interact with the Selection Committee in English or Hindi. The notice does not print an interview date.

CSIR IIIM Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many positions are advertised in CSIR-IIIM Recruitment 2026?
A
The notice lists 16 project positions across eight position codes, including 1 Senior Project Associate, 1 Project Associate-II, 11 Project Associate-I and 2 Project Assistant-II, under Advertisement No. 44/2026.
Q2What is the last date to apply?
A
The last date is 08 December 2026 (Tuesday), up to 05:00 PM. Forms submitted after that time will be rejected.
Q3What is the qualification for Senior Project Associate?
A
M.Sc in Biotechnology from a recognized university or equivalent with three years of research experience, or a Doctoral degree in Biotechnology with two years of research experience.
Q4What is the qualification for Project Assistant-II?
A
The qualification is a Diploma in Mechanical, Electrical or Chemical Engineering. Two positions are advertised under position code 08.
Q5What is the age limit?
A
The upper age limit is 40 years for Senior Project Associate and 35 years for all other posts. Relaxation is as per Government of India Rules, and age is counted as on the last date of submission of application.
Q6What is the stipend for each post?
A
Senior Project Associate gets ₹42,000 plus HRA. Project Associate-II gets ₹35,000 or ₹28,000, Project Associate-I gets ₹31,000 or ₹25,000, and Project Assistant-II gets ₹20,000, each plus HRA.
Q7Who gets the higher stipend of Project Associate-I and II?
A
The higher amount, pay (a), is for candidates who qualified CSIR-UGC/ICAR/ICMR NET (including Lecturership/Assistant Professorship) or GATE, or certain national level examinations conducted by Central Government departments like DBT/DST or their agencies. Others get pay (b).
Q8Is HRA paid on top of the stipend?
A
HRA is added to the stipend, but it will be paid only if institutional accommodation is not availed or provided.
Q9How are candidates selected?
A
Selection is on the basis of an interview held in hybrid mode (online and offline). If many candidates apply, the Screening Committee may revisit the screening criteria.
Q10Is this a permanent job, and how long is the engagement?
A
No. The positions are purely temporary. Engagement is initially for one year or the project duration, whichever is earlier, and the total tenure, including all CSIR labs, cannot exceed six years.
