About the Directorate of Shipping Services

The Directorate of Shipping Services works under the Andaman & Nicobar Administration. The circular is issued by the Deputy Secretary (Shipping), and applications go to The Secretary (Shipping), A&N Administration, at Sri Vijaya Puram. The post is in the Group 'A' Gazetted (Non-Ministerial) cadre of the Directorate.

Who Can Apply

The post is filled by the Composite Method (Deputation (ISTC) plus Promotion). Only serving officers can apply, through their employer. The circular names two groups of eligible officers. Officers not below the rank of Lieutenant in the Indian Navy having sea or command experience. OR Officers working under the Central/States Govts./U.Ts/Public Sector Undertaking/Statutory Bodies and Autonomous Organization who meet condition (A) and condition (B): (A) Any one of the following: (i) holding analogous posts on regular basis in the parent cadre; or (ii) with Five (5) years service in the grade rendered after appointment thereto on regular basis in the Pay Matrix (₹ 53100-167800) Pay Level - 09 revised or equivalent in parent cadre/department; or (iii) with Six (6) years service in the grade rendered after appointment thereto on regular basis in the Pay Matrix (₹ 47600-151100) Pay Level - 08

Pay and Terms

Pay: Pay Matrix (₹ 67700-208700) Pay Level - 11. Pay of the selected officer: regulated in terms of the Department of Personnel & Training's orders contained in their OM No. 6/8/2009-Estt.(Pay-II) dated 17.06.2010, as amended from time to time. Period of deputation: three (03) years. Overall limit: the period of deputation, including deputation in another ex-cadre post held immediately before this appointment in the same or some other organisation/department of the Central Govt., shall ordinarily not exceed 04 (four) years. The notice does not mention HRA or other allowances.

Important Instructions

Candidates who apply for the post will not be allowed to withdraw subsequently. Applications of officers against whom disciplinary/vigilance cases are pending or being contemplated need not be forwarded. Incomplete applications, advance copies of applications, applications received after the due date and applications without the listed documents will not be entertained.
