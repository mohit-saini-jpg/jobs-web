NIT Mizoram Non Teaching Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

If applications are large in number, NIT Mizoram may shortlist candidates by methods it devises, including a Screening Test. Interviews stand discontinued for Group 'B' and Group 'C' posts; candidates will be recommended for appointment in order of merit on the basis of the Competitive Written Examination. The written papers are objective type. NIT Mizoram Non Teaching Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in NIT Mizoram Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 5 vacancies: 1 Technical Assistant, 1 Stenographer, 1 Technician and 2 Office Attendant posts, all unreserved (UR).
Q2What is the last date to apply?
A
Applications must be received by email by 30 November 2026 (5:00 PM); applications received later will be summarily rejected.
Q3How do I apply for NIT Mizoram Recruitment 2026?
A
Email the prescribed application form, Data Sheet (excel file), self-attested documents and fee receipt in soft copy (word file or pdf) to [email protected].
Q4What qualification is needed for the Office Attendant and Stenographer posts?
A
Office Attendant needs Senior Secondary (10+2) from a recognized Board; Stenographer needs Senior Secondary (10+2) with minimum shorthand speed of 80 w.p.m.
Q5What is the age limit?
A
The age limit is not exceeding 30 years for Technical Assistant and not exceeding 27 years for the other posts, counted on 30 November 2026; candidates must have attained 18 years.
Q6What is the salary for NIT Mizoram non-teaching posts?
A
Minimum basic pay is ₹35,400.00 (Level 6) for Technical Assistant, ₹25,500.00 (Level 4) for Stenographer, ₹21,700.00 (Level 3) for Technician and ₹18,000.00 (Level 1) for Office Attendant.
Q7What is the application fee?
A
The fee is ₹1000/- for General and OBC and ₹500/- for SC/ST candidates, paid online only; women and PWD candidates are exempted.
Q8Is there an interview?
A
Interviews stand discontinued for Group 'B' and Group 'C' posts; appointment is in order of merit based on the Competitive Written Examination, with qualifying trade, stenography or driving tests.
Q9Will TA/DA be paid for the examination?
A
No TA/DA will be paid for attending the Trade Test or Competitive Written Examination.
Q10Within how long must selected candidates join?
A
Selected candidates must join within one month from the date of Declaration of Results / Offer of Appointment.
