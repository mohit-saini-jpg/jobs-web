NFR Sports Quota Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection is based on a 100-mark merit list comprising a Sports Trial and an Interview. Candidates scoring 25 or more out of 40 in the Trial are declared "FIT" and proceed to the Interview stage; those below 25 are declared "NOT FIT" and are not assessed further. Interviews are normally held on the same day as the trial, or the next day at the latest. For educational qualification, 6 marks are given for meeting the minimum qualification; higher qualifications attract higher marks. Minimum qualifying marks: 65 for Level-3 and Level-2 posts, and 60 for Level-1 posts. In case of a tie in marks, preference is given to the younger candidate.
