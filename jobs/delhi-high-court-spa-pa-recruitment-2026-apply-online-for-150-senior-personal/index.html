Delhi High Court SPA & PA Selection Process

Both posts follow a four-stage selection process – English Typing Test, English Shorthand Test, Main (Descriptive) Examination, and Interview. The final merit list is prepared by adding marks from Stage II, III, and IV; Stage I (Typing Test) is qualifying only. Ties are resolved in order: Stage II marks, then Stage III marks, then Stage IV marks, then date of birth (older candidate preferred). No exemption from any stage, including Typing and Shorthand tests, is given to any candidate, including PwBD candidates; there is no provision for a scribe or compensatory time for PwBD candidates in this recruitment.
