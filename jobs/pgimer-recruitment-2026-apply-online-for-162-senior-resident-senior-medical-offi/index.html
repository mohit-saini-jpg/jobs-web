PGIMER Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection will be based on a Computer Based Test carrying 75 marks and a Departmental Interview and Assessment carrying 25 marks. Computer Based Test on 5 December 2026. Department-wise and category-wise shortlisting based on CBT marks. Scrutiny of shortlisted candidates' applications and uploaded documents. Publication of the eligibility notice. Document verification. Departmental Interview and Assessment. Final merit preparation using CBT and departmental assessment marks. Shortlisting Ratio: Candidates will normally be shortlisted in a ratio of 1:3 against the advertised department-wise and category-wise vacancies. Final Merit: Only candidates who separately qualify in both the CBT and Departmental Interview and Assessment will be considered for final selection. Final merit will be based on the combined score out of 100 marks.

PGIMER Recruitment 2026 Department and Institute Preference

Candidates must apply for the relevant post in one department only . Where the same post and department are available at PGIMER Chandigarh and PGI Satellite Centre Sangrur, candidates may indicate preferences for one or both institutes while completing the online form. Important: Candidates who do not indicate a preference for an institute will not be considered for the concerned institute.

PGIMER Recruitment 2026 Tenure and Posting

The advertised positions are tenure appointments for a maximum period of three years. Selected candidates will serve at PGIMER Chandigarh or PGI Satellite Centre Sangrur according to the relevant vacancy, eligibility and institute preference. The vacancies are provisional and may change. The institute may also fill qualifying vacancies arising during the notified period from the waiting list as permitted by the guidelines.
