NEEPCO JE Exam Pattern 2026

The written examination will consist of two parts: Aptitude Test and Technical Test. The Aptitude Test will cover General English, Quantitative Aptitude and Reasoning. The test will be qualifying in nature. Each question carries 1 mark. There will be a negative marking of 0.25 marks for every wrong answer. Qualifying marks: UR/EWS: 40% SC/ST: 30% The Technical Test will contain questions related to the respective engineering discipline. The test will consist of 40 multiple choice questions. Each question carries 1 mark. Negative marking of 0.25 marks will apply for every wrong answer. Qualifying marks: UR/EWS: 40% SC/ST: 30%
