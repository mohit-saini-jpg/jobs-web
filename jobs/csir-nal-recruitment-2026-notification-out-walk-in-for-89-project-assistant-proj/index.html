CSIR-NAL Eligibility Criteria & Qualification 2026

Eligibility depends on the post level and the specialization applied for. General essential qualification for each post category is summarized below; the exact discipline (e.g. Mechanical, Electronics, Chemistry) must match the specialization listed in the Vacancy table for the post code applied to. Only candidates who have already completed their qualifying degree/diploma are eligible - candidates awaiting results of a qualifying examination cannot attend the interview. Where a candidate claims an equivalent qualification, they must produce the order/letter (with number and date) recognizing the equivalence, failing which the application is liable to be rejected. The prescribed qualifications are the minimum required; CSIR-NAL applies its own criteria for shortlisting and possessing the minimum qualification alone does not guarantee an interview call.

CSIR-NAL Age Limit 2026

The maximum age limit is 35 years for all posts under this notification, assessed as on the date of the Walk-in-Interview for the relevant post. Age Relaxation: 5 years for SC/ST/PwBD/Women candidates and 3 years for OBC candidates (or as per Government of India instructions issued from time to time). Candidates must clearly mention their category and attach documentary proof; without this proof, age relaxation will not be entertained and the candidate will not be permitted to appear for interview. CSIR-NAL Selection Process 2026

Selection is based on performance in the interview, or any other criteria adopted by the Selection Committee. If a large number of candidates appear, the Selection/Screening Committee has the discretion to shortlist candidates through a written exam or any other criteria it deems fit before the interview. A panel of suitable candidates is drawn up after the selection process, valid for a maximum of one year; empanelment does not automatically guarantee engagement, and utilization depends on the number of positions available across projects during that period.

CSIR NAL Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1CSIR-NAL Recruitment 2026 has no apply-online option, and applications sent by post, speed post, or e-mail are explicitly not accepted.
  • 2Download the prescribed Application Form for Walk-in-Interview (Advt. No. 13/2026/PS) from the CSIR-NAL website and fill it completely.
  • 3Report in person at CSIR-NAL (RAB Meeting Complex, National Aerospace Laboratories), Adjacent to SBI NAL Branch, Kodihalli, Bengaluru – 560017, between 9:00 AM and 9:45 AM on the date fixed for the post/specialization you are applying for.
  • 4Bring a copy of your Aadhaar Card or another Government-issued photo ID, the duly filled application form, a latest passport-size photograph, and original certificates along with one set of photocopies (including Caste Certificate, if applicable, in the proper format).
  • 5Candidates wishing to apply for more than one position must apply separately for each position.
  • 6Be prepared to return the next day if your interview is not completed on the scheduled date for any reason.
  • 7No TA/DA is paid for attending the Walk-in-Interview. Candidates already working in CSIR-NAL or any other CSIR lab must bring a "No Objection Certificate" from their Project Leader/HOD at the time of interview.

It ranges from SSC/10th plus ITI (Project Assistant-I) and Diploma in Engineering (Project Assistant-II) to Bachelor's/Master's/Integrated Master's degrees in Engineering, Technology, or Science, and Ph.D. for some Principal PAT positions.
