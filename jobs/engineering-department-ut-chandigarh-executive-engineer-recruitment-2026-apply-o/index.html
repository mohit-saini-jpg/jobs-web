Engineering Department UT Chandigarh Executive Engineer (Electrical) Eligibility Criteria

Officers under the Central Government, State Governments, Union Territory Administrations, Public Sector Undertakings, recognized Universities, recognized Research Institutions, Semi Government, Autonomous Bodies, or Statutory Organizations are eligible: Holding analogous posts on a regular basis in the parent cadre or department; OR With five years of service in the grade rendered after appointment thereto on a regular basis in posts in Pay Band-3 of Rs. 15,600 - 39,100 with Grade Pay of Rs. 6,600 or equivalent in the parent cadre or department.
PostQualification Required
Executive Engineer (Electrical)Essential: (i) Bachelor Degree in Electrical Engineering from a recognized University or Institute; and (ii) Ten years of experience in Electrical Engineering in the Government Sector. Desirable: Master's Degree in Electrical Engineering from a recognized University or Institute. Candidates having a Degree of Associate Member of Engineers in Electrical Engineering (AMIE) from a recognized University/Institute who were enrolled for AMIE up to 31.05.2013 are also eligible for consideration for deputation (ISTC).

Engineering Department UT Chandigarh Executive Engineer (Electrical) Age Limit

The maximum age limit for appointment by deputation (including short term contract) shall not exceed 56 years as on the closing date of receipt of applications.

Engineering Department UT Chandigarh Executive Engineer (Electrical) Important Instructions

Applications not accompanied by supporting certificates for qualification and experience will not be processed and will be rejected forthwith. Departmental officers in the feeder category who are in direct line of promotion are not eligible for deputation/absorption. Deputationists are not eligible for consideration for appointment by promotion. Period of deputation/ISTC shall ordinarily not exceed 4 years. Candidates who apply for the post on deputation basis will not be allowed to withdraw their candidature subsequently. Incomplete applications forwarded without proper channel or lacking required clearances will be rejected.

Engineering Department UT Chandigarh Executive Engineer : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Download the application proforma (Annexure-A) from the official website Chandigarh.gov.in/Public Notice.
  • 2Fill in all required details in block letters carefully.
  • 3Attach photocopies of ACRs or APARs for the last five years duly attested by an officer not below the rank of Under Secretary or equivalent.
  • 4Attach supporting certificates and documents for essential qualifications and experience claimed.
  • 5Get Annexure-II countersigned by the employer or cadre controlling authority with seal, certifying cadre clearance, vigilance clearance, integrity, and absence of penalties.
  • 6Forward the complete application through proper channel to the Chief Engineer, Engineering Department, Union Territory, Chandigarh, 1st Floor, Deluxe Building, Sector-9-D, Chandigarh - 160009.
  • 7Ensure the application reaches on or before 24 November 2026.
