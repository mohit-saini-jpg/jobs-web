IIT Delhi Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

IIT Delhi will conduct a shortlisting-based interview. Applications received within the due date are shortlisted by a Committee, and only shortlisted candidates are considered for further selection. The essential qualifications and experience are minimum, and possessing them does not entitle a candidate to be called for the interview. If the number of applications is large, the Institute reserves the right to adopt a shortlisting criteria to restrict the number of eligible candidates called. The decision of the Committee is final and binding. The date of the selection process will be communicated separately by email to provisionally shortlisted candidates only. The notice does not state a date or venue.
