About AIIMS Recruitment 2026

The Centre for Dental Education and Research (CDER) at the All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS), New Delhi, is conducting this recruitment under the National Oral Health Program (NOHP), Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India. The appointed personnel are engaged specifically for the 'National Oral Disease Burden Survey' to execute extensive field-level data collection activities. The operational base for project administration is the NaRRIDS facility located at the CDER building within the AIIMS New Delhi campus.

Vacancy Details

A total of 2 contractual vacancies have been announced for outstation field work, and the number of vacancies may increase or decrease as per the requirement on the day of the interview. Pay and Tenure

Salary and other admissible allowances will be paid in accordance with AIIMS, ICMR, and Government of India guidelines. In addition, TA/DA for travel during survey work will be provided strictly on a reimbursement basis as per Government of India rules upon submission of prescribed proforma, actual bills, and declarations; no advance payment will be granted. The appointment is purely temporary and contractual, initially up to 31 March 2027, with extensions subject to project needs and availability of funds. Performance will be reviewed every 30 days, and the contract may be terminated on one day's notice if found unsatisfactory.

AIIMS Recruitment 2026 Job Description

These appointments are strictly for field visits and house-to-house surveys across various states of India, and not for routine office work at CDER, AIIMS New Delhi. Direct field engagement and data collection in various parts of the country as assigned. Continuous travel between states according to the duty schedule without returning to Delhi between field visits. Submission of daily and periodic work progress reports as an essential requirement. Tasks as assigned by the project-in-charge or authority (during travel, candidates will be answerable to the assigned team leader, and the competent authority retains the right to assign field work travel or any other work for CDER, AIIMS).

Documents Required

Printout of the completed and submitted Google Form. Detailed Curriculum Vitae (C.V.). Self-attested copies of all educational qualification certificates. Self-attested copies of experience certificates issued by valid employers. Self-attested copy of age proof certificate. Valid Photo ID proof. Signed declaration form (obtained via the official QR code/link). Certificate from a recognized board or institution in case an additional degree or advanced course has been undertaken.

Selection and Interview

A merit list will be prepared based on percentage marks obtained in the Essential Qualification (overall for the course), desirable qualifications, merit, and experience. Based on merit and years of experience, candidates will be shortlisted in a 1:5 ratio per post to appear for an in-person interview. In the event of a tie in marks, preference will be given to candidates possessing additional desirable qualifications. The selection committee reserves the right to conduct a written test, computer exercises, or clinical examination before the interview, and a typing speed test will be conducted for the Data Entry Operator post. Interviews will be conducted in person only; no TA/DA will be provided for attending the interview.
