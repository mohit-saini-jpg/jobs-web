OSSSC Radiographer Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Nationality: Must be a citizen of India. Educational Qualification: Must have passed the +2 Science Examination under the Council of Higher Secondary Education, Odisha (or equivalent), and passed a Diploma in Medical Radiation Technology (DMRT) or Bachelor in Medical Radiation Technology (BMRT) from a Government Medical College and Hospital of the state, or any other private institution recognized by the Government of Odisha or the All India Council of Technical Education. Professional Registration: Must have registered their name in the Radiographers Council of the State and possess a valid Registration or Renewal Certificate as on the date of submission of the online application, which must be uploaded online. Odia Language: Must be able to speak, read, and write Odia, satisfied by having passed the Middle School Examination with Odia as a language subject, or Matriculation/equivalent

OSSSC Radiographer Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection consists of a Written Test followed by Document Verification. The Written Test is MCQ type, conducted in OMR/CBRE mode (exact mode to be notified later), in one paper of 100 marks. Negative marking applies: 0.25 marks deducted for each wrong answer; no penalty for unattempted questions; more than one answer marked for a question is treated as wrong. Minimum qualifying marks/normalized score: 30% for ST, SC, PwDs, and Ex-Servicemen candidates; 35% for all other candidates. Tie-break sequence: higher percentage of marks in DMRT, then age seniority (older ranked higher), then higher marks in +2 Science, then the Commission's discretion. A Provisional Master Merit List is prepared from the Written Test; candidates securing minimum qualifying marks are screened for Document Verification at not less than 1.25 times the number of vacancies advertised, category-wise.
