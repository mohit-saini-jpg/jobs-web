About TISS Data Officer

The Tata Institute of Social Sciences (TISS), Mumbai is inviting applications for a short-term research project titled "Saksham Pramaan - Programme Evaluation for National AIDS Control Programme (NACP)". The project is conducted in consultation with the National AIDS Control Organisation (NACO) to design and conduct concurrent and mid-term evaluations of select programmatic interventions under NACP across the country. The Data Officer will work under the technical supervision of a research associate and Manager/s leading the research studies at Saksham Pramaan, TISS.
