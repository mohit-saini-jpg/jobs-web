4Who Can Check the Round 4 Result
Sr.TypeTextStyleItems
1paragraphRound 4 of JEECUP 2026 counselling is significant because it is the stage at which counselling opens up beyond Uttar Pradesh domicile holders. According to reported coverage of this cycle:
2listunordered
  • Rounds 1 to 3 of JEECUP 2026 counselling were restricted to candidates holding UP domicile status.
  • Round 4 is the entry point into JEECUP counselling for eligible candidates from outside Uttar Pradesh.
  • Out-of-state candidates are not eligible for seats reserved under UP's own reservation category quotas; they can only be considered for general category seats that remain vacant after the first three UP-domicile-only rounds.
5How to Check JEECUP 2026 Round 4 Seat Allotment Result
Sr.TypeStyleItems
1listordered
  • Visit the official JEECUP counselling portal at jeecup.admissions.nic.in.
  • Click on the "Candidate Login" option on the homepage.
  • Enter your application number and password, then click "Sign In."
  • Open the "Round 4 Seat Allotment Result 2026" link on your candidate dashboard.
  • Check the details of your allotted institute, course/branch, and category carefully.
  • Download the provisional allotment letter and save a PDF copy.
  • Take a physical printout of the allotment letter - most institutes require the printed copy for reporting, and a screenshot is not accepted.
6Freeze or Float: What Do You Do After the Result?
Sr.TypeTextStyleItems
1paragraphOnce a seat is allotted in Round 4, candidates who wish to continue in the process generally choose between two options on the portal:
2listunordered
  • Freeze: You accept the allotted seat permanently and exit further rounds of counselling. Your admission is confirmed once fee payment and document verification are complete.
  • Float: You keep the current seat as a backup while remaining in contention for a better seat (based on your original choices) in the next round. If no upgrade is available, your current seat is retained.
3paragraphNote: Some earlier JEECUP counselling cycles also offered a "Slide" option (moving to a different, more preferred branch within the same institute). Whether Slide is available in Round 4 of the 2026 cycle is not confirmed in currently available reports - candidates should check the exact options shown on their own login dashboard.
7JEECUP 2026 Round 4: Seat Acceptance Fee
Sr.TypeText
1paragraphBased on the pattern followed in JEECUP 2026 Round 1, the seat acceptance fee for first-time allottees was Rs 3,000, payable online through the Multiple Option Payment System (MOPS), and this amount is later adjusted against the final admission fee at the institute. However, the exact fee amount and payment deadline specifically for Round 4 of the 2026 cycle have not been confirmed in the available official reports. Candidates should verify the applicable fee and last date directly on their login dashboard at jeecup.admissions.nic.in before making any payment.
8How Is the Round 4 Seat Allotment Decided?
Sr.TypeTextStyleItems
1paragraphJEECUP seat allotment in every round, including Round 4, is based on a combination of factors:
2listunordered
  • The candidate's merit rank in JEECUP 2026
  • The order of institute and course preferences (choices) filled by the candidate
  • The candidate's reservation category
  • Real-time availability of seats in each institute and branch, including seats vacated by candidates who did not report or were removed in earlier rounds
9What to Do After Checking the Round 4 Result
Sr.TypeStyleItems
1listordered
  • Log in and download your Round 4 provisional allotment letter.
  • Choose the freeze or float option on the portal, based on whether you want to accept this seat finally or stay in contention for an upgrade.
  • Pay the applicable seat acceptance fee online through MOPS within the deadline shown on your dashboard.
  • Complete document verification - online or at a designated help centre, as instructed on the portal.
  • Report to the allotted institute in person with all original documents and photocopies within the schedule announced by JEECUP.
10Important Instructions for Candidates
Sr.TypeStyleItems
1listunordered
  • Only the candidate login on jeecup.admissions.nic.in shows your authentic, official Round 4 allotment status - avoid relying on third-party websites for your actual result.
  • Keep your application number and password secure and handy for all counselling logins.
  • Failure to complete fee payment, document verification, or institute reporting within the prescribed deadlines may lead to cancellation of the allotted seat.
  • A screenshot of the allotment letter is not accepted by institutes; carry a physical printout.
  • Since exact Round 4 (2026) deadlines for fee payment and document verification are not confirmed in current reports, treat the dates displayed on your own dashboard as final.
