SAMS Odisha +3 Merit List 2026

The SAMS Odisha +3 fourth merit list (spot round) was published on August 12, 2026. Candidates can check the merit list by entering details such as district, college, stream, and subject. The list contains information including barcode, applicant's name, category, stream, marks, and subject. The selection merit list is published on scert.samsodisha.gov.in.

SAMS Odisha Admission Process: Slide, Float & Freeze

The Higher Education Department uses three options during seat allotment rounds:

  • Float: Accept the allotted seat but remain open to a better Honours subject in a higher preference.
  • Slide: Accept the allotted seat but seek a better Honours subject and HEI in a higher preference.
  • Freeze: Fully satisfied with the allotted seat (both HEI and Honours) - requires fee payment and reporting at the HEI.
