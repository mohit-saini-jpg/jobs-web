Central Bank of India Counselor Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Central Bank of India, Regional Office Bhopal, a public sector bank, is engaging an Incharge/Counselor for a Financial Literacy Centre (FLC) for the post Counselor FLC Bhopal Raisen on contract for one year. Applications in the Annexure-A format go to the Regional Head, Central Bank of India, Regional Office, Bhopal, 9, Arera Hills, Jail Road Bhopal, Pin - 462011, by post or by hand, and must be received by 15 November 2026. The notice is for retired bank officers, ex-servicemen and others who are not bank employees, aged above 45 years and less than 65 years.
