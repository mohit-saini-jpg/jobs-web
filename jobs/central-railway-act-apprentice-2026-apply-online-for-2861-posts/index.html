Central Railway Act Apprentice Training Duration 2026

The duration of apprenticeship training is one year for all notified trades . Selected candidates must enter into a Contract of Apprenticeship Training before the programme begins. Central Railway Act Apprentice Selection Process 2026

Selection will be based on a merit list calculated using the simple average of Class 10 and ITI percentage marks . The notification does not prescribe a separate written examination or interview. Online Application: Candidates apply through the RRC Central Railway website. Merit Calculation: Class 10 aggregate percentage and ITI percentage are given equal weightage. Merit List Preparation: Lists are prepared cluster-wise, unit-wise, trade-wise and community-wise. Document Verification: Shortlisted candidates must produce original educational and category certificates. Medical Fitness: Candidates must present the prescribed medical fitness certificate. Apprenticeship Engagement: Eligible candidates are engaged for training after completing the necessary formalities. Is There a Written Exam for Railway Apprentice 2026?

No separate written examination is specified for this Central Railway Act Apprentice engagement. Candidates are shortlisted according to their Class 10 and ITI marks, followed by document verification and medical fitness assessment. Applicants should not confuse this apprenticeship selection with Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) CBT examinations conducted for regular railway posts.

Aadhaar and Identity Requirements for RRC CR Apprentice 2026

Applicants are generally required to enter their 12-digit Aadhaar number during registration. Those who have applied for Aadhaar but have not received the card may enter the 28-digit Aadhaar Enrolment ID. The notification provides special identification provisions for applicants from Jammu & Kashmir, Meghalaya and Assam. Eligible applicants from these locations may use a permitted Government identity document such as Voter ID, valid passport or driving licence. The original accepted identification document must be produced during document verification.

Central Railway Apprentice Document Verification 2026

Candidates shortlisted through the merit process will be called for document verification in the relevant division or unit according to their selected cluster. They must produce the original educational certificates, ITI marksheets, reservation certificates, identity documents and other applicable records. Shortlisted candidates must bring the prescribed medical fitness certificate in the format provided in Annexure VII of the official notification. The certificate must be signed by a Government-authorized Gazetted medical officer not below the rank of Assistant Surgeon in a Central or State Government hospital. Important: Candidates who fail to produce the required medical fitness certificate may not be permitted to undergo document verification and may have their candidature cancelled.
