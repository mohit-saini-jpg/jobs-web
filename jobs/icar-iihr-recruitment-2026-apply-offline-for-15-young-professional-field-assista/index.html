About ICAR-IIHR

ICAR-Indian Institute of Horticultural Research is an ICAR research institute at Hesaraghatta Lake Post, Bengaluru - 560 089. The institute is filling these positions under time-bound ad-hoc projects and schemes. These include NICRA, a DST Vigyandhara project on farm women of Karnataka, ICAR-CRP on Genomics, DUS testing projects, the National Mission on High Yielding Seeds, a DST SCSP project for SC farmers in Karnataka, AINP-EP and the Main Institute Single Budget.

Vacancy Details

The notice prints "01 Post" for each of Items 3 to 17, which makes 15 posts. Items 1 and 2 (Senior Research Fellow, NICRA) do not state a number of posts. The vacancies may increase or decrease as per the requirement of the Institute. By post, the numbered posts are: Senior Research Fellow 3 (Items 9, 12 and 14, plus Items 1 and 2), Young Professional II 10, Young Professional I 1 and Field Assistant 1.

Pay and Tenure

Senior Research Fellow (Items 1, 2 and 12): Rs. 37,000/- PM + HRA. Senior Research Fellow (Item 9): Rs. 42,000/- PM + 27% HRA. Senior Research Fellow (Item 14): Rs. 37,000/- PM + HRA for the first two years, and Rs. 42,000/- PM + HRA from the 3rd year. Young Professional II (all items): Rs. 42,000/- PM (Consolidated). Young Professional I: Rs.30,000/- PM (Consolidated). Field Assistant: Rs.20,000/- PM + 30% HRA. Each item runs up to the date shown in the vacancy table. The period and duration of the scheme is tentative and may undergo change. For Item 10, the tenure up to 31.03.2027 is marked "Likely to be extended". At the time of reporting, the selected candidate shall deposit an amount equal to one month's salary as a refundable 'Caution Money Deposit'.

ICAR IIHR Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in ICAR-IIHR Recruitment 2026?
A
The notice lists 17 items. Items 3 to 17 each have 01 post, which makes 15 posts. Items 1 and 2 (Senior Research Fellow, NICRA) do not state a number of posts.
Q2What is the last date to apply?
A
Applications must reach the Establishment Section, ICAR-IIHR, Bengaluru, by 25 November 2026, 05:30 PM. Late applications will not be entertained, even when the delay is due to postal delays.
Q3What qualification is needed for Young Professional II?
A
Each Young Professional II item requires an M.Sc. in a specified subject, for example M. Sc. Floriculture for Item 11. Item 10 accepts M Tech/MSc Bioinformatics Or MSc (Agri) Plant Biotechnology.
Q4What is the age limit?
A
For Senior Research Fellow, the age limit is 35 years for men and 40 years for women. For Young Professional I, Young Professional II and Field Assistant, it is 21 to 45 years.
Q5What is the salary for ICAR-IIHR Young Professional and SRF posts?
A
Young Professional II gets Rs. 42,000/- PM (Consolidated) and Young Professional I gets Rs.30,000/- PM (Consolidated). Senior Research Fellow gets Rs. 37,000/- PM + HRA, except Item 9 (Rs. 42,000/- PM + 27% HRA) and Item 14 (Rs. 42,000/- PM + HRA from the 3rd year).
Q6Is HRA paid on top of the pay?
A
HRA is paid on top of the pay for the Senior Research Fellow items and for the Field Assistant (Rs.20,000/- PM + 30% HRA). Young Professional I and II are paid a consolidated amount.
Q7How do I apply?
A
Send the prescribed proforma with attested certificates to reach the Office of the Establishment Section, ICAR-Indian Institute of Horticultural Research, Hesaraghatta Lake Post, Bengaluru - 560 089. Write the Item No. and Name of the Post on both the application and the envelope. Email applications will not be accepted.
Q8When are the interviews?
A
After screening, shortlisted candidates for Items 01 to 09 will be interviewed on 15 December 2026, and those for Items 10 to 17 on 16 December 2026 (tentative). Candidates must report at the ICAR-IIHR campus by 8:30 AM.
Q9Can I apply for more than one post?
A
Yes. A separate application must be submitted for each post, and no two applications should be tied together.
Q10Is this a permanent job?
A
No. The positions are purely temporary and co-terminus with the project, and ICAR/IIHR has no liability for absorbing the selected candidates.
