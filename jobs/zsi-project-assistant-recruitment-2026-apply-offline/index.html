About ZSI Project Assistant

The Zoological Survey of India is under the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, Government of India. This recruitment is for its research project on coral reef resilience and the impact of climate change in the Gulf of Mannar and Palk Bay, a four-year project funded by the DST–Anusandhan National Research Foundation (ANRF). Applications are handled by the ZSI Sunderban Regional Centre (ZSI-SbRC) at Canning, West Bengal, and the project's Principal Investigator is Dr. J. S. Yogesh Kumar, Scientist-E.

Who Can Apply

Only Indian nationals may apply. The notice prints the following for the single Project Assistant post. Experience in coral reef field surveys Certified SCUBA diver Underwater camera Driving licence Molecular techniques, GIS applications and biostatistics Applicants must be physically fit and willing to undertake extensive fieldwork in remote and logistically challenging areas, including the Gulf of Mannar Islands and the Palk Bay region. Candidates should check the official notification for the discipline-specific eligibility of the post.

Pay and Tenure

The Project Assistant gets ₹27,000 + HRA; the Hindi version of the notice states this as ₹27,000 + HRA per month. The position is purely temporary and co-terminus with the duration of the project. The notice does not mention any other allowance. As an additional benefit, the selected candidate will be encouraged to register for a Ph.D. under any recognized university, based on eligibility and institutional affiliation, in line with the research objectives of the project.

ZSI Project Assistant Job Description

The selected candidate will be temporarily posted in the ZSI-SbRC, Canning, West Bengal and Gulf of Mannar, Tamil Nadu, and will be responsible for: Coral reef underwater survey Live coral and associated fauna status assessment Population density Statistical analysis GIS mapping Molecular study Ecological assessments Conservation of the coral reef, Gulf of Mannar and Palk Bay

Selection and Interview

Only shortlisted candidates will be informed via email and invited for the interview. The interview will be conducted in hybrid mode (both online and offline) at the Zoological Survey of India, Sunderban Regional Centre, Canning-743329, West Bengal. The date and time of the interview will be communicated separately via email to the shortlisted candidates. A SCUBA diving certificate (Open water and advance open water) is required at the time of the interview. Candidates currently employed in government or semi-government organizations must produce a No Objection Certificate (NOC) at the time of the interview. No TA/DA will be provided for attending the interview.
