Is the Karnataka 2nd PUC Time Table 2027 Final?

No. KSEAB has called this a tentative time table. Students, parents and the public can send objections by email until 22 October 2026. The board has not announced when the final time table will be published. The email address for objections was not available in the sources reviewed, so check the official notification on kseab.karnataka.gov.in.

In the previous cycle, the board published the tentative time table in September 2025 and released the final dates after reviewing objections. The same sequence is likely this year, but KSEAB has not confirmed it.

How to Check the Karnataka 2nd PUC Time Table 2027

  • Open the official KSEAB website, kseab.karnataka.gov.in.
  • Look for the latest notification or time table section for SSLC and 2nd PUC 2027.
  • Open the 2nd PUC Exam 1 and Exam 2 time table file.
  • Check your subject, including your language and optional subjects.
  • Save a copy and recheck the website after 22 October 2026 for the final version.

A direct link to the PDF was not verified, so it is not listed here.

Important Instructions for 2nd PUC Students

  • Treat all dates as tentative until KSEAB publishes the final time table.
  • If a date clashes with another exam or looks like an error, the objection window closes on 22 October 2026.
  • Match each date with your own subject combination, because several subjects share one day.
  • Note the different timing for Hindustani Music (10:00 AM to 12:15 PM in Exam 1).
  • Follow only the official website for hall ticket, practical exam and registration updates.
