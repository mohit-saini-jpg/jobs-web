Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation of India Limited is a Government of India undertaking under the Ministry of Railways. Posts, Eligibility and Pay

Vacancy Notice No. 45/2026 covers one post of Dy. CPM/PM (Mechanical) at the Ahmedabad Unit, on deputation. The service required is IRSME officer of Indian Railways . DFCCIL Recruitment 2026 Job Description

Planning and monitoring enroute examination of IR stock and maintenance of DFC owned rolling stock in co-ordination with IR. Co-ordinate for safe & trouble-free working of crew on DFC network in co-ordination with IR. Develop and implement strategies for locomotive troubleshooting. Co-ordination with IR for restoration work in case of accidents, derailments or liquidation of IR stock marked sick on DFC network. Develop emergency response team/equipment for early restoration of traffic in case of rolling stock defect enroute. Planning and deployment of wayside equipment/diagnostic technologies, preferably AI driven technologies for rolling stock. Data analytics captured through DFIS, FMM, FOIS, SLAM, CMS to formulate and implement strategies to minimise failures and detentions of rolling stock on DFC network. Maintenance and calibration of EFWMBs wherever applicable.
