FACT Diesel Mechanic Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Fertilisers and Chemicals Travancore Limited (FACT), a Schedule 'A' multi-divisional Central PSE, is empanelling apprentices who completed apprenticeship training at the FACT Training & Development Centre on or before 31.12.2025 for engagement as Diesel Mechanic on Fixed Tenure Contract (Adhoc Basis), at a consolidated pay of Rs. 25,000 per month for a two-year term. Candidates need SSLC pass with a National Trade Certificate (NTC) in Mechanic (Diesel). The online form on www.fact.co.in closes on 30.11.2026, and the hard copy must reach by 07.12.2026.

About FACT

The Fertilisers and Chemicals Travancore Limited describes itself as a multi-divisional Central PSE and a pioneer in the manufacture of Fertilisers, Chemicals and Caprolactam, with activities spread over Manufacturing, Engineering Design and Consultancy, Fabrication and more. The notice is issued by its HR Department at Udyogamandal and signed by the Assistant General Manager (HR).

Who Can Apply

Only candidates who successfully completed Apprenticeship training under the Apprentices Act from the FACT Training & Development Centre on or before 31.12.2025 can apply. The notice says the purpose is empanelling candidates to be considered for engagement. The age limit is less than 35 years and above 18 years as on 01.09.2026. For later engagements from the panel, candidates must meet the maximum age limit as on the date of engagement. Relaxation in the maximum age limit is permissible up to 5 years for SC/ST candidates and 3 years for OBC-NCL candidates. Only the Std X certificate having the date of birth is accepted as proof of date of birth. Apprentices who have completed or are completing apprenticeship in 2026 need not apply. The notice says they will be considered for engagement against requirements, if any, from a list sourced directly from the FACT Training & Development Centr

Pay and Tenure

Engaged candidates will be paid a consolidated pay of Rs. 25,000/- per month, with an increase of 3% of the consolidated pay on successful completion of each year. Leave, ESI, Provident Fund, Shift allowance, and TA and DA for duty travel apply based on eligibility, as per the Rules of the Company. The engagement is for two years, renewable for a maximum of 2 spells of one year each, at the discretion of the Management, subject to requirement, performance and assessment. The Management may terminate the engagement with 15 days' notice or pay in lieu of notice, at its discretion and without assigning any reason. Engagement is for a fixed term only and gives no right to extension, permanent appointment or preferential treatment in appointment in the Company. Candidates from the panel may also be considered for engagement for shorter periods, at rates and terms fixed by the Company for such

Selection and Interview

After preliminary scrutiny of the documents submitted with the application, candidates are ranked in the following order: earlier date of passing Apprenticeship Training, then the order of Apprenticeship Token Number. Eligibility and selection are determined from the information and documents the candidate submits, and candidature is provisional at all stages. Meeting the eligibility criteria or inclusion in the Panel does not by itself give any right to selection or engagement. Engagement from the panels is at the sole discretion of the Management, subject to requirements and reservation rules. No TA/DA is paid to candidates for attending the selection process.
