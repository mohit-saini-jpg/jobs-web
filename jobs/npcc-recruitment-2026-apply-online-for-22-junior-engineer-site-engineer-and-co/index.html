NPCC Eligibility Criteria

Eligibility is assessed as on the cutoff date of 31.07.2026 for age, qualification, and post-qualification experience. Only Indian Nationals are eligible to apply, and all qualifications must be from Indian Universities/Institutes recognized by UGC/AICTE. Only post-qualification experience gained after the declaration of the qualifying result/certificate, up to the cutoff date, will be counted. Candidates with degrees carrying CGPA/OGPA or a grading system must indicate the equivalent percentage as per their University/Institute's norms.

NPCC Salary

In addition to consolidated remuneration, the employer's Provident Fund contribution at 12% will be borne by NPCC, and a fixed Medical Allowance of Rs. 1,250 per month will be paid for all posts except Consultant-Projects. The Selection Committee may offer higher consolidated remuneration based on relevant work experience.

NPCC Selection Process

Selection will be based on Personal Interview, or any other appropriate method decided by NPCC depending on the response received for each post. NPCC's decision on the selection method and final result will be final and binding. Selected candidates will be issued an "Offer of Appointment" and must undergo a medical fitness examination by an approved Government hospital/medical board before joining. Merely submitting an application and appearing to meet the eligibility criteria does not guarantee a call for interview.
