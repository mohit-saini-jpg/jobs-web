AIIMS Raebareli Senior Resident Eligibility Criteria 2026

For the post of Senior Resident (Non-Academic), candidates must fulfil the following essential qualifications: A medical qualification included in the first or second schedule, or Part II of the third schedule, of the Indian Medical Council Act, 1956 (candidates with Part II third-schedule qualifications must also satisfy the conditions in Section 13(3) of the Act). Registration with the Central or State Medical Council. For Broad Speciality posts: MD/MS/DNB in the concerned speciality, or an equivalent qualification. For Super Speciality posts: MD/MS/DNB in the related broad speciality, along with DM/M.Ch/DNB in the concerned super speciality.

AIIMS Raebareli Senior Resident Age Limit 2026

The age limit for the post of Senior Resident is 45 years, relaxable by 5 years for SC/ST candidates and 3 years for OBC candidates.

AIIMS Raebareli Senior Resident Salary / Pay Scale 2026

Selected Senior Residents will be placed in Pay Matrix Level-11, with a minimum basic pay of Rs. 67,700/-, plus Non-Practicing Allowance (NPA) admissible to medical personnel, as per the recommendations of the 7th Central Pay Commission. As per the terms and conditions of the notification, appointees on this ad-hoc basis are not entitled to benefits such as Provident Fund, Pension, Gratuity, Medical Attendance Treatment, or Seniority/Promotion available to regular Government servants.

AIIMS Raebareli Senior Resident Selection Process 2026

Selection is based on a personal interview. However, if the number of candidates who apply is large, a written examination consisting of MCQs in the concerned subject will be conducted first, and shortlisted candidates from this exam will then appear for the interview. The list of selected candidates will be uploaded on the official institute website, and candidates are advised to check it regularly for updates.

AIIMS Raebareli Senior Resident Recruitment 2026 - Apply : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1This recruitment does not use an online application portal. Interested and eligible candidates must appear in person as a walk-in candidate, following these steps:
  • 2Download and fill the prescribed bio-data/application proforma (Annexure A) from the official website, www.aiimsrbl.edu.in.
  • 3Attach self-attested copies of all relevant certificates and testimonials supporting qualification, experience, date of birth, and category, along with a self-attested latest photograph.
  • 4If currently employed in a Government/Semi-Government/Corporate body or undertaking of the Central or State Government, obtain a "No Objection Certificate" from the present employer - without this, candidates may not be permitted to appear for the interview.
  • 5Report at AIIMS, Raebareli by 10:00 AM on the interview date.
  • 6Attend document verification and screening of applications between 10:00 AM and 12:00 PM.
  • 7Appear for the interview from 03:00 PM onwards, carrying all original certificates/testimonials for verification.
  • 8Note: Candidates are not entitled to any TA/DA for attending the interview or joining the Institute.
