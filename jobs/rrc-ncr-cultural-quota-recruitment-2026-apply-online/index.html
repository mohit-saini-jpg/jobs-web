RRC NCR Cultural Quota Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

North Central Railway (NCR), through its Railway Recruitment Cell (RRC) at Prayagraj, has issued Notification (EN CQ-02/2026) inviting online applications from eligible and willing candidates for recruitment against the Cultural Quota (Open Advertisement) for the year 2026-27. The recruitment is for Pay Matrix Level-2 (Grade Pay Rs. 1900/-) posts in the Sitar and Tabla streams. Online applications opened on 20 August 2026 and close on 19 September 2026 at 23:59 Hrs. Applications must be submitted only through the RRC/NCR website, www.rrcpryj.org.

NCR RRC Cultural Quota Selection Process

Eligible candidates are assessed on the basis of a Written Test and an Assessment of Talent, totalling 100 marks. The Written Test carries negative marking : 1/3rd of the marks allotted to a question is deducted for every wrong answer. Only candidates who qualify in the Written Test will be called for Practical Demonstration. Selected candidates will have to pass the requisite medical examination and, where prescribed, complete initial training before posting.

NCR RRC Cultural Quota Examination Fee

Fee must be paid online via internet banking, debit/credit card or UPI; bank charges are borne by the candidate. Fee is not refunded for candidates found ineligible. Minority candidates (Muslims, Christians, Sikhs, Buddhists, Zoroastrians/Parsis, Jains) must upload a self-declaration as per Annexure-H to claim the fee concession, and must furnish a notarised Minority Community Declaration affidavit at the time of Document Verification, failing which their candidature will be rejected and the fee will not be refunded. EBC candidates (family annual income below Rs. 50,000) must upload an EBC certificate as per Annexure-D.
