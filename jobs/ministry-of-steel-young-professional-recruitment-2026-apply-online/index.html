Pay and Tenure

Young Professionals receive a consolidated remuneration of ₹70,000/-, inclusive of all applicable taxes. No other facility or allowance is allowed. Payment is released within one week after the month ends, based on biometric attendance or certification by the officer concerned if the Young Professional is deputed elsewhere. Tenure: fixed two years, which may be extended by three additional years on an annual basis (2 + 1 + 1 + 1), up to a maximum of five years. Continuation depends on a satisfactory Annual Performance Review and the Ministry's requirements, based on Key Performance Indicators. Remuneration review: if the contract exceeds one year, remuneration may be reviewed annually. Ministry of Steel Recruitment 2026 Job Description

Should preferably possess expertise in raw material demand and supply analysis, mining policy, mineral exploration and beneficiation, monitoring of mining projects and associated environmental and forest clearances, transport logistics, infrastructure planning, and GIS-based mapping under PM Gati Shakti. Provide analytical inputs on international trade and undertake country-wise steel trade analysis. Identify and share export opportunities in existing and emerging markets with industry; assess tariff and non-tariff barriers affecting steel exports. Explore opportunities for steel import substitution, support online export promotion campaigns, technical/sectoral analysis/ case study and review various portals. Proficiency in data analysis, policy research, report writing and stakeholder coordination would be desirable. The engagement would facilitate timely examination of policy proposals

Selection and Interview

Applications received within the stipulated time are scrutinized and shortlisted by a Screening Committee. Shortlisted applicants are invited for interview before a Selection Committee, and candidates numbering about 10 times the vacancies in each post will be invited. The Selection Committee may devise its own method for selecting suitable candidates and may recommend a panel of names for a reserve list valid for one year from the date of recommendation. The notice does not mention an interview date or venue. Shortlisted candidates must carry original documents to the interview. Requisite documents, in original and self-attested photocopies, supporting eligibility and identity must be produced at the interview for verification; otherwise the candidate may not be permitted to appear.
