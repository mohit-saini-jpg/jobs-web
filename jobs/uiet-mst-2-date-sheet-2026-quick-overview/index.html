What Is MST-2 at UIET Hoshiarpur?

MST stands for Mid Semester Test. MST-2 is the second of the two mid-semester tests held in a semester. The first one, MST-1, for the odd semester of session 2026-27 had its own date sheet notice, published on the PUSSGRC notice board on 23 September 2026.

How to Download the MST-2 Date Sheet

  • Open the PUSSGRC notice board using the link in the Important Links section below.
  • Look for the entry dated 23/11/2026 titled “MST-2 Date Sheet for UIET, PUSSGRC, Hoshiarpur.”
  • Click the title to open the PDF.
  • Find your branch, semester and subjects in the schedule.
  • Download or save the PDF, and note the dates in your own calendar.

Who Should Check This Notice?

The notice is meant for students of the University Institute of Engineering and Technology (UIET), PUSSGRC, Hoshiarpur. Which branches and semesters are covered is not stated in the sources checked. The earlier notice of 17 November 2026 mentions B.E. classes. Read the PDF to confirm that your branch and semester are included.

Important Instructions

  • Instructions such as reporting time, admit card or ID card requirements, and rules for the exam room are not stated in the sources checked.
  • If the PDF includes instructions, follow them.
  • For anything unclear, contact your department or the UIET office.
