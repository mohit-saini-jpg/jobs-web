About CSIR-TKDL

The CSIR-Traditional Knowledge Digital Library Unit is a constituent of the Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR), located on Satsang Vihar Marg in New Delhi. It protects and preserves India's traditional knowledge found in ancient texts by digitizing it into modern languages in a structured format. Its key objective is to prevent misappropriation of this knowledge through the wrong acquisition of intellectual property rights.

CSIR-TKDL Project Associate-I Vacancy Details 2026

Both positions are under the project "The Importance of Traditional Wisdom: Environmental Consciousness in Vedic and other literature and its Relevance for Modern Climate Strategies (GAP-000001)". Rs. 31,000/- + HRA applies to candidates who have qualified CSIR UGC/ICAR/ICMR/NET, including lectureship/assistant professorship, or GATE. It also applies to those who have qualified national-level examinations conducted by Central Government Departments like DBT/DST, or equivalent, and/or their agencies or institutions. Rs. 25,000/- + HRA applies to all other candidates. HRA is payable as per the extant Government of India rules applicable to CSIR employees. A panel of waitlisted candidates, in order of merit, will be formed to fill vacancies that may arise in the near future. The panel remains valid until the project is completed. CSIR-TKDL Project Associate I Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection is through a walk-in interview, with original certificates checked for age, qualification, marks obtained and experience. Meeting the minimum qualification does not by itself give a candidate any right to selection. The decision of the Head, CSIR-TKDL Unit, is final and binding.

Service Conditions

The engagement is purely temporary, time-bound and on contract basis, and is co-terminus with the project. Total tenure as project staff, including all CSIR postings and under one or more projects, cannot exceed six years. Performance is reviewed periodically, and staff not found up to the mark could be terminated without assigning any reason. Either side may end the contract with one month's notice or one-month stipend in lieu. The engagement gives no right to continuation beyond the tenure, absorption or regularization in CSIR, and no claim for a permanent position will be entertained. Project staff will not be upgraded in the same or any new project. The selected candidate is expected to join within the timeframe in the offer letter, after formalities such as medical examination and character verification.
