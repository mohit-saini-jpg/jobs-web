MOIL SAP Functional Consultant Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

MOIL Limited, a Schedule 'A' Miniratna category-I PSU under the Ministry of Steel, Government of India, has invited applications for six (06) SAP S/4HANA with RISE Functional Consultants on Fixed-Term Employment (FTE): one each in SAP HCM, SAP FICO, SAP MM, SAP PM, SAP SD and SAP PP, all under the UR category. Applications are sent by post, in the Annexure A form, to the General Manager (Mines) & Additional Charge HoD (Pers.), MOIL Ltd, MOIL Bhavan, 1-A, Katol Road, Nagpur-440 013, on or before 28 November 2026. The consolidated pay is ₹1,33,490 per month for each post.

About MOIL

MOIL Limited (formerly Manganese Ore (India) Limited) is a Government of India enterprise with its office at MOIL Bhavan, 1A, Katol Road, Nagpur. The notice describes it as the largest manganese ore producer in the country, operating ten mines in Maharashtra and Madhya Pradesh. The six consultants are being engaged for the company's SAP S/4HANA with RISE implementation.
