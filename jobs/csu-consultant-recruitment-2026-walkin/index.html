About CSU Prayagraj

Central Sanskrit University is a university under the Ministry of Education. Its Ganganath Jha Campus is at Chandrashekhar Azad Park, Prayagraj-211002 (phone (0532) 2460957). The Director of the Ganganath Jha Campus has issued this engagement notice, and the Consultant (Finance) will work for the campus.

Posts and Eligibility

The notice invites applications for one position of Consultant (Finance), on contract basis and full time. According to the notice, applications are invited from eligible candidates having the required qualifications and experience from Central/State Government Universities/Institutions or equivalent. Bachelor's Degree in Accounting with second division or above. Operate, guide and train staff to operate Tally software. Good knowledge of basics of accounting, TDS and bank operations. Minimum five years' experience of accounting job. Basic knowledge of GFR, GEM, TA/DA rules etc. Post graduate of commerce. The candidate must be above 35 years of age. The notice does not print a cut-off date for counting age or any age relaxation. Candidates should not have been convicted by any Court of Law. No educational qualification will be considered unless it is supported by a Degree Certificate or P

CSU Prayagraj Consultant (Finance) Job Description

The notice lists the following duties for the Consultant (Finance): Coordinate audit replies. Review the accounting procedures and recommend improvements. Develop and submit annual budget plan and finance plan to senior management for approvals. Monitor and manage all expense within the allotted budget. Prepare and submit monthly financial report and expense report to management. Perform financial analysis to support institution development planning. Create and maintain financial models to achieve set goals. Guide and motivate university team to enhance productivity and revenue. Any other work pertaining to financial matters.

What to Bring

No application is sent in advance. The application form in the prescribed format (Annexure-I) is part of the notice and is also available on https://csu-prayagraj.res.in . Candidates bring the filled form with them on the interview day. The form asks for: a passport size photograph; name in block letters and in Devanagari; father's name, date of birth and gender; address for correspondence, telephone/WhatsApp number and e-mail; category (GEN/GEN(EWS)/SC/ST/OBC/PH/OTHER); Matriculation, Intermediate, Bachelor's and Master's details: university or board, year of passing, percentage of marks, division and subject; experience details: duration, institution's name and working position; the signed undertaking. What Happens on the Day

The original documents, the filled Annexure-I form, the self-attested copies and the CV are checked for verification on the day of the interview. If the original of any requisite document is not produced, the candidate becomes ineligible to appear in the interview. A candidate found not fulfilling the criteria at document verification will not be allowed to appear in the interview. Selection is made on the basis of an interview before the Selection Committee of the University. The interview is held in offline mode only at Ganganath Jha Campus, Prayagraj. Requests for an online interview or for a change of venue or date will not be considered. If there is any dispute in the selection process, the decision of the Vice-Chancellor, CSU will be final.
