CPCB Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Recruitment will be made through interview, subject to document verification . Relevant documents, mark-sheets and experience certificates will be verified, and screening will be carried out. In case a large number of candidates apply for a position, the Selection/Screening Committee has discretion to shortlist candidates for interview based on a written exam, percentage of marks, desirable qualification/relevant experience, higher qualification, or any other criteria it deems fit. Only candidates qualifying the document verification and screening criteria will be called for interview by a Selection Committee. Candidates must bring original mark sheets, degrees, testimonials, date-of-birth certificates, experience certificates, self-attested photocopies of each document, Aadhaar card (or other Govt.-approved photo ID), and one passport-size photo for document verification. CPCB Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Applications must be submitted online only, in two steps, through the CPCB website:
  • 2Step 1 – Registration: Visit www.cpcb.gov.in, go to the "Jobs" section, and register with a valid E-mail ID and active mobile number (both must be preserved till the end of the recruitment process). Enter the Name exactly as per the degree certificate (this field cannot be edited after registration), select the Post and Essential Qualification from the dropdown, and enter Date of Birth. Note down the generated Registration Number.
  • 3Step 2 – Application Submission: Log in using the Registration Number and Date of Birth (which also serves as the password). Fill in post selection, qualification and experience details as per the advertisement; upload JPEG images of photo and signature, and the Experience Certificate. Recheck all entries before final submission.
  • 4After submission, confirm the application status shows "submitted" - incomplete applications will not be considered, and CPCB will not be responsible for incomplete applications.
  • 5Applicants required to upload only scanned copies of: (i) Experience Certificate(s), (ii) copy of PhD discipline (if applicable), and (iii) the page showing the applicant's name as author/co-author of any published Paper/Book/Journal; the same must also be produced at the time of interview.
  • 6Candidates applying for more than one post must apply separately for each post.
  • 7Applications, once submitted, cannot be altered, resubmitted, or deleted under any circumstances - candidates are advised to keep all data ready before starting the online form.
