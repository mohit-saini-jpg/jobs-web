DCPU Jangaon Selection Process

Selection will be by a written examination followed, on the same day, by an oral test of 25 marks. The selection list will be declared the same day. For posts at Sl.No.1 to 9: The paper carries 200 objective questions for 90 minutes, in English. Part A of 100 questions is common to these posts. Part B of 100 questions is on the subject of the post applied for. For posts at Sl.No.10 to 12: The paper carries 100 objective questions for 60 minutes, in English and Telugu, and is common to these posts. Marking Scheme: Each question has five options and carries one mark. One fourth of a mark will be deducted for every wrong answer. Nothing is deducted for a question left unanswered. Answers are marked on an OMR answer sheet. Experience Weightage: For the posts of Social Worker, Data Analyst, Assistant cum Data Entry Operator and Outreach Worker, two marks will be added for each completed year
