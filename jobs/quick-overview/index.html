How to Check Andhra University Result 2026 Online

Follow these steps to check and download your Andhra University Result 2026:

  • Visit the official Andhra University result portal at results.andhrauniversity.edu.in.
  • Look for the result link that matches your course, semester, and exam type - for example, "B.Tech/B.Tech+M.Tech Second Semester (1-2) Regular and Supplementary" or the relevant revaluation result link.
  • Click on the correct result link for your course.
  • Enter your registration number or hall ticket number in the field provided.
  • Submit the details to view your result on screen.
  • Check all the details on your result carefully.
  • Download and save the result page, or take a printout for your records.

The exact login fields required (registration number, hall ticket number, or date of birth) may vary slightly depending on the specific course and result link, so students should follow the fields shown on their particular result page.

Details Mentioned on the Andhra University Result

A typical Andhra University result/marks memo generally includes details such as the student's name, registration number, course and semester, subject-wise marks or grades, and overall result status. The exact layout and fields shown may vary by course. Students should verify every detail on their result carefully and contact the university without delay if any discrepancy is found.

Revaluation Results: What to Know

Two of the results released on 07-08-2026 are revaluation results - the B.Pharmacy Seventh Semester (4-1) revaluation (exam held December 2025) and the M.Sc Organic Chemistry Third Semester (2-1) revaluation (exam held November 2025). These are updated results issued after the university re-checked answer scripts for students who had applied for revaluation.

Specific details such as the revaluation application fee, the window during which revaluation could be applied for, and the number of students affected are not available in the source notification. For queries on revaluation status, Andhra University's examination section advises students to contact the Superintendent, E-X Section, or reach out via the university's revaluation support email channel published on the official examinations portal.

Important Instructions for Students

  • Keep your registration number or hall ticket number ready before checking the result.
  • Cross-check your name, registration number, and subject-wise marks carefully once the result is displayed.
  • Download and save a copy of the result as soon as it is available, since results on university portals are sometimes accessible only for a limited period.
  • For any errors or discrepancies in the result, contact the Andhra University examinations section directly rather than relying on third-party sources.
  • The result available online is provisional; refer to the official Andhra University notification for confirmation of its exact status.
