Who Can Apply

Candidates must meet the post-wise essential qualifications and age criteria established for the project: Desirable and Other Qualifications: Project Research Scientist – II (Medical): Candidates having knowledge and experience in clinical analysis of TMD will be preferred. Project Research Scientist – I (Non-Medical): Preference will be given to candidates having good knowledge in Finite Element Modelling and analysis / AI/ML and biomedical engineering, particularly related to TMJ. Selected candidates may have the opportunity to register for a regular Ph.D. program (PS category) at IIITDM Jabalpur as per Institute norms. Project Technical Support - III: Candidates having good knowledge in (1) text and multimodal data handling, CAD modelling, and willing to perform field studies and /OR (2) having experience in oral/dentistry and willing to assist in clinical evaluation will be preferred

Pay and Tenure

All positions are purely temporary, initially for one year and renewable annually subject to satisfactory performance up to a maximum of four years or project completion. Increments of 5% on initial emoluments are provided after every two years subject to performance review. No TA/DA is admissible for appearing in the interview.

Selection and Interview

The selection process involves shortlisting candidates based on qualifications, followed by a skill test and an interview. The skill test and interview for shortlisted candidates will be conducted on 13 November 2026 (Friday) at PDPM IIITDM Jabalpur, Dumna Airport Road, Jabalpur 482005. Exact timings will be communicated to shortlisted candidates via email.
