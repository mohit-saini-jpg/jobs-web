TNPSC Group 4 Eligibility 2026

Eligibility for TNPSC Group 4 varies according to the post code. Candidates must satisfy the required educational, technical and age conditions for the post they select. For a large number of posts, including Village Administrative Officer, Junior Assistant, Bill Collector, Revenue Assistant and several other Group IV posts, the minimum general educational qualification is an SSLC / 10th Standard pass or equivalent . Some posts require additional qualifications. Typist and Steno-Typist posts require the prescribed typewriting and/or shorthand qualification. Certain Junior Assistant, Executive Officer, Junior Inspector and other specialized posts require a degree, specific subject qualification, diploma, computer qualification or other technical certificate. Candidates should check the exact educational qualification against the relevant post code in the official Notification No. 11/2026

TNPSC Group 4 Application Fee 2026

One Time Registration (OTR) is valid for five years from the date of registration. Candidates who already have a valid TNPSC OTR do not need to complete a new registration only for this examination. The ₹100 examination fee must be paid while submitting the application unless the candidate is eligible for an exemption under the TNPSC rules. Applicable fee concessions and free chances vary by category and should be checked in the official notification. Payment should be made through the online modes permitted by TNPSC. Candidates claiming fee exemption must ensure that they satisfy all applicable conditions.

TNPSC Group 4 Photo and Signature Size 2026

Candidates applying for the TNPSC Group 4 Notification 2026 must upload their photograph and signature in the size, dimensions and format prescribed by TNPSC. Incorrect, unclear or improperly sized images may result in problems while submitting the online application. The photograph uploaded for the TNPSC Group 4 2026 application should be a recent colour passport-size photograph taken in a photo studio against a white background . The candidate should face the camera directly, with both ears and part of the neck clearly visible. The photograph should measure 3.5 cm in width and 4.5 cm in height (130 × 170 pixels) . The image file should be between 20 KB and 50 KB , scanned at 200 DPI and saved in JPG format. TNPSC Group 4 Exam Pattern and Syllabus 2026

The TNPSC Group 4 written examination will be conducted as a single OMR-based objective paper . The paper contains 200 questions for a total of 300 marks and has a duration of three hours. Exam Duration: 3 hours. Candidates must secure at least 40% or 60 marks in Part A for Parts B and C to be evaluated. Part A is the Tamil Eligibility-cum-Scoring Test. Eligible differently abled candidates covered by the notification may have the option specified by TNPSC for the language component. The detailed syllabus is provided with the TNPSC notification. TNPSC Group 4 Selection Process 2026

Selection under TNPSC Group 4 Notification 2026 is primarily based on performance in the written examination followed by verification and counselling. Single-stage OMR-based written examination. Preparation of merit/ranking based on the applicable examination marks. Onscreen certificate verification for shortlisted candidates. Physical certificate verification, where applicable. Counselling and post allocation according to merit, reservation rules and candidate preference.
