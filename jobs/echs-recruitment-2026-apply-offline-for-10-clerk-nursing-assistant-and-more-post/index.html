Pay and Terms

The contractual appointments carry fixed monthly remuneration structured as follows: Medical Officer receives Rs. 75,000/-, Dental Officer receives Rs. 75,000/-, Nursing Assistant, Pharmacist, Lab Technician, and Dental Assistant receive Rs. 28,100/- each, while Clerk, Chowkidar, Female Attendant, and Safaiwala receive Rs. 16,800/- each. Appointments are made on a contractual basis for a period of one year for Ex-Servicemen and 11 months for Civilian staff, which is renewable for additional periods of one year or 11 months depending on performance and other conditions. The maximum age limit is 65 years subject to performance.
