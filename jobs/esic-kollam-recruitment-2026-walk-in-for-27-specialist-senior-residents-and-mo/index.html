ESIC Kollam Vacancy 2026 Details

A total of 27 contractual vacancies are available across three post categories: Super Specialist, Specialist, and Senior Resident. The notification clarifies that in case of non-availability of candidates in the advertised category, a post may be filled for 44 days with a UR (Unreserved) candidate. ESIC Kollam Eligibility Criteria 2026

Eligibility requirements differ by post and department. Candidates should check the qualification, experience, and age limit applicable to the specific post and speciality they wish to apply for. For Superspeciality departments, DM/DrNB in the concerned superspeciality is preferred over MD/DNB in General Medicine. For Specialty departments, a Post Graduate Degree/DNB, or Diploma in the concerned speciality after MBBS with TCMC registration is required. ICU: PG Degree or PG Diploma holders in Anaesthesiology/General Medicine/Pulmonary Medicine/Emergency Medicine/Critical Care Medicine, registered with the Medical Council. Casualty: PG Degree in Emergency Medicine is preferred. In its absence, PG degree or PG diploma holders in Anaesthesiology/General Medicine/Pulmonary Medicine/Critical Care Medicine registered with the Medical Council will be considered. ESIC Kollam Selection Process 2026

Selection for all posts will be based on qualification, experience, and a walk-in interview. The interview will be conducted at ESIC Model & Super Specialty Hospital, Asramam, Kollam. Candidates belonging to OBC/SC/ST categories must produce a valid caste certificate issued by the Tahsildar/Revenue Divisional Officer; OBC certificates must confirm the candidate does not belong to the creamy layer, and EWS certificates must be valid and issued in the prescribed government format. Candidates applying under the PWD category must possess a disability certificate issued by a duly constituted Medical Board of a State or Central Government hospital/institution. The appointing authority reserves the right to terminate the service of an appointee at any time, even before completion of tenure, without assigning any reason, by giving one month's notice, or immediately in case of disciplinary action

ESIC Kollam Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is ESIC Kollam Recruitment 2026?
A

Q2How many vacancies are available in ESIC Kollam Recruitment 2026?

Q3What is the last date to apply for ESIC Kollam Recruitment 2026?
