HOCL Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Hindustan Organic Chemicals Limited (HOCL), a Government of India Enterprise, is filling one Unreserved (UR) post of Executive Director & Unit-in-Charge on a regular basis, in the scale of pay of Rs. 120000-280000 (Grade E8 as per Pay Revision w.e.f 01.01.2017) with allowances as admissible under the company's rules. Applicants need a B.E. / B.Tech in one of the listed engineering disciplines and minimum 25 years of post qualification experience as an Executive. Applications are accepted only online through www.hoclindia.com; the portal will be open from 12 November 2026 to 25 November 2026.

About HOCL

Hindustan Organic Chemicals Limited (HOCL) describes itself in the notice as a Government of India Enterprise. The advertised post of Executive Director & Unit-in-Charge is posted at Kochi, where the court of jurisdiction for any dispute about this recruitment also lies. The notice is signed by the Chief Manager (HR) and dated 11 November 2026.

Who Can Apply

B.E. / B.Tech in Chemical / Petrochemical / Petroleum / Mechanical / Electrical / Civil / Instrumentation Engineering. Qualifications should be from a recognized university / institution. Minimum 25 years of post qualification experience as an Executive in PSUs or reputed/leading organizations. At least 5 years' experience during the last 10 years at a Senior Management level in Technical / Operations / Maintenance / Administration in a large industry, preferably a Chemical/Petrochemical/Refinery plant. Indian Nationals only need to apply. Candidates should be in employment at the time of applying for the post. Candidates working in Govt. / Semi-Government / PSUs should have minimum two years of experience in the immediate lower scale of the advertised post. Pay and Terms

The selected candidate will be entitled to Pay and Allowances in the scale of pay of Rs. 120000-280000 (Grade E8 as per Pay Revision w.e.f 01.01.2017), as admissible under the rules of the company. The notice does not list the individual allowances or mention the in-hand salary. Appointment is on a regular basis. Posting will be at Kochi; the company at its discretion may depute the officer to any of its Offices / Projects anywhere in India as per its requirement.
