IGNOU Vacancy 2026 - School-wise and Post-wise Details

A total of 42 vacancies (plus backlog vacancies from previous years) have been notified across 14 Schools of Studies for the posts of Professor, Associate Professor, and Assistant Professor. As per the notification, this also includes backlog vacancies of SC, ST, and OBC categories advertised in earlier years. Category Codes: UR - Unreserved; SC - Scheduled Caste; ST - Scheduled Tribe; OBC - Other Backward Classes; EWS - Economically Weaker Sections; PwBD - Persons with Benchmark Disability; PwBD(a) - Blindness and Low Vision; PwBD(c) - Locomotor Disability including cerebral palsy, leprosy cured, dwarfism, acid attack victims, and muscular dystrophy.

IGNOU Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

The minimum eligibility conditions for all posts shall be as per the University Grants Commission (UGC) Regulations, 2018, and subsequent amendments, along with the norms of concerned regulatory bodies such as NCTE and MCI, wherever applicable. The cut-off date for determining eligibility conditions will be the last date of receipt of online applications.
