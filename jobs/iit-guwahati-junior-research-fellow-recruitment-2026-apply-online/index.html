About IIT Guwahati JRF(GATE)

The vacancy is advertised by the Research and Development Section of the Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati, Guwahati-781039, Assam. The post supports a research project at the School of Interdisciplinary Studies and Sustainability. The project studies the risk of temperature-humidity extremes caused by climate change and their effect on human well-being and energy demand in the Indian sub-continent. Sreeja Pekkat is the project's Principal Investigator.

Who Can Apply

The notice prints the qualification for the JRF(GATE) post as follows: Graduate / Post Graduate Degree in a professional course such as Water Resources Engg. / Energy /Electrical Engg., selected through a process described through any one of the following: 1.
