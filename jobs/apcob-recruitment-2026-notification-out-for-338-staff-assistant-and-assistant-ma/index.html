APCOB Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Each DCCB recruits only for its own district's requirements, so candidates must be local to the erstwhile district of the post they are applying for. For example, only candidates local to erstwhile Chittoor district can apply for Chittoor DCCB posts, and only candidates local to erstwhile Kadapa district can apply for Kadapa DCCB posts, and so on for Nellore, Prakasam, Visakhapatnam, and Vizianagaram. Local status is generally determined based on either of the following: Having studied in an educational institution in the local area for at least 4 consecutive academic years up to the SSC (or equivalent) examination; or Having resided in the local area for at least 4 years immediately preceding the SSC (or equivalent) examination, if not covered under the study criterion above. APCOB Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection to both Staff Assistant and Assistant Manager posts (open market category) is based purely on performance in the Online Written Test, followed by document verification and a medical fitness check. There is no interview for either post. The broad stages are: Stage 1: Online Written Examination Stage 2: Document Verification Stage 3: Medical Fitness Examination Stage 4: Final Appointment (subject to executing the service bond) The online test for Staff Assistant and Assistant Manager (open market candidates) will be conducted in English, carries 200 marks, and has a composite duration of 2 hours (120 minutes). There is a negative marking of 0.25 marks for every wrong answer; unattempted questions carry no penalty. Candidates applying under the PACS quota for Staff Assistant posts will be tested separately, on the working and functions of PACS. The same negative marking rule of 0.
