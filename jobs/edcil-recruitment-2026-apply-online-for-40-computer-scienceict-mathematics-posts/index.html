About EdCIL

EdCIL (India) Limited is a Mini Ratna Category-1 CPSE under the Ministry of Education, located at EdCIL House, 18-A, Sector 16-A, NOIDA 201301 (UP). This notice is titled "Recruitment of STEM Teachers for the Royal Government of Bhutan – Phase IV". The teachers are engaged for the Bhutan Government's higher secondary schools, and the decision on shortlisting and final selection rests with the Royal Government of Bhutan.

Pay and Tenure

Teachers are paid a consolidated gross remuneration of Rs. 1,40,000/- (Rupees One Lakh Forty Thousand only) per month from the date of joining. Local tax as per the "Income Tax Act and Health" contribution of 1% applies on the gross remuneration. The tenure is a minimum of two years. It may be renewed based on performance and subject requirements. Teachers may be posted and relocated anywhere in Bhutan depending on the requirement of the RGoB. The RGoB provides all necessary assistance for the work permit as applicable. Leave, medical benefits, travel passage and other terms of employment are as per the contract with the RGoB. Selected candidates get air tickets for to-and-fro travel on the India–Bhutan sector at the start and end of the tenure only. Selection and Interview

A panel of officials from India and the Royal Government of Bhutan shortlists eligible candidates for interview. Meeting the eligibility norms does not entitle a candidate to an interview. EdCIL/RGoB reserves the right to call only the requisite number of candidates after preliminary screening and short-listing by qualifications, suitability and experience. Shortlisted candidates are informed of the venue, date and schedule on their registered email IDs only. The physical interview is held in New Delhi only. Document verification takes place at the interview. Candidates bring the application printout with photograph affixed and original self-attested copies of certificates for age, qualification, work experience and others as applicable.
