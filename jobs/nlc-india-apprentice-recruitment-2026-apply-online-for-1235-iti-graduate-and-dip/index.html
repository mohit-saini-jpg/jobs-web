NLC India Apprentice Selection Process 2026

The selection process is merit based. The method differs slightly according to the apprentice category. Selection of Trade Apprentices will be based on the percentage of marks obtained in the qualifying ITI examination. For Engineering Graduate, Non-Engineering Graduate and Technician Diploma Apprentices, selection will be based on the percentage of marks obtained in the relevant Bachelor's Degree or Diploma, calculated according to the procedure followed by the concerned university or institution. If grades or CGPA are awarded instead of percentage marks, conversion will be carried out according to the procedure prescribed by the university or institution. If two or more candidates obtain the same percentage of marks at the cut-off level, preference will be given to the older candidate based on date of birth.
