UPPSC Samiksha Adhikari Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must possess all the requisite qualifications up to the last date for submitting applications. The Commission does not advise candidates about their eligibility. Candidates who are still appearing in the examination of the essential qualification are not eligible and need not apply. Qualification: Bachelor's degree, with specific requirements for some posts (see below). Age: 21 to 40 years on 01 July 2026, with relaxations. Marital status: Male candidates who are married and have more than one wife living, and female candidates who have married a person already having a wife, are not eligible unless the Hon'ble Governor has granted an exemption. Reservation and age relaxation: Candidates of reserved categories who are not permanent residents of U.P. are not given the benefit of reservation or age relaxation. Nationality: Not specified in the pages provided.

UPPSC Samiksha Adhikari Selection Process

Preliminary Examination: Held at various centres in the districts of U.P. to select candidates for the Main (Written) Examination. Objective type, with negative marking. Main (Written) Examination: Only candidates declared successful in the Preliminary Examination are admitted. Candidates must fill a fresh application form and pay the Main Examination fee, and must appear against the same Roll No. allotted for the Preliminary Examination. Selection is made on the basis of total marks obtained in the Main (Written) Examination. UPPSC RO ARO Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Candidates must apply online through the O.T.R. based system. Applications sent through any other medium are not accepted. The online application is completed in four stages.
  • 2It is mandatory to complete One Time Registration (O.T.R.) and obtain an O.T.R. number before applying.
  • 3Candidates without an O.T.R. number must obtain it from https://otr.pariksha.nic.in at least 72 hours before submitting the online application.
  • 4Only after obtaining the O.T.R. number can a candidate apply through https://uppsc.up.nic.in.
  • 5First Stage: On the Commission's website, click "ALL NOTIFICATIONS/ADVERTISEMENTS" and open the online advertisement (User Instructions, View Advertisement, Apply). Click "Apply" and then "Authenticate with O.T.R." and tick "Yes" or "No" for "Have You Completed Your O.T.R. Registration". If "Yes", enter the O.T.R. number, click "Proceed", and authenticate through the O.T.P. received on the registered mobile number/email ID or the O.T.R. password. Personal details from the O.T.R. are displayed automatically, and the candidate fills only the essential qualification required for the post. If "No", first obtain the O.T.R. number from the O.T.R. portal and then continue.
  • 6Second Stage: The Applicant Dashboard is displayed. Click "Submit Details" under "Application Part-2" against the applied post. The permanent and correspondence address and application form appear from the O.T.R., along with the preferential qualifications for the post. Choose Yes or No against each preferential qualification.
  • 7Third Stage: In the "Fee Confirmation Window", click "Yes" in front of "Proceed for fee payment". The SBI MOPS page opens with three modes of payment: Net Banking, Card Payments and Other Payment Modes. After payment, a "Payment Transaction Slip" is displayed and should be printed using the Printer Icon. If payment fails, log in through "Candidate Dashboard Login" with the O.T.R. number, authenticate, and click "Pending Payment" to pay the fee.
  • 8Fourth Stage: The application form is displayed on screen and its print should be taken and kept safely, as it must be produced in the Commission's office if any discrepancy arises. After applying, a candidate can modify the qualification details of the applied post only once through "Candidate Dashboard Login", until the last date for correction/modification.
  • 9On the final application page there is a Declaration. Clicking "I do not agree" drops the application. A Preview is available before final submission, and a successful submission report can be printed.
