CG Vyapam Lecturer Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Chhattisgarh Karmachari Chayan Mandal (CGSSB), formerly known as CG Vyapam, has started the online application process for Lecturer (Vyakhyata) posts in government schools of Chhattisgarh. The recruitment is being conducted for Lok Shiksha Sanchalanaya, Chhattisgarh (Directorate of Public Instruction) under two cadres: E Samvarg (E Cadre) and T Samvarg (T Cadre). Online applications are open from 29 September 2026 and the last date to apply is 26 October 2026 (till 5:00 PM). The written recruitment exam (LSL26) is scheduled tentatively for 29 November 2026

CG Vyapam Lecturer Eligibility Criteria

A candidate must hold a postgraduate or equivalent degree in second class in one of the subjects below from a recognised university or institution, along with a B.Ed. degree. The educational qualifications will be treated as valid as per the notifications issued by the National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE) from time to time and the court orders in force. The certificate proving the required educational qualification and other eligibility must be obtained on or before the last date of online application set by CGSSB. Dual, joint and double degrees are governed by the University Grants Commission letter D.O. No. F.1-6/2007 (CPP-II) dated 5 June 2025, as mentioned in the notification. The minimum age is 21 years (completed) and the candidate must not have completed 35 years of age as on 1 January 2026. Age relaxation is available as per the rules mentioned in the notification. CG Vyapam Lecturer Application Fee

The examination fee must be paid online while filling the application form. The fee is charged as per the order dated 17 December 2015 mentioned in the notification. Candidates who are local residents of Chhattisgarh and appear in the exam will get their exam fee refunded after the exam result is declared. The amount will be returned to the same bank account from which the fee was paid.

CG Vyapam Lecturer Salary

The Lecturer (Vyakhyata) post is a Gazetted Class II post under Pay Matrix Level-09. Selected candidates will also get Dearness Allowance and other allowances as per the orders issued by the Chhattisgarh government from time to time. The exact monthly salary amount is not specified in the provided notification.

CG Vyapam Lecturer Selection Process

The selection list will be prepared on the basis of the result of the written recruitment exam conducted by CGSSB. The notification does not mention an interview round. Appointments will follow the Chhattisgarh School Education Service (Academic and Administrative Cadre) Recruitment and Promotion Rules, 2026. The selection list will be valid for six months from the date of its release. A waiting list will also be prepared for each category, with names up to 25 percent of the vacant posts (minimum one). The waiting list will be valid for nine months from the date of the selection list. Selected candidates will be appointed on probation for three years, which can be extended by up to one year if work is found unsatisfactory.
