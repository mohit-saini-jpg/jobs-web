Pay and Tenure

The engagement is purely temporary. The term ordinarily does not exceed an initial 3 years and may be extendable up to a maximum of 5 years. Only a consolidated honorarium is paid; allowances such as DA, HRA, MA, GPF and NPS are not admissible. The pay rules and service terms are below. Need-based visits post: a total of max. 15 visits of medical or allied health professionals in a month, with at least 4 hours per visit. Medical Professionals with MBBS/PG receive Rs. 3750/- per visit; allied health professionals receive Rs. 3000/- per visit. Paid leave of absence may be allowed at 1.5 days for each completed month of service. An annual increment of 5% is based on completion of one year from the date of joining and a satisfactory performance report evaluated by the department. Q3What is the qualification for NIEPMD consultant posts?
A
It varies by post. For example, a Nurse needs a Diploma in Nursing and a Physiotherapist needs BPT, each with a minimum of 2 years' relevant post-qualification experience. A Data Entry Operator needs a graduate degree with knowledge of computer.
Q4What is the age limit for NIEPMD Recruitment 2026?

The maximum age is 56 years for all positions except Sl. No. 11, the need-based visits post, which has no age limit. Age is reckoned as on the closing date of receipt of application.
