MPHC Stenographer Selection Process

The MPHC Stenographer selection process has two stages: an Online Preliminary Examination (100 marks) and a Hindi Stenography/English Stenography Skill Test (100 marks). English Stenography is held for candidates who choose the Assistant Grade-III (English Knowing) post. The examination is common for all the posts and has multiple-choice (M.C.Q.) questions for a maximum of 100 marks, with 1 mark for each question. The duration is 120 minutes (2 hours). It may be held in one shift, or in more than one shift if required, at centres including Jabalpur, Bhopal, Indore, Gwalior, Sagar, Satna and Ujjain (all, or one or more as required). If it is held in more than one shift, the question papers will be different. The date(s), time and centres will be published through a notice on the High Court website.
