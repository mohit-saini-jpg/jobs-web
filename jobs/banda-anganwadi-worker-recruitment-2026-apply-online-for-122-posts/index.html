Banda Anganwadi Worker Eligibility 2026

This post is open only to women candidates . Candidate's age must be a minimum of 18 years and a maximum of 35 years, calculated as on 1 July 2026. Candidate must be a resident of the same Gram Sabha, or of the relevant Ward for urban-area posts, where the vacant centre is located; residence, income and caste proof are required. For urban-area posts, the ward number and ward name must be mentioned in the residence certificate, which must be digitally signed at the Tehsil level. Merit list is prepared based on marks obtained from Intermediate up to Post-Graduate level qualifications. Note: While the age is determined strictly from the date of birth recorded in the High School (10th standard) certificate, the merit list for selection is prepared using marks scored from Intermediate (12th standard) through Post-Graduate qualifications.

Banda Anganwadi Worker Selection Process

Selection is on a merit-list basis – there is no written examination or interview mentioned in the notification. Key points: Merit is determined from marks scored in qualifications from Intermediate (12th standard) up to Post-Graduate level. Income eligibility is assessed against the rural/urban poverty-line income limits fixed under the Revenue Section-9 Government Order dated 30.09.2011 and the Social Welfare Section-2 (UP Government) Order No. 22/2015/2123/26-2-2015 dated 14.09.2015 (as amended); the merit list within the applicable income limit is prepared and selection is carried out accordingly. For widowed candidates, a death certificate of the spouse and a copy of the family register, attested by the Gram Panchayat Adhikari (or the concerned Nagar Nikay for urban areas), is required. This recruitment is open only to women candidates who are residents of the specific Gram Sabha/Ward where the vacant centre is located. Applications will be accepted only through the official online portal, upanganwadibharti.in; offline or postal applications will not be accepted or recognized.
