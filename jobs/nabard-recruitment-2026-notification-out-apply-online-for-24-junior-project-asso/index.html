NABARD Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD), an all-India apex organization wholly owned by the Government of India, has invited online applications under Advertisement No. 05 for 24 Specialists on contract: 21 Junior Project Associates (₹75,000 - ₹1,00,000) and 3 Senior Project Associates (₹1,25,000 - ₹1,50,000). The essential qualification is a graduate degree in Agriculture with at least 55% marks in aggregate. Applications are accepted only online on www.nabard.org from 21.11.2026 to 07.12.2026.

NABARD describes itself in this notice as an all-India apex organization, wholly owned by the Government of India and an equal opportunity employer. Pay and Tenure

The Specialist is liable for tax as per the Income Tax Act and Rules in force, with tax deducted at source. The amount includes any statutory payments NABARD may have to pay on the Specialist's behalf. An annual increment up to a maximum of 10% can be granted based on satisfactory performance and at the discretion of the Bank. The contract is for 02 years from the date of appointment, extendable for a further period of 03 years. Extension is reviewed one year at a time and will in no case go beyond 05 years. The Specialist is not entitled to any superannuation benefits.

NABARD Recruitment 2026 Job Description

The notice prints the same key accountabilities (KRAs/KPIs) for both posts, listed separately for each. The Junior Project Associate will support ERP-enabled transformation and strengthening of FPOs through: ERP & Digital Enablement: onboarding FPOs onto ERP platforms; supporting data migration, validation and entry; providing user training and troubleshooting; monitoring adoption and data quality Project Implementation: coordination with CBBOs, FPOs and stakeholders; supporting programme rollout and activities; assisting in training, workshops and exposure visits Monitoring & Evaluation: tracking physical and financial progress; maintaining dashboards and MIS; conducting field visits and validation Compliance & Documentation: supporting claims scrutiny and documentation; tracking statutory and governance compliance Other Responsibilities: capacity building and digital literacy promotion

Selection and Interview

Candidates are shortlisted in the ratio of 1:3 based on their qualifications, relevant experience etc. The stages differ by post. Senior Project Associate: Technical Assessment (20 marks) and Interview (80 marks). The Technical Assessment evaluates Excel-based reporting and data analysis, problem-solving and analytical ability, and presentation skills. Junior Project Associate: Interview only (100 marks). Roll numbers of candidates shortlisted for interview will be published on www.nabard.org , and the Bank may send intimation to download call letters through the registered email ID. Final appointment is based on the decision of the Selection Committee. Selected candidates must undergo a pre-recruitment medical examination and are appointed only if declared medically fit by the Bank's Medical Officer. NABARD Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

NABARD admits candidates on the basis of the information in the online application and verifies eligibility at the stage of interview / joining. Candidature is cancelled if information is false or incorrect, or if the candidate does not satisfy the eligibility criteria according to the Bank. Contract terms: either side may end the engagement by giving one month's notice, and the Bank may terminate at any time without prior notice. If the notice period is not served, the Specialist pays a sum equal to the pay for the notice period. Leave is a maximum of 30 days per calendar year on a proportionate basis, and un-availed leave is not carried forward. Total absence cannot exceed 10 days at any point except on emergency medical grounds with a medical certificate. Quarters may be allotted on request, subject to availability, against a licence fee of 0.5% of remuneration. If quarters are not allotted, a fixed HRA of 10% of remuneration is payable. Travelling and Halting Allowance is payable for official tours as admissible to Bank officers of equivalent grade. Medical facility is for self only, at the Bank's Dispensary wherever available. The Specialist has no right or claim for regular employment or placement in NABARD. Legal proceedings can be instituted only in Mumbai. Any corrigendum will be published only on www.nabard.org. For problems with the form or fee payment, complaints may be made at https://cgrs.ibps.in/ with "NABARD – Recruitment to the post of Specialists on contract" in the email subject.
