Service Conditions

The posts are to be filled on a regular basis, at Functional Pay Level FPL-6 under the 7th CPC. Any advertised post may be withdrawn at any stage without assigning a reason, and the University may choose not to fill any of the posts. If the recruitment is not completed within six months, the posts will be re-advertised. If an inadvertent mistake in selection is detected at any stage, even after the appointment letter is issued, the University may modify, withdraw or cancel any communication made to the applicant. No TA/DA is paid for attending any written test, skill test, presentation or interview. All disputes are subject to the jurisdiction of Palwal Court only.

SVSU Skill Instructor Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1Which discipline has the most Skill Instructor vacancies?
A
CSE/IT has the most, with 10 posts under Advt. No. T-42/2026. Agriculture and Medical Lab. Technology follow with 03 each.
Q2Can a diploma holder apply for SVSU Skill Instructor posts?
A
Yes, in some disciplines. Civil, Electrical, Electronics and Mechanical (RAC), for example, accept a first class three-year Engineering Diploma with six years' experience.
Q3Is a National Trade Certificate accepted?
A
Yes, for Civil, Electrical, Electronics, Hospitality, Mechanical (RAC) and Printing & Technology. These need eight years' experience, including the training period.
Q4Can candidates from outside Haryana apply?
A
Yes, but a reserved-category applicant from another state is considered only against General posts and pays Rs. 1000/-.
Q5Can I apply for more than one discipline?
A
Yes. Each post needs its own application and its own fee.
Q6Is my eligibility checked before the written test?
A
No. All applicants may appear in the written test, and eligibility is checked only after a candidate qualifies both tests.
Q7What marks must a General candidate score to reach document verification?
A
A General candidate needs 40% in each test and 50% cumulative marks across the written and skill tests.
Q8I am a government employee and my NOC is delayed. Can I still apply?
A
Yes. Apply directly with your experience certificate and hand in the NOC from your present employer at Document Verification.
Q9Will test dates be published in newspapers?
A
No. The dates of any test or interview and the shortlisted lists will be posted on the University website.
Q10Which registration is mandatory for health science posts?
A
Nursing needs State Nursing Council registration, Pharmacy needs State Pharmacy Council registration, and Physiotherapy needs registration with the State council/NCAHP.

A
