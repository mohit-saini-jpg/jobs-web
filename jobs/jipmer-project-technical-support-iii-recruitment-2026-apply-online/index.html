About JIPMER Project Technical Support-III (PTS III)

JIPMER is a medical education and research institute at Dhanwantri Nagar, Puducherry-6. The post is part of the GiveMeTIME project, a multicenter observational study run by the Department of Medical Oncology and funded by ICMR, an external agency. The Project Investigator (PI) is Dr. Prasanth Ganesan.

Who Can Apply

The notice prints two routes to eligibility. A candidate needs one of the following: B. Sc in Psychology with three years' experience in field of psychology, or with a PG degree in any of these subjects: counselling psychology, applied psychology or psycho-oncology. B.Sc/UG degree in Social/Health Sciences with three years' experience in field of medical social work/patient counselling, or with a PG degree in any of these subjects: Medical social work, Master of Public Health. Clinical work with patients Working in cancer field or with cancer patients Experience in qualitative research Qualification and experience should be in a relevant discipline/field and from a reputed institution/organization. JIPMER Project Technical Support-III (PTS III) Job Description

Conduct both qualitative and quantitative interviews with patients. Sometimes provide psychological support and counseling. Work with patients with advanced cancer. According to the notice, the person in this post is likely to gain knowledge and skill in psycho oncology, research methods, writing project reports and analysing qualitative data. The application form also states that the project work involves travel to the field or to other centres for the study, and it asks applicants whether they are willing to travel.

Selection and Interview

JIPMER will shortlist applicants based on the details in their application, CV and documentary proof. The list of eligible candidates will also be displayed on the JIPMER website. Only shortlisted candidates will be called for a written test and/or interview. The final selection will be based on the CV, the application and the written test/interview. If the number of applications is very large, stricter criteria than those listed above may be used to decide eligibility. The decision of the Project Investigator will be final. The written test will cover basic knowledge of cancer, knowledge about clinical research, GCP, patient care related skills, the consenting process and drug storage. The interview will cover the same areas as the written test, plus the ability to interact and respond to situations in clinical research and patient management.
