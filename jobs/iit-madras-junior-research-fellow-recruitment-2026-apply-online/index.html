IIT Madras Junior Research Fellow Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

IIT Madras Recruitment 2026 is for 2 Junior Research Fellow (JRF) posts in the Department of Civil Engineering. The pay is Rs 37,000 + 27% HRA per month. The posts are purely on contract, initially for six months, and may be extended later based on performance. Candidates need the four-year bachelor's and two-year master's engineering degrees set out below. They must also have passed GATE or clear the national-level selection process that IIT Madras will conduct for this announcement. Applications are accepted online only, through the IC&SR careers portal, until 9 November 2026.

Who Can Apply

The notice prints these minimum qualifications for the Junior Research Fellow (JRF) post: A four-year bachelor's degree in Civil/Agricultural/Geomatics Engineering, and A two-year master's degree in Civil/Agricultural and allied Engineering disciplines with specialisation in Hydrology/Hydraulics/Water Resources/Irrigation Water Management/Irrigation and Drainage Engineering, or Equivalent. In addition to the degrees, a candidate must meet one of these conditions: (a) Candidates should have passed GATE, OR (b) the candidate should clear the National Level selection processes to be conducted by IIT Madras specific to this announcement. Candidates with expertise in GIS (QGIS, ArcGIS), Field Hydrology, measurements and hydrologic modelling (Mike+, HEC-RAS, HEC-HMS, SWMM) would be given preference. Pay and Tenure

The pay is Rs 37,000 per month, with 27% HRA on top of it. The appointment is temporary and purely on a contract basis. The duration is initially six months.
