DSSSB Combined Exam 2026 Selection Process

Separate examinations are held for each post code. Minimum qualifying marks (MCQ/Descriptive stages): UR/EWS – 50% , OBC(Delhi)/SC/ST/PwBD/ESM – 45% . Each MCQ carries 1 mark, with negative marking of 0.25 for each wrong answer. DSSSB conducts the MCQ, Descriptive and Shorthand/Typing tests; Delhi District Courts (Tis Hazari) conducts the Driving Test/Interview and prepares the final merit list Marks from Computer Based Examinations are normalized (where applicable) as per DSSSB's published formula No re-evaluation/re-checking of answer sheets is permitted In a tie, the senior-in-age candidate ranks higher; if DOB also matches, alphabetical order of first name (English) decides
