MBSPSU Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

For Assistant Professor (Physical Education), candidates generally require a Master's degree with at least 55% marks or an equivalent grading-system qualification in the concerned or allied discipline from a recognized university. The notification refers to UGC Regulations, 2018, and subsequent applicable amendments. Applicants claiming Ph.D. exemption must meet the notified conditions rather than relying on possession of a Ph.D. degree alone. Applicants must have a recognized Ph.D., a Master's degree with at least 55% marks, a minimum of eight years of relevant teaching or research experience and at least seven publications in peer-reviewed or UGC-listed journals. A research score of at least 75 under the applicable UGC Academic/Research Score provisions is also required. Candidates must possess a graduate degree in any discipline along with the prescribed Sports Coaching qualification
