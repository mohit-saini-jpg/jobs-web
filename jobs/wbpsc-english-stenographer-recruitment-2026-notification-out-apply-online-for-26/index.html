Candidates must meet the prescribed educational qualification, computer typing requirement, language eligibility and age conditions. WBPSC English Stenographer Selection Process 2026

The recruitment examination consists of three papers carrying a combined maximum of 600 marks . Paper I: General English examination. Paper II: Dictation and Transcription examination. Paper III: Computer Typing examination. Final merit preparation using the combined marks obtained in all three papers, subject to qualifying criteria. Medical fitness examination and verification of antecedents before appointment. The Commission will first conduct Paper I. Based on Paper I results, a limited number of candidates will be admitted to Paper II and Paper III. Final Merit Rule: The final merit list will be prepared using the combined marks in Paper I, Paper II and Paper III. Paper I is not merely a qualifying screening examination; its marks also contribute to final merit.

A
Not below 18 years but not more than 44 years as on 01.01.2025 (i.e. born not earlier than 2nd Jan, 1981 and not later than 1st Jan, 2007).
