  • 1Visit jrhms.jharkhand.gov.in or recruitment.jharkhand.gov.in.
  • 2Fill and submit the Online Application Form (OAF) - no other mode of submission is allowed.
  • 3Provide all mandatory information (marked with an asterisk) in the application form.
  • 4Upload the required documents: recent profile photograph, signature image, 10th marksheet & certificate, 12th marksheet & certificate, essential qualification documents, essential experience documents (or, for currently employed candidates, appointment letter and last 3 months' salary certificate/bank statement), caste certificate (if required), PH certificate (if applicable), registration certificate (if applicable), and any other document required for the specific position.
  • 5Pay the application fee, if applicable, and ensure the form is submitted before the closing date.
  • 6Confirm that the Applicant Status shows "Submitted Successfully" before the deadline of 13 September 2026, 11:59 PM.
