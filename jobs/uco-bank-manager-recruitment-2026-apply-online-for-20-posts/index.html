UCO Bank Specialist Officer Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

A candidate must be a citizen of India, or a subject of Nepal or Bhutan, or a Tibetan refugee who came to India before January 1962, or a person of Indian origin who has migrated from specified countries with the intention of permanently settling in India (subject to an eligibility certificate from the Government of India for the latter categories). Educational qualification and experience are assessed as on 01.08.2026. All educational qualifications must be from a University/Institution/Board recognized by the Government of India, with the final result declared on or before 01.08.2026. Candidates must indicate the percentage of marks obtained in Graduation while registering; where CGPA/OGPA is awarded, it must be converted to a percentage as per the University's norms.

UCO Bank Specialist Officer Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection will be through an Online Written Examination and/or Screening and/or Group Discussion and/or Interview, or any other process decided by the Bank. A screening committee will examine whether candidates fulfil the required eligibility criteria; the Bank may decide the parameters for screening. Shortlisting is purely provisional, without document verification; candidature is subject to verification of details/documents as and when called by the Bank. Only the most suitable candidates, as decided by the Bank, will be called for the selection process - mere satisfaction of eligibility norms does not entitle a candidate to be called forward. Qualifying marks for the written examination/interview/selection process will be decided by the Bank. The merit list will be prepared in descending order based on scores in the Online Written Examination and/or Group Discussion and/or Interview.
