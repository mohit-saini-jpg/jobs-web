What Is Spot Admission Round 1 and CW Round 1?

Spot Admission Round 1 is an on-the-spot seat allocation round for candidates who registered for DU B.Tech counselling but were not admitted in the earlier regular allocation rounds, and for seats that remain vacant after those rounds. It is run alongside the Declaration of Round 1 of CW, which is a separate allocation exercise for the CW supernumerary category.

The notice itself does not spell out what "CW" stands for. In DU's general admission nomenclature, this abbreviation is commonly used for the "Children/Wards of Defence and Paramilitary Personnel" supernumerary category (which can include war widows' children). This explanation is based on DU's standard admission terminology and not on text explicitly stated in this particular notice, so candidates unsure of their category eligibility should confirm with the Admission Branch directly.

How to Apply for DU BTech Spot Admission Round 1

  • Visit the official DU admission portal at admission.uod.ac.in.
  • Log in to your CSAS/B.Tech admission dashboard using your registered credentials.
  • Locate the option for Spot Admission Round 1 on your dashboard.
  • Select "Spot Admission" and choose from the available vacant seats by programme and category.
  • Submit your application within the window: July 30, 2026, to 4:59 pm, August 1, 2026.

How to Accept an Allocated Seat

  • Log in to the admission dashboard after the allocation result is declared.
  • Check the allotted programme and category under Spot Round 1 or CW Round 1, as applicable.
  • Click "Accept" on the allocated seat within the deadline given in the schedule table above.
  • Wait for Faculty of Technology verification and approval of the online application.
  • Complete the online payment of admission fees before the last date specified for that round.

Important Instructions

  • Candidates are advised to strictly adhere to the schedule published in the notice.
  • Vacant seat numbers are indicative as of July 29, 2026, and may change due to reconciliation, upgrades, or grievance resolution.
  • Separate deadlines apply for CW Round 1 and Spot Admission Round 1 check the correct row in the schedule table for your category.
  • Seat acceptance, document verification, and fee payment must all be completed within their respective windows to retain the allotted seat.
