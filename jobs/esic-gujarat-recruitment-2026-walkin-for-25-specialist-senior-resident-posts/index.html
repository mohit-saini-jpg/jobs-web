ESIC Gujarat Recruitment 2026 - Eligibility Criteria

Full Time Specialist: PG Degree / Diploma in concerned specialty and registered with Medical Council. Part Time Specialist: PG Degree / Diploma in concerned specialty and registered with Medical Council. Senior Resident: PG Degree or PG Diploma holders in concerned specialty and registered with Medical Council. In the absence of PG qualified doctors, MBBS graduates who possess at least 2 years working experience in the concerned specialty will be considered for selection. However, preference will be given to PG qualified candidate/s.

ESIC Gujarat Recruitment 2026 - Application Fee

No application fee is mentioned in the official notification.

ESIC Gujarat Recruitment 2026 - Age Limit

Full Time Specialist: Maximum age limit is 69 years. Part Time Specialist: Maximum age limit is 69 years. Senior Resident: Maximum age limit is 45 years.

ESIC Gujarat Recruitment 2026 - Salary / Stipend

Full Time Specialist: Rs. 60,000/- fixed per month. + (15,000/- for Visiting charges in case of emergency call); additional remuneration Rs. 800.00/-per call for every additional hour beyond 16 Hrs/week. Available on call 24x7 if he/she is drawing emergency call charges. Part Time Specialist: Rs. 1,35,129/- fixed per month for candidate having PG/Diploma. In addition to the pay, he will be entitled for transport allowance with DA on transport allowance as per rules. An amount of Rs.1,688/- will be deducted for S.R.s possess only Diploma and Rs. 2,813/- will be deducted if the S.R.s does not possess Diploma/Degree.
