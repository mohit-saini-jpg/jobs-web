AIIMS Bathinda Selection Process

Applications received within the prescribed date/time, with the requisite fee, are scrutinized by the Institute's scrutiny committee. Only eligible candidates are called for a personal interview before the Standing Selection Committee of the Institute. If the number of applicants is large, the selection committee may devise shortlisting criteria, which may include a written examination. Interview date, time, and shortlisting information will be published only on the AIIMS Bathinda website, and interview letters will be sent by email only. The interview will be held at AIIMS Bathinda only; online interviews (e.g., via Skype/Viber) are not permitted, and no TA/DA is paid for attending. Selected candidates must be declared medically fit by the competent authority; appointment is subject to a review/probation period of two years.
