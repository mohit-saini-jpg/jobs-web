Karnataka Beat Forester Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

The Karnataka Forest Department follows a multi-stage recruitment process involving academic merit, physical fitness and written examination performance. Online application and eligibility scrutiny. Shortlisting based on PUC or equivalent examination marks. Physical Standard Test (PST). Physical Endurance Test. Physical Efficiency Test. Written Examination. Preparation of merit list. Original document verification. Final selection, medical verification and appointment formalities. Candidates are initially shortlisted for physical tests in a ratio of 1:20 based on their PUC or equivalent examination marks, subject to the applicable recruitment rules.

Karnataka Beat Forester Physical Standard Test 2026

Candidates must satisfy the prescribed physical measurements before proceeding to subsequent physical tests. Relaxed standards apply only to candidates meeting the specific eligibility and certificate conditions for the notified local forest-dweller category. Candidates must satisfy the prescribed eyesight, hearing and medical fitness requirements. The notice specifies distance vision standards of 6/6 in the better eye and 6/9 in the worse eye, with or without glasses. Disqualifying medical conditions specified in the notification include knock knees, bow legs, flat feet, varicose veins, specified speech or hearing impairments, hydrocele in men and disqualifying vision conditions. Candidates should read the complete medical and physical standards on pages 16 and 17 of the notification before applying.
