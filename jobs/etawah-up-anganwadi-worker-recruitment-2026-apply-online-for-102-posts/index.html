Etawah UP Anganwadi Worker Eligibility Criteria & Qualification 2026

Only female candidates are eligible for these posts. Minimum educational qualification: Intermediate (12th) or its equivalent. Candidates with higher qualification up to postgraduate level will also be considered; merit will be prepared based on the maximum qualification held. A candidate must be a permanent resident of the ward/Gram Sabha where the vacant post exists. If no eligible candidate is available from that Gram Sabha, a permanent resident of the concerned Nyay Panchayat is considered. For urban (Shahri) posts, the relevant ward number and ward name must be mentioned in the online residence certificate. Only updated residence and caste certificates issued online with digital signature from the Tehsil, and verifiable online, will be accepted. Etawah UP Anganwadi Worker Reservation & Priority Order 2026

The notification lays down a priority (वरीयता) order for selection within an eligible Gram Sabha/ward, applied in the following sequence: Permanent residents of the same Gram Sabha/ward (urban areas) who are from families living below the poverty line (BPL), including widows and legally divorced/deserted women. If no such candidate is available, widowed/divorced/deserted women from BPL families in the same Gram Sabha/ward. If still unavailable, women from families living above the poverty line in the same Gram Sabha/ward. If no eligible candidate is available in the Gram Sabha under any of the above categories, selection is made category-wise from the concerned Nyay Panchayat. BPL category is determined as per Social Welfare Department (Anubhag-2) Order No. 22/2015/2123/26-2-2015 dated 14 September 2015 (as amended). Etawah UP Anganwadi Worker Selection Process 2026

Selection is merit-based, prepared according to the candidate's educational qualification (from Intermediate up to postgraduate level), subject to the residence requirement and the priority/reservation order described above. A written examination or interview is not mentioned in the notification. The final decision on filling, not filling, or cancelling any post lies with the selection committee.

Etawah UP Anganwadi Worker Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

Only online applications submitted through upanganwadibharti.in within the prescribed timeframe will be accepted; offline applications will not be considered. Documents must be uploaded exactly as declared in the online form; no offline or postal submission of documents is valid. If any error, false, or fabricated information/document is found, the candidature will be rejected. The number of vacant posts may increase or decrease. Filling, not filling, or cancelling the recruitment for these posts rests with the selection committee.
