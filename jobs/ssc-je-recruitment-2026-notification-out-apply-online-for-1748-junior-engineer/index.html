SSC JE Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

To apply for SSC JE 2026 , candidates must meet the nationality, educational qualification, and age criteria prescribed for the post and organization they are applying to. A candidate must be: A citizen of India, or A subject of Nepal, or A subject of Bhutan, or A person of Indian origin who has migrated from Pakistan, Burma, Sri Lanka, or specified East African countries with the intention of permanently settling in India (an eligibility certificate from the Government of India is required for these categories).

SSC JE Qualification

Essential educational qualifications differ by organization and post. Broadly: JE (Civil): Degree in Civil Engineering from a recognized university/institute; or a three-year diploma in Civil Engineering plus relevant work experience (requirement varies by organization). JE (Electrical) / JE (Mechanical) / JE (Electrical & Mechanical): Degree or diploma in the relevant Electrical/Mechanical/Automobile engineering discipline from a recognized university/institute, with experience required in some organizations. JE (Telecom): Bachelor's degree in Engineering/Technology in Electrical, Electronics, Electrical Communications, Computer Science, Telecommunications, Information Technology, or Instrumentation. Scientific Assistant (IMD): Bachelor's degree in Science with Physics as one of the subjects, or a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science/Computer Applications/Electronics/Electronics & Comm

SSC JE Salary

Selected candidates will be appointed to Group 'B' posts in Pay Level-6 of the Pay Matrix under the 7th Central Pay Commission, with a pay scale of ₹35,400 – ₹112,400 . Actual in-hand salary will also include applicable allowances (such as DA, HRA and TA) as per the rules of the respective recruiting organization, which are not detailed in this notice.

SSC JE Selection Process

Selection for SSC JE 2026 is based on the following stages: Paper-I (Computer Based Examination) – Qualifying in nature. Covers General Intelligence & Reasoning (50 questions/50 marks), General Awareness (50 questions/50 marks), and the relevant engineering/technical subject based on the post applied for (100 questions/100 marks). Total duration is 2 hours (2 hours 40 minutes for candidates eligible for a scribe). Negative marking of 0.25 marks applies for each wrong answer. Paper-II (Computer Based Examination) – For candidates shortlisted from Paper-I. Covers the relevant engineering/technical subject in depth (100 questions/300 marks) in 2 hours (2 hours 40 minutes for scribe-eligible candidates). SSC JE Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Complete One-Time Registration (OTR) on the SSC website (ssc.gov.in). An OTR already generated on the old website (ssc.nic.in) is not valid on the new site.
  • 2Log in and fill the online application form for the JE Examination 2026, either via the SSC website or the "mySSC" mobile app.
  • 3Upload photograph and signature, or opt for Aadhaar-based authentication, which exempts candidates from certain photo/signature format rejections.
  • 4Select your organization, post, and relevant subject/stream as per your educational qualification - this choice cannot be changed later.
  • 5Pay the application fee online (if applicable) using BHIM UPI, Net Banking, or a Visa/MasterCard/Maestro/RuPay debit card.
  • 6Preview and submit the form, and save/print a copy for your records.

SSC JE Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is SSC JE Recruitment 2026?
A
It is the official recruitment notice from the Staff Selection Commission for filling Junior Engineer (Civil/Electrical/Mechanical/Telecom) posts and Scientific Assistant (IMD) posts across various Central Government organizations.
Q2How many vacancies are available in SSC JE 2026?
A
There are 1748 tentative vacancies. The final, post-wise and category-wise vacancy numbers will be published separately on the SSC website.
Q3What is the last date to apply for SSC JE 2026?
A
The last date to submit the online application is 22.09.2026 (23:00 hours), and the fee payment deadline is 23.09.2026 (23:00 hours).
Q4What is the qualification required for SSC JE 2026?
A
It varies by post and organization — generally a relevant engineering degree or diploma (Civil/Electrical/Mechanical/Telecom) for JE posts, and a Bachelor's degree in Science/Computer Science/Electronics for the Scientific Assistant (IMD) post. Check the organization-wise table in the official notice for exact requirements.
Q5What is the age limit for SSC JE 2026?
A
Age limit is either up to 30 years or up to 32 years, depending on the organization/post, as reckoned on 01.08.2026. Category-wise age relaxation is applicable as per government rules.
Q6What is the application fee for SSC JE 2026?
A
The fee is ₹100. Women candidates, and SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen candidates, are exempted from paying the fee.
Q7How can I apply online for SSC JE 2026?
A
Candidates must complete One-Time Registration (OTR) and then fill the online application form on ssc.gov.in or the mySSC mobile app, upload photo/signature (or use Aadhaar authentication), pay the applicable fee, and submit the form before the deadline.
Q8What is the selection process for SSC JE 2026?
A
Selection involves Computer Based Examination Paper-I (qualifying), Paper-II, Document Verification, and — for specific organizations such as BRO — Physical Standards and Medical Examination.
