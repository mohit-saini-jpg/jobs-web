DPCC Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Download the advertisement and the prescribed application form from the official DPCC website: dpcc.delhigovt.nic.in
  • 2Fill the application form in the prescribed proforma, in English or Hindi.
  • 3Attach a recent passport-size photograph and all required self-attested documents.
  • 4Route the application through proper channel, along with bio-data, attested APAR dossier copies for the last 5 years, Integrity Certificate, Vigilance Clearance, and statement of Major/Minor penalties (if any).
  • 5Superscribe the name of the post applied for on the envelope.
  • 6Send the completed application to: The Administrative Officer, Delhi Pollution Control Committee, 3rd Floor, IT Park, DMRC Building, Shastri Park, Delhi-110053.
  • 7Ensure the application reaches DPCC within the relevant phase deadline (31 July 2026 / 14 August 2026 / 31 August 2026).
