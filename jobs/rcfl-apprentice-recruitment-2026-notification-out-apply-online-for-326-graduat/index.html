RCFL Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Rashtriya Chemicals and Fertilizers Limited (RCFL) is a leading profit-making Public Sector Enterprise engaged in manufacturing and marketing fertilizers and industrial chemicals, with annual revenue of approximately ₹18,690.90 crore. RCF was accorded "Navratna" status by the Government of India in August 2023 and operates two manufacturing units in Maharashtra - at Thal (Raigad district) and Trombay (Chembur, Mumbai). Under this notification, RCF is engaging a total of 326 apprentices across Graduate Apprentice, Technician Apprentice, and Trade Apprentice categories under the Apprentices Act, 1961. RCFL Apprentice Eligibility Criteria 2026-27

The prescribed qualification must be from a recognised University/Institute, completed as a regular full-time course. Distance Learning, Part-Time, or Correspondence mode qualifications are not eligible. Allied branches of Diploma in Engineering are not applicable/acceptable. Candidates who have completed Act Apprentice training or job experience of one year or more, or who are pursuing higher studies, are not eligible. The candidate's passing certificate/final year mark sheet of the required qualification must be dated on or after 1 July 2023, with the minimum percentage calculated on the final year or the aggregate of the last two semesters. Applicants with a B.Sc in Chemistry, Physics, and Mathematics will be considered only for Attendant Operator (Chemical Plant) (AOCP) and Laboratory Assistant (Chemical Plant) (LACP) trades under the RDSDE scheme, with Chemistry as the major subject

RCFL Apprentice Stipend 2026-27

The stipend applicable to apprentices follows the notification issued by the Board of Apprenticeship Training (Western Region), Mumbai (BOAT WR/2026/529, dated 25.09.2019) under the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. Apprentice trainees are not entitled to any other benefits or allowances besides the consolidated stipend under the Apprentices Act and applicable guidelines. Stipend payable by RCF is paid monthly on a fixed date. Stipend payable by BOAT is disbursed directly to ACT Apprentices, and RCF is not responsible for any delay or non-payment by BOAT. Additionally, RCF provides morning breakfast, tea, and a vegetarian lunch thali in the company canteen during training (no reimbursement if not availed), OPD-basis medical facilities at RCF Hospital for trainees themselves, and uniform and Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) as per company policy. RCFL Apprentice Posting & Training Timing

Apprentices will be trained at RCF's units at Trombay, Mumbai, or Thal, Raigad District, as per the company's requirements. Candidates must select either Trombay or Thal in the "Posting At" drop-down while applying; this choice cannot be changed once selected. Final posting depends on the approved intake quota of the respective unit. The place of posting is non-transferable throughout the apprenticeship training period; no transfer requests between units will be entertained. Late attendance or punching results in a proportionate stipend deduction. All ACT Apprentices must register on the Face Recognition-based Attendance System and record both IN and OUT punches daily; missing punches will be treated as absence for that day, subject to regularisation under applicable rules.

RCFL Apprentice Selection Process 2026-27

A merit list is drawn based on the percentage criteria and applicable reservation, using marks in the prescribed essential educational qualification. Where CGPA/CPI or grades are awarded, the equivalent percentage (as per the University/Institute's own norms) must be indicated in the application, with a supporting certificate submitted at document verification. Candidates from the merit list are called for Document Verification. Candidates are appointed as Apprentice Trainees only after successful document verification and approval of their Apprenticeship Contract on the respective portal (NATS/NAPS). Trainees are expected to join upon contract approval; failure to report on the stipulated date may lead to cancellation of candidature. RCFL Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Visit the company's website: www.rcfltd.com.
  • 2Click on "RECRUITMENT" and then "ENGAGEMENT OF APPRENTICES – 2026-27."
  • 3Click on "I Accept" and then "Apply Online" to open the application form.
  • 4Choose your posting area, "Trombay" or "Thal," from the drop-down.
  • 5Keep a scanned passport-size colour photograph (JPG/JPEG, up to 75 KB) and signature (JPG/JPEG, up to 25 KB) ready to upload.
  • 6Fill out the application form completely, then click "SAVE/SUBMIT."
  • 7After successful submission, the application form will be generated. Click "Print" to print it - this printed form must be produced at the document verification stage, but candidates are not required to send a hard copy to RCF.
  • 8Candidates aspiring for Trade Apprenticeship (NAPS) must register on apprenticeshipindia.gov.in.
  • 9Candidates aspiring for Technician, Diploma-holder, or Graduate/Degree Apprenticeships must register as a student on nats.education.gov.in.

RCFL Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is RCF Apprentice Recruitment 2026-27?
A
It is a recruitment drive by Rashtriya Chemicals and Fertilizers Limited (RCF) to engage 326 Graduate, Technician, and Trade Apprentices under the Apprentices Act, 1961, for training at Trombay, Mumbai and Thal, Raigad District.
Q2How many vacancies are available?
A
326 posts in total - 145 Graduate Apprentice, 105 Technician Apprentice, and 76 Trade Apprentice posts, split as 177 at Trombay and 149 at Thal.
Q3What is the last date to apply for RCF Apprentice Recruitment 2026-27?
A
20 September 2026, till 5:00 PM. Online applications open from 4 September 2026, 8:00 AM.
Q4What is the qualification required for RCF Apprentice Recruitment?
A
It varies by trade - B.Com/BBA/Graduate degrees for Graduate Apprentice posts, relevant engineering diplomas for Technician Apprentice posts, and B.Sc (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics) or 12th pass with Science for Trade Apprentice posts. See the Eligibility section for full details.
Q5What is the age limit for RCF Apprentice Recruitment 2026-27?
A
Candidates must not be less than 18 years and not exceed 25 years of age as on 1 September 2026, with age relaxation of 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC, 10 years for PwBD, and 5 years for children/family members of 1984 riot victims.
Q6What is the stipend for RCF Apprentice Recruitment 2026-27?
A
Stipend ranges from ₹14,600 to ₹17,300 per month depending on the trade, combining amounts paid by RCF and by BOAT (Board of Apprenticeship Training) where applicable. See the Stipend section for the trade-wise breakdown.
Q7How can I apply for RCF Apprentice Recruitment 2026-27?
A
Apply online at www.rcfltd.com under "Recruitment" > "Engagement of Apprentices – 2026-27," choose your posting location (Trombay or Thal), fill the form, and submit before 20 September 2026, 5:00 PM.
Q8What is the selection process for RCF Apprentice Recruitment?
A
Selection is based on a merit list drawn from percentage marks in the qualifying examination and applicable reservation, followed by document verification and approval of the apprenticeship contract on the NATS/NAPS portal. There is no written exam or interview mentioned in the notification.
Q9Can I apply for more than one trade?
A
No. Candidates must apply for only one discipline/trade; applying for more than one will lead to rejection of all applications submitted.
Q10Will RCF offer a permanent job after the apprenticeship?
A
No. The company has no obligation to offer regular employment to apprentices during or after the training period, and apprentices have no right or claim to regular employment based on this apprenticeship.
