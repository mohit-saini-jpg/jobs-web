NSPCL Recruitment 2026 Educational Qualification
PostQualification
Sr. Assistant Officer (HR)Graduate with at least two years full-time Post Graduate Degree/Post Graduate Diploma/Post Graduate Program in management with specialisation in Human Resource/Industrial Relations/Personnel Management, or Masters in Social Work, or MHROD, or MBA with specialisation in HR from a recognised Indian University/Institute recognised by an appropriate statutory authority, with not less than 60% marks, as per respective university/institute norms. Computer knowledge is essential; a degree in law is desirable.
Sr. Assistant Officer (Law)Bachelor's degree in Law (LLB or equivalent full-time degree) from a recognised Indian university/institute with a minimum of 60% marks.

Sr. Assistant Engineer (Mechanical)B.E/B.Tech degree in Mechanical/Production with at least 60% marks from a recognised University/Institution.
Sr. Assistant Engineer (C&I)B.E/B.Tech degree in Electronics/Control & Instrumentation/Instrumentation with at least 60% marks from a recognised University/Institution.
Sr. Assistant Engineer (IT/ERP)B.E/B.Tech degree in Information Technology/Computer Science/Electronics & Communication with at least 60% marks from a recognised University/Institution.
NSPCL Recruitment 2026 Post-Qualification Experience
PostExperience Required
Sr. Assistant Officer (HR)Minimum 2 years post-qualification in-line experience in an organisation of repute dealing with Human Resources, Personnel & Administration matters, preferably in areas such as establishment matters, policy advocacy, industrial relations, recruitment and promotion, manpower planning, performance appraisal systems, general administration, statutory compliance, or HR policies.
Sr. Assistant Officer (Law)Minimum 2 years post-qualification in-line experience in an organisation of repute dealing with litigation handling, drafting and vetting contracts and legal documents, providing legal opinions, arbitration and dispute resolution, statutory and regulatory compliance, coordination with advocates and regulatory authorities, and advising on legal matters. Experience in the power, infrastructure, engineering, or manufacturing sector is desirable.
Sr. Assistant Engineer (Electrical/Mechanical/C&I)Minimum 2 years of post-qualification executive experience (including training period, if any) in the power sector (Coal, Gas, Lignite) in Operation/Maintenance/Commissioning/Erection/Engineering or a combination thereof in the relevant discipline in a power plant/power project.
Sr. Assistant Engineer (IT/ERP)Minimum 2 years of post-qualification executive experience (including training period, if any) in an organisation of repute, handling and managing IT infrastructure, systems and operations that may involve overseeing network security, software development, database management, and communication systems. Certified Network Engineer with CCNA/CCNP or equivalent valid certification is preferable.

NSPCL Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Eligible candidates will have to appear for an All India based Online Selection Test. The selection process for NSPCL Recruitment 2026 consists of the following stages: Online Selection Test: Comprises two parts - Subject Knowledge Test (SKT) and Executive Aptitude Test (EAT). Candidates are required to qualify both SKT and EAT separately. Personal Interview: Based on merit and requirement of the post, shortlisted candidates will be called for Personal Interview. The final selection will be based on 85% weightage of the online selection test and 15% weightage of interview marks . Candidates have to choose any two cities as Preference-I and Preference-II as their test centre; no change will be considered subsequently, and NSPCL reserves the right to cancel or add any centre depending on candidate response for that area/centre.

NSPCL Recruitment 2026: Reservations, Relaxations & Concessions

Reservations, relaxations, and concessions for SC/ST/OBC (non-creamy layer)/XSM/PwBD candidates will be provided as per Government orders. Candidates claiming to belong to any particular category shall necessarily have a valid EWS/SC/ST/Disability certificate/OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) certificate, as the case may be, from the competent authority in the latest prescribed format of the Government of India. Category once filled in the online application form will not be changed and no benefit of other category will be admissible later on. Where there is a vacancy identified for such category, upper age limit is relaxed by 5 years for SC/ST candidates, 3 years for OBC candidates, and 10 years for PwBD candidates. Relaxation of age to Ex-Servicemen will be as per Government guidelines.

NSPCL Recruitment 2026: Service Agreement Bond & Health

Selected candidates belonging to the General category will be required to execute a service agreement bond of Rs. 5,00,000/-. Candidates belonging to SC, ST, PwBD categories will need to execute a bond of Rs. 2,50,000/-, to serve NSPCL for at least 4 years after joining the company. Candidates should have sound health. Before joining, candidates will have to undergo medical examination at any NSPCL-notified/empanelled hospital, and the decision of NSPCL will be final and binding. No relaxation in health standards is allowed. Detailed health norms are available on the NSPCL website.
