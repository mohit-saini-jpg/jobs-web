Notification DatePost Date: 27/07/2026

The Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) will release the Karnataka DCET 2026 Round 1 final seat allotment result on July 27, 2026, after 6:00 PM, on the official counselling portal, cetonline.karnataka.gov.in. How to Check Karnataka DCET 2026 Round 1 Final Allotment Result

Follow these steps to check your DCET 2026 Round 1 final seat allotment status online:

  • Visit the official KEA counselling portal: cetonline.karnataka.gov.in.
  • Look for the link titled "DCET 2026 Round 1 Final Seat Allotment Result" on the homepage.
  • Enter your DCET Application Number and Password/Secret Key in the login window.
  • Submit the details to view your allotted college, branch, and category as per your merit rank and submitted preferences.
  • Download and save the allotment result/letter for future reference and admission purposes.

What to Do After the Result Is Declared

Once the Round 1 final allotment result is out, candidates need to act within the timelines set by KEA:

  • Carefully verify the allotted college, course/branch, and category printed on the result.
  • Select the appropriate choice (as provided by KEA, typically ranging from choice 1 to choice 4) between July 28 and July 31, 2026, indicating whether you wish to accept the seat, freeze it, or remain in consideration for further rounds.
  • Candidates satisfied with their allotted seat should proceed to download the challan and complete fee payment within the specified window (challan download open July 27 to August 3, 2026).
  • Report to the allotted college with the required original documents for verification and admission confirmation.
  • Contact KEA immediately through official channels if there is any discrepancy in the allotment details.

The exact meaning and implications of each numbered choice option (accept and freeze, accept and upgrade, etc.) should be confirmed from the official DCET 2026 counselling instructions, as this article does not have the specific wording of KEA's 2026 choice-option policy.

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Candidates who do not select a choice or remain inactive during the choice-selection window may not be considered for subsequent counselling rounds, based on how KEA has handled this in past DCET cycles.
  • Once the Round 1 final allotment result is declared, no further changes can be made to the options that were submitted.
  • Keep the DCET application number and password/secret key handy, as these are mandatory to log in and check the result.
  • Regularly check the official KEA website for any revisions to the schedule, since counselling timelines are sometimes extended.
