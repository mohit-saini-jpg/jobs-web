TEAM Job Description

PG Qualified AYUSH Doctor Deliver specialized Ayurvedic, Unani, or Homeopathic medical care and treatment requiring postgraduate expertise. AYUSH Medical Officer Provide general Ayurveda, Unani, or Homeopathic healthcare services and medical examinations to beneficiaries. Dietitian Manage nutritional planning, dietary assessments, and nutritional counseling for targeted missions and healthcare programs. Social Counselor Conduct counseling sessions, psycho-social assessments, and manage mental health support cases on call and during designated visits. Yoga Professional Demonstrate and instruct yoga exercises, therapeutic postures, and wellness modules for health promotion. Multi Purpose Worker Perform assigned multipurpose field and operational duties, utilizing motorcycle driving skills as required.
