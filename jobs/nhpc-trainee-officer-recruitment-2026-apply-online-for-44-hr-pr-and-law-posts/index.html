NHPC Trainee Officer Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

NHPC Limited has released the NHPC Trainee Officer Recruitment 2026 notification for 44 vacancies in Human Resources (HR), Public Relations (PR) and Law, including three posts under a special recruitment drive for Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD). Recruitment will be based on UGC NET June 2026 and CLAT PG 2026 scores, followed by a Group Discussion and Personal Interview.

NHPC Recruitment 2026 Notification

The notification specifies that HR and Public Relations vacancies will be filled through UGC NET June 2026 scores, while Law vacancies require a valid CLAT PG 2026 score. Scores from other examination sessions or years will not be accepted. Candidates appointed through this recruitment will undergo one year of training before being absorbed as Officers, subject to successful completion of the training period. NHPC Trainee Officer Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must be Indian nationals, possess the prescribed full-time regular educational qualification and have a valid score in the relevant UGC NET June 2026 or CLAT PG 2026 examination. Final-year students are not eligible to apply. Candidates applying for the HR discipline must possess one of the following prescribed full-time regular two-year qualifications from a recognized Indian university or eligible AICTE-approved institution: Postgraduate Degree, Postgraduate Diploma or Postgraduate Program in Management with specialization in Human Resource, Human Resource Management, Human Resource Management and Labor Relations, Industrial Relations, Personnel Management, Personnel Management and Industrial Relations, or Industrial Relations and Personnel Management. NHPC Trainee Officer Salary 2026

NHPC Trainee Officers will receive a pay scale of Rs. 50,000 to Rs. 1,60,000 under E2 Grade with a 3% increment structure (IDA). The approximate total annual emoluments for newly appointed candidates will be Rs. 16 to 17 lakh. In addition to basic pay, employees will receive benefits and allowances in accordance with company rules, including: Dearness Allowance (DA) House Rent Allowance (HRA) Cafeteria Allowance Performance Related Pay (PRP) Medical facilities Provident Fund, pension and gratuity After successfully completing one year of training, candidates will be absorbed as Officers in the same pay scale and placed on probation for one year.

NHPC Trainee Officer Selection Process 2026

The selection process consists of shortlisting based on UGC NET June 2026 or CLAT PG 2026 marks, followed by a Group Discussion and Personal Interview. The qualifying examination score carries 75% weightage in the final selection process. Candidates will be shortlisted based on their marks in the relevant UGC NET June 2026 or CLAT PG 2026 examination. Normally, candidates numbering up to five times the available vacancies will be shortlisted for Group Discussion and Personal Interview. Candidates securing equal marks in the same discipline and category at the shortlisting threshold may be called even if the prescribed ratio is exceeded. Shortlisted candidates must submit the required consent form and attend the Group Discussion and Personal Interview.
