What Is an RV Grade List?

An RV (Revaluation) Grade List is issued after DAVV re-checks answer sheets for students who applied for revaluation of their original result. It reflects only the updated grades for students who requested revaluation it is not a fresh result for all B.Com. 2nd Year students. Students who did not apply for revaluation should continue to refer to their original grade list or pass list for the April-May 2026 exam.

Who Can Check This RV Grade List

  • Students enrolled in the B.Com. 2 Year program at DAVV or its affiliated colleges.
  • Students who appeared for the April-May 2026 examination.
  • Students who specifically applied for revaluation (RV) of one or more subjects/papers.

Students who did not apply for revaluation will not find their name in this specific RV grade list; their result remains as per the original pass list/grade list issued earlier for this exam session.

How to Check DAVV B.Com. 2nd Year RV Grade List 2026

  • Visit the official DAVV results page at https://www.dauniv.ac.in/results.
  • Look for the entry titled "RV: B.Com., 2 Year APR-MAY 2026 (GRADE LIST)" dated 23-07-2026.
  • Click on the corresponding result/grade list link to open the PDF.
  • Use Ctrl+F (or the search function in your PDF viewer) to search for your name, enrollment number, or roll number within the document.
  • Check your updated AGPA/CGPA and result status as shown in the grade list.
  • Download and save the PDF, or take a screenshot/printout for your records.

Documents/ Details You May Need

  • Enrollment number
  • Roll number
  • Name as per college records (for search verification within the PDF)

DAVV grade lists are typically published as PDF documents listing students college-wise, so having your enrollment or roll number ready makes it easier to locate your result quickly.

Grade List vs Pass List vs Mark List

TypeWhat It Shows
Grade ListGrades/AGPA and CGPA earned in each subject, along with overall result status.
Pass ListConsolidated list of students who have passed, generally with division/result status.
Mark ListDetailed subject-wise marks obtained by the student.

The result covered in this article is specifically a Grade List for the revaluation (RV) category.

Important Instructions

  • This result is issued only for students who applied for revaluation; it does not replace the original result for other students.
  • Cross-check your name, enrollment number, and roll number carefully in the grade list PDF.
  • The result available online should be treated as provisional; the physical marksheet/grade card issued by the college or university remains the official document.
  • In case of any discrepancy in your grade list, contact your college examination department or the DAVV examination section directly.
