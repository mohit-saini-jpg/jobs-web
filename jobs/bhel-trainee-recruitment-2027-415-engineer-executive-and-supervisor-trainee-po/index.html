BHEL Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

The educational qualifications differ between Engineer/Executive Trainee and Supervisor Trainee recruitment. Candidates must possess the prescribed full-time qualifications in the respective disciplines. Candidates must possess both of the following qualifications: A full-time regular Bachelor's Degree from a recognized Indian university with at least 60% aggregate marks. A two-year full-time regular postgraduate Degree or Diploma in HR, Personnel Management, Industrial Relations, Social Work or an MBA, with at least 55% aggregate marks. Candidates with postgraduate qualifications in Social Work or Business Administration must have studied Personnel Management, Labour Welfare or HRM as a specialization or elective in their final year. Candidates must be qualified Chartered Accountants (CA) or Cost and Management Accountants (CMA) from institutions recognized by the appropriate statutory

BHEL Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

The selection process is different for Engineer/Executive Trainee and Supervisor Trainee posts. Selection includes a Computer-Based Examination and interview. Online application and eligibility scrutiny. Computer-Based Examination (CBE). Shortlisting for interview in a 1:5 ratio, based on vacancies, discipline and reservation category. Personal interview. Verification of documents and eligibility. Final merit preparation and medical fitness assessment. Supervisor Trainees will undergo a two-stage selection procedure: Computer-Based Examination (CBE). Document Verification / Scrutiny for shortlisted candidates. Candidates will be shortlisted for document verification in the ratio of 1:3 based on their CBE performance, discipline, vacancies and reservation category. Document verification is a qualifying stage. The final merit list will be based entirely on the CBE score of candidates found

BHEL Recruitment 2026 Posting and Transfer Conditions

Selected candidates will be posted to BHEL manufacturing units, Power Sector regions, project sites or other notified establishments according to vacancies and recruitment requirements. Applicants must indicate their preferred units or sites while filling out the application. Final allocation will depend on merit, preferences, reservation norms and vacancy availability. Important Posting Condition: Selected candidates are required to serve at their allotted unit or site for at least 10 years from the date of joining . Requests for transfer or change of posting will generally not be entertained during this period.
