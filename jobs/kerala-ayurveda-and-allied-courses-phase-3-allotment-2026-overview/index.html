Kerala Ayurveda Phase 3 Provisional Allotment List 2026 Released

CEE Kerala published the third phase provisional allotment list for Ayurveda, Homoeopathy, Siddha, Unani and medical allied courses on 29 October 2026. The list was prepared based on online options registered by candidates until 28 October 2026 at 11:00 AM.

The provisional allotment PDF contains candidate application numbers, ranks, allotted colleges, courses, candidate categories, allotment categories and option numbers.

Candidates were permitted to submit genuine complaints regarding the provisional allotment to the Commissioner for Entrance Examinations by email until 30 October 2026 at 1:00 PM.

The provisional list is not the final admission confirmation. CEE Kerala has since released the final allotment list, which candidates should refer to for further admission formalities.

How to Download Kerala Ayurveda Phase 3 Allotment List 2026?

Candidates can follow these steps to download the third phase allotment result from the official CEE Kerala website.

  • Visit the official website at cee.kerala.gov.in.
  • Open the KEAM 2026 Candidate Portal.
  • Select the Allotment List section.
  • Choose Medical (AYUSH/Allied).
  • Open the Third Phase Allotment List.
  • Download the PDF and search for your application number.
  • Check the allotted college, course and category.
  • Save the PDF for future reference.

Kerala Ayurveda Phase 3 Provisional Allotment Complaint Deadline

CEE Kerala allowed candidates to submit genuine complaints relating to the Phase 3 provisional allotment list through the email address [email protected].

The deadline for submitting complaints was 30 October 2026 at 1:00 PM.

After considering valid complaints received during the prescribed period, the authority published the final Phase 3 allotment result on 30 October 2026. The provisional objection window should therefore not be treated as an active admission facility.

Kerala Ayurveda and Allied Courses Phase 3 Final Allotment 2026

CEE Kerala has published the third phase final allotment list for eligible candidates seeking admission under the applicable state quota seats in government and private self-financing institutions.

The final result was prepared after addressing valid complaints against the provisional allotment list. The authority also excluded candidates who had obtained AYUSH allotment or admission through AACCC counselling from the state third round allotment.

Allotted candidates should download the final allotment memo and data sheet through their candidate accounts before completing college reporting.

How to Download Kerala KEAM 2026 Allotment Memo and Data Sheet?

Candidates who receive an allotment must obtain their individual admission documents through the KEAM 2026 Candidate Portal.

  • Visit KEAM 2026 Candidate Portal.
  • Log in to the candidate homepage using the required credentials.
  • Open the allotment details and download the allotment memo.
  • Select the Data Sheet option to download the candidate information sheet.
  • Print both documents.
  • Check the applicable fee details and follow the payment instructions.
  • Report to the allotted institution before 3 November 2026 at 4:00 PM with the required documents.

Kerala Phase 3 Allotment 2026 Fee Concession Rules

The official final allotment notification states that certain eligible candidates are exempt from paying tuition fees at the time of admission.

The eligible groups include SC, ST and OEC candidates; Children of Fishermen; specified communities entitled to OEC-equivalent educational concessions; and eligible candidates from Sreechitra Home, Juvenile Home and Nirbhaya Home.

However, such candidates allotted Management Quota seats in self-financing AYUSH colleges must pay the fee shown in their allotment memo and are not eligible for the stated fee concession.

Candidates should refer to their individual allotment memo and the official notification for the applicable payment requirements.

Kerala AYUSH and Allied Courses Stray Vacancy Allotment 2026

CEE Kerala has stated that vacancies remaining after the third phase of allotment will be filled through the stray vacancy filling process.

The schedule for stray vacancy filling has not yet been announced. Candidates should regularly check the official CEE Kerala website for subsequent notices and eligibility instructions.
