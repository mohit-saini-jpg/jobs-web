BPSC TRE-4.0 Eligibility 2026

Candidate must be a citizen of India. Candidate must hold the educational and training qualification prescribed by the National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE) for the relevant school level, as detailed in the official notification. For special school teacher posts, qualification recognised by the Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI) is required. Teachers appointed and working before 2012 who have passed the Dakshata Pariksha (competency exam) are exempted from the TET requirement. Candidates who hold the required educational qualification and are appearing in the Secondary Teacher Eligibility Test (STET, 2026) may also apply, but must pass STET 2026 before the Preliminary Examination; the admit card for the Preliminary Examination will not be issued if STET 2026 is not cleared. BPSC TRE-4.0 Selection Process

Selection is made in two stages - a Preliminary Examination and a Main Examination. There is no interview . Objective-type General Studies paper; all questions carry equal (1) marks. Duration: 2 hours ; Total marks: 150 . Each question offers 5 options (A, B, C, D, E); candidates must mark one option. Option (E) must be chosen if the candidate does not wish to attempt the question. Negative marking: 1/3 mark deducted for each wrong answer, and 1/3 mark deducted for any question left unanswered without selecting option (E). Covers general science, current national and international events, Indian history and Bihar's history, general geography, Bihar's geographical features, India's polity and economy, the freedom movement and Bihar's contribution, and general mental ability. Important Conditions

Candidates can apply for a maximum of 38 districts, listed in order of preference; the merit list and posting will follow district priority against category-wise availability. A candidate may take part in this recruitment examination a maximum of 5 times , as per the Bihar Vidyalaya Adhyapak (Appointment, Transfer, Disciplinary Action & Service Conditions) Amendment Rules, 2024. Reservation benefits are applicable only to candidates who are original/domiciled residents of Bihar. Candidates must select only one subject - the one in which they have qualified the relevant Teacher Eligibility Test (CTET/BTET/STET). Reserved-category candidates applying against unreserved posts will not receive reserved-category age or qualifying-mark relaxations. BPSC TRE-4.0 Recruitment 2026 Notification Out - 32,388 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Visit the official BPSC OTR portal bpsconline.bihar.gov.in.
  • 2Complete One Time Registration (OTR) via the "New User Registration" button using a valid, active email ID and mobile number (mandatory for all BPSC exams).
  • 3After OTR, sign in to DigiLocker's Meri Pehchan portal (create a new account if you don't have one) and give consent to link your documents with the application, or proceed directly to the online application via the browser back option.
  • 4Log in at bpsconline.bihar.gov.in/candidate/login and complete your profile in six steps: Personal Information, Address Information, Other Information, Qualification Information, Experience Information, and Photo/Signature Upload.
  • 5Upload a live webcam photograph and scanned signatures in both Hindi and English.
  • 6Review all entries, tick the declaration checkbox, and click "Submit and Lock Profile" (profile details cannot be edited after locking, except by unlocking before final submission).
  • 7Go to the "New Application" tab, select this advertisement (No. 14/2026), choose your post/subject and up to 38 preferred districts in order of priority.
  • 8Pay the applicable fee online (SBI Payment Gateway) and confirm payment.
  • 9Click "Proceed to Application" and then "Submit Application" to finalise your form.
  • 10Download and print the confirmed Application Form from the "My Account" tab for future reference.
