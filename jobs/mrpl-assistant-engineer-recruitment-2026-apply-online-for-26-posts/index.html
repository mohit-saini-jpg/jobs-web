MRPL Assistant Engineer Selection Process 2026

Initial shortlisting is based purely on valid GATE-2026 marks (out of 100) ; marks from any previous/other GATE examination are not considered. Shortlisted candidates are called for Group Discussion (GD) and Personal Interview (PI) in the ratio of 1:5 (1 post : 5 candidates), category/reservation-wise. In case of a tie in GATE marks, the candidate with higher marks in the essential qualification (degree) will be ranked higher. The final merit list is prepared as per MRPL's prevailing rules; MRPL reserves the right to add, modify or delete any test in the selection process. Document Verification is conducted at the time of Personal Interview. A panel of suitable candidates may be created and kept valid for one year.

Documents Required (at Document Verification / Interview)

Proof of age: SSLC marks card / Matriculation certificate / School leaving certificate / Municipal or Gram Panchayat birth certificate All mark sheets and certificates for Class X, XII, Diploma, and Degree/PG Degree (individual semester/year mark sheets required) GATE-2026 Score Card showing marks scored out of 100 Caste certificate [SC/ST/OBC(NCL)] or Income & Asset Certificate (EWS), as applicable PwBD certificate (minimum 40% disability), where applicable Ex-servicemen status proof, where applicable No Objection Certificate (NOC) or proper-channel forwarding, for Government/PSU/Autonomous body employees Recent passport-size photograph and signature (scanned copies for online upload)

Important Conditions

Candidates called for GD/PI will be reimbursed travelling expenses (3AC train fare or AC bus fare limited to 3AC fare) by the shortest route; local travel cost is borne by the candidate. PwBD candidates get reimbursement for one attendant as per Government guidelines. Working knowledge of Hindi is desirable. MRPL Assistant Engineer Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Visit www.mrpl.co.in/careers and click on "Advt. No. 94/2026: Recruitment of Assistant Engineers in 'E2' Grade of Management Cadre through GATE 2026".
  • 2Click "Click here to register online" to generate your Login ID and Password using a valid email ID and mobile number (must remain active for at least 1 year).
  • 3Sign in and fill in educational qualification, category, and other required details in the online application form; an Application ID will be generated and sent via email/SMS.
  • 4General/OBC(NCL)/EWS candidates: click "Pay Now", select "Advt. 94/2026", and pay the application fee of ₹118/- online; save the payment receipt.
  • 5After payment, return to the home page, click "Already Paid", sign in, and enter the SBI e-Collect Reference Number, bank name and payment date; upload the payment receipt (JPG).
  • 6SC/ST/PwBD/ESM candidates (fee-exempt) can directly proceed to document upload.
  • 7Upload scanned photograph, signature, GATE-2026 score card, and category/PwBD/EWS certificate as applicable, then click "Submit".
  • 8Save the PDF of the submitted online application for your records – no printout needs to be sent to MRPL, unless forwarding through proper channel (see below).
