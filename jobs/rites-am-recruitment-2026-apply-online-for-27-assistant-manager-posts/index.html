RITES AM Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection consists of a Written Test and an Interview. Copies of qualification and experience documents will be verified against the originals at the appropriate stages. Appointment also depends on being found medically fit in the Medical Examination under RITES Rules and Standards of Medical Fitness. The test has 125 objective type questions of one mark each and lasts 2.5 Hours. PwBD candidates are eligible for a compensatory time of 50 minutes. No minimum qualifying marks are required in the aggregate. The interview may be taken in Hindi or English. Written test centres are tentative and depend on the number of candidates. Service Conditions

The appointment is purely on contract basis, initially for one year. It can be extended until completion of the assignment, subject to mutual consent and satisfactory performance. Services can be terminated without notice or pay in lieu for unsatisfactory performance, inappropriate conduct, misbehaviour or wrongdoing, or if the client discontinues services. Selected candidates are liable for posting anywhere in India as per Company requirements. Offers will be issued only on project or company requirement and on meeting the criteria for deployment in projects. Departmental candidates of RITES and candidates working in Government Departments/ PSU can join only after being properly relieved by their parent organisation. Management may cancel, restrict, enlarge, modify or alter the selection process at any stage without further notice. Legal jurisdiction for any dispute is Delhi.

RITES AM Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1Can I apply for both AM-Mechanical-II and AM-Mechanical-III?
A
You can apply for any number of vacancies separately. However, the written test for CL/10/26 and CL/11/26 is held on the same date and at the same time, so check your eligibility and apply accordingly.
Q2Is there negative marking in the RITES written test?
A
No. No marks are deducted for an incorrect answer.
Q3How many candidates are called for the interview?
A
Candidates are called in the ratio of 1:6 to the number of vacancies.
Q4Does training or internship count as experience?
A
No. Training (except on the job training), internship, teaching, academic, fellowship and PhD research experience are not counted.
Q5Is an offer letter accepted as proof of experience?
A
No. You must submit a joining and relieving order or an experience certificate to claim that experience.
Q6Will SC, ST and PwD candidates get the fee back?
A
Their fee is refunded after they take part in the further selection process of written exam or interview, as applicable, and their category certificate is scrutinised. The refund goes to the source account or card used for payment, after deducting GST and bank charges as applicable.
Q7What if my mark sheet shows only CGPA?
A
You must provide the conversion formula used by your university or institution. If it has no formula, submit an undertaking to that effect signed by its authorised representative. A minimum of 6 on a 10-point scale will then be considered as 60%.
Q8Does RITES pay travel allowance for the test or interview?
A
No. The notice states that no fares, TA or DA shall be payable.
Q9Does being placed on the panel guarantee a job?
A
No. Empanelment against a vacancy does not guarantee selection or a right to the job, and deployment depends entirely on project or company requirements.
Q10Where will changes to this recruitment be published?
A
Any corrigendum or addendum will be displayed only on www.rites.com. Other recruitment updates may be sent to your registered e-mail address or uploaded on the RITES website.
