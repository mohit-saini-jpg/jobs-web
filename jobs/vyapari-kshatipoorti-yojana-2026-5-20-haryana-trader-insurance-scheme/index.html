Vyapari Kshatipoorti Yojana 2026 | 5 लाख से 20 लाख तक मुआवजा Overview

Vyapari Kshatipoorti Yojana Haryana sarkar ki ek nayi scheme hai jo chhote aur medium traders ko business loss ke case mein 5‑20 lakh tak ka financial backup deti hai. Iska maksad local commerce ko stable rakhna aur entrepreneurs ko risk se protect karna hai.

Vyapari Kshatipoorti Yojana 2026 | 5 लाख से 20 लाख तक मुआवजा Expert Analysis

Yeh yojana especially un traders ke liye game‑changer hai jinke paas insurance cover nahi hai, kyunki claim process simple hai aur payout fast hota hai. Scheme ka premium bhi subsidised hai, isliye cost‑benefit ratio bahut accha hai.

Who Is Eligible: Vyapari Kshatipoorti Yojana 2026 | 5 लाख से 20 लाख तक मुआवजा

Scheme un sabhi vyapariyon ke liye hai jo Haryana mein registered business chalate hain, chhote se medium scale tak, aur jinke paas koi existing commercial insurance nahi hai.

Vyapari Kshatipoorti Yojana 2026 | 5 लाख से 20 लाख तक मुआवजा Application Process

1. Apna business registration certificate, GSTIN aur bank account details ready rakhein. 2. Recent profit‑and‑loss statement ya sales ledger attach karein. 3. Online portal ya nearest District Trade Office se application form download karke, sabhi documents ke saath submit karein.

Vyapari Kshatipoorti Yojana 2026 | 5 लाख से 20 लाख तक मुआवजा Benefits Details

Yojana ke tahat eligible loss claim par 5 lakh se 20 lakh tak ka muaawza milta hai, jo inventory replacement, rent ya employee salary cover kar sakta hai. Isse cash‑flow crisis ke time mein business continuity bani rehti hai.

How Vyapari Kshatipoorti Yojana 2026 | 5 लाख से 20 लाख तक मुआवजा Works

Trader apna claim online ya offline file karta hai, verification team loss documents check karti hai, aur approved hone par 30‑45 din ke andar bank transfer ke through payout hota hai. Scheme ka monitoring Haryana Trade Development Corporation ke through hota hai.

Vyapari Kshatipoorti Yojana 2026 | 5 लाख से 20 लाख तक मुआवजा Application Tips

Application form ko dhyan se fill karein, sabhi mandatory fields ko fill karein aur original documents ki scanned copies attach karein; incomplete ya mismatched info se rejection ho sakta hai. Time‑bound deadlines ka palan zaroori hai.

Q1Is scheme ka premium kaun pay karega?
A
Premium ka 70% Haryana government subsidise karti hai, trader ko sirf 30% hi pay karna hota hai.
Q2Claim process kitna samay leta hai?
A
Document verification ke baad payout usually 30‑45 din ke andar bank account mein transfer ho jata hai.
Q3Kya existing commercial insurance ke saath bhi claim kar sakte hain?
A
Agar aapke paas already insurance cover hai to aapko Vyapari Kshatipoorti Yojana ka benefit milne ke liye usse surrender karna hoga, kyunki yeh scheme duplicate coverage nahi deti.
