Vyapari Kshatipoorti Yojana 2026 | 5 लाख से 20 लाख तक मुआवजा Overview

Vyapari Kshatipoorti Yojana 2026 ek sarkari scheme hai jo Haryana ke traders ko business loss ke case mein 5 lakh se 20 lakh tak ka financial support deti hai. Ye scheme unke liye hai jo apne dukaan ya stall ko natural calamities, fire ya theft se protect karna chahte hain.

Vyapari Kshatipoorti Yojana 2026 | 5 लाख से 20 लाख तक मुआवजा Expert Analysis

Yeh yojana risk management ka ek smart tool hai; isse small aur medium traders ko capital loss se bachne ka mauka milta hai aur business continuity bani rehti hai. Government ka direct payout model claim process ko transparent banata hai.

Who Is Eligible: Vyapari Kshatipoorti Yojana 2026 | 5 लाख से 20 लाख तक मुआवजा

Scheme un sabhi registered traders, shop owners, stall holders aur kirana dukandaron ke liye hai jo Haryana mein apna vyapar chalate hain.

Beneficiaries ko loss ke hisaab se 5 lakh se 20 lakh tak ka compensation milta hai, jo inventory replacement, rent ya employee salary cover karne mein madad karta hai. Payout claim approved hone ke baad 30 din ke andar bank transfer hota hai.
