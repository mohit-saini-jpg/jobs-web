Vyapari Kshatipoorti Yojana 2026 | 5 लाख से 20 लाख तक मुआवजा FAQs

Q1Yojana ke liye kaun kaun se documents chahiye?
A
Aapko GST registration, PAN, Aadhaar, last 2 saal ka profit‑loss statement, aur business address proof upload karna hoga.
Q2Muawza kitne dinon mein milta hai?
A
Verification ke baad, amount aapke registered bank account me 15‑20 din ke andar transfer ho jata hai.
Q3Kya ek hi vyapari multiple claims kar sakta hai?
A
Nahi, har financial loss ke liye sirf ek hi claim accept hota hai, aur same loss ke liye dobara claim nahi kiya ja sakta.
