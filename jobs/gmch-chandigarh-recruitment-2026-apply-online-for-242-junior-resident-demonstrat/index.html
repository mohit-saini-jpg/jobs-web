GMCH Chandigarh Senior Resident Eligibility Criteria 2026

PostQualification Required
Senior Resident, CMO (General Medicine & General Surgery), Resident Anaesthetist & Resident PathologistPostgraduate Medical Degree i.e. (M.D./M.S./DNB/M.Ch./D.M) or equivalent in the concerned specialty (recognized by MCI and registered either with Medical Council of India or State Medical Council). Candidates who have completed a three-year tenure of Senior Residency are not eligible.
Demonstrators / Medical Officer / EMO / LMOMBBS (Preference will be given to PG degree holders). Candidates having M.Sc. (Medical) in concerned specialty from a Medical Institute will be considered only in case of non-availability of medical candidates. Note: Only 01 post of Demonstrator in Physiology is available for non-medical candidates (subject to non-availability of medical candidate). No post of Demonstrator is available for non-medical candidates in Anatomy, Biochemistry, Microbiology & Pharmacology.
Junior Resident (MBBS & BDS)MBBS or BDS degree, registered with State Medical Council/MCI. Candidates who have completed or are likely to complete their one-year rotary internship by 31/12/2026 are eligible. Candidates who have already done one year Junior Residency are not eligible.

GMCH Chandigarh Senior Resident Age Limit 2026

The upper age limit is applicable as on 28.10.2026: For Senior Residents, Resident Anaesthetist, Resident Pathologist, CMO (General Medicine & General Surgery): 45 years. For Demonstrators, Emergency Medical Officer & Medical Officers (GMCH & MHI): 37 years. For Junior Resident (MBBS & BDS): 30 years.
