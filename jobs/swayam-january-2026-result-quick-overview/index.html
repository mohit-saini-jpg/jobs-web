SWAYAM January 2026 Result - Quick Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 20/08/2026

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has declared the SWAYAM January 2026 semester examination result for the 69 courses that were conducted in Hybrid mode (a combination of Computer-Based Test and pen-and-paper format) on August 19, 2026. Candidates who appeared for these 69 hybrid-mode papers can now check and download their scorecards from the official SWAYAM examination website.

How to Download the SWAYAM January 2026 Hybrid Result / Scorecard

  • Visit the official SWAYAM examination website: exams.nta.ac.in/swayam.
  • Look for the notice titled "Declaration of Results of the January 2026-Semester Exams of the courses held in Hybrid (CBT + Pen & Paper) Mode under SWAYAM."
  • Log in using your Application Number and password.
  • View your result/scorecard on screen.
  • Download the scorecard and save a copy.
  • Take a printout for future reference.

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Keep your Application Number and login password ready before visiting the portal, since both are required to view the scorecard.
  • Cross-check personal and course details displayed on the scorecard after downloading.
  • Retain a downloaded copy and a printout of the scorecard, as advised by NTA, for future reference.
  • NTA's role is limited to conducting the exam, declaring scores, and forwarding results - for queries about certificates or further academic action, candidates should also refer to the course-offering institution as applicable.
