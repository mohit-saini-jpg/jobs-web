HP High Court Stenographer Eligibility Criteria

Candidates for appointment to any of the advertised posts must be an Indian National and possess requisite educational and other qualifications as prescribed under R&P Rules, 2015 as on the date of submission of online application.
PostQualification Required
Stenographer (Group-C)Graduation with three (3) years’ experience as Steno-typist or Judgment Writer or Stenographer in any government office or reputed organisation. Candidates must be having speed of 100 W.P.M. in English Stenography and accurate transcription of the matter dictated within a period of 5 times to the time allotted for dictation and typing speed, in English, at 50 W.P.M. on computers, wherefor a separate test shall be held. Only 10% of the mistakes in transcribing the dictated matter and typed matter in typing test shall be allowed.
