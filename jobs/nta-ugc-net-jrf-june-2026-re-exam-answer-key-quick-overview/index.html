How to Download the UGC NET JRF June 2026 Re-Exam Answer Key

  • Visit the official website: ugcnet.nta.nic.in or nta.ac.in.
  • Click on the "UGC-NET June 2026 Re-Exam Answer Key / Challenge Provisional Answer Key" link.
  • Log in using your Application Number and Date of Birth/Password, then enter the Security PIN.
  • Open the Answer Key, Question Paper, and Recorded Responses, then download the PDF.
  • Save and print the Answer Key for score calculation and future reference.
