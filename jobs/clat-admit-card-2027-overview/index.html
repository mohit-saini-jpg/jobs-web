CLAT Admit Card 2027 - Overview : Admit Card Overview

The Consortium of National Law Universities (NLUs) has released the CLAT Admit Card 2027 on 21 November 2026 for the Common Law Admission Test (CLAT) undergraduate and postgraduate examinations. Candidates who completed their registration can download the admit card from the official website, consortiumofnlus.ac.in, using their registered mobile number and password.

Notification DatePost Date: 21/11/2026

The CLAT 2027 examination will be conducted on 6 December 2026 (Sunday), from 2:00 PM to 4:00 PM, in offline mode. The download facility will remain available until 1:30 PM on 6 December 2026. How to Download CLAT Admit Card 2027?

Candidates can follow these steps to download their CLAT 2027 hall tickets:

  • Visit the official Consortium of NLUs website at consortiumofnlus.ac.in.
  • Open the CLAT 2027 section.
  • Access the candidate login portal.
  • Enter the registered mobile number and password.
  • Sign in to the application dashboard.
  • Click on the Download Admit Card option.
  • Open the CLAT 2027 UG or PG admit card, as applicable.
  • Check the candidate details, examination centre, date and reporting instructions.
  • Download the admit card and take a clear printout.

Candidates who have forgotten their login password should use the password recovery facility available on the official CLAT candidate portal.

Registered UG applicants can download their hall tickets by logging in through the official CLAT 2027 candidate portal.

Details Mentioned on CLAT Admit Card 2027

After downloading the hall ticket, candidates should verify the following information:

  • Candidate's name
  • Application number and roll number
  • Examination level (UG or PG)
  • Examination date and timing
  • Allotted test centre name
  • Examination centre address
  • Reporting time and entry instructions
  • Candidate identification details, where displayed
  • Other examination-day guidelines

Candidates should immediately contact the Consortium of NLUs if any important information on the admit card is incorrect.

CLAT Admit Card 2027 Not Downloading?

If the admit card is not visible or the login fails, candidates should check their registered mobile number and password.

  • Confirm that the correct mobile number has been entered.
  • Check the password carefully.
  • Use the Forgot Password option if required.
  • Confirm that the CLAT 2027 candidate account is being accessed.
  • Try accessing the portal again if there is a temporary loading issue.
  • Contact the Consortium helpdesk if the problem continues.

CLAT 2027 Exam Day Instructions

Candidates appearing for the examination should follow the instructions issued by the Consortium and printed on their individual admit cards.

  • Download and print the CLAT 2027 admit card before the download deadline.
  • Verify the allotted examination centre and reporting time.
  • Carry the printed admit card and the identity documents specified in the official instructions.
  • Reach the examination centre within the prescribed reporting period.
  • Follow the instructions for marking answers on the OMR sheet.
  • Do not carry prohibited items into the examination hall.
  • Follow the invigilator's instructions during the examination.
  • Check the official Consortium website for any revised examination notices.
