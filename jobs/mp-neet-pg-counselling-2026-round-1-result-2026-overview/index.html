MP NEET PG Round 1 Seat Allotment Result 2026 Released

The Directorate of Medical Education, Madhya Pradesh, has announced the first-round provisional seat allotment result for NEET PG 2026 counselling. The result was released on 29 October 2026 for candidates who completed the required registration and choice filling procedures.

Candidates can check the published allotment list to identify their allotted institution, postgraduate course and relevant seat category. The result is provisional, and admission remains subject to verification of documents and completion of the prescribed formalities.

How to Download MP NEET PG Round 1 Result 2026?

Candidates can follow these steps to check the MP NEET PG 2026 Round 1 provisional seat allotment result.

  • Visit the official counselling portal at dme.mponline.gov.in.
  • Open the PG Medical Counselling section for MD/MS admissions.
  • Find the First Round Allotment List for MP State Combined NEET PG Counselling 2026.
  • Open the provisional allotment result PDF.
  • Use the PDF search option to find the candidate's NEET PG roll number or name.
  • Check the allotted college, course and applicable seat category.
  • Download the PDF for future reference.
  • Log in to the candidate account to download the individual provisional allotment letter if a seat has been allotted.

The individual allotment letter is required for the subsequent admission process. Candidates should not rely only on a screenshot or the general allotment list when reporting to their allotted institution.

Details to Check in MP NEET PG Round 1 Allotment List 2026

The Round 1 allotment list provides information about candidates and the postgraduate medical seats allotted through state counselling.

Candidates should check the following details, wherever shown in the official result document:

  • Candidate's Name
  • NEET PG Roll Number
  • State Common Merit Rank
  • NEET PG All India Rank
  • Candidate Category
  • Allotted Medical College
  • Allotted Course and Speciality
  • Allotted Seat Category or Quota

Candidates should verify that their allotted course and institution match the information in their individual allotment letter. Any discrepancy should be through the official counselling or allotted college authorities.

MP NEET PG 2026 Provisional Allotment Letter

Candidates who have received a seat in the first round must download their provisional allotment letters through the official DME MPOnline candidate login.

The allotment letter contains the admission details required for reporting to the allotted medical institution. It must be presented along with the applicable original documents during the verification process.

Downloading the allotment letter does not by itself complete the admission process. Candidates must satisfy the document verification, fee payment and reporting requirements within the prescribed schedule.

MP NEET PG Round 1 Reporting Dates 2026

Physical reporting and admission at allotted medical colleges will take place between 30 October and 10 November 2026, up to 6:00 PM.

Candidates who have been allotted a postgraduate medical seat must report in person to their respective institutions for the prescribed verification and admission procedures.

Documents Required for MP NEET PG Counselling 2026 Round 1 Reporting

According to the admission instructions, allotted candidates must carry their original documents along with two sets of self-attested photocopies for verification.

  • MP NEET PG 2026 Provisional Allotment Letter
  • NEET PG 2026 Admit Card
  • NEET PG 2026 Scorecard or Rank Letter
  • MBBS Degree Certificate or Provisional Passing Certificate
  • MBBS Mark Sheets of All Professional Examinations
  • Compulsory Rotatory Internship Completion Certificate
  • Permanent or Provisional Medical Registration Certificate
  • Valid Government Photo Identity Proof
  • Madhya Pradesh Domicile Certificate, if applicable
  • SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PwD Certificate, if applicable
  • In-Service Experience or Sponsorship Certificate, if applicable

Candidates should verify the complete document requirements with their allotted college and the latest official counselling instructions before reporting.

MP NEET PG Round 2 Upgradation 2026

DME Madhya Pradesh has provided an option for eligible Round 1 allotted candidates to express willingness for upgradation in Round 2.

The upgradation willingness window is scheduled from 30 October to 10 November 2026, closing at 11:59 PM.

Candidates considering upgradation should review the official counselling rules before submitting their willingness. The implications for retaining or changing the Round 1 allotted seat are governed by the applicable counselling instructions.

The detailed Round 2 choice filling and seat allotment dates were not provided in the supplied Round 1 result announcement.

MP NEET PG 2026 Round 1 Opening and Closing Rank

In addition to the provisional allotment list, an opening and closing rank document for the first round has been publicly linked on the DME MPOnline domain.

The document is intended to provide course-wise and college-wise allotment rank information. Candidates may use it to understand the ranks at which seats were allotted in the first round.

These opening and closing ranks describe completed Round 1 allotments. They should not be treated as guaranteed admission thresholds for subsequent counselling rounds.
