SBI Junior Associate Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Graduation in any discipline from a recognized University, or any equivalent qualification recognized as such by the Central Government. Candidates with an Integrated Dual Degree (IDD) certificate must ensure the date of passing the IDD is on or before 31.12.2026. Candidates in their final year/semester of graduation may also apply provisionally, subject to producing proof of having passed the graduation examination on or before 31.12.2026. Matriculate Ex-servicemen who have obtained the Indian Army Special Certificate of Education, or the corresponding certificate in the Navy or Air Force, after completing not less than 15 years of service in the Armed Forces of the Union, are also eligible. Such certificates should be dated on or before 31.12.2026. Not below 20 years and not above 28 years - i.e., candidates must have been born not earlier than 02.04.1998 and not later than 01.04.2006

SBI Junior Associate Recruitment 2026 Reservation

4% horizontal reservation is provided to Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD), as per Section 34 of the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016, for categories including Blind/Low Vision, Deaf/Hard of Hearing, locomotor disabilities (one/both arms, one/both legs, cerebral palsy, leprosy cured, dwarfism, acid attack victims, muscular dystrophy, spinal deformity/injury), Autism Spectrum Disorder (mild), intellectual disability, specific learning disability, mental illness, and multiple disabilities. 4.5% of total vacancies are reserved for Disabled Ex-Servicemen and dependents of servicemen killed in action, in addition to the 10% earmarked for Ex-Servicemen (a total of 14.50%). SBI Junior Associate Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection consists of an online Preliminary Examination, an online Main Examination, and (where applicable) a Local Language Proficiency Test. Each test has a separate timing. There is negative marking of 1/4th mark per wrong answer in the objective tests. No minimum qualifying marks are prescribed for individual tests or the aggregate score; sectional marks are not maintained. Approximately 10 times the number of vacancies in each category (subject to availability) will be shortlisted for the Main Exam, based on descending order of aggregate marks in the Preliminary Exam.   • 1Visit the Bank's website, https://sbi.bank.in/web/careers/current-openings.

  • 4Upload your scanned photograph, signature, left-hand thumb impression, and hand-written declaration as per the specified guidelines, and capture a live photograph via webcam or mobile phone.
  • 5Pay the requisite application fee online through debit card, credit card, or Internet Banking (fee is Nil for SC/ST/PwBD/XS/DXS, and ₹750 for General/OBC/EWS candidates).
  • 6Review your application carefully before final submission - no corrections or edits are allowed after final submission.
  • 7Submit the application before the last date, 31.08.2026, and take a printout of the system-generated application form for your own record (this printout is not to be sent to SBI).
  • 8For any problem in filling up the form, payment of fee, or receipt of admission/call letter, candidates may contact the helpdesk at 022-22820427 (between 11:00 AM and 5:00 PM on Bank working days) or lodge a query at http://cgrs.ibps.in, mentioning 'Recruitment of Junior Associate-2026' in the subject line.
