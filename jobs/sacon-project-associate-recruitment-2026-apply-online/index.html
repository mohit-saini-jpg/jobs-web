About SACON

SACON is the South India Centre of the Wildlife Institute of India, located at Moongilpallam, Anaikatty (PO), Coimbatore – 641 108, Tamil Nadu. These posts are in SACON's own research projects and are a government institute's temporary project engagements, not regular posts.

SACON Recruitment 2026 Job Description

Conduct species population surveys and ecological studies in remote areas of Andaman and Nicobar Islands with limited resources and challenging field conditions. Collect and analyze field data and contribute to preparation of reports. Assist with conducting social surveys among local communities to understand their views on species conservation. Work closely with the forest department and local communities to develop sustainable conservation/management plan. Assist the Project Investigator and senior project staff in achieving project goals and other institutional activities.
