DSSSB MTS Salary 2026

The DSSSB MTS salary is based on Pay Level 1. Candidates selected for MTS, MTS General Duty and MTS Aaya (Female) posts will receive a basic pay scale of Rs.18,000 to Rs.56,900. The starting basic salary is Rs.18,000 per month, excluding applicable allowances. The DSSSB MTS in-hand salary depends on the starting basic pay, applicable Dearness Allowance (DA), House Rent Allowance (HRA), Transport Allowance and deductions. Although the starting basic pay is Rs.18,000 per month, the final take-home salary will vary according to the employee's allowances and deductions. The official notification does not specify a fixed in-hand salary.

DSSSB Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

The DSSSB selection process differs by post code. Multi-Tasking Staff recruitment will be conducted through a One-Tier (General) examination, while Junior Engineer candidates will appear for a Two-Tier (Technical) examination. Additional skill or trade tests may be conducted if required by the relevant job. The MTS selection process includes an objective One-Tier examination carrying 200 marks. The merit list will be prepared based on examination marks, subject to minimum qualifying criteria and verification of eligibility and documents. The Junior Engineer selection process includes Tier I and Tier II examinations. Tier I is qualifying in nature and used for shortlisting candidates for Tier II. The Tier II examination is considered for final merit preparation, subject to the prescribed qualifying requirements.

DSSSB MTS Exam Pattern 2026

The DSSSB MTS Exam Pattern 2026 consists of 200 multiple-choice questions carrying 200 marks. The examination duration is two hours, and questions are divided equally among five subjects. Duration: 2 Hours. Negative Marking: 0.25 marks for each incorrect one-mark question.
