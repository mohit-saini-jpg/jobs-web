RRC SWR Scouts & Guides Quota Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Holders of the President Scout/Guide/Rover/Ranger award or a Himalayan Wood Badge (HWB) can apply for 11 posts on South Western Railway under the Scouts & Guides Quota for 2026-27. There are 3 Group-C posts in Level-2 (7th CPC, Grade Pay Rs.1900) and 8 Erstwhile Group-D posts in Level-1 (7th CPC, Grade Pay Rs.1800). The posts are open to all communities, and none is reserved for any community. Applications are accepted online only on the RRC/SWR website www.rrchubli.in until 23.59 hours of 13 December 2026.

Application Fee

The fee is paid online using internet banking or debit/credit cards. Bank service charges are borne by the candidate. Applications without the prescribed fee will be summarily rejected. Candidates applying for both Level-1 and Level-2 must pay the fee separately. To claim the lower fee, candidates must upload the prescribed certificates: Annexure-A (SC/ST), Annexure-F, G, H (disability), Annexure-D (minority self-declaration) or Annexure-E (EBC income certificate). Minorities means Muslims, Christians, Sikhs, Buddhists, Jains and Zoroastrians (Parsis). EBCs means candidates whose family income is less than Rs.50,000/- per annum. Candidates must enter beneficiary bank account details (beneficiary name, bank name, account number, IFSC) in the form for the refund. There is no refund for ineligible, erroneous, incomplete or rejected applications, or for eligible candidates who do not attend

Selection and Interview

Eligible candidates will be assessed out of 100 marks: a written test for 60 marks and marks on certificates for 40 marks. Candidates recommended for appointment must then pass a medical fitness examination conducted by the Railway Administration. Only Medically FIT candidates will be empanelled. Selection is conducted separately for Level-2 and Level-1. The written test has 40 objective type questions (40 marks) in CBT/TBT mode and 1 essay type question (20 marks). The questions relate to the Scouts and Guides Organization and its activities, and to General knowledge. The notice does not mention the duration of the test. Written examination dates will be notified only on www.rrchubli.in , and the admit card can be downloaded from the same website. Call letter information will be sent by SMS and e-mail. The syllabus is: History of Scouting/Guiding; Law and Promise; Organisation – Distt.

There are 11 posts in total: 03 Group-C posts in Level-2 and 08 Erstwhile Group-D posts in Level-1.
Q2What is the last date to apply?
A
The online application closes at 23.59 hours of 13 December 2026.
Q3What qualification is needed?
A
Level-2 needs 12th (+2) with not less than 50% aggregate, or Matriculation plus Course Completed Act Apprenticeship/ITI for Technical Categories. Level-1 needs 10th Pass or ITI or equivalent or NAC. Both levels also require a President Scout/Guide/Rover/Ranger award or HWB, 5 years of active membership, and two National/All Indian Railways level and two State level events.
Q4What is the age limit?
A
As on 01.01.2027, candidates must have attained 18 years. Level-2 candidates must not have attained 30 years, and Level-1 candidates must not have attained 33 years. Category-wise relaxations apply.
Q5What is the pay?
A
Level-2 posts are in Level-2 (7th CPC) with Grade Pay Rs.1900. Level-1 posts are in Level-1 (7th CPC) with Grade Pay Rs.1800.
Q6What is the application fee and is it refundable?
A
The fee is Rs.500/-, with Rs.400/- refunded to those who appear in the written examination. For SC/ST/Ex-Servicemen/PwBD/Women/Minorities/EBCs, the fee is Rs.250/-, refunded in full on appearing. Bank charges are deducted from both refunds.
Q7How are candidates selected?
A
Selection is through a written test of 60 marks and marks on Scouts & Guides certificates worth 40 marks, followed by a medical fitness examination.

Selected candidates will be posted in safety category posts only, anywhere on South Western Railway and Rail Wheel Factory, Yelahanka.
