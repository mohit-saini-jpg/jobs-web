Where and How to Send

Address the application to: The President, Governing Body, Nowboicha College, Nowboicha. The notice does not mention a postal mode such as speed post or registered post, and it does not say what to write on the envelope. Use the prescribed proforma issued by the DHE, Assam. The notice says this proforma is available on the College/DHE, Assam website ( www.nowboichacollege.ac.in ). It is titled "Application Form for the Post of Principal (Provincialised Colleges) in Assam" and is also printed in the notice in two parts: For conference, seminar or workshop participation, the form gives these points: International 7 each, National 6 each, Regional / State 5 each, Local 3 each. The application must be submitted through the proper channel with an NOC from the concerned authority.

A Master Degree with at least 55% marks (or an equivalent grade in a point scale wherever grading system is followed) by a recognized University. Professor / Associate Professor with a total service/experience of 15 (fifteen) years of teaching/research in university, college and other institutions of Higher education or Research institutions. Nowboicha College Principal Recruitment 2027 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Applications are scrutinised first. Incomplete or defective applications will be rejected at the time of scrutiny, and no call letter will be issued to those applicants. Candidates are then called for interview. Call letters will be issued in due course of time to the candidate's email as well as through WhatsApp. The interview date and venue are not specified in the notice.

Nowboicha College Principal Recruitment 2027 : Important Instructions

TA/DA will not be paid to the candidates called for interview. A working Principal in any Provincialized/Government College may apply with proper permission from the competent authority. The demand draft fee of Rs.5000.00/- is non-refundable. Give a valid email address and a WhatsApp-reachable phone number in the form, because call letters are sent through these. For queries, the letterhead gives Mobile No. 6000067739 and Mail ID [email protected] (Office of the President, Governing Body).

Nowboicha College Principal Recruitment 2027 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is the last date to apply for the Nowboicha College Principal post?
A
Applications must be sent within 15 days from the date of publication of the advertisement. That is about 15 November 2026 when counted from the covering letter dated 31 October 2026.
Q2What qualification is required for the Principal post?
A
Candidates need a Master Degree with at least 55% marks from a recognized University and a Ph.D. in the concerned/allied/relevant discipline with evidence of published work. They also need at least 10 research papers and a minimum research score of 110.
Q3How much experience is needed?
A
Candidates must be a Professor / Associate Professor with a total service/experience of 15 years of teaching/research in a university, college or other institution of higher education or research.
Q4What is the age limit for the Nowboicha College Principal post?
A
The upper age limit is 55 years as prescribed rules.
Q5What is the application fee?
A
The fee is a non-refundable demand draft of Rs.5000.00/- in favour of Principal, Nowboicha College.
Q6How do I apply for the Nowboicha College Principal post?

Fill the DHE, Assam proforma and attach the demand draft, your bio-data and attested testimonials from HSLC onwards. Send the application through the proper channel with an NOC, addressed to the President, Governing Body, Nowboicha College, Nowboicha.
Q7How will the Principal be selected?
