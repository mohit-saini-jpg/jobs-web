Karimnagar Anganwadi Teacher Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must have passed Intermediate (12th class) or an equivalent qualification. Candidates must be between 18 and 35 years of age as on 01-07-2026. Applicants must belong to the local area of the concerned Anganwadi Centre - for rural AWCs, the corresponding Gram Panchayat/revenue village; for urban AWCs, the corresponding ward. For posts reserved for SC or ST categories, only candidates who belong to that same local Gram Panchayat/habitation and hold the corresponding SC/ST status are eligible to apply. Candidates should check the "Reserved For" column against their specific AWC in the centre-wise tables above before applying.

Karimnagar Anganwadi Teacher Selection Process

Selection is made purely on a merit-marks basis out of a total of 100 marks, as follows: The District Collector/Chairman of the District Level Selection Committee, Karimnagar, has full authority to cancel this notification or amend the marks criteria.

Telangana Anganwadi Recruitment 2026 Job Profile

As an Anganwadi Teacher (AWT), the candidate will be responsible for providing nutrition and health services to children and women in rural areas. They will also be involved in conducting health check-ups, providing immunization services, and promoting child development and education.
