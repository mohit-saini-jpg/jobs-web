Q3What qualification is needed for the IIM Mumbai Manager (Civil) and Manager (Electrical) posts?

Each post needs a Bachelor's degree (B.E./B.Tech) in its own engineering discipline (Civil or Electrical) from a recognized university with a minimum of 60% marks, plus a minimum of 10 years of relevant experience. The qualification is an MBBS Degree from a recognised university with 05 years' experience as Medical officer in a reputed hospital or dispensary, OR MD, or MS, in an appropriate branch of Medicine.
