DPCC Group A Vacancy 2026 (17 Posts)

A total of 17 tentative vacancies are notified across three Group 'A' posts. The Pay Level (7th CPC) and category-wise break-up are given below. Important: The posts of AEE are exclusively reserved for OBC (NCL) Delhi and PwBD candidates. Applications received for the AEE post from candidates outside these categories will not be entertained. Benchmark disability categories suitable for these posts are: CAT (a) – Deaf & Hard of Hearing, and CAT (b) – Locomotor Disability (OL, BL, OA) including Leprosy Cured (OA, OL, BL), Dwarfism, Acid Attack Victims, and Muscular Dystrophy.

DPCC Group A Eligibility Criteria & Qualification

Related field for all three posts means: Civil / Environmental / Chemical / Electrical / Mechanical / ECE / Biomedical Engineering / Water Resources / Remote Sensing / GIS / Hydrology, or an equivalent qualification. The candidate must hold the required educational qualification from a University/Institution recognized by the appropriate Statutory Authority, and it must be regular and full-time, as on the closing date of receipt of applications. Applicants must satisfy themselves that they possess the essential qualifications before applying; no inquiry regarding eligibility will be entertained by DPCC. The candidate must be a citizen of India. A candidate applying for more than one post must submit a separate Application Form for each post. Candidates already working in Government, Semi-Government, Public Sector Undertakings, or Autonomous Bodies should apply through the proper channel
PostEssential Qualification Experience
Senior Environmental Engineer (SEE)Master's degree in Engineering in a related field, from a recognized University or Institution, with 10 years' experience in pollution control or a related field, acquired after obtaining the Master's degree.
Environmental Engineer (EE)Bachelor's Degree in Engineering in a related field, from a recognized University/Institution, with 8 years' experience in pollution control or a related field after the Bachelor's degree; OR Master's degree in Engineering in a related field with 6 years' experience in pollution control or a related field after the Master's degree.
Assistant Environmental Engineer (AEE)Degree in Engineering in a related field, from a recognized University/Institution, with 2 years' experience in pollution control/related field; OR Master's degree in Engineering in a related field from a recognized university.

DPCC Group A Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Applications must be submitted in physical/hard-copy form; there is no online application process for this recruitment.
  • 2Download the detailed advertisement and the prescribed Application Form from the DPCC website (dpcc.delhi.gov.in) under the "What's New" section.
  • 3Fill in the Application Form in block letters, providing details such as Name, Father's/Husband's Name, Date of Birth, Nationality, Email ID, Contact/Mobile Number, Correspondence Address, Category (SC/ST/OBC(Delhi)/PH/Gen), Educational/Professional Qualifications (in chronological order from the highest), and Employment Record (in reverse chronological order).
  • 4Affix a recent passport-size photograph on the form and sign the declaration certifying that the information furnished is correct and complete.
  • 5Fill in Annexure-1 (Details to be provided by the applicant) covering: percentage of marks in the minimum qualification, higher/additional qualifications, relevant experience, UPSC-selected technical exam status (if any), and valid GATE score (if any).
  • 6Attach self-attested copies of all relevant supporting documents/certificates along with the Application Form and Annexure-1. Applications submitted without relevant supporting documents will not be given weightage marks for the corresponding claims.
  • 7Place the signed application, photograph, and all documents in a sealed envelope superscribed: "Application for the Post of ___________" (mentioning the specific post applied for).
  • 8Send the sealed envelope through Registered Post/Speed Post/By-Hand to: The Administrative Officer, DPCC, 3rd Floor, Block-I, DMRC IT Park Building, Shastri Park, Delhi-110053.
  • 9Ensure the application reaches DPCC within in the Employment News/Rozgar Samachar.
  • 10Candidates applying for more than one post must submit a separate Application Form (with separate Annexure-1 and documents) for each post.

DPCC Group A Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is DPCC Group A Recruitment 2026?
A
It is a recruitment notification (No. DPCC/(3)(2)(86)/Admn-26) by the Delhi Pollution Control Committee to fill 17 Group 'A' posts of Senior Environmental Engineer, Environmental Engineer, and Assistant Environmental Engineer on a regular basis.
Q2How many vacancies are available in DPCC Group A Recruitment 2026?
A
A total of 17 tentative vacancies are notified: Senior Environmental Engineer (4), Environmental Engineer (2), and Assistant Environmental Engineer (11). The AEE posts are exclusively reserved for OBC (NCL) Delhi and PwBD candidates.
Q3What is the last date to apply for DPCC Group A Recruitment 2026?
A
Applications must be submitted within 21 days from the date of publication of the advertisement in the Employment News/Rozgar Samachar. This notification does not state the exact publication date, so candidates should check the DPCC website and Employment News/Rozgar Samachar for the confirmed deadline.
Q4What is the qualification required for DPCC Environmental Engineer posts?
A
Senior Environmental Engineer requires a Master's degree in Engineering with 10 years' relevant experience; Environmental Engineer requires a Bachelor's degree with 8 years' experience (or a Master's degree with 6 years' experience); and Assistant Environmental Engineer requires a Bachelor's degree in Engineering with 2 years' experience, or a Master's degree in Engineering.
Q5What is the age limit for DPCC Group A Recruitment 2026?
A
The age limit (not exceeding, as on the closing date for receipt of applications) is 50 years for Senior Environmental Engineer, 40 years for Environmental Engineer, and 35 years for Assistant Environmental Engineer, with relaxation for SC/ST/OBC and other special categories as per government guidelines.
Q6How can I apply for DPCC Group A Recruitment 2026?
A
Applications must be submitted in the prescribed physical form along with Annexure-1 and self-attested supporting documents, sent by Registered Post/Speed Post/By-Hand to the Administrative Officer, DPCC, 3rd Floor, Block-I, DMRC IT Park Building, Shastri Park, Delhi-110053, within 21 days of the advertisement's publication.
Q7What is the selection process for DPCC Group A Recruitment 2026?
A
Selection is based on a 100-mark weightage system covering percentage of marks in the minimum qualification (10), higher/additional qualifications (10), relevant experience (25), UPSC-selected technical exam status (20), valid GATE score (20), and an interview (15).
Q8What is the pay scale for DPCC Group A posts?
A
Senior Environmental Engineer is at Pay Level-12 (7th CPC), Environmental Engineer at Level-11, and Assistant Environmental Engineer at Level-10, as per the 7th Central Pay Commission pay matrix.
