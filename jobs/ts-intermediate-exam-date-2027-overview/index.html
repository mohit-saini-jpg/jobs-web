TS Intermediate Exam Date 2027 - Overview : Admit Card Overview

The Telangana Board of Intermediate Education (TGBIE) has released the TS Intermediate Exam Date 2027 for First Year and Second Year students on 19 November 2026. According to the tentative examination timetable, the Telangana Inter 1st Year examinations will be conducted from 24 February to 19 March 2027, while the 2nd Year examinations are scheduled from 25 February to 20 March 2027.

TS Intermediate Exam Date 2027 - Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 19/11/2026

The Intermediate Public Examinations (IPE) February/March 2027 will be held from 9:00 AM to 12:00 noon. The timetable covers General and Vocational stream students.
