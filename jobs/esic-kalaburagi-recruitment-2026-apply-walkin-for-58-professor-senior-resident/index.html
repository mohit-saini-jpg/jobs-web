ESIC Kalaburagi Recruitment 2026 Notification PDF

Employees' State Insurance Corporation (ESIC), ESIC Medical College, PGIMSR & Hospital, Kalaburagi has officially released Notification No. 532/GLBMC/A/12/12/RCT-SR/2025 dated 03 September 2026 for recruitment of Teaching Faculty and Senior Residents. ESIC Kalaburagi Eligibility Criteria 2026

Qualification and experience for Professor and Associate Professor posts are as per NMC (National Medical Commission) Norms published in the Gazette of India dated 30.06.2025 and 04.07.2025. Qualification and experience for the Senior Resident post are also as per NMC Norms published in the Gazette of India dated 30.06.2025 and 04.07.2025. The notification does not specify the exact degree-wise qualification content beyond referencing these NMC Gazette norms; candidates are advised to check the NMC norms directly for department-wise qualification details.

ESIC Kalaburagi Salary 2026 (Pay & Allowances)

ESIC has adopted a consolidated remuneration structure for contractual faculty, as per ESIC Headquarters letters dated 13.04.2022 and 08.12.2022 (updated from time to time). Travelling Allowance (TA) and Dearness Allowance (DA) on TA are payable as applicable. The notification also states there is no claim for services such as PF, Pension, Gratuity, Medical Allowance, Seniority, Promotion or Leave, although selected faculty are entitled to Earned Leave and Half Pay Leave as admissible to a temporary Government servant under the Central Civil Services (Leave) Rules, 1972, and Senior Residents are entitled to 2.5 Casual Leave on completion of a calendar month.

ESIC Kalaburagi Selection Process 2026

Applications submitted on the day of the walk-in interview, along with the requisite fee, are scrutinized by the Institute's scrutiny committee; only eligible candidates are allowed to appear before the selection committee. If the number of applicants is large, the selection committee may devise shortlisting criteria, which may include a Screening Test. 50% of the total marks is the criterion for eligibility for selection. Selection is made on the basis of a personal interview conducted by a duly constituted selection board. Selected candidates report for Medical Examination and verification of original certificates after receiving a provisional offer of appointment through email. The offer of appointment is provisional, subject to verification of original certificates at the time of joining. ESIC Kalaburagi EWS Reservation Criteria

Candidates not covered under the SC/ST/OBC reservation scheme, whose family's gross annual income is below Rs. 8.00 Lakh, may be identified as Economically Weaker Section (EWS). Families owning agricultural land of 5 acres or more, a residential flat of 1000 sq. ft. or above, or specified residential plot sizes in notified or other municipalities are excluded from EWS status regardless of income. The EWS benefit requires an Income and Asset Certificate issued by a Competent Authority as specified in the notification. ESIC Kalaburagi Recruitment 2026 - Apply Walkin : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is ESIC Kalaburagi Teaching Faculty & SR Recruitment 2026?
A
It is a walk-in interview recruitment by ESIC Medical College, PGIMSR & Hospital, Kalaburagi, for engaging Teaching Faculty (Professor, Associate Professor) and Senior Residents (SR) on a contractual basis in Super Specialty departments.
Q2How many vacancies are available in ESIC Kalaburagi Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 58 total vacancies: 9 Professor, 8 Associate Professor, and 41 Senior Resident posts, spread across 11 departments. The notification states these numbers are provisional and may change.
Q3What is the last date to apply for ESIC Kalaburagi Recruitment 2026?
A
There is no online "last date"; instead, registration for the walk-in interview takes place on 16.09.2026 between 9:00 AM and 10:30 AM, and the interview is on 17.09.2026.
Q4What is the qualification for ESIC Kalaburagi Teaching Faculty and SR posts?
A
Qualification and experience for both Faculty and SR posts are as per NMC Norms published in the Gazette of India dated 30.06.2025 and 04.07.2025. Specific subject/degree requirements are not detailed in this notification.
Q5What is the age limit for ESIC Kalaburagi Recruitment 2026?
A
Teaching Faculty candidates must be less than 69 years and Senior Resident candidates less than 45 years of age (including all category relaxations) on the date of interview.
Q6What is the application fee for ESIC Kalaburagi Recruitment 2026?
A
The fee is Rs. 300/- for general category candidates, payable by Demand Draft in favour of "ESI Corporation" payable at Kalaburagi. SC/ST candidates and female candidates are exempted from the fee.
Q7How can I apply for ESIC Kalaburagi Teaching Faculty & SR Recruitment 2026?
A
There is no apply online option. Candidates must fill the prescribed application form enclosed with the advertisement and submit it in person at the walk-in interview venue, along with the fee and required documents.
Q8What is the selection process for ESIC Kalaburagi Recruitment 2026?
A
Selection is through scrutiny of applications followed by a personal interview conducted by a duly constituted selection board; a screening test may be held if there are too many applicants, and 50% of total marks is the eligibility criterion.
