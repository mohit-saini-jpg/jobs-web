CPCL Intern Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Chennai Petroleum Corporation Limited (CPCL) is offering nine-month internships under the Prime Minister Internship Scheme (PMIS). There are 12 internships in different disciplines, and each pays a stipend of Rs. 9000 per month including DBT. Candidates must be 18 to 25 years old and hold the qualification printed for their internship, such as B.E / BTech, Any Graduate (3 Yr. Program), MSW in HR, B. Com, BBA in Marketing or a Diploma. Applications are accepted only on the PMIS Portal from 21 October 2026 to 11 November 2026.

About CPCL

The notice describes Chennai Petroleum Corporation Limited (CPCL) as a leading refining company and a Navratna Central Public Sector Enterprise under the Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas, Government of India. CPCL is based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. The HRD department issued this notification for internship training under the Prime Minister Internship Scheme (PMIS).

Vacancy Details

The notice lists 12 internships, each with its own Internship ID. It does not print a number of seats for any of them, so each internship is counted here as 1 vacancy, making 12 in total. According to the notice, the number of internship opportunities is indicative and may vary depending on organisational requirements and the training facilities available. Pay and Tenure

All 12 internships pay a stipend of Rs. 9000 Per Month Including DBT. Each internship lasts Nine (9) Months under the Prime Minister Internship Scheme (PMIS). No travel expenses are paid for attending certificate verification or during the internship period. CPCL will not provide boarding or lodging during the internship period. Under PMIS, the internship is intended only for training and skill development. CPCL has no obligation to offer employment, absorption or regularisation during or after the internship. Completing the internship does not give any right or claim to employment in CPCL.

CPCL Intern Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Candidates are shortlisted on the criteria prescribed under PMIS. Shortlisted candidates receive internship offers through the PMIS Portal and then attend certificate verification, where they must produce their original documents. Engagement depends on successful verification of certificates, meeting the eligibility conditions and meeting the medical fitness requirements. CPCL may ask for additional documents to support eligibility, and failing to provide them within the given time may lead to rejection of candidature.
