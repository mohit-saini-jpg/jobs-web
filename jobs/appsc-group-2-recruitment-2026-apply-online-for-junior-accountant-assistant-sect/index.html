APPSC Group 2 Selection Process 2026

The APPSC Group 2 recruitment notification provides for an offline OMR-based Screening Test and a Main Examination. The annexure also contains a Computer Proficiency Test scheme. Candidates should check the detailed notification for the complete post-wise selection rules. The preliminary screening examination will be conducted offline in objective-type OMR format. The test contains 150 questions for 150 marks, with a duration of 150 minutes. The main examination consists of two objective-type papers carrying 150 marks each, for a total of 300 marks. The brief notification includes a Computer Proficiency Test scheme covering computer operations and office automation. The applicability of this test to individual posts should be confirmed from the detailed notification.

APPSC Group 2 Exam Pattern 2026

The APPSC Group 2 examination scheme includes a 150-mark Screening Test and a 300-mark Main Examination. The notification prescribes negative marking of one-third of the marks assigned to each incorrect answer. Exam Mode: Offline OMR-based examination. Negative Marking: One-third of the marks prescribed for a question will be deducted for each incorrect answer in the Screening Test and Main Examination.
