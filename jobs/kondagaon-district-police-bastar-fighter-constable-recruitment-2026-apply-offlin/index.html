District Police Kondagaon Bastar Fighter Constable Eligibility 2026

Applications are invited only from local residents of Kondagaon district. Candidates must be not less than 18 years and not more than 28 years old on 1 January 2026. SC, ST and OBC candidates: upper age limit relaxed by up to 5 years. Local women candidates of Kondagaon district: upper age limit relaxable by up to 10 years, under the Chhattisgarh Civil Services (Special Provisions for Appointment of Women) Rules, 1997. Permanent government servants: not more than 36 years. Temporary post holders applying for another post: not more than 36 years. This also covers employees paid from contingency funds, work-charged employees and those working under a project implementation committee. Retrenched government servants: temporary service of up to 07 years may be deducted from their age, but the resulting age must not be more than three years above the upper age limit. Kondagaon District Police Bastar Fighter Constable : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection takes place in stages. Document verification and physical measurement come first, followed by the tests below. Candidates get only one chance in each event and must take part in all of them. Unreserved candidates must score 60 percent of the 100 marks. SC, ST and OBC candidates must score 50 percent. A candidate unhappy with the assessment can appeal to the selection committee. The appeal is settled at once and the committee's decision is final. The paper covers general knowledge, intelligence, analytical ability, local geographical structure, culture, tradition, social values and arithmetic. Up to 15 times the number of advertised posts will be called, and all candidates with the same cut-off marks remain in the exam. The final list is made from the combined marks of the physical efficiency test, written test and interview, plus bonus marks.

Kondagaon District Police Bastar Fighter Constable : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1Can I send the form by ordinary post or courier?
A
No. A candidate applying by post must use registered post. The other option is to hand in the form at one of the four listed offices.
Q2Which certificate proves that I am a local resident?
A
Item 13 of the form asks for a certificate issued by a competent authority showing that you are a local resident of Kondagaon district.
Q3Can a Class 5 pass candidate from the OBC or general category apply?
A
Yes, if the candidate belongs to a Naxal-affected family or a family living in a relief camp in a Naxal-affected area. Otherwise, 10th pass is required unless the candidate is from the ST category.
Q4What is the highest age allowed for a government employee?
A
Permanent government servants and temporary post holders applying for another post must not be more than 36 years old.
Q5By what date must I hold my bonus mark certificates?
A
Licences and NCC or NSS certificates used for bonus marks must be held on the last date for submitting the form, which is 12 December 2026.
Q6Which part gives the most bonus marks?

Knowledge of the district's local dialect carries 20 of the 30 maximum bonus marks.
Q7Do I have to take part in every physical event?
A
Yes. All five events are compulsory, so a candidate must attempt the long jump, high jump, shot put and both races.
Q8Does submitting the form confirm that I am eligible?
A
No. Applying does not establish eligibility, and the selection committee's decision on eligibility is final and binding.
