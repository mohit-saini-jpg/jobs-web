Pay and Terms

The recruitment is purely on an outsourcing basis up to 31.03.2027 to carry out AYUSH Public Health Programmes in Siddipet District under the administrative control of the Director, Department of AYUSH, T.G., Secunderabad. Thereafter, services will be as per the need and subject to satisfactory performance. The selection will be made through the District Selection Committee based on merit out of 100 total marks: AYUSH Medical Officer, Pharmacist, Psychosocial Counselor, and Yoga Professional: 90 marks will be allotted based on the marks obtained in the qualifying examination. Multi-Purpose Worker (MPW): 90 marks will be allotted for a written test. Conduct of the exam will be notified through the official website of Siddipet District.
