KSRTC Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Kerala State Road Transport Corporation (KSRTC) is hiring a Public Relations Officer and an Executive Engineer (Civil) on contract. There is 1 vacancy in each post. The Public Relations Officer gets a maximum consolidated pay of Rs. 45,000 per month and needs a degree plus a PG Degree or Diploma in Public Relations, Mass Communication or Journalism. The Executive Engineer (Civil) post is for retired Executive Engineers and above from PWD or CPWD with a B Tech in Civil Engineering. Applications are accepted online only, on the Centre for Management Development (CMD) website, www.cmd.kerala.gov.

Pay and Tenure

Public Relations Officer: maximum consolidated pay of Rs. 45,000/- per month. The contract is initially for a period of one (1) year. Executive Engineer (Civil): for those who retired from Govt. service, remuneration as per re-employment basis. Selection and Interview

Applications will be scrutinised based on the details the candidate provides, and a list of candidates eligible for the further selection process will be prepared. Selection will be based on an interview of the shortlisted candidates. KSRTC/CMD reserves the right to add another selection process if required. KSRTC/CMD may shortlist only a limited number of candidates, based on relevancy of experience, quality of academic achievements and overall suitability for the position. CMD may decide the selection process based on specific requirements. KSRTC Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in KSRTC Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 2 vacancies under KSRTC Recruitment 2026: 1 for Public Relations Officer and 1 for Executive Engineer (Civil). Both posts are on contract basis.
Q2What is the last date to apply for KSRTC Recruitment 2026?
A
The last date to submit the online application is 6 November 2026, 05.00 PM. The application link on the CMD website opens on 24 October 2026.
Q3What qualification is needed for the KSRTC Public Relations Officer post?
A
Candidates need a degree in any discipline from a UGC recognized university or National institute under the GoI. They also need a recognized PG Degree/Diploma in Public Relation, Mass communications, Journalism, Public Relations and advertising, or Public relations and visual media communication. Proficiency in Malayalam and English and experience in Print media approved by RNI List are also required.
Q4Who can apply for the KSRTC Executive Engineer (Civil) post?
A
Retired Executive Engineers and above from PWD / CPWD who were engaged in the design and construction of major buildings may apply. They need a B Tech in Civil Engineering degree, or an equivalent qualification recognized by University of Kerala, and at least 10 years' experience in the construction of buildings.
Q5What is the age limit for KSRTC Recruitment 2026?
A
As on 01.10.2026, the upper age limit is 60 years for Public Relations Officer and 65 years for Executive Engineer (Civil). For Executive Engineer (Civil), the age limit and experience conditions may be relaxed for exceptionally qualified candidates.
Q6What is the salary for the KSRTC posts?
A
The Public Relations Officer gets a maximum consolidated pay of Rs. 45,000/- per month. For the Executive Engineer (Civil), remuneration for those who retired from Govt. service is as per re-employment basis.
Q7How do I apply for KSRTC Recruitment 2026?
A
Apply online only, by filling the prescribed form on the CMD website (www.cmd.kerala.gov.in). Upload your CV, photograph, signature and certificates there. KSRTC/CMD will not entertain applications submitted at its office.
Q8What is the selection process for KSRTC Recruitment 2026?
A
Applications are scrutinised and eligible candidates are shortlisted. Selection is then based on an interview of the shortlisted candidates. KSRTC/CMD may add another selection process if required.
Q9Is the KSRTC job permanent?
A
No. Both posts are on contract basis, initially for one (1) year. The Executive Engineer (Civil) contract is extendable on the basis of performance.
Q10Will KSRTC pay TA/DA for attending the interview?

No. The notification states that no TA/DA will be paid for attending the interview.
