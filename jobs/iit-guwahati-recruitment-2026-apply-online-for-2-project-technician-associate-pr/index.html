IIT Guwahati Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Department of Electronics and Electrical Engineering, IIT Guwahati, has invited applications for one Associate Project Engineer post (Rs. 45,000 pay plus Rs. 8,100 HRA, 11 months) and one Project Technician post (Rs. 16,000 pay plus Rs. 2,880 HRA, 4 months) in the project "An Unmanned Reliable Water Quality Monitoring System for Indian Rivers". Candidates need a B.Tech in electronics engineering or a closely related discipline with 3 years' experience (Associate Project Engineer), or an ITI degree in electrician or a closely related trade (Project Technician). Pay and Tenure

The Associate Project Engineer post carries Rs. 45,000 recommended pay and Rs. 8,100 HRA, with Rs. 0 medical, a total of Rs. 53,100, for an appointment of 11 months. The Project Technician post carries Rs. 16,000 recommended pay and Rs. 2,880 HRA, with Rs. 0 medical, a total of Rs. 18,880, for an appointment of 4 months. No campus accommodation will be available for the selected candidates.

Selection and Interview

Shortlisted candidates will be informed by e-mail on 22 November 2026. They must appear for the in-person interview at the date, time and venue given in the notice. Selection will be based on the performance of the candidate in the in-person interview.
