MPYPIL Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Madhya Pradesh Yatri Parivahan and Infrastructure Limited (MPYPIL), Bhopal, a State Transport Undertaking, has released three separate rule books inviting applications for 399 contractual vacancies across Junior Management, Senior Management and Higher Management categories. Online applications open on 29 July 2026 and close on 18 August 2026. All posts are contractual, for a period of one year, extendable at MPYPIL's discretion.

MPYPIL Recruitment 2026 Application Fee

Candidates must pay a non-refundable portal charge of ₹200 (excluding GST) at the time of submitting the online application. This is the same for all three categories - the notification does not specify category-wise fee concessions (SC/ST/OBC/EWS/Women), so no separate fee slab should be assumed. Payment is accepted only through an online payment gateway or an authorized MPOnline Kiosk. Only applications with successful payment will be considered; applications without successful payment will not be considered.

MPYPIL Selection Process

Selection for all MPYPIL posts (Junior, Senior and Higher Management) is through Interview cum Document Verification - there is no written examination stage specified in the rule books. MPYPIL may shortlist candidates for Interview cum Document Verification based on eligibility criteria and merit. The final merit/selection list is prepared based on eligibility, qualification, experience, suitability and the Interview cum Document Verification. Shortlisted candidates must bring all original documents supporting eligibility, qualifications, experience, age and category; two sets of self-attested photocopies; two recent passport-size colour photographs; and a copy of the submitted online application form. In case of a tie in final merit, preference goes to the elder candidate; if candidates are of the same age, preference goes to whoever has a higher percentage in the prescribed essential q

MPYPIL Recruitment 2026: Other Important Conditions

All appointments are contractual, for one year, extendable at MPYPIL's discretion; the Managing Director, MPYPIL is the Appointing Authority. Fulfilment of eligibility or inclusion in a merit/selection list does not confer any right to appointment; MPYPIL reserves the right to increase, decrease, modify or cancel vacancies or recruitment at any stage. The contractual appointment may be terminated by MPYPIL with one month's notice (or one month's consolidated pay in lieu), and without notice in cases such as suppression of material facts, forged documents, misconduct or breach of confidentiality. Selected candidates must maintain confidentiality of all official information, data and systems, both during and after the contract. MPYPIL Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is MPYPIL Recruitment 2026?
A
MPYPIL Recruitment 2026 is a drive by Madhya Pradesh Yatri Parivahan and Infrastructure Limited, Bhopal, to fill 399 contractual posts across Junior, Senior and Higher Management categories.
Q2How many vacancies are available in MPYPIL Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 399 vacancies in total - 199 in Junior Management, 54 in Senior Management, and 146 in Higher Management.
Q3What is the last date to apply for MPYPIL Recruitment 2026?
A
The online application closes on 18 August 2026. Applications open on 29 July 2026.
Q4What is the qualification required for MPYPIL Recruitment 2026?
A
Qualification varies by post, ranging from Class 12 pass with an ITI diploma for Helper/Supervisor posts, to Graduation/Diploma/BE-BTech for Assistant Manager posts, MBA/CA/ICWA/Postgraduate qualifications for senior posts, and Postgraduate/professional qualifications for Chief General Manager posts. See the qualification tables above for each post.
Q5What is the age limit for MPYPIL Recruitment 2026?
A
Age limits range from 25–45/50/63 years for Junior Management posts, 25–50 or 35–63 years for Senior Management posts, and 35–63 or 45–63 years for Higher Management posts, calculated as on 1 January 2026. See the age limit tables above for post-wise details.
Q6What is the application fee for MPYPIL Recruitment 2026?
A
A non-refundable portal charge of ₹200 (excluding GST), payable online or via an authorized MPOnline Kiosk.
Q7How can I apply online for MPYPIL Recruitment 2026?
A
Applications must be submitted only through the MPOnline portal at iforms.mponline.gov.in. Offline applications are not accepted.
Q8What is the selection process for MPYPIL Recruitment 2026?
A
Selection is through Interview cum Document Verification, based on eligibility, qualification, experience and suitability. There is no written exam.
Q9What is the salary for MPYPIL Recruitment 2026?
A
Consolidated pay ranges from ₹32,760/- for Helper/Police Constable posts up to ₹2,06,808/- for Chief General Manager posts. See the salary table above for post-wise figures.
Q10Is this a permanent Government job?
A
No. All MPYPIL posts under this recruitment are contractual, for a period of one year, extendable at MPYPIL's discretion.
