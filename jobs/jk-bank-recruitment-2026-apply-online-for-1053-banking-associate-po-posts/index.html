J&K Bank Banking Associate Selection Process 2026

The Banking Associate recruitment involves an online examination conducted by IBPS , followed by provisional selection and document verification according to the bank's recruitment rules. The notification does not prescribe a separate interview or Main Examination for Banking Associates. Exam Language: English. Negative Marking: One-fourth of the marks assigned to an incorrectly answered question will be deducted. Candidates must qualify in each section and meet the overall cut-off decided by IBPS in concurrence with J&K Bank. These are minimum qualifying standards. The actual category-wise selection cut-offs may be higher. Candidates domiciled in J&K who successfully completed apprenticeship training with J&K Bank will be awarded additional marks equal to 2.5% of the maximum marks in the online examination, as prescribed by the notification. J&K Bank Probationary Officer Selection Process 2026

The Probationary Officer recruitment consists of three stages: Preliminary Online Examination. Main Online Examination, including Objective and Descriptive Papers. Personal Interview. Shortlisting to the Main Examination is generally limited to ten times the vacancies in each category, while shortlisting for interview is limited to three times the vacancies in each category, subject to the bank's recruitment provisions. The Preliminary Examination is conducted in English and has separately timed sections. Minimum Aggregate Qualifying Marks: 40% for General Category and 35% for reserved categories, subject to overall merit and sectional cut-offs. Important: The PO Main Examination includes a descriptive paper assessing essay writing and comprehension, potentially covering economic, social, banking, technology, ethics and current-affairs topics. J&K Bank Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is the total number of vacancies announced in J&K Bank Recruitment 2026?
A
Jammu & Kashmir Bank Ltd. has announced a total of 1053 vacancies for Banking Associates and Probationary Officers under advertisement numbers JKB/HR/Rectt/2026-493 & 494. Candidates can apply online through the official website.
Q2What are the posts available in this recruitment?
A
The recruitment drive offers two positions: Banking Associate with 1033 vacancies and Probationary Officer with 20 vacancies. Both roles require candidates to possess a graduate degree from a recognized university along with required domicile credentials.
Q3What is the start and last date to apply online?
A
The online application process begins on 7 November 2026 and concludes on 26 November 2026. Interested and eligible candidates must submit their applications well before the closing date to avoid last-minute technical glitches.
Q4What is the minimum educational qualification required?
A
Candidates applying for either the Banking Associate or Probationary Officer post must hold a Graduate degree in any discipline from a recognized university. Additional specific certificates like domicile documents are also mandatory.
Q5What is the age limit for J&K Bank recruitment?
A
The age limit for all posts requires candidates to be a minimum of 20 years and a maximum of 30 years old. Upper age relaxations are applicable for reserved categories such as SC, ST, OBC, and PwBD as per guidelines.
Q6What is the selection process for these posts?
A
The selection process comprises an Online Examination followed by Document Verification for Banking Associates. For Probationary Officers, an additional Interview round is conducted after clearing the online examination and document check.
Q7What is the application fee for Banking Associates?
A
The application fee is Rs. 600/- for SC/ST/SEBC/PwBD/ESM candidates applying for the Banking Associate position, while all other categories are required to pay Rs. 800/- through the online payment mode.
Q8Are candidates from other states eligible to apply?
A
No, only domiciles of the Union Territory of Jammu & Kashmir and the Union Territory of Ladakh are eligible to apply for these positions as per the special conditions outlined in the official notification.
Q9What salary can selected candidates expect?
A
Banking Associates receive a basic pay of Rs. 24,050/- in a structured scale, whereas Probationary Officers start with a basic pay of Rs. 48,480/- in their respective scale along with applicable allowances.
Q10How can candidates apply for this recruitment?
A
Eligible candidates need to visit the official website at https://jkb.bank.in/, navigate to the careers section, complete the registration, fill out the online application form, upload necessary documents, and pay the fee online.
