Orissa High Court District Judge Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The High Court of Orissa, Cuttack is filling 38 posts in the cadre of District Judge by direct recruitment under Advertisement No. 03 of 2026, in the pay scale of ₹ 1,44,840 – ₹ 1,94,660 with usual D.A. and other allowances. Law graduates who are advocates with not less than seven years of continuous practice, or who have the combined advocate and judicial officer experience the notice allows, and who are not below 35 and not above 45 years of age as on the date of application, can apply online at www.orissahighcourt.nic.in from 10:00 A.M. of 9 November 2026 till 11:59 P.M. of 25 November 2026

Vacancy Details

Out of the 38 posts, 03 posts will be filled by horizontal reservation for Persons with Benchmark Disabilities having physical disability as specified in the Schedule to the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 (49 of 2016), excluding cerebral palsy. If eligible women candidates of a category are not available or are insufficient, the remaining vacancies will be filled by eligible male candidates of that category. If candidates of the S.C., S.T. and S.E.B.C. (Non-Creamy Layer) categories are not available or are insufficient, the vacancy will be filled by eligible candidates of the unreserved category. The number of vacancies is subject to change at the discretion of the Court, and the Court reserves the right to cancel the entire recruitment process or any part of it at any stage.

Who Can Apply

The candidate must be a graduate in Law from a recognized University or an Institution recognized by the Government. The candidate must meet one of these four conditions: be an advocate in continuous practice for not less than seven years as on the date of application; or be an advocate who has previously served in the judicial service and possesses a combined experience of not less than seven years as judicial officer and as an advocate, as on the date of application; or be a Judicial Officer who has completed seven years of continuous practice as an advocate before joining the judicial service; or be a Judicial Officer who possesses a combined experience of not less than seven years as an advocate and as a judicial officer, as on the date of application. Pay and Tenure

The scale of pay is ₹ 1,44,840 – ₹ 1,94,660 with usual D.A. and other allowances as admissible from time to time. Every candidate recruited as District Judge by direct recruitment will undergo a course of training under Rule-30-A and will be on probation as per Rule-33 of the O.S.J.S & O.J.S Rules 2007. The selected candidates must be physically fit and must appear before the State Medical Board before final appointment.

Selection and Interview

Selection has two parts: a Written Examination and an Interview, held under the O.S.J.S. and O.J.S. Rules, 2007. Orissa High Court District Judge Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

Incomplete or incorrect online applications will be summarily rejected. If a candidate submits more than one application, only the latest one with the higher Acknowledgement Number is considered. Admission to every stage is provisional; candidature is liable to rejection if eligibility is not met. No Admission Letter is sent by post; the dates and the e-Admit Card are published on www.orissahighcourt.nic.in , and the list of eligible applicants on the Court's website/notice board. Missing any stage of the examination or document verification leads to rejection. No T.A./D.A. is paid for appearing in the Examination, and canvassing in any form is a disqualification. Impersonation, fabricated or tampered documents or false statements may lead to debarment, permanently or for a period, besides criminal prosecution. For help with the online application, contact the Recruitment Cell on 9437498

  • 20Judicial officers obtain their experience certificate from the Registrar (Administration) of the High Court of Orissa, or the equivalent officer of other High Courts.
  • 21The S.E.B.C. (Non-Creamy Layer) certificate must not be more than 3 years old by the last date of online application; an O.B.C. certificate will not be accepted in place of it.

Q3What qualification is needed for Orissa High Court District Judge?
A
A graduate in Law with not less than seven years of continuous practice as an advocate, or the combined advocate and judicial officer experience listed in the notice, plus passing Odia of Middle English School standard.
Q4What is the age limit for Orissa High Court District Judge 2026?
A
Candidates must not be below 35 and not above 45 years as on the date of application. All candidates get 1 year relaxation; SC, ST, S.E.B.C. (Non-Creamy Layer) and Women get 5 years and PwBD 10 years.

Q7How are District Judges selected by Orissa High Court in 2026?
A
Through a written examination of three papers and a 30-mark interview; the merit list uses Paper-1, Paper-2 and interview marks, with Paper-3 General English qualifying at 45%.
