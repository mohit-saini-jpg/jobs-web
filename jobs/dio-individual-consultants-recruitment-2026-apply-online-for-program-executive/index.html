DIO Individual Consultant Salary (Consultancy Fee)

Selected candidates will be engaged as Individual Consultants and paid a consolidated monthly consultancy fee, inclusive of all taxes, as decided by the competent authority based on the recommendation of the Interview & Selection Committee. The individual consultant is liable to pay Goods and Services Tax (GST) as applicable, though provision exists for reimbursement of GST paid against the consultancy fee received. An annual increment of up to 10% of the existing consultancy fee may be given based on annual appraisal, as per DIO rules. However, during any extension period or at the time of annual increment, the monthly consultancy fee cannot exceed 1.80 times the initial monthly fee.
