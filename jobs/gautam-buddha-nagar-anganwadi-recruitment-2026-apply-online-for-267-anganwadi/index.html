Gautam Buddha Nagar Anganwadi Bharti 2026-27 Vacancy Details

Vacancies are distributed project-wise (Pariyojna-wise) across 5 Child Development Project areas of Gautam Buddha Nagar district, with category-wise reservation for each post. The full Gram Sabha/Ward-wise vacancy list and reservation eligibility details are uploaded on the departmental website http://upanganwadibharti.in and are also available at the notice boards of the Collectorate, Vikas Bhavan, Tehsil, Vikas Khand, and Child Development Project offices in the district. The number of vacant posts may increase or decrease.

Gautam Buddha Nagar Anganwadi Bharti 2026-27 Eligibility Criteria

Only women (female) candidates are eligible to apply for the advertised posts. Educational Qualification: Minimum qualification for both Anganwadi Karyakatri and Sahayika posts is Intermediate (12th pass) or its equivalent, up to a maximum of Postgraduate. Merit will be determined based on qualification level from Intermediate to Postgraduate. Residency: The applicant must be a resident of the Gram Sabha (for rural areas) or of the same Ward (for urban areas) where the vacant post is located. If an eligible candidate is not available within the Gram Sabha, selection will be made from the concerned Nyaya Panchayat as per seniority. Widows: A death certificate and a certified copy of the family register (Parivar Panjika) issued by the Gram Panchayat Officer/concerned Nagar Nikay will be considered valid proof. Gautam Buddha Nagar Anganwadi Bharti 2026-27 Selection Process

Selection for vacant Anganwadi Karyakatri/Sahayika posts will be conducted entirely online through the portal, as per the guidelines set out in the Government Order. No written examination or interview will be conducted. Selection will first be made from among eligible candidates below the poverty line (BPL), as per seniority within the age limit. If eligible BPL candidates are not available, selection will proceed to candidates above the poverty line, as per seniority. Merit will be determined based on educational qualification, ranging from Intermediate to Postgraduate. Residency in the relevant Gram Sabha/Ward is a mandatory eligibility condition; in its absence within the Gram Sabha, candidates from the concerned Nyaya Panchayat will be considered as per seniority. Two women belonging to the same family will not be appointed to Anganwadi Karyakatri/Sahayika posts at the same centre.
