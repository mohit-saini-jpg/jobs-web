NFSU Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1NFSU Recruitment 2026 has an online application followed by a walk-in interview. The application window is open until 2 December 2026.
  • 2Open the application link given in the notice: https://forms.gle/53LozQnQhxrsU1Un9.
  • 3Fill in the application and upload your eligibility documents through the same link, as the notice requests of all candidates.
  • 4Pay the application fee of INR 500 + GST through the payment link or QR code printed in the notice (SC/ST/PwD candidates are exempted).
  • 5Complete the application by 02.12.2026.
  • 6Report for the walk-in interview on the date fixed for your school, at 09.30 am, at NFSU Delhi Campus, Outer Ring Rd, near Jaipur Golden Hospital, Institutional Area, Rohini, Delhi, 110085.
  • 7Carry all original documents and the online fee transaction receipt to the interview.
  • 8There shall not be any separate intimation for interviews by telephone or email. All candidates fulfilling the eligibility criteria need to report on the interview dates with all supporting documents. A candidate is allowed to appear before the interview panel only subject to fulfilment of the eligibility criteria. Depending on the number of candidates, interviews may go till late in the day or may be conducted on the subsequent day, and candidates are required to make their arrangements accordingly. No TA/DA will be paid for attending the interview.
  • 9The fee is paid online through the payment link and QR code given in the notice. Candidates are requested to bring the online transaction receipt at the time of the walk-in interview.

NFSU Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in NFSU Recruitment 2026?
A
The notice lists 42 contractual posts at the NFSU Delhi Campus: 2 Associate Professor, 29 Assistant Professor, 3 JSO, 5 SSA and 3 Laboratory Assistant posts.
Q2What is the last date to apply for NFSU Recruitment 2026?
A
The last date to apply through the online link is 2 December 2026 (02.12.2026).
Q3What qualification is needed for NFSU Assistant Professor?
A
The essential qualification is a Ph.D. in relevant/allied discipline with first class in preceding degree; experience in the relevant field and one SCI journal publication are desirable.
Q4What is the age limit for NFSU JSO, SSA and LA posts?
A
JSO candidates must not be more than 35 years of age, and SSA and LA candidates must not be more than 30 years. For JSO and SSA, the upper age limit does not apply to candidates in the service of State or Central Government/University/Board/Corporation.
Q5What is the salary of NFSU Assistant Professor?
A
An Assistant Professor with Ph.D. gets a consolidated fixed monthly remuneration of Rs. 90,000/-, and an Assistant Professor UGC NET Qualified gets Rs. 75,000/-. JSO gets Rs. 60,200/-, SSA Rs. 47,400/- and LA Rs. 34,200/-.
Q6What is the application fee for NFSU Recruitment 2026?
A
The application fee is INR 500 + GST, paid online; SC/ST/PwD candidates are exempted. The transaction receipt must be brought to the walk-in interview.
Q7How do I apply for NFSU Recruitment 2026?
A
Apply and upload eligibility documents through the link https://forms.gle/53LozQnQhxrsU1Un9 by 2 December 2026, then report for the walk-in interview at NFSU Delhi Campus, Rohini, at 09.30 am on your school's date.
Q8When is the NFSU Delhi Campus walk-in interview?
A
Interviews are on 12 December 2026 (School of Management / School of Law), 13 December 2026 (School of Forensic Science) and 14 December 2026 (School of Cyber Security & Digital Forensics / School of Behavioral Forensic). The schedule for JSO, SSA and LA posts shall be communicated shortly.
Q9Are NFSU Recruitment 2026 posts permanent?
A
No. The University is filling these positions purely on a contractual basis for its Delhi Campus.
Q10Is RCI registration required for NFSU Assistant Professor (Clinical Psychology)?
A
Yes, for one vacancy under RCI, the registration as Clinical Psychologist under RCI Act is mandatory.
