IISER Bhopal Project Scientist Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Department of Biological Sciences at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research Bhopal (IISER Bhopal) has advertised one Project Scientist post for the BIRAC-funded INSACOG wastewater surveillance project, an 18-month engagement at Rs. 67,000 per month plus 20% HRA. Applicants need a doctoral degree in life sciences, or in computational sciences or bioinformatics, or in bioinstrumentation, with at least 60% aggregate marks and five years of research experience. Applications go by email to [email protected] by 10 November 2026.

Who Can Apply

Only eligible Indian Nationals can apply. The qualification and experience for the Project Scientist post are as follows. Experience: At least five years of experience in research and development during or after Ph.D in microfabrication and scientific instrumentation in industrial and academic institutions, and in sciences and technology organizations. Age: The candidate should be under 40 years of age as of 1 December 2026. Age relaxation is granted as per the rules of the Government of India; the notice prints no further detail. Experience in developing and running scientific instruments. Familiarity with anaerobic culturing and bioreactors. Preferably next-generation sequencing data analysis for genome and metagenome. Programming, script writing in Perl or Python or C, database construction, and development of AIML tools. Managerial skills, including project management, report writing
PostEssential Qualification
Project ScientistDoctoral degree in life sciences OR Computational sciences or Bioinformatics OR Bioinstrumentation from a recognised university or an equivalent institution, with a minimum of 60% aggregate marks (relaxation will be given as per the GOI guideline)

Pay and Tenure

The remuneration is Rs. 67,000/- per month + @20% HRA. The project runs for 18 months, and the notice states that all positions are purely temporary and coterminous with the project. No TA/DA is paid for attending the interview.

IISER Bhopal Project Scientist Job Description

The notice describes the role as follows: the candidate will engage in anaerobic culturing of microbes and metagenomes; perform and coordinate sample collection activities; maintain a systematic data repository; manage experimental data; prepare reports; collaborate with other institutions.

Selection and Interview

Candidates are shortlisted through a comprehensive application screening process, as outlined in position-specific guidelines. Shortlisted candidates are invited for an interview before a screening committee, and the notice says shortlisted applicants will be asked to appear for an online interview. The names of shortlisted candidates and the interview dates will be published on the IISERB website. The interview committee's decision is final, and no further correspondence is entertained.
