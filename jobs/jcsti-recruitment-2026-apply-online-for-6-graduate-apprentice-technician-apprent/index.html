About JCSTI

The Jharkhand Council on Science, Technology and Innovation (JCSTI) works under the Department of Higher and Technical Education, Government of Jharkhand. Its office is at Yojna Bhawan, Nepal House Campus, Doranda, Ranchi, Jharkhand - 834002. The apprentices will be engaged at JCSTI under the National Apprenticeship Training Scheme and the provisions of the Apprentices Act, 1961 (as amended).

Seats by Trade

JCSTI has 6 apprentice seats in total. The category-wise break-up is as printed in the notice: The fields covered are Mechanical, Civil, Electrical and Computer Engineering / Computer Science Engineering. The notice does not split the seats by individual engineering branch.

Who Can Apply

Candidates who have graduated / passed diploma in the field of Mechanical, Civil, Electrical and Computer Engineering / Computer Science Engineering from any recognized University/College/Polytechnic of Jharkhand State between the years 2024 to 2026 are eligible to apply. Graduate Apprentice (Engineering): graduated in one of the above fields. Technician Apprentice: passed diploma in one of the above fields. The institution must be in Jharkhand State, and the year of passing must fall between 2024 and 2026. Refer to the official notification for the discipline-specific eligibility of each post. Graduate Apprentice (Engineering): 01 SC and 01 ST. Technician Apprentice: 02 UR, 01 ST and 01 BC-I. In addition, 01 seat each under Graduate and Technician Apprentice shall be horizontally reserved for Divyangjan applicants.

Stipend and Training Period

The apprentices are selected for a one-year period. The monthly stipend is shared between JCSTI and the Board of Practical Training (BoPT): JCSTI shall only be responsible for payment of its share of the stipend and bears no responsibility for payment of the BoPT share of the monthly stipend. The engagement is valid for one (01) year only from the date of generation of the Apprenticeship Contract. Selected candidates will be awarded a Certificate of Proficiency (CoP) by the Board of Practical Training, Eastern Region, on successful completion of 1 year of apprenticeship training. The engagement is purely temporary and does not confer any right or claim, explicitly or implicitly, for regular appointment in any post of JCSTI.

JCSTI Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection is by shortlisting, a Personal Interview and a final merit list based on a cumulative score. Shortlisted candidates will be called for a Personal Interview at a place to be communicated in due course of time. The final selection is based on the cumulative score, combining the percentage of marks in the qualifying examination (Diploma/Degree) and the Personal Interview in the ratio of 70:30 respectively. The Personal Interview carries a maximum of 100 marks. Formula: Cumulative Score = (0.7 × Percentage of Marks obtained in Qualifying Examination) + (0.3 × Marks obtained by candidate in Personal Interview). Illustration from the notice: with 70% in the qualifying degree and 60 marks in the Personal Interview, the cumulative score is (0.7 × 70) + (0.3 × 60) = 49 + 18 = 67.
