AIIMS Guwahati Senior Resident Selection Process

Selection for AIIMS Guwahati Senior Resident Recruitment 2026 is based purely on performance in a personal interview, after scrutiny of the online application and supporting documents. The list of eligible candidates, after scrutiny, is uploaded on the Institute website. Document Verification (DV) reporting time: 8:00 AM. Interview time: 9:00 AM onwards. Interview venue: Director's Board Room, Medical College Building, AIIMS Guwahati. No TA/DA is paid for appearing in the interview. A separate merit list (selected and waiting list) is prepared for each category - UR, EWS, OBC, SC, and ST - based on the interview. Selected candidates must undergo document verification before the offer of appointment is issued. Q2How many vacancies are available in AIIMS Guwahati Senior Resident Recruitment 2026?
A
A total of 103 vacancies are available: 34 UR, 37 OBC, 17 SC, 7 ST, and 8 EWS, spread across 33 departments, with an additional 4% horizontal reservation for PwBD candidates.
Q3What is the last date to apply for AIIMS Guwahati Senior Resident Recruitment 2026?

Medical candidates need a Postgraduate Medical Degree (MD/MS/DNB) or equivalent under the Residency Scheme; dental candidates need MDS in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery; non-medical candidates need an M.Sc. and PhD in the relevant subject (Anatomy, Physiology, Biochemistry, Microbiology, or Pharmacology).

The upper age limit is 45 years as on the closing date of online applications, with age relaxation of up to 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC, and additional relaxation for PwBD candidates.
