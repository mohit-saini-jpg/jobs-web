AEES Teacher Salary 2026 - PRT, TGT, PGT Pay Scale

The AEES Teacher Salary 2026 depends on the post and pay level under the applicable pay matrix. The starting basic pay for AEES Primary Teachers is Rs. 35,400 per month under Pay Level 6. The pay matrix extends up to Rs. 1,12,400. Actual gross salary and take-home salary will depend on applicable allowances and deductions. AEES TGT teachers receive a pay scale of Rs. 44,900 to Rs. 1,42,400 under Pay Level 7. The starting basic pay is Rs. 44,900 per month. The AEES PGT pay scale is Rs. 47,600 to Rs. 1,51,100 under Pay Level 8.
