About AIIMS Mangalagiri

AIIMS Mangalagiri is described in the notice as a healthcare Institute of National Importance, established by the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Government of India under the Pradhan Mantri Swasthya Suraksha Yojna (PMSSY). Its address is Guntur Dist., Andhra Pradesh, 522503. The notice is signed by the Registrar.

Pay and Terms

Medical candidates are paid as per the 7th CPC at Pay Level-11 of the Pay Matrix, with an entry pay of Rs. 67,700 plus NPA and other allowances. Non-medical candidates (M.Sc with Ph.D) receive Rs. 56,100 in Level-10 under the 7th CPC plus other allowances. The appointment is as per the Government of India's Residency Scheme, for a maximum of 3 years. The appointee is on a whole-time appointment of AIIMS for 12 months at a time, continued up to 3 years on the recommendation of the HOD and at the discretion of the Competent Authority. Either side may end the appointment by giving one month's notice or paying one month's salary, without assigning any reason. It can also be terminated for failure to complete a period of three months to the satisfaction of the competent authority. The appointee shall not take any other assignment, paid or otherwise, and private practice of any type is strictl

AIIMS Mangalagiri Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in AIIMS Mangalagiri Senior Resident Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 3 vacancies: 1 in Anatomy and 2 in Medical Haemotalogy. By category, the notice shows 2 UR and 1 ST.
Q2What is the last date to apply?
A
The closing date of applications is 12 December 2026 (Saturday). The notice states there is no walk-in interview.
Q3What is the qualification for the posts?
A
Anatomy needs MD/DNB in Anatomy, or M.Sc/M.Biotech in the subject concerned with Ph.D. in the subject concerned/allied subject. Medical Haemotalogy needs MD/DNB in Medicine/Paediatrics or DM/DrNB (Clinical Haematology).
Q4What is the age limit?
A
The upper age limit is 45 years as on the closing date of application. SC/ST get up to 5 years' relaxation and OBC up to 3 years; PwBD get 10, 13 or 15 years by category.
Q5What is the salary?
A
Medical candidates get Pay Level-11 with an entry pay of Rs. 67,700 plus NPA and other allowances. Non-medical candidates (M.Sc with Ph.D) get Rs. 56,100 in Level-10 plus other allowances.
Q6What is the application fee?
A
General/EWS/OBC candidates pay Rs. 1500/- and SC/ST candidates pay Rs. 1000/-, through NEFT/UPI. PwBD candidates pay no fee, and the fee is non-refundable.
Q7How do I apply?
A
Fill the Google Form given in the notice and upload the scanned application in the prescribed format with documents and fee transaction details. The filled original forms are submitted at document verification.
Q8What is the selection process?
A
Selection is by interview only. If many candidates apply, an MCQ screening test in the subject concerned is held first, followed by the interview for shortlisted candidates.
Q9Is this a permanent job?
A
No. The appointment is under the Government of India's Residency Scheme for a maximum of 3 years, in 12-month periods, and carries no Provident Fund, Pension or Gratuity.
Q10Do I need an NOC to apply?
A
Applicants working in Central/State Government, Semi Government or Autonomous Institutions must produce an original NOC at the interview. They are allowed to appear for the interview only on producing it.
