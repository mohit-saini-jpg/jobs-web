Kerala PSC Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Visit the Kerala PSC official website at www.keralapsc.gov.in.
  • 2Open the One Time Registration or Thulasi Login section.
  • 3New candidates must complete the required One Time Registration process.
  • 4Existing registered candidates can log in using their User ID and password.
  • 5Update personal, educational and contact information in the profile.
  • 6Check that the required photograph, identity and qualification details are correctly entered.
  • 7Open the Notification section.
  • 8Find the relevant category number between 175/2026 and 192/2026.
  • 9Download and read the complete notification.
  • 10Confirm educational qualification, age limit, experience and reservation eligibility.
  • 11Click the Apply Now option for the selected category.
  • 12Complete the required declaration and application details.
  • 13Upload any mandatory supporting certificates, such as an in-service certificate where applicable.
  • 14Review the application carefully before final submission.
  • 15Submit the application before 16 December 2026, midnight.
  • 16Check the application status in the candidate profile.
  • 17Save or print the submitted application details for reference.
  • 18Monitor the Kerala PSC profile for examination confirmation, admission tickets and selection updates.
  • 19Important: Candidates applying for NCA, special recruitment or by-transfer vacancies must ensure that they belong to the exact notified eligible group. Incorrect category claims can lead to rejection.
