NIT Rourkela Field Assistant at a Glance

Register for Job Alerts National Institute of Technology Rourkela (NIT Rourkela) has notified 2 vacancies for the Field Assistant post under the ANRF-PAIR project. The appointment is project-based, purely temporary, and co-terminus with the project for an initial period of 1 year, extendable based on satisfactory performance. Eligible candidates must hold a Bachelor’s Degree in any Science discipline (B.Sc.) or a 3-year Diploma in Engineering & Technology with a minimum of 55% marks, and be up to 50 years of age with relaxations as per norms. Applicants must send a single PDF file containing the filled and signed application form, mark sheets, certificates, research papers, and work experience certificates via email by 29 October 2026. Selection will be through shortlisting followed by an online interview.

Pay and Tenure

Selected candidates will receive a consolidated remuneration of Rs. 27,000/- per month plus HRA as applicable per ANRF norms. The appointment will be for 1 year initially and the tenure will be extended based on satisfactory performance. The position is purely temporary and co-terminus with the ANRF-PAIR project (up to 13-October-2030) and does not confer any right or claim for regular appointment at NIT Rourkela.

NIT Rourkela Field Assistant Job Description

The Field Assistant will be responsible for the following duties and key responsibilities: Support Junior Research Fellows (JRFs) and the Co-Principal Investigators with day-to-day operational logistics, experiment setups, and report drafting. Assist the Co-Principal Investigators' research team in field visits, surveys, sample/data collection from mines and nearby industries, laboratory and experimental activities, maintenance of field records, and other related work as assigned. Conduct and coordinate field visits, surveys, data/sample collection, and other project-related field activities. Assist in planning field schedules and managing on-site logistics for surveys and data-collection campaigns. Selection and Interview

Candidates will be shortlisted based on merit and the need of the project. Only short-listed candidates will be informed for an Online Interview. The period of experience in a discipline or area of work, wherever prescribed, shall be counted after the date of acquiring the minimum prescribed educational qualifications for that position. Mere possession of minimum qualification does not guarantee invitation to the interview.
