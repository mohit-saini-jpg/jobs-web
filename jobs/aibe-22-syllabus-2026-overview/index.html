How to Download AIBE 22 Admit Card 2026?

Registered candidates can follow these steps once the Bar Council of India activates the admit card download facility.

  • Visit the official website at allindiabarexamination.com.
  • Open the AIBE XXII candidate login link.
  • Enter the registered User ID or Login ID and password.
  • Complete any additional verification requested by the portal.
  • Open the AIBE XXII admit card download option.
  • Check the candidate's name, roll number and examination details.
  • Download the admit card PDF.
  • Take a clear printout for the examination.
  • Keep an additional copy for reference.

AIBE 22 Exam Date 2026

The All India Bar Examination XXII will be conducted on Sunday, 29 November 2026.

The examination will be conducted in offline, pen-and-paper mode using an OMR answer sheet.

The purpose of the examination is to assess the required legal knowledge of eligible law graduates and other eligible applicants under the applicable BCI rules.

Qualifying AIBE is connected with obtaining the Certificate of Practice, subject to the candidate fulfilling the prescribed enrolment and other regulatory conditions.

AIBE 22 Exam Day Instructions 2026

Candidates should read the instructions printed on their admit cards and issued by the examination authority.

  • Carry a printed copy of the AIBE XXII admit card.
  • Bring a valid original photo identity document.
  • Reach the examination centre before the specified reporting deadline.
  • Check the examination centre location in advance.
  • Use the permitted ballpoint pen for the OMR answer sheet.
  • Enter the roll number and question booklet details carefully.
  • Follow the official rules concerning Bare Acts and other reference material.
  • Do not carry mobile phones, smartwatches or prohibited electronic devices.
  • Follow the invigilator's instructions during the examination.
  • Submit the answer sheet according to the examination rules.

Forgot AIBE 22 Login Password?

Candidates who have forgotten their login password should use the official account recovery option.

  • Visit the AIBE Candidate Portal.
  • Open the Forgot Password option.
  • Enter the information requested by the portal.
  • Follow the displayed account recovery instructions.
  • Set or recover access to the account as instructed.
  • Log in and download the admit card after release.

How to Correct Errors on AIBE 22 Admit Card 2026?

If the hall ticket contains an incorrect name, photograph, examination centre or other relevant detail, candidates should raise the issue with the official examination authority.

The regular application correction period ended on 30 October 2026. Therefore, candidates should not assume that the earlier application editing facility will remain available after admit card release.

Applicants should contact the official AIBE helpdesk, explain the discrepancy and retain any acknowledgement or instructions received.

Candidates should not independently modify or digitally alter their admit card PDFs.
