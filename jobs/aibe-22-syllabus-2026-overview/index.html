How to Download AIBE 22 Syllabus 2026 PDF?

Candidates can download the AIBE 22 syllabus from the official All India Bar Examination website. Follow these steps.

  • Visit allindiabarexamination.com.
  • Open the official AIBE XXII syllabus PDF.
  • Check the syllabus date of 13 October 2026.
  • Review all 19 law subjects listed in the document.
  • Check the number of questions allotted to each subject.
  • Save the syllabus PDF for examination preparation.

How to Prepare for AIBE 22 Exam 2026 Using the Syllabus?

Candidates can use the official subject-wise question distribution to create a structured revision plan. The syllabus identifies the weightage of each subject but does not prescribe individual chapters or detailed subtopics.

  • Begin revision with Constitutional Law, CrPC and BNSS, and CPC, which carry 10 questions each.
  • Prepare IPC and BNS, Evidence Act and BSA, Family Law, and Contract-related laws, which carry 8 questions each.
  • Revise Tort Law, including the Motor Vehicle Act and Consumer Protection Law, for its 5-question allocation.
  • Cover ADR, Public Interest Litigation, Professional Ethics, Labour Law and Taxation, which carry 4 questions each.
  • Include Administration Law, Company Law, Environmental Law, Cyber Law, Land Acquisition Act and Intellectual Property Laws.
  • Use the official syllabus to track completed subjects and remaining revision.

Important Instructions

  • Syllabus scope: The official syllabus has 19 law subjects and 100 questions. It covers areas such as constitutional, criminal, civil, family, tax, company and cyber law, plus professional ethics.
  • Exam date: The Bar Council of India (BCI) will hold AIBE 22 on 29 November 2026. The exam runs for 3 hours 30 minutes.
  • Registration: Registration began on 19 August 2026 and is reported to close on 27 October 2026. That closing date comes from only one source, so confirm it on the official portal.
  • Official source: Download the syllabus PDF only from the official portal, allindiabarexamination.com. Other sites may differ on weightage, and the BCI notification is the authority.
  • Study approach: Questions are framed from the subjects the BCI notifies, so plan your preparation subject by subject. Coverage reportedly includes India’s new criminal laws, so use updated bare acts and practise previous years’ papers
