Purnea Civil Court Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Retired employees of the Civil Courts can apply for 3 support staff posts (1 Stenographer, 1 Clerk and 1 Peon / Orderly) in the Evening Court of Purnea Judgeship. These posts are filled on a contractual basis. The Office of the Principal District & Sessions Judge, Purnea has issued the notice. Applications must reach the Office of the Principal District and Sessions Judge, Purnea, Bihar - 854301 on or before 30 November 2026. You can deliver the application in person or send it by registered post.

About Purnea Civil Court

The Office of the Principal District & Sessions Judge, Purnea heads the Purnea Judgeship in Bihar. The judgeship includes the Civil Court, Purnea and the Sub-Divisional Civil Courts at Banmankhi, Baisi and Dhamdaha. This recruitment is for support staff in the Evening Court of the Purnea Judgeship. The notice was circulated to all judgeships of Bihar.

Where and How to Send

Send the application to this address: The Principal District and Sessions Judge, Office of the Principal District and Sessions Judge, Purnea, Bihar - 854301. Mode: You can deliver the application personally, which the form calls physical mode. You can also send it through registered post. Deadline: The application must reach the office on or before 30 November 2026. The notice counts the date of receipt, not the date of dispatch. Posts, Eligibility and Pay

The notice prints one rule for the pay of all three posts. The period of engagement, remuneration and other conditions are governed by the applicable rules, guidelines and orders of the competent authority. No amount is printed.
