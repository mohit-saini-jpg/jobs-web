KSSSCI Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Kalyan Singh Super Specialty Cancer Institute, Lucknow, will hold a walk-in interview on 2 November 2026 to fill 5 short-term posts. Four of these are Senior Resident (Hospital Services) posts in Surgical Oncology and Gynaecological Oncology, and one is a Medical Physics Resident post. Candidates must report at the Admin. Block by 09:30 AM. Anyone who arrives after 11:00 AM will not be allowed to take the interview. The upper age limit for both posts is 45 years, and the application fee is Rs. 500, paid by RTGS/NEFT before the interview.

About KSSSCI

Kalyan Singh Super Specialty Cancer Institute (KSSSCI) is an autonomous institute of the Government of Uttar Pradesh. It is located at C.G. City, Sultanpur Road, Lucknow-226002. Its Executive Registrar Office issued this notification.

KSSSCI Senior Resident and Medical Physics Resident Vacancy Details 2026

A Senior Resident (Hospital Services) starts on an initial pay of Rs. 67,700, and a Medical Physics Resident starts on Rs. 35,400. Pay and allowances follow the 7th Pay Commission under State Government or institute rules. The single Medical Physics Resident post is in the UR category. If no suitable EWS candidate applies, UR candidates will be considered for the EWS posts. The notice says the Resident positions are indicative only. KSSSCI Senior Resident and Medical Physics Resident Eligibility 2026

The upper age limit is 45 years for both posts, counted as on the date of the interview. Candidates must be registered with the Medical Council / Dental Council of U.P. The prescribed qualification is only the minimum requirement, and having it does not guarantee selection. Age relaxation for SC/ST/OBC candidates, government servants and other categories follows UP Government rules/DoPT rules. Reservation and age relaxation apply only to candidates with Uttar Pradesh domicile. OBC candidates must attach a self-attested copy of a caste certificate issued within the last 6 months. For the full eligibility of each post and discipline, check the official notification.

KSSSCI Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Candidates are selected through the walk-in interview, and their original documents are checked at the same time. The selection committee's decision is final and binding, and no representations will be entertained. Results will be declared on the institute website ( https://cancerinstitute.edu.in ). Selected candidates will receive their appointment letter by email.

Service Conditions

Tenure: 12 months. The contract expires automatically at the end of 12 months. It may be renewed at the absolute discretion of the Director, KSSSCI. No claim to a regular post: The appointment gives no right to a regular appointment, permanent absorption or continued contractual appointment. Termination: The institute can end the contract at any time without a notice period. An employee can leave by giving 30 days' notice or salary in lieu of notice. Residents' services may also be ended without notice if bonded candidates from DGME join the institute. Work: This is a whole-time appointment, and private practice of every kind is prohibited. Appointees work in shifts and can be posted anywhere in the institute. Accommodation: Appointees must stay in campus accommodation if it is provided. KSSSCI Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1When and where is the KSSSCI walk-in interview?
A
The interview is on 2 November 2026 at the Admin. Block of KSSSCI, C.G. City, Sultanpur Road, Lucknow. Reporting time is 09:30 AM.
Q2Can I join the interview if I arrive late?
A
Only until 11:00 AM. Candidates who report after 11:00 AM on the interview day will not be allowed to appear.
Q3How many Senior Resident posts are there in each department?
A
There are 3 posts in Surgical Oncology (1 OBC, 1 SC and 1 EWS) and 1 EWS post in Gynaecological Oncology.
Q4What qualification is needed for the Gynaecological Oncology Senior Resident post?
A
MD/DNB (Obstetrics & Gynaecology), along with registration with the Medical Council / Dental Council of U.P.
Q5What is the starting pay of a Medical Physics Resident?
A
The initial pay is Rs. 35,400 at Level-6 of the pay matrix (Rs. 35,400 to Rs. 1,12,400).
Q6How do I pay the application fee?
A
Pay Rs. 500 by RTGS/NEFT to account number 37868945001 ("Director, SSCI&H, Academic Account") at State Bank of India, SGPGIMS branch, IFSC SBIN0007789. Then enclose the receipt with your form.
Q7Can candidates from outside Uttar Pradesh get reservation benefits?
A
No. Reservation and age relaxation are given only to candidates with Uttar Pradesh domicile.
Q8How long is the appointment?
A
The contract is for 12 months and ends automatically after that. Renewal is at the absolute discretion of the Director, KSSSCI.
Q9How will I know if I am selected?
A
Results will be published on https://cancerinstitute.edu.in. The appointment letter is sent to selected candidates by email.
Q10What should employed candidates bring?
A
A No Objection Certificate from the competent authority of their current organisation, or an application forwarded by the head of their institution.
