Odisha Grameen Bank Stock Auditor Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Odisha Grameen Bank invites Chartered Accountant firms to apply for empanelment as Stock Auditors. The firms will conduct stock audits of the bank. Applications go by registered post only to the General Manager (Inspection), Odisha Grameen Bank, Bhubaneswar. They must reach the bank on or before 31 October 2026, by 5 p.m. Applications sent by email will not be accepted.

About Odisha Grameen Bank Stock Audit Empanelment

Odisha Grameen Bank is a bank with its office at Gandamunda, Khandagiri, Bhubaneswar. The notice is signed by the General Manager, Credit Monitoring Department. It seeks firms that will conduct stock audits of the bank. These audits cover accounts with an overall working capital limit (Fund+Non-Fund) of Rs. 1 crore and above.

Where and How to Send

Firms must fill in the biodata in Annexure-A (the prescribed format), on the firm's letterhead, and attach the supporting documents. The application goes by registered post only to this address: General Manager (Inspection), Odisha Grameen Bank, At- Gandamunda, P.O.-Khandagiri, Bhubaneswar-751030. The bank must receive the application by 31 October 2026, 5 p.m. Applications received by email will not be accepted. The Annexure-A form is addressed to "The General Manager (CMD), Odisha Grameen Bank, Head Office, Bhubaneswar". All partners or directors must sign the form, and each one's photograph must be fixed on it. Posts, Eligibility and Pay

Chartered Accountant firms that meet the following criteria may apply: The firm of Chartered Accountants (proprietorship/partnership/company) should have at least 5 years' experience. The applicant firm or company should be registered with the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India. The applicant should have at least 5 years of post-qualification practising experience. At least 2 years of this should be in stock audits in different types of industry or sectors. For firms, the experience of the Managing or Senior partners is considered the experience of the firm. Some valuers may also apply for empanelment as stock auditor for specialised categories. They must be a registered valuer of stocks under section 34AB of the Wealth Tax Act and a fellow or associate member of the Institution of Valuers. Odisha Grameen Bank Stock Auditor Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is the last date to apply for Odisha Grameen Bank Stock Auditor empanelment?
A
Applications must reach the bank on or before 31 October 2026, by 5 p.m.
Q2Who can apply for empanelment as Stock Auditor?
A
Chartered Accountant firms registered with ICAI with at least 5 years' experience may apply. The applicant needs at least 5 years of post-qualification practising experience, including at least 2 years in stock audits.
Q3What is the age limit for Stock Auditor empanelment?
A
The minimum age is 25 years at the time of application. At empanelment, the individual applicant or proprietor must not be older than 75 years. For partnership firms, at least one partner should be below 75 years.
Q4What fee does Odisha Grameen Bank pay for a stock audit?
A
The fee payable is Rs.7,500/- (Rupees Seven thousand Five hundred only). Stock audits cover accounts with an overall working capital limit of Rs. 1 crore and above, and are conducted on a yearly basis.
Q5How do I apply?
A
Fill in the Annexure-A biodata on the firm's letterhead and attach the supporting documents. Send it by registered post only to General Manager (Inspection), Odisha Grameen Bank, At- Gandamunda, P.O.-Khandagiri, Bhubaneswar-751030.
Q6Can I send the application by email?
A
No. The notice states that applications received through email will not be accepted.
Q7How long is the empanelment period?
A
Empanelment is for five years. The bank will review the stock auditors' quality of service and performance annually.
Q8Can a former employee of the bank be part of an applicant firm?
A
Yes, but only after a cooling period of three years from that employee's date of retirement.
Q9Is empanelment with other banks required?
A
No. Empanelment with PSBs, FIs, DRT, a High Court or the Supreme Court as a stock auditor is preferred, but it is not mandatory.
