NIT Andhra Pradesh Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The National Institute of Technology Andhra Pradesh (NIT Andhra Pradesh) has officially announced the Ad-hoc Faculty Recruitment 2026 with 2 openings. NIT Andhra Pradesh Recruitment 2026 - Eligibility Criteria

School of Humanities and Management (Management): First Class in B.Tech. (any branch of Engineering) and First Class in MBA (Full time) OR First class in Bachelor's Degree (in any discipline) with First Class in M.Com. (Full-time) and First Class in MBA (Full time). Ph.D. in the relevant or equivalent discipline desirable. M.Com not mandatory with Ph.D. Electronics and Communication Engineering: First Class in both B.Tech./B.E. and M.Tech./M.E. or equivalent in respective branch of Engineering or allied branches. Ph.D. in the relevant or equivalent discipline desirable.

NIT Andhra Pradesh Recruitment 2026 - Salary

School of Humanities and Management (Management): Rs. 70000 per month with Ph.D.; Rs. 55000 per month with M.Tech or equivalent Electronics and Communication Engineering: Rs. 70000 per month with Ph.D.; Rs. 55000 per month with M.Tech or equivalent
