About MoHFW Consultants

The Ministry of Health and Family Welfare is a ministry of the Government of India. The Establishment – II Section of its Department of Health and Family Welfare issued this circular from Kartavya Bhavan-1, New Delhi-110001. The Consultants will work in the Department's Integrated Finance Division (IFD), which handles budget matters, financial concurrence and the scrutiny of financial proposals.

Where and How to Send

Applicants who can join immediately when called may submit their particulars in the application format enclosed with the circular. They must attach a copy of their PPO and LPC and send the application within one (01) month from the date of issue of the circular. The application must be addressed to: Under Secretary (Establishment – II), DoH&FW, Work Hall No. 11049, 'A' Wing, First Floor, Kartavya Bhavan – 1, New Delhi – 110001 The application format is titled "Application Format for Appointment as Consultant in IFD, Ministry of Health & Family Welfare". It asks for the following details: Position applied for, name, father's/husband's name, date of birth and Aadhar number Date of retirement, the Ministry/Department you retired from and the designation you last held Last pay drawn (Basic), with copies of the PPO and LPC enclosed Pay Level / Grade Pay and basic pension Telephone number, mob

Posts, Eligibility and Pay

There are five (05) posts, all titled Consultants for IFD, DoH&FW. All five carry the same terms: Eligibility: Retired Government servants at the level of Deputy Secretary or equivalent. The circular is addressed to retired Central Government servants. Educational qualification: The official notification does not specify the minimum educational qualification. The application format only asks applicants to state their educational qualification. Experience: Substantive experience in handling budget matters, financial concurrence, scrutiny of financial proposals and e-Office file routing within an IFD setting. Age limit: Maximum 63 years of age as on the date of this circular (the circular is dated 30.09.2026). Availability: Applicants should be in a position to join immediately when called. The official notification gives the full eligibility for the Consultant post in Annexure I and Annex

MoHFW Consultants Job Description

Annexure I lists these key responsibilities for the Consultants in IFD, DoH&FW: Scrutinizing expenditure and financial sanction proposals with reference to GFR/DFPR. Examining Annual Accounts, financial statements and draft resolutions. Assisting in budget preparation, re-appropriations and monitoring of expenditure. Tendering financial advice on procurement, contract management and audit paras. The Consultant will also perform the services assigned by the controlling officer.

MoHFW Consultant Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in MoHFW Recruitment 2026 for Consultants?
A
There are five (05) Consultant vacancies. All of them are in the Integrated Finance Division (IFD) of the Department of Health & Family Welfare.
Q2What is the last date to apply for the MoHFW Consultant posts?
A
Applications must be submitted within one (01) month from the date of issue of Circular No. Z-28015/16/2022-Estt.II. The circular is dated 30 September 2026 and has a handwritten "Issued 05/10/2026" endorsement.
Q3Who can apply for the MoHFW Consultant posts?
A
Retired Central Government servants at the level of Deputy Secretary or equivalent can apply. They need substantive experience in handling budget matters, financial concurrence, scrutiny of financial proposals and e-Office file routing within an IFD setting.
Q4What is the age limit for MoHFW Consultants?
A
The maximum age is 63 years as on the date of the circular, which is dated 30 September 2026.
Q5How is the pay of a retired employee fixed for the MoHFW Consultant post?
A
The Consultant gets a consolidated remuneration worked out as last pay drawn minus pension, subject to TDS etc. Each month's remuneration is paid in the following month.
Q6Are any allowances paid on top of the remuneration?
A
The only allowance is TA/DA on official tours, at the entitlement the Consultant had at retirement. No medical facility is provided, because the remuneration is deemed to include an element for medical cover.
Q7How do I apply for MoHFW Recruitment 2026 for Consultants?
A
Fill in the enclosed application format and fix a recent passport-size photograph on it. Attach a copy of your PPO and LPC, and send it to the Under Secretary (Establishment – II), DoH&FW, Work Hall No. 11049, 'A' Wing, First Floor, Kartavya Bhavan – 1, New Delhi – 110001.
Q8Is the MoHFW Consultant job permanent?
A
No, the engagement is on contract basis for a maximum period of one year and can be extended as required. Either side can end it by giving one month's notice.
Q9What are the working hours for MoHFW Consultants?
A
The normal working hours are 9.00 am to 5.30 p.m., Monday to Friday, with a lunch break from 1.00 p.m. to 1.30 p.m. In special circumstances, Consultants could be called on holidays or beyond these hours.
