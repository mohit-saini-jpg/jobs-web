UP Anganwadi Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Office of the District Programme Officer (Zila Karyakram Adhikari), Maharajganj has released a notification for direct recruitment to vacant honorarium-based (mानदेय) posts of Anganwadi Karyakartri (Anganwadi Worker) and Anganwadi Sahayika (Anganwadi Helper) under the Anganwadi centres functioning in the urban and rural project areas of Maharajganj district, Uttar Pradesh. Only women candidates are eligible to apply for these posts. Applications must be submitted online through the department's website, upanganwadibharti.in, on or before the last date. Maharajganj Anganwadi Eligibility Criteria

Only women (female) candidates are eligible to apply for the Anganwadi Karyakartri and Sahayika posts. Minimum educational qualification: Intermediate (12th) or an equivalent qualification. Maximum educational qualification considered for merit: Postgraduate. The candidate must be a resident of the same Gram Sabha (for rural areas) or the same ward (for urban areas) for which she is applying; local residency is mandatory. At one Anganwadi centre, two women from the same family cannot be appointed as Karyakartri and Sahayika together. Widowed applicants: The husband's death certificate and an attested copy of the family register (parivar panjika) issued by the Gram Panchayat Officer or the concerned Nagar Nikay are required. Maharajganj Anganwadi Selection Process

Selection will be made entirely on the basis of merit, following the procedure laid down in Shasanadesh No. I/2025/3313/58-1-2025 (1917687) dated 17.09.2025. No written examination or interview will be conducted for these posts. The entire selection process will be conducted online through the portal, as per the directions given in the government order. Merit will be determined on the basis of the qualification from Intermediate up to Postgraduate level. Marks obtained in High School will not be included in the merit calculation. To calculate merit, the percentage of marks obtained by a candidate is divided by 10. For example, 45% marks will count as 4.5 marks, and 59% marks will count as 5.9 marks.
