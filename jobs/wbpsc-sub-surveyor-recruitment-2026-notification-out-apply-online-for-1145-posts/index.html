WBPSC Sub Surveyor Eligibility Criteria 2026

Candidates must satisfy one of the two notified educational qualification routes. The experience requirement applies to the ITI Surveyor route, not to the three-year Survey Engineering Diploma route. Candidates applying through the ITI route must possess one year of experience in Survey and Settlement work involving modern survey techniques. The notification requires familiarity with: Differential Global Positioning System (DGPS) and/or Electronic Total Stations (ETS). Modern methods of surveying. Large-scale mapping and land records preparation. The experience must be obtained from an eligible Government-empanelled organization or an Indian GIS/surveying company recognized under the Digital India Land Records Modernisation Programme, Department of Land Resources, Government of India, with at least three years of experience in preparing large-scale maps and land records. WBPSC Sub Surveyor Selection Process 2026

The recruitment examination comprises Paper I and Paper II . Paper I is qualifying, while final merit is prepared using Paper II marks only. Paper I - Bengali/Nepali conventional examination. Paper II - Objective examination covering English, Elementary Mathematics and Survey Engineering. Preparation of the final merit list using Paper II marks of candidates qualifying Paper I and meeting the prescribed Paper II standard. Verification of educational, experience, reservation and other eligibility documents. Final recommendation subject to fulfilment of recruitment conditions. Important Merit Rule: Candidates must score at least 30% in Paper I for their Paper II marks to be considered. Paper I marks are not included in final merit. WBPSC may separately prescribe qualifying marks in Paper II.

WBPSC Sub Surveyor Examination Rules 2026

Paper I is a conventional examination carrying 50 marks. Paper II contains 200 objective questions for 200 marks. Paper I requires at least 30% qualifying marks. Paper II marks are considered only for candidates who qualify in Paper I. The Commission may prescribe qualifying marks for Paper II. Final merit is prepared using Paper II marks alone. Each incorrect answer in Paper II carries a deduction of 0.25 mark. Where two or more candidates obtain equal Paper II marks, the older candidate receives preference. The examination date will be announced separately.
