India Post Staff Car Driver Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must have passed 10th standard from a recognized Board or Institute , possess valid LMV and HMV driving licences, and have at least three years of experience driving light and heavy motor vehicles. Candidates must provide a valid experience certificate issued by a Gazetted Officer of a Central or State Government office or by the head of a registered firm, company, agency or institution. The certificate must include the organization's full address, door number, PIN code, issuing officer's name and designation, and date of issue. It must clearly specify the periods of LMV and HMV driving experience. Important: The claimed driving experience must fall within the validity period of the relevant driving licence.

India Post Staff Car Driver Selection Process 2026

Selection will be based on a two-stage examination comprising a theory test and a practical test assessing motor mechanism and driving ability. Stage I - Theory Examination: Objective questions covering General Knowledge, Reasoning, Arithmetic, Road Sense, Traffic Rules and Vehicle Maintenance. Stage II - Practical Test: Assessment of motor mechanism knowledge and light/heavy vehicle driving skills. Final Merit: Prepared from the total marks obtained in both stages, subject to the prescribed qualifying standards. Only candidates who qualify in Stage I will be eligible to appear in Stage II.

India Post Staff Car Driver Exam Pattern 2026

The theory examination consists of 80 multiple-choice questions carrying 80 marks , with a duration of 90 minutes. The practical examination carries 20 marks and assesses the candidate's motor mechanism knowledge and ability to drive light and heavy vehicles. The qualifying standards apply to each prescribed examination paper as specified in Annexure-I. Meeting the minimum standard does not guarantee final selection.
