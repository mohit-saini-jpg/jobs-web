Indian Coast Guard (ICG) is recruiting Enrolled Persons through the Coast Guard Enrolled Persons Test (CGEPT) for Batches 01/2027 and 02/2027. ParameterStandard As Per Notification
HeightMinimum height standard for male candidates is 157 cms and for female candidates is 152 cms. Height standards may be reduced by up to 05 cms for candidates having domicile of Assam, Nagaland, Mizoram, Meghalaya, Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur, Tripura, Garhwal, Kumaon and Sikkim. WeightProportionate to height and age as applicable for ICG.
HearingThe candidate should have normal hearing, i.e. able to hear focused whisper from a distance of 06 m by each ear separately.
TattooPermanent body tattoos are not permitted on any part of the body. However, certain concessions are permitted to candidates belonging to tribal areas communities as declared by the Govt. of India. For other candidates, permanent body tattoos are only permitted on inner face of forearms i.e. from inside of elbow to the wrist and on reverse side of palm/back (dorsal) side of hand.
DentalShould have healthy gums, good set of teeth and minimum 14 dental points.
General HealthCandidate should be fit for the Armed forces, must conform to the medical standards, have normal anatomy without loss or deformity of any appendages, and be free from any active or latent, acute or chronic, medical or surgical disability or communicable disease, infection and skin ailments. Candidate shall be physically and mentally fit to perform duty in any part of the world, in any climate and terrain places.
GenderAny candidate if found to have predominant characteristics of the opposite gender as evident on external physical examination will be rejected as unfit. Any candidate having undergone gender reassignment surgery will be declared unfit.
Pregnancy (female candidates only)Any candidate, if found to be pregnant shall be disqualified and her candidature will be rejected.
Gynaecological Examination (female candidates only)The examination covers external genitalia, hernia orifices and the perineum. Any evidence of stress urinary incontinence or genital prolapse outside introitus shall lead to disqualification of the candidate.

ICG CGEPT Application Fee

Candidates, except those entitled and desirous of applying in vacancies for the SC/ST category, are required to pay a fee of Rs. 550/- (Rupees Five Hundred and Fifty Only). SC/ST candidates are exempted from payment of the fee. The fee is paid online through net banking or by using Visa/Master/Maestro/Debit Card/UPI. The E-admit card will be issued for the examination only to those candidates who have successfully paid the examination fee and who are entitled to waiver of examination fee. Fee once paid will not be refunded under any circumstances nor can the fee be held in reserve for any other examination or selection. Candidates applying for vacancies under the SC/ST category are entitled to waiver of examination fee. Candidates are liable to be discharged/rejected at any stage of recruitment if it is found that a false SC/ST certificate has been produced to claim waiver of examination

ICG CGEPT Selection Process

The selection is based on performance in Stage-I, Stage-II, Stage-III and Stage-IV, meeting the laid down medical standards during medical examination and the number of vacancies available for the post in the respective batch. Qualification in Stage-I, II, III and IV is compulsory for recruitment in ICG. Selection of candidates for the post of Yantrik will be only for CGEPT-01/27, and for Navik (DB) and Hygienist-I only for CGEPT-02/27. Selection of candidates for the post of Navik (GD) in either CGEPT-01/27 or CGEPT-02/27 batch will be based on order of merit, available vacancies and administrative requirement. Candidates cannot claim their selection in a particular batch. All candidates will be compulsorily subjected to identity check prior to commencement of the examination at Stage-I, II and III of CGEPT. ICG CGEPT Medical and Physical Standards

The medical examination is conducted by authorised Medical Officers of Defence Forces as per the medical standard prescribed in current regulations applicable to Enrolled Persons on entry. Candidates using glasses are not eligible for GD Branch. Candidates are advised to get their ears cleaned for wax and tartar removed from teeth prior to medical examination. Candidates claiming height relaxation are to submit a domicile certificate to the medical officer. Failure to submit the domicile certificate will lead to non-waiver of height relaxation. No waiver will be given for laid down medical standards. The Indian Coast Guard (ICG) order on medical standard will be the final authority.

ICG CGEPT Documents Required at Each Stage

Aadhaar Card and mobile number linked with Aadhaar Card is mandatory at Stage-III for opening of bank account and generation of Permanent Retirement Account Number. Training: Basic training for CGEPT-01/2027 will commence in May 2027 and for CGEPT-02/2027 in November 2027 at INS Chilka, followed by sea training and professional training in allotted trade. Branch/trade will be allocated as per requirement of service and performance during basic training. Candidates are liable to be discharged as UNSUITABLE if their progress (including academic) or conduct is unsatisfactory at any time during training. Further, at any stage of training or service, if the online application or any document furnished by the individual is found to be falsified, the individual shall be liable to be discharged from ICG service.

ICG CGEPT Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is ICG CGEPT Recruitment 2026?
A
It is the recruitment of Enrolled Persons in the Indian Coast Guard (ICG) through the Coast Guard Enrolled Persons Test (CGEPT) for Batches 01/2027 and 02/2027, conducted through the Staff Selection Commission for the posts of Navik (General Duty), Navik (Domestic Branch), Yantrik and Hygienist-I.
Q2How many vacancies are available in ICG CGEPT Recruitment 2026?
A
For CGEPT-01/27, the notification lists 117 male and 13 female vacancies for Navik (GD), and 28 Yantrik (Mechanical), 23 Yantrik (Electrical) and 09 Yantrik (Electronics) vacancies. For CGEPT-02/27, it lists 117 male and 13 female vacancies for Navik (GD), 25 for Navik (DB) and 20 for Hygienist-I. The vacancies are tentative and may change.
Q3What is the last date to apply for ICG CGEPT Recruitment 2026?
A
The last date to apply online is 21 October 2026 (1730 hrs). Applications opened on 06 October 2026 (1100 hrs).
Q4What is the qualification for ICG Navik and Yantrik posts?
A
Navik (GD) requires Class 12th with Maths and Physics, Navik (DB) requires Class 10th, and Hygienist-I requires Matriculation OR ITI or equivalent. Yantrik requires Class 10th plus a 03 or 04-year AICTE-approved Diploma, or Class 10th and Class 12th plus a 02 or 03-year AICTE-approved Diploma, in the specified Electrical/Mechanical/Electronics/Telecommunication (Radio/Power) Engineering branches.
Q5What is the age limit for ICG CGEPT Recruitment 2026?
A
Navik (GD): born between 01 November 2005 and 01 May 2009. Yantrik (01/27 batch): born between 01 May 2005 and 01 May 2009. Navik (DB) (02/27 batch): born between 01 November 2005 and 01 October 2009. Hygienist-I (02/27 batch): born between 01 November 2002 and 01 October 2009. Age relaxation is 03 years for OBC and 05 years for SC/ST.
Q6What is the application fee for ICG CGEPT?
A
The fee is Rs. 550/-, payable online. SC/ST candidates are exempted from payment of the fee.
Q7How can I apply online for ICG CGEPT Recruitment 2026?
A
Candidates have to log in to www.ssc.gov.in, register, fill in the online application, upload the required documents and pay the fee (if applicable) between 06 October 2026 and 21 October 2026.
Q8What is the selection process for ICG CGEPT?
A
Selection is based on Stage-I (Computer Based Online Examination), Stage-II (Physical Fitness Test, Assessment and Adaptability Test, document verification and recruitment medical examination), Stage-III (identity check, document verification and pre-enrolment medicals at INS Chilka) and Stage-IV (verification and authentication of documents), along with the laid down medical standards.
Q9What is the salary of ICG Navik, Yantrik and Hygienist-I?
A
Yantrik basic pay is Rs. 29200/- (Pay Level-5) plus Yantrik pay of Rs. 6200/-. Navik (GD), Navik (DB) and Hygienist-I basic pay is Rs. 21700/- (Pay Level-3). Dearness Allowance and other allowances are payable as per the prevailing regulations.
Q10What are the passing marks in the Stage-I examination?
A
The passing marks are 40 for UR/EWS/OBC and 35 for SC/ST candidates in the 100-mark Computer Based Examination. There is no negative marking.
