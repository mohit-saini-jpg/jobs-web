Who Can Download the PGIMER Paramedical Admit Card 2026?

  • Only candidates who have successfully submitted the PGIMER Paramedical application form and completed the fee payment can download the admit card.
  • The admit card is issued exclusively in online mode; PGIMER does not send it through post, courier, or email.
  • Candidates must use the Login ID and password created at the time of registration to access the admit card.

How to Download PGIMER Paramedical Admit Card 2026

  • Visit the official PGIMER website: pgimer.edu.in.
  • Click on the "PGIMER Paramedical Admit Card 2026" link on the homepage or the candidate login section.
  • Enter your registered Login ID (or User ID) and password.
  • Submit the login details to access your candidate dashboard.
  • Locate and click the admit card / hall ticket download option.
  • Download the admit card PDF and verify all printed details carefully.
  • Take at least two to three clear printouts on A4-size paper for the exam day and future reference.

PGIMER Paramedical Exam Day Guidelines and Dress Code

Based on the exam day instructions reported for the PGIMER Paramedical exam, candidates should keep the following in mind:

Recommended

  • Wear light-coloured clothing with half sleeves.
  • Wear simple trousers or jeans.
  • Wear open slippers or low-heeled sandals.
  • Follow the reporting time strictly, as no candidate is allowed entry after the gate closing time.

Not Allowed

  • Closed shoes or boots
  • Clothes with heavy embroidery or metallic decorations
  • Large buttons, badges, or brooches
  • Cargo pants with multiple pockets
  • Metallic hair accessories

Important Instructions

  • The admit card is a mandatory document; candidates will not be permitted to appear for the exam without it.
  • Carefully verify your name, photograph, exam centre, date, and reporting time printed on the admit card immediately after downloading it.
  • If there is any discrepancy in the date, time, or other details mentioned on the admit card, it should be brought to the notice of the Training Branch, PGIMER, Chandigarh, via email without delay.
  • The admit card will not be issued or resent in offline mode (post or email); it must be downloaded from the official website only.
  • Keep multiple printouts and a digital copy of the admit card safe until the admission process is complete.

Important Links

  • Official website to download the admit card: pgimer.edu.in
  • For discrepancies in admit card details: contact the Training Branch, PGIMER, Chandigarh, through the official email address listed in your information bulletin
