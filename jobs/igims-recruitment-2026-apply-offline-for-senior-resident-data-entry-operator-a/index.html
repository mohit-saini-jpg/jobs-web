IGIMS Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

For Assistant Professor/ Senior Consultant and Senior Resident/ Consultant (Sl. No. 1 & 2): Selection will be made through Screening and Interview . For Clinical Psychologist/ Psychiatric Social Worker/ Psychiatric Nurse, Project Co-ordinator, and Data Entry Operator (Sl. No. 3, 4 & 5): Screening will be done, and if necessary, a written test may also be conducted in case a large number of applications are received. Applications will be scrutinized by a Screening Committee, and the final decision regarding appointment rests solely with the competent authority. The list of shortlisted candidates for interview/ test will be published on the IGIMS, official website. No separate communication will be sent regarding the date, time, and venue of the interview/ written test; candidates must keep checking www.igims.org. Mere fulfilment of the essential qualification does not guarantee selection.
