डॉ. भीमराव अंबेडकर Scholarship Haryana Ambedkar Scholarship Overview

Haryana Sarkar ne Dr. Bhimrao Ambedkar Scholarship Yojna ki shuruwat ki hai, jo backward classes ke bachchon ko padhai ke liye madad karegi. Yeh yojna un bachchon ke liye hai jo Haryana ke resident hain aur unka annual family income 6 lakh se kam hai.
