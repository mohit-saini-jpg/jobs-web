CPCL Recruitment 2026 Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection comprises a Computer Based Test (CBT) , followed by Document Verification of shortlisted candidates, and a qualifying Skill, Proficiency and/or Physical Test (SPPT) . Objective, multiple-choice questions; total CBT duration 2 hours . Negative marking: ¼ mark deducted for each wrong answer. Minimum qualifying marks: 40% in CBT for General/OBC/EWS; relaxed by 5% (i.e. 35%) for SC/ST/PwBD candidates against reserved positions. Candidates are shortlisted for SPPT in a ratio of approximately 1:3 (three candidates per post, factoring in reserved posts); tied candidates at the cut-off are all included. SPPT is of a qualifying nature (does not carry marks toward merit). Category-wise merit list is drawn from CBT marks, considering only candidates who qualify the SPPT; final selection is subject to being found medically fit.

CPCL Non Executive Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Visit www.cpcl.co.in, read the instructions, tick the declaration checkbox, and click Continue.
  • 2Select the post from the "Post Applying For" dropdown and complete new-user signup with basic personal details; validate your e-mail ID and mobile number via OTP.
  • 3Log in using the User ID and password sent to your e-mail/mobile.
  • 4Fill in Personal Details (parents' names, marital status, photo ID, addresses), then Academic Details and Work Experience (mandatory for all four posts).
  • 5Upload a recent passport-size photograph (20–60 KB, JPG/JPEG/PNG) and scanned signature (10–30 KB, JPG/JPEG/PNG).
  • 6Upload scanned documents/certificates in PDF (60 KB–2 MB).
  • 7Review all details on the Preview page, using "Edit" links to correct any section if needed.
  • 8Click Proceed to Payment, accept the declaration, and click Submit — no further changes are possible after this.
  • 9Complete payment via Net Banking/Debit Card/Credit Card/UPI (where applicable), then download the application form and payment receipt from the dashboard.
