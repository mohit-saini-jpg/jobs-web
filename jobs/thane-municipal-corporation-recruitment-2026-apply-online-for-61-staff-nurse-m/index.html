TMC GNM & MPW Selection Process

Selection will be made on a 100-point merit scale based on the following criteria: Marks obtained in the qualifying exam (final year), converted to a percentage-based proportion out of 50 marks Additional educational qualification beyond the minimum required, up to a maximum of 20 marks Relevant work experience in the post applied for, at 6 marks per year, up to a maximum of 30 marks Based on this merit list, candidates will be called for document verification in a 1:3 ratio against the number of vacancies. Candidates absent on the day of document verification will be declared ineligible, and the next eligible candidate on the merit list will be given a chance.
