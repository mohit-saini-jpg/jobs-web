LBS Hospital Senior Resident Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

The tenure of Senior Residents will be for a period of 44 days, which can be extended for further 44 days by giving 01 day break after receiving written request from the concerned doctor which is duly recommended and forwarded by the concerned in-charge till regular incumbent joins whichever is earlier (maximum 03 years including any service rendered as Senior Residents earlier on ad-hoc/regular basis). Selected candidates must submit an undertaking that he/she is not working at any Government or private health care facility at the time of joining and will not work during tenure.
