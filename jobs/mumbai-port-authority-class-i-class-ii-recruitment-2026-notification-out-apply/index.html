MbPA Application Fee

The application fee or intimation charges must be paid online between 29/09/2026 and 28/10/2026 (both dates inclusive). Bank transaction charges for online payment must be borne by the candidate. The fee is non-refundable and cannot be held in reserve for any other examination or selection. An SC or ST candidate who applies for a post as UR is not eligible for the fee relaxation. Payment can be made by Debit Card (RuPay/Visa/MasterCard/Maestro), Credit Card, Internet Banking, IMPS, Cash Cards or Mobile Wallets.

MbPA Selection Process

Selection starts with an online objective examination for all posts. The later stages differ for Class I and Class II posts. Online examination Verification of original documents for shortlisted candidates, in the order of performance in the online test Interview (30 marks) The final selection for Class I posts is based on overall performance in the online examination and the interview. MbPA decides how many candidates are called for document verification and interview. Online examination Document verification Physical Requirement Test Test of Proficiency in Marathi, Hindi and English (minimum 35% marks in each language) There is no interview for the Class II post. Final selection is based on the online examination, subject to qualifying the Physical Requirement Test and the language proficiency test. MbPA reserves the right to modify the selection procedure. Mumbai Port Authority Class I & Class II Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Applications are accepted only online. No other mode of application will be accepted.
  • 2Go to the MbPA website www.mumbaiport.gov.in and open the "People & Career/Jobs" menu.
  • 3Click "APPLY ONLINE", then choose "Click here for New Registration" and enter your name, contact details and email ID.
  • 4Note down the Provisional Registration Number and Password shown on screen (also sent by email and SMS).
  • 5Fill in the application form. You can use "SAVE AND NEXT" to save and verify details before final submission.
  • 6Upload the photograph, signature, left thumb impression and handwritten declaration as per the specifications.
  • 7Preview the entire form and click "COMPLETE REGISTRATION" only after checking every detail. No change is possible after this.
  • 8Click the "Payment" tab and pay the fee online.
  • 9Click "Submit", then take a printout of the application form and e-receipt. If no e-receipt is generated, the payment has failed and should be repeated by logging in again.
  • 10Candidates must also capture and upload a live photograph using a webcam or mobile phone. The declaration text is: "I, _______ (Name of the candidate), hereby declare that all the information submitted by me in the application form is correct, true and valid. I will present the supporting documents as and when required." Candidates need a valid personal email ID and mobile number that stay active until the recruitment process ends.
