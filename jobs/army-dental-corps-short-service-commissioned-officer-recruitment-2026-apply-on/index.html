Army Dental Corps SSC Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Candidates will be shortlisted for interview based on the marks scored in NEET (MDS)-2026. The list formulated will be used for screening candidates in a ratio of 1:10 - for each advertised vacancy, ten candidates will be called for interview. Shortlisted candidates will be called to appear before a duly constituted Selection Board at New Delhi, to assess suitability and merit for grant of SSC. The final merit list for grant of SSC in Army Dental Corps is based on interview performance alone. Candidates who qualify the interview must mandatorily undergo a detailed Medical Examination by a duly constituted Medical Board of the Armed Forces Medical Services, to ascertain their medical fitness. The decision of the Medical Board is final. Candidates declared unfit are classified as Temporary Unfit as per the outcome of the commissioning medical examination.
