IIIT Allahabad Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Indian Institute of Information Technology Allahabad (IIIT Allahabad) has invited suitable internal candidates for 4 promotional posts in its administrative and technical cadre: Assistant Engineer (Electrical) (1), Technical Superintendent (2) and Superintendent (1), all at Pay Matrix Level-7. Applications must be submitted by hand to the Deputy Registrar (Estt.), Establishment Section, on or before 15 December 2026, till 05:00 PM; applications received after the closing date and time will be summarily rejected.

About IIIT Allahabad

IIIT Allahabad is described in the notice as an Institute of National Importance declared under an Act of Parliament to provide for education and research in IT-focused and allied branches of Engineering, Technology, Applied Sciences and Management Studies. Its address is Deoghat Jhalwa, Prayagraj-211015 (U.P.). This notice is issued by its Establishment Section.

Posts, Eligibility and Pay

The posts are to be filled on a promotional basis. The notice invites suitable internal candidates. Essential qualification with experience: Degree/Diploma in Engineering with 06 years of experience as Jr. Engineer in PB-2 with GP-4200 (Pay Matrix Level-6 as per 7th CPC). IIIT Allahabad Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Applications are scrutinised first, and only scrutiny-cleared applicants are called for the selection process. The final selection is based on the candidate's performance in the written test and skill test, along with overall qualifications and experience. The screening and selection criteria follow RPN and the prevailing practice for promotional recruitment. Mere fulfilment of the stated qualifications does not automatically entitle a candidate to be called for the written test and skill test. The notice does not give a test pattern, syllabus or dates.
