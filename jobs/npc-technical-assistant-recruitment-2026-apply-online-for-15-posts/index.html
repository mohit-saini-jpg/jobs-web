NPC Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Only Indian nationals possessing the prescribed qualification and relevant professional experience are eligible to apply. Candidates must possess a recognized Degree in Commerce and at least five years of relevant experience in accounts, finance, auditing, taxation or budget-related work. The experience must have been gained in Central Government, State Government, a Public Sector Undertaking or an autonomous organization. A postgraduate qualification in Commerce or passing the Intermediate Examination conducted by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India or the Institute of Cost Accountants of India is desirable but not mandatory. Applicants must possess a recognized Bachelor's Degree in Arts, Science, Commerce or Management, along with five years of experience in administrative or office-related work. NPC Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

The National Productivity Council will select candidates through a two-phase examination system and subsequent verification. Online application submission and fee payment, where applicable. Phase-I Computer-Based Preliminary Examination. Shortlisting for Phase-II in the prescribed ratio. Phase-II Descriptive Examination. Digital Competency IT Practical Test. Preparation of post-wise merit lists. Original document verification. Medical fitness and character verification. Final appointment. Important: NPC explicitly states that there will be no interview .

NPC Technical Assistant Exam Pattern 2027

The examination comprises a Preliminary MCQ paper and Phase-II assessments. The examination medium is English. The Phase-I examination is a screening test. Its marks will be used for shortlisting candidates for Phase-II and for tie-breaking where applicable, but will not be included in the final merit calculation. Candidates will be shortlisted for Phase-II in a 1:12 ratio , subject to the prescribed category-wise rules and tie conditions. All candidates obtaining the same marks as the last shortlisted candidate will also be shortlisted.

NPC Phase-II Main Examination Pattern 2027

The Phase-II Main Examination includes a Descriptive Examination and a qualifying Digital Competency Test. The descriptive examination will be conducted in pen-and-paper mode. Separate papers will be held for Technical Assistant (Accounts) and Technical Assistant. The examination will assess post-specific professional knowledge, office or financial procedures, communication skills and the ability to prepare notes, drafts and written answers. The marks obtained in the descriptive examination will form the basis of final merit for candidates who qualify in the IT Practical Test. The Digital Competency Test is common to both posts and will assess practical computer skills using MS Office 2019 or later. NPC Recruitment 2026 Final Merit List

The final merit list will be prepared separately for the two posts. Only candidates who qualify in the Digital Competency Test will be considered for final merit ranking based on their Phase-II Descriptive Examination marks. If two candidates obtain equal marks, preference will be determined in the following order: Possession of a desirable qualification. Higher marks in the Phase-I examination for the relevant post. Older age. Candidates who qualify for both posts will be considered according to the first-preference post indicated in their application and the applicable merit and selection rules. NPC may maintain a reserve list valid for one year from the date of declaration of the result.

NPC Recruitment 2026 Reservation and Certificates

NPC Technical Assistant Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Visit the official recruitment portal at npcrecruitments.in.
  • 2Read Advertisement No. NPC/TA/1/2026 carefully.
  • 3Check the essential qualification, five-year experience requirement and age conditions.
  • 4Click the online registration option.
  • 5Enter your name, date of birth and gender exactly as recorded in the Class 10 certificate.
  • 6Provide a valid email address and mobile number.
  • 7Complete OTP verification and receive the registration credentials.
  • 8Log in using the registration number and password.
  • 9Enter your personal information and reservation category.
  • 10Select Technical Assistant (Accounts), Technical Assistant or both posts.
  • 11If applying for both posts, select the first-preference post.
  • 12Enter educational qualifications and marks.
  • 13Provide details of desirable qualifications, where applicable.
  • 14Enter each period of employment separately, including employer, designation, dates and duties.
  • 15Select the applicable age-relaxation category, if eligible.
  • 16Choose a Phase-I examination centre.
  • 17Enter PwBD and scribe-related information, where applicable.
  • 18Upload the required photograph, signature, qualification certificates and experience documents.
  • 19Review the complete application.
  • 20Pay the prescribed application fee, if applicable.
  • 21Accept the declaration and click Final Submit.
  • 22Complete submission before 28 December 2026 at 5:00 PM.
  • 23Download and preserve the final submitted application PDF.
  • 24Important: NPC will not provide an application correction window after Final Submit. Candidates should carefully check all details before completing final submission.
