CBSE Board Class 10th & 12th Date Sheet 2027 Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 10/11/2026

The CBSE Class 10 first board examinations will take place from 16 February to 9 March 2027, while Class 12 examinations will continue from 16 February to 9 April 2027. All examinations will begin at 10:30 AM IST, but the finishing time varies according to the subject. Students can download the combined official date sheet PDF containing subject-wise dates, examination timings and subject codes.

How to Download CBSE Board Class 10th & 12th Date Sheet 2027?

Students can download the official timetable through the CBSE website without entering a roll number or logging in.

  • Visit the official website at cbse.gov.in.
  • Open the CBSE main website.
  • Find the Latest @ CBSE section.
  • Click the notice titled "Date Sheet for Class X and Class XII Board Examinations, 2027".
  • The official PDF will open.
  • Check the examination schedule for the relevant class.
  • Find the registered subjects using the subject names and codes.
  • Verify the examination dates and timings.
  • Download and save the PDF.
  • Keep a copy for examination preparation.
