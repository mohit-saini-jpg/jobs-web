MSEDCL Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in MSEDCL Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 2 posts: 1 Company Secretary and 1 Joint Company Secretary. The number of posts is provisional and likely to change.
Q2What is the last date to apply for the MSEDCL Company Secretary posts?
A
The application must be received by email by 17:30 hours on 20 November 2026.
Q3What qualification is required for MSEDCL Company Secretary and Joint Company Secretary?
A
Both posts require a Graduate Degree with Associate Membership of the Institute of Company Secretaries of India (ACS), and a Degree in Law is preferable. Company Secretary needs a minimum of 15 years' post-qualification experience and Joint Company Secretary a minimum of 12 years, in each case with at least 2 years at General Manager level in listed companies with paid up capital of ₹ 100 crore or more.
Q4What is the age limit for MSEDCL Recruitment 2026?
A
The upper age limit is 50 years as on the date of advertisement, 14/11/2026, for both posts.
Q5What is the salary of MSEDCL Company Secretary and Joint Company Secretary?
A
Company Secretary gets 4 to 6 lakh per month (CTC) during the fixed tenure, and Joint Company Secretary gets a fixed 3.5 to 5 lakh (CTC) per month. The notice calls these amounts suggestive.
Q6How do I apply for MSEDCL Recruitment 2026?
A
Email the completed application form, with self-attested scanned copies of your SSC certificate, qualification and experience certificates, to [email protected] from your personal email ID. Applications by courier or by hand are not accepted.
Q7What is the selection process for the MSEDCL Company Secretary posts?
A
Candidates are shortlisted on qualification, experience, age and job responsibility, and are then called for a selection process/interview. The interview is tentatively scheduled at Mumbai on 23 November 2026.
Q8Is the MSEDCL Joint Company Secretary post permanent?
A
No. The Joint Company Secretary post is on contract for three years and may be extended yearly up to 05 years, with no claim to regular employment. The Company Secretary may be considered for absorption into the Chief Engineer pay scale after the fixed tenure, based on a performance review.
Q9Can I apply for both MSEDCL posts?
A
Candidates may apply for either post, and MSEDCL reserves the right to offer either post based on performance in the selection process. Only one application per post should be sent.
Q10Do government or PSU employees need an NOC for MSEDCL Recruitment 2026?
A
Yes. Employees of a Govt. Department, Public Sector Undertaking or Semi Govt. Undertaking must produce a No Objection Certificate at the time of interview.
