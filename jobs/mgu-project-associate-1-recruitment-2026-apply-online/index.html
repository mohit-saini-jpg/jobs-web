Who Can Apply

Candidates must possess an M.Sc. in Chemistry with a minimum of 60% marks. Preference will be given to candidates who have qualified with NET/GATE. Additionally, candidates with prior research experience in steady-state or laser spectroscopy will be given preference. The age limit is below 28 years. Job Description: The project will involve conducting laser spectroscopy experiments to investigate photophysical properties of organic molecules and materials along with sample preparation, spectroscopic data acquisition, analysis, and interpretation. It will also involve small-scale synthesis of organic molecules and their characterisation.

Pay and Tenure

The selected candidate will receive a consolidated amount of Rs. 25,000 per month. The fellowship is initially for 1 year and will be renewed based on the candidate's performance and the availability of funds. The fellowship is coterminous with the PI's Ramanujan Fellowship.
