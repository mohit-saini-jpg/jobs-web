About RWF Recruitment 2026

Rail Wheel Factory is a production unit under the Ministry of Railways, located in Yelahanka, Bengaluru. This recruitment is specifically for the re-engagement of retired non-gazetted staff to fill 11 posts of Supervisors (JE/SSE) in Level-6 & Level-7 Maintenance unit in the Mechanical department, as per the guidelines issued by the Railway Board.

RWF Supervisor Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

The validity of the scheme is as per Railway Board's Letter No.E(NG)II/2024/RC-4/9 dated 22.09.2026 (RBE 73/2026) for a period upto 15.10.2028 or till further orders, which is earlier. Retired Non-gazetted staff, cannot exercise the financial and D&AR powers and they shall not be authorized to issue any safety certificate. RWF Supervisor Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are available in this recruitment?
A
There are a total of 11 vacancies, comprising 03 posts of Junior Engineer (JE) in Level-06 and 08 posts of Senior Section Engineer (SSE) in Level-07.
Q2What is the last date to apply for these posts?
A
The last date for the receipt of applications is 06.11.2026 by 17.00 Hrs.
Q3Who is eligible to apply for this re-engagement?
A
Retired non-gazetted staff who held the same cadre/category upto three levels higher than the post against which re-engagement is being considered and are up to 65 years of age are eligible, with specific provisions for those granted financial upgradations under MACPS.
Q4How is the monthly remuneration fixed for re-engaged staff?
A
The remuneration is a fixed monthly amount arrived at by deducting the basic pension from the pay drawn at the time of retirement, as per RBE No. 96/2024 dated 15.10.2024.
Q5Can I apply if I was covered under the LARSGESS scheme?
A
No, retired staff who were covered under the Safety Related Retirement Scheme/Liberalized Active Retirement Scheme for Guaranteed Employment for Safety Staff (LARSGESS) are not eligible for re-engagement.
Q6What documents must be enclosed with the application?
A
Applicants must enclose self-attested photocopies of their Service Certificate, Pensioner Identity Card, and Pension Payment Order (PPO).
Q7How will the selection be conducted?
A
The selection will be based on the assessment of suitability/competency by a committee of officers before their re-engagement duly taking into account safety and other operational requirements.
