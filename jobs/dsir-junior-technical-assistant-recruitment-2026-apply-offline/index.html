DSIR Junior Technical Assistant Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Department of Scientific and Industrial Research (DSIR) Junior Technical Assistant (JTA) Draft Recruitment Rules 2026 have been released on 27 July 2026 on the official DSIR website. A total of 5 (subject to variation) Junior Technical Assistant vacancies have been proposed through the draft Recruitment Rules.

DSIR JTA Recruitment 2026 - Eligibility Criteria

Eligibility under the draft rules covers age, educational qualification, and a general disqualification clause: Age limit: not exceeding 30 years for direct recruits (see age limit section below) Educational qualification: a recognised Bachelor's degree in a specified science discipline, or a three-year Diploma in Engineering/Technology (see qualification section below) General disqualification: a person who has a living spouse and marries again, or marries a person who already has a living spouse, is not eligible for appointment - unless the Central Government grants an exemption where such a marriage is permissible under the applicable personal law

DSIR JTA Recruitment 2026 - Educational Qualification

As per column 7 of Schedule I, direct recruits must hold one of the following: A Bachelor's degree from a recognised university or institute in a discipline specified in Schedule II of the rules, or A three-year Diploma in Engineering/Technology from a recognised university or institute Candidates with a postgraduate background in life sciences, biotechnology, agriculture, or related science disciplines fall under the eligible discipline groups listed above, provided the qualifying degree specifies the relevant subject as the main discipline.

DSIR JTA Recruitment 2026 - Selection Process

The draft rules do not specify a written-exam or interview scheme for direct recruitment through SSC (this will follow SSC's own recruitment process once the rules are finalised and a vacancy notification is issued). For confirmation/selection matters, the rules specify a Departmental Selection/Confirmation Committee comprising: Scientist not below the rank of Scientist 'F' - Chairman Deputy Secretary or Under Secretary in charge of Establishment - Member Scientist not below the rank of Scientist 'C' - Member Consultation with the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) is not applicable under this post's rules.

No. AB-14017/61/2008-Estt.(RR) dated 13 October 2015, which requires draft rules to be published for 30 days to invite stakeholder comments before finalisation. These draft rules supersede the DSIR Junior Technical Assistant (Group 'C' post) Recruitment Rules, 1996, except for matters already done or omitted before this supersession. Stakeholders wishing to comment on the draft rules should do so within 30 days from the date of publication of the notice (27 July 2026).
