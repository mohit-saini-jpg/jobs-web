Telangana Anganwadi AWT Eligibility Criteria

Candidates applying for Anganwadi Teacher posts must have passed Intermediate (12th class) or an equivalent qualification. The notification does not specify a separate qualification requirement for Anganwadi Helper (AWH) posts. Applicants must be between 18 and 35 years of age as on 01.07.2026. Before submitting the application, candidates are advised to verify that they meet the required age criteria. Use our Age Calculator to calculate your exact age as per the official cut-off date. Only women candidates can apply. In addition, the notification lays down a local residency condition: for the Anganwadi Teacher post, only women who are permanent residents of the specific village/ward covered by that Anganwadi Centre are eligible to apply for the post at that centre. Telangana Anganwadi AWT Selection Process

As per the notification, candidates for the Anganwadi Teacher post will be selected based on a merit list prepared out of a total of 70 marks. The broad basis for these marks, as stated in the notification, includes: Merit based on Intermediate (12th class) marks Additional weightage marks for certain special categories, including widows and orphans Marks for an interview conducted by the District Selection Committee The exact mark-wise break-up for each special category is not fully legible in the scanned notification, so candidates are advised to verify the complete selection criteria from the official notification or the WCD Telangana website before applying. As noted above, eligibility for each Anganwadi Centre is further restricted to women who are permanent residents of that centre’s village/Gram Panchayat area.
