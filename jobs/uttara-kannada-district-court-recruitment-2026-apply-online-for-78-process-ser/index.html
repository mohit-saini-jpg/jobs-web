Uttara Kannada District Court Selection Process

Process Server: Candidates are shortlisted for interview based on percentage of marks obtained in the qualifying educational examination, in a ratio of 1:25 per post (as per reservation for the notified posts). The interview carries a total of 10 marks. Peon: Candidates are shortlisted for interview based on percentage of marks obtained in the SSLC examination, in a ratio of 1:10 per post. The interview carries a total of 10 marks. Final selection for both posts is based on marks scored in the interview. If two or more candidates score equal marks, seniority in age is used to determine merit order, with the older candidate ranked higher.
