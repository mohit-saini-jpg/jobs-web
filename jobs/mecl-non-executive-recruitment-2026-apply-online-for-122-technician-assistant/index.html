MECL Non-Executive Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Each post has its own essential educational qualification, listed in the vacancy table above. For several technical posts, an ITI certificate or Diploma is required along with specific licences (such as a Wireman certificate for Electrician or an HMV licence for Technician/Mechanic-Drilling posts and Junior Driver). Where relevant post-qualification experience is required (for example, for Accountant, Technician-Survey, Technician-Sampling, Technician-Laboratory, Electrician, Machinist, Technician-Drilling, Mechanic and Mechanic-cum-Operator posts), it is counted only after passing the essential qualification, as on the cut-off date. Minimum essential qualifications are considered for all purposes; higher qualifications without the essential qualification are not considered. MECL Non-Executive Recruitment 2026 Age Limit

The maximum age limit for all 16 posts under this recruitment is 30 years for UR/EWS candidates, as on the cut-off date of 01.09.2026. Age relaxation is available as follows: SC/ST candidates: 5 years relaxation. OBC-NCL candidates: 3 years relaxation (subject to a valid non-creamy-layer certificate issued on or after 01.04.2026 and a self-declaration). PwD candidates: 10 years (General), 15 years (SC/ST), 13 years (OBC-NCL). Ex-Servicemen: As per Government of India guidelines. Retrenched ex-contingent workmen of MECL: Up to 20 years. Internal candidates: Up to 1 year relaxation may be granted with CMD approval; no relaxation applies in qualification or experience criteria for internal candidates. Candidates should ensure that they fulfill the prescribed age criteria. Calculate your exact age using our Age Calculator before submitting your application to confirm your eligibility.

MECL Recruitment 2026 Application Fee

The application registration fee is Rs. 500/- for General, OBC, and EWS candidates, payable online through the payment gateway available on the application portal. The fee is not applicable for SC, ST, PwD, Ex-Serviceman, and Departmental candidates.

MECL Non-Executive Recruitment 2026 Salary

Selected candidates will be placed in IDA pay scales ranging from Rs. 19,600-47,900 (Grade W-3, Junior Driver) up to Rs. 22,900-55,900 (Grade W-7, Accountant), with most Non-Executive posts falling in Grade W-4 with a pay scale of Rs. 20,200-49,300. Besides Basic Pay, MECL offers IDA, HRA, Perks & Allowances (under a cafeteria approach), PF contribution, Group Performance Related Incentive, Group Medical Insurance Facilities, the MECL-Employee Defined Contributory Superannuation Scheme (NPS), Group Personal Accident Insurance, Group Term Insurance, vehicle loan facility, and House Building Advance, as per Company guidelines.

MECL Non-Executive Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection for all posts follows a multi-stage process, with all tests conducted at Nagpur only: Screening of applications: A Screening Committee segregates applications into "Eligible" and "Not Eligible" based on the information provided in the online application. Written Test (100% weightage): Conducted at Nagpur by an independent external agency notified as a "Public Examination Authority". Cut-off marks are 50% for UR/OBC-NCL/EWS, 45% for SC/ST, and a relaxed standard for PwD candidates as per government guidelines. Document Verification: Candidates clearing the written test are called for document verification, ordinarily in a ratio of up to 1:7 per vacancy, in order of merit. Skill Test / Trade Test (qualifying in nature): Conducted by an internal committee, which segregates candidates as "Suitable" or "Not Suitable". This stage does not carry marks toward the final merit list. Fina

Selected candidates may be posted at any MECL Project, Office, or Establishment located anywhere in India, or overseas, as per the Company's requirement.
