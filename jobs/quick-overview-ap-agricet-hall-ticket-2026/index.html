Quick Overview - AP AGRICET Hall Ticket 2026 : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 14/08/2026

AP AGRICET Hall Ticket 2026 is expected to release by Acharya N.G. Ranga Agricultural University (ANGRAU) on August 14, 2026, with the download expected to go live between 10:00 AM and 11:00 AM. Candidates who registered for the AP Agriculture Common Entrance Test (AGRICET) 2026 can download their admit card from the official website, angrauagricet.aptonline.in, using their Registration Number, Date of Birth, and registered mobile number. The AGRICET 2026 Computer-Based Test (CBT) will be conducted on August 18, 2026, from 10:00 AM to 11:30 AM for admission into B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture and allied diploma-entry degree courses across Andhra Pradesh.

How to Download AP AGRICET Admit Card 2026

Follow these steps to download your AP AGRICET 2026 hall ticket from the official portal:

  • Visit the official ANGRAU AGRICET portal: angrauagricet.aptonline.in.
  • Click on the link labelled "Download AGRICET Hall Ticket 2026."
  • Enter your Registration Number, Date of Birth, and Registered Mobile Number.
  • Select your respective diploma stream - Agriculture, Seed Technology, or Organic Farming.
  • Your hall ticket will appear on the screen. Download it and take at least two printed copies for exam day.

Candidates should keep their login credentials ready in advance so the hall ticket can be downloaded quickly once the link is activated.

AP AGRICET 2026 Exam Pattern

  • The exam is conducted in Computer-Based Test (CBT) mode.
  • The question paper contains 120 objective-type multiple-choice questions (MCQs).
  • Each correct answer carries 1 mark, for a total of 120 marks.
  • There is no negative marking for incorrect answers.
  • The paper is bilingual (English and Telugu). In case of any translation ambiguity, the English version is treated as final.
  • The question paper is based strictly on the prescribed ANGRAU polytechnic diploma syllabus.

Qualifying Marks and Category-Wise Cutoffs

  • General category candidates must secure a minimum of 25% marks (30 out of 120) to qualify for ranking.
  • SC and ST candidates have no minimum qualifying cutoff and are admitted up to the extent of seats reserved for their category as per government norms.

Photograph and Signature Attestation Rule

Candidates must inspect their printed hall ticket immediately after downloading it. If your photograph or signature is missing, blurred, or not visible on the printout:

  • Paste a recent passport-size photograph in the space provided on the hall ticket.
  • Get the photograph and hall ticket attested by a Gazetted Officer with an official seal.
  • Carry a photocopy of the attested hall ticket and submit it to the invigilator on exam day.

AP AGRICET 2026 Exam Centres

ANGRAU has set up 13 examination centres across Andhra Pradesh for the Computer-Based Test:

  • Srikakulam

  • West Godavari

  • Guntur

The exact venue address for each candidate will be printed on their individual hall ticket. Candidates are advised to verify their allotted centre and plan travel in advance to avoid last-minute delays.

AGRICET 2026 Mock Test

ANGRAU provides an official sample/mock test on its website, angrau.ac.in, to help candidates get familiar with the computer-based exam interface, including screen navigation, marking questions for review, and final submission. The practice test link is typically activated about a week before the exam date.

Important Instructions for AP AGRICET Hall Ticket 2026

  • Download the hall ticket only from the official website, angrauagricet.aptonline.in, or the main university site, angrau.ac.in. Avoid unauthorised third-party links to protect your credentials and personal information.
  • Cross-check all details on the hall ticket immediately after download - name, roll number, registration number, father's name, exam centre address, exam date, diploma stream, and photo/signature visibility.
  • Carry at least two printed copies of the hall ticket to the exam centre.
  • Reach the exam centre well before the reporting time on August 18, 2026.
