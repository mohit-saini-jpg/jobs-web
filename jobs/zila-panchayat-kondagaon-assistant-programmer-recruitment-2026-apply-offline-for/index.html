Zila Panchayat Kondagaon Assistant Programmer Recruitment : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

The selection process consists of a merit list prepared by assigning weightage to educational qualifications, a computer practical test, and work experience: Educational Qualification Weightage: Up to 45 marks based on the percentage obtained in the qualifying degree (minimum 60% marks required to be eligible). Computer Practical Test: 40 marks to test computer proficiency. Experience Weightage: Up to 15 marks based on experience working in government rural development schemes in Chhattisgarh (5 marks for at least 1 to 2 years of experience, 10 marks for up to 5 years, and 15 marks for more than 5 years of experience). Candidates will be called for the computer practical test in a 1:10 ratio based on merit.
