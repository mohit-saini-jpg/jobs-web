UPSSSC Forensic Science Laboratory Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must hold the essential educational qualification prescribed under the Uttar Pradesh Vidhi Vigyan Prayogshala Araajpatrit (Praviधik) Seva Niyamavali, 2016, and must have appeared in PET-2025 with a valid, non-zero and non-negative score in at least one domain. Candidates must hold a qualification in one of the following four domains: Biology Domain: B.Sc. in Biological Science / Forensic Science with specialization in Biological Science, Biotechnology, Bio Chemistry, Genetics, Cell Biology, or Microbiology from a recognized university/institute. Physical Science Domain: B.Sc. in Physical Science / Forensic Science with specialization in Physical Science, Nuclear Science, Instrumentation, or Electronics; OR B.Tech in Instrumentation/Electrical & Electronics Engineering/Civil and Mechanical Engineering. UPSSSC Forensic Science Laboratory Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection is made purely on the basis of the Main (written) Examination. Shortlisting of candidates for the Main Examination is done using the normalized PET-2025 score, category-wise, at 15 times the number of advertised vacancies (including all candidates scoring at or above the final normalized cut-off up to two decimal places). Candidates with a zero or negative actual/normalized PET-2025 score will not be shortlisted. The written examination will consist of a single question paper of 100 objective-type, multiple-choice questions carrying 100 marks, to be completed in 2 hours (120 minutes). Each question carries 1 mark, and there is a negative marking of 25% (1/4 mark) for each wrong answer. Time allowed for the complete examination is 2 hours (120 minutes). UPSSSC FSL Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Visit the official UPSSSC website: upsssc.gov.in
  • 2Go to the Live Advertisement Segment on the homepage and click on the relevant advertisement to download/view it.
  • 3Log in using your PET-2025 Registration Number and verify via the OTP sent to your registered mobile number/email.
  • 4Review your auto-displayed personal details (name, parentage, category, EWS/horizontal reservation status, date of birth, gender, marital status, contact details) carried forward from PET-2025.
  • 5Select (tick) the post(s) you wish to apply for from the displayed list of advertised posts; unselected posts will not be considered for your candidature.
  • 6Confirm your educational qualification for the selected post(s) by choosing Yes/No, and enter board/university, year of passing, certificate/roll number, and marks details.
  • 7Enter the verification code shown on the registration page and click Submit to generate your application form; download and save a copy.
  • 8Review your PET-2025 photograph and signature, which will be auto-displayed, then click Continue.
  • 9Indicate post preference (if multiple posts selected) and Yes/No for preferential qualification, then confirm your correspondence address.
  • 10Read the declaration carefully, tick all checkboxes to agree, enter the verification code, and click Save and Submit.
  • 11Pay the required fee online via Credit Card/Debit Card/Internet Banking/UPI or SBI e-Challan; on successful payment, the application is auto-submitted.
  • 12Download and print the final submitted application form for your records.
