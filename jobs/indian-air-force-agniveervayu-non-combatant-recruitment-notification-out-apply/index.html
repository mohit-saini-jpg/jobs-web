IAF Agniveervayu Non-Combatant Eligibility Criteria

Candidates born between 01 January 2005 and 01 July 2009 (both days inclusive) are eligible to apply. If a candidate clears all stages of the selection procedure, the upper age limit as on the date of enrolment should be 22 years. Only unmarried male candidates are eligible for enrolment as Agniveervayu Non-Combatant. Candidates must give an undertaking not to marry during the four-year engagement period. Any Agniveervayu Non-Combatant who marries during this period will be dismissed from service. Only unmarried Agniveervayu Non-Combatant will be eligible for later selection in the regular cadre as NC(E). Candidates must sign a voluntary undertaking accepting this condition at the time of enrolment. Job Profile for IAF Agniveervayu Non-Combatant

Selected candidates will be required to perform duties under either the Hospitality or Housekeeping stream, in addition to any specific duties assigned in organisational interest. Hospitality Stream: Cooking, kitchen management, presentation of dishes, maintaining a clean and orderly kitchen, safe storage of food and other items, handling crockery/cutlery/refrigeration, laying tables/chairs, and serving food and water. Housekeeping Stream: Cleaning of floors, roads, rooms, bathrooms, commodes, and urinals; making brooms; cutting grass/hedges; ground levelling and digging trenches; watering gardens; washing utensils and clothes; filling and carrying water; handling office equipment; and additionally, hair cutting, shaving, head massage, ironing clothes, stitching or repair of leather items, shoe polishing, and cutting/stitching/altering garments.

IAF Agniveervayu Non-Combatant Salary and Benefits

Agniveervayu Non-Combatant recruited under this scheme will be paid an Agniveer package of ₹30,000 per month with a fixed yearly increment, along with risk and hardship allowances (as applicable in IAF), dress and travel allowances, and perks such as ration, clothing, accommodation, and Leave Travel Concession (LTC) as per existing rules. After four years, the total contribution to the Agniveer Corpus Fund is approximately ₹5.02 lakh from the individual and a matching ₹5.02 lakh from the Government of India, giving an approximate exit amount of ₹10.04 lakh as the Seva Nidhi package (absolute amount, excluding interest). Agniveervayu Non-Combatant are not required to contribute to any Provident Fund, and there is no entitlement to gratuity or any pensionary benefit. IAF Agniveervayu Non-Combatant Selection Process

Candidates whose applications are accepted will be issued admit cards for the written test, which will state the date, location, and time of the test. The selection process has four phases: The written test is common for both streams and will be conducted at the station/location where the candidate submitted the application. Candidates reporting for the written test must carry their Aadhaar card (candidates from Jammu & Kashmir, Assam, and Meghalaya may carry another valid ID if they do not have an Aadhaar card), a character certificate signed by a Gazetted Officer/Panchayat Officer/School Principal/College Principal (not older than 6 months, with office seal), the original 10th/Matriculation passing certificate (or a DigiLocker-verified printout), the original 10th marksheet, and an experience certificate if any.
