JCI Deputy Manager Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Jute Corporation of India Limited (JCI) has one unreserved vacancy for Deputy Manager (Law), to be filled on a regular basis. Applicants need a recognised law degree with at least 60% marks, five years of post-qualification experience and must be no older than 35 on 30 September 2026. The paper application must reach JCI in Kolkata by Speed Post before 4:00 PM on 16 November 2026, so the receipt date matters more than the posting date. Applications sent by e-mail, fax, courier or by hand are not accepted.

About JCI

JCI is a Government of India enterprise set up in 1971 under the Companies Act, 1956, as an official agency to provide minimum support price to jute cultivators. It also carries out commercial trading in raw jute and allied products. Its head office is in New Town, West Bengal, and it has a presence in West Bengal, Bihar, Assam, Tripura, Odisha and Andhra Pradesh.

JCI Deputy Manager Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1Can I apply for the JCI Deputy Manager (Law) post by e-mail?
A
No. JCI accepts applications only by Speed Post, and those sent by e-mail, fax, hand or courier are treated as rejected.
Q2Does the 16 November 2026 deadline apply to posting or receipt?
A
It applies to receipt. Applications received after 4:00 PM on that date will be rejected.
Q3What must I write on the envelope?
A
Write "APPLICATION FOR THE POST FOR DEPUTY MANAGER (LAW) – Notification No. 01/2026". JCI will reject an envelope without it and will not inform the candidate separately.
Q4Can SC, ST or OBC candidates apply for this UR vacancy?
A
Yes, if they meet the eligibility criteria. They are treated as General candidates and get no relaxation in any criteria, though SC and ST candidates pay no fee.
Q5Will my internship count towards the five years of experience?
A
No. Internships during the LLB programme are excluded, and only full-time work experience counts.
Q6How is a CGPA converted to percentage if my university has no formula?
A
JCI uses the principle of proportion, so a grade of 6.0 on a 10-point scale is considered 60%.
Q7Where will document verification be held?
A
It will be held at the Corporation's Head Office at Kolkata. Candidates receive the date and time by e-mail.
Q8Do I need a No Objection Certificate?
A
Yes, if you are presently working in a government body, PSU, PSB or autonomous body, including nationalised banks and financial institutions. A hard copy issued by your current employer must be furnished at document verification.
Q9Will JCI pay my travel cost for document verification?
A
Outstation candidates who qualify the verification stage get to-and-fro AC 3-Tier rail fare by the shortest route on submitting valid tickets. Payment is made through ECS after the selection process is complete.
Q10What happens if I am selected but do not join?
A
You will be debarred from being considered for any other position in the Corporation for a minimum of 02 years from your date of selection.
