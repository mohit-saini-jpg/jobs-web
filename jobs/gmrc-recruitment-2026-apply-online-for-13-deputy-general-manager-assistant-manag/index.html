About GMRC

Gujarat Metro Rail Corporation (GMRC) Limited is a 50:50 Special Purpose Vehicle jointly owned by the Government of India and the Government of Gujarat. It is entrusted with the implementation of the Ahmedabad Metro Rail Project and the Surat Metro Rail Project and with their operations and maintenance. This is a recruitment by a government-owned company, on contract, deputation or post-superannuation terms.

Pay and Tenure

Pay for each post is the revised IDA scale shown in the vacancy table. For deputation, pay is as per standard terms and conditions. Deputy General Manager posts on post-superannuation basis carry a consolidated pay of ₹ 1,00,000 per month. Engagement is for a minimum of 3 years, extendable up to 5 years based on performance, subject to the organisation's standard terms and conditions. For IDA/CDA scale employees, the percentage of HRA may vary according to the place of posting, as per Government guidelines and company policy. Other benefits are paid as per Company Policy. Selected candidates get Group medical coverage and Group personal accident insurance coverage as per GMRCL policies. Selected candidates are likely to be posted at Ahmedabad, Gandhinagar, Surat or any other project of GMRC anywhere in Gujarat.

Selection and Interview

Selection comprises Personal Interviews, followed by Document Verification and Medical Examination as per the category prescribed for the post. The process judges knowledge, skills, experience, expertise, aptitude and physical fitness. Call letters with time, date and venue are issued to shortlisted applicants through registered email. The venue, date and time of interview will be informed in advance; no interview date is printed in the notice. Document verification is done before the interview. Candidates must carry original certificates, otherwise they will not be allowed to attend the interview. Photocopies are to be submitted with the resume at the interview. Preference goes to candidates with experience in the same grade or the immediate lower grade with 2 years' experience in the grade. GMRC Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in GMRC Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 13 vacancies: Deputy General Manager (Civil-Safety) 2, Deputy General Manager (Civil - QA/QC) 1, Assistant Manager (Civil-Safety) 4, Assistant Manager (Civil-QA/QC) 1, Section Engineer (Civil-Safety) 2, Section Engineer (Civil-QA/QC) 1, Engineer – Sr. Grade (M&P) 1 and Engineer – Jr. Grade (Signalling) 1.
Q2What is the last date to apply for GMRC Recruitment 2026?
A
The last date for submission of the online application is 18 November 2026.
Q3What is the qualification for GMRC posts?
A
Qualification depends on the post. Civil QA/QC posts need B.E/B.Tech in Civil Engineering; safety posts need an engineering degree in Civil/Mechanical/Electrical along with a minimum one-year full-time diploma in (Industrial) Safety; the Engineer posts need B.E/B.Tech in the disciplines listed for them. Experience is also required.
Q4What is the age limit for GMRC Recruitment 2026?
A
The maximum age as on 3 November 2026 is 45 years (contract), 57 years (deputation) or 62 years (post-superannuation) for Deputy General Manager; 35 years for Assistant Manager; and 32 years for Section Engineer and both Engineer posts. The age limit does not apply to GMRCL employees.
Q5What is the salary for GMRC posts?
A
Pay is in the revised IDA scale: ₹ 70000-200000 for Deputy General Manager, ₹ 50000-160000 for Assistant Manager, 40000-140000 for Section Engineer, ₹35000-110000 for Engineer – Sr. Grade and ₹30000-120000 for Engineer – Jr. Grade. Deputy General Manager on post-superannuation basis gets consolidated pay of ₹ 1,00,000 per month.
Q6How can I apply for GMRC Recruitment 2026?
A
Apply online through the "Careers" section of http://www.gujaratmetrorail.com. The notice states that no other mode of application is accepted.
Q7What is the selection process for GMRC posts?
A
Selection is by Personal Interview, followed by Document Verification and Medical Examination. Shortlisted applicants receive interview call letters by registered email.
Q8Are these permanent jobs?
A
No. The posts are on Contract/Deputation basis (Deputy General Manager also on post-superannuation basis) for a minimum of 3 years, extendable up to 5 years based on performance.
Q9Can retired employees apply, and what will they be paid?
A
Retired officials can apply for Deputy General Manager on post-superannuation basis, if they retired from IDA pay scale ₹ 70000-200000 or above or Pay Level 10 or equivalent. The age limit is 62 years and the consolidated pay is ₹ 1,00,000 per month.
Q10What do private-sector candidates need to submit?
A
Private-sector candidates must attach their latest CTC break-up at the time of online application, or the application will not be considered. The minimum CTC is ₹ 1,00,000 per month for Deputy General Manager, ₹ 70,000 for Assistant Manager and ₹ 50,000 for Section Engineer.
