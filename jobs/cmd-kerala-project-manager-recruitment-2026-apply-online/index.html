About CMD

The Centre for Management Development is an autonomous institution under the Government of Kerala that provides management consultancy, project management and training to Central and State Governments, local self-governments, development agencies and private and public sector institutions. The Travancore Devaswom Board is a statutory body constituted under the Travancore-Cochin Hindu Religious Institutions Act. It administers more than 1,250 temples and other religious institutions across southern Kerala. As per the directions of the Hon'ble High Court of Kerala, TDB has undertaken a digital transformation of its administrative, financial and operational systems, with Kerala State IT Infrastructure Limited (KITFRA) as Technical Consultant. This notification is a revised version of Notification No. CMD/TDB/03/2026 dated 17.10.2026, as amended by Addendum-1 (No. CMD/TDB/03/2026/Addendum-1)
