CMD Kerala Project Manager Job Description

Plan, schedule and track delivery of the ERP modules allotted by the Special Officer, across the five functional clusters (Governance; Finance; Revenue & Temple; Operations; Administration & Legal) of the Programme. Lead and coordinate the development squads, Team Leads and technical resources deployed in the PMU; conduct sprint planning, reviews and retrospectives; and monitor resource performance and attendance. Coordinate the requirement documentation chain (AS-IS, BRS, BRD, BDS, SRS, FRS) with the Technical Consultant (KITFRA), the Chief Software Architect and TDB domain experts, and ensure traceability from requirement to delivered functionality. Undertake field visits to temples and TB offices for requirement validation, process study, inspection, data collection, user acceptance testing, rollout and training. Maintain project plans, risk and issue registers, change-control records
