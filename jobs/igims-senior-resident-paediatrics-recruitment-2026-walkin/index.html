IGIMS Senior Resident (Paediatrics) Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Indira Gandhi Institute of Medical Sciences, Patna (IGIMS) is recruiting candidates for 6 Senior Resident vacancies through a walk-in interview scheduled for 4 November 2026, reporting at 8:30 AM at the Paediatrics Seminar room, 5th floor, Medical College Building, I.G.I.M.S., Patna-14 under advertisement number 03/PAEDI/2026. Applicants must possess an MD/DNB Paediatrics qualification and meet the age limit of 45 (Forty-Five) Years as on 3 November 2026. The appointment is on a contract basis for a period of 89 days on ad-hoc terms, with pay as per IGIMS rules along with admissible allowances

IGIMS Senior Resident Salary

The selected candidates receive emoluments as per the Senior Residency programme under 89 days ad-hoc terms. In addition to Pay, NPA and other allowances are admissible as per IGIMS rules.

The application fee must be paid in the form of a Demand Draft payable to The Director, IGIMS, Patna . The fee is non-refundable. Payment by cash, cheque, money orders, postal orders, banker's cheques, or postal stamps is not accepted.

What Happens on the Day

Candidates must report at the venue by 08:30 AM on 04/11/2026. Document verification will be conducted on the day of the interview. No reporting of candidates will be accepted after 10:00 AM. The interview commences at 9:30 AM on the same day. Selection is based on bio-data and the interview according to the institute's marking scheme.

IGIMS Senior Resident Important Instructions

The post is non-practicing. Private practice of any kind, direct or indirect, including laboratories, is strictly prohibited. Candidates employed in Government, Semi-Government, or other employers must submit a "No Objection Certificate" at the time of interview, or state in writing that they are not employed anywhere. Selected candidates must submit a legal bond to deposit an amount equivalent to one month's emoluments for every year of the Senior Residency programme if they quit or resign mid-way before completing the programme. Reservation benefits apply to Bihar state domicile candidates only. No TA/DA is admissible for attending the interview or joining the post. In case of unavailability of eligible female candidates, male candidates may be considered, and vice versa.
