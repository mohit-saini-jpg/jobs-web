About CSIR-CFTRI

CSIR-Central Food Technological Research Institute is in Mysuru, Karnataka. The notice describes it as a constituent National Laboratory of the Council of Scientific & Industrial Research (CSIR). This post is for the project "Development of Highly Efficient Oxygen Scavenger Sachets Using Natural Rubber Silica Composites for Enhanced Food Preservation" (project number GAP00667).

CSIR-CFTRI Project Associate – II Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Candidates are selected through a walk-in interview at CSIR-CFTRI, Opp. Railway Museum, Mysuru-570020. If a large number of candidates apply, a committee will shortlist them using one or more of these methods: The Director, CSIR-CFTRI, makes the final decision on who may appear for the interview.

Service Conditions

The post is purely temporary and on contract, at CSIR-CFTRI, Mysuru, Karnataka. The notice gives two terms for the engagement. Page 1 says it runs at first up to 30 November 2026, and can be extended up to November 2027 if funds are available and the work needs it, or until the project ends, whichever is earlier. Instruction 12 says it runs at first for one year from the date of joining, depending on funds. It may then be extended or cut short based on conduct, performance and the institute's needs, and extended further on the Review Committee's recommendation. The engagement cannot go beyond the project's term and ends automatically when the project ends. Being engaged as project staff gives no right to register for higher degrees, and no right to a permanent job at CSIR-CFTRI, CSIR or its labs and institutes. CSIR-CFTRI Project Associate – II Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many Project Associate-II posts does CSIR-CFTRI have?
A
There is 01 post, under project GAP00667 on oxygen scavenger sachets made with natural rubber silica composites.
Q2When and where is the walk-in interview?
A
It is on 5 November 2026 (Thursday) at CSIR-CFTRI, Opp. Railway Museum, Mysuru-570020. Reporting is from 09.30 AM to 10.30 AM.
Q3What is the stipend for Project Associate-II?
A
It is Rs. 35,000 plus HRA if you have qualified CSIR-UGC NET, GATE or a similar national test, and Rs. 28,000 plus HRA otherwise.
Q4What qualification do I need?
A
You need a first class in M. Sc. in Food Science and Technology / Food Science and Nutrition or equivalent from a recognized university, and 2 years' R&D experience in industrial or academic institutions or S&T organisations.
Q5What is the age limit, and is there relaxation?
A
The upper age limit is 35 years on the interview date. SC, ST, PwBD and women candidates get 05 years of relaxation, and OBC candidates get 03 years.
Q6I am doing a PhD under AcSIR. Can I attend?
A
Yes. Bring the No Objection Certificate in the Annexure-II format, signed and stamped by your supervisor or guide and by the Registrar or administrative authority.
Q7How long will the engagement last?
A
Page 1 of the notice says it runs at first up to 30 November 2026 and can be extended up to November 2027, depending on funds, or until the project ends, whichever is earlier. It never goes beyond the project's term.
Q8Will I be paid travel allowance for the interview?
A
No. The notice says no TA/DA will be given for attending the interview.
Q9Who cannot apply even if qualified?
A
Anyone who has already completed a total of 6 years as project staff in any CSIR lab or institute, under CSIR OM No. 5-1(342)/2017-PD dated 02.09.2024.
