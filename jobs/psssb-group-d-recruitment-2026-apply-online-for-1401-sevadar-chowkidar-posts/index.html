PSSSB Group-D Eligibility Criteria

Educational qualification for Group-D posts is governed by the Punjab State (Group-D) Service Rules, 1963, as amended: Matriculation standard, with Punjabi language as one of the subjects, from a recognized educational institution This educational qualification condition does not apply to: (i) blind persons; (ii) riot/terrorist-affected persons; (iii) persons appointed as Sweeper Separately, all candidates must pass a qualifying Punjabi Language test equivalent to Matriculation standard with a minimum of 50% marks - this is mandatory and qualifying in nature; failing to secure 50% in this test disqualifies the candidate from the final merit list regardless of their score in other papers

PSSSB Group-D Salary

Selected candidates will draw pay in the scale of ₹18,000–56,900 (Level-1, as per 7th Pay Commission). Initial pay and other allowances are governed by the Punjab Finance Department (Personnel-1 Branch) letter No. 7/204/2012-4FP1/66 dated 15.01.2015, and subsequent instructions issued from time to time.

PSSSB Group-D Age Limit

Age is reckoned as on 01 January 2026: Minimum age (all categories): 18 years Maximum age (General category): 37 years Maximum age for Punjab-resident SC and BC candidates: 42 years Maximum age for State/Central Government employees (with relaxation): 45 years - an NOC from their department is required at counseling Punjab-resident Ex-Servicemen: upper age limit as per the Punjab Recruitment of Ex-Servicemen Rules, 1982, as amended Punjab-resident Divyang (disabled) candidates: 47 years (10-year relaxation) Widows, divorcees, and certain other categories of married women: relaxed up to 40 years, per Government of Punjab letter No. 1/50/83-5PP(1368)/3454 dated 23.04.1984

PSSSB Group-D Selection Process

Selection is through an Objective-type (Multiple Choice Questions) written examination, in two parts: Part A: Matriculation-standard Punjabi language paper - qualifying in nature only; candidates must score a minimum of 50% to qualify Part B: Determines the merit list; each question carries 1 mark, with negative marking of 1/4 mark for each wrong answer The Common Merit List is prepared based solely on Part-B marks In case of a tie in Part-B marks, the candidate with the earlier date of birth (older in age) ranks higher; if age is also equal, the candidate with a higher percentage in the required educational qualification ranks higher Candidates are shortlisted from the merit list for document verification/counseling; the number called is decided by the Board Successful candidates at counseling are recommended to the respective department as per the Common Merit List and category-wise po
