ESIC Medical College Kolkata Eligibility Criteria

PostQualification Required
ProfessorMD/MS/DNB in the concerned subject with 8 years post PG experience (including 3 years as Associate Professor) and required research publications, Basic Course in Biomedical Research, and Basic Course in Medical Education.
Professor, Emergency MedicineMD/DNB (Emergency Medicine) OR MD/MS/DNB in General Medicine, Gen. Surgery, Resp. Medicine, Anaesthesia, Orthopaedics with 8 years post PG experience and required publications/training.
Professor, Transfusion MedicineM.D./DNB (Immuno / Haematology and Blood Transfusion) or M.D./DNB (Transfusion Medicine) M.D./DNB (Pathology) with 8 years post PG experience and required publications/training.
Associate ProfessorMD/MS/DNB in the concerned subject with 5 years post PG experience (including 4 years as Assistant Professor or 5 years for direct Assistant Professor in Govt. institutions) and required publications/training.
Associate Professor, Physical Medicine and RehabilitationM.D./DNB (Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation) or M.S./M.D./DNB (Medicine with Diploma in PMR) M.S./DNB (General Surgery) M.S./DNB (Orthopaedics) with required teaching/research experience.
Associate Professor, Emergency MedicineMD/DNB (Emergency Medicine) OR MD/MS/DNB in General Medicine, Gen. Surgery, Resp. Medicine, Anaesthesia, Orthopaedics with 5 years post PG experience and required research publications (must be amongst first three authors).
Assistant ProfessorMD/MS/DNB in the concerned subject with 1 year as Senior Resident in the concerned subject in a recognised medical institution, or equivalent Armed Forces/diploma/non-teaching qualifications.
Assistant Professor in Emergency MedicineMD/DNB (Emergency Medicine) OR MD/MS/DNB in General Medicine, Gen. Surgery, Resp. Medicine, Anaesthesia, Orthopaedics with 1 year Senior Resident experience or equivalent qualifications.
Assistant Professor, Physical Medicine and RehabilitationM.D./DNB (Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation) or M.S./M.D./DNB (Medicine with Diploma in PMR) M.S./DNB (General Surgery) M.S./DNB (Orthopaedics) with 1 year Senior Resident experience or equivalent.

ESIC Medical College Kolkata Age Limit

Age limit: Not exceeding 69 years on the date of Document verification & Interview.

ESIC Medical College Kolkata Salary

In addition to the consolidated remuneration, Teaching Faculty will be entitled to transport allowance with DA on transport allowance as per rules. No other allowances will be paid. Private practice is strictly prohibited during the tenure of service in ESIC.

ESIC Medical College Kolkata Teaching Faculty Recruitment : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

The selection will be made on the basis of performance of the candidate in an interview before the Selection Board.

Documents Required

One set of the prescribed application form filled and signed Two copies of recent self-attested color passport size photographs Proof of age (Date of Birth certificate) Category certificates (SC, ST, OBC, EWS) in updated Government of India Format Educational qualifications certificates (Matriculation, Higher Secondary, Graduate, Post Graduate, and any other) Registration certificate issued by State Medical Council/MCI MCI recognized teaching experience certificate issued by the Head of the Institute or Head of the Department Research Publications in Indexed Journals Pension Payment Order (PPO), in case of retired employees from Govt. organizations NOC for in-service candidates from employer Service bond release certificate, if applicable No objection certificate from present employer, in case of Government employees

ESIC Medical College Kolkata Important Instructions

Candidates should report between 09:30 AM and 10:30 AM for verification of documents at the Academic Block 2nd Floor of ESI-PGIMSR & ESIC Medical College, Joka. Candidates reporting beyond 10:30 AM for the interview shall not be considered. Mere submission of application does not confer any right to the candidate to be interviewed. Incomplete applications will be summarily rejected. No TA/DA will be admissible for joining the institution or medical test and for submission of other documents. No private practice is allowed during the tenure of service in ESIC. The Dean, ESI-PGIMSR, ESIC Medical College & Hospital, Joka reserves the right to vary vacancies, increase or decrease or cancel filling up of any or all posts without assigning any reason.
