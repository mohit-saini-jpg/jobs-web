BPSSC Company Commander Eligibility Criteria

This recruitment is not open to the general public. It is restricted to candidates already serving within the Bihar Home Guard organization who meet the following conditions. Must be an Indian citizen. Men, women, and third gender Home Guards may apply. Must be enrolled and trained in the Bihar Home Guard Vahini organization. Must have completed at least 5 years of service/duty in the organization. Must have at least 10 years of remaining service left as on the cut-off date considered for appointment (i.e., the application cut-off date). Must meet the physical and educational qualifications prescribed for the post. Candidates must have passed a graduate examination from a recognised university, or an equivalent examination recognised by the State Government, on or before 01-08-2026. BPSSC Company Commander Selection Process

Selection to the post of Company Commander will be made through a written competitive examination in two phases, followed by a Physical Efficiency Test, as detailed below. The Preliminary exam covers General Knowledge and Current Affairs. Candidates scoring less than 30% will be declared unsuccessful for the Main exam. Successful candidates will be shortlisted for the Main exam at 20 times the number of vacancies, category-wise, in merit order (this ratio may be reduced if sufficient qualified candidates are not available). A negative marking of 0.2 marks per wrong answer applies in both the Preliminary and Main examinations. The answer sheet will be prepared in two copies, one of which will be retained by the Commission. BPSSC Company Commander Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Applications must be submitted online only, in two parts, through the official BPSSC website.
  • 2Visit bpssc.bihar.gov.in and go to the "Home Guard" tab, then click the link for Advertisement No. 11/2026.
  • 3Complete Part 1 (Registration) with name, nationality, mobile number, email ID, Bihar domicile status, reservation category, gender, and date of birth, then make the online fee payment.
  • 4After successful registration and payment, a registration number and password will be sent via SMS and email - use these to log in and complete Part 2 of the form with father's/husband's name, mother's name, address, and educational qualification details.
  • 5Upload a recent colour photograph (taken within the last 2 months, white background, 15–25 KB, JPG/JPEG/GIF format) and a scanned signature (black or blue ink, on white background, 15–25 KB, JPG/JPEG/GIF format).
  • 6Review all entered details carefully using the "Edit Details" option before final submission - no edits are possible once the application is confirmed and submitted.
  • 7After final submission, download and print the Acknowledgement on A4 size paper and retain it for future reference and correspondence.
  • 8Note: Only one online application can be submitted per candidate and per mobile number. Submitting more than one application will lead to rejection of all applications by that candidate.
