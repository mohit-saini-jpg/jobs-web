SWD UP IAS/PCS Free Coaching Result 2026 has been declared by the Social Welfare Department (SWD), Uttar Pradesh, Lucknow, on 29 July 2026. This result is for candidates who appeared in the Joint Entrance Examination held on 05 July 2026 for admission to the Free Pre-Exam Coaching Institutes run by SWD UP for the Civil Services (IAS/PCS) Preliminary Examination 2026. Candidates can check and download their result, including individual marks, from the official SWD portal. This article covers everything you need to know - important dates, eligibility, seat details, how to check the result, and answers to common questions.

How to Check SWD UP IAS/PCS Free Coaching Result 2026

Follow these steps to check and download your result:

  • Visit the official Social Welfare Department, Uttar Pradesh website: socialwelfareup.upsdc.gov.in
  • Go to the "IAS/PCS Free Coaching Result 2026" or Latest Updates section on the homepage.
  • Click on the result link, or directly open the result PDF.
  • Search for your name using your Enrollment Number, Registration Number, or Date of Birth.
  • Check your marks and qualifying status against your roll number.
  • Download and save the result page/PDF for future reference.
  • Take a printout to keep for the admission/counselling process.

How to Apply for SWD UP IAS/PCS Free Coaching (Reference: 2026 Cycle)

For candidates checking the process followed for this year's admission cycle (useful for future batches):

  • Visit the official SWD UP website.
  • Click on the "IAS/PCS Free Coaching Online Form" link.
  • Register using basic details (name, mobile number, email).
  • Fill in the application form with personal, educational, and category details.
  • Upload scanned copies of required documents and photograph/signature.
  • Review the form carefully and submit it before the last date.
  • Save/print the confirmation page for future reference.

Important Instructions

  • Keep your Enrollment Number/Registration Number safe - it is required to check the result and download the admit card.
  • Cross-check all personal details (name, roll number, category, marks) shown in the result against your original documents.
  • In case of any discrepancy in the result, contact the SWD helpdesk or visit the official website for correction procedures.
  • Only candidates who meet the OBC/SC/ST, UP-domicile, and income-limit criteria are eligible; the result does not override eligibility verification at the admission stage.
  • Selected candidates should watch the official website regularly for coaching joining instructions (coaching is scheduled to start in August 2026).
