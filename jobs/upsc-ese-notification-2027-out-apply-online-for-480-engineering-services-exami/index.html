A candidate must have attained the age of 21 years and must not have attained the age of 30 years on 01.01.2027 - i.e., born not earlier than 02.01.1997 and not later than 01.01.2006. UPSC ESE 2027 Application Fee

Candidates (except Female/SC/ST/PwBD, who are exempted from fee payment) are required to pay a fee of ₹200 (Rupees Two Hundred only), payable through net banking of any bank, or by Visa/Master/RuPay Credit/Debit Card/UPI. Payment through any other mode is not valid or acceptable, and applications submitted without the prescribed fee (unless fee remission is claimed) will be summarily rejected. Fee once paid will not be refunded under any circumstances, nor can it be adjusted against any other examination. No fee exemption is available to OBC/EWS candidates - they must pay the fee in full.

UPSC ESE 2027 Exam Pattern

ESE 2027 is conducted in three stages: Stage-I (Preliminary Examination, objective type), Stage-II (Main Examination, conventional type), and Stage-III (Personality Test/Interview). Only candidates who qualify the Preliminary/Stage-I Examination are eligible for admission to the Main/Stage-II Examination. Marks obtained in Stage-I by qualified candidates count towards the final order of merit. Approximately 6 to 7 times the number of vacancies are admitted to the Main Examination. Conventional papers must be answered in English; question papers are set in English only. Carries 200 marks, with no minimum qualifying marks. Candidates summoned are about twice the number of vacancies to be filled. UPSC ESE Notification 2027 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Visit the UPSC Online Application Portal, https://upsconline.nic.in.
  • 2Complete Account Creation, Universal Registration (to generate a Universal Registration Number, URN - needed only once in a lifetime), and the Common Application Form (CAF) - these are common to all UPSC examinations and can be filled anytime.
  • 3Fill the fourth, examination-specific module (including fee and exam centre selection) during the notified application window for ESE 2027.
  • 4Upload your photograph and also capture a live photograph as prompted while filling the CAF.
  • 5Sign three times (one below the other) on plain white paper using black ink, and upload the scanned signature.
  • 6Upload the required scanned documents: Proof of Date of Birth (Matriculation/10th certificate), Certificate of Educational Qualification, and Certificate in support of age/fee concession claims (wherever applicable).
  • 7Pay the applicable fee online (if not exempted) via net banking or Visa/Master/RuPay Credit/Debit Card/UPI.
  • 8Review all details carefully - no correction/alteration is allowed after submission, except through the specific one-time URN edit facility described in the notification.
  • 9Submit the application before the closing date, 06.10.2026.
  • 10Applicants are strongly advised to use their Aadhaar Card as the ID document for smoother verification and authentication.

UPSC ESE Notification 2027 : Important Instructions

Candidates should retain their Application Number, Roll Number (once received), and other registration details for all future correspondence with the Commission. If any irrelevant matter, signage, or marks that could disclose a candidate's identity are found on the answer script, the Commission will impose a penalty of deduction of marks, or may not evaluate that script. Candidates should read the detailed Rules of ESE 2027 (notified by the Ministry of Communications, Department of Telecommunications) and the full Examination Notice carefully before applying, including the Functional Classification and Physical Requirements for PwBD categories set out in Annexure-I of the Rules. The Commission does not enter into correspondence with candidates regarding reasons for non-selection or non-shortlisting. Canvassing in any form will lead to disqualification of the candidate.

UPSC ESE Notification 2027 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is UPSC ESE 2027?
A
UPSC ESE 2027 (Engineering Services Examination, 2027) is a national-level examination conducted by the Union Public Service Commission, under Examination Notice No. 02/2027-ENGG., for recruitment to various Group 'A' and Group 'B' engineering services and posts in the Government of India.
Q2How many vacancies are there in UPSC ESE 2027?
A
The expected number of vacancies is approximately 480, including 19 reserved for Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD), though this number is liable to change.
Q3What is the last date to apply for UPSC ESE 2027?
A
The last date for online application submission is 06.10.2026.
Q4What is the qualification required for UPSC ESE 2027?
A
Candidates need a degree in Engineering from a recognised university/institution, or equivalent qualifications such as passing Sections A & B of the Institution of Engineers (India) examinations, subject to specific eligibility notes regarding admission dates post 31.05.2013.
Q5What is the age limit for UPSC ESE 2027?
A
Candidates must be between 21 and 30 years of age as on 01.01.2027, with relaxation of up to 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC, and up to 10 years for PwBD candidates.
Q6What is the application fee for UPSC ESE 2027?
A
The fee is ₹200, with Female, SC, ST, and PwBD candidates exempted from payment.
Q7What is the exam pattern for UPSC ESE 2027?
A
ESE 2027 has three stages: a 500-mark objective Preliminary Examination (Stage-I), a 600-mark conventional Main Examination (Stage-II), and a 200-mark Personality Test/Interview (Stage-III), with 1/3rd negative marking in objective papers.
Q8When is the UPSC ESE 2027 Preliminary Examination?
A
The Preliminary (Stage-I) Examination is scheduled for 31.01.2027, and the Main (Stage-II) Examination for 18.06.2027.
Q9How can I apply online for UPSC ESE 2027?
A
Candidates must register on https://upsconline.nic.in, complete the Universal Registration and Common Application Form, fill the examination-specific module, upload required documents, pay the fee (if applicable), and submit before 06.10.2026.
Q10Which engineering disciplines are covered under UPSC ESE 2027?
A
Four disciplines are covered: Civil Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, and Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering.
