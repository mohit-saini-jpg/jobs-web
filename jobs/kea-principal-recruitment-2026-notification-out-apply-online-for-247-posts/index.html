KEA Principal Eligibility Criteria

KEA Principal Recruitment 2026 is not open to the general public or fresh candidates . Only Lecturers currently working in Government Pre-University Colleges under the School Education Department are eligible, subject to the qualification and service conditions below.

KEA Principal Qualification

The applicant must be a serving Lecturer in the Lecturer cadre of Government Pre-University Colleges under the School Education Department. The applicant must have completed a minimum of 10 years of service in the Lecturer cadre. The applicant must have satisfactorily completed the probation period in the Lecturer post. The notification specifies that if an applicant participates in the recruitment process without holding the required educational/service qualification, their candidature may be rejected at any stage by the Government, the Department, or the Authority. The prescribed qualification must be held as on the last date fixed for submission of applications.

KEA Principal Age Limit

No age limit is prescribed for KEA Principal Recruitment 2026. The notification states that since this recruitment fills vacancies in the Principal post from among serving Lecturers in Government Pre-University Colleges who have completed 10 years of service under the School Education Department, no upper age limit applies.

KEA Principal Salary

Selected candidates will be placed in the revised 2024 Karnataka state pay scale of Rs. 90,200 – Rs. 1,59,200 (90200-2300-99400-2700-115600-3100-134200-3500-155200-4000-159200). Pension benefits for officers appointed as Principal will continue to be governed by the pension rules applicable to their original appointment as Lecturer.

KEA Principal Selection Process

Selection is through a competitive examination conducted by KEA, either in Online (CBT) mode or Offline (OMR) mode. The exam consists of three papers. A minimum of 50 marks in Paper 1 (Kannada) is required to qualify, but Paper 1 marks are not counted while preparing the merit list – qualifying nature only. Only marks scored in Papers 2 and 3 are used to prepare the final merit list. Negative marking of 0.25 marks applies for each wrong answer in Papers 2 and 3 (bilingual Kannada/English question papers). Shortlisting for document verification is done at 1:2 ratio against vacancies, category-wise, based on Paper 2 & 3 scores.

Q1What is KEA Principal Recruitment 2026?
A
It is a recruitment drive by the Karnataka Examinations Authority, on behalf of the School Education Department, to fill 247 Principal posts in Government Pre-University Colleges across Karnataka through a competitive examination for serving Lecturers.
Q2How many vacancies are available in KEA Principal Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 247 vacancies in total – 31 under the Kalyana-Karnataka (KK) Local Cadre and 216 under the Residual Parent Cadre (RPC).
Q3Who is eligible to apply for KEA Principal posts?
A
Only serving Lecturers in Government Pre-University Colleges under the School Education Department, with a minimum of 10 years' service and satisfactory completion of probation, are eligible. It is not open to fresh candidates or the general public.
Q4What is the last date to apply for KEA Principal Recruitment 2026?
