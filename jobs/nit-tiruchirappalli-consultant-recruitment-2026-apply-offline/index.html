NIT Tiruchirappalli Consultant Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

National Institute of Technology, Tiruchirappalli – 620 015 has invited applications for the temporary position of Consultant (Internal Audit). Eligible and interested candidates must send their filled applications along with self-attested photocopies of certificates to The Registrar, National Institute of Technology, Tiruchirappalli – 620015 by Speed / Registered post on or before 18.11.2026 till 05.00 PM, mentioning on the cover APPLICATION FOR THE POST OF CONSULTANT (INTERNAL AUDIT). About NIT Tiruchirappalli Consultant (Internal Audit)

National Institute of Technology, Tiruchirappalli operates under the Ministry of Education, Government of India, located in Tiruchirappalli, Tamil Nadu. The institute has announced a temporary recruitment drive to engage a Consultant (Internal Audit) to manage pre-audit functions, establishment-related financial scrutiny, department audits, and coordination with C&AG audit teams. The initial tenure of the contract appointment is six months and the tenure of the appointment may be extended subject to satisfactory performance of the candidate and necessity for the continued retention for not more than three years.

NIT Tiruchirappalli Consultant (Internal Audit) Job Description

The selected candidate will be assigned the following duties and responsibilities: Pre-audit of the payment vouchers and other F&A related vouchers pertaining to the Institute, including the scrutiny of Statutory remittances by contractors. Pre-Audit of establishment-related matters involving financial matters, viz, Scrutiny of Pay Fixations and retirement benefits, etc. Audit of Accounts of Depts, Pre-check/Vetting of the purchase proposals, Scrutiny of works/estimates/agreements/MoUs/MoAs/documents/reports, etc., Conducting annual Internal Audit of the departments to ensure compliance with all Government rules Coordinate with C&AG on Certificate audit/transaction audit and other CAG audits, and assist in replies to CAG paras, and audit observation settlements. Any other assignment given by higher authorities, depending upon administrative requirements

Enclosures

Candidates must attach self-attested photocopies of the following documents along with the application form: 10th/Matriculation/SSLC Mark sheet +2/HSC Mark sheet Consolidate Mark sheet of Degree Degree certificate Category Certificate Experience Certificates ID proof (Aadhaar card/Driving license/passport/PAN card or any Government issued ID with address) Copy of Pension Payment Order (PPO)
