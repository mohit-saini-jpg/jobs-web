Notification Details

  • Notification No. 284 (Corrected): Applies to B.A. Sem-4th students appearing under the Regular (Re-appear) category, for the May-2025 syllabus. This is a one paper re-exam, and the notification has been marked as "Corrected," meaning it supersedes any earlier version of the same notice.
  • Notification No. 283: Applies to B.A. Sem-3 (Regular) students under the December-2024 syllabus, for a one paper special exam.
  • Notification No. 285: Applies to MBA Financial Markets & Applied, Sem-5 students, and concerns a preponement exam (an exam rescheduled to an earlier date than originally planned).

Required Details

As with most university exams, students are generally expected to carry:

  • Valid roll number slip / admit card
  • College or university identity card
  • Any additional document specified in the individual notification

How to Check the Datesheet

  • Visit the official Punjabi University Patiala datesheet portal at the link given below.
  • Look for the notification matching your course, semester, and category (e.g., "B.A. Sem-4th... Notification No. 284 Corrected").
  • Click on the entry to open or download the notification PDF.
  • Check the exact exam date, time, and any instructions listed inside the PDF.

Important Instructions

  • Always refer to the most recently corrected or revised version of a notification - for example, Notification No. 284 is explicitly marked "Corrected," so it should be treated as the final version over any earlier release.
  • A "preponement exam" (as in Notification No. 285) means the exam date has been moved earlier than originally scheduled - students should check this carefully to avoid missing the exam.
  • A "special exam" or "re-exam" notification is usually meant for a specific category of students (such as those with a clash, reappear case, or one-paper pending) and may not apply to all students of that semester.
  • For any detail not covered in this article - such as exact dates, timings, or exam centers - check the official notification PDF directly, as that information was not available in the source used for this summary.
