IOCL Apprentice Recruitment 2026 Stipend

The stipend payable to apprentices is governed by the Apprentices Act, 1961, the Apprentices Rules, 1992, and applicable amendments/guidelines. In addition to the statutory stipend, IOCL (MD), NR will pay ₹2,500 per month towards miscellaneous expenses. For Graduate and Technician Apprentices, a portion of the stipend is disbursed directly via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into the apprentice's Aadhaar-seeded bank account by the Board of Apprenticeship Training (BOAT). Trade Apprentices (NAPS apprentices) receive their full stipend directly from IOCL (MD), NR. These arrangements are subject to change as per government/BOAT/NAPS guidelines issued from time to time. A specific fixed monthly stipend amount is not stated in the official notification.

IOCL Apprentice Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Online Application: Candidates registered on the NAPS/NATS portal and applying against the notification are considered for selection. There is no written test or interview. Merit List: Prepared based on the percentage of marks secured in the prescribed qualification for the respective post. Ties are resolved first by earlier date of birth, and then by higher marks in matriculation if the date of birth is also the same. Preference: Given to candidates applying for apprenticeship training for the first time. Document Verification: Shortlisted candidates are called for document verification. Pre-Engagement Medical Fitness Test: Candidates must be adjudged medically fit as per IOCL's pre-engagement medical standards.
