The notification (No. B-9/XI/Rect of Dvrs/DP/Dlgs/2025, dated 01.08.2026) seeks to fill Staff Car Driver (Ordinary Grade) posts in General Central Services, Group C, Non-Gazetted, Non-Ministerial category, in Pay Level-2 of the 7th CPC pay matrix, at the Mail Motor Service, Bengaluru. India Post Staff Car Driver Eligibility Criteria

Eligibility for this India Post Staff Car Driver Recruitment 2026 is limited to serving government employees, considered in the following order of preference. Regular Dispatch Riders (Group C) or MTS (Group C) employees within the Department of Posts, in Pay Matrix Level-1 (₹18,000–₹56,900, 7th CPC). Must hold a valid driving licence for Light and Heavy Motor Vehicles, and competence will be assessed through a driving test. Officials holding the post of Dispatch Rider on a regular basis, or regular Group C employees in Pay Matrix Level-1 (₹18,000–₹56,900) in other Ministries, who meet the following: Possession of a valid driving licence for Light and Heavy Motor Vehicles Knowledge of motor mechanism (able to remove minor defects in vehicles) At least three years of experience driving Light and Heavy Motor Vehicles Pass in the 10th standard from a recognised Board or institute Desirable q

India Post Staff Car Driver Age Limit

The maximum age limit for appointment by deputation/absorption is 56 years as on the closing date for receipt of applications. The application proforma (Annexure-I) additionally asks candidates to compute their age as on 30.09.2026.

India Post Staff Car Driver Selection Process

Selection is based on a driving test and trade test, conducted as per the pattern and syllabus prescribed vide Directorate letter No. F.No.08-01/2019-SPN-I dated 17.06.2022, to assess the candidate's competence in driving Light and Heavy Motor Vehicles. The final allotment of division (Bengaluru or Mysuru) will be based on the merit list, at the discretion of the competent authority.

India Post Staff Car Driver Required Documents

Applications must be submitted in duplicate, in the prescribed proforma (Annexure-I), together with a certificate from the forwarding authority (Annexure-II) and the following documents: Integrity Certificate List of major/minor penalties imposed during the last 10 years (or a "NIL" certificate if none) Vigilance Clearance Certificate Attested photocopies of APAR for the last five years (2020-21 to 2024-25), attested on each page by a Gazetted Officer wherever applicable Experience certificate for driving LMV and HMV vehicles for three years, attested by a Gazetted Officer Valid driving licence for LMV and HMV, attested by a Gazetted Officer 10th standard certificate, attested by a Gazetted Officer Caste certificate, if applicable (for SC/ST candidates, to be verified at a later stage)

India Post Staff Car Driver Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Download the application proforma (Annexure-I) and the forwarding authority certificate format (Annexure-II) from www.indiapost.gov.in.
  • 2Fill the application in duplicate, attaching self-attested copies of age proof, educational qualification, driving experience certificate, driving licence, appointment letter, and caste certificate (if applicable).
  • 3Have the forwarding authority complete and sign Annexure-II, along with the Integrity Certificate, penalty list/NIL certificate, Vigilance Clearance Certificate, and attested APAR copies for the last five years.
  • 4Send the complete application, through proper channel only, to: The Manager, Mail Motor Service, Bengaluru-560001.
  • 5Applications must be sent by Speed Post only, within 60 days from the date of publication of the notification in Employment News.
