About NUITL

NICDC Uttarakhand Industrial Township Limited (NUITL) has been incorporated as a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) for the establishment of the Integrated Manufacturing Cluster (IMC) in Khurpia, Uttarakhand, under the Amritsar Kolkata Industrial Corridor (AKIC) Project of the National Industrial Corridor Development Programme (NICDP). It is a Joint Venture between the Government of Uttarakhand, represented through the State Infrastructure and Industrial Development Corporation of Uttarakhand Limited (SIIDCUL), and the National Industrial Corridor Development and Implementation Trust (NICDIT), a Government of India Trust under the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Ministry of Commerce and Industry. Its registered office is at 29, IIE IT Park, SIIDCUL Building, Sahastradhara Road, Dehradun, Uttarakhand (Tel: 0135-2708100).

Pay and Tenure

CFO: CTC up to Rs. 25,00,000/- per annum; CS: CTC up to Rs. 16,00,000/- per annum. For both posts, the notice states that the salary, based upon the suitability of the candidate, is negotiable. After selection, the candidate will be fixed on minimum remuneration (where a range is given) as prescribed and will be entitled to a yearly increment. The selected candidate will be on probation for one (01) year, which may be extended for an additional one (01) year based on performance. The appointment is contractual and will not confer any lien or right to regularisation, or any other benefit allowed to regular employees. The qualified incumbents may be taken under the payroll of NICDC Uttarakhand Industrial Township Limited; joining will be on an immediate basis after selection. Appointment is subject to the Service and Conduct Rules of the Company as may be framed from time to time.

Selection and Interview

Selection is through a personal interview of shortlisted candidates, held at Dehradun only. Meeting the requirements does not give a right to be called for interview. The interview date will be informed through e-mail/phone message. Candidates must produce original documents of educational qualification, experience and caste certification, with self-attested photocopies; a candidate whose identity is in doubt, who cannot produce the documents, or whose claims are found incorrect will not be allowed to appear.
