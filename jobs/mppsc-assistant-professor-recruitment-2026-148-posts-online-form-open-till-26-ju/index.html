MPPSC Assistant Professor Recruitment Expert Analysis

This recruitment notification is a great opportunity for candidates who want to join the Madhya Pradesh government as Assistant Professors. The selection process will be based on a written exam and interview.

Who Should Apply MPPSC Assistant Professor Recruitment

Ideal candidates are postgraduates in relevant subjects with a good academic record and teaching experience. They should also be proficient in Hindi and English.

MPPSC Assistant Professor Recruitment Preparation Tips

Start preparing for the written exam by studying the syllabus and previous year's papers. Focus on improving your teaching skills and knowledge of the subject. Practice mock interviews to boost your confidence.

MPPSC Assistant Professor Recruitment Salary Insights

The salary for Assistant Professors in Madhya Pradesh government is ₹21,700-₹69,100. This is a good salary compared to other government jobs. With experience, you can also get promotions and increments.

MPPSC Assistant Professor Recruitment Job Profile

As an Assistant Professor, you will be responsible for teaching and guiding students. You will also be involved in research and academic activities. With experience, you can move to senior positions like Head of Department or Dean.

MPPSC Assistant Professor Recruitment Selection Strategy

To get selected, focus on improving your teaching skills and knowledge of the subject. Practice mock interviews and be prepared to answer questions on your academic record and teaching experience.

MPPSC Assistant Professor Recruitment 2026 – 148 Posts : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many total vacancies are there in MPPSC Assistant Professor Recruitment 2026?
A
MPPSC has announced a total of 148 vacancies for Assistant Professor posts across six subjects: Sanskrit (34), Geography (74), Psychology (10), Law (17), Geology (10), and Yogic Science (3).
Q2What is the last date to apply for MPPSC Assistant Professor 2026?
A
The last date to submit the online application for MPPSC Assistant Professor 2026 is 26 June 2026 at 11:59 PM.
Q3When does the online application window open for MPPSC Assistant Professor 2026?
A
The online application starts from 25 May 2026, as revised through the official corrigendum dated 07 May 2026. The original start date of 10 May 2026 was changed.
Q4What is the application fee for MPPSC Assistant Professor?
A
General and other state candidates must pay ₹500/-. SC, ST, OBC, and PwD (Divyang) candidates from Madhya Pradesh pay ₹250/-. An additional MP Online portal charge of ₹40/- is applicable for all.
Q5What is the age limit for MPPSC Assistant Professor?
A
Candidates must be between 21 and 40 years of age as on 01 January 2026. Age relaxation is available as per MP government rules for reserved categories.
Q6What qualifications are required for MPPSC Assistant Professor?
A
Candidates must have a Postgraduate degree in the relevant subject plus either a Ph.D., NET, or SLET/SET qualification. This is in line with UGC eligibility norms for college-level teaching posts.
Q7What is the salary for MPPSC Assistant Professor posts?
A
The pay scale is Level-10 with a basic pay of ₹57,700/- per month, along with applicable state government allowances.
Q8What is the selection process for MPPSC Assistant Professor?
A
Selection is based on four stages: Written Examination, Interview, Medical Examination, and Document Verification.
Q9What is the exam date for MPPSC Assistant Professor?
A
The written examination is scheduled for 12 July 2026, with admit cards expected to be released on 02 July 2026.
Q10Why was a corrigendum issued for MPPSC Assistant Professor?
A
MPPSC issued a corrigendum on 07 May 2026 to revise the online application start date from 10 May 2026 to 25 May 2026. It also made it mandatory for guest faculty (Atithi Vidwan) working against sanctioned posts in MP government colleges to upload their experience certificate and calculation sheet along with the application.
