How to Download CA Inter September 2026 Result?

Candidates can check and download their CA Intermediate September 2026 examination results by following these steps.

  • Visit the official ICAI examination results website at caresults.icai.org.
  • Click the Intermediate Examination result link for September 2026.
  • Enter the six-digit examination roll number.
  • Enter the ICAI registration number.
  • Complete the CAPTCHA verification displayed on the page.
  • Submit the information to view the examination result.
  • Check the individual paper marks, group totals and pass or fail status.
  • Download or print the result for future reference.CA Inter September 2026 Pass Percentage

CA Inter September 2026 Group 2 Result

According to the result figures, 68,400 candidates appeared for Group II and 12,938 candidates passed.

CA Inter September 2026 Both Groups Result

The published statistics show that 38,339 candidates appeared for both groups of CA Intermediate in September 2026.

Of these candidates, 5,066 passed both groups, corresponding to a pass percentage of 13.21%.

Candidates should check their scorecards to confirm whether they passed Group I, Group II or both groups under the applicable examination regulations.

How to Check CA Inter September 2026 Merit List?

ICAI provides a dedicated Intermediate Examination merit-list login for September 2026.

  • Open the official ICAI result portal.
  • Select the Intermediate Examination Merit List for September 2026.
  • Enter the six-digit examination roll number.
  • Complete the CAPTCHA verification.
  • Submit the details to check the available merit-list information.
  • Save or print the displayed information for reference.

Unlike the individual scorecard facility, the official merit-list login asks for the six-digit roll number and CAPTCHA.

CA Inter September 2026 Result: What to Do After Passing?

Candidates who have cleared the required CA Intermediate groups can check their eligibility for the next stage of the Chartered Accountancy course.

Students should review ICAI's current registration, practical training and CA Final eligibility requirements before proceeding.

Candidates who have passed one group but not the other should check the relevant pending group requirements and upcoming examination registration schedule.

All candidates should retain their scorecards and check subsequent ICAI announcements concerning official statements of marks, certificates and result-related services.

CA Inter September 2026 Marks Verification and Certified Copies

ICAI provides procedures for candidates who wish to seek verification of their examination marks or obtain certified copies of evaluated answer books.

The application process, fee and submission deadlines are governed by the relevant ICAI examination instructions.

A specific September 2026 verification deadline and fee were not independently confirmed in the sources reviewed for this article.

Candidates should therefore consult official ICAI announcements and submit requests only through authorised facilities.

CA Inter September 2026 Result Not Opening: What to Do?

Candidates facing difficulty accessing the result website should check the following points.

  • Open the official ICAI results page directly.
  • Choose the Intermediate Examination result for September 2026.
  • Check that the roll number contains six digits.
  • Enter the correct ICAI registration number.
  • Refresh the CAPTCHA and enter it carefully.
  • Retry through the official portal if the website is temporarily unavailable.
  • Save the result once it loads successfully.

Students should avoid sharing their registration credentials or personal result information with unofficial websites.
