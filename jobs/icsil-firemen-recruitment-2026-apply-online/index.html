ICSIL Firemen Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

ICSIL Firemen Recruitment 2026: Intelligent Communication Systems India Ltd. (ICSIL) has announced 3 Firemen vacancies for Dr. D.P. Rastogi Central Research Institute of Homoeopathy (CRIH), Noida, on a contractual outsourced basis. Candidates who have passed Class 12 and have one year of relevant experience can apply online from 22 October 2026 to 25 October 2026 (6:00 PM). Selected candidates will receive a monthly salary of Rs. 22,411. Applicants should check the eligibility criteria, age limit, application fee and selection process before applying.

Who Can Apply

PostEssential Qualification Experience
Firemen1. Should be 12th pass from the recognized board.2. Should have working knowledge of fire safety. Experience: One year experience of fireman in any reputed organization.

Pay and Tenure

Selected candidates will receive wages of Rs. 22,411/- per month (As per minimum wages notified by Labour Department, GNCT of Delhi). Fifteen (15) days salary to be deducted from the selected candidate as security in two parts i.e. 50% in first month and 50% in second month. This shall be refunded to the candidate with saving bank interest as applicable after comply with following: No Dues certificate is received from the client department, and after completing contractual liabilities with ICSIL as per appointment letter. The engagement is purely on a contractual/outsourced basis for a fixed period or till expiry of contract or till regular incumbents join posts.

Selection and Interview

In case number of eligible applicants is disproportionally exceed number of positions, then shortlisting of applicants for interview may be on made on the basis of any criteria as deemed fit and found to be rational. The short listing of candidates for the panel will be based on scrutiny of documents of their age, qualification, experience, etc. and as found eligible as per prescribed criteria and subsequent interaction/interview of the candidate with the department. Candidates must produce original documents in respect of DOB, Qualification and Experience as per eligibility criteria on the date of document verification and interview/interaction; otherwise they will not be allowed to appear in the interview/interaction. Candidates must bring 1 set of photocopies of all relevant documents and 2 photographs.
