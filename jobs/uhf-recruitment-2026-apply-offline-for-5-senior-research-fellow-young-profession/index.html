UHF Nauni Senior Research Fellow and Young Professional Eligibility 2026

Age limit: You must be at least 18 and no more than 45 years old. Both limits are counted on the last date for receipt of applications, 16 November 2026. You must also be a citizen of India. Qualifications and experience are checked as on the same date. Essential: Master degree in Entomology/Nematology Desirable: at least one year of experience working with analytical instruments used for pesticide residue analysis Desirable: "Minimum one year paper published in the area of pesticide residue" (as printed in the notice) Desirable: Acquaintance with ISO/IEC 17025;2017 Essential: M.Sc. in Fruit Science Desirable: Working experience in research projects, especially on temperate fruits Essential: M.Sc. in Floriculture and Landscaping/ Floriculture and Landscape Architecture Desirable: Experience in handling projects, and knowledge of handling the Alstroemeria crop and maintaining its germplas

UHF Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection is by interview and evaluation of documents. The interview is held in the office chamber of the Director of Research, Directorate of Research, UHF, Nauni (Solan). No interview letter is sent. Candidates attend on the date at their own expense and must bring all their original testimonials. The notice also says the concerned Dean, Head of Department or Principal Investigator may attend. Excuses such as a breakdown, mail problems or illness are not accepted for missing the interview. The University may choose not to fill any or all of the positions, without giving a reason.

Service Conditions

The positions are purely temporary and co-terminus with the project. They depend on the funding agency providing the grant and approval. Salary is fixed. The engagement ends when the project ends or when funds run out, whichever is earlier. The selected candidate must sign a service contract agreement on judicial stamp paper at joining. The contract is renewed each year based on performance and continued funding. The positions are non-sanctioned and exist only for the short-term project. They give no claim to regularisation, absorption or appointment to a regular post in the University.

UHF Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1Can I apply online for these UHF Nauni posts?
A
No. Download the form from www.uhf.ac.in and send it with an IPO or bank draft to the Directorate of Research, Nauni (Solan). It must arrive by 05:00 PM on 16 November 2026.
Q2Which post pays the most?
A
The SRF in the pesticide residue project (HGI-118-07) pays Rs. 42,000 plus fixed HRA in its 3rd year. The two Young Professional-II posts pay a fixed Rs. 42,000.
Q3Is there a post for B.Sc. graduates?
A
Yes. The Young Professional-I post in the white sandalwood project at Neri (Hamirpur) needs a B.Sc. in Horticulture and pays Rs. 30,000 a month (fixed).
Q4Is NET required for any post?
A
Yes, for the Young Professional-II post at the DUS Center for Dahlia, Dhaulakuan. It needs an M.Sc. in Floriculture and NET in Floriculture. A Ph.D. in Floriculture gets preference.
Q5To whom is the bank draft made payable?
A
Make it payable to the Comptroller, Dr. Y.S. Parmar University of Horticulture & Forestry, Nauni (Solan). Draw it on State Bank of India/UCO Bank Nauni (Solan)-173230.
Q6Can I apply for more than one position?
A
Yes, but send a separate application form and processing fee for each position.
Q7Where is the interview held?
A
In the office chamber of the Director of Research, Directorate of Research, UHF, Nauni (Solan), from 11:00 AM on 20 November 2026. Bring all your original testimonials.
Q8How much age relaxation do reserved categories get?
A
SC, ST, OBC and Persons with Disabilities get up to 5 more years on the upper limit of 45 years, as per H.P. Government rules.
Q9Will these posts become permanent?
A
No. They are temporary and co-terminus with the project. The contract is renewed yearly on performance and funding, with no claim to a regular University post.
