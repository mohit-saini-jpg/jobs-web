Kochi Metro Rail Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Candidates applying for Kochi Metro Rail Apprenticeship Recruitment 2026 must have completed the relevant qualification through a full-time regular course from a government-recognized institution. Educational qualifications obtained through part-time, correspondence, distance education or online modes are not accepted. Applicants must have completed a full-time regular four-year B.Tech or B.E. degree in one of the engineering disciplines notified by KMRL. Applicants must possess a full-time regular three-year degree in BBA, B.Com or BCA from a recognized institution. Candidates must possess a full-time regular three-year engineering diploma in the relevant notified discipline. Applicants must have completed a full-time regular ITI course in the relevant advertised trade. Candidates must have passed the qualifying examination within five years from the application closing date of 10 Novem

Kochi Metro Rail Apprentice Stipend 2026

The monthly stipend depends on the apprentice's qualification category. Graduate apprentices receive Rs. 12,300, diploma apprentices Rs. 10,900 and ITI trade apprentices Rs. 9,600. An additional Rs. 1,200 per month is payable as food/messing allowance. The notification states that Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) under the NATS scheme will provide Rs. 6,150 for graduate apprentices and Rs. 5,450 for diploma apprentices. The remaining applicable stipend will be paid through KMRL's arrangements, as prescribed. The DBT amounts form part of the notified monthly stipends and are not additional payments.

Kochi Metro Rail Apprentice Selection Process 2026

The KMRL Apprenticeship Recruitment 2026 selection process does not include a written examination or interview. Candidates will be shortlisted from the applications received and called for verification of original documents and certificates. Application Screening: KMRL will review applications for completeness and eligibility. Shortlisting: Eligible candidates will be shortlisted for document verification. Certificate Verification: Candidates must present their original qualification, identity and other prescribed documents. Medical Fitness and Police Verification: Selected candidates must satisfy the applicable requirements before engagement. Apprenticeship Contract: Engagement will require acceptance of the tripartite apprenticeship contract through the NATS/NAPS system. Incomplete or ineligible applications will be rejected without individual intimation.

Kochi Metro Rail Apprentice Bio-Data Form 2026

The official recruitment notification contains a two-page Apprentice Bio-Data Form. Candidates must complete the form in CAPITAL LETTERS and mark the relevant apprenticeship trade or category. The form requests: NAPS/NATS Enrolment ID. Apprenticeship category and selected trade or discipline. Applicant's full name and father's name. Marital status and date of birth. Nationality, religion and category. Whether the candidate's father or mother is employed by KMRL. Permanent and mailing postal addresses. Phone number and email address. Educational qualifications, institution, branch and duration. Blood group. Extracurricular activities, if any. Candidate's declaration, date, place and signature. The applicant should complete only the candidate-related sections. Fields marked for office use are to be completed by KMRL.
