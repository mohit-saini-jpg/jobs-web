AEES Teaching Recruitment 2026 Application Fee

The application fee is Rs. 750 plus Rs. 250 processing fee for non-exempt candidates. Women, SC, ST, PwBD and Ex-Servicemen applicants are exempted from the application fee but must pay the processing fee. Note: Applicable GST charged by the bank is additional. Payment can be made online using Debit Card, Credit Card or Net Banking. Fees paid through other modes are not accepted, and application fees once remitted are non-refundable. Candidates applying for multiple permitted posts must pay the requisite fee separately for each post.

AEES Teaching Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection includes a Computer Based Test (CBT) followed by an Interview for Principal and Vice-Principal or a qualifying Skill Test for other posts. Medical fitness is also required before appointment. Computer Based Test (CBT). Interview for Principal and Vice-Principal, or Skill Test for teaching and Librarian posts. Verification of educational qualifications, experience and applicable certificates. Medical fitness examination before appointment. Final selection for Principal and Vice-Principal is based on 50% CBT marks and 50% Interview marks , subject to meeting the prescribed qualifying standards separately in both stages. These candidates must qualify in the CBT and then undergo a teaching skill test conducted on a Go / No Go basis . AEES Teaching Recruitment 2026 Important Instructions

The recruitment is for 254 tentative vacancies under Notification 01/2026. Applications are accepted online through the dedicated AEES portal only. The online application window is 2 November to 22 November 2026. Post-specific qualifications and CTET requirements must be satisfied by the prescribed eligibility cut-off. Applicants can select only one subject within the PGT category and only one subject within the TGT category. Separate fees apply when applying for different permitted posts. CBT carries no negative marking. Principal and Vice-Principal selection uses CBT and Interview marks with 50:50 weightage. Other notified posts require a qualifying Go / No Go Skill Test after CBT. Admit cards and call letters must be downloaded from the candidate login. Applicants may be posted anywhere in India. All appointment offers are subject to document verification and medical fitness.
