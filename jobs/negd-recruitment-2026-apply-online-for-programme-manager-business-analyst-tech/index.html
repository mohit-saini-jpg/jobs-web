NeGD Eligibility Criteria

Programme Manager: Minimum 15 years of experience after qualification, including at least 7 years managing large IT programmes with multiple vendors. Running a programme of 25 or more people across development, operations and helpdesk. Taking over a live system from an incumbent vendor, or handing one over, without service disruption. Managing an application that carries financial transactions or benefit payments at national scale. Desirable: Programme management on a Government of India platform, and familiarity with GFR 2017, GeM and government procurement; work in agriculture, insurance, or a Direct Benefit Transfer scheme. Business Analyst: Minimum 6 years of experience after qualification, including at least 4 years as a business analyst on web or mobile applications. Writing BRDs, FRDs or SRS documents that a development team has built from. Process mapping and requirement workshop

Programme ManagerEssential: B.E. or B.Tech in any discipline, or MCA, from a recognised University or Institution.Desirable: MBA or PGDM; PMP, PRINCE2 or an equivalent programme management certification.
Business AnalystEssential: B.E. or B.Tech in any discipline, or MCA, or MBA, from a recognised University or Institution.Desirable: Graduate or postgraduate degree in Agriculture, Agricultural Economics or Insurance; CBAP, CCBA or an equivalent business analysis certification.
Technical LeadEssential: B.E. or B.Tech in Computer Science, IT, Electronics or an allied branch; or MCA; or M.Sc. in Computer Science or IT, from a recognised University or Institution.
