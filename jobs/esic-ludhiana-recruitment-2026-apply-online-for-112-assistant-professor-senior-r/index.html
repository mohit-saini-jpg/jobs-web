Pay and Terms

Teaching faculty: initially three (03) years on a contractual basis, extendable up to a maximum cumulative period of five (05) years, subject to satisfactory performance, annual performance appraisal and renewal/approval by the Competent Authority. Faculty contracts are renewed yearly and end on completion of the tenure, on attainment of 70 years of age or on the joining of a regular incumbent, whichever is earlier. Senior Residents: one year at a time, extendable subject to satisfactory performance and applicable NMC regulations, for a maximum tenure of three (03) years under the Senior Residency Scheme. A draft of Rs. 1,00,000/- must be deposited as security deposit at the time of joining; it will be forfeited if the candidate resigns within six months of joining. ESIC Ludhiana Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection is through scrutiny of applications followed by an interview before the Selection Board. The scrutiny committee of the Institute checks the applications submitted with the requisite fee, and only eligible candidates are allowed to appear for the interview. Eligibility for the interview is provisional, based on the documents emailed and pending scrutiny of the originals. The selection is made on the basis of performance in the interview, which is out of 100 marks. The general instructions add that selection is based on eligibility, academic credentials, professional experience, research contributions, interview performance and overall suitability for the post.   • 1Fill the application in the prescribed proforma only (Annexure-1, attached to the notice). A separate application is needed for each post.

  • 4Email the PDF to the Dean at [email protected] by 19 November 2026.

  • 6Check the provisional eligibility list on 20 November 2026; candidates will also be informed by email about their eligibility to attend the interview.

ESIC Ludhiana Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in ESIC Ludhiana Recruitment 2026?
A
The vacancy table adds up to 112 posts: 14 Professor, 22 Associate Professor, 16 Assistant Professor, 45 Senior Resident (3 Year), 8 Senior Resident (1 Year) and 7 Tutor. The vacancies are provisional and may increase or decrease.
Q2What is the last date to apply?
A
The last date to email the application is 19 November 2026. Under the rolling advertisement, the last date is the 19th of each month until the posts are filled.
Q3When and where is the walk-in interview?
A
The interview is on 23 November 2026 (Monday), with reporting from 09:00 AM to 10:00 AM at the 2nd Floor, Dean's Office, ESIC Medical College & Hospital, Ludhiana – 141001, Punjab.
Q4What qualification is required?
A
Education and experience are as per the latest NMC norms for all advertised posts. The notice says MSc, PhD, MBBS, Diploma & DNB [Alternate routes], MD, MS, MCH & DM holders can apply as per the latest NMC Regulations.
Q5What is the age limit?
A
Faculty must not exceed 69 years and Senior Residents must not exceed 45 years, both as on the date of interview. Age relaxation is as per GOI norms and the discretion of the competent Authority.
Q6What is the salary of a Senior Resident at ESIC Ludhiana?
A
Both Senior Resident posts carry a fixed basic pay of Rs. 67,700 (Level 11) plus allowances, with a gross salary of about Rs. 1.45 lakh. Rs. 1350 per month is deducted for diploma holders and Rs. 2250 per month if no Degree/Diploma is held.
Q7What is the application fee?
A
The fee is Rs. 500 for all other categories and Nil for SC/ST, ESIC regular employees, female candidates, defence ex-servicemen and PH candidates. It is paid by Demand Draft in favour of ESI Fund A/c No. 1, payable at Ludhiana.
Q8What should be written in the email subject line?
A
The subject line must read: NAME OF CANDIDATE – POST APPLIED FOR – DEPARTMENT. The form, documents and demand draft must be sent as one single PDF to [email protected].
Q9How are candidates selected?
A
Selection is on the basis of performance in an interview out of 100 marks before the Selection Board, after scrutiny of applications. The minimum marks are 50 for UR/EWS, 45 for OBC and 40 for SC/ST/PwBD.
Q10Is this a permanent job?
A
No. The posts are contractual: faculty for three years extendable up to five, and Senior Residents for one year at a time up to three years, with no right to regularisation. A security deposit of Rs. 1,00,000 is required at joining.
