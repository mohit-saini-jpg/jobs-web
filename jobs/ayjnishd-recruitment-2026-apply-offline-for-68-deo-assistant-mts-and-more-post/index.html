AYJNISHD Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

The contractual appointment will be on a full-time basis. The candidate will not be permitted to take up any other commercial assignment during the period of engagement. The engagement may be terminated by the Competent Authority at any time without assigning any reason by giving 30 days' notice.
