About LAHDC-KSSRB

The Kargil Subordinate Service Recruitment Board works under the Office of the Secretary, Ladakh Autonomous Hill Development Council, Kargil, in the Administration of Union Territory of Ladakh. Its job is to select candidates and recommend them to departments, based on the vacancies those departments report. The departments, not the Board, decide the number of vacancies and the reservation roster. The Board's Chairman is the Deputy Commissioner/CEO, LAHDC, Kargil, and its e-mail address is [email protected] .

Vacancies by Department

The notice advertises 181 posts across 20 items in six departments. Category-wise, there are 4 UR, 155 ST, 1 SC, 6 ALC and 15 EWS posts. The horizontal reservation is 5 posts for PwD, 8 for ESM and 5 for OSP. Most of the posts are in the School Education Department (107 posts, 70 of them Class-IV). The Ladakh Power Development Department has the next largest share (47 posts, 45 of them Technician-III). The other departments have 13 posts (Animal Husbandry), 8 (Rural Development Department & PR), 5 (PHE/I&FC) and 1 (Handicraft). Vertical reservation is for Scheduled Castes (SC), Scheduled Tribes (ST), Actual Line of Control (ALC) and Economically Weaker Sections (EWS). Horizontal reservation is 6% for Ex-Servicemen (ESM), 4% for Persons with Disabilities (PwD) and 4% for Outstanding Sportsperson (OSP), vide S.O 247 dated 18.08.2026. LAHDC KSSRB Recruitment 2026 : Application Fee Details

The fee is non-refundable and non-transferable. It is paid online by credit card, debit card, net banking or UPI by 27 November 2026, and the candidate bears all bank service charges. The fee is not refunded if the application is incomplete or rejected, and it cannot be adjusted against any other examination. A separate fee is paid for each category of post applied for. Apart from this fee, LAHDC-KSSRB does not ask for any payment at any stage of selection.

LAHDC KSSRB Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Candidates are selected in this order: A CBT, TBT or OMR-based written examination of objective multiple-choice questions A skill test of qualifying nature, where one (such as a Physical Efficiency Test) is prescribed in the essential qualification Document Verification Recommendation to the department Selection is done as per Rule 7 of the Ladakh Reservation (Amendment) Rules, 2025. LAHDC-KSSRB will decide whether the exam is CBT, TBT or OMR-based and will inform candidates. The exam is held at three levels (Matric, 10+2 and Graduate), matching the level printed for each post. Each question carries 1 mark, with negative marking of 0.25 marks for each wrong answer. The exam is in English. The minimum qualifying mark is 20%. LAHDC KSSRB Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Applications are accepted only through the LAHDC-KSSRB online portal from 27 October 2026 to 27 November 2026. Applications sent by post, by hand, by mail or by any other means will be summarily rejected.
  • 2Open the portal www.dssrbkargil.in and click the "New User Registration" link for one-time registration. Use your own mobile number and a valid, active personal e-mail ID, and keep both active during the recruitment cycle.
  • 3Fill in the basic and contact details and upload a scanned passport-size photograph and signature in JPEG format. The notice's application section allows 20 KB to 100 KB, while its instructions to candidates say 20 KB to 50 KB. The photograph should be about 3.5 cm (width) x 4.5 cm (height) and no more than three months old, with the face covering about 40% of the area (samples in Annexure-XV(A)).
  • 4Log in with the user ID and password sent to your registered e-mail. Check the advertisements and click "Apply Now" against the post.
  • 5Fill in the application. Write your name, father's name and date of birth exactly as in the Matriculation/Secondary certificate. Apply separately for each category of post, and submit only one application per category of post.
  • 6Pay the fee online (₹250 Unreserved, ₹200 other categories) by 27 November 2026.
  • 7Read the declaration at the end of the form, then submit. The application is then accepted provisionally, and no corrections are allowed after final submission.
  • 8Print the application form. You do not send it to the Board. A duly signed printed copy must be produced, with self-attested documents, at the scrutiny or Document Verification stage.

LAHDC KSSRB Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in LAHDC-KSSRB Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 181 vacancies across 20 post items in six departments. They include Class-IV (70), Technician-III (45), Laboratory Assistant (31), Attendant (9) and Library Assistant (5).
Q2What is the last date to apply for LAHDC-KSSRB Recruitment 2026?
A
The last date to submit the online application and to pay the fee online is 27 November 2026. Applications open on 27 October 2026.
Q3What qualification is needed for LAHDC-KSSRB posts?
A
It depends on the post. Class-IV, Mirab, Store Khalasi and Attendant need Matriculation/Std. X pass. Junior Assistant needs graduation with a 200-hour or 6-month computer course and a typing speed of 35 words per minute. Technician-III needs a two-year NTC/STC in the relevant trade from NCVT/SCVT.
Q4What is the age limit for LAHDC-KSSRB Recruitment 2026?
A
The age limit is 18-40 years as on 1 January 2026. The upper limit is 45 years for SC/ST/ALC, 50 years for PwBD and 55 years for PwBD who are also SC/ST/ALC.
Q5What is the salary for LAHDC-KSSRB posts?
A
The notice gives pay levels, not amounts. Junior Assistant, Receptionist, Veterinary Pharmacist and other L-4 posts are at Level L-4. Laboratory Assistant, Technician-III and other L-2 posts are at Level L-2. Class-IV, Mirab, Bearer, Store Khalasi and Attendant are at Level SL-1.
Q6What is the application fee for LAHDC-KSSRB Recruitment 2026?
A
The fee is ₹250 for Unreserved candidates and ₹200 for other categories. It is paid online by 27 November 2026, separately for each category of post, and is non-refundable.
Q7How do I apply for LAHDC-KSSRB Recruitment 2026?
A
Register through "New User Registration" on www.dssrbkargil.in and log in with the ID sent to your e-mail. Then click "Apply Now" against the post and pay the fee online. Applications by post, by hand or by mail are rejected.
Q8What is the selection process for LAHDC-KSSRB posts?
A
Selection is through a CBT/TBT/OMR objective exam of 100 questions, with 0.25 negative marking and 20% minimum qualifying marks. Shortlisted candidates then go to Document Verification. A qualifying skill test is held where the essential qualification prescribes one.
Q9Who can apply for LAHDC-KSSRB Recruitment 2026?
A
Only residents of Kargil District (Erstwhile District Kargil) who hold a Domicile Certificate in the Annexure-V format can apply.
Q10Where will the LAHDC-KSSRB exam be held?
A
Exam centres will be in Kargil, Drass, Zanskar and Sankoo districts only. The exam date will be announced on the LAHDC-KSSRB website, and admit cards are expected there tentatively one week before the exam.
