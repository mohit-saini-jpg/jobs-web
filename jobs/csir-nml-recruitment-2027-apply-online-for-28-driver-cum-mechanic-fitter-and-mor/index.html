About CSIR-NML

CSIR-NML is a constituent laboratory of the Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR), an autonomous body under the Government of India's Ministry of Science and Technology. According to the notice, it is a research and development organisation dedicated to metallurgy and materials engineering research. It is located at Burmamines, Jamshedpur, Jharkhand-831007. The notice states that CSIR-NML aims for a workforce that reflects gender balance and encourages women candidates to apply.

Vacancy Details

The notice lists 28 vacancies across 12 post codes. The number of vacancies against each post code is tentative and may increase or decrease. Pay Level-2 applies to all of them. Of the 28 posts, 3 are reserved for ex-servicemen. One post under T-04 is reserved for PwBD with locomotor disability [OA, OL, BL], which includes Dwarfism (Dw) and Acid Attack Victims (AAV). One post under T-08 is reserved for PwBD in the B (blind)/LV (low vision) category. Of the 7 posts under T-04, 2 are reserved for the Digha centre and 2 for the NML Madras centre.

Who Can Apply

Applicants must be citizens of India. The qualification pattern is the same for every post code, with the trade changing. A candidate needs 10th/Secondary or equivalent examination with minimum 55% marks with science subjects, together with one of the following in the trade of the post code applied for: ITI certificate or National/State Trade Certificate in the trade; OR 2 years' full-time experience as an apprentice in the trade from a recognised institute; OR 3 years' work experience in the trade in a Ministry/Department/Organisation/Public Sector Undertaking/Autonomous Body under the Government of India/State/UT. The prescribed qualifications and experience are minimum. Holding a higher qualification does not replace them. Pay and Tenure

The posts are at Pay Level-2 (Rs. 19,900-63,200). The notice puts total emoluments at about Rs. 37,000 per month, including house rent allowance and transport allowance at the minimum pay scale applicable in Jamshedpur. Dearness Allowance, House Rent Allowance and Transport Allowance are paid as admissible to central government employees and applicable to CSIR. Council employees are eligible for accommodation under the CSIR Residence Allotment Rules-2022, subject to availability. In that case HRA is not payable. If the Director, CSIR-NML makes it compulsory, selected candidates must stay in CSIR-NML staff quarters. Medical reimbursement, leave travel concession, computer advance and house building advance are available as per CSIR rules. All new recruits come under the New Pension Scheme (contributory), as adopted by CSIR for employees joining on or after 01.01.2004. Selected candidates

Place of Posting

The general place of appointment is CSIR-NML, Jamshedpur. Appointees may also be posted to the CSIR-NML units at Digha (West Bengal) and Chennai, and, at the discretion of the competent authority, to any section or division of CSIR-NML or any CSIR laboratory or institute in India, including its regional centres. Selected candidates may be placed in any division or section of CSIR-NML according to the trade.

Selection and Interview

Selection has three stages: shortlisting by the screening committee, a trade test (qualifying test) and a competitive written examination. The notice does not describe an interview stage. Candidates recommended by the screening committee are invited for the trade test in the concerned trade/subject. The committee adopts its own criteria to shortlist candidates; having the prescribed minimum qualification and experience alone does not entitle a candidate to be called. Applicants should therefore mention all relevant qualifications and experience in the application, with supporting documents. Candidates who pass the trade test are invited for the written examination. It is an OMR-based or computer-based objective multiple-choice test of 150 questions in 2 hours 30 minutes, at SSC + ITI / Class 12 level.
