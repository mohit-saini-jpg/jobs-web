About TMC MPMMCC Ad-hoc Medical Officer (Haemato-pathology)

Mahamana Pandit Madan Mohan Malaviya Cancer Centre & Homi Bhabha Cancer Hospital in Varanasi conducts recruitment to appoint qualified medical professionals. This position of Ad-hoc Medical Officer (Haemato-pathology) is for a period of six months or till the occupancy of regular post, whichever is earlier.

Posts and Eligibility

For D.M.: Experience not required. For M.D./ D.N.B: 3 years post MD / DNB experience as a senior resident in a Hematopathology Laboratory with exposure in Bone Marrow morphology. Flow cytometry, molecular techniques related to Hematological malignancies. Candidates with less than 3 years experience will be considered for a lower grade, if there are no suitably experienced candidates. Experience in Minimal residual disease detection and molecular techniques like Next generation sequencing will be an added advantage.
