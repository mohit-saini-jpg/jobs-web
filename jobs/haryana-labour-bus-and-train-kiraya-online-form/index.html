Haryana Labour Bus and Train Kiraya Online Form Overview

Haryana government ne labourers ke liye bus aur train kiraya ka sahara diya hai. Online form bhar ke aap apni daily travel cost ka reimbursement le sakte hain, bina kisi jhanjhat ke.

Haryana Labour Bus and Train Kiraya Online Form Expert Analysis

Yeh yojana especially un workers ke liye bani hai jo rozana public transport use karte hain. Scheme ka maksad unki commuting cost ko kam karna aur unki financial burden ko halka karna hai.

Who Is Eligible: Haryana Labour Bus and Train Kiraya Online Form

Jo bhi Haryana ka registered labourer hai, jo daily bus ya train se kaam par jata hai, wo is scheme ke liye eligible hai.

Haryana Labour Bus and Train Kiraya Online Form Application Process

1. Apna Aadhaar, Voter ID aur latest salary slip ready rakhein. 2. Online portal par apna email aur mobile verify karen. 3. Form bharte waqt travel details aur receipt numbers accurately fill karein.

Haryana Labour Bus and Train Kiraya Online Form Benefits Details

Har eligible labourer ko mahine ke ₹1,500 tak ka travel allowance mil sakta hai, jo unki daily commute cost ko kaafi had tak cover karta hai.

How Haryana Labour Bus and Train Kiraya Online Form Works

Scheme ke tahat, applicant ka data portal me register hota hai, verification ke baad monthly allowance bank account me direct credit hota hai. Disbursement har mahine ke 10 tareekh tak hota hai.

Haryana Labour Bus and Train Kiraya Online Form Application Tips

Form bharte waqt sabhi fields ko dhyan se fill karein, galat details se rejection ho sakta hai. Receipt numbers aur travel logs ko attach karna na bhoolen, aur deadline se pehle submit karen.

Haryana Labour Bus and Train Kiraya Online Form FAQs

Q1Yeh kiraya allowance kaise calculate hota hai?
A
Allowance aapke daily travel distance aur ticket cost ke hisaab se monthly ₹1,500 tak limit me calculate hota hai.
Q2Kya mere bank account me direct credit hota hai?
A
Haan, verification ke baad allowance aapke registered bank account me direct transfer hota hai.
Q3Form submit karne ki last date kab hai?
A
Har mahine ki 5th tareekh tak online form submit karna zaroori hai, uske baad aapka application next cycle me consider hoga.
