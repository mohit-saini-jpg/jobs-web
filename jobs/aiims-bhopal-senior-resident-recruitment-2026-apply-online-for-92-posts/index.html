AIIMS Bhopal Senior Resident Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

All India Institute of Medical Sciences, Bhopal (AIIMS Bhopal), an Institute of National Importance (INI), has released Advt. No. AIIMS Bhopal/SR (Non-Acad.)/2026/08 dated 30/10/2026. It invites online applications from Indian Nationals for the post of Senior Residents (Non-Academic), purely on a temporary basis.

AIIMS Bhopal Senior Resident Recruitment 2026 – Quick Summary

Who is recruiting? All India Institute of Medical Sciences, Bhopal (AIIMS Bhopal). Which post? Senior Residents (Non-Academic), purely on temporary basis. How many vacancies? 64 posts (Sl. No. 1 to 26) plus 28 posts (Sl. No. 27 to 40) in Annexure-IV, 92 in total when both tables are added. Who can apply? Indian Nationals with a postgraduate medical degree (MD/MS/DNB/MDS) and valid registration. Application start date: 30.10.2026. Last date: 15.11.2026. Application mode: Online only. Job location: The written test/interview will be conducted at AIIMS Bhopal-462020 (MP). A separate posting location is not specified in the notification beyond this.

AIIMS Bhopal Senior Resident Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must be Indian Nationals who satisfy all eligibility conditions. Eligibility will be considered as on the date of Interview.

AIIMS Bhopal Senior Resident Qualification

A Postgraduate Medical Degree, viz. MD/MS/DNB/MDS, in the respective discipline, recognized by NMC/DCI/NDC or an Institute of National Importance. Valid registration with NMC/State Medical Council/Dental Council of India (DCI). Only candidates declared passed in their qualifying degree exam on or before the date of Interview can apply. The tenure of the qualifying degree should be completed on or before the date of Interview. In super specialty departments, candidates possessing a relevant MD/MS/DNB qualification may also apply. AIIMS Bhopal Senior Resident Salary

The pay scale is Rs. 67,700/- (Level-11, Cell No. 01 as per 7th CPC) plus usual allowances including NPA, as admissible under rules.

AIIMS Bhopal Senior Resident Tenure

The appointment is initially for one (01) year, extendable up to three (03) years. The total duration cannot go beyond three years under the Residency Scheme in any circumstances, including Residency services already rendered by the applicant. Candidates who have already completed 3 years of Senior Residency will not be considered. If a candidate has served as Senior Resident in any Institute/Hospital of Govt./Public Sector Undertaking/Autonomous Bodies, only the balance period will be considered, so that the total does not exceed 03 years. For posts at Sl. No. 27 to 40 (Annexure-IV), the appointment is for a maximum of 6 months, which may be further extended to a maximum of 6 months. AIIMS Bhopal Senior Resident Reservation

Reservation for SC/ST/OBC/EWS and PwBD candidates is as per Central Govt. rules/policy. Candidates claiming reservation must attach a copy of a valid category certificate issued by the appropriate authority. SC/ST: Category certificate as per the format in Annexure-I. OBC: Certificate from the competent authority as per Annexure-II for Central Government jobs, along with a certificate that the candidate does not belong to the Creamy Layer. The non-creamy layer certificate should be valid for the financial year 2026-2027; certificates issued from 1st April 2026 to the date of interview are considered valid. EWS: Reservation as per Govt. of India policy (Office Memorandum No. 36039/1/2019-Estt (Res) DoPT dated 19th January 2019 and 31st January 2019). EWS persons not covered under SC, ST and OBC reservation get 10% reservation. The certificate must be in the form prescribed in Annexure-III

AIIMS Bhopal Senior Resident Selection Process

The notification mentions that a written test/interview will be conducted at AIIMS Bhopal-462020 (MP). Date, time and venue details will be uploaded on the Institute website in due course. The exact stages and any marks distribution are not specified in the provided notification. Tie cases (more than one candidate with the same marks in the interview) are resolved on date of birth. The older candidate is ranked above the younger candidate. Selected candidates will undergo a medical examination/test to ascertain medical fitness. If not found fit, the offer of appointment will be treated as withdrawn. No TA/DA will be paid for appearing in the selection process.
