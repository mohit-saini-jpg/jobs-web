DLSA Chengalpattu Members Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

About DLSA Chengalpattu

The District Legal Services Authority, Chengalpattu is headed by the Chairman/Principal District Judge and functions from the Combined Court Building, Chengalpattu. This selection is being made as per the communication received from the Tamil Nadu State Legal Services Authority, Chennai in Ref: TNSLSA No.297/E/2025, dated 24.02.2026. The selected Member will serve in the Permanent Lok Adalat (Public Utility Services), Chengalpattu, which deals with matters connected with public utility services.

Where and How to Send

The application must be sent through Registered Post and must reach the following address on or before 27 November 2026, up to 05.30 p.m.: The Chairman/Principal District Judge, District Legal Services Authority, Combined Court Building, Chengalpattu - 603001. Fill in the prescribed application format (Bio-data) enclosed with the notification, giving details of qualification, age and experience. Paste a recent passport size coloured photograph on the form. Sign the "Undertaking/Declaration by the Applicant" enclosed with the notification, in support of the facts given in the form. Attach self attested copies of all testimonials, educational certificates and experience certificates, and an attested copy of proof of date of birth and age. Enclose a self attested stamped envelope with a cover. Send the application by Registered Post so that it reaches the above address by 27 November 2026,

Posts, Eligibility and Pay

The notification is for one post of Member in the Permanent Lok Adalat, Chengalpattu. The eligibility criteria require a person having adequate experience in any of the Public Utility Services defined in the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987. Applicants should have the following: Educational qualification: pass in minimum degree standard as on the date of notification. Language: must have the ability to write and speak the local language fluently. Experience: experience in Public Utility Service defined under section 22 A (b) of The Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987 of not less than 10 years. Age limit: the maximum age for the selection of member is below 62 years as on the date of Notification (11 November 2026). DLSA Chengalpattu Members Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

The applications will be scrutinised, and only the shortlisted candidates list will be published on the court website for interview. The venue, date and time of the interview will be published on the Chengalpattu e-court website (chengalpattu.dcourts.gov.in); a separate communication letter will not be sent to the applicants. Shortlisted candidates should appear for the interview with their original certificates and attend at their own expenses. The selection criteria will follow the guidelines of the Permanent Lok Adalat (Other terms and conditions of Appointment of Chairman and other Persons) Amendment Rules, 2016.

Q3What qualification is required for the Member post?
A
Applicants should have passed minimum degree standard as on the date of notification, be able to write and speak the local language fluently, and have not less than 10 years' experience in Public Utility Service under section 22 A (b) of The Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987.
Q4What is the age limit for the DLSA Chengalpattu Member post?

The maximum age is below 62 years as on the date of Notification, that is, 11 November 2026.
Q5What is the salary of a Permanent Lok Adalat Member?
A
A Member is entitled to a sitting fee of Rs.2000/- (Rupees Two Thousand) per sitting under Rule 3 (b) of the Permanent Lok Adalat Amendment Rules, 2016.
Q6How do I apply for the DLSA Chengalpattu Member post?
A
Send the filled bio-data form with photo, the signed undertaking, self attested certificates and a self attested stamped envelope by Registered Post to The Chairman/Principal District Judge, District Legal Services Authority, Combined Court Building, Chengalpattu - 603001.

A
Applications will be scrutinised and the shortlisted candidates will be called for an interview, whose venue, date and time will be published on the Chengalpattu e-court website.
Q8What is the term of office for the Member?
A
The Member shall hold office for a term of 5 years or till the age of 65 years, whichever is earlier, and must work the whole day as a full-time worker.
