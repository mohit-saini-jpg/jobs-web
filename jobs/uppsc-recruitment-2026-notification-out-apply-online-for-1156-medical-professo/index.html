UPPSC Professor & Assistant Professor Eligibility Criteria

Only those candidates who possess the requisite qualification acquired from a recognized university/institution at the time of applying, and who hold the essential qualification till the last date of receipt of the online application, are eligible. Minimum educational qualification alone is not sufficient for being called for interview - mere eligibility does not entitle a candidate to selection. Relevant Service Rules: The Uttar Pradesh State Medical College Teacher's Service Rules, 1990 (as amended).
PostGeneral Qualification Pattern
ProfessorRelevant postgraduate medical degree (MD/MS/DNB/DM/M.Ch. as applicable to the specialty), plus 3 years' experience as Associate Professor in the subject in a recognised medical institution (or equivalent alternative routes specified per specialty, e.g. Specialist Medical Officer of Armed Forces, Senior Consultant with NBE-recognised teaching experience), plus at least 2 research publications after becoming Associate Professor (as first three authors) and completion of the Basic Course in Biomedical Research
Assistant ProfessorRelevant postgraduate medical degree (MD/MS/DNB/DM/M.Ch. as applicable to the specialty), plus 1 year as Senior Resident in the concerned subject after obtaining the degree (or equivalent alternative routes specified per specialty, e.g. Tutor/Demonstrator experience, Specialist Medical Officer of Armed Forces, non-teaching Consultant with 2 years' experience in a 220+ bed Government hospital, or diploma holders with specified years of Senior Resident/Specialist experience)

UPPSC Professor & Assistant Professor Selection Process

Under the "Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission (Direct Recruitment through Screening Examination) Regulations, 2025," the Commission may hold a Screening Examination for selection of candidates for interview. Only candidates who qualify in the Screening Examination will be entitled to an Interview. The final merit order is determined by adding 75% marks in the screening test and 25% marks in the interview . The Screening Examination is Objective Type (OMR-based), with 4 alternatives per question. Negative marking: one-third (0.33) of the marks assigned to a question is deducted for a wrong answer; a blank question carries no penalty; multiple answers to one question are treated as wrong. Minimum efficiency standard: SC/ST candidates must secure at least 35% marks in the Screening Examination; candidates of other categories must secure at least 40% marks, to be placed in the merit/sel

UPPSC Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Obtain a One Time Registration (O.T.R.) Number from the Commission's O.T.R. portal, https://otr.pariksha.nic.in, at least 72 hours before submitting the online application (if not already registered).
  • 2Visit the Commission's official website, https://uppsc.up.nic.in, and click "ALL NOTIFICATIONS/ADVERTISEMENTS."
  • 3Click "Apply" against the relevant post, then "Authenticate with O.T.R." and enter your O.T.R. Number; authenticate via O.T.P. or O.T.R. password.
  • 4Second Stage: On the Applicant Dashboard, click "Submit Details" under "Application Part-2"; your address and personal details will auto-populate from O.T.R., along with preferential qualifications for the post - select Yes/No for each.
  • 5Third Stage: Proceed to fee payment via SBI MOPS (Net Banking/Card Payments/Other Payment Modes); take a printout of the Payment Transaction Slip.
  • 6Fourth Stage: Your completed application form will be displayed; take a printout and keep it safe.
  • 7After submitting online, send self-attested photocopies of all academic/required documents along with the printout of the online form via registered/speed post or by hand, to reach the Commission's office by the hard-copy submission deadline.
  • 8Use a separate A-4 size envelope for each post applied for, with the auto-filled address-slip (downloaded from the Candidate Dashboard) pasted on it.
