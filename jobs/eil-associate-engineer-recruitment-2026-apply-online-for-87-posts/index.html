EIL Associate Engineer Salary 2026

Selected Associate Engineers will receive consolidated monthly remuneration based on their grade and posting city classification. The above remuneration includes House Rent Allowance (HRA) and Provident Fund (PF). Selected fixed-term engineers will be eligible for the following benefits subject to company rules: Reimbursement of medical insurance premium for self for insurance coverage up to Rs. 2,00,000 per annum. Reimbursement of eligible out-of-pocket expenses for working beyond standard working hours. Coverage under the company's GPAI scheme. Travelling allowance for official duties outside the place of posting. Two days of paid leave for each completed month. Applicable living, R&R and other allowances during foreign postings.
