About AIIMS Raipur

AIIMS Raipur describes itself as an apex healthcare institute, established by the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Government of India under the Pradhan Mantri Swasthya Suraksha Yojana (PMSSY). It is located at G. E. Road, Tatibandh, Raipur- 492 099 (Chhattisgarh). This walk-in is issued by its Academic Section for Senior Residents (Non Academic) under the Govt. of India Residency Scheme.

Posts and Eligibility

AIIMS Raipur has 25 posts of Senior Resident-Non Academic (Group A) in the departments listed in Annexure-A of the notice. The total includes 1 post for PwBD, and the notice says the vacancies are provisional and subject to variation. Reservation for ST/SC/OBC candidates is as per Central Govt. rules, with 4% for PwBD candidates on a horizontal basis. EWS posts are not carried forward or considered as backlog if unfilled, so candidates of other categories may be allowed provisionally to apply; they will be considered as UR candidates only if the EWS seat is otherwise not filled. Only candidates who have been declared successful in their qualifying degree exam and will be completing or likely to complete their tenure on or before the date of walk-in-interview are eligible. What to Bring

The notice includes an Application Form for the post of Senior Resident (Non-acad) with a passport size self-attested colour photograph to be affixed. Self-attested photocopies may be annexed to the hard copy of the application, and the originals must be produced along with photocopies for verification at the walk-in. What Happens on the Day

Candidates report between 10:00 am and 11:00 am on 4 December 2026. Based on bio-data, the Search cum Selection Committee may short-list candidates for the walk-in-interview, or candidates may be asked to appear in a written examination if there is a large number of applicants. Short-listing may be done on the basis of educational qualification, experience etc. and/or criteria decided by the Competent Authority. If a large number of applications are received for any post, a Screening Test (computer based or offline) may be conducted, and its syllabus, scheme and method will be notified on the AIIMS Raipur website. Final selection will be made on the basis of the walk-in-interview, and the decision of the Executive Director & CEO, AIIMS Raipur is final. AIIMS Raipur Senior Resident Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1When is the AIIMS Raipur Senior Resident walk-in interview 2026?
A
The walk-in interview is on 4 December 2026 (Friday), with reporting time 10:00 am to 11:00 am.
Q2Where is the AIIMS Raipur Senior Resident walk-in venue?
A
The venue is the Academic Section, 4th Floor, A block, New Admin Building, AIIMS Raipur, Tatibandh, G.E. Road, Raipur (C.G.) 492099.
Q3How many Senior Resident vacancies are there at AIIMS Raipur?
A
There are 25 posts (UR-10, OBC-7, SC-4, ST-2 and EWS-2), including 1 post of PwBD, across Anaesthesia, Biochemistry, three Dentistry specialties, Radio Diagnosis and Trauma & Emergency.
Q4What qualification is required for AIIMS Raipur Senior Resident (Non Academic)?
A
A Postgraduate Medical Degree viz. MD/MS/DNB/Diploma in the respective discipline from a recognized University/Institute is required; for Dentistry, a Postgraduate Degree i.e. MDS in the concerned specialty.
Q5What is the age limit for the AIIMS Raipur Senior Resident posts?
A
The upper age limit is 45 years as on the date of walk-in-interview, with relaxation of 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC and 10 to 15 years for PwBD candidates.
Q6What is the salary of AIIMS Raipur Senior Resident (Non Academic)?
A
The pay is Rs. 67,700/- (Level-11, Cell No. 01 As per 7th CPC) plus usual allowances including NPA (if applicable).
Q7Are allowances paid on top of the pay?
A
Yes, usual allowances including NPA (if applicable) are paid on top of Rs. 67,700/-, but the appointee does not get Provident Fund, Pension, Gratuity or other regular-employee benefits.
Q8What is the application fee for the AIIMS Raipur Senior Resident walk-in?
A
The fee is Rs. 1,000/- for General/OBC/EWS candidates and Nil for Women/SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-servicemen, paid through NEFT in online/digital mode only.
Q9How will AIIMS Raipur select Senior Residents?
A
Final selection will be made on the basis of the walk-in-interview; candidates may first be short-listed on bio-data or asked to take a written examination or Screening Test if applicants are many.
Q10Is the AIIMS Raipur Senior Resident post permanent?
A
No, the appointment is under the Govt. of India Residency Scheme for a tenure period up to 3 years and can be terminated by one month's notice on either side.
