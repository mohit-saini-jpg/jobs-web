ESIC Empanelment of Advocates 2026 Eligibility Criteria

The Advocate should hold a Bachelor's Degree in Law from a recognized University in India. The Advocate should be enrolled/registered as an Advocate with the Bar Council of India or a State Bar Council. The Advocate should have an office at the place where empanelment is sought, with adequate infrastructure (accessible office, chambers, library, manpower, etc.) and excellent communication skills. The Regional Director, ESIC RO-Maharashtra, may relax the prescribed experience or specify any additional qualification or condition as deemed fit.

ESIC Empanelment of Advocates 2026 Fee Schedule

No application fee is mentioned for submitting the empanelment application. The professional fee payable to empanelled Advocates for handling ESIC's cases is as prescribed by ESIC vide Office Memorandum No. T-11/15/2/2016-Vol-II/T-11/15/12/2016-Vol-II dated 25.03.2026; the exact fee amounts are not specified in this notification and applicants must agree to this fee schedule as part of their undertaking. No retainer fee is paid to any Panel Advocate merely for being empanelled.

ESIC Panel Advocate Tenure

The panel is ordinarily constituted for a period of 3 years (2026–2029), in the interest of continuity and stability. The panel can be discontinued before expiry of the term for reasons recorded in writing, and the process for constituting a new panel is started before expiry of the existing one. If a panel is reconstituted before the existing one expires, the existing panel continues until the new panel is constituted, so that ongoing litigation is not affected; the tenure of the existing panel may also be extended until the new empanelment process is completed.

ESIC Empanelment of Advocates 2026 Selection Process

Applications must be submitted in the prescribed format only (Annexure-II for fresh applicants, Annexure-III for existing panel Advocates); no other format will be entertained. ESIC reserves the right to shortlist applications based on the requirement and the number of applications received. Fulfilling the eligibility criteria alone does not confer any right to be called for interaction or selected. Shortlisted Advocates may be called for further interaction before empanelment is finalized. No TA/DA is admissible for attending such interaction. The Selection Committee consists of the Regional Director, ESIC RO-Maharashtra, and two external members of eminence from the legal field. Date, time, venue, and mode of interaction will normally be communicated by e-mail or letter; SMS may be used as an additional mode of communication in exceptional circumstances. Key Terms, Duties and Conditions of Empanelment

Empanelment confers only a right to be considered for legal work and does not bind ESIC to award work to any empanelled Advocate at any time. Allocation of cases is at the sole discretion of authorized ESIC Officers; empanelled Advocates must handle cases themselves and must not delegate them. Empanelled Advocates cannot use ESIC's name, logo, or designations such as "Legal Advisor of ESIC" on their letterhead, signboard, or other materials, and cannot represent themselves as ESIC's Standing Advocate unless specifically authorized. Advocates must act only on ESIC's instructions, report on the proceedings of every hearing, and furnish copies of court orders, failing which ESIC may not settle bills.   • 1Download/obtain the prescribed application form: Annexure-II (for fresh applicant Advocates) or Annexure-III (for existing panel Advocates), from the ESIC website https://romaharashtra.esic.gov.in/ (recruitment list).
  • 2Fill the form in block letters, providing personal details, educational qualification, current empanelment status with other bodies (if any), details of associated junior Advocates (if any), number of ESIC-related cases handled earlier, details of any landmark case, bank/PAN/Aadhaar details, and disclosures regarding any disciplinary or criminal proceedings.
