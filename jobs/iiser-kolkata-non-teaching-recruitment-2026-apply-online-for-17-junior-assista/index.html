IISER Kolkata Non Teaching Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

The IISER Kolkata non-teaching selection process consists of the following stages: Shortlisting of online applications Computer Skill Test and/or Trade Test Written Examination Document Verification Medical fitness and verification of character and antecedents An institute-level Scrutiny Committee will examine the applications. If IISER Kolkata receives a large number of applications, candidates may be shortlisted based on qualifications, relevant experience and other prescribed criteria. Shortlisted candidates may be required to appear for a Computer Skill Test, Trade Test or both. These tests will be qualifying in nature, and their marks will not be added to the final written examination score. The final selection will be based on the merit of marks obtained in the written examination. The indicative post-wise syllabus is available on the IISER Kolkata recruitment website.
