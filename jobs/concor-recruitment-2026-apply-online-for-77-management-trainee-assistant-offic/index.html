CONCOR Recruitment 2026 Pay Scale & CTC

Management Trainees undergo one year of on-the-job and off-the-job training and, on successful completion, are absorbed in the Assistant Manager grade/E1. Selected MT candidates must execute a service bond of ₹2 Lakhs plus 12% interest per annum, to serve CONCOR for a minimum of three years (excluding the training period).

CONCOR Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Management Trainee: Computer Based Test (CBT), followed by Group Discussion and/or Interview, as decided by CONCOR. CONCOR's decision is final and binding. Assistant Officer: Selection is purely based on performance in the Online Test (CBT) – there is no GD or interview stage for this post. No correspondence will be entertained for non-calling of a candidate for the Online Test/GD/Interview, or for non-selection. Duration: 90 minutes total (120 minutes for eligible PwBD candidates availing a scribe). Negative marking: 1/4 (0.25) mark deducted for each wrong answer. Each question has 5 answer choices. The question paper is set in English and Hindi only; the English version prevails in case of any discrepancy.
