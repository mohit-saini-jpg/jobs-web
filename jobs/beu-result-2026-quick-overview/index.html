BEU Result 2026: Quick Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 22/08/2026

Bihar Engineering University (BEU), Patna has been releasing semester results for its UG and PG programmes in phases through 2026. Results for B.Tech, B.Arch, MBA, and M.Tech semester examinations have been declared on the university's official result portal at different points in the year. Students can check and download their provisional marksheet online using their registration number, roll number, and date of birth.

BEU Result 2026: Important Dates

BEU has not issued a single unified result date for all courses. Instead, results have gone out semester-wise across the year. Reported release points include:

ResultReported Release
M.Tech, MBA, B.Arch, and B.Tech (1st to 8th Semester, various)Around January 17, 2026
B.Tech and B.Arch 2nd SemesterAround March 13, 2026
B.Tech 8th SemesterJune 24, 2026

If your specific semester or course result is not listed above, it means that date is not available in the official notification we could verify. Check the official BEU result portal directly for the most current status of your semester.

How to Check BEU Result 2026 Online

  • Visit the official BEU result portal.
  • Look for the "Examination" or "Results" section on the homepage.
  • Select your specific exam - for example, B.Tech 2nd Semester, B.Arch 2nd Semester, or B.Tech 8th Semester - from the list of declared results.
  • Enter the required details, typically your Registration/Roll Number and Date of Birth.
  • Submit the form to view your result on screen.
  • Click "Download" or "Print" to save the marksheet as a PDF.
  • Check your name, roll number, and marks carefully before closing the page.

Details Mentioned on the BEU Marksheet

Once downloaded, students should verify the following details on their BEU result PDF:

  • Student's name
  • Roll number / registration number
  • College/institute name
  • Course and semester
  • Subject-wise marks and grades
  • Overall result status (pass/fail/reappear, where applicable)

Note: Figures such as overall pass percentage or topper details for BEU Result 2026 are not available in the official notification we could verify, and are not included here to avoid reporting unconfirmed numbers.

What This Result Means for You

  • The downloaded marksheet is provisional. Students are advised to keep it safe for academic reference until the official/original mark sheet is issued by the university.
  • For students in the final semester (8th Semester B.Tech), this result is relevant for degree completion, campus placements, and higher-education applications such as postgraduate admissions.

Discrepancy, Revaluation, or Backlog: What To Do

  • Error in result: If there is a mistake in your name, roll number, or marks, report it to your college's examination department or BEU's examination branch without delay.
  • Rechecking/Revaluation: If BEU has announced a revaluation or rechecking window, apply within the deadline specified in that notification. The exact fee and process for rechecking are not available in the official notification we could verify.
  • Backlog/ATKT: Students with a backlog should watch for BEU's official notification on supplementary/back examinations and register within the specified deadline.

Important Instructions for Students

  • Do not rely solely on the online result - download and print a copy for your records.
  • Double-check that you are on BEU's genuine official website before entering your registration number and date of birth.
  • Keep your registration number and date of birth handy before you start; both are typically required to view the result.
  • If the result page does not load, try clearing your browser cache, re-checking your roll number, or accessing the portal again after some time before contacting the exam department.
