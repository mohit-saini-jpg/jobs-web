SBI Specialist Cadre Officer Important Dates

Online registration and payment of fees run from 30.09.2026 to 21.10.2026. The notification does not specify an interview date or result date.

SBI Specialist Cadre Officer Vacancy 2026

The Bank has announced 6 vacancies across 5 posts. All vacancies are listed as Regular, and no Backlog vacancies are shown. One vacancy (Central Operations Team (COT) Support, OBC) carries a horizontal PwBD vacancy under the Visually Impaired (VI) category. The number of vacancies, including reserved vacancies, is provisional and may vary according to the Bank's actual requirement. Abbreviations used in the notification: UR – Unreserved; OBC – Other Backward Class (Non-Creamy Layer); SC – Scheduled Caste; ST – Scheduled Tribe; EWS – Economically Weaker Section; VI – Visually Impaired; HI – Hearing Impaired; LD – Locomotive Disability; d&e – PwBDs as mentioned in Sec 34 (1) clause (d) & (e) of The Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act 2016. EWS vacancies are tentative and subject to further directives of the Government of India and the outcome of any litigation.

The age limit is calculated as on 31.08.2026. The maximum age shown is for Unreserved category candidates. Upper age relaxation for reserved categories is available as per Government of India guidelines, wherever applicable.

SBI Specialist Cadre Officer Application Fee

The application fee and intimation charges (non-refundable) are ₹750/- (Seven Hundred Fifty only) for UR/EWS/OBC candidates. There are no fees or intimation charges for SC/ST/PwBD candidates. Payment is made online through the payment gateway using Debit Card, Credit Card, Internet Banking, etc. Transaction charges, if any, are borne by the candidate. Registration is complete only when the fee is deposited online on or before the last date. The fee, once paid, will not be refunded on any account and cannot be adjusted for any other examination or selection in future. If online payment is not completed in the first instance, candidates should make fresh attempts. In case of multiple applications, only the last valid (completed) application is retained, and the fee paid for other registrations is forfeited.

SBI Specialist Cadre Officer Salary (CTC) and Contract Period

SBI has specified an annual CTC upper limit for each post. The annual CTC is negotiable and depends on the candidate's experience, current emoluments in present employment and place of posting. The CTC upper range covers Fixed Pay plus Variable Pay/PLP (based on Performance Rating) plus other perks. The notification does not give a monthly salary figure. Variable pay: Officers are eligible for Performance Linked Pay and Annual Increment based on the performance rating measured as per the Bank's Policy. Contract period: 5 years, renewable for another 4 years at the discretion of the Bank, subject to fulfilment of any conditions the Bank may stipulate. SBI Specialist Cadre Officer Selection Process

The selection process comprises shortlisting of candidates, followed by one or more rounds of personal/video interview and CTC negotiation. There is no written test mentioned in the notification.
