Indian Bank Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Indian Bank, a Government of India undertaking with its Corporate Office in Chennai, is engaging 5 AI - Cybersecurity Specialist Officers for its Information System Security Department (ISSD) on a contractual basis: 1 Vice President - AI Cybersecurity, 2 Assistant Vice President - AI Cybersecurity and 2 Associate Manager - Senior Officer- AI Cybersecurity. The engagement is Fixed Term Employment for an initial period of 3 years, extendable up to 5 years, with remuneration described as negotiable and not a limiting factor for a deserving candidate. About Indian Bank

Indian Bank describes itself in the notice as a leading Public Sector Bank and a Government of India undertaking, with headquarters in Chennai and a geographical presence all over India and abroad. Its Corporate Office is at 254-260, Avvai Shanmugam Salai, Royapettah, Chennai – 600014. The posts in this notice are for the bank's Information System Security Department (ISSD), which will use AI/ML capabilities to protect the bank's assets from AI enabled cyber threats.

Vacancy Details

The notice lists 5 vacancies across three posts. The number of vacancies and the number of reserved vacancies are provisional and may vary according to the actual requirement of the Bank. The notice's "Out of which" columns for PwBD categories (VI, HI, OC, ID) show no posts for any of the three posts. The notice adds that there is no reservation for Ex-servicemen in Officers' Cadre, and that EWS vacancies are tentative and subject to further directives of the Government of India and the outcome of any litigation.

Pay and Tenure

Period of engagement: Fixed Term Employment (FTE) on contractual basis for an initial period of 3 years, extendable up to 5 years, to be renewed yearly or at shorter intervals subject to performance review and the needs and requirements of the Bank. Remuneration and perquisites: negotiable and not a limiting factor for deserving candidate. The notice does not print an amount. Leave: 30 days per calendar year on pro-rata basis; leave balance is not carried forward and leave encashment is not permitted. Termination: three months' notice or three months' fixed component of the compensation in lieu thereof, from either party, at the sole discretion of the Bank. Place of work: Chennai or as decided by the Bank. The engagement is not to be construed as an offer of employment or a regular employment in the Bank, and ends automatically on completion of the contractual period.
