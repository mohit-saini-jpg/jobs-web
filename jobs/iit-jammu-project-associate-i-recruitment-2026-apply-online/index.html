IIT Jammu Project Associate I Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Department of Biosciences and Bioengineering at Indian Institute of Technology Jammu is recruiting one Project Associate-I (PA-I) for an Anusandhan National Research Foundation (ANRF) project on a bone-on-a-chip device. The post is initially for 36 months, at Rs. 37,000 per month plus 20% HRA as per project norms. A relevant postgraduate degree is needed, and applications are open on the online portal https://apply.iitjammu.ac.in/#/home until 9 December 2026.

About the Project

The research will focus on hydrogel fabrication and characterization, microfluidic device development, and the integration of biomaterials for advanced in-vitro bone models. The notice describes the work as interdisciplinary, at the interface of biomaterials, tissue engineering, microfluidics and biomedical engineering.

Who Can Apply

The eligibility of candidates holding M.Tech./M.E., B.Tech./B.E., M.Sc. or equivalent degrees, and their admissibility for engagement as Project Associate-I (PA-I), is determined under ANRF Office Memorandum No. ANRF/OM/N-01/2024 dated 14.10.2024 and other applicable ANRF guidelines. The eligibility of each candidate is decided in accordance with that Office Memorandum and the applicable ANRF guidelines. Experience in hydrogel synthesis and characterization, biomaterials, or polymer science. Familiarity with soft lithography, microfluidics, tissue engineering, or cell culture techniques. Knowledge of material characterization techniques and experimental data analysis. Strong motivation to work in an interdisciplinary research environment. The upper age limit is 28 years. The notice prints no age cut-off date. As per ANRF norms, the relaxation is 5 years for SC/ST/PwBD/EWS and Women, and

Pay and Tenure

The advertised pay is Rs. 37000/- per month plus 20% HRA as per project norms, for an initial period of 36 months. The notice says the monthly remuneration is as prescribed under the ANRF Office Memorandum, as amended from time to time:
Candidate GroupMonthly Remuneration
Candidates fulfilling the criteria relating to NET, GATE or other eligible national-level examinations under applicable ANRF normsRs. 37,000/- plus applicable HRA
Candidates not fulfilling those criteria, subject to their eligibility for engagement as Project Associate-I under ANRF normsRs. 30,000/- plus applicable HRA

Selection and Interview

Candidates may be shortlisted based on merit and the project's requirements. Having the prescribed qualification does not ensure a call for interview. The interview is conducted for all shortlisted candidates, and only shortlisted candidates are called. The date and time of the interview are informed to shortlisted candidates by e-mail. The interview is held on an online platform. Shortlisted candidates must present an updated CV and original and attested photocopies of mark sheets/certificates supporting their academic qualifications on the interview date.
