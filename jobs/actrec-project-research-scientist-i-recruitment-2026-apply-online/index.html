About ACTREC

ACTREC is the Advanced Centre for Treatment, Research and Education in Cancer of Tata Memorial Centre, at Kharghar, Navi Mumbai. The letterhead describes it as a grant-in-aid institution of the Department of Atomic Energy, Government of India. The notice is issued by the In charge (Academic & Project Cell).

Who Can Apply

The notice lists one post, Project Research Scientist - I (Non-Medical), on project. The notice does not state the funding agency. Post Graduate Degree, including the integrated PG degrees. Candidates should have hands-on experience in stem cell culture, molecular biology techniques, cloning, lentiviral (plasmid) construct designing, optimization, virus production and transduction, gene-editing tools such as CRISPR/Cas9, and flow cytometry. Experience in iPSC culture and differentiation, mammalian cell culture, 3D culture and stable cell line generation is preferred. Prior experience in in-vitro transcription (IVT), mRNA synthesis and production, and mRNA-based cellular differentiation will be an added advantage. Not exceeding 35 years at the time of interview. The notice prints no age relaxation. Candidates should refer to the official notification for any further eligibility details.

Pay and Tenure

The consolidated salary is Rs.72,800/- per month, printed as Rs.56000/- + 30% HRA. The post is for six months and extendable. The work progress of candidates will be monitored, and an extension after six months will depend on satisfactory progress of the work.

Selection and Interview

Only shortlisted candidates will be informed about the interview date, the Zoom link and the timings via email. The notice does not give the interview date or describe how candidates are shortlisted.
