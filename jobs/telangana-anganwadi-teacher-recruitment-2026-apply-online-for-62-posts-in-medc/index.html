Eligibility Criteria

The applicant shall have passed Intermediate (10+2). The candidate shall be 18 years to 35 years of age as on 01.07.2026. Only women candidates are eligible to apply. The candidate must be a local resident of the concerned Anganwadi Centre area for which she applies. In rural areas, she must be a resident of the concerned Gram Panchayat. In urban areas, she must be a resident of the concerned Municipal Ward (GHMC/MMC/CMC). For Anganwadi Teacher posts reserved under the SC/ST Sub-Plan, only eligible SC/ST women candidates belonging to the same Hamlet/Habitation concerned are eligible. A Residence Certificate issued by the Tahsildar is mandatory, and only certificates issued within one year from the date of this notification will be accepted. Candidates belonging to the Community/Category specified against the respective Roster Point are eligible to apply. If there is any change in the can

Eligibility for Persons with Disabilities (PwDs)

The following categories of women candidates with disabilities are also eligible to apply, provided they are capable of performing the duties of an Anganwadi Teacher: Candidates with hearing impairment who can hear with the assistance of hearing aids. Candidates with visual impairment/blindness who can independently perform their duties without the assistance of an escort. Candidates with locomotor disability affecting the upper or lower limbs, provided they are capable of imparting pre-school education and caring for children without difficulty.

Documents Required (Scanned Copies to be Uploaded)

SSC Certificate (Proof of Date of Birth) Community Certificate issued by the Tahsildar Intermediate Marks Memo/Pass Certificate Residence Certificate issued by the Tahsildar (issued within one year from the date of Notification) SADAREM Disability Certificate (where applicable) Husband's Death Certificate (for Widow candidates) Orphan Certificate issued by the Tahsildar (where applicable) Voter Identity Card Aadhaar Card

Certificate Verification

Applicants must submit all relevant certificates along with Gazetted Officer-attested photocopies (2 sets/Xerox) at the concerned ICDS Project Office - i.e., ICDS Alwal, Medchal, or Quthbullapur - from 27.07.2026 (10:30 AM) to 05.08.2026 (5:00 PM), during office working hours. Applications with pending or unsubmitted certificate verification will not be considered.

Modes of Application NOT Accepted

Applications submitted through Post, E-mail, Prajavani, or manual applications submitted directly at the CDPO Office or the Office of the District Welfare Officer will not be considered under any circumstances.

The notification does not specify a written exam, interview, or a detailed selection procedure. It confirms only that candidates are selected against reservation Roster Points specific to each Anganwadi Centre, subject to local candidature and eligibility/document verification. Any additional selection process detail is not mentioned in the official notification.
