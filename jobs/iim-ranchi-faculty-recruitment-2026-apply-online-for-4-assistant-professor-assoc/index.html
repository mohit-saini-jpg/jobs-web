About IIM Ranchi

IIM Ranchi describes itself as an autonomous institution founded by the Ministry of Education, Government of India, in December 2009. It currently offers a two-year MBA, MBA-HRM and MBA-BA, a full-time Ph.D., an Executive Ph.D. (ePh.D.) and an Executive Post Graduate Programme in Management (Executive MBA).

Qualification and Experience

The minimum qualification for all four posts is a Ph.D. or equivalent degree. Candidates are required to have a good publication record in ABDC/ABS journals. Candidates must have made the final submission of their Ph.D. thesis for external review as on the last date of application. For designation-wise shortlisting criteria for experience, the notice refers to the Ministry of Human Resources Development circular F.No.23-1/2008-TS.II dated August 18, 2009, September 16, 2009 and September 22, 2009. The research/teaching/industry experience is expected to be in a relevant area. All qualifications must be from a UGC recognized University/Deemed university or an AICTE approved autonomous institution (wherever applicable); courses from autonomous institutions should be equivalent to the relevant course approved/recognized by the Association of Indian Universities (AIU). Pay and Terms

The notice gives these as the details of pay and the total minimum emoluments admissible at the time of joining. In addition, benefits such as LTC, medical re-imbursement, contribution towards EPF/NPS, faculty development allowance and financial support towards national and international conferences shall be permitted as per Institute norms. Faculty members are permitted to pursue industry consultation with income sharing as per the Institute rules. There is a provision for additional compensation for teaching/training, subject to fulfilling the minimum work-norm of the Institute. Depending on the academic credentials of the candidate, the Institute may consider them for an offer on a contractual basis of one/two/three year(s). Relaxation in qualification and experience, or a higher start in the time scale, may be considered for exceptionally bright candidates.

IIM Ranchi Recruitment 2026 Job Description

The notice gives one set of expectations for all the faculty posts. Selected candidates are expected to: Teach across various programmes. Publish high-quality research papers in leading academic journals. Contribute to academic administration from time to time as per the norms set by the Institute. Engage in institution development. Conduct training programmes. Engage in consultancy work. Candidates should have a strong academic background and an understanding of current developments in their field of expertise.
