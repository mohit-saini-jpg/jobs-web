About PFI Analyst - Research

Power Foundation of India (PFI) is a not-for-profit organisation promoted by leading power sector CPSUs to undertake policy advocacy, capacity building, research, knowledge dissemination, and sectoral development initiatives for the Indian power sector. The organisation functions from its registered office situated at B-28, Qutub Institutional Area, New Delhi 110016.

Who Can Apply

Candidates applying for the Analyst – Research position must fulfill the following criteria: Educational Qualification: Bachelor's Degree in Engineering, preferably in Electrical/Power from a recognized university, and an MBA in Power/Energy/Finance or an M.Tech in an Energy related field. Experience: 2 years of relevant post-qualification professional experience in the power sector, with demonstrated experience in policy research, regulatory analysis, tariff assessment, power markets, or related areas. Applicants must possess experience in analysing power sector policies, regulations, tariff petitions, regulatory orders, and legal judgments, with an understanding of the Indian electricity sector and its regulatory framework. Pay and Tenure

Selected candidates will be appointed on a regular, full-time basis, subject to satisfactory verification of credentials and fulfillment of the organisation's applicable service rules. The CTC of the selected candidate shall be protected, and an additional increase of 10% to 30% over the existing CTC may be offered as per PFI policy, depending upon qualifications, relevant experience, competencies, and overall suitability. Candidates will also be entitled to PF, gratuity, medical/insurance coverage, and other applicable benefits in accordance with the rules and policies of PFI.

PFI Analyst - Research Job Description

The appointed Analyst – Research will report to the Head- Regulatory and Research. Key responsibilities and performance areas include: Conducting research on key power sector policy and regulatory issues, including power markets, retail competition, parallel licensing, contracts for difference, and emerging areas. Preparing policy notes, research papers, analytical reports, briefing notes, and presentations based on detailed research and analysis. Monitoring and analysing policies, regulations, guidelines, and orders issued by MoP, CERC, CEA, SERCs, and other relevant authorities. Examining tariff petitions and regulatory filings of GENCOs, TRANSCOs, and DISCOMs, including assessment of tariff structures, cost components, and financial implications.
