UP NEET UG Round 2 Merit List 2026 Postponed - Quick : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 18/09/2026

The Directorate General of Medical Education and Training (DGME), Uttar Pradesh, has officially postponed the UP NEET UG 2026 Round 2 online counselling process for state quota MBBS and BDS admissions. As per the notification issued on September 17, 2026, the release of the Round 2 merit list and the choice filling process have been put on hold "until further orders."

Steps to Check the Official Postponement Notice

  • Visit the official UP NEET website at upneet.gov.in and dgme.up.gov.in.
  • Go to the Notifications / Latest Announcements section on the homepage.
  • Look for the notice referencing notification number ME-3/2026/1692, dated September 17, 2026.
  • Download the PDF to read the official wording regarding the postponement.
  • Keep checking the same section for the revised schedule, since DGME has not specified a date for this announcement.

Important Instructions for UP NEET UG Round 2 Candidates

  • Choice locking is separate from choice saving: Choices filled between the counselling window must be explicitly "locked." If a candidate saves choices but does not manually lock them before the deadline, the system automatically locks the last saved preference list for seat allotment.
  • No limit on number of college preferences: There is no restriction on how many college options a candidate can add during choice filling. Adding the maximum number of valid, genuine preferences increases the chance of seat allotment.
  • Choices can be edited before locking: As long as a candidate has only clicked "Save Choices" and not "Lock Choices," they can log in multiple times to reorder or add colleges until the choice filling window closes.
  • Verify private college fee structures before locking: Candidates opting for private medical or dental colleges must carefully check the total tuition, hostel, and miscellaneous fees on dgme.up.gov.in before finalizing their choices, since fee details vary by institution.
  • Reporting after seat allotment is mandatory: Candidates allotted a seat in Round 2 must physically report to their assigned medical or dental college with original documents, identity proof, and the allotment letter to complete admission formalities.
