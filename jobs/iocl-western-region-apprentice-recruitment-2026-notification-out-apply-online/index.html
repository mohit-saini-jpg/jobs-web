IOCL Western Region Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many apprentice vacancies has IOCL notified in Western Region?
A
335 vacancies across Technician (99), Trade/ITI (40), Graduate (174), and Data Entry Operator (22) apprentice categories, spread over 7 states/UTs.
Q2What is the last date to apply?
A
4 November 2026, up to 5:00 PM.
Q3Which portal do I use?
A
Technician (Diploma) and Graduate Apprentice candidates use the NATS portal; Trade (ITI) and Data Entry Operator candidates use the NAPS/Apprenticeship India portal.
Q4Is there a written test?
A
No. Selection is purely merit-based on percentage of marks in the qualifying examination, followed by document verification and a medical fitness test.
Q5What is the age limit?
A
18 to 24 years as on 30 September 2026, with relaxation for OBC-NCL, SC/ST and PwBD candidates.
Q6Can PwBD candidates apply for all disciplines?
A
No, PwBD candidates are not eligible for Technician or Trade/ITI disciplines (Codes 101-111); they may apply only for Graduate Apprentice and Data Entry Operator posts.
Q7Can I apply for more than one discipline code?
A
No, applying for more than one discipline code leads to summary rejection of all your applications.
Q8Does this apprenticeship guarantee a job at IOCL?
A
No. IOCL has no obligation to offer employment on completion of the apprenticeship training.
