GMC Jalna Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Government Medical College, Jalna is filling 20 teaching posts on contract: 6 for Professor, 2 for Associate Professor and 12 for Assistant Professor, across 15 medical subjects. Applicants need an MD, MS or DNB in the subject and must be citizens of India living in Maharashtra. You hand the filled form in person at the college office by 5.00 pm on 12 November 2026, and interviews take place on 17 November 2026. The monthly consolidated pay is Rs. 1,00,000 to Rs. 2,00,000, depending on the post.

About GMC Jalna

Government Medical College, Jalna is a Maharashtra Government medical college. It works under the Commissioner, Medical Education, Research and AYUSH, Mumbai, and the Directorate of Medical Education and Research, Mumbai. Its address is Global Gurukul School, opposite Sushiladevi Lawn, Ambad Mantha Road, Jalna - 431213. Its phone number is 02482-299907.

Application Fee

The fee is Rs. 200/- for each application. You need a separate application for each post you apply for.

GMC Jalna Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Candidates are selected through the Departmental Selection Board and the Local Selection Board, by interview. The interview starts at 11.00 am sharp on 17 November 2026 in the Dean's chamber at Government Medical College, Jalna. You must bring the originals of every document you attached to your application. You travel to the interview at your own cost, and no allowance is paid. For Professor and Associate Professor, the college accepts the applications, interviews the candidates and then sends the eligible candidates' applications to the Commissioner, Medical Education and Research, Mumbai, for the appointment process.

Service Conditions

The appointments follow Government Resolution LEC-2024/Pr.Kr.181/Vaiseva-3 dated 21 August 2024 and Government Resolution MES-1508/94/08/MED dated 07.09.2011.

GMC Jalna Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1Which subjects have Assistant Professor vacancies at GMC Jalna?
A
There are 12 Assistant Professor posts: Anatomy 2, Biochemistry 2, Pharmacology 2, and 1 each in Physiology, Medicine, Skin & VD, ENT, Anaesthesiology and Radiology.
Q2Can I send the application by post or email?
A
No. You must hand in the filled form in person at the inward section of the college office, Global Gurukul School, opposite Sushiladevi Lawn, Ambad Mantha Road, Jalna - 431213.
Q3How much fee do I pay if I apply for two posts?
A
The fee is Rs. 200/- per application, and you apply separately for each post. Two posts means two applications, at Rs. 200/- each.
Q4What experience do I need for the Professor post?
A
At least 3 years as Associate Professor in an NMC-eligible medical college. You also need 4 national and international research papers, 2 of them from your time as Associate Professor.
Q5Do I need to be from Maharashtra?
A
Yes. You must be a citizen of India and a resident of Maharashtra, and you attach a domicile certificate with your form.
Q6Can I resign partway through the contract?
A
Not before the end of at least one academic session after your appointment. You must give an affidavit to this effect before you are appointed.
Q7What should I bring to the interview on 17 November 2026?
A
The originals of every document you attached to your application. The interview starts at 11.00 am sharp in the Dean's chamber, and no travel allowance is paid.
Q8Which training courses must Assistant Professor candidates have?
A
The basic course in biomedical research and the basic course in Medical Education Technology from an NMC-designated institution. You should have completed them or be required to undergo them.
Q9Will this contract post become permanent?
A
No. The appointment gives no claim to a regular post. It lasts 364 days or until a regular candidate is available, whichever comes first.
