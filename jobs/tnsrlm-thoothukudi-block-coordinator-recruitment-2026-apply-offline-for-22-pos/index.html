TNSRLM Thoothukudi Block Coordinator Vacancy 2026

The notification lists 22 vacancies in total. They are distributed across 10 blocks of Thoothukudi district. Alwarthirunagari, Kayathar, Kovilpatti and Pudur have the highest number of posts, with 3 vacancies each. Category-wise or reservation-wise vacancy details are not specified in the notification.

TNSRLM Thoothukudi Block Coordinator Important Dates

The notification gives only one clear deadline. The application start date is not mentioned, and no date for the exam or interview is announced yet.

TNSRLM Thoothukudi Block Coordinator Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must meet all of the following conditions given in the notification. Gender: Women candidates only. Residence: The applicant must be a resident of the block in Thoothukudi district where the vacancy is available. Age: Must have completed 18 years and must not be above 35 years, as on 1 July 2026. Qualification: Must meet the educational and computer skill requirements explained in the next section. Driving licence: Must hold a licence for a two-wheeler (இருசக்கர வாகனம்). Experience: Must have a minimum of five years of experience in people's organisation-based projects (மக்கள் அமைப்பு சார்ந்த திட்டங்கள்).

TNSRLM Thoothukudi Block Coordinator Qualification

The notification mentions an undergraduate degree (இளங்கலை பட்டம்) and computer skills. A candidate must satisfy one of the two options below. The specific degree name for Option 1 is not specified; the notification refers to an undergraduate degree in general.

TNSRLM Thoothukudi Block Coordinator Age Limit

The age limit is calculated as on 1 July 2026. Candidates must have completed 18 years and must be within a maximum age of 35 years.

TNSRLM Thoothukudi Block Coordinator Salary

The post carries a consolidated monthly payment of ₹12,000, along with a travel allowance of ₹750, making a total of ₹12,750. The notification does not state whether this amount is paid per month. The contract period is also not specified.
