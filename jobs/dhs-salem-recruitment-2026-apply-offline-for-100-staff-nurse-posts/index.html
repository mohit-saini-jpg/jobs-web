DHS Salem Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The DHS Salem Recruitment 2026 fills Staff Nurse posts created under the National Health Mission for the CEmONC & SNCU centres in Salem district and, under the Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA) project, for the newly built Government Ammapet Peripheral Hospital, Salem. The official notification does not specify the application fee.

About DHS Salem

The District Health Society, Salem works under the Department of Public Health and Preventive Medicine and is headed by the District Collector, Salem as Chairman, with the District Health Officer, Salem as Executive Secretary. The Staff Nurse posts in this notice serve the Comprehensive Emergency Obstetric and Newborn Care centres and Special Newborn Care Units (CEmONC & SNCU) under the Joint Director of Health Services, Salem and the Dean, Government Mohan Kumaramangalam Medical College Hospital, Salem, and the new Ammapet Peripheral Hospital under Salem Medical College and Hospital.

Both posts carry the same printed qualification. Valid registration with the Tamil Nadu Nurses and Midwives Council is mandatory before the date of notification. A Tamil Nadu Nursing Council registration certificate, renewed up to date, must be submitted with the application. DHS Salem Selection Process 2026

Posts are filled on the basis of merit marks. The 82 Contract Staff Nurse (CEmONC & SNCU) posts at S.No. 1 are filled first. After that, the 18 Contract Staff Nurse (Government Peripheral Hospital, Ammapet, Salem) posts at S.No. 2 are filled. The notice does not mention a written test or an interview.

DHS Salem Recruitment 2026 Important Instructions

The posts are filled on a contract basis and are purely temporary. A candidate allotted a post must submit a willingness letter and an undertaking letter. The number of posts mentioned is subject to change. The notice is signed by the Executive Secretary, District Health Society (District Health Officer, Salem) and the Chairman, District Health Society (District Collector, Salem).
