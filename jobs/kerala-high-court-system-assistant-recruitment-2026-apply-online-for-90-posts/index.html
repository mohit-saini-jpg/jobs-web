Kerala High Court System Assistant Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The High Court of Kerala will fill 90 posts of System Assistant on contract in the District Judiciary throughout Kerala, at a consolidated pay of ₹24,590 per month, for an initial term of 2 years. Candidates need a Government approved 3 years Diploma in Computer/Electronics or a B.Sc. (Computer Science)/BCA (or a higher qualification), plus at least 2 years of hardware troubleshooting experience. Applications are accepted only online through the High Court's Recruitment Portal (hckrecruitment.keralacourts.in) from 20 November 2026 to 14 December 2026, and no application fee is charged.

About Kerala High Court System Assistant

The High Court of Kerala, located at Ernakulam-682031, is recruiting System Assistants to provide technical support to the system and services in the District Judiciary throughout Kerala. The notification (reference HCKL/7863/2026-REC3-HC KERALA, dated 10/11/2026) is issued by the Registrar (Recruitment). It invites applications from qualified Indian citizens.

Vacancy Details

The notice lists 90 System Assistant vacancies across 14 judicial districts. No category-wise break-up is printed. Candidates must indicate their preference of the district in which they opt to work. Selected candidates will be allotted districts based on the rank list and as per the decision of the High Court. The High Court holds the right to appoint, relocate or transfer appointed candidates based on requirement, to supervise their activities and to take necessary action if they fail to perform their duties promptly.

Pay and Tenure

Remuneration: ₹24,590/- per month (consolidated). The appointment will be on contract basis, initially for a period of 2 years. It may be extended based on performance, requirements and orders of the High Court, subject to Administrative Sanction from the Government for extension. The appointment will be on a temporary basis, and candidates will not be entitled to any preferential claim for future appointment. A person appointed on contract basis will not have any claim for regularisation under any circumstances. The rank list will be valid for two years from the date of publication of the final rank list. The High Court may extend it in light of Government sanction for continuing the post, or until a fresh rank list is published, whichever is earlier. Vacancies arising during the validity period shall be filled from the list.

Kerala High Court System Assistant Job Description

According to the notification, System Assistants are appointed to provide technical support to the system and services in the District Judiciary throughout Kerala.

Selection and Interview

Selection is based on screening/shortlisting of the applications followed by an interview. Meeting the eligibility criteria alone does not entitle a candidate to be called for interview, and candidates will be called only after scrutiny of the uploaded documents. The High Court reserves the right to alter the mode of selection. Call letters must be downloaded by logging in to the candidate profile on the Recruitment Portal. They will be ready two weeks before the interview, and candidates will be informed by SMS/e-mail. A candidate unable to download the call letter should contact the High Court at 0484-2562235. If no communication about non-receipt reaches the High Court at least one week before the interview, the candidate alone is responsible. Candidates should visit the recruitment portal at least once a week to know the interview schedule. Admission to the interview is provisional.
