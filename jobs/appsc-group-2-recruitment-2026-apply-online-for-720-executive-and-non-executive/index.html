APPSC Group 2 Selection Process 2026

The selection process includes a Screening Test, Main Examination, qualifying Computer Proficiency Test and applicable certificate verification. Screening Test: Offline OMR-based objective examination for shortlisting. Main Examination: Computer-based objective examination carrying 300 marks. Computer Proficiency Test: Qualifying test for shortlisted candidates. Certificate Verification: Verification of qualifications, age, category and local status. Final Selection: Merit based on Main Examination marks, subject to qualifying CPT and fulfilling all eligibility conditions. The Commission proposes to admit candidates to the Main Examination in a ratio of 50 times the number of vacancies , irrespective of community, subject to the notification's provisions.

APPSC Group 2 Local Reservation Rules 2026

The notification provides 95% local reservation for designated zonal and district-cadre positions under the applicable Andhra Pradesh Presidential Order, 2025. For zonal-cadre posts, Andhra Pradesh is divided into six zones. Local candidature is determined using the study or residence conditions specified in the notification, generally involving the relevant four-year or seven-year period ending with Class VII. Important: Candidates who studied in educational institutions must ordinarily provide study certificates. Residence certificates are not accepted as replacements where the notification requires study certificates.

APPSC Group 2 Reservation 2026

Reservation is provided according to Andhra Pradesh Government rules for SC, ST, BC, EWS and applicable horizontal reservation categories. Women: 33 1/3% horizontal reservation, as applicable. Meritorious Sportspersons: 3% horizontal reservation under the notified rules. Ex-Servicemen: 2% horizontal reservation for notified Non-Executive categories. Persons with Benchmark Disabilities: Reservation according to the post-specific suitability rules. SC/ST/BC/EWS: Applicable vertical reservation according to Government rules. The notification specifies that candidates belonging to reserved categories of other states are not entitled to Andhra Pradesh's state-specific community reservation benefits.
