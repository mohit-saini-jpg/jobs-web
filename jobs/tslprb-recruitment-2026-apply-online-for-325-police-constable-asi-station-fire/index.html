TSLPRB Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection stages differ slightly depending on the post group. Preliminary Written Test (PWT): One objective paper, 3 hours - Arithmetic & Test of Reasoning/Mental Ability (100 questions) and General Studies (100 questions), 200 marks total. Qualifying marks: 30% OC/EWS, 25% BC, 20% SC/ST/Ex-Servicemen. Physical Measurement Test (PMT) / Physical Efficiency Test (PET): 1600m run (Men)/800m run (Women), height measurement, Long Jump/Shot-put. The run is qualifying only for Post Codes 11, 17, 18, 19, while it carries marks for Post Codes 12, 13, 14, 16. Final Written Examination (FWE): Four papers of 3 hours each - English, Telugu/Urdu (both qualifying), Arithmetic & Test of Reasoning/Mental Ability, and General Studies. Qualifying marks: 40% OC/EWS, 35% BC, 30% SC/ST/Ex-Servicemen per paper. Certificates Verification. Final Written Examination Pattern: Papers I and II are qualifying only. F

TSLPRB Recruitment 2026 Reservation Details

Standard categories apply: BC-A, BC-B, BC-C, BC-D, BC-E, SC, ST, Ex-Servicemen, Meritorious Sports Persons (MSP), and EWS, per Rule 22 of the Telangana State & Subordinate Service Rules, 1996. Women's Reservation (ASI FPB and applicable SI-group posts): 33⅓% horizontal reservation across all categories, including EWS. Special Category Quotas - PC Mechanic: 10% Home Guards, 3% Children of Police Personnel (CPP), 1% NCC. Special Category Quotas - PC Driver: 20% Home Guards, 3% CPP, 1% NCC. Local Reservation (ASI FPB): 95% of vacancies in each Contiguous Zonal Cadre are filled from Local Candidates of the respective zones under the 2018 Local Cadres Order.

TSLPRB Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Visit the official TSLPRB website: www.tgprb.in
  • 2Register using your mobile number as the User ID.
  • 3Select the notification(s) and post(s) you wish to apply for.
  • 4Note the applicable fee and complete payment via Credit Card, Debit Card, or Net Banking.
  • 5Log in and fill in Part-I of the Application Form.
  • 6Scan and upload your passport-size photograph and specimen signature as a single JPG file (30 KB-100 KB).
  • 7Review all entries, confirm via OTP sent to your registered mobile number, and submit Part-I.
  • 8Download and print the generated Part-I application form in PDF.
  • 9Candidates who qualify the Preliminary Written Test (SI Civil/RSI/SPF SI/SFO/Deputy Jailor group) - or, for PC Mechanic/Driver and ASI FPB (which have no Preliminary Written Test), at the appropriate later stage - must revisit the website to submit Part-II along with scanned certificates/documents.
  • 10Download and retain a copy of the final submitted form for future reference.
  • 11Note: Candidates should not register more than one application form, since a single registration collects information across the linked notifications (Rc. Nos. 189, 225, 234, and 279 of 2026, all dated 29-07-2026).

TSLPRB Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many total vacancies are there in TSLPRB Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 325 vacancies in total: 275 for SI Civil/RSI/SPF SI/Station Fire Officer/Deputy Jailor, 27 for PC Mechanic and PC Driver combined, and 23 for ASI, Fingerprint Bureau.
Q2What is the last date to apply for TSLPRB Recruitment 2026?
A
The Last date of online application is 09 September 2026
Q3What is the age limit across TSLPRB Recruitment 2026 posts?
A
21–25 years (as on 1st July 2026) for SI Civil, RSI, SPF SI, SFO, Deputy Jailor, and ASI FPB; 18–22 years for PC Mechanic and PC Driver. An additional 5-year relaxation applies for uniformed services, plus further category-based relaxations.
Q4Are women eligible for all TSLPRB Recruitment 2026 posts?
A
Women are eligible for SI Civil, RSI (AR), SPF SI, Station Fire Officer, Deputy Jailor (Women), and ASI Fingerprint Bureau. PC Mechanic and PC Driver posts are open to men only, and Deputy Jailor (Women) and RSI (SAR CPL/TGSP)/Deputy Jailor (Men) are gender-specific as noted above.
Q5What is the educational qualification required across posts?
A
A degree (any discipline, UGC-recognized) for SI Civil/RSI/SPF SI/Deputy Jailor; the same plus an LMV licence for Station Fire Officer; SSC + ITI or Intermediate + HPMV/HGV licence for PC Driver; SSC + ITI for PC Mechanic; and a B.E./B.Tech in IT/CS (or related) or B.Sc. in CS/Computer Applications for ASI Fingerprint Bureau.
Q6What is the selection process for TSLPRB Recruitment 2026?
A
SI Civil/RSI/SPF SI/SFO/Deputy Jailor: Preliminary Written Test → PMT/PET → Final Written Examination → Certificate Verification. PC Mechanic/Driver and ASI FPB have no Preliminary Written Test: PMT/PET → Trade/Driving Test (or directly Written Examination for ASI FPB) → Certificate Verification.
Q7What is the application fee for TSLPRB Recruitment 2026?
A
The exact fee is not disclosed in the notifications; it varies by category (OC/BC, SC/ST) and will be shown on the online application portal at www.tgprb.in.
Q8Can a candidate apply for more than one TSLPRB post in this recruitment cycle?
A
Yes. Candidates can apply for multiple posts among the linked notifications (SI Civil, RSI, SPF SI, SFO, Deputy Jailor, ASI FPB, PC Civil, PC Mechanic, PC Driver — all dated 29-07-2026) and are eligible for a PMT/PET fee rebate on additional posts.
Q9What is the pay scale across TSLPRB Recruitment 2026 posts?
A
SI Civil/RSI/SPF SI: ₹42,300–1,15,270. Station Fire Officer/Deputy Jailor: ₹38,890–1,12,510. ASI Fingerprint Bureau: ₹33,750–99,310. PC Mechanic/PC Driver: ₹24,280–72,850.
Q10Is there a Preliminary Written Test for all posts?
A
No. Only the SI Civil, RSI, SPF SI, Station Fire Officer, and Deputy Jailor posts have a Preliminary Written Test. PC Mechanic, PC Driver, and ASI Fingerprint Bureau do not.
