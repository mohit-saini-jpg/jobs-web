About GRID India Intern

Grid Controller of India Limited (GRID-INDIA), a Miniratna-I & Schedule “A” CPSE, is a wholly owned Government of India Enterprise under the Ministry of Power. GRID-INDIA has been entrusted with the responsibility of operating the National Load Despatch Centre (NLDC) and the five Regional Load Despatch Centres (RLDCs). To address the growing need for specialized expertise due to renewable energy integration and new grid technologies, GRID-INDIA has introduced विद्युत प्रज्ञा - a specialised internship programme for Power System Simulation Studies.

Pay and Tenure

The selected interns will receive an all-inclusive stipend of ₹50,000 Per Month, and no other allowances, benefits or emoluments shall be admissible. The duration of engagement is six (06) months, which is further extendable by three (03) months on mutual agreement, on the same terms & conditions.

GRID India Intern Job Description

Interns will undergo training and work in the identified work area of Power System Simulation Studies, gaining structured exposure to practical applications of simulation and modelling tools in the Indian power system.

Selection and Interaction

The applicants shall be shortlisted on the basis of their fulfilment of prescribed essential eligibility criteria; preference will be given to candidates possessing desirable qualifications. Shortlisted candidates shall be called for an interaction at NLDC, New Delhi. No TA/ DA shall be paid to the candidates appearing for the interaction.
