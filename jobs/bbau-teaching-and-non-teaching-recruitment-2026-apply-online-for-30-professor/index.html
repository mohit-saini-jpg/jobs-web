BBAU Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Screening of applications as per eligibility criteria; the ratio of candidates called for interview to vacancies is generally up to 1:15. Written test with an objective-type qualifying paper (Paper I, 100 marks, minimum 40% qualifying marks) and a descriptive-type paper (Paper II, 100 marks); only candidates clearing Paper I are evaluated in Paper II. Candidates securing 50% marks in Paper II are called for the skill test/interview, wherever applicable. Skill test carries 50 marks with a minimum qualifying score of 25. The final merit list is based on Paper II (descriptive test) marks, subject to qualifying the skill test. Interview marks, wherever applicable, carry 20% weightage and are added to the Paper II/single written test score for the final merit list. The University may, at its discretion, hold a single written (descriptive) test and skill test instead of the two-stage process,
