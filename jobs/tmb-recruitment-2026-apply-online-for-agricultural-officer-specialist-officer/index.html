TMB Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection for all posts is through a Personal Interview only - there is no written examination. Shortlisted candidates will be called for interview through Direct mode or Video Conferencing, and the mode, date, and time of the interview will be communicated to eligible candidates individually. The Bank's decision on eligibility, conduct of interview, and selection is final, and no correspondence in this regard will be entertained.

TMB Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Visit the Bank's careers website, https://apps.tmbdigital.bank.in/tmb_careers/, and download the detailed advertisement for the relevant post before applying.
  • 2Click "Apply Online" and register for the specific post using a valid personal e-mail ID and mobile number.
  • 3On successful registration, an activation link will be sent to the registered e-mail ID, and a Registration Number and Password will be sent to the registered mobile number - note these carefully, as they are required to proceed.
  • 4Click the activation link sent to your e-mail, which will direct you to the careers portal.
  • 5Log in using the Application/Registration Number and password, and fill in all details carefully, clicking "Save/Continue" on every page. Ensure your name and your father's/husband's name are spelt exactly as they appear on your certificates/mark sheets.
  • 6Upload your photograph (390 x 520 pixels preferred, maximum 120 KB) and signature (240 x 240 pixels preferred, maximum 60 KB, not in capital letters) in the specified format.
  • 7Upload the following enclosures, each not exceeding 250 KB: self-attested copy of SSLC or Higher Secondary Mark Sheet/Transfer Certificate (for age proof), self-attested copy of Degree Certificate(s), Experience Certificate(s) and Relieving Letter for previous employment, and last month's salary slip.
  • 8Review all details, then click "Confirm/Submit" - a message confirming "Registration is successful" will be displayed on successful submission.
  • 9A copy of the system-generated application form will be sent to your registered e-mail; take a printout for future reference.
