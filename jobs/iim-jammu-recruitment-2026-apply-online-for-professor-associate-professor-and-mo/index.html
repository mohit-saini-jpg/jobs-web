About the Institute

Indian Institute of Management (IIM) Jammu is an Institute of National Importance established by the Government of India, Ministry of Education in 2016 at Jammu. The Institute says it is looking for dedicated and committed faculty members who believe in institution building and have passion for excellence. Candidates should have a demonstrated ability to make a relevant intellectual contribution to experienced and potential managers through teaching, training, research and published work. They should also have a strong academic background and understanding of current developments in the related field.

Pay and Terms

The scales for Professor and Associate Professor are under the 7th Pay Commission w.e.f. 1 January 2016. The pay carries allowances such as DA (currently 60% of basic), Leased Accommodation/HRA (20% of basic), LTC, Medical Allowance, Term Insurance, Children Education Allowance, NPS, Reimbursement of Telephone Bills, Incentive for Research Publications, Faculty Development Fund for attending National and International Conferences, Membership of Professional Bodies etc. as per IIM Jammu Rules. Faculty members are permitted to pursue MDPs and Management Consulting as per IIM Jammu Rules. For regular appointment, there would be a probation period of 2 years. For contractual appointment, depending on the academic credentials of the candidate, the Institute may consider an offer on a contractual basis of one/two years. IIM Jammu Recruitment 2026 Job Description

Faculty members are expected to carry out research, design and teach courses, engage in institution development, conduct training programmes and undertake consultancy. Senior academicians and professionals are required at IIM Jammu for assignments as consultant in developing the academic and research programs, academic administration, taking classes at degree awarding programs, organising senior level management development programs (MDPs) and conducting consulting assignments. The job profile is: Senior Consultant will be given responsibility to head a 'Centre of Excellence' or any major activity of the Institute and may be designated as Head of the Centre or the Activity. He/she will teach a minimum two courses in UG/PG programs, conduct at least one MDP and organise one international conference per year on contemporary issues relevant to the Institute.

IIM Jammu Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Shortlisted candidates will be invited for interview before the Faculty Selection Committee. Papers published only in the journal categories listed above are considered for shortlisting of applications for regular/contractual positions. Mere fulfilment of the qualifications and experience does not entitle a candidate to be called for an interview. The Institute will prepare an area-wise merit list, valid for one year, and the appointment letter will be issued as per the requirement of the Institute. Candidates called for interview to fill a vacancy may be considered for a lower post if otherwise found suitable. The interview date and venue are not specified in the notice.

IIM Jammu Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is the last date to apply for IIM Jammu faculty recruitment 2026?
A
The last date for receipt of online applications is 10 December 2026, 5:00 PM.
Q2Which posts are covered by IIM Jammu Advt. No. IIMJ/Advt./Faculty-Rect./2026/02?
A
The advertisement covers Professor, Associate Professor, Professor of Practice, Associate Professor of Practice and Senior Consultants, in the areas listed in the notice.
Q3What is the qualification for Professor at IIM Jammu?
A
A Ph.D. in the appropriate branch with first class or equivalent in the preceding degree, and minimum 10 years of post-Ph.D. experience, of which at least 4 years at Associate Professor level in the institutions named in the notice.
Q4What is the experience required for Associate Professor?
A
Six years of post-Ph.D. experience, of which 3 years should be as Assistant Professor at Level-12 or equivalent. Industry candidates (Government / PSU / Research Organization) need 6 years of post-Ph.D. experience at a level equivalent to Assistant Professor.
Q5What is the salary of Professor and Associate Professor at IIM Jammu?
A
Professor is paid Rs. 1,59,100 - 2,20,200 (Level 14A) and Associate Professor Rs. 1,39,600 - 2,11,300 (Level 13A2), with allowances such as DA, HRA and medical allowance as per IIM Jammu Rules.
Q6What is the pay and age condition for Senior Consultants?
A
Senior Consultants receive consolidated emoluments of Rs. 1,50,000 per month. The notice speaks of eminent persons above 55 years of age with minimum 20 years of experience, and the upper age limit to serve the Institute is 70 years.
Q7How do I apply for the IIM Jammu faculty posts?
A
Apply online through the Online Application for Faculty Recruitment link on www.iimj.ac.in before 10 December 2026, 5:00 PM.
Q8How are candidates selected?
A
Applications are shortlisted on the basis of papers published in the listed journal categories, and shortlisted candidates are invited for interview before the Faculty Selection Committee. An area-wise merit list valid for one year is prepared.
Q9What is the tenure of Professor of Practice?
A
A Professor of Practice is appointed initially for one/two years, renewable every year for a maximum period of up to 5 years based on performance. The retirement age is 65 years.
Q10Do candidates who applied earlier in the last year need to apply again?
A
No, they need not apply again unless there is a substantial addition in research publications as per IIM Jammu rules.
