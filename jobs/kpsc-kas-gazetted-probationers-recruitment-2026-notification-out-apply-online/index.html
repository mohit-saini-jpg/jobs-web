KPSC Gazetted Probationers Eligibility Criteria

As per Rule 7 of the Karnataka Recruitment of Gazetted Probationers (Appointment by Competitive Examinations) Rules, 1997 (as amended by the 8th Amendment Rules, 2010), no candidate is eligible for recruitment under these rules unless they possess a Bachelor's Degree or Master's Degree awarded by a University established by law in India , or an equivalent qualification. Candidates who have appeared for an examination that would render them educationally qualified, but whose results have not yet been declared, are also eligible for admission to the Preliminary Examination; however, they must produce proof of passing the qualifying examination along with their Main Examination application, failing which they will not be admitted to the Main Examination. KPSC Gazetted Probationers Selection Process

Selection for the Gazetted Probationers posts under this notification follows a two-stage process as prescribed under the Gazetted Probationers Rules, 1997: a Preliminary Examination, followed by a Main Examination and Personality Test (interview) for candidates who qualify. The Preliminary Examination is objective type (multiple choice) and consists of two papers, each of 200 marks and 2 hours' duration (400 marks total). It is a screening test only; marks scored in the Preliminary Examination are not counted towards the final merit. There is negative marking of 0.25 marks (one-fourth) for every wrong answer in the Preliminary Examination. Candidates are admitted to the Main Examination in a ratio of 1:15 against notified vacancies, in order of merit, subject to category-wise representation.
