About AIIMS Bibinagar

The All India Institute of Medical Sciences, Bibinagar, Hyderabad describes itself as "An INI under the aegis of Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Govt. of India". It is located in the Hyderabad Metropolitan Region (HMR), Telangana-508126. These Senior Resident posts are filled under the Government of India's Residency Scheme.

Vacancy Details

There are 32 Senior Resident (Non-Academic) vacancies across 20 departments. Each department's category-wise vacancies are shown below. Notes on the vacancies: The vacancies are provisional and subject to variation. The Executive Director, AIIMS Bibinagar reserves the right to vary the vacancies, including reserved vacancies, as per Govt. of India rules, circulars and requirements. If suitable EWS candidates are not found, the EWS posts will not be carried forward or treated as backlog vacancies. UR candidates may also be allowed to apply provisionally against the EWS posts, and they may be considered if suitable EWS candidates are not found. Reservation will be as per Government of India Policy. If SC/ST/OBC and EWS candidates are not available for a post, that post will be filled for 89 days on contract basis from the available candidates, whatever their category.

Pay and Tenure

The appointment is for 89 days or up to the next regular interview, whichever is earlier. It gives no right to claim permanent absorption in the institute or a continued contractual appointment. The Institute may renew or end the appointment based on its evaluation of the candidate's performance. The post is a tenure engagement of a maximum of 89 days. Including any earlier engagement, it must not exceed the prescribed period for SR ship. Service as a Senior Resident in a previous appointment will be counted together with the present engagement at AIIMS Bibinagar. Leave will be as per the Central Residency Scheme and Institute policy. The appointee will not get benefits such as Provident Fund, Pension, Gratuity, Seniority, Promotion or any other benefit available to regularly appointed Government servants. The appointment is full time, and private practice of any kind is prohibited. Eith

AIIMS Bibinagar Senior Resident Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1UR/OBC/EWS candidates pay the application fee of Rs.1770 (including 18% GST) plus transaction charges into the AIIMS Bibinagar bank account given below. SC/ST/PwBD/Women candidates pay no application fee. The cut-off date for paying the fee is 3 November 2026.
  • 2Fill in the online application form (Google form), available on www.aiimsbibinagar.edu.in. The notice also gives a direct link, "Google form application for SR(NA)".
  • 3Submit the Google form by 3 November 2026, up to 5:00 PM. Late and incomplete applications will be summarily rejected.
  • 4Fill in the application form in Annexure-I (personal details in capital letters) and attach a recent passport-size photo.
  • 5Bring the filled Annexure-I form, the original documents and one set of self-attested photocopies to the document verification and interview.
  • 6The application fee will not be refunded under any circumstances once paid. Candidates working in Govt. or Semi-Govt. PSUs must apply through proper channel.
  • 7The Annexure-I form asks for the advertisement number, the department applied for, the post (Senior Resident (Non-Academic), 89 days tenure), full name, father's name, correspondence and permanent addresses with PIN code, email, phone and alternate number, date of birth (with a supporting document), nationality, state, PwBD type and percentage, and category. It also asks for educational qualifications (10th, 12th, MBBS/BDS/M.Sc., MD/MDS/MS/Ph.D., DM/DNB/M.Ch, any other, with the number of extra attempts), work experience, publications in indexed national and international journals, and the minimum time needed to join. The form ends with a signed declaration.

AIIMS Bibinagar Senior Resident Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in AIIMS Bibinagar Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 32 Senior Resident (Non-Academic) vacancies in 20 departments: UR 11, EWS 04, OBC 08, SC 06 and ST 03. General Medicine has the most, with 4 posts.
Q2What is the last date to apply for the AIIMS Bibinagar Senior Resident posts?
A
The last date to submit the online Google form is 3 November 2026, up to 5:00 PM. Late or incomplete applications will be summarily rejected.
Q3What qualification is needed for AIIMS Bibinagar Senior Resident (Non-Academic)?
A
Each department asks for its own postgraduate medical degree, for example MD/DNB in General Medicine or MS/DNB in Orthopaedics. Some departments also accept a DM/M.Ch/DNB. For Anatomy and Biochemistry, non-medical candidates (Senior Demonstrators) need an M.Sc./M.Biotech in the subject concerned and a Ph.D in the subject concerned or an allied subject.
Q4What is the age limit for AIIMS Bibinagar Recruitment 2026?
A
The upper age limit is 45 years as on the last date of submission of application, 3 November 2026. The maximum relaxation is 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC, and 10, 13 or 15 years for PwBD candidates in the General, OBC or SC/ST category.
Q5What is the salary of a Senior Resident at AIIMS Bibinagar?
A
Medical candidates get Pay Level 11 of the 7th CPC pay matrix, with an entry pay of Rs.67700/- plus NPA and other usual allowances. Non-medical candidates with M.Sc. and Ph.D. get Pay Level 10, with an entry pay of Rs. 56100/- plus other usual allowances.
Q6What is the application fee for AIIMS Bibinagar Senior Resident posts?
A
UR/OBC/EWS candidates pay Rs.1770 (including 18% GST) plus transaction charges into the AIIMS Bibinagar account at Bank of Baroda. SC/ST/PwBD/Women candidates pay no application fee, and the fee is not refundable.
Q7How do I apply for AIIMS Bibinagar Recruitment 2026?
A
Submit the online Google form, available on www.aiimsbibinagar.edu.in, by 3 November 2026 up to 5:00 PM. Then bring the filled Annexure-I form, the original documents and one set of self-attested photocopies to the interview.
Q8How will AIIMS Bibinagar select Senior Residents?
A
Selection is based on an interview held on 16 and 17 November 2026 in the Mini Board Room, 04th Floor, Academic Block, AIIMS Bibinagar. Before the interview, documents are verified in the Examination Hall, II Floor, Academic Block, with reporting at 8.30 AM.
Q9Is the AIIMS Bibinagar Senior Resident (Non-Academic) job permanent?
A
No. The appointment is for 89 days or up to the next regular interview, whichever is earlier. It gives no right to claim permanent absorption or a continued contractual appointment.
Q10Do government employees need an NOC for the AIIMS Bibinagar interview?
A
Yes. Candidates in service in any Government institution must produce a No Objection Certificate from their employer at the interview. Without it they will not be allowed to take part in the interview.
