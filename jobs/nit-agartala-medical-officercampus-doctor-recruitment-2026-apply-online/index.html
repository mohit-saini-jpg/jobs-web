Qualification and Experience

The desirable qualification is a Postgraduate Qualification, preferably MD in General Medicine, or an equivalent qualification included in any of the Schedules to the Indian Medical Council Act, 1956 (102 of 1956), with registration in a State Medical Register or the Indian Medical Register. The medical qualifications held by candidates should have the recognition of the Medical Council of India. Retired Medical Officers up to 62 years of age may also apply. The age limit for the NIT Agartala Medical Officer/Campus Doctor post is a maximum of 35 years, reckoned as on 31-10-2026. Retired personnel not exceeding 62 years of age as on 31-10-2026 may also be considered. The notice does not list any category-wise age relaxation. For the full eligibility conditions of the post, candidates should check the official notification.

Pay and Terms

The Medical Officer/Campus Doctor gets Rs.70,000/- per month consolidated . For official tours or visits, TA/DA will be admissible as per institute rules. No other additional perks or benefits shall be admissible, and no other service benefits, including benefits arising out of any past service, shall be admissible. The engagement shall initially be for a period of one year and may be renewed annually, subject to satisfactory performance and the requirement of the Institute. The total period of the initial engagement and subsequent renewals shall not exceed three years. The engagement is purely contractual in nature and for a fixed tenure. It is not against any sanctioned post. The selected candidate shall not be entitled to any benefits applicable to regular employees and shall have no claim for regular appointment, absorption, continuation or any other form of employment in NIT Agartal
