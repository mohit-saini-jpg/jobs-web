UPESSC Assistant Teacher (Urban) Recruitment 2026 Exam Pattern & Selection Process

Selection for both posts is based on a Merit List prepared from a written examination of 360 marks , comprising 120 objective-type (MCQ) questions . Exam duration: 2 hours . Each question has 4 options, with only one correct answer to be marked on the OMR sheet using a black ball-point pen. Marking scheme: 3 marks awarded for each correct answer; 1 mark deducted for each wrong or multiple answer (negative marking). The question paper will be bilingual - in both Hindi and English. If the exam is held in multiple shifts, a score Normalization process will be applied (as per Commission notice No. Shiksha Seva Aayog/2018/2025-26 dated 26.02.2026) to enable fair comparison across shifts; only normalized scores will be used for selection. UPESSC UP PRT Assistant Teacher Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Visit the official website, www.upessc.up.gov.in.
  • 2Complete One Time Registration (OTR) - mandatory before filling the application; OTR done on any other/older portal will not be valid.
  • 3Use the online payment gateway to pay the applicable examination fee via Credit/Debit Card or Net Banking.
  • 4Fill in the application form carefully and completely, in English only, indicating your preference between the two school-type options (Urban / Sambaddh Primary) - this preference cannot be changed after the deadline.
  • 5Upload a scanned signature on a white background (JPG/JPEG, 10 KB–200 KB, 140x110 pixel resolution) and a scanned passport-size photograph (JPG/JPEG, 30 KB–300 KB, 300–600 pixel resolution).
  • 6Upload all required certificates/documents in PDF format, each between 50 KB and 500 KB in size.
  • 7Review all entered details carefully - after final submission, changes to category, sub-category, date of birth, or gender will not be permitted.
  • 8Use the correction window (until 19.10.2026) only for correcting genuine errors; fees paid against an erroneous application are non-refundable and non-adjustable.
  • 9Submit the application and retain 3 printed copies for future reference - 2 of these copies, along with the required documents, must be submitted at the time of Document Verification.
