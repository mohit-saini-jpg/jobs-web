Malegaon Municipal Corporation Recruitment 2026 Notification PDF

Malegaon Municipal Corporation has officially released the recruitment notification for the appointment of 63 candidates against 18 medical and paramedical posts at N.N. The notification includes complete details on post-wise vacancies, educational qualifications, experience requirements, age limits, monthly honorarium, interview dates and timings, selection process, required documents and terms and conditions of temporary appointment. Malegaon Municipal Corporation Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection is made through the following process, as stated in the notification: Scrutiny of candidates' certificates and documents Only candidates found eligible after document scrutiny will be called for a walk-in interview; ineligible candidates will not be considered for interview Preference will be given to candidates with experience in a local self-government body and to those who have actual COVID-19 period work experience, as well as to local candidates

Important Terms and Conditions

These posts are for a maximum period of six months on a lump-sum honorarium basis; the appointment ends automatically on completion of the period, and no separate termination order will be issued The Commissioner, Malegaon Municipal Corporation, holds full rights over the recruitment process, including the right to cancel or postpone the process at any stage, change the number of posts, alter terms and conditions, change the place of posting, and increase or decrease the honorarium These are temporary, specific-duration posts created only to manage current work requirements; selected candidates will have no claim to permanent employment or regularisation If a selected candidate's work performance during the honorarium period is found unsatisfactory, the engagement can be terminated immediately without prior notice The recruitment process is intended to be transparent, and candidates are

A
It is a recruitment drive by Malegaon Mahanagarpalika (Malegaon Municipal Corporation) to fill 63 posts across 18 medical and paramedical roles on a lump-sum honorarium basis for six months, to operationalise the newly constructed N.N. Wadia Hospital.
Q2How many vacancies are available in Malegaon Mahanagarpalika Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 63 vacancies in total, spread across posts such as Medical Officer (MBBS and BAMS), Specialist Doctors, Staff Nurse, ANM, Lab Technician, X-Ray Technician, Ward Attendants and other support roles.
Q3What is the last date to apply for Malegaon Mahanagarpalika Recruitment 2026?
A
There is no application deadline mentioned, since this is a walk-in interview process rather than an online application. Interviews are scheduled on 7 and 8 September 2026, post-wise.
Q4What is the qualification required for Malegaon Mahanagarpalika Recruitment 2026?
A
Qualification requirements differ by post, ranging from a 7th class pass for Ward Attendant posts to postgraduate medical degrees (MD) for specialist doctor posts. See the eligibility table above for post-wise details.
Q5What is the age limit for Malegaon Mahanagarpalika Recruitment 2026?
A
The general age limit is 18 to 38 years, and 18 to 43 years for backward class candidates. Medical Officers who served during COVID-19 and other emergency periods are exempt from the maximum age limit.
Q6What is the application fee for Malegaon Mahanagarpalika Recruitment 2026?

The application fee is not specified in the notification, as this is a walk-in interview process with no online application or fee payment step mentioned.
Q7How can I apply for Malegaon Mahanagarpalika Recruitment 2026?

There is no online application. Eligible candidates must attend the walk-in interview in person at the Manpa Mukhyalaya, opposite Killa, Malegaon, with all required original and attested documents, on the date and time allotted to their post.

Selection is based on document scrutiny followed by a walk-in interview. Only candidates found eligible after scrutiny are interviewed; preference is given to candidates with local self-government or COVID-19 period work experience and to local candidates.
