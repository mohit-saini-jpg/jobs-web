About JIPMER

The Jawaharlal Institute of Postgraduate Medical Education and Research (JIPMER) is a medical institute at Dhanwantri Nagar, Puducherry-6. These posts belong to an externally funded research project run by its Department of Emergency Medicine & Trauma. The Project Investigator (PI) is Dr Amaravathi U, Assistant Professor, and the funding agency is ICMR.

Pay and Tenure

The pay for both posts is a consolidated monthly amount that includes 20% HRA. No other allowances are paid. The notice adds that the consolidated salary may change from time to time. No other allowances or facilities are given apart from the consolidated salary.

JIPMER Recruitment 2026 Job Description

Screen adults aged 60 years and above who come to the Emergency Department with trauma to check their eligibility, and assess frailty using the Clinical Frailty Scale (CFS). Obtain informed consent from patients or their legally acceptable representatives (LAR) as per the approved protocol, in Tamil and English. Activate and coordinate the frailty-triggered co-management pathway with the emergency, trauma and geriatric teams, and record time-stamped process measures. Perform daily delirium screening (CAM-ICU) and record clinical data in the Case Record Forms. Provide counselling on transitional care before discharge and make warm referrals. Carry out the community contact 7 to 14 days after discharge and the 90-day structured follow-up by telephone and field/home visits. Work in rotational shifts, including nights, Sundays and holidays, as required by the project. Any other project-relat
