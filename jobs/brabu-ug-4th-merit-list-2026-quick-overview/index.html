BRABU UG 4th Merit List 2026 - Quick Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 22/07/2026

Babasaheb Bhimrao Ambedkar Bihar University (BRABU), Muzaffarpur has released the online 4th Merit List and Cut Off for admission into the 1st semester of the Undergraduate (UG) four-year programme under the Choice Based Credit System (CBCS) for the academic session 2026-30. Eligible candidates can download the 4th merit list PDF from the official BRABU website, brabu.ac.in, from 21 July 2026 onwards.

This article explains how to download the BRABU UG 4th Merit List 2026, where to find the subject-wise B.A, B.Sc and B.Com merit list PDFs, how to check the cut off list, and how to download your seat allotment letter through the UMIS student login.

How to Download BRABU UG 4th Merit List 2026

Follow these steps to download the BRABU UG 4th Merit List and Cut Off for admission session 2026-30:

  • Visit the official website of Babasaheb Bhimrao Ambedkar Bihar University: https://brabu.ac.in.
  • On the home page, click on the STUDENT CORNER link, then click on the UG (2026-30) Admission link.
  • You will be redirected to the UMIS Portal for BRABU UG Admission Session 2026-30.
  • On the STUDENT CORNER page, click the link titled "UG (2025-2029) – CBCS 4th Provisional Merit List" to download the merit list and cut off in PDF format.
  • To download your seat allotment letter, go to the UMIS Student Login portal and enter your User Name and Password correctly.
  • Click the Log In button to access and download your college seat allotment letter. Take a printout or save a digital copy for future reference.

Important Instructions

  • The 4th merit list and cut off list are published subject-wise and course-wise (B.A, B.Sc, B.Com); download the PDF specific to your subject.
  • The exact date for admission on the basis of the 4th merit list has not yet been announced by BRABU and will be notified later.
  • Keep your UMIS User Name and Password ready to log in and download your seat allotment letter.
  • For any query or discrepancy, contact the university helpline email: [email protected].
  • Always cross-check merit list and admission details on the official website, brabu.ac.in, before taking any admission-related action.
