VNSGU Eligibility Criteria

SC, ST, SEBC, and EWS candidates of origin of Gujarat can only avail the benefit of reservation. For Junior Clerk/Typist: Prior experience in university or government departments.
PostQualification Required
Technical Assistant (D.C.A.) & Computer Operator (D.C.A.)A Bachelor's degree from a UGC-recognized university in Computer field (e.g., BCA, B.Sc. Computer Science, B.Sc. IT, B.E. Computer Engineering, B.Tech Computer Engineering, B.E. Information Technology etc.) or equivalent degree from a UGC-recognized university. Selected candidates must pass the CCC (Course on Computer Concepts) and Hindi/Gujarati higher-level examinations during their five-year contractual period if not already passed.
Junior Clerk/Typist1. A Bachelor's degree from a UGC-recognized university.2. A certificate of basic computer application knowledge from a recognized institute, as per General Administration Department, Gandhinagar resolutions number CRR-10-2007-120320-G.5 dated 13-08-2008 and 18-03-2016.Selected candidates must pass the CCC (Course on Computer Concepts) and Hindi/Gujarati higher-level examinations during their five-year contractual period if not already passed.

VNSGU Age Limit

The upper age limit is 35 Years for all posts. Relaxation applies as per university/state government norms.

VNSGU Salary

Recruitment for the above-mentioned posts shall be as per Resolution No. Khrch/2002/57/Z1, dated 16-02-2006, and Resolution No. Khrch/2002/57/(Part-4)/Z.1, dated 18-10-2023, of the Finance Department, with fixed pay for the first five years.
PostPay Level Pay
Technical Assistant (D.C.A.) & Computer Operator (D.C.A.)Fixed Salary of Rs. 49,600/- per month for the first five years. After successful completion of five years: Pay Matrix Level-07 (Rs. 39,900 - 1,26,600) as per 7th Pay Scale.
Junior Clerk/TypistFixed Salary of Rs. 26,000/- per month for the first five years. After five years completion: Pay Matrix Level-02 (Rs. 19,900 - 63,200) as per 7th Pay Scale.

VNSGU Application Fee

The notification does not specify exact fee amounts in the text, but mentions that candidates applying for more than one post are required to apply separately and pay an additional application fee along with each application.

VNSGU Important Instructions

Candidates must submit applications exclusively through the online recruitment portal on or before 11 November 2026 till 6:00 PM. Reservation benefits are restricted to SC, ST, SEBC, and EWS candidates of the origin of Gujarat. Applicants desiring to apply for more than one post must submit separate applications and application fees. Direct applications from candidates already in service will not be considered unless sent as an advance copy through proper channels. No TA/DA shall be paid to candidates attending Written Examinations or Verification of Documents. Admit cards, hall tickets, and call letters will be sent to candidates by e-mail only. Any change in address must be communicated in writing to the Registrar, Veer Narmad South Gujarat University.
