Maharashtra 3-Year LLB Second List 2026 - Quick Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 31/08/2026

The State Common Entrance Test (CET) Cell, Maharashtra has released the second seat allotment list for the 3-year LLB course under CAP Round 2, part of the ongoing Centralised Admission Process (CAP) for the 2026-27 academic session on August 29, 2026 . According to reports, 9,314 students have been offered seats in this round across participating law colleges affiliated with universities in Maharashtra. Candidates who registered for CAP Round 2 can check their allotment status on the official portal, llb3cap26.mahacet.org, and must complete the reporting formalities within the specified window to confirm their admission.

How to Check the Second List and Complete Admission

  • Visit the official CAP portal: llb3cap26.mahacet.org
  • Log in using your CAP application number/ID and password (or date of birth, as applicable)
  • Navigate to the Round 2 / second list seat allotment section
  • View or download your seat allotment status
  • If allotted a seat, download the allotment letter
  • Report to the allotted college with the required documents within the reporting window (August 28 - September 1, 2026)
  • Pay the applicable fee as communicated by the college/portal and complete admission confirmation

Important Instructions

  • Candidates who fail to report within the stipulated window may lose the allotted seat, as per standard CAP rules.
  • Candidates awaiting a further round or wishing to improve their college allotment should check whether the CAP rules allow retention of the current seat while participating in the next round; this year-specific rule is not confirmed here and should be checked in the official brochure.
  • Keep the CAP login credentials and all original documents ready before the reporting date.
  • Any discrepancy in the allotment should be raised through the grievance process specified on the official portal, if available.
