How to Check JKBOSE 10th Bi-Annual Result 2026 Online

Students can check their JKBOSE Class 10 Bi-Annual/Private Result 2026 by following these steps:

  • Visit the official website jkbose.nic.in or jkresults.nic.in.
  • Look for the link titled "Result of Secondary School Examination (Class 10th) – Annual (Private)/Bi-Annual 2026."
  • Click on the relevant link for your division (Jammu or Kashmir).
  • Enter your roll number and registration number as printed on your admit card.
  • Click submit to view your result on screen.
  • Download and save the scorecard, and take a printout for future reference.

Checking the Result Through the Gazette PDF

JKBOSE has also released an official result gazette PDF for this session. Students can open the PDF and use their device's search/find function to locate their roll number, name, or other examination details. This method can be useful if the online result portal is slow due to heavy traffic.

Documents Required to Check the Result

  • Admit card / hall ticket (for roll number and registration number)
  • Previous year's mark sheet, if required for reference

Once declared, the online result and scorecard typically display the following details:

  • Candidate's name
  • Parent's/guardian's name
  • Date of birth
  • Roll number
  • Subject-wise marks
  • Total marks obtained
  • Overall passing status/division

JKBOSE Class 10 Passing Criteria

Based on the board's standard evaluation rules, students need to meet the following criteria to pass:

  • At least 33% marks in each individual subject.
  • At least 33% aggregate marks across the five compulsory subjects (excluding additional/optional subjects).
  • For practical subjects, a minimum of 33% marks separately in both the theory and practical components.
  • JKBOSE provides grace marks of up to 6 marks where a student falls short in one or more subjects by that margin.

Important Instructions for Students

  • The result available online is provisional. Treat it as a reference document until the original marksheet is issued by your school or the board.
  • Carefully verify all details name, roll number, date of birth, and subject-wise marks on your result as soon as it is displayed.
  • Report any discrepancy to your school or the JKBOSE regional office promptly.
  • Keep a downloaded copy or printout of your result safe until you receive the original marksheet.
  • Specific information on re-evaluation/rechecking windows, fees, or the process for the 2026 Bi-Annual result has not been officially confirmed in the sources available for this article. Students should check the official JKBOSE website or contact their school for the latest update on this.

What Happens If You Do Not Qualify?

Students who do not clear the JKBOSE Class 10 Bi-Annual examination can typically appear for supplementary/compartment exams or reappear in the next private/bi-annual session, based on the board's policy. Exact details for the 2026 session's re-appear schedule were not confirmed in the sources reviewed, so students should verify this directly with JKBOSE or their school.
