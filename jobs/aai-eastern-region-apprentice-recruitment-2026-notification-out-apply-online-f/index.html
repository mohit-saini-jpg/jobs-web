AAI Apprentice Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Nationality: Indian Nationals only Domicile: Candidates must belong to West Bengal, Odisha, Bihar, Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, Sikkim, or Andaman & Nicobar (Eastern Region states) Qualification: Must have passed the relevant degree, diploma, or ITI course in or after 2024, as assessed on 30/06/2026 Age Limit: Not specified in the notification

AAI Eastern Region Apprentice Stipend 2026

Selected apprentices will receive a monthly stipend based on their category. Graduate apprentices will receive ₹15,000 per month, Diploma apprentices ₹12,000 per month, and ITI apprentices ₹9,600 per month during the first year of training and ₹10,560 per month during the second year, applicable to National or State Trade Certificate holders whose prior training period is accounted for in determining the stipend rate.
