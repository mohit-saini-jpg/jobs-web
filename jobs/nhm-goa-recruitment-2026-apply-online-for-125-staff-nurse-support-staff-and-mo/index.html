NHM Goa Recruitment 2026 Salary

Selected candidates will be engaged on a consolidated monthly remuneration ranging from ₹8,000/- (Dental Assistant, part-time) to ₹1,00,000/- (Specialist Cardiologist), as specified per post in the vacancy table above. The Vaccine Cold Chain Logistic Manager post additionally carries a Travelling Allowance of ₹10,000/- along with the consolidated remuneration of ₹22,000/-.

NHM Goa Recruitment 2026 Application Fee

No application fee details are specified in the notification.

NHM Goa Recruitment 2026 Age Limit

As per the notification, the age limit for all posts will preferably be not less than 18 years and not exceeding 40 years, relaxable as per Government instructions/orders issued from time to time. SC/ST candidates: 5 years relaxation OBC candidates: 3 years relaxation PH (Persons with Disabilities) candidates: 10 years relaxation Age relaxation is applicable from the date of application. Before submitting the application, candidates are advised to verify that they meet the required age criteria. Use our Age Calculator to calculate your exact age as per the official cut-off date.

NHM Goa Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

The notification specifies that hard copies of the application and all supporting documents must be produced at the time of Interview, indicating that selection involves an interview stage. The detailed selection process, exam pattern, or written test (if any) is not specified in the notification. Shortlisted candidates may be subject to background checks, verification of qualifications, and other assessments as deemed necessary by NHM-Goa. Failure to produce necessary documents during the recruitment process may result in disqualification.
