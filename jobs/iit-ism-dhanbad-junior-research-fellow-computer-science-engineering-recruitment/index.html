IIT (ISM) Dhanbad ANRF - Junior Research Fellow (JRF) Job Description

The notice states that the JRF will: develop and evaluate compressed vision and NLP models for NVIDIA Jetson edge devices; implement ONNX/TensorRT inference engines; acquire live GPU, CPU, memory, temperature and power telemetry; design an energy-aware runtime scheduler for multimodal workloads; integrate concurrent object-detection and language-processing pipelines; measure latency, accuracy, throughput, energy per inference and scheduler overhead; conduct COCO and appropriate NLP benchmark experiments; maintain research code, datasets, experimental logs, reports and publications.

Selection and Interview

Shortlisted candidates will be notified of the date of the interview. Candidates will be shortlisted based on their merit and the project's requirements. Mere possession of minimum qualifications does not guarantee an invitation to the interview. The interview will be conducted via video conferencing, and a link will be shared with the shortlisted candidates in due course. The interview date is not given in the notice.
