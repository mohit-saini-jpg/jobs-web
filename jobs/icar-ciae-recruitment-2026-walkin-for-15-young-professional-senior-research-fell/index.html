About ICAR-CIAE

ICAR-Central Institute of Agricultural Engineering (ICAR-CIAE) is an institute of the Indian Council of Agricultural Research located at Nabi Bagh, Berasia Road, Bhopal- 462 038 (Madhya Pradesh). Its letterhead describes it as an ISO 9001:2015 Certified Institute. These posts serve its Establishment & Recruitment Section, Library Section, Agricultural Knowledge Management Unit, Farm Machinery Training and Testing Centre and several research projects, including projects under the "Mission for Cotton Productivity (Kapas Kanti)", the Central Corpus Fund-Indian Council of Agricultural Research (CCF ICAR) - National Priority Project (NPP), and a Global Network project with ICAR-Indian Institute of Millet research Hyderabad.

What Happens on the Day

Candidates report at 10:00 AM on the date fixed for their post at the institute in Nabi Bagh, Berasia Road, Bhopal. A written test may be conducted before the oral interview for the post. A candidate will not be permitted to appear in the interview without original certificates of educational qualifications. No TA/DA is admissible for attending the interview.

ICAR CIAE Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

The posts are purely time bound/non-regular temporary/contractual engagement and co-terminus with the concerned project. The selected candidate will have no right whatsoever for absorption/regularization at ICAR-CIAE, Bhopal or any other ICAR Institute. Canvassing in any form will disqualify the candidature. A candidate whose near or distant relative is an employee of ICAR/CIAE has to declare the relative's name, designation, nature of duties and relationship in writing. ICAR CIAE Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1When are the ICAR-CIAE walk-in interviews in 2026?
A
The interviews run on separate dates for each post from 26 November 2026 to 16 December 2026, starting with Young Professional-I (Establishment & Recruitment Section) on 26 November and ending with Young Professional-I (IT) for AKMU on 16 December.
Q2What is the reporting time and venue?
A
Every interview is at 10:00 AM at ICAR- Central Institute of Agricultural Engineering, Nabi Bagh, Berasia Road, Bhopal.
Q3How many vacancies are there in ICAR-CIAE Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 15 posts: Young Professional-I (6), Young Professional-I (IT) (1), Young Professional-II (4), Young Professional-II (IT) (1) and Senior Research Fellow (3).
Q4What qualification is needed for Senior Research Fellow?
A
A Master degree in Farm Machinery and Power (FMP) with 4 years / 5 years of Bachelor's Degree in Agricultural/Mechanical Engineering having 1st division or at least 60% marks or equivalent overall grade point average.
Q5What is the age limit?
A
For Senior Research Fellow, the maximum is 35 years for men and 40 years for women; for Young Professional-I & II, the minimum is 21 years and the maximum 45 years.
Q6What is the salary for Young Professional posts?
A
Young Professional-I gets Rs.30,000/- and Young Professional-II gets Rs.42,000/- consolidated per month.
Q7Is HRA paid on top of the pay?
A
HRA is mentioned only for Senior Research Fellow, whose pay is Rs.37,000/- + HRA consolidated per month.
Q8What documents should I bring to the interview?
A
Bring a bio-data with photograph pasted on the top right, original certificates with attested copies, relevant publications (if any), original experience certificate (if any), and an NOC if you are already employed.
Q9How will candidates be selected?
A
Selection is by oral interview, and a written test may be conducted before the oral interview for the post.
Q10Are these permanent jobs?
A
No. The posts are purely time bound/non-regular temporary/contractual engagement, co-terminus with the concerned project, with no right to absorption or regularization at ICAR-CIAE or any other ICAR Institute.
