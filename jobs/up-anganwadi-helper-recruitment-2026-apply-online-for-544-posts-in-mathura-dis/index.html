Mathura Anganwadi Helper Eligibility Criteria

Only women candidates are eligible to apply for these posts. The candidate must be a resident of the same Ward/Gram Sabha where the post is vacant; applications from candidates of other wards/gram sabhas will not be considered valid.

Mathura Anganwadi Non-Employee Qualification

The minimum educational qualification for the Anganwadi Helper post is Intermediate (12th) or its equivalent. The merit list for all Anganwadi Helper posts will be prepared considering qualifications ranging from the minimum (Intermediate) up to the maximum (Postgraduate) level, as applicable.

Mathura Anganwadi No Age Limit

As on the cut-off date of 01 July 2026, the candidate's age must be a minimum of 18 years and a maximum of 35 years. To avoid any confusion regarding age eligibility, candidates can use our Age Calculator to calculate their exact age based on the prescribed cut-off date before applying.

Mathura Anganwadi Helper Selection Process

Selection will be made through a merit list prepared on the basis of educational qualification (minimum Intermediate to maximum Postgraduate). As per government order, priority will be given to candidates from the BPL category, widows, and destitute/abandoned women. No written examination or interview is mentioned in the provided notification. For widowed candidates, a certificate issued by a competent authority will be valid. For divorced and destitute/abandoned women, an order issued by a competent legal/court authority will be valid. Redetermination of the BPL income limit will follow Samaj Kalyan Anubhag-2 Government Order No. 22/2015/2123/26-2-2015 dated 14 September 2015.

Mathura Anganwadi Helper Required Documents

Based on the provided notification, candidates are required to submit the following: Educational qualification certificates supporting the minimum eligibility (Intermediate or higher). Income certificate issued by a competent authority at the Tehsil level (must be online-verifiable). Residence certificate issued by a competent authority at the Tehsil level (must be online-verifiable). Category-specific certificate where applicable - widow certificate from a competent authority, or a court/legal order for divorced or destitute/abandoned women. Any other document requirements are not specified in the provided notification.

Complete eligibility details and the application form are available on the official website; further information can be obtained from the Child Development Project Officer's office or the District Programme Officer's office at Rajiv Bhawan, Mathura, during working hours.
