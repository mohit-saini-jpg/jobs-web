About SVSU

Shri Vishwakarma Skill University (SVSU) is located at Village Dudhola, Palwal, Haryana. The notice is issued by its Registrar under Ref. No. SVSU/2026/Estt./3576 dated 03.11.2026. The University says the complete details, including eligibility conditions, selection criteria and general instructions, are available on its website www.svsu.ac.in .

Where and How to Send

Applicants are to fill the offline application form printed in the notice and send it by the closing date, 26.11.2026 (up to 05:00 P.M.). Each page of the form must be signed by the applicant. The signed copy, along with all supporting self-attested documents, goes to this address: The Assistant Registrar (Estt.), Takshashila Bhawan, Administrative Block, Shri Vishwakarma Skill University, Village-Dudhola, Palwal, Haryana- 121002 The notice names no postal mode and gives no text to write on the envelope. It does not give an email address for applications. Incomplete applications, or those received after the last date for any reason including postal delay, are rejected outright. The notice says the fee is accepted through offline mode only and prints http://forms.eduqfix.com/svsurectff/add in the same sentence. The payment slip must be attached to the application form. SVSU Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

The notice describes screening, a written test, a skill test and an interview of shortlisted candidates. The dates of the tests and the interview are not given. Candidates found eligible by the Screening Committee and shortlisted by the University will be called for interview. Those declared ineligible will not be informed. Mere possession of the minimum qualification and experience does not entitle a candidate to be called for the written test or interview, and the University may restrict the shortlisted applicants to a reasonable number. The written and skill tests are two mandatory components. A General category candidate must secure minimum 40% marks, and DSC/OSC/BCA/BCB/PwBD candidates minimum 38% marks, in the written test as well as in the skill test. SVSU Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in SVSU Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 10 regular non-teaching posts: Assistant 2, Steno-typist (Hindi & English) 2, Hostel Attendant (Male & Female) 2, Sanitary Officer 1, Plumber 1, Restorer 1 and Carpenter 1.
Q2What is the last date to apply for SVSU non-teaching posts?
A
The application must be sent by 26 November 2026, up to 05:00 P.M. Applications received after the last date, including by postal delay, are rejected.
Q3What is the age limit for SVSU recruitment?
A
The age limit is 18 to 42 years as on last date. Age relaxation follows the Haryana Government letter dated 25.03.2022, only for Haryana categories and only where the post is reserved for them.
Q4What is the qualification for the SVSU Assistant post?
A
The Assistant post needs a Bachelor's Degree with minimum 50% marks and five years' experience as Clerk or Clerk-Cum-Data Entry Operator, plus Hindi/Sanskrit up to Matric or higher level. Computer and Internet proficiency is desirable.
Q5What is the application fee for SVSU Recruitment 2026?
A
The fee is Rs. 1000/- for male General category candidates including DESM of Haryana, and for all candidates of other states. It is Rs. 250/- for Female General candidates of Haryana and for DSC/OSC/BCA/BCB/ESM/EWS candidates of Haryana. It is Nil for Haryana PwD candidates with at least 40% disability.
Q6How do I apply for SVSU Recruitment 2026?
A
Fill the offline form, sign each page and attach the fee payment slip and self-attested documents. Send it to The Assistant Registrar (Estt.), Takshashila Bhawan, Administrative Block, Shri Vishwakarma Skill University, Village-Dudhola, Palwal, Haryana- 121002.
Q7What is the selection process for SVSU non-teaching posts?
A
The notice describes a written test of 50 marks and a skill test of 50 marks, with a minimum of 40% for General and 38% for DSC/OSC/BCA/BCB/PwBD candidates. Shortlisted candidates are called for interview.
Q8Is there a skill test for the Steno-typist post?
A
Yes. The Steno-typist written test of 50 marks comes first, with minimum 40% to qualify, followed by a skill test (stenography) of qualifying nature for those who pass the written test.
Q9Are the SVSU posts permanent?
A
The notice invites applications for the posts on regular basis.
Q10Which posts are reserved in SVSU Recruitment 2026?
A
Only the Assistant post is reserved, with one of its two posts for Gen and one for DSC (Deprived Scheduled Caste of Haryana). The other six posts are marked Gen.
