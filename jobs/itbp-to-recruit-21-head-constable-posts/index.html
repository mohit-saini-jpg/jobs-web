ITBP Head Constable Motor Mechanic Selection Process 2026

Selection will be conducted in multiple phases covering physical fitness, eligibility verification, written examination, trade skills and medical fitness. Phase I: Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST). Phase II: Verification of Original Documents and Written Examination. Phase III: Practical Skill Test and preparation of the merit list. Phase IV: Detailed Medical Examination (DME) and Review Medical Examination (RME), where applicable. Final Merit Rule: The Practical Skill Test is qualifying in nature. Final merit is prepared on the basis of written examination marks among candidates who qualify the prescribed stages.

ITBP Head Constable Final Merit and Medical Examination 2026

After the practical test, ITBPF will prepare category-wise merit lists based on written examination marks. Shortlisted candidates will undergo Detailed Medical Examination (DME). Those declared medically unfit may seek Review Medical Examination (RME) according to the prescribed procedure. Candidates seeking RME must submit written consent within 24 hours of receiving the DME unfitness decision. The older candidate will receive a higher merit position. If the tie continues, the candidate whose name comes first alphabetically in English will receive preference. The notification states that no reserve list will be maintained.
