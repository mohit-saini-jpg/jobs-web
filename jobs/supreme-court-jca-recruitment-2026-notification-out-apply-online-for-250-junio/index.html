Supreme Court JCA Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

The selection of Supreme Court Junior Court Assistant will be made through the following four stages. Candidates have to appear in all the tests, and the minimum qualifying marks must be secured in each. General English including comprehension section: 20 questions General Aptitude: 15 questions General Knowledge: 15 questions Provisions relating to Judiciary in the Constitution of India: 15 questions Supreme Court Rules, 2013: 10 questions Supreme Court Officers and Servants (Conditions of Service and Conduct) Rules, 1961: 10 questions Computer Knowledge: 15 questions There is negative marking of 0.25 marks for each incorrect answer. Answer keys will be uploaded on 22-11-2026. Objections may be sent through online mode only, and the decision of the competent Authority on objections will be final.
