ASRB ICAR T-3 T-4 Selection Process 2026

The selection process consists of a qualifying Preliminary CBT and a merit-based Mains CBT, followed by eligibility and document verification by ICAR. Online application submission. Combined Preliminary Computer Based Test. Shortlisting for Mains in a 1:15 ratio against functional-group vacancies, subject to qualifying marks. Combined Mains Computer Based Test. Preparation of functional group/subgroup-wise merit lists. Post allocation according to rank, eligibility, preferences and available vacancies. Verification of original qualifications and other relevant documents by ICAR. Appointment subject to applicable conditions and suitability. No Interview: The notification explicitly confirms that no interview will be conducted. Final selection merit is based on Mains Examination performance only.

ASRB ICAR T-3 T-4 Merit List and Tie-Breaking Rules

The Board prepares merit lists based on Mains marks or applicable normalized scores. If candidates obtain equal Mains marks, the following tie-breaking rules apply in order: The candidate securing higher marks in the Preliminary CBT receives preference. If still tied, the candidate securing higher marks in the Quantitative Aptitude section of the Preliminary CBT is ranked higher. If the tie remains, the older candidate receives preference. The notification also provides for a reserve panel under specified conditions, including certain vacancies arising from non-joining or departure within one year of joining where a fresh panel is unavailable.

ASRB has scheduled the application correction window from 18 to 20 December 2026 . However, the notification also restricts changes to submitted application details and makes post preferences final. ASRB ICAR Technical Assistant Exam Centres 2027

The Preliminary CBT is planned at 62 tentative examination centres across India, while the Mains CBT has a separate list of 31 centres. Major Preliminary examination cities include Delhi NCR, Patna, Lucknow, Kanpur, Kolkata, Bengaluru, Chennai, Coimbatore, Hyderabad, Vijayawada, Visakhapatnam, Mumbai, Pune, Jaipur and other notified locations. Examination centres may be changed by ASRB depending on administrative requirements. Candidates must choose their preferences carefully because later requests for centre changes will not ordinarily be accepted.
