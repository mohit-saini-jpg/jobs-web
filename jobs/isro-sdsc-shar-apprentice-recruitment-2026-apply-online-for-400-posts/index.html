ISRO SDSC SHAR Apprentice Eligibility Criteria 2026

Applicants must possess the educational qualifications prescribed for their apprenticeship category. Engineering and Non-Engineering Graduate Apprentices require the relevant degree, while Technician, Trade and Vocational Apprentices must possess the specified diploma, trade or vocational qualification. Important Eligibility Conditions: Graduate and Diploma Apprenticeship candidates must have passed their relevant qualifications in 2022 or later . Applicants with sandwich-course certificates, short-term/certificate courses or those pursuing higher studies are not eligible under the stated conditions. Candidates who have already completed or are currently undergoing apprenticeship training, have one year or more of experience in an organization, or have been terminated from or withdrawn from apprenticeship training are also not eligible. Only Indian nationals may apply.

ISRO SDSC SHAR Apprentice Selection Process 2026

Selection for ISRO SDSC SHAR Apprenticeship 2026 will be based on academic merit and document verification. The notification provides for category-wise merit lists using the marks obtained in the relevant educational qualification. It does not announce a separate written examination or interview as part of the stated selection procedure. Academic Merit List: Merit lists will be prepared based on academic scores separately for UR, OBC, SC, ST and EWS categories. Discipline-Wise Shortlisting: Applicants will be shortlisted according to their position in the merit list for the relevant discipline, branch, trade or subject. Document Verification: Shortlisted candidates will be required to attend document verification with original certificates. Final Consideration: Only candidates who attend verification and satisfy all prescribed eligibility requirements will be considered for apprenticeshi

ISRO SDSC SHAR Apprentice 2026 Training Duration

The apprenticeship training duration is 12 months , commencing from the execution of the apprenticeship contract. Selected candidates must execute the contract in accordance with the Apprentices Act and submit a medical fitness certificate at the time of joining. Completion of apprenticeship training does not provide any claim to temporary, ad hoc or permanent employment at SDSC SHAR or any other ISRO/Department of Space centre or unit.

ISRO SDSC SHAR Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Candidates must complete the prescribed apprenticeship registration before applying for the SDSC SHAR apprenticeship through the UMANG portal. The official notification provides the following procedure.
  • 2Visit the NATS portal at nats.education.gov.in or the National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme portal at www.apprenticeship.gov.in, as applicable.
  • 3Register on the appropriate apprenticeship portal using the required details and upload the applicable Degree/Diploma certificate and valid government-issued identification documents.
  • 4After successful registration, retain the generated Enrolment Number or Apprenticeship Registration Number.
  • 5Ensure that your Aadhaar number is linked to your bank account and enabled for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
  • 6Visit the UMANG portal at web.umang.gov.in/landing/.
  • 7Register or log in to UMANG and search for SDSC SHAR Apprenticeship.
  • 8Select the appropriate apprenticeship position and enter the required details.
  • 9Upload the relevant supporting documents in JPG, JPEG or PNG format, not exceeding 1 MB per document, as specified in the notification.
  • 10Upload the required photograph, signature and category certificates in the format prescribed on UMANG.
  • 11Carefully verify the educational qualification, marks, passing year, category and other details before submitting the application.
  • 12Submit the online application on or before 26 November 2026 and retain a copy for future reference.
  • 13Important: No changes will be permitted after final submission. Applications submitted through any mode other than the prescribed online portal will not be accepted.

ISRO SDSC SHAR Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is the last date to apply for SDSC SHAR Recruitment 2026?
A
The last date is 26 November 2026. Applications opened on 6 November 2026.
Q2How many vacancies are there in SDSC SHAR Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 400 vacancies: 50 Engineering Graduate Apprentice, 60 Non-Engineering Graduate Apprentice, 35 Technician Apprentice (Diploma), 60 Diploma (DCCP), 180 Trade Apprentice, 15 Vocational Apprentice.
Q3How do I apply for SDSC SHAR Recruitment 2026?
A
Apply online through the link in the Important Links table, on or before 26 November 2026.
Q4What is the salary for this recruitment?
A
It depends on the post: 12300 for Engineering Graduate Apprentice, 12300 for Non-Engineering Graduate Apprentice, 10900 for Technician Apprentice (Diploma), 9600 for Trade Apprentice, 9600 for Vocational Apprentice.
Q5What qualification is needed for this recruitment?
A
B.E/B.Tech, or B.Sc./B.Com./B.A., or Diploma in first class with minimum 60% marks/6.32 CGPA (passed in 2022 or later); SSLC/SSC+ITI/NTC from NCVT for Trade Apprentice; AICTE-recognised two-year vocational certificate for Vocational Apprentice. The requirement for each post is in the eligibility table above.
Q6Are there other conditions for this recruitment besides qualification and age?
A
Yes. Only Indian Nationals need to apply. Aadhaar number must be seeded with bank account and enabled for DBT. Graduate and Diploma applicants must have passed the qualification in 2022 or thereafter.
Q7Who cannot apply for this recruitment?
A
Holders of sandwich course certificates, short term/certificate courses, etc. Applicants pursuing higher studies. Those who have already undergone or are currently undergoing apprenticeship training under the Apprentices Act, 1961. Those having experience of one year or more in any organisation.
Q8How will candidates be selected for this recruitment?
A
Category-wise (UR/OBC/SC/ST/EWS) merit list based on academic score for each apprenticeship category. Shortlisting by position in the merit list against the respective discipline/branch/trade/subject. Document verification. Reservation as per Government of India norms applied at apprenticeship category level.
Q9What are the terms of engagement for this recruitment?
A
Training period of 12 months from execution of the contract of Apprenticeship. Selected applicants must execute a contract of Apprenticeship under the Apprentices Act, 1961 and Amendment Act 1973. No claim for any adhoc/temporary/permanent employment in SDSC SHAR or ISRO/DOS after training.
Q10Will ISRO provide permanent employment after completing the apprenticeship?
A
No. The notification states that completion of apprenticeship training does not entitle candidates to claim regular, temporary or permanent employment in ISRO, SDSC SHAR or the Department of Space.
