Uttarakhand Forest Department Recruitment 2026 Job Description

Personnel may be deputed to various forest divisions of Bhagirathi Circle (Narendra Nagar Forest Division, Tehri Forest Division, Uttarkashi Forest Division, Tehri Dam-I Forest Division, Tehri Dam-II Forest Division, and Soil Conservation Forest Division) to provide technical assistance to respective DFOs, SDOs, and field staff. Key duties include: Field visits of proposed ANR, CAT Plans, Eco Restoration Works, and GIM Research Work plots from time to time. Preparation of site-specific plans for all sites as prescribed in the annual plan for the next Silvicultural year. Preparation of maps and other required data for site-specific plans. Conducting surveys such as Rapid Mapping Exercise, rootstock calculation, and carbon stock calculation for prescribed plots. Providing technical and scientific assistance in tree marking according to working plan rules and guidelines. Establishing sample
