Punjab ETT Result 2026 - Overview : Result Overview

How to Check Punjab ETT Result 2026?

Once the Punjab ETT 2026 result is made available through the official candidate account, applicants can follow these steps:

  • Visit the official SCERT Punjab examination portal at ps.ctestservices.com/etadee/.
  • Find the Punjab ETT / D.El.Ed. Entrance Exam Result 2026 option when announced.
  • Open the candidate login page.
  • Enter the registered username and password.
  • Complete the CAPTCHA verification.
  • Sign in to the candidate dashboard.
  • Open the result or scorecard option, if available.
  • Check the displayed marks and qualifying status.
  • Download or print the result for admission-related use.
