CMO DNH Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Specialist doctors with MBBS and a PG Degree or Diploma (MS/MD/DNB) in the respective subject can apply for 14 posts on Short Term Contract basis at Sub-District Hospital, Khanvel, advertised by the Office of the Chief Medical Officer, DNH under the Department of Health & Family Welfare, U. T. Administration of Dadra Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu. Pay ranges from Rs.90,000/- to Rs.1,75,000/- per month depending on degree or diploma and experience, plus an incentive of Rs.25,000/-. The notice prints one qualification for all 14 posts, each in its own speciality ("respective subject"). All the qualifications will be considered from recognized universities / Colleges of India.
