About NCL Recruitment 2026

Northern Coalfields Limited is a Miniratna company and a subsidiary of Coal India Limited, with its headquarters at Post- Singrauli Colliery, Distt: Singrauli, M.P., PIN 486889. The notice is issued by the General Manager (HRD), CETI, NCL Headquarters, Singrauli, through the HRD Department. The training is offered under the Mukhyamantri Seekho-Kamao Yojana of the Government of Madhya Pradesh.

NCL Eligibility Criteria 2026

The candidate must be a resident of Madhya Pradesh. The candidate must hold the minimum educational qualification prescribed for the trade/subject applied for. The candidate must have a valid Samagra ID, and the prescribed e-KYC process must be complete. The candidate's bank account must be linked with Aadhaar, with the Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) facility active. The candidate must also meet all other eligibility conditions prescribed under MMSKY. Educational qualification and other eligibility conditions will be determined as per the applicable MMSKY provisions and guidelines. Candidates should check the official notification for the trade-specific eligibility of each post.

NCL Age Limit 2026

The age of the candidate must be 18 years completed and less than 29 years on the date of appearing at NCL for document verification/joining. The same limit applies to all five trades/subjects. Age eligibility will be verified specifically on the date of document verification/joining, and candidates who do not meet the age condition on that date will not be considered eligible for training as Student-Trainees.

NCL Stipend 2026

The stipend is paid monthly, shared between NCL (25%) and the Government of Madhya Pradesh (75%), and is paid as per the applicable MMSKY provisions and guidelines. The contributions of NCL and the Government of Madhya Pradesh are regulated as per the scheme guidelines. After trainees join their respective Areas, their duly authenticated attendance is verified regularly by the Area and sent to the HRD Department, NCL Headquarters, for stipend payment. NCL does not provide accommodation, food or transport.

NCL Selection Process 2026

Applications received against the training opportunities published by NCL will be scrutinised as per the prescribed eligibility conditions. Eligible candidates may be called for an interview/interaction online, by telephone or by another suitable means, as per the scheme provisions. Shortlisted candidates may be called to a designated place for document verification and further joining formalities. Final selection will be made by NCL as per the applicable MMSKY provisions and guidelines. Selection is subject to verification of eligibility and approval of the training contract by the competent authority on the MMSKY portal. Merely submitting an application gives no right to selection or training.

NCL Recruitment 2026 Important Instructions

Selected candidates are placed as Student-Trainees for On-the-Job Training and will not get the status of regular NCL employees. Training duration, attendance and other conditions follow the MMSKY provisions and the instructions issued by the Government of Madhya Pradesh from time to time.
