Delhi Nursery Admission 2027-28 - Overview : Admission Overview

The Directorate of Education (DoE), Government of NCT of Delhi, has released the Delhi Nursery Admission 2027-28 schedule for entry-level admissions in private unaided recognised schools. According to the official circular dated 20 November 2026, the admission process and availability of application forms will begin on 1 December 2026. Parents can submit applications until 28 December 2026.

Delhi Nursery Admission 2027-28 - Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 24/11/2026

The admission process covers Nursery (Balvatika 1), Lower KG (Balvatika 2), Upper KG (Balvatika 3) and applicable Class 1 entry points. The first selection list will be published on 20 January 2027, while the second list, if required, will be released on 5 February 2027. Schools may charge only Rs. 25 as a non-refundable admission registration fee. Parents should check each school's admission criteria, age requirements, available seats and application procedure before submitting their forms.

Delhi Nursery Admission 2027-28 Age Limit

The Directorate of Education has prescribed age criteria for entry-level admissions under the revised foundational-stage structure. A child's age will be calculated as on 31 March 2027.

ClassAge CriteriaAdmission Category
Nursery (Balvatika 1 / Pre-School 1)3+ Years up to 4 YearsNew Admissions
Lower KG (Balvatika 2 / Pre-School 2)4+ Years up to 5 YearsPromotion from Nursery 2026-27 and New Admissions
Upper KG (Balvatika 3 / Pre-School 3)5+ Years up to 6 YearsNew Admissions
Class 15+ Years up to 6 YearsPromotion from KG 2026-27 Under Transitional Arrangements

Important: The Class 1 age provision for 2027-28 is part of the transitional arrangement for students promoted from KG during 2026-27. It should not be interpreted as a general fresh Class 1 admission age rule.

The circular also allows the Head of School to consider age relaxation of up to one month in the minimum or upper age limit. Parents seeking such relaxation may approach the school principal through a manual application.

Delhi Nursery Admission 2027-28: New LKG and UKG Structure

The Directorate of Education is implementing changes to the foundational stage in phases in accordance with the National Education Policy 2020.

For academic session 2027-28, the following arrangements apply:

  • Nursery admissions are for children aged 3+ years as on 31 March 2027.
  • Lower KG (Balvatika 2) is being introduced for children aged 4+ years, including eligible new admissions and students promoted from Nursery.
  • Upper KG (Balvatika 3) is being introduced for new admissions of children aged 5+ years.
  • Children studying KG in session 2026-27 will move to Class 1 under the existing transitional progression arrangement.
  • Students joining Lower KG in 2027-28 will progress to Upper KG in the following academic session.

Parents should confirm the child's eligibility for the particular class and whether the selected school offers fresh admissions at that entry level.

How to Apply for Delhi Nursery Admission 2027-28?

Parents can follow these steps to complete the admission process:

  • Visit the official Directorate of Education website at edudel.nic.in.
  • Check the Admission Criteria 2027-28 information published by private schools.
  • Identify the schools offering admission to the required entry-level class.
  • Review the school's admission criteria, available seats and application instructions.
  • Confirm the child's age eligibility as on 31 March 2027.
  • Open or obtain the application form from the selected school on or after 1 December 2026.
  • Enter the child's and parents' details accurately.
  • Provide the documents requested under the applicable admission procedure.
  • Pay the prescribed Rs. 25 registration fee.
  • Submit the application by 28 December 2026.
  • Keep the application acknowledgement for future reference.
  • Check the school's published applicant points and selection lists according to the official schedule.

The exact mode of application and submission instructions may vary by school.

Delhi Nursery Admission 2027-28 Selection Criteria

Private unaided recognised schools must publish fair, transparent, well-defined and non-discriminatory admission criteria for open-seat admissions. The selection process commonly includes a points-based system.

Schools may assign points to applicants according to their published and legally permissible admission criteria. Neighbourhood distance, sibling status and eligible alumni-related conditions are among the criteria reported by several schools.

However, there is no single common points distribution applicable to all Delhi private schools. Each school must publish its approved criteria and corresponding points.

Delhi Nursery Admission 2027-28 Documents Required

Parents should prepare documents needed to establish the child's date of birth, identity, residence and eligibility under any applicable admission criterion.

Documents that may be required include:

  • Child's birth certificate or acceptable age proof
  • Parent's or guardian's identity proof
  • Valid residential address proof
  • Child's passport-size photograph, if requested
  • Parent's photograph, if requested
  • Sibling-related documents, if claiming sibling points
  • Alumni-related documents, if claiming applicable alumni points
  • Other documents required under the individual school's published criteria

The Directorate of Education circular identifies several acceptable types of residential proof.

Delhi Nursery Admission 2027-28 EWS, DG and CWSN Seats

The 20 November 2026 admission circular specifically covers open seats in Delhi's private unaided recognised schools. It does not serve as the complete admission schedule for Economically Weaker Section (EWS), Disadvantaged Group (DG) or Children with Special Needs (CWSN) reserved-category seats.

Under the applicable reservation framework, private unaided recognised schools must reserve 25% of entry-level seats for the specified categories wherever fresh admissions are made, subject to the relevant legal provisions and directions.

Parents seeking admission through the reserved-category process should follow the separate Directorate of Education notifications and procedures applicable to those seats.

Delhi Nursery Admission 2027-28 School Guidelines

The Directorate of Education has issued instructions to ensure a fair and transparent admission process.

  • Schools must publish their admission criteria and corresponding points by 26 November 2026.
  • Available entry-level seats must be declared through the prescribed school information system.
  • Application forms must remain available until 28 December 2026.
  • Only Rs. 25 may be charged as the non-refundable registration fee.
  • Parents cannot be forced to buy a school prospectus.
  • Additional application processing fees and prohibited donations cannot be charged.
  • Schools must publish applicant details and points within the prescribed schedule.
  • Parents must be given the specified opportunities to raise objections about awarded points.
  • Schools must not adopt prohibited or discriminatory admission criteria.
  • The admission process must close by 12 March 2027.
