How to Check Your SGBAU Result 2026

SGBAU results are checked through a search form on the official result portal rather than a single downloadable list. Based on the fields available on the portal, follow these steps:

  • Go to the official SGBAU result page: https://sgbau.ucanapply.com/result-details.
  • Select your Session (for example, Summer 2026 or Winter 2025).
  • Select your Course Type - UG, PG, or PHD.
  • Select your specific Course from the dropdown.
  • Select the Result Type - Regular, Back, Reval, or EVS, depending on which result you are checking.
  • Enter your Roll Number.
  • Select your Semester or year.
  • Click Search Result to view your result.

The portal also displays a running list titled "Results Declared," which shows each course, semester, session, result type, and the date the result was declared - this is the list used to compile the table above.

Important Instruction From SGBAU

The official result portal carries this note: students whose result is shown as W/N are requested to contact their respective college.
