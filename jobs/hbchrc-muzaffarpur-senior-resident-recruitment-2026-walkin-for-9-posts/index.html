Qualification and Experience

The age limit for all four posts is 45 years, relaxable by 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC and 10 years for Physically Handicapped candidates. Age and experience are reckoned as on the date of the walk-in interview. All graduate, postgraduate and post-doctoral degrees must be recognized by the National Medical Commission or the Dental Council of India (erstwhile Medical Council of India). Pay and Terms

Pay: a monthly consolidated salary of Rs. 1,33,623/- (Rupees One-Lakh Thirty Three Thousand Six Hundred Twenty Three Only) for a Senior Resident. Tenure: tenure-based positions for a fixed period up to one year, further extendable subject to the requirements of the centre. Security deposit: the selected Senior Resident has to deposit Rs. 1,33,623/-, refundable after successful completion of tenure. Accommodation: provided on a sharing basis, subject to availability. Joining: the selected candidate has to join immediately, subject to being found medically fit.

Documents Required
