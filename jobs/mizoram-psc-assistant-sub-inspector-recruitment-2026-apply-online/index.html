MPSC Assistant Sub-Inspector of Excise & Narcotics Recruitment 2026 - Selection Process

Selection will be based on Written Examination, Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Personal Interview. Total marks obtained in Physical Efficiency Test (PET) will be added to determine the final score on top of written exam and personal interview. If two or more candidates obtain equal final marks, preference will be given to the taller candidate. Physical measurement shall be conducted before the Physical Efficiency Test.
