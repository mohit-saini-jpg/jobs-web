About OIL

Oil India Limited is described in the notice as a Maharatna Public Sector Undertaking and the second largest national upstream Oil & Gas company, with pan India presence and a global footprint. The posts are placed under the Centre of Excellence for Energy Studies (CoEES), Guwahati, Assam, for OIL's Critical Mineral Exploration Programme. The work is purely temporary in nature and may involve shifts, arduous and hazardous jobs in remote or far-flung OIL installations, and "On-Call" duty.

Posts and Eligibility

SC, ST and EWS show no posts for either position. The notice says reservation for PwBD and Ex-Servicemen is as per Govt. of India Guidelines. For PwBD suitability it lists "b) HH; c) OA, OL, CP, LC, Dw, AAV; d) SLD, MI; e) MD involving b to d" for both posts. In-depth knowledge of Economic Geology, Mineral Deposits and Ore Genesis relevant to the exploration and exploitation of mineral resources. Publications in reputed journals and/or conference proceedings in the above fields will be an added advantage. Experience in field geological mapping and mineral resource estimation in mineralized zones is desirable. Prior work experience in mining-related industries will be preferred. The age limit for both posts, counted as on the date of registration for the Walk-in-Interview, is a minimum of 18 years and a maximum of 40 years for General candidates and 43 years for OBC (NCL) candidates. Q1What are the walk-in interview dates for OIL Contractual Geologist?

Q2What is the reporting time for the OIL walk-in?
A
Registration is from 09:00 A.M. to 11:00 A.M. on the respective date, and no registration is allowed after that time.
Q3Where will the OIL walk-in interviews be held?

Q4How many Contractual Geologist vacancies are there?

Q5What is the qualification for OIL Contractual Geologist?

Candidates need M.Sc. (Geology / Applied Geology) or M.Tech. (Geology / Applied Geology) of minimum two years duration from a Govt. recognized University/Institute.

A

Q9Is the OIL Geologist job permanent?

No. It is purely contractual for 1 year, extendable by another 1 year, for a maximum of 2 years, and it gives no right to regularization.

No. No Travelling Allowance or Daily Allowance is paid for the Walk-in-Interview, and candidates arrange their own travel and accommodation.
