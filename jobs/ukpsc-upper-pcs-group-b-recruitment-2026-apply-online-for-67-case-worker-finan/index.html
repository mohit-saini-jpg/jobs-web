UKPSC Combined State Civil Services Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

Selection follows a three-stage process: Preliminary Examination, Main Examination, and Interview. Preliminary Examination (Objective type): A screening exam only - its marks are not added to the Main Examination or Interview marks. It has two objective-type papers. Paper-2 (General Mental Ability Test) is qualifying only, requiring a minimum of 33% marks for all categories. Ranking for qualifying to the Main Examination is based solely on Paper-1 (General Studies) marks. Calculators are not allowed. Negative marking applies: 1/4 of the marks allotted to a question is deducted for each wrong answer; multiple answers to one question are treated as wrong; unattempted questions attract no penalty. Main Examination (Written/Descriptive type): Held at examination centres in Haldwani and Haridwar, using a Question-Answer Booklet (QAB) format. Answers must be given entirely in English or entire

UKPSC Upper PCS Group B Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Visit the official website psc.uk.gov.in or pscuk.net.in
  • 2Go to the "How to Apply" link in the menu bar, review the Advertisement Details, Important Dates, and Instructions for filling up the online application form
  • 3Click "Apply Now" and complete the Registration form with accurate details; create a password and submit
  • 4Verify the Basic Information displayed; tick the verification declaration and submit, or edit if any correction is needed
  • 5After successful Primary Registration, a message will be sent to your registered mobile number and email - click "Click here to login"
  • 6Fill in Educational Details (High School, Intermediate, Graduate, and other qualifications) and select the post(s) you are eligible for
  • 7Upload your photo and signature as per the specified format, and tick the declaration
  • 8Review all filled details carefully, then click the Final Submit button to complete the application
  • 9Pay the application fee online via Net Banking/Debit Card/Credit Card/UPI
  • 10Take a printout of the completed online application form for future reference
  • 11If an error is found before Final Submission, applicants can go back and edit the form. After Final Submission, no changes are possible except during the separate Edit/Correction window (07–16 October 2026); the mobile number cannot be edited even during this window.
