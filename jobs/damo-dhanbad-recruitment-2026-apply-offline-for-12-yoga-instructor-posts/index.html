About the Recruiting Office

The Office of the District AYUSH Medical Officer, Dhanbad works under the Health, Medical Education and Family Welfare Department and is located near Sadar Hospital, Dhanbad, Jharkhand - 826001. The press release (Press Vigyapti) fills Yoga Instructor positions for the Ayushman Arogya Mandir (AYUSH) centres and the District Combined Dispensary run under the Dhanbad District AYUSH Samiti. The instructors conduct yoga and other wellness activities at these centres.

Vacancy Details

The notice lists 12 Yoga Instructor positions: 11 for male instructors and 1 for a female instructor. The centre-wise list attached to the notice is reproduced below. The only position for a female Yoga Instructor is at the District Combined Dispensary (Homoeopathic), Sadar Dhanbad. The notice gives no category-wise reservation break-up.

Who Can Apply

The essential qualification printed in the notice for the Yoga Instructor (Male/Female) post is: Degree/Diploma/Certificate in Yoga from a recognised University/Institution, or Yoga Certification Board / Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana certified Yoga Instructor. The application form asks candidates to list their qualifications from Class 10 upward to the highest, with year of passing, board or university, marks and percentage, and to give work experience if any. Work experience is not listed as an essential condition. Yoga Instructors living within a 05 to 10 km radius of the Ayushman Arogya Mandir (AYUSH) / Health and Wellness Centre / Government AYUSH Dispensary and the District Combined Dispensary will be given priority. The age limit is not specified in the notice. Candidates should check the official notification for the full eligibility conditions of the post.

Pay and Tenure

A Yoga Instructor is paid Rs. 250/- for each session. The engagement is on a remuneration basis, and working days and yoga sessions will follow the guidelines of the Ministry of AYUSH, Government of India. The number of sessions at each Ayushman Arogya Mandir (AYUSH) is fixed as follows: Each session lasts two hours and must be held on the fixed date and time. The notice does not state the period over which these sessions are counted. No claim regarding regularisation of the job after appointment will be considered, and a candidate who claims regularisation in future will have the candidature ended.

Dhanbad AYUSH Yoga Instructor Job Description

Conduct yoga and other Wellness Activities at the allotted Ayushman Arogya Mandir (AYUSH) or District Combined Dispensary. Hold the required number of two-hour yoga sessions on the fixed date and time. Sessions take place at a marked place in the Ayushman Arogya Mandir; when no space is available there, a place may be fixed in the nearest school, panchayat bhawan, anganwadi centre or other community building.

Selection and Interview

The notice refers to an interview, which it also calls a Walk-In Interview, but the interview date, time and venue are not specified in the notice. If more than one candidate is selected, the marks obtained in the yoga course will be the second deciding factor. Instructors living within 05 to 10 km of the centre get priority. No travelling allowance is payable for attending the interview. The District Selection Committee reserves the right to change the Walk-In Interview date as needed.
