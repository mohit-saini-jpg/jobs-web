About BDO Matiali Recruitment 2026

The Office of the Block Development Officer, Matiali Block, Jalpaiguri - 735206, is a block-level office of the Government of West Bengal, located at Chalsa. Under Paschim Banga Samagra Shiksha Mission, an Ashram hostel has been built in one school of Matiali Block – Matelli High School (H.S.) – and the hostel is for girls. The Lady Tutors selected through this notice will work at this hostel.

Department-wise Vacancies

The notice lists 9 Lady Tutor posts, one in each subject, all reserved for women (মহিলা). Six are at the Secondary Level and three at the Higher Secondary Level. The notice does not give a category-wise (UR/SC/ST/OBC) break-up of the posts.

Qualification and Experience

The application form also asks for work experience with the name of the post, duration and organisation, but the notice does not make experience compulsory. All 9 posts are for women only. The applicant must be a local resident; as proof, a Residential Certificate issued by the concerned Gram Panchayat Pradhan must be given. The applicant must be a member of a Self Help Group (স্ব-নির্ভর গোষ্ঠী); as proof, the SHG ID must be given. An active mobile number and the applicant's own full address must be written in the application. The age limit for all Lady Tutor posts is 21 to 60 years, counted as on 01/01/2026. The notice does not mention any age relaxation. Candidates should check the official notification for the subject-wise eligibility of each post.

Matiali Block Jalpaiguri Lady Tutor Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Applications are accepted only offline, through the Drop Box at the BDO office, from 25 November 2026 to 4 December 2026 till 5 PM, during office hours, excluding all holidays. Applications received through any other mode will be rejected.
  • 2Fill in the Application Format printed on page 3 of the notice, writing the post applied for in "Application for the Post of".
  • 3Give the details asked: name in block letters, father's name, spouse name (if married), full postal address, date of birth, age as on 01/01/2026, caste, gender, educational qualification table, work experience, contact number, SHG ID No, EPIC No and AADHAAR No.
  • 4Affix a recent coloured passport size photograph attested by the candidate, and sign the form with place and date.
  • 5Attach the self-attested photocopies listed under Documents Required.
  • 6Put the application with all documents in a sealed envelope addressed to "To The Block Development Officer, Matiali Development Block" and mention the subject on it.
  • 7Drop the envelope in the Drop Box kept at the Office of the Block Development Officer, Matiali Development Block, Chalsa, Jalpaiguri, by 4 December 2026, 5 PM.
  • 8Selection for all posts will be by interview only. The list of candidates nominated for the interview for all posts will be published at the Matiali Block office and on the district website www.jalpaiguri.gov.in, and nominated candidates must download it from the district website.
