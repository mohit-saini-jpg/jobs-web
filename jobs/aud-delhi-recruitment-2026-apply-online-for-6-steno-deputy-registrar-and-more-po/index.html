About Dr. B.R. Ambedkar University Delhi

Dr. B.R. Ambedkar University Delhi is a State University established through an Act by the Legislature of the National Capital Territory of Delhi. The notice is signed by the Registrar and covers four non-teaching posts, all on direct recruitment.

Vacancy Details

The notice advertises 6 posts in total (1 + 1 + 1 + 3). The posts are tentative, and the University reserves the right to increase or decrease their number. One post of Assistant Registrar is reserved for persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD) on horizontal reservation basis. The functional requirements and suitable category of Benchmark Disability are regulated as per the Gazette of India notification dated 07.01.2021 of the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, along with other instructions issued by the Govt. of India (DoPT OM dated 15.01.2018) and GNCT of Delhi.

Who Can Apply

The maximum age for each post is counted on the closing date of the receipt of applications. Candidates must hold the essential qualifications as on the closing date of application. Age relaxation is admissible as per University Rules/ Government Orders. Essential: Master's Degree with at least 55% of the marks or an equivalent grade in a point scale wherever grading system is followed. Essential experience: nine years of experience as Assistant Professor in the Academic Level-10 and above with experience in educational administration; OR comparable experience in research establishment and/ or other institutions of higher education; OR 5 years of administrative experience as Assistant Registrar or in an equivalent post. Desirable: Master's degree in Management/ Law/ Computer Applications; Chartered Accountancy/ Cost Accountancy/ Company Secretary; good knowledge of computer applications.

Selection and Interview

The notice refers to a test/ interview. Only short-listed candidates will be invited. Where many applications are received, the University may, at its discretion, restrict the number of candidates called on the basis of qualifications/ experience higher than the minimum prescribed or any other criteria it decides. Meeting the minimum qualification does not entitle a candidate to be called. The dates of test/ interview will be notified on the University website and sent by email to shortlisted candidates, at the email address given in the application form. The Scheme of Examination applicable to the posts is on https://aud.delhi.gov.in . The University reserves its right to test a candidate's skill at the time of selection.
