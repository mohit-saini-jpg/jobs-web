The online registration window is short, so candidates who have not yet registered on the NATS portal should do so first. The dates for document verification and the local language test are not specified in the notification. Further announcements will be published only on the Bank's website.

Canara Bank has announced 3500 training seats across states and Union Territories. Candidates can opt for training seats in only one state. The seats are provisional and may vary according to the actual requirement of the Bank. Canara Bank Apprentice Eligibility Criteria

All eligibility conditions, including age and qualification, are computed as on 01.09.2026 (inclusive). The tables below summarise the main conditions.

Canara Bank Apprentice Age Limit

The age limit is a minimum of 20 years and a maximum of 28 years as on 01.09.2026. Candidates must have been born not earlier than 02.09.1998 and not later than 01.09.2006 (both days inclusive). The maximum age limit applies to General Category and EWS candidates. Candidates should ensure that they meet the prescribed age limit as on the specified cut-off date. You can use our Age Calculator to check your exact age and confirm your eligibility before applying. Age relaxation for SC/ ST/ OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) candidates is allowed on a cumulative basis with only one of the remaining categories for which age relaxation is permitted. Candidates seeking age relaxation must submit the necessary certificate(s) in original along with photocopies at the time of document verification/ reporting for On-the-Job training and at any later stage required by the Bank. Canara Bank Apprentice Reservation Details

Reservation for SC/ ST/ OBC/ EWS/ PwBD candidates is as per the prescribed reservation percentages of the respective States/ UTs as per Government guidelines. Candidates from reserved categories, including those for whom no reservation is mentioned, may apply for unreserved vacancies if they fulfil all eligibility conditions applicable to the unreserved category. Canara Bank Apprentice Stipend and Salary

Apprentices are not employees of Canara Bank. A monthly stipend is paid instead of a salary during the apprenticeship period.

Canara Bank Apprentice Application Fee

The fee is non-refundable and includes GST. It is payable online only. Payment can be made by Debit Cards (RuPay/ Visa/ MasterCard/ Maestro), Credit Cards, Internet Banking, IMPS, Cash Cards or Mobile Wallets. An e-receipt is generated after a successful transaction. Candidates should not press Back or Refresh after submitting payment information, to avoid double charge.

Canara Bank Apprentice Selection Process

Selection is merit-based. The notification does not describe a written examination. Merit list: Prepared state-wise in descending order of marks/ percentage in 12th Std (HSC/ 10+2)/ Diploma Examination. Ties are ranked by age in descending order. Minimum marks: Not less than 60% (55% for SC/ ST/ PwBD) in 12th Std/ Diploma. Fractions are ignored, so 59.99% is treated as below 60% and 54.99% as below 55%. Document Verification and local language test: Conducted during the Document Verification process. Original documents are checked. Any discrepancy leads to summary disqualification. Final selection is subject to verification of eligibility and application information, qualification in the local language test of the opted State where applicable, and being declared medically fit. Where CGPA/ OGPA is awarded, it must be converted into percentage in the online application, with a certificate

Canara Bank Apprentice Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Candidates must apply online. No other mode of application will be accepted.
  • 2Register on https:/ / nats.education.gov.in and complete your profile to 100%. Keep the enrollment number generated.
  • 3Visit www.canarabank.bank.in, open Careers, then Recruitment, then the link "Engagement of Graduate Apprentices in Canara Bank under Apprentices Act, 1961 for FY 2026-27".
  • 4Click "CLICK HERE FOR NEW REGISTRATION" and enter basic information. A provisional registration number and password are generated and sent by email and SMS. Saved data can be reopened using them.
  • 5Enter the NATS enrollment ID, fill all fields, and choose one State along with district preferences.
  • 6Upload the photograph, signature, left thumb impression and handwritten declaration as specified below.
  • 7Use "SAVE AND NEXT" to verify details. No change is possible after clicking FINAL SUBMIT.
  • 8Pay the fee online (if applicable), then take a printout of the application and e-receipt.
  • 9Apply well before the closing date to avoid website load issues. Only one application should be submitted; if multiple are submitted, only the latest valid completed application is retained.
  • 10The declaration text is: "I, ____ (Name of the candidate), hereby declare that all the information submitted by me in the application form is correct, true and valid. I will present the supporting documents as and when required." Candidates without a left thumb may use the right thumb or other alternatives given in the notification, and must mention the finger and hand/ toe used in the uploaded document. Candidates who cannot write may type the declaration and put their left thumb impression below it. Images should be scanned at a minimum of 200 dpi in true colour, in JPG/ JPEG format. Unclear or smudged images may lead to rejection.
  • 11Candidates also need a valid personal email ID and mobile number, active until the process ends, and should spell their name as in the SSC/ SSLC/ X Standard marks card (or as per Gazette Notification/ Marriage Certificate if changed). An application that is incomplete or has unsuccessful fee payment is invalid. If no email/ SMS intimation is received, the application is considered not successfully registered.
