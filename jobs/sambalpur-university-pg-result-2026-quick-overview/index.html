M.A. (Economics) Second Semester Result, April 2026

This notice states that the results of the listed candidates in the M.A. Examination, April 2026 are declared. The list is arranged by serial number and gives each candidate's roll number, name, GPA and a Pass or Fail result. The notice is addressed to the Principals of several affiliated colleges and to the Head of the P.G. Department of Economics, Sambalpur University, among others.

This notice declares the results of the listed candidates in the M.A./M.Sc. (Statistics) Second Semester Examination, April 2026. How to Check Sambalpur University Result 2026

  • Open the university notice board at suniv.ac.in/notice-board.php.
  • Find the notice titled “Provisional Results of M.A. (Economics) Second Semester Examination, April 2026” or “Provisional Results of M.A./M.Sc. (Statistics) Second Semester Examination, April 2026”.
  • Click the download link in the same row to open the PDF.
  • Search for your roll number or name in the list. On most devices, Ctrl+F (or the search option in your PDF viewer) works.
  • Read your GPA and result status, then save or print the PDF for your records.

Is This Result Final?

No. Both notices state that the published results are provisional and subject to modification after post-publication scrutiny. The university says that all care has been taken to ensure accuracy, but any mistake found should be reported.

What to Do If There Is a Mistake in Your Result

  • Bring the mistake to the notice of the Controller of Examinations, Sambalpur University.
  • Do this within 15 days from the date of publication of the results.
  • Keep a copy of the result PDF and your admit card or roll number details ready.

Important Instructions from the Notices

  • The results are provisional and may change after scrutiny.
  • Errors must be reported to the Controller of Examinations within 15 days of publication.
  • Copies of the Economics notice were forwarded to the P.G. Department of Economics and the Principals of the listed colleges for information and necessary action.
  • The Statistics notice was forwarded to the P.G. Department of Statistics, the Section Officer (Computer Unit) and E-Governance for uploading.
