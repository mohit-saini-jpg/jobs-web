What Happens on the Day

Candidates register at the School of Vocational Studies (SVS) between 09:00 AM and 11:00 AM on 30 November 2026. Candidates who do not fulfil the eligibility criteria will not be considered for the selection process. The selection may be by a presentation, a seminar, an interview, or a combination of these. The academic score as specified in Appendix II (Table 3A) for Universities will be considered only for short-listing candidates for interview. Selections will be based only on performance in the interview. Assessment will be based on evidence the applicant produces. Examples include copies of publications, project sanction letters, utilisation and completion certificates, patent filing acknowledgements and approval letters, and students' Ph.D. award letters. No TA/DA will be paid for document verification or the interview.

AUD Guest Faculty Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1When is the AUD Guest Faculty walk-in interview?
A
The AUD Guest Faculty walk-in interview is on 30 November 2026. Registration runs from 09:00 AM to 11:00 AM.
Q2Where is the AUD Guest Faculty walk-in venue?
A
The venue is G-3, Block-A, School of Vocational Studies (SVS), Dr. B.R. Ambedkar University Delhi, Shivaji Marg, Karampura Campus (Near Moti Nagar Metro Station), Delhi – 110 015.
Q3How many Guest Faculty positions are there?
A
There are 12 positions: UR – 03, ST – 02, SC – 02, EWS – 02 and OBC – 03. They cover Tourism & Hospitality Management, Accounting & Finance, Early Childhood Education and Retail Management.
Q4What qualification is required for AUD Guest Faculty?
A
Candidates need a Master's degree with 55% marks in a relevant subject plus NET/SLET/SET, or a Ph.D. as per UGC Regulations 2009/2016. Alternatively, a Ph.D. from a top-500 ranked foreign university qualifies. A Master Degree with 55% marks plus minimum 5 years of relevant industry experience also qualifies.
Q5What is the age limit for AUD Guest Faculty?
A
Superannuated teachers may be considered for engagement as Guest Faculty, subject to a maximum age limit of 70 years.
Q6What is the honorarium for AUD Guest Faculty?
A
The honorarium is ₹1500/- per lecture, subject to a maximum of ₹50,000/- per month as per UGC norms. Allowances, pension, gratuity and leave admissible to regular teachers are not given.
Q7What documents should I bring to the walk-in?
A
Bring all original certificates/mark sheets with an attested photocopy of each, a recent passport size photograph and an updated Bio-data. Also bring identity proof, date of birth proof, any applicable category or NOC certificates, and the Annexure I or II Ph.D. certificate if applicable.
Q8How will Guest Faculty be selected?
A
The selection may be by a presentation, seminar, interview or a combination of these. The academic score is used only for short-listing, and selections are based only on interview performance.
Q9How long is the AUD Guest Faculty panel valid?
A
The panel is valid for one year. Its validity may be extended till the ongoing Semester is completed, if required.
Q10Will TA/DA be paid for attending the interview?
A
No. The notice states that no TA/DA will be provided for attending the interview or for document verification.
