  • 1Applications must be submitted offline in person between 23 and 28 October 2026, excluding Sunday, during the notified office hours.
  • 2Visit the official Medchal Malkajgiri District website at medchal-malkajgiri.telangana.gov.in.
  • 3Locate the relevant DMHO recruitment notification dated 19 October 2026.
  • 4Select the appropriate notification for the Basti Dawakhana 57-post recruitment or the NHM/NUHM 63-post recruitment.
  • 5Download the prescribed application form for the selected recruitment.
  • 6Fill in the required personal, educational, professional registration, experience and category details.
  • 7Enter local-area particulars carefully and attach the applicable study certificates.
  • 8Paste a recent photograph on the prescribed application and sign across it.
  • 9Prepare original certificates and one set of self-attested photocopies.
  • 10Obtain the prescribed Rs. 500 Demand Draft from a nationalized bank in favour of the District Medical & Health Officer, Medchal-Malkajgiri.
  • 11Visit the Office of DMHO, F-1 IDOC Building, Anthaipally, Shamirpet.
  • 12Submit the completed application and required documents between 11:00 AM and 3:00 PM during the notified application period.
