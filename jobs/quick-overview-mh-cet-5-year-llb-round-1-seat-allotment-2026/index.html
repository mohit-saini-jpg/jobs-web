How to Check MH CET LLB 5-Year Round 1 Seat Allotment Result 2026

  • Visit the official CET Cell website, cetcell.mahacet.org, or the dedicated 5-year LLB CAP counselling portal for the current admission cycle.
  • Click on the link related to 'CAP 2026-27' or '5-Year LLB Round 1 Seat Allotment Result' on the homepage.
  • Log in using your application ID/registration number and password (or date of birth, wherever applicable).
  • Your Round 1 seat allotment status will be displayed on screen, showing the allotted college, course, and category.
  • Download and save the provisional seat allotment letter for future reference.
  • Take a printout of the allotment letter - it will be needed at the time of reporting to the college.

What Details Appear on the MH CET LLB Round 1 Allotment Letter

  • Candidate's name, application ID and merit rank
  • Category/reservation under which the seat has been allotted
  • Name of the allotted law college and course
  • Reporting instructions and the last date to report

Freeze, Float and Betterment: What Are Your Options After Allotment?

Once the Round 1 result is out, allotted candidates generally have the following choices, based on the CAP rules notified for this admission cycle:

  • Accept and report: Candidates satisfied with their allotted college can report and complete admission formalities within the reporting window.
  • Betterment: Candidates who wish to try for a higher-preference college can opt for betterment to participate in the next CAP round.
  • Auto-freeze change this year: As per this year's revised CAP rules, candidates allotted their first-preference college are no longer automatically frozen out of later rounds, unlike in some previous years - candidates should read the official instructions carefully before deciding whether to freeze or float.

Important Instructions for Candidates

  • Always access the seat allotment result through the official CET Cell website; avoid third-party links claiming to offer faster or alternative access.
  • Keep your application ID, password, and registered mobile number/email handy, as these are required for login.
  • Report to the allotted college within the notified reporting window - missing the deadline can affect your admission.
  • If you are not satisfied with your Round 1 allotment, check the official rules on betterment and float options before the next round's choice-filling window closes.
  • Retain scanned and printed copies of the allotment letter and all supporting documents until the admission process is fully complete.
