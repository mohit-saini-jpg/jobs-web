GIPMER Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Ten Junior Resident posts in Cardiology (4), CTVS (1), G.I. Surgery (2), Biochemistry (1) and Psychiatry (2) are to be filled purely on adhoc basis at the Govind Ballabh Pant Institute of Postgraduate Medical Education & Research (GIPMER), Govt. of NCT of Delhi, at Pay Level 10 (Rs. 56100-Rs. 177500) plus usual allowances, for 44 days or till regular incumbents join, whichever is earlier. The application form (Annexure-I) must be submitted at 5th Floor, D-Block, Academic Cell, GIPMER on or before 30 November 2026 up to 04.00 P.M., and the interview will be held on 2 December 2026 at 12:00 Noon

About GIPMER Junior Resident

The Govind Ballabh Pant Institute of Postgraduate Medical Education & Research (GIPMER) is an institute of the Government of NCT of Delhi located at 1, J.L. Nehru Marg, New Delhi-110002. This interview notice was issued by the Institute's Administration Branch with the prior approval of the Medical Director, GIPMER, and the posts are filled under the Residency Scheme of the Government of India.

Qualification and Experience

Further eligibility conditions printed in the notice: Foreign Medical Graduates (FMGs) who have completed their internship outside India and have qualified the Foreign Medical Graduate Examination (FMGE) shall be considered eligible based on the date of declaration of the FMGE (date of passing FMGE), irrespective of the date of completion of internship. Candidates who have completed their internship within one year of the last date for submission of application are eligible. Applicants who have completed their internship one year or more before the last date of receiving applications will not be considered and need not apply. A valid DMC registration is required at the time of joining. Candidates who have applied and have a valid DMC acknowledgement receipt will be allowed for the interview. If selected, candidates should produce the DMC certificate with U.G. qualification before joining

Age Limit and Relaxation

The age limit is 30 years (as on the closing date, i.e. 30/11/2026) for General/EWS category candidates. OBC/EWS candidates from Delhi only are eligible and must submit a caste certificate issued by the competent authority of GNCT of Delhi, mentioning that they belong to the Non Creamy Layer for the current financial year. In case of non-availability of candidates, candidates may be considered under the relaxation provided in circular No.F.121/26/2010/H&FW/1996-2045, dated 10.06.2011, issued by the Health & Family Welfare Department, Govt. of NCT of Delhi, under which the upper age limit for eligibility will be 40 years in place of 30 years.

Pay and Terms

Junior Residents will get Pay level 10 (Rs. 56100-Rs. 177500/-) plus usual allowances as per rules/residency rules. The appointment and services will be governed under the Residency Scheme of Govt. of India. The appointment is purely on adhoc basis for 44 days or till the regular incumbents join, whichever is earlier. The tenure is 44 days with renewal after 44 days for a maximum of one year, including any service rendered as Junior Resident earlier on adhoc/regular basis in any recognized institution. Extension up to one year is subject to satisfactory performance and the work and conduct report from the concerned HOD. Posting of Junior Resident will be made in the interest of the Institute. GIPMER Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Candidates will be selected on the basis of the interview only, and selection will be made strictly on merit. The interview will be held on 2 December 2026 at 12:00 Noon in the concerned department; reporting time is 11:00 AM. The interview may be staggered over a few days if a large number of applications is received. If the interview cannot be completed on the scheduled date, it shall be conducted on the following working day. A waiting list of up to 20% of the advertised vacancies may be prepared, subject to the availability of suitable and eligible candidates; it shall remain valid till 10 days after declaration/publication of the result. If a selected candidate fails to submit acceptance or does not join within the stipulated time, the vacancy may be offered to candidates from the waiting list, strictly in order of merit. No TA/DA will be paid for appearing in the interview.

GIPMER Recruitment 2026 : Application Fee Details
