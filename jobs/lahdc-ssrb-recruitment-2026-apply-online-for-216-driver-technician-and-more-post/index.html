LAHD SSRB Domicile Eligibility 2026

Only eligible residents of the erstwhile Leh District can apply for the LAHD SSRB Recruitment 2026 district cadre posts. Candidates must possess the prescribed Domicile Certificate issued by the competent authority under the applicable Union Territory of Ladakh rules. Applicants from outside the prescribed erstwhile Leh District domicile area are not eligible merely because they reside elsewhere in Ladakh. Candidates must verify their domicile status before submitting the online form.
