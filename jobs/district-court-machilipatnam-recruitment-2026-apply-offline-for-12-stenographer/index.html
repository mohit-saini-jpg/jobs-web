District Court Machilipatnam Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Principal District Court, Krishna at Machilipatnam, has 12 contract posts to fill in the Fast Track Courts sanctioned to Krishna District: Head Clerk, Stenographer, Junior Assistant-cum-Typist and Office Subordinate (Attender). The posts are offered first to eligible retired judicial employees, and to eligible outside candidates only when no suitable retired employees are available. Applications are addressed to the Principal District Judge, Krishna at Machilipatnam, and must be received by 13 November 2026 up to 5:00 P.M.

About Krishna District Court

The Principal District Court, Krishna at Machilipatnam, is the principal district court of Krishna District in Andhra Pradesh, headed by the Principal District Judge, Krishna, Machilipatnam. This recruitment fills posts in the Fast Track Courts sanctioned to Krishna District.

Where and How to Send

Address the application to the Principal District Judge, Krishna at Machilipatnam . The notice prints no further address or PIN code. Applications received after 13 November 2026, 5:00 P.M. will not be accepted, and applications sent before the notification date (26 October 2026) will not be considered. The office is not responsible for postal delays or for delays caused by courier or other delivery services. The notice includes two application forms: Retired employees: "Application for the post of ____ in Fast Track Courts (For the Retired Employees)". It asks for the name (in capital letters), father's or husband's name, date of birth, age as on 01.07.2026, academic and technical qualifications, address for communication, mobile number, date of retirement, designation on the date of retirement, and whether any punishment was imposed during service in the Judicial Department. District Court Machilipatnam Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in Krishna District Court Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 12 vacancies: 04 Head Clerk, 05 Stenographer, 02 Junior Assistant-cum-Typist and 01 Office Subordinate (Attender), all in the Fast Track Courts of Krishna District.
Q2What is the last date to apply?
A
Applications must be received by 13 November 2026 up to 5:00 P.M. Applications received after that will not be accepted, and the office is not responsible for postal or courier delays.
Q3What qualification do outside candidates need?
A
For Stenographer, outside candidates need a Bachelor's Degree plus the AP Government Technical Examination in English Typewriting (Higher Grade) and English Shorthand (Higher Grade) or equivalent. For Junior Assistant-cum-Typist, they need a Bachelor's Degree plus English Typewriting (Higher Grade). For Office Subordinate (Attender), they need a 7th class pass, and anyone with a higher qualification than that is not eligible.
Q4What is the age limit?
A
Outside candidates must have completed 18 years and must not have completed 44 years as on 1 July 2026, with 5 years' relaxation for SC, ST, BC and EWS and 10 years for Persons with Benchmark Disabilities and Ex-Servicemen. Retired judicial employees must not have reached 65 years.
Q5What is the salary for these posts?
A
Selected candidates receive a consolidated monthly remuneration, paid in accordance with the applicable rules and orders.
Q6How do I apply?
A
Fill in the right application form in the notice (one for retired employees, one for outsiders), attach the required certificates and a self-addressed stamped postal cover, and address it to the Principal District Judge, Krishna at Machilipatnam. It must reach the office by 13 November 2026, 5:00 P.M.
Q7What is the selection process?
A
Depending on the post and category, selection is through an objective test, a skill test or a computer proficiency test, followed by an interview. For Office Subordinate (Attender), selection is by interview only.
Q8Can retired employees apply, and do they get preference?
A
Yes. Eligible retired judicial employees are considered first for all posts, and outside candidates are considered only if no suitable retired employees are available. Retired employees must have retired on superannuation and must not have reached 65 years.
Q9Can outside candidates apply for the Head Clerk post?
A
No. The Head Clerk post is for retired judicial employees only: those who retired as Superintendent in Category III of the A.P.J.M.S.S. Rules, or above.
Q10Is this a permanent job?
A
No. The appointment is purely temporary, contractual and ad hoc. Selected candidates sign an agreement with the Principal District Judge, Krishna District, Machilipatnam, initially for one year.
