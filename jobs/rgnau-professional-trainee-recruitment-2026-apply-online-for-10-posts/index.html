About RGNAU

Rajiv Gandhi National Aviation University (RGNAU) is a Central University under the Ministry of Civil Aviation, Govt. of India. It was established by the Rajiv Gandhi National Aviation University (RGNAU) Act, 2013 (No. 26 of 2013), and its headquarters are at Fursatganj, Dist. Amethi, Uttar Pradesh. The University describes itself as an institution of higher learning in the aviation field that aims to provide research for the aviation industry in India.

Vacancy Details
