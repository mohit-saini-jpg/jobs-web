NHM Janjgir Champa Recruitment 2026 Notification Out

National Health Mission (NHM), District Health Society, Janjgir-Champa, Chhattisgarh has released the NHM Janjgir Champa Recruitment 2026 Notification for 66 contractual vacancies across various healthcare, nursing, technical, administrative, and support staff posts. The recruitment includes Staff Nurse, Laboratory Technician, Radiographer, Community Health Officer (CHO), ANM, Dental Surgeon, Senior Nursing Officer, Accountant, Block Manager, Security Guard, Class IV, Cleaner, Attendant, and several other positions. Eligible candidates with qualifications ranging from 8th Pass, 10th Pass, 12th Pass, Diploma, ANM, GNM, B.Sc Nursing, Graduation, BDS/MDS, and other relevant disciplines can apply. Contract Period

The appointments are being made on a contractual basis . The notification states that the engagement will initially be valid up to 31 March 2027 . The contractual appointment may be extended depending on satisfactory performance, availability of the post, approval in the applicable ROP and other governing conditions. Contract employees will receive the fixed monthly contractual remuneration applicable to their post. Additional allowances or facilities outside those admissible under the applicable rules are not automatically payable.

Chhattisgarh Domicile Requirement

The notification requires candidates to be original residents of Chhattisgarh and to submit the prescribed residence certificate issued by the competent authority.

Important Points Before Applying

Check the exact educational qualification for the post before applying. Professional registration must be valid wherever the post requires registration. Applications containing incomplete or incorrect information can be rejected. Separate applications are required when applying for more than one post. The applicable application fee must also be paid separately for each post. Self-attested copies of the required educational and supporting documents should be enclosed. Relevant experience will be considered only when supported by acceptable experience certificates. Reservation will be provided according to applicable Chhattisgarh Government and NHM rules. Selection does not create a claim for permanent government employment. Candidates should regularly check the Janjgir-Champa district website for recruitment updates.
