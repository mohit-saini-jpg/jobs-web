ISRO SDSC SHAR Exam Pattern 2026

The minimum written-test qualifying marks are 32 out of 80 for unreserved candidates and 24 out of 80 for eligible candidates against reserved posts. The minimum qualifying marks for the skill test are 50 out of 100 for unreserved candidates and 40 out of 100 for eligible candidates against reserved posts. The examination duration is 120 minutes, with one mark for each correct answer and a deduction of 0.25 mark for each incorrect answer. The subsequent driving skill test is qualifying in nature. The MTS examination covers Numerical and Mathematical Ability, Reasoning Ability and Problem Solving, General Awareness, and English Language and Comprehension.
