About GGSGH

Guru Gobind Singh Govt. Hospital is a Government of NCT of Delhi hospital at Raghubir Nagar, New Delhi-110027. This recruitment is run by the Office of the Medical Superintendent. The posts are vacant within the sanctioned strength of Senior Residents and against vacant posts of non-teaching specialists as on 27 October 2026. They are being filled under order No. F.11/92/H&FW/2017/HR-Med/688-92 dated 17 July 2017 of the Health & Family Welfare Department, Govt. of NCT of Delhi.

GGSGH Senior Resident Vacancy Details 2026

The number of vacancies may change. It will be counted as on the date the result is published, and the number of SR vacancies may vary at any time. If no SC, ST, OBC or EWS candidates are available, UR candidates may be considered. The Senior Resident (Radiology) will be allowed to work 2-3 days a week in the Department of Radiology, Deen Dayal Upadhyay Hospital, Hari Nagar, New Delhi. GGSGH Senior Resident Eligibility 2026

Age limit: 45 years. Qualification (mandatory): MBBS with P.G. Degree/Diploma/DNB, or three years of experience in the concerned specialty, from a recognized university or institution. Candidates must also hold a valid registration with the Delhi Medical Council. Senior Residency condition: (a) Candidates should not have completed 03 years of Senior Residency in any recognized institution, counting both regular and ad hoc periods. Candidates who have completed 03 years of Senior Residency can also apply, but preference will be given to those under (a). The notice says all other provisions for persons with disabilities will follow the applicable norms and guidelines. For the full eligibility conditions for each department, see the official notification.

Pay

Senior Residents are paid at Level-11, with an initial basic pay of Rs. 67,700 – Rs. 208700, plus admissible allowance.

GGSGH Senior Resident Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

Selection is by walk-in interview. The hospital checks original certificates at the interview. Results and other notices are posted on www.health.delhigovt.nic.in , ggsgh.delhi.gov.in and the hospital notice board.

Service Conditions

The appointment is purely ad hoc, for 89 days or until the post is filled by a regular or ad hoc SR or specialist of the department, whichever is earlier. A DMC certificate must be submitted within 2 weeks of joining. If it is not, the service may be terminated. No TA/DA will be paid for attending the interview.

GGSGH Senior Resident Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Prepare an application on standard form. It must include: name of the post applied for, name, father's/spouse's name, date of birth, category, address, DMC registration number, date of internship completion, educational qualification, contact number and experience (if any).
  • 2Attach attested copies of all certificates, including ID/residential address proof, and one recent passport-size photograph.
  • 3Report between 09:30 AM and 10:00 AM to the Establishment Branch, B Block, 3rd Floor, Guru Gobind Singh Govt. Hospital, Raghubir Nagar, New Delhi-110027.
  • 4Bring all original certificates for verification.

GGSGH Senior Resident Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1Which departments have Senior Resident vacancies at GGSGH?

There are vacancies in Radiology, Paediatrics and Medicine. Paediatrics has the most posts: 06 (Gen 01, SC 02, ST 01, OBC 01, EWS 01).

A
Interviews are held every day until the posts are filled. Candidates must report between 09:30 AM and 10:00 AM.
Q3Where should candidates report?
