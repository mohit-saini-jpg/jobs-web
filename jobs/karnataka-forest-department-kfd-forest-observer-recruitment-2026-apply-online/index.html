KFD Forest Observer Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for KFD Forest Observer Recruitment 2026, candidates must meet all the conditions below, along with the qualification and age limit given in the next sections. The candidate must be a citizen of India. A male candidate with more than one living wife, or a female candidate married to a man who already has a wife, is not eligible for appointment without prior permission of the Government. The candidate must be mentally and physically healthy and free from any physical defect likely to interfere with efficient performance of duties. The candidate must meet the physical standards and pass the physical endurance and physical efficiency tests described below. The recruiting authority may reject a candidate as physically unfit on the advice of a medical board, and the Government's discretion is not limited by these rules. KFD Forest Observer Qualification

The educational qualification for KFD Forest Observer is SSLC. The candidate must hold the SSLC examination conducted by the Karnataka Secondary Education Examination Board, or an equivalent qualification, on the last date to apply. As per Government circular No. CASUE 81 SENENI 2017 dated 27.02.2018, the following are treated as equivalent to SSLC: Class 10 examination conducted by CBSE and ICSE boards, or Class 10 examination conducted by other State Government examination boards, or Secondary-level course conducted by the National Institute of Open Schooling (NIOS), or Secondary-level course conducted by the Karnataka Secondary Education Examination Board (KOS). Candidates who have not received the result of the prescribed qualification, or who do not hold the marks card, as on the last date to apply are not eligible. KFD Forest Observer Selection Process

KFD Forest Observer Selection Process is based on physical tests and SSLC marks. The notification does not mention any written examination. The stages below follow the order given in the notification. Screening of online applications: Applications are considered on the basis of the details filled in and the documents uploaded. Applications with incorrect, incomplete or unclear information are rejected at the initial stage. Provisional list for physical tests: Candidates are shortlisted in the ratio of 1:20 (post:candidate), based on marks obtained in SSLC or the equivalent examination. This list is for the Physical Standard Test, Physical Endurance Test and Physical Efficiency Test and is published on aranya.gov.in under "Nemakati" (Recruitment). After appointment as a Forest Observer, the candidate must serve in the department for at least 3 years from the date of appointment.
