BEL Project Engineer Salary Structure 2026

Project Engineers are engaged on a consolidated remuneration basis that increases each year, along with additional benefits. Additional benefits: Area allowance at 10% of the consolidated remuneration per month - applicable only to candidates posted at various sites/customer locations. Rs. 12,000 per year towards insurance premium, attire allowance, stitching charges, footwear allowance, etc. Tenure: Project Engineer-I is initially engaged for a period of 3 years, which may be extended by one more year based on project requirement and individual performance. Retention Bonus: Project Engineer-I candidates who complete an overall tenure of 4 years are entitled to a Retention Bonus of Rs. 25,000 for every completed year, payable at the end of 4 years - i.e., a total of Rs. 1,00,000.

BEL Project Engineer Application Fee 2026

Application fee must be paid through SBI Collect, either online or by visiting an SBI branch. No cheque, demand draft, or cash will be accepted. Fees once paid will not be refunded under any circumstances. Go to onlinesbi.sbi and select "State Bank Collect". Accept the Terms and Conditions and click "Proceed". Select the state of Corporation/Institution as "All India". Select the type of Corporation/Institution as "PSU - Public Sector Undertaking" and click "Go". Select "Bharat Electronics Limited" under PSU - Public Sector Undertaking and click "Submit". Select the payment category "Project Engineer Raj/Guj Walk In". Complete the payment as per the instructions shown. Take a print of the payment receipt and attach it with the application form. Candidates must ensure they enter the same mobile number and email ID in SBI Collect as mentioned in their application form.

BEL Project Engineer Selection Process

Selection will be conducted through a walk-in process for pre-registered candidates only. Candidates must pre-register for the walk-in selection through the official QR code by 09:00 AM on 11.08.2026. Those who do not register will not be allowed to participate. The selection process starts at 08:00 AM; no candidate is allowed entry after 08:30 AM. Selection consists of a written test followed by an interview. Based on the written test score, candidates are shortlisted for interview in the ratio of 1:5 in each category, in order of merit. No travelling expenses/allowance will be given for attending the walk-in selection. Candidates may be required to stay for an additional day for the selection process. Assessment weightage is 85% for the written test and 15% for the interview.
