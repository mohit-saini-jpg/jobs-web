AIIMS Jammu Faculty Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Visit the official AIIMS Jammu website and open the online application link between 14.10.2026 and 13.11.2026.
  • 2Register and fill out the online application form separately for each post if applying for more than one position.
  • 3Upload scanned copies of required documents, certificates, photograph, and signature in PDF/JPEG format (200 dpi grey scale).
  • 4Pay the requisite application fee online through the payment gateway.
  • 5Submit the online application form and print the online application along with the covering letter.
  • 6Send the hard copy of the signed application form with self-attested copies of all relevant certificates, proof of payment, and testimonials by post or by hand to the Registrar, Academic Block, 6th Floor, All India Institute of Medical Sciences, Vijaypur, Jammu, 1841134 on or before 18.11.2026.
