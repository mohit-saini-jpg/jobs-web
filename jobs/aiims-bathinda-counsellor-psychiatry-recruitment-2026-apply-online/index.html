AIIMS Bathinda Counsellor (Psychiatry) Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The Addiction Treatment Facility (ATF) at All India Institute of Medical Sciences, Bathinda, set up under the National Action Plan for Drug Demand Reduction (NAPDDR) and supported by the Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment, is engaging a Counsellor on a purely contractual basis for 2 vacancies (UR-02) at consolidated remuneration of Rs 56,100/- per month. Eligible Indian Nationals with M.A./M.Sc. in Psychology with M.Phil. in Clinical Psychology, aged between 21-35 years, must fill the Google Form from 20 November 2026 to 19 December 2026, 05:00 PM IST, and then attend a walk-in interview

About AIIMS Bathinda

All India Institute of Medical Sciences, Bathinda is located at Jodhpur Romana, Mandi Dabwali Road, Bathinda, Punjab - 151001. This recruitment is issued by its Recruitment Cell for the Addiction Treatment Facility, which is coordinated by the National Drug Dependence Treatment Centre (NDDTC), AIIMS, New Delhi.

Who Can Apply

Only Indian Nationals may apply. The post is unreserved: the notice shows UR-02 and no other category. Age limit: between 21-35 years. The cut-off date for the upper age limit and for all other qualifications and experience is the last date of Google Form submission, 19 December 2026. The upper age limit is relaxable by 10 years for Persons with Benchmark Disability. No age relaxation is available to reserved category candidates applying for the unreserved vacancy. Other age relaxation is regulated by the instructions of the Department of Personnel and Training issued from time to time. Candidates in service in any Government Institution must submit a No Objection Certificate from the present employer at the time of interview. Candidates should check the official notification for the full eligibility conditions of the post.

Pay and Tenure

The Counsellor receives consolidated remuneration of Rs 56,100/- per month, as approved under the ATF Scheme/NHM norms and adopted by AIIMS Bathinda. No annual increment, Dearness Allowance, House Rent Allowance, Transport Allowance or other allowance is admissible unless specifically approved by the funding agency. Statutory deductions are made where applicable. The engagement is purely on contract, initially for 1 year or till continuation of the project, whichever is earlier. The notice says it may be extended subject to satisfactory performance, availability of funds and continuation of the scheme, but also that the contractual appointment shall not be extended beyond one year; if not extended, it lapses automatically. The appointment gives no right to regular appointment or permanent absorption. The appointee may leave by giving one month's notice or paying salary in lieu of the not

The fee is non-refundable and is paid through the online payment gateway only, at https://www.onlinesbi.sbi/sbicollect/icollecthome.htm?corpID=2322756 . The transaction reference number and date of transaction must be written in the application form. OBC/SC/ST candidates may apply but get no concession or relaxation. Selection and Interview

Selection is on the basis of the interview. Document verification at AIIMS Bathinda is followed by the interview of eligible candidates only, on the same day. The interview date is to be notified later. The Institute may shortlist candidates on educational qualification, experience, suitability or any other criteria considered appropriate by the Selection Committee. Meeting the prescribed qualification does not by itself entitle a candidate to selection. The interview may extend to the next day if the number of applicants is large. No TA/DA or accommodation is provided for attending it. Original documents are verified before the interview and again at joining.
