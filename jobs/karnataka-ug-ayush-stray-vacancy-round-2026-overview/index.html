Karnataka UG AYUSH Stray Vacancy Round 2026 - Overview : Job Overview

The Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) has released the Karnataka UG AYUSH Stray Vacancy Round 2026 Schedule on 24 November 2026. The authority will conduct the online stray vacancy seat allotment process to fill undergraduate AYUSH seats remaining vacant after the third round of counselling. Candidates who qualified in NEET UG 2026 and meet the prescribed eligibility conditions can participate through the official KEA website, cetonline.karnataka.gov.in.

Karnataka UG AYUSH Stray Vacancy Round 2026 - Overview : Important Dates & Timelines

Notification DatePost Date: 25/11/2026

New registrations began on 24 November and will close on 25 November 2026 at 11:59 PM. Eligible candidates must download their verification slips and pay the Rs. 50,000 caution deposit by 26 November 2026. The provisional seat allotment result will be announced on 28 November, followed by the final allotment on 29 November. Candidates receiving seats must complete the fee payment, download their Seat Confirmation Slip and report to the allotted college by 30 November 2026.

Karnataka AYUSH Stray Vacancy Caution Deposit Refund and Forfeiture Rules

Candidates must understand the financial consequences of participating in the stray vacancy counselling round.

According to the published KEA schedule, a candidate who receives a seat but fails to join the allotted college within the prescribed deadline will lose the Rs. 50,000 caution deposit. Additional financial or legal consequences may also apply under the counselling rules.

The available news report does not independently establish the complete refund procedure for candidates who do not receive seats. Candidates should read the applicable KEA notification and refund instructions before making payment.
