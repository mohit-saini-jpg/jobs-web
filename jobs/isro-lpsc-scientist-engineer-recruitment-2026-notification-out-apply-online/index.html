LPSC Scientist Engineer Recruitment 2026: Quick Summary

Who is recruiting? Liquid Propulsion Systems Centre (LPSC), ISRO, Department of Space, Government of India. Which posts? Scientist Engineer 'SC' in Welding (Post No. 820), Turbo Machinery (Post No. 821) and Power Electronics (Post No. 822). How many vacancies? 01 vacancy for each of the three posts, which adds up to 3 vacancies. All posts are Un-Reserved. Who can apply? Indian nationals holding the prescribed B.E./B.Tech and M.E./M.Tech qualifications as on 13.10.2026, with a maximum age of 30 years as on 13.10.2026. Application start date: 29.09.2026 (Tuesday) from 1400 hrs. Last date to apply: 13.10.2026 (Tuesday) up to 1400 hrs. Application mode: Online only, through the online Recruitment Portal on the LPSC website. Job location: LPSC, Valiamala, Thiruvananthapuram. Candidates may be posted to any ISRO/Department of Space Unit or Centre in India as required.

LPSC Scientist Engineer Salary

Scientist Engineer 'SC' is placed at Level 10 of the Pay Matrix, with a pay range of ₹56,100 to ₹1,77,500. The notification does not give the in-hand salary or allowance details. It states that, as per Central Government orders, ISRO employees also receive benefits such as medical facilities for self and dependents, a subsidised canteen, House Building Advance, Leave Travel Concession, the National Pension System/Unified Pension Scheme and other social security measures. Career advancement for scientific and technical personnel in ISRO is based on the Merit Promotion Scheme, under which employees are reviewed and promoted to the next grade on merit after a prescribed residency period, without reference to vacancies.

LPSC Scientist Engineer Selection Process

The selection process for all posts consists of a Written Test followed by a Personal Interview. The Aptitude/Ability Test questions will be based on Numerical Reasoning, Logical Reasoning, Diagrammatic Reasoning, Abstract Reasoning and Deductive Reasoning. Based on performance in the written test, candidates will be shortlisted for the interview in a 1:5 ratio, with a minimum of 10 candidates. Candidates called for the interview will have the option to answer the questions in Hindi also. For the final panel, 50% weightage will be given to the written test marks and 50% weightage to the Personal Interview marks. The final selection will be made from among candidates who qualify in both the written test and the interview, and the panel will be prepared purely on the total score obtained after applying this weightage.
