TN NHM Staff Nurse Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The District Health Society, NHM-TN, has invited applications from eligible candidates for engagement as Contractual/Temporary Staff Nurse. TN NHM Staff Nurse Eligibility Criteria

Candidates should check both the qualification and age requirements below before applying. Applicants must have completed GNM (General Nursing and Midwifery) or B.Sc. Nursing from a Government institution or a Government-approved private nursing college recognised by the Indian Nursing Council. Candidates must also hold valid registration with the Tamil Nadu Nurses and Midwives Council.

TN NHM Staff Nurse Age Limit

The upper age limit for applicants is 50 years. The notification does not specify a minimum age or any upper-age relaxation details for reserved categories. Candidates should ensure that they meet the prescribed age limit as on the specified cut-off date. You can use our Age Calculator to check your exact age and confirm your eligibility before applying.

Important Note

As per the official notification, this recruitment drive is not applicable to Tiruppur and Chengalpattu Districts, where the Model Code of Conduct for elections is currently in force.

TN NHM Staff Nurse Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Applications are to be submitted offline, not through an online portal. Eligible candidates should follow these steps:
  • 2Download the application form and the district-wise vacancy details from the official NHM-TN website: nhm.tn.gov.in.
  • 3Fill in the application form completely and attach photocopies of the relevant supporting documents.
  • 4Send the filled application, along with document photocopies, to the Office of the District Health Officer/District Health Society of the concerned district.
  • 5For all districts except Chengalpattu and Tiruppur, the application should be addressed to the Executive Secretary, District Health Office, respective District Health Society.
  • 6Ensure the application reaches the office on or before 25.09.2026, 5:00 PM.

Shift work hota hai, lekin regular government holidays milte hain. Experience badhne par permanent ya higher grade posts ke liye eligibility mil sakti hai.
