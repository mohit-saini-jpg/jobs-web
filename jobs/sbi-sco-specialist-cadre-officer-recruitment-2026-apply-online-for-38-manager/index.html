SBI Specialist Cadre Officer Selection Process 2026

Shortlisting: Merely meeting the minimum qualification and experience does not entitle a candidate to an interview call. The shortlisting committee decides the parameters, and the Bank's decision on which candidates to call is final. Interview: Carries 100 marks; qualifying marks are decided by the Bank. Merit List: Prepared in descending order of interview scores; candidates tied at the cut-off are ranked by age (older candidates ranked higher). CTC Negotiation (contractual posts only - DVP, DCTO, VP): Conducted with candidates in merit order, one by one. If a selected candidate does not agree to the negotiated CTC, they are treated as a Voluntary Exit, and the next candidate in the merit list is called. Final Selection: Top-most candidates in the merit list (with successful CTC negotiation, for contractual posts) are finally selected.

SBI SCO Specialist Cadre Officer Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1What is SBI Specialist Cadre Officer Recruitment 2026?
A
It is a combined recruitment drive by State Bank of India for 38 Specialist Cadre Officer posts across three advertisements (CRPD/SCO/2026-27/11, 12, and 13), covering Cyber Security, AI, IT Security, HR Management, and Enterprise Technology Architecture roles on Regular and Contract basis.
Q2How many vacancies are there in SBI Specialist Cadre Officer Recruitment 2026?
A
38 vacancies in total - 34 under Advt 11 (Cyber Security/AI roles), 2 under Advt 12 (HR Manager roles), and 2 under Advt 13 (Enterprise Technology Architecture roles).
Q3What is the last date to apply for SBI Specialist Cadre Officer Recruitment 2026?
A
27.08.2026, for all three advertisements. Online registration opened on 07.08.2026.
Q4Can I apply for more than one post?
A
Within Advt 12, candidates may apply for both Manager posts if eligible. Under Advt 11, candidates are not allowed to apply for more than one post. Candidates should check the specific advertisement for cross-advertisement application rules.
Q5What is the age limit for SBI Specialist Cadre Officer posts?
A
It varies by post: 25–35 years for Deputy Manager posts, 28–40 years for Manager posts, 35–45 years for DVP posts, 40–48 years for Vice President, and 42–50 years for Dy. Chief Technology Officer, all assessed as on 31.07.2026.
Q6What is the application fee for SBI Specialist Cadre Officer Recruitment 2026?
A
₹750 for General/EWS/OBC candidates; no fee for SC/ST/PwBD candidates, across all advertisements.
Q7What is the selection process for SBI Specialist Cadre Officer Recruitment 2026?
A
Shortlisting followed by an Interview (100 marks). For contractual posts (DVP, DCTO, VP), a CTC Negotiation stage follows the interview before final selection.
Q8What is the salary/CTC for SBI Specialist Cadre Officer posts?
A
It ranges from Regular-post basic pay scales (e.g. ₹64,820–93,960 for Deputy Manager, ₹85,920–1,05,280 for Manager) to contractual Annual CTC of up to ₹60 lakhs (DVP posts), ₹96 lakhs (Vice President), and ₹170 lakhs (Dy. Chief Technology Officer).
Q9Where will selected candidates be posted?
A
Suggested places of posting are Navi Mumbai (for Manager and DCTO/VP posts) or Mumbai/anywhere in India (for DVP and Deputy Manager posts), at the Bank's sole discretion; the Bank reserves the right to post or transfer officers to any SBI office, associate, or subsidiary in India.
Q10Is prior Government/PSU/Bank experience required to submit an NOC?
A
Yes. Candidates currently serving in Government/Quasi-Government offices, PSUs, Nationalized Banks, Financial Institutions, or SBI Group companies must submit a No Objection Certificate from their employer at the time of interview, failing which their candidature may not be considered.
