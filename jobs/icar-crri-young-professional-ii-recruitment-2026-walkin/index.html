ICAR-CRRI Young Professional II Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

The ICAR-Central Rice Research Institute, Cuttack will hold a walk-in interview for Young Professional-II (YP-II) on 02.12.2026, with reporting at 10:00 AM at ICAR-CRRI, Cuttack. There are two (02) positions under project EAP-488, paying Rs. 42,000/- p.m. (consolidated). Candidates need an M.Sc. in one of the listed subjects and must be aged between 21 and 45 years on the date of interview.

About ICAR-CRRI

ICAR-Central Rice Research Institute is located at Cuttack-753 006, Odisha. The positions are for the project "Frontline Extension System (FES) for Climate Change Induced Livelihood Vulnerability: Interventions Pathways in Hazard-Prone Coastal Ecosystem" (EAP-488). The place of posting is ICAR-CRRI, Cuttack.

Posts and Eligibility

Two Young Professional-II positions are to be filled. The essential qualification is an M.Sc. in Agricultural Extension / Agricultural Economics / Agricultural Statistics / Environmental Science / Agricultural Meteorology / Geography. The pay is Rs. 42,000/- p.m. (consolidated). The age limit is a minimum of 21 years and a maximum of 45 years as on the date of interview, with relaxation as per rule. The age limits are relaxable for SC, ST and OBC candidates as per Govt. of India norms. The notice does not print the amount of relaxation. Candidates should check the official notification for the full eligibility details.

ICAR-CRRI Young Professional-II (YP-II) Job Description

Preparation of Survey Schedule using KoboToolbox software. Climate data extraction and analysis using suitable software like RStudio. GIS mapping in QGIS/ ArcGIS software. Data enumeration using household survey, personal interview and focussed group discussion. Data analysis in statistical software like RStudio/ STATA. Preparation of drafts of research report, research article, research bulletin, power point presentation, etc. Literature review and meta-analysis. Other Project related works as time-to-time assigned by the PI.

Duration of Engagement

The engagement is initially up to March, 2027, which may be extended or curtailed as per the decision of the competent authority. The project runs up to 30.09.2029 or till completion of the project, whichever is earlier. The position is purely temporary and co-terminus with the scheme/project. The service of the appointed candidate will stand terminated automatically after expiry of the project or on completion of the period indicated in the offer of engagement, whichever is earlier. The candidate will not have any right for absorption in ICAR-CRRI/ICAR.

What Happens on the Day

Candidates appear for the walk-in interview on the scheduled date, time and place. If a large number of candidates appear, a screening test may be conducted to shortlist candidates for the interview. No candidate will be entertained strictly after the scheduled reporting time.

ICAR-CRRI Young Professional II Recruitment 2026 : Important Instructions

No TA and DA for appearing in the interview are admissible. Canvassing in any form will render the candidate liable to disqualification for the position. Director, ICAR-CRRI reserves the right to increase / decrease the number of position(s) or to cancel the recruitment process. The Director's decision will be final and binding in all aspects.
