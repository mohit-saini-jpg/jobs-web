Etah Anganwadi Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Only women candidates are eligible for the notified posts. The minimum educational qualification for both the Anganwadi Karyakartri and Sahayika posts is Intermediate (12th pass) or an equivalent qualification. The maximum educational qualification considered is postgraduate level; merit will be prepared on this basis. Marks obtained in High School (10th) will not be included while preparing the merit list. The minimum age for candidates is 18 years and the maximum age is 35 years, as on the cut-off date of 01 July 2026. The date of birth recorded in the High School mark sheet and certificate will be treated as valid for age calculation. No separate age relaxation is available to candidates from Scheduled Caste, Other Backward Class, or General category. The candidate must necessarily be a resident of the ward (in urban areas) or gram panchayat against which the vacant post is notified.

Etah Anganwadi Recruitment 2026: Order of Preference for Selection

For direct recruitment to Anganwadi Karyakartri and Sahayika posts, the following order of preference applies within the same gram sabha/ward: A widow woman from a Below Poverty Line (BPL) family, resident of that gram sabha/ward. A legally divorced/deserted woman from a BPL family, resident of that gram sabha/ward. If no candidate is available under the above two categories, women from BPL families in that gram sabha/ward. If no candidate is available under any of the above, in the same order of preference: a widow woman from an Above Poverty Line (APL) family; a legally divorced/deserted woman from an APL family; and if none is available, women from APL families in that gram sabha/ward. If no eligible candidate is available in any of the above categories within the gram sabha, category-wise selection will be made from within the concerned Nyaya Panchayat. Etah Anganwadi Recruitment 2026: Nature of Post

The Anganwadi Karyakartri and Sahayika posts are entirely temporary and honorarium-based. The Uttar Pradesh State Employee Service Rules do not apply to these posts, and the posts can be terminated at any time as directed by the Government. Two women from the same family will not be appointed as Karyakartri and Sahayika at the same Anganwadi centre.
