Pay and Terms

Selected Junior Residents will be placed at level 10 of the pay matrix, with an entry pay of Rs. 56,100 per month, plus usual allowances as admissible. The appointment is for the vacant posts of Junior Residents for the July 2026 session, valid for the remaining tenure up to 31.12.2026. Original certificates (MBBS/BDS Degree Certificate and Medical Registration Certificate) of candidates joining as Junior Residents will be retained by the Academic Section and returned upon completion of tenure after 31 December 2026 or upon resignation (after obtaining all no-dues from concerned departments/sections). If a candidate desires to resign, one month's notice is required; any shortfall in notice period will be treated as a penalty. In the month of December, 15 days' mandatory notice is required.

Documents Required

The following documents must be submitted along with the physical form at Academic Section-II, AIIMS, New Delhi: DOB certificate / 10th mark sheet / Passing Certificate MBBS mark sheet, Provisional Degree / Degree Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PWBD where applicable; valid OBC-NCL and EWS certificates based on FY 2025-2026 income issued between 01.04.2026 and 31.10.2026) Permanent Delhi Medical Council (DMC) / State Medical Council registration Foreign Medical Graduate Examination (FMGE) Scorecard (for foreign graduates) INI-CET Exam Scorecard with Rank (July 2026) Internship certificate with completion date between 01.11.2023 and 31.10.2026 Disability certificate issued by a duly constituted and authorised Medical Board of the State or Central Govt.
