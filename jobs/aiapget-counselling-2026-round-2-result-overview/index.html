How to Check AIAPGET Round 2 Seat Allotment Result 2026?

  • Visit the official AACCC website at aaccc.gov.in.
  • Open the PG Counselling section.
  • Find the Round 2 Provisional Result links for academic year 2026-27.
  • Select Ayurveda, Siddha, Unani or Homoeopathy, as applicable.
  • Open the corresponding seat allotment PDF.
  • Use the PDF search feature to locate your AIAPGET roll number or rank.
  • Check the Round 2 allotted institution, course, quota, category and remarks.
  • Download the PDF for reference.
  • Follow the final allotment and institute reporting instructions published by AACCC.

Candidates should examine the Round 2 columns carefully because the result documents may also display details of their previous Round 1 allotment.

AIAPGET Round 2 Category or Quota Change Instructions

The official allotment PDFs contain a specific instruction for candidates whose category or quota changes while they remain allotted to the same institution and subject.

Such candidates must obtain an online-generated relieving letter and complete the admission process against the changed category or quota seat. They must also obtain a fresh online-generated admission letter for the revised allotment.

AACCC warns that failure to complete this procedure can result in cancellation of the allotted seat due to non-admission.

Documents for AIAPGET Round 2 Institute Reporting 2026

Candidates reporting to their allotted institutes should follow the document requirements specified in the AACCC PG Information Bulletin and the instructions issued by the allotted institution.

They should keep the applicable examination, qualification, identity, counselling and category documents ready, including their official allotment letter and other supporting certificates as prescribed.
