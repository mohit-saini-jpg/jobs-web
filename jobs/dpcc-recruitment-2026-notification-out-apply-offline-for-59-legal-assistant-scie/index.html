DPCC Recruitment 2026 : Job Overview

Delhi Pollution Control Committee (DPCC), Department of Environment, Government of NCT of Delhi, has issued a rolling advertisement for 59 posts on deputation, including short-term contract, open only to officers holding a regular post in the Central or State Government, a Union Territory, a recognised research institute, an autonomous or statutory body, or a PSU. Candidates must be no older than 56 on the closing date of the phase they apply in. Officers working in private organisations, on contract, retired or unemployed are not considered.

Post-wise Eligibility

"Analogous post" means the same post held on a regular basis in the parent organisation. Where the circular names a pay level, the years of regular service must be in that level or its equivalent. "Related field of engineering" for the engineering posts means Civil, Environment, Chemical, Electrical, Mechanical, ECE, Biomedical Engineering, Water Resources, Remote Sensing, GIS or Hydrology. Senior Environmental Engineer (Level 12, 8 posts): analogous post, or five years' regular service in Level 11; and a Master's degree in engineering in a related field with ten years' experience in pollution control or a related field after the Master's. Environmental Engineer (Level 11, 15 posts): analogous post, or five years' regular service in Level 10; and either a Bachelor's degree in engineering in a related field with eight years' experience in pollution control after the degree, or a Master's

Pay and Tenure

Pay: each post carries the pay matrix level shown in the vacancy table. Pay, allowances and other terms of deputation are regulated by DoPT O.M. No. 6/8/2009-Estt.(Pay II) dated 17 June 2010, as amended, which governs pay fixation and deputation (duty) allowance for officers on deputation. Tenure: one year, extendable up to three years. Deputation including any immediately preceding ex-cadre post should not ordinarily exceed three years in total. Withdrawal: candidates who apply will not be allowed to withdraw their candidature later.

DPCC Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process & Exam Pattern

The circular does not set out a written test or a scoring scheme. It refers to candidates being called for interview, and the NOC requirement for advance-copy applicants is specifically for "appearing in interview". DPCC reserves the right not to fill any post, and the number of posts may change. Selection details will be posted on the DPCC website from time to time.

DPCC Recruitment 2026 : How to Apply Online Step-by-Step

  • 1Applications are accepted only through proper channel, in the prescribed proforma, in English or Hindi.
  • 2Download the advertisement and the application form from dpcc.delhigovt.nic.in under "Office Order & Circulars".
  • 3Fill in the proforma: post applied for, personal and department details, date of retirement, education, a point-by-point statement of how you meet each essential and desirable requirement, employment history with pay level of the post held on a regular basis (enclose your latest salary slip), nature of present employment, feeder-grade status, total emoluments, and supporting information such as publications, awards and professional affiliations. Affix a recent passport-size photograph and sign the declaration.
  • 4Attach self-attested copies of proof of age, educational qualifications, work experience, and SC/ST/OBC/PwD certificate if claiming.
  • 5Have your employer or cadre-controlling authority complete the certification on the last page: that the particulars are correct, you hold the qualifications and experience, you will be relieved if selected, no vigilance or disciplinary case is pending, integrity is certified, the CR dossier or attested ACR/APAR copies for the last five years are enclosed, and whether any major or minor penalty was imposed in the last ten years.
  • 6Your department forwards the application with the signed bio-data, attested APAR copies for five years, integrity certificate, vigilance clearance and penalty statement to: The Administrative Officer, Delhi Pollution Control Committee, 3rd Floor, IT Park, DMRC Building, Shastri Park, Delhi – 110053
  • 7Write the name of the post on the envelope.
  • 8If your department's forwarding may be delayed, you may send an advance copy. In that case you must produce a No Objection Certificate from your department to appear for the interview, with an undertaking to submit all requisite documents before joining. An advance copy does not replace the proper-channel application.
  • 9Applications that are incomplete, not through proper channel, or without attested APAR copies are not considered; there is no second chance within the phase.
  • 10If you already applied to DPCC's earlier rolling advertisement with complete documents, you do not need to apply again.
  • 11Each post has two independent conditions (service and qualification). Check both before asking your department to forward the application, since candidature cannot be withdrawn later.
  • 12No correspondence or representation is entertained about interview calls or selection.
