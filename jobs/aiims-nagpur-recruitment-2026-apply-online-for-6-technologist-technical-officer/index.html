About AIIMS Nagpur

AIIMS Nagpur describes itself as an apex healthcare institute set up by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India, under the Pradhan Mantri Swasthya Suraksha Yojana (PMSSY). It aims to correct regional imbalances in quality tertiary-level healthcare and to make the country self-sufficient in graduate and postgraduate medical education and training. The institute is located at Plot No. 02, Sector-20, MIHAN, Nagpur – 441124.

AIIMS Nagpur Recruitment 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1How many vacancies are there in AIIMS Nagpur Recruitment 2026?
A
There are 6 vacancies: 2 for Technologist (Nuclear Medicine), 2 for Technical Officer (Nuclear Medicine) and 2 for Senior Technical Officer (Nuclear Medicine), all in the UR category.
Q2What is the last date to apply for the AIIMS Nagpur Nuclear Medicine posts?
A
The first cut-off date is 10 November 2026. Applications must reach AIIMS Nagpur through the Google form by 05:00 PM on that day. Cut-off dates for any unfilled vacancies will be notified on the institute's website.
Q3What qualification is needed for the Technologist (Nuclear Medicine) post?
A
You need a B.Sc in Nuclear Medicine Technology from a recognized university/institution. The alternative is a B.Sc in life sciences with a post graduate diploma in Nuclear Medicine (DMRIT/DNMT/DFIT).
Q4What is the eligibility for Senior Technical Officer (Nuclear Medicine)?
A
You need an M.Sc Nuclear Medicine Technology with 60% marks, or an M.Sc with minimum 60% marks plus a post graduate diploma in Nuclear Medicine (DMRIT/DNMT/DFIT) with minimum 50% marks. You also need 02 years' experience.
Q5What is the age limit for AIIMS Nagpur Recruitment 2026?
A
The age limit is "Upto 40 years" for all three posts. Age is counted as completed years on the last date of submission of the application.
Q6What is the salary for the AIIMS Nagpur Nuclear Medicine posts?
A
The consolidated pay is Rs. 56,640/- per month for Technologist, Rs. 89,760/- for Technical Officer and Rs. 1,08,320/- for Senior Technical Officer. The pay does not increase during the contract period.
Q7What is the application fee for AIIMS Nagpur Recruitment 2026?
A
General/OBC/EWS candidates pay a non-refundable fee of Rs. 1,000/- by NEFT to the AIIMS EXAM FEE account at Bank of Baroda, AIIMS Nagpur Campus. PwBD/SC/ST candidates are exempted.
Q8How is the selection done for these AIIMS Nagpur posts?
A
Candidates are shortlisted on qualification, relevant experience and/or criteria set by the Executive Director, and a screening test may be held if many apply. After application scrutiny and document verification, eligible candidates are interviewed and must reach the minimum qualifying standard.
Q9Is the AIIMS Nagpur Nuclear Medicine job permanent?
A
No. The appointment is purely on contract for 11 (Eleven) Months and cannot be regularized at any stage. It may be extended depending on the institute's needs and satisfactory performance.
Q10Do government employees need a No Objection Certificate?
A
Yes. Candidates working in Central/State Government, Semi Government or an Autonomous body must submit a No Objection Certificate from their organisation at the time of interview. Without it, the candidature may be rejected.
