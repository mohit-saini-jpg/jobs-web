About DLRL

Defence Electronics Research Laboratory is a DRDO laboratory under the Ministry of Defence, located at Chandrayangutta Lines, Hyderabad. The Consultant is wanted for specific work on Software Quality Assurance (SQA), verification and validation, and software testing of software products developed by the laboratory, including software for its EW systems.

Where and How to Send

Interested eligible retired officials of Central Government, State Government, PSU or autonomous bodies may submit their applications in the enclosed format at Annexure II, along with copies of the documents referred to in the application. The postal address given in the notice is: The Director, Defence Electronics Research Laboratory, Government of India, Ministry of Defence, Defence Research & Development Organization, Chandrayangutta Lines, Hyderabad - 500 005. The notice does not mention the postal mode, any wording for the envelope or an email address for applications. Its cut-off is for receipt of the application, so the application must reach the address by the cut-off.

DLRL Consultant Job Description

The Terms of Reference (Post Code 2026/DLRL/362, category of work: Technical; domain: Software quality assurance and Independent Verification and validation) list these objectives and tasks. Use knowledge of the software development process and standards and guidelines (DO-178B/C, DGSD, IEEE-12207) for SQA and independent verification and validation of critical electronic systems and software of DLRL for EW systems. Conduct reviews of software development life cycle documentation as per standards and guidelines. Test EW software products to comply with functionality and certification requirements. Verify and audit activities such as configuration management and version control management. Generate relevant information for the software quality assurance plan (SQAP). Guide the work on SQA, verification and validation activities as per standards and guidelines. Collaborate in reviewing SDLC

Q7How is the pay of a retired pensioner fixed?
A
The fixed monthly amount is the pay drawn at the time of retirement minus the basic pension. It stays unchanged for the term of the contract, with no annual increment or percentage increase.
