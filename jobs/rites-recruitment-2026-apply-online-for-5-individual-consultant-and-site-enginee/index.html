RITES Ltd. describes itself in the notice as a multi-disciplinary Engineering and Consultancy organization providing services in all facets of transport infrastructure, building and other related technologies. Its manpower is provided on CV-based assignments, and this engagement is for deployment of personnel at one of its clients' sites. The notice gives the RITES Corporate Office address as Shikhar, Plot No.01, Sector-29, Gurugram-122001.

Pay and Tenure

The payment for services is a fixed monthly amount per post (shown in the vacancy table). The notice also states: Tentative project/work duration is 12 months for each post; the initial tenure of engagement is 12 months, extendable based on the performance of the individual and/or requirement of the project. The engagement may be extended by one year at a time, on the same project only, subject to a satisfactory Annual Performance Review based on Key Performance Indicators. No extension is given beyond the age of 65 years. Where the contract is for more than one year, remuneration may be reviewed annually, based on the escalation received in the client's fee for the previous year. Payment is released within one week after completion of the month, based on attendance registered. There are no statutory benefits such as PF/ESI/Gratuity. Income Tax is deducted at source with TDS certificates

Selection and Interview

The mode of selection is interview only, preceded by document scrutiny. Document Scrutiny/verification: all candidates who submit the online application are provisionally allowed to appear; eligibility for the post applied is verified. Candidates must be present in person or online. Personal Interview: only eligible/shortlisted candidates appear. They are evaluated on technical skills, subject knowledge, experience, communication and interpersonal skills. RITES reserves the right to shortlist the number of candidates. Client approval: CVs of candidates found suitable are shared with the client; only those whose CVs are approved receive an Engagement Letter. Actual deployment depends on award of work/project/company requirement.
