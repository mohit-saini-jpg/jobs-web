14Important Instructions
Sr.TypeStyleItems
1listunordered
  • Any modification(s) in the above datesheets due to a clash, etc., will be notified on the MDU website at the link mdu.ac.in/admin/EventPage.aspx?id=1015. Students must keep themselves updated.
  • Students must report at their respective examination centres at least one hour before the commencement of the examination.
  • No student having a mobile phone or any electronic gadget in his/her possession will be allowed to sit in the examination.
  • Before answering the question paper, candidates should ensure they have been supplied the correct question paper. Complaints in this regard, if any, shall not be entertained after the examination.
  • The use of a simple calculator is allowed. Exchanging of calculator, eraser, pen, pencil, etc. is not allowed.
  • For DDE/CDOE programmes, Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) for students appearing in examinations through Online Mode are available on the university website; students must adhere to the SOP for online exams.
  • For DDE/CDOE programmes, if a student is cautioned/warned by the invigilation staff up to a maximum of 5 times during the examination, the attempt shall be treated as unfair means, and the paper shall be cancelled.
