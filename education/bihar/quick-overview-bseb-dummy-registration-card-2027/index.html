4What Is the Dummy Registration Card 2027?
Sr.TypeText
1paragraphThe dummy registration card is a preliminary record containing a student's registration details, uploaded by BSEB for verification before the final registration card is issued. It is meant for students of the 2025-27 session appearing in the Secondary (Class 10th) and Intermediate (Class 12th) Annual Examination 2027, whose institutions submitted the online registration application, including the required declaration, within the stipulated time.
5Who Can Download the Dummy Registration Card 2027
Sr.TypeStyleItems
1listunordered
  • Students enrolled in the 2025-27 session appearing for the Secondary (Class 10th) Annual Examination 2027.
  • Students enrolled in the 2025-27 session appearing for the Intermediate (Class 12th) Annual Examination 2027.
  • Only those students whose registration application and declaration were submitted by their school/college within the time set by the Board.
6How to Download BSEB Dummy Registration Card 2027
Sr.TypeTextStyleItems
1paragraphStudents and institution heads can download the dummy registration card by following these steps:
2listordered
  • Open the official website: biharboardonline.org for Class 10th (Secondary) or intermediate.biharboardonline.com for Class 12th (Intermediate).
  • Click on the link labelled "Click Here to Download Dummy Registration Card."
  • On the next page, enter the required details: School Code, Student's Name, Father's Name, Date of Birth (and Faculty - applicable only for Class 12th/Intermediate students).
  • Click the Submit button.
  • The dummy registration card will be displayed on screen. Download and save it, or take a printout for further use.

8Process for Institutions (Principals/ Heads)
Sr.TypeText
1paragraphPrincipals of the concerned schools and colleges can log in to the respective portal (biharboardonline.org for Secondary, intermediate.biharboardonline.com for Intermediate) using their User ID and Password starting 25th July 2026 to download the dummy registration cards. They are required to provide two signed and stamped copies to each student for verification. Students also have the option to download their own copy directly from the official websites.
