3What Is the PGCET PWD Verified Provisional List?
Sr.TypeText
1paragraphCandidates applying for PGCET 2026 under the PWD category are required to submit disability certificates, which KEA verifies before confirming eligibility for reservation benefits in the entrance exam and admission process. This provisional list is the outcome of that verification exercise. It is called "provisional" because it is subject to review candidates who disagree with their status can typically raise objections, though the exact objection process and deadline are not mentioned in this document.
4Understanding the Three Status Categories
Sr.TypeStyleItemsText
1listunordered
  • Eligible: The candidate's disability percentage has been verified and accepted, and they can proceed under the PWD category for PGCET 2026.
  • Not Eligible: The candidate's application under the PWD category has not been accepted based on the verification carried out. The document does not state the specific reason for each rejection.
  • Referred: The candidate's case has been sent for further examination or a second opinion rather than a final decision. No disability percentage is listed for these candidates in the document, indicating their verification is still pending.
2paragraphImportant: The document does not explicitly state a minimum disability percentage required for eligibility. While most candidates marked "Eligible" have 40% or higher disability, one candidate with 50% disability is listed as "Not Eligible," and candidates with 40–45% appear in both the "Eligible" and other candidates with lower percentages appear in the "Not Eligible" list. Since no official eligibility criterion or reason is stated in the source document, readers should not assume a fixed percentage cutoff verify the exact criteria from KEA's official PGCET notification.

6How to Check Your PGCET PWD Status
Sr.TypeStyleItems
1listordered
  • Find your application number or registration number (as used during PGCET 2026 registration).
  • Match it against the "Application/Reg No" column in the table above.
  • Note your listed status: Eligible, Not Eligible, or Referred.
  • Cross-check the same details on the official KEA website, since this article is based on a single provisional list document and KEA may issue updates or a final list separately.
7What to Do Based on Your Status
8If You Are Marked "Eligible"
Sr.TypeText
1paragraphYou can proceed as a verified PWD category candidate for PGCET 2026. The document does not mention any further action required for eligible candidates.
9If You Are Marked "Not Eligible"
Sr.TypeText
1paragraphThe document does not specify the reason for non-eligibility or whether an appeal/objection process is available. Candidates in this category should contact KEA directly or check the official website for guidance on re-verification or objection procedures, as this detail is not available in the uploaded list.
10If You Are Marked "Referred"
Sr.TypeText
1paragraphA "Referred" status means your case needs further review before a final decision is made. The document does not state the timeline for this review or the next step. Candidates with this status should watch for further communication from KEA or check the official website for updates.
11Important Instructions
