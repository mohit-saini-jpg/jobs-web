4How to Check Annamalai University CDOE Result 2026 Online
Sr.TypeTextStyleItems
1paragraphFollow these steps to check your Annamalai University Centre for Distance and Online Education result:
2listordered
  • Visit the official CDOE result page: annamalaiuniversity.ac.in
  • Locate the field labelled "Enter Register/Roll Number".
  • Enter your Register Number or Roll Number as issued by Annamalai University.
  • Submit the form to view your result.
  • Download or take a screenshot/printout of the result for future reference.
5Important Instructions for Students
Sr.TypeStyleItems
1listunordered
  • Keep your Register/Roll Number ready before visiting the result portal.
  • Cross-check your programme code against the list above to confirm you are viewing the correct programme's result.
  • For queries related to marks, revaluation, or certificates, students are advised to use the official "May I Help You" support link on the Annamalai University Examinations website.
  • Details such as pass percentage, toppers list, revaluation schedule, and mark sheet dispatch dates are not published on the official result page and are therefore not available.
