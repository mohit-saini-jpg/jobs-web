9JAIIB PPB Marking Scheme and Passing Criteria
Sr.TypeStyleItems
1listunordered
  • The paper carries 100 marks for 100 questions, with each question worth 1 mark.
  • There is no negative marking, so candidates should attempt every question.
  • To pass, a candidate needs a minimum of 50 marks out of 100 overall, or 45 marks with at least 50% marks in each of the four modules.
10How to Prepare for the JAIIB PPB Paper?
Sr.TypeTextStyleItems
1paragraphThe following preparation strategy is recommended for the JAIIB PPB paper:
2listordered
  • Focus on high-weightage modules first. Identify the modules that have contributed the most questions in previous years and prioritise them early in your preparation.
  • Go through previous year questions. Reviewing past papers helps you understand the pattern and recurring topics within each module.
  • Rely on official study material. Use the official IIBF study material along with good-quality notes or cheat sheets that summarise key points. Official material is available at www.iibf.org.in.
  • Practice a large number of MCQs. Solve chapter-wise questions and take full module tests to build accuracy and speed.
  • Analyse mistakes after every mock test. Identify weak areas and revise them specifically rather than re-reading everything equally.
