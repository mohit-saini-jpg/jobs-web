12How Second Merit List Cut-Offs Differ From the First List
Sr.TypeTextStyleItems
1paragraphSecond merit list cut-offs at Maharani College are generally lower than the first list because they are recalculated based on seats that remain vacant after first-round document verification. Candidates who did not qualify in the first round, or who applied after the first list closed, get a fresh chance to check their eligibility here.
2paragraphWhere a category shows "Nil" or a dash, it usually means either:
3listunordered
  • All available seats in that category were already filled during the first round, or
  • No further applications are pending consideration against that category and subject combination
4paragraphCandidates should keep monitoring subsequent lists, since cut-offs typically continue to ease with each round as higher-ranked candidates confirm admission elsewhere.
13What to Do If You Qualify in the Second List
Sr.TypeTextStyleItems
1paragraphCandidates whose aggregate marks meet or exceed the cut-off for their category and chosen subject/programme should complete admission formalities promptly to secure their seat.

  • Visit the Maharani College campus with original documents for verification

  • Retain the admission slip and receipt for future reference
3paragraphIf your marks fall short of the second-list cut-off, a further round (third merit list or spot round) may follow, depending on seat vacancy in your category and subject.
