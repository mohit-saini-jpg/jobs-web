5How to Check MDSU Ajmer Semester Result 2026 Online
Sr.TypeTextStyleItems
1paragraphFollow these steps to view and download your BA, BSc, or BCom semester result from the official MDSU portal:
2listordered
  • Visit the official MDSU result portal at erp.mdsuexam.org (or mdsuexam.org).
  • Click on the "Exam and Result" or "Result Panel" option on the homepage.
  • Select your result type - UG, PG, or Other Courses - and choose the relevant class/course (BA, BSc, or BCom).
  • If your college follows the NEP pattern, click on "Panel for NEP Classes" and log in with your student credentials.
  • Enter your roll number and any other requested details (such as date of birth).
  • Click "Search Result" or "Submit" to view your result on screen.
  • Download the result or take a printout for your records.
3paragraphNote: MDSU does not currently offer a name-wise result search; results can only be checked using your roll number and date of birth.
6What If There's an Error in Your MDSU Marksheet?
Sr.TypeTextStyleItems
1paragraphThe result available online is provisional. If you notice a discrepancy - such as missing marks, an incorrect subject, or a wrong name - you should act quickly. Common steps include:
2listunordered
  • Take a printout of the provisional result/marksheet showing the error.
  • Contact your college examination cell or the MDSU Controller of Examinations office directly.
  • Apply for revaluation or scrutiny if your university/college permits it for the affected subject.
  • Collect your original marksheet from your college once it is issued, and verify all details carefully against your provisional result.
