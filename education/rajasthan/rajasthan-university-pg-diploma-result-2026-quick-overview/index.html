4Courses for Which Results Have Been Declared
Sr.TypeTextStyleItems
1paragraphRajasthan University has released results for three one-year postgraduate diploma programmes offered by the university. Here is what each course covers, in brief:
2listunordered
  • P.G. Diploma in Labour Law and Labour Welfare: A diploma programme focused on labour legislation and industrial relations, typically pursued by law graduates or working professionals in HR and labour administration.
  • P.G. Diploma in Criminology & Criminal Administration: A diploma programme covering criminology, criminal justice administration, and related legal and sociological concepts.
  • P.G. Diploma in Taxation Law and Practice: A diploma programme covering direct and indirect tax law and its practical application, generally pursued by commerce or law graduates.
5How to Check Rajasthan University PG Diploma Result 2026 Online
Sr.TypeStyleItems
1listordered
  • Visit the official Rajasthan University result website at result.uniraj.ac.in.
  • On the homepage, look under the "Postgraduate" menu and select the relevant category (Law, for the Labour Law and Criminology diplomas; the applicable category for Taxation Law and Practice).
  • Find your specific exam from the results list, for example "P.G. Dip. Course in Labour Law, Labour Exam.-2026."
  • Click on the result link for that exam.
  • Enter your roll number (as printed on your admit card) or the details requested on the result page.
  • Submit the form to view your result.
  • Download or take a screenshot of the result for your records, and print it if needed.

8Important Instructions for Students
Sr.TypeStyleItems
1listunordered
  • Keep your admit card safe, since the roll number on it is required to check the result.
  • Cross-check your name, father's/mother's name, and subject-wise marks carefully once the result is displayed.
  • The online result is provisional; the original marksheet/certificate is issued separately by the university as per its process.
  • In case of any discrepancy in the result, contact the Rajasthan University Examination Section or Student Information Centre.
  • Avoid third-party or unofficial websites for checking results always use the official Rajasthan University portal.
