4Steps to Check NEHU Exam Result 2026
Sr.TypeTextStyleItems
1paragraphStudents can use the following steps to check their NEHU Exam Result 2026 in PDF format and, where applicable, download detailed marksheets.
2listordered
  • Open the NEHU PG results portal in your browser at http://exams.nehu.ac.in/PGresults or directly click on the specific result link for your course and semester from the table above.
  • On the PG results page, scroll through the list of recently declared results to locate the notification matching your programme, such as: Education (JEC/Synod/NEHU) 3rd Semester Regular 24 Batch, December 2025 MSW (SEC) 1st or 3rd Semester Regular, December 2025 English (Tura) 1st Semester Regular 2025 Batch ACE (LLE) 1st Semester Re-Appear 2023 Batch Zoology 1st Semester Re-Appear 24 Batch.
  • Education (JEC/Synod/NEHU) 3rd Semester Regular 24 Batch, December 2025
  • MSW (SEC) 1st or 3rd Semester Regular, December 2025
  • English (Tura) 1st Semester Regular 2025 Batch
  • ACE (LLE) 1st Semester Re-Appear 2023 Batch
  • Zoology 1st Semester Re-Appear 24 Batch.
  • Click on the relevant result link; the result PDF will open in a new tab or download to your device.
  • Use the search function (Ctrl+F on Windows or Command+F on Mac) within the PDF and type your Roll Number to quickly find your result entry.
  • Check your name, roll number, course, semester, batch and subject-wise marks or grade/grade points, as well as the overall result status (Pass/Fail/Re-appear).
  • Save or print the PDF for future reference. For an individual marksheet (if enabled for your course), visit the Logisys result portal at results.logisys.org/nehu, select the exam and semester, and enter your roll number to view your detailed marks.
  • If you notice any discrepancy in your details or marks, contact your department or NEHU examination branch and follow the official procedure for clarification, correction or revaluation.
