4What Is the TG PGECET Round 1 Provisional Seat Allotment List?
Sr.TypeText
1paragraphThe Round 1 provisional seat allotment list is a college-wise list prepared by TGCHE that shows which candidates have been provisionally allotted a seat in Phase 1 of counselling. It is called "provisional" because the allotment is confirmed only after the candidate reports to the allotted college with original documents within the given deadline.
5How Is the TG PGECET 2026 Seat Allotment Decided?
Sr.TypeTextStyleItems
1paragraphTGCHE prepares the Phase 1 allotment list college-wise based on the following factors:
2listunordered
  • TG PGECET rank or GATE/GPAT score: Within each category, GATE/GPAT-qualified candidates are ranked ahead of TG PGECET rank holders.
  • Category/reservation status of the candidate (OC, BC, SC, ST, PH, etc.)
  • Local/Non-Local status, applied as per Osmania University (O.U.) Area norms - broadly 85% local and 15% unreserved seats
  • Web options (college and course preferences) submitted by the candidate during Phase 1
  • Seat availability in each college and course at the time of allotment

8How to Check TG PGECET 2026 Round 1 Seat Allotment Result
Sr.TypeStyleItems
1listordered
  • Visit the official website: pgecetadm.tgche.ac.in
  • Log in using your hall ticket number and rank (TG PGECET rank or GATE/GPAT score, as applicable)
  • Go to the "Seat Allotment Status" section on your candidate dashboard
  • View your Round 1 provisional seat allotment details, including the allotted college and course (if any)

9How to Report to the Allotted College After Round 1 Allotment
Sr.TypeStyleItems
1listordered
  • Download and take a printout of the provisional seat allotment order from pgecetadm.tgche.ac.in
  • Report in person to the allotted college within the stipulated reporting window (by July 30, 2026)
  • Carry all original certificates along with the required sets of attested photocopies
  • Complete document verification and pay the applicable seat acceptance/tuition fee at the college as instructed
10Important Instructions for Candidates
Sr.TypeStyleItems
1listunordered
  • If you do not report to your allotted college within the deadline, your allotted seat is automatically cancelled and made available for the next round of allotment.
  • If you are not allotted a seat in Round 1, you remain eligible to participate in subsequent counselling phases, based on vacancy.
  • TG PGECET counselling does not operate a formal freeze/float/slide upgrade mechanism; after Round 1 allotment, the choice is largely binary - report and accept the seat, or skip it and try again in the next phase.
  • If you withdraw after accepting a seat, the fee already paid is not fully refunded; a penalty is deducted as specified in the official counselling brochure.
  • Always cross-verify dates, fee amounts, and procedures from the official TGCHE notification, as these are subject to revision.
