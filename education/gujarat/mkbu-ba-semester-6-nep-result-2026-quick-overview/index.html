6What Does "Withheld" and "Cancel" Mean in MKBU Results?
Sr.TypeTextStyleItems
1paragraphThe notification uses these categories without further explanation:
2listunordered
  • Pass: The candidate has cleared the examination.
  • Cancel: The candidate's result for this examination has been cancelled.
  • Withheld: The candidate's result is on hold and has not been finalized.
3paragraphThe specific reason for a cancelled or withheld result (such as pending fees, discrepancy, or malpractice) is not stated in this notification. Affected candidates should contact the Academic Section of M.K. Bhavnagar University directly for case-specific information, as this detail is not available in the official document.
