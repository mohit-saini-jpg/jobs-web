10Exam Timing
Sr.TypeText
1paragraphThe exam timing printed across four of the five notifications (S.No. 477, 478, 479, and 480) reads "9.00 am To 12.00 a.m." This appears to be a printing inconsistency in the official circulars, since exams starting at 9:00 AM would logically end at 12:00 noon (12:00 PM), not 12:00 AM (midnight). We are reporting this exactly as printed in the source documents rather than assuming the intended correction - candidates should verify the exact timing with the university or their college before the exam. Only the M.Ed. (2-year RIE) notification (S.No. 481) has an unambiguous timing: 11:00 AM to 2:00 PM.
