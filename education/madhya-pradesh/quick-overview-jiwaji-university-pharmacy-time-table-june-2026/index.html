10Important Instructions for Students
Sr.TypeStyleItems
1listunordered
  • Exams for all three programmes begin on 4 August 2026 and are held between 11:00 A.M. and 2:00 P.M.
  • B. Pharmacy IVth and VIth Semester papers are scheduled on the same dates in several instances (for example, 5, 7, 11, and 13 August 2026); students should check their own semester's paper code carefully.
  • The notifications advise students to note their respective exam centre - B. Pharmacy students appear at S.O.S. in Pharmaceutical Sciences, while M. Pharmacy students appear at the Institute of Pharmaceutical Science.
  • Any change or corrigendum to the time table, if issued later by Jiwaji University, will supersede the dates listed here.
