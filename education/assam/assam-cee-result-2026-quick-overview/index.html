4How to Download Assam CEE Rank Card 2026: Step-by-Step Process

1paragraphDownloading the rank card is a straightforward login-based process that takes only a couple of minutes once you have your credentials ready. Here's exactly how to do it:
2listunordered
  • Go to the official ASTU website, astu.ac.in, or the result portal at astu.formsrec.in.
  • Look for the "Assam CEE Result 2026" or "Download Rank Card" notification on the homepage and click it.

6Assam CEE 2026 Merit List and Tie-Breaking Rules
Sr.TypeTextStyleItems
1paragraphAfter individual results are released, ASTU compiles a consolidated merit list, which is what actually determines counselling eligibility and seat allotment not just your raw score. Since the exam is held in a single shift, no percentile normalisation is applied; ranking is based directly on marks scored.
2paragraphIn cases where two or more candidates secure identical total marks, ASTU follows a defined tie-breaking order:
3listunordered
  • First, the PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics) aggregate marks of Class 12 are compared.
  • If the tie persists, the combined aggregate of Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and English in Class 12 is considered.
  • If candidates remain tied even after this, the matter is resolved at the discretion of the Chairman of the Selection Committee.
4paragraphThe merit list is generally published both as an overall list and separately for each reserved category.
7What Happens After Assam CEE Result 2026: Counselling and Seat Allotment
Sr.TypeTextStyleItems
1paragraphClearing the Assam CEE 2026 entrance exam is only the first step qualifying candidates must still register for counselling to actually be allotted a college and branch. This stage is typically coordinated through the Directorate of Technical Education (DTE), Assam, working alongside ASTU.
2paragraphThe general counselling process includes:
3listunordered
  • Registration on the official counselling portal using rank card credentials
  • Document upload and verification (Class 10/12 marksheets, category certificates, photographs, signature, etc.)
  • Submission of college and branch preferences in order of priority
  • Payment of the counselling registration fee through the prescribed online mode
  • Seat allotment based on rank, preferences submitted, and seat availability
  • Confirmation of admission within the stipulated deadline to avoid cancellation of the allotted seat
4paragraphBased on current updates, the counselling and seat allotment process for Assam CEE 2026 is expected to begin in July 2026, though candidates should keep checking astu.ac.in for the confirmed schedule, as dates can shift.
8Assam CEE 2026 Exam Snapshot - Eligibility and Pattern
Sr.TypeTextStyleItems
1paragraphFor context, it helps to understand what the Assam CEE 2026 exam itself looked like, especially for candidates checking results on behalf of younger siblings or planning for next year. Assam CEE is a state-level entrance test conducted annually for admission into B.Tech programmes at engineering colleges across Assam, including institutions like Assam Engineering College, Jorhat Engineering College, and Bongaigaon Engineering College.
2paragraphKey features of the exam:
3listunordered
  • Eligibility required candidates to be permanent residents of Assam, having passed (or appeared in) Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and English, securing a minimum aggregate of 50% (45% for SC, 40% for ST candidates).
  • The age criterion was 17 to 21 years as of 1st August 2026, with a 3-year relaxation for SC/ST candidates.
  • The exam consisted of multiple-choice questions, with 40 questions each from Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry, for a total of 480 marks.
  • It was conducted offline in OMR format across exam centres including Guwahati, Dibrugarh, Jorhat, Silchar, Tezpur, Nagaon, Nalbari, Lakhimpur, Diphu, and Bongaigaon.
4paragraphThis background is useful for understanding how your rank and score translate into your competitive standing for counselling.
