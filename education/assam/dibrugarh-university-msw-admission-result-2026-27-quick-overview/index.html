3Important Dates for Dibrugarh University MSW Admission 2026
Sr.TypeStyleItemsText
1listunordered
  • 20 July 2026 (Monday): Document verification for provisionally selected candidates before the Admission Board, 10:00 a.m. to 12:30 p.m.

16Required Documents for Dibrugarh University MSW Admission Verification
Sr.TypeTextStyleItems
1paragraphAll provisionally selected and waiting list candidates must bring the following documents in original, along with one set of self-attested photocopies, on 20 July 2026:
2listordered
  • Original marksheets and pass certificates (from HSLC onwards).
  • Valid caste certificate, including a Non-Creamy Layer certificate for OBC category candidates, wherever applicable.
  • Valid EWS certificate for candidates selected under the EWS category.
  • Fitness certificate issued by a Registered Medical Practitioner.
  • One hard copy of the downloaded application form along with one set of self-attested photocopies of all relevant documents.
  • Proof of admission fee payment made online through the Dibrugarh University ERP Portal.
  • A valid debit card, credit card, or internet banking facility to make the online admission fee payment.
  • A mobile number registered with the bank account used for online payment, since the OTP for the transaction will be sent to that number.
17How to Proceed After Selection
Sr.TypeStyleItems
1listunordered
  • Report to the Centre for Social Work Studies, Dibrugarh University on 20 July 2026 within the allotted time slot.
  • Carry all original documents listed above along with self-attested photocopies.
  • Pay the admission fee online through the Dibrugarh University ERP Portal using a valid debit card, credit card, or internet banking.
  • Ensure the mobile number linked to the payment bank account is active to receive the OTP.
18Important Instructions
Sr.TypeStyleItemsText
1listunordered
  • Failure to report for document verification within the stipulated time on 20 July 2026 will lead to cancellation of candidature without further notice.
  • Waiting list candidates will be considered strictly in order of merit, only against vacant seats, if any.
  • A separate notification for admission under the Endowment Category (3 seats) will be issued on 20 July 2026.
  • The notification is signed by Prof. Nitul Kumar Gogoi, Chairperson, Centre for Social Work Studies, Dibrugarh University.
2paragraphNote: The official notification does not specify the admission fee amount, the exact ERP Portal link, or the venue/room number for document verification. This information is not available in the source document; candidates should check the official Dibrugarh University website or contact the Centre for Social Work Studies directly for these details.
