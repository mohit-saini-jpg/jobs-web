6How to Check and Download AP SBTET Diploma Result 2026
Sr.TypeTextStyleItems
1paragraphFollow these steps to check and download your AP SBTET Diploma Result 2026 online:
2listordered
  • Visit the official AP SBTET website: sbtet.ap.gov.in and apsbtet.net
  • Click on the "Results" or "Examination" tab on the homepage.
  • Select the relevant scheme link, for example "Diploma C23 Results," "Diploma C16/ C20 Results," or "Pharmacy ER-91/ ER-2020 Results."
  • Enter your PIN or Hall Ticket Number as requested on the results page.
  • Enter your date of birth in the specified format.
  • Click "Submit" or "View Result" to display your result on screen.
  • Download and save the result page as a PDF for future reference.
