4Who Conducts and Declares the AP EAMCET Result?
Sr.TypeText
1paragraphThis section clarifies the authorities behind the exam and the result, since candidates often search for this distinction separately.
2paragraphAP EAMCET, officially renamed AP EAPCET (Engineering, Agriculture and Pharmacy Common Entrance Test), is conducted by Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University (JNTU), Kakinada, on behalf of the Andhra Pradesh State Council of Higher Education (APSCHE). JNTU Kakinada handles the technical declaration of the result and rank card, while APSCHE governs the overall admission and counselling process for engineering, pharmacy, and agriculture courses across the state.
