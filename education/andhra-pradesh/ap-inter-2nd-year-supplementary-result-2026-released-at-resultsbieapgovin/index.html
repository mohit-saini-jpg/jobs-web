3How to Check AP Inter 2nd Year Supplementary Result 2026 Online
Sr.TypeTextStyleItems
1paragraphStudents need just their hall ticket number and date of birth to retrieve their result online. This section walks through the exact sequence of steps to download the AP Inter 2nd Year supply marks memo from the official portal.
2listunordered
  • Visit the official BIEAP results website at resultsbie.ap.gov.in or bie.ap.gov.in.
  • Locate and click on the link labelled "AP Inter 2nd Year Supplementary Result 2026."
  • Enter your hall ticket number and date of birth in the format requested (DD/MM/YYYY).
  • Click on "Submit" or "Get Result" to retrieve your scorecard.
  • Your AP Inter 2nd Year supply marks memo will display on screen with subject-wise marks and pass/fail status.
  • Download the result and save a PDF copy, or take a printout for future reference.

8What to Do After Checking the AP Inter 2nd Year Supplementary Result
Sr.TypeText
1paragraphOnce the result is verified, students who pass should know the next steps, while those who don't clear the exam need to understand their options. This section explains both scenarios.
2paragraphStudents who have cleared the supplementary exam should download and save their online marksheet, then collect the original Pass Certificate-cum-Memorandum of Marks from their respective junior college once the board issues the official documents. The original marks memo and pass certificate are required for admission into degree colleges, professional courses, and counselling rounds. Students who remain unsatisfied with their marks can apply for recounting or revaluation as per BIEAP's separate notification on application dates and fees. Students who still do not meet the minimum passing criteria will need to reappear in the next available examination cycle.
