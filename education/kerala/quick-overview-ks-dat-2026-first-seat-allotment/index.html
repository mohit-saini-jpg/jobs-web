4KS DAT 2026 Selection and Seat Allotment Process
Sr.TypeTextStyleItems
1paragraphAdmission to the B.Des programme through KS DAT follows two broad routes:
2listordered
  • Government seats: Allotted purely on the basis of the candidate's rank in the KS DAT 2026 merit list, prepared by the Director of Technical Education, Thiruvananthapuram.
  • Management/self-financing seats: Private, self-financing colleges may consider the KS DAT score, and in some cases scores from national-level design exams such as NID DAT, UCEED, or NATA, for management quota seats.
3paragraphThe overall seat allotment works as follows:
4listunordered
  • Candidates log in to the official portal using their registered credentials.
  • Eligible candidates submit their preferred list of colleges and courses (choice filling).
  • Seats are allotted based on the candidate's rank, submitted preferences, and category-wise seat availability.
  • Allotted candidates must complete document verification and fee payment within the window specified by the authority to confirm their seat.
  • Candidates who do not get their preferred option, or who wish to be considered for a better option, may remain in the process for subsequent allotment rounds, subject to the rules of the counselling process.
5How to Check KS DAT 2026 First Seat Allotment Result
Sr.TypeStyleItems
1listordered
  • Visit the official website: lbsapplications.kerala.gov.in.
  • Look for the KS DAT 2026 link or the candidate login section on the homepage.
  • Log in using your registered credentials (such as application number/email ID and password).
  • Navigate to the seat allotment or "First Allotment Result" section.
  • View your allotment status, allotted college, and course details.
  • Download and save/print the allotment memo for further admission steps.
6What to Do After Checking the KS DAT 2026 Seat Allotment
Sr.TypeStyleItems
1listunordered
  • Read the allotment memo carefully to confirm the allotted college, course, and category.
  • Check the deadline mentioned on the portal for document verification and fee payment.
  • Keep all original certificates and documents ready before visiting the allotted institution or completing online verification, as instructed.
  • If not satisfied with the current allotment, check whether the option to retain the choice for the next round, upgrade, or opt out is available, as per the counselling rules displayed on the portal.
7Important Instructions for Candidates
Sr.TypeStyleItems
1listunordered
  • Always cross-check dates and instructions on the official portal, since schedules can be revised by the authority.
  • Keep your login credentials (application number and password) secure and readily available.
  • Complete document verification and fee payment strictly within the window specified for allotted candidates, since delays can result in the seat being cancelled.
  • For any discrepancy in the allotment result, contact the LBS Centre for Science and Technology through the official communication channels listed on the portal.
