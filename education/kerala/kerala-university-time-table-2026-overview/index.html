8Important Instructions
Sr.TypeStyleItems
1listunordered
  • Reporting time, hall ticket requirements, and conduct-of-exam instructions are not specified in either notification as provided - candidates should refer to the official portal for these details.
  • Note the different reporting timings for the BA Honours English exam: 1:30 PM-4:30 PM on all days except Friday, and 2:00 PM-5:00 PM on Friday (11.09.2026).
  • B.Tech candidates should note all four exam sessions are scheduled for 09:30 AM-12:30 PM.
  • Both notifications are signed by the Controller of Examinations, University Buildings, Thiruvananthapuram.
