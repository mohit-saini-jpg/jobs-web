6How to Download the Chhattisgarh NEET UG Merit List 2026
Sr.TypeStyleItems
1listordered
  • Visit the official Chhattisgarh medical counselling portal.
  • Look for the link titled "CG-NEET UG (MBBS/BDS) Round 1 Merit List" or "NRI Eligible List" under the notifications/documents section.
  • Click the link to open or download the PDF.
  • Use Ctrl+F (or Cmd+F on Mac) to search your roll number or application number within the PDF instead of scrolling manually.
  • Verify your name, category, NEET score, percentile, and state merit rank against your own records.
  • Save or print a copy of the page showing your entry for future reference during document verification and reporting.
7Important Instructions for Candidates
Sr.TypeStyleItems
1listunordered
  • Registering only for MCC's All India Quota counselling does not make a candidate eligible for any Chhattisgarh state quota seat. The two processes are separate and must be completed independently.
  • Choices, once locked during choice filling, generally cannot be edited, so candidates should rank colleges by genuine preference before locking.
  • Missing the reporting window at the allotted institute typically results in forfeiture of the allotted seat.
  • Keep scanned copies of all required documents ready in the file format and size specified by the portal before starting registration, to avoid last-minute upload issues.
  • Cross-check all official dates directly on the DME Chhattisgarh counselling portal, as counselling schedules can be revised or extended.
