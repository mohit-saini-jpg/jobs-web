5How to Check CG PAT 2026 Result and Download the Final Answer Key
Sr.TypeStyleItems
1listordered
  • Visit the official website: vyapamcg.cgstate.gov.in
  • Look for the notification titled "Release of Final Answer Key and Result of PAT-2026" on the homepage or notice board section.
  • Open the linked PDF to read the official notification in full.
  • Navigate to the Result section for PAT/PVPT and log in using the credentials specified in the notification (typically roll number/application number along with date of birth or a security code, as per the board's standard process).
  • View and download your scorecard/result.
  • Separately download the Final Answer Key PDF, if provided as a distinct document, to cross-check your responses.
  • Save and print a copy of both documents for future reference during counselling and document verification.
6Important Instructions for Candidates
Sr.TypeStyleItems
1listunordered
  • Rely only on the official website, vyapamcg.cgstate.gov.in, for the result, final answer key, and any further updates. Do not trust unofficial or third-party websites claiming to host the result ahead of the official portal.
  • Keep your login credentials (roll number/application number, date of birth, password/security code) ready before attempting to check the result.
  • Read the complete official notification PDF for any specific instructions, deadlines, or annexures mentioned by the board.
  • Watch the official website for the merit list and counselling schedule, which is typically announced after the result.
