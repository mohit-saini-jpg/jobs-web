4Who Was Eligible for CCMN 2026 Special Counselling
Sr.TypeTextStyleItems
1paragraphCCMN 2026 special round 1 counselling was open to candidates who had already registered for the special CCMN counselling round. Broadly, the general CCMN 2026 eligibility criteria applicable through the counselling process are:
2listunordered
  • Candidates belonging to General, General-EWS, and OBC categories are required to have passed a BSc or equivalent qualifying degree with at least 60% aggregate marks or 6.5 CGPA.
  • Candidates belonging to reserved categories (SC, ST, PWD) are required to have at least 55% aggregate marks or 6.0 CGPA.
  • Candidates in the final year of their qualifying degree were also allowed to apply, provided they submit their final mark sheet as required.
  • For certain programmes at a few participating institutes, special eligibility criteria and restrictions apply — these are listed separately on the CCMN portal under "View Special Eligibility/Restrictions."
  • A valid IIT JAM 2026 score is mandatory for participation in CCMN 2026 counselling.
5Freeze, Float, Slide or Withdraw: What Candidates Can Do
Sr.TypeTextHeadersRows
1paragraphAfter checking the special round 1 allotment result, allotted candidates must choose one of the willingness options within the given deadline:
2table
  • Option
  • What It Means
    • Freeze
    • Candidate accepts the currently allotted seat and confirms admission to that institute/programme; no further participation in upcoming rounds.
    • Float
    • Candidate accepts the current seat but remains open to a better allotment in subsequent rounds.
    • Slide
    • Candidate wants to be considered for a different programme within the same or another institute in later rounds while keeping current allotment as backup.
    • Withdraw
    • Candidate withdraws entirely from the allotted seat and the counselling process.
3paragraphCandidates must also complete online document upload for verification within the same window, since institutes will be verifying submitted documents during this period.
6How to Check the CCMN 2026 Special Round 1 Seat Allotment Result
Sr.TypeStyleItems
1listordered
  • Visit the official website: ccmn.admissions.nic.in.
  • Click on the "CCMN 2026 Special Round 1 Seat Allotment Result" link on the homepage.
  • Log in using your registered JAM 2026 credentials (registration ID and password).
  • View your allotted institute, programme, and category status.
  • Download/save the allotment result for your records.
  • Proceed to upload the required documents and submit your willingness (freeze/float/slide) or withdrawal option before the deadline.
7What Happens After Special Round 1
Sr.TypeText
1paragraphThe CCMN allotment process for the special rounds is typically followed by a Special Round 2 and then a National Spot Round (NSR), which is the last counselling round in the CCMN cycle. Candidates who take admission through any round are usually required to complete physical reporting at the allotted institute after the process concludes, as per institute-specific instructions.
8Important Instructions for Candidates
Sr.TypeStyleItems
1listunordered
  • Keep your JAM 2026 registration ID and password ready before logging in to check the result.
  • Complete document upload, verification, and willingness options strictly within the notified deadlines to avoid cancellation of the allotted seat.
  • Regularly check the official CCMN 2026 website for updates, since institute-wise seat vacancy figures may be revised as candidates upgrade from earlier rounds.
  • For queries specific to a participating institute's admission process, use the contact details listed on the CCMN portal for that institute.
